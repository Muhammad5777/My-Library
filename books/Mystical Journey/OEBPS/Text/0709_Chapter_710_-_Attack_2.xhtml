However, that monster’s fluid was still sticking onto Nasira, and even after she entered her dreamland, she was not able to completely get rid of it. It was horrifying because this meant that the tentacle monster’s fluid had a certain degree of spiritual energy polluting toxicity.

Bang! Poof!
