"I'm not abandoning it, but I won't learn it further." He whispered.

'Isn't that the same as abandoning it?' Black Sethe felt hopeless.

In front of Garen was a black copper mysterious mask.

This mask was a relic that had been able to provide him with the most potential points. He found it and placed it by his side.

It had been at least ten days since he fought with Nadia and Garen had gathered the Void cores that he had obtained these past few days. This item was strangely solid as if it had some sort of unique effect. It surprisingly had amazing effects in treating the pollution and damage caused by the void creatures.
