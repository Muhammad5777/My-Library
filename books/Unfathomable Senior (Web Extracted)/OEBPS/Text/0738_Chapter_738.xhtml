Chapter 738

“Hah, are you sure about this? You still have time to bow your head and surrender before you get hurt~”

“Why would I bow my head? This time you are going down!”

“Did little Jun’er finally grow a pair? I hope you’ll do better than last time.”

“I’ll have you eat those words and stop calling me little!”

“Make me then!”

Two young fighters were staring at each other. One was a beautiful young girl with light pink hair that stood out of the crowd. Her hair was a delicate shade of pink, reminiscent of cherry blossoms in full bloom, and it cascaded down to her shoulders with a silky, radiant sheen. Her skin had a slightly tan complexion, giving her a healthy and sun-kissed appearance.

Her opponent was a young man of around the same age who looked to be in his later teenage years. His white hair and striking figure gave him a somewhat otherworldly charm that enticed the onlooking ladies to cheer him on. His hair was long and flowed loosely around his shoulders, moving like a soft waterfall of silk.

“Young Master Jun, we believe in you!”

“Young Lady Xiu, take it easy on the young master, we all know that you are the best!”

The young lady, Zhang Xiu, was known for her exceptional martial skills, which had earned her the admiration and respect of many in Spirit Spring City. She was often considered the most promising disciple of the United Element Sect. Her mastery of elemental control was unparalleled, and she had a natural affinity for water and wind, which allowed her to perform incredible feats of rapid water manipulation. Her water-based martial arts were graceful and mesmerizing, yet deadly in their precision. She was a splitting image of her mother, Zhang Liena.

On the other hand, Zhang Jun, Zhang Dong's son was more similar to his father and proficient in both lightning and holy elemental attacks. He had been diligently practicing martial arts under the guidance of his father and the sect's most skilled warriors. Although he had come a long way, he was still considered an underdog compared to Xiu. The crowd watched with bated breath as the two young warriors squared off, ready to witness a thrilling contest.

They both were standing on a large circular arena that had risen up into the air. It was much larger than a standard football field at around ten times the size. It was encased in a grand translucent barrier that allowed the onlookers to peek inside. Then to make things even clearer there were massive screens floating in the background and close to the stands the viewers were residing in.

The battle began with an explosion of energy as Xiu lunged forward with a graceful, fluid motion. Her control over water was truly a sight to behold, as she sent torrents of water spiraling towards Zhang Jun, who barely managed to evade by creating an electrical barrier around himself.

The crowd cheered as the two young warriors clashed, each displaying their unique skills. Xiu danced like a water nymph, her movements flowing like a river, while Zhang Jun moved with the agility and precision of a lightning tiger. Sparks of electricity crackled around him as he deftly avoided Xiu's watery attacks.

The battle raged on, with neither giving an inch. Xiu continued to use her water manipulation to her advantage, creating intricate patterns and deadly strikes, while Zhang Jun countered with swift lightning bolts that danced around the arena. Their elemental powers clashed and intertwined, creating a breathtaking display of beauty and power.

As the battle continued, it was clear that Zhang Jun had improved significantly since their last encounter. He was more agile and had a better understanding of his elemental abilities. The audience watched in amazement as he executed a complex lightning and holy elemental combination attack, creating a dazzling display of power that left Xiu momentarily stunned.

But Xiu was not one to be underestimated. With a determined look in her eyes, she summoned a massive water dragon that surged towards Zhang Jun, threatening to engulf him. However, Zhang Jun was not to be outdone. He unleashed a powerful burst of holy energy that encased him in a radiant glow. The technique allowed him to enhance his power several times as he pushed the water dragon back.

“They are improving each day but…”

“Yes, Jun is being a bit too hasty, he isn’t conserving his spiritual energy enough and also has less of it than Xiu.”

While the two youths were fighting their parents and other members of the sect looked on from afar. Some years had passed since the creation of the new Golden Empire that this sect now resided over. Their father Zhang Dong along with his lovely wife Liena were looking at their children who were competing with each other. The older sister still remained superior but her brother was quickly catching up.

As the battle between Zhang Xiu and Zhang Jun continued, their parents, Zhang Dong and Zhang Liena, couldn't help but feel a sense of pride and nostalgia. Watching their children grow and develop their elemental abilities brought back memories of their own journeys and struggles. Zhang Dong, the Patriarch of the United Element Sect and the Emperor of the Golden Empire, looked at his wife with a fond smile.

"Our children have come a long way, haven't they? It seems like just yesterday when they were still in their early training stages."

Liena chuckled while nodding.

"Yes, they have. It's incredible to see how much they've grown, their progress is also staggering. I was nowhere near this level at this age they might soon reach the nascent stage if this continues!”

“Yes, I bet the two will boast about how they were able to reach it before their parents did.”

“Haha, let them. They have earned it!”

Liena continued to fawn over her children while Dong worried about their future. The couple watched as Xiu and Jun continued their fierce but friendly competition. The battle was a testament to their dedication and hard work, as well as the guidance and training they had received from their parents and the sect.

Zhang Dong couldn't help but think about the future. He knew that one day, he would have to pass on the leadership of the sect and the empire to the next generation. Seeing the potential in his children, he believed that they were more than capable of carrying on his legacy but there was one elephant in the room, their ages.

Both their parents were still relatively young and outshone their children in every aspect. The two of them were now twenty and seventy years of age, and the age gap between them and their parents wasn't particularly significant. The true future leaders of this Golden Dynasty would emerge more than five hundred years from now, as Zhang Dong's reign was destined for at least another six hundred years. That is, if he decided to remain in this world for that amount of time.

Considerable time had elapsed since the last major incident, and at this point, he was confident they were in the epilogue of the story. There was nothing left but for him to enjoy his happy ending and ride off into the sunset. His status as the new emperor had already been secured, and his path to ascension was clearly defined. With each passing day, he accumulated spirit points that he could utilize to further enhance his cultivation. The rate at which they accrued was so staggering that he couldn't help but wonder if this was the intended amount his hidden adversaries had in mind.

It wouldn't be unusual for him to ascend to the immortal realm in a relatively short span of time, but he wasn't sure if he should. There was a significant decision he needed to make, something he had been postponing while watching his children grow. As the two engaged in their dispute, he couldn't help but reminisce about their childhood appearances and how soon they would become full-grown adults, a process he couldn't halt.

There was still time to make the decision, and he would wait until the very last moment. After finding happiness in this world with his wife, he wanted to indulge in a bit of selfishness. The time he spent with his wife, children, and friends was what he treasured the most, and eventually, it would all come to an end. He stood before two choices, neither of which he had fully committed to yet.

One choice would lead him towards an early death at a thousand years of age. He would live out a full life in this world and ensure that his children prosper for many generations to come. With Bob by his side, there was a possibility of transferring into other devices that could aid his people if they ever encountered a new system holder. Perhaps it was possible to make this world truly safe and prepare people to defeat future system holders who decided to follow an evil path.

The second option was to ascend and depart from this place. He would follow the scenario created by whoever had designed this world and prepare accordingly. There were still many years left for him to live out. He could slowly make preparations for the occasion, but he would need to shorten his stay in this world. His predecessor had chosen to wait until they were on their deathbed, but he wasn't sure if that was the right path leading to victory.

The available data did not provide enough information about what awaited him. One thing that could enhance his chances of survival was a youthful body and mind. Eventually, his cultivator body, which couldn't progress any further, would start deteriorating, and his power would diminish. Becoming an immortal at a younger age was likely better than doing so at a later stage. As in the tales of old, the younger a cultivator appeared, the more powerful they were as they achieved higher realms at younger ages.

As Zhang Dong pondered these choices, the battle between Zhang Xiu and Zhang Jun continued, each moment bringing them closer to the realization of their potential. The crowd roared with excitement as they witnessed the clash between these two exceptional young warriors.

But amidst the cheers and the spectacle, Zhang Dong knew that the true challenges and mysteries lay ahead. The enigmatic puppeteer who had manipulated the scenarios in this world still eluded him. As an Emperor and the Patriarch of the United Element Sect, he had a responsibility not only to his family but to the people of the entire new Golden Empire.

He couldn't afford to be complacent. The path he chose would not only affect his future but the future of this world. With the knowledge and experiences he had gained over the years, he was determined to make the right decisions and prepare his people for the unknown challenges that lay ahead. However, for now, he would continue to revel in the joy of this moment, watching his children grow and thrive, and basking in the love and companionship of his family…
