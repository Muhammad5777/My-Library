Chapter 630: Evenly Matched

The fiery golden figure let loose a furious roar upon seeing this, and they instantly swelled to several times their original size, revealing themselves to be a burly man with a head of golden hair. His entire body was enveloped within a layer of murky golden light that was releasing formidable law power fluctuations, and it seemed to be his spirit domain, but only limited to a range of several dozen feet.

At the same time, a pair of golden dragon horns appeared on his head, and a layer of golden scales surfaced over his face, as well as the exposed sections of his arms. To complete the transformation, his hands turned into a pair of golden dragon claws.

A golden dragon projection appeared around him, then shot forth through the air before vanishing into the fiery draconic claw up above.

The fiery claw immediately lit up further, and it was able to keep the giant lightning hand at bay again.

The golden-haired man let loose a thunderous roar while thrusting a fist forward, summoning an enormous golden fist projection that instantly destroyed the two bolts of black lightning before reaching down toward the crimson bead below.

However, it was already too late.

A burst of crimson light flashed over the bead, following which it exploded with a deafening boom, releasing a giant crimson sun that encompassed all of the silver giants within it.

The golden-haired man up above was also inundated by the crimson sun, which was giving off bursts of tremendous law power fluctuations.

The ground down below was also unable to escape the crimson sun's incredible heat, and it melted away rapidly like wax, disintegrating into plumes of smoke.

Many of the combatants from both armies were also swept up in the crimson sun, and they were instantly melted into puddles of blood.

That bead contained blood law powers!

Han Li was astonished to see this.

The master of Red Moon Island had also been a user of the laws of blood, but he was only a True Immortal who had attained his law powers through the power of faith, so his blood law powers couldn't even begin to compare with that of the crimson sun.

A streak of golden flames shot out of the crimson sun before landing in the distance, then faded to reveal the golden-haired man.

The golden dragon scales on many parts of his body had become damaged, with some even splitting open entirely, and he immediately made a hand seal to conjure up a layer of golden flames over his own body.

As a result, his injuries began to rapidly heal, and in the blink of an eye, he had made a full recovery.

Meanwhile, the crimson sun was rapidly fading, but the silver giants within it had been completely wiped out with no trace left behind.

The golden-haired man's expression darkened slightly upon seeing this, and he looked up into the sky right as a purple-robed middle-aged man slowly descended out of the dark clouds above.

The man appeared to be in his forties, and there was a series of purple devilish patterns all over his face. His eyes were long and thin, while his nose resembled a hook, and there was cold baleful qi surging out of his entire body.

Incredibly tremendous auras erupted out of the bodies of both men before clashing violently, causing the nearby space to tremble and ripple incessantly, while heaven and earth also began to tremble and sway.

Han Li was astonished to discover that both of them possessed auras far superior to that of the late-High Zenith Stage Gold Devouring Immortal, indicating that they were most likely at the Great Encompassment Stage.

The battlefield down below was quite far away from the two of them, but both armies seemed to have been affected by the spiritual pressure that they were releasing, and a temporary ceasefire ensued as everyone cast their eyes upward.

The clashing High Zenith cultivators also disengaged themselves from battle before arriving behind the golden-haired man and the purple-robed man.

"Did you think that destroying my Lightning God Brigade would turn the tide of this battle? How naive!" the golden-haired man sneered as he swept a hand through the air, and golden light flashed within the spatial passageway behind him, following which a fleet of around forty to fifty golden ships emerged from within.

The ships were all glowing with radiant golden light that illuminated the dark clouds in the sky a golden color.

All of the ships were tens of thousands of feet in length and were riddled with golden and silver runes, resembling a pack of resting giant beasts.

The decks of all of the ships were packed with Heavenly Court cultivators that were busy at work, and there were thick golden pillars extending out of the front of each ship. All of these pillars were hollow in the center, resembling cannon barrels, and the ships spread out in a horizontal line with those barrels aimed straight at the devilish army.

The purple-robed man's pupils contracted slightly upon seeing this, and even from afar, Han Li could sense that these ships were extremely dangerous.

"As long as this spatial passageway remains, more and more reinforcements will continue to come through, and it'll only be a matter of time before we destroy the entire Devil Realm. if you know what's good for you, then immediately pledge your allegiance to our Heavenly Court to oppose the Reincarnation Palace with us!" the golden-haired man threatened.

"Our Devil Realm has already made it plenty clear that we'll play no part in the conflict between your Heavenly Court and the Reincarnation Palace," the purple-robed man replied with a shake of his head.

"In that case, prepare to suffer the consequences of your decision!" the golden-haired man declared in a cold voice as he raised a hand high above his head, and the Heavenly Court army immediately began charging at the devilish army.

The devilish army stood firm in their path, and the battle resumed.

However, the purple-robed man refrained from entering the fray. Instead, he was observing the battlefield with a hint of purple light in his eyes.

All of a sudden, the golden-haired man in the distance burst into laughter as he shot forth through the air, appearing above the giant golden ships in a flash. At the same time, his right arm swelled to several times its original size, transforming into a giant golden dragon claw before grabbing viciously down upon the space beside him.

The purple-robed man's expression darkened slightly upon seeing this.

The golden dragon claw tore straight through the space there before vanishing into it, and an earth-shattering boom rang out, followed by an eruption of tremendous spiritual pressure that tore the spatial rift there ten times wider.

A purple figure stumbled out of the spatial rift, and it was none other than the purple-robed man. His left sleeve had been completely torn away, revealing several bleeding wounds.

Bursts of golden flames emerged from those wounds before attempting to burrow into his body as if they were living creatures, and his expression instantly changed slightly as purple light flashed over his wounds to snuff out the golden flames, following which he sped away into the distance.

Meanwhile, the purple-robed man who was standing in the distant sky above the devilish army vanished into thin air.

"You tried to use the same trick again? You must take me for a fool!" the golden-haired man sneered as he shot forth like a golden arrow, aggressively pursuing the purple-robed man while making a hand seal, upon which all of the runes on the surfaces of the dozens of giant golden ships lit up.

Bursts of golden light began to flow over the ships, while thunderous rumbling rang out within them.

The black barrels in front of the giant ships began to glow with golden light that was rapidly becoming brighter while giving off astonishing spiritual power fluctuations, and all of a sudden, a resounding boom rang out as over a hundred thick pillars of golden light erupted out of the ships before striking the devilish army.

Agonized howls instantly rang out in all directions as all of the devilish beings who came into contact with the pillars of golden light were instantly obliterated, and each pillar of golden light only slowly dissipated after piercing through hundreds of devilish beings.

In the wake of this first round of attacks, tens of thousands of devilish beings had disappeared without a trace.

At this moment, Han Li was laying behind the devilish army, so he wasn't affected, but he was still stunned to see this.

Each of those pillars of golden light had been comparable in power to an all-out attack from a High Zenith cultivator.

The purple-robed man was furious to see the pillars of golden light inflicting such extensive damage throughout the devilish army, and he loose a thunderous roar as he thrust a palm toward the oncoming golden-haired man.

An antiquated wheel flew out amid a flash of black lightning, and the wheel was entirely black in color with a diameter of around a foot.

There were all types of demon beasts engraved onto the edge of the wheel, while at the center was a black humanoid design.

The humanoid figure was wearing a tall hat, and his mouth protruded outward like the beak of a bird. There were four huge black wings on his back, and he was standing atop a pair of black lightning snakes. It was a very strange sight to behold, and it seemed to be depicting some type of ancient deity.

As soon as the antiquated wheel emerged, it immediately began to expand rapidly, swelling to over a hundred feet in size in the blink of an eye. At the same time, all of the designs etched onto its surface began to release radiant black lightning, and in particular, the humanoid deity was flashing incessantly, as if it had sprung to life.

A burst of incredibly formidable lightning law power fluctuations erupted forth, causing the nearby space to shudder violently, and bolts of black lightning came falling incessantly out of the sky before fusing into the black wheel, making it glow brighter and brighter.

A stunned look flashed through Han Li's eyes as he observed the black wheel from afar, and the golden-haired man had also adopted a far more serious expression as he made a hand seal to summon a golden flag that was around ten feet in size with a fiery river depicted on its surface.

Fire law power fluctuations that weren't inferior to the law power fluctuations released by the black wheel erupted out of the golden flag, yet before the golden-haired man had a chance to activate the flag, a sharp bird call rang out from the black wheel.

The sound wasn't very loud, but it had an excessively piercing quality, and the golden-haired man shuddered as a hint of disorientation flashed through his eyes.

Immediately thereafter, seven or eight tree-branched-shaped bolts of lightning sprang out of the black wheel before hurtling silently toward the golden-haired man.

Even though this lightning was still black in color, it was a far lighter shade that was almost transparent, but the lightning law powers that it contained were extremely formidable.
