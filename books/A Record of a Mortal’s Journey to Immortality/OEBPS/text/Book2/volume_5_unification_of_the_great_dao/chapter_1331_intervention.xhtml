Chapter 1331: Intervention

Feng Qingshui shuddered violently, and his aura was significantly weakened with the destruction of his spirit domain.

However, he clearly wasn't going to simply resign himself to his fate, and his shattered spirit domain was still resisting against Han Li's time spirit domain, but as time passed, it was clear that the gold was overwhelming the blue.

Finally, as the last bits of blue were devoured, Feng Qingshui threw up a large mouthful of blood as he plummeted out of the sky like a meteorite.

Meanwhile, Han Li was also looking a little pale. After all, maintaining a spirit domain of this caliber for such a long time as a peak-Great Encompassment cultivator was quite a strenuous task.

However, this was a battle that he had to win, and he couldn't afford to ease up the pressure even for a moment.

As Feng Qingshui came falling out of the sky, he threw an almighty punch in his direction, and five bursts of bright, golden light shot out of his spirit domain, then intertwined to form another Great Five Elemental Annihilation Fist.

The golden fist raced toward Feng Qingshui with unmatched speed and power, and in response, Feng Qingshui hurriedly expelled a mouthful of blood essence, before casting a rapid string of incantation seals into it.

A streak of radiant, blue light shot out from within, and it was none other than the blue sun from the Mist Dragon Secret Area, currently in the form of a blue halberd that was around a thousand feet in size.

The halberd was giving off incredibly fearsome law powers that were around twice as formidable as Feng Qingshui's own law powers, and it was clear that this was his trump card.

That's a second tier immortal treasure!

The blue halberd hurtled through the air as a streak of dazzling, blue light at Feng Qingshui's behest, and it pierced its way into the giant, golden fist, shattering it into countless specks of golden light.

The blue halberd sped through the specks of golden light, leaving a blue trail in its wake as it hurtled directly toward Han Li with astonishing speed.

"You were a fool to think that a mere Great Encompassment cultivator like yourself could challenge a Dao Ancestor!" Feng Qingshui declared as a hint of insanity flashed through his eyes.

Han Li remained completely unfazed by the oncoming blue halberd, and a sense of foreboding welled up in Feng Qingshui's heart at the sight of his composed reaction.

Right as the halberd was no more than a thousand feet away from Han Li, his time spirit domain abruptly shuddered, and the blue halberd was stopped cold in its tracks.

Immediately thereafter, a layer of golden light appeared over its surface, and it exploded into countless pieces right before Feng Qingshui's astonished eyes.

A horrified look appeared on his face upon seeing this, and he frantically pulled out a blue tablet before crushing it in his hand.

At the same time, he drew up all of his remaining power, and a vast expanse of misty, blue light began to proliferate outward from his body.

In the blink of an eye, he had conjured up hundreds of identical avatars to himself, all of which fled in different directions at an incredible speed.

A cold sneer appeared on Han Li's face upon seeing this, and countless arcs of golden lightning erupted out of the lightning net around the Sky Sea Continent at his behest.

The arcs of lightning converged toward the fleeing avatars from all directions at an even more astonishing speed, and in the blink of an eye, all of the avatars were destroyed, leaving only Feng Qingshui left.

Countless arcs of golden lightning had converged around him as well to form a lightning cocoon, and he was completely immobilized as his entire body spasmed uncontrollably.

"Let this serve as a warning to Gu Huojin. If he doesn't release my dao partner, the Heavenly Court will meet the same fate that will befall you now!" Han Li declared as he unleashed yet another Great Five Elemental Annihilation Fist, this one even more massive than the previous two, and it sped toward Feng Qingshui with unfathomable destructive might.

Right at this moment, the entire Dragon Abyss Immortal Region suddenly began to tremble violently, and a burst of tremendous power came crashing down upon the golden lightning net from the outside.

This burst of power was ten times more formidable than what Feng Qingshui was able to muster, and the lightning net immediately began to twist and warp, following a huge gash was torn into its surface.

At the same time, a turbulent vortex appeared on the surface of the sea below Feng Qingshui, and he was sucked into it along with the lightning cocoon around him.

As a result, Han Li's Great Five Elemental Annihilation Fist struck nothing but thin air, while Feng Qingshui was engulfed into the sea.

An earth-shattering boom rang out as a golden sun that was millions of kilometers in size emerged, decimating everything within it, but Han Li paid no heed to it as he cast a grim gaze out of the lightning net.

All of a sudden, the burst of fearsome power had vanished, as if it had gone into hiding, and Feng Qingshui's aura was also nowhere to be detected.

Han Li harrumphed coldly as he made a rapid string of hand seals, and the golden lightning net instantly reverted back into seventy-two Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords that raced away in different directions.

As a set of third tier immortal treasures, the Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords were extraordinarily fast, and they instantly flew to all corners of the immortal region, following which arcs of golden lightning began to fan out from them, as if they were searching for something.

In the next instant, Han Li's eyes suddenly lit up as he vanished from the spot amid a flash of golden lightning, then instantly reappeared beside an Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Sword that was hovering above a sea on the edge of the Dragon Abyss Immortal Region.

This was one of the new abilities attained by his Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords upon their evolution into third tier immortal treasures. Regardless of where they were, Han Li would be able to instantly teleport to whichever Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Sword that he pleased through their spiritual connection.

From there, he released his spiritual sense, and at the same time, his time spirit domain erupted out of his body before pouncing down on the sea below like a giant, golden beast.

The seawater down below instantly evaporated, revealing Feng Qingshui enveloped in a ball of blue light on the seabed.

Caught within Han Li's spirit domain, the ball of blue light had been stopped cold in its tracks.

Right at this moment, the same burst of fearsome power from before reappeared, and an incredibly pure, blue spirit domain flew out of the nearby sea to encompass Feng Qingshui.

The spirit domain was filled with all types of water-based entities, such as rivers, clouds, glaciers, and ripples, and it was as if were the origin of all water under the heavens.

The spirit domain clashed violently with Han Li's time spirit domain, and Han Li felt as if his spirit domain had crashed into a soft, yet extremely resilient wall.

A deep indentation had appeared on the blue spirit domain, but it refused to shatter.

It was as if all of the unfathomable power in Han Li's time spirit domain had plunged into a bottomless sea, and it was completely nullified.

However, the blue spirit domain was clearly struggling immensely as well, and it looked as if it were on the verge of being overwhelmed.

"Who's there?" Han Li yelled as he conjured up the golden spatial gates within his time spirit domain once again, preparing to draw upon the power of other realms and immortal regions to crush the opposing spirit domain.

This spirit domain was far more powerful than Feng Qingshui's, but Han Li was confident that he would be able to crush it.

Right at this moment, a voice rang out from within the blue spirit domain.

"Hold on a second, Fellow Daoist Han, I am not your enemy. I've come here to return Fellow Daoist Nangong Wan to you!"

As soon as the voice trailed off, a ball of blue light shot out from within the blue spirit domain, and it contained none other than Nangong Wan.

"Wan'er!"

Han Li yelled in an ecstatic manner as he hurriedly swept a sleeve through the air, releasing a burst of golden light to catch her.

Through the power of his spirit domain, Han Li was able to verify that this was truly Nangong Wan and not some trick.

However, she was currently in an unconscious state, and it seemed as if her soul had been sealed away.

Han Li rested a fingertip against her glabella, injecting a burst of soul power into her consciousness.

In doing so, he discovered that the seal in her consciousness was a very unremarkable one that he could break with ease, but he didn't dare to do so with these two formidable adversaries before him.

With another sweep of his sleeve, he sent Nangong Wan into the Flower Branch domain, then withdrew his spirit domain until it had shrunk down to only several dozen kilometers in size, but didn't withdraw it entirely.

The opposing, blue spirit domain also shrank down significantly before retreating to a safe distance away, and Han Li made no effort to pursue it.

"You must be Han Li, right? I can see that you've fully harnessed the inheritance of the True Mantra Sect. Congratulations!"

A delicate young woman with a head of blue hair emerged from the blue spirit domain.

She had a pair of sparkling eyes, a delicate nose, a pair of small, cherry lips, and her blue hair was arranged into many little braids, giving her the appearance of a mischievous young girl.

She was wearing a blue dress that left her arms and calves exposed, and there were no shoes on her feet. There was an intricate, blue band around each of her ankles and wrists, hanging from which were some tiny bells that produced a tinkling chime with her movements.

However, her most eye-catching trait was the pair of delicate dragon horns on her forehead, as well as the faint, true spirit bloodline aura swirling around her.

Han Li's brows furrowed slightly upon hearing this.

Even though he was on the Heavenly Court's wanted list, his cultivation base had been too lowly to attract the attention of the Dao Ancestors, and that was why Feng Qingshui had failed to recognize him.

However, this woman, who was clearly a more powerful Dao Ancestor than Feng Qingshui, was somehow aware of exactly who he was.
