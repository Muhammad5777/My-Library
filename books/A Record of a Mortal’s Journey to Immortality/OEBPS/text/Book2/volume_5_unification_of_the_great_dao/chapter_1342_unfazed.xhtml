Chapter 1342: Unfazed

Han Li's Great Five Elemental Annihilation Fist struck the golden wall with tremendous power before instantly exploding, sending incredibly fearsome shockwaves sweeping through the air in all directions.

The golden wall shuddered as countless massive cracks appeared over its surface, but it didn't shatter. Instead, it reflected the destructive shockwaves to send them sweeping back at Han Li.

Having just unleashed his Great Five Elemental Annihilation Fist, Han Li wasn't in time to take any evasive measures, and he was sent flying back through the air.

Behind the golden wall, Xuanyuan Jie wasn't faring that well, either. His entire body was basked in the time law power fluctuations from the detonated Great Five Elemental Annihilation Fist, instantly rooting him to the spot as a result.

Right at this moment, the seventy-two Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords that had made their way behind him at some point transformed into seventy-two delicate, jade-like children that raced through the air on their own to form a sword array.

Immediately thereafter, they reverted back into a series of giant flying swords, each of which was around a thousand feet in length, and a rumbling thunderclap rang out as a gargantuan heavenly gate appeared within the inky darkness.

The heavenly gate was surrounded by constantly churning golden clouds, and even though the gates were tightly shut, there was still an extremely formidable aura of lightning emanating from within.

Yin Wushuang was astonished to see this from afar.

How is this possible? He's a cultivator of time law powers, how is he able to command such formidable lightning law powers solely through the use of a sword array?

Unbeknownst to him, every single one of Han Li's Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Sword was a third tier immortal treasure that had absorbed the law powers of the Lightning Kui's eye.

A third tier immortal treasure was something that was out of reach even for the average Great Encompassment cultivator, so these seventy-two flying swords were the equivalent of seventy-two Great Encompassment cultivators combining their powers in a collaborative effort!

Even as Han Li was still flying back through the air, the heavenly gate slowly opened at his behest, and an almighty waterfall of golden lightning came gushing out from within, while countless lightning wyrms of different colors raced toward the gates with lightning beads clasped in their mouths.

A chorus of deafening thunderclaps rang out as countless gaping spatial rifts appeared in the face of the heavenly gate's unmatched power, and Han Li steadied himself before bringing his hands down in a forceful motion.

The restrictive forces within the heavenly gates instantly faded away, and as a result, the countless lightning wyrms inside were no longer restrained as they came bursting out of the sea of golden lightning in a frenzy.

In the blink of an eye, the entire area was inundated by a vast and fearsome sea of lightning.

"Protect the Insect Ancestor!" Qing Feng yelled, and the three of them immediately disengaged themselves from their battles against Yin Wushuang's trio to rush to Jin Tong's aid.

At this point, the golden vortex around her had already vanished, and there were also no longer any Gold Devouring Beetles flying in through the surrounding spatial rifts, indicating that all of the Gold Devouring Beetles across all of the immortal regions had already returned to her.

However, for some reason, she was still unable to overcome the final hurdle to reclaim her status as a Dao Ancestor.

Meanwhile, Yin Wushuang's trio hurriedly sprang back in retreat, clearly very fearful of the spreading sea of lightning.

All of the Heavenly Court cultivators that were still able to move also fled in all directions in a blind panic, while those who were not so fortunate were quickly inundated.

Countless lightning wyrms of different colors were swimming through the sea of lightning, converging toward Xuanyuan Jie with unstoppable might.

The lightning beads clasped within their mouths were imbued with the purest lightning true intent between heaven and earth, and a look of fierce killing intent flashed through Xuanyuan Jie's eyes upon seeing this.

"If I don't kill you here, you'll surely grow into a threat just as potent as the Reincarnation Palace Master someday!"

As soon as his voice trailed off, he clasped his hands into a seal as he began chanting an incantation.

The entire Extraheavensphere seemed to have begun resonating with Xuanyuan Jie's incantation, and all of the space within a radius of hundreds of thousands of kilometers abruptly fell still for an instant while a burst of loud rumbling rang out from all directions.

Han Li immediately sprang up into the air with an alarmed expression, right as the floating continent beneath him completely exploded, disintegrating into a vast body of earth law powers that came together to form a river of yellow light.

With a swing of Xuanyuan Jie's fist, the entire yellow river was sent sweeping toward the oncoming sea of lightning.

Countless lightning wyrms raced headfirst into the yellow river before exploding into enormous lightning vortexes that erupted in all directions, and the darkness of the Extraheavensphere was illuminated once again.

High up in the sky, lightning vortexes of all types of different colors stirred up the river of earth law powers into a frenzy, while enormous whips of lightning lashed out in all directions, annihilating the surrounding space.

All of the remaining Heavenly Court cultivators hurriedly flew into the spatial rifts that led to the immortal regions for self-preservation.

The shockwaves resulting from this clash were so formidable that even the space around Jin Tong was beginning to shatter, and Yi Jiao's trio had combined their powers to create a spherical light barrier to try and ward off the shockwaves, but the light barrier clearly wasn't going to be able to last much longer, either.

Meanwhile, Han Li abruptly vanished from the spot before reappearing beside one of his Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords, and with a sweep of his sleeves, all five of his time law treasures flew out of his body.

All of a sudden, an entire world seemed to have taken shape within the Extraheavensphere, complete with celestial bodies and a lush mountain range with a river flowing through it.

The Five Elemental Illusory World had encompassed the entire surrounding space within a radius of millions of kilometers, and the pressure on Yi Jiao's trio was significantly lessened, but the three of them were still supporting the spherical light barrier with all their might, not daring to let their guards down.

"You've successfully infuriated me, Han Li. Let me show you the true might of a Dao Ancestor!" Xuanyuan Jie declared in a rumbling voice that echoed like thunder.

"I look forward to it," Han Li replied with a relaxed smile.

A sense of intense admiration welled up Yi Jiao's heart as she witnessed the faint smile on Han Li's face from afar.

In her eyes, the gulf between a Great Encompassment cultivator and a Dao Ancestor was simply insurmountable, particularly when the Dao Ancestor in question was one that was so formidable that he had even outmatched the Insect Ancestor of old.

Countless years had passed since then, and Xuanyuan Jie's powers had become even more unfathomable.

In Yi Jiao's eyes, Han Li's fate was already sealed from the moment he decided to challenge Xuanyuan Jie, and the fact that he was able to smile in spite of this was a reflection of his courageous heart and noble spirit.

In reality, Han Li's palms were currently sweating profusely.

He was only putting on this composed display in order to further infuriate Xuanyuan Jie as only by bringing out Xuanyuan Jie's full power could he hope to defeat him.

Some time later, the sea of lightning and river of yellow light faded away, leaving behind gaping black holes that refused to close.

Xuanyuan Jie began to advance through the air, and with each step that he took, a wave of golden light would spread beneath his foot, accompanied by a deafening boom.

At this moment, his eyes were completely hollow and devoid of emotion, and two strange-looking runes could be seen within them.

Layers upon layers of energy fluctuations that were invisible to the naked eyes were spreading through the air with every single step, proliferating outward to what appeared to have been infinity and beyond.

Han Li knew that those energy fluctuations were imbued with the true meaning of earth.

In this instant, it was as if Xuanyuan Jie's body had become one with the earth, while his will had become one with the Heavenly Dao.

All of a sudden, Xuanyuan Jie stopped in his tracks, and a burst of immense suction force erupted out of his body, acting upon the entire surrounding Extraheavensphere.

He spread his arms open as a layer of golden light began to ripple over his entire body, and there was no sense of humanity left in his aura. Instead, he resembled an ancient deity that had existed for as long as heaven and earth had been in existence.

However, as opposed to dismay, a hint of excitement appeared in Han Li's eyes upon seeing this.

This is it!
