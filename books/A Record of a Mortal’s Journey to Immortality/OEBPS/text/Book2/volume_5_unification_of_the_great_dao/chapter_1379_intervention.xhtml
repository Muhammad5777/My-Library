Chapter 1379: Intervention

Extraheavensphere.

Han Li suddenly stopped in his tracks before looking down with a puzzled expression.

He was just about to examine whatever had caught his attention when his head suddenly began to throb in pain, and an image depicting the Reincarnation Palace Master and the Devilish Monarch's battle against Gu Huojin surfaced in his mind.

At the same time, a sense of intense hatred for Gu Huojin that he had never felt before welled up in his heart, and this emotion was coming from the Reincarnation Palace Master.

Instinctively, Han Li had a feeling that his thoughts and emotions were also being transmitted to the Reincarnation Palace Master, and somehow, a spiritual connection had formed between the two.

Han Li was rather taken aback by this, and he immediately made a hand seal, upon which an arc of golden lightning shot out of his sleeve before vanishing into the space up ahead.

Immediately thereafter, he latched onto Jin Tong, and the two of them vanished from the spot.

Meanwhile, on the Heavenly Palace Continent.

The Reincarnation Palace Master and the Devilish Monarch were engaged in a fierce battle against Gu Huojin.

All three of them had summoned their spirit domains, and numerous spatial, time, and reincarnation passageways had opened up, leading to countless realms and immortal regions to draw upon more power to fuel their spirit domains.

Following an almighty clash, the three combatants flew back through the air, and after steadying himself, the Devilish Monarch swiped his hands through the air in rapid succession with silver light flashing between his fingers.

Dozens of mountainous, silver giants instantly emerged within his spirit domain before pressing themselves tightly against Gu Huojin's golden spirit domain.

These giants were incredibly physically imposing, and their facial features were identical to the Devilish Monarch's. Their entire bodies were riddled with a type of translucent, silver crystals, which were formed by the power of shattered space, containing incredibly violent spatial law powers.

All of a sudden, all of the silver giants exploded in unison at the Devilish Monarch's behest, forming dozens of silver mushroom clouds that sent boundless, devastating spatial power erupting in all directions.

A huge hole was instantly torn into the golden spirit domain, and the Reincarnation Palace Master appeared before the hole in a wraith-like manner before throwing a punch, upon which a gargantuan Six Paths Reincarnation Plate appeared behind him.

The surface of the plate was riddled with countless complex patterns, and countless projections of all types of living beings were flashing over each of its six corners. There were humans, demons, ghosts, devils, Gray Realm beings... It was as if all of the living beings in this world were gathered here.

The gargantuan plate revolved rapidly as it crashed straight into Gu Huojin's spirit domain, and it was giving off an indescribably fearsome aura, far more formidable than the aura that it had displayed back when it claimed the life of Bone Emperor.

The golden spirit domain trembled violently as a series of huge cracks appeared over its surface, while the sections of the spirit domain nearest to the plate had fallen away altogether.

The Reincarnation Palace Master's entire body was riddled with dark red patterns, and his sunken cheeks became even more gaunt and emaciated, as if all of his vitality were being sucked dry, but he he paid no heed to his own condition as he made a hand seal before thrusting it forward, releasing a thick beam of dark red light into the reincarnation plate.

The Six Paths Reincarnation Plate instantly swelled drastically in size, while the light radiating from it also became far brighter, shattering even more of Gu Huojin's spirit domain.

The rate at which the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate was advancing was also sped up by tenfold, and it seemed completely unstoppable as it rumbled its way toward Gu Huojin.

A serious look appeared on Gu Huojin's face as he made a hand seal, and a thin, golden veil rose up from his body with countless specks of golden light shimmering over its surface, giving it the appearance of a golden galaxy.

Boundless time law powers were emanating from the veil, and it was clearly an extremely formidable artifact that was infinitely approaching a first tier immortal treasure.

The veil unfurled itself before transforming into nine massive, golden light barriers, which had only just taken shape when the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate crashed into them with almighty power.

A string of dull thumps rang out as one golden light barrier was shattered after another.

In the blink of an eye, all nine light barriers had been broken, and the golden veil reappeared, having already been torn into close to twenty pieces.

However, the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate was also slowed down for an instant, and Gu Huojin took advantage of this brief window of respite to make a rapid string of hand seals before pointing a finger forward.

The torn golden veil erupted into flames, and countless golden stars flew out of it before spreading throughout the surrounding spirit domain.

All of a sudden, it was as if the golden light within the spirit domain had been ignited, and a turbulent golden galaxy appeared, sweeping up the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate, the Reincarnation Palace Master, and the Devilish Monarch behind him.

Boundless time law powers were churning within the galaxy, and the Devilish Monarch was caught completely off guard, inundated before he even had a chance to react.

The Six Paths Reincarnation Plate was also swept up by the golden galaxy, and it instantly began to tremble slightly.

The Reincarnation Palace Master hurriedly made a rapid string of hand seals upon seeing this, and the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate immediately stabilized itself before crashing down upon Gu Huojin, only for his body to dissipate into disintegrate into specks of golden light upon contact.

The Reincarnation Palace Master faltered slightly upon seeing this, while the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate stopped in its tracks at his behest.

Right at this moment, the turbulent, golden galaxy suddenly began to revolve, forming a massive vortex in the blink of an eye.

The surrounding tearing forces instantly became several times more formidable, and the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate began to drift toward the vortex in an unsteady fashion.

The Reincarnation Palace Master clasped his hands together into a seal upon seeing this, and the runes on the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate instantly began to glow radiantly as it sped up drastically in its revolution.

Tremendous reincarnation law powers erupted out of it, helping it ward off the tearing forces of the golden vortex, and it was quickly able to stabilize itself again.

However, right at this moment, countless golden chains shot out of the golden vortex, then wound themselves around the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate to form a cage.

Every single chain was giving off extremely formidable, restrictive time law powers, and the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate instantly ceased in its revolution, as if it had been sealed away.

Several of the golden chains had also wound themselves around the Reincarnation Palace Master, instantly immobilizing him.

Gu Huojin appeared above the golden vortex in a flash before flicking a finger through the air to release a golden arrow projection, which was traveling at an astonishing speed, reaching the Reincarnation Palace Master in the blink of an eye.

However, not only did the Reincarnation Palace Master not display any panic at the sight of the arrow projection, a faint smile had appeared on his face instead.

Right at this moment, a golden lightning sword emerged out of thin air up above, following which Han Li appeared beneath the sword in a flash.

He was already familiar with the situation, having witnessed everything through the Reincarnation Palace Master's eyes, and he immediately lashed out upon his arrival, unleashing a Great Five Elemental Annihilation Fist.

The golden fist projection was able to intercept the arrow projection in a timely fashion before it could strike the Reincarnation Palace Master, and a resounding explosion rang out as the arrow projection imploded into countless specks of golden light.

"Oh? You're still alive? So be it, I guess I'll just have to take care of you myself!" Gu Huojin harrumphed coldly, yet right as he raised a hand in preparation to do something, a rapidly revolving golden wheel projection suddenly appeared behind Han Li, and he instantly vanished from the spot before abruptly reappearing in front of the Reincarnation Palace Master.

With a sweep of his sleeve, a burst of golden flames from his Time Severing Torch erupted forth, forming a fiery, golden sword that struck the cage of golden chains in a flash.

The golden cage was sliced into two to free the Reincarnation Palace Master, who gave Han Li a grateful nod.

A look of dismay appeared in Gu Huojin's eyes at how easily Han Li was able to traverse through his spirit domain and destroy his Heaven Locking Cage.

However, as opposed to pursuing Han Li's duo, he vanished into the surrounding spirit domain again.

The golden vortex vanished in the blink of an eye, and the section of the spirit domain that had earlier been destroyed by the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate also instantly recovered. At the same time, the spirit domain swelled to over a hundred times its original size, and it was rippling like a vast, golden sea.

Unlike Han Li's more substantial spirit domain, Gu Huojin's spirit domain seemed to have been in a liquid form, giving it a gentle and frail appearance, but in reality, it was nigh indestructible.

Tremendous pressure was converging from all directions in relentless waves, bringing it with bursts of invasive time law powers.

Han Li's brows furrowed slightly as he immediately conjured up his own time spirit domain, which was able to ward off Gu Huojin's intrusive time law powers.

Gu Huojin's time spirit domain was flashing urgently as it attempted to stop the expansion of Han Li's spirit domain, but it was powerless to do so.

However, Han Li's spirit domain had only taken up less than 1% of the total area of Gu Huojin's spirit domain, so it was far from a major concern.

"The Devilish Monarch was swept away by Gu Huojin's time law powers earlier, and I don't know where he is right now. His power will be indispensable if we want to oppose Gu Huojin, so would you be able to rescue him as well?" the Reincarnation Palace Master asked through voice transmission.

Han Li immediately began scouring his gaze over the surrounding area upon hearing this, and his gaze quickly settled on something, following which he made a grabbing motion in that direction.

A streak of golden light erupted out of his hand before vanishing into a certain section of Gu Huojin's spirit domain, following which the streak of light returned with none other than the Devilish Monarch.
