Chapter 1330: Battle of the Great Daos

"In that case, it's time for you to pay for what you've done!" Han Li declared in a cold voice as his robes began flapping audibly around him, despite the lack of wind.

A burst of dazzling, golden light erupted out of his body alongside tremendous time law powers, and an astonished look appeared on Feng Qingshui's face as he exclaimed, "You're a cultivator of time law powers!"

At the same time, he sprang back in retreat while sweeping both sleeves through the air, and countless strands of blue light appeared out of thin air before sweeping toward Han Li like a blue sea.

A cold sneer appeared on Han Li's face as he transformed into a streak of golden light that raced through the air at an incredible speed, leaving a trail of afterimages in its wake.

Immediately thereafter, Han Li appeared behind Feng Qingshui without any warning, and all of the strands of blue light in the air had already been severed, while Feng Qingshui's body had been split into two from head to toe by a line that ran down the center.

However, even as his body split into two, not a single drop of blood appeared, and all of a sudden, the two halves of his body exploded into two clouds of blue mist that darted away into the distance before fusing back together to reform him.

In the wake of the ordeal that he had just suffered, he was looking a little pale, but he quickly recovered, and he exclaimed, "Those time law powers... There's no mistaking it, you must be a disciple of the True Mantra Sect!"

"You're a smart man, but people who are too smart tend to not live very long," Han Li harrumphed coldly as his spirit domain erupted out of his body.

Within the spirit domain, Han Li's Mantra Treasured Axis emerged before transforming into a bright, golden moon that began rapidly revolving while releasing countless golden ripples.

As soon as these golden ripples swept over Feng Qingshui's body, he was instantly immobilized, resembling a snapshot in time.

Han Li pounced on this opportunity to throw an almighty punch, and five bursts of time law powers erupted out of his fist before combining as one.

However, instead of forming his Five Elemental Illusory World, the five bursts of time law powers came together to form a golden fist that struck Feng Qingshui with the force of a hurtling asteroid.

The five bursts of time law powers within the golden fist clashed violently against one another, but didn't completely explode, and they were giving off terrifying power that seemed capable of destroying anything in its path.

This was an ability that Han Li had created, and he had named it "Great Five Elemental Annihilation Fist".

A loud thump rang out as Feng Qingshui's body exploded into countless scraps of blood and flesh, and on top of that, a giant, golden sun that was millions of kilometers in diameter appeared around him, with bursts of fearsome time law powers raging within it.

All space, matter, and even time within the golden sun was reduced to nothingness, and the scraps of Feng Qingshui's body were naturally no exception.

At the same time, astonishingly destructive shockwaves swept through the air in all directions, and huge cracks appeared all over the Mist Dragon Secret Area before it collapsed violently.

The space above the Mist Dragon Sect was pulverized as a boundless sea appeared out of thin air, encompassing the entire sect and close to half of the Sky Sea Continent.

The bright, blue sun in the secret area had also appeared, and it continued to give off rays of blue radiance, while all of the dragons in the sea were trembling and cowering in fear.

These dragons were extremely proud and looked down on all other living beings, but they still harbored nothing but awe and veneration toward Dao Ancestors.

The entire continent was about to be inundated by the vast sea crashing down from above, and all of the disciples of the Mist Dragon Sect erupted into a blind panic.

Elder Ji and his two companions were also looking up at the sky with stunned expressions, and the three of them hurriedly flew up into the air before collectively conjuring up a blue light barrier to oppose the falling seawater.

Meanwhile, all of the residents of the Sky Sea Continent could only pray that they would somehow survive this cataclysmic disaster.

Han Li had also appeared in the sky above the Mist Dragon Sect, and his brows furrowed slightly at the sight of the terrible fate that was about to befall the continent down below.

He raised a hand to the heavens, and the entire Dragon Abyss Immortal Region was instantly filled by boundless blue light, while all of the seawater and water-attribute spiritual qi in the air fell completely still.

The vast sea that had gushed out of the Mist Dragon Secret Area was also suspended in mid-air as a gargantuan ball of water, which then raced toward the sea at Han Li's behest.

The three Great Encompassment Stage Mist Dragon Sect elders were unable to get away in time before the unfathomably massive ball of water crashed into them, and blood came gushing out of their mouths as they were swept away into the distance.

The ball of water flew out of the Sky Sea Continent in the blink of an eye, where it was then released into the sea by Han Li, and with that, the crisis had been averted.

The ball of water crashed down into the sea below with an almighty splash, and countless, enormous waves were sent sweeping over the surface of the sea in all directions.

All of the dragons in the ball of water hurriedly fled in all directions, forced to swallow their pride as they frantically swam for their lives.

All the while, Han Li remained hovering high up in the sky with a contemplative look on his face.

While he was extremely confident in his own powers, it didn't make sense for a Dao Ancestor like Feng Qingshui to have met his demise so easily.

As the long-established Water Dao Ancestor, he stood at the very pinnacle of the entire True Immortal Realm, yet the Feng Qingshui that had been slain just now really had been a body of flesh and blood, rather than any illusion or avatar.

Unwilling to take any chances, Han Li released his unfathomable spiritual sense, and right at this moment, a cacophony of rumbling thunderclaps rang out as a section of the golden lightning net that had previously vanished into thin air around the Sky Sea Continent reappeared.

Han Li immediately turned to that direction, then vanished from the spot amid a flash of lightning.

At the same time, countless balls of golden lightning and lighting sword projections erupted out of the lighting net, and every single one of them was imbued with unfathomable lightning law powers.

A deafening boom rang out as all of the space within a radius of hundreds of thousand of kilometers was completely annihilated, and Feng Qingshui was sent flying out of thin air in an unsteady manner.

At the moment, his complexion was very pale, and his aura had also receded significantly.

In the next instant, Han Li appeared before him as he harrumphed coldly, "So you really did use an avatar. You did manage to fool me, but the entire continent has already been surrounded, so you're not going anywhere today!"

As soon as his voice trailed off, an enormous, golden spirit domain erupted out of his body, and this one was far more powerful than the one that he had conjured up earlier, warping all of the space encompassed within it.

Han Li made a hand seal, and a series of giant, golden gates appeared above the spirit domain, leading to various other immortal regions and even many lower realms.

Beyond the largest of the golden gates was a boundless space that was filled with golden light, and it contained the power of the laws of time within the Heavenly Dao.

All of the golden gates lit up at his behest, and bursts of time law powers funneled their way through them before fusing into the surrounding time spirit domain.

The time in those immortal regions and lower realms was instantly thrown into a state of disarray, while the world's origin qi within them also began churning violently in a doomsday-like scene.

All of the time law powers within the Dragon Abyss Immortal Region were also converging toward the golden spirit domain, altering the complexion of the entire immortal region drastically.

The gate that was connected to the Heavenly Dao also lit up as a thick pillar of golden light descended from it before fusing into the spirit domain, and it instantly swelled to several times its original size, while the golden light radiating from it became so bright that it completely outshone the light of the sun.

A stunned look appeared on Feng Qingshui's face upon seeing this, and he let loose a loud roar as a blue spirit domain erupted out his body as well.

The spirit domain was filled with blue mist that refracted the light in countless different angles, presenting a captivating yet confounding sight to behold.

A series of spatial gates also appeared within the blue spirit domain as it attempted to draw upon power from other immortal regions and realms, but right at this moment, a gargantuan, golden lightning net emerged over the Sky Sea Continent.

All of the space on the other side of the lightning net seemed to have been cut off, and as a result, the spatial gates within Feng Qingshui's water spirit domain were filled entirely with golden lightning.

An alarmed look appeared on Feng Qingshui's face upon seeing this, but before he had a chance to do anything, a burst of almighty power came crashing down upon him from Han Li's time spirit domain.

The constrictive force was so potent that Feng Qingshui was almost completely immobilized, and he let loose a loud roar as he threw a punch with great difficulty.

His blue spirit domain began to rumble as it drew upon some power from the sea down below to help it oppose Han Li's time spirit domain.

In the next instant, the two spirit domains clashed with an almighty boom, and this was the most formidable clash that had taken place between the two of them thus far.

This was not just a clash between laws, but also a battle between Great Daos!

However, as one of the three paramount laws, the laws of time were a fundamental part of the Heavenly Dao, one that the laws of water couldn't hope to oppose.

The entire Dragon Abyss Immortal Region had begun trembling violently, and the sky and the sea were collapsing in a cataclysmic fashion.

Several enormous spatial rifts had appeared in the center of the immortal region, and they were rapidly widening and elongating, threatening to tear the entire immortal region into pieces.

The seawater in the Dragon Abyss Immortal Region gushed into the spatial rifts, and some of the smaller fragments of the immortal region on the edges of the spatial rifts were sucked in before plummeting toward the lower realms.

Thankfully, the vast majority of the immortal region was comprised of ocean, so even in the face of such a calamitous disaster, there weren't too many casualties.

Before long, a series of massive cracks appeared over the surface of the blue spirit domain, and it was shattered into pieces by the might of Han Li's time spirit domain.
