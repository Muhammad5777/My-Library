Chapter 1373: Reinforcements

A hint of relief flashed through the Reincarnation Palace Master's eyes upon seeing this, and he continued to make a rapid string of hand seals.

As more and more gray lightning surged into the gate, it began to glow more and more radiantly, completely outshining everything around it.

At the center of the gate were countless arcs of lightning, which were being manipulated by some type of force to form a swirling vortex, within which a black spatial passageway was quickly taking shape.

Gu Huojin's expression remained unchanged as he casually swept a sleeve through the air, and his eyes suddenly began to glow with golden radiance.

At the same time, a series of strange patterns appeared over the exposed parts of his skin, including his hands, his neck, and his head, and they connected with the patterns on his robes like a system of blood vessels, all of which were also a bright golden color.

What was most peculiar of all was that the same strange patterns had appeared on his wheelchair as well, and waves of golden light were emanating from his body, proliferating outward in all directions before slowly fading away.

In the blink of an eye, countless waves of golden light had spread through the air, all of which were imbued with tremendous time law powers, but in contrast with the time law powers that Gu Huojin had displayed thus far, these time law powers were extremely violent and untamed.

These rays of golden light intertwined with the surrounding silver light, presenting a dazzling sight to behold.

The space that had been frozen by the silver light began to tremble violently in the face of the endless waves of golden light, and finally, it collapsed violently into an almighty spatial storm.

The shattered space was reduced to countless translucent, silver scraps, which were scattered in all directions by the ensuing spatial storms.

The Devilish Monarch wasn't surprised that his spatial seal had been broken, and he thrust a palm forward to release a burst of spatial power, enhancing the power of the raging spatial storms by tenfold.

Countless spatial shards were swept up by the ferocious spatial storm before racing toward the golden fist projection and Gu Huojin, and the fist projection was instantly inundated before exploding violently.

Gu Huojin was caught off guard and sent flying back some distance, but he was able to quickly steady himself, and he remained completely unscathed.

At this point, the Reincarnation Palace Master was already finished with what he was doing, and at this point, the vortex within the gray lightning gate had already swelled to fill up the entirety of the gate.

All of a sudden, all of the gray light faded away at his behest, revealing everything within the gate.

It was a murky, gray world with a white sky that was enshrouded in a vast blanket of gray clouds, beneath which was a black landscape. Everything was black and white, giving the entire world a drab and desolate appearance.

The black landscape was riddled with gray figures stretching as far as the eyes could see, all of whom were Gray Realm cultivators, and their numbers were comparable with those of the devilish and primordial armies.

Judging from their cultivation bases, it was clear that all of the elite cultivators of the Gray Realm had been assembled, and there were even some severed corpse souls among them.

Many of the Heavenly Court's cultivators had turned to these severed corpse souls with alarmed looks on their faces.

The gray clouds were filled with the origin qi of the Gray Realm, which clashed with the world's origin qi to produce a thunderous commotion.

The two types of energy were complete counterparts to one another, and the world's origin qi near the Jade Pond Paradise was rapidly being whittled away.

Right at this moment, three figures emerged from the spatial doorway, the first of which was none other than Yin Chengquan.

As for his two companions, one of them was extremely tall and imposing with pitch-black skin, and he seemed to have come from the Black Rope Region, while the other one's face was completely concealed under the hood of their gray cloak, but a pair of long arms were extending out of the hood.

The arms were covered in brown, wrinkled skin, resembling a dead body that had been submerged in water for many years, presenting a harrowing sight to behold.

Judging from their auras, those two were clearly also Dao Ancestors.

As soon as the three of them appeared, one of them pointed a finger at the Heavenly Court cultivators, and countless Gray Realm cultivators came flooding out of the spatial doorway in a formidable, gray wave to intercept the Heavenly Court's forces.

The origin qi of the Gray Realm naturally suppressed the world's origin qi, so even though the Heavenly Court cultivators possessed a superior cultivation base overall, they were still immediately stopped cold in their tracks.

At the forefront of the Gray Realm amy were dozens of severed corpse souls, who had engaged themselves in battle against several dozen Heavenly Court cultivators.

Many of them had gone after the hosts that had severed them in the first place, and due to how familiar all of them were with one another, they were quite evenly matched, even though the Heavenly Court cultivators had their powers enhanced by the Divine Dao Seal.

As a result, the Heavenly Court army that had appeared unstoppable just a moment ago had been brought to a juddering halt, allowing the devilish and primordial armies to regroup before surrounding the Heavenly Court's forces once again.

With the situation stabilized again, the Devilish Monarch and the others heaved a collective sigh of relief.

As for the Reincarnation Palace Master, he remained completely unmoved by the unfolding situation as he continued to stare intently at Gu Huojin.

All of a sudden, Li Yuanjiu's eyes sprang open, and a burst of golden light surged out of his body to suppress the nine-colored light around him.

Taking advantage of this opportunity, he opened his mouth to release a ball of five-colored light, which contained a small vial that was radiating golden, red, blue, green, and yellow light, identical to the vial that Wyrm 3 and the others had stolen from the Nine Origins Temple.

As soon as the five-colored vial flew out of Li Yuanjiu's mouth, it immediately released a burst of five-colored light that enshrouded his entire body.

The five-colored light contained metal, wood, water, fire, and earth-attribute law powers, but not only were they not clashing against one another, they were fused together in a harmonious fashion, much like the law powers of the Divine Dao Seal.

A burst of tremendous suction force surged out of the vial, and all of the nine-colored light around Li Yuanjiu was sucked into the vial in a flash.

The golden light emanating from his body glowed even brighter, forcing out even more nine-colored light, all of which continued to be sucked into the vial, and after no more than a few seconds, the nine-colored symbol on his glabella had faded away.

Gu Huojin's expression changed slightly upon seeing this, but before he had a chance to contemplate what he had just seen, the three Gray Realm Dao Ancestors appeared near the Reincarnation Palace Master and the Devilish Monarch in a flash, and the five of them formed an encirclement around him.

Meanwhile, Li Yuanjiu pointed a finger at Chi Rong, and the five-colored vial above his head immediately flew through the air to settle above her.

With his powers fully restored, Li Yuanjiu was able to make much better use of the vial, and the suction force that it released became far more potent than before as it quickly devoured the Divine Dao Seal that had been placed upon Chi Rong.

With the two of them freed from the Divine Dao Seal, they also joined the encirclement around Gu Huojin.

"I'm very impressed that you were even able to convince these three Gray Realm Dao Ancestors to join you," Gu Huojin chuckled as he turned his gaze to the Reincarnation Palace Master.

"You've left everyone with no choice but to oppose you. You only have yourself to blame for your excessive greed," the Reincarnation Palace Master replied.

His voice wasn't very loud, but it traveled far and wide, and a hesitant look flashed through Chen Ruyan's eyes as she fought against the two devilish Dao Ancestors.

Ripples of blue light were surging out of her fingertips, and they rapidly swelled to form five enormous waves that inundated the entire sky before crashing toward the seductive woman and the giant beast that the rotund woman had transformed into.

The dark purple flowers around the seductive woman were filled with corrosive poison, making them very difficult to deal with, but they had to make direct contact with their target to take effect. In the face of these massive waves, they weren't able to do much aside from sway aimlessly like seaweed.

The seductive woman's expression darkened slightly upon seeing this, and she swept her right hand through the air, upon which a long, purple saber that was riddled with devilish patterns appeared in her grasp.

With a slash of the saber, an almighty saber projection was sent crashing into the fearsome waves.

At the same time, the nine eyes of the giant beast that the rotund woman had transformed into sprang open in unison to release nine pillars of black light, which came together to form a single incredibly thick pillar of black light, within which countless arcs of black lightning were intertwined, and the pillar of light also crashed into the mighty waves.

Two huge gashes were instantly torn into the waves, and the saber projection and pillar of black light were rapidly closing in on Chen Ruyan.
