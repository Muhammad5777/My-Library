Chapter 1341: Power of the Great Dao

"Jin Tong? You mean Qu Lin? Who is she to you?" Xuanyuan Jie asked in a surprised manner.

"She is my spirit pet," Han Li replied.

"How dare you refer to our Insect Ancestor as your spirit pet!" Yi Jiao immediately protested in a furious manner.

"Cut the chit-chat and focus on your own recovery. I can only handle Xuanyuan Jie, so you'll have to keep all of the other Heavenly Court cultivators at bay," Han Li said.

Yi Jiao was rendered speechless as she detected the rising aura emanating from Han Li's body.

Who is he? How could he possess such formidable time law power fluctuations?

"Forget it, I don't even know why I bothered to ask. In any case, now that you're here, I'll just have to get rid of you as well as a little added bonus," Xuanyuan Jie sneered.

Han Li offered no response as he swept a sleeve through the air, conjuring a giant time spirit domain that encompassed the entire surrounding area in a radius of hundreds of thousands of kilometers.

All of the Heavenly Court cultivators were instantly slowed down to a crawl.

In addition to Yin Wushuang's trio, there was a small handful of them that were still able to move relatively freely, but they were also far slower than before.

Xuanyuan Jie maintained an expressionless facade, but internally, he was stunned by Han Li's power.

What the hell is Gu Huojin doing, allowing this bastard to cultivate the laws of time to this extent?

Additionally, this only made him more determined to kill Han Li here.

With a sweep of his sleeve, the entire floating continent began to race toward Han Li like a battleship.

In response, Han Li made a hand seal, and his seventy-two Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords immediately shot forth toward Xuanyuan Jie as streaks of swordlight.

Xuanyuan Jie brought his palms together before thrusting them forward, and two bursts of yellow light appeared before him. In the instant that all of the flying swords pierced into the yellow light, they were immediately diverted away, as if they had just slid over a smooth, icy surface.

All of a sudden, Han Li launched himself toward Xuanyuan Jie at an extraordinary speed, enhanced by his Reversal True Axis ability.

Xuanyuan Jie was ecstatic to see this. A direct clash played right into his hands, and he was more than happy to see Han Li trying to fight fire with fire.

However, in the instant that the two of them were about to clash, a flurry of lightning suddenly flashed over Han Li's body, and he instantly vanished from the spot.

In the next instant, he abruptly reappeared, standing atop one of the Azure Bamboo Cloudswarm Swords that had just been diverted away by Xuanyuan Jie earlier.

Xuanyuan Jie immediately swung around as a wary look surfaced in his eyes.

He could tell that Han Li seemed to have just employed some type of instantaneous teleportation ability that was related to his flying swords, rather than through using his time law powers.

Upon arriving on the continent behind Xuanyuan Jie, Han Li joined his middle and index fingers before slashing them through the air, and a burst of golden light that was imbued with time law powers instantly swept through the air, forming a golden wave that swept over most of the continent.

The waves of earth surging over the continent were instantly suppressed by this burst of time law powers, allowing Shuang Bai and Qing Feng to free themselves.

"Go and protect Qu Lin. Leave Xuanyuan Jie to me," Han Li said.

Qing Feng and Shuang Bai exchanged a glance with one another, and they were still a little displeased by Han Li's flippant remark about their Insect Ancestor being a mere spirit pet of his, but given the urgency of the situation, they naturally prioritized Jin Tong's protection first.

With that, the two of them were reunited with Yi Jiao, and fierce battle ensued between them and Yin Wushuang's trio.

As for the rest of the Heavenly Court cultivators, the vast majority of them were still rooted to the spot by Han Li's spirit domain.

"What makes you think a mere Great Encompassment cultivator like yourself can stand up against me?" Xuanyuan Jie scoffed as he swept a sleeve through the air, and a burst of yellow light instantly rose up over the entire continent beneath Han Li's feet.

The yellow light was giving off immense suction force that rooted him firmly to the spot, following which Xuanyuan Jie raised a fist to the heavens before bringing it down in a swift and forceful motion.

A thunderous commotion instantly rang out from deep within the Extraheavensphere, and Han Li looked up to see thousands of asteroids of different sizes crashing down toward him, drawn down by some type of immense power.

The descending asteroids were creating so much friction with the surrounding space that they erupted into crimson flames, resembling a storm of shooting stars under the backdrop of the dark sky.

All of these shooting stars were enhanced by Xuanyuan Jie's earth law powers, granting them tremendous power.

Han Li's brows furrowed slightly upon seeing this, and he channeled his Heavenly Baleful Purgatory Arts as all of his profound acupoints lit up, following which he was able to forcibly tug his leg out of the yellow light emanating from the ground below.

Xuanyuan Jie was rather surprised to see this, but he immediately reacted as he joined his palms together.

Two mountains instantly rose up over the floating continent on either side of Han Li, then came rumbling toward him from either side.

Han Li had only just freed one of his legs when he was crushed between the two mountains with a deafening boom, and the entire continent trembled violently as the pair of mountains crumbled in unison.

A look of disappointment appeared in Yi Jiao's eyes upon seeing this.

She didn't know who Han Li was to Jin Tong, but it was clear that he had come to her, and his demise most likely meant that the end was near.

Immediately thereafter, a series of gaping spatial rifts appeared over the sky above the continent, and the storm of asteroids came crashing down amid a thunderous commotion.

The entire continent was inundated by the storm of fiery asteroids, which fell continuously without rest for close to twenty seconds.

After the dust settled, it was revealed that all of the mountains and landmarks on the continent had been razed to the ground, leaving it riddled with countless charred craters, some of which were still alight with scorching flames.

Xuanyuan Jie took a pleased glance at his own handiwork, yet right as he was about to turn his attention to Jin Tong, his gaze suddenly snapped back to the point where the two mountains had previously collided.

The ground there trembled violently as a massive figure slowly stood up from the scorched earth, spreading its six arms to dust itself off before casting its three pairs of eyes toward Xuanyuan Jie.

"That barely tickled. It's time for you to get a taste of my fists now," Han Li's three heads declared in unison as he channeled his Great Five Elemental Illusion Mantra while adopting a fist stance, raising his six fists in a menacing display.

At the same time, five different types of time law powers began to rapidly gather around him before fusing together.

Xuanyuan Jie could sense that Han Li was preparing some type of extremely formidable attack, one that he didn't dare to take lightly, and he immediately joined his palms in front of his own chest before closing his eyes.

As soon as he did so, rays of yellow light instantly began to radiate from his entire body before fading away into the surrounding air.

At the same time, he began to give off a very peculiar aura.

Even though his body was still hovering in the same spot as before and was fully visible, Han Li was struck by the false feeling that he wasn't there at all.

This was Xuanyuan Jie's Dao Ancestor Body, a manifestation of his Great Dao.

Is this what happens after one becomes integrated with the Heavenly Dao? Han Li thought to himself with a hint of awe in his eyes.

However, this was not the time to be overawed. The five types of time law powers around him had already fully combined, and a series of black lines had also begun to surface over his body.

All of his time law treasure manifestations appeared on his back, and they were radiating bright, golden light.

With a thunderous roar, Han Li lashed out with his six giant fists in unison, conjuring up countless black and golden fist projections that hurtled through the air.

The barrage of fist projections appeared before Xuanyuan Jie almost instantaneously, but right as they were about to strike him, his eyes abruptly sprang open, and his pupils had disappeared, leaving behind only solid panels of earthy yellow light that appeared a little hollow, but contained boundless power.

"The might of the Great Dao is not something that you can oppose with brute strength alone!" Xuanyuan Jie roared as he slowly thrust a palm forward, and the entire surrounding space instantly shuddered violently.

A vortex emerged over the palm of his hand with boundless yellow light surging out of it, forming a massive, golden wall that was sent racing toward Han Li.
