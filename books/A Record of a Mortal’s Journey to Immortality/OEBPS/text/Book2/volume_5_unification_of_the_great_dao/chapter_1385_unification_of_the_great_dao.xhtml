Chapter 1385: Unification of the Great Dao

All of the Great Daos under the heavens are in this vial...

Han Li began to roam through the internal world of the Heavenly Controlling Vial, and only then did he notice that the surrounding balls of light were not stationary. Instead, they were all displaying different patterns of movement.

A hint of agitation began to well up in Han Li's heart. How was he supposed to refine all of these Great Daos?

Right at this moment, he suddenly noticed that there were some balls of light that were flashing in a rhythmic fashion, as if they were calling out to him.

He flew over to them to discover that all of them contained law powers that had some affinity with his Great Dao, including lightning and the five elements.

The closer he drew to the center of the vial's internal space, the sparser the balls of light became, but the law powers that they contained also became more immense. At the very center of the internal space was a massive, chaotic cloud, floating around it were the three largest balls of light that were golden, silver, and dark red in color.

The law power fluctuations emanating from the chaotic cloud were identical to those of the chaotic vortex outside, while the three balls of light represented the three paramount laws.

Of course, Han Li had the greatest affinity with the ball of golden light, and as soon as he approached it, wisps of time law powers began to separate from it before flowing into his spiritual sense.

He instantly felt as if his body were full of power, and as he turned his gaze elsewhere, he discovered some hidden details that he hadn't been able to see before.

As it turned out, there were countless threads extending out of the chaotic cloud, connecting it to all of the balls of light in this boundless space to form an enormous net.

Thanks to his connection with the laws of time, Han Li seemed to have also become a part of this net.

Meanwhile, in the outside world, more and more white light was surging out of the chaotic vortex, forcing the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate back on a constant retreat.

As more and more of the white light infiltrated the reincarnation plate, it began to glow brighter and brighter, as if it were unable to bear the influx of chaotic power.

"Fellow Daoist Shi, I need your help," the Reincarnation Palace Master implored through gritted teeth.

The Devilish Monarch was already prepared, and he gave the Reincarnation Palace Master a firm nod.

Right at this moment, the Reincarnation Palace Master suddenly switched to a different hand seal, and a hole suddenly appeared at the center of the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate.

The chaotic pillar of light that encompassed the entire reincarnation plate instantly became shrank down and shot through the hole, emerging from the other side in a more condensed and powerful state.

The Devilish Monarch flew directly toward the pillar of light, and right as the two about to clash, he suddenly brought his hands apart, conjuring up a giant spatial gate that the chaotic pillar of light plunged directly into.

In the next instant, another spatial gate appeared beside Gu Huojin, and the pillar of white light shot out from within, having been redirected back at him.

Gu Huojin was instantly inundated by the pillar of white light, but his gleeful cackling indicated that this was playing right into his hands.

"He's using our attack to accelerate his absorption of chaotic law powers! Once the process is complete and he becomes the Chaotic Dao Ancestor, he'll have full control over that chaotic vortex, and he'll truly be invincible then!" the Reincarnation Palace Master exclaimed in a grim voice, following which the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate reverted back to its original state at his behest to continue opposing the chaotic pillar of light.

The Devilish Monarch flew back to the Reincarnation Palace Master's side, following which he turned his gaze to Han Li with an astonished look in his eyes.

Immediately thereafter, he conjured up a spatial barrier around Han Li to contain his aura so that Gu Huojin wouldn't notice that anything was amiss.

......

Meanwhile, in the Heaven Controlling Vial's internal space.

Han Li's spiritual sense had already transformed into a giant ball of white light.

The Three Thousand Great Daos never existed independently of one another. Instead, all of the laws under the heavens complement and support each other. That is the essence of the Heavenly Dao...

As soon as he arrived at this epiphany, all of the balls of light in the entire boundless space began converging toward the center.

First, all of the three thousand laws returned to the five elements that they belonged to, following which the five essential elemental laws converged toward the three paramount laws. In the end, all of the laws combined into one before fusing into the cloud of chaotic laws.

A burst of dull rumbling rang out within the Heaven Controlling Vial's internal space, and the chaotic cloud took on a dark green color as it began to revolve rapidly.

At the same time, Han Li's eyes sprang open as he sprang to his feet.

A layer of faint ripples emanated from his body, and that was enough to shatter the spatial barrier that the Devilish Monarch had conjured up around him.

His eyes were glowing with five-colored light as he raised his hands to the heavens, and the Heaven Controlling Vial rose up into the air to hover above him.

In the next instant, all of the patterns engraved onto the surface of the vial lit up in unison, while a series of dark green clouds surged out of its opening to form a swirling vortex that was hundreds of thousands of kilometers in size.

The Devilish Monarch's expression changed slightly upon seeing this, while the Reincarnation Palace Master exclaimed in an ecstatic manner, "This is it!"

All of a sudden, numerous massive bolts of lightning sprang out of the dark green vortex in all directions, shattering the surrounding space.

The chaotic vortex seemed to have been resonating with the dark green vortex from afar, and its rate of expansion was instantly sped up by tenfold.

An incredulous look flashed through Gu Huojin's eyes as his gaze fell upon the dark green vortex.

Given his connection to the vortex that was the manifestation of the laws of chaos, he was able to sense the threat that the dark green vortex posed.

Realizing that he had to strike, he joined his palms in front of himself, then thrust both hands forward, and countless pillars of chaotic light erupted out of the chaotic vortex before crashing down upon the dark green vortex as a series of gargantuan blades.

Han Li remained completely unfazed as a flurry of golden light surged out of his hands before descending upon the Heaven Controlling Vial, and the dark green vortex instantly swelled drastically in size, resembling the cavernous mouth of an all-devouring beast as it sped directly toward the oncoming blades of chaotic light.

As the two clashed, there was no resounding explosion, as one may have expected.

Instead, the blades of light sank into the dark green vortex without a sound, but as they pierced halfway into the vortex, the latter began to rapidly revolve, twisting the blades of light together like lengths of rope to form a bridge that connected the two vortexes.

As soon as the bridge was established, both of the vortexes began to rumble violently.

Chaotic clouds surged out of the chaotic vortex before flowing over the bridge, while plumes of green mist emerged from the dark green vortex, and initially, the two seemed evenly matched, with neither side able to get the better of the other.

However, a burst of deafening rumbling then suddenly began to ring out from within the chaotic vortex, and it sped up drastically in its revolution.

The dark green vortex was far smaller, and it appeared to have been significantly weaker as well. Before long, the dark green mist rising up from it was beginning to be sucked involuntarily into the chaotic vortex.

"The chaotic vortex has already devoured all of the world's origin qi in the entire Middle Earth Immortal Region, and it's continuing to devour more from other immortal regions. In comparison, the Heaven Controlling Vial is completely outmatched!" the Reincarnation Palace Master said in a concerned manner.

"Palace Master, Fellow Daoist Shi, I need your help!" Han Li yelled.

His entire body had already turned a radiant, golden color as he injected his time law powers into the Heaven Controlling Vial with no reservation.

The Reincarnation Palace Master and the Devilish Monarch exchanged a glance with one another, then immediately sprang into action, injecting their law powers into the Heaven Controlling Vial as well.

Three giant pillars of light, one golden, one silver, and one red were rising up from the bodies of Han Li's trio before surging into the Heaven Controlling Vial, and with this injection of power, the vial began to rapidly expand until it had reached the size of a mighty pagoda.

Balls of light of different colors, containing all types of law powers, began flying out of the vial's opening before being guided up into the heavens by a blurry figure.

"Senior Vial Spirit!" Han Li exclaimed at the sight of the indistinct figure.

As soon as his voice trailed off, all of the balls of light began to connect with one another before fusing as one, forming a dazzling, rainbow ribbon of light.

The ribbon of light was giving off an unprecedented, yet somewhat familiar conflicting aura.
