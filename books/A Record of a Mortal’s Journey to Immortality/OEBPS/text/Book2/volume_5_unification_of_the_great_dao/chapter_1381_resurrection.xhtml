Chapter 1381: Resurrection

The deranged look on Gu Huojin's face became even more pronounced at the sight of the oncoming attacks, and he slit his left wrist with his finger, releasing a large stream of blood essence into the chaotic vortex beneath him.

A layer of faint, crimson light instantly appeared over the chaotic vortex, and it began to expand at a far faster rate than before.

With a sweep of his sleeve, countless flaming rocks flew out of the chaotic vortex like fiery asteroids to strike the golden sword projections and silver spatial rifts.

Unlike the brown arrow projections, these flaming rocks were imbued with nine types of different law powers.

The fiery rocks instantly exploded upon making contact with the sword projections and spatial rifts, creating a scorching sea of fire.

Nine bursts of law powers were intertwining with one another in a frenzy within the sea of flames, devouring the golden sword projections and spatial rifts.

At the same time, Han Li's trio was struck by a burst of tremendous power from afar, sending them flying back through the air.

A pleased grin appeared on Gu Huojin's face as he swept both sleeves through the air once again, and the chaotic vortex shuddered violently as countless balls of lightning the size of houses came raining down upon the trio.

These balls of lightning didn't appear to have been imbued with any types of law powers. Instead, they were giving off a chaotic aura, and it looked as if each of them contained a miniature world.

"Get back! This is Chaotic Divine Lightning!" the Reincarnation Palace Master yelled in a panicked manner while making a rapid string of hand seals, and six bursts of dark red light flew out of the six holes in the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate to envelop his entire body as he shot back in retreat.

Han Li and the Devilish Monarch didn't know how much of a threat this Chaotic Divine Lightning posed, but they also hurriedly flew back in retreat upon seeing the Reincarnation Palace Master's reaction.

The Devilish Monarch swept his hands through the air, and the surrounding space instantly formed a spherical, semi-transparent cocoon around him.

Even though the cocoon was clearly visible, it also seemed extremely distant, as if it were in some type of alternate dimension.

Meanwhile, Han Li shrank down his spirit domain to only less than two hundred feet in size, making it extremely condensed and resilient.

At the same time, he conjured up an Immortal Golden Cloud around himself, and the golden cloud had only just taken shape when the Chaotic Divine Lightning crashed down upon his spirit domain.

The entirety of heaven and earth took on a chaotic coloration as everything was completely obliterated. The Chaotic Divine Lightning inundated everything, and a large section of the space that had already disintegrated into nothingness collapsed once again.

The spirit domain around Han Li wasn't even able to hold itself together for an instant before it was torn apart, following which the lightning crashed down upon the Immortal Golden Cloud, which immediately began to thin rapidly.

In the blink of an eye, most of the cloud had already been eradicated, leaving only a thin layer behind.

Han Li was astonished to see this, and he finally understood why the Reincarnation Palace Master had displayed such a panicked reaction at the sight of the Chaotic Divine Lightning.

He channeled his Great Five Elemental Illusion Mantra with all his might as his time law powers gushed into the Immortal Golden Cloud in a frenzy.

Immediately thereafter, he summoned his five time law treasures, which began orbiting around him while releasing tremendous time law powers.

The nearby space instantly twisted and warped into a golden vortex that resembled a space-time passageway with incredibly formidable time law powers swirling around within, pulverizing anything that dared to enter the vortex.

At the same time, a series of black, devilish patterns and black scales appeared over his body as he transformed into a devilish deity with twelve heads and thirty-six arms.

The oncoming Chaotic Divine Lightning crashed into the golden vortex, causing it to tremble violently like a small boat in a raging storm, and despite Han Li's best efforts, the vortex was simply unable to devour the Chaotic Divine Lightning.

Balls of Chaotic Divine Lightning exploded around the vortex, causing it to shrink rapidly, and Han Li's five time law treasures were quickly revealed.

As more Chaotic Divine Lightning began to crash down from above, the five time law treasures were quickly obliterated, and Han Li's complexion turned deathly pale as he threw up a mouthful of blood with an alarmed look on his face.

His five time law treasures were the most important treasures in his possession, and they had helped him overcome countless formidable foes, yet they had just been shattered like fragile pieces of chinaware!

While it was true that he could slowly reform them over time, how was he supposed to oppose Gu Huojin in his current state?

With the golden vortex destroyed, the Immortal Golden Cloud around Han Li was also significantly weakened, and it was being rapidly whittled away by the Chaotic Divine Lightning.

Bursts of destructive power infiltrated his body from all directions, causing his meridians to tremble violently, and even in his most formidable devilish deity form, he was struggling to withstand the assault.

He hurriedly channeled his Heavenly Baleful Purgatory Arts and the remaining time law powers in his body with all his might, and bursts of tremendous power erupted out of his body to expel the infiltrating destructive power.

Right at this moment, the Immortal Golden Cloud around Han Li finally collapsed under the might of the Chaotic Divine Lightning, forcing him to lash out with his fists to defend himself.

In the blink of an eye, thirty-six golden fist projections were unleashed, every single one of which possessed the power to smash a hole in the heavens, yet they were instantly eradicated upon contact with the Chaotic Divine Lightning without so much as a whimper.

A vast expanse of chaotic lightning came cascading down upon Han Li, tearing his thirty-six arms to shreds.

At the same time, countless scales were flying off his body, following which his skin and flesh were torn away to reveal the black bones underneath.

If it weren't for the fact that he was protecting these bones with all his might by channeling both his Heavenly Baleful Purgatory Arts and his time law powers, they would've also been destroyed.

His ravaged body was sent flying like a tattered rag, and he reverted back to his human form in the process.

The Chaotic Divine Lightning in the sky dissipated shortly thereafter, and the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate had managed to weather the storm, but several cracks had appeared over its surface, and the dark red light emanating from it had also dimmed significantly.

Behind the reincarnation plate was a humanoid ball of light, from within which emerged the Reincarnation Palace Master.

His complexion was also extremely pale, clearly indicating that withstanding the storm of Chaotic Divine Lightning had taken quite a toll on him.

To his dismay, the Devilish Monarch's lifeless body was hovering nearby, having already been dismembered into several pieces.

Han Li was able to survive the ordeal, but was also only just barely clinging onto life.

"Did you enjoy the taste of my Chaotic Divine Lightning? Now that both of your little helpers are done, you're the only one left. How are you going to exact your revenge now?" Gu Huojin chortled with glee.

Bursts of chaotic light were streaming into his body from the surrounding vortex, and his aura swelled to even greater heights.

"Your Chaotic Divine Lightning is indeed formidable, but it's not over yet!" the Reincarnation Palace Master harrumphed coldly as he flew back in retreat while opening his mouth to release a ball of dark red light, within which were several dozen reincarnation law threads, all of which vanished into the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate in a flash.

The reincarnation plate immediately began to revolve rapidly while releasing a burst of tremendous suction, drawing in the bodies of Han Li and the Devilish Monarch.

One of the six holes on the reincarnation plate swelled drastically in size as it devoured the Devilish Monarch's remains, and the Reincarnation Palace Master made a rapid string of hand seals, upon which the reincarnation plate began to radiate dazzling, dark red light that seeped into the space around it.

Gu Huojin's brows furrowed slightly upon seeing this.

Immediately thereafter, the Devilish Monarch flew out of another hole on the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate, and he was back to peak condition, having just been resurrected.

He looked around in a bewildered manner for a moment, but he was quickly able to make sense of what had just happened, and he extended a grateful bow toward the Reincarnation Palace Master.

"No need for formalities, Fellow Daoist Shi. I was able to resurrect you with my reincarnation law powers, but it's cost you one future lifetime, so it's not to be used repeatedly," the Reincarnation Palace Master said, then turned to Han Li to try and bring him back from the brink of death, only to suddenly stop what he was doing as a surprised look appeared on his face.

At this moment, Han Li was hovering in mid-air with only the faintest of auras emanating from his body.

The remaining time law powers in his body had been forced out and dissipated into nothingness, leaving his body completely empty.

He was no longer able to feel any pain, and his five senses were also gradually fading.

Is this it for me?

This thought didn't evoke any fear in his heart. Instead, he felt as if he had fused as one with this world.

Everything around him suddenly began to slow down and present itself to him with incredible clarity.

He was no longer able to see with his eyes, but he was sensing this world through alternative means...
