Chapter 1300: Battle for the Reincarnation Plate

Deep within the gray stone palace in the Yellow Springs.

The Six Paths Reincarnation Plate was revolving rapidly at the Reincarnation Palace Master's behest, and a beam of red light shot out of its center, then spread over Nangong Wan's body to form a red light barrier.

At this point, Nangong Wan's entire body had already risen up out of the pond to be suspended in mid-air, and there was a film of hazy, white light over her entire body. Countless flashing images could be seen within the film of light, and they seemed to have been depicting different memories.

If Han Li were present, he would be able to identify those images as ones that depicted Nangong Wan's past, and there were many that featured him.

The Reincarnation Palace Master swept his gaze over the flashing images, then cast an incantation seal into the center of the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate.

All of the runes on the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate instantly lit up, and at the same time, six glowing, black holes appeared on its surface.

The holes were pitch black with no bottom in sight, as if they were connected to some mysterious place.

With a sweep of his other sleeve, a burst of dark red light shot out of the Reincarnation Palace Master's hand, then transformed into a hand projection that reached into one of the black holes.

Moments later, the hand returned with a cloud of rainbow light held in its grasp, and within the cloud were countless more flashing memories.

A hint of elation flashed through the Reincarnation Palace Master's eyes upon seeing this, and he cast the cloud of light into Nangong Wan's body.

Nangong Wan shuddered violently, and a layer of dreamy, orange light surfaced over her body to join the layer of white light, and the two layers of light began revolving around her, while the Reincarnation Palace Master used the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate to try and assist the layer of orange light in subjugating the white one.

However, the layer of white light proved to be very resilient, and it continued to firmly occupy its own territory.

Nangong Wan's eyes were firmly shut, and her brows were tightly furrowed in agony, while Wyrm 3 looked on with a tense expression.

The Reincarnation Palace Master's brows furrowed slightly as he stopped what he was doing for a moment.

"Is there something wrong, Palace Master?" Wyrm 3 asked in an apprehensive manner.

"Your mother's physical constitution in this lifetime is very compatible with the laws of reincarnation. As a result, her memories are proving to be more stubborn than I anticipated, and they're very difficult to suppress. If I do so by force, I'm worried that I'll harm her in the process," the Reincarnation Palace Master explained.

"Is there any other way?" Wyrm 3 hurriedly asked in a concerned manner.

The Reincarnation Palace Master was just about to respond when he suddenly cast his gaze outside, right as the sounds of a battle began to ring out.

"Are we under attack?" Wyrm 3 exclaimed with a surprised expression.

"It's Bone Emperor. He put on an act and pledged his subordination to me all those years ago, but it seems like he's still not willing to give up on the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate. Oh? He's here as well. What a coincidence," the Reincarnation Palace Master remarked with a hint of surprise in his voice.

"Who's he?" Wyrm 3 asked.

The Reincarnation Palace Master offered no response.

"Right now, the majority of our forces have already evacuated due to the Six Paths Gale winds, and we don't have many people on Yellow Springs Island," Wyrm 3 said with a concerned expression.

"It's fine. I've already made preparations for this scenario, and it'll serve as a good opportunity for me to eradicate some disloyal subordinates. It's just a pity that fewer people have turned up than what I expected. Use this Reincarnation Badge to support the array outside and keep Bone Emperor at bay for now, I'll be with you as soon as I'm done here," the Reincarnation Palace Master said as he swept a sleeve through the air to release a ball of dark red light.

Within the ball of light was a strange, hexagonal badge that resembled a miniature Six Paths Reincarnation Plate, and there were countless specks of dark red starlight shimmering on its surface.

Wyrm 3 caught the badge and immediately flew out of the palace, while the Reincarnation Palace Master murmured to himself, "It's been a very long time since I've displayed my powers. Perhaps everyone has already forgotten about me and is only aware of Gu Huojin now."

......

Han Li descended near the stone palace with Bone Emperor and the others, and an intrigued look appeared on his face at the sight of the dark red light barrier around the palace.

The light barrier was imbued with extremely formidable reincarnation law powers, and for some reason, it struck him with a sense of familiarity.

"He's trying to keep me out with this Reincarnation Array? What a joke!" Bone Emperor sneered as he swept a sleeve through the air once again, and a vast expanse of white light instantly appeared over the entire sky.

An earth-shattering boom rang out as the heavens were split into two, and a gargantuan, white, bone claw descended from above before grabbing down onto the dark red light barrier below.

This bone claw was over ten times more powerful than the previous one, and the entire island was split into pieces by its unfathomable power.

The pieces of the island sank into the Yellow Springs, leaving only the gray palace and the surrounding light barrier hovering in mid-air.

At this moment, the dark red light barrier was trembling violently, and even though it was able to keep the bone claw at bay, it clearly wasn't going to be able to resist for much longer.

Right at this moment, a ball of dark red light flew out of the stone palace before vanishing into the surrounding light barrier, and it was instantly bolstered to its original state.

At the same time, a layer of dark red light appeared over the surface of the entire Yellow Springs, and the dark red light also rose up into the air to clash against the white light above.

The Six Paths Gale winds above the Yellow Springs were swept aside with ease in the face of the clash between the two bursts of tremendous power, and the nearby space became completely pulverized, forming countless spatial rifts that threatened to tear heaven and earth apart.

Immense suction force surged out of the surrounding fractured space, and Han Li's group of five immediately banded together upon seeing this, summoning their protective immortal treasures to form a barrier around themselves.

Xue Li and the others had also gathered around Bone Emperor, and after a brief hesitation, Gui Wu drifted over to Bone Emperor's camp.

Han Li raised an eyebrow upon seeing this, but made no attempt to stop him.

Thanks to the Reincarnation Array, the Yellow Springs down below had also been preserved instead of being devoured by the fractured space.

Right at this moment, a petite figure flew out of the palace, and Han Li was surprised to discover that it was none other than Wyrm 3.

She had also noticed Han Li's group as well, and she was clearly surprised to see them, but the situation was too urgent for her to acknowledge their presence.

She began chanting an incantation while making a string of hand seals, and a radiant, dark red light surged out of her body to form a wheel-like projection.

The dark red light spread over the Yellow Springs also began to transform, taking on the form an enormous wheel that overlooked the entirety of the Yellow Springs with the gray palace at its center.

A dark red array that was over a hundred feet in size had also appeared over the light barrier around the palace, and there were countless tiny, dark red runes dancing within it.

A burst of dull rumbling rang out as a pillar of dark red light that was several hundred feet thick erupted out of the array before piercing straight into the heavens.

The pillar of light was filled with countless flashing, dark red runes, and it was giving off an incredibly heavy, yet simultaneously peerlessly sharp aura.

All of this had taken place in the blink of an eye, and a deafening boom rang out as the giant, white bone claw was sent flying back through the air, while Bone Emperor himself also shuddered slightly before taking several involuntary backward steps.

Xue Li and the others were gathered around Bone Emperor, and they were sent flying like fallen leaves in a fierce storm.

His four early-Great Encompassment Stage subordinates were struck by the power of the pillar of dark red light, and they didn't even get a chance to cry out before their bodies exploded violently.

The rest of Bone Emperor's entourage was able to get away just in time to avoid suffering the same fate, but somehow, Gui Wu was also able to save himself, even though he should've been the most vulnerable one of the group, given that he was only a soul fragment.

As for Han Li's group, they had been standing quite far away to begin with, and Han Li had led everyone on a retreat as soon as the pillar of dark red light appeared, so they remained completely unscathed.

"Isn't that Wyrm 3, Master? What is she doing here? Isn't this supposed to be the Reincarnation King's territory?" Weeping Soul asked through voice transmission.

"Perhaps this Reincarnation King has something to do with the Reincarnation Palace," Jin Tong speculated.

"That's definitely a possibility. In any case, let's just stand by and observe for now," Han Li said, and Weeping Soul and Jin Tong nodded in response.

Han Li then also instructed Shi Chuankong and Violet Spirit to stay put, and neither of them raised any objections, but at the moment, there was a rather grim look on Shi Chuankong's face.

He and Violet Spirit had only been released from the Flower Branch domain not too long ago, and only then had he learned that Bone Emperor and his entourage were also after the Six Paths Reincarnation Plate.

Given the current situation, not only had his chances of securing the treasure become very slim, he seemed to have also been swept up in a conflict that he wished to take no part in.

In contrast, Violet Spirit was looking quite calm, and it seemed that she was content to follow Han Li's lead.

Han Li could sense Shi Chuankong's unease, and he turned his gaze back to the stone palace down below as he said, "If anything goes wrong, all of you should return to the Flower Branch domain."

Whatever attack that Wyrm 3 had just unleashed had been so formidable that it had managed to even force back Bone Emperor, so it was most likely the case that the array around the stone palace had been set up by none other than the Reincarnation Palace Master himself.

With that in mind, it was clear that the Reincarnation King and the Reincarnation Palace Master were one and the same.

At this moment, Wyrm 3 was confronting Bone Emperor on her own without displaying any fear, so could it be that the Reincarnation Palace Master was in the stone palace behind her?
