Chapter 1190: Five Light Lightning Pond

The Ink Dragon Flying Boat quickly left Eight Plains Mountain and flew away in a certain direction.

Not long after leaving Eight Plains Mountain, Han Li summoned some puppets to operate the flying boat in his stead, then cast several concealment and protective restrictions upon the flying boat, following which he made his way into the cabin of the boat before entering the FLower Branch domain with Xiaobai.

Upon entering the domain, Han Li activated the Celestial Time Array and the Heavenly Wheel Sundial to accelerate the flow of time inside.

Xiaobai immediately sensed the change in the flow of time, and he said, "Something feels different in here. Did you do something with this place, Master?"

"Time flows around fifty times faster than it does outside. Find a place to cultivate," Han Li replied.

"That's incredible! Your mastery over the laws of time must've improved significantly since we last parted ways!" Xiaobai praised, then sat down with his legs crossed to cultivate.

Han Li also found a place to sit down, then put on a Reincarnation Palace to examine the advertisement he had released to purchase time law materials.

Unfortunately, even with the high price that he was offering, there were still no takers, and there were no messages from Wyrm 3, either.

A hint of agitation flashed through Han Li's eyes upon seeing this, and he clenched his fist slightly, causing the space by his hand to quiver and warp.

With his increased physical prowess, even the slightest gesture was enough to have a physical impact on the space around him. On top of that, he had a multitude of trump cards up his sleeve, such as the Eon Divine Lamp and the Almighty Sword Array.

However, his power was limited by the fact that he was still only at the pinnacle of the High Zenith Stage.

So long as the Great Encompassment Stage remained out of his grasp, his potential would remain capped.

All of a sudden, Han Li's gaze fell upon a message that had appeared in the trading interface, and a smile appeared on his face as he murmured to himself, "Finally, a glimmer of hope after so long. I suppose it can't hurt to give it a try."

After that, he stowed the mask away, then sat down with his legs crossed and continued cultivating the Almighty Sword Array, as well as his Mantra Domain and Reversal True Axis abilities.

......

On the northwestern border of the primordial land near the greater Golden Origin Immortal Region, many of the primordial tribes in the area had been forced to evacuate due to the pressure exerted by the Heavenly Court, leaving only some unintelligent primordial beasts behind.

The beast cores, blood essence, bones, and other body parts of these primordial beasts were precious materials, so there were more and more cultivators drawn to the area on hunting trips.

Even though there were no longer any primordial tribes present, there were still many hazards in the area. In addition to all of the primordial beasts, there were also many naturally occurring perilous areas that could trap and kill even High Zenith and Great Encompassment cultivators.

There was an area in this region where the mountains and ground were all black in color, and there was also a blanket of dark clouds in the sky with thick bolts of lightning flashing through them. Occasionally, the lightning would strike down from above, and the deeper one ventured into the area, the more frequent the lightning strikes became.

In the deepest part of the area, lightning fell from the sky like a destructive waterfall, and it was as if the entire sky were falling.

The lightning here came in all types of different colors, and just the mere sight of them was enough to raise goosebumps on the skin of the beholder.

This lightning area was enormous, stretching as far as the eyes could see in all directions.

The area was called the Five Light Lightning Region, and it was a very renowned perilous area near the greater Golden Origin Immortal Region.

Occasionally, some cultivators could be seen near the Five Light Lightning Region, venturing into the area in search of treasures under the cover of all types of protective immortal treasures.

The Five Light Lightning Region was an extremely perilous place, but it was also home to a vast array of lightning-attribute materials.

The outskirts of the Five Light Lightning Region weren't all that dangerous, but the same couldn't be said for the area in the heart of the region. The lightning on the outskirts wasn't all that powerful, so as long as one remained vigilant and didn't encounter terrible luck, they generally wouldn't find themselves in any life-threatening danger.

Of course, it was a different story for those who did encounter bad luck.

Somewhere on the outskirts of the Five Light Lightning Area, three cultivators, consisting of two men and one woman, were standing together with their backs facing one another.

Circling around them were around a dozen purple snakes that were over a thousand feet in length. All of them were formed entirely by purple lightning, and thick arcs of lightning were erupting out of their mouths before intertwining to form a giant lightning net around the trio.

Among them was a late-Golden Immortal Stage gray-robed elderly man, and he was joined by a hideous man clad in a suit of black armor and a young woman in red, both of whom were at the mid-Golden Immortal Stage.

The immortal treasures that they were using were also quite powerful, but the attacks from these purple lightning snakes were extremely difficult to withstand.

If it weren't for the fact that they had cultivated a secret technique that allowed them to connect their immortal spiritual power together, their defenses would've already been breached long ago.

"Why are there so many lightning beasts so far out?" the armorclad man yelled in an agitated voice as he conjured up a flurry of staff projections with a thick purple staff to keep the oncoming attacks at bay.

In addition to the lightning in the Five Light Lightning Region, the lightning beasts that naturally arose here also posed a major threat.

The lightning beasts here were formed by the lightning essence in the Five Light Lightning Region, and all of them were extremely powerful, but they generally only resided deep in the region, only venturing into the outskirts on very rare occasions.

"Complaining isn't going to do us any good, Fellow Daoist Zhao. Celestial Maiden Miao, you still have more Meridian Fire Beads, right? Use them to break this lightning net so we can make one final attempt to break out of this encirclement. If we're stuck here any longer, all of us are going to die!" the gray-robed elderly man yelled in an urgent voice.

"I only have one Meridian Fire Bead left, and we've tried that before. The bead is able to break this lightning net and temporarily free us, but it's unable to harm these lightning snakes, and they're far faster than us, so we won't be able to get away anyway," the woman in red said in a hesitant manner.

"Not to worry, I have a spatial restriction talisman that can immobilize these lightning snakes for a short period of time. I didn't use it because we were still quite deep in the Five Light Lightning Region earlier, but now that we've reached the outskirts, all we need is to have the lightning snakes off our backs for a short while to allow us to escape," the gray-robed man explained.

"You should've told us that earlier, Fellow Daoist Sun!" the woman in red said in an ecstatic manner as she flipped a hand over to summon a fist-sized crimson bead.

The bead was riddled with dense, fiery patterns that were giving off intense fire law power fluctuations, and it was clear that the woman was getting ready to use it.

The armorclad man's brows furrowed slightly upon seeing this, but before he had a chance to say anything, the woman had already released the crimson bead, sending it flying toward the purple lightning net.

An earth-shattering boom as an enormous crimson sun that was over a hundred thousand feet in diameter rose up in the sky, sending huge waves of fire sweeping through the air in all directions.

The purple lightning net was instantly torn to shreds by the crimson sun, and the two of the nearby lightning snakes were also swept up by the spreading waves of crimson fire, but not only were they not startled into retreat, all of them pounced at the trio of cultivators instead.

The gray-robed elderly man flipped a hand over to produce a white talisman with a series of silver patterns engraved upon it, then opened his mouth to release a ball of globule of blood essence.

The blood essence vanished into the talisman in a flash, and it transformed into a ball of silver light that vanished into the nearby space.

Countless rays of silver light instantly emerged from the surrounding space in a radius of dozens of kilometers to bind the lightning snakes and render them immobile, but the same also applied to the armorclad man and the red-robed woman.

In contrast, the gray-robed elderly man was enshrouded in a layer of silver light that shielded him from the same immobilizing effects.

"Sun Chongshan, what is the meaning of this?!"

The armorclad man and the red-robed woman were naturally furious at this turn of events.

"Please forgive me, fellow daoists. This talisman of mine immobilizes all living beings in a nearby area upon activation, and I'm afraid there was nothing I could've done to prevent this," the gray-robed elderly man said in an apologetic manner, then cupped his fist in a parting salute before flying away as a streak of silver light.

The armorclad man and the red-robed woman were staring daggers after him, but they could only watch helplessly as he made his escape.

Meanwhile, the lightning snakes were roaring in fury while struggling with all their might, and the rays of restrictive silver light around them was beginning to give out.

The armorclad man and the red-robed man also began struggling vehemently upon seeing this, hoping to free themselves before the lightning snakes could.

However, the rays of silver light were extremely resilient, and neither of them had much immortal spiritual power, so their struggles proved to be completely futile.

In contrast, the lightning snakes were able to absorb the nearby lightning to bolster their powers, and it didn't take long before the rays of silver light around them were snapped one after another.

Looks of despair appeared on the faces of the armorclad man and the red-robed woman upon seeing this, but right at this moment, an earth-shattering boom suddenly rang out from deep in the lightning region, drowning out even the rumbling thunderclaps ringing out in the area.

Immediately thereafter, seventy-two streaks of golden swordlight appeared in the distance, traversing through the lightning like seventy-two roaming dragons.

Each streak of swordlight was giving off unrivaled sword intent and lightning law power fluctuations, and they were devouring all of the lightning in their path.

Even though the streaks of swordlight were still very far away from the pair of trapped cultivators, their immense sword intent and law power fluctuations were clearly detectable even from afar.
