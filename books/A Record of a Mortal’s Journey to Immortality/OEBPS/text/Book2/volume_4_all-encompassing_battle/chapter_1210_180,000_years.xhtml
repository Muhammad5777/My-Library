Chapter 1210: 180,000 Years

It didn't take long before the immortal spiritual power in Han Li's body was completely sucked dry, but the devouring force didn't fade, turning to target his immense bloodline power instead.

He gritted his teeth tightly as he continued to persevere, but even after his immortal spiritual power had been completely exhausted, the devouring force released by the spirit domain was only becoming stronger, seemingly intent on sucking him completely dry.

This isn't going to work!

Han Li was just about to give up and release the Immortal Origin Stones again when a burst of devouring force that was several times more powerful than before suddenly converged toward him from all directions, instantly rendering him completely immobile, unable to even lift a finger.

An alarmed look appeared on his face as he tried to struggle against the devouring force with all his might, but to no avail.

His bloodline power was still being rapidly devoured, and a sense of feebleness was beginning to arise in his body, while his mind was also dipping into unconsciousness.

Weeping Soul didn't know what was happening, but she could sense that something was very wrong, and she yelled out in alarm as she hurriedly approached Han Li.

However, right at this moment, a burst of powerful golden light erupted out of his body in a formidable wave, sending Weeping Soul flying back through the air.

Countless more waves of golden light continued to sweep out of Han Li's body, while he seemed to have entered a half-asleep state.

All of a sudden, the waves of golden light formed a golden ball that was around ten feet tall around him.

Countless golden runes were swirling over the golden ball to keep out the spirit domain's devouring force, and the outflow of bloodline power instantly ceased, snapping Han Li back into a fully conscious state.

He took a moment to compose himself, then looked around at the surrounding golden ball as a surprised look flashed through his eyes.

In the next instant, a burst of incredibly profound law intent entered his mind from the golden ball.

The cultivation art of the Great Five Elemental Illusion Mantra flowed through his mind character by character, sentence by sentence, and all of pieces of guidance that Patriarch Miro had offered to him also arose, along with the many insights that he had gleaned himself from his cultivation of the Great Five Elemental Illusion Mantra.

All of these fused together as he extended his right hand forward, almost in a trance-like state, and a thick beam of golden light erupted out of the palm of his hand, contained within which was a multitude of time law threads.

He had gained many more time law threads during his ascension to the Great Encompassment Stage, and he currently possessed over three hundred and thirty time law threads.

The golden converged toward a single spot with all of the time law threads entangled within, and a burst of tremendous time law powers erupted forth from within.

All of the golden light and time law threads instantly vanished, replaced by a golden ball that was roughly the size of a human head. It resembled a miniature sun that was giving off boundless time law power fluctuations, and in the next instant, the golden ball abruptly fused into the surrounding spirit domain.

The entire spirit domain began to tremble and sway violently, and countless cracks appeared over its surface like a shattered mirror.

The spirit domain quickly fell away, and the Celestial Time Array also ceased operating, while Han Li reappeared in the Flower Branch space.

The golden ball around him had also vanished, and he fell onto his backside with a loud thump while gasping for air.

His complexion was incredibly pale, but his eyes were glowing with elation.

That golden ball just now seemed to have been the supposed final form of law power manifestation, known as a law orb.

Patriarch Miro had once told him about the states that law powers could exist in, namely law threads, law rings, and law orbs.

Only after truly mastering a type of law powers to a highly advanced level could one create a law orb.

Somehow, his near-death experience had granted him this unexpected blessing.

"Are you alright, Master? You scared me to death just now!" Weeping Soul exclaimed as she flew over to his side and helped him into an upright position.

Han Li gave her a nod of reassurance, then took some pills to aid in his recovery.

Weeping Soul could see that Han Li was a little feeble, so she remained by his side to keep a lookout while he recuperated.

The world's origin qi within the Flower Branch domain converged toward Han Li, while bursts of the world's origin qi also flowed into the domain from outside.

Not only that, but energy of all types of other attributes were also flowing into his body from the domain, including fire, lightning, wood, and even devilish and ghostly qi.

As a Great Encompassment cultivator, his control over the energy of heaven and earth had become countless times more proficient than before.

As the name suggested, the powers of a Great Encompassment cultivator encompassed everything around them, granting them an unrivaled level of control over their environment.

After an indeterminate period of time, Han Li slowly opened his eyes, having recovered all his lost energy.

Great Encompassment cultivators were able to recover from their injuries extremely quickly thanks to their ability to draw upon all types of energy from heaven and earth.

On top of that, the Twin Birth Tree seemed to possess healing properties that had been inherited by the Eastern Vicissitudes Divine Tree, thereby further enhancing Han Li's recovery abilities.

"What happened just now, Master? Are you alright now?" Weeping Soul asked in a concerned manner.

"I'm fine. The space of accelerated time that I set up wasn't perfect, so I suffered some backlash from it just now, but I've already made a full recovery."

Han Li didn't have a comprehensive understanding of the space of accelerated time himself, so he could only give Weeping Soul a rather ambiguous explanation.

"That's good to hear," Weeping Soul said with a sigh of relief.

Han Li cast his gaze toward a certain direction in the Flower Branch domain, then made a grabbing motion to release two bursts of golden light, sweeping up the cultivating Xiaobai and the Essence Fire Child before carrying them to his side.

His control over his own power had become so proficient that the two of them continued to cultivate, unaware of the fact that they had been moved.

With a sweep of his sleeve, Han Li released his time spirit domain once again, which integrated itself with the Celestial Time Array to create the ten-thousandfold space of accelerated time.

Encompassed within the spirit domain, Xiaobai instantly snapped to his senses before looking around with a surprised expression.

Meanwhile, the Essence Fire Child continued to refine the demon core of the Three-Legged Golden Crow in an oblivious state.

"The flow of time in this space is ten thousand times faster than that of the outside world, so all of you can cultivate in here from now on," Han Li said.

"Does that mean ten thousand years in here is only the equivalent of one year in the outside world?" Xiaobai exclaimed.

"Let's go cultivate over there so we don't disturb Master," Weeping Soul said as she ushered Xiaobai away to the side, while Han Li closed his eyes and took a moment to compose himself before channeling his Great Five Elemental Illusion Mantra.

Now that the issue regarding his space of accelerated time had been resolved, he could focus on his cultivation.

With the Reincarnation Palace mission looming so close, he had to take full advantage of this time to enhance his own powers as much as possible.

Unfortunately, he didn't have any pills in his possession that could aid him in this process, so he could only cultivate by absorbing the world's origin qi. Thankfully, he had already opened almost all of the one thousand eight hundred profound acupoints over his body, so opening immortal acupoints was going to be far easier for him than for others.

Bursts of radiant, golden light surged out of his body, forming a ball of golden light the size of a house, and it was pulsing incessantly in a rhythmic fashion like a giant, beating heart.

With each pulse, immense bursts of the world's origin qi would converge from all directions to be funneled into it.

180,000 years flew by in a flash.

All of a sudden, the space of accelerated time began flashing before rapidly dimming.

The ball of golden light shuddered before instantly fading away to reveal Han Li with over four hundred and sixty immortal acupoints glowing all over his body.

Even without the assistance of pills, he had managed to open a hundred immortal acupoints over the past 180,000 years of cultivation.

He looked up into the air and made a beckoning motion, and the moon formed by the Mantra Treasured Axis descended from above right as the final Time Dao Rune on its surface was fading away.

Just as he had anticipated, with all of the Time Dao Runes snuffed out, the space of accelerated time would unravel on its own.

Having opened a hundred immortal acupoints during this bout of seclusion, he had attained two hundred Time Dao Runes, bringing the total to over one thousand eight hundred Time Dao Runes, which had supported the space of accelerated time for 180,000 years.

This meant that each Time Dao Runes could grant him about a hundred years of cultivation.

It seemed like he was going to have to make it a priority to accumulate more Time Dao Runes.

At the same time, Weeping Soul and Xiaobai also awakened from their cultivation.

Xiaobai had already completely refined his father's bloodline power, making his Ink-Eyed Pixiu aura completely restrained and undetectable, so he appeared no different from a pure human.

His aura had almost reached the mid-Great Encompassment Stage, and a surprised look flashed through Han Li's eyes upon sensing, but it then quickly occurred to him that this was really no cause for surprise.

Xiaobai was the only descendant of the Ink-Eyed Pixiu, who had been very close with Bai Ze, so the latter was sure to have granted him a multitude of cultivation resources.

For the average Great Encompassment cultivator, 180,000 years of cultivation really wasn't much at all, yet Xiaobai had managed to make significant progress during this time.

Weeping Soul's aura had also become a lot stronger, but her improvement was severely lacking compared with Xiaobai. Having said that, the Soul Scattering Ghost Flute had already fused as one with her aura, indicating that it had already been completely refined.

Even though it currently wasn't being played, a series of ghostly notes could be seen flashing around it, presenting a mesmerizing sight to behold.

As soon as Han Li's gaze fell upon the flute, a chorus of ghostly howls instantly began ringing out inside his mind.

He hurriedly averted his eyes in an alarmed manner while channeling his Spirit Refinement Technique to quell the unrest in his soul.

The Essence Fire Child had also awakened, and not only had it regained all of its lost power, it had become a lot more powerful, with the flames over its body giving off boundless light and heat.
