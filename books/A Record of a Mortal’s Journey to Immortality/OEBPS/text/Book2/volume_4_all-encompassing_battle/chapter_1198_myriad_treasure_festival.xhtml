Chapter 1198: Myriad Treasure Festival

Over ten years flew by in a flash.

Greater Golden Origin Immortal Region, Gilded City.

Gilded City was situated in the northwestern part of the Greater Golden Origin Immortal Region, and it was one of the immortal region's largest cities, ranking alongside the likes of Nine Origins City, Golden Origin City, and Hundred Creations City, home to the Nine Origins Temple, the Golden Origin Immortal Palace, and the Hundred Creations Mountain, respectively.

In contrast with the other three major cities, Gilded City wasn't ruled over by any superpower, so it was a relatively free place.

The city attracted many cultivators who wished to be free from the oppression of the Nine Origins Temple, the Hundred Creations Mountain, and the Golden Origin Immortal Palace, and they had formed an alliance called the Sun Moon Alliance.

The top-end power of the Sun Moon Alliance was inferior to that of the Nine Origins Temple and the Golden Origin Immortal Palace, but it had immense potential and was very friendly to some lesser powers and vagrant cultivators. As a result, more and more smaller powers had joined the sect over time, significantly bolstering its overall strength.

Inside a booth in a lavish, nine-story restaurant in the city, three figures were seated at a table, having some wine together, and one of them was none other than Han Li.

He was joined by Weeping Soul and Xiaobai, and all three of them were of course in disguise.

Even though the Reincarnation Mission hadn't yet commenced, Han Li had some other matters to attend to, so he had arrived in the greater Golden Origin Immortal Region early.

Now that he was at the Great Encompassment Stage, he had become far more confident and wasn't as timid as before.

In another pleasing turn of events, Weeping Soul had finally awakened and also reached the Great Encompassment Stage.

"I didn't think that there would be a place in the greater Golden Origin Immortal Region free from the influence of the Nine Origins Temple and the Golden Origin Immortal Palace," Xiaobai remarked as he took large swigs out of a green cup, clearly very fond of the wine inside.

"Don't get complacent. Gilded City may be under the jurisdiction of the Sun Moon Alliance, but I'm sure there must be Nine Origins Temple and Golden Origin Immortal Palace spies here, so we still have to proceed with caution," Han Li warned.

"It's fine, we have nothing to worry about with Big Sister Weeping Soul on our side. She can detect animosity from a mile away, so no one will be able to sneak up on us," Xiaobai said with a wide smile, and Han Li also turned to Weeping Soul with a faint smile.

Not only had Weeping Soul's powers taken a significant stride following her progression to the Great Encompassment Stage, her sensory abilities had also been further enhanced, allowing her to detect the presence of animosity.

Han Li had conducted an experiment to discover that even he couldn't mask his animosity from Weeping Soul, despite his tremendous spiritual sense.

Of course, it was possible for him to mask his animosity from Weeping Soul by enveloping his body in his own time law powers, but anyone who tried to approach them with their entire body enveloped in law powers would be a huge red flag in itself, and their intentions would be made abundantly clear.

"The law powers that I've cultivated are related to souls, so I'm naturally more sensitive in this regard, but don't place too much trust into this ability of mine. It's possible for other cultivators of similar law powers to get past my detection by using certain soul fluctuation concealment techniques," Weeping Soul said.

"Cultivators of such law powers are very rare, so it's unlikely for us to encounter one. On top of that, I'm sure you'll be able to detect the presence of such cultivators, so if such an encounter does arise, then we'll just have to be extra careful," Han Li replied with a faint smile, seemingly very confident in Weeping Soul.

"That's right, I have full trust in Big Sister Weeping Soul!" Xiaobai chimed in with a wide smile.

"I'll do my best," Weeping Soul said with a faint smile of her own.

Han Li cast his gaze out the window of the booth as a slightly troubled look appeared on his face.

He had already been in Gilded City for some time, and during this time, he had explored many avenues to try and find out information about the Nine Origins Temple and Jin Tong.

However, given the turmoil in the greater Golden Origin Immortal Region, the Nine Origins Temple was exercising an even stricter policy of secrecy than normal, making it impossible for Han Li to uncover any useful information.

"Don't worry, Master, there's still loads of time left until the Bodhi Banquet, so I'm sure Boss is fine," Xiaobai reassured.

Han Li nodded in response, then cast his gaze out the window again.

Right at this moment, the sound of a gong strike rang out from afar, and Han Li's eyes instantly lit up as he cast his gaze toward a giant shop in the distance, where the sound had come from.

The shop stretched for close to ten kilometers, and it was entirely constructed from a sky blue material that was riddled with countless specks of starry light, presenting a breathtaking sight to behold.

Placed on either side of the shop's entrance was a jade Qilin statue, and standing beside the statues were units of bodily refinement cultivators clad in suits of blue armor, all of whom had opened over a hundred profound acupoints.

Judging from the faint light emanating from their suits of armor, they were clearly all graded immortal treasures, serving as further testament to the power and prestige of the establishment.

Above the entrance of the shop hung a huge plaque that read "Sun Moon Pavilion" in elegant, flowing characters that were giving off an immense aura, indicating that their inscriber had to have been someone of an extremely lofty cultivation base.

At this moment, many streaks of light were converging toward the entrance of the Sun Moon Pavilion from all directions before fading away to reveal a growing group of cultivators, all of whom were quite advanced in cultivation base.

There was no lack of High Zenith cultivators among them, and all of them were chatting among themselves in an excited manner.

"This is what I've been waiting for! The Myriad Treasure Festival happens once every ten thousand years. I've heard that there are always many exceptional treasures presented during the Sun Moon Pavilion auction."

"The Sun Moon Pavilion has been constantly reaching new heights of late. I've heard that they're planning to compete with the Hundred Creations Mountain during this Myriad Treasure Festival, so they'll definitely have prepared some outstanding treasures. Our budget is rather tight, so we may not be able to buy anything, but it'll certainly be an exciting event to behold."

"I've saved up as many Immortal Origin Stones as I could. Hopefully, I can purchase a pill or two that can help me make a breakthrough."

"I'm planning to purchase a good immortal treasure so I can hunt for more powerful beasts in the primordial land."

......

"Let's go, the auction is about to begin," Han Li said as he rose to his feet in the booth.

Xiaobai immediately sprang up from his chair with an excited look on his face.

"Xiaobai, your aura is being concealed by the Reincarnation Palace mask, but it may not be able to fully conceal the aura of your Ink-Eyed Pixiu bloodline. There are going to many powerful cultivators at the auction, so it's best for you to remain hidden," Han Li suddenly said, then swept a sleeve through the air to release a burst of golden light, sweeping the dumbstruck Xiaobai into the Flower Branch domain before he had a chance to protest.

"Let's go," Han Li said to Weeping Soul, and the two of them emerged from the restaurant.

In addition to trying to find information about the Nine Origins Temple, Han Li's second objective for coming to Gilded City was to attend this auction.

In order to enhance its own reputation and status, the Hundred Creations Mountain hosted a celebratory event once every ten thousand years with all of its branches in the greater Golden Origin Immortal Region in participation, and this event was referred to as the Myriad Treasure Festival.

All of the other major trading houses in the greater Golden Immortal Stage would take this opportunity to hold auctions of their own, hoping to profit from the event.

The Sun Moon Alliance was almost on par with the Hundred Creations Mountain in terms of power, and it was also operating an enormous trading house, so it was naturally not going to remain a passive bystander.

With the steady growth of the Sun Moon Alliance, competition between the two superpowers during the Myriad Treasure Festivals had become more and more fierce over time.

The greater Golden Origin Immortal Region was one of the largest immortal regions of the True Immortal Realm, and it was also home top-tier sects like the Nine Origins Temple and the Hundred Creations Mountain, so there were countless precious treasures and materials constantly flowing into the immortal region from smaller immortal regions, and everything that appeared during the Myriad Treasure Festival was the cream of the crop.

Back when Han Li first left Eight Plains Mountain, the primary concern on his mind was finding the final time-attribute law material to establish equilibrium in his Great Five Elemental Illusion Mantra, and he was very relieved to have heard about the Myriad Treasure Festival from the Reincarnation Palace.

His original plan was to purchase the final time-attribute law material during the festival at all costs, but that was no longer necessary as he had obtained it from Wyrm 3.

Even so, he was still very much looking forward to the festival.

After all, he was going to be venturing into the Nine Origins Temple soon, so he had to make some preparations.

On top of that, Weeping Soul was also missing a suitable immortal treasure to use, so this was the ideal opportunity to find something for her as well.

The problem was that even though the biggest auction of all was going to be held at the Hundred Creations Mountain main branch, given the Hundred Creations Mountain's close ties with the Nine Origins Temple and the Heavenly Court, Han Li naturally didn't dare to go there, and that was why he had settled for the Sun Moon Pavilion auction.

Han Li and Weeping Soul made their way into the Sun Moon Pavilion, and in order to avoid attracting attention, they had both suppressed their cultivation bases to the late-High Zenith Stage. However, they didn't suppress it any further than that as keeping too low a profile during events like auctions came with its own drawbacks.

They were approached by a beautiful Golden Immortal Stage woman with a pair of blue eyebrows that matched her blue dress, and she greeted, "Welcome, seniors. My name is Lan Mei, and I am an administrative disciple of the Sun Moon Pavilion. Please allow me to serve as your guide."

"Lead the way," Han Li said.

"Given your cultivation bases, both of you are entitled to VIP seating. Please come with me," Lan Mei said as she led the way ahead.

"Do you have a list of the treasures that are going to appear during the auction?" Han Li asked.

"Yes, I have them here," Lan Mei said as she offered a pair of blue jade slips to Han Li and Weeping Soul.

Han Li was rather surprised to see this.

"This is generally strictly confidential information, is it not? Keeping the auction items a secret can pique the interest of the auction-goers and generate more buzz, so I'm surprised the Sun Moon Pavilion is readily handing out this information."

He had only raised the question just in case, and he wasn't expecting an affirmative response.

"The leader of our Sun Moon Alliance says that integrity and sincerity are the keys to business, and that there's no need to engage in such tacky maneuvers. That is the policy that our Sun Moon Alliance has always abided by," Lan Mei replied with a faint smile, and it was clear that she harbored a great deal of admiration for the alliance leader.
