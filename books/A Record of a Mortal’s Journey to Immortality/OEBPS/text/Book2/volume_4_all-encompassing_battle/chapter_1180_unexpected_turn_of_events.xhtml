Chapter 1180: Unexpected Turn of Events

"It looks like the Rahu and the Sky Roaming Kun Peng True Spirit Kings still have no intention of returning to the primordial land," Lekima sighed upon seeing this.

"Why didn't they stay in the primordial land like Senior Bai Ze?" Han Li asked.

"The Rahu and the Sky Roaming Kun Peng are both very free-spirited and spend all their time traveling between realms, so even I've never seen them in the flesh before. Given the tumultuous state of the True Immortal Realm, I can only pray that they'll return soon to help Father return the primordial land to its former glory," Lekima sighed.

Han Li nodded in response and remained silent.

Once all of the True Spirit King projections had been manifested, the Bai Ze projections also faded away, following which Bai Ze declared, "The five True Spirit King bloodlines have been summoned. They're not complete, but they should be enough. It's time to select the heirs to inherit these five True Spirit King bloodlines."

All of the primordial beings present immediately rose to their feet before extending grateful bows toward Bai Ze.

It was all thanks to Bai Ze's assistance that they were able to successfully summon the five True Spirit King bloodlines. Otherwise, their blood arrays alone wouldn't have been anywhere near sufficient to get the job done.

Bai Ze gave them a dismissive wave, then made his way toward the Huntun and Lightning Bird Tribe.

The five tribes had already selected their heirs.

Qing Dian, the white-haired young man, and Yuan Shanbai emerged from the Qing Ape Tribe, the Zouwu Tribe, and the Mountain Moving Ape Tribe, respectively, then sat down with their legs crossed in mid-air to begin absorbing the true spirit bloodlines.

Xiao Bai was the only candidate to inherit the Ink-Eyed Pixiu bloodline, so a choice didn't even have to be made there.

From the Heavenly Fox Tribe emerged Liu Le'er, while many other young Heavenly Fox beings looked on with envious expressions, yet right as she was about to begin absorbing the Nine Tailed Immortal Fox bloodline, a dissenting voice suddenly rang out from outside.

"Hold on a second!"

Immediately thereafter, a streak of gray light flew into the crimson space, then faded away to reveal a gray-robed, middle-aged man.

The man had a gentle and refined appearance, but there was a dark and cold look in his eyes, as if his heart were enshrouded in shadow.

"Who goes there?" many of the primordial beings present exclaimed.

This was an extremely important occasion to all of the primordial tribes, and a watertight encirclement had already been formed around the entirety of Eight Plains Mountain, whlie a multitude of powerful restrictions had been set up outside the Asura Blood Gate to prevent the ceremony from being interrupted, so everyone was naturally quite alarmed by this unannounced arrival.

A surprised look appeared on Han Li's face at the sight of the gray-robed man, who he was able to identify as none other than Liu Zizai.

However, he then immediately shook his head as he thought to himself, No, he's not Liu Zizai. He's identical to Liu Zizai in appearance, but their auras are somewhat different.

The cold and menacing aura being given off by the gray-robed man was very different from Liu Zizai's aura, and for some reason, the man struck Han Li with a sense of familiarity, as if they had met somewhere in the past.

Judging from Lekima's expression, it seemed that he also recognized the gray-robed man, while many of the Heavenly Fox beings were staring at the man as if they had just seen a ghost.

"Please pardon my intrusion, everyone. I am also a member of the Heavenly Fox Tribe, and I've come here to compete for the right to inherit the True Spirit King bloodline of the Nine Tailed Immortal Fox." the gray-robed man said as he cupped his fist in an apologetic salute toward everyone.

At the same time, an extremely pure Heavenly Fox bloodline aura erupted out of his body, and the aura was so immense that it was causing the space around him to ripple violently like the surface of a disturbed lake.

All of the Heavenly Fox beings were forced to stumble back in retreat by this tremendous aura, and only Liu Qing and two other men standing beside him were able to stand their ground.

Liu Le'er was also sent flying by this aura, and her complexion had paled slightly from the impact.

However, Han Li immediately appeared beside her to catch her, then swept a sleeve through the air to form a golden light around before them, warding off the immense aura that continued to surge out of the gray-robed man's body.

Liu Le'er was able to catch her breath before expressing her gratitude to Han Li, but instead of looking at her, Han Li was staring intently at the gray-robed man.

The man's cultivation base was at the pinnacle of the Great Encompassment Stage, a sliver more advanced than even the Dark Heavens Devilish Deity's cultivation base.

"This man is too powerful for us to contend with, so let's take a step back for now," Han Li communicated to Liu Le'er through voice transmission.

Liu Le'er raised no objections to this, and the two of them flew back in retreat.

At the same time, Han Li cast his gaze toward Bai Ze, and he discovered that Bai Ze was also looking at the gray-robed man, but he didn't seem irked at all by the man's unannounced intrusion.

Instead, he was merely observing as the situation unfolded, seemingly content with being a passive bystander.

The expressions of all of the other primordial beings in the primordial space changed drastically upon sensing the gray-robed man's cultivation base, and no one dared to raise any further objections at his intrusion.

All of the Heavenly Fox beings hurriedly steadied themselves, while Liu Qing took a glance at Bai Ze out of his peripheral vision.

His brows furrowed slightly at the sight of Bai Ze's inaction, but he then immediately composed himself before instructing several of his tribesmen to go and protect Liu Le'er.

He had seen Liu Le'er retreating under Han Li's protection, and he made no effort to stop them as he knew that Han Li had her best interests in mind.

Three Heavenly Fox beings appeared around Han Li and Liu Le'er in a flash, namely Fox 3, Elder Mu, and a dark-skinned elderly man who was at the pinnacle of the High Zenith Stage.

"Thank you for protecting Young Mistress Le'er, Fellow Daoist Han," Fox 3 said as he cupped his fist in a grateful salute.

His gratitude was echoed by the dark-skinned elderly man, while Elder Mu also cupped his fist in a salute toward Han Li, albeit in a very reluctant manner.

"There's no need to thank me, Le'er is just as important to me as she is to all of you," Han Li replied with a nod.

"It hasn't been long since we last met, but you've made some significant strides once again, Fellow Daoist Han. It's very impressive that you're completely mentally unaffected in the face of all of these powerful cultivators," Fox 3 remarked.

Han Li's heart stirred slightly upon hearing this.

At the moment, all of the Great Encompassment cultivators in the crimson space had released their own auras to contend with that of the gray-robed man, so the entire space was filled with terrifying Great Encompassment Stage pressure. Liu Le'er, Fox 3, and the other High Zenith cultivators were able to keep up a calm facade, but their souls were trembling in their bodies in an instinctive, submissive reaction to the power of all of the Great Encompassment cultivators.

Not only was it a huge stride in cultivation base to progress from the High Zenith Stage to the Great Encompassment Stage, one's soul would also undergo a massive evolution and begin to fuse as one with one's body.

Every single gesture made by a Great Encompassment cultivator carried with it this crushing soul power, so it was almost inevitable for High Zenith cultivators to feel intimidated by their Great Encompassment Stage counterparts, often losing the battle before it had even begun.

Han Li could also feel this sense of spiritual subjugation from Great Encompassment cultivators, but given his mastery of the Spirit Refinement Technique, his soul was far more powerful than that of the average High Zenith cultivator, so the pressure on him was far lessened.

However, in this moment, he couldn't feel this pressure at all, and that was why he was able to react before all of the Heavenly Fox beings to jump to Liu Le'er's aid before everyone else.

It didn't take long for Han Li to realize the reasons behind this change.

Even though he hadn't progressed in his cultivation base of late, he had gained control over the Eon Divine Lamp and attained a large number of time law threads. On top of that, he had also figured out the inner workings of the Almighty Sword Array and successfully set up a space of accelerated time in his Flower Branch domain.

All of these achievements had instilled within him immense confidence, and even though his soul hadn't become any more powerful, this confidence had bolstered his mentality to the extent that the powerful souls of Great Encompassment cultivators no longer had any subjugative effect on him.

This was naturally a very pleasing discovery for Han Li, but he didn't allow his expression to betray his emotions.

Instead, his expression remained unchanged as he said, "You're far too kind, Fellow Daoist Liu."

Fox 3 didn't dwell on the subject, and he cast his gaze toward Liu Qing with a hint of concern in his eyes.

At this point, all of the Heavenly Fox beings had already recovered from their astonishment, and they were locked in a confrontation against the gray-robed man.

"How dare you come to the Asura Blood Gate after what you did, Liu Tianhao!" Liu Qing spat with a cold expression.

Liu Tianhao was completely unfazed by the hostility in Liu Qing's voice, and he smiled as he asked, "What did I do? I'll have to ask you to elaborate, Fellow Daoist Liu Qing."

"It seems like you don't have the best memory. No matter, you'll have plenty of time to contemplate your own sins in the afterlife!" Liu Qing said with a cold smile as he swept a sleeve through the air, and all of a sudden, Liu Tianhao found himself in a boundless expanse of inky darkness with a massive river of stars rapidly revolving around him.

It was as if he had suddenly been teleported deep into a boundless starry sky.

All of a sudden, the giant river stars swelled drastically to fill up the entire starry sky, and a burst of terrifying power surged out of it, sweeping up Liu Tianhao and threatening to tear his body to shreds.

At the same time, a storm of asteroids came hurtling out of the river of stars, battering against Liu Tianhao to send him flying back through the air, while blood splattered in all directions from his body.

Liu Tianhao paid no heed to his own injuries as he remarked, "This is not a bad illusory spirit domain, but unfortunately for you, it won't be enough to trap me."

Immediately thereafter, a gray mark appeared over his glabella, then split open to reveal a gray vertical eye, out of which came pouring an endless torrent of gray flames.

The gray flames were giving off extremely pure illusory law powers, and every part of the starry sky that came into contact with them instantly collapsed.

In the blink of an eye, the starry sky was completely destroyed, and Liu Tianhao found himself back in the crimson space, with all of his injuries also instantly erased.
