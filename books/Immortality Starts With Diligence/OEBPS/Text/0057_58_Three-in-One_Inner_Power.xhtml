58. Three-in-One Inner Power

Lin Fei stayed in the room all the time, reciting Buddhist scriptures. He could feel that the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique was steadily improving. He didn't know how long had passed when he felt a slight tremor in his body, followed by the sound of the system in his mind.

"You recite the Buddhist scriptures with a joyful mood, and the progress of Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique is increased by 1%."

"Your Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique has reached its limit and cannot be improved further."

As the system's voice fell, Lin Fei's whole body began to shake with a "crackling" sound. His bones seemed to be vibrating madly, and a familiar warmth flowed out from the depths of his bones, attaching to his bones and flowing throughout his body.

Lin Fei clearly felt that his bones seemed to be expanding at a terrifying speed. He stretched out his hand, and at that moment, he felt that the bones in his arm were indestructible.

Of course, this was Lin Fei's illusion caused by his bones improving too much all at once.

"Inner power! Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique has finally given birth to inner power!"

Lin Fei opened his eyes with excitement and immediately summoned the system panel to check his martial arts.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Acquired First Level

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Perfection (0% progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Perfection (18% progress)

Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique: Body Refining Limit (cannot be improved)

Flowing Clothes Skill: Transcendence (0% progress)

Lin Fei saw that his Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique had indeed reached its limit and could not be improved further. An inner power had been automatically generated.

This was the third inner power that had been born in Lin Fei's body. The inner powers borning in his bones circulated throughout his body, and the two-in-one inner power seemed to be stimulated and was also restless.

At this point, Lin Fei no longer hesitated.

"Integrate!"

Suddenly, Lin Fei proactively mobilized his two-in-one inner power to integrate into the inner power of the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique. The two Inner Powers quickly entangled and merged into a completely new Inner Power.

"Boom!"

Lin Fei shook all over.

The two Inner Powers rapidly entangled and merged, gradually transforming into a completely new Inner Power that instantly skyrocketed. The Three-in-One Inner Power!

Lin Fei felt that this new Inner Power was filled with terrifying power, about ten times stronger than the previous Inner Power of the Giant Whale Technique. This was purely a feeling, but he thought this feeling was not wrong.

"Qi and blood, tendons and bones, and skin and membranes, these three Inner Powers have merged into one. This kind of Inner Power should be the best among all martial artists in terms of both quality and quantity! If I return to the Nine Profound Sect, I can choose the acquired level martial arts in the martial arts pavilion and officially step into the acquired level of cultivation!"

Lin Fei's mood was surging. He finally waited for this day! Three Inner Powers merged into one, creating the strongest Inner Power.

It was not that the more Body Refining techniques one practiced, the more Inner Powers one would get. In fact, all Body Refining martial arts could be basically categorized into three types: Qi and blood, tendons and bones, and skin and membranes.

Practicing Vajra Iron Cloth and then hard martial arts like Golden Bell Cover would not have much effect, and ultimately only produces one Inner Power, which was skin and membranes Inner Power.

Therefore, Lin Fei had now merged his three Inner Powers of Qi and blood, tendons and bones, and skin and membranes into one. In a sense, this was the great perfection of the Body Refining level.

"These days, thanks to the two Masters of the Hongfa Temple. It's time to leave the Hongfa Temple. I should say goodbye to the two Masters first!"

Lin Fei was going to get up and bid farewell to the two Masters. During these days, he discussed and debated Buddhist teachings and doctrines with them. Lin Fei admired the character and Buddhist realm of the two abbots very much.

"Screech."

Lin Fei opened the door and find that there was no one outside. Normally, the temple was deserted, but there were often monks passing by. One could even hear the chanting of sutras in the main hall. But now it was empty and very quiet.

Just as Lin Fei frowned, a novice monk ran over from a distance.

When he saw Lin Fei, the novice monk came to him panting and said anxiously, "Donor Lin, there's something going on in our temple today, and we've temporarily closed the temple. So please leave the temple first."

"Hmm? The temple is closed? May I ask what's going on?" Lin Fei was a little surprised.

The Hongfa Temple was a famous temple far and wide, and pilgrims came in large numbers every day. The temple was open almost every day of the year. Why was it closed today for some special reason?

Seeing that Lin Fei refused to leave, the novice monk became anxious and said, "Donor Lin, please follow me and leave quickly. There is a wicked guest who has come to the temple today, and our martial monks may not be able to withstand them. If you stay in the temple, you may be in danger. That's why our abbot has asked me to take you away."

"Wicked guest?" Lin Fei's face changed.

He could faintly smell a hint of blood coming from the direction of the back door.

"Both Masters have shown great kindness to me. I'd like to see what kind of wicked guest dare to kill in this pure land of Buddhism." Lin Fei used his Flowing Clothes Skill and his figure flashed, taking him several yards away.

Even the novice monk was stunned to see this.

"Donor Lin..." The novice monk wanted to call out loudly. But in the blink of an eye, Lin Fei's figure disappeared.

The novice monk was horrified. He had no idea that the person who was discussing Buddhism with the two masters was actually a skilled martial artist.

The little novice monk gritted his teeth and ran towards the direction of the back door as well.
