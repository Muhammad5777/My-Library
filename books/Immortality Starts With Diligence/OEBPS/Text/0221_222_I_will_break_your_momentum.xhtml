222. I will break your momentum

No one who hadn't directly faced a saber momentum especially a transcendent saber momentum would ever know how terrifying it was. And Lin Fei's saber momentum was not just ordinary saber momentum.

Lin Fei's saber momentum was the integration of dozens of top-notch saber techniques. It was supported by the foundation of hundreds of saber techniques and eventually evolved into the current saber momentum.

Therefore, Lin Fei's saber momentum was different from other people's saber momentum. Some people's saber momentum was quite extreme. But Lin Fei? His saber momentum encompassed everything. It had countless saber technique foundations. It had the integration of countless saber momentum. There's no weak point at all. Very comprehensive.

But comprehensive doesn't mean mediocre. On the contrary, once saber momentum is comprehensive, it will be even more terrifying. Under the suppression of the saber momentum, a peer's strength will be suppressed to a very low level.

"What a terrifying saber momentum! Fame is not as good as seeing it in person."

"Although it was known long ago that 'Dao Seeker' Lin Fei suppressed hundreds of Martial Saints with his saber momentum, I thought it was simply because those hundreds of Martial Saints were loose cultivators and were too weak. But now it seems that this is not the case. It's just that Lin Fei's saber momentum is too strong! Even if it is also saber momentum transcendence, it will be suppressed by Lin Fei's saber momentum. This level of saber momentum, once it reaches perfection in the future, will be the top martial momentum, no one can compare!"

Laymen watch the bustle of the crowd, while professionals perceive the knack and how. Those inner sect elders who had condensed the Energy Flower, Qi Flower, or comprehended momentum could see the terror of Lin Fei's saber momentum at a glance.

Martial momentum also had strengths and weaknesses. And Lin Fei's saber momentum was top-notch saber momentum, second to none. Under this kind of saber momentum suppression, one's strength would be reduced to a very low level.

At this moment, on the stage, Ao Yunxiao personally experienced the horror of Lin Fei's saber momentum. He felt as if his whole body was being cut by countless sabers. Even the true essence in his body and his physical body were suppressed. Moreover, this suppression far exceeded Ao Yunxiao's imagination.

"Swish".

Ao Yunxiao raised his head. He looked at Lin Fei and said word by word, "Worthy of 'Dao Seeker', your saber momentum is much stronger than I imagined. But if you think that you can suppress me just by relying on saber momentum, then you are wrong, very wrong! I'm not one of those martial artists with a weak will. Even if it's saber momentum, it can't break my will!"

In the next moment, Ao Yunxiao's body also burst with momentum. It was just momentum, not martial momentum. But a person's momentum represents his mental will. What's more, the true energy in Ao Yunxiao's body was like a volcano erupting. It exploded violently.

"Boom."

Under such terrifying saber momentum, Ao Yunxiao's true essence was still able to flow smoothly. Even more, the more Lin Fei's saber momentum was suppressed, the more Ao Yunxiao's true essence was shaking violently.

At the same time, Ao Yunxiao's body also moved.

One step, two steps, three steps...

Every step Ao Yunxiao took was under the immense pressure of Lin Fei's saber momentum. Every step he took came at a great cost. It was as if he was carrying several large mountains on his back, but not even Ao Yunxiao's back bent a bit, showing the strength of his body.

Ao Yunxiao was getting closer to Lin Fei. In a blink of an eye, he was very close to Lin Fei. It must be said Ao Yunxiao could be considered the strongest opponent Lin Fei had ever seen, whether among the Martial Saints or Foundation Establishment level. Facing his terrifying saber momentum, his mind and will were not affected at all.

"Shoo."

Lin Fei flicked his finger. Suddenly, a saber essence flew out instantly and turned into a rainbow light. In the blink of an eye, it reached Ao Yunxiao's face.

Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique! This is a real Martial Saint level saber technique. Many true disciples might not necessarily be able to cultivate it to transcendence. But now, Lin Fei just used a saber essence to display Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique. And the speed of that rainbow light was definitely a transcendent Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique!

How long had Lin Fei joined Ancient Origin Sect? At most, not even a month!

"Bang."

The rainbow transformation saber slashed at Ao Yunxiao. Ao Yunxiao's eyes were indifferent. He punched out directly.

"Boom."

This punch seemed to shake the entire saber momentum. After all, Ao Yunxiao's punch was not just physical. True essence also exploded together!

Whether it was the physical body or the true essence, Ao Yunxiao was infinitely close to condensing the Energy Flower and Qi Flower. But Lin Fei's rainbow-transforming saber was not weak either. It was a Martial Saint level, transcendent saber technique. Moreover, it was also boosted by saber momentum. Its power was increased by several times!

What's more important was Lin Fei's saber essence. It was not any divine weapon. But it was more terrifying than any divine weapon. It contained terrifying power.

"Bang bang bang".

Ao Yunxiao threw several punches in a row. Above his head, it seemed that there were shadows of mountains and rivers. That was his fist technique.

Lin Fei's eyes were cold and his face was expressionless.

"Shoo."

He flicked his finger again. Another saber essence flew out.

"Boom."

This saber essence was like a volcano erupting. There was a faint sense of heat as if it was a ball of fire. It broke through the void and slashed toward Ao Yunxiao.

"Is that... Flame Burning Heaven Saber!"

"How can Lin Fei display two kinds of Martial Saint level saber techniques?"

"This Flame Burning Heaven Saber is not at transcendence, but it should be at the mastery realm. Moreover, it seems not far from transcendence."

"Flame Burning Heaven Saber and Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique. Both of these are Martial Saint level saber techniques, and both can evolve saber momentum. Generally, Martial Saints who can learn one saber technique have already expended countless efforts. Lin Fei actually practices two saber techniques?"

"Moreover, he can even evolve it from within his saber momentum. How amazing is his true essence?"

For a moment, the esteemed practitioners of the saber technique rose abruptly from their seats, their gazes riveted upon the figure of Lin Fei on the stage.

Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique, and Flame Burning Heaven Saber – two distinct saber techniques. To the observers, it appeared as if two saber saints had taken control. Lin Fei, though stationary at the center of the stage, put Ao Yunxiao in a challenging defensive position. The two saber techniques were not to be easily quelled, let alone when enhanced by the power of saber momentum!

Yet, before Ao Yunxiao could fully grasp the situation, Lin Fei's finger twitched in another subtle motion, "Whoosh," unleashing the third surge of saber essence. This time, the saber essence was suffused with a divine, monumental aura, akin to the grand arrival of celestial spirits from above, directing their wrath towards Ao Yunxiao.

The Holy Spirit Saber Technique! Three successive saber techniques, each one a pinnacle of martial saint level, all under the empowerment of saber momentum, increasing their force manifold.

"Bang, bang, bang."

One saber attack followed another, ceaselessly, each strike potentially lethal for a typical martial saint. Even Ao Yunxiao could barely stand against such an onslaught. Consequently, he took a step back.

One step, two steps, three steps...

From whence Ao Yunxiao had come, he retreated, continuing further until he was nearly at the edge of the stage.

And Lin Fei? He remained as placid as a tranquil cloud, hands casually behind his back, showing no signs of exertion. The mere three saber essences had put Ao Yunxiao into a retreating position, nearly at the brink of defeat. This spectacle left the disciples, who were expecting a fierce clash, with wide-eyed disbelief. This was no fierce duel – it was a downright crushing defeat!

Yes, a crushing defeat!

Lin Fei was overpowering Ao Yunxiao, leaving the latter helpless, unable to retaliate. No one had expected such a lopsided battle.

"Ao Yunxiao is being overwhelmed by Lin Fei – completely suppressed. Is saber momentum truly that potent?"

"There's a vast difference between having martial momentum and not. But hasn't Ao Yunxiao defeated those who transcended martial momentum before?"

"It's not that Ao Yunxiao is weak, it's just that Lin Fei is too strong! Lin Fei's saber momentum is unbelievable, arguably one of the strongest. At least in the current Ancient Origin Sect, there's no martial momentum this powerful. Moreover, Lin Fei's comprehension of the saber technique is unimaginable. The Holy Spirit Saber Technique, Flame Burning Heaven Saber, Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique – mastering any of these saber techniques requires a martial saint to dedicate decades or even a century

But what about Lin Fei? It hasn't even been a month since he joined the Ancient Origin Sect. In other words, he mastered these three saber techniques in less than a month. Even though they haven't reached perfection, it's only a matter of time. Such saber talent and comprehension are stunning, making him a born saber genius! And what about the true essence Lin Fei exerts? Do any of you think it's just true essence? It's far stronger! Along with the saber technique and saber momentum, they seem to be perfectly fused. That's why Lin Fei is so strong!

However, don't assume Ao Yunxiao will be easily defeated. He's been gathering his energy. Now it's a crucial moment. With Ao Yunxiao's willpower, he might be able to overcome the immense pressure and perceive his fist momentum. Who wins and who loses is still uncertain."

Many disciples were abuzz with talk. Even some with discerning eyes saw the current situation of Ao Yunxiao. His situation was indeed dangerous. But for Ao Yunxiao, was it not also an opportunity? Under pressure, Ao Yunxiao might understand the martial momentum!

Indeed, Ao Yunxiao was doing just that at the moment. His whole body was trembling violently. That was the power being exerted to its extreme. Even his genuine energy and physical body were suppressed to the utmost by Lin Fei, injuries started to appear on him, and Ao Yunxiao's fighting will do not diminish at all. Instead, his fighting spirit was rising more and more prosperous.

"No one can defeat me, no one can! I will counterattack under pressure! Lin Fei, the pressure you give me is destined to make me understand the martial momentum. I have to thank you for that! Today, I will comprehend the momentum!"

With a roar, the next moment, Ao Yunxiao threw a punch. With this punch, the air trembled. Vaguely, Ao Yunxiao seemed to be about to break through a certain threshold. As if a great martial momentum was about to be born.

Under pressure, in the dire straits, Ao Yunxiao was about to break through and understand the martial momentum! This was also what Ao Yunxiao had been dreaming of. This was his fundamental goal of fighting Lin Fei.

However, Lin Fei's eyes became colder and colder. His battle with Ao Yunxiao today was not to help Ao Yunxiao succeed, was it? Ao Yunxiao had injured Shen Lianxin. Shen Lianxin would be unable to cultivate for a year. Lin Fei had to make Ao Yunxiao pay ten times, a hundred times the price. If he could not kill Ao Yunxiao, he would make Ao Yunxiao regret it for a lifetime!

"You want to comprehend the momentum? Then I will break your momentum!"

Lin Fei's eyes were icy cold. The next moment, his saber momentum shrank violently, almost condensed to a point. At the same time, the three sabers merged into one. In the void, a terrifying great saber appeared.

"Slash!"

The next moment, saber momentum, saber essence, and saber law all merged into one, transformed into a heavenly saber, descending from the sky.

A saber slashed into Ao Yunxiao's body. Ao Yunxiao only felt a tremor in his body. It felt as if his body was being torn apart. The genuine energy instantly became chaotic. But that was not important. He knew very well that Lin Fei would not kill him. This was the iron rule of the Ancient Origin Sect!

Since he would not die, he would have no fear. He only wished that the martial momentum could succeed! However, Lin Fei's saber was not just the saber essence and saber technique. It also contained saber momentum!

This saber, actually directly severed Ao Yunxiao's fighting will that was about to reach a critical point, causing Ao Yunxiao's momentum to collapse in an instant.

"No... my momentum!"

Ao Yunxiao roared, his voice even carrying a touch of fear.

"Sputter."

The next moment, Ao Yunxiao's momentum plummeted. He spat out a mouthful of fresh blood and was knocked off the stage by Lin Fei's saber!
