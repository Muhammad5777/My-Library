85. Saber Intent

At this moment, in Lin Fei's mind, the light of the Saber-drawing Technique surged. It seemed as if something was being nurtured within it. A terrifying power seemed to be trying to break out from the light.

Time passed by, minute by minute.

Surprisingly, the system's voice did not ring in Lin Fei's mind. Apparently, the system also determined that Lin Fei's fusion of saber techniques had not yet ended. No one knew how much time had passed.

"Boom."

Finally, the light of the Saber-drawing Technique in Lin Fei's mind burst instantly, turning into tiny spots of light. However, the shattering of the light was not the end, but merely the beginning.

Immediately after, a new force was born. This force was even sharper and purer than the Saber-drawing Technique. It was a more refined "saber" power. It was an "invisible" force.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei suddenly opened his eyes. At that instant, it seemed as if countless saber shadows faintly appeared above Lin Fei's head.

These saber shadows were invisible and intangible. They could only be sensed. Because this was the saber intent!

"Your Saber-drawing Technique and Nine Shadow Saber Technique have engaged in a fierce battle."

"Your Saber-drawing Technique has the upper hand, defeating the Nine Shadow Saber Technique."

"Nine Shadow Saber Technique willingly merged into the Saber-drawing Technique."

"Your Saber-drawing Technique's cultivation progress has reached 100%."

"Your Saber-drawing Technique has given birth to the saber intent!"

In Lin Fei's mind, one voice after another rang out, all were related to the Saber-drawing Technique.

"As expected, after fusing the Nine Shadow Saber Technique with the Saber-drawing Technique, the saber intent was successfully born!"

Without hesitation, Lin Fei immediately called up the system panel.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Acquired Fifth Level

Saber Intent: Beginner (1% Progress)

Three Vitality Technique: Fifth Level (61% Progress)

Quadruple Wave Technique: Perfection (50% Progress )

Leaping Technique: Perfection (0% Progress)

Flowing Clothes Skill: Transcendence (0% Progress)

On the system panel, both the Saber-drawing Technique and the Nine Shadow Saber Technique disappeared. Of course, this didn't mean that the two martial arts techniques had vanished.

It was simply because they had reached their limits and could not be improved further. Therefore, they were no longer displayed on the system panel. This also saved panel space, making it easier to read at a glance.

Both saber techniques had reached their limits. So after fusing, the saber intent was successfully born.

This saber intent was just at the "beginner" stage.

"Saber intent..."

Lin Fei naturally had memories of saber intent in his mind. He was also proficient in mastering the saber intent.

In the next moment, Lin Fei unleashed his saber intent.

Although Lin Fei didn't draw his saber, His entire being seemed to transform into a peerless, sharp treasure saber, radiating a fierce saber edge, making people feel palpitations from a distance.

Lin Fei attached the saber intent to the Star Saber.

"Buzz."

At this moment, the Star Saber began to vibrate slightly. As if it was gently humming.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei drew his saber. Nineteen shadows with a single strike!

Yes, Lin Fei's Nine Shadow Saber Technique fused with the Saber-drawing Technique.

Although it merged into the Saber-drawing Technique, it didn't mean that Lin Fei couldn't use the Nine Shadow Saber Technique anymore. Not only could Lin Fei use the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, but it was even stronger!

It was as if the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, based on its perfection, had reached 100% in cultivation progress.

That meant nineteen shadows with a single saber strike!

The sky was filled with saber shadows. What was even more terrifying was that after attaching the saber intent, the speed and power skyrocketed.

It seemed to have been enhanced in all aspects. Moreover, the saber intent could attack independently. Invisible and intangible, making it difficult to defend against.

Once a martial artist comprehended saber intent, sword intent, or fist intent, their means of attack would become very diverse. Moreover, they could contend with Qi cultivators!

Lin Fei sorted through the memories in his mind. The birth of the saber intent had many benefits.

For example, the Saber-drawing Technique. The current Saber-drawing Technique not only had inner power nurturing it, but the saber intent was also nurturing it.

Once the saber was drawn, it would be earth-shattering. How powerful it would be, even Lin Fei himself was not sure. Moreover, the Saber-drawing Technique also integrated the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

When drawing the saber, it could create nineteen shadows with a single strike!

In other words, when the Saber-drawing Technique was used with a single strike, it was actually nineteen strikes!

How powerful would that be?

Lin Fei didn't plan to test it now. After all, he had been nurturing the saber for a month. Nurturing the saber for a month was not easy. It couldn't be wasted casually.

But even without trying, Lin Fei was confident. His Saber-drawing Technique was very powerful!

In fact, Lin Fei felt that with the dual nurturing of inner power and saber intent, once he drew his saber, he might be able to defeat an innate expert!

"The saber intent has been born, and its progress has reached 1%. That is to say, the saber intent can actually be improved. However, I wonder how fast the saber intent can be improved?"

Many thoughts flashed through Lin Fei's mind.

Now that the saber intent was born. Lin Fei didn't need to worry about his saber techniques for the time being.

This Saber-drawing Technique could be used until the innate realm. Even among innate martial arts, it was very powerful. Lin Fei's saber intent could even contend with innate experts!

However, Lin Fei's martial arts realm was only at the acquired fifth Level.

"Now I can focus on improving my martial arts cultivation. The progress of the Three Vitality Technique is too slow; I need to think of a solution. Perhaps I can take pills."

A thought struck Lin Fei's heart.

His current weak point was actually his martial arts cultivation. The Three Vitality Technique itself was characterized by its stability. Its cultivation speed was quite ordinary. But there were almost no significant bottlenecks.

Lin Fei now felt that the cultivation speed of the Three Vitality Technique was too slow. He could use external help. So, Lin Fei got up and went directly to the Treasure Pavilion. Only the Treasure Pavilion had elixirs that could enhance acquired martial arts.

"Elder."

Lin Fei respectfully said to the elder of the Treasure Pavilion, "I would like some pills or other treasures that can enhance inner power. I wonder if the elder has any recommendations?"

"Enhance inner power?"

The elder glanced at Lin Fei and said indifferently, "Relying on pills and other external forces to enhance inner power will result in impure inner power, which requires a lot of energy to operate martial arts techniques and gradually purify it. It's not worth it. Are you sure you need an elixir to enhance inner power?"

Lin Fei thought for a moment, without hesitation, nodded, and said, "Please recommend one, Elder!"

Lin Fei naturally had his considerations.

Using pills to enhance inner power would indeed lead to impure inner power. And it would take time to purify. However, would this affect Lin Fei? His martial arts were so diligent that they cultivate almost every moment.

It was equivalent to cultivating martial arts techniques for twenty-four hours a day to purify inner power every day. So, Lin Fei wasn't worried about taking elixirs leading to impure inner power.

The Treasure Pavilion elder shook his head.

The young disciples nowadays were so eager for quick success and instant benefits. They hated not being able to achieve innate in just one or two years.

Is it possible? Many geniuses, because of their eagerness for quick success, took pills, which resulted in impure inner power.

They consumed a lot of energy to purify it. In the early stage, the cultivation speed was extremely fast. But in the later stage, especially after the acquired ninth Level, breaking through the realm was even more difficult than ascending to the heavens. There was no hope of achieving innate in their lifetime.

However, these matters had nothing to do with the Treasure Pavilion elder.

So, the elder took out a bottle of pills directly. He introduced it to Lin Fei, "This is the Gather Qi Pill, with a mild medicinal effect. It can to a certain extent speed up the circulation of inner power, thereby enhancing inner power."

Lin Fei thought for a moment. This Gather Qi Pill only accelerated the circulation of inner power. But his inner power was already circulating very quickly. And it was circulating every moment. The Gather Qi Pill had little effect on Lin Fei.

So, Lin Fei shook his head and said, "Elder, is there any pill with a strong effect that can quickly enhance inner power?"

"Strong effect?"

The elder's face darkened, and he said coldly, "Then you might as well use the Burst Qi Pill. One Burst Qi Pill can instantly increase inner power by ten to fifty percent.

However, the Burst Qi Pill is not a cultivation pill but is used by martial artists when fighting desperately. If you encounter a strong enemy and are in a life-threatening crisis, you can take the Burst Qi Pill, and your inner power will surge, thereby greatly increasing your strength in a short period.

However, once the effect of the Burst Qi Pill passes, there will be pill poison residue left in the inner power. It takes several months to gradually remove it.

Taking too many, the pill poison residue will be too much, and it will also affect the foundation of the martial artist."

The elder of the Treasure Pavilion was just speaking casually. No one would use the Burst Qi Pill for cultivation. They all used it as a trump card for desperate situations.

However, Lin Fei's eyes brightened after hearing this.
