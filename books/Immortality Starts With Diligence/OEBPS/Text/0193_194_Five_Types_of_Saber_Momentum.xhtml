There is great terror between life and death.

True Person Tongxuan, a look of terror flashed across his face. He was a high and mighty Foundation Establishment True Person. He could live for five hundred years! Even now, he was not even two hundred years old and still had three hundred years of life to live. He didn't want to die!

But, Lin Fei's saber momentum, pressing down on him, already caused his spiritual power to run not smoothly. Now there was even a thirty-meter heavenly saber locked on him. True Person Tongxuan could feel, once the heavenly saber fell, he would die!

"Sky Reaching Tower!"

A small and exquisite magic treasure flew out of True Person Tongxuan's body. This was an imitation of the real Sky Reaching Tower. Although it was an imitation, it was still a genuine magic treasure.

Once the Qi cultivators of the Sky Reaching Sect reached the Qi Condensation 7th level or above, they would be bestowed a Sky Reaching Tower magical artifact by the sect. This could grow. With daily cultivation, once the Foundation Establishment is successful, becoming a Foundation Establishment True Person, then this Sky Reaching Tower magical artifact, will also transform into a magic treasure. Incredibly powerful!

With this method, the strength of the Sky Reaching Sect's high-level Qi Condensation cultivators above the 7th layer was quite good. Especially the Foundation Establishment True Persons. The longer the cultivation, the more terrifying the power of the Sky Reaching Tower.

At this moment, True Person Tongxuan deployed the Sky Reaching Tower. This was his lifebound magic treasure. But he still felt insecure. He pointed a finger.

"Shoo."

A flying sword instantly flew towards Lin Fei. True Person Tongxuan, actually had two magic treasures! The Sky Reaching Tower in the open, the flying sword in the dark. When he fought someone, both of his magic treasures came out, even a Foundation Establishment True Person couldn't withstand it.

However, facing True Person Tongxuan's two magic treasures, Lin Fei didn't even look at them. The thirty-meter heavenly saber still slashed down.

In an instant, the flying sword arrived in front of Lin Fei. Lin Fei directly stretched out his fair hand. With his flesh and blood, he directly grabbed the flying sword.

"Ding."

The flying sword stabbed toward Lin Fei's hand. It was as if it stabbed at metal. Then, Lin Fei grabbed the sharp flying sword. With a hard squeeze,

"Crack."

the flying sword was crushed and turned into fragments. It was then thrown by Lin Fei, and fell down.

"My flying sword! You actually destroyed my flying sword?"

True Person Tongxuan's face turned pale white. His eyes instantly turned red. This was his magic treasure. He forged it with great difficulty. Now that the magic treasure was destroyed. It was simply heart-wrenching!

However, Lin Fei didn't care. The next moment, Lin Fei's thirty-meter heavenly saber finally fell. It slashed harshly onto the Sky Reaching Tower.

"Boom."

A tremendous force came from the thirty-meter heavenly saber. The Sky Reaching Tower was bright. Spiritual Qi surged, but it couldn't hold for long.

"Crack."

A crack appeared. Then, the cracks grew more and more.

"Boom."

The next moment, the Sky Reaching Tower was directly shattered. Shattered by Lin Fei's thirty-meter heavenly saber.

"Pfft."

True Person Tongxuan spits out a mouthful of fresh blood. The Sky Reaching Tower was different from the flying sword. This was his lifebound magic treasure. Once it shattered, it would cause a backlash for him. But now, he didn't even care about the consequences of the backlash. Because when the Sky Reaching Tower shattered, once the heavenly saber fell, he would die!

"Senior Brother, save me......"

The Sky Reaching Sect, this time two Foundation Establishment True Persons came. The other Foundation Establishment True Person, seeing that Tongxuan was in danger, immediately deployed his own Sky Reaching Tower.

"Suppress!"

Immediately, a huge Sky Reaching Tower, shone brightly as if it had suddenly become hundreds of meters tall. From top to bottom, it covered Lin Fei's head. Moreover, not only the Foundation Establishment True Person of the Sky Reaching Sect but there were also other Foundation Establishment True Persons. They also took action together to besiege Lin Fei.

There were about five Foundation Establishment True Persons in total. They besieged Lin Fei together. Lin Fei's face remained unchanged. The saber essence in his body was, even more, exploding wildly.

"Shoo, shoo, shoo."

Lin Fei flicked his finger. One after another saber essence instantly flew out, flying toward the five Foundation Establishment True Persons around him. Moreover, these saber essence directly condensed into treasure sabers. These treasure sabers were just like they were controlled by someone, exuding a fierce aura.

Saber intent!

These treasure sabers condensed from saber essence. They also had saber intent. Moreover, just like living creatures, they each performed the entire saber technique.

Three Abyssal Sabers!

Every saber was full of murderous aura. Just like a demon from hell. The terrifying saber killing intent was impacting the Foundation Establishment True Person's mind, causing their heart to tremble.

In the face of danger, where would they have the energy to save True Person Tongxuan?

Twenty-Three Sabers!

That was pure speed! One saber was faster than the other! So fast that in the end, one couldn't even see the saber. Just incredibly sharp edges, surrounding a Foundation Establishment True Person.

That Foundation Establishment True Person could only shrink in the protection of his magic treasure and dare not act rashly.

Eternal Night Saber Technique.

With a single saber strike, the whole world seemed to fall into a dead silence of darkness. The Foundation Establishment True Person could only try to tear the night in this darkness.

Samsara Saber Technique.

One saber, one world. Every saber seemed to create a world. Beautiful and fascinating, people unconsciously get lost in it.

God Slaying Saber Technique was the most terrifying.

With one saber, it seemed as if the spirit had been heavily hit. Every saber struck at the heart. After several sabers, the spirit and soul of the Foundation Establishment True Person showed flaws.

The saber intent was everywhere. No one expected that it would turn out like this.

"How could this be?"

"Are we... are we really fighting a Martial Saint?"

"Even if the founder of Great Chang came back to life, he wouldn't be this terrifying, right?"

"One person fighting against six Foundation Establishment True Persons. Even, completely suppressing the six Foundation Establishment True Persons."

Seeing this scene, the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons were shocked. They underestimated Lin Fei too much. Especially Lin Fei's methods at this moment, even though they had never heard of them, could a Martial Saint fight like this?

A Martial Saint evolves saber momentum, then he uses saber essence, but can it make the saber essence fight on its own?

The key is that those saber techniques could actually fight against the Foundation Establishment True Person on equal terms.

This is a bit unbelievable. However, no matter how shocked these Foundation Establishment True Persons were, Lin Fei's thirty-meter heavenly saber did not stop for a moment. Finally, it fell on True Person Tongxuan's head.

"No..."

True Person Tongxuan let out a miserable scream. He wanted to escape. But under the suppression of the saber momentum, even escape is a luxury now.

"Pfft."

The next moment, the thirty-meter heavenly saber slashed down fiercely. True Person Tongxuan's body protection spell, just like tofu, was easily cut open by Lin Fei's heavenly saber. At the same time, the terrifying saber essence directly shredded True Person Tongxuan's body.

"Boom."

Not even the body of True Person Tongxuan did remain. It was directly shredded by the saber essence and turned into a mist of blood, permeating in the void, emitting a strong smell of blood.

True Person Tongxuan was dead! A Foundation Establishment True Person had fallen! For a moment, everyone seemed to be still at this moment, their thoughts stopped!

Another Foundation Establishment had fallen! Although before, Lin Fei had slain Yuan Kongzi with his saber, no one saw it. At least, the Foundation Establishments and Martial Saints present, none of them saw it.

But now, they watched with their own eyes, Lin Fei, under the siege of six Foundation Establishment True Persons, still forcefully killed a Foundation Establishment True Person.

Moreover, it was True Person Tongxuan from the Sky Reaching Sect. Among the Foundation Establishments, he was not weak. But now, he had turned into a pool of blood mist. He died without a whole body!

Moreover, the five saber essence split by Lin Fei also produced unexpected effects. In Lin Fei's mind, the system's voices sounded one after another.

"Your Saber Technique, Three Abssyal Sabers, with the help of saber momentum, suppressed the Foundation Establishment True Person. In a good mood, it began to evolve the Killing Saber Momentum."

"Your Saber Technique, Twenty-Three Saber, with the help of saber momentum, suppressed the Foundation Establishment True Person. In a good mood, it began to evolve Fast Saber Momentum."

"Your Eternal Night Saber Technique, with the help of saber momentum, suppressed the Foundation Establishment True Person. In a good mood, it began to evolve Eternal Night Saber Momentum."

"Your Samsara Saber Technique, with the help of saber momentum, suppressed the Foundation Establishment True Person. In a good mood, it began to evolve Samsara Saber Momentum."

"Your God Slaying Saber Technique, with the help of saber momentum, suppressed the Foundation Establishment True Person. In a good mood, it began to evolve God Slaying Saber Momentum."

Lin Fei never expected, just by dividing into five types of saber techniques to fight against the Foundation Establishment True Person, these saber techniques actually had insights during the battle and began to evolve the saber momentum!?

This was really... too promising!

After all, Lin Fei hadn't even thought about letting other saber techniques evolve the saber momentum. After all, it's too hard! But now, the five kinds of saber techniques were all too promising. They had already begun to evolve the saber momentum. Five kinds of saber momentum, once they evolve and integrate into Lin Fei's saber momentum, Lin Fei's saber momentum could probably make rapid progress immediately!

Moreover, Lin Fei's saber momentum not only integrated these five top-tier innate saber techniques but there were also even more top-tier innate saber techniques. Once those saber techniques evolved the saber momentum, then how strong would Lin Fei's saber momentum be?
