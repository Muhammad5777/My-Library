23. Fused SaberTechnique

"Your saber technique aptitude is strong. Perhaps you may comprehend the legendary saber intent in the future.”

"Saber intent? May I ask, Senior Sister Ling, what does saber intent mean?"Lin Fei asked curiously.

Ling Yao was not a talkative person, but Lin Fei's talent in saber technique was truly amazing, and he was not an ordinary person. Therefore, she did not mind befriending him.

So Ling Yao replied, "It is already very difficult to reach transcendence in ordinary martial arts. As for perfection, unless you have extraordinary talent and comprehension, it is impossible to achieve it.

And above perfection, there is actually another realm called 'the realm of the divine.'

If you can reach the realm of the divine through saber technique perfection, then perhaps you can comprehend the saber intent. This saber intent is the realm of the divine."

Lin Fei listened carefully, "May I ask how to practice saber intent, Senior Sister Ling?"

Ling Yao shook her head, "If I knew, I would have already practiced it."

"I only know that once you have grasped saber intent, others will have no chance of drawing their saber in front of you."

"That powerful?" Lin Fei was shocked.

This saber intent was simply amazing, a completely different realm.

"Saber intent is even more difficult than achieving innate. So, you don't have to worry too much about it.

Although I don't know how to achieve saber intent, I have heard that those who have achieved saber intent are masters of various saber techniques.

So, if you want to achieve saber intent, you can practice other saber techniques more, and perhaps you can find a way."

After speaking, Ling Yao turned around and left the courtyard.

"Thank you, Sister Ling."

Lin Fei thanked her and looked up again.

Ling Yao had disappeared without a trace.

"Saber intent is even more difficult than innate..."

Lin Fei shook his head and looked at the system panel.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial arts realm: Body Refining Fifth Level

Giant Whale Body Refining Technique: Fifth Level (71% progress)

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Transcendence (1% progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Perfection (0% progress)

Vajra Iron Cloth: Fifth Level (1% progress)

Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique: Fourth Level (51% progress)

His Nine Shadow Saber Technique had already reached perfection.

But the progress of the cultivation behind perfection was directly 0.

It was estimated that it would be difficult to make any progress in the future.

The Nine Shadow Saber Technique had reached the level of "one saber, nine shadows," and had already reached its limit.

"The method that Ling Yao mentioned might be worth a try."

Lin Fei made a calculation in his mind.

Wasn't it just practicing more saber techniques? This was nothing for Lin Fei.

His martial arts could practice themselves.

"Let's try a few more saber techniques first and see if it works."

So Lin Fei went straight to the martial arts pavilion.

The elder of the martial arts pavilion was still the same old man with white hair.

This time, the old man didn't say much.

Lin Fei went straight to the first floor and chose some basic saber techniques.

Each of these saber techniques only required 100 contribution points, and ten of them added up to only 1,000 contribution points.

Lin Fei exchanged for all of them, took some time to memorize them, and then returned to his yard.

Now, he just needed to wait quietly.

One day, two days, three days, four days...

In the blink of an eye, six days had passed.

"Your Giant Whale Body Refining Technique, the cultivation progress has increased to 101% after a day of hard cultivation."

"Your Giant Whale Body Refining Technique has broken through to the sixth level!"

After six days, a voice rang in Lin Fei's mind.

Immediately, Lin Fei's blood and qi roared inside his body like a great river, surging continuously. A powerful momentum emanated from Lin Fei's body.

Body Refining sixth level!

Lin Fei finally broke through, and the seventh level was just a step away.

Moreover, given Lin Fei's age, once he reached the Body Refining seventh level, he would have a hundred percent chance of becoming an inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect.

Although there was only one word difference between inner and outer disciples, the treatment was vastly different.

Lin Fei's Body Refining martial arts had been steadily improving. But no one had yet taken the task of instructing him in the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

Lin Fei even suspected that no one in the Nine Profound Sect had perfected the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

Lin Fei checked the ten basic saber techniques again.

These saber techniques were very simple.

In just a few days, he had already reached the transcended level.

Of course, this was because these saber techniques were all fast and had some similarities to the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

Lin Fei continued to wait.

In the blink of an eye, another ten days had passed.

"After many days of hard work, your Thirty-Six Fast Saber Technique has reached perfection."

"After a fierce battle with your Nine Shadow Saber Technique, your Thirty-Six Fast Saber Technique was defeated and willingly merged into the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, increasing its foundation."

"Your Nine Shadow Saber Technique has increased by 1% in practice progress."

These were the series of voices that suddenly rang in Lin Fei's mind.
