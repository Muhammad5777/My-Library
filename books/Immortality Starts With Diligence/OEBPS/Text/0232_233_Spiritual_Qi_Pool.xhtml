233. Spiritual Qi Pool

The Spiritual Qi Pool, as the name implies, is a pool made up of Spiritual Qi. However, it's not merely Spiritual Qi within this pool but Spiritual Essence! That is to say, Spiritual Qi is continuously compressed, finally becoming a liquid, bound within the Spiritual Qi Pool. This involves not merely a simple array, but also some treasures containing rich Spiritual Qi. In short, this Spiritual Qi Pool is precious to the Ancient Origin Sect. It is one of the Sect's important locations.

The Ancient Origin Sect expended considerable effort to construct this Spiritual Qi Pool. The fundamental goal was to assist the Martial Saints within the sect to successfully condense the Qi Flower.

When Lin Fei arrived outside the Spiritual Qi Pool, there were not many people there. After all, the Spiritual Qi Pool was for condensing the Qi Flower. How many Martial Saints within the whole Ancient Origin Sect are qualified to condense a Qi Flower?

However, to maintain the consumption of the Spiritual Qi Pool, it was also open to inner disciples and true disciples. The reason is simple. These inner disciples and true disciples, either innate or martial saints, have certain requirements for Spiritual Qi, and though not as great as those Qi cultivators, there is still a demand.

The wealthier ones could spend contribution points to cultivate within the Spiritual Qi Pool. Cultivating here, the results would be twice as effective with half the effort. But it's expensive! One day requires a thousand contribution points. Therefore, the martial artists who can come to the Spiritual Qi Pool all seize every minute to cultivate.

"Swoosh."

Lin Fei arrived outside the Spiritual Qi Pool. Many disciples saw Lin Fei and also recognized him. After all, Lin Fei was not an unknown person in the Ancient Origin Sect now. He was the one who defeated Ao Yunxiao under everyone's watch, He was a prominent figure in the Ancient Origin Sect. Moreover, Lin Fei was a "Dao Seeker." Therefore, when he arrived, all eyes were focused on him.

"Isn't that Elder Lin, the 'Dao Seeker'?"

"Elder Lin has actually come to the Spiritual Qi Pool."

"He's only in the early stage of Martial Saint, having just arrived at the Ancient Origin Sect about half a year ago, does he already need the aid of the Spiritual Qi Pool for cultivation?"

"Elder Lin has an extraordinary understanding of saber momentum. However, he doesn't seem to have anything particularly outstanding in physical cultivation and True Essence. Therefore, spending contribution points to come to the Spiritual Qi Pool to assist with True Essence cultivation seems normal."

Many people knew about Lin Fei's past. They knew Lin Fei was a "Dao Seeker". He had joined the Ancient Origin Sect for only half a year. And he was merely in the early stage of Martial Saint.

How much could he improve in half a year? They didn't think much about it. Instead, they assumed that Lin Fei was here to cultivate using the Spiritual Qi Pool. After all, cultivating in the Spiritual Qi Pool gives one twice the result with half the effort, and it's much more efficient.

Lin Fei did not pay attention to the discussions of others. He walked straight into the Spiritual Qi Pool. There was more than one Spiritual Qi Pool. There were nine.

There is a difference among these nine Spiritual Qi Pools. There is one located in the best spot. Its Spiritual Essence is the densest. But regular disciples can't go in and even ordinary Martial Saints can't go in. Even if they spend contribution points, they can't go in. Because this Spiritual Qi Pool is for those Martial Saints who are condensing the Qi Flower and it doesn't cost any contribution points. But once one enters the Spiritual Qi Pool, he must try to condense the Qi Flower.

This rule deters most people. And no one would try to exploit loopholes. After all, once one enters this Spiritual Qi Pool, he must condense the Qi Flower. If he doesn't attempt to condense, he will be severely punished!

There was no one in this Spiritual Qi Pool at the moment. After all, in the whole Ancient Origin Sect, there are very few instances of two people attempting to condense the Qi Flower at the same time. Most of the time, this Spiritual Qi Pool is empty. Now was the same.

However, when Lin Fei headed straight for that one Spiritual Qi Pool, the elder who was guarding the Spiritual Qi Pool had already blocked Lin Fei's path.

"The Spiritual Qi Pool, one thousand contribution points for one day's use."

The Martial Saint Elder spoke calmly.

"Do I also need to spend contribution points to use that highest Spiritual Qi Pool?"

Lin Fei asked.

"Hm?"

The elder raised an eyebrow. He examined Lin Fei carefully from head to toe. Seemingly, he realized something.

"You are the 'Dao Seeker' Lin Fei, I recognize you. You defeated Ao Yunxiao, and your understanding of saber momentum is indeed strong. But you've only been in the Ancient Origin Sect for half a year. Even if you've been cultivating True Essence all this time, how far could your cultivation have progressed? If you forcefully try to condense the Qi Flower and fail, it could even damage your foundation, making it impossible for you to condense it in the future."

The Martial Saint Elder was also reminding Lin Fei. Going to that Spiritual Qi Pool, one must condense the Qi Flower.

"Please open the Spiritual Qi Pool for me, elder."

Lin Fei's tone was still calm. But it was filled with unwavering determination. The elder didn't try to persuade him any further. After all, whether or not to condense the Qi Flower was Lin Fei's own choice.

So Lin Fei passed the elder directly. He entered the highest-located Spiritual Qi Pool.

The disciples around who were originally cultivating hard all opened their eyes one by one, looking at Lin Fei in surprise.

"That... That is the Spiritual Qi Pool for breakthroughs, Elder Lin is going there, is he trying to condense the Qi Flower?"

"So Elder Lin didn't come to cultivate with the help of the Spiritual Qi Pool, but to try to condense the Qi Flower. But, he just joined the Ancient Origin Sect half a year ago, can he condense the Qi Flower?"

"Even if Elder Lin's talent is high. How much True Essence can he cultivate in half a year? If he fails to condense the Qi Flower, it will damage his foundation."

"Elder Lin is too impatient. Energy Flower, Qi Flower, Spirit Flower. Among these three flowers, the Spirit Flower is the hardest. But Elder Lin has already transcended in saber momentum, he has a great chance to condense the Spirit Flower first. When the time comes, wouldn't it be better to slowly condense the Essence Flower and Qi Flower, with the guarantee of successful condensation? He's being too rash."

Many disciples were somewhat puzzled. Lin Fei was trying to condense the Qi Flower so early. This is very hard to succeed. Did Lin Fei think that condensing the Qi Flower was easy? Or was Lin Fei too confident? Almost everyone was surprised and puzzled by Lin Fei's choice to condense the Qi Flower now. They even felt it was a pity.

But Lin Fei didn't hesitate at all. His expression was still calm. Step by step, he came to the front of this Spiritual Qi Pool.

Lin Fei looked down. Inside the Spiritual Qi Pool, the green Spiritual Essence filled the whole pool. Moreover, around the Spiritual Qi Pool, there were at least dozens of array formations densely packed, keeping the Spiritual Essence fixed within the pool.

These array formations were set up by the Foundation Establishment True Persons who were invited. Presumably, the Ancient Origin Sect also paid a heavy price. Only with these dense array formations could they firmly fix that Spiritual Essence inside the Spiritual Qi Pool.

Lin Fei took a deep breath. Then, without hesitation, he jumped directly into the Spiritual Qi Pool. With a "plop", Lin Fei had already fallen into the Spiritual Qi Pool. Suddenly, a large amount of Spiritual Essence surrounded him. They desperately tried to drill into Lin Fei's body.

If one cultivates here, the efficiency would increase at least tenfold, or even higher! No wonder so many innate martial artists or Martial Saints were willing to spend a thousand or even several thousand contribution points. However, Lin Fei did not come to the Spiritual Qi Pool to cultivate. He was preparing to condense the Qi Flower.

Lin Fei closed his eyes. He slowly adjusted his state. He could sense numerous gazes around him were focused on him, but Lin Fei didn't care. He slowly calmed his mind. His thoughts gradually calmed down as well.

It's not known how long it had been.

"Whoosh".

Lin Fei opened his eyes. His eyes were as calm as an ancient well. Clearly, he had completely adjusted his mood. Moreover, having soaked in such precious Spiritual Essence for such a long time, his body was also almost adjusted to its best condition!

"Let's start."

Lin Fei took a deep breath, no longer suppressing the True Essence in his body.

"Boom".

The next moment, Lin Fei's True Essence exploded from within his body. A terrifying aura emanated from him, instantly enveloping the entire Spiritual Qi Pool.
