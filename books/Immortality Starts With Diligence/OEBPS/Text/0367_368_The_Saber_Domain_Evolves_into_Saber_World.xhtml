368. The Saber Domain Evolves into Saber World

Even the faintest aura of the Martial King Bloodline had the power to utterly suppress the Martial Sovereigns. Thus, within the grand hall of the Ancient Origin Sect, as Lin Fei unveiled the might of the Martial King Bloodline, a colossal phantom silhouette manifested behind him. Intricate patterns adorned its form, and a majestic flood dragon draped over its shoulders and back.

A terrifying aura enveloped the entire hall. Every Martial Sovereign was on the verge of suffocation. The sheer pressure alone was unbearable. Yet, no one felt dread; they were awestruck, even ecstatic. They understood the significance of the aura Lin Fei was exuding.

"That's the aura unique to the Martial King Bloodline!"

"Has Sovereign Lin achieved the Martial King status?"

"Inconceivable! In such a short time, Sovereign Lin has become a Martial King?"

"Has our Ancient Origin Sect birthed a Martial King?"

Hearts of the Wind Sovereign, the sect leader, and others were filled with unparalleled astonishment. They had witnessed Lin Fei's ascent, step by step, even reaching unparalleled prowess beneath the Martial King and Nascent Soul.

Yet, their current shock dwarfed any before. No matter Lin Fei's strength, nothing could compare to the awe of becoming a Martial King. A Martial King signified an eternal lifespan. It meant that as long as Lin Fei existed, the Ancient Origin Sect could thrive, possibly for tens of thousands of years.

In the future, the Ancient Origin Sect could claim lineage to the Martial King, standing on par with the Nascent Soul lineage.

"Sovereign Lin... Have you truly become a Martial King?"

Although many had their suspicions, the sect leader sought confirmation. All eyes turned to Lin Fei, eagerly awaiting his affirmation.

With a sweeping gaze, Lin Fei nodded, "During my recent journey, I encountered a few fortuitous events. I awakened the Martial King Bloodline ahead of time and achieved the status of Martial King. Hence, I intend to venture to Taiyi Dao Palace once more, to eradicate it and put an end to future threats."

His words brought immense relief to the members of the Ancient Origin Sect.

"He truly has become a Martial King!"

"Wonderful! At last, our Ancient Origin Sect has produced a Martial King!"

"The Taiyi Dao Palace could never fathom that Sovereign Lin is now a Martial King, could they?"

"Seize this chance and annihilate Taiyi Dao Palace completely!"

"Then, our Ancient Origin Sect could even replace them!"

The sect members were filled with elation, and all rallied behind Lin Fei. The time had come to confront the Taiyi Dao Palace and obliterate it, for leaving it be would always pose a threat.

"I'll first head to the Martial Dao Tower," Lin Fei announced, "Then, set forth to Taiyi Dao Palace. Gather intel on the Taiyi Dao Palace and prepare thoroughly."

With that, Lin Fei departed from the grand hall, making his way toward the Martial Tower. The hall remained occupied only by the sect leader of the Ancient Origin Sect, the Wind Sovereign, and a few others. Even though Lin Fei had left, the profound shock lingered in their hearts, as if they were still grappling with the revelation.

"From now on, Sovereign Lin shall be the sole Martial King of our Ancient Origin Sect!"

"It's always said that Dao Seekers have boundless potential. Yet, who would have thought that Lin Fei could achieve the stature of a Martial King?"

"It is the fortune of our Ancient Origin Sect!"

"Perhaps, in time, we might rival the glory of Taiyi Dao Palace, shining brilliantly for tens of thousands of years."

These Martial Sovereigns, one by one, were ecstatic, imagining the future grandeur of the Ancient Origin Sect.

...

Lin Fei ventured to the fourth floor of the Martial Dao Tower. He had come there first because he needed a plethora of saber Divine Abilities to integrate into his Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline, evolving the Saber Domain into the Saber World. By then, visiting Taiyi Dao Palace would be all the more prudent. And Lin Fei wouldn't need to expend much time or energy.

Gazing at the array of Divine Abilities before him, Lin Fei took a deep breath. With a resonating hum, he manifested his True Martial King Form, a form powered by his Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline. As such, he resembled a divine saber, his entire being emanating fierce saber edges. Another hum echoed, and the complete Saber Domain enveloped the entire fourth floor.

"I possess the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline and the complete Saber Domain. If you wish to aid in the perfection of my Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline, willingly merge into my saber bloodline in your complete form. The bloodline flows on, and the Divine Ability remains eternal!"

After Lin Fei spoke, he fell silent. Now, he was a Martial King and even held the saber bloodline - a lineage every saber Divine Ability would dream of, yearning to merge into it immediately. How could they resist?

With a series of swift motions, one by one, the saber Divine Abilities transformed into beams of light, infusing into Lin Fei's body and eagerly integrating with his Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline. Instantly, the growth of Lin Fei's Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline commenced.

Perhaps the Ancient Origin Sect didn't possess the most potent saber Divine Abilities, and most might have been Lesser Divine Abilities. But to the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline, whether it was a Lesser Divine Ability or a Great Divine Ability, it mattered not. What Lin Fei sought were Divine Abilities that resonated with the philosophy of the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline.

As long as they could enhance the cultivation progress of the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline, any Divine Ability would suffice. Thus, Divine Abilities began to merge into the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline.

Lin Fei's cultivation of the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline advanced rapidly, reaching 30%, 50%, 70%, and 90%. The progress of Lin Fei's Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline was swift. In such a short span, dozens of saber Divine Abilities were incorporated. With so many saber Divine Abilities, it was only natural for the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline's cultivation progress to soar.

With a thunderous boom, the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline within Lin Fei finally achieved perfection. A tremor coursed through Lin Fei's being, and even his mind echoed with resonating reverberations. For, with the culmination of the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline, the progress of Lin Fei's Saber Domain also surged, reaching a cultivation progression of 100%.

Simultaneously, a voice from the system resonated within Lin Fei's mind.

"Your Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline's cultivation progress has reached 100%."

"Your Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline has achieved perfection!"

"Influenced by the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline's completion, your Saber Domain's cultivation progress has ascended to 100%."

"Your Saber Domain has broken through."

"Your Saber Domain has begun its evolution into the Saber World..."

Lin Fei had anticipated this. He surmised that once his Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline reached perfection, his Saber Domain might also transform directly into the Saber World. And now, it seemed his presumption was accurate. His Saber Domain had commenced its autonomous evolution into the Saber World, a process even Lin Fei couldn't control.

With a resonating hum, Lin Fei's Saber Domain began to spread uncontrollably.

"Go!" he commanded. He was unsure of what would transpire once the Saber Domain evolved into the Saber World. However, he was certain he couldn't remain within the Ancient Origin Sect during this breakthrough. For even a minor ripple from the Saber World might potentially obliterate the entirety of the Ancient Origin Sect.

Without hesitation, Lin Fei soared from the Martial Dao Tower, departing the Ancient Origin Sect to a desolate mountain range.

"Let it begin," he declared, no longer restraining his Saber Domain.

Another rumbling sound filled the air, and the Saber Domain expanded magnificently and wildly. In an instant, the entire mountain range was enveloped within it. Dazzling saber lights and fierce saber edges gleamed brilliantly. Creatures within the mountain range cowered, trembling in fear.

And amidst this grand spectacle, Lin Fei's Saber Domain began its gradual evolution into the Saber World.
