669. Return to Dark Demon Domain

In the Divine Realm, affairs were largely managed by Azure Emperor Supreme and several other Supremes. Most of the day-to-day operations were delegated to various True Immortals and Martial Ancestors.

Azure Emperor Supreme's primary concern remained the chaotic events and the potential resurgence of the Dark Demon Domain. Should it rise again, the whole Divine Realm would need to be ready for battle.

However, it had been three quiet years since any news came from the Dark Demon Domain, which was a relief.

"According to our agreement, Supreme Lin should be returning soon, right?" Azure Emperor Supreme mused quietly to Black Tortoise Supreme, who was present with him.

Black Tortoise Supreme smiled, "He should be back soon, though it's hard to say how he has fared over these three years. The Chaos is not as forgiving as one might imagine."

Both Black Tortoise Supreme and Azure Emperor Supreme knew all too well the complexities and dangers of the chaos from their own experiences. Venturing into such chaos was fraught with great risk and often little reward. Many Supremes spent their lives battling through dangers without any significant improvement in their strength.

For Azure Emperor Supreme and his peers, advancing to the level of a top-tier Supreme was nearly impossible, and becoming the ruler of the Divine Realm was out of the question. Thus, their role remained to safeguard the Divine Realm.

"Three years without a peep from the Dark Demon Domain feels like the calm before the storm," Azure Emperor Supreme expressed his unease.

Black Tortoise Supreme chuckled, "Azure Emperor Supreme, it's the Dark Demon Domain and the Dark Demon Lord that should be worried, not us. We have Supreme Lin. What will he be like after three years? What about thirty years from now? Solving the Dark Demon Domain issue is just a matter of time for Supreme Lin."

Black Tortoise Supreme had an unwavering confidence in Lin Fei. After all, anyone who meticulously examined Lin Fei's past growth trajectory would be left dumbfounded, and even find it unbelievable.

Many Supremes were skeptical, but that was the truth! Who could have imagined that Lin Fei had now become the foremost Supreme of the Divine Realm? Indeed, he was among the topmost Supremes!

Of course, Lin Fei was merely a Martial Ancestor, holding the title in the Divine Realm. Yet, he was rightfully the preeminent Supreme of the Divine Realm. If there was a solution to the Dark Demon Domain issue within the Divine Realm, then only Lin Fei held the promise of resolving it.

"Hum."

Suddenly, there was a slight alteration in the rules of the Divine Realm.

"Hmm? Someone has entered the Divine Realm."

Azure Emperor Supreme's expression became serious. When he was about to sense further, "Swoosh." a figure appeared before Azure Emperor Supreme.

"Lin Fei!"

Azure Emperor Supreme and Black Tortoise Supreme abruptly stood up. Unexpectedly, it was Lin Fei who had returned.

"Azure Emperor Supreme, Black Tortoise Supreme, I am back. What has transpired in the Divine Realm during these three years? Any movements from the Dark Demon Domain?"

Upon his return to the Divine Realm, Lin Fei immediately inquired about its affairs, particularly concerning the Dark Demon Domain, to which he paid great attention.

"The Divine Realm has been quite uneventful, all is serene and tranquil. As for the Dark Demon Domain, there has been no activity. No signs of them stirring anew. Of course, we have never ventured into the Dark Demon Domain, nor do we know the current state of affairs there. The Dark Demon Domain has its Dark Demon Lord, perhaps plotting in the shadows, but we are completely in the dark."

Azure Emperor Supreme slightly shook his head. He was well-versed with the affairs of the Divine Realm, but the situation in the Dark Demon Domain remained obscure to him.

Lin Fei pondered thoughtfully, "It's good that nothing has happened in the Divine Realm. On this trip to Kunyu City, I stumbled upon something that might just allow us to test the Dark Demon Domain. Let us see if we can eliminate the Dark Demon Lord and ultimately neutralize the threat of the Dark Demon Domain."

A glint of sharpness flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. This time, his Eternal Realm had soared to over 10%. His strength had undergone a revolutionary transformation. The Dark Demon Domain remained an ever-looming threat, and Lin Fei was eager to resolve it.

"Lin Fei, when will you go to the Dark Demon Domain? Do you need us to accompany you?"

Azure Emperor Supreme asked with a grave expression. Lin Fei's return to the Dark Demon Domain clearly was not just a casual visit.

Lin Fei glanced at Azure Emperor Supreme and Black Tortoise Supreme, then shook his head slightly, "This time, I should go alone. Bringing more Supremes would not make much difference. The Dark Demon Lord reigns invincible within the Dark Demon Domain. Numbers will not impact him. As long as my strength has indeed increased, dealing with the Dark Demon Lord shouldn't pose much of a problem. The only question is whether I can completely annihilate him."

Lin Fei was well aware that solving the Dark Demon Domain was not going to be easy. His strength had increased, but to what extent? Whether it was enough to resolve the Dark Demon Domain was indeed a question. Only by venturing there himself could Lin Fei truly determine if he could resolve the issue of the Dark Demon Domain once and for all.

Thus, Lin Fei continued to inquire about matters concerning the Divine Realm. He also met with his wife and family. The couple shared tender moments together.

After a few days, Lin Fei set out again. The matter of the Dark Demon Domain was urgent and he was not one to delay.

"Swoosh."

Transforming into a streak of light, Lin Fei had already soared into the depths of chaos, heading towards the Dark Demon Domain. Familiar with its location, Lin Fei did not need a guide and could find the Dark Demon Domain on his own...

Lin Fei flew for a long time. Time passed indeterminably. The unchanging chaos finally showed a hint of variation.

Lin Fei stopped. He saw a Chaos Realm ahead and it was one he knew well.

The Dark Demon Domain!

Finally reaching the Dark Demon Domain, Lin Fei did not rush to enter. Instead, he carefully observed the situation of the Dark Demon Domain. It seemed much the same as before. From the outside, there were no discernible changes.

Furthermore, Lin Fei had secretly observed for a long time. No one had entered the Dark Demon Domain, nor had anyone left. After all, it had only been three years. It was unlikely that any significant changes had occurred in the Dark Demon Domain.

However, for Lin Fei, this was a favorable development. The lack of any changes in the Dark Demon Domain suggested that the Dark Demon Lord likely had not gained much in strength.

Yet Lin Fei's power had transformed dramatically. With this advantage, Lin Fei might truly be capable of resolving the issue with the Dark Demon Lord.

"Swoosh."

The next moment, Lin Fei hesitated no longer. With a single step, he crossed over and instantly flew into the Dark Demon Domain.

Within the Dark Demon Domain, the Dark Demon Lord sat loftily on his throne. His expression was calm, yet his eyes flickered with a hint of coldness.

"Three full years, and you have found nothing?"

A dangerous aura emanated from the Dark Demon Lord. The Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain below, each displayed a look of terror. They feared punishment from the Dark Demon Lord, or even, execution! The Lord had grown increasingly irritable over the years, so much so that even a Supreme could be executed by him! It mattered little to him whether they were exalted Supremes.

"Your Majesty, the Supreme of Doomsday you seek, their movements are erratic and unpredictable; no one knows their whereabouts. We have guarded Kunyu City for years, to no avail. We can only continue to wait..."

Before he could finish, the Dark Demon Lord's gaze turned icy, his tone calm as he spoke: "If you cannot find the Supreme of Doomsday, then what use are you to me?"

"Boom."

With a mere thought from the Dark Demon Lord, the power of the Dark Demon Domain descended. Instantly, it crushed one of the Dark Demon Domain Supremes into dust. Even his core essence was destroyed by the Dark Demon Lord.

At that moment, all the Supremes fell silent, daring not to utter another word.
