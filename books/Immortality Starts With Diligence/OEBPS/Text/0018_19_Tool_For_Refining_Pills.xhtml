19. Tool For Refining Pills

The caravan continued on its journey, but this time with an extra person. The young Taoist also joined the caravan on its way to Su City.

He had already used the Heart Pulse Technique to let Lin Fei control his blood essence, and the feeling was quite wonderful. It was as if Lin Fei could sense the Taoist's every move.

Moreover, Lin Fei couldn't sense even a hint of malice in the Taoist. That was why he was so at ease, letting the Taoist follow along.

After entering Su City, the caravan rested for a day to handle the goods before heading back to Zhou Mountain Mansion City.

The return journey was quite peaceful because Lin Fei had already cleared the way for the caravan. The Lin family's caravan would no longer encounter any troubles. Therefore, it only took a few days for them to return to Zhou Mountain Mansion City.

"Big brother, father, mother, I have cleared the way for the caravan on this escort mission. From now on, the Lin family's caravan can leave anytime and head to Su City. Along the way, as long as we raise the flag of the Lin family, no one will dare to trouble the caravan."

Upon hearing this, the elder brother was overjoyed. "That's great! Hahaha, our Lin family is saved!"

Lin Wen was also pleasantly surprised. Sending Lin Fei on this escort mission was actually a last resort, and they didn't have much hope for it.

But unexpectedly, Lin Fei not only successfully delivered the goods to Su City but also cleared the way for the caravan. This was simply incredible.

"It seems that my decision to send Lin Fei to Nine Profound Sect despite objections was the right one!" said Lin's father, feeling proud and satisfied.

The disciple from a major sect was indeed different. Just look at the old man, Bai, who had practiced martial arts his whole life but only relied on his reputation to travel the Jianghu.

But Lin Fei was different. It had only been a few years, three years at most, and he had already subdued so many bandits on his last trip.

Lin Fei was the pride of the Lin family.

After lunch, Lin Fei arranged for the little Taoist to stay in a separate courtyard. The Taoist was nameless and had been a beggar until he was taken in by an old Taoist.

Lin Fei thought for a moment and said, "You can't reveal your identity, so I'll give you a name that won't stand out in the Lin family. From now on, you will be called Lin Miao."

"Thank you, Young Master, for giving me a name," said Lin Miao, the Taoist, who didn't care much about his name as long as he could survive.

"Stay in this courtyard and don't leave unless it's necessary. As for the zombies, I brought them back for you, but you must not harm anyone without my orders. Remember, your top priority is to cultivate and strive to reach the Qi Condensation Period and be able to refine pills. I'll find time to bring you some books and materials on pill refining."

Lin Fei's positioning of Lin Miao was simple. He was just a tool for refining pills.

After arranging Lin Miao, Lin Fei returned to his room.

He hadn't soaked in a medicinal bath for many days, so he had someone prepare one for him.

"System."

While soaking in the medicinal bath, Lin Fei opened the system panel.

Host: Lin Fei

Cultivation Level: Body Refining Fifth Level

Giant Whale Body Refining Technique: Fifth Level (16% Progress)

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Transcendence (1% Progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Transcendence (1% Progress)

Vajra Iron Cloth: Fourth Level (45% Progress)

Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique: Third Level (91% Progress)

Lin Fei took a look.

Only the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique and Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique could still be improved.

The Giant Whale Body Refining Technique diligently increased by 5% every day, which was indeed very diligent.

As for the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique, it was even simpler.

Lin Fei carried Buddhist sutras and Taoist scriptures with him. All he needed to do was recite a section every day to improve it.

As for the Vajra Iron Cloth, there would be no improvement without a medicinal bath.

The most troublesome ones were the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist and Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

Since the two martial arts reached transcendence, it was as if their practicing progress had stopped.

It seemed that there was a hurdle from transcendence to perfection in martial arts practice.

It was extremely difficult and required some opportunities to achieve perfection.

"Body Refining martial arts are the foundation! Combat martial arts can be put aside for now."

Lin Fei was very clear.

Body Refining was the key and his foundation. He needed to reach the Body Refining Seventh Level as soon as possible so that he could become an Inner Sect disciple of the Nine Profound Sect and obtain more benefits and resources.

However, whether it was the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique, Vajra Iron Cloth, or Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique, the cultivation progress was not particularly fast.

This was still due to Lin Fei's lack of resources.

He knew that there were some pills in the sect that could increase his blood and strengthen his bones.

Eating one would be like having divine help during the Body Refining stage.

However, the contribution points required for those pills were too terrifying, and only Inner Sect disciples were eligible to exchange for them.

"It seems that I have to find a way to get the pill recipe and let Lin Miao try to refine it."

Lin Fei had many thoughts in his mind.

However, he could think about it after returning to the Nine Profound Sect. The top priority was to complete the mission first before returning to the sect. After all, he had been away for a long time.
