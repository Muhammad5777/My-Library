507. A Tenfold Increase!

Within the secret chamber of the Ancient City, Lin Fei solemnly took out a porcelain bottle. This porcelain bottle, akin to white jade, contained a vast amount of the world's origin.

Do not underestimate this mere white jade bottle. In reality, it was a spatial treasure. It harbored an expansive space within. The gains Lin Fei made in the True Spirit Realm, were all contained within this white jade bottle.

As for North Wind True Person and Heart Demon Martial God, they too bid their farewells and departed. They naturally had their means to shake off any pursuit.

After all, those who could survive in the Divine Realm, be they Martial Gods or Deity Transformation cultivators, none were ordinary.

"Come, let's see how impactful will fifty percent of the world's origin be?"

Lin Fei took a deep breath. Then, he tilted the mouth of the white jade porcelain bottle downwards. Instantly, a dense stream of the world's origin poured out from the bottle, which Lin Fei inhaled in one gulp, swallowing it all.

"Boom".

A vast amount of the world's origin entered Lin Fei's body, resonating thunderously within his physical form. The world's origin was the purest form of energy. This energy was voraciously absorbed by the particles of Lin Fei's body. Each particle, exceedingly "thirsty", yearned for a substantial amount of the world's origin, to strengthen itself.

Lin Fei's physical body, in fact, was not particularly strong. After all, compared to the Divine Realm's Martial Gods, who had accumulated over many years, and used an abundance of the world's origin to fortify their bodies, Lin Fei's strength lay in his Particle World.

Other Martial Gods did not possess such a Particle World. But in terms of overall physical strength, Lin Fei was far inferior to other Martial Gods.

However, now things were different. The last shortcoming of Lin Fei, his physical body, was rapidly being addressed.

Lin Fei continuously utilized the world's origin, his particles growing wildly in strength. One-fold, two-fold, three-fold, four-fold, five-fold...

Lin Fei's physical particles almost instantly grew more than tenfold. This was nothing short of a revolutionary transformation. That is to say, Lin Fei, purely in theory, with his particles increased tenfold, had his physical strength augmented by a factor of ten!

Yet in reality, the situation was far from being so straightforward. If the power of the particles was fully unleashed, Lin Fei's strength would increase, not just tenfold, but dozens of times over! Or even more!

Furthermore, Lin Fei made an astonishing discovery. He found that particles enhanced by more than tenfold, if used to create a new Particle World, following the same world laws, would result in a world more than ten times larger!

This was simply beyond imagination. If all these particles were to create their own Particle Worlds, Lin Fei's strength would, perhaps, increase by more than a hundredfold.

At this moment, Lin Fei truly realized the terrifying potential hidden within his physical body. No wonder Martial Gods strive to condense a Martial God's Body. The latent potential within this Martial God's Body is indeed formidable.

Initially, a Martial God's Body might seem ordinary. However, as the Martial God continues to train, such as by increasing the number of particles, or by creating worlds within these particles, the Martial God's Body would advance by leaps and bounds, even transforming to an inconceivable extent.

This is the essence of potential! Possessing boundless latent capabilities is what truly makes the Martial God's Body terrifying. This aspect is something that even divine-level exotic beasts do not possess. It is a unique advantage bestowed upon Martial Gods.

Before Lin Fei, there surely were Martial Gods who developed and trained their Martial God's Body. But they were few. These Martial Gods, like Lin Fei, were nearly invincible among their peers, and could even challenge those beyond their level.

Of course, every Martial God has their own methods and ways of training. They tread different paths. For instance, Lin Fei integrates his own world laws to create internal Particle Worlds. Other Martial Gods may follow other routes.

However, so far, Lin Fei had not encountered any Martial God who trained their Martial God's Body through other means. But they undoubtedly exist. It's just that Lin Fei hasn't encountered them yet.

"It seems I must swiftly transcend my fourth heavenly tribulation. To acquire a wealth of world laws and thereby create a Particle World. Then, my strength will undergo a qualitative leap!"

A glint of sharpness flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. He had spent an entire month refining all the world's origin from the True Spirit Realm. And now, Lin Fei rose to leave his seclusion. He needed world laws and a substantial amount of them.

Thus, without hesitation, Lin Fei headed straight to the site of the heavenly tribulation.

When Lin Fei arrived at the tribulation site, he was immediately recognized.

"Martial God Lin, are you about to transcend the heavenly tribulation again?"

"Yes, is there still an available spot?"

"Yes, of course, this way, please!"

As Lin Fei entered the site, news of his arrival spread swiftly. By now, Lin Fei in the Ancient City was no longer a nameless entity. Not only because of his domineering presence during the last tribulation but also due to his actions in the True Spirit Realm over the past month, which had become widely known throughout the realm.

Many in the Ancient City knew of Lin Fei. A man who stood as a gate against ten thousand foes. With his own strength, he intimidated many powerhouses of the Divine Realm. He even slew the Extinction Martial God! Destroyed over a thousand avatars of the Meditation True Person! And his presence was unmatched, deterring anyone from daring to enter the True Spirit Realm.

This news, upon reaching the Ancient City, left the people of the Ancient Covenant in disbelief. When did the Ancient Covenant give rise to such a formidable figure?

It was only the Initial Evil True Person, with tens of thousands of battle avatars, who dared to be so reckless and domineering.

Could a Martial God be so tyrannical? ‘

But as they continued to investigate, they confirmed it. It was indeed Lin Fei, the Martial God of the Ancient Covenant.

In alliance with Heart Demon Martial God and North Wind True Person, he dominated the True Spirit Realm. Particularly noteworthy was Lin Fei's great battle against Meditation True Person and the Extinction Martial God.

That battle was earth-shattering. Extinction Martial God was slain. All battle avatars of Meditation True Person were annihilated, probably never to stir trouble again.

For a time, Lin Fei's fame spread far and wide. Now, Lin Fei has come to the tribulation site to transcend his fourth heavenly tribulation. Only here did many realize Lin Fei had only transcended three tribulations so far. He hadn't even undergone the fourth. Once he transcends this fourth tribulation, how formidable will Lin Fei become? And what about after the ninth tribulation?

Many began to understand Lin Fei's future was boundlessly promising. At least, becoming a True Monarch should be no issue. With Lin Fei's strength and potential, reaching the level of shattering the void would surely be effortless. Compared to Deity Transformation cultivators comprehending the mysteries of the void, for Martial Gods to shatter the void seemed much easier. It doesn't require comprehension, just sheer overwhelming power.

Hearing of Lin Fei's tribulation, a myriad of powerful beings from the Ancient City all gathered at the tribulation site.

Carrying various intentions, even if it was just to catch a glimpse of Lin Fei. Thus, the tribulation site became incredibly crowded. Many rarely seen top-tier Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators now appeared at the site, silently observing Lin Fei.

At this moment, Lin Fei was within the formation. He was unaware of the situation outside. Moreover, even if he knew, he wouldn't care. His only goal was to transcend the tribulation. The Fourth Heavenly Tribulation!

This time, Lin Fei didn't consume any Saber World. Like other Martial Gods, he had only absorbed a vast amount of the world's origin, making his physical body stronger. He faced the fourth heavenly tribulation with his physical body alone.

Even though Lin Fei felt his physical body was incredibly strong, whether it could withstand the heavenly thunder was uncertain. He could only try and see. Only through practice would he know.

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei murmured softly. The next moment, his consciousness ascended into the void. In the unknown, he "saw" that boundless sea of origin!
