255. Collision

"Golden Core magic treasure?"

Lin Fei unmistakably caught the exclamation of True Person Lu Zhen. Speaking of Golden Core magic treasures, he possessed one. It was the Sky Reaching Tower!

However, the Sky Reaching Tower was a sealed magic treasure. Moreover, Lin Fei was not a cultivator. He lacked spiritual power. Thus, he couldn't unleash the full potential of the Sky Reaching Tower.

Yet this "Great Mountain Seal", evidently, was a fearsomely potent magic treasure. Moreover, it was jointly activated by two Foundation Establishment True Persons. Its might was earth-shattering. Nearly enough to subdue any power beneath the Golden Core True Person!

At this moment, escape was undeniably futile. The towering Great Mountain Seal, its dreadfulness was unparalleled. Once it descended, even unparalleled speed would be moot.

The several thousand meters saber essence was previously displayed by Lin Fei. Now, juxtaposed with the Great Mountain Seal seemed utterly insignificant. Such is the way of the Immortals!

Qi Cultivators' strength was never merely about their spiritual powers. It was about their magic treasure! A powerful magic treasure could make a Foundation Establishment True Person's strength skyrocket, even rivaling a Limit Martial Saint. And a Golden Core-tier magic treasure could render a Foundation Establishment True Person invincible!

Yes, invincible. Invincible beneath the Golden Core! In the Ancient Continent, though both the paths of Immortals and Martial Arts coexisted, in reality, the pinnacle martial artist of the same tier was largely dominated by the Qi cultivators. The reason was simply: magic treasure!

Qi cultivators could strike disproportionately. For instance, if a Qi Condensation cultivator possessed a magic treasure, he could easily dominate all Martial Grandmasters. A Foundation Establishment True Person with a Golden Core magic treasure could also nearly dominate Martial Saints.

However, the martial artist had its advantages. That is, martial artists rely solely on their own strength. Be it Grandmasters or Martial Saints, their power is inherent. Independent of any external objects. Without their magic treasure, many Foundation Establishment True Persons often stand inferior to Martial Saints.

Even with a Golden Core-tier magic treasure, do you truly think they're so easily obtained? Clearly, it's not likely. When a magic treasure reaches the Golden Core tier, it must undergo the baptism of heavenly tribulation.

Each Golden Core magic treasure is immensely precious. Unless supported by a major Immortal sect, it's virtually impossible for a Foundation Establishment True Person to have it.

Clearly, behind the Five Light True Person and the Clear Light True Person, there must be a powerful Immortal sect! Perhaps, their backgrounds are even incredibly prominent.

However, delving into their identities now is of no further significance. The Great Mountain Seal descended rapidly, threatening to grind Lin Fei and everyone on the Immortal Mist Island into dust.

"Hum".

The very next moment, without any hesitation, Lin Fei's Saber Momentum erupted.

The terrifying Saber Momentum surged forth, engulfing the entirety of the Fairy Mist Island. And that wasn't all.

The essence of Lin Fei's blood vibrated.

"Boom".

Above his head, another flower surprisingly materialized. The Energy Flower!

With the appearance of the Energy Flower, a sanguine aura, spanning a thousand meters, instantaneously spread out, subtly merging with the Saber Momentum.

The Blood Energy Domain!

In this instant, Lin Fei channeled every ounce of his power. Every technique was fully unleashed.

Energy Flower, Qi Flower, Blood Energy Domain, Saber Momentum, saber essence! And yes, the saber techniques!

"Swoosh".

The Qi Flower above Lin Fei's head quivered slightly. Following this, massive amounts of saber essence shot out, morphing into a sacred, solemn, and vast saber.

Holy Spirit Saber!

"Swoosh."

Another surge of saber essence shot forth, transforming into a beam of rainbow light, spanning across the void.

Rainbow Transformation Saber!

"Swoosh."

The third stream of saber essence emerged, bursting forth, it was enveloped in raging flames, as if the entire sky was painted in a fiery hue.

Flame Burning Heaven Saber!

"Merge!"

Suddenly, the three sabers converged, and the saber essence intensified, one thousand meters, five thousand meters, ten thousand meters! It reached an astounding ten thousand meters!

At this moment, Lin Fei held nothing back, every ounce of his saber essence erupted, combining the three sabers into one, expanding it to ten thousand meters!

Furthermore, Lin Fei's Blood Energy Domain seemed to merge seamlessly with the Saber Momentum. Under the augmentation of the Saber Momentum and blood essence, the enormous ten thousand meters saber essence faintly bore a shade of blood-red. A terrifying aura immediately descended upon the entire Fairy Mist Island.

"Thud."

At last, someone couldn't withstand the pressure of the Great Mountain Seal and Lin Fei's Saber Momentum and saber essence, collapsing directly to the ground. Even the Foundation Establishment True Persons had to desperately channel their energy to ensure they weren't overwhelmed and forced to the ground.

Everyone stared in shock at the massive saber and towering peak in the sky.

"Two flowers. And even the Blood Energy Domain! Elder Lin is even more formidable than I imagined. In fact, Elder Lin's Saber Momentum has transcended. Once it's perfected, wouldn't he be able to condense three flowers? By then, with the Three Flowers Converging Atop, he might achieve the status of a Martial Sovereign! But facing a Golden Core-tier magic treasure, I fear..."

Lu Zhen True Person murmured softly, he knew all too well, at this point, even if he was a formidable figure on the Hades List, he was utterly powerless. He didn't even have the right to intervene.

"What a Martial Saint! To think he could manifest two flowers. And even the Saber Momentum and Blood Energy Domain. Among the Martial Saints, he must be the most elite. Perhaps even worthy of being labeled as a Martial Sovereign seed. Pity, he's up against our Great Mountain Seal. You're doomed! Great Mountain Seal, descend!"

Five Light True Person shouted loudly. Together with Clear Light True Person, they channeled their full power to activate the Great Mountain Seal.

Clearly, they had recognized Lin Fei's extraordinary prowess. The martial Saint before them wasn't just any ordinary Martial Saint. So, they took no chances. They unleashed all their power, even consuming elixirs to pour all their energy into the Great Mountain Seal.

Looking at the hovering Great Mountain Seal above, it resembled an ancient divine mountain as if it could crush anything and everything.

Such was the might of a Golden Core-level magic treasure. Compared to the Sky Reaching Tower that Lin Fei had encountered previously, the difference was night and day.

After all, the Sky Reaching Tower was only activated by Qi cultivators and paled in comparison to the two Foundation Establishment cultivators before him. These two Foundation Establishment cultivators were undoubtedly among the elite, their prowess matching that of the Limit Martial Saints.

Lin Fei could see that much. Moreover, he even suspected, That these two Foundation Establishment True Persons were on the brink of facing their tribulations, but for reasons known only to them, they hadn't. However, all of this was now inconsequential.

"Slash!"

Lin Fei clapped his hands together, it seemed as though he was gripping the ten thousand meters blade in the void. He fiercely slashed at the hovering Great Mountain Seal.

"Boom!"

The void seemed to crack open. With this slash, Lin Fei channeled every ounce of his skills and power. The Saber Momentum evolved, taking on myriad forms of saber techniques and intentions, all of which poured into the ten thousand-meter blade.

This was a true strike, where spirit, energy, and vitality were perfectly aligned! With this slash, Lin Fei felt as if he had surpassed the Martial Saint realm, reaching a level that was somewhere between a Martial Sovereign and a Martial Saint. He felt as though he had become one with the slash. It appeared as a singular slash, but it was a culmination of hundreds, even thousands of Lin Fei's saber techniques, melded with his profound understanding and mastery of the saber.

"Rumble!"

At last, Lin Fei's ten thousand meters saber fiercely collided with the Great Mountain Seal. The terrifying aftermath instantly tore the void asunder. The vast sea below was instantly stirred, giving rise to monstrous waves and whirlpools. It felt like the world was being turned upside-down.

A muffled boom echoed, sending shockwaves through everyone present. It felt as if their consciousness had been halted at that moment. All that remained was the wildly rampaging saber in the void and the all-suppressing, annihilating Great Mountain Seal!
