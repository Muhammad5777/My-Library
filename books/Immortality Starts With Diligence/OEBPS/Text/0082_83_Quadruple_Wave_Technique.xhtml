83. Quadruple Wave Technique

"How amazing..."

Lin Fei found it quite novel as well. He had never encountered such a situation before.

For example, when the Nine Shadow Saber Technique absorbed and merged with other saber techniques, the system would directly notify him of the results.

But now, it was a first with the fist techniques so vividly appearing in his mind to compete!

Lin Fei believed that it was because the two fist techniques were evenly matched and quite similar, which led to this situation.

Regardless, the two martial arts within Lin Fei's body were now engaged in a fierce battle.

It was a rare opportunity for Lin Fei to witness this as an onlooker, quietly watching the two perfected fist techniques competing against each other!

The golden giant formed by the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist took the initiative to strike first. There seemed to be a faint dragon and elephant roaring in the air.

"Boom."

The golden giant launched a punch, causing the air to vibrate. This was the unique "shock power" of the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist, extremely firm and Yang in nature, full of explosive force.

The blue Triple Wave Technique also charged forward. However, it transformed into the shape of waves. A massive wave surged toward the golden giant with great force.

"Bang."

The two sides clashed forcefully.

The Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist was not only perfect but also integrated with many basic fist techniques. With an 11% training progress in the realm of perfection, it was incredibly powerful! As a result, the explosive power of this punch managed to disperse the Triple Wave Technique.

However, this was only the first layer of the waves. It was merely Lin Fei's original strength. The next moment, the Triple Wave Technique launched another wave of water. This was the second layer of waves. Its power was twice as strong as the first layer!

"Bang."

In this clash, the Triple Wave Technique remained unshaken, and the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist was equally stable. Both sides were evenly matched.

Immediately after, the Triple Wave Technique unleashed another wave. This was the third layer of the Triple Wave Technique. Its power had doubled again based on the second layer.

Lin Fei already knew the outcome at this point. The winner was clear!

"Boom."

As expected, with this collision, the golden giant formed by the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist seemed to roar angrily. It couldn't withstand the fourfold power of the Triple Wave Technique. It was shattered by the enormous wave.

The golden giant turned into strands of golden light, gradually merging into the blue light of the Triple Wave Technique.

"Your Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist and Triple Wave Technique engaged in an intense battle."

"Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist was defeated by Triple Wave Technique and willingly merged into it."

"Your Triple Wave Technique has mastered the skill of Shock Power."

"Your Triple Wave Technique's cultivation progress has increased by 50%."

"During the comprehension of the Triple Wave Technique, you unexpectedly derived a fourth move, and the technique was renamed Quadruple Wave Technique."

"The fourth move of the Quadruple Wave Technique has double the power of the third move."

In Lin Fei's mind, the system's voices echoed one after another. After a while, when the system's voice ceased, Lin Fei seemed to come back to his senses. Sorting out the system's information just now, he couldn't help but widen his eyes.

"Triple Wave Technique has become Quadruple Wave Technique? Derived the fourth move during comprehension? Triple Wave Technique merged with Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist, and the cultivation progress increased so significantly?"

Lin Fei was somewhat bewildered. So, he immediately accessed the system panel to check his current martial arts status.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Acquired Fifth Level

Three Vitality Technique: Fifth Level (28% Progress)

Quadruple Wave Technique: Perfection (50% Progress)

Leaping Technique: Transcendence (68% Progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Perfection ( 43% Progress)

Saber-drawing Technique: Transcendence (78% Progress)

Flowing Clothes Skill: Transcendence (0% Progress)

Indeed, on the system panel, Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist had disappeared. Even Triple Wave Technique had vanished. Instead, it was replaced by Quadruple Wave Technique!

Of course, martial arts only disappeared from the system panel. It didn't mean that Lin Fei couldn't use these martial arts. Lin Fei could still use Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist or Triple Wave Technique. It's just that the two martial arts had merged into a more powerful one.

Moreover, the Quadruple Wave Technique had not only reached perfection, but its cultivation progress had also reached 50%.

"If I merge another martial art technique of the same level as the Triple Wave Technique, can the cultivation progress reach 100%, thus comprehending the fist intent?"

Lin Fei's heart was moved by this thought. It seemed like a method worth trying. However, he couldn't hold too high hopes for it. After all, Lin Fei's Triple Wave Technique reached perfection this time purely due to a combination of luck and coincidence.

It had witnessed the waves in the ocean, which allowed it to achieve perfection, only then could it merge with the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

Now that the Triple Wave Technique had transformed into the Quadruple Wave Technique, its power had increased significantly. The fourth move of the Quadruple Wave Technique was twice as powerful as the third move, which was almost eight times the original power!

How terrifying was this power? Even someone with innate divine strength couldn't compare to Lin Fei.

Lin Fei's Quadruple Wave Technique was now even comparable to a Saber-drawing Technique that had been nurtured for two months.

"If the Saber-drawing Technique could reach perfection and then merge with the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, I wonder if saber intent could be born?"

Lin Fei's heart was filled with a hint of expectation.

The storm at sea continued for several hours. By the next day, the sea was calm again.

"Senior Brother Lin, we have found the lair of the pirates." A disciple from the Nine Profound Sect came to report outside the cabin.

"Found it?"

Lin Fei opened the cabin door and came to the deck. Sure enough, as he looked into the distance, he saw an island far away. Soon, the ship slowly approached the island. Everyone disembarked one after another.

There were many signs of human activity on the island. It didn't take much effort for the disciples of the Nine Profound Sect to find the pirates.

Once they confirmed that they were pirates, Lin Fei ordered to kill them all. However, there were still many innocent people on the island. Most of them were women who had been kidnapped by the pirates and forced to serve them on the island.

Lin Fei felt pity for these women. So, he ordered his men to bring the women along. He also ordered them to find the pirates' hidden treasures and bring them onto the ship.

Afterward, Lin Fei gave the order. The entire island was burned to the ground with a raging fire. The fleet soon returned to Haizhou City.

The pirates were completely wiped out. The merchants could once again set sail and resume their maritime trade. Haizhou City returned to its former prosperity.

Lin Fei had completed his mission in Haizhou City. He did not linger in the city's prosperity. He directly took the Nine Profound Sect disciples and returned to the Nine Profound Sect.

In the main hall of the Nine Profound Sect, the Sect Master summoned Lin Fei for a private meeting.

"Lin Fei, you've done an excellent job in this mission, eliminating the pirates in Haizhou City and ensuring the interests of our Nine Profound Sect.

As for the matter of the mysterious Qi cultivator, I've issued a gag order forbidding the Nine Profound Sect disciples from spreading the news.

I didn't expect that an evil cultivator among the Qi cultivators would become a pirate and even attempt to blood-refine Haizhou City. He truly deserved to die!"

The Sect Master's voice was filled with murderous intent. The actions of the black-robed Qi cultivator had enraged both humans and heaven alike. Naturally, he would be furious as well.

"Sect Master, if I hadn't been able to kill that Qi cultivator, how would the Nine Profound Sect have dealt with it?" Lin Fei asked.

"Lin Fei, you are different from ordinary martial artists. Ordinary martial artists would feel intimidated when facing a Qi cultivator and be afraid to fight.

However, you were able to fight back and kill the Qi cultivator in a desperate situation. Ordinary martial artists wouldn't be able to do that. Basically, acquired martial artists would be easily crushed by a Qi cultivator.

If you had failed this time, I would have personally requested several Grand Elders to descend the mountain and ensure the elimination of that evil Qi cultivator.

Although cultivators are powerful and their methods are unpredictable, innate experts can still contend with them, and even kill them."

Lin Fei understood.

Even without him, the Nine Profound Sect would have sent innate experts to descend the mountain and kill that Qi cultivator.

"Alright, since you have encountered a Qi Condensation cultivator, you must naturally be aware of their formidable power. Even though you killed him, it was because he was careless.

If your Saber-drawing Technique had not been effective, you would have likely faced a disastrous outcome. Only those who have reached the innate realm can contend with Qi cultivators!

Therefore, you must continue to practice martial arts diligently and strive to achieve the innate realm as soon as possible."

The Sect Master's words made sense.

Lin Fei nodded and said, "Disciple understands."
