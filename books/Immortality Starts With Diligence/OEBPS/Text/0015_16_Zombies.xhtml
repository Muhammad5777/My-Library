16. Zombies


"Nine Shadow Saber Technique!"

Lin Fei slashed out with his saber. Six saber shadows as fast as lightning.

"Boom."

Lin Fei's saber struck one of the black-cloaked men, but he encountered great resistance. It was like slashing at a rock. At most, it only cut a bit of skin.

"This kind of hard martial arts..."

Lin Fei's heart was shaken. However, he reacted very quickly.

After the first strike missed, he pulled out his saber again and slashed it down toward his opponent's head.

He didn't believe that the opponent's hard martial arts could make their head just as hard.

"Swish."

As Lin Fei slashed down with his saber, the black cloak shattered with a hard sound, revealing the true face of the enemy.

In front of Lin Fei, the opponent's face was dark and purplish. Their pupils were white, and there was a faint smell of decay all over their bodies.

This was obviously not a living person. It was a dead person!

"A... corpse?"

Lin Fei was shocked.

"Retreat."

Lin Fei retreated frantically. Bai Ying and Tie Shan also saw the true faces of their opponents at this moment.

Indeed, they were all the same. All dead people. Or rather, zombie!

"Second Young Master, these people are corpses. To be able to control corpses, it must be a demonic technique. The old Taoist in the ruined temple must be a demon Taoist!"

Bai Ying's voice was trembling. Demonic techniques were occasionally rumored in the martial arts world.

However, they didn't expect to encounter one today.

"Zombies. That's it, this must be the most malicious evil technique rumored in the martial arts world, the zombie demonic technique!" Lin Fei said solemnly.

In his mind, he had some memories. They were some martial arts trivia from the Nine Profound Sect.

Among them, he had heard there were some Taoists who specialized in refining corpses into zombies impervious to sabers and spears, extremely powerful.

Such zombies had almost no weaknesses or vulnerabilities, which was very terrifying.

Perhaps their only drawback was that they were not very agile. But even so, the four zombies were closing in step by step.

At such a close distance, it was difficult for them to escape.

"These zombies have immense strength, and their bodies are impervious to sabers and spears. They are difficult to deal with. To solve these zombies, we need to deal with the demon Taoist first. I'll go into the temple and take care of that demon Taoist!"

Lin Fei came up with a plan. To catch the thief, first catch the king.

These zombies had no consciousness. There must be someone controlling them. Undoubtedly, the main culprit was the demon Taoist in the temple.

Therefore, as long as Lin Fei killed the demon Taoist, the zombies would naturally lose their threat.

So, Lin Fei bypassed the four zombies and headed straight for the temple.

There was poisonous sandalwood smoke inside the temple, but this couldn't stop Lin Fei. He just had to hold his breath.

However, just as he held his breath and was about to enter the temple, a tall and burly man suddenly strode out of the temple.

A zombie!

This was another zombie.

Behind the demon Taoist, there were a total of five zombies. Previously, only four had appeared. This fifth zombie should be for the protection of the demon Taoist.

Without dealing with this zombie, Lin Fei couldn't enter the temple.

"Nine Shadow Saber Technique!"

Lin Fei's large saber in his hand furiously slashed at the zombie. Each slash was extremely fast and almost all of them landed solidly on the zombie's body.

However, it was useless. It didn't hurt the zombie at all.

Instead, the zombie grabbed Lin Fei's large saber and Lin Fei could only let go.

"Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist!"

Lin Fei didn't hesitate. He punched out.

He was not only skilled in saber techniques but also in boxing.

Moreover, his boxing style was powerful and heavy.

"Boom."

The zombie made a muffled sound and was actually pushed back by Lin Fei's punch.

Lin Fei's eyes lit up. He immediately closed in on the zombie.

"Boom, boom, boom, boom, boom."

Lin Fei landed a series of over ten punches on the zombie's body, each blow could break through solid rock.

But when it landed on the zombie, it was like punching cowhide. It had no effect at all.

It could only push the zombie back.

"Your Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist has met an evenly matched opponent. In the heat of battle, it has comprehended the Shock Technique, your Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist has reached transcendence!"

In the midst of the battle with the zombie, a voice suddenly appeared in Lin Fei's mind.

"Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist has transcended? And what does the Shock Technique mean?"

Lin Fei was somewhat puzzled. But in the next moment, a memory suddenly surfaced in his mind.

It was the memory of the transcended Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

At the same time, Lin Fei naturally understood the meaning of the Shock Technique.

It turned out that one must comprehend a certain trick to achieve transcendence with the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist. That was the Shock Technique.

The Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist was indeed known for its powerful and heavy force. But its more advanced aspect was the power of shock.

It could deliver the force into the enemy's body. This was the true power of the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

Without comprehending the Shock Technique, the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist would never be able to reach transcendence. Just like the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

The Nine Shadow Saber Technique also required the comprehension of the "fast saber" realm, only then could it achieve transcendence.

The Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist was already close to transcendence after a few days of hard training. It was just a little bit short.

Now, Lin Fei was fighting against the zombie. Breaking through in battle was normal.

"Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist!"

Lin Fei's eyes sparkled with brilliance. The next moment, the transcended Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist struck the zombie's body once again.
