89. Magical Artifact

The Lin Mansion was now in a state of panic. A large group of yamen runners and constables searched the Lin Mansion in all directions. Moreover, they were also interrogating some of the servants and maids of the Lin Mansion.

"What's the panic?!"

A loud shout came from the Lin Mansion. Immediately, the many servants dared not make any noise. Lin Wen was also at the Lin Mansion at this time.

At this moment, he stared at the Prefect with a gloomy expression and said coldly, "My lord, you lead the search of the Lin Mansion. Is this the attitude of the Wang family? Or, does my lord want to capture the entire Lin family in one fell swoop?"

The Prefect had a Wang family behind him. A large family, all in Zhou Mountain Mansion City. The Prefect's actions were tantamount to completely tearing off his face. It was a complete break from his previous low-key and discreet demeanor.

Therefore, Lin Wen was very puzzled. What caused the Prefect's drastic change of attitude?

The Prefect's face was solemn, and he said coldly, "The Taoist is catching a demon. Some people have seen that your Lin Mansion is harboring a demon. By the Taoist's orders, we will search your Lin Mansion thoroughly, not let any demon go! Anyone who interferes with the yamen's affairs will be arrested and thrown in jail!

"How dare you! This is the Lin Mansion, don't be presumptuous!" Lin Wen shouted loudly. Immediately, many Lin family guards stepped forward. They drew their sabers one after another, blocking the yamen officials.

The Prefect's face turned pale. Seeing that the Taoist didn't speak, he understood the Taoist's intention. This was to test his performance.

Thinking about this, the Prefect took a deep breath and said sternly, "The yamen is arresting demons, and anyone who resists will be killed without mercy!"

"Swish."

Immediately, the yamen's constables and runners also drew their sabers one after another. Suddenly, the bright sabers, under the sunlight, reflected dazzling rays of light, causing fear in people's hearts.

At this moment, the entire Lin Mansion became quiet. The atmosphere was extremely tense.

"Whoosh."

Suddenly, a figure burst out from the backyard of the Lin Mansion.

The figure stopped.

Everyone could see clearly.

"Young Master Lin Fei!"

The people of the Lin Mansion seemed to have found their backbone. Their morale soared instantly, and they looked at the yamen officials with murderous intent.

Everyone knew the real "core" of the Lin family was actually Lin Fei.

"With just you, you dare to show your sabers in front of me?"

The next moment, Lin Fei released a sharp aura from his body. It was as if he had transformed into a peerless treasure saber in an instant.

"Hum."

The sabers of those yamen runners and constables actually began to tremble slightly as if they were about to be pulled out of their hands.

"Get back!" Lin Fei shouted loudly.

For the first time, Lin Fei unleashed his saber intent in front of others.

Saber intent is invisible and intangible. It can only be sensed.

At this moment, in front of a martial artist who had comprehended saber intent, drawing a saber was a luxury.

"Clang."

Suddenly, the sabers of the yamen runners and constables, which were originally drawn out, seemed to be controlled by an invisible force, instantly pulling back into their sheaths.

Moreover, no matter how hard the yamen runners and constables tried to draw their sabers, they simply could not pull them out. It was as if these sabers had grown roots.

"You..."

The Prefect's face was full of horror as if he had seen a ghost. Everyone was shocked beyond belief. Especially those constables and yamen runners. Their whole bodies were trembling. In front of Lin Fei. They couldn't even draw their sabers. What kind of technique was this?

"Immortal, we can't draw our sabers!"

"Immortal, is this a demon technique?"

"How could this be?"

Many constables and yamen runners all turned to the Taoist for help. In their eyes, Immortals were omnipotent, representing invincibility!

Even if Lin Fei had some tricks up his sleeve, how could he compare with the high and mighty immortal?

The Taoist from He Mountain Dao frowned at this moment. He had been a Qi cultivator since he was a child. The servants on the mountain were all acquired martial artists.

The Taoist looked down upon acquired martial artists, and even all martial artists. So why would he bother to understand the techniques of martial artists? Therefore, the Taoist knew nothing about Lin Fei's saber intent.

So, the Taoist sneered and said, "It's just some insignificant tricks of a martial artist. Daring to show off in front of me is simply seeking death!"

The Taoist flicked his finger. Suddenly, a talisman emitted a brilliant light.

It instantly transformed into a small sword, as fast as lightning, directly stabbed towards Lin Fei.

"Ding."

Lin Fei forcefully drew the Star Saber from behind his back.

And with a single slash, nineteen saber shadows instantly merged into one, striking the black light.

The small sword, or rather, the talisman sword.

Lin Fei's slash cut the talisman sword into two pieces.

It fell to the ground and transformed back into a talisman.

This was not a real magical artifact. It was merely a talisman.

"He actually broke my talisman sword?"

The Taoist's eyes narrowed slightly. He was somewhat surprised.

His talisman sword was extremely fast. Although it wasn't a real magical artifact, such speed was rarely reacted to by acquired martial artists.

Basically, when the Taoist released the talisman sword, it was a one-hit kill for acquired martial artists, without exception.

But now, the unexpected happened.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei moved. His figure suddenly leaped forward.

In the blink of an eye, he reached the front of the Taoist.

This was the Leaping Technique, and it was the perfection realm Leaping Technique. The speed was incredibly fast, beyond imagination.

Lin Fei knew that the Taoist was a Qi cultivator. Moreover, he should be an orthodox Qi cultivator. He even had talismans.

Who knew how many mysterious and strange techniques he had?

Facing a Qi cultivator, Lin Fei never dared to be careless. Therefore, he made his move. He went all out from the start.

Lin Fei's hand was already holding the Hidden Saber at his waist.

"Swish."

He drew the saber from his waist.

Saber-drawing Technique!

Lin Fei had been nurturing his saber for more than a month. Moreover, it was the perfection realm Saber-drawing Technique.

The saber had been nurtured for a month, its power was incredibly strong. Far stronger than nurturing the saber for two or three months before!

The entire Lin Mansion, everyone's eyes widened.

At this moment, it seemed as if everyone's thoughts had come to a halt.

In their line of sight, there was only this one saber.

That ball of white light filled with cold and sharp saber intent.

It made everyone feel as if they were standing on a mountain of sabers. As if they were surrounded by sharp saber edges in all directions!

At the moment of drawing the saber, there were nineteen saber shadows.

However, when the saber slashed down, there was only one saber shadow left.

This was the union of nineteen saber strikes.

Saber-drawing Technique and Nine Shadow Saber Technique had completely fused together.

Lin Fei's inner power had fully exploded to this saber. He felt that this saber could even cut through the innate realm!

"Bang."

Finally, the saber fell. It struck directly at the top of the Taoist's head.

With a muffled sound, Lin Fei's eyes suddenly widened.

His gaze was fixed on the Taoist in front of him.

His saber was right above the Taoist's head.

However, Lin Fei's saber couldn't fall further.

Because, above the Taoist's head, a small shield was floating, as if out of nowhere.

This small shield was only the size of a palm. It emitted a green light, like a protective barrier, protecting the Taoist within.

Even Lin Fei's fully powered saber, nurtured for a month that Lin Fei had confidence could cut through the innate realm, still, it couldn't break through the green light of the small shield.

"Magical artifact!"

Lin Fei said in a low voice. He instantly understood what the green small shield was.

One of the strongest means of a Qi cultivator was magical artifacts!
