56. Hongfa Temple

"Patron Lin, are you talking about Hongfa Temple?" Miss Zhang thought for a moment and spoke.

In Luo City, the most famous temple was Hongfa Temple. Because there was once a great monk in Hongfa Temple.

"Yes, it's Hongfa Temple. I wonder if Miss Zhang knows which true high monks are currently in Hongfa Temple?"

"If you're talking about high monks, Hongfa Temple does have some. Master Fayan and Master Fajue are both venerable monks of the contemporary Hongfa Temple."

Lin Fei nodded and said, "Miss Zhang, I need your help with something. I hope you can use the influence of the Zhang family to introduce me to Masters Fayan and Fajue. I want to discuss Buddhist principles with the two venerable monks."

The Zhang family was a wealthy merchant in Luo City. They were also frequent customers of temples like Hongfa Temple.

Naturally, he could guarantee to see the two high monks of Hongfa Temple with the Zhang family's help.

Miss Zhang did not ask why Lin Fei wanted to meet the high monks. She nodded and said, "This is easy, but Patron Lin will have to stay with the Zhang family for a while. Within three days, I will make sure Patron Lin sees the two high monks."

"Thank you, Miss Zhang."

Lin Fei decided to stay with the Zhang family for a few more days.

Returning to his room, Lin Fei immediately called up the system panel.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Acquired Level

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Perfection (0% progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Perfection (18% progress)

Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique: Seventh level (68% progress)

Flowing Clothes Skill: Transcendence (0% progress)

Lin Fei's Giant Whale Body Refining Technique and Vajra Iron Cloth had reached their limits, giving birth to inner power, and could no longer be improved. Therefore, he deleted them from the system panel to save space. Otherwise, the panel would look too cluttered and confusing.

Lin Fei mainly focused on Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique, which was only at the seventh level. The current progress of its cultivation had reached 68%.

Compared to the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique and Vajra Iron Cloth, the cultivation speed of the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique was simply too slow.

This was not because Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique wasn't working hard. In fact, Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique was also working hard in cultivation.

But the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique required both Taoist scriptures and Buddhist sutras, especially the latter. Lin Fei suspected that it was created by a monk.

This time, Lin Fei stayed in Luo City to search for a high monk from the Hongfa Temple, hoping to listen to his Buddhist teachings and gain insights to enhance his cultivation progress. He aimed to reach his limits as soon as possible and give birth to the third inner power, which would merge the three types of inner power into one.

Then, Lin Fei could enter the Martial Arts Pavilion and select acquired martial arts, officially beginning his training in the acquired realm.

In just three days, the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique only increased the cultivation progress by 3%, which was as slow as a snail. Fortunately, Miss Zhang finally had good news for him.

"Patron Lin, two high monks from the Hongfa Temple have agreed to see you. You can enter the temple with this Buddhist bead as a token."

"Thank you, Miss Zhang. I will leave for the Hongfa Temple immediately. Time is of the essence," Lin Fei replied without hesitation.

He left Zhang Mansion right away and headed to the Hongfa Temple, which was located on the outskirts of the city, it was not isolated since the temple was always bustling with people. Upon arrival, Lin Fei saw a crowd of people and smoke rising from the temple, as well as merchants selling their goods outside the temple.

Knowing it would be difficult to see the high monks with so many people around without Miss Zhang's help, Lin Fei felt thankful. He walked straight to the front of the temple.

"Donor, please queue up for incense," a monk stopped Lin Fei.

"I have come to see Masters Fayuan and Fajue. This is the token," said Lin Fei.

Lin Fei took out a string of Buddhist beads directly.

The novice monk scrutinized Lin Fei carefully and said, "Seems that the Donor is the Lin Fei that the Zhang family mentioned. The two masters are already waiting for you in the meditation room. Donor Lin, please follow me."

Lin Fei followed the novice monk into the Hongfa Temple.

The Hongfa Temple was bustling with incense and activity outside, but inside, it was very peaceful and did not disturb the meditation of the senior monks.

"Ha!"

Suddenly, as they passed through an empty space, Lin Fei saw some young monks practicing martial arts in the open area.

"Hongfa Temple also has monks practicing martial arts?" Lin Fei asked. He remembered that Hongfa Temple was not a sect.

The novice monk replied, "Donor, those are the martial monks of Hongfa Temple. They are only practicing some basic boxing and footwork to protect the safety of many monks in the temple."

"I see," Lin Fei nodded.

With his eyesight, he could naturally see that those martial monks were indeed only practicing basic boxing and footwork, and did not have Acquired realm ones, only Body Refining. It was indeed far inferior to those martial arts sects.

"Donor Lin, the two masters are in the meditation room, please come in," The novice monk stood outside the meditation room and did not intend to go in.

Lin Fei nodded and pushed open the door of the meditation room, walking straight in.
