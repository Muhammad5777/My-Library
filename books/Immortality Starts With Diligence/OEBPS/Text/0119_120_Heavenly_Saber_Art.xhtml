120. Heavenly Saber Art

Lin Fei unleashed his Saber Intent on the third floor of the Martial Arts Pavilion. Unbeknownst to him, his Saber Intent permeated through the third floor, enveloping the entire Martial Arts Pavilion.

Thus, Lin Fei's Saber Intent erupted. Everyone in the Martial Arts Pavilion felt as if a peerless treasure saber hung above their heads. Even some saber-wielding martial artists found their sabers humming uncontrollably, as if eager to break free from their sheaths.

"What's going on?"

"Why can't I control my saber?"

"What a terrifying aura, who is responsible for this?"

In an instant, martial artists on both the first and second floors felt the Saber Intent. The Martial Arts Pavilion elder instantly sobered up. His eyes widened as he looked towards the third floor.

"Is this... Saber Intent? Did Grand Grand Elder Lin not only advance to the Innate realm but also comprehend Saber Intent?"

As a Martial Arts Pavilion elder, he was well-versed in Martial Dao True Intent. Naturally, he recognized that this was Martial Dao True Intent and most likely released by Lin Fei.

"Saber Intent, huh! I wonder what martial art Grand Elder Lin has chosen? But if he continues releasing Saber Intent like this, the disciples won't be able to choose their martial arts..."

The Martial Arts Pavilion elder could only shake his head, a bitter smile on his face.

Faced with such terrifying Saber Intent, disciples in the Body Refining and Acquired realm felt uneasy, their minds seemingly sliced by the Saber Intent repeatedly.

However, there were benefits too. At the very least, it could temper the disciples' mental fortitude. After all, Lin Fei didn't attack anyone with his Saber Intent; he simply released it.

As for asking the Martial Arts Pavilion elder to remind Lin Fei to restrain his Saber Intent? The elder dared not.

Thus, the disciples within the Martial Arts Pavilion could only endure the "intimidation" of the Saber Intent.

Lin Fei, on the third floor, released his Saber Intent and thirty-meter-high saber energy to attract martial arts techniques, allowing them to come to him on their own accord. As the saying goes, the destined shall have the martial arts come to them!

Time passed by the minute and second. Lin Fei didn't notice anything unusual.

Suddenly, a voice resounded within Lin Fei's mind.

"Water Splitting Chop, requesting to be your martial art."

"Mountain Cleaving Slash, requesting to be your martial art."

"Water Splitting Chop and Mountain Cleaving Slash are competing to become your martial art."

"Three Abyssal Sabers defeated Water Splitting Chop and Mountain Cleaving Slash, requesting to be your martial art."

One after another, voices emerged in Lin Fei's mind. However, Lin Fei was somewhat bewildered. Hadn't he made it clear that those destined would come on their own?

Water Splitting Chop and Mountain Cleaving Slash could be considered compatible with Lin Fei's Saber Intent and Saber Qi. But what was the deal with the Three Abyssal Sabers?

Its killing intent soared to the heavens! Moreover, practicing the Three Abyssal Sabers would lead to comprehending Saber Intent on its own and the Saber Intent of the Three Abyssal Sabers would certainly differ from Lin Fei's Saber Intent. Even so, the Three Abyssal Sabers still joined the fray. What was the meaning of this? And it even defeated Water Splitting Chop and Mountain Cleaving Slash.

However, whether Lin Fei would accept the Three Abyssal Sabers as his martial art depended on his approval.

"Are there no other saber techniques that can defeat the Three Abyssal Sabers?"

Lin Fei furrowed his brow. The Three Abyssal Sabers, judging from the description, seemed to be a very powerful saber technique. However, it was unsuitable for Lin Fei's saber technique. Even if it were powerful, Lin Fei wouldn't practice it.

"Twenty-Three Sabers suddenly ambushed the Three Abyssal Sabers, heavily injuring it."

"The Three Abyssal Sabers retaliated in anger, fiercely battling with Twenty-Three Sabers."

"Twenty-Three Sabers eventually defeated the Three Abyssal Sabers and requested to become your martial art."

Just when Lin Fei thought of rejecting the Three Abyssal Sabers, another saber technique emerged.

Twenty-Three Sabers! Moreover, this saber technique was somewhat "treacherous." It had ambushed the Three Abyssal Sabers. Twenty-Three Sabers could command a saber to kill enemies. It seemed quite good as well.

However, Lin Fei already had Saber Qi. Saber Qi itself could leave his body and be controlled by Lin Fei. He could effortlessly control Saber Qi. Therefore, the saber-commanding technique of Twenty-Three Sabers was of little use to him.

Lin Fei was still contemplating whether to let Twenty-Three Sabers become his martial art. At present, Twenty-Three Sabers seemed quite good.

"Boom."

The next moment, a saber appeared in Lin Fei's mind. A magnificent saber, condensing out of thin air. The moment this saber appeared, Lin Fei's mind seemed to be illuminated. It was as if countless saber edges were slicing through the void.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei's gaze, guided by his intuition, instantly looked toward the corner of the bookshelf. There, a saber technique manual lay.

"Heavenly Saber Art!"

Lin Fei murmured softly as he saw the name of this saber technique.

"Twenty-Three Sabers, fearing the Heavenly Saber Art, voluntarily distance itself from the host."

"Heavenly Saber Art requests to become your martial art."

The moment the Heavenly Saber Art appeared, a voice resounded within Lin Fei's mind. Moreover, Twenty-Three Sabers feared the Heavenly Saber Art and withdrew from the competition voluntarily.

This surprised Lin Fei. After all, Twenty-Three Sabers was not an ordinary martial art. It had defeated the Three Abyssal Sabers. Although it was due to an ambush... Even so, strength was required.

It resisted the counterattack of the Three Abyssal Sabers and defeated it in the end. This showed that Twenty-Three Sabers was definitely a top-tier innate-level saber technique. However, even so, when the Heavenly Saber Art appeared, Twenty-Three Sabers voluntarily retreated.

Lin Fei went to the corner of the bookshelf and took down the Heavenly Saber Art manual hidden in the corner.

Flipping open the Heavenly Saber Art manual, he read:

"Heavenly Saber Art, when cultivated to perfection, can condense a Heavenly Saber. With the Heavenly Saber unleashed, it can break the void and make countless sabers bow!"

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly.

"What a domineering tone. Heavenly Saber unleash, countless sabers bow. What kind of Heavenly Saber can make countless sabers bow?"

Lin Fei was somewhat skeptical that this saber technique was bound to have some exaggeration or even boasting.

Nevertheless, judging solely from the introduction of the Heavenly Saber Art and the fact that it had made Twenty-Three Sabers voluntarily withdraw from the competition, the Heavenly Saber Art seemed to be the most suitable saber technique for Lin Fei.

Lin Fei continued to study the contents of the Heavenly Saber Art.

The Heavenly Saber of the Heavenly Saber Art had to be cultivated to perfection in order to condense a Heavenly Saber. When the Heavenly Saber was unleashed, countless sabers would bow, shattering the void!

This could be described as the ultimate domineering and powerful. It was in line with the attributes of a "saber." After all, sabers were the epitome of domineering and sharpness.

The Heavenly Saber Art took the domineering and sharpness of sabers to the extreme. However, it was clear that not everyone could cultivate the Heavenly Saber Art to perfection. Therefore, the specific cultivation steps of the Heavenly Saber differed.

At the perfection realm, one could condense a Heavenly Saber. At the transcendence realm, one could condense a Tyrant Saber. At mastery or below, one could condense a Fierce Saber. But even without reaching perfection, the power of the Heavenly Saber Art was not inferior to other top-tier saber techniques.

Seeing this, Lin Fei had already made up his mind.

"Heavenly Saber Art, since you have chosen to follow me, you should know you are the Heavenly Saber, the king among sabers! As a top-tier and mature saber technique, you should disdain to remain at the beginner level. So, come on, let's reach the mastery level directly! Show off the elegance of your Heavenly Saber!"

After Lin Fei finished speaking, he silently agreed to Heavenly Saber Art's request.

"Boom."

The next moment, the Heavenly Saber in Lin Fei's mind shattered instantly. At the same time, a surge of new memories emerged in his mind.
