238. Accept the Task

"Wind Sovereign, since the sect has assigned me to carry out this task, I naturally comply. However, I still feel unsuited to the position of vice hall master. Just a leisurely elder will suffice. If the Law Enforcement Hall needs me, I am at its disposal at any time."

Lin Fei opened his eyes and said slowly. His words meant that he had agreed. However, he rejected the position of vice hall master. Even if it's more leisurely, as a vice hall master in charge of external affairs, how could it possibly be truly leisurely?

Even if the position of vice hall master has great benefits, Lin Fei didn't want to be entangled in mundane affairs, which would affect his cultivation. His goal is to reach the pinnacle of martial arts. How could he be distracted by dealing with mundane affairs?

Wind Sovereign laughed. He nodded slightly and said, "Alright, since you don't want to be the vice hall master, then be an elder. However, this time you will definitely represent the Ancient Origin Sect, and even more so, the Law Enforcement Hall. A common elder is also not suitable. With your strength, you should be the first elder of the Law Enforcement Hall! This position happens to be vacant."

Wind Sovereign gave Lin Fei another arrangement. This "First Elder" is suitable. After all, "First Elder" is just an honorary title. In terms of specific duties, he is still an elder. And the elders of the Law Enforcement Hall, most of the time are quite leisurely. Unless something big happens in the Ancient Origin Sect. Then the elder will be dispatched. As for Lin Fei, this First Elder, unless there's an extremely big event, he probably won't be disturbed.

"Thank you, Wind Sovereign!"

Lin Fei gave a slight bow.

"Alright, Elder Lin, you can go back to your cave dwelling first. Once the Law Enforcement Hall arranges the candidates, you can set off for Illusion Worry City together. This is equivalent to going on a mission. The sect also offers a large number of contribution points as rewards."

Lin Fei nodded.

There are rewards for going on missions. For this kind of task where the sect needs a Martial Saint to exert great effort, the reward of contribution points is extremely generous. Therefore, Lin Fei was not worried about the rewards at all. With his current strength and potential, the Ancient Origin Sect would only look for more excuses to give him contribution points.

Lin Fei returned to his cave dwelling. Although he believed that Wind Sovereign would not deceive him, he had to lead a team to a strange place. Even places where Golden Core True Person and Martial Sovereign are stationed.

Lin Fei of course had to be more careful. So, he found some information about Illusion Worry City. He learned about the beginning and end of Illusion Worry City.

The establishment of Illusion Worry City actually carries some legendary color. Illusion Heart True Person and Worry-Free Sovereign are actually independent cultivators!

Lin Fei found it somewhat incredible to see the introduction to the information. After all, it's not easy for independent cultivators to become Martial Saints or Foundation Establishment True Persons. Not to mention achieving the level of Golden Core True Person and Martial Sovereign. But it is indeed the case.

Illusion Heart True Person and Worry-Free Sovereign rose step by step and were chased to the place where Illusion Worry City is now. There, they were protected by a group of martial artists and Foundation Establishment True Persons who were also independent cultivators. Later, they grew step by step, and actually achieved Golden Core True Person and Martial Sovereign. This is indeed legendary.

Of course, Lin Fei also knew. The past experiences of these two people are not simple. There must be some adventures. Because, according to the information, Illusion Heart True Person and Worry-Free Sovereign were not ordinary Golden Core True Person and Martial Sovereign. They could be considered among the top existents in Martial Sovereigns and Golden Core True Persons.

Therefore, the three major forces of the Ancient Origin Sect, Profound Light Cave Heaven, and Red Hades Mansion, finally acknowledged the existence of Illusion Worry City. Otherwise, with the domineering style of these three forces, where would there be any no man's land? Only with strength can the existence of Illusion Worry City be guaranteed.

After the establishment of Illusion Worry City, because of its relative safety and the freedom of trading, and even many treasures that could not see the light of day could be traded through Illusion Worry City, gradually, it developed.

Nowadays, even some disciples of the three major forces would go to Illusion Worry City to exchange for some things they couldn't get in their sects. It had truly become a big scene!

Among them, Illusion Worry City is known for its "safety". No matter how malicious, once in Illusion Worry City, it's safe. Because, within Illusion Worry City, fighting is not allowed. Once a fight happens, the law enforcement team of Illusion Worry City will strictly punish it!

Lin Fei finally understood why even Wind Sovereign was troubled. Even specifically summoned Lin Fei. Turns out, going to Illusion Worry City to catch someone is not easy at all.

Once a fight happens, it will inevitably conflict with Illusion Worry City. Either immediately kill the real culprits who killed the Ancient Origin Sect disciples. Then leave Illusion Worry City immediately. Once out of Illusion Worry City, the forces of it would not take action or confront the forces.

Over the years, Illusion Heart True Person and Worry-Free Sovereign had also recruited a lot of Limit Martial Saints and top Foundation Establishment cultivators. Therefore, their power is very strong.

A slight carelessness. Lin Fei and others might even be in danger. If they break the rules of Illusion Worry City and get killed inside, perhaps the Ancient Origin Sect wouldn't make a fuss about it.

Therefore, this trip carries certain dangers. However, it's not really dangerous for Limit Martial Saints. To kill a Limit Martial Saint is too difficult. Moreover, it's not just Lin Fei going this time. There are also some elders at the Law Enforcement Hall. Among them, there are Limit Martial Saints!

"Although it's dangerous, I now have saber momentum, and with the Qi Flower condensed, my saber essence has greatly increased. Even if I'm not a match, I shouldn't be in danger."

Lin Fei muttered to himself. How strong his strength is now, even Lin Fei himself was not clear. After all, Lin Fei's strength cannot be quantified. His current strength was made up of saber momentum, saber essence, and saber method. Only by really giving a full fight, perhaps, can Lin Fei's real combat power be confirmed.

Lin Fei waited for three days. Finally, he received news from the Law Enforcement Hall.

So, Lin Fei went directly to the Law Enforcement Hall. This time, he didn't see Wind Sovereign but a total of thirteen Martial Saints. Almost all were elders of the Law Enforcement Hall.

Among them, there were two Limit Martial Saints who had condensed Energy Flower in their bodies, and one who had condensed Qi Flower. Apart from them, there were ten powerful Martial Saints in total. Such a lineup was actually very strong. It showed that the Wind Sovereign of the Law Enforcement Hall was not just talking.

They were really serious. Those who killed the true disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect, the Ancient Origin Sect must kill them even if it had to chase them to the ends of the earth.

This is the style of a big sect! Otherwise, a sect that can't protect its true disciples, where would there be any cohesion?

"Respect to the First Elder!"

These Martial Saint elders apparently already knew Lin Fei's identity. Upon seeing Lin Fei, they immediately paid their respects. However, Lin Fei could vaguely feel. The three Limit Martial Saints, their gazes seemed a bit strange.

Lin Fei's eyes grew cold. His gaze swept over these Martial Saint elders. He said coldly, "I want to ask a question first. Does anyone feel that I, as the First Elder, am not qualified? I give you a chance. One move, if I can't beat you, I will voluntarily resign to Wind Sovereign. The opportunity is given to you. If you want to try, you can step forward!"

After speaking, Lin Fei just stood there with his hands behind his back, quietly standing in front of the thirteen Martial Saint elders. The atmosphere in the whole Law Enforcement Hall immediately became tense.
