672. The Conditions of the Clairvoyant Supreme

Amidst the boundless chaos, Lin Fei glanced back at the Dark Demon Domain.

"The Supreme of Doomsday..." Lin Fei murmured softly.

The Dark Demon Lord's greatest reliance, or hope, against Lin Fei, was the Supreme of Doomsday. Naturally, Lin Fei had to be cautious about the Supreme of Doomsday.

But could the Supreme of Doomsday truly challenge Lin Fei? Lin Fei was unsure.

When he returned to Kunyu City, he would naturally investigate. For now, Lin Fei still could not resolve the Dark Demon Domain. A chaos realm was indeed not so easily dealt with.

"Even if my Eternal Realm were to increase to 20%, 30%, or even 40%, I doubt it would be enough to completely resolve the Dark Demon Domain. Perhaps, the Eternal Realm needs to undergo a transformation. Only by reaching above 50% could it possibly suppress the Dark Demon Domain completely. Only by fully suppressing the Dark Demon Domain could I then annihilate it and thereby resolve the Dark Demon Lord!"

Lin Fei murmured lowly. A sharp gleam flashed in his eyes.

Lin Fei did not believe that to resolve the Dark Demon Domain, he necessarily had to become the Master of Chaos. In fact, he was currently merely a Martial Ancestor.

Many people had forgotten, or rather, could not believe, he had not even achieved the rank of Supreme! Only when the Eternal Realm reached 100%, could one become a Supreme. By that time, Lin Fei would likely already be invincible among Supremes.

Lin Fei did not continue to linger in the Dark Demon Domain. He transformed into a streak of light, heading back to the Divine Realm.

Soon, Lin Fei returned to the Divine Realm. He spent some tender moments with his wife.

After spending some time with his family, Lin Fei set off again, preparing to head to Kunyu City. In the Divine Realm, he could not enhance his strength at all. He ultimately had to venture into the chaos.

Lin Fei also promised to return again after three years. Once every three years. It was unlikely to cause any serious issues.

Moreover, even if something serious did occur, the Azure Emperor Supreme could always go to Kunyu City to find Lin Fei. Once in Kunyu City, the Supremes of the Divine Realm could contact Lin Fei.

After spending some time in the Divine Realm, Lin Fei finally returned to Kunyu City. Upon his return, Lin Fei immediately visited the task hall. It had taken him a year to return to the Divine Realm, and another year to get back to Kunyu City. He had also spent time with his family in the Divine Realm.

That meant, from the time Lin Fei left Kunyu City until now, more than two years had passed. In those two years, the task hall of Kunyu City had updated many missions.

Lin Fei wanted to see if there were any tasks related to the Chaotic Behemoth. So far, the consumption of the Chaotic Behemoth's essence had consistently boosted the progress of his Eternal Realm. To rapidly enhance his strength in a short period of time, he still needed to rely on the Chaotic Behemoth.

However, Chaotic Behemoths were ultimately rare. Even after two years, there were no tasks concerning the Chaotic Behemoth in the task hall.

Lin Fei frowned. Without tasks involving Chaotic Behemoths, he would have to continue waiting.

Just then, Lin Fei's communication stone vibrated.

Seeing this stone, Lin Fei's gaze shifted slightly. This was the communication stone he left when posting tasks. The vibration of this stone meant only one thing: someone was willing to take on a task.

Could it be that someone had information or news about a Chaotic Behemoth?

Lin Fei immediately took out the communication stone. Displayed on the stone was a specific location—presumably the position of the other party.

However, they had not claimed the task. This seemed somewhat odd. In other places, Lin Fei might be wary and cautious.

But here, in Kunyu City, no one dared to cause trouble. This city was under the domain of the omnipotent, immensely powerful Master of Chaos. The safety of everyone could still be assured.

"Who could it be? So secretive, it might be worth checking out."

Lin Fei was indeed curious. Moreover, he was not willing to let go of any news about the Chaotic Behemoth.

As long as there was news or intelligence about a Chaotic Behemoth, Lin Fei was open to negotiating any conditions.

Quickly leaving the task hall, Lin Fei flew towards the specified location. In Kunyu City, he arrived at a secluded courtyard.

"Supreme Lin, please come inside."

Lin Fei had just reached the gate when it swung open, a voice calling from inside.

Without hesitation, Lin Fei entered the courtyard. Inside, he saw a man of human stature, presumably a human Supreme. Even to Lin Fei's discerning eyes, the man appeared exceptionally handsome.

"Supreme Lin, please have a seat."

Lin Fei sat down.

The man began, "You may call me the Clairvoyant Supreme. I invited Supreme Lin here somewhat presumptuously because I have a favor to ask."

"A favor?" Lin Fei responded coolly. "Clairvoyant Supreme, be straightforward. I do not offer my assistance lightly. Unless you have news or intelligence about a Chaotic Behemoth."

With news of a Chaotic Behemoth, everything was negotiable. Otherwise, Lin Fei would not entertain the Clairvoyant Supreme.

The Clairvoyant Supreme paused for a moment, then sighed deeply. "Actually, I do not know specific information about any Chaotic Behemoth. But I do know where to find Chaotic Behemoths. Not just one or two, but many Chaotic Behemoths."

"Many Chaotic Behemoths?" Lin Fei's gaze sharpened. "Is this true?"

It wasn't just one or two Chaotic Behemoths but many.

Could this be possible?

Lin Fei had never heard of a location with many Chaotic Behemoths. He had been searching for a long time and had gathered a lot of information about Chaotic Behemoths. But he had only ever found basic information.

For instance, Chaotic Behemoths were rare and generally did not congregate. Even if some Chaotic Behemoths had offspring, they typically left as soon as the young could fend for themselves. How could non-gregarious Chaotic Behemoths appear not just singly or doubly, but in many?

The Clairvoyant Supreme's expression grew somber, his voice grave as he affirmed, "It is indeed absolutely true. How could I dare deceive Supreme Lin in such a matter? However, the small favor I wish to ask of you is not so simple. And the place with many Chaotic Behemoths is not so easily accessed. Whether you are willing to help depends on Supreme Lin's decision."

After speaking, the Clairvoyant Supreme fell silent, waiting quietly for Lin Fei's response.

Lin Fei pondered for a moment before asking, "Tell me, what do you need me to do?"

He needed to know exactly what was involved before he could make a decision.

"It's simple, help me obtain the Chaos Pearl. If I can secure the Chaos Pearl, I will gladly provide you with all the information about Chaotic Behemoths. However, obtaining the Chaos Pearl is not easy. You will be facing not just any Supremes, but top-tier Supremes, and possibly more than one. If you do not obtain the Chaos Pearl, then I'm sorry, I will not be able to provide any information about Chaotic Behemoths."

The Clairvoyant Supreme laid out his request.

The Chaos Pearl! This Supreme was asking Lin Fei to help him obtain a Chaos Pearl!

Lin Fei had heard a bit about the Chaos Pearl. It was a treasure of immense benefit to those aiming to become the Master of Chaos. Numerous top-tier Supremes were vying for the Chaos Pearl. Whenever there was news of a Chaos Pearl, those Supremes would desperately compete for it.

The Clairvoyant Supreme's request was certainly not a minor one. In fact, the demands were exceedingly high! Even for a top-tier Supreme, once involved in the struggle for the Chaos Pearl, there would be dangers involved. It was not going to be easy.

For a moment, Lin Fei also fell silent. Clearly, for any Supreme, this decision was a difficult one to make!
