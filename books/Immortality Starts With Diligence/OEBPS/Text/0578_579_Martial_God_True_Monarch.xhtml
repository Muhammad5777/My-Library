579. Martial God? True Monarch!

"Is this Observing Heaven Pavilion?"

Lin Fei stood with his hands behind his back, quietly suspended in the void. Behind him, a large number of Eternal Life Alliance members also stood in the void. Lin Fei was quite curious about Observing Heaven Pavilion because its headquarters were located in the void, to be precise, inside a special magic treasure. It resembled a massive pagoda, and within the pagoda was Observing Heaven Pavilion itself.

Lin Fei didn't conceal his presence, and the actions of the Eternal Life Alliance were not hidden either. Therefore, many strong individuals and factions secretly followed them, all the way to Observing Heaven Pavilion. They watched silently from the surroundings. This battle would have a significant impact on the entire situation in the Ancient Continent.

Among the members of the Eternal Life Alliance, there was only one Martial God – Lin Fei. However, when he stood in the void, no one dared to underestimate him.

"Swoosh, swoosh, swoosh."

Soon, streaks of light flew out from inside the Observing Heaven Pavilion, revealing three figures. Each of them emitted a strong divine-level aura. Many factions that had been secretly observing were astonished.

Observing Heaven Pavilion was indeed formidable, but it had always been shrouded in mystery. People had speculated whether it possessed two divine-level cultivators, but now that three divine-level cultivators had appeared from Observing Heaven Pavilion, it was truly shocking.

Who could have imagined that Observing Heaven Pavilion had not just two, but three divine-level cultivators? Underestimating Observing Heaven Pavilion's strength could lead to disastrous consequences.

"Observing Heaven Pavilion having three divine-level cultivators?"

"Did we estimate wrongly? Could our Alliance Leader be in danger?"

"We should have planned more carefully. Observing Heaven Pavilion has profound foundations and should not be underestimated."

"Who would have thought that Observing Heaven Pavilion had three divine-level cultivators?"

Many members of the Eternal Life Alliance were filled with shock. Three divine-level cultivators!

Although it was just one more divine-level cultivator than they had estimated, a single divine-level cultivator could have a decisive impact. A divine-level cultivator was not comparable to a Nascent Soul or a Martial King.

However, the three divine-level cultivators appeared humble and showed no signs of arrogance. Instead, they looked at Lin Fei with solemn expressions.

"Alliance Leader Lin, we do not oppose your goal of unifying the Ancient Continent. Furthermore, even Observing Heaven Pavilion can be integrated into the Eternal Life Alliance."

"But as the Pavilion Masters of Observing Heaven Pavilion, we cannot remain uninvolved. Regardless, we would like to give it a try."

"If we are defeated, Observing Heaven Pavilion will be fully integrated into the Eternal Life Alliance."

"Please, Alliance Leader Lin, enlighten us!"

The three god-level cultivators displayed a lowly attitude.

"Oh? You are all willing to give up Observing Heaven Pavilion, and even willing to merge it into the Eternal Life Alliance? Are you saying that the legacy of Observing Heaven Pavilion, which has been passed down for so many years, can be discarded at any moment?"

Lin Fei was somewhat surprised. He hadn't expected the three divine-level cultivators of Observing Heaven Pavilion to be so willing to abandon their legacy. It seemed like they could give it up at any time.

"Observing Heaven Pavilion doesn't really have a legacy. When Observing Heaven Pavilion was established, it was formed by several divine-level cultivators joining forces. Nowadays, if it's not feasible, why should we insist?"

Lin Fei understood the situation. He stood still and said, "Go ahead, make your move."

"Alliance Leader Lin, we won't hold back!"

The next moment, the three divine-level cultivators attacked. Two Deity Transformation cultivators unleashed their magic treasures, while the Martial God delivered a direct punch.

The attacks from the three god-level cultivators simultaneously struck Lin Fei. It was evident that these three divine-level cultivators had a high level of cooperation. Their coordination was not something achieved overnight.

However, facing the combined attack of three divine-level cultivators, Lin Fei remained motionless, standing in the void. He allowed their attacks to land on him.

"What's going on? Is Martial God Lin really trying to confront three divine-level cultivators head-on?"

"Is he overestimating himself just because he went to Divine Realm for a while?"

"Could he really be invincible now?"

Some powerful individuals who had been secretly observing the situation, including Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators, were gradually growing resentful. They had their reasons for not going to the Divine Realm, but it didn't mean they acknowledged being inferior to Divine Realm Martial Gods. Lin Fei was confronting three opponents head-on as if he didn't take them seriously at all.

With a loud "bang," the three divine-level cultivators unleashed their full power. They poured their formidable forces into Lin Fei, expecting to crush him completely. However, what they witnessed left them in shock. Lin Fei's body remained completely unscathed, not a single particle destroyed.

These three divine-level cultivators couldn't believe their eyes. How could a Martial God withstand their combined attacks without any damage?

"Are you really a Martial God?" One of the Martial Gods trembled as he spoke.

"Martial God?" Lin Fei chuckled. "I just passed my ninth Heavenly Tribulation. I don't know if I can be considered a Martial God. In the Divine Realm, many don't call me a Martial God but rather a True Monarch."

With a thunderous roar, Lin Fei's body expanded instantly, reaching tens of thousands of meters in size. Although the power of the particle world within him didn't burst out, every particle in his body exploded. His aura spread far and wide, shaking the entire Ancient Continent. This imposing aura was not directed at the three divine-level cultivators intentionally, but they still felt suffocated.

This level of aura far exceeded their expectations, leaving them stunned.

"True Monarch? Nine Heavenly Tribulations signify the limit of a Martial God, while a True Monarch surpasses it."

"Lin Fei has been in the Divine Realm for only two or three years and has already become a True Monarch?"

"But this aura is too terrifying, definitely not something a Martial God can possess. Could it really be a True Monarch?"

Those who had been secretly observing, including Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators, now understood that Lin Fei had likely achieved the status of a True Monarch. The three divine-level cultivators from the Observing Heaven Pavilion were in a state of shock and disbelief.

"True Monarch! I never imagined that Lin Fei had already become a True Monarch."

"No wonder the three of us couldn't defeat Lin Fei even when we joined forces."

"So, the reason Lin Fei could return from the Divine Realm is that he has already become a True Monarch."

"Faced with the power of a True Monarch, what are we mere divine-level cultivators?"

"We admit defeat. The Observing Heaven Pavilion will submit to True Monarch Lin's judgment."

The three god-level cultivators were in a state of shock, their faces ashen. They had been defeated, and it was a devastating loss. Throughout the battle, Lin Fei hadn't even actively attacked; it was the three of them who initiated the confrontation.

Yet, they ended up suffering a humiliating defeat. Lin Fei's physical body couldn't be harmed or even a single particle annihilated by their combined efforts. It was a crushing defeat.

However, despite their defeat, they couldn't help but be amazed. Who would have thought that Lin Fei, who had only been in the Divine Realm for two or three years, would achieve the status of a True Monarch? They accepted their loss with respect and humility.
