662. The Awakening of the Chaotic Behemoth!

"Hmm?"

Lin Fei's eyebrows slightly raised.

"Come in."

He invited the woman into the courtyard.

The two sat in the garden.

Lin Fei asked calmly, "Tell me, what do you need me to do?"

He was well aware of these Supremes. When they come seeking him with news of Chaotic Behemoths, they surely have a request.

"Murder! Kill three Supremes! If you can do that, then I can tell you about the Chaotic Behemoth."

The woman stated straightforwardly. Her objective was to have someone killed!

Lin Fei scrutinized the woman carefully. Clearly, the woman was also a Supreme. However, an ordinary Supreme could not kill other Supremes. Only a top-tier Supreme could slay other Supremes.

"Who are these three Supremes? Why do you want to kill them?" Lin Fei further inquired.

The woman frowned slightly and asked, "Do you need a reason?"

"Of course. Even if it's a transaction, I do not wish to kill without cause. There must be some reasons."

Lin Fei had his principles. Even in a transaction, he needed to know the reasons. Otherwise, he would rather forego the information about the Chaotic Behemoth.

Call Lin Fei hypocritical or superfluous if you will, but these indeed were Lin Fei's principles. He had principles! Just like the convictions people hold in their hearts. They cannot be discarded or breached arbitrarily.

"You need a reason?" The woman took a deep breath and said solemnly, "Those three invaded my Realm and enslaved my people. Is this reason sufficient?"

"Invading a Realm? That reason is sufficient!"

Lin Fei nodded slightly. He agreed to the woman, to slay the three Supremes.

"Take me to the place of the Chaotic Behemoth. I shall slay the beast, and will then help you kill those three Supremes."

"Very well."

The woman was very decisive. She immediately transformed into a streak of light, flying toward the depths of chaos.

Lin Fei followed closely behind.

Until now, Lin Fei did not know the woman's name. However, it didn't matter. Their interaction was merely a transaction, after all. There was no need to know her name.

Deep in the Chaos, Lin Fei and the woman both stopped. This time, without needing the woman's prompt, Lin Fei could "see" it himself. Ahead of them lay a Chaotic Behemoth. Yet, this one seemed different.

Lin Fei's expression grew grave as he observed the Chaotic Behemoth in front of him. Its massive body was whale-like, but it bizarrely possessed three heads, each festooned with countless eyes. The densely packed eyes made it look exceedingly fearsome. The Chaotic Behemoth lay sleeping within the chaos, its eyes closed, yet it still exuded a terrifying aura.

This was no ordinary Chaotic Behemoth. In fact, Lin Fei wondered if it might be a top-tier Chaotic Behemoth. Among Chaotic Behemoths, there were indeed those who stood at the pinnacle, even stronger than a top-tier Supreme. Sometimes, even a slight mistake could lead a top-tier Supreme to death at the maws of such a creature.

Therefore, the presence of this suspected top-tier Chaotic Behemoth genuinely made Lin Fei wary.

Lin Fei glanced at the woman again. "When you discovered it, had it ever awakened?" he asked.

The woman pondered for a moment before shaking her head. "From my observation, it appears to have never awoken, and it seems to have never noticed me either," she replied. "However, I've always felt a strong sense of danger emanating from it, so I've never dared to approach too closely. I don't know much about its actual condition."

The woman spoke candidly. Naturally, she would not and dared not hide anything.

Lin Fei's expression remained serious. He had a feeling that this Chaotic Behemoth might actually be awake, but he had not approached close enough to be certain. Still, this Chaotic Behemoth seemed very dangerous.

Yet Lin Fei was not content to just take a look and leave; he was not willing to walk away. Moreover, he did not believe that this Chaotic Behemoth could kill him.

At most, Lin Fei might not be able to defeat this Chaotic Behemoth, but killing him was beyond its capability. After all, even if it was a top-tier Supreme, Lin Fei was no ordinary top-tier Supreme, especially when it came to preserving his own life. Lin Fei felt he was second to none.

"Illusory Realm!"

With his resolution firm, Lin Fei unleashed the Illusory Realm.

"Hum."

The Illusory Realm descended abruptly, enveloping the Chaotic Behemoth.

Immediately, the colossal body of the Chaotic Behemoth began to tremble. It seemed that the Illusory Realm did not suppress its spirit.

"Swipe."

In the next moment, the Chaotic Behemoth opened its eyes.

"Roar..."

There was confusion in the eyes of the Chaotic Behemoth, but it was fleeting moment was brief. Clearly, the Chaotic Behemoth had awakened. It realized that someone had taken action against it.

Enraged, the Chaotic Behemoth let out a furious roar and extended its massive claws. With a gentle swipe, "Hum." A layer of eerie chaotic void ripples formed. Circles upon circles rolled towards Lin Fei.

"This is..."

Faced with these strange chaotic void ripples, Lin Fei felt a tinge of threat.

As a result, Lin Fei immediately retreated. He flicked his finger, testing the power of these eerie chaotic void ripples with a bit of his strength.

"Pfft."

However, when Lin Fei's power touched the chaotic void ripples, Instantly, his power was crushed into dust, vanishing without a trace.

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. His intuition was not wrong. These ripples of chaotic void were truly dangerous. They must not be touched. To touch them was to court certain death.

Of course, if Lin Fei were to deploy his Eternal Realm fully and face it head-on, He might be able to withstand it. But there was no need.

The opponent's layer of eerie chaotic void ripples seemed to have limitations. They could not spread over a large area. And their speed was also slow. As long as one did not confront them head-on, it was still possible to dodge them.

"What a formidable Chaotic Behemoth!"

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. This was the first time he had faced difficulty with a Chaotic Behemoth. Or rather, the first time the Illusory Realm had not been effective. Even willful Supremes could not withstand Lin Fei's Illusory Realm. Yet, this Chaotic Behemoth was not affected at all. Furthermore, it counterattacked immediately. Its counterattack was incredibly sharp. Even Lin Fei dared not face it directly.

The Chaotic Behemoth was fully awake now. Its enormous body was locked on Lin Fei. A malevolent aura emanated from its whole being. Clearly, the temper of this Chaotic Behemoth was not to be trifled with.

"Human Supreme, why do you disturb my slumber?" The Chaotic Behemoth astonishingly spoke in human tongue. This was not unusual, as Chaotic Behemoths were not lacking in intelligence; it was just that many were impulsive and reckless. However, the Chaotic Behemoth before Lin Fei seemed different from the ordinary.

Lin Fei, his gaze steady on the Chaotic Behemoth, spoke calmly, "I require the essence of Chaotic Behemoth flesh. Unfortunately, I have discovered your whereabouts. I merely wanted to test if you could withstand the suppression of my Illusory Realm.

However, it seems you have some methods, as you were not suppressed by my Illusory Realm. Whether you can preserve your life, though, will depend on your own fate!"

Lin Fei did not conceal his intentions. He was here to slay the Chaotic Behemoth. Instantly, the Chaotic Behemoth grew immensely angry, yet it did not act impulsively. Its eyes seemed to flicker with a glint of wisdom.

"Human Supreme, you are very confident," it responded. "But even a top-tier Supreme should not dream of defeating me. Since you are so arrogant, then I shall swallow you whole!"

The Chaotic Behemoth had no intention of sparing the female Supreme either. It had been restrained, but once it decided to act, it would be an earth-shattering event, destined to turn the heavens and the earth upside down.

At this moment, the enormous body of the Chaotic Behemoth swelled again, and its three heads roared skyward. Then, it opened its massive maws wide.

"Whoosh..."

Three terrifying suction forces emerged instantly from the three gaping mouths of the Chaotic Behemoth. Even the female Supreme's complexion drastically changed. Her form uncontrollably flew toward the gaping maws. She could not resist!

Even Lin Fei's expression was gravely serious at this moment. He finally confirmed that this Chaotic Behemoth was indeed a top-tier one.

"Eternal Realm!"

At this time, Lin Fei no longer held back. He immediately deployed his Eternal Realm.

"Hum."

As soon as Lin Fei's Eternal Realm appeared, it swept over the female Supreme. Instantly, her figure vanished, pulled into the Eternal Realm by Lin Fei. Simultaneously, the Eternal Realm rapidly swept toward the body of the Chaotic Behemoth.
