664. A Top-tier Supreme Comes Knocking!

Deep in the chaos, a Realm slowly emerged before Lin Fei.

"This is the place," the woman said. "My former Realm, the Astral Realm, but now it's occupied by those three Supremes. I have only one request—kill them!"

Her voice was tight with anger, clearly holding a deep grudge against the three Supremes, wishing for Lin Fei to dispatch them immediately.

"Astral Realm..." Lin Fei observed the Realm in front of him. It was small, even smaller than the Divine Realm, but it was a Chaos Realm nonetheless.

"Lead those three out," he suggested calmly. "Otherwise, if a battle breaks out in the Astral Realm, it might destroy the domain. You wouldn't want that, would you?"

The woman nodded slightly. She definitely did not want to destroy the Astral Realm, as it was her homeland. "Alright, I'll go in and draw them out."

With that, her figure flashed, and she swiftly flew into the Astral Realm. Lin Fei waited quietly in the Chaos.

"Hmm? They're coming out!"

Before long, Lin Fei sensed the woman and three figures following her.

"Swoosh."

The woman flew to Lin Fei's side. The three figures that were in hot pursuit stopped abruptly upon seeing Lin Fei, revealing themselves as the three Supremes.

"Did you actually bring help?" one of them remarked, surprised. "No wonder you dared to come back to the Astral Realm alone. But do you really think your helper can stand against us? How naive! Even if he's also a Supreme, we are not ordinary Supremes. Moreover, with the three of us united, aside from a top-tier Supreme, who could possibly handle us?"

Their tone mixed skepticism with dismissive arrogance.

The three Supremes eyed Lin Fei and the woman with a chilling gaze. The woman had always been a thorn in their side, and they had long sought to permanently eliminate this threat.

However, it wasn't easy. The woman was a Supreme herself, and even with the combined strength of three Supremes, killing another Supreme in the Chaos was exceedingly difficult. Likewise, they viewed the woman's assistant as negligible; unless he was a top-tier Supreme, he couldn't trouble them.

And as for a top-tier Supreme—what sort of existence was that? They couldn't believe the woman could possibly enlist the help of such a figure. If she had that kind of connection, she would have used it to kill them long before now.

"Is it these three?" Lin Fei suddenly asked the woman.

"Yes, these are the three," she replied through gritted teeth. "Just help me kill them, and the task will be completed."

"Alright," Lin Fei responded succinctly. Now that the targets were confirmed, he wasted no time.

"Boom."

Lin Fei didn't even bother to employ his Illusory Realm. He transformed into a world himself. His vast Eternal Realm released the light of eternity, sweeping toward the three Supremes.

"Swoosh."

Under the glow of eternity, the three Supremes had no time to react before they were engulfed into the Eternal Realm.

"This is... a realm?"

"What's going on?"

"How did we end up in this strange world in the blink of an eye?"

"And it seems we're even being suppressed—what kind of world can suppress us?"

"This is embodying a realm! Moreover, to suppress us and even make us feel the presence of death, only a top-tier Supreme could achieve this."

"That woman actually managed to enlist a top-tier Supreme..."

Suddenly, the three Supremes turned pale, their voices trembling. Top-tier Supreme! Lin Fei was indeed a top-tier Supreme! The woman had actually managed to bring a top-tier Supreme against them. But how could this be?

"Annihilate!"

Lin Fei didn't bother with further discussion. He activated his Eternal Realm, beginning the process of annihilating the three Supremes.

"Boom, boom, boom."

Shortly after, the three Supremes were obliterated within the Eternal Realm, completely reduced to dust.

However, the three Supremes did not perish. They employed a peculiar method to continue existing, albeit in a hidden manner. This was surely a life-preserving tactic unique to each Supreme.

Some Supremes possess especially potent and covert means of survival, difficult to anticipate and defend against. In the case of another Supreme, perhaps a moment of oversight could have allowed these three to escape.

Yet, they were within Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, where no concealment or survival tactic could go undetected.

"Continue the annihilation!" Lin Fei thought, and the relentless power of eternity resumed its work, obliterating the hidden life-preserving methods of the three Supremes.

"No..." A faint scream echoed within the void of the Eternal Realm before everything returned to silence.

They were dead.

The three Supremes had fallen, unable to muster even a hint of resistance. Naturally, the essence of their flesh also nourished Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

"Swipe."

Lin Fei opened his eyes and saw the woman. His look was expectant, and he nodded calmly, "They are slain. The task is completed. The rest is for you to handle."

The woman shuddered, her body trembling... They were dead! Those three Supremes, like immense boulders pressing down upon her, had finally "crumbled." A weight lifted from her shoulders, she felt an immense relief.

As for the remaining matters, they would be easy to handle without the three Supremes. With her capabilities, she could effortlessly manage the situation.

"Thank you, fellow daoist!" The woman bowed deeply to Lin Fei. This time, her gratitude was sincere and heartfelt.

Lin Fei did not linger after completing his task. He headed straight back to Kunyu City. Soon, he was back in the bustling yet tranquil city that seemed unchanged, still a hub of calm and peace where many Supremes exchanged wisdom and experiences.

However, Lin Fei's purpose was different; he was on the lookout for any news about Chaotic Behemoths, yet such information had eluded him thus far. All he could do was wait.

As time passed, Lin Fei realized his time was running short. If two years passed, he would have to return to the Divine Realm, regardless of whether his Eternal Realm progress had reached 10% or not. Lin Fei knew he must go back.

Approximately a month later, a distinctive Supreme approached Lin Fei's residence. "May I ask if this is the dwelling of Supreme Lin?" a new voice inquired from outside.

Lin Fei opened his eyes. With a slight sweep of his senses, he immediately detected something unusual about the Supreme outside—this was no ordinary Supreme, and very likely a top-tier one.

"Creak."

Lin Fei opened the door and saw the figure standing before him.

"Who might you be?" Lin Fei inquired.

"I am Mirrorborn Supreme," the visitor introduced himself. "I have come specifically to seek assistance from Supreme Lin on a certain matter. Of course, if Supreme Lin is willing to help, I have information regarding Chaotic Behemoths to offer."

Lin Fei's gaze sharpened, his intense presence seemingly ineffective against Mirrorborn Supreme, who remained smiling, his demeanor unchanged. This confirmed Lin Fei's suspicion: Mirrorborn Supreme was indeed a top-tier Supreme. Otherwise, how could he remain so composed and unaffected by Lin Fei's imposing aura?

A top-tier Supreme seeking him out was no trivial matter, especially when coupled with the promise of Chaotic Behemoth information—a piece of intelligence Lin Fei couldn't afford to ignore.

"Please, Mirrorborn Supreme," Lin Fei said, stepping aside to welcome his guest inside.
