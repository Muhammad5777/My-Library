336. One Against a Hundred

The sky was filled with an oppressive darkness, formed by the sheer number of Golden Core True Persons. Considering that Taiyi Dao Palace produced twenty to thirty Golden Core True Persons every millennium, the accumulated force for thousands of years was staggering. Almost a hundred Golden Core True Persons had now awakened. This was the true foundation of Taiyi Dao Palace!

Witnessing such a formidable lineup, the Ancient Origin Sect Master, Wind Sovereign, and others turned pale. Previously, the long-standing stalemate between Taiyi Dao Palace and the three major forces had seemed rather insignificant. Taiyi Dao Palace had not tapped into the power of the Golden Core True Persons of generations past. Otherwise, with this overwhelming force, they could have easily wiped out the three major forces. It was undeniable that the heritage of the Nascent Soul lineage was unparalleled and not to be underestimated.

"Are you Lin Fei?" One of the Golden Core True Persons, with a cold gaze, addressed Lin Fei.

Lin Fei remained nonchalant. The sight of these numerous Golden Core True Persons seemed no different than if he were facing one or two of them.

"You are all nearing the end of your lifespans, wrapped in the aura of death," Lin Fei remarked, "Did Taiyi Dao Palace awaken you from your slumber? Bold move. But awakening you is merely sending you to your deaths."

"Sending us to our deaths?" A chuckle arose. "We are already on borrowed time. But before we meet our end, we will clear all obstacles for Taiyi Dao Palace and obliterate the Ancient Origin Sect!"

Then, with a thunderous roar, the horde of Golden Core True Persons launched their attack. Channels of energy activated, the sky filled with dense clouds, and the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth converged into the void.

Even if these Golden Core True Persons only cast a single, identical spell, its power would be earth-shattering. The main techniques of a Golden Core True Person are spells, formations, and magic treasures. Together, they invoked a powerful spell.

"Heavenly Fire Art!"

Flames engulfed the sky, aiming to consume Lin Fei. But Lin Fei only sneered.

Suddenly, a massive Life Wheel emerged behind him. The Life Wheel was illuminated by five radiant layers, making it look extraordinarily vast. Each layer represented a Divine Ability. And these weren't ordinary Divine Abilities. These five radiant layers, combined with the Life Wheel, exerted a pressure that was truly terrifying.

The emergence of Lin Fei's Life Wheel made the numerous Golden Core True Persons tense. Their life's aura paled in comparison to the sheer pressure emanating from Lin Fei's Life Wheel. The only plausible explanation was Lin Fei's Divine Ability, which was significantly more potent than any typical Martial Sovereign.

Upon Lin Fei's command, "Gravity Domain!" the second layer of his Life Wheel shone brightly.

"This is... Gravity?"

"Such an eerie and dreadful Divine Ability."

"Form a formation!"

A palpable force of gravity descended upon the Golden Core True Persons. They felt as if they were being crushed by a mountain. Even their protective spells struggled to hold against the relentless pressure of the Gravity Domain.

To Lin Fei, anyone within the range of his Gravity Domain would experience its effects equally. Whether it was one or hundreds of Golden Core True Persons, they would all bear the brunt of the gravitational force.

However, the Golden Core True Persons reacted swiftly. Combining their strengths, they formed a massive formation. The formation's energy surged, counteracting Lin Fei's Gravity Domain.

But as the Heavenly Fire began its descent from the void, Lin Fei, without hesitation, pointed at the vast Spiritual Qi vortex in the sky, commanding, "Disperse!"

With his words, a bright white light enveloped Lin Fei, the sharp edge of a saber emanating from it. This was the Saber Domain. With the Saber Domain covering the entire void, Lin Fei was its ruler. Everything would bend to his will. Thus, when he commanded the Spiritual Qi cloud to disperse, it did.

Despite the dispersal of the Spiritual Qi cloud, the Heavenly Fire continued its descent. The Spiritual Qi had merely enhanced the potency of the Heavenly Fire spell. Still, all these raging fires were now under the dominion of Lin Fei's Saber Domain.

"Fire!"

With a simple command, Lin Fei's Saber Domain was set ablaze. The flames extinguished the descending Heavenly Fire. Moreover, these raging flames even scorched the numerous Golden Core True Persons.

"Saber Domain, along with a gravitational Divine Ability," one murmured in awe.

"This youngster is truly formidable," another remarked.

"It's no wonder our leader woke us," a third added.

"A Martial Sovereign with such powers isn't something a few top-tier Golden Core True Persons can handle," a fourth mentioned.

"However, even with the Saber Domain and the gravitational Divine Ability combined, breaking through the formation formed by hundreds of us Golden Core True Persons is impossible!" another declared confidently.

The Golden Core True Persons were indeed shaken by Lin Fei's Saber Domain. But they also held their ground, banking on the power of their combined formation. Past encounters with Golden Core True Persons had always ended with their inevitable defeat against Lin Fei's Saber and Gravity Domains. But this was a unique situation. There were hundreds of top-tier Golden Core True Persons here, and defeating them wouldn't be a simple task.

Moreover, Lin Fei was cautious. They were all from the Taiyi Dao Palace and practiced the Great Mountain Seal technique. They hadn't used it yet. If all these Golden Core True Persons were to execute the Great Mountain Seal, the resulting force would be unimaginable.

Nevertheless, Lin Fei had a plan. The next moment, countless energies from his Life Wheel converged before him, slowly forming an awe-inspiring saber. Though only tens to hundreds of meters in length, this saber exuded an aura that made one's heart race just by looking at it.

"Dual-World Saber!" Lin Fei uttered coldly. With both hands coming together, he unleashed a single, devastating slash.

The saber, spanning hundreds of meters, struck down, aiming directly at the formation formed by the Golden Core True Persons. Dual-World Saber is a technique that splits the soul from the body. One slash meant physical death, and another ensured the spirit's demise.

When the saber finally landed on the formation, it didn't cause a massive explosion or a dramatic clash. Instead, it vanished silently, like a gentle touch. But the faces of the hundreds of Golden Core True Persons changed drastically.
