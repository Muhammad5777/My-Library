221. The Big Battle Begins

Three days later, the area under the Ancient Origin Sect's arena was already crowded with people. It was filled with disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect. The Ancient Origin Sect's arena, whether they were true disciples, inner disciples, or outer disciples, all could challenge on the stage.

Ordinary disciples could go to the stage to resolve their conflicts, but they cannot kill the opponent. Otherwise, whether intentional or unintentional, they will be heavily punished!

After all, martial artists are all impulsive. They could start a fight at the slightest disagreement. If life-and-death battles were allowed, many disciples probably would go to the stage and fight to the death. By then, the Ancient Origin Sect would also lose many talented disciples. This was undoubtedly an unnecessary loss. Fighting was okay. Resolving grievances was also okay. But they cannot kill the opponent. This was the bottom line of the Ancient Origin Sect.

At this moment, the area under the stage was crowded with people. This was because the people who challenged the stage today were extraordinary.

Ordinary disciples fight on the stage to resolve grudges. Actually, the disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect had grown accustomed to it. But this time it was different. One side was Ao Yunxiao, who was currently gaining prominence among the true disciples!

It is said that Ao Yunxiao, whether in physical strength or true essence was not far from condensing the Energy Flower and Qi Flower. Moreover, Ao Yunxiao was still accumulating momentum. He had already defeated many true disciples. Once he defeated his opponent today, then Ao Yunxiao's momentum could be established. There is a great possibility of comprehending the martial momentum!

By then, Ao Yunxiao could even become a seed of Martial Sovereign in the Ancient Origin Sect! Being able to witness the birth of a Martial Sovereign seed with their own eyes was something that many disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect cannot miss.

As for Ao Yunxiao's opponent, he was not a nobody. Dao Seeker Lin Fei! The title "Dao Seeker" alone was enough to make Lin Fei famous! After all, "Dao Seekers" were often associated with some legends. Even, there's a saying a seeker will surely become a Golden Core!

That is to describe those Foundation Establishment seekers. What if it's a Martial Saint Seeker? Some people speculate that was it possible that they must become Martial Sovereigns!?

Of course, this was just speculation. Or some private guesses. And when Lin Fei was a beginner, he suppressed hundreds of Martial Saints with his saber momentum with his own power! Such a feat was not inferior to Ao Yunxiao!

Therefore, the battle between Lin Fei and Ao Yunxiao could be described as a fierce battle between dragons and tigers. It immediately attracted the attention of all the disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect.

Even some of the top Martial Saints who have condensed the Energy Flower and Qi Flower were all paying attention to this battle.

"Who knows who will win this battle?"

"Ao Yunxiao has been in the Ancient Origin Sect for many years, stepping up from an outer disciple to a true disciple. Such a true disciple, both in talent and intelligence, is among the best. I favor Ao Yunxiao more!"

"Ao Yunxiao is indeed strong and has even defeated many true disciples. However, Ao Yunxiao has not comprehended the Momentum, which is his biggest disadvantage. A Martial Saint with or without Momentum is completely different."

"Even without Momentum, Ao Yunxiao's true essence and physical body have reached the level of top Martial Saints. How long has Lin Fei been a beginner? He hasn't even studied his techniques for a few days. Even if he has Momentum, he is not invincible."

For a time, many people were discussing, analyzing the odds of victory between Lin Fei and Ao Yunxiao. There were some elders also among the crowd. They had a better understanding of Ao Yunxiao.

"Ao Yunxiao, this kid is extraordinary and determined. In this battle, he will most likely win. Once he wins, he will definitely comprehend the Martial Momentum!"

Most of the elders, actually, favored Ao Yunxiao. Only they knew how strong Ao Yunxiao's physical body and True Yuan were.

Although, true essence, the physical body, especially the physical body seemed that they could be stacked with resources and quickly become strong. But even if they could be stacked with resources, the entire Ancient Origin Sect, even the true disciples, how many of them could condense the Energy Flower?

Easy to say, hard to do.

Moreover, Ao Yunxiao also had powerful true essence. On the other hand, they just knew that Lin Fei had comprehended the saber momentum. Even, the saber momentum had reached transcendence.

However, those saber momentums could be very effective against martial artists of average will. For a martial artist like Ao Yunxiao with a firm will, even if there was a suppression effect, Ao Yunxiao could withstand it.

Having saber momentum, even saber momentum transcendence, was not invincible. However, it didn't matter who favors whom. The fight hadn't started yet. Only when the fight is over could they see who was the winner.

"Ao Yunxiao is here!"

Suddenly, someone looked up at the sky. A streak of light fell directly onto the stage.

Ao Yunxiao was in a white robe. He didn't bring any weapons, because he practiced boxing. No need for any weapons.

Ao Yunxiao landed on the stage. He glanced around. Then closed his eyes and stopped talking. Even if Lin Fei didn't come. He was not anxious at all. Very steady. Very calm. As if he had the victory in his hands! For a while, the noise below the stage gradually diminished.

"Is that 'Dao Seeker' Lin Fei?"

Suddenly, another figure flew from the void.

"Dao Seeker" Lin Fei. The current elder of the Ancient Origin Sect's outer door, although there was much news about "Dao Seeker" Lin Fei in the Ancient Origin Sect, and even though Lin Fei's fame was not small, very few people had actually seen him.

Most people did not recognize him.

Only today when Lin Fei step by step, descended from the sky and landed on the stage, did the disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect see Lin Fei and recognized him.

"'Dao Seeker' Lin Fei is quite calm!"

"Worthy of someone who can suppress hundreds of Martial Saints with his own power."

"This kind of courage is not inferior to any true disciple!"

With the arrival of Lin Fei, there was complete silence around the stage, all eyes were on Lin Fei.

"Swish".

Ao Yunxiao opened his eyes.

"Lin Fei, you're finally here!"

Ao Yunxiao's voice was very calm. Although this battle was very important to him, he was still not impatient.

Lin Fei's gaze was also looking at Ao Yunxiao. He did not see any arrogance or domineering on Ao Yunxiao's face. What he saw was only confidence. Extreme confidence!

This kind of confidence, Lin Fei also has. Or to say, Ao Yunxiao and Lin Fei were actually the same kind of person.

"Ao Yunxiao, you intentionally injured Shen Lianxin for the second time, didn't you? How could a person as determined as you start a fight because of some words? You intentionally injured Shen Lianxin to show me. To provoke my anger, forcing me to take action! Once I take action, you defeat me, you can fully comprehend the Martial Momentum!

You are very confident. You are confident that you can win! I have to say, you succeeded. I indeed have a bit of anger. All your plans and every step were successful. But you can't defeat me. If this step fails, you will fall into the abyss! You should never have injured Shen Lianxin to force me to take action. With me here, you won't comprehend Momentum!"

Lin Fei spoke word by word. Although his tone was calm, every word was attacking the heart. He spoke out all of Ao Yunxiao's plans. Injuring Shen Lianxin was just a trick. Everything was just Ao Yunxiao's plan, in effect, he was forcing Lin Fei to take action.

This was very dangerous. Once defeated by Lin Fei, all the momentum that Ao Yunxiao had accumulated before would become nothing, even casting a shadow on Ao Yunxiao's mind, harder to comprehend the Martial Momentum again.

Ao Yunxiao's move was like betting all he had, betting everything, leaving no room. This was ruthless. Ruthless to others. Even more ruthless to himself!

Ao Yunxiao's plan was exposed by Lin Fei, but he didn't look surprised. Instead, he calmly said, "The path of martial arts is like rowing against the current, if you don't advance, you will retreat. Without the courage to go all out, how can one reach the peak of martial arts? Moreover, I will definitely win this battle! No one can shake my belief!"

Indeed, Ao Yunxiao's belief was very strong. A verbal confrontation. Both sides were equally matched. No one had the upper hand.

Lin Fei didn't speak anymore. There's no point. A martial artist still has to prove himself with his strength!

"You want to fight, I will give you the opportunity! However, whether you can seize this opportunity, it's not up to you. I'm the one who has the final say!"

"Boom".

On Lin Fei's body, a huge, fierce aura burst out instantly. A circle of white light instantly enveloped the entire stage.

Without any hesitation, Lin Fei released his saber momentum at the first moment!
