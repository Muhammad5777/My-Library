Ten Foundation Establishment True Persons! Now, they had all descended upon the Nine Profound Sect.

At this time, the people of the Nine Profound Sect finally noticed the anomaly. The ten figures in the void. The aura they exude was simply shocking. The aura of each one seemed to be far stronger than that of Yuan Kongzi. Obviously, they were all Foundation Establishment True Persons!

Zhao Kongming, the sect master, and others immediately left the cave.

"Sect Master, master, you better not come over. These are the Foundation Establishment cultivators of the immortal sects. Your presence won't make any difference. If I lose, the Nine Profound Sect will naturally be unprotected. If I win, the Nine Profound Sect can prosper for hundreds of years!" Lin Fei said calmly.

Zhao Kongming, the sect master, both of their eyes reddened instantly.

Ten Foundation Establishment True Persons! Such a lineup is simply unheard of and it's all just to besiege Lin Fei?

Zhao Kongming's hand clenched tightly on the hilt of his sword. However, he also knew that Lin Fei was telling the truth. He could no longer intervene in this kind of battle. This is a battle of Foundation Establishment! It is the pinnacle battle of the entire Great Chang!

"Indeed, Martial Saint Lin does have self-awareness. If you lose, the Nine Profound Sect will naturally cease to exist. As for winning, Martial Saint Lin, do you think there's a chance?"

One of the Foundation Establishment True Persons said with a sneer.

"Whoosh".

Lin Fei stared straight at him. He asked coldly, "Who are you?"

"Sky-Reach Sect, True Person Tongxuan!" The Foundation Establishment True Person responded coldly as well.

"Perhaps, I can't kill the ten True Persons. But it's still easy for me to kill you! If I lose, I will kill you!"

Suddenly, a cold murderous intent emanated from Lin Fei. True Person Tongxuan's heart shivered, and even the expression on his face froze slightly. Even though there were nine other Foundation Establishment True Persons by his side, True Person Tongxuan still felt a chill all over his body.

Martial Saint!

He was facing a Martial Saint who was no less than the founder of Great Chang! Moreover, Lin Fei's saber had slain Yuan Kongzi and trampled on the mountain gates of the Fairy Crane Sect and the Water Moon Sect. This was not just talk. This was a reputation earned through killing!

"Martial Saint Lin, you are also a Martial Saint, there's no need to make threats. In fact, the Nine Profound Sect can coexist peacefully with our immortal sects. But you were just too aggressive. Trampling on the Water Moon Sect and the Fairy Crane Sect is an insult to our immortal sects' fundamentals, we had no choice but to take action!"

"Is that so? You're afraid of the appearance of a second founder of Great Chang, aren't you?"

Lin Fei stood with his hands behind him, his expression still calm. In fact, as long as Lin Fei achieved Martial Saint status and showed dominance, this battle was inevitable. It was just a matter of time.

After all, if Great Chang collapsed and the immortal sects controlled the world. Then the disciples of the Nine Profound Sect and other martial artists would inevitably come into conflict with the immortal sects sooner or later.

At that time, where would Lin Fei go? In the end, he could only fight! What Lin Fei was defending was not Great Chang but the martial arts! The world of Great Chang was too small to accommodate both martial arts and immortal cultivation.

"Alright, the formation has been set up! Martial Saint Lin, you shouldn't have talked so much with us. Now that the formation is complete, you have no hope of escape."

"Buzz".

With these words from the Foundation Establishment True Persons, the entire Nine Profound Sect was shrouded in a layer of faint light. This was the formation! The formation covered the entire Nine Profound Sect.

Just now these Foundation Establishment True Persons were "talking nonsense" with Lin Fei, in fact, they were buying time, setting up the formation. The purpose of setting up this formation was to prevent Lin Fei from escaping. They cut off Lin Fei's only hope of escape.

"Escape?"

Lin Fei's eyes were deep, his expression indifferent, he coldly said: "Have you ever heard of me running away in this world? Moreover, you delay time and set up a formation. How is it not that I am also buying time, waiting for reinforcements?"

The ten Foundation Establishment True Persons were taken aback for a moment. Then they burst into laughter: "Reinforcements? Martial Saint Lin, in this world, there is only you as a Martial Saint. What kind of reinforcements could you have? Is it some innate martial artists? Or, do you have any Foundation Establishment True Persons who are on good terms with you?"

These Foundation Establishment True Persons all laughed. In the entire Great Chang, Lin Fei was the only Martial Saint. As for the Foundation Establishment True Persons, they were all in the immortal sects.

Even if, this time, some of the immortal sects' Foundation Establishment True Persons didn't come with them, there were also no Foundation Establishment True Persons from any immortal sect who would lend a hand to Lin Fei and confront the eight major immortal sects. So, the reinforcements that Lin Fei was waiting for were probably a joke.

Lin Fei's gaze was deep. Suddenly, he looked towards the void outside the Nine Profound Sect. His eyes were calm, and a smile appeared at the corner of his mouth: "Who said that there is only one Martial Saint in Great Chang?"

"Huh?"

The ten Foundation Establishment True Persons' faces changed slightly. The next moment, they all turned their gazes to the rear in unison. Behind them, a figure was walking step by step. It was a black-robed saber wielder. In his hand was a plain and unadorned saber. Most importantly, he was in the void. Walking on air!

"Martial Saint?"

The ten Foundation Establishment True Persons were somewhat uncertain. How did another Martial Saint appear? And they had no prior information.

"Brother Lin, I'm glad I'm not late."

Shen Lianxin quietly looked at Lin Fei and the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons. There was not a trace of fear on his face.

"Congratulations, Brother Shen, on your successful Foundation Establishment and becoming a Martial Saint!"

Lin Fei smiled slightly. Shen Lianxin had arrived! This was the person Lin Fei had the most confidence in. Shen Lianxin was like the saber in his hand. Simple, cold, but a man of his word. Shen Lianxin owed Lin Fei a life. Then he would repay it! Even if faced with the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons, it would be the same!

"The second Martial Saint! I didn't expect that Great Chang has a second Martial Saint!"

"That's right, Lin Fei was able to become a Martial Saint, he must have found the flesh and blood of an exotic beast."

"So, there must not be a small amount of exotic beast flesh and blood, creating a second Martial Saint is not impossible!"

"So what if there's a second Martial Saint? In the face of us, the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons, it's still a dead end!"

"Correct, it's good that this second Martial Saint has come out, perfect for exterminating them all in one fell swoop. It will also save some obstacles in the future when implementing the prohibition of martial arts."

"Hm? Prohibition of martial arts?"

The faces of Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin grew cold. The ten Foundation Establishment True Persons now had the upper hand. Even if Shen Lianxin appeared, it would be the same. Therefore, there was nothing to hide about the prohibition of martial arts.

"If we immortal sects were to hold the world's power, naturally we would want to implement the prohibition of martial arts to avoid having characters like Great Chang's founding emperor or Martial Saint Lin in the future. Without powerful martial artists, who will compete with the Qi cultivators? Wouldn't the world be at peace then?"

"Hahaha, what a peaceful world!"

At this point, from the void outside the Nine Profound Sect. Another figure walked step by step. Still walking on air! Martial Saint! Another Martial Saint! This was the third Martial Saint!

For a moment, the faces of the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons changed drastically. They could tolerate one variable. But now, there was a second variable. Who knows if there is a third, a fourth variable?

"Brother Liu, I didn't expect you to come too. Congratulations, Brother Liu, for also achieving the status of Martial Saint!"

Lin Fei was a bit surprised. He didn't expect Liu Yibai to come as well. The relationship between Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin was naturally different. Shen Lianxin owed Lin Fei a life. While Liu Yibai only had a cooperative relationship with Lin Fei. At most, there was a bit of friendship.

However, this bit of friendship was not enough for Liu Yibai to stake his life and the entire Hidden Sword Sect to help Lin Fei. But Liu Yibai still came!

"If I don't come, the immortal sects will implement the prohibition of martial arts. How can we Hidden Sword Sect avoid this?"

Liu Yibai's eyes were getting colder. Prohibition of martial arts! Liu Yibai didn't expect these immortal sects to be so crazy. They wanted to implement the prohibition of martial arts!

Once Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin were both defeated, the Hidden Sword Sect only had him as a Martial Saint. He would probably be unable to hold on alone. By then, he would still not be able to escape the fate of being destroyed by those immortal sects.

This battle was no longer the immortal sects besieging Lin Fei. It was a confrontation between the Dao of Immortality and the Martial Arts of Great Chang!

As the world's number one swordsman and the Martial Saint of the Hidden Sword Sect, at this time, he had to stand up!

"Hidden Sword Sect, the current world's number one swordsman, didn't expect him to become a Martial Saint as well."

"That's miscalculated."

"If I'm not wrong, Lin Fei and the others becoming Martial Saints must have something to do with Liu Yibai."

"Hidden Sword Sect, the foundation is indeed not weaker than our immortal sects."

"If I knew this, I should have destroyed the Hidden Sword Sect first!"

"But now it's not too late. Three Martial Saints, we ten Foundation Establishment True Persons join hands and each carrying our sect's treasure, can't we deal with three Martial Saints?"

"Hmph, we ten Foundation Establishment True Persons join hands, even if the founding emperor of Great Chang is reborn, he will undoubtedly die!"

For a moment, the spiritual power of the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons surged. True Person's Magic Bodies were attracting the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth within hundreds of miles, madly converging in the void. The momentum of the three Martial Saints and the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons also gradually confronted silently in the void.

"Slash!"

Shen Lianxin made the first move! The saber in his hand was drawn instantly.

"Clang."

A dazzling saber light, as if tearing the void. Like a ribbon that penetrated heaven and earth, it slashed fiercely on the array set up by the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons.
