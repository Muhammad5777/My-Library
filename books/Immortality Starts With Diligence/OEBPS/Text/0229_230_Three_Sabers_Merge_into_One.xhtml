230. Three Sabers Merge into One

"Slay!"

Lin Fei's voice was cold, carrying a hint of killing intent.

Immediately, the hundreds of meters long saber in the void emitted surging flames, falling from the sky and slicing onto the eight zombies.

"Boom".

The raging flames were burning madly. Flame Burning Heaven Saber! Although it was just a saber technique, under the impetus of saber essence and saber momentum, this saber seemed to really have the power of burning heavens and boiling seas.

Moreover, Lin Fei used most of his saber essence to execute this saber. Therefore, the power of this saber was beyond belief. Of course, the key point was that the Flame Burning Heaven Saber carried flames, which could precisely restrain these zombies.

Otherwise, just in terms of the power of the saber technique, Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique, and Holy Spirit Saber Technique, none of them were inferior to Flame Burning Heaven Saber. But when facing the eight zombies, Flame Burning Heaven Saber undoubtedly could play a stronger role.

As this saber fell, endless flames, seemed to fill the entire void.

The Yin Qi and corpse Qi on the eight zombies, under this saber, seemed to have all turned into fuel for the fire, thereby enhancing the fire, burning even more fiercely.

"No...Come back to me!"

The expression on the mysterious Foundation Establishment True Person was twisting, madly forming hand seals, wanting to recall the eight zombies. After all, this was his only hope of crossing the tribulation. He had gone through all kinds of hardships to find the eight martial saints who were born on Yin days, only then would he be able to form the Nine Yin Zombie Array to help him resist the heavenly tribulation.

He even risked coming to Tianqi Country, under the eyes of the Ancient Origin Sect, to undertake this risky task. It was because there were enough Martial Saints in the Imperial City of the Tianqi Country only then was it possible to find Martial Saints born on Yin days.

Originally, he was about to succeed. He was short of only one zombie. Just give him a little more time, he would be able to successfully gather nine zombies born on Yin days and thus achieve great success!

But everything was ruined. The arrival of this Martial Saint from the Ancient Origin Sect, not only did it expose him ahead of time, it even destroyed his eight zombies. Now, he had no hope of crossing the tribulation. For a top Foundation Establishment True Person, the most important thing is to cross the tribulation!

For the purpose of crossing the tribulation, these top Foundation Establishment True Persons could do anything at any cost! But, if anyone ruins their hope of crossing the tribulation, then they would immediately become insane.

"You destroyed my hope of crossing the tribulation! You destroyed my Dao! Then let the entire Imperial City accompany me in burial!"

This top Foundation Establishment True Person, his Magic Body's spiritual power vibrated.

"Boom."

Immediately, above the entire Imperial City, a huge Spiritual Qi cloud gathered. Like a huge vortex.

At this moment, Lin Fei didn't make a move. Because voices were echoing in his mind.

"Your Flame Burning Heaven Saber, having slain the Yin-Evil Martial Saint zombie, comprehended the Supreme Yang state, cultivation progress increased by 100%."

"Your Flame Burning Heaven Saber has broken through to transcendence!"

Lin Fei's Flame Burning Heaven Saber, unexpectedly, had reached transcendence at this time.

"Supreme Yang state?"

Lin Fei closed his eyes. A piece of memory also appeared in his mind. With a slight organization, he immediately understood the so-called Supreme Yang state. However, that top Foundation Establishment True Person was trying to cast a terrifying spell that could destroy heaven and earth. He had gathered such a terrifying Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth.

"Suppress!"

Lin Fei's saber momentum suddenly contracted. A terrifying suppressing power suddenly all fell on that Foundation Establishment True Person.

Immediately, the Foundation Establishment True Person's expression changed. His whole body was trembling. Obviously, he was enduring tremendous pressure. However, the talisman in front of him was truly miraculous. It could actually withstand Lin Fei's suppressing saber momentum.

"It's useless, with my Void Talisman here. Even if your saber momentum is stronger, it won't affect me. Unless it's a Limit Martial Saint, otherwise, no one can kill me! Today, all of you will die!"

The Foundation Establishment True Person's expression twisted, his demeanor was like madness.

"Is that so?"

Lin Fei's expression was still calm.

Qiu Long and Li Yuan, looking at the huge Spiritual Qi cloud in the sky, were almost in despair. Such a terrifying Spiritual Qi cloud, once a spell was cast, how strong would the power of that spell be? Even if a Martial Saint could barely resist it without dying. But what about ordinary people? The entire Imperial City, with a population of millions. Now, they are probably all going to die under this terrifying spell.

"Shoo."

Lin Fei flicked his finger. The hundreds of meters long Flame Burning Heaven Saber was suspended in the void.

"Shoo."

Another saber essence. The hundreds of meters long Holy Spirit Saber also appeared in the void.

"Shoo."

A rainbow light flashed past. That was the hundreds of meters long Rainbow Transformation Saber.

Each saber emanated a formidable heavenly might, all reaching hundreds of meters!

"Unite, three sabers as one!"

The thought flitted through Lin Fei's mind. Instantaneously, the three terrifying heavenly sabers merged into one. They formed an imposing heavenly saber, reaching one thousand meters high. Near this heavenly saber, even the void itself seemed to birth a multitude of dark lines. These were fractures in the fabric of space!

"Slay!"

With a single slash, Lin Fei struck down. This fusion of three sabers was even more powerful than the last, several times over. Lin Fei's True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique was advancing rapidly. Though he hadn't broken through, his saber essence was continually increasing, and at a breakneck pace. The power of Lin Fei's united three sabers depended on his saber essence and saber momentum. The stronger the saber essence, the greater the power of the saber!

As Lin Fei slashed his saber, the void itself seemed to be rent open by the blade. Every person within the imperial city looked skyward, their eyes drawn to the void. The awe-inspiring heavenly saber elicited a feeling akin to suffocation. This saber was no longer of the mortal realm!

"Mere martial saint yet to reach the limit, how could you possibly wield such a saber?"

The Foundation Establishment True Person, driven to madness, was suddenly stirred to lucidity by the stimulation of the endless saber points. However, it was too late. The saber descended mercilessly, striking his Void Talisman. The Void Talisman flickered wildly, even sending out ripples. But, after just a moment, the spiritual light on the talisman dimmed.

"Snap."

The talisman shattered, instantly turned to ashes by the saber essence. A trace of fear flashed across the eyes of the Foundation Establishment True Person. Despite his madness, he didn't want to die. If this saber fell, his death would be certain!

"No..."

The Foundation Establishment True Person let out a horrified scream.

"Boom."

In the next moment, the saber fell. The entire coffin shop, and even the whole street, was instantly turned to ashes. Not ruins, but truly ashes, completely annihilated by the terrifying saber essence. There was no corpse left, not even a trace of blood.

The entire imperial city was eerily silent. Everyone was staring blankly at the void and the powerful saber. The Foundation Establishment True Person was dead! The once frenzied, Foundation Establishment True Person, who was on the brink of a breakthrough, had been ground to dust under Lin Fei's terrifying saber. Even the Spiritual Qi cloud in the void gradually dissipated until it vanished without a trace.

With the fall of the Foundation Establishment True Person and the collapse of the aura cloud, the void, which turned pitch black by the shadowy and necrotic air, brightened again. A ray of warm sunlight fell on everyone's faces.

"This saber..."

Qiu Long and Li Yuan were so shocked they couldn't utter a word. Even if they had been in the Ancient Origin Sect, they had never seen a martial saint unleash such terrifying power.

Was this the power of a top martial saint? Clearly, it was a power of a Limit Martial Saint! Even an average Limit Martial Saint might not have such terrifying power. Only a martial saint who had condensed the Spirit Flower and evolved his martial arts to perfection could potentially unleash such a terrifying saber.

"Something must have happened in the Ancient Origin Sect that caused Outer Sect Elder Lin Fei..."

The two men understood very well that a man like Lin Fei couldn't remain nameless. But they hadn't heard of him. It could only mean one thing: they were out of touch with the Ancient Origin Sect, and maybe something major had happened there recently.

"Mission accomplished. Remember to report to the Ancient Origin Sect. I need to go back to debrief on the mission."

Lin Fei didn't seem particularly shocked. He looked quite calm. He was more concerned about the ten thousand contribution points from the sect mission.

"Elder Lin..."

Qiu Long wanted to say something more, but Lin Fei had already taken a step, transforming into a streak of light that disappeared into the horizon.
