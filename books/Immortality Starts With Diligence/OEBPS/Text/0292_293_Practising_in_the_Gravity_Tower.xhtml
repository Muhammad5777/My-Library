293. Practising in the Gravity Tower

Now, Lin Fei had acquired two Great Divine Abilities, both of which were top-tier. Even without the Saber Domain, Lin Fei's status in the Ancient Origin Sect was that of a top-tier Martial Sovereign.

In fact, considering the three major powers, he stood out as an elite Martial Sovereign! If he were to add the Saber Domain into the mix, Lin Fei's strength would be immeasurable.

In reality, the power gaps between different Martial Sovereigns are vast. Some Martial Sovereigns, having cultivated Lesser Divine Abilities, might have higher realms. They might even possess multiple Divine Abilities that, when combined, can be formidable in combat.

However, this is in comparison to the average Martial Sovereign. Once a Martial Sovereign has cultivated two or even three Great Divine Abilities, the disparity becomes evident. And if they are top-tier Great Divine Abilities, even having one or two can make a significant difference.

Naturally, Great Divine Abilities are challenging to cultivate, not to mention the top-tier ones. The fact that Lin Fei could perfect the Divine Ability Black Wind Prison in such a short period was nothing short of a miracle.

Lin Fei was pondering the Gravity Domain. He had just begun to grasp its essence. Although he hadn't planned to cultivate the Gravity Domain so soon, the memories he acquired informed him of the detailed cultivation process.

The Gravity Domain seemed to be more effectively cultivated in areas with abnormal gravitational forces. Lin Fei immediately thought of the Ancient Origin Sect's Gravity Tower, which was designed to temper one's physical body. Having ventured into the Gravity Tower before, he wondered if cultivating the Gravity Domain there might yield better results.

This was purely coincidental, but Lin Fei didn't want to miss such an opportunity.

"Let's give it a try," Lin Fei thought. With a single step, he left his dwelling and flew straight to the Gravity Tower.

Outside the Gravity Tower in the Ancient Origin Sect, many disciples came and went, many wishing to temper their physical bodies within the tower. The required contribution points to enter the Gravity Tower were minimal, so it was popular among disciples.

As Lin Fei landed outside the tower, revealing himself, a wave of recognition swept through the crowd. Their hearts trembled slightly at his sight, and they hurriedly bowed, saying, "Greetings, Sovereign Lin!"

Lin Fei might not yet fully realize the esteem in which he was held within the Ancient Origin Sect. The last time, he had decisively defeated the Worry-free Sovereign from the Illusion Worry City. This news had reached the Ancient Origin Sect, shocking both the disciples and the elders. They were not only amazed but also thrilled. Many viewed Lin Fei as the number one figure in the Ancient Origin Sect. Therefore, seeing him in person was naturally exciting for many.

The elder guarding the Gravity Tower hurriedly approached Lin Fei and respectfully said, "Sovereign Lin, do you wish to use the Gravity Tower? We will immediately evacuate the disciples inside."

This was Lin Fei's "privilege" or, more accurately, the privilege of a Sovereign. Whenever a Sovereign wished to use certain facilities within the Ancient Origin Sect, they had priority.

Lin Fei pondered for a moment. He didn't really want to disturb others. However, if he were to cultivate the Gravity Domain inside the tower, he might inadvertently unleash its power, which could be very dangerous. If someone were to be trapped within his Gravity Domain, even a Martial Sovereign would not be able to withstand it. Not to mention those at lower ranks. They could be instantly crushed to death by the Gravity Domain.

Therefore, Lin Fei straightforwardly stated, "I need to use the Gravity Tower to cultivate a Divine Ability. If someone comes near me, it might be dangerous. So, it's better to evacuate everyone from the tower for now."

"Yes, Sovereign Lin," the elder replied. "But, may we observe from outside the tower?" he asked with hopeful eyes. Watching a Martial Sovereign cultivate a Divine Ability was a rare opportunity.

Lin Fei did not refuse, saying, "Stay at least three hundred meters away from the tower."

"Thank you, Sovereign!" The elder's face lit up with joy. He then proceeded to evacuate all the disciples from the Gravity Tower.

"Sovereign, the Gravity Tower is now empty. You may enter at your convenience."

"Alright." Lin Fei nodded and then stepped into the Gravity Tower.

Once Lin Fei's figure disappeared, some disciples outside hurriedly asked the elder, "What is Sovereign Lin doing inside the Gravity Tower? He is already a Martial Sovereign, so he doesn't need to temper his body in the tower, does he?"

The elder glanced over the curious crowd and said solemnly, "Sovereign Lin is here to cultivate a Divine Ability. Everyone must stay more than three hundred meters away from the tower."

Upon hearing that Lin Fei was cultivating a Divine Ability, the crowd erupted with excitement.

"What? Cultivating a Divine Ability? Sovereign Lin is cultivating a Divine Ability in the Gravity Tower? This is a must-watch!"

The idea that witnessing this might be beneficial for their own cultivation sparked further interest.

Although disciples and elders of the Ancient Origin Sect knew about Martial Sovereigns possessing Divine Abilities, they were unclear about what these abilities looked like or how they were cultivated. In fact, most had never even seen a Martial Sovereign in action.

Having received Lin Fei's permission to watch, the elder spread the word. Many disciples and elders were drawn to the Gravity Tower out of curiosity. Eventually, the activity even caught the attention of the Ancient Origin Sect's leader and Wind Sovereign. Both of these prominent Martial Sovereigns made their way to the outside of the Gravity Tower.

"Greetings, Sect Leader and Wind Sovereign."

Both the Sect Leader and Wind Sovereign nodded slightly, their gaze immediately fixated on Lin Fei's figure inside the Gravity Tower. The Ancient Origin Sect's leader seemed somewhat incredulous.

"Are you certain that Sovereign Lin mentioned he wishes to cultivate a Divine Ability?" he inquired once more.

"We are sure. Sovereign Lin indeed said he wishes to cultivate a Divine Ability," the elder of the Gravity Tower confirmed with certainty.

For a moment, both the Sect Leader and Wind Sovereign exchanged glances. They were well aware that the first Divine Ability Lin Fei had cultivated was the Black Wind Prison, which surely had nothing to do with gravity. Thus, there would be no need for the Gravity Tower for its cultivation.

Therefore, the Divine Ability that Lin Fei aimed to cultivate wasn't the Black Wind Prison. It was his second Divine Ability! Only after mastering the first Divine Ability could one begin to cultivate the second.

"Could it be that Lin Fei has already perfected his first Divine Ability?" Even Wind Sovereign found it hard to believe. Black Wind Prison was a top-tier Great Divine Ability. How much time had passed since Lin Fei began his cultivation journey? And he was already starting on his second Divine Ability! Moreover, he was cultivating it openly. How confident must he be?

"No matter what, let's just observe for now," said the Sect Leader, setting the tone for the discussion. "We can inquire further once Sovereign Lin emerges."

Above all else, Lin Fei's cultivation was of the utmost importance. Besides, both the Sect Leader and Wind Sovereign were curious to see for themselves - what kind of Divine Ability was Lin Fei's second?
