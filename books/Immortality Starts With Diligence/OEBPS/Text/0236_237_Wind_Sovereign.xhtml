237. Wind Sovereign

The Law Enforcement Hall was a place that everyone in the Ancient Origin Sect feared. There was no disciple who wanted to be taken into the Law Enforcement Hall. Because, once that happened, it basically meant that they had "caused trouble". Moreover, the trouble would certainly not be small. Otherwise, there would be no need to mobilize the Law Enforcement Hall.

This was Lin Fei's first visit to the headquarters of the Ancient Origin Sect's Law Enforcement Hall. This was not the Outer Law Enforcement Hall but the one that governed the entire Ancient Origin Sect's inner sect and true disciples. Even the elders of the Ancient Origin Sect were all within the jurisdiction of the Law Enforcement Hall.

As long as someone "caused trouble", the Law Enforcement Hall would take action. Therefore, the hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall held high authority.

The power was so great that it was only below the sect master. Therefore, the hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall has always been held by a Martial Sovereign. If not for the Martial Sovereign, probably no one could uphold the reputation of the entire Law Enforcement Hall.

Following the Martial Saint of the Law Enforcement Hall, Lin Fei arrived at a spacious room.

"Vice Hall Master Lin, please, the hall master is inside."

Lin Fei nodded and walked straight in. As soon as he entered the room, Lin Fei saw an old man. The old man had his eyes closed. He was sitting cross-legged on the ground. There was no strong aura around him.

However, Lin Fei vaguely felt a kind of oppressive feeling as if the old man in front of him was a terrifying existence.

Lin Fei had never felt this way before. So, he was taken aback. He knew that the old man in front of him was extraordinary. It was probably the hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall, Wind Sovereign!

Wind Sovereign, the hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall. It's pretty much well-known in the Ancient Origin Sect.

"Swish".

Wind Sovereign opened his eyes.

"You must be Vice Hall Master Lin, right?"

"Lin Fei pays his respects to Wind Sovereign!"

Lin Fei respectfully saluted. A Martial Sovereign equivalent to a Golden Core True Person. With a lifespan of a thousand years, Wind Sovereign was one of the real core figures in the entire Ancient Origin Sect, even considered a pillar of heaven!

"No need to be so formal. Vice Hall Master Lin must be very curious now. Why did I summon you and even make you the vice hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall?"

Lin Fei nodded. He was indeed curious.

"Very simple, strength!" Wind Sovereign smiled and said, "You defeated Ao Yunxiao, and you are very strong in your own right. Now that you have condensed a Qi Flower, coupled with your saber momentum. Your strength, even among the Limit Martial Saints, can be considered top-notch. Therefore, it's only fitting for you to serve as the vice hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall!"

Lin Fei's face remained calm. He knew it wasn't that simple. If it was simply because of his strong strength that he was appointed as the vice hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall, there was no need for Wind Sovereign to summon him personally.

Not to mention Lin Fei, even a Limit Martial Saint who had condensed Qi Flowe and Energy Flower might not necessarily be able to meet Wind Sovereign.

Martial Sovereign, high above. Even if two flowers have been condensed, as long as one does not become a Martial Sovereign for one day, there will always be a world of difference with the Martial Sovereign.

Seeing that Lin Fei didn't speak, Wind Sovereign's expression turned serious and he said in a deep voice, "Of course, there is indeed a reason for making you the vice hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall so quickly. The vice hall masters of my Ancient Origin Sect's Law Enforcement Hall are in charge of the inner hall and the outer hall respectively. The inner hall mainly deals with disciples or elders who have violated the rules of the Ancient Origin Sect, mainly responsible for internal matters of the Ancient Origin Sect.

The outer hall mainly deals with external matters. For instance, if the disciples or elders of my Ancient Origin Sect are killed by external forces or individuals, the outer hall of the Law Enforcement Hall will handle it. A big incident has recently occurred in my Ancient Origin Sect. Three true disciples were killed by some Foundation Establishment cultivators. But the culprits escaped to the Illusion Worry City. This Illusion Worry City is a bit special. It has two city masters, namely Illusion Heart True Person and Worry-free Sovereign! One is a Golden Core True Person, the other is a Martial Sovereign.

It is located at the junction of the three forces: Ancient Origin Sect, Profound Light Cave Heaven, and Red Hades Mansion, but it has become a no man's land! Anyone can enter the Illusion Worry City, and no fighting is allowed there. Over time, Illusion Worry City has become a carefree place in the eyes of some Martial Saints and True Persons. But this time is different. The culprits killed three true disciples of our Ancient Origin Sect, one of whom even had the potential to become a Martial Sovereign seed.

Originally, I planned to personally go down the mountain. But the Ancient Origin Sect, Pround Light Cave Heaven, and Red Hades Mansion once had some agreements with the two city masters of Illusion Worry City. Martial Sovereign and Golden Core True Persons are not allowed to enter Illusion Worry City without permission. So, the matter of capture can only be handled by the Martial Saints.

You have grasped the transcendence saber momentum, which is currently the only one in the Law Enforcement Hall. When it comes to arresting people, your saber momentum has more advantages than other Martial Saints who have condensed two flowers. Moreover, your strength is not bad, it may not be worse than the Martial Saints who have condensed two flowers. It's most appropriate for you to capture the real culprit."

Wind Sovereign didn't hide anything. He revealed the hidden information. It turns out that Lin Fei was to be sent to capture some troublesome figures.

Illusion Worry City. No man's land and there were even Golden Core True Persons and Martial Sovereigns. It doesn't sound like an easy task.

However, Lin Fei was originally an elder of the outer Law Enforcement Hall. The Law Enforcement Hall has the right to order Lin Fei to perform some tasks. This is also a duty of Lin Fei as an elder of the Ancient Origin Sect. After all, he has gained cultivation techniques from the Ancient Origin Sect.

He got resources. Since he got the benefits, then of course he has to pay.

"Wind Sovereign, Illusion Worry City has Golden Core True Persons and Martial Sovereigns. And it's explicitly forbidden to take action in Illusion Worry City. If I go to Illusion Worry City to arrest or even kill people, wouldn't that break the rules of Illusion Worry City? If the Golden Core True Person and Martial Sovereign take action..."

Lin Fei didn't continue his words. But Wind Sovereign naturally understood Lin Fei's meaning.

If the Golden Core True Person and Martial Sovereign took action, then Lin Fei would be in danger. Even if he's strong, how could he possibly contend with a Golden Core True Person or a Martial Sovereign?

Wind Sovereign smiled faintly and said, "You don't need to worry about this. You represent my Ancient Origin Sect. The Ancient Origin Sect, Profound Light Cave Heaven, and Red Hades Mansion had an agreement with Illusion Worry City. The two city masters of Illusion Worry City are not allowed to take action against people from our three sects.

Similarly, our three sects' Golden Core True Persons or Martial Sovereigns are not allowed to enter Illusion Worry City and take action. So, when you take action in Illusion Worry City, you don't need to worry about Golden Core True Persons or Martial Sovereigns.

However, the strong ones under the command of the two city masters and those who killed the true disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect may take action against you. And the Law Enforcement Hall will also dispatch some elders to accompany you to Illusion Worry City."

Lin Fei understood. The Golden Core True Person and Martial Sovereign would not take action. After all, if they take action, it would be equivalent to tearing up the agreement. It's not a difficult task for the Ancient Origin Sect to raze Illusion Worry City to the ground with all its strength. But the strong ones below the Martial Sovereigns could take action. So, this trip of Lin Fei actually faced a great challenge!

Wind Sovereign stopped talking. He waited quietly. Lin Fei also closed his eyes. Many thoughts flashed through his mind. He was quietly considering his options.
