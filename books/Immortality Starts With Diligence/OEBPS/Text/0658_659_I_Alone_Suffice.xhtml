659. I Alone Suffice

In the vast expanse of Chaos. A colossal city sprawled amidst the Chaos. Even from a great distance, its presence could still be felt.

"Kunyu City! What a splendid Kunyu City! In this open, barren Chaos, it shines like a beacon, dazzlingly bright."

Lin Fei, beholding Kunyu City from afar, couldn't help but marvel in his heart. He was no stranger to the world.

On the contrary, Lin Fei had seen many facets of the world. Yet, such a magnificent city still sent a tremor through his heart.

With a whoosh, Lin Fei stepped forward, rapidly closing in on Kunyu City.

As he neared, Lin Fei saw that the number of Supremes in the vicinity had increased.

These Supremes varied in appearance. Life forms were quite extraordinary. Not all were humanoid. Their forms were indeed myriad.

Moreover, entry and exit from Kunyu City were unrestricted, As long as one did not cause trouble within its walls. And indeed, none dared to stir trouble.

The lord of Kunyu City was none other than the Master of Chaos! Exalted above the Supremes and Rulers, he ensured the peace of Kunyu City. It was precisely because of the presence of the Master of Chaos that Kunyu City had endured for so long.

Otherwise, the various Supremes with ulterior motives would have long since transformed Kunyu City into something unrecognizable.

But with the presence of a Master of Chaos, everything was subdued, allowing Kunyu City to stand firm in the Chaos. Similar cities existed in the Chaos, But Lin Fei only knew of Kunyu City for now. He was in no hurry. First, he entered Kunyu City. There would be time later to slowly learn more.

Lin Fei entered Kunyu City. After wandering for several hours, Lin Fei had nearly figured it out.

Kunyu City was a hub for Supremes to interact and cultivate. Inside, various transactions were possible. Supremes were not allowed to engage in combat directly.

However, there were places to resolve feuds. One needed to ascend to the Chaotic Arena. Within the Chaotic Arena, life or death battles unfolded, and no one would intervene.

In the Chaos, various kinds of information and intelligence can be exchanged within Kunyu City. Naturally, with so many Supremes in Kunyu City, most actually possess their own realms.

However, no one would disclose the location of their realms. Navigating through the Chaos, the most crucial aspect is the ability to "conceal" the location of one's realm. Otherwise, one might fall into irreversible calamity!

The information Lin Fei most desired was about the Chaotic Behemoths. He understood well that the cultivation of Supremes had nearly reached its zenith. Above the Supremes, many do not dare to aspire for more, merely hoping to find an unclaimed realm to become its Ruler. That represents the ultimate pursuit of the Supremes.

As for the Master of Chaos? There are no specific cultivation methods to speak of—only enlightenment. Yes, enlightenment! One must comprehend it amidst the Chaos. Those who can comprehend it can become the Master of Chaos. Those who cannot, will not.

How vast is the Chaos, how many realms are there? And how many Supremes or Rulers? It seems each Supreme or Ruler is a pinnacle presence in a realm. Yet, the realms are innumerable in the vast Chaos.

Therefore, the number of Supremes and Rulers is also incredibly high. But how many Masters of Chaos are there? They can be counted on one's fingers! Some even speculate that there are at most only a few dozen Masters of Chaos. Compared to the vast number of Supremes and Rulers, there are only a few dozen Masters of Chaos, highlighting the immense difficulty in attaining such a status.

But Lin Fei is different. Others may have reached the pinnacle of their cultivation, but his path of cultivation is far from over. That others cannot become the Master of Chaos does not mean he cannot! After all, he already has his own direction in cultivation. This alone surpasses ninety-nine percent of Supremes and Rulers.

What Lin Fei needs now is the Chaotic Behemoths. He needs to hunt these behemoths. To obtain the essence of their flesh and blood. With this, he aims to elevate his Eternal Realm, thereby enhancing his strength.

Thus, Lin Fei sought specific intelligence on the Chaotic Behemoths. He went to the mission hall in Kunyu City. This hall was essentially a place for posting and accepting missions, all issued by Supremes. These tasks often promised rewards, typically in the form of Chaos Stones.

In Chaos, countless streams of chaotic energy could condense into heavy chaotic water, which in turn could form Chaos Stones. These stones could replenish the physical body's energy and even various types of energy. No matter the energy, Chaos Stones could replenish it.

Moreover, at the brink of death, a Chaos Stone could keep one clinging to life, barely holding off death and possibly finding a chance to survive. That was the utility of Chaos Stones.

Furthermore, Chaos Stones were rare, naturally forming only within the Chaos itself. Hence, they had become the common currency in these realms. Many issued missions rewarding Chaos Stones. For instance, some Supreme tasks offered one or two Chaos Stones as rewards.

Lin Fei glanced through the offerings. The task he was looking for was simple: to find some help in dealing with a Chaotic Behemoth. Apart from Lin Fei, there were indeed others who wished to confront such creatures.

However, these missions were rare. Chaotic Behemoths were difficult to kill, and even trapping them was a challenge. Not many Supremes were willing to provoke such beings.

Lin Fei spent a long time in the mission hall, reviewing one task after another. He finally spotted a mission that particularly caught his eye: "Confront a Chaotic Behemoth!" "Requires at least nine Supremes." "Rewards based on contribution, with a minimum of two Chaos Stones!"

Seeing this, Lin Fei felt a stir within him. Nine Supremes, each assured at least two Chaos Stones—such a generous reward indicated that this was no ordinary task. Moreover, it required the cooperation of nine Supremes, which was extraordinary. Even though a Chaotic Behemoth was formidable, typically five Supremes could manage to restrain it, even if they couldn't kill it. But this task demanded nine Supremes.

Despite the dangers, for Lin Fei, these were inconsequential. All he needed was the presence of a Chaotic Behemoth; nothing else mattered.

"This task, I'll take it!"

With that, Lin Fei accepted the mission. Upon accepting a task, one could obtain the specific location of the task's issuer, which had to be managed at the mission hall's counter. This was the domain of the Chaos Master of Kunyu City, and no one dared act rashly. Only thus could the operation of the mission hall be maintained.

Lin Fei's acceptance of the mission went smoothly. He also obtained the location of the task issuer, located within a courtyard in Chaos City. Without hesitation, Lin Fei went directly to the address provided.

Soon, Lin Fei arrived at the courtyard. With a swift motion, he reached the front of the courtyard and transmitted his voice directly: "I have accepted the task concerning the Chaotic Behemoth, and request the issuer to come forth for a meeting!"

Lin Fei's voice naturally penetrated into the courtyard. At that moment, there were actually five Supremes within. Abruptly, all five stood up.

"Another has arrived," one noted. "It must be someone accepting the task."

"Another Supreme has come; soon we will have the nine Supremes needed," another added quickly, rising to open the grand doors of the courtyard with a creak.

Lin Fei was met by an unfamiliar Supreme, humanoid in appearance.

"How may I address you, fellow daoist?"

"Lin Fei."

"Ah, fellow daoist Lin, please come in."

The stranger then welcomed Lin Fei into the courtyard.

"Fellow daoist Lin, these are the Supremes who have accepted the task. Including you, we are now number five. We just need to patiently wait until we gather nine Supremes, and then we can immediately undertake the mission. So, I must ask you to wait a little longer."

Lin Fei, however, was somewhat surprised.

"Wait even longer?"

"Indeed," one of the other Supremes confirmed. Lin Fei looked over the other four Supremes and then, shaking his head slightly and speaking in a calm tone, said, "Fellow daoists, there's no need for such trouble. To complete this task, the other Supremes may withdraw. I alone suffice!"

As Lin Fei's words fell, the entire courtyard fell silent instantly, so quiet one could hear a pin drop!
