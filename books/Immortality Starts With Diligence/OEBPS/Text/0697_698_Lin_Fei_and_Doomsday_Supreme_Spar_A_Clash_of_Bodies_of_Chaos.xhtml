698. Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme Spar! A Clash of Bodies of Chaos!

"Ha ha ha, a Body of Chaos, it truly is a Body of Chaos!" Doomsday Supreme could not contain his jubilation and burst into hearty laughter. "In the Chaos, it feels completely natural, like a fish in water. A Master of Chaos, I have hope now!"

His laughter was filled with excitement, a stark contrast to Lin Fei's own journey. Unlike Lin Fei, who hadn't been cultivating for long, Doomsday Supreme had ventured through the Forbidden Tower countless times.

He had faced numerous failures at the ninth layer, even reaching the brink of despair, until he met Lin Fei and found a glimmer of hope. Together, they had tackled the ninth layer, finally succeeding in transforming into Bodies of Chaos.

Naturally, Doomsday Supreme was ecstatic.

His declaration sent shockwaves through the other Supremes around them, stirring up a tempest of awe and speculation in their hearts. Although they had their suspicions, hearing it confirmed by Doomsday Supreme himself made it all the more momentous.

"He truly transformed into a Body of Chaos!" they murmured among themselves. "The legends of the Forbidden Tower are true, then. If one can surpass the ninth layer, there is indeed a chance to transform."

"It's incredible, truly," another added, though with a hint of resignation. "But reaching the ninth layer is challenging enough, let alone surpassing it. Without becoming an Invincible Supreme, one shouldn't even dream of reaching it."

"As for surpassing the ninth layer, it seems beyond the reach of even a typical Invincible Supreme. Otherwise, Doomsday Supreme wouldn't have failed so many times."

"True, if it weren't for Supreme Lin Fei, Doomsday Supreme might have spent another thousand, or even ten thousand years without ever surpassing the ninth layer. This time, the key to their success was likely Supreme Lin Fei."

"It seems we all underestimated Supreme Lin Fei. Perhaps, as of now, Supreme Lin Fei is the foremost among the Supremes, surpassing even Doomsday Supreme..."

As the Supremes speculated, the truth of their conjectures became evident. After all, none were foolish, and the results spoke for themselves.

Amidst this realization, Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme prepared themselves for a friendly spar, ready to test the extent of their newly acquired powers.

As many eyes focused on Lin Fei, Doomsday Supreme's gaze was also filled with a palpable sense of eagerness. Having just transformed into a Body of Chaos and in high spirits, he was keen to test the limits of his new-found capabilities.

Before, he and Lin Fei were equals, both as Invincible Supremes. Now, both having successfully transitioned into Bodies of Chaos, Doomsday Supreme was curious about the extent of their newfound strength.

Aside from the Masters of Chaos, Lin Fei was the only one who could stand on equal footing with Doomsday Supreme in the entire realm of chaos. While he still lacked confidence against a true Master of Chaos—given he was only a step into that state—he felt on par with Lin Fei, who was also now embodied in chaos.

"Shall we do it right here?" Lin Fei was ready to test himself as well. He could sense the monumental changes within his Eternal Realm, not just the 23% progression. Most importantly, it had acquired chaotic traits, and its capabilities were yet to be fully understood. Perhaps, Doomsday Supreme would serve as a good measure.

"They want to see? Then let them watch."

Doomsday Supreme carried an air of pride. Always presenting himself as an Invincible Supreme, he was nearly unrivaled, second only to the Masters of Chaos, and he didn't mind letting other Supremes witness his power.

Lin Fei, too, was unbothered by the attention. At his current level, he faced no true threats. This duel might also give him insights into whether he could resolve the issues in the Dark Demon realm without reaching the full status of a Master of Chaos.

"Ha ha, Supreme Lin, witness the power of my Body of Chaos!" Doomsday Supreme declared as he made his move, employing the pure physical strength characteristic of his Body of Chaos.

"Boom."

With just a casual punch, Doomsday Supreme seemed to ignite the entire chaos around them. The chaotic void itself vibrated as massive waves of Chaos Qi surged toward Lin Fei with his punch.

This was no ordinary strike; in the realm of chaos, it felt like an explosion setting off, the energies unleashed were immense and far-reaching, embodying the raw power of the Body of Chaos that Doomsday Supreme now controlled.

Despite the distance between Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme, the impact of the latter's punch was suffocating for Lin Fei. It felt as if the force of the punch permeated from all directions.

This casual strike by Doomsday Supreme, amplified by Chaos Qi, was far more powerful than any punch he had delivered as an Invincible Supreme—even multiple times stronger. The power was terrifying.

A flash of understanding crossed Lin Fei's eyes. He knew that a Body of Chaos would be amplified in the realm of chaos, but the extent of this amplification was beyond his expectations.

Under such a terrifying boost, even an Invincible Supreme seemed trivial, and Lin Fei felt that even the particles of an Invincible Supreme could be annihilated by such a force.

However, Lin Fei chose not to counterattack or even defend. Instead, he stood quietly in the chaotic void, letting Doomsday Supreme's punch hit him directly. He wanted to test the defensive capabilities of his Body of Chaos.

"Boom."

Lin Fei remained immobile as the punch struck him, his Body of Chaos vibrating slightly. It seemed like it might tear apart at any moment, but Lin Fei's physical form held firm. He withstood the attack. While the erosion from the Chaos Qi was feared the most, it was insignificant to Lin Fei now, for he too was composed of the same chaotic essence.

Of course, Lin Fei was not completely unscathed. The punch, after all, was from Doomsday Supreme—a being nearly a Master of Chaos. Lin Fei's Body of Chaos did feel some impact, but it was minor.

Lin Fei inhaled slightly, and a "hum" sounded as the chaotic energy around him surged, drawing into his Body of Chaos and repairing any damage inflicted by the punch.

"Is the Body of Chaos's defense this strong?" Doomsday Supreme's eyes lit up with realization. He understood what Lin Fei was doing. If they had both still been at the Invincible Supreme level, that one punch from Doomsday Supreme could have potentially obliterated Lin Fei's physical form.

Certainly, merely bursting him apart would not annihilate Lin Fei's particles. But now, both of them embodied as Bodies of Chaos, and even Doomsday Supreme found it impossible to shatter Lin Fei's physical form.

There was but one reason for this—Lin Fei's Body of Chaos was exceedingly strong, far surpassing what Doomsday Supreme had ever imagined. Under such circumstances, posing any threat to Lin Fei was fundamentally impossible.

"Doomsday Supreme, it appears your punch cannot harm me," Lin Fei declared, a faint smile playing on his lips. "How will you fare against my punch, I wonder?"

With that, Lin Fei too raised his hand.

"Boom."

Lin Fei launched a punch towards Doomsday Supreme. This was no mere chaotic body's punch; it was infused with the power of the Eternal Realm.

To be precise, this punch unleashed the full might of the Eternal Realm through his physical form. While not his strongest tactic, which would involve transforming into the world itself and completely suppressing with the Eternal Realm, this punch still carried about seventy percent of his full power, a truly formidable force.

As the punch was thrown, Doomsday Supreme's expression drastically changed. In his eyes, the very fabric of chaos seemed altered. Vast currents of chaotic energy, even those surging from the depths of chaos, rallied behind the force of Lin Fei's punch, sweeping toward Doomsday Supreme.

For the first time, Doomsday Supreme felt a sense of danger—a mortal threat!
