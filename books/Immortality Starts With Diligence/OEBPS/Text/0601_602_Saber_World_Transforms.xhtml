602. Saber World Transforms

"Who are you, fellow Daoist? And which Supreme issued this decree?"

Lin Fei asked cautiously. He did not recognize the True Immortal before him.

As for the Supremes, how many were there in the entire Divine Realm? Lin Fei knew fewer of them.

"I am Mystical Cloud True Immortal! Under the command of the Original Yang Supreme from the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain. We are to facilitate your needs for True Monarch-level world laws."

"Original Yang Supreme?"

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. He wasn't very familiar with the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain, having visited it only once or twice. He certainly did not know Original Yang Supreme.

However, this unfamiliar Supreme was offering him great convenience, even willing to meet his needs for True Monarch-level world laws.

Lin Fei considered this. If he continued going through each True Monarch-level world, it would take a lot of time. It was more efficient to obtain a large number of world laws directly from Mystical Cloud True Immortal.

Lin Fei estimated that for his Saber World to fully transform, he might need ten world laws from True Monarch-level worlds. Seven or eight might suffice, but ten would ensure no errors.

With this in mind, Lin Fei clasped his hands in thanks and said, "Thank you, Mystical Cloud True Immortal, and thank you, Original Yang Supreme!"

Regardless of the reason Original Yang Supreme offered this convenience, Lin Fei would remember this favor, as it provided him with immense benefits.

"Ten world laws from True Monarch-level worlds? Though it's somewhat troublesome, the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain can provide them."

Mystical Cloud True Immortal waved his hand.

"Whoosh."

A series of world laws from True Monarch-level worlds fell into Lin Fei's hands. It seemed Mystical Cloud True Immortal was well-prepared, likely under Original Yang Supreme's arrangements.

"Martial Ancestor Lin, now that you have obtained ten world laws, you must not disturb the True Monarch-level worlds in our Yin-Yang Immortal Domain anymore."

Mystical Cloud True Immortal said to Lin Fei.

The Ancient God Realm was part of the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain. Otherwise, why would Mystical Cloud True Immortal appear and even attract Original Yang Supreme's attention?

"Naturally. Thank Original Yang Supreme for me."

With that, Lin Fei wasted no time, shattering the void and stepping into the spatial passage. In an instant, he vanished from sight.

The only ones left were the inhabitants of the Ancient God Realm and Azure Mountain True Immortal, each with complex expressions. Eventually, they all departed, for the situation was now clear.

Even Original Yang Supreme sought to ally with Lin Fei. What other choice did the remaining True Immortals and Martial Ancestors have?

...

In Ancient City, at Lin Fei's residence.

After traversing through the void, Lin Fei quickly returned to his abode, retreating into his secluded chamber.

"Ten world laws should be enough!"

In the chamber, Lin Fei took out the ten world laws. Given his current state, perhaps seven or eight world laws would have sufficed.

However, to ensure absolute certainty, the more world laws, the better.

Now, Lin Fei was ready.

"Let's begin, Saber World fusion!"

With Lin Fei's command.

"Boom."

The Saber World descended!

As soon as the Saber World appeared, it instantly absorbed one of the world laws.

Immediately, Lin Fei's Saber World expanded dramatically!

Lin Fei felt that his Saber World was very close to its transformation. But this was an illusion. It was like running toward a distant mountain; it appeared close but was actually far away.

Nevertheless, Lin Fei was not discouraged. He continued to fuse the world laws into the Saber World.

One, two, three, four, five...

Each world law was from a True Monarch-level world, providing a massive boost to Lin Fei's Saber World.

Especially when fused consecutively with five world laws, Lin Fei felt the transformation was imminent but still slightly out of reach. So, he continued the fusion.

Six, seven, eight, nine...

When Lin Fei fused the ninth world law, his Saber World finally began to change.

"Boom."

The ninth world law integrated into the Saber World.

At this moment, Lin Fei's Saber World vibrated violently. Suddenly, the Saber World erupted intensely within.

Lin Fei could sense that the Saber World was indeed transforming. Moreover, this transformation was fundamental and all-encompassing. But it differed significantly from the world Lin Fei had created.

The Saber World, although manifesting from the void to reality, was essentially an illusion. Even when materialized, it was not a true world. The Saber World was not inherently strong.

After all, a Martial King could evolve the Saber World. In other words, the Saber World was fundamentally at the Martial King level. Beyond the Martial King, there was no further method of cultivation for the Saber World.

Lin Fei's Saber World reached its current state through gradual and meticulous enhancement. Step by step, he elevated it. This allowed it to reach its present state, almost comparable to the True Immortal and Martial Ancestor levels. It was practically a miracle!

Of course, Lin Fei's method of enhancement was a special case. It could not be generalized.

In other words, even Lin Fei could not establish a method that would suit all cultivators evolving the Saber World. The Saber World itself was inherently imperfect.

Lin Fei was perfecting it step by step. Each step required his own exploration.

The Saber World was still undergoing transformation. Even Lin Fei did not know what it would ultimately become.

Lin Fei did not have a clear path of cultivation for the Saber World. The transformation of the Saber World was purely a result of accumulating power. It meant that Lin Fei kept increasing the power of the Saber World.

When the power reached a certain level, it naturally caused a qualitative change. With time, the changes in the Saber World became more significant. Eventually, the Saber World roared continuously, then gradually calmed down.

"Is the transformation complete?"

Lin Fei was curious about what the Saber World had become after the transformation.

It was undoubtedly different from his Eternal Realm. So, Lin Fei focused his mind.

"Saber World!"

Instantly, the Saber World descended, enveloping Lin Fei entirely. However, Lin Fei did not feel a particularly strong suppression.

"What is this? Hasn't it already transformed? After merging so many True Monarch-level world laws, the transformation should have reached the True Immortal or Martial Ancestor level. Why do I feel no suppression at all?"

Lin Fei frowned. The transformation of the Saber World was entirely different from what he had imagined.

"Eternal Realm!"

Lin Fei transformed into the Eternal Realm. He attempted to pit the Eternal Realm against the Saber World. Upon summoning the Eternal Realm, he found that the Saber World enveloped the Eternal Realm. Yet, it had no significant impact on the Eternal Realm.

"Break!"

Lin Fei unleashed the power of the Eternal Realm. Instantly, a violent force erupted, tearing through the Saber World.

"Torn apart?"

Lin Fei was even more astonished.

Was the Saber World so weak? Or had it not truly transformed? Was the transformation he sensed earlier just an illusion? Or perhaps the Saber World could not reach the True Immortal or Martial Ancestor level at all? Could its limit be the peak True Monarch level?

Many possibilities existed.

"Buzz."

Just as Lin Fei was contemplating in surprise, ready to retract the Saber World, the previously shattered Saber World instantly restored itself.

Moreover, it appeared undamaged.

"This Saber World, even the Eternal Realm couldn't break it. No, it's not that it couldn't break it, but rather the two powers are fundamentally different. The Eternal Realm cannot affect the Saber World. The Saber World is still an illusion!"

Lin Fei felt a sense of familiarity with the current Saber World. Then, a realization struck him. He immediately thought of a very familiar place.

The Great Dream City! Or rather, the current Saber World resembled a dream world!
