"Bang."

The exotic beast before Lin Fei, undoubtedly a divine-level one, bore a resemblance to a colossal elephant. However, under the onslaught of Lin Fei's thousands of particle worlds, it was no match. With just a single punch, the beast was obliterated.

This wasn't even Lin Fei at his full strength; it was merely the eruption of his thousands of particle worlds combined with his own physical prowess. Ordinary divine-level exotic beasts stood no chance against him.

"One down!"

Lin Fei's gaze remained icy as he approached a second exotic beast. This one, anticipating the attack, unleashed a terrifying stream of flames from its mouth. Lin Fei, with a casual wave of his hand, summoned a world law.

"World Law, Seal!"

Lin Fei's world law could seal anything, making a mere flame trivial. The fire was instantly sealed by his world law, and Lin Fei closed in on the exotic beast.

"Bang."

Another punch, and this second beast, too, could not withstand the attack.

"Second one down."

Lin Fei's expression was unwaveringly cold as he repeated the process. The third, fourth, fifth, sixth... every exotic beast he struck was annihilated by his overwhelming power. None could recover once obliterated. Unless possessing a clone, death was certain for them.

As for the innate abilities of the exotic beasts, Lin Fei effortlessly sealed them with his laws. He now wielded eighteen laws, more than enough for the task at hand.

Shen hadn't anticipated Lin Fei breaking the deadlock in such a brute-force manner. In its understanding, warriors who comprehended world laws typically fought using those laws, refined in their application for sealing and suppressing.

However, the number of laws one could use was limited. Thus, Shen summoned its underlings, intending to deplete Lin Fei's world laws. But unexpectedly, Lin Fei's physical strength was terrifying, seemingly even more formidable than his world laws. In this direct confrontation, not a single exotic beast could stand in his way.

This turn of events was beyond Shen's expectations. Feeling the tide of battle turn against it, Shen sensed its own demise looming. Without a second thought, it turned to flee.

Even with some exotic beasts left to defend it, Shen's decision to escape was immediate. It even considered seeking refuge with a True Monarch-level exotic beast, believing Lin Fei, despite his strength, would not dare to provoke such a formidable opponent. Shen planned to deal with Lin Fei later, after devising a strategy.

However, Lin Fei, already alert to Shen's movements, was well aware that Shen was his primary target. If Shen escaped, eliminating all other exotic beasts in the vicinity would be meaningless. Thus, seeing Shen attempting to flee, Lin Fei acted instantly.

"Think you can escape? Stay! Saber World! Eighteen Laws!"

"Boom."

Lin Fei's eighteen world laws merged with the Saber World, expanding it several-fold. Like a colossal cage, it enveloped Shen in an instant. Under Shen's command, its few remaining exotic beasts desperately tried to break through Lin Fei's Saber World. But the combined force of eighteen laws with the Saber World was not easily penetrated.

Taking advantage of this moment, Lin Fei launched his attack.

"Hum."

All particle worlds within Lin Fei erupted. This time, he held nothing back. Nineteen hundred particle worlds empowered Lin Fei's True Martial King Form, transforming him into a towering giant. His massive fist, like a hammer from the heavens, crashed down with enough force to seemingly split the earth asunder.

"No..."

Shen immediately commanded several divine-level exotic beasts to intercept Lin Fei's attack. But the force of the punch was overwhelming, obliterating the beasts in an instant. The residual force even struck Shen.

Shen, though powerful in illusion and mind control, was physically weak. This was precisely why it had controlled so many exotic beasts for protection. Faced with the eruption of Lin Fei's nineteen hundred particle worlds, with six hundred of them ten times stronger than the others, Shen stood no chance. Even a fraction of this terrifying force, nearing the power to shatter the void, was too much for Shen to bear.

"Pfft."

In the next moment, Shen's body burst apart. In such a situation, even if a single particle escaped, it could potentially recover later. However, within Lin Fei's Saber World, not even a speck of dust could escape, let alone a particle of Shen's body.

"True Fire, refine!"

Lin Fei commanded coldly. Instantly, his Saber World blazed with intense flames. This was True Fire, capable of burning everything to ashes.

"No..."

Shen's voice shrieked, its particles attempting to reform. But against the True Fire within Lin Fei's Saber World, they were powerless and turned to ash.

Shen was dead, obliterated by Lin Fei in the face of numerous divine-level exotic beasts, leaving not a single particle behind.

Shen likely had no clone, meaning its fall was absolute. Not every divine-level exotic beast possesses the ability to create clones. While beings like Taotie might have clones, it didn't imply Shen had one as well. Lin Fei could distinctly feel Shen's demise.

"Wait, Shen is dead, completely annihilated. Where do we find Shen's bloodline now?"

Suddenly, Heart Demon Martial God voiced his concern.

Caught up in the moment, the Heart Demon Martial God had only been spectating. Now, he remembered that his goal was Shen's bloodline and heart. With Shen's particles gone, how could he obtain Shen's bloodline?

"Who says it's gone?"

Lin Fei extended his hand, revealing a heart that was still beating, though sealed and suppressed by his world laws. He had secured Shen's heart early on, ensuring that Shen could never be revived as long as the heart was intact.

"Fortunately, you didn't completely annihilate Shen," the Heart Demon Martial God sighed in relief.

"Shen's heart is right here, and you can extract Shen's bloodline from it. But can you suppress Shen's heart? Without my laws to constrain it, it will immediately recover."

Lin Fei faced a dilemma regarding Shen's regeneration. Even a single particle could enable Shen to revive. What could the Heart Demon Martial God use to suppress Shen and ensure it didn't recover? If his strength was insufficient, he wouldn't even be able to suppress a drop of blood from a divine-level exotic beast.

The Heart Demon Martial God smiled, "Lin, I have made preparations. This is a sealing bottle, crafted by a Dao Integration True Immortal. It can suppress and seal the flesh and blood of Martial Gods and avatars of Deity Transformation experts, eliminating any chance of revival."

"Sealing bottle?" Lin Fei was surprised. He handed over Shen's heart to the Heart Demon Martial God, who placed it in the sealing bottle. A miraculous force immediately suppressed and sealed the heart, rendering it completely immobile. The Heart Demon Martial God had indeed prepared thoroughly, even considering this detail.

"You mentioned a juvenile Shen nearby, right?" Lin Fei said

"It should be around here. Keep looking, Lin."

Lin Fei closed his eyes, his Saber World rapidly expanding, beginning to sense all life within Shen's territory.
