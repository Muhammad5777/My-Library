650. Sweeping Away the Dark Demons!

Lin Fei shook his head and said, "I am not yet a Supreme. I remain merely a Martial Ancestor. However, since my Eternal Realm has transformed into a True Immortal-level world, it seems much stronger. Now, even an ordinary Supreme can be easily crushed. The Dark Demon Lord is no match for me in the Divine Realm."

The words of Lin Fei made the Azure Emperor Supreme and others nod slightly.

"Hahaha, then this is indeed great news. Our efforts to gather the essence of the world for you were indeed correct!" the Black Tortoise Supreme said with a laugh.

In fact, there was another important reason— the prototype of the realm. Without obtaining the realm's prototype, two years would not have allowed Lin Fei's Eternal Realm to transform. If it could not evolve into a True Immortal-level world, Lin Fei might not necessarily be a match for the Dark Demon Lord.

Thus, the realm prototype was also quite crucial.

"Lin Fei, are you truly intending to go to the Dark Demon Domain?" asked the Azure Emperor Supreme.

Indeed, Lin Fei had suppressed so many Dark Demon Domain Supremes without rushing to kill them, which led the Azure Emperor Supreme to speculate.

Lin Fei nodded and said, "I must visit the Dark Demon Domain! But rest assured, Azure Emperor Supreme, I will not recklessly venture into danger. I just want to know the exact location of the Dark Demon Domain.

As long as I know the specific location of the Dark Demon Domain, it poses a threat to the Dark Demon Lord. He cannot kill me outside, yet I can continue to grow. In time, even within the Dark Demon Domain, I will be able to slay him. Time is on my side, not on him."

"I must also make the Dark Demon Lord feel the constant threat looming over him," Lin Fei said with an icy tone, even filled with murderous intent. He had no fondness for the Dark Demon Lord. If possible, he wished to slay the Dark Demon Lord!

This visit to the Dark Demon Domain was also to see what it was truly like. If there was a chance, he would slay the Dark Demon Lord! If not, he would at least intimidate him.

"Before I go, I will first sweep away those Dark Demons from the Yin-Yang Immortal Realm," Lin Fei declared. He had never considered releasing those Dark Demons.

Moreover, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm also needed their flesh essence. Let the flesh essence of those Dark Demons nourish the life of the Eternal Realm.

With that, Lin Fei stepped forward, already traversing space, vanishing without a trace.

Yin-Yang Immortal Realm, Dark Demon stronghold.

At this moment, many Dark Demons were extremely excited. Even in secret, they were prepared. After all, they knew the Dark Demon Lord had returned. Soon, they would overcome the obstructive force of the Divine Realm. Then, they could embark on a killing spree, breaking free from the Yin-Yang Immortal Realm.

"I wonder if the Lord has dealt with Lin Fei and the Supremes of the Divine Realm yet?"

"This time, after the Lord returned to the Dark Demon Domain, he surely must have been fully confident in his ability to resolve the Divine Realm's Supremes. Just be patient a bit longer, perhaps news will arrive soon."

"Right, with the Lord leading the team, could dozens of supremes fail to handle the obstructive forces of the Divine Realm?"

"Just wait a little longer..."

When Lin Fei emerged from the spatial passageway, he saw the stronghold of the Dark Demon Domain still in peace, even preparing for further "invasions" into other parts of the Divine Realm. Each of the Dark Demons was brimming with combat fervor.

But what was the actual situation?

The Dark Demon Lord had been defeated, and dozens of supremes were slain by Lin Fei. This also indicated one thing—that the Dark Demon Lord had never returned to the stronghold. He hadn't even considered these Dark Demons important at all. He didn't care in the slightest, not even if they were all annihilated.

Hence, he had not informed these Dark Demons in advance, nor had he stayed; he had simply left the Divine Realm and returned to the Dark Demon Domain.

As a result, these Dark Demons were left without any news, still desperately waiting for the Dark Demon Lord's "triumphant return." But was that even possible?

Lin Fei shook his head slightly. Then, he flew over the stronghold.

"Halt, who are you?"

"It looks like Lin Fei from the Divine Realm!"

"What, him? But wasn't Lin Fei supposed to have been slain by our Ruler, who led the charge? How come Lin Fei is still appearing here?"

"Where is the Lord? And the other Supremes? Why hasn't there been any news? Could it be..."

Suddenly, many Dark Demons finally "awoke" to the reality. They seemed to realize something, their expressions filled with immense fear. They stared fixedly at Lin Fei.

"Lin Fei, where is our Lord of the Dark Demon Domain?"

"Do you mean the Dark Demon Lord? Perhaps the Dark Demon Lord, in his disgraceful flight from the Divine Realm, forgot to notify you.

Actually, it might not be that he forgot, but rather that he simply didn't care about you at all. You all have been abandoned by the Dark Demon Lord, turned into expendables!"

Lin Fei stated emphatically, each word striking with certainty.

Instantly, many of the Dark Demons seemed unable to believe their ears. Faces contorted in terror and anger, they yelled at Lin Fei, "No, it's impossible, absolutely impossible! How could our glorious Ruler be defeated by you, let alone flee?"

"Indeed, you must be trying to shake our resolve and confidence. You will not sway our faith!"

"When the Lord arrives, you will undoubtedly die!"

"The Divine Realm will surely be conquered by us!"

Looking at these Dark Demons, they simply did not believe it. Or perhaps, they did not want to believe it. Lin Fei could not be bothered to explain further.

He took a deep breath and with a thought, "Boom."

Suddenly, Lin Fei transformed into the Eternal Realm. The vast Eternal Realm expanded wildly in all directions.

Eventually, it even enveloped the entire Yin-Yang Immortal Realm. The Eternal Realm was like a "giant maw," swallowing the Yin-Yang Immortal Realm whole.

Meanwhile, the Dark Demons stared down the Eternal Realm as if facing a formidable enemy.

"Kill!"

The Dark Demons, sensing the threat, erupted with the power of Dark Demons, attempting to contend with Lin Fei's Eternal Realm to stop it from "sweeping" through the Yin-Yang Immortal Realm.

However, it was futile. Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, grand and overwhelming, crushed everything in its path. Any attempts to block the advance of the Eternal Realm were directly crushed.

Those Dark Demons drawn into the Eternal Realm plunged into Chaos and madness.

"We are really going to be wiped out by Lin Fei!"

"Lord, where have you gone? Could it be, has my Dark Demon Domain truly been defeated?"

"How could dozens of Supremes, along with the Ruler, fail?"

"But if not, would Lin Fei dare so brazenly attack our stronghold in the Dark Demon Domain?"

"Moreover, there has been no word from the Ruler. Not even a single supreme has been heard from. Perhaps, the Ruler and all the supremes have truly been defeated..."

Many Dark Demons seemed to come to this realization. If the Supremes and the Dark Demon Lord had indeed been defeated, thoroughly at that, then they were in danger.

With a "hum," the Eternal Realm continued to expand rapidly, and a terrifying force descended. All Dark Demons felt as if the apocalypse had come. One by one, they were obliterated. No matter how special their methods, it was all the same. They could not escape the annihilation of the Eternal Realm.
