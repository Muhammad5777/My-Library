670. A Direct Confrontation with the Dark Demon Domain!

"Search the Chaos. Search Kunyu City. At all costs, the Supreme of Doomsday must be found." The Dark Demon Lord stated coldly.

"Yes, Your Majesty."

The remaining Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain dared not say more. They responded with immediate deference and promptly departed.

The Dark Demon Lord's ruthlessness was not without reason. As long as the Dark Demon Domain existed, Supremes would inevitably arise.

Thus, slaughtering some Supremes was hardly a matter of consequence. But, should he perish or should the Dark Demon Domain be annihilated, all would be too late. The Supreme of Doomsday had once slain a top Supreme. He possessed the power to kill a top Supreme, heralded as one of the strongest entities beneath the Master of Chaos.

The Dark Demon Lord conceived a plan to deal with Lin Fei— to invite an existence more formidable than Lin Fei to execute him.

The one who was more formidable than Lin Fei could only be the Supreme of Doomsday. After all, Lin Fei was comparable to a top Supreme. Only a being like the Supreme of Doomsday could possibly slay Lin Fei.

Yet, the Supreme of Doomsday was elusive. The Dark Demon Lord himself had sought after him but to no avail. Therefore, he dispatched all the Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain to search.

To date, they had yet to uncover any clues. If the Supreme of Doomsday could not be found, the Dark Demon Lord would be unable to confront Lin Fei.

Should too much time pass—It has been but three years. What of thirty years or even three hundred years from now?

The Dark Demon Lord even believed that Lin Fei might one day become the Master of Chaos. Should that time come, his demise would be certain. Even the Dark Demon Domain might utterly collapse.

Not to mention becoming the Master of Chaos, once he became a terrifying existence like the Supreme of Doomsday, even a Realm Ruler could be slain.

Thus, the Dark Demon Lord felt a profound sense of crisis. He felt as though a critical moment was imminent. He must slay Lin Fei at all costs.

A low hum resounded.

Just then, a figure appeared instantaneously before the Dark Demon Lord. Upon seeing this figure, the Dark Demon Lord's eyes widened in shock.

"You... Lin Fei? How could you enter the Dark Demon Domain so silently? Impossible, this simply cannot be!"

The Dark Demon Lord was astounded. Not merely at seeing Lin Fei once again, but at the fact that Lin Fei had reached the Dark Demon Domain undetected by him. Not a trace of his presence had been sensed.

This was highly irregular. After all, he was the Ruler of the Dark Demon Domain. Any minute alteration within the Dark Demon Domain was usually clear to him as day.

Yet here stood Lin Fei, a supreme being of the highest order, right before him, and he had only just noticed. The implications were crystal clear to the Dark Demon Lord.

"Dark Demon Lord, we meet again. There is nothing impossible, for I am constantly advancing, and improving at every moment. And you, you have ceased to advance. It appears you have not progressed even slightly in three years."

Lin Fei shook his head. Seeing the state of the Dark Demon Lord, he knew that he remained just as he was three years ago. No advancement whatsoever.

Moreover, Lin Fei had also heard some rumors. The Dark Demon Lord was seeking the Doomsday Supreme to counter him.

Doomsday Supreme was a name unfamiliar to Lin Fei. But judging by the demeanor of the Dark Demon Lord, he seemed certain that the Doomsday Supreme could challenge him.

This suggested that the Doomsday Supreme indeed possessed the power to confront a supreme being of the highest caliber. Such was the strategy of the Dark Demon Lord. To seek out another, even more formidable Supreme, to contend against Lin Fei.

As long as Lin Fei remained unresolved, so too would his peace be disturbed. The Dark Demon Lord would exhaust every method to oppose Lin Fei.

"What method did you use to evade my detection?" the Dark Demon Lord asked, his voice tinged with a trace of fear. He had never encountered such a situation before. Lin Fei's strength was indeed formidable. It was comparable even to the highest Supreme.

Yet even the most powerful Supreme did not have the means to appear so silently before him. But now, Lin Fei had done just that. What manner of technique could this be?

A slight smile touched the corners of Lin Fei's mouth. Indeed, his method was quite simple. Following the transformation of his Eternal Realm, it had become integrated with the Void, complementing and even deceiving the Dark Demon Domain.

Thus, the Dark Demon Domain could not sense Lin Fei's Eternal Realm presence at all. By mimicking the life essence of the Dark Demon Domain with the Void, Lin Fei remained undetected.

After all, the Dark Demon Domain did not have an absolute ruler—only a Dark Demon Lord who could not entirely control every aspect of his realm. As long as the Dark Demon Domain did not raise an alarm, the Dark Demon Lord remained oblivious. It was only when Lin Fei appeared right before him that the Dark Demon Lord felt astonishment.

"Having said so much," Lin Fei continued, "Dark Demon Lord, I have come to the Dark Demon Domain again to test something to see if you truly are a ruler."

A fierce glint flashed through Lin Fei's eyes.

"Boom."

In the next moment, Lin Fei's light of eternity swept up the Dark Demon Lord, pulling him into the Eternal Realm.

"Bang."

The Dark Demon Lord didn't even utter a word before being crushed to dust by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Dispatching the Dark Demon Lord posed no challenge for the current Lin Fei—not the slightest difficulty. His true concern was whether he could permanently vanquish the Dark Demon Lord.

Within Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, the Lord was reduced to dust. Yet, Lin Fei did not feel particularly elated. He looked up into the void. He could sense that the Dark Demon Lord had not perished. The Lord was still there.

"Hum."

Indeed, from the void, another form of the Dark Demon Lord swiftly coalesced. Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. This new form had appeared out of nowhere and was indeed the body of the Dark Demon Lord. Despite being turned to dust in Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, it had been of no real consequence.

"Lin Fei, I am the Ruler, and you cannot kill me in the Dark Demon Domain!" the Lord declared, his face grim and his tone icy.

Though he could not kill Lin Fei, the Dark Demon Lord felt no joy. Because he could do nothing against Lin Fei. Three years ago, when Lin Fei first arrived in the Dark Demon Domain, the Lord at least had the power to retaliate.

But now?

The Dark Demon Lord had no power to fight back. He had already tried to mobilize the power of the Dark Demon Domain but to no avail. Beside Lin Fei, there seemed to be an invisible force field.

No matter how the Dark Demon Lord summoned the power of the Dark Demon Domain, he could not affect Lin Fei. And thus, Lin Fei obliterated his body time and again. How could the Dark Demon Lord possibly feel joy?

"Is that so? Unable to kill you? Then let Lme witness just how formidable the Dark Demon Domain truly is."

Lin Fei took a deep breath. He no longer targeted the Dark Demon Lord directly. It was clear to him that the Dark Demon Lord was indestructible. At least within the confines of the Dark Demon Domain. Only by resolving the entire Dark Demon Domain could the Lord truly perish.

"Hum."

In the next moment, Lin Fei transformed into his Eternal Realm. The Eternal Realm radiated an endless light, sweeping majestically in all directions. Its expanse continued to grow.

Ten percent, twenty percent, thirty percent, forty percent, fifty percent...

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was vast, enormously so. When it expanded to its fullest, it covered nearly half of the Dark Demon Domain. Yes, half the realm. Lin Fei's Eternal Realm could envelop half of the Chaos Realm!

This sufficiently demonstrated the immense scale of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Not to be underestimated. Yet, the Eternal Realm could not completely encompass the Dark Demon Domain. It could only cover half.

However, this did not hinder Lin Fei's use of the Eternal Realm's power. He intended to confront the Dark Demon Domain head-on with the force of the Eternal Realm.

"Be annihilated."

Lin Fei uttered softly.

Instantly, the areas of the Dark Demon Domain that were enveloped began to collapse!
