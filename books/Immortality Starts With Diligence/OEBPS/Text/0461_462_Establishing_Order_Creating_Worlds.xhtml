462. Establishing Order, Creating Worlds!

Lin Fei summoned the system panel to review his current status.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Martial Gods (Thunder Tribulation not yet crossed)

Technique: Four Symbols and Five Elements Creation Technique (Perfected)

Indeed, Lin Fei's martial arts realm was that of a Martial God, yet he had not yet crossed any thunder tribulation. Additionally, a new technique appeared - the Four Symbols and Five Elements Creation Technique, already perfected.

This technique wasn't found in the Dubhe Dao Artifact; in fact, no techniques were left in it. It primarily contained methods and insights for crossing tribulations, but no actual techniques. At the Martial Gods stage, reliance on personal comprehension becomes paramount since every Martial God comprehends different laws and faces unique tribulation methods. What technique could be universally applicable to all Martial Gods?

This technique was Lin Fei's own creation, tailored to his unique traits. It combined his Four Symbols and Five Elements bloodlines to create worlds. Since it was his invention, it naturally reached completion immediately.

However, whether it was effective or how effective it remained to be seen, Lin Fei wasn't sure. However, its presence on the system panel suggested that, theoretically, it was viable.

Lin Fei had been spending his time with Feng Ling and hadn't been actively cultivating. Sometimes, he would have brilliant ideas in his mind. Initially, the idea of creating a world in each particle of his body was a wild fantasy.

But as Lin Fei pondered more deeply, he seriously considered making it a reality, even devising a technique for it. Significantly, the system panel reflected Lin Fei's imaginative concept.

In Lin Fei's vision, a space with earth, fire, water, wind, and the five elements of metal, wood, water, fire, and earth could constitute a world. It was a simple and straightforward concept, yet it seemed feasible enough to try.

This path was uniquely suited to Lin Fei, given his Four Symbols and Five Elements bloodlines. Other Martial Gods seeking advancement would need to follow their own paths. This was Lin Fei's Dao!

Taking a deep breath, Lin Fei focused all his attention and spirit into his body, particularly on a single particle. This particle was not specially chosen but randomly selected. With countless particles in his body, losing one to failure was inconsequential, allowing Lin Fei to experiment boldly.

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei concentrated all his attention on a single particle. He then extended a strand of the law's power into this particle. With a burst, the power of the law exploded within, creating a space inside the particle.

Particles are incredibly resilient and not easily destroyed. Even if damaged, they can quickly recover, and this particle was no exception. Lin Fei's small explosion of law did not exceed the particle's limit, allowing it to recover quickly but leaving a significant space inside.

"Earth, Fire, Water, Wind - the Four Symbols!"

"Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth - the Five Elements!"

Using the power of the law as a foundation, Lin Fei quickly introduced the elements of the Four Symbols and the Five Elements into the particle's space. This combination formed the basic conditions for a world's existence.

As Lin Fei released the elements of the Four Symbols and the Five Elements within the particle space, he observed the space trembling, with various elements rampantly colliding and overwhelming each other.

Then, in the next moment, the particle annihilated. The Four Symbols and the Five Elements seemed chaotic and unable to support the entire particle space.

"It failed," Lin Fei frowned. Losing a particle was inconsequential for him, but this failure was a setback. It wasn't guaranteed to succeed just because the technique was recognized by the system. In fact, it was not easy at all.

"The Four Symbols and Five Elements should be correct. The particle space was also successfully created. But why did the particle annihilate?" Lin Fei pondered deeply. He thought about the world he lived in and the small world where the Phoenix resided. What was different?

Upon reflection, Lin Fei realized that the Phoenix's small world wasn't much different from the Ancient Continent he inhabited. The Phoenix's world was also full of greenery, with a clear blue sky and gentle breezes. The ancient land was similar, featuring mountains, rivers, skies, and more.

"Ah? Wait, are there mountains and rivers? And moreover, flames that can scorch everything. Water flows to the lowest point. The wind whispers through the void. Indeed, these are the elements I took for granted in my daily life. In the Phoenix's microcosm, these commonplace elements are, in fact, the most crucial. Why do my particles annihilate?

Earth, fire, water, wind, metal, wood, water, fire, earth - these elements forge the mountains, rivers, and all else I speak of. But they lack one essential element. And that is Order! Water flowing downward is Order. The wind blowing in the air, the land under our feet, and so forth, all represent Order. With Order, the world can be stable."

Lin Fei suddenly saw the light. He understood. Everything became clear. Order is the key. Otherwise, chaos reigns.

The four symbols and the five elements are unbound by Order. How could they then support the entire world? They would only lead to the world's collapse.

Therefore, Order is of utmost importance. And Order requires Lin Fei, the 'Divinely Appointed Creator'.

Even more, Lin Fei had a brilliant idea. If the Order he 'Divinely Appointed' differed from the outside world, or if he altered it slightly, then perhaps the development of the entire world would be radically different.

This thought greatly intrigued Lin Fei. Of course, he would experiment with this slowly in the future. But for now, he proceeded methodically. First, successfully carving out a stable world was crucial.

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei then exerted a trace of the law's power.

"Boom".

Particles exploded within. A vast space emerged.

Then, Lin Fei deployed the Four Symbols and Five Elements. As the Creator of the World, Lin Fei established Order.

For instance, the mutual generation and overcoming of the five phases. For instance, some fundamental truths between heaven and earth.

Water flows to the lowest point. Heaven above, earth below, and so on. Many of the most basic Orders.

As Lin Fei set these Orders. Soon, the Four Symbols and Five Elements dispersed throughout the entire space.

"Rumble".

The entire particle space continued to tremble. Yet, there was not a hint of collapse. On the contrary, it gradually stabilized.

Of course, this was just stabilization. To create life, that was far more challenging. That's another issue.

But Lin Fei did not dwell on the issue of life. He only cared whether the world was stable or not.

One day, two days, three days...

As time passed, the first particle world was created by Lin Fei. Finally stabilized completely. No longer showing any signs of collapse.

"It's done! The world I've created! Though devoid of life. With the Four Symbols and Five Elements, over time, it might even possess the conditions necessary for the birth of life. In the future, becoming a world teeming with life is not impossible!"

Lin Fei's heart swelled with immense joy. At this moment, he truly felt like a god. Moreover, a god of creation!
