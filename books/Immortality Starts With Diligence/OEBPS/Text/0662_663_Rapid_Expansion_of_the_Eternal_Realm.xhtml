663. Rapid Expansion of the Eternal Realm!

"Boom."

Lin Fei ensnared the body of the Chaotic Behemoth within his Eternal Realm. Simultaneously, the power of the Eternal Realm relentlessly crushed down on the behemoth, intent on utterly obliterating it.

"Roar..."

However, the Chaotic Behemoth struggled continuously, attempting to break free from Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. This Chaotic Behemoth was indeed a top-tier one. Lin Fei had slain several Chaotic Behemoths before, but those once caught within the Eternal Realm were typically suppressed without resistance.

Any struggle? It was futile, merely the throes of the dying. Lin Fei didn't need to concern himself with them. But this Chaotic Behemoth was different.

"Pfft."

Its claw viciously tore through the void of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, instantly ripping a tear in it.

Lin Fei's heart shook slightly!

He knew this Chaotic Behemoth was strong, a top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, comparable to a top-tier Supreme. But to be this powerful was beyond anything Lin Fei had seen before.

Remember, this was the Eternal Realm—a realm that had slain Chaotic Behemoths. Yet, it couldn't withstand a single claw from the behemoth before him.

But then, Lin Fei's expression turned icy. He took a deep breath.

"Once you've entered my Eternal Realm, it's not so easy to leave! Eternal Realm, explode!"

Lin Fei mobilized all the power of the Eternal Realm. At that moment, explosions began within the realm—a collapse of the void itself. The void in every direction completely collapsed, making escape impossible.

This was a maneuver of the Eternal Realm. Such an explosion, no Supreme could withstand. Even a Chaotic Behemoth would be pulverized.

Now, this Chaotic Behemoth was no exception. As Lin Fei's Eternal Realm exploded in this manner, caught off guard, the vast body of the Chaotic Behemoth was obliterated by about one-tenth.

But this was not the end. It was merely the beginning.

Afterward, time and again, the Eternal Realm continuously exploded.

The Chaotic Behemoth was enraged. It roared furiously, wreaking havoc within the Eternal Realm, attempting to escape. Yet, every time it managed to breach the Eternal Realm, another explosion would follow, obliterating a part of its body.

Ultimately, the Chaotic Behemoth had no choice but to cease its attempts. It could not safely escape from the Eternal Realm.

Of course, completely obliterating a Chaotic Behemoth was difficult. Lin Fei was essentially in a war of attrition with the behemoth. He kept the explosions coming.

However, this had a significant impact on the Eternal Realm itself, even risking its collapse. The cost was enormous, but Lin Fei was determined to slay the Chaotic Behemoth and extract its essence.

Even if the opponent was a top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, Lin Fei was resolved to try, whatever the cost.

Time passed slowly. Lin Fei's Eternal Realm became increasingly unstable, and the Chaotic Behemoth was also suffering greatly. Nearly half of its body had been obliterated. Now, it was a battle of desperation, each side continuously depleting the other.

After a long struggle, the Chaotic Behemoth was the first to speak, "If we continue this way, we will both die!"

"You will die, I won't!" Lin Fei retorted.

"You lunatic..." The Chaotic Behemoth was frantic. It was a top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, a sovereign in the chaos, who could usually rest easy. Even in its slumber, no one dared to confront it. But who could have anticipated encountering someone as mad as Lin Fei? It was sheer insanity. It seemed like a fight to the death.

Yet, the Chaotic Behemoth couldn't recall any deep grudge or significant enmity that would warrant such a relentless battle. Was such a desperate struggle necessary?

The woman within the Eternal Realm also witnessed this scene. She now felt somewhat reckless. Perhaps she shouldn't have brought Lin Fei here so directly, to engage in a deadly battle with a top-tier Chaotic Behemoth. If Lin Fei couldn't hold on, she too would die. But now, she could no longer stop the proceedings.

As time went on, Lin Fei continued as before. His Eternal Realm gradually stabilized, simply because the Chaotic Behemoth could no longer withstand it. The destructive power of the Chaotic Behemoth gradually weakened, and its body was nearly completely obliterated.

50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, 90%...

The body of the Chaotic Behemoth rapidly disintegrated, from being half-destroyed to more than ninety percent obliterated. And it continued to vanish.

What destructive power could such a diminished Chaotic Behemoth still possess?

For a moment, fear gripped the Chaotic Behemoth.

"Do not kill me," it pleaded. "I can submit to you. I can serve you forever. I am a top-tier Chaotic Behemoth; I will be of great help to you. Just please spare my life!"

The Chaotic Behemoth was afraid. It was at a disadvantage and ultimately could not outlast Lin Fei. As it begged Lin Fei for mercy, Lin Fei simply shook his head and said, "A top-tier Chaotic Behemoth is indeed rare. Unfortunately, I do not need a living Chaotic Behemoth. What I need is the essence of flesh! So, it is best for you to die."

"Boom."

As Lin Fei's words fell, all the power of the Eternal Realm surged toward the remaining tenth of the Chaotic Behemoth's body.

"No..." The Chaotic Behemoth screamed in agony, its voice filled with unwillingness.

But its reluctance changed nothing. It was defeated, utterly and completely. Defeat meant paying a price, and for the Chaotic Behemoth, that price was its life.

"Pfft." The last vestige of the Chaotic Behemoth's body was obliterated.

A vast amount of flesh essence filled Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, causing it to rapidly advance. Previously, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm had progressed to 3%. Now, Lin Fei could clearly feel a significant increase in progress.

4%, 5%, 6%, 7%, 8%...

The body of this Chaotic Behemoth far exceeded Lin Fei's expectations. The effect was extraordinarily beneficial, much better than the results from several Chaotic Behemoths Lin Fei had consumed.

"System." Lin Fei thought, immediately accessing the system panel to check:

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Martial Ancestor

World Level: True Immortal-level World (Progress 9%)

Illusory Realm: Supreme Level

Lin Fei observed his world progress: from 3% to now 9%. The difference was merely from slaying a single top-tier Chaotic Behemoth. The essence of one top-tier Chaotic Behemoth had skyrocketed his progress by 6%. It was incredible!

Indeed, the more powerful the Chaotic Behemoth, the greater the benefit to Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

"Only 1% away from 10% progress," he thought. "One more Chaotic Behemoth should suffice to reach that milestone. Once I reach 10% progress, there will be a minor leap in the strength of the Eternal Realm."

Lin Fei made up his mind to find another Chaotic Behemoth before returning to the Divine Realm. After all, achieving a 10% increase was a notable accomplishment. He couldn't return to the Divine Realm without gaining anything significant.

However, the immediate priority was to fulfill his promise. Although he had slain the Chaotic Behemoth, he had yet to address the matter of the three Supremes the woman wanted dead.

"Swipe."

Lin Fei transported the woman out.

The woman, already aware that Lin Fei had slain the top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, looked somewhat conflicted.

"Alright, I've obtained what I needed from the Chaotic Behemoth and achieved my goal," Lin Fei declared. "Now, it's time for me to keep my promise. Lead the way to where those three Supremes are located. I will resolve this matter as well!"

Lin Fei's words jolted the woman. Without hesitation, she led Lin Fei, flying towards the depths of chaos.
