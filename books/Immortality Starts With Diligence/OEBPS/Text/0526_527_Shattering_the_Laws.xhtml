527. Shattering the Laws!

When power reaches a certain magnitude, can it break or even annihilate laws? Many have pondered this question, their doubts stemming from the fact that their own strength has not reached a level capable of contending with such fundamental laws. Now, Lin Fei was attempting just that.

The power of his two thousand six hundred Particle Worlds, combined with the strength of his body, enhanced by absorbing countless world origins, was unleashed in full force. His punch, directed at the ubiquitous laws of the void, caused a visible rupture.

Everyone witnessed as Lin Fei's fist shattered the void of the minor world—a feat not overly surprising given Lin Fei's current level of power and the relative fragility of a minor world's void compared to the Divine Realm.

However, in Lin Fei's perspective, his punch did more than just shatter the void. It primarily targeted the laws! The omnipresent laws of the world were smashed under his fist, causing not only the void to break but also the many threads connected to these laws to fracture. The breaking of these threads had far-reaching effects.

"Boom!"

The entire Heavenly Secret Realm began to tremble slightly. Those who were watching Lin Fei were utterly astounded by this development.

"Has Lin Fei's punch actually shaken the entire Heavenly Secret Realm?" the onlookers marveled in disbelief. "The Heavenly Secret Realm, stronger even than the True Spirit Realm, has resisted the assaults of dozens of Martial Gods and Deity Transformation powerhouses. Yet, Lin Fei has managed to impact it."

"Indeed, he is worthy of the title 'Number One Martial God of the Divine Realm.' Such strength is unfathomable. Is he really not a True Monarch?" someone speculated.

"He can't be a True Monarch; otherwise, how could he enter the Heavenly Secret Realm? If a True Monarch entered, the realm would instantly collapse."

"But what is Lin Fei's purpose in doing this? Is he trying to intimidate us?"

"It's unlikely to be mere intimidation. With his reputation, just entering the Heavenly Secret Realm is intimidating enough. He doesn't need to resort to such actions. Lin Fei must be attempting to accelerate the collapse of the Heavenly Secret Realm."

"What? Accelerate its collapse? How is that even possible? Can he, alone, hasten the collapse of the Heavenly Secret Realm?"

The crowd was astounded. Accelerating the collapse of the Heavenly Secret Realm meant breaking a minor world with one's power alone. True Monarchs could achieve this, but could a Martial God do the same?

The Heavenly Secret Realm had been contested by countless Martial Gods and Deity Transformation figures for thousands of years without showing signs of collapse. Could Lin Fei alone cause its downfall?

Despite Lin Fei's reputation as the Number One Martial God of the Divine Realm, a slayer of Deity Transformations and Martial Gods, and a dominant force in his own right, many found it hard to believe that he could accelerate the collapse of the Heavenly Secret Realm with his singular effort.

However, Lin Fei paid no mind to the speculations of the Martial Gods and Deity Transformation figures in the Heavenly Secret Realm. His focus was entirely on the laws within the void. Having exerted all his strength in that punch, he shattered the laws and severed the connecting threads. The Heavenly Secret Realm trembled and roared in response.

To the onlookers, it might have seemed like nothing significant had changed. But Lin Fei noticed that, with the depletion of those laws, the operation of the Heavenly Secret Realm began to falter ever so slightly.

The effect was minimal now due to the limited number of laws that had been annihilated. But Lin Fei understood that once a substantial number of laws were destroyed, the impact on the realm would become unmistakably evident.

Resolved, Lin Fei ceased his hesitation and unleashed his formidable strength.

"Boom, boom, boom!"

Time and again, he struck out with the power of his physical body, his two thousand six hundred Particle Worlds relentlessly bombarding the laws of the Heavenly Secret Realm.

Each strike shattered and annihilated a portion of the realm's laws, causing continuous tremors and roars throughout the Heavenly Secret Realm. The once-stable realm could no longer find peace. Its myriad native beings trembled in fear as if facing an apocalyptic scenario.

The Martial Gods and Deity Transformation powerhouses, initially puzzled, gradually grew solemn as they witnessed the unfolding events. After about a day of Lin Fei's relentless assault, he finally paused. By then, many laws of the Heavenly Secret Realm had collapsed under his relentless attacks, and the realm began to destabilize.

A faint aura of destruction pervaded the Heavenly Secret Realm, and everyone could sense its impending doom. It wouldn't take more than a month, perhaps even less, for the realm to completely collapse.

This development shocked many Martial Gods and Deity Transformation powerhouses. Lin Fei had indeed accomplished it—single-handedly pushing the Heavenly Secret Realm to the brink of collapse.

"Martial God Lin really did it! By himself, he's bringing about the collapse of the Heavenly Secret Realm. Just how powerful is he? Could he truly rival a True Monarch?"

"Unbelievable. The Heavenly Secret Realm, a mighty world, where dozens, even hundreds of Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators, have schemed for thousands of years without hastening its collapse."

"And now, with Lin Fei's arrival, the realm is nearing its end. Is this our opportunity?"

"Opportunity? Maybe, maybe not. It depends on Lin Fei's next move. Remember, in the True Spirit Realm and Heavenly Principle Realm, he drove everyone away and dominated those worlds alone."

Amidst these discussions, many Martial Gods and Deity Transformation powerhouses began to ponder their strategies. Once the Heavenly Secret Realm collapses, what should they do?

Should they contest at all costs for the world's origin, or withdraw from this turbulent situation? After all, Lin Fei was not known for his leniency or tolerance towards others.

Observing the situation in the Heavenly Secret Realm, Lin Fei could tell that the realm was teeming with an aura of destruction. And on the brink of collapse, it might hold up for about a month. But if he continued to obliterate its laws, it wouldn't last even that long.

However, Lin Fei chose to halt his assault. He didn't wish to pave the way for others to benefit – essentially, he wasn't willing to 'make the wedding dress for someone else.'

The Heavenly Secret Realm still needed to be 'cleared.' To claim all of its world origins, he had to drive away every other Martial God and Deity Transformation.

With this in mind, Lin Fei looked towards the depths of the void and declared, "You all have three days to leave the Heavenly Secret Realm. In three days, I will sweep through the realm. Any Martial God or Deity Transformation who remains will be eliminated without exception!"

His voice wasn't loud, but he used a special power to ensure it resonated throughout the entire Heavenly Secret Realm. All Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators received Lin Fei's stern warning. Their expressions turned grim. Although they had anticipated some action from Lin Fei upon his arrival, the direct threat still made them uneasy.

"Indeed, just like in the Heavenly Principle Realm, Lin Fei intends to drive us all out and dominate the Heavenly Secret Realm alone!"

"Is Lin Fei planning to stand against all the powers of the Divine Realm? He does have the backing of the Ancient Covenant, and these major powers usually don't interfere in disputes among Martial Gods and Deity Transformations."

"Remember the fate of those two Deity Transformations in the Heavenly Principle Realm. We can't afford to get involved in this mess."

With these considerations, many decided to leave the Heavenly Secret Realm. After all, even though the world origins were valuable, they were not worth risking their lives over. Facing Lin Fei's formidable strength, the wisest course of action was to avoid confrontation and leave the Heavenly Secret Realm.
