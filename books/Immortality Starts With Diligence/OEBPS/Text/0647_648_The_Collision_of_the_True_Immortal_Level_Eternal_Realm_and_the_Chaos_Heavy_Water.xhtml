648. The Collision of the True Immortal Level Eternal Realm and the Chaos Heavy Water!

The sealing power of the Sphere of Demonic Clouds was truly terrifying, originating from the Chaos Heavy Water.

The Dark Demon Lord did not believe that any Supreme could contend with the Chaos Heavy Water. Perhaps only a Ruler within their own domain could block the Chaos Heavy Water.

But certainly not Lin Fei. Within the Divine Realm, no one could withstand the Chaos Heavy Water.

"Boom."

At last, Lin Fei's punch collided fiercely with the Sphere of Demonic Clouds. The Sphere of Demonic Clouds, containing a vast amount of Chaos Heavy Water, was inherently extremely heavy.

The strength it possessed was unimaginably immense. Not to mention its sealing power.

A faint glow emerged from within the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, falling directly onto Lin Fei.

Instantly, Lin Fei's whole body shook. But indeed, his punch had already struck the Sphere of Demonic Clouds.

"Crack."

In an instant, Lin Fei's bones seemed to fracture. But likewise, the Sphere of Demonic Clouds was smashed away.

"This power...a seal?"

Lin Fei struggled, struggling violently. He was indeed sealed. It seemed that all his strength could not be exerted, locked down by a mysterious force.

However, the Sphere of Demonic Clouds was also smashed away. As for Lin Fei's fractured bones, what of it?

In an instant, Lin Fei could recover.

"What, my Sphere of Demonic Clouds was smashed away?"

Seeing that Lin Fei had actually smashed the Sphere of Demonic Clouds away, the Dark Demon Lord was greatly startled. He knew all too well how terrifying the power of the Sphere of Demonic Clouds was. Even he, facing it head-on, unless he was in the Dark Demon Domain, could not withstand a single blow from the Sphere of Demonic Clouds.

Yet now, Lin Fei had directly, forcefully smashed away the Sphere of Demonic Clouds. This could only prove that Lin Fei's current strength had even surpassed that of the Dark Demon Lord.

Without the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, even the Dark Demon Lord would be in great danger.

"Fortunately, I made a decisive decision to control the Sphere of Demonic Clouds. In such a short time, Lin Fei could even smash the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, which is simply unbelievable.

Therefore, Lin Fei must not be allowed to remain! Fortunately, although Lin Fei's strength is strong, it seems that he was also sealed by the Chaos Heavy Water within it."

Seeing Lin Fei sealed, the Dark Demon Lord finally breathed a sigh of relief. If Lin Fei truly could not be thwarted even by the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, then the troubles of the Dark Demon Domain would be significant. Not to mention invading the Divine Realm, even the Dark Demon Domain might not be safe.

Thus, the Dark Demon Lord was now brimming with murderous intent. He even thought of paying any price to kill Lin Fei, to eliminate future troubles; to allow Lin Fei to continue growing would be truly too terrifying and too dangerous.

At this moment, Lin Fei remained motionless.

The binding power of the Chaos Heavy Water was indeed terrifying, rendering even Lin Fei completely immobile.

Yet, Lin Fei was not the least bit perturbed. Formidable though this binding force was, it was not his sole tactic. Or rather, it was not Lin Fei at his most formidable. Lin Fei's strongest state was always his transformation into the world itself!

Since Lin Fei could not move, he simply decided to transform into the world. He was curious to see whether this sphere could also bind his Eternal Realm.

After all, his Eternal Realm was now a True Immortal-level world. What terrifying force would be required to fully bind a True Immortal-level world?

"Eternal Realm!"

With a mere thought from Lin Fei, a rumble resounded, and the Eternal Realm was manifested. Lin Fei transformed directly, and his vast Eternal Realm began to expand continuously. Even under the suppression of the Chaos Heavy Water, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm still managed to spread unceasingly.

A buzz sounded, and for the first time, the binding force showed a hint of loosening. The Dark Demon Lord saw clearly as the Sphere of Demonic Clouds vibrated incessantly. Even the Chaos Heavy Water within the sphere trembled as if enduring immense pressure.

In the eyes of the Dark Demon Lord, Lin Fei's transformation into the world represented a colossal realm. As it expanded, the Sphere of Demonic Cloud's binding power was continuously suppressed.

"How is this possible?" he wondered aloud. "Lin Fei can resist the binding power of Chaos Heavy Water? Even his transformation into a world, amidst such vast quantities of Chaos Heavy Water, cannot be fully bound? What level of world is this?"

The Dark Demon Lord was astounded. If the binding power of Chaos Heavy Water could not confine any world, what could?

Yet, now the reality was evident. As Lin Fei's Eternal Realm spread, the Chaos Heavy Water within the Sphere of Demonic Clouds truly could not completely bind the Eternal Realm. Even the sphere itself vibrated violently, obviously enduring tremendous pressure.

A soft pop sounded after an unknown duration. Lin Fei felt his body relax. Subsequently, he no longer felt that sensation of being restrained or bound.

The Sphere of Demonic Cloud's binding power had vanished. Or rather, Lin Fei had already broken free from the sphere's confinement.

"Impossible..." The Dark Demon Lord's face twisted as he witnessed this scene, a look of astonishment flashing in his eyes. He seemed almost unable to believe it.

The Sphere of Demonic Clouds and the Chaos Heavy Water, upon which the Dark Demon Lord had pinned his hopes, truly could not bind Lin Fei.

Lin Fei had broken free from his shackles. What level of power must Lin Fei possess to break free from the binding of Chaos Heavy Water?

Ruler! Without a doubt, Lin Fei's power had now reached a level comparable to that of a Ruler, possessing such formidable strength wherever he went. Otherwise, the Chaos Heavy Water would have surely bound him.

The emergence of a Ruler meant something profound, and the Dark Demon Lord understood this well. Despite his disbelief, the Dark Demon Lord made a decisive command.

"Retreat!"

The Dark Demon Lord was resolute. He understood that it was no longer possible to slay Lin Fei. In fact, even his own safety was at risk. Thus, he promptly withdrew the Sphere of Demonic Clouds.

With a flicker of his figure, he then darted into the void, fleeing precipitously. Yes, he fled! None had anticipated that the Dark Demon Lord would flee.

At that moment, the supremes of the Dark Demon Domain widened their eyes in mutual astonishment, deeply shocked.

How could the illustrious Dark Demon Lord, the Ruler of the Dark Demon Domain, flee? Yet, this was the reality.

"Lord, how could he flee? Moreover, so hastily that he didn't even inform us?"

"Let's go, we must flee immediately. After all, if even the Lord has fled, there must be a reason, and we shouldn't stay here to die."

"Clearly, the Lord could not contend with Lin Fei. Even Lin Fei's power far exceeded his expectations, posing a severe threat to him, prompting him to flee at all costs."

Many supremes in the Dark Demon Domain quickly realized this. However, their escape would not be so easy. At least, Lin Fei would not let them go easily.

"Eternal Realm, gather!"

With a thought from Lin Fei, the Eternal Realm rapidly expanded, majestically sweeping across the area and violently enveloping dozens of the Dark Demon Domain's supremes within it.

Still, a few Supremes, the fastest among them, ultimately managed to escape, not being swept up by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm and finding their narrow escape.

But the remaining dozens of Supremes were not so fortunate, suppressed by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm!
