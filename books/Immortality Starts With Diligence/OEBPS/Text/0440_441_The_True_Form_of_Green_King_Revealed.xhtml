441. The True Form of Green King Revealed

"Azure Flood Dragon, it seems you've encountered a dilemma," a voice echoed.

At that moment, a silhouette slowly stepped in from the ancestral lands beyond.

"Master!" The Azure Flood Dragon King exclaimed with evident joy. The title "Master" was now uttered with genuine reverence.

"Master?" The Green King's eyes shimmered with a hint of surprise. "You must be the Martial King of the human race. It's unexpected that the esteemed Azure Flood Dragon King would be tamed by a mere human Martial King..."

Before the Green King could finish his words, Lin Fei's figure blurred. With swift precision, he thrust forward with a forceful punch, sending the Green King hurtling through the air.

Lin Fei's movement was lightning fast, without any prior indication. In just a fleeting moment, the Green King was airborne. He crashed into the ground, creating a massive crater, mirroring the impact of the Azure Flood Dragon King earlier.

Silence.

An eerie stillness.

Even the Azure Flood Dragon King was taken aback. It recognized the might of Lin Fei, perhaps even surpassing its peak strength. But this? This was the Green King they were speaking of!

The Azure Flood Dragon King had summoned the power of the ancestral lands, combining all its forces. Yet, they couldn't touch the indomitable Green King. And now, a mere punch from Lin Fei had sent him reeling.

This wasn't a mere ambush.

Given the Green King's prowess, the idea of an ambush was implausible. Even if the Azure Flood Dragon King were to attack him outright, the Green King would likely remain unscathed. Such was the transformative power when strength reached a certain pinnacle.

The Azure Flood Dragon King felt as if the Green King had undergone a metamorphosis. Even though both were King-tier exotic beasts, the Green King toyed with the Azure Flood Dragon King as if he were a mere plaything. There was no equilibrium between their strengths; the Green King could easily crush the Azure Flood Dragon King. Yet, with a single blow, Lin Fei had thrown the Green King off balance.

Such a feat was far from simple!

A rumble echoed, causing the earth to tremble, and the Green King emerged once more.

"Hmm?" Lin Fei studied the Green King with a hint of bewilderment. The punch he had just delivered was no ordinary strike. It was imbued with the power of the Four Symbols, harnessing the forces of earth, fire, water, and wind from his four bloodlines.

This punch contained the Triple Yin True Fire Bloodline, with genuine fire that could silently incinerate. One misstep and one could be reduced to ashes by the Triple Yin True Fire. Likewise, the Grand Yin Wind Dao Bloodline was just as potent, its chilling winds capable of exposing bones.

Yet now, be it the Triple Yin True Fire or the Grand Yin Wind, both seemed ineffective against the Green King. He remained unscathed.

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. Only one explanation was plausible: The Green King's physical body was incredibly robust, having reached an unparalleled extreme. Hence, neither the Triple Yin True Fire nor the Grand Yin Wind could harm him in the slightest.

"Boom, boom, boom!"

The Green King charged forward. In human form, he clashed wildly with Lin Fei.

In an instant, the two exchanged dozens of blows. It was a pure contest of strength.

Lin Fei exploded with his bloodline power. While the Green King boasted a formidable physique. Their clashes seemed to break the very void itself, causing the emptiness to show traces of dark cracks.

"Retreat!"

The Azure Flood Dragon King quickly protected his clan members and retreated frantically. Under such circumstances, even with the protection of the ancestral land formation, they were in peril. Such power could break through the formation of the ancestral land.

The two combatants had already left the ancestral land of the Azure Flood Dragon clan, hovering above the vast sea.

At this moment, the Green King's subordinates who were still on the island, seemed to have realized their leader had met a worthy opponent.

All stopped in their tracks. Looking up at the void above, they refrained from engaging with the Azure Flood Dragon King.

The reason was simple. The outcome between them was of little significance. Only the result between Lin Fei and the Green King would determine the ultimate victor.

Suddenly, both halted. Each retreated a few steps. The righteous indignation that had previously shown on the Green King's face vanished entirely, returning to the calm demeanor he had shown before. It seemed as though his earlier expression was merely a facade. He remained utterly composed, never acting rashly under any circumstance.

"Just a warm-up earlier," The Green King commented. "How do I address you?"

"Lin Fei!"

Lin Fei stared coldly at the Green King. From the Green King, he felt a hint of threat. This demonstrated that the Green King was no ordinary being. Even the Triple Yin True Fire and the Grand Yin Wind Dao couldn't affect him. He was truly extraordinary.

"Lin Fei, you deliberately led me to hover over the sea to avoid the aftermath of our battle affecting the ancestral land of the Azure Flood Dragon King, didn't you? Now that we're here, far away from the island, no matter how we fight, the ancestral land will remain untouched. It's time you showed your true capabilities!"

The Green King smirked, revealing his understanding of Lin Fei's intentions.

"Green King, I'm very curious about your true form," Lin Fei expressed his intrigue.

"Well, let's see if you have the ability to make me reveal it," the Green King responded with a challenge.

"Hmph."

Lin Fei snorted coldly.

"Boom!"

In the next moment, Lin Fei no longer held back, instantly activating his four bloodline powers.

The Power of the Four Symbols! True Form Martial King! Or, the True Form of the Four Symbols!

Growing to an immense size, one hundred thousand meters tall. A terrifying aura spread in all directions.

"Good, very good!"

Upon witnessing Lin Fei's True Form of the Four Symbols, the Green King showed no fear but exhilaration, charging directly towards Lin Fei.

"Boom!"

Lin Fei threw a punch, landing squarely on the Green King.

Instantly, a terrifying force reverberated throughout the void.

How powerful was Lin Fei's True Form of the Four Symbols? Moreover, he also had the strength of four bloodlines.

It wasn't as simple as just adding the power of the four bloodlines. It was a tenfold increase in strength, perhaps even more.

With this first punch, Lin Fei shattered the Green King's illusion. In terms of strength, the power of Lin Fei's True Form of the Four Symbols already surpassed that of the Green King.

Of course, obliterating the Green King was still not possible. After all, Lin Fei had more than just the Four Symbols' True Form. Especially considering the Green King's true form had yet to reveal itself, Lin Fei still had to remain cautious.

"Heh heh, not bad, very impressive. It seems I can't contend with you without revealing my true form. However, everyone who has seen my true form... has died!" The Green King sneered.

The next moment, he finally showed his true form.

"Boom!"

An aura, terrifying to its core, spread throughout the void.

Lin Fei stood unwavering, his gaze firmly fixed on the Green King.

The humanoid figure of the Green King had disappeared. Or rather, the human-shaped Green King was no more. What stood before him was the Green King's true self!

Lin Fei's eyes widened in shock, and his heart raced. It seemed he couldn't believe what he was seeing. Before him was an incomparably massive green carp.

Yes, a green carp. A type of fish found in rivers and lakes, not associated with the sea at all.

Carps couldn't even live in the ocean. But this was the Green King's true form. The Green King was actually a green carp!

It was baffling. And incredibly shocking. Who could have imagined that the being stirring trouble in the vast sea was a green carp, a fish native to rivers and lakes?

Lin Fei finally understood why every exotic beast that had seen the Green King's true form ended up dead. The Green King clearly didn't want anyone to know his true form was that of a green carp.

After all, green carp belonged to rivers and lakes. If he were to rule a stretch of the ocean, it's possible other marine creatures might harbor suspicions or doubts.

But here lay the question. How could a mere carp possess such overwhelming power? So much so that it could swallow the 39th generation Azure Flood Dragon King and dominate a vast ocean territory?

There had to be a secret hidden within the Green King. Perhaps an earth-shattering one!
