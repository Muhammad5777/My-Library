51. Two Inner Power Combined Into One

Vajra Iron Cloth produced a peculiar inner power that adheres to the skin, forming a protective layer.

Lin Fei took out a dagger and used all his strength to want to make a deep cut, but the sharp blade seemed to hit a tough leather and couldn't penetrate it.

"The inner power generated by Body Refining martial arts has different effects. Some have terrifying attacking power with explosive effects, while others have defensive effects," Lin Fei thought to himself.

He manipulated the inner power on his skin and concentrated it in one spot, then used the dagger to cut it. This time, the inner power was not there to protect his skin, and the blade easily cut through it.

The peculiar inner power generated by Vajra Iron Cloth could attach to the skin and protect it like an invisible armor. Its characteristics were completely different from the inner power generated by the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique. Nevertheless, Lin Fei had now developed both kinds of inner power in his body, and his only option was to merge them.

"What would happen if Qi and Blood's inner power and hard martial arts inner power merged?" Lin Fei wondered as this was a relatively rare situation.

Most martial artists would be lucky to cultivate one hard martial art to the point where it generated inner power, let alone two, and the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique was an especially challenging one to master.

And if Lin Fei had not found the Golden Light Snake Blood, he might have had difficulty cultivating the Vajra Iron Cloth the hard martial arts.

"Let's begin," Lin Fei closed his eyes and focused on controlling the two inner powers in his body. He then merged them without hesitation.

"Boom!" As the two inner powers met, Lin Fei's body trembled violently. The Qi and Blood and skin also shook intensely, but it was the inner power that shook the most.

Merging two different types of inner power was not as simple as adding them together, and the process was far from over.

It was not an increase in quantity. It was a qualitative change!

Two inner powers collided and entangled with each other. In the end, they merged completely into one.

Suddenly, the combined inner power surged. It was once just a faint mist, but now it seemed like a flowing stream.

Wherever the inner power went, Lin Fei's body was covered in an "invisible armor".

Moreover, it seemed that his defensive power had also increased by a few levels.

"Swish!"

Lin Fei opened his eyes. A glint of light flashed in his eyes.

He extended his fair hand and pressed it onto the ground once again.

"Crush!"

The hard ground, under Lin Fei's palm, became as fragile as bean curd and sunk 10cm deep.

Lin Fei still had some power left. If he used all of it, a big hole would be made in the ground.

"What a terrifying inner power! The combined inner power of two inner powers is actually this powerful. It's at least five times stronger than the Giant Whale Technique's inner power alone! If the third inner power is also merged, is it possible that the power will increase tenfold or more?"

Even though Lin Fei had always been calm, he couldn't help but feel extremely shocked at this moment!

He didn't expect the merged inner power to be so terrifying.

It was not a case of one plus one equals two, but an explosive increase of several times at once.

"My plan is correct," Lin Fei said.

"I shouldn't rush to cultivate acquired martial arts without first developing inner power. Otherwise, I wouldn't have such strong inner power now."

Lin Fei knew that once he cultivated martial arts of the acquired realm, his inner power would operate in a special way, constantly producing inner power. The foundation was based on the original inner power.

How could one type of inner power compare to two or even three types of fused inner power?

Lin Fei became even more determined. He must find a way to cultivate the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique to its limit, thus producing inner power.

By that time, the three types of inner power—qi and blood, tendons and bones, and skin and membrane—would merge into one.

Lin Fei's inner power would probably be unmatched among acquired martial artists.

However, the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique was a bit troublesome.

It was only at the seventh level of Body Refining.

Reciting a passage from a Taoist or Buddhist scripture every day only increased its progress by 1%.

Once he reached the eighth level, reciting scriptures might not improve his progress at all.

He had to find a way to quickly improve the Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique.

"Perhaps I need to find some monks who have a deep understanding of Buddhist philosophy to personally explain it to me. The same goes for Taoist scriptures. Maybe they can make its progress faster!"

Many thoughts flashed through Lin Fei's mind. Then, he got up and left the room. He was going to ask the butler about something.

Who are the monks in Zhou Mountian Mansion City who have a profound understanding of Buddhism?

"Young Master, you finally came out."

When the butler saw Lin Fei, his eyes lit up, and he walked over.

"Butler, what's the matter?"

Judging from the butler's expression, he must have something to tell him.

"Young Master, this is the letter sent from Luo City yesterday."

"Letter from Luo City?"

Lin Fei immediately thought of a figure.

Miss Zhang!

If it was a letter from Luo City sent to him, it was probably from the Zhang family.

However, he had only left Luo City for less than a month. What could happen to the Zhang family?

So, Lin Fei immediately opened the letter.
