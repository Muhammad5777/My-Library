641. The Descent of the Illusory Realm, Suppressing Dozens of Chaos Supremes!

"Insolence! Whoever you are, regurgitate the prototype of the realm immediately, or your demise today is certain!"

"Bold enough to swallow the realm's prototype, do you take us Supremes for mere decorations?"

"Daring to ingest the realm's prototype, such a transgression demands death!"

Upon seeing Lin Fei, they were indeed outraged to witness him daring to "swallow" the realm's prototype.

The Supremes, one after another, were incensed. They had guarded the prototype of this realm for so long.

Could it be that Lin Fei would reap the benefits so easily?

Across the entire Chaos, there were dozens of Supremes. Each eyed Lin Fei with fierce indignation, staring intently at him alongside the Azure Emperor Supreme and the Black Tortoise Supreme.

Even a murderous intent was apparent. Should Lin Fei's response fail to satisfy them, perhaps, they might indeed resort to slaughter!

The expressions of the Azure Emperor Supreme and the Black Tortoise Supreme were gravely solemn. They glanced back at Lin Fei, their intent clear. They queried whether it was time to leave now.

However, a Supreme seemed to have discerned their intentions. Supremes from all directions had already encircled the trio. Escape now seemed an impossibility!

Yet, Lin Fei remained utterly still, in truth, he was fervently integrating with the realm's prototype.

Lin Fei realized that merging with the realm's prototype was not an overnight affair. It might require a considerable amount of time.

Though the integration process was lengthy, the prototype posed no immediate troubles for Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

With a swift motion, Lin Fei stirred.

"The Illusory Realm!"

With a thunderous roar, Lin Fei unleashed the Illusory Realm; instantly, it enveloped the dozens of Supremes within.

Despite the presence of some supremely powerful beings amongst these Supremes, and those with formidable mental fortitude, none could withstand the power of Lin Fei's Illusory Realm.

Regardless of their stature, all were effectively subdued. Expressions of bewilderment crossed the faces of all the Supremes.

Only a few struggled, their faces showing a hint of struggle. Clearly, such Supremes could not be suppressed by Lin Fei's Illusory Realm for long.

"Let's go."

At that moment, Lin Fei, along with the Azure Emperor Supreme and others, transformed into a streak of light, swiftly exiting the Chaos.

As Lin Fei departed, the Illusory Realm was also retracted. Instantly, without the influence of the Illusory Realm, the Supremes regained their senses. They looked at each other, exchanging puzzled glances.

"Where are those three unknown Supremes?"

"And the realm's prototype?"

"Did they truly escape?"

"But, I felt as though I was ensnared by an illusion just now..."

Many of the Supremes didn't even understand what had happened. Only a few among them actually knew the events that had just transpired. Even the Illusory Realm had not detained them for long.

Yet now, they remained silent. The Illusory Realm! They had been subdued by it.

Such an occurrence was unprecedented. These were dozens of Supremes!

To think that a single entity could "subdue" so many was astonishing. Such a Supreme must indeed be of the highest echelon.

Even if they pursued it, what then? Especially if pursued alone, it might even be perilous.

Thus, they remained speechless. Even though Lin Fei had "swallowed" the realm's prototype, to them, it was merely a loss of some time.

For Supremes, time was least scarce. But to pursue might mean death! The importance of risks versus gains was clear to them.

"The Supremes we just faced were extraordinary, certainly not of common stock."

"Indeed, let's not pursue. The realm's prototype has been consumed, and what if we caught up? It would still be dangerous, and we need not take such risks."

"Those three Supremes clearly came prepared. And with one among them being a top-tier Supreme, our defeat is not unjust."

"Can a Supreme really be so powerful?"

"I had thought the differences among Supremes were minor, but now I see the gap is indeed vast..."

Despite some disappointment, they were well aware that the strong reign supreme in the Chaos. Those three Supremes, having the courage and strength to "seize" the realm's prototype were undoubtedly powerful, especially Lin Fei.

Alone, he subdued dozens of Supremes. This was truly staggering.

"Let it be, let's return."

"In the Chaos, there are plenty of opportunities. We may have lost the chance for the realm's prototype this time, but other opportunities will arise."

"Indeed, there are not few opportunities within the Chaos..."

These Supremes, each had "come to terms" with the situation. Moreover, unable to trace Lin Fei's path, They could only depart.

...

In the midst of the Chaos.

This was an unfamiliar territory of Chaos, seemingly unexplored even by the Black Tortoise Supreme.

"No pursuit."

Lin Fei surveyed his surroundings. With a slight sensing, he knew that the other Supremes had not followed.

Lin Fei was well-prepared. To avoid being tracked, he had no intention whatsoever of returning to the Divine Realm, nor was he traveling in the usual directions.

This was a deliberate choice by Lin Fei. Yet, he remained uneasy, concerned for the Azure Emperor Supreme and the Black Tortoise Supreme.

Should they, like the Radiance Supreme, be tracked by special means, their return to the Divine Realm could expose it to further threats.

Thus, Lin Fei prepared to perform a thorough "purge" here, at least to remove any potential tracking methods on himself or on the Azure Emperor Supreme and the Black Tortoise Supreme.

For other Supremes, such a removal might prove challenging. Indeed, the variety of tracking methods was daunting, each Supreme practiced a different cultivation system and employed unique tracking techniques.

Hence, eradicating all traces of tracking was exceedingly difficult, even for a top-tier Supreme.

Yet, in the Chaos, to ensure one's own safety and that of their realm, one must learn how to cleanse any potential tracking traces.

While other Supremes might find it difficult or impossible to remove these traces, Lin Fei was different.

For him, this task was surprisingly simple and effortless, all due to one reason: Lin Fei possessed the Eternal Realm!

Once transformed into the Eternal Realm, it could detect any trace of tracking, or rather, any power not belonging to oneself.

It could even assist the Azure Emperor Supreme and the Black Tortoise Supreme in purging their traces.

"Azure Emperor Supreme, Black Tortoise Supreme, please enter my Eternal Realm," Lin Fei invited. "Once inside, if there are any tracking methods or residual powers from other Supremes on you, the Eternal Realm will surely detect them. Under its power, all will be purged. No Supreme will be able to trace us or locate the Divine Realm."

The Azure Emperor Supreme and the Black Tortoise Supreme nodded in agreement: "Very well, we trust you, Martial Ancestor Lin!"

With no resistance, both Supremes were enveloped into the Eternal Realm by Lin Fei.

Within the Eternal Realm, Lin Fei immediately detected residual methods left by other Supremes on himself, the Azure Emperor Supreme and the Black Tortoise Supreme were similarly affected, all bearing marks of tracking and power.

However mysterious or hidden these tracking methods or powers were, Under the scope of the Eternal Realm, nothing could remain hidden.

Lin Fei used the power of the Eternal Realm and eradicated all traces.

"Azure Emperor Supreme, Black Tortoise Supreme, we can now return to the Divine Realm."

Thus, the Azure Emperor Supreme, the Black Tortoise Supreme, and Lin Fei, finally set their course back toward the Divine Realm.
