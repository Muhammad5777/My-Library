186. The Arrival of the Martial Saint, Thousands of Troops Kneel Down

Lin Fei stood in the void. At this moment, his saber momentum exploded, like a large net, instantly covering the sky above Green Dragon Prefecture, suddenly enveloping the entire prefecture.

Immediately, the entire Green Dragon Prefecture seemed to be enveloped by a faint white light. Moreover, everyone seemed to suddenly fall into a quagmire. It was difficult to move! It seemed there was heavy pressure on them as if they were carrying a mountain.

"What... what's going on?"

"I... I can't seem to stand up."

"Such a heavy pressure, I can't even stand up."

For a moment, many ordinary soldiers, their hearts were filled with fear. Under the suppression of the saber momentum, whether they had a saber or not, it didn't matter. They all were suppressed.

Lin Fei took a step forward.

"Plop".

Tens of thousands of rebel soldiers, at this moment, couldn't bear the heavy pressure. They actually all fell to their knees. Whether they were ordinary soldiers or body refining martial artists, or even acquired martial artists at this moment, all fell to their knees as if greeting a deity.

This scene was very shocking. And Lin Fei was step by step, slowly moving to the sky above Green Dragon Prefecture's city wall. He looked down and saw the densely packed rebels on the ground and some ordinary people. Now they were all kneeling on the ground.

A single person suppressed tens of thousands of troops! This is a Saber Saint! Even a Martial Saint couldn't do this, only the Saber Saint who had evolved "saber momentum" could reach this level.

By now, in the eyes of ordinary people, Lin Fei was actually no different from a deity! However, in Green Dragon Prefecture, there were still some people struggling. For example, those few Qi cultivators.

When Lin Fei's saber momentum descended, the Qi cultivators activated their magical artifacts and turned into a streak of light. When they tried to escape, they found their bodies shook. Then, they all fell to the ground as if carrying a mountain on their bodies, making them unable to move.

"Impossible, this... what on earth is going on?"

"A Martial Saint couldn't possibly have this kind of power. Even if it's saber intent, no matter how strong the saber intent is, no matter how strong the Martial Dao True Intent is, it wouldn't make it impossible for us to even move an inch."

"Move, move!"

"Lin Fei hasn't even made a move. What is this method?"

Some Qi cultivators hadn't fallen to their knees. They were only suppressed on the ground. Their spiritual power was surging and their magical artifacts were shaking. There was a faint struggle that was resisting Lin Fei's saber momentum.

Lin Fei came to the top of these Qi cultivators. His eyes coldly looked at them. When had Qi cultivators ever been so embarrassed?

"Kneel down!"

Lin Fei's voice was calm. Yet it was like thunder, ringing in the ears of the Qi cultivators. Suddenly, the saber momentum pressed down again.

"Plop".

These few Qi cultivators finally knelt down as well. Like the other rebels, they all knelt on the ground. Their knees seemed to have grown roots, firmly stuck to the ground. These Qi cultivators felt as if they were going to suffocate. All of them looked at Lin Fei in the void with horrified expressions.

Is this the Martial Saint of the Nine Profound Sect who cut down the Foundation Establishment stage with a saber?

Their hearts were already filled with shock. In their sects, in fact, there were also records of some methods of the Martial Saints. However, although the Martial Saints were strong, they were not strong enough to this extent. They hadn't even made a move yet, they were immobilized by the mere pressure. Of course, they didn't know about the existence of saber momentum. Only some ancient Immortal Sects could know about the existence of "momentum".

They just felt that Lin Fei's pressure was too terrifying. So much so that they couldn't even lift their heads. These Qi cultivators felt suffocated. They felt extremely suffocated in their hearts.

As a respectable Qi cultivator, apart from the elders of their sect. When had they ever knelt to someone? Especially, to kneel to a martial artist! Even if Lin Fei was a Martial Saint, so what? They were the lofty Qi cultivators! They were Immortal Masters!

But now, all their pride had been shattered. They had knelt down right in front of Lin Fei. And no matter what they tried to do, it was wishful thinking even to stand up. Lin Fei just stood quietly in the void.

At this moment, it was quiet in Green Dragon Prefecture. Tens of thousands of rebels had knelt down. Even the lofty Immortal Masters were kneeling on the ground. This scene was witnessed by the distant imperial armies assigned to suppress the rebellion. The imperial armies seemed unable to believe the scene before them.

What was this? A miracle?

Lin Fei was just one person, even if he was a Martial Saint. He had only ascended above Green Dragon Prefecture. There was no sign of him taking action. And yet, an army of ten thousand had knelt! Even those arrogant, lofty Immortal Masters, too obediently knelt on the ground.

"Is this the power of a Martial Saint?"

This thought unconsciously flashed through many people's minds. The power of a Martial Saint was terrifying! When a Martial Saint arrived, ten thousand soldiers knelt!

For a moment, the morale of the imperial armies surged. With such an invincible Martial Saint, what couldn't they conquer?

Lin Fei didn't care if the morale of the imperial forces surged. To him, whether the morale of the army was high or not, it didn't matter much. When his saber momentum was released, whether it was an army of one hundred thousand or several hundred thousand, it didn't matter, even the slightest movement was wishful thinking. Sure enough, a Martial Saint with or without saber momentum was a completely different concept!

Lin Fei had been silent the whole time. The Qi cultivators below couldn't bear it any longer.

One of the top Qi condensation cultivators gritted his teeth and said loudly, "Martial Saint, you are a lofty Martial Saint. We, the Fairy Crane Sect and the Water Moon Sect admit that we have never troubled any member of the Nine Profound Sect. We dare not provoke you, Martial Saint. Now, the entire world is suffering due to the incompetence of the royal family, causing the people to rise up and resist. We only hope to end the war as soon as possible and provide a stable environment for the people, because we cannot bear to see the people suffer from the saber and soldiers. Where is our wrongdoing?"

Lin Fei looked coldly at this Qi cultivator. Everyone knew the real reason these Qi cultivators supported the rebels. It was for the benefit of their sects. Arguing was meaningless, so Lin Fei wouldn't argue.

"Whether there is a mistake or not, I'm too lazy to think about it. You rebelled and supported the rebels. I don't allow it. If I don't allow it, then it's a serious crime! And furthermore, the crime is unforgivable and must be punished!"

With those words, a strand of saber essence flew out from his body.

"Whoosh".

The saber essence instantly transformed into a big saber, directly chopped down at the top Qi Condensation cultivator. Suddenly, this top Qi condensation cultivator let out a scream. But he couldn't move at all. He could only watch as the saber essence cut into his own head.

"Thump".

This top Qi Condensation cultivator was directly split into two halves by the saber essence. His body fell to the ground. The other Qi Condensation cultivators' faces turned pale, even their bodies were trembling.

"Not only are you guilty, but the Fairy Crane Sect and the Water Moon Sect are also guilty. I will personally pay a visit to the Water Moon Sect and Fairy Crane Sect and sever their lineage as a warning!"

Lin Fei's tone was calm. But the content made several Qi cultivators' faces change instantly. In their hearts, they couldn't help but feel a sense of fear.
