297. One Against Five

"Great Mountain Seal!"

The Five Elders simultaneously cast the Great Mountain Seal. All five were Gold Core-level magic treasures. More crucially, they could merge with each other!

With Lin Fei's Saber Domain suppressed by the Five Elders' Five Directional Array Flags, it was the first time his domain had lost its ability to overwhelm opponents.

"You've certainly come prepared," Lin Fei remarked coldly. "But did you think I rely solely on the Saber Domain? I am a Martial Sovereign!" His eyes were icy, filled with lethal intent.

In the next instant, a massive Life Wheel materialized behind Lin Fei. Two layers of radiant light adorned the Life Wheel, dazzling to behold. A formidable aura of suppression immediately descended upon the Five Elders.

This was the pressure from the Life Wheel!

However, the Five Elders were all top-tier Gold Core True Persons with a high life level, so they remained unaffected by Lin Fei's Life Wheel. But Lin Fei hadn't intended to merely suppress them with it. He was preparing to use a Divine Ability!

"Using your Divine Ability will be pointless," one of the elders declared. "We are well aware of your Black Wind Prison Divine Ability. We're protected by magic treasures. Your corrosive wind won't touch us. Great Mountain Seal, descend!"

The Five Elders seemed to be highly informed about Lin Fei, even knowing about his Black Wind Prison, and had prepared accordingly. However, mere preparation didn't guarantee success. If simply knowing about a Divine Ability and preparing defenses against it were enough, why would Martial Sovereigns bother to cultivate them?

As a result, the Five Elders not only had their defenses ready, but they also immediately merged with five Great Mountain Seals, attacking Lin Fei directly.

The Great Mountain Seal, towering tens of thousands of meters, was like an ancient divine mountain. The terrifying pressure enveloped Lin Fei instantly. This was the fusion of five Gold Core-level magic treasures, and its power was earth-shattering.

With the combined might of the Five Elders, they could easily overwhelm most Martial Sovereigns. Many immortal sects and martial arts schools would be defeated by them. Yet, at this moment, all this formidable power was being used to deal with just one person: Lin Fei. This showed the extent of the Taiyi Dao Palace's determination to eliminate him.

In the sky, the colossal ancient divine mountain descended towards Lin Fei. Deprived of his Saber Domain's power, and even with the limited effectiveness of the Black Wind Prison (which was mainly designed for humans and not magic treasures like the Great Mountain Seal), many onlookers believed they knew the outcome.

After all, with the Five Elders taking action, they must have been confident in their success.

"What a pity! If the Five Elders are taking action, Lin Fei must be truly skilled."

"The Great Mountain Seal of Taiyi Dao Palace can merge with one another. Such a technique is terrifying. This is the essence of the Nascent Soul tradition!"

"The power of the Nascent Soul tradition is indeed formidable. Just this one technique alone makes Taiyi Dao Palace an incredibly powerful sect."

"Even without a Nascent Soul overseeing them, they're still immensely powerful."

Witnessing the Five Elders merging the Great Mountain Seal and displaying the Five Directional Array Flags, many Gold Core True Persons and Martial Sovereigns in the Cangli Ancient City felt a chill in their hearts.

They empathized with Lin Fei, wondering if they, in his place against the Five Elders, would face a similar inevitable doom. No one dared to underestimate Taiyi Dao Palace.

In the sky, the Great Mountain Seal thundered down. The Five Elders were confident in their impending victory. If they could suppress Lin Fei's Saber Domain, dealing with him would be effortless.

Yet, Lin Fei appeared unfazed. He stared at the descending Great Mountain Seal while the second layer of light on his Life Wheel flickered slightly.

A hum resonated.

"Divine Ability, Gravity Domain!"

Upon Lin Fei casting the Gravity Domain, an invisible force of gravity immediately enveloped the Great Mountain Seal and the Five Elders. The Seal accelerated under the influence of this gravitational pull.

Although Lin Fei couldn't destroy the Great Mountain Seal with his gravity, he could alter its descent path. Guided by Lin Fei's gravitational pull, the Seal unexpectedly veered directly toward the Five Elders.

"What?!"

Witnessing this, the Five Elders were astounded. They hadn't anticipated this twist. How could the Great Mountain Seal, out of their control, plummet towards them?

They tried to dodge, but their bodies felt incredibly heavy as if an overwhelming force had pinned them down, making even the slightest movement seem nearly impossible. They could only watch helplessly as the Great Mountain Seal descended.

"Divine Ability, Lin Fei's second Divine Ability!"

The Five Elders instantly realized the issue. It was Lin Fei's Divine Ability! They knew he had cultivated a second Divine Ability but were unaware of its specifics. They hadn't expected it to manifest as this fearsome gravitational force.

"Great Mountain Seal split!"

Without any delay, the Five Elders separated the Great Mountain Seal. Instantly, the single Seal was divided into five. Although still influenced by Lin Fei's gravity, the Elders managed to control them, turning them into beams of light that swiftly returned to their hands.

Lin Fei extended his hand.

"Gravitational Collapse!"

The very next moment, a terrifying force of gravity converged on the five Baoquan Peak elders.

"Boom!"

The void itself seemed to distort. Such a horrifying gravity, even the physique of a Martial Sovereign couldn't withstand. How could the Five Elders endure?

Their defensive magic treasures were immediately distorted. As for their defensive spells, they were instantly shattered, not able to withstand the gravitational force. Only the Great Mountain Seal remained, shielding them.

However, under the Gravity Domain, the Great Mountain Seal was also rendered ineffective. They could only watch as their bodies were gradually compressed by the overwhelming gravity, threatening to collapse.

"Five Directional Array Flags, return!"

The Five Elders were frightened and immediately recalled the Five Directional Array Flags. These flags were adept in both offense and defense. More importantly, they could create their own space. This space could suppress Lin Fei's Saber Domain and also fend off external attacks.

"Whoosh."

As the Five Directional Array Flags were swiftly recalled by the elders, they encapsulated the Five Elders within, creating a space of their own that shielded them from Lin Fei's Gravity Domain. The flags even seemed to resist Lin Fei's gravitational realm, freeing the Five Elders from its pressing force.

However, while the Five Directional Array Flags resolved the immediate peril for the elders, they released Lin Fei's Saber Domain.

"Bang!"

Lin Fei's Saber Domain descended, covering the Five Directional Array Flags. At the same time, the Saber Domain began to merge slowly with the Gravity Domain. The two powers melded into one.

"Fire!"

A thought emerged in Lin Fei's heart, and flames roared to life, turning the void into a blazing inferno. The Five Elders and the Five Directional Array Flags were trapped within, continuously scorched. In a matter of moments, the situation for the Five Elders became dire. Their position was precarious.

Lin Fei, with his power alone, entirely suppressed the Five Elders!

Witnessing this, the numerous Gold Core True Persons and Martial Sovereigns in the Cangli Ancient City were utterly astounded!
