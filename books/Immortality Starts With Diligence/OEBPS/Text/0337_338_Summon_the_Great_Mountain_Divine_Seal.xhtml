338. Summon the Great Mountain Divine Seal

In the void above, the silhouette of Lin Fei manifested once again. That nebulous mist of blood never dissipated. Instead, it coalesced swiftly, forming Lin Fei.

Witnessing this spectacle, over a hundred Golden Core True Persons gazed in sheer disbelief as if they couldn't fathom the truth.

"How can this be? He was clearly reduced to a blood mist by the Great Mountain Seal. How could he possibly remain unscathed?"

"Transformed into a blood mist and yet able to reform; what sort of Divine Ability is this?"

"Even the famed undying physique doesn't possess such terrifying regenerative prowess."

For a moment, a profound shock gripped the hearts of all Golden Core True Persons present. They had indeed seen the undying physique of Martial Sovereigns before. In fact, they'd witnessed its power on numerous occasions.

However, even the mightiest of these undying physiques, at most, boasted formidable defense and rapid recovery. Never before had they seen a Divine Ability where one, having been reduced to mere mist, could be reborn in such a manner.

Perhaps 'reborn' wasn't the right word. After all, Lin Fei's Saber Domain and Gravity Domain remained intact, signifying that he had never truly perished to begin with. But how could he have survived the combined force of the Great Mountain Seals, summoned and driven by over a hundred Golden Core True Persons?

A sense of hopelessness engulfed many of the Golden Core True Persons. Their most potent weapon had failed. The numerous Great Mountain Seals had shattered. Bereft of the Great Mountain Seals, what other means did they possess to combat this threat?

There, in the vast expanse, Lin Fei stood. He cast a cold gaze upon the multitude of Golden Core True Persons ensnared within the formation. Indeed, moments ago, he had been reduced to a blood mist by the numerous Great Mountain Seals. Yet, he did not perish. The sensation was peculiar. All of this was due to the effect of his Divine Ability, the Undying Seal.

The Undying Seal, a passive Divine Ability, is only triggered when one's life is endangered or one's body is gravely harmed. Having been reduced to what could aptly be termed "utter annihilation," Lin Fei would certainly have met his demise had it been before. The combined might of over a hundred Golden Core True Persons and their myriad of magic treasures wasn't mere rhetoric; it was a terrifying force to be reckoned with.

Choosing the Undying Physique as his fifth Divine Ability was a decision Lin Fei had made after considerable contemplation. It now seemed his choice was judicious. Battling a power like Taiyi Dao Palace, survival was paramount. After all, no one truly knew the depths of Taiyi Dao Palace's strength. Who would have imagined that Taiyi Dao Palace would suddenly bolster its ranks with so many Golden Core True Persons?

In the midst of the intense atmosphere, Lin Fei's voice resonated, cold and unwavering, signaling the unleashing of his divine abilities.

"Divine Ability, Annihilation! Divine Ability, Dual-World Saber! Saber Domain, tri-saber merge, Strike!"

Lin Fei's gaze was chilling. The Life Wheel behind him emitted two distinct rays of luminescence, each signifying the release of top-tier Great Divine Abilities. Added to that, the infinite saber beams from his Saber Domain surged toward the group of Golden Core True Persons like a tsunami.

A colossal explosion erupted. Despite the combined strength of the hundred Golden Core True Persons, they found themselves outmatched by Lin Fei's overwhelming assault. The two Divine Abilities and the Saber Domain's onslaught clashed head-on with the formation. The formation, already compromised due to the destruction of the Great Mountain Seal and subsequent backlash, barely lasted three breaths before shattering.

The ear-piercing shatter echoed. The formation, bolstered by the combined power of over a hundred Golden Core True Persons, crumbled instantaneously under the combined weight of the Annihilation, Dual-World Saber, and the Saber Domain. This jarring event left each Golden Core True Person physically and mentally shaken.

A subtle tremor emanated from the first layer of Lin Fei's Life Wheel. The wind began to blow, but it wasn't any ordinary gust. This was the bone-eroding wind, capable of reducing flesh to mere bones in an instant.

As this deathly wind swept over, terror painted the faces of the Golden Core True Persons. Their flesh rapidly dissipated, revealing pale skeletons. Given their current vulnerable state, they'd only be able to withstand this wind for a few breaths. Beyond that, certain death awaited.

Although they were nearing the end of their lifespans, few among them could face the reality of death with true equanimity.

Inside Taiyi Dao Palace, all eyes were trained on the epic showdown between Lin Fei and the Golden Core True Persons. Every elder, disciple, and even the Palace Master knew the terrifying power of these hundred Golden Core True Persons. When they saw the Great Mountain Seal transform Lin Fei into a mist of blood, relief, and joy were evident on their faces.

However, Lin Fei's "resurrection" and subsequent annihilation of the Great Mountain Seal and the formation stunned them into fearful silence. Now, with the formation shattered, the lives of these Golden Core True Persons hung by a thread. Although these Golden Core True Persons were nearing their natural life's end, there was a stark difference between dying of natural causes and being slain by Lin Fei's hand.

If Lin Fei managed to defeat over a hundred Golden Core True Persons from Taiyi Dao Palace single-handedly, it would signify a catastrophic blow to the prestige and might of the Taiyi Dao Palace.

"Palace Master, we must find a solution!"

"Call back the Golden Core True Persons from outside."

"How has Lin Fei become so formidable? Not even hundreds of Golden Core masters can challenge him."

"Lin Fei possesses five top-tier Great Divine Abilities. He also has the Saber Domain, which even reaches the level of Transcendence."

"A Saber Domain of Transcendence is almost equivalent to the power of two, perhaps even three top-tier Great Divine Abilities."

"By this calculation, Lin Fei can nearly match a Martial Sovereign with seven or eight top-tier Great Divine Abilities."

"He's merely a step away from becoming a Martial King..."

Many of the elders went silent. Perhaps some disciples are unaware of the significance of possessing seven or eight top-tier Great Divine Abilities, but the elders are fully aware.

For a typical Martial Sovereign, having one or two Great Divine Abilities is already remarkable. If every one of their Divine Abilities is of the 'Great' variety, they certainly are on the path to becoming a Martial King.

Most of these individuals hail from Martial King lineages, bearing the bloodline of a Martial King and inheriting the Divine Abilities within that bloodline. This is how they might possess multiple Great Divine Abilities. However, it's rare, almost unheard of, to have each one at the top-tier level.

As for Lin Fei? Every one of his abilities is top-tier! The distinction is monumental! Even the Martial Sovereigns from those esteemed Martial King families would likely be overshadowed when faced with Lin Fei.

It's not that the multitude of Golden Core True Persons are weak; it's simply that Lin Fei is overwhelmingly strong. His strength surpasses their wildest imaginations.

They had initially discouraged the Palace Master from using the Great Mountain Divine Seal, thinking it might be an overreaction. But now, in hindsight, they realize their grave error. Only the Palace Master had the foresight to truly understand the threat Lin Fei posed from the beginning. Yet, even with that awareness, they couldn't thwart Lin Fei when he was still in his nascent stages. Now, he has fully come into his own.

The Palace Master remained silent, gazing quietly at the mountain entrance. What solutions can he possibly think of now? Every tactic and ounce of power at his disposal has been exhausted. He even went to the extent of summoning past generations of Golden Core True Persons. Yet, they were still no match for Lin Fei. The only strategy remaining is the final resort of Taiyi Dao Palace – its very essence and foundation.

The Palace Master was hesitant to employ this, unless absolutely necessary. But now, there's no choice.

"Summon the Great Mountain Divine Seal!"

In the following moment, the Palace Master presented his command token. Channeling his Spiritual Qi into it, a resonating hum filled the air, and the token illuminated brightly. Mysterious runes emerged from the token, ascending and embedding into the towering mountain peak above.

With a deafening rumble, the towering mountain quaked vigorously, exuding an aura of ancientness, wilderness, and vastness. This ancient divine mountain is Taiyi Dao Palace's guardian treasure – the Great Mountain Divine Seal!
