671. Lin Fei Sweeps the Dark Demon Domain Supreme

"What are you doing? Stop!"

The sight caused the Dark Demon Lord's face to change instantly.

Collapse!

The Dark Demon Domain was collapsing!

Laws, rules, the very essence of the realm itself. All were in upheaval.

The Dark Demon Domain was not without its defenses. On the contrary, it was formidable!

A thunderous roar filled the air. It seemed the Dark Demon Domain had been "enraged."

It was not just the Lord of Dark Demons. The Dark Demon Domain itself seemed to possess a certain consciousness.

This consciousness, however, was only protective. It only erupted in the face of existential threats. And now, the consciousness of the Dark Demon Domain had erupted.

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was annihilating the Dark Demon Domain. How could its consciousness bear it?

It wasn't just a slight annihilation. It was half! If truly half were annihilated, the Dark Demon Domain would be destroyed. Thus, the consciousness of the Dark Demon Domain began to resist.

A terrifying force collided fiercely with Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Both forces intertwined and clashed continuously.

Lin Fei's expression grew solemn. He was being suppressed! His Eternal Realm was gradually being overwhelmed. The Dark Demon Domain was gaining the upper hand.

"Indeed, it is still lacking. My Eternal Realm still cannot completely suppress the Dark Demon Domain. Just suppressing half will be countered by the other half of the Dark Demon Domain. If I can't resolve the Dark Demon Domain, I can't deal with the Dark Demon Lord."

Lin Fei shook his head. He could not resolve the Dark Demon Domain for now. Thus, he retracted his Eternal Realm.

However, Lin Fei could do nothing against the Dark Demon Domain. Likewise, the Dark Demon Domain could do nothing against him.

Now, even as Lin Fei strolled through the Dark Demon Domain, the Dark Demon Lord, seething with frustration, could only watch helplessly. He was powerless against Lin Fei.

"Dark Demon Lord, did you not seek to find the Supreme of Doomsday? Only a Supreme can venture through the Chaos. Then I shall annihilate all the Supremes of your Dark Demon Domain! Which Supreme will you send into the chaos to search for the Supreme of Doomsday now?"

Lin Fei scoffed coldly. He might not be able to defeat the Dark Demon Lord. But he could certainly handle the other Supremes. Even if the Supremes possessed dark demon hearts, even if they could be reborn, Lin Fei knew that breeding a Supreme of the Dark Demon Domain would take a long time and consume massive resources.

By the time the Dark Demon Lord chose to breed a new Supreme. It might be years, even decades later. By then, Lin Fei might have become the Master of Chaos.

With a humming sound, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, accompanied by the light of eternity, began to spread majestically in all directions.

This time, Lin Fei could only cover half. But it was enough. Even if Lin Fei couldn't annihilate the Dark Demon Domain, he could annihilate all Dark Demon Domain Supremes.

"Annihilate!"

Lin Fei commanded softly.

Instantly, the power of the Eternal Realm erupted. Those Dark Demon Domain Supremes were enveloped by the Eternal Realm.

They didn't even know what was happening. They only felt a terrifying force descending, instantly pressing down upon them. And their bodies began to crumble.

"What's happening?"

"Why is my body starting to disintegrate?"

"Is the Ruler trying to kill me?"

"No, no, that's wrong, it's not the Ruler. This radiance... this is the Eternal Realm, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm!"

"Damn it, Lin Fei has come to the Dark Demon Domain again? Why isn't the Ruler stopping him?"

For a time, many Dark Demon Domain Supremes couldn't help but roar in anger. They were all too familiar with Lin Fei.

Previously, many Dark Demon Domain Supremes had been annihilated by Lin Fei. It had taken years for the heart of dark magic to breed them anew.

But now, Lin Fei had appeared again. And again, within the Dark Demon Domain. And the Dark Demon Lord did nothing.

With a "pfft", one after another, the bodies of the Dark Demon Domain Supremes were annihilated.

As for the other half of the Dark Demon Domain Supremes, that was simple too. Lin Fei could move. And within the Dark Demon Domain, no one could stop him. It could be said that Lin Fei took whatever he wished in the Dark Demon Domain. Even the Dark Demon Lord could only watch helplessly.

Thus, with a flash, Lin Fei teleported away. As Lin Fei teleported to the other half of the Dark Demon Domain, the Supremes there too were soon engulfed by his Eternal Realm.

"Boom, boom, boom."

As the bodies of the Dark Demon Domain Supremes burst open, the Dark Demon Lord seethed with rage. He watched the scene before him, grinding his teeth in helpless fury.

One by one, the Dark Demon Domain Supremes were extinguished by Lin Fei. Even the few who tried to flee were destroyed by him. Almost all the Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain were annihilated, except for the Dark Demon Lord.

Lin Fei had indeed kept his word!

Watching Supreme after Supreme being extinguished by Lin Fei, the Dark Demon Lord was powerless. The mighty Ruler, even within his own domain, was unable to prevent Lin Fei's relentless destruction of the Supremes.

Eventually, the Dark Demon Lord fell silent. His gaze fixed on Lin Fei filled with murderous intent. Today's actions by Lin Fei had utterly disgraced the Dark Demon Lord. He had lost all face.

Lin Fei's unrestrained actions in the Dark Demon Domain had left the Dark Demon Lord without any dignity. Yet, he could not kill Lin Fei. Facing the towering murderous intent of the Dark Demon Lord, Lin Fei's eyes remained calm.

"Dark Demon Lord, the next time I come, I shall extinguish the Dark Demon Domain!"

With those words, Lin Fei left the Dark Demon Domain, leaving only the Dark Demon Lord behind who was staring at the fading figure of Lin Fei.

"Next time? There will be no next time..."

The Dark Demon Lord had made up his mind, even decided. If other Supremes could not find the Supreme of Doomsday, then he would go himself!

As long as he could find the Supreme of Doomsday, the Dark Demon Lord was confident he could persuade the Supreme of Doomsday to act. He would spare no expense! As long as he could kill Lin Fei!

This incident made the Dark Demon Lord realize that if he did not kill Lin Fei, the time left for him might truly be running out.

It had been only three years. Lin Fei had already become comparable to a top-tier Supreme. No, he was even beyond a typical top-tier Supreme. If it were just any top-tier Supreme, the Dark Demon Lord would not be so passive.

In the Dark Demon Domain, the Dark Demon Lord was considered a genuine top-tier Supreme.

Yet, even so, he was powerless against Lin Fei. Even making a move would only bring him humiliation.

It had been only three years. If this continued, three, five, or ten years later, could he still withstand Lin Fei? That was uncertain!

Thus, he must kill Lin Fei before he becomes the Master of Chaos to completely remove the threat!

So, the Dark Demon Lord resolved in his heart. He stepped forward.

The figure of the Dark Demon Lord also vanished within the Dark Demon Domain. He too ventured into the depths of the chaos to seek the Supreme of Doomsday.
