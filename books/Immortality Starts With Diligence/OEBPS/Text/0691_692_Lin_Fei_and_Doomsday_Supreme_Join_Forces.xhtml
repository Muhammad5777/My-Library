692. Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme Join Forces

A resonant hum echoed. Outside the Forbidden Tower, a figure suddenly appeared, looking somewhat disheveled.

"Failed again..." Mystic Dragon Supreme furrowed his brow. He had been attempting to breach the eighth layer of the Forbidden Tower to advance to the ninth, but alas, he had failed. This was his eighth attempt, yet he remained confined to the eighth layer.

"Isn't that Mystic Dragon Supreme?" someone noted.

"Yes, that's Mystic Dragon Supreme. He's been on the eighth layer of the Forbidden Tower. If we ask him, he might know whether Supreme Lin has passed through the eighth layer, and that could explain why the tower suddenly started shaking."

"Right, let's ask Mystic Dragon Supreme."

With that, the group immediately flew toward Mystic Dragon Supreme. "Mystic Dragon Supreme, we mean no harm," they began. "We just wish to ask if you know whether Supreme Lin has entered the eighth layer of the Forbidden Tower? Mystic Dragon Supreme, do you know of Supreme Lin's situation?"

Mystic Dragon Supreme frowned. Why were so many Supremes suddenly inquiring about Lin Fei? He was well aware of Lin Fei's circumstances but had previously chosen not to disclose them. However, things were different now.

"Wait, what exactly happened?" he asked. "Why are you all so concerned about Supreme Lin?"

"Mystic Dragon Supreme, aren't you aware?" one replied. "The Forbidden Tower is shaking violently. We suspect someone has reached the ninth layer, possibly even joining forces with Doomsday Supreme to challenge it."

"That's why the tower is undergoing such significant changes. Only a few Supremes have reached the eighth layer. Perhaps only Supreme Lin is likely to have advanced to the ninth layer alongside Doomsday Supreme. We naturally want to clarify the situation."

Mystic Dragon Supreme's eyes flickered with a sharp glint as he spoke in a solemn voice, "So it is..."

"Supreme Lin has indeed arrived at the eighth layer of the Forbidden Tower. And just as you have surmised, shortly after reaching the eighth layer, Supreme Lin successfully breached it and proceeded directly to the ninth. As to whether the tremors in the Forbidden Tower are due to Supreme Lin joining forces with Doomsday Supreme, I cannot say."

Mystic Dragon Supreme's revelation caused a stir among the crowd. They had been debating whether Lin Fei could pass the eighth layer, whether he could be comparable to Doomsday Supreme. Now, the results spoke for themselves.

"Supreme Lin really reached the ninth layer?"

"Incredible, the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower, that's the true realm of the Invincible Supreme!"

"Only Doomsday Supreme and now Supreme Lin have managed to reach the ninth layer. Only a true Invincible Supreme could accomplish such a feat!"

"Does this mean Supreme Lin is truly an Invincible Supreme?"

For a moment, many were left exchanging glances in disbelief. Had another Invincible Supreme truly emerged within Chaos? It was incredibly challenging to achieve such a status—not merely a realm, but a measure of combat power.

It was a standard that could only be met by those whose strength dominated nearly any other Supreme, meriting the title Invincible Supreme. Until now, only Doomsday Supreme had met the criteria of an Invincible Supreme.

Now, it seemed likely that Lin Fei had joined those ranks.

"Doomsday Supreme brought Supreme Lin to the Forbidden Tower."

"Yes, I saw it too. Even more, the way Doomsday Supreme treats Supreme Lin—it's with a genuine respect as equals."

"It's likely that Doomsday Supreme has already tested Supreme Lin and found him to be an Invincible Supreme. That's probably why he specifically brought Supreme Lin along to the tower."

"Doomsday Supreme most likely came with Supreme Lin to the Forbidden Tower, hoping to tackle the ninth layer together, perhaps aiming to transform their physical forms. At the very least, to achieve the state of Half-Step Chaos!"

The hearts of many Supremes heated up at the thought. Half-Step Chaos! They knew that once someone breached the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower, there was a chance to transform their physical body into a Body of Chaos. At that point, they would truly be a Half-Step Chaos, with even greater strength!

Certainly, the extent to which their strength might increase was of little consequence to individuals like Doomsday Supreme. What truly mattered to them was the transformation into Half-Step Chaos. This meant they were tantalizingly close to achieving the status of Masters of Chaos.

"Can Supreme Lin and Doomsday Supreme successfully conquer the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower?" many wondered as they gazed towards the tower. This time, it was highly probable that Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme, both Invincible Supremes, were joining forces. Such an alliance was no ordinary matter and perhaps, they could truly overcome the ninth layer.

Regardless of the external speculations, inside the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower, Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme had stepped together into the combat zone and were transported to a separate space within the tower. They remained together, not separated.

Yet, despite both being Invincible Supremes, neither of them relaxed their guard; their expressions were gravely serious. They were acutely aware of the formidable opponent they were about to face.

A humming sound reverberated as ripples spread through the air. Lin Fei sensed something and sharply focused his gaze on the void. Within the vast emptiness, rings of light continuously spread out. A terrifying behemoth appeared in the void—a Chaos Behemoth! But this was no ordinary Chaos Behemoth.

Lin Fei could faintly sense an aura emanating from the beast, a presence that dominated over that of a regular Chaos Behemoth, one that even made his heart palpitate. It seemed to be a force that transcended the level of the Supreme. And feeling this presence, Lin Fei experienced a profound suppression, a deep, internal force stifling his very essence—this was not merely a top-tier Chaos Behemoth.

It was the Chaos Behemoth King!

"It really is the Chaos Behemoth King..." Lin Fei murmured under his breath. Although he had visited the Chaos Behemoth King's city previously, he hadn't encountered the Chaos Behemoth King itself, which had still been slumbering at the time. This lack of direct interaction had left Lin Fei without a complete understanding of the Chaos Behemoth King's formidable nature.

Now, having seen the Chaos Behemoth King, the profound sense of suppression emanating from it was unmistakable. It was a force capable of overwhelming Lin Fei at this moment—a force undoubtedly at the level of a Master of Chaos, a life form existing on a higher plane than both Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme.

"Wait, there's more movement..." Doomsday Supreme's eyes suddenly narrowed. Besides the first Chaos Behemoth King, another glimmer of light slowly emerged.

As the light dissipated, it revealed another Chaos Behemoth—a second Chaos Behemoth King! This meant that Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme were now facing not one, but two Chaos Behemoth Kings.

"Two Chaos Behemoth Kings... it seems my previous guess was correct," said Doomsday Supreme. "Supreme Lin, are you ready to test the might of these two Chaos Behemoth Kings?"

Doomsday Supreme's gaze turned towards Lin Fei, filled with anticipation and a trace of apprehension. Clearly, the Chaos Behemoth Kings were formidable adversaries; even one was a formidable challenge. Doomsday Supreme himself, if he were capable of handling them alone, wouldn't have sought Lin Fei's assistance.

One was difficult enough to contend with—what of two?

Doomsday Supreme could only hope that Lin Fei might stand a chance against these twin Behemoths.
