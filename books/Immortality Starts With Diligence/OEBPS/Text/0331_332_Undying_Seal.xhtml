332. Undying Seal

The Blood Seal Divine Ability was just a Lesser Divine Ability, so Lin Fei could easily dominate it. Lin Fei executed his Life Wheel, shining with the brilliance of the four Great Divine Abilities, covering the entire fourth floor of the Martial Tower.

Basically, the Divine Abilities here were at Lin Fei's beck and call. None of them would refuse to become Lin Fei's Divine Ability. After all, the potential demonstrated by the four top-tier Great Divine Abilities was enough evidence of Lin Fei's potential and determination to become the Martial King. Every Divine Ability aspired to achieve immortality, and only by integrating into the bloodline of the Martial King could they be considered truly immortal.

Lin Fei was meticulous in his selection. He personally picked Lesser Divine Abilities to merge with the Blood Seal. The second Divine Ability he chose was named the Blood Sea. This too had a vast power of flesh and blood, similar to a grand ocean, another life-saving Divine Ability.

Lin Fei then merged the two Divine Abilities. However, the integration of Divine Abilities required aligned philosophies. Lin Fei felt these two Divine Abilities had similar philosophies, so they should be able to merge. Still, the actual ability to merge depended on the Divine Abilities themselves.

Time passed, and as Lin Fei integrated about twenty Divine Abilities, the Divine Ability began a significant transformation, evolving from a Lesser Divine Ability into a Great Divine Ability. But this wasn't enough. He aimed for a top-tier Great Divine Ability.

Lin Fei continued the integration process, reaching 21, 23, 25, 28, 30... By the time he integrated 31 Divine Abilities, the Divine Ability trembled slightly. The feedback he received was clear: this was its limit. This Divine Ability could no longer be integrated further.

"Reached the limit? If that's the case, then come inside. Become my Divine Ability, and in the future, birth the Martial King bloodline, and be immortal for eternity!"

As Lin Fei's voice fell, "Whoosh!" instantly, that massive Divine Ability light shot into Lin Fei's body in a flash.

"Boom!"

On Lin Fei's back, a massive Life Wheel suddenly appeared. On the Life Wheel, the original four layers of light shone brilliantly. But now, the fifth layer of light suddenly lit up.

Five layers of light! Five Great Divine Abilities!

Lin Fei cultivated the fifth Divine Ability. At the same time, a set of memories appeared in Lin Fei's mind. It was the memory of the fifth Divine Ability.

"Divine Ability, Undying Seal!" Lin Fei murmured softly. In his mind, all the memories related to the fifth Divine Ability, the Undying Seal, emerged. This Divine Ability was fundamentally different from the four Divine Abilities Lin Fei had practiced before. That is, this Divine Ability is a passive one. No need to actively invoke it. Once this Divine Ability is cultivated, even if it's just at the beginner level, An "Undying Seal" will be formed.

This "Undying Seal" is, in essence, similar to the blood seal. With the Undying Seal, Lin Fei's Vitality becomes extraordinarily terrifying. Even the gravest injuries can almost be fully healed.

"System."

Lin Fei accessed the system, checking the Divine Ability.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Mid-stage of Martial Sovereign

Great Divine Ability: Black Wind Prison: Perfection

Great Divine Ability: Gravity Domain: Perfection

Great Divine Ability: Dual-World Saber: Perfection

Great Divine Ability: Annihilation: Perfection

Great Divine Ability: Undying Seal: Beginner (Progress 0%)

Saber Domain: Transcendence (Progress 1%)

Looking at the system panel, indeed, a new Divine Ability was added. It was clearly the fifth Divine Ability, the Undying Seal! It's just at the beginner level, the cultivation progress is even at 0%.

Lin Fei closed his eyes. It's a passive Divine Ability, he could clearly feel that his body was being transformed. Within his flesh, gradually, a mark began to form. This mark is very special. Once it appeared, it permeated into the flesh and blood. It seemed that every inch of flesh had this mark. This is the Undying Seal!

With the presence of this Undying Seal, Lin Fei's healing and vitality skyrocketed by ten or even dozens of times in an instant. Even with severe injuries, he can recover. Even at the beginner stage, Lin Fei was aware of one feature of this top-tier Great Divine Ability, the Undying Seal. That is the ability to regenerate lost limbs!

"Regeneration of severed limbs..."

A thought stirred within Lin Fei's heart. With a swift "whoosh," a brilliant saber light erupted from him. Instantly, it aimed and slashed at Lin Fei's fingers.

A sharp "splurt" resounded. The intense brilliance of the saber, even when directed at Lin Fei's fingers, effortlessly severed them. Immediately, blood spurted from Lin Fei. His detached fingers lay on the ground.

Yet, Lin Fei lifted his hand, the very one from which fingers had just been severed. Astonishingly, at a speed discernible to the naked eye, the fingers grew back. In a fleeting moment, his hand was restored to its original state, looking exactly as it had before the injury.

A shiver coursed through Lin Fei's heart. He realized that this was the power of the Divine Ability called "Undying Seal" – the ability to regenerate limbs. Under such circumstances, assassinating Lin Fei would be an arduous task.

Of course, if the Undying Seal only granted the ability to regenerate limbs, it certainly wouldn't earn the title of a top-tier Great Divine Ability. It wouldn't befit the moniker of "Undying Body." This was merely the beginner realm of the "Undying Seal."

The Undying Seal Divine Ability had stringent requirements for one's realm. Each realm possessed distinct abilities. For instance, at the beginner realm of Undying Seal, one could regenerate limbs. At the mastery level, one could achieve the ability of flesh proliferation, where every inch of flesh acted as if it were a living entity. This was the essence of flesh proliferation.

At the Transcendence level, it was even more phenomenal. One could achieve the ability to be reborn from a single drop of blood. In other words, as long as one drop of blood remained, full recovery was possible. Such an ability defied the heavens. Unless Lin Fei could be instantly turned to dust, he would remain undying. Still, this couldn't be truly termed as "undying."

There was another level: Perfection. Upon reaching the realm of Perfection, one would achieve true immortality. By then, the Undying Seal would permeate the tiniest particles of one's physical form. As long as these particles persisted, Lin Fei would remain alive.

Even Lin Fei's Annihilation Divine Ability couldn't obliterate these particles. Perhaps, only the supreme techniques of the Martial King could annihilate such particles. At the level of a Martial Sovereign, there was hardly any power that could destroy these physical particles. That is to say, once the "Undying Seal" reached Perfection, Lin Fei would truly be "Undying and Indestructible."

"What a remarkable Undying Seal! Such a Divine Ability truly represents an undying body! If the Undying Seal achieves perfection, who could possibly slay me?"

A glint of sharpness flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. He had prioritized the fifth Divine Ability on the "Undying Body." Clearly, he had chosen the right path. However, one question lingered: How could the Undying Seal achieve perfection?
