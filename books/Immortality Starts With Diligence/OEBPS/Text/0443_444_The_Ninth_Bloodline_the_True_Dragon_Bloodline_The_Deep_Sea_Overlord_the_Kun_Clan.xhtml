444. The Ninth Bloodline, the True Dragon Bloodline! The Deep Sea Overlord, the Kun Clan!

"The blood of the true dragon... isn't it in you?" Lin Fei stared at Green King, making him feel uneasy.

"Master... what are you talking about?" Green King asked with a trembling voice.

"Green King, since you've already crossed the Dragon Gate and are no longer a green carp, but a dragon fish, don't you have the bloodline of the true dragon within you?" Lin Fei inquired.

Green King's heart skipped a beat. Had Lin Fei set his sights on the true dragon bloodline in his body? But the blood of the Azure Flood Dragon King also contained a dragon bloodline.

Regardless, Green King dared not deceive Lin Fei and said bravely, "Master, it's true that I have the bloodline of the true dragon in my body. If you need it, I can offer you some."

Lin Fei, picking up on the implied message, glanced at Azure Flood Dragon King and commented, "The Azure Flood Dragon King has the blood of the azure dragon in him, which is also a dragon bloodline, but it's too diluted. You're different. You've just crossed the Dragon Gate, and your true dragon bloodline is abundant.

Moreover, you can even increase the bloodline in your body. All you need to do is devour, right? Consume whatever exotic beasts you need, as long as they aren't mythical creatures or divine-grade beasts. This way, you can accumulate a vast amount of true dragon blood, and I can extract your bloodline without harming you. Are you willing?"

What could Green King say? Even if Lin Fei decided to completely drain the blood from his body, what could Green King possibly object to? His very life and death were at Lin Fei's whim. So, without hesitation, Green King nodded, "Master, I agree. Why don't you try integrating my bloodline into yours first?"

Accepting the suggestion, Lin Fei made a move. Green King promptly produced a few droplets of essence blood, each drop rich with his potent true dragon bloodline. Lin Fei integrated it into his body, pure and concentrated, freshly taken from Green King who had just transformed.

Soon after, a system notification echoed in Lin Fei's mind.

"Your azure dragon bloodline has absorbed a significant amount of true dragon blood, increasing the cultivation progress by 50%. Your azure dragon bloodline was too diluted. With the influx of true dragon blood, it's becoming diluted and is gradually transforming into a true dragon bloodline..."

"System."

Lin Fei immediately accessed the system to check.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Late-stage Martial King

Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline: Perfection

Grand Yin Wind Dao Bloodline: Perfection

Triple Yin True Fire Bloodline: Perfection

Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline: Perfection

Annihilation Frost Bloodline: Perfection

Earth Pulse Divine Mountain Spirit Communication Bloodline: Perfection

Endless Vitality Green Wood Bloodline: Perfection

Phoenix Bloodline: Perfection

True Dragon Bloodline: Beginner (82%)

Looking at the system panel, Lin Fei noticed that he had a total of nine bloodlines. However, the ninth one, originally the Azure Dragon bloodline, had transformed into the True Dragon bloodline.

This was the first time Lin Fei had encountered such a situation. It was likely due to the fact that the Azure Dragon bloodline was a derivative of the True Dragon bloodline.

The change was probably influenced by the dominant True Dragon bloodline from Green King. At the moment, the True Dragon bloodline was just at a beginner level. However, Lin Fei had only absorbed a single drop of essence blood. There were more to come...

Lin Fei continued to merge with the essence blood from Green King.

"Your True Dragon bloodline has progressed to Mastery!"

As Lin Fei incorporated more of the True Dragon bloodline, it quickly reached mastery. However, after reaching mastery, the progression slowed down. Especially after ten drops, Green King seemed to be at his limit.

"Master, I..."

Understanding that Green King was reaching his breaking point, Lin Fei stopped. "Green King, that's enough for today. Go and devour the sea beasts. Accumulate ten drops of essence blood and bring them to me. If you encounter a sea beast that's too difficult to handle, come find me. But with your strength, I believe you'll manage."

Lin Fei gave his orders, and Green King responded, "Yes, Master." He then left immediately, even taking his subordinates with him. As for escaping, that was impossible.

If Lin Fei sensed Green King straying too far, he could annihilate him with a mere thought. Plus, with the connection of their bloodlines, there was a deep bond that would alert Lin Fei of any mischief.

So, Lin Fei took a brief rest in the ancestral land of the Azure Flood Dragon clan. Meanwhile, the Azure Flood Dragon King began to settle the members of his clan, ensuring their propagation and selecting a new Azure Flood Dragon King. After all, the Azure Flood Dragon King couldn't stay on the island forever. He was Lin Fei's servant and had to return to guard Great Chang.

Green King became even more brutal. At first, he was cautious, slowly expanding his territory. Under the guise of territorial expansion, he devoured some sea beasts, making it hard for other sea beasts to notice anything unusual. They were unaware that he was trying to increase his True Dragon bloodline.

However, after being defeated by Lin Fei and becoming his servant, Green King no longer bothered to conceal his intentions and showed no restraint.

Whenever he encountered a sea beast that could enhance his True Dragon bloodline, he would devour it immediately. His strength was formidable, making him a match for even the top king-level exotic beasts. It's probable that only ancient rare species could challenge him.

Thus, in the nearby seas, Green King acted with impunity, wildly consuming countless sea beasts. But he didn't gain much from these actions himself. Every time his True Dragon bloodline surged, he felt closer to his transformation into a dragon.

However, he had to crystallize the essence blood and hand it over to Lin Fei. Consequently, while Lin Fei's strength kept increasing, Green King remained stagnant. He even began to doubt if he would ever successfully transform into a dragon.

However, Green King's reckless devouring of sea beasts eventually angered the titans among them. Those who had been lurking in the deep seas for tens of thousands, if not hundreds of thousands, of years—genuine rulers of the deep, beings of myths and legends.

At this moment, at the bottom of the deep sea, many sea beasts gathered, seemingly lamenting about something. Their cries echoed through the depths, "If the Ancestral Spirit has grace, we hope he awakens."

"Great Ancestor, if you don't wake up, we will be completely devoured."

"That Green King is too domineering. He's like a bottomless pit, crazily consuming the exotic beasts in the ocean."

"Our Kun clan is on the verge of extinction."

"Great Ancestor, are you still there?"

This was the lament of the Kun clan from the depths of the sea. Legends spoke of a clan known as Kun in the deep sea. Their ancestor, a ruler of the deep sea, was the legendary Kun from myths.

But nobody had ever seen this Kun. Even members of the Kun clan hadn't seen him. They had only heard rumors passed down through generations that their ancestor Kun was in deep slumber within the ocean depths, and they were instructed not to disturb him. But now, facing the threat of extermination, they had no choice but to seek help from their legendary ancestor.
