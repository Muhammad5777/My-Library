115. High Level Qi Condensation Cultivators

"Mm?"

He Yuanzi and He Qingzi's eyes slightly narrowed.

"Layman Lin knew we were coming, yet he calmly stayed here. It seems Layman Lin is quite confident. Originally, we didn't mind the insignificant disciple you killed, as you have the princess and Nine Profound Sect on your side. He Mountain Dao didn't care.

But He Yunzi, you shouldn't have killed! He Yunzi was the future hope of our He Mountain Dao. Now, our sect leader is furious. So, we have to use Layman Lin's head to pay tribute to He Yunzi!"

The two Daoists spoke of murder, yet their tone was nonchalant as if it was merely an ordinary matter, not taking it to heart at all.

"Using my head to pay tribute to He Yunzi. Well said. If I were the He Mountain Dao sect leader, I would do the same. However, there's one point you may have overlooked. Can you actually kill me?"

The next moment, Lin Fei slowly stood up. At the same time, the saber intent within him exploded instantly.

"Boom."

Lin Fei's saber intent erupted. To ordinary people, they wouldn't be able to sense the saber intent. But they could feel Lin Fei transforming into a supreme treasure saber, suddenly revealing his sharpness, imposingly aggressive.

"Hum."

The next moment, the sabers in the guards' hands began to hum softly as if they wanted to unsheathe themselves.

"What's going on?"

"My saber seems to want to unsheathe itself."

"What kind of spell is this?"

These guards were utterly astonished. They didn't know what was happening.

"No... it's not a spell, it's not a spell at all. If I'm not mistaken, this is saber intent! The legendary Martial Dao True Intent that only martial artists can cultivate. Unexpectedly, this man has actually cultivated Martial Dao True Intent, even daring to confront two Qi cultivators, it's... unbelievable!"

Speaking was the young lady, her face full of disbelief. Although she hadn't practiced martial arts, she had been well-educated since childhood and she was quite interested in martial arts. Therefore, she had read many martial arts secrets and she knew some secrets of martial arts.

Lin Fei's display of saber intent at this moment caused the sabers of the many guards to "hum" loudly as if they couldn't control their sabers. This was characteristic of saber intent! In front of a martial artist who comprehended saber intent, drawing a saber was a luxury.

At this moment, in the eyes of He Yuanzi and He Qingzi, a phantom great saber appeared above Lin Fei's head. That was the saber intent!

Lin Fei didn't display his fist intent. He knew that in terms of lethality, fist intent was ultimately inferior to saber intent. Facing high-level Qi Condensation cultivators, Lin Fei still "respected" his opponents. Not underestimating them in the slightest, he fully unleashed his saber intent!

"What a saber intent!"

A glint flashed in He Yuanzi's eyes. At the same time, He Yuanzi and He Qingzi both displayed their magical artifacts, protecting themselves by their side.

"Slash!"

Lin Fei's saber intent fiercely slashed down.

"Boom."

He Yuanzi and He Qingzi felt their minds shake.

Their willpower was quite strong. Moreover, they didn't have any mental flaws. But even so, they felt as if their minds were being torn apart by a raging storm and painful to the extreme. Saber intent wasn't that easy to endure. Even if they could withstand it, it would still be very painful.

However, He Yuanzi and He Qingzi eventually resisted Lin Fei's saber intent.

He Yuanzi's face was a bit pale, and he smiled coldly, "Do you know the difference between high-level Qi Condensation cultivators and low-level ones? The biggest difference is actually the spiritual power within their bodies. When a Qi Condensation cultivator reaches the seventh level, their spiritual power can circulate autonomously.

Even if their consciousness stops, their spiritual power can still circulate. That is to say, no matter how strong your saber intent is, as long as it doesn't kill us in an instant, you can't break through our magical artifact defense. Our spiritual power will keep circulating without any pause. Acquired martial artists may be able to kill low-level Qi Condensation cultivators by relying on some tactics or luck. But acquired martial artists will never be able to kill high-level Qi Condensation cultivators!"

He Yuanzi and He Qingzi resisted Lin Fei's saber intent. In their opinion, Lin Fei had no hope left. He was undoubtedly going to die! Because no matter what, Lin Fei, an acquired martial artist, couldn't break through their magical artifact defense. This was the advantage of high-level Qi Condensation cultivators!

"High-level Qi Condensation cultivators..."

Lin Fei calmly looked at He Yuanzi and He Qingzi. The opponents were high-level Qi Condensation cultivators. Indeed, they were somewhat different from He Yunzi.

Although He Yunzi was at the sixth level of Qi Condensation, when he encountered Lin Fei, his consciousness was heavily damaged by the saber intent.

He Yunzi's spiritual power stopped for a moment. Then Lin Fei found an opportunity and killed him with a single saber strike. But facing high-level Qi Condensation cultivators, Lin Fei couldn't find any "opening" anymore.

When martial artists vs. high-level Qi Condensation cultivators, There was only one way to defeat them. That was to fight them head-on, breaking the high-level Qi Condensation cultivators' magical artifacts or spells.

Lin Fei suddenly withdrew his saber intent. He no longer used his saber intent. Apparently, his current mastery realm saber intent only had a few effects on high-level Qi Condensation cultivators. As for directly killing with saber intent, Lin Fei's saber intent hadn't reached that level yet.

"Have you given up? An acquired realm cultivator who can comprehend saber intent, your talent probably rivals that of the founder of the Nine Profound Sect. But unfortunately, you are still mortal. How can a mortal body fight against us Qi Condensation cultivators?"

He Yuanzi stepped towards Lin Fei, one step at a time. Beside He Yuanzi, a small sword floated. It was not a talisman but a real magical artifact flying sword. High-level Qi Condensation cultivators could use it to kill from a distance.

Even innate martial artists were extremely wary of flying swords. The flying sword emitted a chilling and sharp aura. As long as He Yuanzi had a thought, he could instantly pierce Lin Fei's head.

"Whoosh."

A soft sound.

It wasn't He Yuanzi who used the flying sword, but Lin Fei.

Lin Fei surprisingly didn't retreat but advanced, even rushing towards He Yuanzi.

"The so-called bravery of a martial artist is nothing more than a futile struggle before death."

He Yuanzi shook his head slightly.

"Go."

He Yuanzi had a thought. Immediately, the flying sword turned into a flash of white light. It instantly flew towards Lin Fei.

At this moment, Lin Fei was only dozens of meters away from He Yuanzi, but this short distance seemed like an insurmountable chasm. He Yuanzi seemed to be able to "see" the look of despair and fear on Lin Fei's face at the end.

The flying sword arrived in an instant. Not only was the flying sword in Lin Fei's line of sight but also He Yuanzi, who was dozens of meters away.

Suddenly, Lin Fei stopped. At this distance, Lin Fei felt it was about right.

The next moment, Lin Fei extended his fair right hand. He didn't draw his saber. Instead, he lifted his hand.

"Slash!"

Lin Fei let out a low shout. His hand slashed fiercely through the air, aimed at He Yuanzi.
