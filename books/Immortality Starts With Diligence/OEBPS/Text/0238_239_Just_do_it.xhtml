239. Just do it

A note from RighteousEgg

Monsters Doctor(Litrpg)

[This is a world steeped in mystique.]

[Vampires, werewolves, technology, elixirs, ghosts, bioengineered beings, divination, and puppets; every terrifying element you could conceive of, existed in this world.]

[One must carry a lantern when stepping outside.]

[One must not wander far.]

[Ten meters beyond the gates, the darkness would consume you.]

[Whether attending to humans, ghosts, or stitched monsters, one must maintain composure.]

[Should you lose your composure, malicious intent would arise.]

In the Law Enforcement Hall, thirteen Martial Saints were all motionless now. Looking at Lin Fei, they looked a bit complicated.

Strong! Obviously, they didn't expect Lin Fei the First Elder to be so dominant! If they disagreed, they had to take action. Those who might be dissatisfied with Lin Fei were probably only the three Limit Martial Saints.

After all, their strength was comparable to Lin Fei's. However, they also knew very well. Lin Fei had saber momentum and it was transcendence. Coupled with Lin Fei's Qi Flower, it was not inferior to them either. If they really wanted to do it, the three Limit Martial Saints, none of them were confident that they could beat Lin Fei. If there was the strength to surpass Lin Fei, Wind Sovereign wouldn't have asked for Lin Fei immediately.

Seeing no one speaking, Lin Fei nodded and said coldly, "Since no one is making a move, they are acknowledging me. Once we leave the Ancient Origin Sect, my orders, you can't have any doubts. Otherwise, my saber will not just be slashing at the enemy. If there are any doubts, we will talk about it when we return to the Ancient Origin Sect. Set off!"

Lin Fei immediately turned around and left the Ancient Origin Sect. The thirteen Martial Saints looked at each other. However, they still followed immediately.

Even if Lin Fei was a bit dominant, they were all Martial Saints of the Law Enforcement Hall. Naturally, they cannot violate the orders of the Law Enforcement Hall. Moreover, they all knew this time was to go to Illusion Worry City. They had actually been to that place. Even if they hadn't been, they all heard about it.

The Illusion Worry City was not simple. If it was not dominant enough, they were afraid the task really couldn't be completed. Therefore, the more dominant Lin Fei was, the more reassuring it was for everyone.

......

Illusion Worry City is busy and unusually prosperous. However, the people who came here would hide their identity. No one wants to reveal their identity. Even if Illusion Worry City is safe, once one leaves here, it's not necessarily safe. Therefore, everyone who enters Illusion Worry City, whether it's a martial artist or a Qi cultivator, uses aliases.

At this moment, in the distant void, dozens of streaming lights flew in. And although there was no revealing of momentum, there was terrifying pressure. Obviously, they were all Martial Saints. After all, those who can walk in the air, at least are all Martial Saints.

"Swoosh".

More than a dozen Martial Saints suddenly landed outside Illusion Worry City. It attracted the attention of many people.

"Why are there so many Martial Saints?"

"And they all look very powerful, definitely not ordinary Martial Saints."

"Looking at their clothes, they seem to be Martial Saints of the Ancient Origin Sect."

"They didn't want to hide their identities at all."

"Ancient Origin Sect Martial Saints, more than a dozen came all at once, what are they going to do?"

"Are there any of our people in Illusion Worry City?" Lin Fei asked.

"Of course. We also have secret informants in Illusion Worry City. This secret informant, I'm afraid it's not a secret to the two city lords of Illusion Worry City. Not only do we have them, Profound Light Cave Heaven and Red Hades Mansion also have informants."

Lin Fei nodded and said, "Then let's go ask the informant about the specific situation in Illusion Worry City first."

So, Lin Fei and his group directly came to a courtyard. The courtyard gate opened. Two innate martial artists came out from inside. Seeing Lin Fei and the others, they showed joy on their faces. They immediately saluted and said, "Greetings all the Law Enforcement Hall Elders."

Lin Fei and others entered the courtyard.

"Are there only the two of you? Have you found out who is the real killer of our Ancient Origin Sect disciples?" Lin Fei asked directly.

Since Wind Sovereign asked him to come to Illusion Worry City, there must be clear clues. Otherwise, why would they make such a big movement and put up such a big formation?

"Reporting to First Elder, there are indeed specific clues."

The two innate martial artists said all the information they had investigated in Illusion Worry City. It turns out that the killer who killed the three Ancient Origin Sect's true disciples had indeed been found. They were three Foundation Establishment cultivators and they were not obscure in their fame.

They were called Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals!

Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals, their names seem to be full of immortal charm, but they are undoubtedly demonic heads in the immortal dao. They are all top Foundation Establishment True Persons.

Facing the threat of heavenly tribulation plus the arrival of the end, they were almost unscrupulous and extreme in their actions. They killed three Ancient Origin Sect true disciples just because the three Ancient Origin Sect true disciples discovered a cave house left by a Golden Core True Person.

As a result, they were killed by the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals. Later, they were afraid that the incident would be revealed, and the Ancient Origin Sect would not let them go. They immediately fled back to Illusion Worry City and they have never left. They plan to even cross the tribulation in Illusion Worry City.

"First Elder, there is no sign that the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals are leaving Illusion Worry City. Even, they may stay in Illusion Worry City until the end comes, and then they will make a desperate move to cross the tribulation. We probably can't force them to leave Illusion Worry City by any means."

The Martial Saints of the Law Enforcement Hall all frowned. They seemed to be at a loss.

"We are here to arrest the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals. Since we've found them, then just do it! If they resist, kill them directly!"

Lin Fei stood up, his eyes cold and his tone calm.
