267. Pefect Saber Momentum

"Bang!"

Lin Fei's strike was incredibly fast. From the moment he evolved his Rainbow Transformation Saber Momentum to the slashing of his blade, it was all done in a fleeting moment. With the enhancement of his newfound saber momentum, the speed of his slash wasn't just a mere increase. The speed of his saber technique multiplied manifold, thereby multiplying its power — even tenfold!

As such, when his strike landed on the Great Mountain Seal, it paused ever so slightly. Then, the luminance of the Great Mountain Seal dimmed instantly.

"Crack."

Surprisingly, cracks began to appear all over the massive Great Mountain Seal.

"How could this be?"

"The Great Mountain Seal is breaking apart?"

"This is a merger of over twenty Great Mountain Seals, including five that are at the Golden Core level!"

"How can anyone below the Golden Core True Person break the Great Mountain Seal?"

For a moment, every Foundation Establishment True Person from Taiyi Dao Palace was stunned.

"Boom."

Finally, the Great Mountain Seal shattered, scattering back into individual seals, their luminance dim. Even if they weren't destroyed, they were severely damaged. They couldn't be used for some time and would require significant nurturing before they could be activated again.

Witnessing this, whether it was the people from Taiyi Dao Palace or those from Red Hades Mansion and Profound Light Cave Heaven, a single word resonated in their minds.

Invincible!

Invincible below the Golden Core!

In the ancient continent, it was challenging to label anyone as invincible. No matter who, no matter the realm, there always seemed to be someone stronger lurking in the shadows.

"Leave, let's go now!"

The people of Taiyi Dao Palace, without a second thought, decided to flee immediately. Their combined force, even with the fusion of the Great Mountain Seals, couldn't handle Lin Fei. Staying would mean certain death.

"Those who obstruct my path will be shown no mercy!"

Lin Fei's eyes were cold, emitting an intense killing intent. The people from Taiyi Dao Palace had delayed him for a significant amount of time. Considering the impending closure of the immortal mansion, they had effectively hindered his progress. Naturally, Lin Fei would show no mercy.

"Swish, swish, swish."

With a flick of his fingers, Lin Fei sent out multiple streaks of saber essence hurtling towards the Foundation Establishment True Persons of Taiyi Dao Palace. Now, under Lin Fei's incredible saber momentum, every streak of saber essence was further augmented in speed. Their velocity was almost inconceivable.

His saber essence reached cultivators in virtually an instant, a testament to the enhancement of the saber momentum. Lin Fei was beginning to understand the difference between the saber momentum derived from martial saint-level techniques and those from innate-level techniques.

When martial saint-level techniques evolved into saber momentum, they often incorporated a unique power. For instance, when the Flame Burning Heaven Saber's momentum was unleashed, it engulfed everything in flames, making it feel like one was trapped in purgatory. The Rainbow Transformation Saber's momentum, on the other hand, boosted Lin Fei's saber speed, amplifying its swiftness manifold.

This is why every single streak of Lin Fei's saber essence landed on these cultivators, cutting through their defenses.

"Splurt...sizzle."

Even if Lin Fei manifested only a single streak of saber essence, its potency was awe-inspiring. Most of the cultivators couldn't defend against it. Some didn't even have the time to react, unable to even cast their protective spells before they were split in half by the power of Lin Fei's saber essence. A few managed to barely summon their damaged Great Mountain Seal, but the seals, with their dimmed spiritual light, were instantly shattered by Lin Fei's blade.

Out of more than twenty elite Foundation Establishment True Persons, only five managed to escape. Those five managed to survive only because they had the Golden Core level Great Mountain Seals, which barely held off Lin Fei's saber essence.

"Quick, we need to leave the immortal mansion at the fastest speed possible!"

The remaining five cultivators were in a frenzied panic, desperate to escape without lingering within the confines of the mansion. They were willing to pay any price just to get out. Once outside the mansion, they could find safety under the protection of the Golden Core True Persons of Taiyi Dao Palace.

Inside the mansion, an eerie silence took over. The representatives from the Red Hades Mansion and Profound Light Cave Heaven seemed to still be in shock, staying motionless.

Lin Fei glanced at the people from the Red Hades Mansion and Profound Light Cave Heaven. These individuals hadn't acted against him, so he had no intention of wasting further time on them. He sat down cross-legged and declared impassively, "Leave or die."

Profound Light Cave Heaven and Red Hades Mansion had a unique relationship with the Ancient Origin Sect, and since they hadn't taken action, Lin Fei didn't want to go to the extreme of slaughtering them.

"Leave!" The people from Red Hades Mansion and Profound Light Cave Heaven dared not linger any longer and fled from the immortal mansion's prison as if escaping for their lives.

Once more, the prison fell into silence, leaving Lin Fei alone with the scattered bodies on the ground. No storage bags were to be found near the corpses, likely held by the five cultivators who had Golden Core-level magic treasures.

However, Lin Fei had no intention of profiting from these bodies. He had something more crucial at hand – the continued honing of his Holy Spirit Saber Intent.

At present, the saber intent of his Holy Spirit Saber reached 97% completion. It was approaching 100%, and Lin Fei was almost there.

Soon, Lin Fei immersed his mind in his practice and stepped closer to the prison. As he did, he could see not corpses, but a horrifying slash mark within the prison, radiating intense saber intent and momentum.

A single cut, emitting such terrifying saber momentum, made Lin Fei's heart shudder. Determined to quickly polish his Holy Spirit Saber Intent to perfection, he gritted his teeth and approached the prison gate.

"Boom!"

The terrifying saber intent and momentum struck him immediately.

"Hum."

Lin Fei's Holy Spirit Saber Intent erupted at once, seeming on the verge of collapse. However, Lin Fei's control over the saber momentum allowed him to isolate its crushing pressure just before the intent collapsed. It was a risky move, but the benefits were clear.

98%, 99%, 100%...

Finally, the saber intent of Lin Fei's Holy Spirit Saber reached 100%.

"Your Holy Spirit Saber Intent has reached a cultivation progress of 100%."

"Your Holy Spirit Saber Intent has achieved perfection!"

At the pinnacle of perfection with the Holy Spirit Saber Intent, Lin Fei immediately commanded, "Evolve saber momentum."

Without hesitation, the saber intent began to evolve its momentum. A radiant white light emanated from within Lin Fei, filling the space with a sense of the divine, weightiness, and mystery. Being enveloped in this glow would amplify one's war intention, courage, and much more. This was a kind of saber momentum that could amplify one's soul and emotions—a rarity indeed.

"Three saber techniques, three types of saber momentum, done!" A unique glint appeared in Lin Fei's eyes.

"Merge."

The crucial step had arrived. Lin Fei immediately integrated the Holy Spirit Saber Momentum into his Heavenly Saber Momentum. The two forms of saber momentum intertwined, clashing and merging with one another. As they did, Lin Fei's progress in cultivating his saber momentum accelerated rapidly.

"Boom."

Perhaps much time had passed, or perhaps it was just a mere instant, but Lin Fei felt the Holy Spirit Saber Momentum vanish, merging fully into the Heavenly Saber Momentum. Simultaneously, Lin Fei's Transcendent Saber Momentum broke through its threshold, undergoing a complete metamorphosis.

"Your saber momentum cultivation progress has reached 100%."

"Your saber momentum has broken through to perfection!"

At this moment, Lin Fei's saber momentum had finally achieved perfection!
