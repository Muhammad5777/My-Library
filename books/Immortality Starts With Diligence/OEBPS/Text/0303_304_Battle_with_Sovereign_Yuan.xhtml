304. Battle with Sovereign Yuan

At the Xia Family's martial arts arena, Xia Xuan looked at Ring Sovereign and laughingly said, "Ring Sovereign, why do you wish to battle with Sovereign Yuan again? You've already lost twice. What treasures could you possibly have left to wager?"

Xia Xuan was in high spirits. Being from the Xia family, she had awakened her bloodline three times and possessed three Great Divine Abilities as a Martial Sovereign. They all belonged to the same group of prestigious Martial King families. Within this group, there was a hierarchy and competition. Ordinarily, Xia Xuan and Ring Sovereign weren't on the best of terms. Having won against Ring Sovereign twice, Xia Xuan naturally felt ecstatic.

Ring Sovereign's face darkened, and he coldly retorted, "Xia Xuan, today will be the last time. And for this last time, I intend to win back everything I've lost!"

"Is that so? So confident. Will you be sending Aurora Sovereign into the fray, or will you step up yourself?" Xia Xuan asked, her eyes falling upon Aurora Sovereign, assuming Ring Sovereign's newfound confidence came from inviting her.

"Neither," Ring Sovereign turned to Lin Fei, "Sovereign Lin, please."

Lin Fei nodded and flew directly onto the platform.

With a swoosh, Sovereign Yuan opened his eyes. In a soft voice, Xia Xuan whispered, "Sovereign Yuan, if we can defeat this Sovereign Lin, perhaps we can end this sooner. This time, both Ring Sovereign and Aurora Sovereign have wagered a lot of treasures. Of course, we cannot lose. If we do, most of the treasures we've won previously will be lost."

Evidently, Xia Xuan was gearing up for a big win, but with the understanding that a loss would come at a great cost.

"I will not lose! As long as he does not surpass the power of three Divine Abilities, I cannot be defeated!" Sovereign Yuan replied with a calm face but a determined voice. Subsequently, he stepped onto the platform.

Although it's called a platform, it wasn't particularly safeguarded. After all, what barrier could possibly withstand their immense power? Even the top-notch barriers would be instantly torn apart by their Divine Abilities. They chose this location primarily because of the spaciousness of the martial arts arena.

There were many spectators around the arena. When Lin Fei and Sovereign Yuan took their positions on the platform, the eyes of many in the Xia family brightened.

"It's Sovereign Yuan again. I wonder where Xia Xuan found such a competitor."

"He seems to have a nineteen-win streak, right?"

"Yes, he's already defeated nineteen Sovereigns. What's more incredible is that these weren't ordinary Sovereigns but those with the Martial King bloodline, the elites of Martial King families."

"Does anyone know Sovereign Yuan's background?"

"That unfamiliar Sovereign seems to have been brought by Ring Sovereign and Aurora Sovereign from the Xiao family."

"Ring Sovereign has already lost twice. She must be getting desperate to come for the third time."

"Pity though, she'll likely lose again."

The members of the Xia family were already quite familiar with Sovereign Yuan. They knew of Sovereign Yuan's terrifying strength. Nearly all who challenged him met with defeat.

At that moment, in the arena, Lin Fei was also sizing up Sovereign Yuan. The whispers from the Xia family martial artists, Lin Fei naturally overheard.

Sovereign Yuan had defeated nineteen Martial Sovereigns. And these were Martial Sovereigns who possessed the bloodline of Martial Kings. That was no small feat!

"To think Ring Sovereign and Aurora Sovereign specifically invited you, Your strength must indeed be formidable. Ring Sovereign and Aurora Sovereign, they're well aware of my capabilities, but they still chose to trust you, letting you challenge me. You surely have something to rely on. Then, display it all. Otherwise, you won't have another chance."

Sovereign Yuan's tone was even, exuding confidence as if victory was already in his grasp. Regardless of Lin Fei's methods, He was certain of his triumph.

Lin Fei gazed at Sovereign Yuan, A faint smile crept up the corner of his lips. For some unknown reason, he saw a reflection of himself in Sovereign Yuan. Sovereign Yuan was so much like Lin Fei, Only, Lin Fei wasn't one for so much talk.

"Boom!"

In the blink of an eye, Lin Fei made his move. A radiant white light enveloped the entire arena. Suddenly, the skies were filled with flames and the gleam of sabers. The sharp edge of the saber seemed as if it was slicing through Sovereign Yuan.

"This is... the Saber Domain!"

For the first time, Sovereign Yuan's complexion shifted. The Saber Domain! He had actually encountered a Martial Sovereign with a domain.

"Boom!"

Without hesitation, Sovereign Yuan erupted with his own radiant aura. The Sword Domain! Furthermore, Sovereign Yuan's Sword Domain was pure. Within the Sword Domain, it seemed there was nothing but swords, razor-sharp swords. Swords capable of cutting through everything.

Immediately, the two luminous forces intertwined and clashed. The Sword Domain and Saber Domain were in constant collision.

"At last, I've met a Martial Sovereign who has comprehended a domain. I had long anticipated this day. While domains are difficult to grasp, there will always be those who do. If you believe that just any Martial Sovereign with a domain can contend with me, you are sorely mistaken. Because my domain has already Transcended!"

As Sovereign Yuan's words echoed, the power of his Sword Domain astonishingly kept intensifying. A Transcendent Sword Domain was indeed terrifying. The skies were filled with sword lights!

In truth, Lin Fei's Saber Domain was mighty. His Saber Domain, infused with countless saber momentums, had evolved into its current form. Even if it was merely in its beginning stages, A typical domain, even if mastered, might not match up to the Saber Domain. Yet, when faced with the Transcendent Sword Domain, Lin Fei's Saber Domain was inevitably at a disadvantage.

"Hum."

However, Lin Fei's repertoire was not limited to just the Saber Domain. His Saber Domain might be in its nascent phase, But he also possessed a Divine Ability! And it was a top-tier Great Divine Ability at that.

"Gravity Domain!"

Suddenly, behind Lin Fei, a massive Life Wheel materialized. On the Life Wheel, two layers of intense light flickered briefly. A profound gravitational force instantly acted upon Sovereign Yuan.

Furthermore, Lin Fei's Gravity Domain merged with the Saber Domain. The tide of battle shifted immediately. The saber momentum astonishingly surged, suddenly suppressing Sovereign Yuan's Sword Domain.

"This kind of Divine Ability..."

Sovereign Yuan felt a jolt in his heart. Such a Divine Ability was immensely powerful. In just a moment, His physical body began to struggle, even starting to crumble, bleeding profusely.

"Hum."

Behind Sovereign Yuan, a grand Life Wheel likewise emerged, radiating a three-layered light. This implied that Sovereign Yuan had cultivated three Divine Abilities. One more than even Lin Fei's.

"Such a powerful Divine Ability. Divine Ability, Vajra Unbreakable Chains!"

One layer of Divine Ability light on Sovereign Yuan's Life Wheel also started to shimmer. Subsequently, on Sovereign Yuan, Chains, one after another, appeared.

"Clang clang."

The chains vibrated intensely. This too was a Great Divine Ability, Moreover, a defensively formidable one at that. With the chains materialized the Sovereign Yuan was no longer suppressed. His physical body began rapidly healing.

"Blaze."

With a mere thought from Lin Fei, On the surface of those chains wrapping Sovereign Yuan, flames ignited. The fire roared intensely.

"Futile. My Vajra Unbreakable Chains are a Great Divine Ability. Once deployed, there's no force capable of damaging them. Can mere flames harm me?"

Sovereign Yuan let out a cold chuckle. His Vajra Unbreakable Chains had never let him down.

"Is that so?"

Lin Fei's Gravity Domain sharply contracted, Instantaneously narrowing to just a foot around Sovereign Yuan. The contraction of the Gravity Domain signified a gravitational force magnified several or even tenfold!

"Crack."

A crisp sound.

Broken! The chains around Sovereign Yuan suddenly snapped!
