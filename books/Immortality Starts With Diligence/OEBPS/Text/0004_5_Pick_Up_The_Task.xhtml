5. Pick Up The Task

Lin Fei had a courtyard of his own. The reason why outer disciples were able to have courtyards was simple: wealth.

In Lin Fei's memory, his family should be considered wealthy merchants. Therefore, he was able to buy a courtyard at the Nine Profound Sect.

In the courtyard, Lin Fei heated water by himself. He poured the purchased medicinal herbs into the wooden barrel.

Lin Fei jumped directly into the barrel.

Immediately, Lin Fei felt as if all the pores in his body had opened. Warm currents flowed throughout his body, making him feel extremely comfortable.

"Your Vajra Iron Cloth has detected the nourishing medicinal bath and has begun to practice."

Lin Fei also felt that his skin was itchy and painful, and sometimes red spots would appear. This was due to the practice of Vajra Iron Cloth.

"Your Vajra Iron Cloth is in a great mood, and the practice speed is doubled."

"Your Vajra Iron Cloth has reached the second level."

"Splash."

Lin Fei stood up from the hot water barrel. He looked at the skin on his arm. It felt rough to the touch. But it was very tough.

"Vajra Iron Cloth has reached the second level just like that?"

Lin Fei couldn't help but smile. He had practiced the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique before. He knew very well how difficult it was to practice Body Refining martial arts. It took him two years to step into the second level of the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique.

And now?

In just one day, his Vajra Iron Cloth had reached the second level.

Of course, this must be related to the abundant qi and blood of the Giant Whale Technique. But Vajra Iron Cloth itself was also quite impressive. The more unique the martial art, the more impressive it actually is.

It all depends on whether Lin Fei can satisfy all these martial arts. Only then will these martial arts "practice hard."

So, in the following days, Lin Fei worked hard to find ways to make the martial arts in his body "satisfied."

Lin Fei found that the most stable martial arts were the Giant Whale Technique and Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist. They could practice autonomously every day without doing anything else. Although they couldn't make huge progress all at once, their practice steadily improved.

Nine Shadow Saber Technique and Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique were the quirkiest.

Without Buddhist scriptures or Taoist scriptures, Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique had almost no improvement.

Nine Shadow Saber Technique had improved to proficient since acquiring a saber.

But then it stopped again. It didn't improve any further. It seemed that this Nine Shadow Saber Technique valued "enlightenment" very much.

It was necessary to let the Nine Shadow Saber Technique gain more "enlightenment."

The Nine Profound Sect had some senior masters. They could impart some martial arts experience. However, this required contribution points.

If the contribution points were sufficient, one could even specifically ask a certain master for guidance.

Lin Fei felt that if he could invite a master of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique to give him guidance, his Nine Shadow Saber Technique would definitely make rapid progress.

Unfortunately, he didn't have any contribution points.

As for using silver?

Lin Fei didn't have enough silver now. He had almost used it all to practice martial arts and buy medicinal baths. A few days later, Lin Fei's medicinal baths were all consumed.

"System."

Lin Fei brought up the system panel.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Body Refining Fourth Level

Giant Whale Body Refining Technique: Fourth Level (31% Progress)

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Beginner (61% Progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Proficient (1% Progress)

Vajra Iron Cloth: Third Level (16% Progress)

Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique: First Level (5% Progress)

Among all of Lin Fei's martial arts, the most significant progress was made by Vajra Iron Cloth. It was served with a large number of medicinal baths every day, making it very comfortable. In just a few days, it had already reached the third layer.

However, after reaching the third layer, the progress slowed down. Most importantly, Lin Fei didn't have any more medicinal baths.

"No contribution points, no silver, no medicinal baths. If I continue to stay in the mountain, my martial arts progress will be slow in the future."

After weighing the situation for a moment, Lin Fei decided to leave the mountain. Staying in the mountain, his daily progress was limited. But if he went down the mountain, he could earn contribution points and even go home for silver. With resources, he could soar to new heights.

"So, should I go home for silver or go down the mountain to do missions and earn contribution points?"

Lin Fei preferred going down the mountain to take silver. Although it would be expensive to buy things within the sect with silver, doing missions would entail certain risks.

Lin Fei was still very weak now. There was no need to rush to take on missions and risk his life.

However, Lin Fei still went to the Mission Hall.

Lin Fei looked at the dazzling array of missions in the mission hall, his eyes shining slightly.

Most of the missions in the mission hall were to support certain places. Actually, these represented the power of the Nine Profound Sect.

The Nine Profound Sect was a martial arts sect, not a charitable organization. The sect occupied a vast territory and had branches and sub-divisions in various places to conduct business and other activities, thus obtaining numerous resources.

But where there were people, there were disputes. With disputes, fighting and killing would follow. These support missions could earn a large number of contribution points.

However, they were too dangerous.

Lin Fei felt that it was better for him to prioritize safety at his current stage.

"If there are no suitable missions, I'll go down the mountain first and get some silver. Using silver to exchange for contribution points is a loss, but at least it's safe. However, disciples below the seventh level of Body Refining are not allowed to go down the mountain easily unless they have a mission..."

Lin Fei felt a headache. He still needed to accept a mission.

Otherwise, even going down the mountain would be a luxury.

"Lu Mountain: Collect ten Black-Backed Centipedes. Time limit: two months."

When Lin Fei saw this mission, his eyes lit up.

"Isn't Lu Mountain the mountain range where Lu Mountain County is located? My Lin family's old home is in Lu Mountain County. Although we moved to the Zhou Mountain Mansion City after becoming wealthy, the city is not far from Lu Mountain.

Besides, this mission is just a collection mission, not a support mission, so it shouldn't be dangerous. However, these mere one hundred contribution points are really too few, but better than nothing."

After thinking for a moment, Lin Fei directly accepted the mission. To be more precise, he was going home to get some silver and complete this mission along the way.
