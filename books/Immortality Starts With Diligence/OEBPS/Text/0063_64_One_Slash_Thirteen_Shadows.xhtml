64. One Slash, Thirteen Shadows!

"Saber-Drawing Technique"

Inside the practice room, Lin Fei opened the Saber-Drawing Technique manual. There were very few words written on it.

Saber-Drawing Technique was actually divided into two parts.

The first part was nurturing the saber, and the second part was unsheathing the saber.

The first part was used to nurture the treasured saber with inner power in a special way. The second part was to unsheathe the saber and kill the enemy when it was time to use it. The longer the saber was nurtured, the greater the power when unsheathing it.

Lin Fei realized that Saber-Drawing Technique was just perfect for him.

Other martial artists who practice Saber-Drawing Technique needed to spend time and energy nurturing the saber, but Lin Fei was different.

His martial skills could self-practice. In other words, he could constantly nurture the saber 24 hours a day.

Even if he only had one day, the power of the Saber-Drawing Technique would still be considerable.

The downside of the Saber-Drawing Technique was that it was only one move, but Lin Fei was not concerned.

He still had the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, which was not inferior to any acquired-level saber technique. It was more than enough as a basic move.

If used well, Saber-Drawing Technique would be able to kill with one strike and turn the tide of battle. So, Lin Fei began practicing Saber-Drawing Technique.

He had enough time to become a beginner in Saber-Drawing Technique. This technique was actually very simple.

Both nurturing the saber and unsheathing the saber have a core, which was the way inner power flows through the meridians.

In fact, Saber-Drawing Technique was about making inner power flow through several specific meridians in the hand. Usually, inner power was used to nurture the saber through these meridians.

But when inner power explodes through these meridians in battle, it would be the real Saber-Drawing Technique.

Basically, all acquired combat martial arts were like this.

Compared to Body Refining martial arts, the key of acquired martial arts was to use inner power to enhance explosive power. This applies to all martial arts, including fist, lightness, and sword techniques.

If inner power was not used, it is not much different from Body Refining saber techniques. But if inner power was used, the attack level was completely different.

Lin Fei followed the steps of the Saber-Drawing Technique, slowly flowing inner power through the specific meridians in his hand.

Lin Fei held the saber tightly in his hand. It seemed like he was intimately connected with Saber in an instant. This feeling was very unique.

"Amazing..." murmured Lin Fei in a low voice. He had never felt this way before. It was as if the saber had become a part of his body. Of course, this was just an illusion, a sensation that came from the nurturing saber.

In the early stages of practice, it was necessary to hold a saber in one's hand. But in the later stages, one only needed to keep a saber nearby or carry it on one's back.

After practicing for a while, Lin Fei had a distinct feeling that saber had come to life. Its power seemed to have increased a lot.

Without hesitation, Lin Fei shouted in a low voice, "Saber-Drawing Technique!"

Saber was unsheathed with a "clang" and emitted a sharp aura, directly cutting into the ground and leaving a large hole in the solid stone tiles of the room.

"Terrifying power," thought Lin Fei. "I've only been nurturing Saber for half an hour, and its power had already reached such a level. If I nurture saber for ten days, half a month, or even a year, how much more powerful will it become?"

Lin Fei's eyes brightened. The power of the Saber-Drawing Technique far exceeded his expectations. He had only just started to practice it, and he was still just a beginner.

Line Fei carefully pondered.

The strike from the previous use of the "Saber-Drawing Technique" not only had tremendous power, but it was also incredibly fast. Its speed was even faster than Lin Fei's Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

Of course, the "fast" of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique and the "fast" of the Saber-Drawing Technique was different. The "fast" of the Saber-Drawing Technique was the purest form of speed, a direct and straightforward attack without any twists or changes.

Meanwhile, the Nine Shadow Saber Technique had "saber shadows" that pursued how many times it could stack saber moves in an instant, and it had some techniques.

"Wait, what if I apply some of the techniques of the Saber-Drawing Technique to the Nine Shadow Saber Technique? Would it work?" A thought flashed in Lin Fei's mind.

The Saber-Drawing Technique was indeed very fast, but it was only possible due to the inner power exploding within several special meridians in the hand. What about the Nine Shadow Saber Technique?

Lin Fei had an idea and began to mobilize his inner power to run within the several meridians of his hand. He then tried to perform the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

As time passed, "swish," suddenly, Lin Fei's inner power exploded, and the saber move was exceptionally smooth.

"Your Nine Shadow Saber Technique has taken an unusual path and gained insight into the secret of inner power eruption. Its training progress has increased by 25%."

The system's voice sounded in Lin Fei's mind.

Lin Fei's heart was elated, and he slashed with the techniques of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

"Swish."

Thirteen saber shadows!

As expected, Lin Fei's Nine Shadow Saber Technique had improved once again: one slash, thirteen shadows!
