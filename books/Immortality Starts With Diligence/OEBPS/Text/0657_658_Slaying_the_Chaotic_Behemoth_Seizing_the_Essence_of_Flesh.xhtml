658. Slaying the Chaotic Behemoth, Seizing the Essence of Flesh!

"Illusory Realm!"

Lin Fei instantly deployed his Illusory Realm. The realm was at the level of Supremes, making it highly effective against such beings. It could stun the minds of Supremes, albeit briefly. Even if it was just for a few moments, this was one of Lin Fei's most potent weapons.

"Buzz."

The Illusory Realm descended, swiftly enveloping the massive chaotic behemoth. However, to Lin Fei's surprise, the behemoth's eyes only clouded over momentarily before regaining clarity. It seemed to shake off the effects of the Illusory Realm almost immediately.

"The Illusory Realm is ineffective?"

The chaotic behemoth broke free from the stun faster than Lin Fei expected, a testament to its extraordinary nature. Lin Fei speculated that there might be something unique about the behemoth, perhaps a special constitution or ability that allowed it to resist his Illusory Realm, a capability not even Supremes possessed.

Since the Illusory Realm proved ineffective, Lin Fei decided not to hold back any longer.

"Boom."

The next moment, Lin Fei launched a punch powered by the force of his Eternal Realm. Although he hadn't transformed into his realm embodiment, this punch unleashed the full fury of his power, equivalent to at least fifty percent of his strength when fully transformed into the Eternal Realm. This level of power was what made him a top-tier Supreme.

"Thud."

Finally, Lin Fei's fist slammed into the body of the chaotic behemoth. A pained scream erupted from the creature as its colossal form began to rapidly disintegrate under the sheer force of the punch. The behemoth's flesh collapsed, showcasing the terrifying power Lin Fei wielded.

The three chaotic Supremes witnessing this scene were agape with astonishment. Their faces registered disbelief, unable to fathom that someone could cause such devastation to the formidable chaotic behemoth with a single punch.

Such was the power of Lin Fei—a force capable of crushing Supremes and now proving potent enough to dismantle the formidable body of a chaotic behemoth. The chaotic Supremes were left in shock as they realized the scale of power Lin Fei brought into the fray.

"How did he do that?" one of the Supremes muttered in disbelief, his eyes wide as he witnessed the raw power unleashed by Lin Fei. "It's incredible, truly incredible! He must be a Supreme, right? But his aura, it doesn't quite seem like that of a Supreme..."

"Perhaps he's concealing his true strength, but without a doubt, he is a Supreme—likely even a top-tier Supreme, given that he just exploded a chaotic behemoth with a single punch!"

"Top-tier Supreme? That's someone who can slay Supremes. We need to be extremely cautious around him."

While the three Supremes were shocked internally, they also remained wary.

"Hmm? It's not dead yet!"

Lin Fei's punch had unleashed half his strength. A normal Supreme would certainly not have withstood such a blow, yet the chaotic behemoth had endured it. Indeed, it was stronger than a regular Supreme, which explained why it had previously managed to put those three Supremes in such a dire situation.

However, Lin Fei was not concerned. He had already gauged the behemoth's limit, and although it was stronger than an ordinary Supreme, it was only by a slight margin. Lin Fei felt confident that his Eternal Realm was more than enough to suppress the chaotic behemoth.

"Eternal Realm!"

Without hesitation, Lin Fei transformed into the realm.

"Boom."

The Eternal Realm descended like a gaping maw, engulfing the titanic chaotic behemoth within it. In reality, the behemoth was instantly suppressed by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. The powers from all directions within the realm converged, relentlessly crushing the chaotic behemoth.

"Roar..."

The behemoth roared in rage, but to no avail. Its flesh continued to disintegrate inch by inch, rapidly collapsing and finally transforming into a mass of essence, which was absorbed by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, nourishing every life form within it.

"Splat."

In just a matter of moments, ten breaths of time to be exact, the chaotic behemoth was dead, utterly dead. Even its essence had fully integrated into the Eternal Realm.

Lin Fei could clearly sense it. The behemoth's body indeed contained powers akin to chaotic energies. Furthermore, the integration of its flesh essence into the Eternal Realm brought about subtle changes.

Though these changes were minute, they were real—his Eternal Realm was beginning to adopt some of the chaotic properties.

With the absorption of the chaotic behemoth's essence, Lin Fei reviewed his current status through his system interface:

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Martial Ancestor

World Level: True Immortal-level World (Progress 2%)

Illusory Realm: Supreme Level

Lin Fei noted that his world level remained at the True Immortal level, but the progress had increased from 1% to 2%. At first glance, this seemed like only a minor improvement. However, the implications were significant. A single chaotic behemoth had helped his True Immortal-level world advance by 1%.

Theoretically, one hundred chaotic behemoths could potentially evolve his world into a realm. This was merely theoretical, as increasing strength would likely decrease the incremental progress from each subsequent behemoth.

Still, the key takeaway for Lin Fei was not the exact percentage increase but the direction it provided. It affirmed that certain elements or powers within Chaos were necessary for the transformation of his world. Venturing into the Chaos had been the right decision.

With this newfound understanding, Lin Fei's focus returned to the present. His gaze shifted towards the three Supremes in the distance, who immediately tensed up. They were on high alert, having witnessed Lin Fei's formidable display.

The transformation of Lin Fei into his world form had thoroughly intimidated them—the immense and powerful chaotic behemoth had been subdued and absorbed in mere moments.

Lin Fei, undeniably a top-tier Supreme, posed a significant threat. If he desired, eliminating them would be as effortless as it was dealing with the behemoth.

Thus, one of the Supreme beings, left with no choice, approached Lin Fei and bowed respectfully. "Thank you, fellow daoist, for lending us aid and saving us."

"From whence do you hail?" Lin Fei inquired indifferently.

"We come from the Kunyu City, preparing to undertake a mission."

"Kunyu City?"

A thought stirred in Lin Fei's heart.

These Supremes also hailed from Kunyu City. Yet upon reflection, it seemed only natural. After all, Lin Fei had been soaring through the Chaos for such a long time. It appeared he must be nearing Kunyu City. Since he was close to Kunyu City, encountering Supremes from within its bounds was hardly a rare occurrence.

"Fellow daoist, are you also headed towards Kunyu City?"

"Indeed, this is my first venture into the Chaos, and I am bound for Kunyu City. How much farther is it?"

"Not far. With your speed, a mere ten days or so of flying will suffice."

Lin Fei nodded. A mere ten days was nothing. Indeed, he was already quite near to Kunyu City.

"By the way, do you often encounter the Chaotic Behemoths?"

Lin Fei posed another query. Now that Chaotic Behemoths could further his progress, he naturally wished to continue slaying them.

"Chaotic Behemoths are exceedingly rare in the Chaos. This is also our first encounter with a Chaotic Behemoth; we were quite unlucky. Do you wish to slay another Chaotic Behemoth?"

These three Supremes, having witnessed Lin Fei slaying a Chaotic Behemoth, had guessed his intention. Lin Fei nodded without concealing his desire to kill more Chaotic Behemoths.

However, Chaotic Behemoths were incredibly scarce. To encounter one was fortunate. How to pursue them further?

Lin Fei inquired about some matters concerning Kunyu City, all in line with what the suppressed Supremes of his Eternal Realm had mentioned. Thereupon, the three Supremes took their leave.

Lin Fei continued on his flight towards Kunyu City.
