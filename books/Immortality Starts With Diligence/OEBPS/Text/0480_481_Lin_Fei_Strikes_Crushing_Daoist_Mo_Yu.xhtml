481. Lin Fei Strikes, Crushing Daoist Mo Yu!

In Fang Yunshan's mind, there was only one thought left. Today, he was going to die.

"Ptch."

The next moment, a sharp saber light flashed by. Immediately after, the finger thrust by Daoist Mo Yu was sliced by the saber light, dissipating into nothingness.

"Who?"

Daoist Mo Yu's expression turned stern, fixating on the distant figure. At that moment, in front of Fang Yunshan, a figure had appeared, unknown when.

He glanced at Fang Yunshan and said indifferently, "Had I arrived even a moment later, you would have been decapitated. Are you Fang Yunshan?"

Fang Yunshan also opened his eyes, and upon seeing the figure before him, his heart swelled with joy. He immediately knelt down and pleaded, "Senior, please save my Fang family! We have made a deal. You accepted my task, and I promised your request."

"Your request, was it to drive away Daoist Mo Yu?"

"Yes."

"Alright, then as you wish!"

Lin Fei took a step forward.

"Daoist friend, do you truly wish to make an enemy of me over a mere Fang family?" Daoist Mo Yu narrowed his eyes slightly.

But Lin Fei didn't speak.

"Boom."

The next moment, Lin Fei unleashed a punch.

Within Lin Fei's body, he utilized the power of only a hundred particle worlds.

Despite using the power of just a hundred particle worlds, this punch was still earth-shattering.

Daoist Mo Yu's battle avatar immediately let out a scream, like a paper figure.

"Ptch."

Daoist Mo Yu's battle avatar was annihilated.

Yes, completely annihilated, leaving no trace behind.

This battle avatar turned to ashes.

Seeing this scene, many were stunned, even frozen in place.

Daoist Mo Yu's battle avatar, just like that, annihilated?

Without leaving a single trace. Not even any earth-shattering noise. Crushed!

This was a complete crushing!

"Senior..."

Fang Yunshan's heart trembled. He faintly felt that this time, he really found an extraordinary and formidable senior. That was Daoist Mo Yu's battle avatar. Initially, just this one avatar almost destroyed the Fang family.

"Does he have another battle avatar? Let's deal with that as well."

Lin Fei's figure flashed. He had already flown out of the dungeon. At this moment, inside the dungeon, many exchanged glances.

However, many also quickly came to their senses. They immediately opened the prison and unlocked the shackles on Fang Yunshan.

"Yunshan, we might really be saved."

"Yes, that senior is truly a Martial God, perhaps even an extraordinary one!"

"Let's hurry out and see, hopefully, that Martial God senior can truly expel Daoist Mo Yu completely."

At that moment, the people of the Fang family rushed out of the dungeon in a hurry. Above the skies of Moon City, Lin Fei and the many avatars of Daoist Mo Yu were in a standoff. Among these avatars, only one was the battle avatar. To Lin Fei, the other numerous avatars posed no threat if they weren't battle ones.

"Hmph set up the formation!" Daoist Mo Yu snorted coldly.

The next moment, dozens of avatars quickly dispersed across the void, forming a large array.

Instantly, Lin Fei found himself within the formation. The advantage of a Deity Transformation powerhouse having numerous avatars is the ability to set up formations at any moment.

However, since there was only one battle avatar, the power of the formation was limited.

Lin Fei shook his head slightly.

"World Law, Seal!"

Immediately, he activated the World Law. Combined with the Saber World, it descended instantly, like a small world in itself, rapidly enveloping Daoist Mo Yu and swiftly shrinking. Daoist Mo Yu roared in anger, but within Lin Fei's World Law, it was futile. He could only watch as Lin Fei sealed him.

The terrifying sealing power could even annihilate his battle avatar. By now, Daoist Mo Yu had to recognize that Lin Fei's strength far surpassed his own.

"Fine, Daoist friend, your strength is formidable, I concede!" Daoist Mo Yu said. "But you can protect the Fang family for a moment, can you protect them forever? Once you leave, I'll annihilate every member of the Fang family!"

Daoist Mo Yu was seething with murderous intent. He was no match for Lin Fei, but as a Deity Transformation powerhouse, Lin Fei couldn't kill him either. Since he couldn't do anything to Lin Fei, he would vent his anger on the Fang family. He was determined to annihilate the entire Fang lineage!

Lin Fei frowned, his eyes turning sharp. He could have ignored the situation since driving Daoist Mo Yu away was sufficient. Whether Daoist Mo Yu sought revenge later was none of his concern.

However, Lin Fei couldn't just stand by. Now that he knew of the threat, he had to find a solution.

"Daoist Mo Yu, you have numerous avatars scattered everywhere. But if I decide to kill you, I can lock onto each of your avatars and hunt them down slowly. If you don't want a relentless pursuit and a lifetime of being hunted by me, let this matter end here. If the Fang family is exterminated, I will be the first to slay you!"

Lin Fei's demeanor was calm, But his words left no room for doubt.

Daoist Mo Yu was furious inside, but he had no viable option against Lin Fei. He could, at all costs, engage in a fight to the death with Lin Fei, even entangling with him indefinitely.

However, he was no match for Lin Fei. If he were truly locked on and all his avatars destroyed, it wouldn't be an empty threat.

There had been a case where a Deity Transformation powerhouse, locked in a relentless struggle with a Martial God, was hunted down, avatar by avatar, slowly over a thousand years. Eventually, all avatars were exterminated, and the Deity Transformation perished.

Indeed, with countless avatars, a Deity Transformation powerhouse is hard to kill. But if it comes down to a relentless pursuit, even at the ends of the earth, a Martial God could hunt and slay them.

Daoist Mo Yu had not gained any significant advantage from targeting the Fang family. Now, to engage in an endless feud with such a Martial God was not worth it.

With this in mind, Daoist Mo Yu gritted his teeth.

"Ptch."

The next moment, Daoist Mo Yu's battle avatar annihilated itself.
This was self-destruction. The remaining ordinary avatars also rapidly disintegrated.

"Daoist friend, you are ruthless!"

Daoist Mo Yu self-destructed all his avatars, implying he would no longer target the Fang family. It was a sign of yielding. He did not wish to engage in an endless feud with Lin Fei. After all, for a mere Fang family, it wasn't worth antagonizing a Martial God.

Lin Fei watched as Daoist Mo Yu disappeared. His only thought was that the survival tactics of a Deity Transformation were indeed strong. As long as one avatar remained, they could not die. As for combat power, it could be concentrated in the battle avatar.

Of course, against a Martial God of the same level, it was difficult to prevail. But the ability to not die was impressive.

However, such tactics were not something a Martial God could envy. Martial Gods don't have as many avatars as Deity Transformation powerhouses, not even one.

Martial artists, fighting against heaven and earth, concentrate their power within themselves. How could they possibly have avatars?

It's a case of advantages and disadvantages. But for now, the issue with the Fang family was resolved.

"Fang Yunshan, Daoist Mo Yu has been driven away, and he will no longer trouble the Fang family. Now, it's time to fulfill your promise. Where is the Saber World?"

Lin Fei stepped forward, coming before Fang Yunshan, his gaze calmly fixed on him.
