353. The Invincible White Python?

Lin Fei's Dual-World Saber was mighty and had never missed its target. As the blade struck the White Python, its massive body began to crumble inch by inch. Its consciousness grew blurry as if Lin Fei's attack had destroyed its spirit. After all, the Dual-World Saber was known to kill the body with one blow and destroy the spirit with another.

However, in the next moment, the White Python roared furiously. A large phantom image began to form around it – a white dragon! The python had the bloodline of a white dragon and had now fully activated it.

"Crackling..."

Scales, those of a dragon, started appearing on the python. As these scales grew, the seemingly dying python began to rejuvenate rapidly. Its initially disoriented consciousness became clear with the manifestation of the white dragon phantom.

With a thunderous leap, the python shot into the sky, aiming for Lin Fei. It swung its massive tail fiercely towards him.

"Saber Domain!"

Without hesitation, Lin Fei activated his Saber Domain.

"Fire!"

A rain of flames descended. Yet, when the python's tail swept through after the Saber Domain's activation, Lin Fei instantly vanished from its sight.

Inside the Saber Domain, Lin Fei was supreme. Flames continuously scorched the python. But after its transformation into a quasi-dragon, the python's physical strength might have surged ten or even a hundred times, even the raging flames couldn't harm it.

With another sweep of its tail, Lin Fei's Saber Domain violently trembled.

"Splash!"

The next moment, Lin Fei's Saber Domain was torn apart. Lin Fei felt a chill in his heart. He was well aware of his Saber Domain's potency. Since its evolution, it had been torn apart only once, by the Taiyi Dao Palace's Great Mountain Divine Seal. And now, the transformed python, although not a complete transformation into a dragon, showcased such tremendous power.

"Divine Ability, Annihilation!"

Lin Fei activated his Annihilation Divine Ability. The power of Annihilation enveloped the python. However, a faint glow emanated from its scales. Despite the eroding force of destruction, the python remained unharmed. The python's tail came sweeping once again, and this time, without his Saber Domain, Lin Fei couldn't teleport away.

"Bang!"

The massive tail of the python fiercely struck Lin Fei. In an instant, an overwhelming force directly exploded Lin Fei into a mist of blood. The python calmed down, and in the void, the tormented Azure Flood Dragon King also settled into a somber silence.

A hint of agony appeared in the eyes of the Azure Flood Dragon King. "He died again," it lamented. "A mere White Python was his undoing. When will someone come to liberate me? The pain..."

The Azure Flood Dragon King's face contorted in anguish. Its core was constantly being tormented, making every moment excruciating for it, a fate worse than death. It wished for someone to end its misery.

When Lin Fei entered, the Dragon King saw hope. But now? Lin Fei was defeated by a mere python. As time ticked by, the python's massive eyes scanned the void.

The Azure Flood Dragon King noticed something amiss. "He's not dead?" Without Dubhe making an appearance, there could only be one reason: Lin Fei wasn't dead!

The python seemed to deduce this as well, attempting to find Lin Fei's trace.

"Hum…" The next moment, the blood mist quickly condensed, and Lin Fei reappeared unscathed. He wasn't dead! Although the python's powerful tail had exploded him instantly, Lin Fei had the Undying Seal Divine Ability. Although he couldn't withstand the python's tremendous power, as it hadn't reached the particle level, Lin Fei remained unharmed. Without touching the particle level, he was essentially immortal.

However, there wasn't a hint of joy on Lin Fei's face. He had been defeated! It was the first time he was overpowered by a being of his level. The python, fundamentally at the Martial Sovereign tier, but seemed to completely suppress him. Even if this python had secret techniques or the bloodline of the white dragon, marking it as an ancient unique species, Lin Fei was being dominated. Only his "Undying Seal" was keeping him alive.

"Divine Ability, Pierce!" Lin Fei's eyes were cold. Techniques like the Dual-World Saber, Annihilation, and Gravity Domain had no effect on the python. But the Divine Ability called "Pierce" was different. This technique, known to penetrate anything, was originally "created" by Lin Fei not for combat or attack but to break seals and confinements on magical treasures.

However, this White Python was too powerful, its defense terrifyingly formidable. It seemed none of Lin Fei's Divine Abilities or techniques had any effect, leaving only the Divine Ability Pierce. Lin Fei wanted to see if Pierce truly was invincible.

"Whoosh." As Lin Fei unleashed the Divine Ability Pierce, a beam of light instantly shot out from him, plunging straight into the python's body.

"Splat." A faint sound. Despite the python's strong defenses and its bloodline of the White Dragon, under the piercing force of the Pierce Divine Ability, all defenses were rendered useless. The beam drilled through the python's body, leaving a bloody hole.

"Roar…" The python let out a furious roar, its massive claws lashing out at Lin Fei.

"Bang."

Lin Fei's body was torn apart by the python once again. However, he quickly regenerated, a hint of a smile gracing his face. His Pierce Divine Ability had worked on the python! Even if it was just a small bloody hole, it was a wound, an undeniable vulnerability, and Lin Fei's opportunity.

"Divine Ability, Black Wind Prison!" Light radiated from the first layer of Lin Fei's Life Wheel.

A cold, bone-chilling wind began to blow. This wind wrapped around the python. While the scales of the python remained unaffected, the wind slipped into the bloody hole left by the Pierce Divine Ability. Inside the python, the corrosive wind raged. As the wind blew, bones were revealed!

With a roar of agony, the python thrashed violently, but to no avail. Its flesh rapidly decayed from the inside out, revealing a skeletal structure. In just a moment, the python turned into a massive skeleton, devoid of any signs of life. It had died. The bone-eroding wind had invaded its body, reducing it to bare bones. Stripped of flesh, the White Python could not escape death.

"Congratulations, you have passed the test." At this moment, Dubhe appeared.
