50. Second Inner Power

Zhou Mountain Mansion City.

Wang Xuan blushed. In front of her were two young men dressed as martial artists.

"Junior Sister Xuan, you said your father almost betrothed you to someone else?"

One of the young men, who held a long sword, seemed arrogant.

"Senior Brother Liu Hao, my father didn't know about me and Senior Brother before...I have already explained everything to him. That man is just a nobody in Zhou Mountain Mansion City, how can he compare to Senior Brother? Besides, my father has agreed to our relationship."

Wang Xuan seemed afraid that Liu Hao would misunderstand, so she explained the whole story.

"Junior Sister Xuan, you don't have to worry. You are also a disciple of our Autumn Haze Mountain's inner sect. How can someone from Zhou Mountain Mansion City be compared to you? Can he match up with you? I heard that he is a disciple of Nine Profound Sect's inner sect?"

Liu Hao obviously knew something about it.

Wang Xuan knew that Liu Hao was proud. Even though this matter had nothing to do with Lin Fei. But since he knew, Liu Hao must have felt uncomfortable.

He might even go looking for trouble with Lin Fei. Wang Xuan didn't want to stir up trouble.

So she took the initiative to hold Liu Hao's hand and said, "Senior Brother, Lin Fei is just a Body Refining martial artist. He has only just been promoted to the inner sect of the Nine Profound Sect after three years. He is nothing compared to you.

Senior Brother doesn't need to bother with such an ordinary person and a trivial matter. Isn't Senior Brother Chen Yuan going to Luo City for something? Senior Brother Chen Yuan's matter is more important."

The other martial artist, also an inner sect disciple of Autumn Haze Mountain, was Chen Yuan.

At this time, he also smiled and said, "Senior Brother Liu Hao, Junior Sister is right. How can a mere Body Refining martial artist be worth your attention? Lin Fei is just an ant on the ground, and he will probably only be a Body Refining martial artist for the rest of his life.

But Senior Brother is a divine dragon in the sky, destined to become an innate expert and dominate the world! How can someone like Lin Fei deserve Senior Brother's attention?"

Liu Hao glanced at Chen Yuan and Wang Xuan.

He couldn't help but smile, "Right, Junior Sister Xuan is right. A mere Lin Fei, still a Body Refining martial artist, is indeed not worth our attention. Okay, let's go say goodbye to the uncle first, then accompany Junior Brother Chen Yuan to Luo City, take care of his matter, and then return to the sect."

"Haha, I am also gonna investigate why the Zhang family suddenly withdrew their patronage for me. It's just a small matter that can be solved in Luo City. It's an honor to have the company of senior brother."

"Anyway, it's on the way. Moreover, Junior Brother Chen Yuan just broke through to the acquired, and your inner power is unstable. If you encounter any small problems in Luo City, I will take care of them for you."

"Thank you, Senior Brother Liu Hao!"

So Wang Xuan, Liu Hao, and Chen Yuan said goodbye to the prefect and quickly left Zhou Mountain Mansion City towards Luo City.

...

Lin Fei sat cross-legged in the room, his whole body covered in the golden light snake blood. His skin turned red, and he felt a tingling sensation, but he remained unmoved.

He was used to it by now. He had just reached the limit of the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique yesterday, and he was about to develop inner power today.

And now, his Vajra Iron Cloth also almost reached its limit, which was about to give birth to inner power! When the two inner powers merged, what kind of power would they bring? Lin Fei was not sure.

Today was crucial for him, and he ordered the servants in the Lin mansion not to disturb him at this time.

As time passed, the effect of the golden light snake blood on Lin Fei's body gradually weakened, but he felt that his Vajra Iron Cloth was still a little short of reaching its body refining limit.

However, Lin Fei was not worried because he had prepared a lot of golden light snake blood. So he applied a large amount of it again.

Lin Fei's skin had become somewhat black and even a bit rough to the touch. After all, it was training the skin to become tougher than cowhide.

Suddenly, Lin Fei's skin began to vibrate slightly, and there seemed to ripple appearing on his slightly black skin, like the ripples on water. Then, the tough skin began to crack with a "crackle."

Lin Fei opened his eyes wide and grabbed the skin on his hand and tore it apart with a "chirp." There was no blood or pain. After the old skin cracked open, a new layer of skin was revealed, which looked very fair and tender.

Lin Fei seemed to have been "reborn" and transformed like a completely different person. Looked like a handsome young gentleman from a distance.

However, these details were inconsequential. What mattered was the new layer of skin on his body.

Lin Fei felt as if an invisible force was covering his entire body, and it was tingling with a slight burning sensation.

"The inner power produced by the skin membrane! The Vajra Iron Cloth has finally reached its limit!"

A flash of enlightenment crossed Lin Fei's mind.
