291. Devouring Sky Beast

One cannot see the outside from the inside of the Martial King Tower. However, once someone starts the challenge, the situation inside becomes visible from the outside.

At this moment, as Lin Fei stepped into the first level of the Martial King Tower, a formation was activated, revealing everything inside through a special method.

With a "swish", Lin Fei arrived at the first level of the tower. Darkness enveloped him, but it didn't hinder Lin Fei. There seemed to be no movement around him. But then, with a faint "hum", Lin Fei sensed subtle fluctuations of a formation. These fluctuations were fleeting.

Suddenly, a gigantic head appeared before him, its enormous maw open wide and lunging directly at Lin Fei. It seemed to appear out of nowhere.

An exotic beast!

Within the first level of the Martial King Tower lay a terrifying exotic beast that could launch surprise attacks, catching intruders off-guard.

Lin Fei instinctively felt that he shouldn't be swallowed by this creature. If he were, death might be imminent.

The first level of the Martial King Tower wasn't entirely safe. On the contrary, it was perilous. One could only ensure their safety by admitting defeat.

Outside the tower, the sudden appearance of the exotic beast shocked the elder, who exclaimed, "How could it be the Devouring Sky Beast?"

The elder had been guarding the Martial King Tower for hundreds of years and had seen numerous Martial Sovereigns challenge the first level. The tower would manifest various beings – sometimes a Martial Sovereign, sometimes a mere formation, and often times, exotic beasts. But never had he seen the Devouring Sky Beast.

This beast, only mentioned in ancient texts, was known to be inside the first level of the Martial King Tower. Though calling it a creature that could "devour the sky" might be an exaggeration, it was certainly capable of swallowing a mountain. Its strength was formidable. Even a Martial Sovereign with multiple Great Divine Abilities could be swallowed by the beast if they were careless.

Being ingested by this creature would mean certain death. Yet, the Devouring Sky Beast had rarely appeared on the first level of the tower. Why did it appear now?

In fact, not only the elder recognized the Devouring Sky Beast. Other Martial Sovereigns, who had vast experience and knowledge, also identified the creature.

"That's the Devouring Sky Beast, the legendary exotic beast."

"With such a beast, even if he possesses two Great Divine Abilities, it's not certain he'd win. Unless he has a specific Divine Ability to counter it. But what are the chances?"

"Poor Aurora Sovereign, she invested so much to invite a powerful-looking Martial Sovereign. And now they encounter the Devouring Sky Beast. He won't get past the first level of the Martial King Tower."

Upon seeing the Devouring Sky Beast, many Martial Sovereigns shook their heads, believing that Lin Fei had no other choice but to surrender unless he wanted to be swallowed whole by the beast. Such an outcome would surely mean death. Even the always-confident Xiao Yuchen was taken aback upon seeing the beast. She knew Lin Fei possessed the Saber Domain, but could that even stand against the Devouring Sky Beast?

Inside the tower, Lin Fei saw the massive head and even smelt the putrid odor from the beast's mouth. It was too fast. So swift that even invoking a Divine Ability might be too late. The only choice seemed to be surrendering. If he didn't, he risked being swallowed and possibly facing death.

Lin Fei wasn't one to easily risk his life. But surrender? It never crossed his mind.

With a loud roar, the beast lunged, seemingly swallowing Lin Fei whole. However, sensing something amiss, the beast swiftly turned around. To its surprise, Lin Fei was standing right behind it. Considering the incomprehensible speed of the Devouring Sky Beast, there seemed no way for Lin Fei to escape, yet there he was, unharmed.

Lin Fei's eyes, cold as ice, stared down the beast. True, the beast was fast, so fast that he couldn't react in time. Even with his own speed, he couldn't evade its gaping maw. However, he managed to escape thanks to his Saber Domain!

The very moment the beast appeared, Lin activated his Saber Domain. Within its boundaries, Lin Fei was the sovereign. He teleported – an instant movement beyond mere speed. As long as he was within the domain, Lin could teleport to any corner. So, with a blink, he was behind the beast.

"Fire!" With a step, flames engulfed everything. A white aura covered the entire first level of the Martial King Tower.

Saber Domain! Lin Fei's Saber Domain was fully unleashed.

Within the Saber Domain, raging flames burned fiercely. Moreover, an intangible pressure was exerting its force on the Devouring Sky Beast. Though it couldn't completely suppress the beast, Lin Fei's Saber Domain managed to restrain at least half of its strength. This was the terrifying power of Lin Fei's Saber Domain, effectively halving the beast's prowess.

Suddenly, within the confines of the Saber Domain, a gust of wind began to blow. It was a chilling breeze that swept over the Devouring Sky Beast, causing it to let out a mournful howl. It seemed to have sensed some imminent danger and struggled desperately but to no avail. Within Lin Fei's Saber Domain, unless one possesses a force ten or even dozens of times stronger than the Domain itself, there's no breaking free.

Lin Fei hadn't even used his three-in-one sword technique. He merely unleashed the "Black Wind Prison." This Great Divine Ability summoned the "Bone-Eroding Wind." At its perfected realm, the might of the Black Wind Prison had increased manifold.

As the wind swept through, the massive body of the Devouring Sky Beast began to disintegrate at a visible rate, gradually turning to ashes.

Suddenly, the beast vanished. A tremendous force from all directions sliced through Lin Fei's Saber Domain, taking the Devouring Sky Beast with it. The beast hadn't died. For such an exotic beast, losing part of its body wasn't a big deal. It could regenerate.

"You have passed the first level of the Martial King Tower!"

An emotionless, mechanical voice echoed within the tower. Shortly after, Lin Fei was teleported out of the first level.

"The power of the Martial King Tower?" Lin Fei narrowed his eyes slightly. The force that had just cleaved through his Saber Domain was clearly far superior to it. Lin Fei had a growing suspicion. The power didn't seem to be of the Martial Sovereign. It likely belonged to the Martial King Tower itself.

The Martial King Tower was, after all, forged by the Martial King. Naturally, it possesses mysterious powers.

Lin Fei had been transported outside of the Martial King Tower. He looked around and saw various Martial Sovereigns, each with widened eyes and solemn expressions. Their attention was focused on him.

However, Lin Fei paid them no mind. He glanced at Xiao Yuchen and calmly stated, "Aurora Sovereign, I have passed the first level of the Martial King Tower. Now, you can proceed to the blood pool to try and awaken your bloodline."

Upon hearing Lin Fei's words, Xiao Yuchen quickly realized his meaning. "Alright, I'll head to the blood pool right away. Please wait here outside the Martial King Tower, I'll be back soon."

Xiao Yuchen immediately approached the elder guarding the outside of the Martial King Tower and went through some procedures before entering the tower itself. It seemed that the blood pool was located within the Martial King Tower. Lin Fei had not noticed it when he was on the first level, indicating that the tower truly was extraordinary.

With Xiao Yuchen now inside the tower, the elder turned his gaze to Lin Fei, his eyes filled with a hint of astonishment. "If my eyes do not deceive me, the technique Sovereign Lin displayed within the Martial King Tower was the Saber Domain. Only with the Saber Domain can one teleport.

Moreover, Sovereign Lin's Divine Ability is immensely powerful, definitely among the top-tier Great Divine Abilities. A Saber Domain and a top-tier Great Divine Ability... It's no wonder you managed to pass the first level. It's rare for Aurora Sovereign to find such a top-tier Martial Sovereign like you. Truly extraordinary!"

The elder who guarded the Martial King Tower was a figure of considerable stature and discernment. Having seen countless Martial Sovereigns over the years, receiving praise from such an esteemed figure spoke volumes about Lin Fei's recent performance and abilities. It was no wonder the elder was so impressed.

As for the other Martial Sovereigns from the Xiao family, their gazes towards Lin Fei became even more intricate. Many were filled with admiration, perhaps even envy, that Xiao Yuchen had managed to enlist someone like Lin Fei.
