701. The End

Lin Fei did not directly transform into the Eternal Realm but instead released the Light of Eternity. He wanted to first test the power of the Eternal Light infused with the power of chaos after the transformation.

Last time, Lin Fei attempted to annihilate the Dark Demon Domain using the Eternal Realm directly, but unfortunately, he failed. This time, Lin Fei just deployed the Eternal Light. But the reaction was even more dramatic than last time.

The two powers collided fiercely, surging with immense power toward each other.

"Boom."

The immense force caused both worlds to shake violently.

At the same time, both powers attempted to obliterate each other. However, Lin Fei's Eternal Light, already immensely powerful, now also possessed the characteristics of chaos.

Therefore, as the two powers collided, Lin Fei's Eternal Light clearly gained the upper hand.

Time and again, it obliterated the Dark Demon Domain, and the annihilation proceeded at a rapid pace.

The Dark Demon Lord panicked.

"How has Lin Fei's Eternal Realm become even stronger? The Dark Demon Domain has no power to resist. What level of world is Lin Fei's realm?"

The Dark Demon Lord knew that Lin Fei's power was immense, but he didn't expect Lin Fei to suppress the Dark Demon Domain even more easily than last time.

At least, last time, Lin Fei could not obliterate the Dark Demon Domain. But this time, Lin Fei could indeed obliterate it.

Lin Fei was also a bit surprised. He hadn't even released the Eternal Realm, only the Eternal Light.

"Continue!"

Thus, Lin Fei continued to release the Eternal Light. Since the Eternal Light alone could resolve the Dark Demon Domain, there was no need to use the Realm.

"Hum."

The Eternal Light rippled like water, majestically expanding towards the Dark Demon Domain. And under this expansion, the Dark Demon Domain, under the crushing force of the Eternal Realm, was obliterated.

Everything was obliterated. The void. The life within the Dark Demon Domain. Everything within the Dark Demon Domain was utterly obliterated upon encountering the Eternal Light.

The Dark Demon Domain had never encountered such a situation before. Lin Fei's Eternal Light resembled a colossal net, enveloping the Dark Demon Domain entirely. Every region shrouded by the Eternal Light was utterly obliterated.

However, the sole advantage of the Dark Demon Domain was its immense size. As a Chaos Realm, the Dark Demon Domain was vast.

Even as Lin Fei's Eternal Light continued to expand, catching up to the entire Dark Demon Domain would take a considerable amount of time.

Even so, at this moment, the Dark Demon Domain was continuously collapsing under the pressure of the Eternal Realm. It was a collapse akin to the apocalypse.

The many denizens of the Dark Demon Domain now stared into the void, their faces filled with terror. They could feel the breath of death.

"The collapse has begun; the Dark Demon Domain is collapsing. Where is our great Dark Demon Lord?"

"Yes, how can the Dark Demon Domain collapse? With our great Dark Demon Lord, how can it collapse?"

"Except for the Supremes, no one can escape! Once the Dark Demon Domain is destroyed, even the Supremes—what then? With the Dark Heart still within the Dark Demon Domain, even the Supremes have only death awaiting them!"

"Such a formidable adversary is unheard of."

"Even our Dark Demon Lord is no match, is the Dark Demon Domain truly about to fall?"

In an instant, countless dark beings were wailing and trembling with fear.

However, it was futile. No matter how much they wailed or feared, it had no effect. Lin Fei's Eternal Realm continued to obliterate the Dark Demon Domain relentlessly until it completely destroyed the Dark Demon Domain!

Now, the Dark Demon Domain had no resistance against the Eternal Realm. It was as fragile as paper.

As the Ruler, the Dark Demon Lord saw this very clearly. His expression was grave. He had never imagined, in such a short time, he would fall to such depths?

Of course, the Dark Demon Lord would not die. At least, not before the destruction of the Dark Demon Domain. But this was almost like watching himself step by step towards death.

How could the Dark Demon Lord not be profoundly affected? He was simply powerless.

"No, there is still a chance! Lin Fei's Eternal Realm is indeed strong. So strong that even my Dark Demon Domain cannot confront it directly. However, Lin Fei also has a weakness.

His weakness is that the scope of the Eternal Realm is still too slow. It cannot completely cover the Dark Demon Domain. As long as it cannot destroy the Dark Demon Domain in an instant, then my Dark Demon Domain still has a chance!"

The Dark Demon Lord was making a final struggle. Or rather, he was attempting to save himself. He was the Ruler of the Dark Demon Domain, deeply intertwined with it. There was no escape!

Should the Dark Demon Domain be destroyed, the Dark Demon Lord would surely perish alongside it. Therefore, the Dark Demon Lord was now desperately trying to save himself. He had to find a way to survive.

His only thought was to outlast Lin Fei in a war of attrition.

"Recover!"

The next moment, the Dark Demon Lord immediately mobilized the Dark Demon Domain. He no longer wasted energy in clashing with Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Since no matter how much he bolstered his power, he was no match for Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

The Dark Demon Lord decisively mobilized the Dark Demon Domain, completely abandoning defense, and instead enhancing recovery!

Yes, recovery. The recently obliterated parts of the Dark Demon Domain were rapidly regenerating under the power wielded by the Dark Demon Lord.

Lin Fei paid no heed. He continued to release the Eternal Light of Eternity.

"Annihilate!"

In an instant, the Eternal Realm covered approximately one percent of the Dark Demon Domain, leading to its complete collapse, or rather, its complete annihilation.

Lin Fei wiped out one percent of the Dark Demon Domain without leaving a trace. Such was the terrifying power of the Eternal Realm. Now, the Dark Demon Domain was instantly reduced by one percent. In fact, the impact was tremendous.

How many dark beings lived in that one percent area?

Countless, even losing just one percent, the operation of the Dark Demon Domain became sluggish.

But this was just the beginning. A mere one percent. It was nothing in the eyes of Lin Fei. He was clear. Though it seemed like the Dark Demon Domain had lost one percent and the loss seemed devastating, the Dark Demon Domain showed no signs of collapse.

Moreover, the area obliterated by Lin Fei's Eternal Light, under the mobilization of the Dark Demon Lord, was rapidly regenerating. It was visibly recovering.

Lin Fei continued to wield the Eternal Light, relentlessly annihilating the Dark Demon Domain.

Annihilate! Frenzied annihilation!

One percent, two percent, three percent, four percent, five percent...

When Lin Fei's Eternal Light had swiftly obliterated over ten percent of the Dark Demon Domain, the previously annihilated areas had already regenerated almost halfway.

Faced with this terrifying regenerative power, Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly.

"Well done, Dark Demon Lord. I will no longer hold back!" Lin Fei muttered under his breath as his physical form seemingly vanished, replaced by a world of his own creation—the Eternal Realm.

The Eternal Realm rapidly expanded, and the time and space it passed through were frozen. Before long, the Eternal Realm had covered the entire Dark Demon Domain. Previously, when the Eternal Realm was still at the True Immortal level, it couldn't fully cover the Dark Demon Domain. But now, the Eternal Realm had become the Chaos Realm.

The Dark Demon Domain's restorative power could no longer restore any part of the time-space. The Dark Demon Lord was utterly terrified. Just a moment ago, he could still contend with Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, but why was he completely suppressed in the next instant?

In a panic, he shifted the rules of power within the Dark Demon Domain, converting all restorative power into destructive power in an attempt to break free from the suppression. But the next moment, he realized it was futile—the Dark Demon Domain no longer responded.

With his hands behind his back, Lin Fei calmly walked up to the Dark Demon Lord, gazing at the frozen expression of utter terror on his face.

Lin Fei felt no emotion inside. "All karma will end at this moment," he said. As the words left his lips, a single thought crossed his mind.

The entire Dark Demon Domain shattered like broken glass, and the figure of the Dark Demon Lord before him became illusory, eventually fading away completely.

A Chaos Realm was destroyed just like that, but it didn't even stir a single ripple in the vast expanse of Chaos.

Lin Fei stood in the chaos for a long time before returning to the Divine Realm to inform everyone of the Dark Demon Domain's annihilation.

"What? Lin Fei, you really annihilated the Dark Demon Realm?" the Azure Emperor exclaimed, looking at Lin Fei with delight. Lin Fei nodded.

"Yes, Azure Emperor. From now on, the Divine Realm will no longer face any threats!"

The Azure Emperor felt a sense of nostalgia; not long ago, Lin Fei was merely a Martial Ancestor who needed his guidance, but now Lin Fei had the power to destroy a chaotic world.

At that moment, Feng Ling approached and asked, "Lin Fei, since the Divine Realm is safe now, are you still going to return to Chaos?"

Lin Fei looked at Feng Ling, seeing the expectation in her eyes.

Lin Fei's initial goal in his cultivation was to attain eternity. Now, he could embody the Eternal Realm, and everything within his world was eternal like him. With that being the case, what else could he possibly desire?

Lin Fei turned his head, his gaze seemingly piercing through the Divine Realm, reaching into Chaos. After a long while, Lin Fei walked toward Feng Ling, took her hand, and said, "Perhaps I should take a break."
