170. Immune to all poisons, impervious to all spells

Martial Saint!

At this moment, these two words appeared in everyone's minds and Zhao Kongming, couldn't help but ask.

Everyone knew a magic treasure was equivalent to the power of the Foundation Establishment.

Below Foundation Establishment, let alone shattering a magic treasure with a single saber strike, even if someone could resist a single strike from a magic treasure would be worth recording. Even the founder of Nine Profound Sect and the top Qi Condensation realm cultivator, couldn't handle a magic treasure.

However, Lin Fei had done it. That huge one thousand meters high saber energy, hovering in the void. It was much stronger than the previous hundreds of meters high saber energy. It had already exceeded the limit of a Martial Arts Grandmaster. Therefore, Zhao Kongming asked this question.

Everyone's eyes were on Lin Fei. Especially the Qi cultivators of He Mountain Dao and Guardian Primordial Mountain. All of them looked at Lin Fei in disbelief.

Martial Master and Martial Saint.

Although both were martial artists, these were two different concepts. Once a martial artist reached Foundation Establishment and became a Martial Saint, he would be even more terrifying than a typical Foundation Establishment True Person!

"Martial Saint?"

Lin Fei shook his head and said calmly, "I haven't reached Foundation Establishment, nor have I become a Martial Saint. However, dealing with these Qi Condensation stage cultivators shouldn't be a problem.

He Mountain Dao, Guardian Primordial Mountain, you were looking for me, right? I am here now. Today, my saber will begin its slaughter!"

Lin Fei exuded a cold killing intent. His heart was also filled with vast killing intent. If he had returned just a few hours later, perhaps the Nine Profound Sect would have ceased to exist.

How could Lin Fei not be terrified? How could he not have a killing intent?

"Lin Fei, you destroyed the magic treasure of my He Mountain Dao. Not only will you die, but Nine Profound Sect, and your Lin family, will also all die! All disciples of He Mountain Dao, form the formation!"

He Yuanzi gritted his teeth. His body was also filled with killing intent. The Mountain River Seal shattered. That was the magic treasure of He Mountain Dao! It was even more precious than him. But now it had been destroyed by Lin Fei.

If Lin Fei didn't die, if Nine Profound Sect didn't perish, how would he explain to He Mountain Dao? Therefore, the current He Yuanzi wished he could slash Lin Fei a thousand times!

Thus, the many disciples of He Mountain Dao quickly formed a formation.

Without the Mountain River Seal, there were still many disciples of He Mountain Dao and there was He Yuanzi, a top Qi Condensation realm cultivator.

Once they formed a formation, they might have the power to fight against Lin Fei! Of course, He Yuanzi was very clear Lin Fei was the "Immortal Slayer Saber" and he could shatter the Mountain River Seal.

If it was just the disciples of He Mountain Dao, he wouldn't be so tough. He might turn around and run. However, there were people from Guardian Primordial Mountain here, and there were two magic treasures from Guardian Primordial Mountain!

"Dao friends Yuan Xingzi and Yuan Gangzi. You have also seen it, this Lin Fei can shatter even the Mountain River Seal. If Lin Fei is not eliminated, he will surely leave endless troubles!

Do we have to wait for a Foundation Establishment True Person to make a move? If our two Immortal sects join hands, we can definitely kill Lin Fei!"

He Yuanzi loudly said to the people of Guardian Primordial Mountain. Yuan Xingzi and Yuan Chenzi looked at each other.

Just now, Lin Fei had shattered He Mountain Dao's Mountain River Seal with a single saber, which really scared them a bit. Being able to shatter a magic treasure with a single saber, wasn't that a Martial Saint?

However, Lin Fei later denied it himself. Lin Fei was not a Martial Saint!

Moreover, the whole Great Chang was now devoid of exotic beasts. All top Qi Condensation cultivators knew this.

No martial artist could reach Foundation Establishment and become a Martial Saint.

Lin Fei, with the body of a Martial Master, shattered He Mountain Dao's magic treasure, the Mountain River Seal. This was truly astonishing.

Just as He Yuanzi said, if Lin Fei was not eliminated, endless troubles would ensue in the future!

Besides, they brought two magic treasures from Guardian Primordial Mountain down the mountain.

If they returned without any achievements, once the Grand Elder succeeded in Foundation Establishment, wouldn't they be punished?

Even more so, their magic treasures weren't like the Mountain River Seal of He Mountain Dao. They didn't need to confront Lin Fei head-on.

"Take action!"

In the next moment, Yuan Xingzi and Yuan Gangzi had made up their minds. Moreover, the two top Qi Condensation cultivators took action together.

When they were dealing with the Nine Profound Sect before, they were watching the show. They basically wouldn't take action.

Even if they did, it was just a casual shot from one person. But now, the two of them took action together. They directly activated the two magic treasures.

This showed how much they valued Lin Fei. After all, the reputation of the "Immortal Slayer Saber" wasn't just for show. It was earned through killing!

"Heavenly Thunder."

A blue bead appeared in Yuan Xingzi's hand. It didn't look very impressive. However, when his spiritual power poured into the blue bead, a thunderbolt condensed in the void.

"Boom."

Lightning struck, aiming unerringly for Lin Fei.

Not just Yuan Xingzi but also Yuan Chenzi produced a white porcelain bottle, twisting off the cap.

From within seeped a wisp of greyish-black mist, Yuan Chenzi channeled his spiritual power and blew hard against the escaping greyish-black mist from the bottle.

Instantaneously, the greyish-black mist shot out of the bottle, increasing in volume, and it spread throughout the void. This was the "Earthly Evil."

Upon contact with the human body, it would corrode flesh and bone. In the blink of an eye, it could reduce the human body to a heap of bleached bones. Even the Innate True Qi of a martial arts Master would find it hard to resist.

Two magical treasures. Heavenly Thunder and Earthly Evil, attacking simultaneously.

He Yuanzi also arranged his He Mountain disciples in a formation, pooling the spiritual power of dozens of Qi Condensation disciples. He released a series of talismanic scrolls. The scrolls pulsated with spiritual power, subtly forming a great net.

The net continuously contracted, attempting to trap Lin Fei within.

Three methods, three top-tier Qi Condensation cultivators, and two magical treasures, simultaneously attacking Lin Fei!

It seemed Lin Fei had nowhere to escape, no means to evade!

However, Lin Fei stood his ground, entirely unmoving. He raised his head, seeing the Heavenly Thunder descend.

"Is it true Heavenly Thunder? That is the power of the natural world. How could a Qi Condensation cultivator, a single magical treasure hope to control it?

It is nothing but a strand of thunderous energy. And this talismanic net, does it hope to ensnare me?"

Lin Fei took a deep breath. The Saber Qi within his body no longer held back. It exploded in full force.

"Boom."

In the void, Lin Fei's saber energy formed a sky-high saber reaching one thousand meters. Furthermore, an invisible "saber momentum" shrouded all the Qi Condensation cultivators.

This was the momentum of the Heavenly Saber! Though it could not match the true saber momentum, even if it could suppress the opponent's strength by ten or twenty percent, it was utterly terrifying!

"What sort of power is this?"

For a moment, shock colored the faces of He Yuanzi, Yuan Xingzi, and Yuan Chenzi. They felt as if they were being oppressed by an invisible force. This was beyond their comprehension. Neither saber energy nor saber intent seemed to possess such power.

"Heavenly Saber Art, Slash!"

The next moment, at Lin Fei's mental command, the one thousand meters high saber energy slashed downwards in a grand display.

The sky-high saber in the void finally moved. As the Heavenly Saber congealed, they only felt an awe-inspiring heavenly majesty. But once the Heavenly Saber slashed down, in these Qi cultivators' eyes, it seemed as if the sky was collapsing!

Their senses, seemed to discard everything else, leaving only the Heavenly Saber in the void. The saber carried with the momentum of the heavens falling, slicing down relentlessly!

"Boom."

The immense Heavenly Thunder was severed directly by Lin Fei's massive saber energy. The endless saber energy constantly entwined and clashed with the remaining force of the Heavenly Thunder. The Heavenly Thunder had yet to reach Lin Fei but was already obliterated by the saber energy.

"Crash."

At last, the heavenly saber plummeted. He Yuanzi along with dozens of disciples formed a rune-script web, and it directly clashed with the heavenly saber...

Even though nearly half of the saber energy of the heavenly saber had been consumed by the heavenly thunder, could it really be stopped by the mere rune-script web?

"Thwack."

In an instant, the rune-script web shattered. The heavenly saber ferociously plummeted. Saber energy surged mightily. Countless Qi Condensation disciples wanted to use their spiritual power against the saber energy, which seemed to have no effect at all. Their bodies were torn apart by the saber energy in a flash.

Only three of the topmost Qi cultivators, leveraging their abundant spiritual power, could slightly resist. Yet, even so, they were exceedingly disheveled.

"Crack."

The heavenly saber fell. The saber energy dissipated. The ground was cleaved, a gigantic rift left behind, as if it would split the entire land into two.

He Yuanzi, Yuan Xingzi, and Yuan Chenzi, at this moment, exchanged glances. From the other's gaze, they saw fear and shock.

Was this the power of a martial artist? Was this the power of a martial arts master?

It was even more terrifying than their magic treasures.

"Ssst."

However, Yuan Chenzi's face suddenly lit up with joy.

"It's done! Hahaha, my 'Earthly Evil Qi' has stealthily risen from beneath the ground, binding itself to Lin Fei. The Earthly Evil Qi corrodes bones and souls, Lin Fei will undoubtedly die!"

Everyone was slightly taken aback. Then, there was ecstatic jubilation. Yes, the Heavenly Thunder was broken, the rune-script web was broken. Lin Fei's saber energy was indeed formidable and able to annihilate heavenly thunder and sever the rune-script web.

Yet, it could not eradicate the Earthly Evil Qi. After all, the Earthly Evil Qi was inherently gaseous. It could even sneak into the ground, making it impossible to guard against.

"Whoosh."

Everyone immediately looked toward Lin Fei. Sure enough, in the spot where Lin Fei stood, from the ground, a cloud of gray-black gas erupted, quickly enveloping Lin Fei.

Lin Fei's Saber Qi was rapidly corroding. Saber Qi, a form of Innate True Qi, which could not resist the Earthly Evil Qi. After all, the Earthly Evil Qi was a particularly toxic force. Once afflicted with the Earthly Evil Qi, flesh and blood couldn't resist, death was inevitable!

"Sssst."

Lin Fei's saber energy was quickly corroded by the Earthly Evil Qi. The Earthly Evil Qi, finally covered Lin Fei entirely, starting to corrode Lin Fei's skin.

Lin Fei, however, remained unmoved. He even stretched out his hand. His own fair hand was covered in gray-black Earthly Evil Qi. It looked exceedingly ferocious and unsightly.

"So, this is Earthly Evil Qi?"

Lin Fei, step by step, slowly walked toward He Yuanzi and the others. From his body, there emerged a faint golden glow.

At the same time, a peculiar, refreshing fragrance emanated from Lin Fei's body. This sight had everyone wide-eyed as if they couldn't believe what was happening. Why wasn't the Earthly Evil Qi affecting Lin Fei?

"Body emanates a fragrance, pure and dust-free, skin like a baby's... This is a sign of physical perfection! Physical perfection, also known as the Golden Body! Immune to all poisons, impervious to all spells. Even the Earthly Evil Qi can't harm those with physical perfection. But, Great Chang no longer has any exotic beasts. How could you attain physical perfection?"

He Yuanzi, seeing this scene, had a flash of inspiration. It seemed he thought of something, staring at Lin Fei with a face full of shock!

Physical perfection was a requirement that a martial artist must meet to build a foundation. And Lin Fei was now physically perfected.

Didn't this mean, Lin Fei was about to build the foundation?

Thinking of this, for a moment, the three topmost Qi Condensation disciples, had a blank mind, their spirits trembling.

Because this meant the Nine Profound Sect was about to birth a Martial Saint! Great Chang was about to birth a Martial Saint! The martial arts were about to prosper!
