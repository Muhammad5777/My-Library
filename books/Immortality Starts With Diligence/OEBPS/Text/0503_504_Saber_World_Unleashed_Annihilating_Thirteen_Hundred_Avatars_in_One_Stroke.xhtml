504. Saber World Unleashed, Annihilating Thirteen Hundred Avatars in One Stroke!

"See this?" Meditation True Person declared with a piercing gaze, devoid of any modesty, exuding an aggressive and unmatched aura. "This is my greatest reliance for dominating the Divine Realm. Who in True Spirit Realm can stand against me? North Wind True Person, do you think you can stop me?"

North Wind True Person fell silent. Thirteen hundred prime battle avatars — although he had suspected that Meditation True Person might possess over a thousand, the actual number of thirteen hundred was still a significant leap.

Especially when these densely packed avatars, eclipsing the sky, made their overwhelming presence felt. Each avatar was a match for a Martial God. Thirteen hundred Martial Gods combined — the sheer terror of it was unimaginable, let alone their ability to form a formidable array, virtually making them unstoppable.

No wonder Initial Evil True Person could fight for a minor world, seize all its world origin, and ultimately become a True Monarch. With tens of thousands of prime battle avatars, the shock must have been immense.

"Indeed, a great number is impressive," Lin Fei said, his eyes cold. "Ordinary Martial Gods might indeed be helpless against you. But not me!"

"Buzz."

The next moment, the Saber World descended. The void seemed to invert like a celestial dome, a wave of white light engulfing the space, including Meditation True Person's thirteen hundred avatars.

An ordinary Martial God, even with the law of the world, could seal only one avatar with one world law. Lin Fei, with merely twelve world laws, could at most seal twelve avatars. But what use would it be to seal twelve, twenty, thirty, or even a hundred avatars against Meditation True Person's thirteen hundred?

But Lin Fei was different. Any other Martial God, facing Meditation True Person's thirteen hundred avatars, might not have a good solution unless relying on physical prowess to confront and slay them one by one.

But Lin Fei had the Saber World! His Saber World, once deployed over an area, could suppress everything within its range. This applied even to Deity Transformation entities. As long as they couldn't break his Saber World, they would all be suppressed.

Thus, when Lin Fei unleashed the Saber World, it immediately enveloped Meditation True Person's thirteen hundred prime battle avatars.

"Suppress!" he commanded.

The entire Saber World trembled violently as Lin Fei exerted its suppressive power. The twelve world laws fused with the Saber World, significantly amplifying its might.

To the Saber World, quantity did not matter. What mattered was whether there was any force capable of breaking it. From this perspective, Lin Fei's Saber World could easily suppress Deity Transformation entities, regardless of their number of avatars.

However, suppressing a Martial God was more challenging. After all, the power of a Martial God was inherent, each punch and kick carrying immense might. The effectiveness of the Saber World's suppression was comparatively weaker against them. But suppressing thirteen hundred prime battle avatars? That was easy.

"What's happening?" Meditation True Person panicked. "Why are all my battle avatars suppressed? They can't even move! Is this the Saber World? How can the Saber World be so powerful? Saber World combined with laws?"

He had never encountered such a situation before. He thought that with thirteen hundred avatars, victory was assured. North Wind True Person was no match for him, and as for Lin Fei, a mere Martial God, how many avatars could he possibly destroy?

At worst, he would use a hundred avatars to contain Lin Fei, then deal with North Wind True Person first before slowly encircling and killing Lin Fei. This was a common strategy Deity Transformation cultivators employed against Martial Gods — a gradual, enclosing kill.

But he had never anticipated Lin Fei possessing the Saber World, let alone it suppressing all thirteen hundred of his prime battle avatars. No matter how he tried to command them, the avatars remained unresponsive, completely immobilized.

Only now did Meditation True Person start to panic. However, his anxiety was futile at this stage.

"Boom."

Lin Fei made his move. His particle worlds erupted with formidable power. It seemed as if he had sprouted multiple arms, with each grasp, several of Meditation True Person's prime battle avatars were obliterated.

Under the suppression of the Saber World, these avatars were like stationary targets, utterly defenseless. A single particle world from Lin Fei was sufficient to easily shatter an avatar.

In a brief span of time, Meditation True Person lost dozens of his avatars. Despite having thirteen hundred of them, each was a result of tens of thousands of years of cultivation and some fortunate encounters. To lose so many in just a few breaths was agonizing for him. Worse still, these losses weren't even in direct combat but as if they were mere sitting ducks, methodically eliminated by Lin Fei.

As time progressed, Lin Fei, in his True Martial King Form within the Saber World, relentlessly continued his destruction of avatar after avatar. One hundred, two hundred, three hundred, four hundred, five hundred...

By the time Lin Fei had dispatched nearly five hundred avatars, Meditation True Person couldn't bear it any longer.

"Stop, let's cease the fight," he pleaded. "I concede defeat and will no longer involve myself in the affairs of True Spirit Realm. My avatars have not been easy to cultivate, please, Martial God Lin, show mercy."

However, Lin Fei did not halt his onslaught. He continued to obliterate Meditation True Person's avatars: six hundred, seven hundred, eight hundred, nine hundred, a thousand...

Soon, Meditation True Person fell silent, watching helplessly as all his avatars were annihilated. The despair he felt, after seeing the result of tens of thousands of years of cultivation destroyed in a single battle, was unimaginable.

"Lin Fei!!!" he exclaimed in a venomous tone. "I will remember this. I will always be watching you..."

After issuing this spiteful curse, Meditation True Person detonated his few remaining avatars in a massive self-destruction. He wouldn't die from this act; he still had other avatars in different parts of the Divine Realm.

However, this battle had severely weakened him. To cultivate thirteen hundred prime battle avatars again would take tens of thousands of years, and without any fortunate encounters, it might be impossible to achieve even in that timeframe. Therefore, his malicious curse was of little concern.

Lin Fei was not one to easily forgive others. Eradicating evil was a necessity. Allowing the Meditation True Person to retain his power would only pose a threat in the future. With the destruction of Meditation True Person's avatars and the fall of Extinction Martial God, a solemn silence fell over the void.

Numerous gazes focused on this area, where previously some had contemplated exploiting the situation like a cunning oriole behind a praying mantis. However, witnessing Lin Fei's formidable strength, they abandoned such thoughts. Facing a peerless powerhouse like Lin Fei, their own demise would be swift and inexplicable.

Many powerful beings were genuinely fearful!

"Buzz."

But just at that moment, the void trembled. The very fabric of heaven and earth seemed unstable as cracks in the void appeared suddenly. An aura of apocalypse and destruction spread throughout the void.

"Is this... the world collapsing?"

"The Realm of True Spirit is about to collapse, the world's origin is emerging!"

North Wind True Person's voice, tinged with a hint of excitement, reached Lin Fei's ears.
