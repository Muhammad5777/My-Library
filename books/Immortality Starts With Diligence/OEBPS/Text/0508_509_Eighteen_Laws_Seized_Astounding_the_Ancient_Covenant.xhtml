509. Eighteen Laws Seized, Astounding the Ancient Covenant!

Many were apprehensive about whether Lin Fei could withstand the black heavenly thunder.

At that moment, Lin Fei was experiencing the destructive force of the black thunder on his physical particles. As the black thunder appeared, Lin Fei's Saber World shattered. It was as if made of paper, utterly incapable of hindering the black lightning.

Simultaneously, the black lightning penetrated Lin Fei's body. His physical particles began to rapidly disintegrate, seemingly unable to resist the black lightning.

"Boom."

At this juncture, Lin Fei did not hesitate. He mobilized the power of his one thousand three hundred Particle Worlds. The force of these Particle Worlds, once unleashed, was truly remarkable, instantly engulfing the black lightning. Even so, they intertwined and collided with the black lightning, but were still unable to prevent the disintegration of Lin Fei's physical particles.

However, the black lightning was ultimately exhaustible. Especially with the power of the one thousand three hundred Particle Worlds, it significantly depleted the potency of the black lightning.

Thus, as the black lightning continued to obliterate Lin Fei's particles, it also steadily weakened.

Ten percent, twenty percent, thirty percent, forty percent, fifty percent, sixty percent, seventy percent, eighty percent...

When eighty percent of Lin Fei's physical particles were obliterated, he was in grave danger. If twenty more percent were destroyed, Lin Fei was close to perishing. But within these remaining twenty percent lay the power of one thousand three hundred Particle Worlds, the strongest part of Lin Fei's physical form.

The obliteration of eighty percent of his physical particles finally resisted the black heavenly thunder. As the last wisp of black thunder vanished, Lin Fei knew he had transcended the sixth heavenly thunder. He had safely passed the fourth tribulation!

As Lin Fei emerged from the tribulation site, the crowd was astounded.

"The sixth heavenly thunder, Martial God Lin actually withstood it?"

"Incredible, truly incredible, this is nothing short of a miracle!"

"To withstand six heavenly thunders at once, equivalent to possessing six laws."

"Martial God Lin seized three laws in his first tribulation. Four in the second, and five in the third. Now, in his fourth tribulation, he directly seized six laws. In total, Martial God Lin possesses eighteen laws!"

"Four tribulations, eighteen laws, this is..."

"Unbelievable, utterly unbelievable. Just by the number of laws, Lin Fei has already become one of the top Martial Gods..."

Many were profoundly shocked, their hearts stirring with tumultuous waves. They hadn't previously calculated the number of laws Lin Fei possessed. But now, upon careful consideration, it was indeed startling. Right before their eyes, Lin Fei had acquired eighteen laws!

Many Martial Gods who had transcended eight heavenly tribulations didn't even possess eighteen laws. Some were even far from reaching that number. Possessing more than ten laws was enough to be called a top Martial God. But in comparison to Lin Fei, what did that amount to?

Lin Fei glanced at the crowd. Without much concern, he flew directly back to his residence. The Ancient Covenant, from this moment on, was set into motion. Four tribulations, eighteen laws. This was undoubtedly the mark of a top Martial God!

Yet, Lin Fei had only transcended four tribulations. His strength was top-notch, and his potential was equally unmatched. Many now regarded Lin Fei as a True Monarch seed. Achieving True Monarch status in the future seemed almost a certainty. As long as there were no unexpected incidents, there would be no issues.

However, regardless of the outside commotion, Lin Fei remained clear-headed. Strength was of utmost importance. Thus, after transcending the fourth tribulation, Lin Fei immediately went into seclusion. He intended to use the newly obtained six world laws on his saber. He planned to continue creating Particle Worlds!

And these would be particles more than ten times stronger than before. Of course, with stronger particles, the difficulty of creating Particle Worlds increased. Naturally, this would consume a considerable amount of time. Hence, Lin Fei secluded himself in the Ancient City, quietly cultivating.

...

North Wind True Person was preparing to enter his cave abode when a familiar figure appeared. The Bloodthirsty Martial God! The very one Lin Fei had repelled, now re-emerged.

As an old adversary, North Wind True Person knew the Bloodthirsty Martial God all too well.

"Bloodthirsty Martial God, what are you up to?" North Wind True Person asked in a deep voice.

"Heh, what am I up to? You've been scheming in the True Spirit Realm for so long, could you have gained nothing? Hand over the world's origin."

The target of Bloodthirsty Martial God was naturally the world's origin.

North Wind True Person scoffed, "You dare not rob Lin Fei of his world's origin, so you target me instead to seize what I possess? Bloodthirsty, don't forget, I am a Deity Transformation cultivator! Myriad forms of my Deity Transformation, each capable of carrying the world's origin. Even if you obliterate countless of my incarnations, how could you find the world's origin?"

North Wind True Person truly wasn't afraid of Bloodthirsty Martial God. For a Deity Transformation cultivator who wished to escape, no one could stop them. After all, no one could kill a Deity Transformation cultivator. Even a True Monarch would have to lock onto the incarnation of a Deity Transformation cultivator, and then traverse the void, eradicating one incarnation after another. It was unknown how long it would take to possibly kill a Deity Transformation cultivator.

"Yes, you are a Deity Transformation cultivator, and I can't harm you. But someone can restrain you. Shen, what do you say?"

As Bloodthirsty Martial God's words fell, a beast, existing between illusion and reality, appeared. The divine-level exotic beast, Shen!

North Wind True Person's pupils contracted, an overwhelming fear seizing him. He exclaimed in shock, "Shen? Bloodthirsty, you're actually associated with Shen, even summoning it? Take it, take all my world's origin. Don't kill me. My cultivation has not been easy, tens of thousands of years of arduous practice..."

The dignified North Wind True Person, upon seeing "Shen," immediately revealed a look of utter despair in his eyes, even begging the Bloodthirsty Martial God for mercy.

However, Shen paid no heed to North Wind True Person's pleas. Its eyes emitted faint blue glimmers, instantly captivating North Wind True Person's attention.

North Wind True Person's face contorted as if struggling but to no avail. Soon after, his expression relaxed, his eyes filled with bewilderment.

"Hand over the world's origin."

Shen spoke slowly.

North Wind True Person handed over all the world's origin to Bloodthirsty Martial God.

"Bloodthirsty, it's your call whether to kill or spare him. But remember, this is the favor I owe you. After this, we have nothing to do with each other."

Shen's voice was hoarse.

"Of course. Only you can deal with a Deity Transformation cultivator. This North Wind True Person, if spared, would remain a threat. Just kill him."

Bloodthirsty Martial God said indifferently.

"As you wish."

Shen coldly said, "Annihilate. Let all incarnations self-destruct."

"Boom."

As Shen's words fell, North Wind True Person's body exploded instantaneously. Simultaneously, countless incarnations of North Wind True Person also detonated.

This was self-destruction. Shen, capable of illusion, could completely control the mind of a Deity Transformation cultivator, causing all their incarnations to self-destruct.

This was tantamount to suicide! This was the true terror of Shen!

"The deed is done, Bloodthirsty, take care of yourself. One last piece of advice, do not provoke Lin Fei. The people of the Ancient Covenant are not to be trifled with! Especially, he seems to have come from the Ancient Continent. Those who come to the Divine Realm from the Ancient Continent are a group with limitless achievements..."

Shen's voice grew fainter and more elusive. Finally, its figure disappeared without a trace.

"Lin Fei..."

A flicker of unusual light passed through Bloodthirsty Martial God's eyes. With these world origins, passing the ninth heavenly tribulation would be no problem. Then, it would be time to achieve True Monarch status and shatter the void. If he reached the state of shattering the void, why should he care about a mere Martial God?

"Swoosh."

The next moment, Bloodthirsty Martial God stepped forward and vanished without a trace.
