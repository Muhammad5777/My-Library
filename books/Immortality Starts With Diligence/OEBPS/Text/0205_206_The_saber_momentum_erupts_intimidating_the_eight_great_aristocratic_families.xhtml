206. The saber momentum erupts, intimidating the eight great aristocratic families

Seeing how the representatives of the eight great aristocratic families were so excited, Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin remained calm. Because they had learned some stories about "Seekers of the Dao" during their few days on Fairy Mist Island.

Whoever could cross the sea would usually arrive at Fairy Mist Island first. Those people were either top-tier Foundation Establishment True Person or Martial Saints. They appear once every few hundred or even a thousand years. But every time they appear, they bring great changes to Fairy Mist Island. Because these seekers of the Dao, their final achievements were not small!

There were those who become Golden Core True Person and also those who have become Martial Sovereign. Even, there were those who have achieved even higher! For the eight great Martial Saint families, seekers of the Dao represented a guarantee! A guarantee that they could enter the sect!

"Senior Martial Saints, I believe you both are aware of the situation. Since you are seekers of the Dao, you are destined to climb to the pinnacle of martial arts, you must go to the martial arts sect! Such as Martial Saints like you two, those sects with profound martial arts, capable of climbing to the pinnacle of martial arts, usually don't accept. The only exception is the Ancient Origin Sect. Ancient Origin Sect's purpose is to accommodate all rivers, different paths lead to the same goal. Even if it's a Martial Saint, Ancient Origin Sect will accept. But it needs a recommendation quota.

This recommendation quota is not easy to find. Even if you two are Martial Saints, it won't be easy to get a recommendation quota. But our eight great families on Fairy Mist Island have a recommendation quota. We recommend you two Martial Saints go to Ancient Origin Sect and compete for the position of 18 outer gate elders. This is equivalent to the position of inner gate disciples. Once you become outer gate elders, each of you Martial Saints can recommend five disciples, without exams, directly entering the outer gate of the Ancient Origin Sect.

Joining hands will benefit both, going separate ways will hurt both! I wonder what you two seniors think?"

The representatives of the eight great aristocratic families did not beat around the bush but straightforwardly stated this matter. Joining hands will benefit both. Going separate ways will hurt both. Everything is out in the open.

"That is reasonable, you need Martial Saints and we need recommendation quotas. However, I have one condition. Your eight great Martial Saint families must have collected a lot of martial arts, right? I need innate-level saber techniques that can cultivate saber intent. The more the better."

Lin Fei also proposed a condition. He did not choose martial techniques of the Martial Saint level. Because there was no need. Only those major sects had techniques that could reach the level of Martial Sovereign. Besides, he wanted to enter Ancient Origin Sect. Where would he need the common techniques of these Martial Saint families?

But saber techniques were different. Each top-tier innate level saber technique, especially those that could cultivate saber intent, could increase Lin Fei's foundation in saber techniques.

Lin Fei could find ways to let these top-tier saber techniques evolve saber momentum. When Lin Fei's saber momentum achieved transcendence, its power could increase countless times. Therefore, top-tier saber techniques were very important to Lin Fei.

"Saber techniques that can cultivate saber intent? This is not a problem. Our eight great families have collected some top-tier saber techniques."

The eight great families also nodded. It was just some saber techniques. They could copy them and give them to Lin Fei.

"Two Martial Saints, there is one more thing. Although you two are seekers of the Dao, we would like to know more about your strength. After all, there are many Martial Saints who want to enter Ancient Origin Sect. If you don't have particularly strong power, it might not be easy to get into Ancient Origin Sect smoothly."

The eight great families also made a request to understand the strength of Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin.

"This request is not excessive. How would you like to know?"

"Of course, it's through a spar. Although we're not Martial Saints, we have achieved perfection in our physical bodies. And, the martial arts we have learned are not weak. Even if it's a Martial Saint, the eight of us can resist when joining forces."

The eight people finished speaking and turned their gazes to Lin Fei.

"I will only draw my saber once!"

At this time, Shen Lianxin stepped forward. He swept his gaze over the eight people.

"Thank you, senior, for your guidance!"

Therefore, the eight Martial Grandmasters also exuded a strong momentum.

"Ultimate Saber Slash!"

Shen Lianxin slashed out with his saber. Suddenly, a saber light of thousands of meters cut through the sky, like a dazzling rainbow.

The eight people were greatly shocked.

"Nine Dragons Dance!"

"Eternal Saber Slash!"

"Dragon Gate Shadow!"

One by one, the Martial Grandmasters displayed their own martial arts. True Qi burst out, and even Martial Dao True Intent also burst out. The eight people joined hands, and their momentum reached its peak. It seemed like they could really resist a Martial Saint.

"Bang."

The next moment, the saber light slashed down. The eight Grandmasters, each one of them shook. Their clothes were cut by the saber light. They looked very embarrassed.

"Clang."

Looking at Shen Lianxin again. After slashing that saber, he had already sheathed his saber. The whole person was like an indifferent iceberg, silent and reserved.

"Senior Shen is really too strong."

"Even though he is a Martial Saint, this saber, Senior Shen is even stronger than all the Martial Saints we have seen!"

"And that saber intent, it's simply reached an extreme sharpness."

"With just one saber, we couldn't resist it..."

Although the eight Grandmasters were shocked, they were very happy in their hearts. They used their recommendation quotas to send Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin to the Ancient Origin Sect. The stronger Shen Lianxin and Lin Fei were, the happier they were. If both could enter the Ancient Origin Sect, it might be the turning point for the eight great families in recent centuries.

"Senior Shen's strength is amazing, even among the Martial Saints, he is a strong one!"

The eight innate Masters from the great families recognized Shen Lianxin's strength. So, their gazes turned to Lin Fei.

"I wonder, Senior Lin..."

"Do you also want to spar with me?"

Lin Fei spoke in a calm tone. Shen Lianxin had always kept a cold face. Like an ice sculpture, he remained silent. But now, his gaze swept over the people from the eight great families. A hint of pity was revealed in his eyes.

The people from the eight great families didn't understand. They nodded and said respectfully, "We rudely ask for guidance from Senior Lin!"

Lin Fei's gaze was deep. Looking at the eight innate Masters in front of him. He spoke calmly, "A spar is fine, but don't draw your sabers. No one can draw their saber in front of me!"

Lin Fei's gaze fell on the three Masters who used sabers. Out of the eight innate Masters, there were three saber users. They had even all comprehended saber intent. However, the saber intent had only reached transcendence, far from perfection.

"Uh? Senior, although you are a Martial Saint, we haven't reached the point where we can't even draw our sabers."

The three saber users had anger on their faces. Lin Fei's words made them feel as if they were being "disrespected". So, they put their hands on the hilt of their sabers, trying to draw them.

"Hum."

But the next moment, their expressions changed slightly. The sabers in their hands actually began to tremble violently. No matter how hard they tried, they couldn't draw them. Even using their True Qi didn't change anything. The sabers seemed to be tightly sucked by their sheaths. They simply couldn't draw their sabers.

"This is..."

The eight Innate Masters suddenly looked up. Their gazes were incredibly shocked as they looked at Lin Fei.

"Boom."

The next moment, Lin Fei didn't hold back any longer. His saber momentum instantly exploded. Majestic and overwhelming, it pressed on the eight Innate Masters.

Immediately, the eight Innate Masters, not to mention making a move, even moving a single step was extremely difficult. Without becoming a Martial Saint, in the face of Lin Fei's saber momentum, even making a move was a luxury!

"Saber momentum!"

"It's actually saber momentum!"

"Hahaha, senior can actually comprehend saber momentum!"

"Dao Seeker, this is a true Dao Seeker!"

For a moment, the eight innate Masters, expressions of immense shock appeared on their faces. Even their voices began to tremble with excitement.

"Now, do we still need to spar?"

Lin Fei stood with his hands behind his back. His gaze fell on the eight Grandmasters as he calmly asked.
