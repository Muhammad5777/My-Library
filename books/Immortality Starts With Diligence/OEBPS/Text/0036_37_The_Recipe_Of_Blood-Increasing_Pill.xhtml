A girl escaping from a marriage. An ancient family. A power long forgotten.

Althea wished she could transmigrate into a different world. Even a book would do. And one day she did. But there was a teensy tiny problem.

The character she transmigrated into was the cannon fodder fiance of the male lead. The misunderstood girl that ends up sacrificing herself in the final battle after spending her last days in jail, accused of murder.

So she does the thing her books taught her. Tear the engagement and let the lovebirds deal with each other. That is about when things began to change.

Lin Fei pondered for a long time, and a figure came to mind - Ling Yao! Among the people he knew in the inner sect, perhaps only Ling Yao could help.

After all, Ling Yao had an extraordinary background and was a key disciple cultivated by the Nine Profound Sect. With this in mind, Lin Fei went straight to Ling Yao's courtyard.

"Senior Sister Ling," Lin Fei said as he arrived at Ling Yao's courtyard, where he found other female disciples who seemed to be inner sect disciples.

These female disciples looked at Lin Fei curiously, and he greeted them respectfully, "Greetings, senior sisters."

"Senior Sister Ling, it seems that this junior brother is looking for you for something, then we are gonna leave first."

"Senior Sister Ling, we will come back tomorrow."

"Hee hee, Senior Sister Ling, think about that matter yo~"

Thus, all three female martial artists got up and left the courtyard.

As the three female warriors rose to leave, Ling Yao raised her head and looked at Lin Fei, "You usually keep to yourself, and this is your first time coming to me. What do you need my help with?"

Knowing Lin Fei's character, Ling Yao was sure that he had something important to discuss. Lin Fei hesitated for a moment before asking, "Do you happen to have the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill?"

"The recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill?" Ling Yao frowned and asked, "Only innate masters or mysterious Qi cultivators can make pills. I don't know why you need the recipe, but if you really need it, I'll try to get it for you. But are you sure you want to waste the favor I owe you on the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill?"

Lin Fei could tell what was going on. Ling Yao could obtain the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill. And it didn't seem to be too difficult for her.

"I am sure, please help me, senior sister." Lin Fei replied firmly.

A favor in exchange for the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill. As long as Lin Fei needed it, he did not feel like he was losing out.

"Okay, I will give you the recipe tomorrow."

Lin Fei nodded, naturally trusting her.

He left, and Ling Yao watched his figure disappear, her gaze becoming sharp.

"The recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill. Seems that this junior brother Lin also has secrets..." Ling Yao shook her head and did not think about it any further.

After all, she also had her own secrets. Anyway, she had already befriended Lin Fei, and that was enough.

The next day, Ling Yao personally came to Lin Fei's courtyard.

"This is the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill you wanted."

Ling Yao handed a recipe to Lin Fei directly.

"Thank you, senior sister!"

Lin Fei looked at Ling Yao meaningfully and breathed a sigh of relief.

It was obvious that she was not a simple disciple to be able to obtain the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill so easily.

"In fact, the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill is not as difficult as you think. This is not a unique elixir of the Nine Profound Sect, many other sects have it too.

So, as long as you pay a certain price, you can still get the recipe. After all, the recipe is not the key. The most crucial thing is the ability to refine the pill."

Lin Fei naturally understood what Ling Yao meant.

The recipe could be obtained easily, but it was not so easy to have innate experts or Qi cultivators.

However, Lin Fei also knew that Ling Yao made it sound easy, but she must have paid some price to obtain the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill. It was definitely not easy.

"Anyway, thank you, Senior Sister Ling."

After receiving the recipe for the Blood-Increasing Pill from Ling Yao, Lin Fei packed his luggage and was ready to leave.

He took a look at the various medicinal materials on the Blood-Increasing Pill. They were not particularly precious, and even on the mountain below, they could be purchased.

So Lin Fei rented a fast horse and left the Nine Profound Sect once again. However, this time Lin Fei wasn't in a hurry to travel.

"I need silver."

At the Nine Profound Sect, Lin Fei only had contribution points. But purchasing the raw materials needed for alchemy off the mountain would require silver.

Of course, maybe Lin's family could provide the silver, but refining alchemy was a very expensive process. Especially since Lin Miao might not succeed in refining the pill, and it would require practice, which would cost even more.

Therefore, if he could get some silver, he would try to get as much as possible. Lin Fei's method was also very simple, even crude.

He didn't take the official road, but instead took some smaller roads, preferably in the mountains and rugged terrain. He wanted to encounter some bandits on his own initiative. By killing the bandits, he would naturally be able to get silver.

There was also a reason why Lin Fei had the confidence to do this. The last time Lin Fei went down the mountain, he was only at the fourth level of Body Refining, so he had to be cautious.

But now, Lin Fei had reached the ninth level of Body Refining, and his Nine Shadow Saber Technique had reached perfection, even reaching the level of one slash with ten shadows.

Lin Fei's comprehensive strength was comparable to some acquired martial artists. Therefore, he didn't have to be as cautious as last time.

However, Lin Fei didn't know if his luck was too good or too bad. Even though he took the smaller roads, he didn't encounter a single bandit.

Just as Lin Fei was about to return to the official road, he suddenly smelled the scent of blood.

"Is something happening?" Lin Fei's eyes lit up. Finally, he encountered something. He hoped it was a group of bandits blocking the road so he could kill them and get some silver.

Lin Fei got off the horse and quietly headed toward the direction where the smell of blood was coming from.
