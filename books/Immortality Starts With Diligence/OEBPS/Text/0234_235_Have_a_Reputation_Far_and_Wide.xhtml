235. Have a Reputation Far and Wide

"He did it!"

Lin Fei retracted his Qi Flower. The momentum gradually disappeared. This time, although it was a bit different from what he had imagined, as long as he could condense the Qi Flower, it would be fine.

Moreover, this also gave Lin Fei a reference. To condense the Energy Flower or Spirit Flower, he still needs to practice the technique to perfection. In that way, the chance of condensing the three flowers should be greater.

"Congratulations to Elder Lin, for successfully condensing the Qi Flower and achieving the status of a Limit Martial Saint!"

The elder of the Spiritual Qi Pool also hurried forward to congratulate him.

In the Ancient Origin Sect, Limit Martial Saints hold a lofty status. Basically, they were all high-ranking figures of the Ancient Origin Sect. If they were true disciples, they were seeds of Martial Sovereigns. All kinds of resources would be tilted towards them to the greatest extent.

Whether they were disciples or elders, once they reached the status of ultimate Martial Saints, they would become the real core. Their status was second only to the Martial Sovereign of the sect.

Lin Fei nodded.

The disciples outside the Spiritual Qi Pool also shouted in unison: "Congratulations to Elder Lin for condensing the Qi Flower and achieving the status of a Limit Martial Saint!"

Everyone knew that with Lin Fei achieving the status of Limit Martial Saint, he was set to rise to prominence. His identity and status would undergo qualitative changes. This was completely different from the battle with Ao Yunxiao.

Lin Fei defeated Ao Yunxiao. At most, he was just given attention by the sect. But he was still an outer sect elder. His status and identity did not improve at all. But becoming a Limit Martial Saint, he automatically entered the core high levels of the sect. It's a world apart from before.

Lin Fei also nodded at the many disciples, but he didn't say anything. Instead, he took a step and left the Spiritual Qi Pool.

As Lin Fei left, the news of Lin Fei condensing a Qi Flower and advancing to Limit Martial Saint also immediately spread throughout the entire Ancient Origin Sect. This was no small matter. Lin Fei was also a famous Dao Seeker. He entered the Ancient Origin Sect half a year ago and he condensed a Qi Flower. When this news came out, it immediately shocked the entire Ancient Origin Sect.

In Ao Yunxiao's cave dwelling, Ao Yunxiao's injuries were actually healed a long time ago. However, he was still in closed-door cultivation. His injury was not physical but in his soul. He was defeated by Lin Fei in front of everyone. Not only did he fail in his build-up, but also a shadow was cast on his soul.

If this issue of the shadow on his soul was not resolved, then Ao Yunxiao should not think about comprehending the momentum of martial arts anymore. It was equivalent to cutting off the path of martial arts!

Therefore, for the past half year, Ao Yunxiao had been adjusting his state, gradually adjusting his soul. After half a year of adjustment, he had already made some progress. However, to completely resolve the issue of the shadow on the soul, how easy could it be? It still needed a long time to adjust slowly.

However, it finally gave Ao Yunxiao some hope. He was confident. Within ten years, he would definitely be able to resolve the issue of the shadow on his soul.

Even more, Ao Yunxiao had a feeling. Once he resolved the shadow on his soul, he might be able to turn a calamity into a blessing and rise to great heights, even directly comprehend the martial momentum!

It's just that the time frame of ten years might be a bit long. But Ao Yunxiao could wait! He had patience.

Step by step at the beginning, he was able to become a true disciple because he had patience. Even if it wasted ten years, Ao Yunxiao firmly believed that he could get rid of the shadow on his soul. Even condense the Spirit Flower and achieve the pinnacle of martial arts! At this moment, his maid ran back hastily from outside the cave.

"Report! Master, the outer sect elder Lin Fei, today in the Spiritual Qi Pool, in front of everyone, has condensed the Qi Flower and achieved the status of Limit Martial Saint!"

"What?"

Ao Yunxiao widened his eyes. The aura on his body even became chaotic. The confidence that had just been established disappeared without a trace in a blink of an eye. Even in the depths of Ao Yunxiao's heart, there was a deep sense of defeat.

"How is it possible? Lin Fei has only joined the Ancient Origin Sect for a mere half a year. How could he condense a Qi Flower?"

Ao Yunxiao murmured to himself in a low voice. His voice was trembling. He didn't believe it!

"Master, it's true. The news has now spread throughout the entire Ancient Origin Sect. It's absolutely true!"

"Bang".

Ao Yunxiao's fist slammed into the ground.

"Get out!"

The maid was in a panic, her face pale. It was Ao Yunxiao who had always asked her to collect information about Lin Fei. She didn't dare to linger and immediately fled the cave-like she was escaping.

Ao Yunxiao's face was as pale as paper. The confidence that had just been built up was gone completely. In the dark, he seemed to feel that the little belief that he had been holding onto in his heart had completely collapsed!

......

In the cave of Shen Lianxin. He had been injured by Ao Yunxiao. It's been a whole half year. Half a year's time, he kept recuperating. Although he couldn't cultivate true essence, it was a blessing in disguise for Shen Lianxin. Because his understanding of the saber momentum improved by leaps and bounds. Half a year's time, he kept pondering over saber techniques, saber intent. For the evolution of saber momentum, he already had confidence.

"Report! Master, the outer sect elder Lin Fei, today in the Spiritual Qi Pool, in front of everyone, has condensed the Qi Flower, and achieved the status of a Limit Martial Saint! Now the entire Ancient Origin Sect has been shaken up.."

When the maid entered the cave, after informing Shen Lianxin of the news.

"Swish."

Shen Lianxin instantly opened his eyes.

"Limit Martial Saint......"

Shen Lianxin murmured to himself in a low voice. He wasn't shocked. It was just a bit unexpected. But even if he felt surprised, upon careful thought, it seemed to be within reason.

Shen Lianxin understood Lin Fei too well. Among the entire Ancient Origin Sect, the person who understood Lin Fei the best was Shen Lianxin.

"Master, you don't seem surprised? News about Elder Lin is now being spread all over the Ancient Origin Sect. There are all kinds of speculations."

The maid seemed a bit puzzled. She came to the cave to serve Shen Lianxin, but the number of words she heard from Shen Lianxin was few and far between. She even felt Shen Lianxin could remain silent for years. But such big news, how could Shen Lianxin not be surprised?

"You don't understand," Shen Lianxin shook his head, speaking in a calm tone, "Even if it was a month, not half a year, I wouldn't be surprised if Lin Fei became a Limit Martial Saint. Back in Great Chang, Brother Lin grew all the way and fought unbeaten under the heavens, which was much more shocking than this. If he didn't become a Limit Martial Saint in a year or so, I would be the one surprised."

Shen Lianxin shook his head. Lin Fei became a Limit Martial Saint, condensing the Qi Flower had been within his expectations. Shen Lianxin was not surprised at all. However, even though he wasn't surprised, there was still a slight ripple in his heart.

"Brother Lin, I came to the Ancient Origin Sect with you. Now that you've become a Limit Martial Saint. I can't fall too far behind you. Today, let's evolve the saber momentum."

Shen Lianxin felt a sense of happiness in his heart. He immediately felt that the opportunity had come! After all, Lin Fei becoming a Limit Martial Saint was also a stimulus to Shen Lianxin. He couldn't possibly be truly indifferent. If he were truly indifferent, then his martial arts path would certainly not go far.

"Boom."

The next moment, Shen Lianxin's saber intent erupted. It was vast and mighty, filling the entire cave. Finally, the terrifying saber intent began to evolve. The various saber techniques Shen Lianxin had practiced before all appeared within the saber intent.

This is evolution! The evolution of saber momentum! Everything fell into place naturally. At this moment, Shen Lianxin also successfully evolved the saber momentum!
