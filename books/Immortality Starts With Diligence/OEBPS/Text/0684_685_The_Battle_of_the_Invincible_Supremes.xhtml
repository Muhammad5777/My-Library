685. The Battle of the Invincible Supremes!

"This is?" The Doomsday Supreme muttered under his breath in disbelief. "A Supreme reaching this level, unbelievable."

The auras of the two Supremes collided continuously. Although they had not yet come to blows, any Supreme venturing too close would be instantly pulverized by the mere fringes of their auras. This was no longer merely at the level of Supremes but surpassed it entirely!

Witnessing this, the Clairvoyant Supreme hurriedly intervened, "Both Supremes, you must not fight here. This is Kunyu City! If you alarm His Majesty the Master of Chaos, there will be trouble."

Following the Clairvoyant Supreme's words, both Lin Fei and the Doomsday Supreme simultaneously retracted their auras. The void around them calmed as if the intense moment before had been nothing but an illusion. However, the Clairvoyant Supreme knew it was no illusion. He looked at Lin Fei deeply—stronger again!

He had collaborated with Lin Fei outside the Tranquil Domain for so long; he knew Lin Fei's strength well. Even ordinary top-tier Supremes couldn't match Lin Fei, but at that time, Lin Fei wasn't as overwhelmingly powerful as he was now.

Today, Lin Fei had clearly transcended the level of a Supreme, having completely transformed. He was in a class of his own, now capable of contending even with the Doomsday Supreme—standing on the same level of what they called the "Invincible Supreme."

"Both Supremes, even if you wish to spar, please do not do it in Kunyu City," the Clairvoyant Supreme pleaded. "The Chaos outside is vast; you can spar there."

The Doomsday Supreme responded with a faint smile, "Supreme Lin, this is our first meeting. Initially, I approached this meeting with some skepticism. But now, I have no doubts. In fact, I believe coming here was the right decision. After so many years, the Chaos has finally given birth to another Invincible Supreme. It seems some people's worries were justified. They would do anything to eliminate you, but it appears they will be disappointed."

The Doomsday Supreme's tone was both mocking and clear. He was well aware that many sought to use him as a means to deal with Lin Fei. But now, it was evident that he would not, and perhaps could not, take that action. He believed that Lin Fei had reached the same heights as him. Both were now Invincible Supremes!

"Invincible Supreme..." Lin Fei murmured softly, contemplating his own transformation. If before, the transformation of his Eternal Realm had only caused him a hint of doubt or conjecture, now he was certain. He had indeed reached the level of the Doomsday Supreme. Yet, having never truly battled with the Doomsday Supreme, Lin Fei retained a sliver of uncertainty.

"Doomsday Supreme, would you honor me with a lesson?" Lin Fei initiated, eager to test himself. "What exactly is the level of an Invincible Supreme?"

"Hahaha, I had the same intention," the Doomsday Supreme laughed heartily. "Let us proceed to the Chaos."

With a flash, the Doomsday Supreme transformed into a streak of light, vanishing from Kunyu City. Lin Fei, without hesitation, quickly followed.

Left behind, the Clairvoyant Supreme held the Chaos Pearl, an artifact that could offer profound insights. Yet, the impending clash between Lin Fei and the Doomsday Supreme—although just a spar—was on the level of Invincible Supremes.

Even with the Chaos Pearl, the Clairvoyant Supreme doubted his ability to reach the Master of Chaos or even become an Invincible Supreme himself. Such opportunities were rare and not to be missed.

"Let it be, such an opportunity cannot be missed!" With a heavy heart, the Clairvoyant Supreme made up his mind and swiftly turned into a streak of light, following Lin Fei and the Doomsday Supreme into the vast, cold void of Chaos.

There, Lin Fei and the Doomsday Supreme faced each other, each sizing up the other. Lin Fei felt no immediate danger; his return to the Chaos suggested that perhaps the Behemoth King had not taken special note of him.

After all, the Behemoth King, being on the level of a Master of Chaos, would hardly concern himself with a mere Supreme, no matter how strong Lin Fei appeared.

"Invincible Supreme..." Lin Fei's fighting spirit surged. He was determined to understand what truly defined an Invincible Supreme.

"Illusory Realm!" At that moment, Lin Fei unleashed his Illusory Realm. The Illusory Realm descended, swiftly enveloping the Doomsday Supreme within it. To Lin Fei's surprise, the Doomsday Supreme exuded a faint aura of destruction, and a dark light seemed capable of crushing and distorting everything—even Lin Fei's Illusory Realm. Once it covered the Doomsday Supreme, it began subtly distorting, an absolutely unimaginable sight.

"Psychic and spiritual power? Interesting. But in the face of my distorting power, even psychic and spiritual powers are no exception. They too will be twisted!"

The Doomsday Supreme spoke slowly, revealing his power as the power of distortion—capable of twisting even mental and spiritual forces. This kind of power was truly wondrous, even divine.

Since its inception, Lin Fei's Illusory Realm had almost never faltered. Only a few opponents with strong mental will could withstand it, such as the Dark Demon Lord.

Yet now, faced with the Doomsday Supreme's unique tactics, the complete dissolution of the Illusory Realm right before him was unprecedented. Never before had Lin Fei encountered such a scenario where the Illusory Realm proved futile.

With the Illusory Realm ineffective, Lin Fei had only the Eternal Realm to rely upon—this was a measure of his true, unadulterated strength, devoid of any trickery.

"It has been ages since anyone has compelled me to exert my full power," Lin Fei's voice rose slowly. "Eternal Realm!"

In the next moment, Lin Fei's form vanished, replaced by a vast world, the Eternal Realm. Upon its emergence, Lin Fei's aura surged tremendously, rolling majestically toward the Doomsday Supreme.

"Transformed into a world?" The Doomsday Supreme recognized Lin Fei's state immediately. Such transformation was exceedingly rare. Though not unheard of, the aura emanating from Lin Fei's transformed world was astounding. It seemed to be merely at the level of a True Immortal world, yet it sent shivers down the spine of the Doomsday Supreme.

Even the Clairvoyant Supreme, standing far off, trembled, his innermost senses screaming alarms.

"Astounding, how has Lin Fei grown so powerful in such a short time? What exactly did he do in that world of behemoths?"

The Clairvoyant Supreme was shaken, knowing well that Lin Fei's rapid advancement was undoubtedly connected to the Chotic Behemoth world he had ventured into.

Yet, he could not fathom how, in such a perilous world, Lin Fei could have captured enough Chaotic Behemoths to evolve to his current state. Could it be that Lin Fei had truly swept through all the Chaotic Behemoths in that world? The thought was almost inconceivable.

"Ha ha ha, it has been a long while since I've encountered a foe of equal stature," the Doomsday Supreme bellowed with a laugh. "As one who is called 'Doomsday,' I naturally possess apocalyptic powers. Let us see whether your world is stronger, or if my 'Doomsday' force prevails."

With a thunderous roar, the Doomsday Supreme's distortion power swiftly expanded, warping the entire chaotic void around them. Within this distortion, a unique force seemed to emerge, as if heralding destruction!
