306. Dual-World Saber

"Saber Dao Divine Ability!"

Lin Fei retreated into the room. He beheld the manual of the Divine Ability before him.

The Dual-World Saber!

It was a genuine Saber Dao Divine Ability. Today, Lin Fei witnessed Sovereign Yuan manifesting the Sword Dao Divine Ability. Its potency was undoubtedly impressive.

Where there's Sword Dao Divine Ability, there's Saber Dao Divine Ability. Correspondingly, if there exists a Sword Realm or Saber Domain, cultivating Sword Dao or Saber Dao Divine Abilities becomes relatively easier.

Previously, Lin Fei hadn't practiced the Saber Dao Divine Ability, simply because he hadn't found an apt one. Moreover, he already possessed a Saber Domain, feeling that practicing Saber Dao Divine Ability might not hold much significance.

To achieve the status of Martial King, especially a King of powerful lineage, one's Divine Abilities shouldn't be too uniform. Variety was essential! Diversity was key!

A wide array of skills was preferable. One should have a touch of every Divine Ability. The Ancient Origin Sect, however, lacked top-tier Saber Dao Divine Abilities. If Lin Fei persisted on a single path, acquiring nine top-notch Saber Dao Great Divine Abilities might be a different journey.

Thus, Lin Fei chose other Great Divine Abilities. The outcome had been rather fruitful. Yet, this Dual-World Saber was distinctive. When the saber strikes, it splits two realms! One realm experiences mortal demise, and the other, spiritual annihilation!

This was indeed a supreme Great Divine Ability. Lin Fei even faintly felt that this Dual-World Saber surpassed his Black Wind Prison and Gravity Domain.

However powerful a Divine Ability might be, one must start as a beginner. Facing the Saber Dao Great Divine Ability, Lin Fei naturally invoked his Saber Domain, seeking to "subdue" this Divine Ability.

With a thunderous roar, Lin Fei manifested his Saber Domain. Instantaneously, the Saber Domain enveloped the Dual-World Saber manual.

"Follow me and become my Divine Ability. In the future, if I become the Martial King, you will fully integrate into my bloodline. You will be eternal! Indestructible for all ages!"

Lin Fei addressed the Dual-World Saber. With the support of his Saber Domain, any ordinary Saber Dao Divine Ability would undoubtedly be unproblematic.

Lin Fei waited in stillness. Time ticked away. After a quarter of an hour, Lin Fei's brow furrowed. The Dual-World Saber remained unresponsive.

"No response? Is it unwilling, or are my terms insufficiently enticing?"

Lin Fei had the Saber Domain and ambitions of becoming the Martial King. Was this not enough?

Typically, top-tier Great Divine Abilities, under the influence of Lin Fei's Saber Domain and the grand ambition to become Martial King, would readily succumb. Yet, the Dual-World Saber remained unresponsive.

"The Dual-World Saber is prideful, refusing to serve the host."

"Refused?"

In Lin Fei's mind, the system's voice finally rang out. With seemingly no reason, it was simply unwilling.

"Fire."

Lin Fei's gaze grew icy. Apparently, the Dual-World Saber responded better to force than coaxing. He conjured flames, Immediately setting the Dual-World Saber manual ablaze. After all, he had committed the Dual-World Saber manual to memory. He could easily recreate it if needed.

"The Dual-World Saber would rather die than serve, preferring to be reduced to ashes than to follow the host."

Lin Fei fell silent. It was the first time he encountered such a situation. While he had faced refusals from Divine Abilities or martial arts in the past, Usually, when he threatened to destroy their manuals, they would relent. Yet, this Dual-World Saber showed a true "spine." It preferred death over servitude.

"In that case..."

Lin Fei prepared to truly burn the Dual-World Saber manual. Would a recreated Dual-World Saber have a different temperament?

"Your Divine Ability Black Wind Prison sensed the disdain of the Dual-World Saber and is aggrieved. It has now challenged the Dual-World Saber."

"Your Divine Ability Gravity Domain sensed the disdain of the Dual-World Saber and is aggrieved. It too has now challenged the Dual-World Saber."

Again, the system's voice resonated in Lin Fei's mind. He hadn't expected his two Divine Abilities to defend his honor, directly challenging the Dual-World Saber.

So, Lin Fei closed his eyes, focusing his consciousness inward. Indeed, in his mind's eye, three luminous orbs manifested. They were Black Wind Prison, Gravity Domain, and Dual-World Saber.

Black Wind Prison and Gravity Domain jointly assailed the Dual-World Saber. Yet, the Dual-World Saber was truly remarkable. With a single strike, both Black Wind Prison and Gravity Domain seemed hard-pressed to defend.

Watching as his two Divine Abilities seemed to be steadily losing ground, Lin Fei's heart grew cold. Regardless, Black Wind Prison and Gravity Domain were HIS Divine Abilities. They had even taken the initiative to battle the Dual-World Saber. How could Lin Fei stand by and watch them be defeated?

"Saber Intent!"

In the next moment, Lin Fei unleashed his saber intent. For only the saber intent could aid Black Wind Prison and Gravity Domain within his mind. Moreover, Lin Fei possessed not just one type of saber intent, but hundreds!

"Boom!"

Countless saber intents, like a massive tidal wave, rushed toward the Dual-World Saber. Black Wind Prison and Gravity Domain, sensing Lin Fei's support, were reinvigorated, charging at the Dual-World Saber once more.

There's truth to the saying that many ants can kill an elephant. Eventually, the Dual-World Saber was completely overwhelmed by the myriad of saber intents.

"Your Divine Abilities, Black Wind Prison, and Gravity Domain have together defeated Dual-World Saber."

"Dual-World Saber, defeated by the host's Divine Abilities, has completely surrendered and wishes to become the host's Divine Ability."

"The Divine Ability Dual-World Saber has become the host's Divine Ability."

"Upon sensing the host's Saber Domain, Divine Ability Dual-World Saber is enthused, and its cultivation progress instantly increases by 200%."

"Your Divine Ability Dual-World Saber has advanced to Mastery."

"Your Divine Ability Dual-World Saber has ascended to Transcendence."

A series of notifications from the system echoed in Lin Fei's mind. Simultaneously, a new memory emerged. It was his hard work in cultivating the Divine Ability Dual-World Saber, progressing from beginner to mastery, and then to Transcendence! It took a whole hour for Lin Fei to fully assimilate this memory.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei opened his eyes.

"System."

Immediately, Lin Fei accessed the system panel to check on his martial arts progress.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Early Martial Sovereign

Great Divine Ability: Black Wind Prison: Perfection

Great Divine Ability: Gravity Domain: Perfection

Great Divine Ability Dual-World Saber: Transcendence (Progress 0)

Saber Domain: Beginner (Progress 0)

Lin Fei's list of Divine Abilities had expanded, with the addition of Dual-World Saber. And it had directly reached Transcendence!

"This Divine Ability..."

Lin Fei was at a loss for words. Dual-World Saber, which was initially so challenging to even begin, had staunchly refused to follow him. But now? It had skyrocketed from beginner to Transcendence!

However, after sifting through his memories about the Dual-World Saber, Lin Fei couldn't help but gasp in astonishment.

Powerful! Incredibly powerful!

He had encountered numerous Divine Abilities, Be it Lesser Divine Abilities, Great Divine Abilities, or even top-tier Great Divine Abilities. Yet, none could compare to this Dual-World Saber.

In a single word, it was: Ferocious!

It was a Divine Ability that epitomized ferocity to the utmost!

"Let's give it a try."

With a single step, Lin Fei exited the room.
