49. Limit Of Body Refining

A note from RighteousEgg

Thanks for your review. Four chapters here.

Ten Blood-Increasing Pills were swallowed into his stomach, transforming into a warm current that quickly flowed throughout his body.

Simultaneously, the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique automatically activated, causing Lin Fei's blood and qi to surge continuously inside him.

He could clearly sense that his blood and qi had reached a limit, as if a barrier was pressing down on them, preventing them from increasing any further.

This was the limit of his blood and qi, the limit of his Body Refining. Once a martial artist reached the limit of Body Refining, their inner power would emerge.

"Body Refining limit, inner power is born..." Lin Fei muttered softly. He was feeling the situation inside his body carefully. As his blood and qi continued to surge, he gradually felt a slight warm current appearing inside him. This warm current was like a mist, barely perceptible, but undoubtedly real.

"Inner power!" Lin Fei suddenly realized.

With a slight movement of his mind, his eyes opened, and he extended his hand. In the next moment, a trace of inner power gathered in his palm.

He lightly pressed his hand against the ground, and there was a soft sound as the ground trembled. Lin Fei widened his eyes. His palm seemed to have sunk into a piece of bean curd, leaving a clear palm print on the ground.

"inner power, it really is inner power! But... why is this inner power so terrifying?"

Lin Fei's heart trembled slightly. His inner power was really terrifying.

He had encountered acquired martial artists before and even killed two.

One was the Wind Chasing Fragrance, whom he had killed in Yi Mountian Mansion City. The other was Zhao Yihu, whom he had killed not long ago in Luo City. Both of them were acquired martial artists with inner power.

Lin Fei had also collided head-on with their inner power. However, compared to the trace of inner power he had just produced, their inner power seemed too weak.

"Pff!"

Lin Fei increased his strength. The trace of inner power that had just been generated in his body was instantly depleted. However, a clear handprint appeared on the ground.

"There should also be differences between different types of inner power. The stronger the Body Refining technique, the harder it is to produce inner power. But once inner power is generated, it's stronger than others!"

A glint flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

He already had a general idea of why his inner power, although small in quantity, was much more powerful than the inner power of the Wind Chasing Fragrance and Zhao Yihu.

The difference lay in the Body Refining martial arts.

Whether it was the Wind Chasing Fragrance or Zhao Yihu, they were all lone martial artists

What kind of Body Refining martial arts could they get? Just very ordinary martial arts.

Therefore, the inner power they produced was naturally very ordinary.

But Lin Fei was different. He had been practicing the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique all along.

Even among all the Body Refining martial arts in the Nine Profound Sect, it belonged to a rather powerful type.

It was precise because of this that Lin Fei had found it so difficult to practice before.

But once inner power was born, it was not something that could be compared to the Wind Chasing Fragrance or Zhao Yihu.

"System."

Lin Fei immediately called up the system panel to check his martial arts situation.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Acquired Realm

Giant Whale Body Refining Technique: Limit (Unable to improve)

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Perfection (0% Progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Perfection (Progress 18%)

Vajra Iron Cloth: Ninth Level (95% Progress)

Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique: Seventh Level (68% Progress)

Flowing Clothes Skill: Transcendence (0% Progress)

The first thing Lin Fei saw was the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique.

Sure enough, it had reached its limit. Even the cultivation progress was not displayed, but instead showed "unable to improve."

This was the limit of the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique.

Once inner power was born, the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique would be almost at its end.

Martial arts also had limits. It cannot be infinitely improved.

However, Lin Fei's realm had already reached the Acquired.

In other words, from the moment he was born with inner power, he was no longer a Body Refining martial artist but an Acquired!

Lin Fei rested for a while. His inner power slowly recovered bit by bit. However, he only had a little inner power, which was not much.

Moreover, at the moment, Lin Fei could only rely on his huge supply of blood and qi to automatically generate inner power. Therefore, the increase in inner power was very slow.

He needed martial arts in the Acquired realm.

Lin Fei had two Acquired martial arts. They were obtained from the Wind Chasing Fragrance and Zhao Yihu.

However, Lin Fei did not want to practice these two martial arts. Because they were too ordinary.

He could receive three Acquired martial arts for free at the Nine Profound Sect.

However, Lin Fei would not choose to rush to practice Acquired martial arts at the moment.

"Acquired martial artists rely on inner power. The stronger the inner power, the stronger the Acquired martial artists' strength.

To make inner power strong enough, the foundation of martial arts must be strong enough.

Otherwise, even if the same Acquired martial arts are cultivated in the same realm, the difference in inner power will be huge, and the strength will naturally be vastly different.

So, I still need to wait. Wait until the Vajra Iron Cloth gives birth to inner power, and even wait for the Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique to give birth to inner power.

At that time, the three inner powers will merge into an unparalleled huge inner power. With this inner power as a foundation, practicing Acquired martial arts will be twice the result with half the effort!"

Lin Fei's eyes shone with brilliance. He had a plan in his mind.
