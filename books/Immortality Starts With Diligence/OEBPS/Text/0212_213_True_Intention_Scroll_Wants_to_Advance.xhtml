213. True Intention Scroll Wants to Advance

Time passed little by little. Lin Fei was quietly waiting. He was very patient. However, an incense stick's time had passed, and it seemed there was no movement. A quarter of an hour passed, and there was still no movement.

Lin Fei couldn't help but frown slightly.

"It seems that martial arts techniques at the level of Martial Saint are indeed different. They are high-spirited and arrogant and have unique personalities. Just listening to my few words, I'm afraid they won't be persuaded..."

Lin Fei compared it to his time in Great Chang. At that time, whether Lin Fei was a Martial Master or a Martial Saint, he still had a strong appeal to some top martial arts techniques. Because, at that time, Lin Fei had a great reputation. Moreover, in the history of Great Chang, martial artists stronger than Lin Fei were few and far between!

So, at that time, as long as Lin Fei was willing, those top martial arts techniques flocked to him like moths to a flame. For instance, in the True Intention Scroll, with Lin Fei's understanding, it was difficult to even be a beginner in a short period of time. But the True Intention Scroll wanted to get out, so it simply helped Lin Fei "begin", becoming Lin Fei's martial art technique. This was almost like a "reverse charge".

Also, when Lin Fei needed some top saber techniques to increase his saber foundation, or to increase the progress of saber momentum cultivation, he needed perfection-level top saber techniques. And those top saber techniques actually integrated into Lin Fei's saber momentum in the state of "perfection-level saber techniques". That was very "proactive".

However, when he arrived in the Ancient Origin Sect, things were different. Lin Fei was just an outer sect elder. Even his cultivation was only at the early stage of Martial Saint. Therefore, it's not hard to imagine his appeal to these Martial Saint level techniques in the Ancient Origin Sect.

"It seems that if I can't attract these Martial Saint level techniques, I need to find a way."

Lin Fei pondered for a while. These techniques were all at the Martial Saint level. Moreover, almost every martial art could reach the Martial Sovereign realm. To impress such martial arts, he must have some of his own characteristics.

And what was Lin Fei's current advantage?

Lin Fei quickly had an answer. Saber momentum and Saber Essence!

Obviously, Lin Fei's biggest advantage compared to other Martial Saints was his saber momentum and saber essence. Transcendence-level saber momentum, even among the core disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect, not many could reach this level.

As for saber essence, it was also very special. The founder of the Nine Profound Sect, although only a martial arts Grand Master, not even a Martial Saint, was astonishingly talented. He actually created such an amazing technique as the True Intention Scroll which could merge saber intent into true qi.

Now that Lin Fei had become a Martial Saint, it had evolved into saber essence. Perhaps only the combination of the Spirit Flower and the Qi Flower could achieve a similar effect to the saber essence. Therefore, this saber essence of Lin Fei's was unique. Other Martial Saints simply couldn't possess it.

Thinking of this, Lin Fei took a deep breath.

"Boom."

The next moment, he released his saber momentum. The transcendence-level saber momentum enveloped the entire third floor. There were few people usually coming and going on this third floor, but there were a few Martial Saints.

At this moment, these Martial Saints felt as if they were carrying a mountain on their backs. They were all suppressed from head to toe. A hint of shock appeared on their faces.

"Huh? Is this... saber momentum?"

The two Martial Saint elders guarding the third floor raised their eyebrows and immediately looked inside the third floor. They too felt the saber momentum. Even, this saber momentum gave them a bit of pressure.

"Transcendence-level saber momentum. It seems that this newly-promoted outer sect elder is not simple."

"Being able to comprehend transcendence-level saber momentum at the early stage of Martial Saint is indeed not simple."

"But what is he doing releasing his saber momentum in the Martial Dao Tower?"

"It doesn't matter, as long as he doesn't take action and doesn't break the rules."

The two Martial Saint elders shook their heads slightly. Lin Fei had only released his saber momentum. He did not break the rules of the Martial Dao Tower. They naturally did not need to pay attention.

At this moment, in the saber momentum, Lin Fei stretched out his hand. Suddenly, a large amount of saber essence surged out of his hand. These saber essence condensed into a sharp Heavenly Saber.

Lin Fei spoke again, "I wish to reach the pinnacle of martial arts. Is there any divine technique that would lend me a hand? I will make you famous throughout the Ancient Continent and praised for eternity!"

As Lin Fei's voice fell, the entire third floor of the Martial Dao Tower became very quiet.

Time passed little by little.

"The Glutton Technique wishes to be your martial art. However, the Glutton Technique is sealed and needs the host to unseal it."

"Seal?"

Lin Fei immediately understood. It must be a restriction. On the third floor of the Martial Dao Tower. every martial art had restrictions. As long as one touched it, he could lift the restriction.

Lin Fei was not in a hurry. If the martial art chose him, then he would choose the martial art. He had seen the Glutton Technique before. It can devour the flesh and blood of exotic beasts, increase essence blood, and ultimately condense the Energy Flower. This should be a martial art for cultivating the body.

Once the Glutton Technique was chosen, Lin Fei would probably have to go and hunt exotic beasts constantly, and then swallow their flesh and blood.

Soon, a voice from the system sounded in Lin Fei's mind.

"The Heavenly Blood Transformation Technique wishes to become your martial art. But the Heavenly Blood Transformation Technique is sealed and needs the host to unseal it."

"The Glutton Technique is enraged, feeling its dignity challenged by the Heavenly Blood Transformation Technique daring to compete with it."

"Thus, the Glutton Technique and the Heavenly Blood Transformation Technique started a fierce confrontation."

"The two are evenly matched, and it will take at least a few days to determine the winner, so neither can temporarily become the host's martial art."

Lin Fei listened to the system's voice in his mind. He didn't know what to say. The Glutton Technique and the Heavenly Blood Transformation Technique were fighting and thus didn't have time to become his martial arts. So, he still didn't have any martial art to choose?

"So fiercely competitive, these two martial arts are too aggressive and do not consider the overall situation. Even if I chose these two martial arts, I'm afraid they would be difficult to manage."

Lin Fei shook his head. He was very dissatisfied with the performance of these two martial arts. Even if these two martial arts were earth-shattering and wonderfully created, if they became Lin Fei's martial arts and were impulsive and inconsiderate, Lin Fei would never be able to cultivate the Energy Flower in his lifetime. That would be a big problem.

However, Lin Fei was a little curious. Why were all the martial arts he came across related to body cultivation? He displayed saber momentum and saber essence. Aren't there any martial arts for cultivating true essence?

Just then, the true essence in Lin Fei's body suddenly started to run wildly. This was the first time such a thing had happened.

Lin Fei was taken aback. Then, the voice of the system sounded in his mind.

"Your True Intention Scroll wishes for the host to give it a chance."

"The True Intention Scroll hopes that the host will unlock the seal of the Myriad Transformation Technique. The True Intention Scroll is willing to duel with the Myriad Transformation Technique, devour it, and upgrade to the Martial Saint level!"

Lin Fei was a little surprised. The True Intention Scroll wanted to duel with the Myriad Transformation Technique. Even more, it was prepared to devour the Myriad Transformation Technique and upgrade to the level of a Martial Saint Technique. However, could the True Intention Scroll succeed?

Lin Fei was a bit uncertain. The True Intention Scroll, although previously a bit willful and domineering, it had been diligent after being tamed by Lin Fei's saber intent, fulfilling its mission, allowing Lin Fei's saber qi to successfully transform into saber essence. It certainly made a considerable contribution!

Unexpectedly, the True Intention Scroll now had such grand ambitions. It even wanted to upgrade to the level of Martial Saint Techniques.

However, Lin Fei did not rashly agree. He had to first take a look. What kind of martial art is the Myriad Transformation Technique that the True Intention Scroll wanted to devour?

So, Lin Fei looked around at the bookshelf. Finally, he found the Myriad Transformation Technique and some introductions about it.
