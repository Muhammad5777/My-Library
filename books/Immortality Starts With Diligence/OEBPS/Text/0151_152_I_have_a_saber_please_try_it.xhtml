152. I have a saber, please try it

"How audacious, Lin Fei, do you realize what you're saying?"

The three elders of the royal family's faces instantly changed.

What was Lin Fei doing? He was actually thinking about challenging the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family!

One must know the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family within the royal family was like a steadfast pillar. His prestige was unimaginably high. Lin Fei's "disrespect" naturally made the three elders furious.

However, Lin Fei's expression remained calm.

At this point in time, Lin Fei had already slain six top-tier Qi Condensation cultivators. The power of the Five Directions Killing Array set up by the five top-tier Qi Condensation cultivators earlier was even more formidable than a perfect Qi Condensation cultivator.

Now the only thing that can make Lin Fei feel cautious was only Foundation Establishment! Foundation Establishment was very mysterious and high above. Like a dragon that is often heard but seldom seen.

Even though Lin Fei had heard of Foundation Establishment True Person many times now. Great Chang's Ancestor, Hidden Sword Sect's Sword Saint, and the Foundation Establishment True Persons of various major sects, etc.

However, Lin Fei had never seen even one of them, let alone "sparring" with them. Lin Fei really wanted to try where was his current limit? Or to put it another way, where was the gap between him and Foundation Establishment?

"A martial artist should be fearless and dauntless. He should have the courage to battle against heaven and earth! I just want to see the gap between me and Foundation Establishment. True Person, won't you even grant me this small request?"

Lin Fei suddenly looked up, looking at the ceiling. There was nothing on the ceiling of the Ascending Dragon Chamber. However, Lin Fei seemed to have pierced through the Ascending Dragon Chamber.

Even, through countless distances, he seemed to "see" a Foundation Establishment True Person with an immortal demeanor somewhere.

Supreme Elder of the Royal Family!

Lin Fei believed that at this moment, whatever he saw, the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family would definitely know.

Even more so, the saber intent on Lin Fei gradually began to rise.

Facing the transcendent saber intent, even the three elders of the royal family, high-level Qi Condensation cultivators, couldn't help but take a few steps back. They looked at Lin Fei with shock and awe on their faces. The 'Immortal Slayer Saber' was renowned. They were no match for him.

"Lin Fei, I hope you don't misjudge yourself. The Supreme Elder is the pillar of our royal family and even of Great Chang, how can you challenge him at will?"

"Not to mention you, even if the founder of Nine Profound Sect were to reincarnate, he wouldn't dare to speak to the Supreme Elder like that."

The three elder's faces were dark and they reprimanded loudly. However, Lin Fei didn't pay any heed. Instead, he continued to gaze at the ceiling. After a long time, a sigh echoed.

"Enough. Since you've made a request. I will satisfy you. Bring Lin Fei to the Imperial Garden, do not alert others."

A hoarse voice came from the void. This was the voice of the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family.

"Yes, Ancestor!"

The three royal elders responded respectfully. They might look very old. Even though they were the elder of the royal family. However, compared to the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family who has lived for five hundred years, they were merely juniors.

"Lin Fei, come with us."

Since the Supreme Elder had spoken, the three royal elders no longer stopped him. They directly left the Ascending Dragon Chamber with Lin Fei.

Lin Fei followed behind the three elders, turning several corridors. Finally, it was wide open in front of him.

This was an open area surrounded by lush trees. Although it was already winter, but here, it was warm like spring. Even some flowers and trees didn't seem to be affected by the winter.

Lin Fei looked up, looking at the void. In the void, there was a faint light, visible to the naked eye.

"Formation!"

Lin Fei understood. The entire Imperial Garden was covered by a formation. That's why it could be warm like spring here.

In the Imperial Garden, there was a black-robed old man standing quietly. This old man, in fact, didn't look very old. He seemed to be about fifty years old. He even looked younger than the three elders. However, despite his youthful appearance, his eyes revealed a sense of age.

"Greetings to the Ancestor!"

The three elders were very excited. They respectfully saluted the black-robed old man. The old man waved his hand, his gaze fell on Lin Fei.

"'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei. Although your Innate True Qi is not perfect, and your saber intent is not perfect either, you are the most talented person I've seen in the last five hundred years. Even the Sword Saint of the Hidden Sword Sect from three hundred years ago did not have your martial arts talent."

The old man spoke in a hoarse, calm voice, his hands behind his back.

"True Person!"

Lin Fei also respectfully saluted. Then he lifted his head, looking at the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family, and said in a deep voice, "Without becoming a Martial Saint, all is empty. The founder of the Nine Profound Sect was like this. What's the use of being more brilliant? In the end, isn't it just turning into a handful of dust?"

The Supreme Elder nodded, "Indeed, you are very clear-headed. Without becoming a Martial Saint, all is empty, no matter how brilliant, it can't withstand the passage of time. Only by reaching Foundation Establishment, can one extend one's life by hundreds of years.

However, whether you can establish a foundation and become a Martial Saint, I can't help you. After all, I don't have any exotic beast flesh to give you, and I don't have the good fortune of the Founding Emperor, who was able to obtain a thousand-year-old blood orchid. Whether or not you can establish a foundation depends on you."

Lin Fei nodded, he didn't expect the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family to give him exotic beast meat. That was impossible.

"Senior, this time I came to see you. I have only one request. I hope you can let me know how strong Foundation Establishment is."

The eyes of the Supreme Elder were profound. He calmly looked at Lin Fei, "How would you like to test?"

"Whoosh."

In the next moment, the aura on Lin Fei began to rise bit by bit. Even his saber intent gradually spread out. Above his head, a sharp great saber vaguely formed. A terrifying saber intent suddenly filled the entire Imperial Garden.

Lin Fei's expression was very serious. The opponent he faced was the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family, a True Person who had lived for five hundred years!

Furthermore, the Supreme Elder was definitely not an ordinary True Person. He was very likely to be the top True Person. Otherwise, how could he suppress the world for hundreds of years?

The suppressed immortal sects could only hide in their mountain gates. They could only wait until the time limit of the Supreme Elder was approaching, and then slowly move.

Therefore, facing this unprecedented opponent, Lin Fei would concentrate all his attention, and adjust the state of his body to reach its peak state!

The three elders of the royal family, at this moment, after feeling Lin Fei's saber intent, couldn't help but retreat a few steps. It was just saber intent, but they couldn't even handle it.

This also exposed their shortcomings. They were always in the royal family, living in comfort. Their minds and wills were too poor. Naturally, they couldn't block Lin Fei's transcendent saber intent.

However, Lin Fei's opponent, the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family seemed to be very casual. Even his spiritual power didn't fluctuate.

As for Lin Fei's saber intent. It was as if he didn't feel it at all. He was still standing quietly in the same place.

Lin Fei's momentum had reached its peak. At this point, he took a deep breath.

"Boom."

A sky-high saber energy, up to two hundred and fifty meters, condensed in the void. And this wasn't just saber energy. It was the Heavenly Saber condensed by Lin Fei!

Under the Heavenly Saber, all sabers bowed! The might of the Heavenly Saber suddenly pressed down.

The three elders, at this moment, felt like they were carrying a mountain on their backs.

Under these circumstances, if they were to face Lin Fei, they couldn't even resist, let alone fight back.

"So this is the 'Immortal Slayer Saber'? So terrifying..."

It was only at this time that the three elders understood how terrifying Lin Fei was.

High-level Qi Condensation cultivators? Top-level Qi Condensation cultivators? It was nothing more than a casual saber from Lin Fei.

When the Heavenly Saber was condensed, the power of the Heavenly Saber covered the Supreme Elder of the Royal Family.

Lin Fei's momentum finally reached its peak. He reached out his hand as if holding the Heavenly Saber in the void. His gaze was arrogant, with a momentum of cutting down all enemies with one saber!

"I have a saber, please try it!"

As soon as Lin Fei's words fell, he used his hand as a saber, swinging it down.

"Boom."

The two hundred and fifty meters Heavenly Saber instantly cut through the void. It came down from the sky and slashed toward the Supreme Elder!
