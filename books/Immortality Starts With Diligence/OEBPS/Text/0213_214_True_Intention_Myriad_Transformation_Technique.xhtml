214. True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique

"Myriad Transformation Technique: transform heaven and earth, sun, moon, stars, and everything, there's nothing it can't transform! It can open up any power and integrate any power. It's hard to get started, and it requires an extraordinary genius to start practicing. After practice, everything, such as heaven and earth, sun and moon, and stars, can be transformed into energy and converted into True Essence. The more energy you transform, the more True Essence you can convert."

When Lin Fei saw this Myriad Transformation Technique, his eyes lit up. This Myriad Transformation Technique seemed really extraordinary. The phrase "transform heaven and earth, sun, moon, stars, and everything, there's nothing it can't transform" certainly wasn't ordinary.

Moreover, according to the Myriad Transformation Technique, the speed of this martial art could be fast or slow. The speed completely depended on the "transformed energy".

As long as it could transform a strong power, even if it condensed the Qi Flower in one day, there probably wouldn't be a problem. However, it was too hard to get started. This martial art did not want to become Lin Fei's martial art.

If it were before, Lin Fei really didn't have a way. After all, this martial art seemed to have "no fate" with him. But now it was different.

The True Intention Scroll wanted to duel with the Myriad Transformation Technique. Once successful, the True Intention Scroll would devour the Myriad Transformation Technique. At that time, the Myriad Transformation Technique would naturally become Lin Fei's martial art. Moreover, it could perfectly integrate with Lin Fei's saber essence.

"True Intention Scroll, I hope you won't disappoint me!"

Lin Fei didn't hesitate at all. He reached out his hand and pressed directly on the secret book of the Myriad Transformation Technique.

"Buzz".

A faint white light flashed. Then, the restriction on the secret book of the Myriad Transformation Technique on the bookshelf was also lifted.

Lin Fei picked up the secret book and began to read. The next moment, Lin Fei closed his eyes.

In Lin Fei's mind, two rays of light appeared. One was the True Intention Scroll. The other was the Myriad Transformation Technique.

However, the light of the True Intention Scroll looked much smaller than the light of the Myriad Transformation Technique, almost only one-tenth of the light of the Myriad Transformation Technique.

"Whoosh".

However, the True Intention Scroll was the first to attack, instantly charged toward the Myriad Transformation Technique. Immediately, the Myriad Transformation Technique seemed to be angered too. It also emitted a great light and went directly toward the True Intention Scroll.

"Boom".

The two martial arts made a big mess in Lin Fei's mind. The True Intention Scroll was full of fighting spirit. It seemed fearless. However, the Myriad Transformation Technique was obviously much stronger than the True Intention Scroll. Therefore, it soon became clear that the True Intention Scroll was gradually unable to hold on.

Lin Fei knew if it continued like this, the True Intention Scroll would undoubtedly lose! Naturally, Lin Fei would like the True Intention Scroll to defeat the Myriad Transformation Technique and even devour it.

Thinking of this, Lin Fei had a thought.

"Saber momentum, go, help the True Intention Scroll!"

Suddenly, another ray of light appeared in Lin Fei's mind. That was saber momentum. The transcendence saber momentum was extraordinary. It covered the Myriad Transformation Technique all at once.

The True Intention Scroll also immediately pounced on it. The Myriad Transformation Technique was expanding crazily, trying to break free from the saber momentum's restriction.

However, both the saber momentum and the True Intention Scroll were supported by Lin Fei. How could the Myriad Transformation Technique break free?

About a quarter of an hour later, under the joint slaughter of the saber momentum and the True Intention Scroll, the Myriad Transformation Technique finally couldn't hold on. The True Intention Scroll immediately incorporated the Myriad Transformation Technique into itself.

"Shh".

The next moment, Lin Fei opened his eyes. The voice of the system also came into his mind.

"Your martial art True Intention Scroll has devoured the Myriad Transformation Technique and started to advance."

"Your martial art True Intention Scroll has advanced to the Martial Saint Technique, True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique!"

Lin Fei digested the memories in his mind. The True Intention Scroll had become the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique. Not only did it possess all the characteristics of the Myriad Transformation Technique, but also it could perfectly integrate with Lin Fei's saber essence.

This allowed Lin Fei to easily convert the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth directly into saber essence. Moreover, not just Spiritual Qi, there was also the energy of the sun and moon, the power of stars, and so on. Even some heavenly treasures could also be transformed. This was the terrifying part of the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique. Everything can be transformed!

The martial art for cultivating true essence was finally settled. However, the martial art for cultivating the body was still without a clue.

"Blood Essence Nine Transformations hopes to become your martial art. But Blood Essence Nine Transformations has been sealed and requires the host to unseal it."

The voice of the system sounded in Lin Fei's mind. By the name, it should be a martial art for cultivating the body. So, he immediately went to check out the Blood Essence Nine Transformations martial art.

Soon, Lin Fei found this martial art.

"Blood Essence Nine Transformations: the first transformation is for accumulating blood essence. The second transformation doubles, the third transformation quadruples, the fourth transformation multiplies by eight, and the fifth transformation multiplies by sixteen. So on and so forth, all the way to the ninth transformation, which multiplies by two hundred and fifty-six! With two hundred and fifty-six times the blood essence, you will surely be able to condense an Energy Flower!"

When Lin Fei saw the content of the Blood Essence Nine Transformations, he couldn't help but feel a tremor in his heart.

This martial art didn't have many fancy moves. It was purely to increase blood essence. Blood essence is the foundation of the body. As long as the blood essence increases, then the body can correspondingly increase. Therefore, this martial art aims to condense the Energy Flower as its ultimate goal.

To condense an Energy Flower, no one knows how much blood essence is needed. However, with the Blood Essence Nine Transformations, as long as he could practice the ninth transformation, the amount of blood essence would be two hundred and fifty-six times. Such a terrifying amount of blood essence can almost guarantee a hundred percent that it can condense an Essence Flower.

"Let it be Blood Essence Nine Transformations then!"

Lin Fei reached out and touched the secret book of the Blood Essence Nine Transformations. Suddenly, the restriction on the Blood Essence Nine Transformations was instantly lifted. Then, in Lin Fei's mind, an extra segment of memory appeared. That was the memory of the Blood Essence Nine Transformations. And after the Blood Essence Nine Transformations became Lin Fei's martial art, it started practicing immediately.

Blood Essence Nine Transformations was very diligent. The first step was to transform blood into blood essence in Lin Fei's body. Originally, Lin Fei was a Martial Saint with a very strong body. Therefore, now transforming into blood essence was also the accumulation of the first transformation of Blood Essence Nine Transformations. Of course, this required some time.

"I have martial arts for cultivating the body and the saber essence. Next, it's time to cultivate the saber momentum."

Lin Fei looked at the martial arts on the bookshelf. There were no martial arts that directly cultivated saber momentum. There were only martial arts for cultivating saber techniques.

To comprehend the saber intent and then evolve the saber momentum is the hardest. Many martial artists of the Ancient Origin Sect, by chance, could condense an Energy Flower or a Qi Flower. Even, both flowers bloom together. But it was the Spirit Flower that they couldn't condense.

That's because the momentum that represents the Spirit Flower was always imperfect. The biggest reason is that there is no martial art that directly cultivates momentum. Momentum completely depends on one's own comprehension!

In this regard, Lin Fei had an advantage. Because his saber momentum was already transcendent.

Lin Fei didn't need to comprehend saber momentum. His saber momentum was a fusion of various saber momentums, and then further enhances it.

The saber momentum which evolved from the innate level saber techniques could no longer improve Lin Fei's saber momentum. To bring his saber momentum to perfection, Lin Fei figured that there was only one way. That is to practice martial arts of the Martial Saint level.

To evolve their saber momentum, and then incorporate it into the saber momentum of the Heavenly Saber. By then, his saber momentum would be able to improve again.

Therefore, Lin Fei needed more than one saber technique. He needed to practice more saber techniques at the Martial Saint level as soon as possible.

"Holy Spirit Saber Technique hopes to become your martial art. But Holy Spirit Saber Technique has been sealed and requires the host to unseal it."

"Flame Burning Heaven Saber hopes to become your martial art. But Flame Burning Heaven Saber has been sealed and requires the host to unseal it."

"Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique hopes to become your martial art. But Rainbow Transformation Saber has been sealed and requires the host to unseal it."

The system's voice came out in Lin Fei's mind. Lin Fei also looked at these three saber techniques in turn. All seemed feasible. So, he chose these three saber techniques.

In fact, Lin Fei could choose more saber techniques. After all, the more saber techniques he practices, the better. However, he had already chosen five martial arts. That is five thousand contribution points. Lin Fei needed to save a thousand contribution points for emergencies.

Wait until the saber momentum was evolved to see how much one Martial Saint level saber technique could increase the cultivation progress of the saber momentum. By then, it's not too late to continue choosing saber techniques.

After choosing five martial arts, Lin Fei didn't stay on the third floor of the Martial Dao Tower. He went directly to the two elders on the third floor.

"You've chosen five martial arts, deducting five thousand contribution points."

Lin Fei nodded. Then he directly left the Martial Dao Tower. He needed to return to his seclusion for cultivation as soon as possible and wait for the first transformation of the Blood Essence Nine Transformations to complete the transformation of the blood essence in his body.
