54. Powerful Inner Power

At this moment, the hall became very quiet. One could hear a pin drop.

Chen Yuan still held his long sword. But his arm seemed to be trembling. He lowered his head.

A strand of his hair quietly snapped off and fell to the ground. In an instant, Chen Yuan's face turned pale as paper.

"You...you are..."

"I am Lin Fei, the one you can kill with one strike," Lin Fei spoke calmly.

Chen Yuan's expression turned very dark.

Although he did not see Lin Fei's saber movement clearly just now, he truly felt the sharpness of the saber. As if it could cut off his head in an instant.

He did not die, but a strand of his hair was cut off.

He and Lin Fei were clearly not at the same level! He couldn't block Lin Fei's attack. He couldn't even see Lin Fei's move clearly before he was defeated. He was completely defeated!

"I spare your life for being a disciple of Autumn Haze Mountain, and cut off only a strand of your hair." Lin Fei said coldly.

Autumn Haze Mountain and Nine Profound Sect were both major sects.

As neighboring sects, their relationship was not particularly good, but it was not bad either. If Lin Fei rashly killed a disciple of Autumn Haze Mountain, there would still be some trouble.

"What a fast saber! And there were no inner power fluctuations. This is the pure saber technique realm. If I'm not mistaken, your saber technique has already reached the perfection realm!" Liu Hao's pupils slightly shrank.

This was terrifying appearing on Body Refining martial artists.

And with Liu Hao's vision, he could naturally see that the saber technique Lin Fei displayed was definitely not ordinary. It must be a very sophisticated saber technique. It must not be simple to be able to practice to perfection.

"Who are you?" Lin Fei's tone was without any fluctuations.

"Liu Hao, a disciple of Autumn Haze Mountain." Liu Hao said calmly.

"Come to think of it, we actually have some connection. What do you think, Xuan?" Liu Hao has now confirmed Lin Fei's identity.

At this moment, Lin Fei finally noticed the woman next to Liu Hao. He felt that she looked familiar.

Soon, Lin Fei remembered where he had seen her before. After all, he had just seen her portrait a few days ago. He wasn't that forgetful.

"Wang Xuan, in consideration of your father's reputation, I won't pursue this matter. You three can leave." Lin Fei said calmly, seemingly not paying too much attention to Liu Hao.

Wang Xuan's heart skipped a beat. She knew Liu Hao's personality.

Liu Hao appeared gentle and easy to get along with. But in fact, he was arrogant and self-important and cared a lot about his face. Ignoring him in front of so many people, Liu Hao would definitely be angry.

Sure enough, Liu Hao's face turned cold, and he said, "Lin Fei, your saber technique is indeed good, but the foundation of a martial artist is still their realm! Even if your saber is fast, how can you break through my sword net using the third-level inner power of Acquired to activate!?"

As Liu Hao finished speaking, he suddenly drew his long sword.

"Shoo." The long sword made a light sound.

Then, Liu Hao's long sword began to move. It actually formed a huge sword net.

This sword net was powered by inner power. It could both attack and defend and was a very comprehensive martial art.

This was a truly acquired martial art. Even the other people in the hall who were far away felt like they were being pricked by needles all over their bodies.

Those who were a little closer felt like they were about to be cut by the sword net.

"Acquired Third Level..." Lin Fei also felt threatened.

The opponent's inner power erupted and the sword net appeared instantly. The sharp sword blades were aimed directly at Lin Fei.

Although Lin Fei had defeated acquired martial artists before and even killed two, the Wind Chasing Fragrance, Zhao Yihu, and Chen Yuan were all only at the acquired first level. In terms of inner power, they were far inferior to Liu Hao.

And Liu Hao's sword net in his hand was not an ordinary sword technique, but a very profound and exquisite acquired-level sword technique. Liu Hao even trained it to the level of transcendence, so it had such terrifying power.

However, Lin Fei did not back down.

"Inner power? I have it too!"

In the next moment, the big saber on Lin Fei's back was unsheathed with a "swish".

Lin Fei swung his saber, and suddenly, it seemed like there were saber shadows everywhere, ten saber shadows emanating sharp edges.

But that was only the surface.

This strike seemed the same as the one Lin Fei had previously used, but in fact, the powerful inner power in his body had surged into his arm, making the saber's power increase by an unknown number of times.

Finally, Lin Fei's saber struck the sword net in the sky with a "bang".

Liu Hao's originally calm face suddenly changed, and then his whole body trembled uncontrollably. With a "crack", the long sword in his hand suddenly appeared with cracks and shattered instantly.

The sharp saber edge enveloped his whole body as if it were going to cut him in half. Liu Hao felt the threat of death for the first time.

"Ah..."

Liu Hao roared in anger, blood flowing from his eyes, nose, and ears due to the impact of Lin Fei's saber. At this moment, Liu Hao had to extend his right hand and hold it in front of him.

With a "chirp", Lin Fei's saber fell.

A sound of metal clashing was heard.

Liu Hao's sleeve was torn, revealing a wrist guard inside.

Lin Fei's saber strike didn't manage to break through the wrist guard, but the power behind the strike was enough to shatter Liu Hao's arm. It would take at least ten days to half a month for him to recover without resting.

Liu Hao's face fell, and he looked as if he saw a ghost. If he hadn't been given a special wrist guard by his elders just now, his arm would have been severed, and he might have been split in half by the remaining force of the saber.

"The fluctuation of inner power...You have inner power, are you an acquired martial artist?" Liu Hao was terrified.

"You'll never leave if you don't get lost now." Lin Fei's tone was cold, staring at Liu Hao and Chen Yuan.

Liu Hao didn't dare to delay, nor did he say a word. He was afraid that Lin Fei would really go crazy and kill them. He had to save his life first and seek revenge in the future.
