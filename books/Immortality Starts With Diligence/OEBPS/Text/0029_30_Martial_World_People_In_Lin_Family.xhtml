A young man and his axe… along with a whole bunch of magic he doesn’t want

Larek Holsten is different from most people, but as long as it helps him become the best Logger he can be, he doesn’t really care all that much.  He and his best friend, an axe with a powerful fusion on it causing it to become sharper and stronger than a normal tool, are feared by the trees in the magical forest north of his home, as he can fell them faster than anyone else in the community.

Unfortunately, his abnormal physical appearance has a bit more of an influence over the population of the Kingdom than he figured, which ends up causing some problems when he visits the nearby village of Rushwood.  After a deliberate misunderstanding lands him in trouble, Larek is taken away from his family and the only home he’s ever known.  Along the way, a magical “potential” inside of him is discovered by those more knowledgeable than him in the ways of magic, and he is ordered to attend some sort of school for Mages.

The problem?  Contrary to many who would jump at the chance of wielding powerful magic to fight monsters, Larek has absolutely no desire to become a Mage.  All he wants is to go back home and chop down more trees with his best friend.

Liu Family, Yi Mountain Mansion City.

In the Liu Family hall, gathered many people from the martial world were dressed in strange clothes.

Mr. Liu was a slick and cunning businessman, looking very friendly.

He said to the martial artists, "Thank you, heroes, for coming to help me in the Liu Family. However, we cannot underestimate the Wind Chasing Fragrance, we have to wait a little longer."

At this point, a burly man carrying a large hammer became unhappy and shouted, "Mr. Liu, we have so many martial arts experts here, even if the Wind Chasing Fragrance is an acquired martial artist, he dare not come to the Liu Family and cause trouble. Why are we still waiting?"

"Yes, who are we waiting for?"

"We'll be in trouble if we don't make a plan now and the Wind Chasing Fragrance arrives."

For a while, these martial artists were all a little dissatisfied.

Mr. Liu continued to smile and said, "Dear heroes, we still have to wait for my niece. My niece is not an ordinary person, she is an inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect. She has already reached the acquired fourth level. I have written to her and invited her to come to the Liu Family to help us. She should be arriving in the next few days."

Mr. Liu's words quieted the many martial artists in the hall.

An inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect. And even reached acquired fourth level.

Such a strong person was not someone these mere and wandering martial artists could compare to.

"Mr. Liu, someone outside claims to be a disciple of the Nine Profound Sect and says was invited to the Liu Family." At this time, a servant hurried over to report.

Mr. Liu's face lit up and he said with a smile, "It should be my niece, please bring her in quickly."

The martial artists in the hall also brightened up.

The inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect. They also wanted to see what she was capable of.

After a while, a strong-looking man was led by the Liu Family servant to the hall.

"Lin Fei from the Nine Profound Sect, greet Mr. Liu."

The man was Lin Fei, who had traveled all the way to the Yi Mountain Mansion City as quickly as possible.

"Hero Lin, where is my niece?"

"Niece?"

Lin Fei quickly realized that the other party was referring to Ling Yao.

Therefore, Lin Fei said directly, "Senior Sister Ling has some important matters to attend to, so she couldn't come. Therefore, she entrusted me to come and protect the safety of the Liu Family. This is Senior Sister Ling's letter."

Lin Fei handed the letter to Mr. Liu.

Mr. Liu quickly opened and read the letter.

His brows furrowed slightly, and he glanced at Lin Fei.

Then, Mr. Liu put away the letter and smiled, "According to what Ling Yao said in the letter, Hero Lin, you are also an inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect?"

"Yes, I was promoted to the inner sect not long ago." Lin Fei answered truthfully.

"That's good. An inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect should be a talent like my niece..." Mr. Liu was quite satisfied.

"Hehe, I heard that according to the rules of the Nine Profound Sect, as long as you reach the seventh level of Body Refining before the age of twenty, you can be promoted to the inner sect. You were just promoted to the inner sect not long ago... you are still in Body Refining?"

Lin Fei raised his head.

The person who spoke was a cold-eyed bald man.

"Hero Lin, aren't you an Acquired Martial Artist?" Mr. Liu was also a bit anxious.

"I haven't advanced to Acquired-realm yet." Lin Fei said calmly.

Suddenly, Mr. Liu's face became a bit unpleasant.

In the hall, many martial arts people whispered and laughed, looking at Lin Fei, a bit meaningful.

"Mr. Liu, since everyone is here, let's discuss how to catch the flower thief the Wind Chasing Fragrance?"

"Right, I heard that the government offices have a bounty of three thousand taels of silver. This is not a small amount."

"I think that the Wind Chasing Fragrance is probably afraid to come to the Liu Mansion now..."

For a time, all the martial arts people in the hall began to discuss loudly.

It seemed that they had completely ignored Lin Fei's presence.

"Hero Lin, I'm sorry for this. You came from afar and must be tired. Let me arrange for you to rest first."

It was evident that Mr. Liu was somewhat disappointed with Lin Fei.

But after all, he was entrusted by Ling Yao, so Mr. Liu could not lose his manners.

"Thank you."

Lin Fei was indeed a bit tired. Then, led by Liu's servants, he was arranged to rest in a guest room.

In the room, Lin Fei thought in his mind.

"System."

Immediately, the system panel appeared in front of Lin Fei.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Body Refining Eighth Level

Giant Whale Body Refining Technique: Eighth Level (42% progress)

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Transcendence (1% progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Perfection (8% progress)

Vajra Iron Cloth: Seventh Level (6% progress)

Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique: Seventh Level (11% progress)

Flowing Clothes Skill: Mastery (42% progress)

Lin Fei looked at the mastery level achieved in the Flowing Clothes Skill and felt quite satisfied. In the past few days of rushing on the road, Lin Fei had encountered some bandits and used the Flowing Clothes Skill a few times. Flowing Clothes Skill did not disappoint him and it reached the mastery level in just a few days.

With the Flowing Clothes Skill, as long as the flower thief the Wind Chasing Fragrance appeared, Lin Fei would have the confidence to catch or kill the opponent.

As for those mocking and ridiculing him, Lin Fei did not care. Those wandering martial artists who could achieve the acquired realm would already be lucky enough. How could Lin Fei bother about them?

Lin Fei closed his eyes to rest and didn't know how long had passed when he heard a knock on the door.

"Click."

Lin Fei opened the door and saw a beautiful and graceful young woman standing outside, accompanied by two maidservants.
