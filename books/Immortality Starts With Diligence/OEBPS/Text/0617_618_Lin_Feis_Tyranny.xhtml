618. Lin Fei's Tyranny

"Boom."

The Stellar Mystique Realm violently shook.

"Swoosh."

A stunning figure of a woman suddenly emerged from the void. It was none other than Moonshadow True Immortal!

Seeing Lin Fei seizing the world laws of the Stellar Mystique Realm, Moonshadow True Immortal's expression darkened, and she urgently spoke: "Fellow Daoist Lin, could you possibly spare the Stellar Mystique Realm from your actions in honor of our acquaintance?"

Lin Fei glanced at Moonshadow True Immortal. He shook his head slightly, his tone even as he spoke: "You should be well aware of the current circumstances, Moonshadow. I've been fighting in the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain, and the situation has deteriorated badly. Extracting the laws of the world won’t lead to its destruction, but when the Dark Demons arrive, everything will be finished. I need to enhance my strength, and the world laws of True Monarch-level worlds are crucial for me. No matter what, I will not give up. If you insist on interfering, I will not hold back, and show no mercy!"

Lin Fei was utterly domineering. Even somewhat arrogant. To a True Immortal, he showed not the slightest courtesy.

However, Moonshadow True Immortal could only grit her teeth. There was nothing she could do nor did she dare to.

Although the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain was almost completely fallen. Now under the complete control of the Dark Demons.

Yet, the rumors that spread were alarming. Lin Fei had been invincible in the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain. Continuously sweeping through and slaughtering Dark Demons. He had killed countless Demon Emperors of True Immortal and Martial Ancestor rank.

Such a ruthless figure, how could Moonshadow True Immortal dare to make a move?

Those Demon Emperors are comparable to True Immortals and Martial Ancestors. Yet, Lin Fei had slaughtered them as if they were mere chickens. It was evident that Lin Fei's power said to be unmatched even among True Immortals and Martial Ancestors, was unlikely to be challenged by many.

"Pfft."

In the next moment, Lin Fei had already extracted the world laws of the Stellar Mystique Realm.

"Meld."

Lin Fei directly threw the world laws into the Illusory Realm, merging them. Instantly, Lin Fei could sense a noticeable enhancement in the Illusory Realm, albeit not a significant one.

"Fellow Daoist Moonshadow, I bid you farewell."

Lin Fei paid no heed to Moonshadow True Immortal. He stepped into the void corridor, heading towards other True Monarch-level worlds.

Moonshadow True Immortal's expression was complex. Ultimately, she could only grit her teeth, disregard everything, and directly traverse through space to leave. Now, the entire Divine Realm was subtly beginning to descend into chaos.

Might makes right!

The influence of former powers, connections, and relationships seemed to have diminished greatly.

Lin Fei was indeed domineering. He swept through one True Monarch world after another, extracting their world laws and integrating them into the Illusory Realm, which significantly elevated the Illusory Realm.

Moreover, the world laws forcibly taken by Lin Fei continued to increase. The number of True Monarch-level worlds he descended upon also kept growing.

Ten, twenty, fifty, eighty, a hundred...

In the blink of an eye, Lin Fei had descended upon over a hundred True Monarch-level worlds.

Effectively, Lin Fei had acquired over a hundred world laws. For Lin Fei's Illusory Realm, this was a monumental enhancement.

However, forcibly taking so many world origins also made Lin Fei offend many True Immortals and Martial Ancestors. Not every True Immortal or Martial Ancestor feared Lin Fei.

Finally, when Lin Fei arrived at the one hundred and eighteenth True Monarch-level world. A Martial Ancestor appeared. Or rather, a Martial Ancestor had been waiting there for Lin Fei.

"Black Doom Martial Ancestor!"

Lin Fei looked at the Martial Ancestor before him. In the entire Divine Realm, Black Doom Martial Ancestor was renowned, dubbed as one of the Martial Ancestors closest to the Supreme. Such a title demonstrated the formidable strength of Black Doom Martial Ancestor.

"Martial Ancestor Lin, you continuously seize the world laws of True Monarch-level worlds to enhance your own power. But you must know the great harm this does to a True Monarch-level world. Others may fear you, but I do not! If you wish to take the world laws under my command. Then you must cut down me first!"

Black Doom Martial Ancestor spoke straightforwardly.

"So you mean to obstruct me, Black Doom Martial Ancestor?"

"Indeed."

"I have said, those who obstruct me will be shown no mercy! If that's the case, then you can't blame me."

A sharp glint flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. This Black Doom Martial Ancestor, perhaps feeling confident in his own strength, believed he need not fear Lin Fei. Even if unable to defeat him, escaping with his life would be no issue. Hence, his fearless attitude.

Unfortunately, in Lin Fei's eyes, Black Doom Martial Ancestor was negligible. He knew nothing of Lin Fei's power!

"Illusory Realm!"

With a thought from Lin Fei, the Illusory Realm descended. Once at the level of True Immortal and Martial Ancestor, it had now merged with over a hundred world laws. Just how much had the power of the Illusory Realm been elevated?

Even Lin Fei himself could hardly estimate. One thing was clear to him. The Illusory Realm, facing True Immortals and Martial Ancestors, was easily overpowering.

Too easy.

"Boom."

Lin Fei's Illusory Realm descended. As the Illusory Realm enveloped Black Doom Martial Ancestor.

Instantly, Black Doom Martial Ancestor's entire body trembled, his expression showing no hint of struggle as his spirit was suppressed in a flash. He was utterly powerless.

This was Black Doom Martial Ancestor, a spirit of incredible resilience, even regarded as one of the martial ancestors with the potential to achieve Supreme status.

Yet, under the suppression of Lin Fei's Illusory Realm, he was completely powerless to resist.

Lin Fei shook his head.

Too weak.

Black Doom Martial Ancestor had been a profound disappointment. Such a frail will of the spirit.

Lin Fei didn't even need to employ the Eternal Realm. Just one Illusory Realm was enough to suppress forever!

"Extinguish!"

With a gentle flick of his finger, an explosive burst from the Eternal Realm.

"Pfft."

Lin Fei's finger landed on Black Doom Martial Ancestor. The mighty body of Black Doom Martial Ancestor shattered like fragile glass. Even the particles were annihilated.

Once the particles were completely obliterated, Black Doom Martial Ancestor was truly dead.

In reality, within the void, numerous gazes were fixed. Surely all from True Immortals, Martial Ancestors.

Black Doom Martial Ancestor was merely a probe. If Lin Fei truly possessed unimaginable strength, then at least among the True Immortal Martial Ancestors, none would dare obstruct Lin Fei.

They, too, sought through Black Doom Martial Ancestor to gauge Lin Fei's capabilities, or perhaps, to push him to his limits. But, they were destined to be disappointed. Lin Fei did nothing. He simply unleashed the Illusory Realm.

Upon its arrival, it instantly suppressed Black Doom Martial Ancestor. Even without showing any further capabilities, Black Doom Martial Ancestor couldn't withstand it. He was on the verge of collapse.

When only one particle of Black Doom Martial Ancestor remained, Lin Fei ultimately refrained from annihilating further. He withdrew the Illusory Realm.

Instantly, Black Doom Martial Ancestor regained consciousness. Then, he realized he was down to a single particle. In a moment, terror consumed him. His entire being was seized with fear. He had roughly understood that he had been defeated.

Only, he was unaware of how he had been defeated. His spirit was suppressed by the Illusory Realm, rendering his body a sitting duck. In front of Lin Fei, the mighty Black Doom Martial Ancestor couldn't withstand a single blow.

Overwhelmed with shame, Black Doom Martial Ancestor took a deep breath and bowed deeply to Lin Fei. "Thank you, Fellow Daoist Lin, for showing mercy and sparing my life."

"This is the first time, and it will be the last. Any further obstruction will meet with no mercy!" Lin Fei's tone was unapologetic as his gaze swept through the void, a clear warning.

Should any True Immortal or Martial Ancestor dare to obstruct him again, Lin Fei would not hesitate to unleash slaughter, unlike the mercy he had shown Black Doom Martial Ancestor this time.

Just as Lin Fei was about to leave, a majestic voice reached his ears. "Martial Ancestor Lin, to the headquarters of the Ancient Covenant."

Lin Fei was startled.

"The Supreme?"

There was no mistaking it—this was the voice of one of the two Supremes of the Ancient Covenant!
