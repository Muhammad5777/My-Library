383. Heading to the Headquarters of the Martial God Alliance!

"The Martial God Alliance?" Lin Fei shook his head, indicating he had not heard of it before.

Seeing Lin Fei's expression, the Mountain King expressed his surprise, "Hasn't the Martial God Alliance invited the Lord of the Ancient Origin? This doesn't make sense. The Martial God Alliance encompasses most of the Martial Kings and those of the Nascent Soul cultivator. How is it that they haven't extended an invitation to you? Could it be that they haven't realized your presence yet?"

After a moment of contemplation, Mountain King mused, "Perhaps the Martial God Alliance genuinely hasn't taken note. After all, Lin Fei's ascension to Martial King has been rather swift."

"What kind of power is the Martial God Alliance?" Lin Fei inquired.

Pausing briefly, Mountain King responded with a hint of a smile, "The Martial God Alliance was founded by a few Martial Gods. Initially, it was established to foster interactions among Martial Gods and Kings. But as time went on, many Martial Kings, Gods, and even those of the Nascent Soul cultivators were assimilated. It's a very inclusive alliance."

"Interactions?" Lin Fei pondered aloud.

"Yes, interactions," Mountain King affirmed. "The vast expanse of the Ancient Continent makes communication between Martial Kings and Nascent Souls challenging. Thus, the Martial God Alliance was born out of necessity. Some of the Martial Gods, as well as powerful beings of the Deity Transformation stage who joined later, crafted a unique magical treasure called the 'Martial God Space'.

Once one gains approval from this realm and leaves their mark within, they can enter the Martial God Space in a spiritual form at will. This enables Martial Kings, Nascent Souls, Martial Gods, and those of Deity Transformation to interact regardless of the vast distances that separate them."

Lin Fei's eyes sparkled with interest. The Martial God Alliance indeed seemed like a promising venture. Presently, Lin Fei's sphere of influence was limited to certain Martial King households and the Nascent Soul lineage. He had encountered only a few significant ones, such as the Mountain King and the Daoist Lord Qianghe.

However, being part of the Martial God Space would allow him to communicate with a plethora of Martial Kings and those of the Nascent Soul stage. Such interactions and the exchange of information could prove immensely beneficial to Lin Fei's cultivation.

Recognizing the immense benefits of the Martial God Space, Lin Fei inquired, "Mountain King, how might one join the Martial God Alliance?"

"It's straightforward," Mountain King responded. "One can either be invited by the Martial God Alliance or be recommended by an existing member. Any Martial King or someone of the Nascent Soul stage is eligible. The alliance is quite lax, imposing almost no constraints. The only real restriction is against the demonic; they cannot join."

Lin Fei nodded, acknowledging he met the criteria to join the Martial God Alliance.

"Where can one join the Martial God Alliance?" Lin Fei continued.

"To join the Martial God Alliance as the Ancient Origin Saber King is quite simple," Mountain King elaborated. "I am a member of the Martial God Alliance. I can recommend you. Once the recommendation is approved, you can head to the headquarters of the Martial God Alliance. After leaving your mark in the Martial God Space, you'll gain access to it."

"I am grateful for your assistance, Mountain King," Lin Fei expressed.

Following their discussion, Mountain King enlightened Lin Fei about some general knowledge and the specifics of the Martial God Alliance headquarters.

"Mountain King, I'm truly grateful," Lin Fei expressed his gratitude with sincerity.

Mountain King waved him off with a hearty laugh, "It's no big deal!"

Subsequently, the Daoist Lord Qianhe took his leave, whereas the Mountain King chose to stay back. Firstly, he wanted to await news from the Martial God Alliance, and secondly, both being Martial Kings, it was a golden opportunity to exchange insights and cultivate together.

About three days later, news finally arrived from the Martial God Alliance.

"Lin Fei, there's an update regarding the recommendation I made on your behalf," Mountain King announced. "The Martial God Alliance has completed their verification and has approved your membership. Now, all you need to do is visit the headquarters of the Martial God Alliance and leave your mark in the Martial God Space. Once done, you will be an official member, and you'll have the privilege to enter the Martial God Space with your spiritual form anytime you desire."

Lin Fei's eyes sparkled with anticipation. Through his recent interactions with Mountain King Wang, he'd gained a deeper understanding of the Martial God Alliance and the Martial God Space. Such an organization held immense benefits for a Martial King, given the presence of not just Martial Kings but also Martial Gods.

Mountain King had explained that the transition from a Martial King to a Martial God wasn't just a step up—it was a complete metamorphosis, akin to a human becoming a deity. Although many speak of the martial path leading to godhood, in reality, only the Martial Gods truly attained such a state. The leap was immense.

If one only followed a predetermined path, even if they perfected all nine bloodline techniques, attaining the status of a Martial God might still be out of reach. The specifics of this ascension remained mysterious, even to Mountain King.

Within the Martial God Alliance, despite the thousands of Martial Kings, not a single one had ascended to Martial Godhood in almost thirty thousand years. This highlighted the immense challenge of the journey. Given the lifespans of the Martial Kings, this spanned almost three generations.

However, it's worth noting that the Martial God Alliance doesn't represent all the Martial Kings of the vast Ancient Continent. Yet, the sheer number within the alliance showcases the difficulty of a Martial King's ascension to godhood.

Joining the Martial God Alliance might make ascending to Martial God challenging, but not being a part of it would make the task even harder, bordering on the impossible. Especially for those like Mountain King and Lin Fei, who hail from relatively minor sects or factions and belong to the realm of smaller powers. For them, the path to becoming a Martial God was unimaginably long and strenuous.

"Well, Lin Fei, now that you have passed the review of the Martial God Alliance," Mountain King paused for a moment, smiling warmly. "You are practically a member now. I shall take my leave."

With that, Mountain King bade his farewell and left, feeling rather pleased with his visit to the Xiao family. His exchange and spar with Lin Fei made the trip worthwhile.

Upon Mountain King's departure, Lin Fei didn't hesitate any longer. "First, to the Martial God Alliance!" He said, his interest in the Martial God Alliance is now piqued.

Relying on the directions provided by the Mountain King, Lin Fei soared towards the headquarters of the Martial God Alliance, which was situated in the City of Martial Gods. Contrary to expectations, the headquarters wasn't perched on some high mountain but instead sprawled across a vast plain. The city was bustling, with endless streams of merchants, martial artists, and travelers. No one dared to provoke the Martial God Alliance.

Though it was a loose organization, with each member having their own faction and power, nobody could deny the formidable influence of the Martial God Alliance. Especially with the fact that it was overseen by a Martial God! The power exclusively belonging to the Martial God Alliance had been terrifying over the years, a testament to its gradual accumulation of strength. With Martial Gods, who possessed lifespans of a hundred thousand years, the glory of the Martial God Alliance was measured in such timescales. And by now, it had surpassed a million years!

Upon entering the City of Martial Gods, Lin Fei observed a plethora of Martial Sovereigns, Martial Kings, Golden Cores, Nascent Souls, and many other ranks of cultivators. The city might not resemble some of the martial or immortal lands elsewhere, but it stood as a unique "alternative sanctuary." At least in terms of its scale and the number of powerhouses, it was second to none!

Finally, Lin Fei stood before the grand hall of the Martial God Alliance headquarters. The majestic structure spread before him, emanating an aura of ancient strength and reverence. Without hesitation, he stepped inside.
