The task hall was still crowded with people. Most of them were there to see if there were any suitable tasks to take on. But Lin Fei was there to post tasks.

He went straight to the task hall elder and respectfully saluted, "Elder, I want to post a task."

"Post tasks?" The elder looked at Lin Fei.

Lin Fei was just an outer disciple. It was rare for outer disciples to come and post tasks.

"Okay. The minimum contribution value for a task is one hundred."The elder said calmly.

"I understand."

Lin Fei knew the rules of the task hall. They didn't charge for posting tasks, but they set a minimum contribution value to prevent people from posting random tasks. One hundred contribution points outside the hall were equivalent to one hundred taels of silver, which was not affordable for everyone.

"What tasks do you need to post?"The elder asked.

"I am posting two guiding tasks. If there are senior members of the sect, or fellow disciples, who have mastered the Nine Shadow Saber Technique or Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist to the perfection level, they can guide me for an hour. The reward for the task is one thousand contribution points!"

Lin Fei gritted his teeth and offered a thousand contribution points directly. This was a big loss.

"One mastery in saber technique or fist technique to perfection level?"

The elder was slightly surprised. But then he looked at Lin Fei with meaningful eyes and said, "You don't understand what it means to master a martial art to the perfection level.

Whether it's the elder or the inner disciples, they won't be interested in your mere thousand contribution points if they have mastered a martial art to the perfection level.

You could wait for ten or fifteen days, but I doubt anyone will pay attention to your tasks."

Lin Fei was taken aback and respectfully said, "Please enlighten me, elder."

"It's simple. You need at least five thousand contribution points to attract the attention of those elders or inner disciples who have mastered their martial arts to the perfection level."

"Um..."Lin Fei hesitated.

One task required five thousand contribution points. If he was going to post two tasks, it would be ten thousand contribution points.

That's a lot of money!

"Okay, then it's 5,000 contribution points. Elder, please help the disciple put on these two tasks," said Lin Fei, gritting his teeth.

Finally, he made up his mind. As long as he could improve his strength, the contribution points spent would be worth it!

"Okay, the tasks are posted, you can go back and wait quietly. Only those who have taken on the tasks can know your information, and they will naturally come to you," said the elder.

"Thank you, Elder," Lin Fei said respectfully, bowing.

Although it was a bit painful, he had no choice.

Lin Fei left the task hall.

About half an hour later, a cold-faced, stunningly beautiful woman arrived at the task hall.

Although she looked beautiful, she exuded a cold aura from head to toe. Like a sharp saber.

No one dared to approach her. And her attire proved that she was an inner disciple.

"Isn't that Senior Sister Ling Yao?"

"I heard that Senior Sister Ling Yao recently broke through to the fourth level of Acquired Realm. With such talent, she has the potential to reach Innate Realm in the future."

"She is one of the most outstanding disciples in our Nine Profound Sect in terms of talent."

This black-clad woman was well-known in the Nine Profound Sect. She was one of the prominent figures in the sect.

Ling Yao ignored the discussion of others.

Her beautiful eyes were scanning the task wall for any easy and high-contribution-point tasks that were not too far from the sect.

She rarely did tasks. But she wanted a pill recently, and she needed contribution points. Therefore, she had to come and look for it. However, such tasks were not easy to find.

Tasks were ranked according to the amount of contribution points rewarded. Ling Yao checked the task list from top to bottom. She wasn't very satisfied with most of the tasks.

"Hmm? Nine Shadow Saber Technique perfection state, teach for an hour, 5,000 contribution points?"

Ling Yao's eyes lit up.

How generous!

Teaching for an hour and getting 5,000 contribution points was not common.

After all, teaching tasks were usually posted by outer disciples. Many senior members of the sect would take on these tasks.

However, this task had specific requirements. Only someone who had reached perfection in Nine Shadow Saber Technique could teach it.

Ling Yao had just reached perfection in this saber technique.

Moreover, in the entire Nine Profound Sect, there were only a few who could reach perfection in Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

Ling Yao went directly to the elder in charge of the task hall and respectfully said, "Elder, I'll take the task of teaching the Nine Shadow Saber Technique."

The elder certainly knew Ling Yao.

She was the star of hope in the Nine Profound Sect. She was expected to become an innate and top talent at the core of the Nine Profound Sect in the future.

But he still had to follow the rules of the task hall.

"Ling Yao, if you want to take on this task, you must first demonstrate your Nine Shadow Saber Technique and show that you have reached perfection."

"Of course, Elder, please watch carefully."

Ling Yao drew her long saber and gently made a cut in the air.

"Swish."

Nine saber shadows appeared in the air.

"One saber, nine shadows, not bad. It has indeed reached perfection. You can take on this task!" The elder's eyes also showed a hint of surprise.

To reach perfection in martial arts, one must be an exceptional genius.

Many elders, even if their strength was stronger than Ling Yao's, cannot reach perfection in martial arts.

Ling Yao took the task information handed to her by the elder.

"Lin Fei, an outer disciple?"Ling Yao was slightly surprised.

She originally thought that only inner disciples would post this teaching task. But she didn't expect it to be an outer disciple.

Moreover, the nameplate showed that Lin Fei was only at the fourth level of Body Refining.

For a moment, Ling Yao was also curious. So she turned around and left the task hall, heading towards the outer disciple area.
