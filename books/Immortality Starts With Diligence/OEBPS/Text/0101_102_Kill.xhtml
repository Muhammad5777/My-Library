102. Kill

"You can't shake my spirit! These little tricks are not worth showing off. My spirit is as solid as a rock. What if you have saber intent? You can't do anything to me!"

He Yunzi's eyes were calm. He constantly used the Heart-Calming Technique to stabilize his own spirit.

However, he didn't know that the more he used the Heart-Calming Technique to stabilize his spirit, the more he had actually exposed the flaws in it. Because his subconscious was wary and afraid of saber intent. His subconscious wanted to guard against Lin Fei's saber intent.

This was the flaw!

Of course, this kind of flaw was also because Lin Fei first used his fist intent to constantly suppress He Yunzi. That was why He Yunzi's spiritual flaw was created. He even magnified He Yunzi's spiritual flaw step by step.

By now, He Yunzi's spiritual flaws had been in front of Lin Fei. It was full of holes.

The next moment, Lin Fei didn't hesitate any longer.

"Shoo."

Lin Fei drew his saber. He had been nurturing this saber for two full months. Moreover, he nurtured the saber under the condition of the Saber-drawing Technique perfection, even with the nourishment of saber intent.

Nurturing inner power and saber intent together for two months, even Lin Fei himself didn't know how powerful this saber was. He only knew, if he drew his saber, He Yunzi wouldn't be able to block it!

No one could see the speed at which Lin Fei drew his saber. Everyone could only see a white light flashing through the void. However, not seeing the saber didn't mean they couldn't sense the saber intent.

Saber intent was different from fist intent. Saber intent was sharp and extremely fierce.

The moment Lin Fei drew his saber, it was as if he had transformed into an unparalleled treasure saber. It seemed like the sky was full of sharp saber edges. Even down in the arena, far away, they could still feel the sharpness of the saber blade.

It seemed to be cutting their skin, making the skin feel a faint pain.

"Slash!"

As Lin Fei drew his saber, the fierce saber intent erupted like a volcano.

Down in the arena, many martial artists carried sabers with them. But now, these sabers were shaking violently. Even the sabers in their sheaths were making a faint ringing sound. It was as if there was an invisible force controlling everyone's sabers.

"Is... is this the saber intent?"

"It's said that once the saber intent erupts, any saber wielder, in front of a martial artist who has comprehended saber intent, can't even hope to draw their saber."

"The saber intent can control all sabers. I originally thought it was an absurd legend, but now it seems to be true..."

"I... I really can't control my saber anymore..."

This scene was simply incredible. It shocked many martial artists and Qi Condensation cultivators! This completely overturned their previous understanding of martial artists. When did martial artists become so terrifying?

If a martial artist comprehended saber intent, once on the battlefield, wouldn't it mean that with just a thought, the sabers in the hands of thousands of soldiers would be instantly controlled? Even drawing the saber would be a luxury.

What would happen on a battlefield?

A martial artist who has comprehended saber intent was no longer as simple as fighting one against a hundred, but one against ten thousand. A true enemy of ten thousand!

At this moment, martial artists seemed to have made a deep impression on many Qi cultivators' hearts. It turned out that martial artists were not just what they usually saw. Martial artists also had "divine powers." This saber intent could be compared to a "divine power."

The people below the arena were merely shocked, but in the arena, He Yunzi, who was facing Lin Fei's saber intent directly. It was not as simple as being shocked.

The moment Lin Fei drew his saber, in his line of sight, it seemed like there was only a ball of white light.

In his mind, which was originally pitch black. At this moment, it seemed to have been "cut open" by a beam of light.

That was a saber!

An indescribable saber!

It flashed by in an instant.

It disappeared into his mind in an instant.

Suddenly, a violent tearing sensation clearly emanated from his mind.

He Yunzi felt as if his mind had been forcibly "cut open."

Then, his vision went dark.

He lost all consciousness.

"Pa."

Lin Fei sheathed his saber. His saber seemed as if it had never left its sheath.

He Yunzi showed no reaction. The only change might be the light from the defensive magical artifact on his body, it disappeared.

No one knew when it disappeared. Perhaps it was the moment Lin Fei drew his saber, or perhaps it was the moment He Yunzi's mind fell into darkness.

In any case, He Yunzi's magical artifact had lost its glow. This meant that without the support of spiritual power, the magical artifact was just a pile of scrap iron.

"Pfft."

From He Yunzi's head, a line of blood appeared from top to bottom. The next moment, He Yunzi's body incredibly split into two halves. Blood splattered everywhere and a strong smell of blood filled the air.

He Yunzi was dead! And he died in such a miserable and shocking way. The two halves of his body fell to the ground with a "plop."

Suddenly, everyone was in an uproar.

"He Yunzi is dead?"

"How could he die?"

"Even if He Yunzi was defeated, he shouldn't have died."

"Besides, he could have admitted defeat, why didn't He Yunzi surrender?"

"He Yunzi had a magical artifact on him, as long as his mind wasn't broken, he wouldn't die as long as he had spiritual power to support him."

"He Yunzi is dead, will He Mountain Dao let this go?"

Many people were shocked. They thought at most one side would lose. Losing, at worst, meant surrendering. How could one side die? Moreover, the death was so tragic.

Lin Fei silently glanced at He Yunzi's corpse on the ground and looked at the three elders presiding over the royal competition.

Lin Fei calmly said, "Saber-drawing Technique, once the saber is drawn, it cannot be stopped! He Yunzi couldn't withstand my saber intent and couldn't maintain the flow of spiritual power. So, he couldn't block my saber either!"

The three royal elders were witnesses and there were so many people witnessing it. He Yunzi was dead, and that was it.

The three royal elders exchanged glances. Others couldn't see it clearly, but as high-level Qi Condensation cultivators, their divine senses were incredibly powerful. Naturally, they saw the whole scene clearly. And it was as Lin Fei said, there was no discrepancy.

Lin Fei just used the Saber-drawing Technique. The saber was drawn, and it was earth-shattering! The speed was so fast, and the saber intent was so sharp made the three royal elders feel a chill in their hearts.

Facing such a terrifying saber intent, He Yunzi couldn't withstand it and his mind was instantly shattered by Lin Fei's saber intent. He Yunzi's willpower was broken by Lin Fei, and he naturally couldn't maintain his own spiritual power.

Without spiritual power, the magical artifact lost its protective function. Then, Lin Fei's saber struck He Yunzi's body, easily cutting him in half with one saber stroke.

The speed of Lin Fei's Saber-drawing Technique was simply too fast, even the three royal elders, who were high-level Qi Condensation cultivators, couldn't intervene in time to save him.

"Lin Fei, this is the arena, and the saber has no eyes. He Yunzi was weak and was killed by you, which is within the rules. We witnessed it with our own eyes and will report the truth to He Mountain Dao. You don't need to worry."

"Thank you, three seniors!"

Lin Fei's expression remained calm as he turned around and slowly walked off the stage.
