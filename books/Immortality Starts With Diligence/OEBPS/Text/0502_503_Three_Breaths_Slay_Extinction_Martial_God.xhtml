Rise of a Planeswalker - My Wife is an Immortal Cultivator

Qin Yun remembered the days when he was just another scientist working on an Interdimensional Matter Shift device until it blew in his face. In fact, he remembered all his lives following that incident.

Some lives were spent as ordinary men just going through life, others as heroes seeking wealth and glory. Still, they all had one thing in common: his death. Indeed, he would always die in the end, which always gave him a headache. Was there a way to live forever?

Even when he was reborn into worlds where immortals roamed the land, he always found a way to get himself killed. Enough was enough. This time, he would remain low-key and cultivate immortality.

There was only one problem. Qin Yun was supposed to marry an inner disciple of an immortal sect who had fallen from grace. In an incident, she had lost her beauty and cultivation. Can Qin Yun help her reach the peak again, all the while keeping a low profile?

Many were still unaware of what had just transpired. They didn't even realize that the Heart Demon Martial God had been defeated. In the brief instant just now, the Heart Demon Martial God had clashed with Meditation True Person and Extinction Martial God, and the result was a crushing defeat for him.

Although it seemed he hadn't sustained any physical injuries, his most prized illusion techniques were ineffective against the two, rendering him practically useless. In terms of physical strength, the Heart Demon Martial God was hardly worth mentioning.

Thus, while it appeared to be a three-against-two scenario with Lin Fei, North Wind True Person, and the Heart Demon Martial God against Extinction Martial God and Meditation True Person, it was effectively a two-on-two battle. Lin Fei could only truly rely on North Wind True Person.

Could North Wind True Person withstand either Meditation True Person or Extinction Martial God? It seemed highly unlikely, unless Lin Fei could slay Extinction Martial God first. Only then would they stand a chance of victory.

"Three breaths, slay Extinction Martial God!" Lin Fei suddenly declared.

The Heart Demon Martial God and North Wind True Person immediately understood his intention. Not only did they grasp it, but so did Extinction Martial God and Meditation True Person, especially Extinction Martial God, who felt as if he had been greatly insulted. A terrifying aura erupted from his entire being.

"For so many years, no Martial God has ever dared to claim they could kill me in three breaths. What makes you think you can?" The aura emanating from Extinction Martial God was immense, symbolizing endless slaughter and murderous intent, overwhelming as if it could shatter the spirit. This very aura of slaughter was what kept Extinction Martial God from falling into the Heart Demon Martial God's illusions.

"Saber World, descend!" Lin Fei didn't speak further. Instead, he immediately invoked the Saber World. In an instant, Saber World descended, and the thirteen laws of the world fearlessly merged with it. The power of the entire Saber World surged overwhelmingly, crushing towards Extinction Martial God from all directions.

One breath!

"Hahaha, Saber World? Just that?!" Extinction Martial God laughed. "It seems to be a very special law, akin to a miniature world. But can it trap me? Can it kill me?"

"Land of Deathly Purification, Infernal Fist, break!" Extinction Martial God roared, ready to shatter the encroaching world with his power.

"Buzz."

Extinction Martial God took action. With a single punch, a chilling, blood-soaked law enveloped him – the Law of Death. This law seemed to be applicable not only against enemies but could also be used on oneself. Sensing the uniqueness of Lin Fei's law, Extinction Martial God did not use the Law of Death to suppress Lin Fei.

Instead, he applied it to himself, instantly creating something akin to a domain of death, a "Purified Land." However, this "Purified Land" was saturated with death, slaughter, and bloodshed, resembling a hellish landscape.

Extinction Martial God, embodying the essence of extinction, death, and slaughter, struck the Saber World with immense force. The impact on the Saber World was staggering.

"Crack."

As expected, the Saber World shattered, a fissure breaking through it. As the Saber World crumbled, an overwhelming aura of death spread out. At this moment, Lin Fei did not hesitate anymore. Two breaths had already passed since he deployed the Saber World in an attempt to suppress and seal Extinction Martial God, to the moment Extinction Martial God shattered it with a punch.

Then came the third breath.

"Boom."

Inside Lin Fei, one thousand three hundred particle worlds erupted. Right at the moment when Extinction Martial God broke the Saber World, Lin Fei's internal particle worlds exploded. His body, surging with ferocious power, grew to a giant-like stature, and the tremendous force seemed to fracture the very void itself, causing thin black fissures to appear.

Even separated by the Saber World, Extinction Martial God seemed to sense the unimaginable power within Lin Fei, a power capable of annihilating everything.

"Three breaths!" Lin Fei roared and threw a punch.

"Boom."

Extinction Martial God's eyes widened. His entire body was engulfed in the Law of Death, appearing like a cloud of black mist. Simultaneously, a set of armor adorned with ghastly bone spurs appeared on him – a Bloodline Armor.

"Crack, crack."

However, under Lin Fei's punch, this Bloodline Armor shattered instantly. The vast power, like an overwhelming wave, mercilessly hammered into Extinction Martial God's physical form. Countless particles in Extinction Martial God's body were annihilated in an instant.

After all, having lost the Law of Death and the treasure of his bloodline, Extinction Martial God was merely a Martial God who had transcended five heavenly tribulations. He wasn't extraordinary. At least, the defense was Extinction Martial God's weakness.

Silence!

Suddenly, the surroundings fell eerily quiet. The entire void stilled. Countless gazes were fixed on this area of the void, the battlefield. However, the owners of these gazes seemed unable to believe what they had just witnessed.

Dead!

Extinction Martial God was dead! Not even a single particle remained. He had completely fallen, and the timing was exactly three breaths.

"How is this possible? Extinction Martial God is dead?"

"Truly dead, not a trace of life left."

"Even Extinction Martial God's bloodline treasure was instantly shattered. How terrifying must Lin Fei's punch have been?"

"Extinction Martial God, always the bringer of slaughter and death to others, has now fallen himself?"

"The Divine Realm will no longer have Extinction Martial God!"

Many were shocked or emotional. Extinction Martial God, a figure of notorious fame, had fallen exactly within the three breaths as Lin Fei had declared.

Even North Wind True Person and Meditation True Person, each manifesting countless avatars in their relentless battle, paused in their fights. Compared to their prolonged struggle, the clash between Lin Fei and Extinction Martial God ended far too swiftly. Just three breaths and Extinction Martial God was truly dead.

In that moment, Meditation True Person was deeply shaken. The formidable Extinction Martial God, who had seemed invincible and inscrutable just moments ago, had not survived even three breaths before falling.

Was it that Extinction Martial God had overrated his own abilities and was actually weak? Or was it that Lin Fei had been concealing his terrifying strength all along? Meditation True Person inclined towards the latter explanation. After all, Extinction Martial God had a well-established reputation backed by genuine accomplishments, not mere boastfulness.

For Meditation True Person, witnessing such a powerful Martial God was a first. However, he was not intimidated. Proud and self-assured, he had stumbled upon Extinction Martial God by chance. In his plans, there was no need for alliances; he alone could dominate the True Spirit Realm and suppress everything within it!

With Extinction Martial God's death, it was an opportunity for Meditation True Person to demonstrate his true strength. He swiftly retreated, then stood with his hands behind his back in the midst of the void.

"North Wind True Person, Martial God Lin," he called out. "You seek battle, do you not? Well, face my one thousand three hundred prime battle avatars. Can you annihilate them all?"

As Meditation True Person's words echoed, there was a powerful tremor in his body. Then, a massive number of prime battle avatars emerged from within him, materializing in the void. They were densely packed, each avatar comparable to an average Martial God in strength.

Furthermore, there were thirteen hundred of these formidable avatars, which formed an array and instantly sealed off the void, even locking down the entire Realm of True Spirit!
