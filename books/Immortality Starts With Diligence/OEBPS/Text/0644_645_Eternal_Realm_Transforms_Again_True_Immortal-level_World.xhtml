645. Eternal Realm Transforms Again, True Immortal-level World!

The Dark Demon Lord was profoundly shaken. His mind immersed within the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, he discovered something unexpected—water! But not just any water, this was Chaos Heavy Water.

Normally, the Chaos was filled with chaotic energies, not water. However, under certain conditions, when chaotic energies continuously condense, influenced by the right environmental factors and over extended periods, they could merge together with Chaos's inherent rules to form a special kind of water—Chaos Heavy Water.

This type of water was exceedingly rare, often taking ages to form even a single drop. Yet, within the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, it was like a raging river, tumultuous with Chaos Heavy Water. Gaining control over the Sphere of Demonic Clouds essentially meant mastering this vast reservoir of Chaos Heavy Water.

Under the influence of this water, even a casual strike from the Sphere could be unbearable for ordinary Supremes.

Moreover, Chaos Heavy Water also had the ability to seal powers. Almost no Supreme below the level of dominion could break through the seal of Chaos Heavy Water.

Thus, the Dark Demon Lord was ecstatic. "Hahaha, the heavens are indeed aiding me! Who would have thought that within the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, there would be such an abundance of Chaos Heavy Water?

With so much of it, I can traverse the Chaos with authority. With the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, even outside the Dark Demon Domain, I can wield power almost comparable to a dominion!"

The Dark Demon Lord was acutely aware of what this meant. In the Chaos or other realms, being able to exert dominion-like power was a quantum leap.

This development meant that future invasions of other realms by the Dark Demon Lord or the Dark Demon Domain could be undertaken with ease. There would be no need for protracted campaigns; a direct intervention by the Dark Demon Lord himself would suffice to conquer other realms.

Elated beyond measure, the Dark Demon Lord now held the Sphere of Demonic Clouds. What did the mere Divine Realm amount to now?

"This time, Lin Fei is undoubtedly doomed!" he thought. "And the Divine Realm will be completely overtaken by the Dark Demon Domain! Once we occupy the Divine Realm, it will certainly elevate the Dark Demon Domain to greater heights!"

A glint of cunning flashed in the Dark Demon Lord's eyes. With the Sphere of Demonic Clouds in hand, he left the Dark Demon Domain and headed straight for the Divine Realm.

A year had passed in the Divine Realm, a rare period of outward calm, particularly from the perspective of the Dark Demon Domain.

However, internally, the Divine Realm was anything but peaceful. Every force within the Divine Realm was frantically searching for minor worlds.

This relentless pursuit of World Origin lasted the entire year, with all collected resources being funneled to Ancient City.

Many had already surmised that the one in need of this World Origin must be Lin Fei, the Martial Ancestor, now the sole hope of the Divine Realm.

The entire realm was prepared to provide whatever Lin Fei needed, no matter the cost.

Inside his chamber in Ancient City, Lin Fei continuously fed the incoming World Origin into the Realm Core of his Eternal Realm.

This World Origin, transformed into realm power by the Core, was incessantly remodeling Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, or rather, elevating it.

Now, after a year of uninterrupted supply thanks to the efforts of the Azure Emperor Supreme and others who had delivered substantial amounts of World Origin, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was on the verge of a significant transformation.

The progress was evident: 91%, 93%, 95%, 97%, 99%...

Despite nearing completion, Lin Fei remained composed, his mind as still as water. This transformation felt natural to him; ever since he had acquired the Realm Core, he knew that this evolution was inevitable.

No essences of flesh, no growth of sentient life, and no need for powerful life forms within the Eternal Realm were necessary any longer. With the Realm Core, nothing else mattered.

As long as the Realm Core could continue converting World Origin into realm power, the Eternal Realm would keep advancing, never stopping until it reached the status of a realm.

Finally, the moment arrived—Lin Fei's Eternal Realm began to expand wildly. It grew tenfold, a hundredfold, a thousandfold, even ten thousandfold—expanding thousands of times over because it had transformed. It was no longer a True Monarch-level world but had become a True Immortal-level world!

A True Immortal-level world is a powerful domain capable of birthing True Immortals and Martial Ancestors, akin to the legendary Ancient World, another True Immortal-level world.

Compared to the countless minor worlds and the significantly fewer True Monarch-level worlds, True Immortal-level worlds are extremely rare, numbering less than ten across the entire Divine Realm.

The transformation of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm continued, signaling that while it had reached a new echelon of power, the evolution was far from over.

This was not just a change; it was a qualitative leap, setting Lin Fei on a path that could potentially alter the balance of power not just in the Divine Realm, but across multiple realms.

Lin Fei suddenly opened his eyes, his mind buzzing with anticipation and questions about his current status. With the Eternal Realm having transformed into a True Immortal-level world and his power potentially increasing tenfold or even a hundredfold compared to before, he was not merely on a different level—he was on an entirely different plane.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Martial Ancestor

World Level: True Immortal-level World (Progress 1%)

Illusory Realm: Supreme Level

Seeing his world level now marked as a True Immortal-level world and the progress already at 1%, Lin Fei understood that his Eternal Realm was still advancing, still transforming as long as it received World Origin.

However, achieving further progress would require significantly more World Origin. Martial realm remained at Martial Ancestor, which meant that despite his enormous power, he was not yet considered a Supreme.

"Could it be that I only attain the Supreme status once the Eternal Realm evolves into a realm?" Lin Fei mused softly to himself. He was nearly certain that was the case. Only the transformation of his world into a realm could elevate him to Supreme status.

But for now, Lin Fei felt an overwhelming surge in his power. He was confident that even if the Azure Emperor Supreme and the other eight Supremes joined forces, they would struggle to defeat him, especially since he no longer needed to rely on the power of the Illusory Realm.

Just the force of his Eternal Realm was enough to slay a typical Supreme—and even the Dark Demon Lord should they meet again.

However, Lin Fei was in no rush. With the Realm Core still in his possession, he could continue to ascend. If the Dark Demon Domain remained quiet, all the better.

That would allow Lin Fei the time to gradually evolve his Eternal Realm into a full realm, achieving true Supreme status. Once he reached that level, what threat could the Dark Demon Domain pose?

Lin Fei alone could then easily sweep through the Dark Demon life forms without needing any help.

The leap from a True Immortal-level world to a realm was a qualitative one, requiring unimaginable amounts of World Origin and potentially a very long time. Time was precisely what Lin Fei lacked the most.

"I hope the Dark Demon Lord will grant me more time..." he murmured, before closing his eyes again to continue absorbing World Origin and reshaping his Eternal Realm.
