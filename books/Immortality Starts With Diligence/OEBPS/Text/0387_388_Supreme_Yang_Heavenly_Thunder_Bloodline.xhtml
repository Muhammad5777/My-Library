388. Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline

Inside the Dubhe Dao Artifact, there were countless bloodline techniques. However, since Lin Fei was set on cultivating a Yang attribute bloodline, the first thing that came to his mind was thunder. Thunder represents the supreme Yang, making it the very essence of Yang attribute bloodlines!

The Foundation Establishment cultivator must endure heavenly thunder to potentially transform into a Golden Core. The path of immortals even refers to this thunder as Heavenly Tribulation, showing just how much fear it instills in cultivators.

"Heavenly thunder... I wonder if there's a bloodline of thunder that's comparable or even surpasses it?" Lin Fei wondered, beginning his search within the Dubhe Dao Artifact for a thunder bloodline technique.

Soon, Lin Fei indeed found a Yang attribute thunder bloodline technique called Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline. Upon mastering this bloodline, one would become the embodiment of utmost Yang, capable of summoning thunder with every gesture, akin to a true god of thunder.

Ever since becoming a Martial King, as Lin Fei cultivated different bloodlines, he felt more and more that his attacks were transcending the scope of martial arts as he knew them. Instead, he seemed more like the legendary deities who could influence the heavens with a mere gesture. Their power was earth-shattering. After all, wasn't the path of the Martial King laying the foundation to become a Martial God? And isn't a Martial God a deity?

Lin Fei made up his mind, "Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Technique. Come, merge with me and become my fourth bloodline!"

As Lin Fei's words faded, his existing three bloodlines erupted.

A point of golden light immediately entered Lin Fei's body, causing him to tremble. Memories regarding the Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline emerged in his mind.

Previously, whether practicing martial arts or Divine Abilities, Lin Fei had nothing that could relate to thunder. Therefore, this time, his original bloodlines couldn't assist him, causing the merging process with the Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline to be slow.

Suddenly, Lin Fei's entire body shook, and the fourth bloodline immediately manifested. Faint thunders flickered around him. The fourth bloodline was completed! At this moment, Lin Fei progressed to the mid-stage of Martial King. Moreover, his Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline was constantly refining and enhancing his physical body. This subtle upgrade might continue for a long time. By then, Lin Fei's physique would become even more formidable!

However, Lin Fei's Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline was still at the beginner stage, with the progress of cultivation only at 1%. This meant that Lin Fei's previous bloodlines and power did not enhance or support the cultivation of the Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline. Thus, he had to search for thunder-related Divine Abilities, specifically those of the Yang attribute. While most thunder Divine Abilities are of Yang attribute, they are quite rare. After all, mastering thunder, a terrifying force between heaven and earth with destructive power, is challenging, whether for immortals or martial artists.

Within the Dubhe Dao Artifact, Lin Fei only found eleven thunder Divine Abilities. Among them, two were of the Yin attribute, which did not match Lin Fei's Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline, making them unsuitable for assimilation. Lin Fei could only integrate with nine of these thunder Divine Abilities. Still, none of them triggered any significant evolution in his Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline; it remained far from perfection.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Middle Stage Martial King

Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline: Perfection

Grand Yin Wind Dao Bloodline: Perfection

Triple Yin True Fire Bloodline: Perfection

Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline: Beginner (Progress 91%)

Saber World: Perfection

The progress of Lin Fei's Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline remained at 91%. It seemed that each Thunder Divine Ability contributed about 10% to his cultivation progress. Still, it was not enough to break through to mastery.

Hence, Lin Fei visited the Xiao Family's Divine Ability Tower. To his disappointment, he couldn't find a single thunder Divine Ability within the vast collection of the Xiao family. This absence further highlighted the rarity of such techniques.

"It's troublesome without any thunder Divine Abilities..." Lin Fei frowned in concern. Given that the Xiao family, a prestigious Martial King lineage, hadn't collected any thunder Divine Abilities over many years, it was likely that other Martial King families faced similar shortages. However, Lin Fei had another avenue to explore: he was a member of the Martial God Alliance.

Sitting cross-legged in a secret chamber, Lin Fei activated the Martial God Alliance's token. In an instant, he found himself in the Martial God Space, a grand hall occupied by many strong individuals, mostly Martial Kings and Nascent Soul experts.

Without hesitation, Lin Fei posted his request on the task wall, seeking Yang-attribute thunder Divine Abilities, with no limit to the quantity. As for the reward, it would be discussed based on the quality and rarity of the Divine Abilities once presented.

After leaving his task in the Martial God space, Lin Fei exited the area. Although Lin Fei doubted that other Martial King families possessed thunder-attributed Divine Abilities, he instructed the Xiao family, in his name, to start collecting them. Especially from other Martial King families. As long as they were thunder-attributed Divine Abilities, everything was negotiable. Given Lin Fei's current high-standing status, his mere words could mobilize countless individuals. Hence, all he had to do was wait patiently.

After waiting several days without any news, Lin Fei returned to the Ancient Origin Sect. Being a Martial King of the Ancient Origin Sect and with only around two months left before the opening of the Tuyuan Realm, Lin Fei realized he hadn't allocated the slots for it. He had eleven Martial Sovereign slots. Apart from him, the Ancient Origin Sect had exactly eleven spots. It had been a while since Lin Fei had taken the time for focused cultivation. Now seemed like the perfect opportunity.

Days went by until suddenly, a sensation stirred within Lin Fei. In the next moment, his consciousness entered the Martial God space. He had felt a disturbance in the Martial God space, which caused his Martial God Alliance token to vibrate, signaling him.

"Who's calling me?"

Once inside, a figure appeared before him. This figure was located using the coordinates Lin Fei had left behind. In the Martial God space, one needs to leave coordinates for others to contact them. This is usually done on the task wall. However, upon seeing the stranger in front of him, Lin Fei's eyebrows knitted slightly, "Nascent Soul cultivator?"

Indeed, standing before Lin Fei was not a Martial King but a powerful Nascent Soul Great Being! Although the Martial God Alliance primarily consisted of martial artists, it didn't exclude immortal cultivators. Hence, some Nascent Soul cultivators had also joined the Alliance.

"Fellow Daoist, you've posted a task seeking a large number of Yang-attributed thunder Divine Abilities. May I ask, are you trying to cultivate a thunder bloodline and hope to comprehend it through these Divine Abilities?" The Nascent Soul cultivator asked, his gaze fixed intently on Lin Fei.

Most were unaware of Lin Fei's ability to integrate Divine Abilities. The only plausible explanation was that Lin Fei intended to use these thunder-attributed Divine Abilities to understand the mysteries of the thunder, thus enhancing his thunder bloodline. Admittedly, this method isn't always effective.

"And if it is?" Lin Fei wasn't about to reveal his capability to integrate Divine Abilities.

"In that case, there are actually better methods to comprehend the mysteries of thunder than merely studying thunder-attributed Divine Abilities. I don't have those Divine Abilities, but I do possess a Nascent Soul magic treasure that has collected a vast amount of heavenly thunder. This should be sufficient for your understanding."

With that, the Nascent Soul Great Being promptly took out a magic treasure and handed it to Lin Fei.
