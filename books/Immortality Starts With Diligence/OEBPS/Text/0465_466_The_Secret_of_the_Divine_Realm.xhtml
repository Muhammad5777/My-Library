466. The Secret of the Divine Realm!

"Do you have any other questions? No worries, take your time to think. "I will answer anything you ask, without hiding a thing!"

Observing Taotie's seemingly cooperative demeanor, Lin Fei found himself in a bit of a quandary. He had no reason to silence Taotie.

Of course, with Taotie being so cooperative, Lin Fei found him useful to keep.

"Let's hold onto this for now."

Lin Fei secured Taotie. After all, Taotie was sealed by his world's laws. He had no fear of Taotie causing an upheaval.

Lin Fei was beginning to understand that his world's laws were likely extraordinary.

Taotie, even as an avatar, was undoubtedly an ancient mythological exotic beast from the primordial era. Yet, this powerful mythological beast was easily sealed by Lin Fei.

Moreover, the power erupting from the hundred particle worlds within Lin Fei, also crushed Taotie. Whether it was Lin Fei's self-created techniques or the world laws he comprehended, they were no small feat.

Returning to the Feng family, Feng Ling solemnly said, "Lin Fei, are you planning to go to the Divine Realm?"

She could tell that Lin Fei was somewhat tempted.

After pondering for a while, Lin Fei slowly spoke, "I had already planned to attempt transcending tribulation, at least once through thunder tribulation. I didn't expect to learn of such matters. Transcending tribulation in the Ancient Continent weakens rather than fully realizing the laws.

One misstep leads to another. I don't want to live with regrets. If I am to transcend, I must do it in the Divine Realm. However, regarding the Divine Realm, we can't blindly trust Taotie's words. We need to investigate further and confirm before making any plans. I plan to visit the Heaven Transformation Sect tomorrow to discuss with the Master Heaven Transformation."

Feng Ling nodded, "Good, then we shall go together tomorrow."

Thus, Lin Fei and Feng Ling rested for the night. The next day, they flew directly to the Heaven Transformation Sect.

Soon, they arrived at the Heaven Transformation Sect.

The Heaven Transformation Ancestor seemed to have known in advance about their arrival. He was well-prepared. The avatar of him came to greet them.

"Two Martial Gods, apologies for not greeting you from afar."

"Heaven Transformation, it seems you knew we were coming."

"This is not a place for conversation, please."

Heaven Transformation Ancestor led Lin Fei and Feng Ling to the dojo. In the entire dojo, there was only Heaven Transformation Ancestor. The three sat down.

Heaven Transformation Ancestor smiled slightly, "Since learning that the two of you had sealed Taotie's avatar, I knew you would surely come."

"Indeed, you knew about Taotie. Then why didn't you intervene to stop Taotie?" Lin Fei asked in a grave tone.

Heaven Transformation Ancestor gave a wry smile and shook his head, "Truth be told, Taotie is immensely powerful. Even just an avatar is difficult for me to stop. If it came to a desperate fight, I might even lose several avatars to Taotie. In this Ancient Continent, with the laws now fragmented and energy diminishing, creating new avatars has become exceedingly challenging.

Therefore, it's best to avoid losses whenever possible. And it's not just me. The other Deity Transformation cultivators are the same, turning a blind eye one after another. After all, Taotie knows its limits and dares not act rashly. If it were to truly cause chaos, someone from the Divine Realm would naturally come to deal with it.

However, I never expected you two, esteemed friends, to be so powerful as to suppress Taotie in one fell swoop, truly astonishing our peers in the Ancient Continent!"

Lin Fei understood. The other Deity Transformation cultivators were all cunning. Just like Heaven Transformation Ancestor before him, they were all aware of Taotie's identity. Each of them was reluctant to exert themselves, fearing their avatars would be devoured by Taotie, Thus weakening their own strength.

Once reaching Deity Transformation, these Immortal cultivators became overly cautious. Instead, it was he and Feng Ling, the two 'naive youngsters,' who stepped up.

To everyone's surprise, Lin Fei and Feng Ling, both Martial Gods, actually managed to suppress Taotie. This must have caused a stir throughout the Ancient Continent.

"Heaven Transformation, we learned some things about the Divine Realm from Taotie. You once said that if we planned to visit the Divine Realm, we must meet with you first. Now we are here, could you please share what you know about the Divine Realm?"

Lin Fei spoke slowly. He didn't know what Heaven Transformation Ancestor was planning, But all he wanted was some information about the Divine Realm.

Moreover, Lin Fei cleverly used "Taotie" as a reminder to Heaven Transformation Ancestor, That he and Feng Ling were not completely ignorant about the Divine Realm.

With this reminder, Heaven Transformation Ancestor had to weigh his words carefully. Any attempt at deception could offend Lin Fei and Feng Ling, or lead to misunderstandings, which would be counterproductive and undesirable.

After a long pause, Heaven Transformation Ancestor finally spoke, smiling, "Friends Lin and Feng, since you both are so forthright, I shall not beat around the bush and speak plainly. The Divine Realm is indeed a place all divine-level experts, especially Martial Gods aspire to reach. For Martial Gods who wish to advance further and grow stronger, they must transcend the thunder tribulation in the Divine Realm only then can they obtain the complete laws and gain even stronger powers.

However, staying in the Ancient Continent also has its advantages. Here, we can establish sects and lineages, enjoying a glorious era spanning hundreds of thousands of years. If one goes to the Divine Realm, such treatment is not guaranteed.

Moreover, it's not that cultivation is impossible in the Ancient Continent, it's just not as straightforward. If your ambitions are lofty, by all means, head for the Divine Realm.

The gateway to the Divine Realm lies within the Mountain of Ascension. However, if one day you achieve greatness in the Divine Realm or even manage to return to the Ancient Continent, I only ask that you bring me a Void Transformation Pill. I am willing to pay any price for this pill!"

With that, Heaven Transformation Ancestor surprisingly bowed deeply to Lin Fei and Feng Ling.

Lin Fei frowned and said, "What is a Void Transformation Pill? Unless you explain clearly, I will not help you."

Heaven Transformation Ancestor replied with a wry smile, "Deity Transformations have a lifespan of a hundred thousand years. I have already lived for several tens of thousands of years. With more than half of my life gone, naturally, I wish to continue living.

I have no ambition to cultivate in the Divine Realm to the Void Transformation stage. How many Deity Transformations can achieve that? Very few!

But if I could get a Void Transformation Pill, it would be different. The Void Transformation stage primarily involves comprehending the laws of space, allowing for spatial traversal. The true Void Transformation stage, in the Divine Realm, means countless avatars traversing numerous small worlds in an instant.

There's also a pseudo-Void Transformation stage. It involves comprehending the spatial laws within a small world, enabling void traversal within that world. Still, it is considered Void Transformation, with a lifespan of a million years!

However, once one becomes a pseudo-Void Transformation, they cannot leave that small world, or else they would perish due to conflicting laws.

The Void Transformation Pill is crafted by Dao Integration True Immortals. Although it's precious, it is attainable. Consuming a Void Transformation Pill gives a Deity Transformation a great chance to penetrate the void laws of a small world, thus becoming a pseudo-Void Transformation.

My only desire is to live for a million years. Even if there's just a glimmer of hope, it's worth pursuing!"

Heaven Transformation Ancestor was sincere in his request. This was his only option. He dared not venture into the Divine Realm for once one steps into the Divine Realm, returning becomes a difficult task. His only choice was to forge good relations with those potential-filled divine-level experts, hoping for a slim chance to one day receive a Void Transformation Pill from them. Such tactics of persuasion, Heaven Transformation Ancestor must have employed before.

"I promise you. If one day, obtaining a Void Transformation Pill becomes an effortless task, I shall willingly give you a chance, as a token of appreciation for your efforts."

Lin Fei nodded in agreement. He made no guarantees. If acquiring a Void Transformation Pill turned out to be a simple task, Lin Fei was naturally willing to help. Otherwise, he would not truly go out of his way to seek a Void Transformation Pill for Heaven Transformation Ancestor.

Having fully grasped the situation of the Divine Realm from Heaven Transformation Ancestor, Lin Fei no longer harbored any doubts. Thus, he left the Heaven Transformation Sect, together with Feng Ling, returning to the Feng family.
