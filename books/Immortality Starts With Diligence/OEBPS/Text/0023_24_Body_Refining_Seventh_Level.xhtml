"Nine Shadow Saber Technique's progress has reached 1%?"

Lin Fei was shocked.

Since reaching perfection in the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, the practice progress had been stuck at 0, with almost no improvement.

But now it has actually reached 1%.

And, it seemed to be because the Thirty-Six Fast Saber had reached perfection and was "absorbed" by the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, adding to its foundation.

So that was why it increased by 1%.

"That means the Nine Shadow Saber Technique can still improve even after reaching perfection.

Just by practicing other saber techniques to perfection, they can be absorbed by the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

Doesn't that mean I have a chance to learn saber intent?"

Lin Fei felt his heart burn with passion.

Saber intent! Drawing a saber before it was a luxury. How powerful would it be?!

Even comprehending saber intent was harder than becoming innate.

It showed how extraordinary the saber intent was.

For ordinary people, it was very difficult to practice their martial arts to perfection, even for basic martial arts.

Lin Fei had practiced ten saber techniques.

Fortunately, they were just basic saber techniques and did not require much insight to reach perfection.

However, even if he merged all ten basic saber techniques into the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, the progress would only be increased to 10%.

Lin Fei continued to wait patiently.

As time passed, he achieved perfection in all the other nine basic saber techniques, and each one was absorbed by the Nine Shadow Saber Technique without exception.

However, the progress of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique had only increased to 8%.

This indicated that some of the basic saber techniques might not be able to improve the progress of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique at all.

Therefore, Lin Fei's idea of searching for countless basic saber techniques and piling up the progress of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique to 100% in order to comprehend saber intent was almost impossible to achieve.

Perhaps, as the progress of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique continued to increase, no matter how many basic saber techniques he practiced, they might not be able to improve the progress of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique any further.

So, Lin Fei went to the Martial Arts Pavilion again and selected some saber techniques that were stronger than the basic ones, such as the Swift Wind Saber Technique, Shadow Saber Technique, and so on.

However, these saber techniques took a longer time to practice, and achieving perfection was not easy either.

Lin Fei's idea of comprehending saber intent in a short period of time was shattered.

Days passed one by one.

Lin Fei's attention once again focused on the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique, because the progress of the technique was approaching 100% again.

This meant that the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique was about to break through, and this breakthrough was different from previous ones and held significant meaning.

Once the breakthrough happened, he would be at the seventh level of Body Refining and could become an inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect.

Lin Fei was filled with excitement as he waited for this day to come. He did not have to wait long before he received the news.

"Your Giant Whale Body Refining Technique has reached 101% after yesterday's intense practice."

"Your Giant Whale Body Refining Technique has broken through to the seventh level."

Many memories flooded Lin Fei's mind, and his blood and qi once again grew stronger. His body underwent an earth-shattering transformation.

"System," Lin Fei muttered to himself as he prepared to check his condition.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Art Realm: Body Refining Seventh Level

Giant Whale Body Refining Technique: Seventh Level (1% progress)

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Transcendence (1% progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Perfection (8% progress)

Vajra Iron Cloth: Sixth Level (31% progress)

Tendon Transformation and Bone Forging Technique: Fifth Level (81% progress)

He noticed that while many of his martial arts had progressed, his Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist remained at "transcendence" and had not shown any progress.

His task to guide the technique seemed to have been ignored, and it appeared that no one in the Nine Profound Sect was capable of perfecting the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

Nevertheless, Lin Fei was happy as he finally achieved the seventh level of Body Refining at the age of 18, which meant he was now qualified to become an inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect.

Having reached the seventh level of Body Refining at the age of eighteen, he was already considered a genuine genius within Nine Profound Sect. Of course, there was still a considerable distance to be covered before reaching the level of the top talents.

There were some who had reached the seventh level of Body Refining at the age of twelve, setting a record within Nine Profound Sect.

The inner sect disciples of Nine Profound Sect were the true backbone of the sect, and as such, many resources were allocated to them. Any Nine Profound Sect inner sect disciple was considered a big shot in their local area.

For example, if Lin Fei were to return to the Zhou Mountain Mansion City as an inner sect disciple, everything would be different. Some officials in the city would even treat him with a special kind of respect.

"Okay. Go to the Transmission Hall first to confirm my martial arts level, and then join the inner sect!" Lin Fei couldn't wait any longer. He had spent enough time in the outer sect and it was time for a change of scenery.

With that, Lin Fei got up and headed straight for the Transmission Hall.
