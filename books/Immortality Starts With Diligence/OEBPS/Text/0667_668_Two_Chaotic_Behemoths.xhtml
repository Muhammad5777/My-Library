668. Two Chaotic Behemoths

Clearly remembering her journey to power, Rainbow Supreme knew her rise had been fueled by her own relentless dedication, coupled with fortuitous opportunities.

She had never encountered a top-tier Supreme before meeting Lin Fei. Intelligent and perceptive, she understood that anyone capable of employing Lin Fei's protection must also be a top-tier Supreme. Yet, apart from Lin Fei, she had never met another of such formidable status.

"Do you wish to know?" Lin Fei asked, noticing her curiosity.

"Please enlighten me, senior!" Rainbow Supreme eagerly responded her desire to uncover the truth evident.

Lin Fei observed her for a moment. After her time in the Time-Space Corridor, Rainbow Supreme's immediate cultivation prospects were largely capped—her future as a top-tier Supreme would hinge on chance.

"Kunyu City, Mirrorborn Supreme," Lin Fei finally disclosed.

"Mirrorborn Supreme, Kunyu City?" Rainbow Supreme murmured, trying to connect the dots with what she knew about Mirrorborn Supreme. Suddenly, a realization struck her.

"Wait, that technique..." Rainbow Supreme recalled an important cultivation technique pivotal to her progress, one of her greatest strokes of fortune. The originator of that technique, it seemed, was none other than Mirrorborn Supreme.

With sudden clarity, Rainbow Supreme understood. She had likely been under Mirrorborn Supreme's watchful eye since she first encountered that crucial technique.

It was Mirrorborn Supreme who had covertly safeguarded her, ensuring her path was strewn with serendipitous escapes from peril and smooth ascension to Supreme status. This revelation explained the seemingly miraculous interventions that had consistently tipped the scales in her favor throughout her journey.

"Thank you, senior." Rainbow Supreme bowed respectfully to Lin Fei.

"Let's go back to Kunyu City. My task here is nearly complete," Lin Fei said in a measured tone. He needed to return to Kunyu City as soon as possible to gather information about Chaotic Behemoths from Mirrorborn Supreme.

"Senior, we..." Rainbow Supreme began, seemingly wanting to express more.

Before she could continue, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm enveloped her and her two companions, drawing them all into the Eternal Realm. In an instant, Lin Fei transformed into a streak of light, speeding toward Kunyu City.

...

In Kunyu City, at Mirrorborn Supreme's abode, a wry smile appeared on Mirrorborn Supreme's face when he saw Lin Fei arrive with Rainbow Supreme in tow.

"Supreme Lin, I didn't expect you to bring her along," he remarked. He had intended to keep his guardianship a secret, but it seemed that was no longer possible.

"Rainbow Supreme can no longer make quick progress," Lin Fei stated calmly. "To reach the status of a top-tier Supreme will take a long time, perhaps without any significant advancement."

Mirrorborn Supreme remained silent for a moment, understanding the truth in Lin Fei's words. After her journey through the Time-Space Corridor, there was no need to keep her in the dark any longer.

"Very well, thank you, Supreme Lin. The information about the Chaotic Behemoths that you requested, I will provide it to you," Mirrorborn Supreme said, handing over the details.

Lin Fei nodded and promptly took his leave, not wanting to interrupt the reunion between master and disciple.

With the specific locations of the Chaotic Behemoths fixed in his mind—information he trusted from Mirrorborn Supreme—Lin Fei was surprised to learn there were two Behemoths. Typically solitary creatures, it was rare for two Chaotic Behemoths to be found together, though not impossible.

Transforming once again into a streak of light, Lin Fei plunged back into the depths of the chaos. After about a month of travel, he finally halted; he had located the Chaotic Behemoths, exactly as described in the information given to him.

Indeed, there were two, both deep in slumber—a common trait he had observed in several Behemoths he had encountered. Perhaps due to their long lifespans and chaotic birthplaces, there was little else for them to do but sleep when undisturbed.

With an unhesitating hum, Lin Fei summoned the Illusory Realm, weaving it together with the Eternal Realm he had mastered so proficiently. Upon the arrival of the Illusory Realm, the two Chaotic Behemoths displayed no sign of disturbance, not even opening their eyes, making it uncertain whether the Illusory Realm had any effect on them at all. Regardless, Lin Fei had more than the Illusory Realm at his disposal.

As the Eternal Realm merged with the Illusory Realm, it swiftly enveloped the two Chaotic Behemoths. With a swish of his commanding energy, Lin Fei encapsulated both behemoths within his Eternal Realm. Such significant disturbance would surely rouse even the most deeply slumbering of creatures.

Indeed, both Chaotic Behemoths awakened abruptly and opened their eyes, but by then it was too late. Lin Fei's Eternal Realm had already subdued them, and the immense pressure from all directions made any struggle futile. They roared and thrashed violently, trying to escape the confines of the Eternal Realm, but to no avail.

"Annihilate!"

Lin Fei finally spoke, his voice summoning the full might of the Eternal Realm. Immediately, the realm's power began to obliterate the two Chaotic Behemoths. Their bodies could not withstand the onslaught, and quickly they started to disintegrate: ten percent, twenty percent, thirty percent, forty percent, and over fifty percent of their bodies were eradicated, and the destruction continued.

Under the relentless suppression of the Eternal Realm, the behemoths couldn't even twitch. This demonstrated Lin Fei's current strength—able to easily suppress Supremes and Chaotic Behemoths alike.

As the bodies of the Chaotic Behemoths continued to dissolve, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm absorbed a vast amount of their essence. The essence nourished and enriched the entire realm.

After an indeterminate amount of time, a deep rumbling resonated through the Eternal Realm. The essence of the realm itself seemed to be undergoing a transformation, signaling something momentous was unfolding within.

"It's done!"

Lin Fei's eyes sparkled with a unique brilliance. His Eternal Realm was evolving, undergoing a profound transformation. The transformation indicated that his realm's progress had reached and perhaps even surpassed the critical threshold of ten percent, especially with the addition of the essence from two Chaotic Behemoths, significantly enhancing the realm beyond merely ten percent. This marked a significant milestone in the potency and capability of his Eternal Realm.

Lin Fei waited patiently until the two Chaotic Behemoths were entirely obliterated and their essence fully assimilated by his Eternal Realm. Only then did the transformative process within the realm begin to subside, eventually reaching a state of complete calm.

"System," Lin Fei commanded, accessing the interface to review his current status:

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Martial Ancestor

World Level: True Immortal Level (Progress: 11%)

Illusory Realm: Supreme Level

Observing his world level, Lin Fei noted it remained at the True Immortal level, but the progress had advanced to 11%, surpassing the critical 10% mark.

This increase suggested that his Eternal Realm had ascended to a new level of power, rendering it formidably strong.

Lin Fei felt confident that even a top-tier Supreme would struggle to escape if ensnared by his Eternal Realm. Such was the terrifying prowess of his current realm.

"Now, it seems a good time to return to the Divine Realm," Lin Fei mused, his thoughts turning to further ambitions. "Perhaps I could also venture into the Dark Demon Domain to see if I can vanquish the Dark Demon Lord and eradicate the threat from that realm once and for all."

His eyes glittered with a fierce light at the thought. Lin Fei had not forgotten the threat posed by the Dark Demon Domain and its Ruler. Given any opportunity, he was determined to neutralize these dangers permanently.

With this resolution, Lin Fei hesitated no longer. In a swift motion, he left Kunyu City, transforming into a streak of light as he headed back toward the Divine Realm.
