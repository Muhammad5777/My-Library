601. One Strike to Annihilate Thousands of True Immortal Avatars

Lin Fei gazed calmly at the True Immortal before him. He did not recognize this figure.

In fact, Lin Fei knew very few True Immortals. After all, he had only recently become a Martial Ancestor. How could he possibly know all the True Immortals?

"Which True Immortal are you, fellow daoist?" Lin Fei inquired.

"I am Azure Mountain True Immortal of the Azure Mountain Sect!"

"Azure Mountain True Immortal, you must have received the message from the Supremes? The Divine Realm is on the brink of destruction. We must all do everything possible to enhance our strength. I urgently need the world laws of True Monarch-level worlds. Thus, I am extracting some world laws. Although this will have some impact on True Monarch-level worlds, with time, they will recover. To increase my power, I must have the world laws of True Monarch-level worlds. Are you truly intent on stopping me, Azure Mountain Daoist?"

Lin Fei's gaze fixed on Azure Mountain True Immortal as he spoke calmly. He believed he had explained himself clearly. He needed to enhance his strength by merging world laws into the Saber World. He was only extracting some world laws from True Monarch-level worlds and would not destroy them. If someone still tried to stop him, Lin Fei would not be held accountable.

"I certainly will stop you. At least, you cannot touch the Ancient God Realm."

As soon as Azure Mountain True Immortal finished speaking, the void suddenly filled with countless avatars of Azure Mountain True Immortal.

Upon closer inspection, there were over ten thousand avatars. These were all True Immortal avatars!

Ten thousand avatars was an alarming number. Clearly, Azure Mountain True Immortal had come prepared. He had studied Lin Fei's previous confrontation with Taihao True Immortal. Thus, once he decided to stop Lin Fei, Azure Mountain True Immortal made thorough preparations.

Seeing the countless avatars of True Immortal, Lin Fei remained calm. His expression did not change.

"You cannot stop me. If you insist on obstructing me, then I will have no choice but to destroy these avatars, Azure Mountain Daoist."

Lin Fei spoke coldly.

The vast number of True Immortal avatars did not intimidate him in the slightest. In reality, it wasn't that Lin Fei specifically needed the world laws of the Ancient God Realm. It was because he couldn't retreat.

If he retreated, when he went to other True Monarch-level worlds, those True Immortals and Martial Ancestor would similarly appear to stop him.

Would Lin Fei have to battle a True Immortal or Martial Ancestor each time? How much time would that waste?

Now, Lin Fei noticed the void concealed many figures and gazes. They were all closely watching his confrontation with Azure Mountain True Immortal.

Lin Fei had no way out. If he retreated now, he would face the same problem when going to other True Monarch-level worlds, perhaps even worse.

Therefore, Lin Fei couldn't retreat. He needed to crush Azure Mountain True Immortal to deter other Martial Ancestor and True Immortal powerhouses. After all, they always had vested interests.

For example, behind the Ancient God Realm stood Azure Mountain True Immortal. Other worlds had other True Immortals or Martial Ancestors behind them. Only by demonstrating absolute overwhelming strength could Lin Fei ensure they wouldn't dare to stop him.

"They say Martial Ancestor Lin is extraordinary. Let me see for myself. Martial Ancestor Lin, what methods do you possess?"

In the next moment, with a thought, Azure Mountain True Immortal's ten thousand avatars formed a massive formation.

At the same time, this formation seemed to resonate with a certain Dao in the Divine Realm. Each avatar emitted a terrifying aura, their power soaring!

However, Lin Fei remained unmoved. He gazed at the ten thousand True Immortal avatars, a gleam of fierceness flashing in his eyes.

"Boom."

Lin Fei made his move. He transformed into the Eternal Realm.

Instantly, an unparalleled world appeared in the void. Simultaneously, the power of the Eternal Realm erupted.

The battle between Lin Fei and Unity True Immortal had only circulated within the Ancient Covenant. Many True Immortals and Martial Ancestors were unaware of the details. They only knew Lin Fei had seemingly defeated Taihao True Immortal but didn't know how.

Now, as Lin Fei unleashed the Eternal Realm, those in the Ancient God Realm and the surrounding True Immortals and Martial Ancestor observing in secret were all shaken to their core!

"This is... transforming into a world?"

Azure Mountain True Immortal was shocked. The moment Lin Fei's Eternal Realm appeared, he felt a terrifying suppression. Instantly, all his power erupted.

Yet, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm encompassed all ten thousand of Azure Mountain True Immortal's avatars.

"Destroy!"

Lin Fei's eyes were cold, and he uttered a soft command.

"Thud."

In the next instant, over ten thousand True Immortal avatars were pulverized into dust within the Eternal Realm following Lin Fei's soft command.

Silence!

A profound silence fell over the scene.

Everyone who witnessed this battle was in disbelief. In their minds, this should have been an earth-shattering confrontation.

Yet, in reality, as soon as Lin Fei acted, Azure Mountain True Immortal's ten thousand avatars were obliterated as if made of paper, vanishing instantly.

"Destroyed? Azure Mountain True Immortal's ten thousand avatars were all obliterated?"

"Azure Mountain True Immortal is a veteran True Immortal. How could his ten thousand avatars be annihilated so quickly?"

"But it's a fact. No matter how unbelievable, it remains true."

"Martial Ancestor Lin, having just become a Martial Ancestor, is already this strong?"

"It seems the rumors that Lin Fei defeated Taihao True Immortal are true."

Some of the True Immortals and Martial Ancestors observing from the shadows were rendered speechless by Lin Fei's display of power, leaving them profoundly shaken. They now had a renewed understanding of Lin Fei's strength. The rumors were not exaggerated—if anything, they understated his power!

Lin Fei's strength had already surpassed that of a typical Martial Ancestor. Generally, a Martial Ancestor might be stronger than a True Immortal, but not to this extent.

"Buzz."

Another figure appeared in the void—it was Azure Mountain True Immortal.

At this moment, his face was somber. This was a newly arrived avatar. He already knew what had transpired.

Over ten thousand of his True Immortal avatars were unable to withstand even a single strike from Lin Fei. This was nearly beyond belief for Azure Mountain True Immortal, but it was the undeniable truth.

"Martial Ancestor Lin, your ability to transform into a world and annihilate over ten thousand of my avatars in a single strike means I can no longer stop you. I hope Martial Ancestor Lin can show mercy to the Ancient God Realm. At the very least, don't let it collapse."

Although Azure Mountain True Immortal had been defeated and lost face, he still sent another avatar because the Ancient God Realm was vital to him.

There were people in the Ancient God Realm he needed to protect. Even now, after his defeat, he had to find a way to preserve the Ancient God Realm.

Lin Fei nodded, not refusing. After all, he only needed the world laws from the True Monarch-level world. Destroying an entire True Monarch-level world was not his intention.

"I only need the world laws!"

With that, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm expanded rapidly, engulfing the Ancient God Realm. The Eternal Realm then suppressed the Ancient God Realm, exerting full-scale pressure.

Using overwhelming force, Lin Fei forcibly extracted the world laws.

Holding the world laws in his hand, Lin Fei's Saber World descended instantly.

"Fuse!"

Lin Fei integrated the world laws into the Saber World. The Saber World expanded rapidly, swelling with newfound power. Yet, it still hadn't fully transformed.

However, it seemed closer to the True Immortal and Martial Ancestor levels, almost within reach. Lin Fei could feel this proximity.

"Still not enough."

Lin Fei frowned. He needed more world laws.

"Martial Ancestor Lin, do you need the world laws of True Monarch-level worlds? How many more do you need? Just now, a Supreme decreed that your requirements for world laws will be met as much as possible."

At this moment, an unfamiliar figure appeared in the void. It was another True Immortal, and his words left Lin Fei slightly surprised.
