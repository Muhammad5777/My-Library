505. A Single Force, Intimidating the Gods of the Divine Realm

"True Spirit Realm is about to collapse?" Lin Fei was slightly startled. The initial estimate was that the realm would collapse in three months, but now it was happening sooner.

Upon reflection, he realized that his fierce battles with Extinction Martial God and Meditation True Person, earth-shattering in their intensity, were likely the real catalysts for the realm's impending collapse. This was a significant development, as only through the collapse of a world could its origin be obtained.

However, the ability to capture the world's origin would depend on the North Wind True Person. This was where his role, and the culmination of his thousands of years of planning, would come into play.

"North Wind True Person, it's all up to you now," Lin Fei acknowledged.

North Wind True Person had long been prepared for this moment. He inhaled deeply and declared, "Nine Heavens and Ten Earths, the Heavenly Net Formation – Activate!"

As North Wind True Person's command echoed, the entire Realm of True Spirit seemed to roar to life. His numerous avatars, strategically positioned across the realm, had laid the groundwork for the formation. At his mental command, all avatars activated the formation. Brilliant lights shot up into the sky from various locations, and the formations, interconnected, covered the entire realm.

This was the manifestation of North Wind True Person's methods and planning. Once True Spirit Realm collapsed, his avatars would be ready to collect the entirety of its world origin. The only caveat was the potential interference of other powerful beings.

Considering the number of Martial Gods and Deity Transformation entities hidden within the realm, it was unlikely they would stand idle at such a crucial moment. They would certainly attempt to seize the world's origin for themselves, especially when it was within arm's reach. How could they resist such a temptation?

With a swift step, Lin Fei moved into the void, preparing himself for whatever was to come next in this rapidly unfolding scenario.

"Within one hour, all powerful beings from the Divine Realm in True Spirit Realm must leave. Those who remain after an hour will be slain without exception!" Lin Fei's voice, brimming with lethal intent, resonated across the void. His proclamation immediately caused a stir.

"What a domineering Martial God, to drive us away and even threaten to kill us!"

"He's so arrogant! Just as True Spirit Realm is collapsing and the world origin is about to appear, he's forcing us out. Is he trying to monopolize the world's origin?"

"I refuse to leave. What can he do to me?"

"Easy for you to say, you're Deity Transformation cultivator with numerous avatars. As a Martial God with only this single body, if I'm targeted, I'm doomed!"

"That's right, we Martial Gods must leave."

"I've planned in this realm for thousands of years, and now to leave empty-handed..."

"Hurry, let's go. It's humiliating to stay here any longer..."

In response, numerous streaks of light left True Spirit Realm. Lin Fei's threat, backed by his feats of slaying Extinction Martial God and destroying Meditation True Person's thirteen hundred avatars, was not something to be taken lightly. Still, some Deity Transformation beings, reluctant to leave, decided to hide their avatars, hoping to seize a share of the world's origin once it appeared.

However, they underestimated the situation. An hour later, Lin Fei deployed his Saber World and began to patrol the entire realm. With the full might of his Saber World, it would take only a few hours to scour the entire Realm of True Spirit.

As the Saber World descended and located some hidden avatars of the Deity Transformation entities, Lin Fei showed no mercy or interest in their explanations. Anyone who violated his decree was eliminated.

Thus, Lin Fei continued his vigilant patrol, executing those who remained. Many Deity Transformation entities regretted their decision, losing numerous avatars. After several hours, True Spirit Realm was cleansed. Many stood outside in the Divine Realm, watching the events unfold. With the powerful beings cleared out, only North Wind True Person, the Heart Demon Martial God, and Lin Fei remained. Especially Lin Fei, patrolling relentlessly, spared no one who dared defy his rule.

As for the native inhabitants of the True Spirit Realm, Lin Fei paid them no mind. Their fate was sealed from the beginning. With the destruction of the True Spirit Realm, all its beings would be annihilated. Such was the natural course of the heavenly dao.

Many worlds, even without any external forces, are destined to collapse and perish one day. External forces merely accelerate this process. Otherwise, the number of small worlds would continue to increase indefinitely, which is impossible. Nothing is truly eternal.

Even the Divine Realm – is it really eternal? That is uncertain. One day, even the Divine Realm might collapse and be destroyed. Those Dao Integration True Immortals, who have merged with the great laws of the Divine Realm, will also fall and perish. In comparison, martial artists have a significant advantage. All their power resides within themselves, including the "Dao" they cultivate.

Lin Fei watched the gradual collapse of the True Spirit Realm, expressionless yet fully aware of the entire realm. His focus was on the world's origin.

As the realm collapsed, the origin, once hidden within the rules of the small world, started to emerge. Streaks of the world's origin began to appear in the void.

With a swift motion, Lin Fei captured a strand of this origin in his hand. This strand quickly merged into his body, slightly increasing his physical strength. It was indeed the world's origin, but the quantity was too little, scattered throughout the void.

Even with Lin Fei's immense strength, the total origin he could gather alone would be minimal. This is where Deity Transformation entities have a distinct advantage, with their numerous avatars and ability to form arrays, allowing them to gather all the world's origins.

"Nine Heavens and Ten Earths, Heavenly Net Formation, collect!" North Wind True Person commanded, and his formation was activated once more. The formation began to gather the world's origin from the void. The entire Realm of True Spirit was covered by the formation.

North Wind's every avatar, not just the battle ones but also countless others comparable to Martial Kings and Nascent Souls, participated. Their sheer number was key in manipulating the formation. This was enough to collect the origin efficiently and effectively.

These avatars, numerous as they were, posed no challenge for North Wind True Person to conjure. Not just hundreds or thousands, but tens or even hundreds of thousands could be easily manifested by him.

Lin Fei looked around and saw the entire Realm of True Spirit enveloped in an invisible net. This unseen web, designed to scour the world's origin from the void, efficiently collected the origin revealed due to the collapse of the realm.

Lin Fei nodded in satisfaction, recognizing the benefits of cooperation. Without it, even with his formidable power, the amount of world origin he could have gathered alone would have been minimal. Once True Spirit Realm completely collapsed, any uncollected origin would have perished along with it — a true waste. With the Deity Transformation entity orchestrating a large formation that covered the entire realm, it became possible to gather all of the world's origins.

Lin Fei's role was equally crucial — his presence served as a deterrent. His task was to intimidate other Martial Gods and Deity Transformation entities, dissuading them from entering the True Spirit Realm to seize the origin. His presence alone was a formidable guard against any invaders. Any who dared to breach the realm in pursuit of the origin would face his wrath, which could easily mean death.

Extinction Martial God's fate served as a stark warning of Lin Fei's ferocity, deterring anyone from challenging the realm. Lin Fei stood as an unyielding guardian, effectively deterring the gods of the Divine Realm with his singular power.
