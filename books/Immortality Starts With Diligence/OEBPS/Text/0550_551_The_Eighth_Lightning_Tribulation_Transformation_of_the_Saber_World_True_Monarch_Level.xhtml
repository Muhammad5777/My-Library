551. The Eighth Lightning Tribulation! Transformation of the Saber World, True Monarch Level!

The sixth heavenly lightning was a black thunderbolt, brimming with a destructive aura. Lin Fei was all too familiar with the sixth heavenly lightning. Having traversed numerous lightning tribulations, he knew precisely what each thunderbolt was like, intimately familiar with them.

Yet, upon witnessing the current sixth heavenly lightning, Lin Fei almost immediately discerned. This was no ordinary thunderbolt. This thunderbolt had undoubtedly reached the True Monarch level!

Anyone below the True Monarch level attempting to withstand this thunderbolt would undoubtedly perish!

Lin Fei was not unfamiliar with True Monarch level heavenly lightnings. However, this time, he didn't choose to resist with his physical body. Instead, he decided to test the transformed Saber World. Had it really reached the True Monarch level? To what extent had its might increased?

"Saber World!"

The next moment, the Saber World descended, instantly enveloping Lin Fei within. Simultaneously, the forty-three World Laws fused into the Saber World in an instant.

"Buzz."

With the integration of these forty-three World Laws, Lin Fei's Saber World immediately underwent a transformation. The walls of the entire Saber World became incredibly stable, almost tangible in nature.

Moreover, Lin Fei could feel that the Saber World could expand over a vast range. But there was no need. Currently, he was undergoing lightning tribulation. The smaller the range of the Saber World, the more concentrated its might, and the stronger the defense!

"Rumble."

Finally, the sixth black heavenly lightning struck down. The might of the True Monarch level was indeed extraordinary. Even other Martial Gods who were not undergoing tribulation felt their hearts palpitate just by glimpsing it. They even felt the destructive aura of the sixth heavenly lightning from afar. Should it strike them, they feared they would be instantly turned to ashes.

"Is the sixth heavenly lightning already at the True Monarch level?"

"The eighth lightning tribulation is truly terrifying."

"The further it goes, the more difficult the tribulation becomes."

"If the sixth lightning is of True Monarch level, what about the seventh, eighth, or even the ninth? How strong could they be?"

"Can True Monarch Lin really survive such a tribulation?"

Some began to doubt whether Lin Fei could make it through. After all, these heavenly lightnings were too terrifying, Far beyond their imagination.

But then, when the sixth black heavenly lightning thunderously struck directly hitting Lin Fei's Saber World, Lin Fei's Saber World endured immense pressure.

Its light was flickering wildly. The Saber World did indeed shatter. The force of the sixth heavenly lightning surged in. But that was all.

When the sixth heavenly lightning struck the Saber World, it was immediately suppressed by the Saber World.

The current Saber World, from the inside out, was of True Monarch level. Once the Saber World fully exerted its suppression, a continuous stream of power bombarded the sixth black heavenly lightning, continuously wearing down its force.

In the blink of an eye, the sixth heavenly lightning was completely dissipated. It didn't even manage to fully break through the Saber World to hit Lin Fei's physical body.

The sixth heavenly lightning was annihilated without even touching Lin Fei's flesh. Instantly, there was a collective gasp of astonishment! Many couldn't believe what they were seeing.

"Is that True Monarch Lin's Saber World?"

"Yes, that's True Monarch Lin's Saber World. He has been seeking other Saber Worlds to merge and enhance his own."

"But who could have thought that the Saber World could be elevated to a level comparable to a True Monarch?"

"With the Saber World's might reaching the True Monarch level, neither the ethereal True Monarchs nor the martial True Monarchs hold any advantage against True Monarch Lin now."

"The Saber World can match a True Monarch?"

Particularly some Martial Kings were somewhat stunned at this moment. Some of them had even been attempting to evolve their own Saber Worlds. But the limit of Saber World was known to be the Martial King level.

Yet, Lin Fei shattered their understanding, breaking through this limit, and elevating the Saber World to the True Monarch level!

This was almost unprecedented, possibly unparalleled in the future! The sixth heavenly lightning was blocked by Lin Fei's Saber World.

A flicker of sharpness passed through Lin Fei's eyes. He wasn't surprised by this outcome. He had known that the Saber World could be elevated to the True Monarch level.

But the extent of the Saber World's enhancement was beyond his expectations. This was not just an ordinary True Monarch level power. Even while withstanding the sixth heavenly lightning, it seemed effortless.

"This Saber World now can easily suppress a typical True Monarch!" Lin Fei muttered softly. Suppressing a typical martial True Monarch was not an issue. Let alone the battle avatars of the Void Transformation True Monarchs. He could suppress as many as came his way, easily overpowering them.

Lin Fei didn't stop there.

"Boom."

Quickly, the seventh heavenly lightning was also summoned by Lin Fei. This seventh heavenly lightning was a massive white column of light. It was a Thunder Pillar!

Majestic and roaring downward. This heavenly lightning was incredibly powerful. Even Lin Fei could feel its terrifying pressure. Compared to the sixth heavenly lightning, the might of this seventh heavenly lightning had clearly increased by at least tenfold!

For a typical True Monarch, a tenfold increase. What did that imply? How terrifying would that power be?

"Saber World!"

Lin Fei had already deployed his Saber World. At this juncture, he must treat the situation with utmost seriousness. True Monarch level heavenly lightnings were not simple. Each one demanded his full effort to confront.

"Pfft."

The next moment, the massive column of thunder fiercely struck the Saber World. And the Saber World shattered upon impact. Although the power within the Saber World sustained it for about the duration of a breath...

That was all. After consuming some of the thunder pillar's energy, the Saber World instantly shattered, turning to ashes. The remaining thunder pillar, still with about seventy percent of its power, ferociously bombarded Lin Fei's physical body.

Instantly, every particle of Lin Fei's body trembled. One percent, two percent, three percent, four percent, five percent...

Finally, after being annihilated by fifty percent, Lin Fei's body finally came to a halt. The seventh heavenly lightning, that massive thunder pillar also completely vanished without a trace.

Lin Fei had withstood the seventh heavenly lightning!

Then, the particles in Lin Fei's body rapidly recovered. His face showed no surprise, appearing very calm. The seventh heavenly lightning was strong, but it was within his expectations.

However, the upcoming eighth heavenly lightning required Lin Fei's serious attention. The eighth heavenly lightning was extraordinary.

Lin Fei deployed the Saber World again. The Saber World gradually recovered.

"Whoosh."

The next moment, the eighth heavenly lightning struck. In a flash, it disappeared. Its speed was unimaginably fast, invisible to the naked eye.

"Crackle."

The Saber World couldn't form any effective resistance. It was instantly penetrated. Simultaneously, the eighth heavenly lightning seemed to reach Lin Fei's body in an instant. But Lin Fei didn't remain motionless as before. Facing the eighth heavenly lightning, he moved.

"Particle Worlds!"

Lin Fei roared lowly. Simultaneously, the Particle Worlds within his body erupted thunderously. One thousand, two thousand, three thousand, four thousand...

A total of four thousand four hundred Particle Worlds all exploded forth. The overwhelming power of his physical body caused Lin Fei's stature to swell to tens of thousands of meters tall, standing tall and mighty.

As Lin Fei's body expanded, he threw a punch towards the eighth heavenly lightning.

"Boom."

Instantly, the void trembled violently. The surrounding space shattered into pieces. The might of the four thousand four hundred Particle Worlds was earth-shattering. The eighth heavenly lightning was truly terrifying, far more fearsome than the seventh.

Yet, under Lin Fei's punch, infused with the power of four thousand four hundred Particle Worlds, the eighth heavenly lightning also shattered to pieces, dissipating into nothingness.

Lin Fei, relying on his own physical strength, actually took the initiative to strike, exploding the eighth heavenly lightning with a single punch!

For a moment, the entire Ancient City fell into silence. A quiet so profound one could hear a pin drop!
