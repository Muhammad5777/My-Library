210. Transcendence Saber Momentum Suppress Martial Saints

Lin Fei stood with his hands behind his back. He stood quietly at the foot of the mountain. He didn't move, and neither did the others. Because of the momentum they had created, they were being noticed by the high-ranking members of the Ancient Origin Sect.

Being noticed in itself was a testament to these Martial Saints' power. Perhaps, each had their own special traits and trump cards. Every single one of them was a highly renowned individual. To rise from an ordinary martial artist to become a Martial Saint was undoubtedly extraordinary.

Even so, at this moment, under the suppression of Lin Fei's saber momentum, everyone felt as if they had fallen into a swamp. Their bodies carried the weight of heavy mountains, trembling. Even the true essence within their bodies couldn't flow smoothly. They all stopped in their tracks.

As for trying to force their way up?

Certainly, it could be done. However, it would consume an enormous amount of true essence. If their true essence was exhausted, what would they do when they encountered attacks from other Martial Saints later?

Therefore, for a while, these Martial Saints didn't move. Their eyes were all fixed on Lin Fei. They recognized Lin Fei.

Dao Seeker Lin Fei.

Now, because of the hype created by the eight major families, his name was known far and wide. Only, they hadn't expected Dao Seeker Lin Fei was actually this terrifying. He had comprehended saber momentum!

"Saber momentum, this is saber momentum!"

"Even if it's saber momentum, it won't trap me. Break!"

"Ah... Break for me!"

Among these Martial Saints, there were still some fierce characters. For example, Lu Xuan, Ying Wuming, etc. Their true essence was indeed powerful. They tried to break free from Lin Fei's saber momentum. If it were before, when he had only mastered saber momentum, Lin Fei's saber momentum might not have been able to suppress these Martial Saints.

But now, it was different. The transcendence of saber momentum wasn't a doubling or tripling of power. But a tenfold, dozens of times increase! Therefore, even if these Martial Saints tried their best to struggle, their efforts were in vain.

As for the disciples at the foot of the mountain, they were even more terrified. Looking at Lin Fei, their eyes were a hint of fear and dread.

Saber momentum, evolved from the saber intent. Not only could it interfere with reality, but it could also deter the spirit! For these acquired martial artists, being suppressed by the saber momentum, even if Lin Fei's saber momentum didn't intentionally suppress them, just a little bit of the leaked power was enough to make them feel incredibly shocked and terrified.

However, at the foot of the mountain, there was one exception. That was the Martial Saint of the Ancient Origin Sect.

Originally, this Martial Saint was merely "supervising." Or, watching the excitement from the side. He was high above, detached from the world. But now, he was also suppressed by Lin Fei's saber momentum. Although there was a hint of surprise on his face, he seemed to have no fear or panic.

"Saber momentum, he actually comprehended saber momentum. Interesting. It seems the Dao Seeker truly lives up to his reputation. How could someone who managed to cross the vast sea from the other end be ordinary? But, you have saber momentum, I have fist momentum!"

At this time, the Martial Saint of the Ancient Origin Sect, a fierce light flashed in his eyes!

Perhaps, many people didn't understand his identity. He was a true disciple! In the Ancient Origin Sect, his status was higher than that of the elders.

Each direct disciple, in the outside world, could become the overlord of a region. All were terrifying Martial Saints standing tall and upright. And he was among the best of them. This time, he presided over the initiation ceremony out of interest. Unexpectedly, he encountered such an "interesting" Martial Saint like Lin Fei. As a dignified direct disciple, how could he be suppressed by Lin Fei's saber momentum?

"Boom."

The Martial Saint of the Ancient Origin Sect, originally bent slightly by Lin Fei's saber momentum, now, bit by bit, slowly straightened up. His body became upright again.

At the same time, a circle of grey light radiated from the body of the Ancient Origin Sect's Martial Saint. One circle after another, exuding an overbearing, fierce, utterly rigid, and masculine aura.

Everyone seemed to "see" an incomparable fist as if it spanned across the void. This fist seemed to be able to destroy everything, crush everything!

Fist momentum!

No one expected that this Martial Saint of the Ancient Origin Sect had actually comprehended fist momentum too!

"Fist momentum... I remember now, he is the true disciple of the Ancient Origin Sect, the Fist Saint Gu Meng!"

"What, he is Fist Saint Gu Meng? The Fist Saint Gu Meng who killed three top Martial Saints with one punch, shocking the entire Tianqi Nation?"

"Fist Saint Gu Meng, the true disciple of the Ancient Origin Sect, is actually presiding over the ceremony."

"What a terrifying fist momentum, is this the true disciple of the Ancient Origin Sect? It's simply incredible..."

"Why do I feel like Fist Saint Gu Meng's fist momentum seems a bit inferior to Dao Seeker Lin Fei's saber momentum?"

For a moment, the hearts of many people at the foot of the mountain were shocked.

A true disciple of the Ancient Origin Sect. Fist Saint Gu Meng.

The combination of these two titles made everyone look at Gu Meng with newfound awe. Even Lin Fei's gaze at Gu Meng's fist momentum held a touch of surprise. But, that was all. If he didn't move, then no one had the right to move under his saber momentum!

"Suppress!"

Lin Fei just spat out a single word. Suddenly, it seemed as if countless strands of saber intent in the void, covering the sky and the earth, pressuring towards the numerous Martial Saints.

"Thud!"

Some of the Martial Saints flew into the void, preparing to walk on air, rushing to the top of the mountain at the fastest speed, but they were directly pressed by Lin Fei's saber momentum, causing them to fall to the ground. Even after falling to the ground, they had to desperately seek support from the ground with both hands, resisting the pressure of the saber momentum.

As for Fist Saint Gu Meng, originally, he detached from the world. Even when Lin Fei displayed his saber momentum, he didn't take it seriously. He felt that once his fist momentum came out, he would be able to easily break free from Lin Fei's saber momentum.

However, the expression on Gu Meng's face had become rigid. Even the smile on his face was gradually disappearing. Because he found that when his fist momentum erupted, the circles of grey light were actually being compressed back.

Yes, bit by bit, they were compressed back. Whether it's fist momentum or saber momentum. The smaller the range, the stronger the power. Now being suppressed back could only mean one thing. His fist momentum was inferior to Lin Fei's saber momentum!

Moreover, Gu Meng himself felt even with the protection of fist momentum, under the cover of Lin Fei's saber momentum, he still felt as if he was surrounded by a mountain of sabers. Countless sabers were ready to fall at any time, cutting him into pieces!

The competition between saber momentum and fist momentum didn't tolerate any deception.

Strength is strength. Weakness is weakness.

Clearly, Gu Meng's fist momentum was inferior to Lin Fei's saber momentum. Even, far inferior! The two were not even on the same level.

"Saber momentum... Transcendence!"

Gu Meng uttered each word slowly. At this moment, his expression had become extremely solemn. He finally understood Lin Fei's saber momentum had reached transcendence.

And him?

Fist momentum at the Mastery level! Even among the true disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect, Fist momentum at the Mastery level could be considered an outstanding achievement. That's why he could always appear so relaxed.

But, Lin Fei's was transcendence-level saber momentum. Only at this time did Gu Meng truly understand what "Dao Seeker" really meant. Why every Dao Seeker ultimately achieved no small feat. Just based on Lin Fei's understanding of the transcendence-level saber momentum, he could make the entire Ancient Origin Sect take him seriously!

Thinking of this, Gu Meng withdrew his fist momentum.

He said to Lin Fei, "Lin Fei, withdraw your saber momentum. Your companion has reached the top of the mountain. As for you, there's no need to compete with them for the quota. It's their misfortune to compete with you for the quota. With you here, they can't even move. Won't they be wiped out by then? You can directly enter the Ancient Origin Sect and become an outer elder!"

The other Martial Saints were also all taken aback. Their eyes were filled with shock!

Lin Fei was going to directly enter the Ancient Origin Sect. He didn't even have to take the examination!

Many Martial Saints, who were proud and arrogant, thought they were not inferior to anyone. But they were easily suppressed by this "Dao Seeker" alone. Hundreds of Martial Saints, not one could move. Their pride was shattered by Lin Fei.

This was a Dao Seeker who truly lived up to his reputation!
