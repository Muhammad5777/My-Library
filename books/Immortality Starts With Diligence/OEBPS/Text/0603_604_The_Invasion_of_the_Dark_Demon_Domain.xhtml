604. The Invasion of the Dark Demon Domain!

"Boom."

The Divine Realm trembled violently. Ordinarily, it would be difficult for a Supreme to break into such a powerful domain from the outside. It might even be impossible.

But the Dark Demon Lord was different. He had special means. After all, he was the Dark Demon Lord, the ruler of an entire domain! He almost completely controlled his domain.

Therefore, he had a profound understanding of domains. The countless black tendrils did more than form a gigantic hand. They transformed into numerous sharp forces, furiously stabbing at a single point on the Divine Realm.

Once, twice, thrice, four times, five times...

Countless times!

After numerous strikes, the Divine Realm could no longer withstand it.

"Crack."

Finally, a small crack appeared in the Divine Realm. A single crack was naturally insufficient for the beings of the Dark Demon Domain to enter. However, with this crack, the black mist controlled by the Dark Demon Lord behaved like a living entity, quickly seeping into the Divine Realm.

The mist began to corrode the crack within the Divine Realm continuously. Gradually, the small crack expanded, forming a large portal.

"Enter, slaughter, and destroy everything!"

With the Dark Demon Lord's command, countless dark demons flew out of the chaotic vessel, rushing into the Divine Realm.

...

In Ancient City, Lin Fei had been closely monitoring the situation regarding the "Dark Demon Domain" invasion. He had been seeking information within the Great Dream City about the invasion.

However, until now, there had been no information about the Dark Demon Domain. It seemed that the Supremes were deliberately blocking this news.

In reality, the best way to deal with the invasion would be to spread the news, informing all the powerhouses of the Divine Realm. But the Supremes had their considerations.

Perhaps they felt it wasn't the right time. The invasion hadn't fully commenced, and announcing it prematurely might cause chaos. Therefore, the Supremes kept the information sealed.

"Lin Fei, come to Great Dream City immediately!"

Suddenly, a familiar voice echoed in Lin Fei's ears. It was his master, the Great Dream Ancestor.

Focusing on his mind, Lin Fei immediately entered the Great Dream City.

"Hmm?"

As soon as Lin Fei entered Great Dream City, he noticed that it bore some similarities to his Illusory Realm. However, there were also differences. For instance, he wasn't sure if Great Dream City could be used to attack enemies.

At this moment, Great Dream City was empty, which struck Lin Fei as unusual. It was the first time he had seen it devoid of people. He quickly made his way to the main hall of Great Dream City.

Inside the hall, many powerful figures were gathered. It turned out that everyone in Great Dream City had assembled here. The Great Dream Ancestor sat in his high seat, and beside him was an empty seat reserved for Lin Fei.

"Greetings, Martial Ancestor Lin!"

Upon Lin Fei's arrival, many respectfully bowed to him. Lin Fei was now a Martial Ancestor and practically a symbol of the Great Dream Sect. Although the Great Dream Sect was founded by the Great Dream Ancestor, he had been reclusive, and many in the sect had never met him, leading to a lack of his presence even within his own sect.

Lin Fei, on the other hand, had once been the number one Martial God in the Divine Realm. He had endured nine heavenly thunders, ultimately ascending to Martial Ancestor status. His achievements had become legendary throughout the Divine Realm, and his deeds were widely known.

"Lin Fei, come, take your seat," the Great Dream Ancestor said, pointing to the chair next to him, clearly reserved for Lin Fei. After all, the only one who could sit beside the Great Dream Ancestor as an equal was Lin Fei, a fellow Martial Ancestor.

"Whoosh."

Without hesitation, Lin Fei stepped forward and took his seat next to the Great Dream Ancestor.

"Everyone, do you know why I have summoned you here?" the Great Dream Ancestor asked slowly.

The crowd shook their heads, clearly puzzled. The Great Dream Ancestor rarely showed himself in Great Dream City, and many of the Martial Ancestor and True Monarchs had never seen him before today. His sudden summoning of nearly all the Martial Gods and True Monarchs from the Great Dream Sect indicated something significant had occurred.

Lin Fei had a hunch it was related to the Dark Demon Domain's invasion, but he remained silent, waiting patiently.

As expected, the Great Dream Ancestor continued, "The reason I have gathered you all here is due to a matter concerning the very survival of the Divine Realm. This issue must be discussed with all of you. Lin Fei is aware of the situation.

Beyond our Divine Realm lies the vast chaos. Within this chaos, there are countless domains, and the Divine Realm is just one among many. Some of these domains' inhabitants continuously invade other domains, occupying them, exterminating their life forms, or turning them into slaves." Currently, our Divine Realm faces such a threat. A domain known as the Dark Demon Domain has begun its invasion of the Divine Realm..."

The Great Dream Ancestor held nothing back, explaining the invasion of the Dark Demon Domain to the assembled Martial Gods and True Monarchs of the Great Dream Sect.

The crowd was in an uproar, deeply shocked.

"What? There are other domains beyond our Divine Realm? And there are countless domains similar to the Divine Realm within the chaos?"

"And now, a domain from the chaos, the Dark Demon Domain, is invading the Divine Realm? So, we need to gather all our strength to resist."

"How strong is the Dark Demon Domain?"

"Even the Ancestor has personally summoned us; this Dark Demon Domain must be extraordinary."

"Perhaps the calamity of the Divine Realm has arrived..."

For a moment, many were discussing among themselves. They had only now realized the existence of so many secrets beyond the Divine Realm. They would have remained ignorant had the Dark Demon Domain not invaded. This revelation felt like a new door opening for them, filling them with awe.

Lin Fei glanced at his master, the Great Dream Ancestor.

Indeed, the Great Dream Ancestor nodded before continuing, "Now I am notifying everyone in the Great Dream Sect that all Martial Gods and Deity Transformations and above must obey the orders of the Great Dream Sect. You are to go to the front lines and resist the invasion of the Dark Demon Domain! This is a command, not a discussion. Anyone who attempts to escape or refuses will be executed without mercy!"

The Great Dream Ancestor spoke coldly, leaving no room for leniency. This was an order, indicating the severity of the current situation, likely far worse than Lin Fei had imagined.

"Ancestor, has the Dark Demon Domain already invaded the Divine Realm?"

One of the Martial Gods couldn't help but ask. The Great Dream Ancestor's sudden sternness suggested a dire situation.

Everyone looked at the Great Dream Ancestor, their eyes filled with confusion.
