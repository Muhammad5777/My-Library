611. The Birth of the Laws of Life

"I'll go," declared a dark demon clad entirely in black, his figure adorned with a pair of wings, as he stepped forward.

"Hmm? With Nightmare Great Emperor going, it should be foolproof," someone remarked upon seeing him.

Among the Demon Emperors, distinctions were clear. A so-called Great Emperor among them could nearly be deemed invincible. Such a Great Emperor could overpower other Demon Emperors but still was not a Supreme. His title was purely a testament to his formidable strength.

In the Dark Demon Domain, those crowned with the title of "Great Emperor" were exceedingly rare, numbering fewer than ten. And Nightmare Great Emperor was among them!

"However, Nightmare, you must also be cautious that Lin Fei is very cunning and strong. Should you encounter him, kill him at all costs! Lin Fei is a Martial Ancestor who can be slain. Once killed, Lin Fei will be gone forever, and there will be no more trouble from him—an end to all troubles!"

Nightmare Emperor nodded, his voice icy, "When I strike, Lin Fei will not survive."

"Whoosh."

In the next instant, Nightmare Emperor's figure vanished without a trace.

"Now, with Nightmare Emperor personally dealing with Lin Fei, we must still face the direct battlefield of the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain, only by utterly breaking the resistance of the Divine Realm will it submit and be occupied and ruled by our Dark Demon Domain."

The Demon Emperors began discussing plans to destroy all the frontal resistance in the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain.

...

In the serene void, a figure suddenly appeared.

Lin Fei quietly gazed at a group of dark demons in the distance. This time, they were his targets.

In Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, the essence of the dark demons' flesh and blood had already stimulated the gradual emergence of life.

Just a bit more was needed. Once life was born, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm would be transformed and become complete.

At that point, Lin Fei's strength and realm would leap qualitatively. He might ascend from an ordinary Martial Ancestor to a top-tier one!

"Slaying these dark demons should be enough for my Eternal Realm to birth life this time."

Lin Fei's gaze was exceptionally cold, looking at the group of dark demons.

To many in the Divine Realm, these dark demons were fearsome and monstrous, but in Lin Fei's eyes, they seemed merely like endless energy.

It was the nourishment for the life within his Eternal Realm.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei stepped forward. In the blink of an eye, he was among the group of dark demons.

"Eternal Realm!"

The next moment, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm began to expand instantly, enveloping the group of dark demons within it. Among these dark demons, there were three Demon Emperors. Upon seeing Lin Fei, the trio showed faces of terror, shouting, "It's Lin Fei, run!"

By now, Lin Fei's reputation in the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain was formidable, particularly to the dark demons—to them, he was akin to the Yama Raja.

An encounter with Lin Fei meant certain death. Nearly all were decimated; none could escape. Many dark demon armies had been reduced to dust under Lin Fei's onslaught.

"Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh."

One by one, the dark demons frantically scattered in all directions. Upon encountering Lin Fei, these dark demons completely lost their will to fight, which also indirectly showed that even these creatures feared death.

Although they possessed dark hearts capable of rebirth, they were loath to lose their current bodies. Clearly, these bodies were crucial to the dark demons and not merely dispensable—each was exceedingly precious, and none wished to lose their physical form under such circumstances, for it was entirely pointless.

Within the Eternal Realm, the dark demons were crushed in droves, never even coming close to touching Lin Fei. Their deaths were utterly suffocating.

"Annihilate!" Lin Fei extended a palm and pressed it towards the void.

"Puchi."

Suddenly, the void fell completely silent. All around, everything was eerily quiet, for there was no longer any movement from the dark demons. All had been slain by Lin Fei, turned into dust, forever remaining within the Eternal Realm.

As all the essence of flesh and blood stayed within the Eternal Realm, it transformed.

Lin Fei had always been sensitive to it. The Eternal Realm seemed to be nurturing life, yet life had never been born—until now.

Lin Fei is the Eternal Realm, and the Eternal Realm is Lin Fei.

Thus, he was acutely aware of every change within it. He distinctly felt that suddenly there was a new law in the Eternal Realm—a new law that exists in other worlds: the law of life!

With the emergence of the law of life, the Eternal Realm was filled with the essence of life.

Lin Fei began to sense what seemed like some floating life forms being born, invisible to the naked eye. These life forms continued to develop, eventually growing into plants such as flowers, grass, trees, and more.

Moreover, the entire Eternal Realm was undergoing rapid changes. With the presence of life, Lin Fei grew stronger by the moment. The more life there was, the more powerful Lin Fei became!

Just then, a long-absent voice echoed in Lin Fei's mind from the system.

"Your Eternal Realm has birthed life."

"Your Eternal Realm's progress has increased by 10%."

Lin Fei was somewhat surprised. The voice of the system had not appeared for a long time, and now, it had made a reappearance.

"System."

Without hesitation, Lin Fei immediately accessed the system to check his status.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Martial Ancestor

World Level: Minor World (Progress 10%)

Illusory Realm: True Immortal Level

Lin Fei noted that his world level was still a Minor World. However, the progress behind the Minor World had reached 10%.

This was extraordinary. Previously, the progress behind Lin Fei's Minor World was always 0. Now it had become 10%, clearly indicating that Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was on the right track, becoming stronger. And the reason for this change was undoubted.

Life! Life had been born in the Eternal Realm! Thus, there was progress in Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

"The birth of life can make a world complete. Only a complete world can possess unimaginable power."

Lin Fei extended his hand and clenched it gently. He felt the power. A power unprecedented. Moreover, this power seemed almost infinite.

Indeed, every moment, Lin Fei could feel the enhancement of his strength.

After all, every moment, life was increasing within Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. The more life there was, the stronger Lin Fei's Eternal Realm naturally became.

As for how strong Lin Fei was now, even he was not clear. Moreover, the potential of his Eternal Realm was incredibly vast.

Now, it was merely some plants. Even then, plants appeared only in a few places. What if animals were born later? Or even intelligent life? What if those intelligent beings began their own way of cultivation? How strong would Lin Fei's Eternal Realm be then?

Lin Fei could hardly imagine it. Even more, Lin Fei faintly felt that he was on a way quite different from other Martial Ancestors and True Immortals. It seemed that Lin Fei had not embarked on the path to Supreme status. But he was becoming even more formidable than a Supreme!

"Hmm? Who's there? Come out."

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. He turned around, his gaze piercing through the void.

"Buzz."

Ripples of space emanated in the void. Following that, a massive figure, entirely cloaked in black, appeared in the void. Its cold gaze fixed directly on Lin Fei.
