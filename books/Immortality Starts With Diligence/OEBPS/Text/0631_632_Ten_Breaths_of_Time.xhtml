632. Ten Breaths of Time!

Despite sensing the imminent danger, Lin Fei could neither flee nor escape. His aura was locked, and he was directly in the presence of the Dark Demon Lord.

Lin Fei had previously stood undefeated before Supremes due to his Illusory Realm's ability to immobilize them.

However, his Illusory Realm could not affect the Dark Demon Lord, stripping Lin Fei of his advantage and forcing him to confront the Dark Demon Lord head-on.

"Boom."

Finally, the Dark Demon Lord's palm struck down. Yet, before it could reach Lin Fei, a familiar figure appeared in front of him—Black Tortoise Supreme!

Black Tortoise Supreme had always been at Lin Fei's side, albeit often unnoticed. Now, his presence became profoundly significant.

As Lin Fei faced danger, Black Tortoise Supreme finally intervened, forcefully blocking the Dark Demon Lord's attack.

"Dark Demon Lord, a supreme ruler attacking a mere Martial Ancestor?" Black Tortoise Supreme sneered coldly, his eyes filled with disdain as he stared at the Dark Demon Lord. "How disgraceful it is to bully the weak!"

The Dark Demon Lord's expression turned ugly. He was well aware of Black Tortoise Supreme's capabilities, whose skills were almost entirely defensive.

With Black Tortoise Supreme present, it was exceedingly difficult for the Dark Demon Lord to kill Lin Fei. Even several Supremes together would struggle to breach Black Tortoise Supreme's defenses.

Although the Dark Demon Lord was a ruler, this was not the Dark Demon Domain. Here in the Divine Realm, he was only marginally stronger than an ordinary Supreme; there was no fundamental difference.

Perhaps the Dark Demon Lord had some ultimate moves or powerful treasures at his disposal, but those were dependent on specific conditions.

Now facing Black Tortoise Supreme, the Dark Demon Lord found himself at a loss. At least in the short term, he had no way to counter Black Tortoise Supreme. However, over a longer period, the situation might not remain the same.

Moreover, although Lin Fei's Illusory Realm could immobilize many Dark Demon Supremes, since it did not affect the Dark Demon Lord, he could disrupt the efforts of the Divine Realm's Supremes. They were unable to kill any Dark Demon Supremes.

This meant that both parties were essentially in a deadlock. They were in a standoff, and neither side was able to overcome the other.

However, the Dark Demon Domain held the advantage. Lin Fei's Illusory Realm could only immobilize for a few breaths. Once that time passed, the other Dark Demon Supremes would regain consciousness.

Then, the eight Supremes of the Divine Realm would face the dozens of Dark Demon Supremes—a perilously precarious situation!

But even in this scenario, escape was but a wishful thought.

"Hmph, even if I don't kill you now," the Dark Demon Lord said coldly, "how long can you hold out? In a few more breaths, they will awaken, and you won't be able to escape! At least, he cannot escape!"

While the other Supremes might be able to flee, the Dark Demon Lord was confident in his ability to prevent Lin Fei from escaping.

As long as Black Tortoise Supreme attempted to take Lin Fei with him, the Dark Demon Lord could intervene. Stopping one person alone was still within the Dark Demon Lord's capabilities.

One breath, two breaths, three breaths!

A full three breaths of time passed. The Dark Demon Supremes, immobilized by Lin Fei's Illusory Realm, began to show signs of instability, subtly struggling against the constraints, causing the faces of many Supremes to change slightly.

Particularly for Black Tortoise Supreme, his expression turned anxious as he asked Azure Emperor, "Azure Emperor, what should we do now? We might be able to escape, but Lin Fei..."

His implication was clear. They could escape, but Lin Fei could not. He was targeted by the Dark Demon Lord, who would allow other Supremes to leave but never Lin Fei.

Azure Emperor's brow furrowed slightly.

"It seems our mission has failed," he admitted. "We thought we could use Lin Fei's Illusory Realm to immobilize the Dark Demon Supremes for a while, then use that time to kill some of them to boost the morale of the Divine Realm and severely damage the Dark Demon Domain.

However, the Illusory Realm cannot hold the Dark Demon Lord, leading our plan to fall apart. We are even trapped in a perilous situation now. But no matter what, we cannot abandon Lin Fei. Even if we all have to stay here, we must ensure that Lin Fei escapes! His importance to us is unquestionable, even more so than our own lives as Supremes."

"Now we go to confront the Dark Demon Lord. We must let Lin Fei escape at all costs!" Azure Emperor then said to Lin Fei, "The Dark Demon Lord has been watching you constantly. If you want to escape, it will require all eight of us to hold off the Dark Demon Lord."

"You need to leave. At any cost! Withdraw your Illusory Realm. Once you do, you won't have much time to escape. But no matter what, all eight of us will shield you from all threats. Only if you escape can the rest of us Supremes manage. They can't do much to us, and we can always escape at any moment."

Lin Fei looked up at Azure Emperor, understanding the gravity of the situation. Azure Emperor made it sound simple, but escaping? That might not be so easy. Facing dozens of Dark Demon Supremes and the Dark Demon Lord himself, even a Supreme would find it difficult to leave.

Perhaps some Supremes might manage to escape, but likely at the cost of leaving half of them behind. Once a Supreme dies, that's a true fall—a cost too great to bear.

Yet, Lin Fei remained silent. He knew clearly—if he didn't leave, he would continue to be targeted by the Dark Demon Lord. If his Illusory Realm could no longer hold the other Supremes, Lin Fei would be in grave danger.

Strength! It was all a matter of insufficient strength. If only his power were greater, would such dire choices even be necessary?

Inside Lin Fei, the Eternal Realm continued its profound transformation, reshaping everything within.

"Ten breaths!" Lin Fei suddenly raised his head, looking determinedly at Azure Emperor. With each word heavy with resolve, he declared, "Give me just ten more breaths of time! Maybe then we can turn the tide!"

His words slightly stunned Black Tortoise Supreme, who hurriedly interjected, "Lin Fei, what can you possibly do in just ten breaths? You need to leave now—that's what's most important!"

Lin Fei didn't elaborate further, but his gaze remained intensely fixed on Azure Emperor. After a long moment of contemplation, the Azure Emperor seemed to reach a decision.

Speaking slowly but with determination, he affirmed, "Alright, we will secure you ten breaths of time! No one will disturb you, nor will anyone threaten you during these breaths. If the situation does not improve after ten breaths, we will risk everything to ensure your safety and see you out!"

With his words firm and resolute, Azure Emperor did not hesitate. Turning, he charged toward the Dark Demon Lord, choosing to trust in Lin Fei's request.

The remaining seven Supremes exchanged glances, their decision unanimous. They too would place their trust in Lin Fei—or rather, in Azure Emperor's judgment.

"Refusing to flee?" The Dark Demon Lord mocked as he observed their actions. "Very well, it seems you are courting death. If that is the case, I shall oblige you!" His annoyance flaring into greater fury, his intent to kill intensified.

"Boom!"

The Dark Demon Lord met the challenge head-on. At the same time, the dozens of Dark Demon Supremes previously immobilized by Lin Fei's Illusory Realm gradually came to their senses. In an instant, a terrifying oppressive force filled the void, nearly sealing off every corner of space.

Lin Fei, along with the eight Supremes, immediately felt the immense pressure bearing down on them.
