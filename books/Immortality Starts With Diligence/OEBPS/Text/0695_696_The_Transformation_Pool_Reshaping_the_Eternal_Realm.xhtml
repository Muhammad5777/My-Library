696. The Transformation Pool, Reshaping the Eternal Realm!

Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme harbored trepidations. The Forbidden One was shrouded in mystery, as was the Transformation Pool. However, their options seemed limited. They could either leave now or enter the Transformation Pool, knowing well that departure might preclude any possibility of return.

"Regardless, transforming into a Body of Chaos was my purpose in coming to the Forbidden Tower," Lin Fei declared resolutely. "Thus, I must enter the Transformation Pool and test its waters."

After a moment, Doomsday Supreme spoke, his decision firm. "I must enter the Transformation Pool."

He looked towards Lin Fei, who inhaled deeply and said thoughtfully, "Have you noticed that even just an apparition of the Forbidden One could easily annihilate us? How could such a supreme being bother to set traps or schemes? To the Forbidden One, we are as insignificant as dust. Even becoming a Master of Chaos would hardly catch the Forbidden One's eye. This Transformation Pool is an opportunity I cannot miss."

Lin Fei had analyzed the situation thoroughly. In the eyes of the Forbidden One, he and Doomsday Supreme were inconsequential. There was no reason for subterfuge.

"Alright, let us enter the Transformation Pool together and see if we can both successfully transform into Bodies of Chaos," said Doomsday Supreme, his eagerness palpable.

"Plunge."

Together, Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme jumped into the chaotic waters of the pool. Immediately upon entry, Lin Fei's body stiffened, a sensation of immense danger washing over him. Yet, he felt no tangible threat.

"Could it be this Chaos Qi?" Lin Fei wondered, looking around him.

Chaos Qi swirled densely, permeating everything, and mingled within it was a novel force that easily breached the defenses of both Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme's bodies. It penetrated directly into the particles of their flesh.

Despite their status as Supremes, their physical forms were almost negligible against the power within the Transformation Pool.

"The energy is terrifying," gasped Doomsday Supreme, his eyes widening as a grimace of pain twisted his features. "I can't withstand it anymore; my particles are being eroded..."

Despite his stature as an Invincible Supreme, renowned for a physical form tempered and formidable beyond measure, the Chaos Qi within the Transformation Pool now eroded him. His physical form was in complete collapse.

Through Lin Fei's observant gaze, the plight of Doomsday Supreme was clear. His body had disintegrated completely, undergoing what seemed an utter and irreversible dissolution, reconfiguring into something new.

"Disintegration, reconstitution...," Lin Fei murmured to himself. "So, transforming into a Body of Chaos is truly a profound metamorphosis. In a sense, it's a transition from a Supreme to a Master of Chaos..."

Both Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme understood then that within the Transformation Pool, they needed to let their physical forms undergo this transformation completely.

Lin Fei was faring slightly better. Unlike Doomsday Supreme, his resistance wasn't solely physical; he was also fortified by his Eternal Realm. The Chaos Qi within the pool was formidable, overwhelming his physical defenses, yet he had not begun his transformation. The reason was simple: his Eternal Realm was holding back the invasive Chaos Qi.

As long as Lin Fei wished, his Eternal Realm could sustain him for a prolonged period, perhaps indefinitely. This showed that Lin Fei's strength far surpassed that of Doomsday Supreme. However, resistance was futile and unnecessary; Lin Fei didn't need to use his Eternal Realm to fend off the Chaos Qi. His goal was to transform into a Body of Chaos. Thus, he had to willingly accept the erosion of Chaos Qi upon his body and his Eternal Realm.

"Let it begin..." Lin Fei resolved, no longer hesitating.

He released the defenses of his Eternal Realm.

"Boom."

Instantly, a torrent of Chaos Qi surged into Lin Fei's body, penetrating the particles of his physical form, which was in essence his Eternal Realm. As the Chaos Qi pervaded, it began its relentless erosion.

Lin Fei shuddered as the Chaos Qi assailed him, delivering a formidable shock to his Eternal Realm. Simultaneously, the particles of his physical form began to disintegrate into disarray.

Lin Fei's consciousness delved into the Eternal Realm, which itself was akin to a True Immortal-level world, teeming with various life forms, notably the dominant Divine Demon race.

At that moment, it seemed as though the sky within this realm had torn open, a colossal rift allowing streams of strange power to surge in. This force reshaped everything it touched.

The void, matter, and even life itself began to reconstitute—transforming irrevocably. Even the mighty Divine Demons crumbled to dust, utterly powerless against this transformative force.

"What is happening?" the cries rang out. "Is this the end of the world? Will everything be destroyed?"

"How can this happen? Have we, the Divine Demon race, been forsaken by the Creator?"

The sages of the Divine Demon race trembled as they gazed into the void, recalling the lore passed down through generations. They were creations of a supreme Creator, a great being who controlled their entire world. But now, they faced what seemed like an apocalypse.

No matter how fervently they prayed or called upon their Creator, nothing could stem the tide of chaos enveloping the sky. The world itself began to collapse.

Watching this unfold, Lin Fei's expression grew somber. He had not anticipated that the invasion of Chaos Qi would wreak such devastating effects on his Eternal Realm. Could a collapsing Eternal Realm genuinely be reshaped successfully?

Lin Fei was uncertain. His challenge was not merely about transforming into a Body of Chaos; it was about reconstructing the entire Eternal Realm, which required an immense amount of Chaos Qi.

While Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme were both submerged in the Transformation Pool, absorbing Chaos Qi, the scale of Lin Fei's absorption was unimaginably vast. He absorbed Chaos Qi at a rate more than tenfold that of Doomsday Supreme. If Doomsday Supreme absorbed a unit of Chaos Qi, Lin Fei was drawing in ten, even dozens of units. The disparity was enormous.

The more Chaos Qi one absorbed, the more pronounced the effects. However, there was a limit to how much Doomsday Supreme could absorb; he could only focus on transforming his physical body.

As time passed—one day, two days, three days...

"Crack."

Every particle of Doomsday Supreme was undergoing a frenzied transformation, while Lin Fei's Eternal Realm completely collapsed under the immense influx of Chaos Qi. At that moment, Lin Fei's consciousness felt adrift, seemingly attached to the crumbling Eternal Realm.

"Wait, has the Eternal Realm not completely shattered?" Lin Fei suddenly realized. His Eternal Realm hadn't broken apart; it still maintained its framework, but all life within it had been extinguished. The rules of the Eternal Realm, its void—all were obliterated, replaced by vast quantities of Chaos Qi and the unique power contained within it.

"Buzz."

Time was indeterminate, and then Lin Fei jolted, abruptly regaining full awareness. He saw that the Eternal Realm had reformed, yet it was beginning to give birth to new forms of life, completely unlike any he had known before.

"These beings... chaos beings?" Lin Fei trembled, a realization dawning upon him. His eyes widened, his gaze filled with incredulity.
