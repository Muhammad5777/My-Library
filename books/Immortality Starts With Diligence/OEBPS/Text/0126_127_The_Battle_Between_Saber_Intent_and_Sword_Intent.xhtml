127. The Battle Between Saber Intent and Sword Intent

Lu Zongyi felt as if he had received enlightenment in his mind. It was as if he had grasped a ray of inspiration.

"What a concept! Having no saber is better than having a saber. No saber in hand, but a saber in the heart. No wonder you are the Immortal Slayer Saber, even though you have just reached the innate level, but in terms of the saber realm, you have already reached the peak..."

Lu Zongyi's emotions surged. For a long time, his sword realm had not made any progress. And now, Lin Fei's few words seemed to have suddenly enlightened Lu Zongyi. Flashes of inspiration kept appearing in his mind.

"Brother Lin, please accept my salute!"

Lu Zongyi was obviously a genuine gentleman, or perhaps, a sword fanatic. He was devoted to the sword all his life, which led him to his current level. Lin Fei's words were worth ten years of hard work!

Lu Zongyi's actions were noticed by others. Especially the martial arts masters nearby. They all looked at Lu Zongyi and Lin Fei with a hint of shock. They didn't know what had gotten into Lu Zongyi, who was actually saluting Lin Fei.

"Whoosh."

The next moment, Lu Zongyi jumped onto one of the empty arenas. Then, he shouted to Lin Fei: "Hidden Sword Sect's Lu Zongyi, specially seeks advice from 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei. I hope Brother Lin can come on stage and exchange pointers with me!"

Lu Zongyi's words left everyone somewhat dumbfounded. It was well known that the arena was for the disciples of various sects to exchange ideas. Why did Lu Zongyi take the stage?

After all, Lu Zongyi was the Grand Elder of the Hidden Sword Sect. He was also a young martial arts master and his swordsmanship was very profound. An innate expert was actually going on stage himself! And most importantly, the one Lu Zongyi was challenging was the "Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei. For a moment, this naturally caused a sensation.

"What is Lu Zongyi doing?"

"How can a respectable innate expert, martial arts master, and the Grand Elder of the Hidden Sword Sect act so recklessly?"

"Although the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference does not forbid martial artists from exchanging ideas, isn't a martial arts master being too impatient?"

"The disciples haven't even finished their matches, what's the hurry for Lu Zongyi?"

"However, I'm looking forward to the battle between Lu Zongyi and Lin Fei."

"Let's see whose swordsmanship is more powerful, Lu Zongyi's or the 'Immortal Slayer Saber's saber techniques?"

The other martial arts masters were somewhat dissatisfied, but the disciples' eyes were shining. Their emotions were extremely exciting. A battle between two innate experts! Moreover, it was a contest between a swordsman and a saber wielder.

There is no first in literature, no second in martial arts. It is the same in the martial arts world. Conflicts between saber and sword often occur. Which is more powerful, the saber or the sword? This has been a long-standing debate in the martial arts world. And now, they could witness such an intense and exciting battle. How could these acquired martial artists not be excited and thrilled?

"Challenge me?"

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. He saw the fighting spirit of Lu Zongyi. However, he did not immediately agree. Even if Lu Zongyi was an innate martial artist and a martial arts master, so what? Not everyone was qualified to challenge Lin Fei. Otherwise, wouldn't Lin Fei be troubled by challenges wherever he went? Therefore, Lin Fei remained silent.

Lu Zongyi seemed to guess Lin Fei's thoughts. He took a deep breath. Then, a terrifying aura suddenly erupted from his body.

"Boom."

This aura condensed into a huge sword phantom above Lu Zongyi's head that only innate martial artists could "see" it.

At the same time. around the arena, disciples of various sects and some wandering martial artists, anyone holding a sword in their hand, these swords were trembling slightly. Faintly, it seemed that there was a light humming sound.

"What's going on?"

"Is my sword moving?"

"Sword intent! Lu Zongyi has comprehended sword intent!"

"Is this sword intent? I thought only one master in the Hidden Sword Sect had comprehended sword intent?"

"Hidden Sword Sect has always been mysterious. Letting Lu Zongyi lead the team this time may be to showcase the strength of the Hidden Sword Sect."

"That's right, although the Hidden Sword Sect is named 'Hidden Sword', in fact, the sword should not be hidden. The sword should be exposed, and it should be displayed openly!"

"Hidden Sword Sect, worthy of being a deep-rooted martial arts sect, once gave birth to a Martial Saint. Even though the Sword Saint Bai passed away three hundred years ago, the Hidden Sword Sect still has outstanding talents. Now another master who has comprehended sword intent has emerged!"

"No wonder Lu Zongyi has the confidence to challenge 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei, it turns out he has comprehended sword intent. Lu Zongyi truly has the qualification to fight Lin Fei."

"What kind of sparks will the collision of sword intent and saber intent create? How exciting would it be?"

For a moment, countless people's gazes were on Lin Fei. Now, Lu Zongyi had already challenged Lin Fei. Moreover, he had even displayed his sword intent. Would 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei accept or decline?

"Sword intent..."

A glint of light flashed in Lin Fei's eyes as well. Ever since Lin Fei had comprehended Martial Dao True Intent, he had not encountered a second martial artist who had comprehended Martial Dao True Intent except for his master Zhao Kongming.

But since Zhao Kongming had comprehended sword intent, Lin Fei had never exchanged pointers with him. Therefore, Lin Fei was indeed very interested in other martial artists with sword intent. Martial artists were meant to battle against heaven and earth. Fighting was the instinct of martial artists.

So, Lin Fei spoke, "The Martial Alliance Exchange Conference is for martial artists to exchange ideas with each other. Since Master Lu Zongyi wants to exchange pointers, I will gladly accompany him!"

Lin Fei slowly stood up. However, Lin Fei did not quickly come to the arena. Instead, he took one step at a time. With a very slow and steady pace, he walked over slowly.

With every step Lin Fei took, the momentum on his body became stronger. After arriving at the arena, Lin Fei's momentum finally reached its peak.

"Boom."

Saber intent!

Lin Fei's saber intent erupted completely. Moreover, Lin Fei had honed his saber intent on Ancestral Master Peak. Although it hadn't reached transcendence, the saber intent had indeed become stronger. Therefore, when the saber intent fully erupted, it instantly enveloped the entire exchange meeting in the sky.

"Buzz, buzz, buzz."

As Lin Fei's saber intent erupted, the sabers in those saber-wielding martial artists' hands also began to vibrate intensely. Moreover, the vibrations were very intense. As a result, a strange scene appeared among the many martial artists.

The sounds of swords and sabers hummed one after another. Many martial artists were astonished. In the void, it seemed as if there were two invisible forces constantly clashing with each other.

"Shh, crack."

Many people seemed to feel that Lin Fei's saber intent and Lu Zongyi's sword intent were constantly entwining and colliding. It was as if even the void would be torn apart.

Of course, this was an illusion. Both Lin Fei and Lu Zongyi's saber and sword intent had not yet reached the level of being able to tear apart space. That was simply impossible. But in the perception of many innate powerhouses, the collision of Lin Fei and Lu Zongyi's Martial Dao True Intent made these innate experts feel a strong sense of threat.

Martial Dao True Intent was not only effective against Qi condensing cultivators, but it was also a part of martial arts strength. Whether or not an innate martial artist had Martial Dao True Intent made a huge difference.

"How terrifying saber intent and sword intent. Both Lin Fei and Lu Zongyi are extremely young. I didn't expect them to have comprehended saber intent and sword intent to such an extent."

"'Immortal Slayer Saber' is truly extraordinary. His saber intent is clearly stronger than Lu Zongyi's sword intent."

"Saber intent is indeed stronger for Lin Fei. However, in a martial artist's battle, as long as Martial Dao True Intent is not crushed, the outcome still depends on their martial arts."

"The swordsmanship of the Hidden Sword Sect is unparalleled in the world. It's hard to predict who will win and who will lose in this battle."

"Although Lin Fei is known as the 'Immortal Slayer Saber', he only faced high-level Qi condensing cultivators, who were suppressed by his saber intent. In terms of real strength, Lu Zongyi is not inferior to Lin Fei, and may even be stronger!"

These martial arts masters' eyes were sharp and experienced. They all knew that the current situation between Lin Fei and Lu Zongyi was just a competition between Martial Dao True Intent.

For martial artists, as long as Martial Dao True Intent doesn't collapse, it still depends on their own martial arts in the end. Both Lin Fei and Lu Zongyi were motionless as if they were looking for each other's flaws. They didn't make a move, but once they did, they would decide the outcome.

At the level of Lin Fei and Lu Zongyi, martial arts, lightness techniques, and Martial Dao True Intent were all top-notch. To decide the outcome, one move was enough!
