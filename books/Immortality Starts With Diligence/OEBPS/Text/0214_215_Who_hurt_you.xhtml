215. Who hurt you？

Lin Fei had been meditating in his cave for three days. Suddenly, Lin Fei's body shook. He felt the Blood Essence Nine Transformations pause slightly. It seemed to stop. The Blood Essence Nine Transformations was quite diligent. It's always transforming the blood essence in the body every moment.

"Your Blood Essence Nine Transformations, after three days of hard cultivation, has finally transformed all the blood essence in your body."

"Your Blood Essence Nine Transformations, cultivation progress increased by 50%."

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei opened his eyes.

"Is the transformation complete?"

Lin Fei knew that his body needed to complete the first transformation of the Blood Essence Nine Transformations cultivation. That was to transform the blood in the body. He also wanted to see how much blood essence can his body now transform.

"System."

Lin Fei immediately brought up the system panel to check the cultivation situation of martial arts.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Early Martial Saint

True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique: Early stage (Progress 3%)

Blood Essence Nine Transformations: First transformation (Progress 50%)

Holy Spirit Saber Technique: Mastery (Progress 8%)

Flame Burning Heaven Saber: Mastery (Progress 15%)

Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique: Mastery (Progress 10%)

Saber Momentum: Transcendence (Progress 0%)

Lin Fei's martial arts were increasing again. For example, the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique's cultivation progress had reached 3%. Almost increasing by 1% each day.

It seemed very little. But actually, it's not.

In about a hundred days, the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique's cultivation progress could reach 100% and thus break through to the middle stage. Once it broke through, Lin Fei's cultivation would also reach the middle stage of Martial Saint!

Under normal circumstances, the cultivation of a Martial Saint actually looks at "Qi". That is the situation of true essence cultivation.

Of course, Lin Fei was very clear nothing was unchangeable.

If he got a bit of heaven and earth's treasures, for example, some spiritual grasses containing abundant spiritual power, then his True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique could advance by leaps and bounds.

Next is the Blood Essence Nine Transformations. The first transformation is to accumulate blood essence.

Currently, it had transformed all the blood essence in Lin Fei's body. The cultivation progress had only reached 50%.

In other words, he could still continue accumulating to break through to the second transformation. So he had to increase his blood essence.

Typically, the augmentation of blood essence came from the blood and flesh of exotic beasts, or from elixirs made from the same. This, essentially, was a resource! As long as he had enough resources, forming the Energy Flower wouldn't be an insurmountable task.

As for the three martial saint level saber techniques, it took him a mere three days to achieve mastery in all of them. This was rather terrifying. These were martial saint level saber techniques, each unfathomably profound. It would be considered quite impressive to achieve proficiency in just one of them within three days.

As for mastery?

That was destined to be challenging for others.

However, Lin Fei was different. After all, Lin Fei had already reached the level of saber saint with his transcendence in saber momentum. His foundation in saber technique was extraordinary, surpassing the majority of martial saints.

Therefore, with such a profound foundation in saber technique, it seemed reasonable that he could master the pinnacle martial saint level saber techniques in just three days.

However, this was just about his limit. Lin Fei's foundational saber technique had been almost completely depleted. Moving forward, If he wished to enhance his saber technique further, enlightenment was his only recourse.

Lin Fei hoped that these saber techniques were "hard-working" enough to reach transcendence or even perfection soon.

To comprehend the essence of the saber, to then reach perfection. Even just hearing this sounded extremely difficult. But this was the only way for Lin Fei's saber momentum to reach perfection. Moreover, this was the only method!

The path of martial arts was challenging, this was clearly demonstrated at the martial saint level. Every step was difficult. Even if the physical body could be enhanced with resources, it required a vast amount of them. It wasn't as simple as just piling them on. Some independent martial saints couldn't amass enough in their entire lifetime.

"I have only a thousand contribution points. To use them to buy resources from the sect at the early stage of Martial Saint would be somewhat uneconomical. For the Blood Essence Nine Transformations, I just need to consume the blood and flesh of exotic beasts. The vast ocean is home to an infinite number of sea beasts. Seems I could make a trip back to Fairy Mist Island. I could hunt sea beasts and consume their blood and flesh. At the same time, I could fulfill my agreement with the eight noble families."

Lin Fei planned a return to Fairy Mist Island. After all, he had an agreement with the eight noble families. Once he and Lin Fei became outer sect elders of the Ancient Origin Sect, each of them could nominate five disciples to join the sect, without the need for an examination.

The eight noble families on Fairy Mist Island were probably yearning for Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin's attention. The reason they had not disturbed Lin Fei yet was probably because they knew he needed time to familiarize himself with the Ancient Origin Sect.

Once Lin Fei gained a foothold in the Ancient Origin Sect, people from the eight noble families would certainly come to him. Now, if Lin Fei took the initiative to go back, he could also settle the agreement with the eight noble families.

Lin Fei thought of Shen Lianxin. He had been in the Ancient Origin Sect for several days now. How was Shen Lianxin doing? Since he had to go back to Fairy Mist Island, Lin Fei naturally had to ask Shen Lianxin if he wanted to go back too. Thus, Lin Fei rose to his feet and flew towards Shen Lianxin's cave dwelling.

Lin Fei was in the Outer Enforcement Hall, and Shen Lianxin was in the Outer Transmission Hall. Their caves were not in the same place and were a distance apart.

Soon, Lin Fei arrived at Shen Lianxin's cave. Shen Lianxin was sitting cross-legged. When he saw Lin Fei, he opened his eyes. However, Lin Fei frowned.

"You're injured," Lin Fei asked coldly, "Brother Shen, who hurt you?"

Shen Lianxin and Lin Fei had journeyed together from Great Chang, struggling to find their Dao, and finally arrived at the Ancient Continent. Naturally, they had to help each other.

Seeing that Shen Lianxin had only been in the Ancient Origin Sect for a few days and had already been injured, Lin Fei felt a trace of anger. However, Shen Lianxin seemed quite calm.

"Brother Lin, it's no big deal. As you know, I'm a martial fanatic. Injuries are inevitable when sparring with others. Moreover, the injuries are not severe. I can handle this."

Lin Fei stared at Shen Lianxin. It seemed Shen Lianxin didn't want Lin Fei to interfere.

Lin Fei did not continue to inquire. In the Ancient Origin Sect, nobody dared to kill the elders. It was just an injury. Since Shen Lianxin said he could handle it, Lin Fei would not press the matter.

"Brother Shen, I am planning to go back to Fairy Mist Island. Do you need to go back?" Lin Fei asked.

Shen Lianxin shook his head and said, "I need to heal now, so I won't go back to Fairy Mist Island for the time being. As for the matter of nominating five disciples from the eight noble families, Brother Lin, I ask you to take care of it for me."

"That's natural. Brother Shen, rest assured, you take good care of your injuries. I will handle the matter with the eight noble families."

"Thank you, Brother Lin."

Lin Fei did not say much. He turned around and left the cave dwelling. Shen Lianxin's injury was a bit unexpected for Lin Fei. However, judging by Shen Lianxin's demeanor, he should be able to handle it himself.

Lin Fei did not want to interfere too much. After all, Shen Lianxin was also a Martial Saint. He and Lin Fei were friends, he was not Lin Fei's follower. Some things, Shen Lianxin could handle himself. He didn't need Lin Fei to step in.

Moreover, even in the Ancient Origin Sect, even though Lin Fei had the transcendence saber momentum, there were still others who were Martial Saints with transcendence martial arts momentum.

Even the two elders who transmitted their skills had formed the Energy Flower. Even with Lin Fei's transcendence saber momentum as pressure, Lin Fei did not feel that he was an opponent for those two transmitting elders. The three flowers of energy, qi, and spirit. Once a flower was formed, it represented a Martial Saint at the peak!

In some aspects, they had reached the limit. Compared to other Martial Saints, the difference was as great as the difference between heaven and earth! They were completely on different levels.

So, Lin Fei also had to enhance his strength as soon as possible. He didn't need to think about the saber momentum for the time being. Reaching perfection was too difficult. On the contrary, forming the Energy Flower or Qi Flower seemed to have a glimmer of hope.

"Let's go."

Lin Fei didn't stay in the Ancient Origin Sect for long. He turned into a streak of light and flew towards Fairy Mist Island.
