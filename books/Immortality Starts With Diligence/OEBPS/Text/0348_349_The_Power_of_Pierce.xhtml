349. The Power of Pierce

"No one has ever dared to address a Golden Core True Person from the Heavenly River Dao Sect in such a manner."

Yuanlong's eyes flashed with restrained fury. He vanquished countless Martial Sovereigns. Many among them, having cultivated certain Divine Abilities, became arrogant. And the result? Divine Abilities paled in comparison to magical treasures!

Yuanlong bore the treasured artifact of the Heavenly River Dao Sect. He merely wished to avoid a grand spectacle. Yet, with Lin Fei's aggressive stance, he had to teach him the might of the Heavenly River Dao Sect.

"Heavenly River Orb," Yuanlong gently touched his forehead. From within, a bead shot out, radiating a white glow. "Go!"

With a gesture towards Lin Fei,

Boom.

The Heavenly River Orb surged, seemingly rending the very fabric of space itself, causing minute fractures. The mere fact it could cause such a distortion was a testament to its immense power.

"Divine Ability, Gravity Domain!"

From behind Lin Fei, the second layer of his Life Wheel illuminated brightly. Suddenly, the force of gravity descended, significantly slowing down the Heavenly River Orb's trajectory.

Yet, though affected, the Orb still hurtled towards Lin Fei. Clearly, this wasn't a mere Golden Core level magical treasure. Otherwise, it couldn't resist Lin Fei's Gravity Domain.

"Divine Ability, Annihilation!"

From Lin Fei's Life Wheel, the fourth layer began to shimmer. The power of destruction descended, instantly falling upon the Heavenly River Orb.

The Orb seemed to face an immense resistance, even quivering violently in mid-air. The force of destruction was formidable, especially at its pinnacle.

Coupled with the Gravity Domain, both top-tier Divine Abilities acted on the Orb simultaneously. Even a hundred Great Mountain Seals would buckle under such force.

Crack.

A tiny fissure appeared on the Heavenly River Orb.

Yuanlong's face paled.

"Impossible! Soul of the True Dragon, merge!"

Yuanlong quickly formed hand signs and produced a talisman. In the next moment, a fearsome, fanged dragon phantom erupted from the talisman. This spectral dragon then rapidly merged into the Heavenly River Orb. Coiling within, the dragon stopped the expansion of the fissure.

The Orb, rejuvenated, sped towards Lin Fei again. Neither the Gravity Domain nor the Annihilation Divine Ability could halt its advance.

Yet, Lin Fei remained unflinching in the face of danger. For he had discerned the truth. Under the bombardment of his two divine abilities, the Heavenly River Orb had indeed suffered a grievous blow. But it seemed the dragon's illusion had shielded the Heavenly River Orb from total destruction.

A resonant hum echoed, And in the next instant, the sixth layer of light from Lin Fei's Life Wheel began to shine brightly.

"Divine Ability, Pierce!"

This was the pinnacle Divine Ability called "Pierce" that Lin Fei had just recently mastered. It was still at a beginner's realm, Yet, this ability possessed a sharpness that was unparalleled and unbreakable, especially crafted for countering hard magical treasures.

The intangible power of "Pierce", like a beam of brilliant light, hurtled towards the Heavenly River Orb in the blink of an eye.

With a soft hiss, the radiant beam vanished, and the Heavenly River Orb's advance halted.

Yuanlong trembled. Gazing upon the Heavenly River Orb, disbelief clouded his eyes. His precious Heavenly River Orb was penetrated. A small hole emerged, And the lustrous glow of the Heavenly River Orb dimmed.

With a crystalline crack, under the assault of Lin Fei's Annihilation Divine Ability, the damaged Heavenly River Orb could withstand no more. It shattered and splintered into oblivion.

This was an important treasure of the Heavenly River Dao Sect!

"How? You actually destroyed the Heavenly River Orb?"

Yuanlong looked as if he couldn't trust his own eyes. It was the Heavenly River Orb! Once, he had roamed the Ancient Continent unchecked because of it. Even when facing the top Martial Sovereigns from the Martial King families, he was an equal match. But now? The Heavenly River Orb lay in ruins, smashed to bits by Lin Fei's Divine Ability.

Lin Fei cast his gaze upon the distant Yuanlong and swiftly extended his hand. Immediately, the Gravity Domain expanded rapidly. In the blink of an eye, it enveloped Yuanlong.

Yuanlong's entire being quaked. Deprived of the Heavenly River Orb, and now with the Gravity Domain bearing down upon him, he truly felt its terror. It felt as though he was sinking in quicksand, unable to wrestle free. Even if he could muster some magic, could such tricks possibly hold against Lin Fei's Divine Ability?

"Lin Fei, you dared destroy my Heavenly River Orb, the next time I come to Great Chang, I swear I will slay you!"

Yuanlong declared through gritted teeth. His hatred for Lin Fei was palpable. In the past, he had always been composed as if everything was within his grasp. But this time, he had miscalculated. And the price was staggering.

"The next time?"

A fleeting suspicion crossed Lin Fei's mind. At this point, if Yuanlong still harbored thoughts of a next encounter, it wasn't arrogance on his part. He must have had something up his sleeve, believing he could surely escape.

With a swift sound, Yuanlong's figure vanished in an instant. It seemed as if droplets of water were falling from the position where Yuanlong had stood.

Yuanlong had executed an escape technique, specifically, the Water Escape! As long as there was water, Yuanlong could escape. And for him, finding water was never an issue. That's the marvel of the escape technique. Though it is a kind of magic, not all Golden Core True Persons can master it.

Moreover, there are varying levels of expertise even within the realm of escape techniques. Yuanlong had honed his technique to perfection. With just a few droplets of water, he could enact the Water Escape, And vanish as if by magic in the blink of an eye. Such prowess was truly formidable.

Given Yuanlong's mastery of this technique, crossing vast oceans would pose little danger to him. After all, boundless seas are replete with water. Even if confronted by formidable exotic beasts, he could simply employ Water Escape.

However, in the distant void, Yuanlong stumbled as if he had collided with an invisible wall. He reappeared, unable to escape.

"What's going on?"

Yuanlong looked baffled and alarmed. Was he truly unable to escape? In the past, no matter the adversary or peril faced, he had always relied on his Water Escape, and had always managed to flee. After all, his Water Escape technique had been refined to perfection.

With a mere thought, he could escape like a single droplet. Regardless of the strength of the opponent's Divine Ability or magic treasure, Unless he was sealed, he could typically escape. Why had he failed now?

He looked around and noticed That at some point, a layer of white light had surrounded the entire area. Even he hadn't noticed its presence until now.

Moreover, this layer of white luminescence seemed to cut him off from the outside world. Thus, no matter how potent his escape technique, he was unable to flee.

"Is this... a martial domain?"

Suddenly, as if recalling something, Yuanlong's face turned as pale as paper as if he had been dealt a massive blow.

"Correct, this is my Saber Domain!" came a voice. "So, Yuanlong, prepare to meet your end!"

Lin Fei's gaze was icy cold. In the end, he had unleashed his Saber Domain. Had he not activated the Saber Domain, Yuanlong would have escaped. Within the Saber Domain, thousands of saber lights flashed, descending like raindrops, converging instantly towards Yuanlong.

"No..."

A cry of despair erupted from Yuanlong. In the next moment, he was submerged, overwhelmed by the torrential onslaught of saber lights.
