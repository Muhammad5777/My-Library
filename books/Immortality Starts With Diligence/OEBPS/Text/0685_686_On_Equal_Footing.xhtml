686. On Equal Footing

"Kill!" Lin Fei did not hesitate. His Eternal Realm surged forward, fiercely enveloping the Doomsday Supreme.

"Swish."

Indeed, the Doomsday Supreme was swept into the vortex of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. By now, apart from the Masters of Chaos, who else could escape being drawn into Lin Fei's Eternal Realm?

Yet, being drawn in did not necessarily equate to Lin Fei's victory, especially not in the case of the Doomsday Supreme.

Inside the Eternal Realm, the Doomsday Supreme was neither panicked nor fearful. He looked around and then smiled faintly, "So this is your world?"

"It is indeed powerful! Even the top-tier Supremes could not withstand the crushing force of your world." He paused, then added with a smirk, "But don't forget, I am known as 'Doomsday.'"

After these words, the distorted power within him unleashed wildly, causing the very fabric of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm to twist. Not just the void twisted, but the Doomsday Supreme himself seemed to transform into an origin of destruction.

The power of obliteration emanated from him, destroying everything in its path. Even circles of dark light rippled out, annihilating everything they touched, including Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. This was troublesome—very troublesome. It was the first time he had encountered someone who, even when suppressed by his Eternal Realm, he could not subdue.

"Suppress!"

Lin Fei intensified the suppressive force of his Eternal Realm. From all directions, the full might of the Eternal Realm tried to crush the Doomsday Supreme, even to the point of grinding him to dust.

However, the reality was that the power bursting from Lin Fei's Eternal Realm could only continuously clash with the dark ripples emanating from the Doomsday Supreme. The notion of grinding the Doomsday Supreme to dust was simply unattainable. Instead, both forces continued to escalate, each trying to overpower the other.

Time ticked slowly by—one hour, two hours, three hours... one day, two days, three days...

Days passed, and Lin Fei's Eternal Realm suffered considerable damage. Yet, its resilience was unparalleled, and its core remained unharmed. The same was true for the Doomsday Supreme—unscathed at the fundamental level. Both continued to expend energy in an ongoing stalemate, neither able to gain the upper hand.

Both combatants understood the reality of their deadlock. They were truly evenly matched. Lin Fei, as things stood, could not possibly best the Doomsday Supreme—even though the latter remained within the confines of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Indeed, the Doomsday Supreme could leave at any moment if he chose.

"Ha ha ha, what a thrill!" the Doomsday Supreme exclaimed. "Supreme Lin, we are equals; what do you say to a draw? Continuing this battle would be pointless."

The Doomsday Supreme then gradually withdrew his powers of distortion and destruction. Lin Fei understood his intent and nodded in agreement, "Indeed, there is no point in continuing."

No sooner had Lin Fei spoken than he retracted his Eternal Realm. The chaotic void was left with only Lin Fei and the Doomsday Supreme facing each other. The Clairvoyant Supreme watched in astonishment.

Lin Fei had actually fought the Doomsday Supreme to a draw, and the latter had been fighting with all his might. Truly, Lin Fei was unassailable.

"Another Invincible Supreme has emerged..." the Clairvoyant Supreme muttered under his breath, his expression complex.

The title of Invincible Supreme, which had previously belonged solely to the Doomsday Supreme, now had to accommodate another—Lin Fei.

Undoubtedly, Lin Fei was also an Invincible Supreme, as even the Doomsday Supreme could not best him. As for other Supremes, Lin Fei could easily overpower them, even the top-tier Supremes, who he could annihilate in a thought. What else could define an Invincible Supreme?

Yet, for Lin Fei, such a title held little significance.

"Do you have the capability to destroy an entire Chaos Realm?" Lin Fei suddenly asked.

"Destroy a Chaos Realm?" The Doomsday Supreme responded, "Of course, I can. I am the Doomsday Supreme, after all, and destruction is my forte."

"Why, Supreme Lin, are you contemplating destroying a Chaos Realm? Let me guess, you're considering this because your adversary is a Ruler? Only by annihilating the Chaos Realm can you truly eliminate the Realm Ruler. Actually, Supreme Lin, your world might also have the potential to completely obliterate a Chaos Realm. If you're patient and willing to pay the price, perhaps you could do it as well."

The Doomsday Supreme instantly discerned Lin Fei's intent. Chaos Realm Ruler! This being was a top-tier Supreme, yet distinctively more formidable than his peers.

Especially within their own Chaos Realms, Rulers were notoriously difficult to defeat, unless one obliterated the entire Chaos Realm. But who could achieve such a feat?

Lin Fei nodded. He indeed wanted to test his own capabilities. Initially, when his Eternal Realm was only at 11% of its cultivation progress, he could not challenge the Dark Demon Domain.

Now, however, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm had improved by more than fourfold, and his combat power had increased more than tenfold. Moreover, Lin Fei had undergone a transformation. With his Eternal Realm, he was now on the level of an Invincible Supreme.

Confronting a Chaos Realm was not out of the question, as long as he had the patience and if his Eternal Realm could completely envelop the Dark Demon Domain. Lin Fei felt that there was a chance he could defeat the Dark Demon Lord.

Of course, it would be ideal if his Eternal Realm could transform once more, reaching 100% of its cultivation progress and evolving from a True Immortal world into a Chaos Realm. But Lin Fei found this step challenging.

"Doomsday Supreme, did you come to see me just to observe or test my strength?" Lin Fei inquired. He hadn't asked before, knowing it would be futile. Beings like the Doomsday Supreme valued strength above all.

Only when one was powerful enough could one garner respect from someone like the Doomsday Supreme. Otherwise, despite any outward courtesies, they would hold no genuine regard.

Now, Lin Fei, using his strength, had earned a place of respect from the Doomsday Supreme. Thus, he felt it appropriate to inquire about the Doomsday Supreme's intentions.

The Doomsday Supreme gave a slight smile, "Supreme Lin, worthy indeed of reaching the level of an Invincible Supreme. I do indeed have a matter I hope you can assist with."

"What is it? Please, speak freely," Lin Fei urged.

The Doomsday Supreme pondered for a moment before speaking gravely, "It's quite simple. At our level, the ultimate goal is to become a Master of Chaos. I have been an Invincible Supreme for a long time. Although it's said that being an Invincible Supreme is lofty, in reality? The gap between us and the Masters of Chaos is as vast as heaven and earth. Supreme Lin, you might think that we are invincible at the Supreme level and that we aren't far from the realm of a Master of Chaos. Even if there is a gap, it couldn't be large."

"However, I can tell you clearly," he continued, "Supremes, whether ordinary Supremes, top-tier Supremes, or us Invincible Supremes, are all insignificant in front of a Master of Chaos. If a Master of Chaos wished to kill us, it would be no more difficult than lifting a hand. We are simply not in the same league."

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. He found it hard to believe. Was there really such a vast difference between a Master of Chaos and an Invincible Supreme?
