458. Reuniting with Phoenix, Phoenix's Shock!

The Heavenly Artifact finally managed to meet with the Heaven Transformation Ancestor. He had once met the Heaven Transformation Ancestor and even received kindness from him. Therefore, the Heavenly Artifact immediately sought him out for help.

"Oh? Heavenly Artifact, what brings you to me?" the Heaven Transformation Ancestor opened his eyes, somewhat surprised to see the Heavenly Artifact.

"I've come to seek your help," the Heavenly Artifact implored. The Heaven Transformation Ancestor was skeptical, wondering who could threaten someone as powerful as a half-step Deity Transformation.

The Sect Master of the Heaven Transformation Sect, standing respectfully, explained the recent developments involving the Ancient Origin Sect and Lin Fei.

Upon hearing about Lin Fei's ascension to Martial Gods, the Heaven Transformation Ancestor's eyes flashed with a hint of admiration.

"Martial Gods? I never expected someone of this era to achieve such a feat on their own. Impressive, truly impressive!"

This reaction was unexpected for the Heavenly Artifact, who then pleaded, "Ancestor, Lin Fei is ruthless. He destroyed my Heavenly Artifact Sect and is even pursuing me. Please save me."

"Hmm, Heavenly Artifact, we do share a past connection. You coming to me is fortuitous indeed," the Heaven Transformation Ancestor mused. "I won't let you suffer too much. Offering you to Martial God Lin will earn me a favor. A favor from a Martial God is something many dreams of but can't obtain. How should I thank you?"

Shocked, the Heavenly Artifact realized the Heaven Transformation Ancestor's intention and immediately attempted to flee, even resorting to self-detonating part of his Deity Transformation body to escape.

However, the Heaven Transformation Ancestor, a true Deity Transformation being, easily captured him regardless of his attempt to self-destruct.

The Heavenly Artifact struggled fiercely, angrily accusing the Heaven Transformation Ancestor, "How can you, a Deity Transformation Ancestor, stoop so low as to betray me to appease a Martial God? You're bringing shame to all of us Qi cultivators!"

Heavenly Artifact Ancestral Master now disregarded any sense of hierarchy or respect in his desperation. Facing imminent death, he furiously cursed, having nothing left to lose. In his view, a Deity Transformation Ancestor stood high above, capable of creating countless incarnations and possessing vast divine abilities. Why would such a figure fear a mere Martial God?

Yet, to his shock, the Heaven Transformation Ancestor seemed eager to please Lin Fei, intent on capturing him as a gift for the new Martial God. This was completely contrary to the Heavenly Artifact's expectations.

The Heaven Transformation Ancestor responded with a cold laugh, "Go on, curse. It's fitting now. You only see the exalted status of immortality cultivators in the early stages, where indeed, they stand high above. Even a Nascent Soul has more means than a Martial King. But once someone achieves Martial Gods status, it changes everything."

The Heaven Transformation Ancestor explained, "Martial Gods are much rarer than Deity Transformations. Think about it: there are far more martial cultivators than immortality cultivators. So why are there so few Martial Gods compared to Deity Transformations?

Because becoming a Martial God is exponentially harder than achieving Deity Transformation. A Martial God captures and comprehends the essence of laws, using them for their own purposes. Deity Transformations merge into these laws, existing in harmony with them. But a Martial God commands the laws!

You might not understand the gap between them. But know this: a single Martial God is far more powerful than a Deity Transformation. The birth of a Martial God is not just a concern for me; even other Deity Transformations have to accommodate them. Trading you for the favor of Martial God Lin is an excellent deal. So, you don't die by my hands, but by those of a Martial God. Heavenly Artifact, your death is not unjust!"

The Heaven Transformation Ancestor's words sent shockwaves through the Heavenly Artifact's heart. The status of a Martial God was so exalted, even surpassing that of a Deity Transformation!

"I hate this..." the Heavenly Artifact roared in anger and frustration, but it was futile. The Heaven Transformation Ancestor captured him effortlessly.

"Let's go," declared the Heaven Transformation Ancestor. "I shall personally visit the Feng family to congratulate Martial God Lin, and present this significant gift."

The Heaven Transformation Ancestor's eyes narrowed slightly, a smile appearing on his face. In a flash, his figure vanished into the sky.

Lin Fei, having achieved the status of Martial Gods, had been spending time informing Feng Ling, preparing her for her own attempt at reaching Martial Gods. To ensure success, Lin Fei even took Feng Ling to the small world where the Phoenix resided.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei and the Phoenix arrived once again in the small world. During his previous visit, Lin Fei hadn't felt much different, but now, as a Martial God, his perception had changed. He felt as if there were restraints everywhere in this small world, making it an uncomfortable place to stay.

Despite this, for the sake of Feng Ling, Lin Fei endured. Soon, they reached the Wutong tree and saw the Phoenix again.

The Phoenix suddenly opened its eyes, seemingly causing the entire space to vibrate. Its gaze fixed on Lin Fei, showing a hint of shock.

"You have become a Martial God?" the Phoenix asked. Lin Fei did not hide it, as it was impossible to conceal such a fact.

"Fortuitously," he replied.

"Good, good, good!" the Phoenix exclaimed joyously. "It seems there's hope for me to leave this small world after all."

A Dao Integration True Immortal was needed to break free from this small world, and the Phoenix had not held much hope before. But Lin Fei's achievement gave it hope. If Lin Fei, at such a young age, could become a Martial God, progressing further and even rivaling Dao Integration was not impossible.

The Phoenix was not concerned about the potentially long wait. It could be reborn from ashes and had an almost eternal lifespan. It could wait, no matter how long it took, as long as there was hope.

"Phoenix, Feng Ling wishes to become a Martial God," Lin Fei explained. "She follows the path of the Phoenix, which should be considered the way of flame. Therefore, she wants to understand what truly is the path of the Phoenix and the Dao of flame."

Lin Fei addressed the Phoenix directly, without any hesitation, asking to witness the Phoenix's "Dao of Flame." He understood the significance of this request, as it pertained to the Phoenix's secret and its unique path, not something casually revealed to others.

The Phoenix gazed deeply at Lin Fei and eventually nodded. "In light of you being a Martial God, I will demonstrate the Dao of Flame for you," the Phoenix agreed. "Remember, even though your body carries the bloodline of the Phoenix and you seek to follow the Dao of Flame, the path you must walk is your own, not mine. Every martial artist, every Martial God, is independent. The Phoenix bloodline in you is merely a stepping stone, a means for you to find your own path."

The words of the Phoenix resonated profoundly, like a wake-up call, bringing enlightenment. Feng Ling experienced a moment of clarity.

"Yes, I carry the Phoenix bloodline, but the path I seek to ascend to godhood is my own!" At that moment, Feng Ling's resolve solidified, her determination unwavering in her journey to find and follow her own unique path.
