10. Martial Arts Sparring

"Greetings, Old Master Bai!"

Lin Fei saluted Old Master Bai with a fist.

"Is this the Second Young Master?"

Old Master Bai also came to his senses.

He, of course, knew Lin Fei, who had been practicing martial arts in the Nine Profound Sect.

"Since the Second Young Master is a disciple of a large sect, his martial arts skills must be extraordinary, and he should be able to take on the escort mission.

However, times have changed. Our Bai family's escort agency flag may not be recognized by the bandits in the Green Forest. If we encounter danger..."

Old Master Bai's meaning was clear. Once they embarked on the escort mission, there would be life-threatening dangers.

"Old Master, why don't you find someone to spar with my younger brother? With your keen eyes, you can naturally determine whether he can lead the escort mission."

Lin Wen suddenly spoke, offering a suggestion.

"That's a good idea. Tie Shan, you go and spar with the Second Young Master."

Old Master Bai's eyes flashed with a brilliant light. He immediately called a burly man standing beside him, who was as tall and strong as an iron tower.

"Brother, are you okay with this?" Lin Wen asked.

"No problem, let's go outside and try."

Lin Fei nodded. He was even somewhat eager to try. After all, he had reached the Body Refining Fifth Level but had little experience in actual combat. This was an opportunity.

So, the group of people left the room and went to the outdoor martial arts training ground.

Many people gathered around. They knew that Lin Fei was a disciple of the Nine Profound Sect.

Tie Shan was Old Master Bai's direct disciple and the future successor of the escort agency.

Lin Wen stood next to Old Master Bai. In fact, Lin Wen also wanted to see Lin Fei's true strength.

After all, seeing is believing and hearing is not. He didn't understand martial arts. But Old Master Bai did. He would naturally be able to see Lin Fei's true strength.

Moreover, Lin Wen knew Tie Shan. Body Refining Fourth Level! This was definitely Old Master Bai's proud disciple.

"Swords have no eyes, since this is a friendly spar, let's not use weapons. How about unarmed combat?" Lin Fei looked at the burly Tie Shan and said.

"Then, please enlighten me, Second Young Master!"

After finishing his words, Tie Shan took a big step forward.

With one stride, he covered a distance of more than three meters.

In an instant, he closed in on Lin Fei.

Tie Shan was burly and had immense strength. As a result, his punch was also very powerful and fierce. And it came very suddenly.

Under normal circumstances, Lin Fei would have to dodge first. However, he showed no intention of dodging.

Seeing Tie Shan's powerful punch, Lin Fei didn't hesitate. He raised his hand and threw a punch as well.

"Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist!"

Lin Fei shouted in a low voice.

"Boom!"

Instantly, the masterful fist technique was unleashed. It automatically mobilized all the power in his body. Blood, qi, and bones all erupted.

The power of the Body Refining Fifth Level was not to be underestimated. As he threw the punch, the air was filled with the sound of a shockwave.

"Bang!"

Finally, the two of them collided with a punch. Tie Shan's face changed drastically at the moment of the clash.

An overwhelming force was transmitted directly from Lin Fei's fist. And it came with momentum like the pressure of a mountain, overwhelming and crushing.

"Crack!"

Tie Shan retreated several steps in a row.

Under his feet, the hard bluestone bricks on the ground cracked like a spider's web. This showed how terrifying the power of Lin Fei's punch just now was.

Tie Shan's body was filled with fluctuating qi and blood. He felt so uncomfortable that he wanted to vomit blood.

He didn't make another move.

In just one strike, the difference in their abilities was clear. Moreover, he noticed that Lin Fei had held back. Otherwise, with that punch alone, his arm would have been disabled.

"Thank you, Second Young Master, for showing mercy."

Tie Shan was an honest man. Lin Fei's strength was greater than his, and he had shown mercy, not making Tie Shan lose face. He naturally felt grateful.

"Hahaha, Tie Shan, you must be at the Body Refining Fourth Level, right? Your strength is really impressive."

Lin Fei also clasped his fists and said with a smile.

In fact, Lin Fei was truly happy in his heart. He didn't use much force just now and it seemed that he had easily defeated Tie Shan.

Tie Shan was certainly not weak. This could only mean that Lin Fei's current strength was indeed very strong.

Moreover, the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist combined with the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique was indeed very powerful.

"Your Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist has just been tested in a real battle, and it has understood the essence of the fist technique in combat, increasing its progress by 60%."

Lin Fei's eyes brightened. He didn't expect that sparring would bring such benefits.

His Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist had made significant progress. It seemed that it wouldn't be long before it advanced from mastery to transcendence.

"How about trying saber techniques?"

Lin Fei couldn't help but itch to try. He wanted to spar with Tie Shan using saber techniques. He wanted to improve his Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

"I haven't practiced saber techniques much, but Bai Ying, my junior sister, is skilled in saber techniques..." Tie Shan said honestly.

"Alright, Lin Fei, that's enough."

Lin Wen turned his head and asked Old Master Bai, "What do you think of Lin Fei?"

"He is qualified to escort the shipment! If I'm not mistaken, the Second Young Master should be at the Body Refining Fifth Level, right? As expected of a disciple from a major sect, truly extraordinary.

However, even with the Body Refining Fifth Level, it won't be easy to deter all the bandits along the way. Tie Shan, Bai Ying, this time you'll lead the team and escort the shipment with the Second Young Master. Do your best to help him succeed!"

Old Master Bai immediately made a decision.
