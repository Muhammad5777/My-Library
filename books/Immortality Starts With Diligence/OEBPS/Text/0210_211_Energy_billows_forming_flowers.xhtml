211. Energy billows, forming flowers

Lin Fei looked up towards the top of the mountain. Shen Lianxin had reached the top. So, Lin Fei nodded and directly withdrew his saber momentum. Once the saber momentum was withdrawn, the suppression of everyone also disappeared in an instant.

"Whoosh".

The next moment, hundreds of Martial Saints immediately rushed frantically toward the top of the mountain. Among them, the Martial Saints like Ying Wuming and Lu Xuan were the fastest.

Hundreds of Martial Saints even started to make their moves. True essence burst forth. Martial arts shocked the heavens and the earth.

It was a shocking battle. If it weren't for Lin Fei's saber momentum, suppressing everything, then this battle of hundreds of Martial Saints would have shaken the heavens and the earth.

Such a battle was something one could only come across but not seek. It made countless disciples yearn for it. However, just now Lin Fei, with his own strength, displayed saber momentum, suppressing hundreds of Martial Saints!

Looking back now, these Martial Saints were fighting passionately. It seemed to have a different taste, but it felt a little bland. Even those acquired disciples felt the same. Their gazes, from time to time, fell on Lin Fei.

Suppressing hundreds of Martial Saints with his own strength, no one dared to move. What kind of power is this? What kind of boldness? What kind of domineering attitude? They also hoped that one day they could be this domineering!

"Brother Lin, let's go up the mountain together."

Gu Meng changed the way he addressed Lin Fei. Obviously, Gu Meng had also acknowledged Lin Fei. Perhaps, other people didn't know what saber momentum transcendence meant. But Gu Meng knew very well.

All the true disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect knew, for a Martial Saint, reaching the peak of cultivation wasn't difficult. The Ancient Origin Sect had various resources piled up. For a typical genius, spending some time reaching the peak of Martial Saint was also possible. But if one wanted to comprehend momentum, that was not something that could be achieved by piling up resources.

Moreover, above Martial Saints, there is the Martial Sovereign! Without comprehending momentum, there would be no breakthrough to achieve the status of Martial Sovereign. Momentum is essential!

Therefore, among the true disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect, only the true disciples who had comprehended momentum could be considered Martial Sovereign seeds.

"Alright, let's go up the mountain together."

Facing the "goodwill" released by Gu Meng, Lin Fei didn't refuse. So, the two of them, step by step, crossed the void, walked in the air, and arrived at the top of the mountain.

At this moment, on the top of the mountain, many Martial Saints had already reached their destination. There were still some Martial Saints fighting. But Gu Meng didn't even look at them. He was just a supervisor. As for gathering those beginner Martial Saints, that was not his jurisdiction. Naturally, there were others in charge. Gu Meng only brought the most special one, the Dao Seeker Lin Fei!

Lin Fei looked at the Ancient Origin Sect in front of him.

The mountain gate was huge. There was no immortal qi lingering inside nor were there any precious birds or exotic beasts. Instead, there was energy rushing to the sky. Like a cloud of smoke, it rushed into the clouds.

"This is energy smoke cultivated to an extremely profound level. When you enter the Ancient Origin Sect and acquire the cultivation method, you naturally will have energy, and even cultivating to the point of energy smoke is not difficult," Gu Meng said with a smile.

Lin Fei nodded. Soon, two figures walked out from inside the Ancient Origin Sect. These two figures didn't have energy smoke, but they had an extremely strong sense of oppression! Even Lin Fei felt the pressure.

Even Gu Meng, seeing these two martial artists, immediately paid his respects: "Greetings. Two transmission elders!"

The others also paid their respects one after another. Obviously, these two transmission elders seemed to have different statuses and positions.

The two martial artists had cold gazes and serious expressions. Only when they saw Lin Fei, their expressions seemed to soften slightly, even a smile appeared.

"The examination is over, this time, a total of eleven people passed the examination, can join our Ancient Origin Sect, and become the outer elder. Dao Seeker Lin Fei, Dao Seeker Shen Lianxin, Lu Xuan, Ying Wuming..."

Then, these two transmission elders began to read out the names. Soon, all eleven names were read. Lin Fei found that there were several Martial Saints who were not those who were mentioned by Gu Meng to build momentum. Instead, they were obscure. Some of the Martial Saints who built momentum failed the examination.

"I don't accept this! I went through all kinds of hardships and expended countless efforts to reach the Ancient Origin Sect. Just a trivial examination eliminated me? If there really was a big fight, more than half of the eleven people you chose are not my matches! Why can't I enter the Ancient Origin Sect?"

Suddenly, a Martial Saint roared. His expression was ferocious. His body was full of momentum. His eyes were red. Obviously, he had reached the point of extreme anger and he was extremely unwilling to accept this.

Lin Fei also looked over. He recognized this person. Zhao Canglong! He was previously named by Gu Meng, one of the Martial Saints who greatly built momentum. But he was not selected.

It was unknown what happened at the foot of the mountain. That prevented Zhao Canglong from reaching the top of the mountain first. It must be that his movement technique was lacking.

In Lin Fei's view, this examination was full of loopholes. It wasn't even an examination. It seemed that anyone could be chosen. It was entirely up to the preferences of the two transmission elders.

Of course, if there were really good talents like Lin Fei who had comprehended momentum, the transmission elders would definitely not miss it. As for the others, it really depended on luck.

"Don't accept it? Hehe, these scattered cultivation Martial Saints from the outside world really think they've reached the peak step by step? Since you don't accept it, then die!"

One of the transmission elders stepped forward. He didn't burst out any momentum, just punched out. This punch, without any extra fancy moves, was just a straightforward punch, even the true essence in his body didn't burst out much.

It was just barely visible that this elder's body erupted with terrifying energy. This was no longer just energy smoke but rather a flower that was vaguely opening on top of his head. A flower completely condensed from energy.

The moment the flower opened, this punch arrived in front of Zhao Canglong.

"Ah..."

Zhao Canglong was as if awakening from a dream. His body's blood and true essence exploded instantly and also punched out. It seemed as if there was a faint sound of a dragon chant. It was obviously some very profound boxing method.

On the other hand, the transmission elder was just an ordinary straight punch.

"Boom".

The two fists collided hard. But the next moment, Zhao Canglong's body exploded on cue. He was directly blown up by the elder's punch and turned into a cloud of blood mist.

He died!

A Martial Saint of such prestige, even everyone could see Zhao Canglong was no ordinary Martial Saint. However, he was still blown up by this ordinary punch from the transmission elder. He didn't have any resistance at all.

This was completely crushing! Even a crushing that didn't leave any hope. Just like an adult and a baby. Worlds apart!

For a moment, the faces of all the Martial Saints present changed drastically. Those two transmission elders obviously were also just Martial Saints. Their realm was the same. Even, they didn't use any momentum, and the true essence wasn't used. Just a punch with physical strength alone instantly exploded a top Martial Saint.

Was this possible?

Even Lin Fei's pupils shrank sharply. Danger! He sensed a trace of danger from the two transmission elders. Especially when the flower made of energy appeared on the elder's head. The danger Lin Fei could sense was more intense.

"This is the Energy Flower! The elder has condensed the Energy Flower, which means that the body has reached its limit! If the three flowers gather at the top. The elder doesn't even have to make a move, just a glance can kill Zhao Canglong. The gap between Martial Saints and Martial Saints is worlds apart. How can a loose cultivation Martial Saint be compared with a Martial Saint from my Ancient Origin Sect?"

Gu Meng sneered.

"Energy Flower?"

A glimmer of surprise flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

"Brother Lin, don't be surprised. Once you enter the Ancient Origin Sect, you will naturally know. Like you, a Dao Seeker who has already comprehended saber momentum, your future achievements will definitely be extraordinary. Even the gathering of the three flowers is not impossible! By then, I'm afraid I'll have to rely on Brother Lin."

Gu Meng said with a smile. In dealing with Lin Fei, his attitude was completely different. After all, even though they were all Martial Saints, not all Martial Saints were the same.

"The eleven Martial Saints whose names were read, follow us to the Ancestral Hall for the beginner ceremony."

After saying that, the two transmission elders turned around and walked towards the mountain gate of the Ancient Origin Sect.

"Brother Lin, go ahead. After you're settled, I'll come to visit you."

Gu Meng said with a smile to Lin Fei. Lin Fei nodded. He then followed the two transmission elders and flew into the Ancient Origin Sect.
