35. Unexpected Surprise

Time passed day by day.

After a few days of rest, the injury on Lin Fei's arm had fully recovered.

Thanks to the Blood-Increasing Pill, his Giant Whale Body Refining Technique was steadily improving.

The Vajra Iron Cloth was also improving. However, his Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique hit a bottleneck.

Despite listening to Buddhist and Taoist scriptures every day, its progress in cultivation had been almost stagnant.

Lin Fei knew he had not found the right method.

If he found the right method, his Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique would also continue to improve.

His Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist and Nine Shadow Saber Technique had hardly improved either.

Especially the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

After Lin Fei fused ten basic saber techniques and reached 8% progress, the Nine Shadow Saber Technique did not improve anymore.

The problem was the other saber techniques he practiced.

For example, the Gale Saber Technique and Shadow Saber Technique had only reached the transcendence level, not perfection.

Therefore, they cannot be integrated into the Nine Shadow Saber Technique to enhance its foundation and progress.

"Perfection..."

Lin Fei murmured. He hoped to cultivate saber intent.

If he could cultivate saber intent, his strength would certainly advance by leaps and bounds.

During this period, the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique had been steadily improving due to the Blood-Increasing Pill. However, Lin Fei could not afford to be idle. He must find a way to continue improving the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

"Oh, I can still issue tasks. Let those who have practiced the Shadow Saber Technique and Gale Saber Technique to perfection guide me. By then, my Nine Shadow Saber Technique will continue to improve with these saber techniques perfected."

Lin Fei's eyes lit up. He thought it was a good idea.

The Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist and Nine Shadow Saber Technique were considered superior martial arts in the body refining realm.

However, the Gale Saber Technique and Shadow Saber Technique were different.

They could only be considered slightly stronger than basic saber techniques.

It was much easier to reach perfection in these techniques than in the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist and Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

He might find someone who had reached the perfection level of these saber techniques in the Nine Profound Sect.

So Lin Fei went to the task hall.

"Elder, I am issuing a task."

"What task?"

"I need to be guided by those who have reached perfection in five saber techniques, including the Gale Saber Technique, Shadow Saber Technique, Falling Leaves Saber Technique, etc., for one hour. The task reward is 3,000 contribution points!"

Lin Fei had previously practiced a total of five saber techniques, all of which had reached the transcendence level.

Therefore, he issued five tasks at once. Each guidance task offered a reward of 3,000 contribution points.

After all, these saber techniques were much inferior to the Nine Shadow Saber Technique. Three thousand contribution points were already a generous reward.

However, this meant that he would need 15,000 contribution points in total. It would almost drain all of Lin Fei's remaining contribution points.

After issuing the tasks, Lin Fei returned to his courtyard and waited patiently.

Sure enough, just an hour later, a man in white martial attire arrived at Lin Fei's courtyard.

"Are you Lin Fei who issued the guidance tasks?"

"How should I address you, senior brother?" Lin Fei bowed and saluted.

"I'm Bai Wei."

"So it's Senior Brother Bai. May I know which saber technique you have taken the task for?"

"It's the Falling Leaves Saber Technique. I will not withhold anything during the hour, but how much you can comprehend depends on your talent."

Then, Bai Wei began to demonstrate the Falling Leaves Saber Technique to Lin Fei.

The Falling Leaves Saber Technique emphasized lightness and agility. Although it was also fast, being light and elusive was the essence of the Falling Leaves Saber Technique.

"Your Falling Leaves Saber Technique has received the careful guidance of a perfection-level expert, and it had understood the ultimate essence of the Falling Leaves Saber Technique, breaking through to the perfection level!"

"Your Nine Shadow Saber Technique had a fierce battle with the Falling Leaves Saber Technique and won, and the Falling Leaves Saber Technique was automatically integrated into the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, enhancing its foundation."

"Your progress in the Nine Shadow Saber Technique has increased by 2%."

A series of voices echoed in Lin Fei's mind.

"Thank you, Senior Brother Bai," said Lin Fei.

Bai Wei was skeptical. Did he really earn 3,000 contribution points just like that?

He didn't think that Lin Fei could learn much in just an hour.

But anyway, his task was completed. Whether or not Lin Fei learned anything was none of his concern. So Bai Wei turned and left.

Lin Fei returned to his room.

"System."

Lin Fei immediately pulled up the system panel.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Body Refining Eighth Level

Giant Whale Body Refining Technique: Eighth Level (51% Progress)

Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist: Transcendence (1% Progress)

Nine Shadow Saber Technique: Perfection (10% Progress)

Vajra Iron Cloth: Seventh Level (21% Progress)

Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique: Seventh Level (12% Progress)

Flowing Clothes Skill: Mastery (56% Progress)

Lin Fei had many martial arts. Although their progress was slow, it was still gradually improving.

The only martial art that had not made any progress at all was the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

No one had taken on the task that Lin Fei had posted.

Lin Fei wondered if he should withdraw the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist task?

But even if he withdrew it. He could only recover 5,000 contribution points. It wouldn't make much of a difference.

"The progress of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique is also very slow. Fusing with the perfection-level Falling Leaves Saber Technique only increased the progress by 2%. It's only at 10%, still far from cultivating saber intent." Lin Fei shook his head.

He felt that investing his energy and resources into the Nine Shadow Saber Technique might have been a wrong decision.

The slight improvement in the Nine Shadow Saber Technique wouldn't make much of a difference.

"The Nine Shadows Saber Technique seems to have changed a little after fusing with the Falling Leaves Saber technique." Lin Fei directly slashed the Saber in the air.

"Swish."

The sharp saber suddenly erupted.

"Hmm? This is..."

Lin Fei's eyes lit up slightly. He found some unexpected surprises.
