218. Killing a Foundation Establishment is as easy as killing a chicken

"What?"

The Foundation Establishment cultivator who controlled the Black Blood Needle had an inexplicably horrified look on his face. He was frantically urging his spiritual power, displaying his seal. However, it was useless. His Black Blood Needle seemed to be firmly suppressed. It couldn't move at all.

"Swoosh."

Lin Fei stepped out of the Sky Reaching Tower.

"Senior, it really is you, please save me..."

Seeing Lin Fei, Zhao Mingyu was overjoyed. This was simply a narrow escape.

"Zhao Mingyu..."

Of course, Lin Fei recognized Zhao Mingyu. Just didn't expect that Zhao Mingyu had already established his foundation. As for the three Foundation Establishments that were pursuing Zhao Mingyu, Lin Fei had also heard Zhao Mingyu's words.

Pirates! These three Foundation Establishment True Persons were all pirates.

Lin Fei had a thought. The Sky Reaching Tower instantly flew into his body. At the same time, he moved. With one step, he arrived in front of Zhao Mingyu. In front of Zhao Mingyu, the Black Blood Needle was still trembling. However, it was suppressed by Lin Fei's saber momentum, unable to move.

Lin Fei stretched out his fair hand and directly reached for the Black Blood Needle.

"Hmm? He's courting death! Flesh and blood body dare to touch my Black Blood Needle. Even if you're a Martial Saint, the Black Blood Needle can instantly pierce through your palm covered with True Essence."

Seeing Lin Fei actually trying to grab the Black Blood Needle directly. The owner of the Black Blood Needle couldn't help but sneer in his heart. They had already seen Lin Fei was a Martial Saint.

After all, the difference between a Martial Saint and a Foundation Establishment True Person was too great. The vitality of a Martial Saint was incredibly powerful, like a blazing sun. It was too conspicuous.

His Black Blood Needle specialized in breaking flesh and blood. There had been several Martial Saints died from the Black Blood Needle before. However, when Lin Fei's hand grabbed the Black Blood Needle, he gave it a hard pinch.

"Crack."

The Black Blood Needle broke! The spiritual light flickered, and then completely disappeared. Lin Fei casually threw the Black Blood Needle into the sea.

"The magical treasure is not bad, but it's too weak."

Lin Fei said in a calm tone. He really didn't use much strength just now. This time, Lin Fei just wanted to test how strong his physical body was now. After all, he had consumed so many sea beasts this time. The blood essence accumulated in Lin Fei's body was already unimaginably large. Although it was still far from condensing the Energy Flower, compared to his previous body, it was at least ten times stronger!

So, with a casual pinch of his physical body, Lin Fei found that the magic treasure was even more fragile than he had imagined, and it broke directly.

Now Lin Fei understood, why back then, Zhao Canglong, facing the elder who had condensed an Energy Flower, exploded with one punch.

Perhaps, the elder who had condensed an Energy Flower back then didn't even use ten percent of his strength.

Once Martial Saints started cultivating their physical body and reached the point of condensing an Energy Flower, that would be indestructible and incredibly powerful. Even the magic treasure of a Foundation Establishment True Person would be easily crushed.

"Not good, his physical body is too powerful, and he's probably not far from condensing the Energy Flower."

"We're no match for a Martial Saint like him."

"Retreat quickly!"

Seeing this scene, the three Foundation Establishments were greatly shocked, knowing that the Martial Saint in front of them was probably not easy to provoke!

Those who could crush magic treasures barehanded, even if they hadn't condensed an Energy Flower, were not far off. This was a top-tier Martial Saint!

Such a Martial Saint is very terrifying. Impervious to swords and spears, immune to fire and water, indestructible. Even magic treasures can be easily crushed.

They were just at the early stage of the Foundation Establishment, and they didn't have powerful magic treasures in their hands. Facing such a top-tier Martial Saint, how could they take advantage? Moreover, they were pirates!

Therefore, without any hesitation, the three Foundation Establishment cultivators made a decision and ran away. Even the owner of the Black Blood Needle, at this moment, was forcibly enduring the pain of backlash and quickly fleeing.

"Hum?"

But just as they wanted to leave, a vast saber momentum enveloped them, instantly trapping them inside.

"Suppress!"

Lin Fei uttered a single word.

"Boom."

The saber momentum was suppressed with all its might. The three Foundation Establishments were like they were stuck in the mud. They were finding it difficult to move. Even the circulation of spiritual power in their bodies became somewhat difficult.

"Saber momentum? How can there be such a terrifying saber momentum, who are you?"

The faces of the three Foundation Establishment cultivators changed dramatically. Even if Lin Fei was about to condense an Energy Flower, they could still escape. But with a Martial Saint with saber momentum, their chances of escape were slim to none. There was only one way. That was to kill the opponent!

"Spare no effort, kill him!"

The three Foundation Establishment True Persons were very decisive. Knowing that Lin Fei had a saber momentum, they could not escape. Therefore, they made a prompt decision. Their internal spiritual power surged. They used their magic treasures and spells again, bombarding Lin Fei.

Lin Fei's saber momentum was indeed strong. It could suppress some of the power of a Foundation Establishment True Person. But it wasn't to the point of completely suppressing the other party, making them immobile.

The three Foundation Establishments were very decisive when they started, they exerted their magic bodies. In the void, a large amount of Spiritual Qi was gathered. The entire saber momentum was filled with storms, flames, etc. These were all spells. There were even magic treasures!

The Black Blood Needle could be suppressed by the saber momentum, but that was because the Black Blood Needle was not very powerful. For other magic treasures, Lin Fei's saber momentum might not be able to suppress them.

Seeing the three's fierce counterattack, Zhao Mingyu couldn't help but tremble in his heart. However, Lin Fei was as calm as ever. In his view, this was a true Foundation Establishment True Person!

The Foundation Establishments he had killed before were too weak. Whether in will or strength, both were weak. As for the sea beasts, although they were also at the Foundation Establishment level, they had no magic treasures, only some innate abilities. Compared to Foundation Establishment, they were somewhat inferior. And these three Foundation Establishment True Persons were all pirates. Being able to fall into piracy, apart from anything else, their combat experience was definitely very rich.

"Swoosh."

Lin Fei reached out his hand. There wasn't much movement. He simply flicked his finger. Three strands of saber essence flew out instantly, condensing into three large sabers.

"Boom."

As soon as these three large sabers appeared, they immediately erupted with an earth-shattering momentum. One of the large sabers swung out. It was like a brilliant sun. Moreover, it seemed to burn the void. This was Flame Burning Heaven Saber. One saber swung out, burned the sky, and boiled the sea!

There was also a large saber turned into a rainbow light. This was the Rainbow Transformation Saber. Its speed was as fast as a startled swan.

There was another saber, holy, grand, magnificent. One saber swung out like a god coming down from the heavens, possessing infinite power. This was the Holy Spirit Saber Technique.

Three great saber techniques were all top-tier saber techniques at the Martial Saint level.

"You..."

They felt Lin Fei was so bold that he didn't make a move and he only used a strand of true essence. Of course, they didn't know about saber essence. They just took saber essence as the true essence of a Martial Saint.

Even if Lin Fei had saber momentum, the three Foundation Establishment True Persons felt insulted. Just a strand of true essence, and he wanted to defeat them? It was a fantasy. They were also dignified Foundation Establishment True Persons! And they were even notorious pirates!

"Puff."

However, the Rainbow Transformation Saber vanished in a flash. Then, a Foundation Establishment True Person widened his eyes as if he couldn't believe it. From his head, a line of blood emerged. With a "plop", the body of a Foundation Establishment True Person fell into the sea. He was dead! The Rainbow Transformation Saber was like a rainbow. Against the enemy, it always took only one slash!

"Boom."

Next was the Holy Spirit Saber Technique. Upright and open-minded, with one slash, the other party's magic treasure was directly shattered.

"Crack."

The magic treasure shattered. The saber essence instantly turned his law body into minced meat. The second Foundation Establishment was also dead. Only the last Foundation Establishment True Person was left. His soul was in great fear. How would he dare to make a move?

The Flame Burning Heaven Saber seemed to carry a sky full of flames. One slash swung out. The flames were rampant, and instantly engulfed the last Foundation Establishment True Person. In an instant, he was burned to ashes. He was dead!

Three Foundation Establishment True Persons had been chasing Zhao Mingyu before. Now, Lin Fei killed them all with a flick of his finger and a strand of saber essence.

Moreover, in the entire process, those three Foundation Establishment True Persons had no power to fight back.

"Killing Foundation Establishment is like killing a chicken..."

This sentence emerged in Zhao Mingyu's mind. At this moment, wasn't Lin Fei's method of killing the Foundation Establishment like killing a chicken?

Zhao Mingyu thought that he had succeeded in Foundation Establishment and he was a dignified Foundation Establishment True Person, feeling that he might not be far behind Lin Fei.

But what was the result? The further he went and the higher he stood, the more terrifying he found Lin Fei was. That was far beyond his imagination.

"Swoosh."

Lin Fei turned around, standing with his hands behind his back, looking calmly at Zhao Mingyu. But it made Zhao Mingyu feel his scalp tingle. His whole body felt like it had fallen into an ice cellar, the pressure was immense.

"Zhao Mingyu, what exactly happened?"

After a while, Lin Fei slowly asked.
