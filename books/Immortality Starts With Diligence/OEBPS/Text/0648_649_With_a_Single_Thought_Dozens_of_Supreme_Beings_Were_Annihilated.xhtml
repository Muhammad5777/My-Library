649. With a Single Thought, Dozens of Supreme Beings Were Annihilated!

Dozens of Supremes from the Dark Demon Domain were utterly suppressed by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. For a moment, the supremes of the Divine Realm, who witnessed all this, were overwhelmingly thrilled.

"Lin Fei suppressed dozens of supremes from the Dark Demon Domain?"

"Yes, absolutely true!"

"Not just dozens of Dark Demon Domain Supremes? Even the Dark Demon Lord himself was put to flight by Lin Fei."

"If I am not mistaken, the tactic used by the Dark Demon Lord just now was capable of slaying Supremes. Yet, it was to no avail against Lin Fei, who forced him to flee instead."

"Hmph, had the Dark Demon Lord not fled, he too would have been suppressed by Lin Fei!"

"How powerful has Lin Fei's Eternal Realm become? It seems our efforts in painstakingly gathering the essence of the world were not in vain."

"Hahaha, Lin Fei's strength now, though not yet a Ruler, must surely be close!"

The eight Supremes were overjoyed. Their discerning eyes could recognize the uniqueness of Lin Fei. Lin Fei's Eternal Realm seemed to have grown beyond their imagination.

Lin Fei alone defeated the Dark Demon Lord and suppressed dozens of supremes. Such strength was no ordinary feat; even top supremes could not achieve this. Only a Ruler. Only the ruler of a realm could possess such power.

At that moment, within the Eternal Realm, the dozens of Dark Demon Domain supremes were all suppressed by Lin Fei. These supremes knew what fate awaited them and thus roared in fury, each unleashing their skills in a desperate attempt to escape Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

"What kind of world is this? Too terrifying; we can't even break out of this realm."

"They said Lin Fei could transform into a realm, but it was never this frightening before."

"We are dozens of supremes, yet now, we are utterly immobilized by Lin Fei's realm, completely unable to move."

"I even feel that if we dare to resist desperately, Lin Fei's realm could completely crush us!"

"Yes, I feel it too. It seems we are going to die..."

Suddenly, amidst their desperation, a familiar figure appeared before them—Lin Fei! The Martial Ancestor Lin Fei!

Though merely a Martial Ancestor from the Divine Realm and presumably not a supreme, it was he who defeated the Dark Demon Lord and suppressed the dozens of Dark Demon Domain supremes. A feat akin to a myth, yet a stark reality!

Lin Fei swept his gaze over them. He did not move to slay these high supremes, for he had his reasons.

Lin Fei spoke slowly, "I have not killed you, which you must have already surmised. Tell me, where is the Dark Demon Domain located? Or perhaps, escort me to the Dark Demon Domain. In due time, I will either release you into the Chaos or send you back to the Dark Demon Domain."

Lin Fei's words sent ripples of astonishment through the supremes of the Dark Demon Domain. Lin Fei was inquiring about the location of the Dark Demon Domain. What did this imply?

Everyone understood clearly. Once the location of the Dark Demon Domain was known, Lin Fei might seek it out and, even more, invade it just as it lives. Lin Fei was intent on retribution, an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, refusing to suffer any disadvantage! Even if the Dark Demon Lord fled back to his realm, Lin Fei would pursue him.

However, the supremes, one after another, did not conceal the truth. Instead, they scoffed, "Lin Fei, even if we tell you the location of the Dark Demon Domain, you wouldn't dare go."

"Indeed, what harm is there in telling you?"

"As for the location of the Dark Demon Domain, we naturally won't hide it from you."

These supremes were so cooperative?

Not a single one intended to conceal the location of the Dark Demon Domain. Lin Fei's mind raced, and he instantly grasped their intention. These supremes even wished for Lin Fei to head immediately to the Dark Demon Domain.

The reason was straightforward. There was a Ruler in the Dark Demon Domain! Once a realm bore a Ruler, everything changed. Within the realm of a Ruler, the Ruler was invincible!

Should Lin Fei recklessly venture to the Dark Demon Domain, the Dark Demon Lord would undoubtedly teach him a severe lesson, even possibly slaying him.

This was also why the Dark Demon Lord had fled at the first opportunity. As long as he returned to the Dark Demon Domain, he would no longer fear Lin Fei. The Dark Demon Lord in his own realm was truly formidable.

"Ruler... I truly wish to see, what capabilities this so-called 'Ruler' truly has."

A sharp gleam flickered in Lin Fei's eyes. He had already experienced the strength of the Dark Demon Lord. Apart from that orb, the power of the Dark Demon Lord was, frankly, negligible to Lin Fei now.

But the Dark Demon Lord was a Ruler. If in the Dark Demon Domain, that changed everything. It was said that Lin Fei's current strength neared or even matched that of a Ruler.

But after all, he was not a true Ruler. Nor had Lin Fei battled a true Ruler before. Thus, how his strength compared to a Ruler remained unclear to all.

"I have no need for so many supremes," Lin Fei's eyes chilled.

With a mere thought, he fiercely summoned the power of the Eternal Realm. A grotesque squelch resounded. In the next moment, from all directions, a terrifying force began to crush them.

Previously, to slay a Supreme or to shatter a Supreme's true form was not so simple for Lin Fei. It required substantial power, often necessitating his utmost effort. But now, it was different.

With a single thought, merely by invoking the power of the Eternal Realm, it was outright domination. The true demonic forms of these Dark Demon Supremes shattered one after another, ground into dust and completely transformed into the flesh and essence of the Eternal Realm, absorbed wholly by it. In just a few breaths' time, dozens of supremes were reduced to merely three.

Yes, only three Dark Demon Domain supremes remained. Witnessing this, the trio was instantly enveloped in an icy chill, as if a bone-deep cold had seized them.

Dozens of Supremes, all dead. They hadn't even seen Lin Fei lift a finger, yet dozens of supremes had perished. The power of the Eternal Realm was beyond their wildest imaginations.

These were dozens of supremes!

The Dark Demon Domain had never faced such a colossal loss in its invasions of other realms. Lin Fei had nearly annihilated all the Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain who had invaded the Divine Realm. A clean sweep!

"Alright, only the three of you remain," Lin Fei declared. "Speak out of turn, and you will meet the same fate as them, executed without mercy!"

After speaking, Lin Fei's figure vanished in an instant. The presence within the Eternal Realm was merely a strand of Lin Fei's consciousness.

Now that he had achieved his goal, he no longer lingered, withdrawing his consciousness back. At this time, the eyes of the eight supremes also fell upon Lin Fei. Each of their gazes radiated shock and anticipation.

"Lin... Fellow Lin," they stammered. "The Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain, were they truly slain by you? With your own strength, you defeated the Dark Demon Lord and slew dozens of supremes. Are you sure you haven't become the Ruler of the Divine Realm?"

The Azure Emperor Supreme knew Lin Fei well. Yet, at this moment, he too seemed to scarcely believe the scene before his eyes.
