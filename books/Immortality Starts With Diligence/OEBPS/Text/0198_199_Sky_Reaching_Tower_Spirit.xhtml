199. Sky Reaching Tower Spirit

"Sky Reaching Tower!"

The next moment, True Person Tongyou's spiritual power surged. As a Foundation Establishment True Person, naturally, he can control the mountain protection formation of the Sky Reaching Sect.

Therefore, facing Lin Fei's indescribable terrifying saber, True Person Tongyou didn't hesitate. He immediately activated the Sky Reaching Tower, maximizing the power of the mountain protection formation! He had to block this saber!

Moreover, he was confident in blocking this saber. After all, the confidence passed down for thousands of years. Generations of Foundation Establishment seniors told him the mountain protection formation is absolutely unbreakable at the Foundation Establishment level. No matter how strong Lin Fei was, he was still a Martial Saint, still at the Foundation Establishment level.

The Sky Reaching Tower shone brightly. Surged Spiritual Qi poured into the mountain protection formation. The phantom of the Sky Reaching Tower expanded continually as if it had appeared in the void in a flash.

At this moment, there were no other thoughts in Lin Fei's heart. In his eyes, there was only this saber!

"Boom."

Finally, this saber fell! It struck the mountain protection formation. Instantly, ripples on the mountain protection formation spread out continuously. Ring after ring. Even the Sky Reaching Tower began to shake violently. The phantom of the Sky Reaching Tower flickered on and off as if it was about to disperse.

All disciples of the Sky Reaching Sect were looking up at the sky. There, one thousand meters tall sabers, like an enormous mountain, gave people a strong sense of oppression. It was as if the next moment, the entire Sky Reaching Sect would be split in half!

"No, impossible! Our Sky Reaching Sect's mountain protection formation, for thousands of years, no one has been able to break it. Not even the founder of Great Chang of the past, and not even the saber saint Lin Fei now!"

True Person Tongyou let out a loud roar. He felt the danger! The mountain protection formation was shaking violently at this moment. Together with the entire Sky Reaching Sect also shaking violently.

Many people looked at the saber in the void with fear. This was the first time the Sky Reaching Sect faced such a big crisis. However, was the mountain protection formation really unbreakable?

"Bang."

The next moment, the phantom of the Sky Reaching Tower suddenly shattered. With the shattering of the phantom of the Sky Reaching Tower. Next, the mountain protection formation also started to totter.

"Rumble."

Finally, the Sky Reaching Sect shook violently. The mountain protection formation hadn't been broken for thousands of years. At this moment, it finally collapsed! Instantly, the sky was filled with that boundless, terrifying to the extreme saber!

"It's broken, how could the mountain protection formation be broken? Impossible, this is impossible! Our Ten Immortal Sects inherited for thousands of years. Even the founder of Great Chang couldn't break the mountain protection formation. Could it be that Saber Saint Lin Fei is stronger than the founder of Great Chang? Today, is it the great catastrophe of our Sky Reaching Sect that hasn't happened for six thousand years?"

True Person Tongyou's soul was trembling.

A great calamity! A real life-and-death calamity! Every Immortal Sect, every Dao lineage, will face a great calamity.

Perhaps, many people didn't know how ancient the Sky Reaching Sect was. It had been passed down for six thousand years! It was arguably the oldest Immortal Sect in all of Great Chang. Without a doubt!

After six thousand years of ups and downs, the Sky Reaching Sect had weathered them all and maintained its lineage intact. Now, however, it is facing such a calamity of extermination. Once the saber in the void fell, the entire Sky Reaching Sect, they were afraid they would turn into ruins.

"No, the Sky Reaching Sect will not perish! With my spirit, awaken the spirit of the tower! Tower Spirit, please awake and save our Sky Reaching Sect!"

True Person Tongyou seemed to have performed a secret method. Suddenly, his whole body surged with spiritual power. Countless Dao marks struck the Sky Reaching Tower.

"Hum."

Suddenly, the towering Sky Reaching Tower actually began to shake violently. At the same time, it was as if a beast's roar, emanated from the void.

"Roar......"

A strange ancient being's phantom emerged from the Sky Reaching Tower.

"Who has awakened me?"

This huge phantom shook its head, seeming somewhat dazed.

"Tower Spirit, our Sky Reaching Sect is facing a crisis of extermination, someone has broken the mountain protection formation, please, tower spirit, make a move and kill the invading enemy!"

True Person Tongyou was overjoyed when he saw the tower spirit appear. He immediately reported. In fact, True Person Tongyou was just giving it a try. As for the spirit of the Sky Reaching Tower, he had always thought of it as a legend. After all, the Sky Reaching Tower was just a magic treasure. How could it have a Tower Spirit?

But now, at the critical moment of life and death for the Sky Reaching Sect, True Person Tongyou could only give it a try. Unexpectedly, there really was a tower spirit awakening.

"The critical moment of life and death for the Sky Reaching Sect? How dare anyone attack the Sky Reaching Sect? Don't they know that the Sky Reaching Sect is a Golden Core Dao lineage? Don't worry, with me here, no one can exterminate the Sky Reaching Sect!"

With the awakening of the tower spirit, the Sky Reaching Tower suddenly shrank.

"Rumble."

The Sky Reaching Tower suddenly shrank from towering into the clouds to a treasure tower of tens of meters. Although it was much smaller, on the Sky Reaching Tower, precious light was abundant and colors were overflowing. It emitted a very strong spiritual pressure. Even True Person Tongyou, at this moment, felt as if he was being suppressed by the Sky Reaching Tower.

In a single moment, elation surged within his heart. A glimmer of hope revealed itself upon his countenance. The moment Tongyou awoke the Tower Spirit, the Sky Reaching Tower transformed into a splendid structure, several dozen meters tall, suspended in the ethereal void.

All of this took less than a breath's time. Yet Lin Fei harbored no hesitation. A saber essence of a thousand meters, after cleaving through the protective mountain formation, proceeded downwards, striking mercilessly upon the Sky Reaching Tower.

"Boom!"

The void resonated, vibrating abruptly.

"Ah... Damn it, what kind of saber intent is this? No, it's saber momentum! The combination of saber momentum and true essence? When did martial arts become like this, where saber momentum can be combined with true essence?"

The Tower Spirit shrieked in agony. This one strike from Lin Fei seemed to inflict on the Tower Spirit an unparalleled torment.

"Crack."

The next moment, the Sky Reaching Tower dimmed, its divine glow waning. A fissure was formed. Although it was a mere, insignificant crack and it was hardly worth mentioning in comparison to the dozens of meters tall Sky Reaching Tower, don't forget about the one thousand meters long saber! Moreover, it was saber momentum! Should this strike truly descend, the Sky Reaching Tower would surely be destroyed!

"Damn you, the old ghost of Tongming, the inheritance you left for your disciples is on the verge of being obliterated. And you expect me to be their scapegoat? Impossible! Sky Reaching Tower, retract!"

In the next moment, the Tower Spirit roared in rage. The dozens of meters tall Sky Reaching Tower astonishingly contracted. In a blink of an eye, it reduced to the size of a grain, vanishing into thin air.

At this moment, True Person Tongyou was dumbfounded. The disciples of the Sky Reaching Sect who cultivated Qi were equally flabbergasted.

The Sky Reaching Tower...had actually disappeared. The proud Tower Spirit that stood there just moments ago, thought of as the savior of the Sky Reaching Sect, was believed to be able to turn the tide.

And what was the outcome?

With a single strike, the spirit of the Sky Reaching Tower had actually fled!

"Thud."

Lin Fei's one thousand meters long saber fiercely crashed into the grounds of the Sky Reaching Sect.

"No..."

The True Person Tongyou, devoid of any resistance, was engulfed by the one thousand meters long saber essence, and instantly ground into dust. Many of the disciples and elders failed to escape, most of them butchered by the vast saber essence.

"Rumble."

The peaks of the Sky Reaching Sect collapsed one after another. Even the ground below bore a colossal chasm. The Sky Reaching Sect was thoroughly reduced to ruins! A millennia-old inheritance was destroyed in a moment!
