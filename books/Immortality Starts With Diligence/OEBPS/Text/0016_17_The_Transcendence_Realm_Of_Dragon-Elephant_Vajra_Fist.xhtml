"Boom."

The transcended Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist punched down, making a sound like a real dragon's roar.

In fact, this was not a dragon's roar. It was the sound of the tremendous force, impacting the zombie's body.

"Splash."

Lin Fei could clearly see that The zombie's body trembled. Inside, there was a sound of "crackling."

The transcended Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist could produce a shockwave. It shattered the zombie's internal organs and bones in an instant. So even the zombie was affected to some extent. However, the zombie still didn't fall.

"Zombies are originally corpses, so even if their internal organs are pulverized, they still won't die. But the zombie will definitely be affected. Moreover, zombies also have weak points. Since it's not in the chest or abdomen, it must be in the head."

Lin Fei observed carefully. The current zombie, although not falling, its actions were still somewhat affected.

For example, its ribs were broken. So its movements became slower.

Lin Fei approached the zombie again and punched the zombie's head.

"Boom."

The zombie's head was extremely hard. It could even be called the hardest part of its body. Therefore, no one would choose to attack a zombie's head.

However, Lin Fei's Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist utilized the power of shock, hitting the zombie's head. It instantly shattered the Blood Core within the zombie's skull.

The zombie relied on the Blood Core in its skull. Once the Blood Core were shattered, the zombie would be useless.

"Thump."

With one blow from Lin Fei, the zombie fell to the ground with a loud noise, motionless.

"Success!"

A hint of joy appeared on Lin Fei's face.

However, seeing the zombie fall, the old Taoist's face changed drastically.

"How is it possible? How could you, a mere Body Refining martial artist, defeat the iron corpse that I refined?"

The old Taoist was obviously caught off guard. He never thought that a mere Body Refining martial artist could defeat his zombie.

His zombie was extremely strong, and immune to sword and spear attacks. Only an Acquired martial artist using inner power to attack the zombie's body would have a chance to destroy the zombie.

As for a Body Refining martial artist?

Even if they could repel the zombie, killing it was absolutely impossible. But Lin Fei made the impossible possible.

"Evil Taoist, die!"

Lin Fei didn't want to miss this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. He held his breath and instantly rushed into the temple.

At this moment, there was only the old Taoist and the fifteen or sixteen-year-old little Taoist behind him in the temple.

The old Taoist was panicked. Seeing Lin Fei come in, he shouted, "How dare you, a mere Body Refining martial artist, be so presumptuous in front of me?"

The old Taoist took out a pill from his pocket and swallowed it.

Immediately, the old Taoist's body was covered with bulging veins, and his body swelled to a terrifying height of about two meters.

"Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist!"

Lin Fei punched out without any hesitation.

"Boom."

The old Taoist's body trembled.

"Ah..."

Following that, the old Taoist spat out blood. His entire body deflated like a punctured ball, instantly returning to his original appearance and the entire person was sent flying by Lin Fei, falling heavily to the ground.

"Slap."

Lin Fei immediately went forward to extinguish the sandalwood. Gradually, the sandalwood poison in the temple disappeared.

"Is he dead?"

Lin Fei waited for a while, but he didn't see any movement from the old Taoist. However, Lin Fei didn't approach. Instead, from a distance, he directly threw the large saber in his hand.

"Thud."

The saber penetrated the body, directly cutting off the old Taoist's head. Even so, the old Taoist still didn't make any movement.

Lin Fei was finally at ease.

Without the protection of the zombie, old Taoist was actually useless and unable to withstand even a single blow.

With the old Taoist dead, the four zombies outside the temple also stopped moving.

Lin Fei approached, using the power of shock, shattering the Blood Core within the skulls of the four zombies one by one to prevent them from going berserk and causing any accidents.

At this moment, there was only the fifteen or sixteen-year-old little Taoist left in the temple.

The old Taoist was already dead. The little Taoist's face was somewhat pale. Seeing Lin Fei and the others coming in with sabers, looking murderous, the little Taoist was terrified.

He immediately knelt down and said, "Hero, spare my life. All of this was done by the old Taoist, it has nothing to do with me, spare me, please!"

The little Taoist kept kowtowing and his forehead was already bleeding.

"What are you to the evil Taoist?"

Lin Fei asked.

"I... I am just someone who was captured by the old Taoist to serve him."

"Serve? I don't think so, you're the old Taoist's disciple!"

A glint flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

The little Taoist's body trembled, and he quickly said, "Hero, I never wanted to be the old Taoist's disciple, he forced me. I'm just a servant, a child in his eyes."

The little Taoist's desire to survive was strong.

Lin Fei looked at the old Taoist's corpse and then looked at the zombies outside.

A thought crossed his mind. So, he took the little Taoist aside for interrogation.

"I'll ask, you answer. Tell the truth, and I can spare your life. If you're dishonest, you'll join your master in death."

"Yes, ask away, hero. I will be honest and answer every question."

A glimmer of joy appeared on the little Taoist's face. As long as Lin Fei didn't kill him, everything was negotiable.
