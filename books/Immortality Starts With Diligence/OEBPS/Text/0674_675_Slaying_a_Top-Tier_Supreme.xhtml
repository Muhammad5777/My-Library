675. Slaying a Top-Tier Supreme

With a thunderous roar, Nebula Supreme immediately felt a terrifying power crushing in from all directions, causing his heart to palpitate. An intense sense of danger enveloped him, giving rise to a foreboding that he might actually perish.

"How could I possibly die?" Nebula Supreme was utterly astonished. How could he, a distinguished top-tier Supreme, possibly face death?

Typically, only a Master of Chaos or a similarly paramount entity like the Supreme of Doomsday, who stood above all others, could slay a top-tier Supreme.

Did Lin Fei possess such strength?

Regardless of Nebula Supreme's disbelief and shock, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was indeed bearing down in full force. A horrifying power struck Nebula Supreme directly.

With a resounding "boom," the next moment saw Nebula Supreme's body crushed to dust.

"What? Nebula Supreme is dead?"

"This Supreme, who became one with his realm, has he actually slain Nebula Supreme?"

"Nebula Supreme was a top-tier Supreme; only the likes of the Supreme of Doomsday are known to easily slay such beings."

"But he is not the Supreme of Doomsday. Could it be that a new force capable of rivaling the Supreme of Doomsday has arisen in the chaos?"

Many were shocked. They had come to the Anlan Realm to compete for the Chaos Pearl, knowing well that ultimately, only a top-tier Supreme could likely claim it. Yet, they had hoped to try their luck, for the Chaos Pearl was crucial for Supremes to advance further.

However, witnessing even a top-tier Supreme like Nebula Supreme slain without resistance sobered them significantly. They were starkly reminded of the vast gap between their own powers and those capable of such feats, almost beyond comprehension.

"Wait, Nebula Supreme isn't dead."

"True, Nebula Supreme has not perished; his physical form has merely been annihilated. It is exceedingly difficult to utterly destroy a top-tier Supreme."

"Nebula Supreme still remains within his adversary's realm."

For a moment, several Supremes discerned the truth of the matter. Nebula Supreme had not died. Naturally, Lin Fei was aware of this as well. If other Supremes could see it, how could Lin Fei not?

Nebula Supreme's survival skills were formidable. Although his physical form was obliterated, his particles endured—particles robust enough that even Lin Fei's Eternal Realm could not erase them in short order. Indeed, a top-tier Supreme!

Lin Fei, though immensely powerful, found it improbable to instantly annihilate a top-tier Supreme. Yet, he was not concerned. Having ensnared Nebula Supreme within his Eternal Realm, his foe's fate was virtually sealed. Even if not immediately fatal, prolonged exposure to the Eternal Realm's erosive forces would inevitably lead to Nebula Supreme's demise.

As time passed, Nebula Supreme would surely be worn away completely within the relentless confines of the Eternal Realm, powerless to alter his grim fate.

"Spare me..." A particle of Nebula Supreme weakly took on a humanoid form, pleading for mercy.

With a thought from Lin Fei, that form collapsed entirely. Lin Fei was uninterested in hearing Nebula Supreme's pleas. His stance was unyielding: execution. Conveniently, the essence of Nebula Supreme's being would nourish the Eternal Realm.

As for any rage or destructive attempts Nebula Supreme might unleash within the Eternal Realm, Lin Fei harbored no worries. He could suppress any rebellion effortlessly. This was Lin Fei's power—holding the Eternal Realm, he feared no Supreme.

The Clairvoyant Supreme's expression was complex. He knew Lin Fei was formidable, which was why he had enlisted his help in the Anlan Realm to compete for the Chaos Pearl.

However, even he had not anticipated the extent of Lin Fei's strength. Nebula Supreme, whom he could not subdue, was utterly powerless before Lin Fei, crushed and suppressed within moments inside the Eternal Realm. This outcome was beyond even the Clairvoyant Supreme's expectations.

Nevertheless, this was a fortuitous development. At least for the Clairvoyant Supreme, the stronger Lin Fei was, the greater his chances of securing the Chaos Pearl became. Only with a powerful Supreme like Lin Fei in command could all other Supremes be deterred.

Thus, the Clairvoyant Supreme sneered, "Who else wishes to enter the Anlan Realm? Feel free to enter, but remember, entering is easy; leaving is not."

His gaze swept across the area. Those Supremes who had previously been eager to try their luck now halted, their eyes complex as they looked towards the Anlan Realm and Lin Fei. They seemed wary, contemplative.

The Chaos Pearl was indeed valuable, but their own lives were even more so. With Lin Fei, a formidable being capable of slaying a top-tier Supreme, what were they in comparison?

By slaying Nebula Supreme, Lin Fei and the Clairvoyant Supreme had established their dominance. With the formation surrounding the Anlan Realm, no Supreme dared to object. Lin Fei had no plans to enter the Anlan Realm himself; instead, he waited quietly, adopting a 'wait-and-see' approach with the Clairvoyant Supreme.

This strategy was evidently approved by Lin Fei. Whoever obtained the Chaos Pearl would surely try to leave the Anlan Realm at the first opportunity, only to run into him and the Clairvoyant Supreme. Lin Fei's task was simple: to subdue anyone who attempted to leave.

Days passed—one, two, three, four, five...

The struggle for the Chaos Pearl was evidently fierce. Almost ten days flew by, and surprisingly, not a single Supreme had left the Anlan Realm. They were all immersed in the battle for the Chaos Pearl.

Given that there were at least dozens of Supremes within the realm, including several top-tier ones, the absence of any departures indicated that the Chaos Pearl was still within the Anlan Realm.

Lin Fei and the Clairvoyant Supreme were patient. No one leaving meant the Chaos Pearl was still up for grabs. This was a good thing. They simply continued to wait.

However, after ten days, someone within the Anlan Realm could no longer restrain themselves. A Supreme attempted to leave the Anlan Realm but immediately collided with the formation set by the Clairvoyant Supreme.

"What is this?" The bewildered Supreme was unaware of the events transpiring outside.

"Ah? Someone has come out!" The Clairvoyant Supreme's spirit stirred. Finally, after ten days, someone had emerged. With so many Supremes inside, it took ten days for one to attempt an exit. Could it be that this Supreme, possibly in possession of the Chaos Pearl, was trying to make a stealthy escape?

This Supreme appeared to be an ordinary one, but who could say for certain that only a top-tier Supreme could secure the Chaos Pearl? It was not a given!

The Clairvoyant Supreme approached, his tone even, "The vicinity of the Anlan Realm has been sealed by us. If you wish to leave, hand over the Chaos Pearl. Or, we shall destroy your physical body to thoroughly check if you are concealing the Chaos Pearl. Only after our meticulous inspection and confirmation that you do not possess the Chaos Pearl will you be permitted to leave."

These words incensed the Supreme, who scoffed in response, "Well, well, I didn't expect to encounter such domineering behavior. Choosing not to compete within the Anlan Realm for the Chaos Pearl, but instead to lie in wait outside. Hmph, I intend to leave, and no one can stop me!"

With a rage-fueled laugh, the Supreme's figure flickered slightly, indicating the use of some unique technique. In the next moment, he transformed into a streak of light, preparing to burst through the formation.
