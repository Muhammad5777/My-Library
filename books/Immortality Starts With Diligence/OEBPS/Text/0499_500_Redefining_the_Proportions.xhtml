500. Redefining the Proportions

Lin Fei cast a fleeting glance at Heart Demon Martial God and North Wind True Person. The trio remained silent.

Eventually, Lin Fei shook his head, conceding, "Ultimately, I still couldn't slay Bloodthirsty Martial God. It seems I won't be able to claim half of the world's origin."

His gaze lingered on Heart Demon Martial God and North Wind True Person. Despite his assertion of not gaining half the origin, it was unmistakably clear to all.

Lin Fei was resolutely intent on securing half of the world's origin!

North Wind True Person's eyes narrowed slightly, he cast a deep, seemingly reproachful look at Heart Demon Martial God. Why had he brought such a formidable force into the fray?

Heart Demon Martial God could only respond with a helpless shake of his head. North Wind True Person felt cornered, having invited Heart Demon Martial God, who, in turn, brought Lin Fei into the mix.

Lin Fei was far from a greenhorn Martial God; he was akin to a massive crocodile, with jaws wide enough to swallow anyone whole. Once Lin Fei had entered the True Spirit Realm, he was not going to leave.

However, the situation being irreversible, North Wind True Person had no other choice. To give up now was inconceivable. After all, he had steadfastly fortified his position in the True Spirit Realm over thousands of years to reach this juncture.

To just abandon it all now would render his millennia of hard work fruitless. The world's origin was something he was determined to acquire. But the distribution could be renegotiated.

"Martial God Lin, with your formidable strength, our united trio can assuredly obtain the origin of the True Spirit Realm! However, the distribution ratio needs reconsideration. With your overwhelming power capable of dominating Bloodthirsty Martial God single-handedly, we will rely on you greatly in the future. Thus, it's only fair for you to claim fifty percent of the world's origin.

However, Heart Demon Martial God, with Lin Martial God's presence, your unique abilities might not have much scope to manifest. Would you agree to receive ten percent world origin? As for me, although not in glory, my effort in the True Spirit Realm over thousands of years justifies claiming forty percent, doesn't it?"

North Wind True Person was anything but courteous. Lin Fei would get fifty percent, he himself forty, leaving Heart Demon Martial God with a mere ten. This allocation visibly darkened Heart Demon Martial God's countenance.

Yet, neither Lin Fei nor North Wind True Person were entities he could easily manipulate. Moreover, even if he disagreed and departed, Lin Fei and North Wind True Person alone were sufficient!

Heart Demon Martial God suddenly felt that inviting Lin Fei had been a mistake. Nevertheless, he didn't leave. Ten percent of the world's origin was still substantial, especially considering the extraordinary nature of the True Spirit Realm.

Besides, his illusions, in the presence of Lin Fei and North Wind True Person indeed had limited effectiveness unless faced with numerous foes, and Lin Fei couldn't handle them alone. That would be Heart Demon Martial God's time to shine! Otherwise, Lin Fei and North Wind True Person were enough to contest the True Spirit Realm.

"Alright, let's proceed with this distribution,"

Heart Demon Martial God eventually concurred.

Relieved, North Wind True Person finally exhaled. Forty percent was quite satisfactory, and almost a sure thing. But contemplating Lin Fei's formidable strength, along with Heart Demon Martial God, an idea sparked in North Wind True Person's mind.

He then spoke solemnly, "Martial God Lin, Mi Tian, my friend. Since our trio is united and nearly invincible, with no rivals unless True Monarchs intervene, why should we concede any part of the world's origin? Let's forcefully take over the True Spirit Realm, expelling all Deity Transformations and Martial Gods. Once the True Spirit Realm collapses, we can divide all its origin among ourselves!"

North Wind True Person's proposal was undeniably tempting and bold. He was planning to dominate the True Spirit Realm!

Initially, only True Person Yuanxie, with his nearly ten thousand combat avatars, could dominate a powerful minor world and claim all its origin. Now, North Wind True Person also aspired to dominate the True Spirit Realm.

Lin Fei's display of power had bolstered his confidence. Their trio might indeed stand a chance. After all, North Wind True Person had numerous combat avatars, and Heart Demon Martial God's illusions could ensnare many Martial Gods and Deity Transformations.

Lin Fei, needless to say, would handle the most elite Martial Gods. United, they genuinely could monopolize the True Spirit Realm.

"I see no problem with that."

"Indeed, since we are invincible as a trio, why should we allow others to share in the world's origin? It should all be ours!"

Both Heart Demon Martial God and Lin Fei agreed. Ambitious as they were, each craved more of the world's origin.

"North Wind True Person, you know the True Spirit Realm well. If we are to forcefully take over the True Spirit Realm, you must orchestrate it. As for the formidable foes, leave them to me. The numerous Deity Transformations and Martial Gods, I believe Mi Tian can handle. And controlling the True Spirit Realm should be manageable with your many avatars, North Wind."

Lin Fei's words outlined the trio's strengths and roles.

"Alright, let's proceed with that plan..."

Thus, North Wind True Person began to strategize. Even the massive entity that was the North Wind Sect under his command started mobilizing. War preparations stirred across the entire Spirit Realm.

Bloodthirsty Martial God escaped the True Spirit Realm. He took a deep breath and cast one last glance at the True Spirit Realm, his eyes brimming with reluctance and frustration.

Bloodthirsty Martial God was far from reconciled. He had almost certainly defeated North Wind True Person initially. Who would have guessed that North Wind True Person would bring Heart Demon Martial God and an unknown Martial God with such terrifying power?

Bloodthirsty nearly perished, a fact he knew all too well. Even now, he shuddered at the thought.

"Cursed North Wind, where did he find such a formidable Martial God? Playing with fire, isn't he afraid of being overpowered and losing all the world's origin? Hmm, a Martial God without avatars can't control so much origin. It's unlikely he would oust North Wind…"

The more Bloodthirsty pondered, the angrier he became, unable to accept the situation. The world's origin had been almost within his grasp, but now the tables had turned. He had nearly lost his life!

"That Martial God is very powerful. Together with North Wind and Mi Tian, their strength is even greater. I wonder if that old entity would be interested in a single True Spirit Realm. But now, I can't be too choosy. I must make that old entity intervene. The origin of the True Spirit Realm is a must-have, only then can I confidently face my ninth thunder tribulation..."

Gritting his teeth, Bloodthirsty Martial God steeled his resolve. Then, he transformed into a streak of light, departing from the True Spirit Realm.
