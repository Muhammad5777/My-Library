196. Unrivaled in the world

Chang City, the palace.

Ever since Ling Yao became the Crown Princess, the emperor even actively delegated some powers to Ling Yao. Therefore, Ling Yao was busy every day.

"Report! Internal strife has occurred among the rebels in Dong Province. The rebel leader was beheaded, his head sent to the imperial army's camp, and the rebels have surrendered!"

"Report! The rebel leader in Lu Province killed several of his confidants in one day. He led the rebels to surrender to the court, asking the court for judgment!"

"Report! Internal strife erupted among the rebels in Xia Province, several factions of rebels fought each other, the majority of the rebels were wiped out in the internal strife, and the remaining rebels were also defeated by our imperial army!"

With one report after another, Ling Yao felt something was not right. These should all be good news. But why is there a series of such good news?

One must know, Dong Province, Lu Province, and Xia Province were all breeding grounds for rebellion. Previously, they were causing chaos. It's just that they didn't make as much noise as the South. But if the court was not powerful enough to suppress the rebellion, given time, the rebels would definitely grow stronger.

But now, these rebels were either having internal strife or surrendering. It seemed that overnight, the whole Great Chang had become peaceful again.

But is this possible?

Ling Yao knew very well. Behind those rebels represented a number of immortal sects! As long as the immortal sects were not extinguished, the rebels would continue to appear.

"Go, investigate thoroughly what exactly has happened. Focus on the news about those immortal sects," Ling Yao immediately ordered.

About a few hours later, Ling Yao's subordinate hurried over. Even, his face had a hint of joy!

"Report! Crown Princess, the matter has been investigated. Just yesterday, the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons of the immortal sects set up a trap and attacked the Nine Profound Sect. The Saber Saint Lin Fei evolved the saber momentum and killed the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons! Now, led by the Sky Reaching Sect, the ten major immortal sects have announced that they are sealing the mountain!"

"Whoosh".

Ling Yao stood up abruptly. She was greatly shocked!

"As expected, it has to do with Lin Fei. Killing the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons of the immortal sects. All the major immortal sects have announced that they are sealing the mountain. They are scared. Even the founding emperor back then was no more than this..."

Ling Yao understood. She understood everything. In various places in Great Chang, the rebels were having internal strife or surrendering. Actually, it was because the immortal sects behind them had sealed their mountains.

The immortal sects no longer support the rebels. Without the support of the immortal sects, those rebels were just a mob. How could they last long? Therefore, internal strife occurred. And Lin Fei, with his own strength, suppressed the immortal sects!

The founding emperor of Great Chang, with his supreme power, swept across all directions, unstoppable. Finally, he established the Great Chang, forcing the major immortal sects to close their gates. This brought about long-lasting peace in the world.

"Killing the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons, Lin Fei is already invincible....."

Ling Yao murmured in a low voice. In the end, she fell silent. How much time has passed since Lin Fei said that he wanted to reach the peak of the martial dao? Ling Yao was still immersed in mundane matters, but Lin Fei had quietly achieved his initial dream. Unrivaled in the world! For a martial artist, this is the highest achievement!

Now, Lin Fei's words could determine the world. Even the Great Chang royal family had to look at Lin Fei's face. This was what Lin Fei said.

When martial arts is practiced to the extreme, one can control fate! Even, not only can one control their own fate, but also the fate of countless people.

"The path, after all, is chosen by me. Besides, it's already impossible..."

Ling Yao shook her head. She had chosen power. From then on, Lin Fei and she were people on two different paths. There were no more possibilities. It was her own choice!

......

In the main hall of the Sky Reaching Sect, everyone was silent. In the entire Sky Reaching Sect, there was only one Foundation Establishment True Person left. Because the two Foundation Establishment True Persons, True Person Tongxuan and True Person Tongguang, went down the mountain, and both were killed by the Saber Saint Lin Fei at the Nine Profound Sect!

"True Person, it seems that Great Chang can still have a few hundred years of national luck."

"Yes, who would have thought that a Saber Saint Lin Fei would emerge?"

"With his own strength, he killed ten Foundation Establishment True Persons, I'm afraid even the founding emperor of Great Chang was no more than this, right?"

"Lin Fei is truly the Saber Saint, evolving the saber momentum. Under the saber momentum, even Foundation Establishment True Persons have to weaken their strength by several times, even if they have precious treasures, they can only be slaughtered."

"I have to admit, the current Lin Fei is truly unrivaled in the world!"

"Unrivaled, just like the founder of Great Chang, truly unrivaled..."

Many Qi Condensation cultivators began to speak. When they received the news from the Nine Profound Sect, they even thought it was a tall tale.

A total of ten Foundation Establishment True Persons! The ten Foundation Establishment True Persons working together could sweep the entire Great Chang. But they were killed by Lin Fei with his own power. This seemed like a tall tale no matter how they look at it.

However, when they learned from other hidden immortal sects that those Foundation Establishment True Persons did not return and more and more news came in, they finally believed the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons were really killed by Lin Fei!

"Unrivaled..."

True Person Tongyuan, the only Foundation Establishment True Person in the Sky Reaching Sect, also sighed slightly, "Yes, Saber Saint Lin Fei is now unrivaled in the world. Just like the founder of Great Chang. Sweeping all directions, invincible, and dominating! Faced with such an opponent, our Sky Reaching Sect has only one choice, and that is to seal the mountain! As long as Lin Fei is there for a day, our Sky Reaching Sect will never come out! Although it's a bit suffocating, this is the way of survival for our immortal sects.

We have a mountain protection formation, at the Foundation Establishment level, which no one can break. As long as we endure until Lin Fei dies, or disappears like the first emperor of Great Chang. That will be our opportunity for the immortal sects! Didn't our Sky Reaching Sect survive in this way for a thousand years?"

The Qi cultivators of the Sky Reaching Sect also nodded one after another. The so-called immortal sects are like iron, and the dynasties are like flowing water.

The inheritance of the immortal sect lasts for hundreds of years, even for several thousands of years. No matter how many strong people are born in the outside world, as long as the mountain gate is sealed, the immortal sect is then cut off from the world. No one can do anything about it. As for the existence of an invincible power in the world, just let him be invincible. After hundreds of years, the immortal sect is still the immortal sect, but the invincible power in the world will die.

Thus, the Sky Reaching Sect announced the closure of the mountain. Not only the Sky Reaching Sect, but other hidden immortal sects also announced the closure of the mountain. As a result, almost all the immortal sects in the world have closed their mountains.

The entire Great Chang seemed to have fallen into peace. And the name of Saber Saint Lin Fei's invincibility also instantly spread all over the country.

Everyone knew Saber Saint Lin Fei, with his own power, suppressed all the immortal sects in the world. He was already like the founder of Great Chang. Unrivaled in the world!
