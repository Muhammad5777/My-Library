148. Unexpected Joy, Heavenly Saber Art Perfection

"The...the city wall is broken?"

Looking at that huge gap, the leader of the Paradise Sect was stunned, with an undeniable look of fear in his eyes. He was afraid!

Two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy descending from the sky, who could stop it? Lin Fei was a martial artist? Is there such a powerful martial artist? He'd believe it if one said Lin Fei was a god!

"What to do, what to do? The city wall is broken, it can't be blocked at all. Immortal Masters, quickly find a way to remedy this."

The leader of the Paradise Sect urgently sought help from the cultivators beside him. However, these Qi cultivators, upon seeing the huge gap in the city wall and "Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei standing below the wall, looked at each other and understood each other's intentions.

"Retreat!"

Almost without any hesitation, the several Qi cultivators immediately left the city wall and even Chiyang Prefecture. They retreated!

After all, with the city wall broken, what could they do? Besides, "Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei was right below the city. They were confident and didn't put martial artists in their eyes. But, this did not include the "Immortal Slayer Saber".

After all, "Immortal Slayer Saber" made his great name known by killing countless Qi cultivators. They did not want to become souls under Lin Fei's saber. So, the several Qi cultivators left very simply, without any hesitation.

As for the leader of the Paradise Sect? They didn't care about him at all.

At this moment, the leader of the Paradise Sect was slightly stunned. It seemed that he hadn't reacted yet to why the cultivators disappeared.

"Immortal Masters, why did you leave? What should I do? What about the Paradise Sect?"

The leader of the Paradise Sect glanced at the huge gap in the city wall. He also didn't dare to stay. He immediately said, "Quickly, retreat first, leave Chiyang Prefecture."

The leader of the Paradise Sect didn't even dare to take many people with him. He just hoped that the cannon fodder of Chiyang Prefecture could hold back some of the imperial troops, and then he could escape smoothly.

Lin Fei saw the movement on the city wall. Regardless of the departure of those Qi cultivators or the leader of the Paradise Sect, Lin Fei didn't care. He and Li Yuhu had agreed before that he would break the city gate, and Li Yuhu would lead the troops to quell the rebellion.

Now, Lin Fei didn't break the city gate but collapsed the city wall. And those Qi cultivators just left, they did not obstruct the imperial army. So Lin Fei had no interest in chasing them. Besides, he might not even be able to catch up with them. As for capturing Chiyang Prefecture and annihilating the rebel forces, that was Li Yuhu's job.

At this moment, the morale of the imperial troops soared. Their side had someone like Lin Fei, who was like a god, what was there to be afraid of? Li Yuhu, as an experienced veteran, made a decisive decision and immediately ordered, "Enter the city and annihilate the rebels!"

The army began to charge. Countless imperial troops, mighty and majestic, rushed into Chiyang Prefecture through the gap in the city wall.

Lin Fei still stood in place, motionless. Because the voice of the system unexpectedly sounded in his mind.

"Your saber intent, because you broke the city wall with one saber, is greatly encouraged and excited. Its training progress has increased by 10%."

"Your True Intention Scroll, because you broke the city wall with one saber, is greatly encouraged and excited. Its training progress has increased by 10%."

"Your Heavenly Saber Art, because you broke the city wall with one saber, greatly displaying the might of Heavenly Saber Art, and being excited, its training progress has increased by 50%."

"Your Heavenly Saber Art has broken through to perfection!"

A glint flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. Unexpectedly, he could gain so much from breaking the city wall with one saber in front of both armies.

The increase in the True Intention Scroll and saber intent was fine. The magnitude of the increase was not large. But the Heavenly Saber Art had increased by a full 50%. This was extraordinary. It directly allowed Heavenly Saber Art to reach perfection from transcendence.

Lin Fei closed his eyes. He carefully sorted out the memories in his mind. There was an additional bundle of memories in his mind. It is the memory of Heavenly Saber Art. Or rather, the memory of Heavenly Saber Art's perfection.

"Heavenly Saber Art Perfection can condense a Heavenly Saber."

"When the Heavenly Saber appears, all sabers bow their heads!"

"When you cast the Heavenly Saber, you can possess a 'Heavenly Saber Momentum', which forms a huge suppression on all saber users."

"Even if it's not a saber user, even Qi cultivators and martial arts Masters, can be suppressed by at least 20% of their strength."

"For those whose will is not firm, even more strength is suppressed."

The more Lin Fei examined the memory, the more shocked he was. This Heavenly Saber Art, once it reached perfection, was completely different from the Fierce saber and the Tyrant saber he had condensed before. Or to say, it was on two different levels.

Only by condensing the "Heavenly Saber" could one truly be considered a Heavenly Saber Art practitioner.

Both the fierce saber and the Tyrant saber were just transitional phases of the "Heavenly Saber". Their power didn't even amount to one-tenth of the Heavenly Saber. Only Heavenly Saber Art Perfection could condense the true Heavenly Saber.

"System."

Lin Fei immediately called up the system panel to check his martial arts status.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Innate Martial Artist

Master: Late Stage

True Intention Scroll: Late Stage (Progress 42%)

Saber Intent: Transcendence (Progress 56%)

Fist Intent: Mastery (Progress 81%)

Heavenly Saber Art: Perfection (Progress 0%)

Immeasurable Fist: Transcendence (Progress 50%)

Close Yet Distant: Transcendence (Progress 0%)

Lin Fei's saber intent had moved a step closer to perfection. even the True Intention Scroll had improved a lot. This time, Lin Fei didn't expect to have an unexpected gain.

Lin Fei raised his head. He saw that on the city wall of Chiyang Prefecture, the flag of the Paradise Sect had fallen and it was replaced with the flag of the imperial court again. This indicated that the battle was going smoothly.

Lin Fei took a step forward and directly arrived at Chiyang Prefecture. There were bodies everywhere, most of them were Paradise Sect rebels. The entire Chiyang Prefecture was filled with the smell of blood.

Soon, a squad of soldiers came to Lin Fei. They respectfully said to Lin Fei, "The general invites Master Lin to the Yamen."

Lin Fei nodded and followed the soldiers to the Yamen. Li Yuhu was in the Yamen.

"Master Lin, you came just in time. We have captured the leader of the rebels. We have learned about the situation in detail. These rebels really are a rabble. The cities they occupied before basically had no troops.

Now that the rebels have suffered a great defeat, they no longer have the strength to fight. Other cities will soon be recaptured by the imperial army. In less than ten days, the rebellion will be completely quelled!"

Li Yuhu was very excited. This was a great achievement!

Originally, Li Yuhu was almost at the end of his rope. If it weren't for Lin Fei, the official army might have had to retreat due to a lack of food. Perhaps they would have been ambushed by the rebels and suffered a defeat.

Fortunately, Lin Fei came. With Lin Fei, Li Yuhu was able to completely crush the rebels and suppress the Paradise Sect rebellion.

"Ten days? That's good. General Li, the task of cleaning up the rebels is left to you."

Lin Fei nodded. Suppressing the rebellion in ten days. He was quite satisfied.

"Master Lin, there's one more thing. According to the confessions of the rebel leader, there were several unidentified Taoists who helped them defend Chiyang Prefecture. However, those Taoists, after Master Lin slashed the city wall with one saber, all fled in all directions, their whereabouts unknown."

Li Yuhu was a bit wary of these fleeing Taoists. After all, they were all Qi cultivators. Their methods were mysterious and unpredictable.

"Let them run. They wouldn't dare to come back. If they dare to, Lin will cut them down with one saber."

Lin Fei said indifferently. Killing Qi cultivators was Lin Fei's most skilled technique. After all, he was the "Immortal Slayer Saber". When the saber comes out, immortals must be slain!
