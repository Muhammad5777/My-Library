234. Limit Martial Saint

Lin Fei sat cross-legged in the Spiritual Qi Pool in a calm manner. A large amount of Spiritual Essence was frantically trying to drill into Lin Fei's body.

Lin Fei's True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique was operating. Terrifying saber essence emanated. Its power instantly covered the entire Spiritual Qi Pool.

Suddenly, everyone in the Spiritual Qi Pool, whether they were disciples or elders, even the top-tier Martial Saints, all felt a terrifying pressure. This wasn't the pressure of saber momentum. It was completely different from saber momentum. It was purely the pressure of True Essence. Or to put it another way, it was the pressure of Lin Fei's saber essence!

Just this pressure alone made many disciples in the Spiritual Qi Pool feel as if they were unable to breathe.

"This aura... has the cultivation technique reached perfection?"

"Such terrifying True Essence, there can be no mistake. Elder Lin must have perfected a technique for cultivating True Essence. Otherwise, it's impossible to have such a terrifying pressure of True Essence."

"How long has Elder Lin been in the Ancient Origin Sect? It's only about half a year at most. How could he have perfected a cultivation technique?"

"Incredible, it's truly incredible! I originally thought Elder Lin was just talented in saber momentum, a natural Saber Saint. I didn't expect that in the cultivation of True Essence, Elder Lin is also a top-tier genius."

"Could Elder Lin really be able to condense the Qi Flower?"

Many disciples were uncertain. Their hearts were filled with shock. They had cultivated for decades, even a hundred years. and they might not be able to reach the point of condensing the Qi Flower.

But what about Lin Fei? It was just a mere half a year. The elders of the Spiritual Qi Pool also immediately changed their expressions.

"All of you leave the Spiritual Qi Pool immediately and go outside. Otherwise, under such great pressure, you won't be able to cultivate. As for the contribution points you've paid, you can continue to cultivate in the Spiritual Qi Pool after Elder Lin finishes condensing the Qi Flower."

Actually, without the elder's words, many disciples also got up and left the Spiritual Qi Pool. Under these circumstances, they simply couldn't cultivate at all. Continuing to stay in the Spiritual Qi Pool was just a waste of time. So, the disciples inside the Spiritual Qi Pool all left the Spiritual Qi Pool one after another and came outside.

However, from the outside, they could still see the situation inside the Spiritual Qi Pool. Almost all the disciples did not leave. The elders didn't stop them either. As long as they weren't noisy and didn't affect Lin Fei's condensation of the Qi Flower. After all, being able to witness a Martial Saint condensing a Qi Flower with their own eyes and seeing the whole process would be of some help to them when they tried to condense a Qi Flower in the future.

Even, some disciples started to send messages to each other. They notified their friends one after another.

"Get to the Spiritual Qi Pool quickly. Outer Sect Elder Lin Fei is about to condense the Qi Flower."

"It's absolutely true, hurry up."

"'Dao Seeker' Elder Lin, is trying to condense the Qi Flower half a year after defeating Ao Yunxiao. It would be a pity to miss this opportunity."

Inside the Ancient Origin Sect. the distance is very close and there are array methods that are interconnected. Therefore, they can communicate with each other.

For a time, many disciples came to the Spiritual Qi Pool. Innate martial artists, Martial Saints, and so on. Even some of the top-tier Martial Saints also came to the Spiritual Qi Pool upon hearing the news.

After all, compared to the ordinary disciples who came to watch the excitement, these top-tier Martial Saints were the most qualified to condense the Qi Flower.

The number of people outside was increasing. But Lin Fei in the Spiritual Qi Pool was not disturbed at all. He didn't pay attention to the situation around him. His entire attention was on himself. Especially the saber essence within his body.

The True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique kept operating. Because he had devoured a Martial Saint-level technique. Therefore, Lin Fei naturally knew how to condense the Qi Flower. A large amount of saber essence was surging and spouting out of his body. At the same time, Spiritual Essence drilled into his body, trying to transform into saber essence.

However, it was of no use. The saber essence in Lin Fei's body had already reached its limit. The greater use of this Spiritual Essence was to help Lin Fei compensate for some of the losses caused by condensing the Qi Flower. The saber essence within Lin Fei's body had completely emanated from his body and gradually started to condense above his head.

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei took a deep breath. He immediately took over control of the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique. According to the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique, he activated the method to condense the Qi Flower.

"Boom".

Immediately, the vast saber essence around Lin Fei actually started to expand frantically, continuing to expand. Finally, it exploded with a loud bang. With the explosion of the saber essence, terrifying power was shaking.

However, at the same time, the countless exploding saber essence above Lin Fei's head faintly condensed into a flower.

Qi Flower!

Lin Fei very smoothly condensed the Qi Flower! It was as easy as eating and drinking. Too easy!

Lin Fei even thought about the possible troubles he might encounter. But he didn't encounter any of them. The phrase "when water flows, a channel is formed" couldn't help but emerge in Lin Fei's mind.

Yes, Lin Fei now felt, condensing the Qi Flower was like water flowing into a channel.

In fact, Lin Fei felt that even if he didn't do it in the Spiritual Qi Pool, he could still condense the Qi Flower.

Too easy! But is condensing the Qi Flower really that easy? Just look at the stunned disciples outside with shocked expressions. Some top-tier Martial Saints had tried to condense the Qi Flower. Unfortunately, they failed. It took them many years to slowly recover. Therefore, they knew very well how difficult it was to condense the Qi Flower. But now they saw Lin Fei actually did it in one go. The whole process was too smooth.

There were no twists and turns, even more, there were no earth-shaking moments. Just like nature, everything falls into place. The Martial Saints who understood the significance of this couldn't help but feel a tremor in their hearts!

"This is the real meaning of 'when water flows, a channel is formed'! When the True Essence reaches a certain level of profoundness, the condensation of Qi Flower is as natural as water flowing into a channel, without any difficulty. I didn't believe it before, but now seeing Elder Lin condensing the Qi Flower, I believe it..."

"So, it can be so easy to condense a Qi Flower."

"Easy? Even if one practices the technique to perfection, it's not necessarily easy to condense a Qi Flower. It can only be said that the technique Elder Lin has practiced must be sophisticated. Moreover, the level of True Essence in Elder Lin's body is unimaginable, far surpassing other top Martial Saints."

"Does this mean that our Ancient Origin Sect has given birth to another Limit Martial Saint? And he is an outer sect elder?"

"Worthy of being a 'Dao Seeker', not only is his combat power amazing, but even in terms of cultivation, his talent is not inferior to any true disciple."

"Elder Lin is now ranked as a Martial Sovereign seed, and his potential is even greater than other Martial Sovereign seeds..."

Many disciples had serious expressions. A hint of excitement flashed in their eyes. When water flows, a channel is formed! Although it was difficult, Lin Fei had proven it. When the True Essence reaches a certain level of profoundness, one can truly naturally condense a Qi Flower.

There is no need to rely on luck. Otherwise, if the True Essence is not profound enough, forcing oneself to condense a Qi Flower can only rely on luck! And even if one condenses a Qi Flower, there is still a difference.

Like Lin Fei, with such a profound level of True Essence, once a Qi Flower is condensed, True Essence will surge. Even among Limit Martial Saints, the level of True Essence might vary greatly.

What's more important is that condensing a Qi Flower is not the end. On the contrary, it's just the beginning. To become a Martial Sovereign, one must unite the three flowers. If the Qi Flower is strong enough, obviously, the chance of becoming a Martial Sovereign can greatly increase.

At this moment, the Qi Flower appeared above Lin Fei's head. At the same time, the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique within Lin Fei's body began to crazily absorb the spiritual essence. A large amount of spiritual essence was absorbed into his body, quickly transforming into saber essence.

One time, three times, five times, ten times...

In just a moment, the amount of saber essence in Lin Fei's body surged and increased by more than ten times in an instant. Such a terrifying increase, no wonder Limit Martial Saints are powerful on a different level. It's a world apart from ordinary Martial Saints.

"Splash."

Lin Fei stood up directly from the Spiritual Qi Pool.

"Boom."

The Qi Flower above Lin Fei's head emerged again. Moreover, the shadow of the Qi Flower immediately reflected in the sky above the Spiritual Qi Pool, forming a huge flower. Grand and impressive, like a huge cloud.

All the martial artists under the coverage of the Qi Flower. Whether they were innate martial artists or Martial Saints, everyone felt that the True Qi, the True Essence in their bodies seemed to be under great pressure. This Qi Flower actually had a similar ability to suppress opponents like martial momentum. It just targeted True Essence and True Qi.

At this moment, no one doubted anymore. Lin Fei had condensed the Qi Flower. He truly reached the pinnacle of Martial Saints, becoming a Limit Martial Saint!
