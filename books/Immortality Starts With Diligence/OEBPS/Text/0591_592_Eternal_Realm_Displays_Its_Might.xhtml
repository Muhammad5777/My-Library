592. Eternal Realm Displays Its Might

"Weak?" A fleeting sharpness flashed through the eyes of Taihao True Immortal.

Thereafter, his voice permeated the void, "Very well, since that is the case, I shall not hold back any longer! I am a True Immortal, fused with the Dao. When the Dao emerges, Divine Abilities manifest! My every word forms the laws! Words uttered, laws follow, the Dao suppresses!"

With a thunderous "boom," Taihao True Immortal invoked the Dao. To the naked eye, the Divine Realm seemed undisturbed.

Yet at this moment, every Dao Integration True Immortal could feel the entire Divine Realm's void trembling intensely. Even the Dao of the Divine Realm itself quaked violently. This disturbance alarmed many True Immortals and Martial Ancestors.

"What's happening? Even the Origin Sea of the Divine Realm is shaking?"

"Somebody is wielding the power of the Dao."

"Which True Immortal is mobilizing the Dao's power?"

"It seems to be Taihao True Immortal!"

"Taihao True Immortal's opponent, isn't that Lin Fei from the Great Dream Sect?"

"Lin Fei has become a Martial Ancestor? He's compelled Taihao True Immortal to exert the Dao's power."

"How long has Lin Fei been in the Divine Realm to achieve the status of Martial Ancestor?"

Suddenly, many True Immortals and Martial Ancestors became aware of the fierce battle between Taihao True Immortal and Lin Fei.

Deep within the void, powerful gazes were now focused on Lin Fei and Taihao True Immortal. Numerous True Immortals and Martial Ancestors were closely watching this battle. Although they were shocked by how quickly Lin Fei had become a Martial Ancestor, they still did not believe Lin Fei could surpass Taihao True Immortal.

Taihao True Immortal was not weak, even among True Immortals, he was at least of moderate rank. Once one achieves True Immortal or Martial Ancestor status, advancing even slightly becomes exceedingly difficult.

Being of moderate rank was already no small feat. As Taihao True Immortal summoned the power of the Divine Realm's Dao, the pressure on Lin Fei multiplied tenfold, several dozen times over. A tremendous force enveloped him. For the first time, Lin Fei felt immense pressure.

"Not enough, still not enough! Ha ha ha, Taihao True Immortal, your Dao is indeed strong. But it seems incapable of troubling me."

A sharp glint flickered in Lin Fei's eyes.

"Eternal Realm!"

In the next moment, Lin Fei's body swiftly transformed into a world. It was the Eternal Realm!

Once the Eternal Realm emerged, the power of Taihao True Immortal's Dao became ineffective. Even the Dao power that struck Lin Fei's Eternal Realm only stirred up a ripple, like ripples in water, spreading in all directions.

But that was all. Taihao True Immortal's "words follow laws," even with the mobilization of the Divine Realm's Dao power, failed to suppress Lin Fei's world.

"Transforming into a world?"

Taihao True Immortal was astounded. Especially since he, merged with the Dao, even using the power of the Divine Realm's Dao, could not contend with Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

Moreover, Lin Fei, having transformed into a world, could now wield the power of an entire world. How terrifying was that power?

"Break!" Lin Fei uttered in a low shout.

The power of the entire Eternal Realm erupted instantly. With a resounding "boom," the surrounding void shattered in an instant.

This was a minor world!

The full might of the entire minor world was unleashed. Everyone witnessed this incredibly shocking scene. The void was shattered, completely obliterated. An unimaginable force instantaneously pierced through the body of Taihao True Immortal.

With a "thud," in the next instant, the body of Taihao True Immortal disintegrated into dust.

At the same time, an even more terrifying force emerged. It collided fiercely with the Dao of the Divine Realm that was fused with Taihao True Immortal.

It was a collision of Dao against Dao. The world power of Lin Fei, its source was the World Dao.

Thus, the collision of the Dao was extremely perilous and equally astonishing.

With a "spurt," in the next moment, Lin Fei's world power proved superior.

However, Lin Fei's minor world also developed a slight crack. Inside the minor world, tempestuous waves were stirred up. The power was so immense, abnormally so,

That it caused severe turbulence within Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Needless to say, Taihao True Immortal fared worse. The fused Dao was penetrated by the force of Lin Fei.

In essence, it was shattered. But that was all. Lin Fei could not completely annihilate the Dao of the Divine Realm.

Thus, this was the extent of what he could achieve. Yet even so, this was the heaviest "injury" Taihao True Immortal had received since the ancient wars. His body was blasted apart.

Of course, Dao Integration True Immortals are immortal and indestructible. As long as the Dao exists, the Dao Integration True Immortal would remain unharmed.

With a "hum," a figure rapidly condensed in the void. It was indeed Taihao True Immortal. His expression was somewhat grim. He stared intently at Lin Fei, seemingly in disbelief.

He had lost. And not just a loss, but a crushing defeat. A total rout! Even his body was blasted apart.

Moreover, Lin Fei had even directly countered a strike from the Divine Realm's World Dao. Yet, it almost did not affect Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. This clearly showed the formidable strength of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

Lin Fei's prowess was clearly not that of an ordinary Martial Ancestor. After all, even a typical Martial Ancestor was no match for Taihao True Immortal. But now, Lin Fei had nearly "crushed" Taihao True Immortal.

However, although Lin Fei appeared to have the upper hand, his demeanor grew solemn upon seeing Taihao True Immortal reform his body. He had previously wondered if he could utterly annihilate Taihao True Immortal.

Yet now, it seemed nearly impossible, despite having exerted his full strength. Aside from not deploying the Saber World, Lin Fei had unleashed nearly every technique at his disposal.

Yet he still could not shake the Divine Realm's Dao. If the Divine Realm's Dao remained unshaken, then a True Immortal was indeed immortal and indestructible.

At least, Lin Fei had no means to fully exterminate a True Immortal, causing their complete downfall.

True Immortal, fused with the Dao, was truly immortal. Ordinary Martial Ancestors stood no chance.

However, it was unknown whether a Supreme Being could slay a Dao Integration True Immortal.

"Taihao, my friend, you have been most gracious!" Lin Fei calmly stated.

Taihao True Immortal coldly replied, "I have lost, and thoroughly at that. Lin, your strength surpasses mine. In light of your grace, I declare that all grievances between us are nullified. As long as you no longer provoke me or the Taihao Palace, I shall no longer trouble you."

Taihao True Immortal addressed these words to Phoenix. He, a dignified True Immortal, would not go back on his word. Since he declared the grievances nullified, they were indeed so.

"Thank you, Taihao True Immortal! Thank you, leader!" Phoenix was visibly excited and pleased.

Unexpectedly, Lin Fei had truly kept his word. From now on, even within the Divine Realm, Phoenix could be at ease. Taihao True Immortal, true to his word, ensured Phoenix's safety.

"Fellow Daoist Lin, please forgive my inadequate care."

Having said this, Taihao True Immortal directly traversed the void, flying back to the Taihao Palace.

Clearly, Taihao True Immortal could no longer bear the sight of Lin Fei and Phoenix. This battle was not merely between Taihao True Immortal and Lin Fei. Many True Immortals and Martial Ancestors had witnessed this event. Taihao True Immortal had lost all face. How could he show a pleasant countenance?

However, Lin Fei was indifferent.

"Let's go, back to the Ancient Continent."

Lin Fei, leading Phoenix, traveled through space, heading toward the Ancient Continent.
