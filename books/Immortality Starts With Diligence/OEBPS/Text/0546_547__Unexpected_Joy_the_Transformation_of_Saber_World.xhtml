547. Unexpected Joy, the Transformation of Saber World!

Saber World!

Lin Fei had always enhanced the power of his Saber World by merging it with other Saber Worlds. However, he had come to realize that even in the Divine Realm, Martial Kings capable of evolving Saber Worlds were extremely rare. Moreover, he had already scavenged numerous Saber Worlds across the Divine Realm. How many more could be left?

If he couldn't merge with other Saber World secret realms, his own Saber World might cease to evolve. This was not an outcome Lin Fei desired. Even though his physical body and particle worlds were strong, there were times when the Saber World could play a crucial role. For instance, collecting the world origin of an entire world would be impossible without the Saber World.

Therefore, from the time he was a Martial King, although Saber World could no longer evolve, Lin Fei had been continuously trying to enhance it, aware of its significant utility. Now, even as a True Monarch, he didn't believe that his Saber World was useless. On the contrary, it still had great potential.

With the increasing rarity of Saber World secret realms, Lin Fei needed to consider alternative methods to strengthen his Saber World. Now, he planned to use the world laws of the Holy Emperor Realm to see if they could merge with his Saber World and enhance its power.

Taking a deep breath, Lin Fei prepared to begin the integration process. "Let's start," he murmured.

"Hum."

Lin Fei's Saber World descended instantly. He then attempted to directly merge the world laws of the Holy Emperor Realm into the Saber World. The laws of the Holy Emperor Realm were, after all, world laws, and the Saber World itself was a kind of world. Lin Fei believed they could be integrated, especially since he had successfully merged thirty-four Divine Realm world laws into his Saber World.

The only way to know for sure if it would work was to try.

Lin Fei took out all the world laws of the Holy Emperor Realm and threw them into the Saber World, attempting to merge them. The Saber World started to tremble slightly and its light flickered. Crucially, it did not reject the Holy Emperor Realm's world laws, which was already a promising sign.

Lin Fei focused intently on the process of merging the world laws of the Holy Emperor Realm with his Saber World. To his surprise, the integration proceeded smoothly. As soon as the Holy Emperor Realm's world laws approached the Saber World, they rapidly merged, just like the Divine Realm's world laws.

However, the integration of the Divine Realm's world laws into the Saber World was only temporary since they were crucial for Lin Fei to understand the World Dao.

But the world laws of the Holy Emperor Realm were different. With the collapse and destruction of the Holy Emperor Realm, Lin Fei couldn't gain any insight into the World Dao from these laws. Therefore, they seemed to have no further use to him.

Hence, he decided to permanently integrate them into the Saber World. The Holy Emperor Realm's world laws were complete and far outnumbered the thirty-four Divine Realm world laws.

The Saber World eagerly "devoured" these new laws. With each bit of integration, the power of the Saber World surged. Its coverage area also expanded rapidly.

Lin Fei was thrilled with this outcome. He had not expected that merging the Holy Emperor Realm's world laws with his Saber World would enhance its power so significantly. This was just a portion of the world laws, yet the improvement was remarkable.

Continuing with the integration, Lin Fei spent ten full days in his chamber, merging the Saber World with the world laws of the Holy Emperor Realm. It took him these ten days to fully integrate all the laws of the Holy Emperor Realm into his Saber World. The results far exceeded his expectations.

"Saber World!"

With a thought, Lin Fei summoned the Saber World, which now appeared instantly. Previously, without merging his thirty-four Divine Realm world laws, his Saber World was at the peak Martial King level.

Even after merging numerous Saber World secret realms, the Saber World itself had not transformed; it remained at the Martial King level. It was only with the integration of the Divine Realm world laws that its power surged to the Martial God level.

However, even at its peak, Lin Fei's Saber World was only at the Martial God level, far from reaching the True Monarch level. The gap was too vast. Lin Fei believed that even merging dozens or a hundred more Saber Worlds would hardly elevate his Saber World to the True Monarch level. It would likely require dozens more world laws to achieve such a feat.

But now, having integrated the complete world laws of the Holy Emperor Realm, Lin Fei's Saber World underwent a significant transformation, a qualitative metamorphosis.

The Saber World, which was previously at the Martial King level, ascended to the Martial God level after merging with the Holy Emperor Realm's laws. Even without incorporating Lin Fei's thirty-four Divine Realm world laws, it was now at the Martial God level, easily overpowering other Martial Gods.

Such an upgrade was not easily achieved. After all, despite merging many Saber World secret realms in the past, Lin Fei had not been able to induce such a transformation. This change marked a pivotal evolution in his Saber World's capabilities.

Indeed, Lin Fei had fallen into a conceptual trap. He had been fixated on merging his Saber World with other Saber World secret realms, but no matter how many he merged, those Saber Worlds were all at the Martial King level. The sheer quantity needed for a qualitative change seemed unimaginably high.

However, the world laws of the Holy Emperor Realm were different. The Holy Emperor Realm was a True Monarch-level world, a cut above ordinary minor worlds, which were typically at the Martial God level. The Holy Emperor Realm stood two tiers above in terms of level, so naturally, its integration significantly elevated the Saber World to the Martial God level.

"Now, the Saber World alone is already at the Martial God level," Lin Fei contemplated. "What would happen if I merge the thirty-four Divine Realm world laws into it? How much more powerful could it become?"

Filled with anticipation, Lin Fei began to merge the Divine Realm's thirty-four world laws into the Saber World. With each law integrated, the power of the Saber World surged exponentially. Once all thirty-four laws were temporarily merged, even this was a monumental enhancement.

"Boom."

The next moment, the power of Lin Fei's Saber World skyrocketed, increasing tenfold, even dozens of times. Lin Fei felt that his Saber World could now easily suppress a minor world entirely.

"It's at the peak of Martial God level! My Saber World can now easily suppress peak Martial God-level powerhouses. Still, it's not quite at the True Monarch level yet, but it's not far off. If I can merge some more True Monarch-level world laws or even a few more Divine Realm world laws, I'm sure I can cause a qualitative transformation in the Saber World and elevate it to the True Monarch level," Lin Fei mused, his eyes gleaming with a peculiar light.

Lin Fei was acutely aware of what it would mean for his Saber World to reach the True Monarch level. It wasn't just about gaining an additional True Monarch-level power.

Once the Saber World underwent its transformation and possessed the power of a True Monarch, Lin Fei could potentially use it to suppress a True Monarch on its own. Coupled with his own physical strength, he might even have the capability to completely annihilate a True Monarch.

At that point, even if Dao Integration True Monarchs had numerous main battle incarnations, they would be rendered useless against Lin Fei's Saber World, which could annihilate these incarnations into dust. However, achieving this required more True Monarch-level world laws or additional Divine Realm world laws.

With this realization, Lin Fei's eyes lit up, and a spark of inspiration flashed through his mind. He had thought of a plan.
