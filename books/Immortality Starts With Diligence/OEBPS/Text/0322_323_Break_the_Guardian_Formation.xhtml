323. Break the Guardian Formation

"Lin Fei has arrived?"

They had just mentioned Lin Fei, yet, unexpectedly, Lin Fei had already arrived. The Golden Core True Person of Taiyi Dao Palace took a deep breath, promptly using his magical treasure to send a message.

"Who would have thought Lin Fei would not only return to Ancient Origin Sect but also arrive at Profound Light Dao?"

"We must immediately inform the palace master."

"True Person Xuanwu, at any cost, must defend Profound Light Dao!"

"The palace master has been preparing for such a long time; he must be thoroughly ready."

"Once we can hold out until the palace master arrives, Lin Fei's fate will be sealed."

"Perhaps even the Ancient Origin Sect and Red Hades Mansion can be taken in one sweep."

Though Xuanwu was somewhat taken aback, his knowledge of Lin Fei was merely from some rumors or what the Golden Core True Person of Taiyi Dao Palace had just conveyed. He had never seen Lin Fei with his own eyes. Regardless of how authentic the rumors might be, he still lacked a direct understanding of Lin Fei.

Thus, True Person Xuanwu confidently nodded, "Rest assured, esteemed True Persons. While I may not promise success in other matters, now that we are in Profound Light Dao, protected by the grand formation. I assure you, defending Profound Light Dao will be no issue. Even for Lin Fei, breaking through Profound Light Dao's protective formation would be utterly impossible!"

With such unwavering conviction, Xuanwu's voice was filled with confidence. Thus, the Golden Core True Person of Taiyi Dao Palace felt somewhat relieved. They pondered upon Profound Light Dao, previously known as Profound Light Cave Heaven, a lineage spanning over ten thousand years. How could its protective formation be anything ordinary? Perhaps there was truly a glimmer of hope. How could Lin Fei, no matter how powerful, breach the Profound Light Dao's formation, refined over millennia?

"I wish to see for myself the capabilities of this Lin Fei."

With that, Xuanwu, along with several Golden Core True Persons, flew out of the mansion.

Outside Profound Light Dao.

At that very moment, Lin Fei stood amidst the void, gazing at the Profound Light Cave Heaven.

No matter its name, a legacy that spanned over ten thousand years. Its protective formation too had withstood the test of time. Breaking through the defenses of Profound Light Cave Heaven was a daunting challenge.

Each immortal sect's most formidable asset was its protective formation. Without power exceeding several times or even tens of times, one simply couldn't breach such a formation.

The Sect Master of Ancient Origin Sect, Wind Sovereign, and the Golden Core True Person from Red Hades Mansion had also arrived outside the gates of Profound Light Cave Heaven. However, they all chose to maintain a respectful distance, with only Lin Fei in the midst of the void.

In a swift moment, several figures emerged from within the Profound Light Cave Heaven. Leading them was none other than Xuanwu.

Upon seeing Xuanwu, both the Sect Master of Ancient Origin Sect and the Golden Core True Person of Red Hades Mansion became somber. They had met True Person Xuanwu before, aware of his current state.

Xuanwu, it was believed, was nearing his life's end, with death's shadow looming over him. Yet, now? He appeared invigorated, brimming with vitality, as if he had been granted a new lease on life. They immediately surmised the reason.

"He must have taken the Nascent Soul Transformation Pill!"

"He consumed the Nascent Soul Transformation Pill and created a 'Nascent Soul'."

"With the 'False Nascent Soul', he can extend their life by a thousand years. Just to live a thousand years more, Xuanwu sold out the entire Profound Light Cave Heaven."

"Xuanwu deserves death!"

Upon seeing Xuanwu, both the powerhouses from the Ancient Origin Sect and the Red Hades Mansion revealed murderous intentions. They felt that Xuanwu was even more detestable than Taiyi Dao Palace.

These three major forces, originally on par with Taiyi Dao Palace, had their millennia-old status quo destroyed by Xuanwu. The Ancient Origin Sect and Red Hades Mansion almost wish they could tear True Person Xuanwu apart, to vent their anger.

However, they couldn't. Xuanwu was inside the gate of the Profound Light Cave Heaven, protected by the guardian formation which has a heritage of over ten thousand years. It was the safest place!

From a distance, Xuanwu spotted Lin Fei floating in the void. He shouted, "Sovereign Lin, they say you are the premier Martial Sovereign of the Ancient Origin Sect. Do you really think you can break the guardian formation of my Profound Light Dao today?"

"Guardian formation…" Lin Fei suddenly felt a sense of déjà vu. He recalled a similar scene in Great Chang. The Sky Reaching Sect had the same overconfidence in their guardian formation, thinking it could withstand any Martial Saint or Foundation Establishment level forces.

But what happened? Lin Fei broke through their formation, destroyed their sect, and even wiped out their lineage. Recalling the past glories in Dachang, everyone knew of the renowned "Saber Saint" Lin Fei. But now in the Ancient Continent, within the Ancient Origin Sect, no one knew of Lin Fei's past achievements.

"Destroying a sect? Feels familiar… Today, let's start with the Profound Light Cave Heaven."

Lin Fei stood with his hands behind him, silently floating in the void. The next moment, he unleashed his Saber Domain with a boom. And it wasn't just any Saber Domain; it was the Transcendence Saber Domain. His Saber Domain was on par with the perfect state Saber Domain of others.

As this fearsome Saber Domain descended, it covered the guardian formation of the Profound Light Cave Heaven.

"Flame!"

Inside the Saber Domain, flames raged and sabers shone, carrying with them a terrifying pressure. They tried to suppress the guardian formation of the Profound Light Cave Heaven. Lin Fei's Transcendence Saber Domain was immensely powerful. As soon as it enveloped the area, it was like a mountain pressing down. Even Xuanwu felt immense pressure. In fact, everyone in the Profound Light Cave Heaven felt this enormous pressure at that moment.

"Saber Domain?"

"The Saber Domain of the Martial Sovereign is so powerful!"

"With just this Saber Domain, Sovereign Lin can suppress countless Golden Core True Persons and countless Martial Sovereigns."

"The guardian formation of Profound Light Cave Heaven is indeed powerful, as it can withstand such a terrifying Saber Domain!"

After witnessing Lin Fei's Saber Domain, no one dared to underestimate him any longer. With such a Saber Domain enveloping them, one's strength would be halved, if not more. How could they possibly fight under such conditions?

Even the guardian formation of Profound Light Cave Heaven was bearing immense pressure. But it managed to hold on.

Xuanwu heaved a silent sigh of relief. However, Lin Fei's expression remained unchanged. He had just tested the guardian formation of the Profound Light Cave Heaven using his Saber Domain. It was powerful indeed, but not completely invincible.

"Life Wheel!" Lin Fei shouted softly. A low humming sound followed, and a massive Life Wheel appeared behind Lin Fei. It was vast and seemingly endless, spanning across the void, and exuding an intimidating aura. Even from behind the guardian formation, its overwhelming pressure could be felt. Notably, there were four bright layers on Lin Fei's Life Wheel, representing four Divine Abilities!

"Four Divine Abilities?"

Seeing this, both Wind Sovereign and the Sect Master of Ancient Origin Sect had their eyes widen in shock. Their minds were overwhelmed with astonishment. Four Divine Abilities! When Lin Fei left the Ancient Origin Sect, he had only mastered one Divine Ability – the Black Wind Prison. And now, he has four!

They were quite familiar with Lin Fei and knew his ambition was to become a Martial King. Thus, any Divine Ability chosen and cultivated by Lin Fei must be a Great Divine Ability at least. Four Great Divine Abilities combined with the Saber Domain – the strength Lin Fei possessed now was simply unimaginable.

"Perhaps, with the combined power of the four Divine Abilities, he could truly break the guardian formation alone!"

Wind Sovereign whispered to himself, a hint of hope gleaming in his eyes.
