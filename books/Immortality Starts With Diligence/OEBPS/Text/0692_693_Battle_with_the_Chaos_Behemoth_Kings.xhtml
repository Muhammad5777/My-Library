693. Battle with the Chaos Behemoth Kings!

"Kill!"

Lin Fei made a decisive move without any hesitation. Despite facing two Chaos Behemoth Kings, he knew they must be flawed in some way—they were not true Chaos Behemoth Kings.

Otherwise, how could Doomsday Supreme have survived until now? He would have perished countless times at the hands of a true Chaos Behemoth King.

Yet, Lin Fei did not know how these two Chaos Behemoth Kings differed from the true Chaos Behemoth Kings.

"Hum."

Lin Fei unleashed the Eternal Light, sweeping fiercely toward the two Chaos Behemoth Kings. However, he soon realized that his Eternal Light could not envelop the two behemoths into the Eternal Realm. No matter how he tried, he couldn't draw them in—it was as if the two Chaos Behemoth Kings were immovable mountains, steadfast and unyielding.

"Eternal Realm!"

Without hesitation, Lin Fei next deployed his Eternal Realm, attempting to envelop the two Chaos Behemoth Kings within its scope.

"Boom."

The Eternal Realm shrouded the immense bodies of the two Chaos Behemoth Kings, but Lin Fei found no cause for celebration. Instead, his expression suddenly darkened. Despite his Eternal Realm cloaking them, the two Chaos Behemoth Kings remained as unresponsive as cold, hard rock, completely unaffected by the powers of the Eternal Realm.

Lin Fei now understood the true might of the Chaos Behemoth Kings. Even motionless and silent, the Chaos Behemoth Kings were impervious to his efforts. Even deploying the Eternal Realm did nothing to sway them.

At this moment, Doomsday Supreme's expression also shifted slightly as he spoke in a grave tone, "Supreme Lin, you too cannot subdue these two Chaos Behemoth Kings? Although these Chaos Behemoth Kings seem to have flaws, such imperfections are still insurmountable for us Invincible Supremes. If we cannot defeat these two Chaos Behemoth Kings, how can we even talk about conquering the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower?"

Doomsday Supreme was truly disappointed. He had hoped that Lin Fei could restrain the two Chaos Behemoth Kings, but it became clear that this was not possible. Lin Fei was unable to subdue them.

Then, the two Chaos Behemoth Kings moved. Their movements seemed slow, but they only had to lift their paws and swipe at Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme.

"Retreat, quick retreat!" Doomsday Supreme immediately shouted.

Lin Fei didn't need to be reminded to recognize the danger. As the Chaos Behemoth Kings made their move, a sense of imminent peril surged within him as if a single swipe could pose a tremendous threat.

"Thud."

The next moment, Lin Fei's body shook violently, and then his body literally shattered, crushed by the Chaos Behemoth King's swipe through the air.

However, this was only the physical disintegration of Lin Fei's body, which was in essence the Eternal Realm. While his body broke apart, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm remained intact and withstood the blow, allowing him to quickly regenerate.

Despite his recovery, Lin Fei was deeply unsettled. He glanced at Doomsday Supreme, who seemed to have gone through this before and recovered just as quickly. Being an Invincible Supreme, surely he had his ways of survival, especially after multiple forays into the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower.

But it was to no avail. The Chaos Behemoth King struck again, wielding absolute power that repeatedly obliterated Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme. The two of them had almost no chance to resist, being shattered time and again—once, twice, thrice...

However, Lin Fei eventually sensed something amiss.

"Wait, something's not right," he said. "We've been obliterated countless times by the two Chaos Behemoth Kings. Yet, we are almost unharmed. Could it be that even the Chaos Behemoth Kings, comparable to the Masters of Chaos, cannot annihilate our particles? Are they unable to completely kill us?"

Lin Fei felt a profound sense of unease. The Chaos Behemoth Kings, comparable to the Masters of Chaos, should be capable of annihilating the particles of a Supreme, even those of an Invincible Supreme.

Yet despite repeated obliterations, neither he nor Doomsday Supreme had suffered fundamental harm. If their particles couldn't be annihilated, their core selves remained unharmed. Normally, a Master of Chaos or a Chaos Behemoth King would indeed have the power to annihilate Supreme particles.

"Retreat, keep retreating!" Doomsday Supreme shouted. "Supreme Lin, you're not aware of the flaw these two Chaos Behemoth Kings possess. Their defect is that they cannot leave a certain area. As long as we retreat to a certain distance, they can't touch us.

Moreover, their particle annihilation abilities have significantly weakened. However, make sure not to get too close to them, especially, never let them swallow you. If a Chaos Behemoth King swallows you, your particles will be instantly annihilated. That's the tricky part."

"Even though I can temporarily avoid the Chaos Behemoth Kings, it's impossible to get past the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower without defeating or restraining them," Doomsday Supreme continued, helping Lin Fei gain a deeper understanding of these two Chaos Behemoth Kings.

It turned out that these Chaos Behemoth Kings were confined to a specific area. Despite their flaws, they still possessed the capacity to kill an Invincible Supreme, but only if they managed to swallow one.

Lin Fei continued to retreat, moving back to what Doomsday Supreme deemed a safe distance, and then finally stopped. He watched the two Chaos Behemoth Kings from afar, contemplating their next move.

Indeed, as Lin Fei retreated from the designated area, creating a significant distance between himself and the Chaos Behemoth Kings, the two behemoths ceased their attacks. They didn't even attempt to approach him. This confirmed that Doomsday Supreme's insights were correct; their operational limits were tied to a specific zone.

However, the question remained: how could they effectively deal with or restrain the two Chaos Behemoth Kings in order to progress through the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower?

"I have another method we might try," Lin Fei suddenly proposed, sparking a glimmer of hope in Doomsday Supreme. "It might be able to restrain these two Chaos Behemoth Kings, perhaps even help us advance beyond the ninth layer. However, I'm not certain it will work."

"Supreme Lin, you have a plan?" Doomsday Supreme asked eagerly.

"I only suggest we try it," Lin Fei cautioned. "Beyond the Eternal Realm, I possess the Illusory Realm, which targets the mind. The two Chaos Behemoth Kings may possess formidable physical strength, but they appear to lack significant intelligence. Their willpower and mental strength might be vulnerabilities we can exploit."

This was Lin Fei's theory, and testing it would require direct action to see if it held true.

"Illusory Realm!" Lin Fei took a deep breath, and without hesitation, he unleashed the Illusory Realm.

"Boom."

The realm descended suddenly. Even from a distance, the Illusory Realm enveloped the two Chaos Behemoth Kings.
