456. Lin Fei Finally Becomes a God!

With a thunderous "boom," as Lin Fei achieved enlightenment in the Sea of Origin, the sea itself trembled. His consciousness vanished from the Sea of Origin, returning to his physical body.

Lin Fei's body at this moment was in shambles, riddled with innumerable wounds. The eruption of the nine bloodlines exerted a terrifying force. With every passing second, his body endured more torment and was on the verge of collapse.

Finally, Lin Fei's body disintegrated, collapsing at the particle level. However, at the very moment of his body's disintegration, a mysterious force emerged within him. It wasn't the power of his bloodlines, nor was it a sudden outburst of the strand of Origin power within him. It was an unprecedented force - the power of laws! The laws of the world!

World Laws appeared within Lin Fei's body, and he wielded them as effortlessly as his own limbs. His mind was suddenly flooded with clarity and understanding.

"So, this is how it is," Lin Fei murmured. "Without destruction, there can be no construction. Only through the elevation of life can the true nature of Martial Gods be manifested. Reconstructing my body with the World Laws, this is the essence of the transformation from Martial King to Martial God..."

Lin Fei's physical body was being reconstructed, based on these laws. This was, in essence, the reconstruction of life itself. No wonder it led to a significant increase in lifespan.

No wonder the life level of Martial Gods was so superior to that of Martial Kings. The two were fundamentally different in terms of life levels.

Once the reconstruction was complete, Lin Fei's body would no longer be a mere flesh and blood body, but a body of laws, a divine body, so to speak. This was the essence of the saying, "The martial dao can lead to divinity."

The strength of the divine body constructed by Martial Gods depended on the laws comprehended. The foundation of laws used to construct the divine body determined the vast differences in power and abilities.

For example, if one used the laws of the saber or sword to construct the divine body, it would become a Saber Body or Sword Body, specialized in respective abilities.

This divine body was extraordinarily terrifying. With every gesture, it could unleash thousands of sword lights, each imbued with the essence of the Sword Dao, creating earth-shattering power, unrivaled in its capability for destruction.

Similarly, a divine body based on the Fire Dao could command all the fires in the world. Any fire would be harmless to it, whether it be Lin Fei's Triple Yin True Fire or the Phoenix's Fire. When such a divine body wielded flames, its power would increase exponentially, capable of incinerating everything with a mere gesture!

Once Martial Gods manifested their bodies, every move they made was intertwined with the laws, which was incredibly formidable. However, Lin Fei's new body was reconstructed based on the World Laws, a rarity almost unheard of among warriors. Hence, even Lin Fei was uncertain about the capabilities of a body built from World Laws.

As time ticked by, Lin Fei's physical body had completely disintegrated, but simultaneously, the laws were reconstructing a new body for him. Interestingly, the strand of Origin power within Lin Fei remained unchanged throughout the reconstruction of his new body, or divine body.

Now, Lin Fei's body was similar to that of an ordinary person, not the tens of thousands or hundred thousand meters monstrosity of before. But a mere glance at Lin Fei could send shivers down anyone's spine, a pressure emanating from the depths of their soul.

When Lin Fei opened his eyes, his gaze instantly fell upon Feng Ling. The sheer pressure of his gaze made her feel as though her body was about to collapse. She couldn't withstand such terrifying pressure from just a look.

"The difference between the immortal and the mundane," Lin Fei thought. Dao Integration signifies ascension to immortality, and true immortals even transcend gods. A mere glance from an immortal is unbearable for a mortal. How could they even interact?

The same applies to gods. Martial Gods are divine, existing on a fundamentally different plane from mortals. They no longer possess mere flesh and blood bodies, but bodies of law, containing the essence of laws within them.

Not only Feng Ling, but everyone in the vicinity of the Feng family felt the overwhelming presence of Lin Fei. No one dared to meet his gaze, trembling and shocked to their core, kneeling in reverence and awe.

"Too weak..." Lin Fei muttered to himself, acknowledging the vast chasm between his newfound divine status and the mortal realm.

Feeling the fragility and insignificance of the people around him, Lin Fei realized that a mere breath of his could probably end their lives. Such was the power of a Martial God, exalted above all beings.

"Today, I, Lin Fei, ascend to godhood!" he declared. "Let the heavens and earth celebrate! The rain of laws shall fall, and how much you comprehend and benefit from it depends on your fate."

Lin Fei's voice, grand and resonant, echoed in everyone's ears. The birth of a Martial God was a momentous event, one that even the heavens and earth would usually react to with anomalies.

However, Lin Fei suppressed these phenomena, fearing the damage they could cause. But he wasn't stingy; he was ready to bestow a rain of laws.

Thunder rumbled, and instantly, the sky was shrouded with dark clouds, and fierce winds howled. In a moment, a gentle rain began to fall. For Lin Fei, now comprehending the World Laws, summoning wind and rain was mere child's play.

But this was no ordinary rain. As the rain fell, many felt a chilling sensation that seemed to penetrate their bones, quickly merging into their bodies. They felt invigorated, filled with newfound strength. The sickly and weak found themselves revitalized, while martial artists felt their bodies breaking through to new levels. Even Martial Saints and Martial Sovereigns gained new insights, sitting down to meditate and comprehend.

Feng Ling, upon touching the rain, sensed an enhancement in her physical being and even gained some understanding of the "Dao." To her, this was no small matter. Everyone in the Feng family and the Ancient Origin Sect realized what this moment signified. Lin Fei had become a god!

"Congratulations to Ancient Origin Saber King on achieving Martial Gods status!" led by Feng Jiushang, everyone in the Feng family knelt and worshiped in reverence.

A god indeed deserved such veneration from all beings!
