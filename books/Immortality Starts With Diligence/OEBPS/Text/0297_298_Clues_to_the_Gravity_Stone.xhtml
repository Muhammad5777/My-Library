298. Clues to the Gravity Stone

"He suppressed them! This Martial Sovereign, with his own strength, managed to suppress the five elders from Taiyi Dao Palace!"

"It's inconceivable. Can a Martial Sovereign be this powerful? Looking at his Life Wheel, he merely possesses two Divine Abilities."

"Hehe, these two Divine Abilities are no simple feat. Moreover, until now, he has only showcased one of them."

"If I'm not mistaken, both of his Divine Abilities are Great Divine Abilities, perhaps even top-tier ones!"

"Furthermore, he has the Saber Domain. His Saber Domain alone can rival several Great Divine Abilities. This Martial Sovereign is indeed powerful."

"No wonder Taiyi Dao Palace went to such lengths as to dispatch the Five Elders just to eliminate this Martial Sovereign."

Watching Lin Fei single-handedly turn the tables, pinning the Five Elders in a precarious situation, many in the city were left in awe. Were they in a similar situation, they would have been powerless.

Even those Martial Sovereigns in the mid or late stage, possessing several Great Divine Abilities, would find themselves in peril. The gap between Martial Sovereigns is indeed vast. Possessing mere Lesser Divine Abilities, even if one has mastered nine, often pales in comparison to mastering two or three Great Divine Abilities. Moreover, Lin Fei possesses two top-tier Great Divine Abilities!

The Five Elders were struggling. They hadn't anticipated that, even with the Five Directional Array Flags, the Great Mountain Seal, and their combined strength as five Gold Core True Persons, they'd end up in this dire situation. They were constantly on the defense, utterly unable to counter Lin Fei.

"Lin Fei's momentum is unstoppable. Even if we risk everything, we cannot overcome him. Let's go!"

The Five Elders knew they couldn't contend with Lin Fei. So, their first instinct was to retreat.

"Trying to leave? It's too late!"

Lin Fei's gaze was icy cold. How could he let the people of Taiyi Dao Palace go? If the Taiyi Dao Palace could dispatch the Five Elders to eliminate him, it proved their unwavering determination to see him dead.

Given this, Lin Fei would naturally not be merciful. Killing the Five Elders would mean the Taiyi Dao Palace would lose five Gold Core True Persons.

"Buzz."

Within Lin Fei's Saber Domain, a blade quickly materialized. A towering blade, thousands of meters in length. Perhaps everyone had forgotten that he was not just a Martial Sovereign but also a Saber Sovereign. His strongest techniques, especially under his Saber Domain, are rooted in his saber skills!

"Slash!"

With a single swing of Lin Fei's saber, the world seemed to rupture.

A force as if the heavens themselves were descending bore down on the Five Elders, slashing mightily. The Five Elders had to use the Great Mountain Seal to protect themselves.

"Boom!"

The Five Elders had no choice but to place the Great Mountain Seal in front of them. However, the power of this saber strike was too overwhelming! Even their Great Mountain Seal was knocked flying by Lin Fei's attack.

The moment the Great Mountain Seal was sent flying, flames scorched the Five Elder's spells.

Now, without their protection, Lin Fei chanted, "Divine Ability, Black Wind Prison!"

A wind began to stir.

Inside Lin Fei's Saber Domain, the Five Elders suddenly felt a chilly breeze. But as they lowered their heads, they realized that where the wind passed, their flesh instantly turned into white bones. It was the wind of bone erosion!

Moreover, Lin Fei's Black Wind Prison had reached perfection. Its power surged tremendously. Anything touched by this wind, unprotected, would be reduced to bones, whether it be Magic Body or Dao Body. The proverbial 'wind that turns flesh to bone' referred precisely to this eroding wind.

"Screams..."

Witnessing this scene, many Martial Sovereigns and Gold Core True Persons in the city shivered in horror.

In an instant, the Five Elders were turned into bones by the bone-eroding wind. Then, with a crushing force, their bones shattered into dust. Flames soon reduced them to ashes.

Dead!

The Five Elders had fallen!

Five top-tier Gold Core True Persons, once renowned, were now annihilated by Lin Fei, leaving no trace behind.

Silence!

The entire ancient city of Cangli was eerily quiet. Many witnessed this scene. The fall of five Gold Core True Persons was no minor event anywhere. Such a group was a powerful force in its own right, especially when they were the top-tier Gold Core True Persons of Taiyi Dao Palace.

"Retrieve."

Lin Fei withdrew his Saber Domain. Simultaneously, the magic treasures, the Great Mountain Seals of the five Gold Core True Persons, were all collected by him.

In fact, Lin Fei already possessed many Great Mountain Seals. However, the Great Mountain Seal was very unique. Only the continuous refining by disciples of the Taiyi Dao Palace allowed for their integration. Otherwise, they remained separated magic treasures. Although still Gold Core level treasures, their effectiveness was clearly reduced.

Lin Fei chose not to use these newly acquired magic treasures for now. Very few people dared to take the Great Mountain Seal due to the fear of Taiyi Dao Palace's pursuit.

But Lin Fei wasn't in a rush. Once he obliterated the Taiyi Dao Palace in the future, he believed there would be no issue in dealing with the Great Mountain Seal. However, now that the Taiyi Dao Palace had openly dispatched the Five Elders to assassinate him, they would undoubtedly go mad upon hearing of their defeat and death at Lin Fei's hands. Given the depth and strength of the Taiyi Dao Palace, they were not limited to just the Five Elders.

"I must increase my strength quickly," Lin Fei pondered. "It's best to perfect the Gravity Domain and then cultivate another Divine Ability. By that time, with three top-tier Great Divine Abilities coupled with my Saber Domain, I'll be ready to face the threat of Taiyi Dao Palace!"

Lin Fei was well aware of his power. Unlike an ordinary Martial Sovereign, with his two top-tier Great Divine Abilities and the Saber Domain, he could already be considered a top-tier Martial Sovereign. One only needed to look at the shocked expressions of the Gold Core True Persons and other Martial Sovereigns in Cangli Ancient City to understand how powerful he had become.

Seizing the moment when most Martial Sovereigns and Gold Core True Persons in Cangli ancient city were focused on him, Lin Fei loudly declared, "I have come here solely for the Gravity Stone. Whoever has it, or clues about it, will be generously rewarded by me!"

His voice echoed throughout the Cangli ancient city. Had Lin Fei been an unknown figure, his words might have fallen on deaf ears. However, having publicly killed the top-tier Gold Core True Persons of Taiyi Dao Palace outside the city, his displayed strength was nothing short of astounding. Thus, his words carried significant weight.

Lin Fei realized that if not for the incident with the Five Elders, he might have had the leisure to search for the Gravity Stone on the ancient battlefield. But now that they had shown up, there was no telling when the next ambush by the Taiyi Dao Palace would come. He couldn't afford to waste any more time.

He didn't want to delay and put himself in danger. This was the only way to obtain the gravity stone the fastest. Yet, as Lin Fei's words settled, the crowd exchanged glances, and no one responded.

After what felt like a long time, a female voice finally whispered into Lin Fei's ear, "I have clues about the Gravity Stone! I guarantee you'll be satisfied, Sovereign. However, I don't wish to reveal my identity. Please wait for me on the outskirts of the ancient battlefield, and I'll come to you."

Lin Fei scanned the area but couldn't identify the source of the message. It seemed the person intentionally hid her identity. However, as long as there were clues to the Gravity Stone, that was enough for him.

With that, Lin Fei took a step forward, transforming into a streak of light. He bypassed Cangli ancient city and headed directly for the ancient battlefield.
