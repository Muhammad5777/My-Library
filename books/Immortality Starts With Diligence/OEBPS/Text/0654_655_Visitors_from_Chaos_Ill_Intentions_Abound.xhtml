655. Visitors from Chaos, Ill Intentions Abound!

"Lin Fei, what's wrong?" Upon seeing Lin Fei's grave expression, the Azure Emperor Supreme asked in a deep voice.

"They have arrived!"

"They? More than one?"

"Yes, not just one, but... a multitude!"

Hearing this, the Azure Emperor Supreme's expression drastically changed.

"Damn it, the White Cloud Supreme really deserves death! I thought there were only one or two Supremes pursuing him, but now it seems there's a whole group? And the Divine Realm has been exposed..."

The Azure Emperor Supreme now harbored no regrets about the death of the White Cloud Supreme.

The White Cloud Supreme deserved death! He had brought upon them a group of powerful chaotic beings. The Divine Realm had just settled down. And now, they faced another crisis.

What if the newcomers were stronger than the Dark Demon Domain, or even the entire Dark Demon Domain?

"Let us go out and resolve this; then we need not expose the location of the Divine Realm anymore!"

Lin Fei said calmly. Blaming the White Cloud Supreme now was pointless. After all, the White Cloud Supreme was already dead. And the Divine Realm had been discovered.

Self-pity served no purpose now. They could only remedy the situation. If they could annihilate this group of chaotic beings. That would be the remedy!

"Whoosh."

In the next moment, Lin Fei stepped forward. Accompanied by the Azure Emperor Supreme and others, he flew out of the Divine Realm.

At this moment, within the Chaos. In the endless chaotic void, there was a colossal spaceship.

From the spaceship, about eight or nine figures in grey robes jumped down. These figures were somewhat odd. Each had curved horns on their heads. They were dressed in grey robes. Their eyes were blood red. Their gaze was fixed intently on the realm ahead.

"Unexpectedly, there is still a realm here."

"Did that chaotic being flee to a realm? Is this his homeland?"

"A whole realm, indeed this is our fortune."

"However, we must not rashly enter. What if this realm has a Ruler?"

Each of these chaotic beings was a Supreme entity in their own right, yet they harbored a deep wariness of Rulers. The presence or absence of a Ruler in a realm could make all the difference. Even if they discovered such a realm, conquering it would be nearly impossible if it was indeed governed by a Ruler.

"Can't we simply dispatch an avatar to test whether there's a Ruler?"

"Indeed, as long as our true bodies remain within the Chaos, even a realm's Ruler stands no chance against us here. In fact, should a Ruler dare enter the Chaos, we might even have the opportunity to slay them!"

These chaotic powerhouses exuded confidence. In their domain, they might not challenge a Ruler, but in the Chaos, they believed they could defeat one.

As these beings prepared to deploy their avatars,

"Whoosh."

A figure flew out from the Divine Realm.

"Hmm?"

The chaotic entities halted immediately, their gazes intently fixed on the emerging figure.

"Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh."

Then, eight more figures appeared. These were the Azure Emperor Supreme and seven other Supremes, all following Lin Fei.

"Eight Supremes! And one who seems not to be a Supreme? How dare you venture into the Chaos? This realm likely has no Ruler. If there really were a Ruler, they would have waited for us to enter the realm before striking. A realm without a Ruler!"

Gradually, these chaotic beings began to discern the situation of the Divine Realm. Though not absolutely certain, they surmised that the Divine Realm likely lacked a Ruler.

"Who are you?" Lin Fei inquired.

"Who are we? Ha-ha-ha, what does it matter who we are in the Chaos? If you wish not to perish, then leave this realm. A realm without a Ruler cannot be defended by you!"

Their eyes gleamed with greed as if the Divine Realm was already theirs for the taking.

Yet Lin Fei remained unmoved. Seeing that these individuals were not inclined to reveal their origins, he lost interest in further questioning. After all, they were all to be slain by him. He would spare a few for interrogation later.

"Since you will not speak, then die!"

Lin Fei hesitated not a moment longer, instantly springing into action. As he moved, he unleashed the Illusory Realm.

"Boom."

Instantly, the Illusory Realm descended. Lin Fei's Illusory Realm, now at a Supreme level, was capable of "stunning" even other Supremes unless they possessed an extraordinarily strong will or some other special means. Otherwise, none could withstand Lin Fei's Illusory Realm.

"This is..."

As expected, with the descent of the Illusory Realm, these chaotic entities began to look bewildered, effectively "stunned" by its power.

However, a few of the chaotic beings seemed to struggle fiercely.

A moment! It was only a moment's time. It seemed a Supreme had regained consciousness.

"Is this a mental manipulation?"

The now alert chaotic being's eyes were filled with astonishment. But then Lin Fei's second attack arrived.

"Eternal Realm!"

At the very moment Lin Fei deployed the Illusory Realm, he had already mobilized the Eternal Realm.

Lin Fei transformed into the Eternal Realm, sweeping majestically toward the chaotic beings.

The interval between these attacks was merely a moment. Thus, even those stunned by the Illusory Realm who regained consciousness in a moment had no chance to escape. They immediately had to face the overwhelming suppression of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

"Thunderous roar."

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm powerfully crushed downwards.

"No..."

This Supreme, although very powerful, especially in physical strength, was still overwhelmed by the full force of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Unable to withstand it, his body disintegrated inch by inch.

Finally, he collapsed into a cloud of blood mist.

"Buzz."

However, the blood mist quickly reformed, seemingly "resurrected."

Of course, this was not resurrection, as the opponent had never truly died. It was likely some form of recovery or perhaps an undying technique.

In the Chaos, there are various cultivation systems. Each system is somewhat different. Although all are Supremes, many focus on survival. Their ability to recover is terrifying.

Slaying a Supreme is no easy task. These chaotic entities possessed remarkably strong survival instincts. If it had been any other Supreme, perhaps the opponent might have indeed escaped. But this was Lin Fei, and moreover, he had transformed into the world itself.

Even as Lin Fei shattered his opponent into a mist of blood, they remained within his Eternal Realm. Even with their life-preserving tricks, any tactics of survival or disguise were futile within the Eternal Realm.

"Not dead yet? Then the suppression continues!"

Lin Fei was in no hurry. The pressure of the Eternal Realm persisted relentlessly. The cloud of blood continued to be obliterated.

"Spare us, spare us!"

"We are willing to become your servants, please spare our lives!"

"If this continues, we will all die!"

At that moment, screams emanated from the blood mist. Indeed, these chaotic beings had not died. Despite being blown into a mist of blood, they had not perished. But within Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, they could not escape. They could only watch helplessly as their blood form was slowly ground away, eventually disappearing without a trace.

Once the blood mist was completely obliterated, they too would die—a total demise!

Two breaths, three breaths, four breaths, five breaths...

As time passed, more and more chaotic beings awoke within the Eternal Realm. The effect of the Illusory Realm soon wore off. But being caught in the Eternal Realm, their fates were far from desirable.

"You are a Ruler! To exhibit the power of a Ruler even in Chaos. This is a top-tier Supreme! That such a top-tier Supreme could emerge from a mere ordinary realm is inconceivable..."

The remaining chaotic beings were visibly shocked, their minds reeling in astonishment.
