617. Invincible Supreme? Lin Fei's Confidence!

Lin Fei left Great Dream City. He returned to his private chamber. There, he sorted through the intelligence he had gathered from Great Dream Ancestor.

The Divine Realm seemed peaceful on the surface. But was it really? It was already in grave danger. Should those Supremes truly decide to leave the Divine Realm, then what awaited the Divine Realm would be an irreversible catastrophe!

Lin Fei understood that placing his hopes on others was extremely risky. He knew none of the Supremes personally. To expect a Supreme to take him away, to enter the chaos, or even another realm—how could that be easy?

Why would a Supreme care about him? If they were to take someone, it would be someone connected to the Supremes. What was Lin Fei in comparison?

He was very clear about his own position. Even though he was a top-tier Martial Ancestor and a legend within the Divine Realm, in the eyes of the Supremes, he was still insignificant.

Depending on others was unreliable. Only relying on oneself was the most dependable. All along, what Lin Fei sought was eternal freedom.

Now, he had become a Martial Ancestor. He had also achieved immortality. That was, as long as no external forces intervened.

But now, with the invasion of the Dark Demon Domain, Lin Fei could no longer guarantee his eternal freedom.

At least, freedom was impossible. As for immortality, what immortality could Lin Fei speak of if a Supreme intervened?

Previously, had the Supreme from the Dark Demon Domain not been careless, Lin Fei might already be dead.

Now, Lin Fei needed to fight for survival! He needed to live!

To survive under such circumstances, there was only one way. That was to become stronger!

To continuously grow stronger!

Ideally, to become an invincible Supreme. Then, not only could Lin Fei himself survive, but the Divine Realm could also survive.

All of Lin Fei's countless relatives and friends could live as well.

But others might think this was a pipe dream.

Becoming an invincible Supreme? Don't even think about it.

But Lin Fei was different. Even though he was only a Martial Ancestor now, Lin Fei's potential was immeasurable. He had already found the path to becoming a Supreme, even the path to becoming an invincible Supreme!

In fact, Lin Fei still didn't know how to become a Supreme. But that didn't matter. How to become a Supreme was not his concern. He only cared about whether he could enhance his own strength. And Lin Fei knew exactly how to do that.

The Eternal Realm! The Illusory Realm! These were the reasons Lin Fei was confident he could enhance his power!

The Eternal Realm was currently just an ordinary minor world. If it could transform into a True Monarch-level world, then Lin Fei's power would increase tenfold, a hundredfold, his strength immeasurable!

Even if not a Supreme, he would be nearly as powerful. Moreover, if he added the power of the Illusory Realm, continuing to obtain True Monarch-level world laws to integrate into the Illusory Realm, its power would also increase.

When it reached the level of a Supreme, combined with Lin Fei's True Monarch-level Eternal Realm, he would even have the confidence to slay a Supreme! And this was just a True Monarch-level world.

What if Lin Fei's Eternal Realm advanced to a True Immortal-level world? Then Lin Fei was even confident that, relying solely on the Eternal Realm, he could become an invincible Supreme!

It was this foundation that gave Lin Fei the audacity to claim he could become an invincible Supreme.

However, elevating the Eternal Realm, even transforming it into a True Monarch-level world was difficult. The method Lin Fei knew of was to continually obtain the essence of dark demonic life, thus nourishing the life within the Eternal Realm.

Eventually, this would naturally elevate the Eternal Realm. But acquiring the essence of dark demonic life was also difficult for Lin Fei since he had been targeted by the Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain.

Once he made an appearance or pursued those dark demons, the Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain would surely appear to slay him.

Thus, Lin Fei needed to think of a more secure method. At the very least, he needed to have the power to defend himself in front of the Supremes of the Dark Demon Domain!

"Illusory Realm! If my Illusory Realm were a bit stronger. Even if the Supremes were prepared, I could stun them. Even if just for an instant, that would be enough. I could then directly traverse the void to escape."

Lin Fei's eyes brightened. He had thought of a way. The Illusory Realm seemed to be the only way he could break the situation.

If the Illusory Realm could continue to increase its power by several times, or even ten times, it would certainly stun a Supreme. Even for just a moment, Lin Fei could escape comfortably from under a Supreme's grasp. Then he could continue to sweep through dark demonic life, obtaining essence to rapidly elevate the Eternal Realm.

in Fei wasted no time. In the current situation, he had to race against the clock. After all, if too much time passed, the Divine Realm might even collapse. Once the Supremes of the Divine Realm chose to leave, that news would definitely not stay hidden.

Then, the Divine Realm would fall into chaos. Perhaps the Dark Demon Domain could soon occupy the entire Divine Realm. Therefore, time was of the essence.

Thus, Lin Fei utilized his status as a Martial Ancestor. He drew on many pieces of intelligence about True Monarch-level worlds. In the entire Divine Realm, True Monarch-level worlds were actually not numerous.

However, they had accumulated over the years without being destroyed, and their quantity was quite terrifying.

Lin Fei quickly found the exact locations of over a hundred True Monarch-level worlds. And if he continued searching, he could find even more.

However, once Lin Fei decided to extract the world laws from those True Monarch-level worlds, it would inevitably offend many True Immortals and Martial Ancestors. But Lin Fei no longer cared about that.

Moreover, there was no True Immortal or Martial Ancestor who could stop the current Lin Fei. After all, with the combination of the Illusory Realm and the Eternal Realm, the current Lin Fei, though not invincible among True Immortals and Martial Ancestors, had few rivals.

"The first world, Stellar Mystique Realm!"

Lin Fei chose the first True Monarch-level world. Then, with a flash, he dove into the void passage and disappeared without a trace.

...

Outside the Stellar Mystique Realm, people came and went. This was a relatively prosperous True Monarch-level world.

However, the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain had almost been occupied by the dark demons. Even ordinary people knew what this meant.

Once the Yin-Yang Immortal Domain fell, it would mean that the Dark Demon Domain had a foothold.

The Yin-Yang Immortal Domain would become a bridgehead, continuously gathering a large number of dark demons. When the time came, they would surge out, ravaging the entire Divine Realm!

Therefore, some far-sighted people were trying every means to enhance their own strength. Only in this way, when facing danger, could they possibly save themselves.

Thus, the Stellar Mystique Realm became even more prosperous. It was filled with martial and celestial cultivators, trading various cultivation resources. The Stellar Mystique Realm had subtly become a trade world.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei stepped forward. His figure appeared outside the Stellar Mystique Realm. At that moment, many people seemed to sense something.

"Is that... Martial Ancestor Lin from the Great Dream Sect?"

"Martial Ancestor Lin has come to the outskirts of the Stellar Mystique Realm again, is he here to seize the world laws again?"

"Most likely. Martial Ancestor Lin greatly needs the True Monarch-level world laws, said to be for cultivating his Saber World?"

"It's not the Saber World anymore, it's now called the Illusory Realm!"

"What should we do? The Stellar Mystique Realm is under Moonshadow True Immortal."

"It's likely that even Moonshadow True Immortal would have difficulty stopping Martial Ancestor Lin, and might not even show up."

Seeing Lin Fei's arrival, many people felt a sinking feeling. They knew that Lin Fei's objective was mostly the world origin of the Stellar Mystique Realm.

Indeed, Lin Fei immediately addressed the Stellar Mystique Realm: "Stellar Mystique Realm people, I need the world laws of the Stellar Mystique Realm. Let no one obstruct, or face death!"

Lin Fei's actions were extremely domineering. But now, he had to be domineering. Thus, Lin Fei reached out with a great hand, grasping towards the Stellar Mystique Realm.
