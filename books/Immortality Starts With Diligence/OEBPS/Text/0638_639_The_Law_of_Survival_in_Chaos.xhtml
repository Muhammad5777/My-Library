639. The Law of Survival in Chaos

Within the blink of an eye, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm vibrated the void, unleashing a strike of supreme magnitude. Instantaneously, the void shattered.

"Crack."

It was as if Lin Fei could "see" the membrane of the Divine Realm tear open, revealing a vast rift. Without hesitation, he retracted his Eternal Realm.

"Swoosh."

Lin Fei stepped forward, entering the crack. Immediately, he felt a distinct sensation, unlike any spatial traversal he had experienced before, enduring immense pressure. Yet, his body, robust enough to withstand it, bore the strain.

"Swish."

Soon, Lin Fei felt a tightening sensation all over. It seemed he had arrived in a strange place. Lifting his head and looking around, he saw nothing but a white expanse. There was no land; only void surrounded him, and faint, peculiar forces were attempting to erode his physical form.

Of course, any erosion was trivial for Lin Fei—his body was, in essence, the Eternal Realm itself. Without his permission, what force could possibly infiltrate the Eternal Realm?

"These must be the Chaotic Qi," Lin Fei murmured quietly.

Chaotic Qi surrounded him on all sides. For a mere Martial Ancestor, resisting these Qi would be quite challenging and even strenuous. A moment's carelessness could lead to erosion by the Chaotic Qi, potentially causing severe injury or even death.

Thus, Chaos was not a place for martial ancestors to venture. Only a Supreme, with a body capable of resisting the erosion of Chaotic Qi, could survive here.

Of course, should an accident occur, and the Chaotic Qi infiltrate the body of a Supreme, the damage could be immense.

"Shatter the true void."

Lin Fei attempted to obliterate the true void with a single blow but to no avail. The space of Chaos seemed exceptionally stable, resistant even to the power of a Supreme. In the Chaos, one could only rely on flight.

Hence, artifacts that enhanced speed were crucial.

"Swoosh, swoosh."

At that moment, the Azure Emperor Supreme and Black Tortoise Supreme also stepped into the Chaos. Upon seeing Lin Fei, both were slightly taken aback.

Lin Fei stood quietly amidst the Chaos, seemingly without any defensive posture, letting the Chaotic Qi swirl around him. Yet, they could not affect him in the slightest.

"Indeed, our worries were superfluous," the Azure Emperor Supreme commented with a laugh. "Martial Ancestor Lin's ability to vanquish a Supreme from the Dark Demon Domain speaks to his formidable strength, particularly his physical prowess, which certainly matches that of a Supreme. The Chaotic Qi here cannot faze Lin Fei!"

"Azure Emperor Supreme, this Chaos seems boundless. It appears we cannot break through space or traverse it; must we really rely on flight to travel? How long might that take?" Lin Fei couldn't help but ask.

The Azure Emperor Supreme slowly nodded, "True, the void of Chaos is difficult to breach, let alone traverse. We can only rely on flight.

As for the time it will take, there's simply no way around it. But that isn't critical. Who enters Chaos who is not at the level of a Supreme?

A Supreme's lifespan is virtually endless, akin to immortality. What does it matter if we travel thousands or even tens of thousands of years in Chaos? It's inconsequential to us Supremes.

Moreover, traveling through Chaos doesn't necessarily rely on our own flight capabilities. Some artifacts designed for flight are incredibly fast and well-suited for flying through Chaos.

Actually, the current distance between the Dark Demon Domain and the Divine Realm might even assist us. I suspect that the recent silence from the Dark Demon Domain indicates that the Lord of Dark Demons has returned there.

Perhaps he went to retrieve some artifact to use against Lin Fei. But such a journey, even with a flight artifact, would take a considerable amount of time.

This gives the Divine Realm an opportunity, at least in the short term, where we don't have to worry about threats from the Dark Demon Domain."

Lin Fei nodded thoughtfully. Indeed, that was a possibility. The current measures of the Lord of Dark Demons were ineffective against him.

Yet, the Lord would certainly not give up on the Divine Realm. To counter Lin Fei, he would need to return to the Dark Demon Domain first. Perhaps the Lord had some hidden methods or artifacts there that could be used against Lin Fei. Such a journey would take a considerable amount of time.

And that's precisely why the Azure Emperor Supreme and Black Tortoise Supreme had accompanied Lin Fei into the Chaos, without fear of immediate issues arising in the Divine Realm.

Lin Fei took another moment to feel the conditions within the Chaos. Then, without further hesitation, he declared, "Enough delay; we must make haste to the location of the nascent realm. Should anything happen to it, or if it's destroyed, all our efforts would be in vain."

The longer they waited, the more they risked the nascent realm being compromised.

"Agreed, let's depart immediately," Black Tortoise Supreme said. "Stay close behind me."

Only he knew where the nascent realm was located, so naturally, Black Tortoise Supreme led the way. Lin Fei and the Azure Emperor Supreme followed closely behind him, speeding through the depths of Chaos.

Within the vast and silent expanse of Chaos, tranquility ruled, a stark contrast to the turmoil they had left behind in the realms of order.

Lin Fei transformed into a streak of flowing light amidst the Chaos. Though his speed was already considerable, within the vast Chaos, it amounted to little.

At such a pace, even eons would not suffice to reach the end of Chaos.

Does Chaos have an end?

Lin Fei did not know. Yet, it was highly probable that an end existed; only, beings of supreme levels could never hope to reach it.

Days passed one after another. One day, two days, three days...

Lin Fei flew through the Chaos. Within the realm of eternity, time flowed tenfold faster.

Under such rapid temporal currents, Lin Fei's realm of eternity continued to ascend, improving with every moment.

One month, two months, three months...

Over three months had elapsed.

"We have arrived!"

At that moment, the Black Tortoise Supreme ceased to move. He no longer continued to fly.

With a thought, Lin Fei also paused. He sensed, not far ahead in the Chaos, powerful auras one after another. All were of Supreme levels.

Clearly, the Chaos ahead teemed with supremes. Perhaps, they were all vying for the nascent realms. After all, in the Chaos, realms were the most precious treasures. The supremes all hoped to find unclaimed realms.

"Lin Fei, in the Chaos, you truly need not hold any reservations. After all, the supremes of Chaos do not recognize each other. Nor do they know from which realms they hail. The most crucial thing is never to reveal your realm's location.

And certainly, never let anyone track you or use special methods to do so. Like the crisis of the Dark Demon Domain this time. It was because the Radiance Supreme was exploited by the Lord of Dark Demons using some special methods, leading him to our Divine Realm.

As long as you aren't tracked, or your realm's location isn't discovered. Then in the Chaos, we can act without any constraints, fearing no one. In Chaos, only strength is respected!"

The Azure Emperor imparted these insights to Lin Fei in great detail. These were the understandings he had gained from years of navigating the Chaos. This was his experience in venturing through the Chaos.

Here, no one presses with power. Because it's utterly useless. And nobody cares. In the Chaos, strength reigns supreme. That is what matters most.

"Strength reigns supreme?"

A sharp glint flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

"Let us depart, the nascent realm shall be mine!"

Instantly, Lin Fei became a streak of light, soaring towards the deep reaches of the Chaos.
