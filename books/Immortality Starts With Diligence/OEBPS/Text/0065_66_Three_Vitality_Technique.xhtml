66. Three Vitality Technique

A note from RighteousEgg

This is an extra chapter for the review.

"Can martial arts bring immortality?"

Lin Fei's voice echoed throughout the entire hall.

This was Lin Fei's doubt. And also his insistence.

His true purpose in practicing martial arts was to achieve immortality.

Lin Fei's words made the sect master's gaze freeze. He did not answer, but instead looked toward the grand elder.

The grand elder suddenly burst out laughing: "Hahaha, that's a good question. Lin Fei, what if martial arts can grant immortality? What if martial arts cannot grant immortality?"

The grand elder's gaze was also fixed on Lin Fei.

Without hesitation, Lin Fei answered calmly and firmly, "If martial arts can grant immortality, the disciple will do everything in his power to pursue the pinnacle of martial arts! If martial arts cannot grant immortality..."

Lin Fei fell silent.

Back in the Lin family, he had told Lin Miao that martial arts might not grant immortality, so he wanted to become a Qi cultivator.

But if he did not have the talent, no matter what, he could not become a Qi cultivator. What should he do then?

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. At this moment, he seemed to vaguely understand what the heart for martial arts was.

"Swish."

Lin Fei raised his head, and a hint of determination appeared in his gaze.

"If martial arts cannot grant immortality, then the disciple will strive to make martial arts able to grant immortality!"

Lin Fei's voice was calm, but his tone was decisive.

"Hahaha, sect master, did you hear that? You were asking what the heart for martial arts is. And now, Lin Fei has already told you what the heart for martial arts is! This is the heart for martial arts!"

The sect master stared intently at Lin Fei. His mind was shaken!

Until today, he finally understood what was the heart for martial arts.

"Grand Elder, I understand now, thank you for your guidance!"

The sect master respectfully bowed to the grand elder.

Today, the grand elder's actions seemed to be to recruit Lin Fei as a disciple.

But in fact, he was also "enlightening" the sect master.

After all, the sect master got acquired-realm the peak for many years. He was only one step away from being innate.

But this one step was stuck because the sect master's will was not firm enough. He did not have the heart for martial arts!

The elder nodded satisfactorily, and his gaze turned to Lin Fei again.

"Lin Fei, I can definitely answer your question. Martial arts can grant immortality!"

The grand elder's tone was very firm.

Lin Fei felt a shock in his heart. He stared at the grand elder with a piercing gaze.

"Is it true?"

"Hahaha, of course. However, it's very difficult to achieve. And, that's something that comes after achieving innate. You should focus on achieving innate first before talking about eternal life. Now, I want to take you as my disciple, are you willing?"

Lin Fei understood that this was an opportunity, especially since the grand elder had given him the answer he most desired.

"Disciple pays respect to Master!"

Lin Fei respectfully knelt down and paid his respects to the grand elder.

"Congratulations to Grand Elder for acquiring a good disciple!"

The sect master also congratulated Zhao Kongming with a smile.

"Good, Lin Fei, follow me."

Zhao Kongming's figure flickered and he actually floated in the air. Step by step, he walked out of the main hall.

Lin Fei was also extremely surprised, but he didn't hesitate and immediately displayed his lightness skill, chasing after Zhao Kongming's figure.

Although Zhao Kongming didn't seem to be moving fast with each step, Lin Fei used all his strength but still couldn't catch up.

Fortunately, Zhao Kongming's cave dwelling was not far from the main hall. It was located on a mountain peak in the Nine Profound Sect.

There were many mountain peaks within the Nine Profound Sect, all towering high with cave dwellings on them. It was said that these cave dwellings were the residences of innate powerhouses within the sect.

"Shoo."

Lin Fei came to a stop. He had already arrived at Master Zhao Kongming's cave dwelling.

The cave dwelling looked very simple, with only some basic household items. There were several maids cleaning and tidying up inside the cave dwelling.

"Lin Fei, even though you have only acquired first level, you have the triple inner power and your foundation is extremely solid, so you have the potential to become innate in the future!

However, this is only a possibility. Don't slack off in your training at the acquired realm. If you aspire to immortality, you can't train like an ordinary person.

Your inner power is already strong enough. Therefore, you don't need martial arts that focus on increasing inner power significantly.

You should seek stability and consolidate your foundation. Only in this way can you have a stronger foundation after reaching innate and go further."

Lin Fei bowed respectfully.

"Good, I will grant you the Three Vitality Technique. This technique is a unique skill that I obtained when I traveled the world. Because it's special, it wasn't offered to the sect.

So even the Martial Arts Pavilion doesn't have this martial art, only my disciples can practice it... The Three Vitality Technique increases inner power only slightly, and once it's practiced to the peak, it's somewhat challenging to break through innate.

But its greatest effect is stability. It opens up the meridians very steadily. No matter how difficult the meridians are, they can be opened one by one, only the speed differs.

Ordinary people may not be able to reach their peak even after practicing for decades. That's because their basic inner power is average.

But you are different. When you practice the Three Vitality Technique, you can continuously gain inner power from your blood, skin, and bones.

So when your Three Vitality Technique reaches its peak, your inner power will also be immense. By then, breaking through innate will not be a problem at all."

Lin Fei seemed to understand and Zhao Kongming explained it to him in detail.

He even started practicing the Three Vitality Technique on Lin Fei's body.

Zhao Kongming used his innate true qi to help Lin Fei's inner power circulate one revolution, letting Lin Fei become a beginner in the Three Vitality Technique.

This was the benefit of having a master. He could be guided at any time, avoiding taking the wrong path.

Lin Fei stayed in the cave until dusk.

Zhao Kongming waved his hand and said, "Alright, you can go down the mountain first. Now that you have learned the Three Vitality Technique, if you want to learn any combat martial arts, you can go to the martial arts pavilion to choose.

Most martial arts are available in the pavilion. You will still live in the inner gate. If you have any questions about martial arts, you can come to the cave to find me directly."

"Yes, Master!"

Lin Fei nodded.

Although he had become the disciple of the grand elder, It was not that contribution points could be used infinitely, nor could martial arts be learned casually.

That was simply impossible.

With a master, the greatest benefit was to be able to receive guidance.

Lin Fei would continue to live his life as he always had.

"By the way, you have two senior brothers and a senior sister. You will know more about them when they come back."

Lin Fei nodded.

"Master, I take my leave!"

After speaking, Lin Fei didn't disturb Zhao Kongming any longer.

He went down the mountain directly and returned to his residence at the inner gate.

Once he arrived at his residence, Lin Fei recalled the Three Vitality Technique his master had taught him today.

For a moment, a gleam of light flashed in his eyes.

"The Three Vitality Technique is slow? Not necessarily."

Lin Fei silently thought to himself, "System."

The system panel appeared in front of him.
