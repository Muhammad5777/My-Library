161. Finally Became A Martial Arts Grand Master

Lin Fei closed his eyes. Memories emerged in his mind.

Saber-drawing Technique!

Lin Fei obviously had affection for Saber-drawing Technique. His saber intent generated was based on the Saber-drawing Technique and it helped Lin Fei to become the "Number One Saber Under Heaven".

However, Lin Fei knew the Saber-drawing Technique was ultimately inferior to the Heavenly Saber Art.

Heavenly Saber Art was not only an innate level saber technique, more importantly, Saber-drawing Technique also had the momentum of the heavenly saber. This was imitating the "saber momentum". There were some traces of saber momentum.

If the saber intent of Heavenly Saber Art was the main one, then when Lin Fei's saber intent tried to transform into saber momentum in the future, there would definitely be advantages.

Thinking of this, Lin Fei made a decision instantly.

"Heavenly Saber Art, slash!"

Lin Fei shouted lowly. Immediately, in his mind, the illusion of the saber intent of Heavenly Saber Art instantly wiped out the saber intent of the Saber-drawing Technique.

"Hum".

The two saber intents in Lin Fei's mind disappeared instantly.

At the same time, Lin Fei felt that the transcended saber intent in his body seemed to have actively integrated into the saber intent of Heavenly Saber Art and the saber intent of Heavenly Saber Art was rapidly improving at a visible speed.

Mastery, transcendence, perfection! There was almost no pause, no obstacles. The saber intent of Heavenly Saber Art directly reached perfection! Even more than that after reaching perfection, the saber intent of Heavenly Saber Art even improved a little bit more.

Lin Fei could vaguely feel the progress of cultivation after saber intent reached perfection. It was actually improving in the direction of "saber momentum". Although it was just a direction, this was already luckier than countless martial artists. After all, even the direction, many martial artists didn't have this method.

Like Shen Lianxin's saber intent had long reached perfection. However, he couldn't find the direction to continue improving his saber intent.

And Lin Fei used the saber intent of Heavenly Saber Art as the main one, integrating the transcendent saber intent of the Saber-drawing Technique, not only did the saber intent reach perfection, he even directly found the direction above the saber intent.

"Did it work? It really worked, the fusion of the two saber intents, Lin Fei's saber intent has reached perfection."

A hint of seriousness appeared on Shen Lianxin's face. Lin Fei actually fused two kinds of saber intents and made the saber intent reach perfection!

"Boom."

The saber intent of Lin Fei's perfection spread out in all directions, vast and powerful.

Shen Lianxin felt as if a huge mountain was weighing on him. So, Shen Lianxin naturally erupted his saber intent as well. His saber intent was also perfection. And before, Shen Lianxin's saber intent had suppressed Lin Fei's transcendent saber intent.

But now, when Shen Lianxin once again erupted his perfection saber intent, he found out his saber intent was actually being suppressed by Lin Fei's saber intent.

"How is this possible?"

Shen Lianxin was very confident in his own saber intent. He knew that saber intent also has strengths and weaknesses. Even if they were all perfection saber intent, there were still strengths and weaknesses.

Shen Lianxin's saber intent had been honed through countless trials. It was the purest saber intent. It could be considered as the "ultimate" saber intent. Therefore, he created the "Ultimate Saber Slash". Its power was extremely terrifying. Among the innate saber techniques, it could be considered one of the top saber techniques. But even so, his saber intent was actually suppressed by Lin Fei's saber intent. This was simply incredible.

Shen Lianxin didn't know Lin Fei's saber intent was based on the saber intent of Heavenly Saber Art the top innate saber technique that imitates "saber momentum".

The heavenly saber was out, and all sabers bow their heads! It could almost suppress all saber techniques. This was the terrifying part of Heavenly Saber Art. Otherwise, Heavenly Saber Art could not capture other saber techniques, forcefully integrate them, then give birth to saber intent.

Lin Fei's saber intent finally reached perfection and even suppressed Shen Lianxin's saber intent, but it was not over yet. On the contrary, this was just the beginning.

With the perfection of Lin Fei's saber intent, the Saber Qi in Lin Fei's body was also increasing crazily, as if boiling. After all, Lin Fei's Saber Qi was closely related to saber intent.

The progress of Lin Fei's True Intention Scroll was only 42%. But now, with the perfection of Lin Fei's saber intent, its progress began to skyrocket.

50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, 90%......

At the same time, in Lin Fei's mind, the system's voice sounded again.

"Your martial arts True Intention Scroll, inspired by the perfection of saber intent, begins to actively integrate saber intent."

"Your True Intention Scroll's progress has increased to 100%."

"Your True Intention Scroll has reached perfection!"

"Boom."

In the void, countless Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth converged above Lin Fei's head, just like a giant vortex. The terrifying spiritual Qi, like a river rushing back, rushed crazily into Lin Fei's body.

Countless heavenly and earthly Spiritual Qi transformed into Innate True Qi, then fused with Lin Fei's saber intent, and converted into Saber Qi.

Lin Fei's Saber Qi almost at a speed visible to the naked eye, quickly expanding. The pressure on Lin Fei was also revealed without concealment. This was the pure pressure of Saber Qi.

"Is this... True Qi perfection? Martial Arts Grand Master?"

Shen Lianxin was even more shocked. Lin Fei tried sabers with the world's saber users not to temper the saber intent but to generate a second type of saber intent. Then the two types of saber intent fused, making the saber intent complete perfection.

Shen Lianxin could understand that. But what did saber intent perfection have to do with Innate True Qi?

The two were completely unrelated! But now, not only had Lin Fei's saber intent reached perfection, but even True Qi had also reached perfection, was this a coincidence?

Shen Lianxin simply didn't believe that this was any coincidence. Lin Fei's Innate True Qi definitely had a lot to do with the saber intent!

"Shh."

Lin Fei opened his eyes. At this moment, his Saber Qi and saber intent all radiated without scruples. Even Shen Lianxin had to take a few steps back. He felt that Lin Fei's strength now had increased by more than ten times!?

If he fought with Lin Fei again, Shen Lianxin estimated that he couldn't even block a move!

"Lin Fei, congratulations. Now that True Qi is perfection and saber intent is perfection. You are truly a martial arts grandmaster now! And it's just a step away from being a Martial Saint!" Shen Lianxin spoke, congratulating Lin Fei.

"Saber Qi perfection. Saber intent perfection. Today, I finally became a martial arts grandmaster!"

Lin Fei's eyes were very calm. But, his heart was not calm.

Martial Arts Grand Master!

This was almost standing at the pinnacle of the innate. The next step was only one goal.

Foundation Establishment!

At this moment, Lin Fei's Saber Qi was boiling inside his body. He couldn't help but let out a long howl.

"Boom."

The next moment, Lin Fei's saber energy quickly condensed into a giant heavenly saber in the void.

Heavenly Saber Art!

And this time the condensed heavenly saber was not two hundred and fifty meters but one thousand meters!

One thousand meters of saber energy, as if it cut open the void. Looking far away, as if a huge crack had appeared in the sky.

"System."

Lin Fei murmured in his heart. Immediately, the system panel appeared in front of him.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Martial Arts Grand Master

True Intention Scroll: Perfection

Saber Intent: Perfection (Progress 5%)

Fist Intent: Mastery (Progress 81%)

Immeasurable Fist: Transcendence (Progress 50%)

Close Yet Distant: Transcendence (Progress 0%)

Refining The Golden Body: (Progress 0%)

Lin Fei looked at the system panel.

The martial arts realm had already become a Martial Arts Grand Master. Moreover, saber intent had reached perfection. And after perfection, there was still a 5% progress in cultivation.

Lin Fei estimated that if the saber intent continued to improve, once it reached 100% progress in cultivation, then the saber intent could transform into saber momentum!

Saber momentum could be compared to Martial Saints!

Of course, this would definitely be difficult. However, this represented that Lin Fei now had a direction toward saber momentum. This point had already surpassed countless martial artists.

Now that Lin Fei's Saber Qi was perfect, and saber intent was perfect. His entire martial arts cultivation had reached a point where it can no longer progress. Next, he only had one goal. That was, to build the foundation.

"Brother Shen, let's go."

Lin Fei left the riverside directly with Shen Lianxin.
