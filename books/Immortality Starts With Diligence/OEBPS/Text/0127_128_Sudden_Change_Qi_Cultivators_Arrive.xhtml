128. Sudden Change, Qi Cultivators Arrive

Just as the momentum of the two reached its peak, preparing to decide the victory with one move, a crane's cry suddenly came from the distance. At the same time, Lin Fei and Lu Zongyi both felt something. They immediately looked towards the distant sky.

In the sky, more than ten immortal cranes flew over the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference. On the cranes, there were more than ten Qi cultivators wearing Daoist robes!

Yes, Qi cultivators.

At a glance, innate martial artists could sense the spiritual power fluctuations of the Qi cultivators. Among them, an old man with white hair and proud eyes glanced at the many martial artists below. Then he shouted loudly, "I am Yuan Gangzi from Guardian Primordial Mountain! I am tracking a demonic cultivator who stole our Guardian Primordial Mountain's cultivation technique, and he deserves death for his crime!

This demonic cultivator has blended into the crowd and may be among the Martial Alliance. So, I have no choice but to offend you all, and I must find this demonic cultivator today! Before the demonic cultivator is found, no one is allowed to leave the mountain. Set up the formation!"

As Yuan Gangzi gave the order, more than ten Qi cultivators behind him who were sitting on immortal cranes, jumped off the cranes directly. Formation flags in their hands were placed in all directions in an instant.

"Buzz."

The next moment, the entire Martial Alliance headquarter was separated by a layer of light. This was a formation that completely sealed the inside and outside. Once someone tried to leave, Yuan Gangzi would be alerted. No one could escape!

"Yuan Gangzi, what do you want? Today is our Martial Alliance's grand event, quickly withdraw your formation to save face."

"That's right, Yuan Gangzi, let's talk about whatever matter you have after the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference is over."

For a moment, many older martial arts masters from various sects stood up, angry at Yuan Gangzi. Although their tone was severe, there seemed to be a hint of apprehension.

"Guardian Primordial Mountain?"

Lin Fei's eyebrows furrowed slightly. He had never heard of Guardian Primordial Mountain. At this time, Lu Zongyi, who was opposite Lin Fei, had already withdrawn his sword intent.

His face was pale, and he said in a deep voice, "Guardian Primordial Mountain is the only immortal sect near Cang City Mountian. Like He Mountain Dao, it is a major sect in the immortal Daoist school. There are many top Qi cultivators in the sect.

More importantly, Guardian Primordial Mountain has a Qi Condensation Perfection cultivator. It is said that within a short period of time, no more than a year, he will definitely succeed in forming his foundation. So, Guardian Primordial Mountain has been acting quite unscrupulously recently."

Lin Fei understood. He looked at Yuan Gangzi who was still sitting on the back of the immortal crane, looking down at all the martial artists below from a high position.

As for those Guardian Primordial Mountain's Qi cultivators, their eyes were indifferent. They didn't care about the curses from the martial artists below. More importantly, there was no fear in their eyes.

Perhaps, in the eyes of the Qi cultivators of Guardian Primordial Mountain, the Martial Alliance is nothing at all. Cang City Mountain, rather than being the territory of the Martial Alliance, it is more like the sphere of influence of Guardian Primordial Mountain.

"Too much bullying!"

"This is the headquarters of the Martial Alliance, not the gate of your Guardian Primordial Mountain."

"Talking about sealing off just like that, what do you Guardian Primordial Mountain think of us?"

The Martial Alliance's thirteen sects were established initially to counter the immortal sects.

But what about now?

Although they were angrily cursing one by one, not a single one dared to take action. Even the mighty innate martial artists and martial arts masters, facing the Qi cultivators of Guardian Primordial Mountain, didn't even dare to make a move.

"Martial Alliance..."

Lin Fei glanced at the martial arts masters of the Martial Alliance's thirteen sects. He was very disappointed.

These martial arts masters, innate experts, were unattainable powerhouses in the eyes of ordinary disciples.

But what about now?

Facing the Qi cultivators of Guardian Primordial Mountain, they didn't even dare to make a move.

Yuan Gangzi had already set up a formation directly. It was equivalent to sealing off all the martial artists here.

This is a disgrace! The disgrace of martial artists!

"Brother Lin, are you very disappointed?"

At this moment, Lu Zongyi suddenly spoke up. His eyes were sharp as a sword.

"This is the Martial Alliance. Or rather, this is the martial artist of today. For hundreds of years, they have been suppressed by the Qi cultivators. Facing the Qi cultivators, martial artists have long lost their bravery.

Moreover, do you know why those innate martial artists don't dare to make a move at all? Because Yuan Gangzi is at the eleventh level of Qi condensation. That is, a top Qi condensation cultivator! Other than martial arts Grand Masters, no one among martial artists can fight against top Qi Condensation cultivators."

Lin Fei understood. No wonder Yuan Gangzi was so fearless. Even in the face of hundreds of martial artists and thirteen martial arts masters, he remained unflustered. Even standing proudly on the back of the immortal crane, looking down at the crowd. That's because Yuan Gangzi had enough confidence!

The eleventh level of Qi condensation was a truly top Qi Condensation cultivator.

Qi condensation also had twelve levels. And those above the tenth level could be called top Qi cultivators. Unless martial arts grandmasters, no one could compete with top Qi cultivators. Of course, except for some rare and exceptional talents.

However, the current martial arts world was in decline. Where were those rare and exceptional talents? Even martial arts grandmasters are few and far between.

On the back of the immortal crane, Yuan Gangzi stood with his hands behind his back. With an indifferent gaze, he looked at the martial artists below and said coldly, "All of you cooperate well with the search by my Guardian Primordial Mountain disciples. Otherwise, you will be killed without mercy!"

Domineering!

This is Yuan Gangzi.

This is Guardian Primordial Mountain.

This is the Immortal Sect!

Some acquired martial artists clenched their teeth, obviously furious to the extreme. Guardian Primordial Mountain's Qi-condensing disciples were about to begin their search.

"Fuck your search!"

"Die!"

Martial artists never lack bravery. Some acquired martial artists or hot-tempered wanderers couldn't hold back the anger in their hearts. They attacked the Qi condensing disciples who came to search.

However, the next moment, these acquired martial artists couldn't even break through the defensive spells of these disciples. They were killed by the magical artifacts activated by the Qi-condensing disciples, their heads severed, or their hearts pierced.

A strong smell of blood filled the air. Many martial artists were extremely angry, but they dared not speak. They were acquired martial artists and they couldn't even break the defensive spells of the Qi cultivators. What could they do to resist?

Only innate martial artists could use Innate True Qi to break through the spells and magical artifacts of the Qi cultivators, to compete with them.

However, out of the thirteen innate martial artists, who would dare to take action?

"Buzz."

Many martial artists' swords suddenly began to hum. Sword Intent! It was Lu Zongyi who had unleashed his Sword Intent.

"Yuan Gangzi, martial artists are not to be insulted!"

Lu Zongyi's eyes were as sharp as a sword. At this moment, his eyes were filled with killing intent!

"Whoosh."

Lu Zongyi drew his sword! He pulled out the sword from behind him. Even just now, when Lu Zongyi challenged Lin Fei, he didn't draw his long sword. But now, he unsheathed his sword.

"Sword Control Slash!"

Lu Zongyi's voice was loud and resounding, echoing in everyone's ears. At the same time, his sword actually flew out directly, carrying a meter of sword energy and soaring Sword Intent, slashed fiercely at Yuan Gangzi!

"Boom."

Everyone looked at Yuan Gangzi in the void. Sword Intent plus a meter of sword energy, none of the martial arts masters present, except Lin Fei, dared to guarantee that they could withstand Lu Zongyi's sword. This was also Lu Zongyi's strongest sword.

However, this sword stopped. It stopped three meters in front of Yuan Gangzi. Yuan Gangzi didn't even move his body. He just stared coldly at Lu Zongyi below. Even his eyes were filled with a hint of pity and mockery.

"Sword Intent is indeed not bad. You martial artists always think that with Sword Intent, you can slash us Qi cultivators? How ignorant! And the recently popular 'Immortal Slayer Saber', killing two useless He Mountain Dao disciples, was exaggerated by you as an immortal slayer?

Today, even if I stand here, the 'Immortal Slayer Saber' won't hurt a hair on my body! As for you, the innate martial artist of Hidden Sword Sect, go to hell!"

Yuan Gangzi stretched out his hand, pointed at Lu Zongyi, and flicked his finger lightly.

"Whoosh."

A silver flash appeared and disappeared in an instant.

Flying sword! This is a real flying sword!

In the blink of an eye, it arrived in front of Lu Zongyi.

The flying sword didn't reveal any earth-shattering aura, only the ultimate sharpness!

Lu Zongyi could only slash out with his sword again. A massive amount of Innate True Qi erupted instantly.

However, the flying sword seemed unaffected. Its speed increased dramatically. It pierced through Lu Zongyi's protective True Qi in an instant.

Lu Zongyi knew he couldn't block Yuan Gangzi's flying sword. So, he closed his eyes in despair.

"Snap."

The next moment, a fair hand reached out in front of Lu Zongyi. It gently grabbed the sharp flying sword that was aimed at him.
