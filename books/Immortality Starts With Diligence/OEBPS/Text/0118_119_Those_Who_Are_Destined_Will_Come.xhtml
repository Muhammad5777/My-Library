119. Those Who Are Destined Will Come

Lin Fei became the Grand Elder of the Nine Profound Sect. Even Lin Fei himself was a little dazed. However, Lin Fei soon calmed down and he went to visit the Lin family members.

The Lin family members were now all in the Nine Profound Sect. As for the business in Zhou Mountain Mansion City, the Lin family naturally handed it over. Now, there was no trouble in Zhou Mountain Mansion City for Lin family. His parents and elder brother wanted to return. But after weighing the pros and cons, Lin Fei decided against it.

"Elder brother, our Lin family needs to shift our focus. In the future, gradually move our business to the towns near the Nine Profound Sect. And the Lin family should stay on the Nine Profound Sect mountain, which is the safest. Now, I am already the Grand Elder of the Nine Profound Sect. I have enough status to arrange for the Lin family."

After careful consideration, Lin Fei decided to move all of the Lin family's properties and slowly relocate them near the Nine Profound Sect. The Lin family members would live on the Nine Profound Sect mountain.

After all, as Lin Fei's strength grew stronger, it was inevitable that he would offend some people. And the strength of those people would grow stronger and stronger, and their power would become more terrifying.

If the Lin family were still in Zhou Mountain Mansion City, Lin Fei would not only be distracted but he would also be unable to guarantee the safety of his parents and relatives.

Moving the entire family was the wisest choice. Lin Fei's move also tied him completely to the Nine Profound Sect. This was also his sense of belonging to the Nine Profound Sect.

Thinking about the founder of the Nine Profound Sect who was once amazingly talented and unparalleled and considering the current sect master and his master's various treatments of him, Lin Fei had absolute trust in the Nine Profound Sect.

They would prosper or decline together!

Although his parents were somewhat reluctant, they were all reasonable people. Especially his elder brother, who finally agreed after careful consideration. He began to arrange for the relocation of the Lin family's properties.

Lin Fei left these matters to his elder brother, Lin Wen, without interfering.

Lin Fei then found Lin Miao.

"Lin Miao, do you know how to set up a Gathering Spirit Formation?" Lin Fei asked Lin Miao.

Now that he had achieved the innate realm and even became the Grand Elder of the Nine Profound Sect. He had killed not just one or two Qi Condensation cultivators, even two high-level Qi Condensation cultivators had been killed by him. He would not be afraid of exposing a mere Qi Condensation cultivator like Lin Miao who was only at the first level.

Lin Miao nodded and said, "The Qi Condensation cultivator from the Yin Ren Mansion had some formations on him. The Gathering Spirit Formation is a very simple one, the effect might not be very good, but I can set it up."

"It doesn't matter. As an innate cultivator, although I need the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth, I don't need it as much as you Qi Condensation cultivators. You can set up a simple Gathering Spirit Formation in my cave."

So, Lin Fei took Lin Miao to his new cave.

Lin Fei was now a Grand Elder. Naturally, he had a new cave. He even had his own mountain peak, just like his master, Zhao Kongming. Anyway, there were many vacant mountain peaks in the Nine Profound Sect. If one day, all the mountain peaks in the Nine Profound Sect were occupied, It would probably be the most prosperous time for the Nine Profound Sect!

Soon, Lin Miao finished setting up the cave.

Lin Fei said to Lin Miao, "Lin Miao, now that you are in the Nine Profound Sect, you don't have to hide anymore. You can cultivate or refine pills as you wish. If you encounter any trouble or problems, come directly to me. I think the sect master would also be very welcome to have a Qi Condensation cultivator like you staying on the mountain."

Lin Miao was overjoyed upon hearing this. This meant that he was truly free! Previously, in the Lin Mansion, Lin Miao had been hiding from others. People from the Lin Mansion didn't dare to come to where he lived and he didn't dare to interact with others either. But now, he had come to the Nine Profound Sect. He no longer needed to worry about his identity as a Qi Condensation cultivator.

"Thank you, Young Master."

"Alright, you can go now."

Lin Fei waved his hand, letting Lin Miao leave.

"Now that I've advanced to the innate realm, how can I not have a suitable combat martial art? I need to visit the Martial Arts Pavilion again."

Lin Fei got up and went straight to the Martial Arts Pavilion. Soon, Lin Fei arrived at the Martial Arts Pavilion. The Martial Arts Pavilion was bustling with people coming and going, just as lively as before.

The elder guarding the Martial Arts Pavilion still holding a wine gourd, occasionally took a sip of the fine wine in the gourd. Lying on the chair, squinting his eyes, he seemed very comfortable.

Lin Fei walked into the Martial Arts Pavilion. The elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion didn't even look at him, just lazily said, "The first floor contains Body Refining level martial arts, and the second floor has acquired level martial arts, go in and choose for yourself."

"Snap."

A token was thrown in front of the elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion. At the same time, a familiar voice rang in the elder's ears, "Where can I choose martial arts at the innate level?"

"Innate level martial arts?"

The elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion suddenly stood up. His murky eyes seemed to clear up instantly. He saw Lin Fei and he seemed to remember Lin Fei. When he was just about to get angry feeling that Lin Fei was teasing him, he saw the token on the table.

It read, "Grand Elder, Lin Fei."

Seeing the identity mark on the token, the elder widened his eyes.

"You are... the inner sect disciple who chose three Body Refining martial arts in the Body Refining realm and combined three types of inner power at the acquired realm?" The elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion recognized Lin Fei.

Lin Fei calmly said, "It seems that Elder still has some impression of me."

"But your token..." The elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion was a bit confused. He had never encountered such a situation before.

"I've advanced to the innate realm, so the sect master gave me this token, is there a problem?"

The elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion's eyes narrowed slightly. His heart was even more shocked by the news.

Innate Realm! Lin Fei actually achieved the innate realm?

He had been in the Nine Profound Sect for decades and had only reached the third level of the acquired realm. He was inferior to many disciples. Otherwise, he wouldn't have been guarding the Martial Arts Pavilion.

"No... No problem. When I first saw Grand Elder Lin, I felt that Grand Elder Lin was extremely talented and had unlimited potential! Now it seems, how could Grand Elder Lin be just extremely talented? You're almost on par with the founder of our sect!"

The elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion's face turned red. Obviously, saying these words was somewhat difficult for him.

"Alright, I've just advanced to the innate realm and need innate-level martial arts. Tell me, where are the innate level martial arts?"

"Grand Elder Lin, the innate level martial arts are all on the third floor. Furthermore, as a Grand Elder, you can browse all the martial arts of our sect without paying any contribution points. And you can practice innate martial arts right on the third floor."

Lin Fei nodded. The elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion respectfully handed the token back to Lin Fei. Lin Fei turned around and headed straight for the third floor of the Martial Arts Pavilion.

There were many people on the first and second floors. But on the third floor, there was only Lin Fei. Moreover, the third floor was almost empty, and there were only a few rows of bookshelves.

The martial arts secret books on the shelves were sparse and very rare. After all, innate level martial arts, even in the Nine Profound Sect, there weren't many. Among them, many were martial arts collected by the founder of the Nine Profound Sect when he traveled the world.

"I don't need any cultivation techniques. The True Intention Scroll, it's probably better than any innate-level cultivation technique. The key is combat martial arts. Saber technique, fist technique, and lightness technique, I should choose one of each."

Lin Fei had a plan in mind. He needed to choose a saber technique first. After all, Lin Fei's body had been cultivated with Saber Qi by the True Intention Scroll. It was a natural fit for saber techniques. With the saber technique displayed by Saber Qi, combined with the support of saber intent, the power would probably be unimaginable.

On the shelves, there weren't many innate-level saber techniques, but each one was extraordinary.

"Water Splitting Chop! When cultivated to perfection, a single saber can cut through a river."

"Mountain Cleaving Slash! When cultivated to perfection, the saber can cleave mountains!"

"Three Abyssal Sabers! When cultivated to perfection, one will surely develop saber intent. The saber emerges as if hell descends, an Asura is born, and its killing intent can sever souls!"

"Twenty-Three Sabers! When cultivated to perfection, one will comprehend saber intent and can use Innate True Qi to control the saber to kill enemies!"

Lin Fei looked at the shelves, filled with various saber techniques, and was quite shocked in his heart. These saber techniques, every single one of them was an earth-shattering divine skill.

Some saber techniques even explicitly stated that once cultivated to perfection, one would be able to develop saber intent. Such saber techniques could already be considered top-notch.

However, which saber technique should Lin Fei choose?

Lin Fei followed the bookshelves, carefully examining each saber technique. Although these saber techniques were very powerful and their power could be considered earth-shattering, they were not Lin Fei's most satisfactory martial arts.

Choosing martial arts wasn't about necessarily having the strongest power, but it must be the one that suits oneself best. And Lin Fei was now selecting the most suitable saber technique for himself.

However, with so many choices, Lin Fei really had difficulty choosing.

"Right, why should I pick one by one? Let these martial arts come to me on their own."

A flash of inspiration appeared in Lin Fei's mind. He thought of a method. So, Lin Fei spoke to a row of saber technique secret books on the bookshelf: "I currently lack a saber technique. I have Saber Qi and saber intent. If you feel compatible with my saber intent and Saber Qi, then take the initiative to come to me."

The next moment, Lin Fei burst out with saber intent.

"Boom."

The saber intent exploded in the empty third floor of the Martial Arts Pavilion, sweeping all directions. At the same time, Lin Fei stretched out his hand. From his hand, a thirty meters high saber energy suddenly condensed, spanning across the void!

"Those who are destined, come forth! Perhaps you can become my martial art!"

Lin Fei's voice echoed throughout the third floor of the Martial Arts Pavilion.
