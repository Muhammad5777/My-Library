526. Lin Fei Arrives in the Heavenly Secret Realm

In the Heavenly Secret Realm, a streak of light appeared on its outskirts, eventually halting and revealing a figure. The sight of this figure caused the avatars of some Martial Gods and Deity Transformation powerhouses to widen their eyes in alarm.

"Lin Fei has arrived!" they exclaimed.

"Lin Fei has come to the Heavenly Secret Realm. Does he intend to contend for it?"

"He's rumored to have recently passed the fifth thunder tribulation and even the earth-shattering seventh thunder. His power is unfathomable."

"The Heavenly Secret Realm shows no signs of collapsing. Is Lin Fei planning to set up a long-term strategy, waiting for thousands or even tens of thousands of years? That doesn't seem like his style."

"Correct, the worlds Lin Fei typically contends for are those on the brink of collapse. Once he competes for them, he can immediately obtain their world origins. But the Heavenly Secret Realm is different. To date, the Heavenly Secret Realm remains stable, showing no signs of collapse. What is Lin Fei planning?"

As Lin Fei appeared, the expressions of various Martial Gods and Deity Transformation powerhouses changed dramatically. They whispered among themselves, their faces revealing a hint of fear.

Clearly, they were apprehensive about Lin Fei. His reputation wasn't built on empty boasts but carved out through formidable and decisive actions. The fallen Martial Gods and Deity Transformation powerhouses served as warnings to anyone who dared to underestimate him. No one dared to belittle Lin Fei any longer.

"Heavenly Secret Realm, I wonder if I can enter?" Lin Fei took a deep breath and, with a swift movement, flew into the Heavenly Secret Realm.

As Lin Fei arrived in the Heavenly Secret Realm, news of his presence swiftly spread throughout the Divine Realm. Major powers were now paying close attention to Lin Fei, recognizing him as a key figure capable of altering the dynamics of world contention. With the title of "Number One Martial God," he was a subject of widespread interest.

While the news of Lin Fei spread, in the Nine Dragons Heaven of the Ten Directions Realm, a Daoist in a green robe sat cross-legged on a mountaintop. His communication stone suddenly vibrated. The Daoist picked up the stone, sensing its message: "Lin Fei of the Ancient Covenant has entered the Heavenly Secret Realm!" The message was brief, but it caught his attention.

The Daoist's eyes opened, flashing with sharpness. "Finally left the Ancient Covenant, has he?" he mused. "I owed him a favor; this will be repayment. A Martial God, even known as the Number One Martial God... Heh, slaying the Divine Realm's top Martial God could be quite interesting."

The Daoist then stood up and stepped forward, creating a ripple in the void before his figure vanished into a void passage, disappearing without a trace. Only True Monarchs could traverse the void like this. This Daoist was indeed a True Monarch!

Meanwhile, inside the Heavenly Secret Realm, Lin Fei was unaware that his departure from the Ancient Covenant had prompted a True Monarch to come after him. He had merely wanted to explore the Heavenly Secret Realm. Upon entering, he immediately felt a greater constraint than in the Divine Realm, as if countless threads bound his entire body. Every movement, every gesture he made seemed to break numerous threads, an incredibly uncomfortable sensation.

Lin Fei had the urge to unleash all his power, even with the augmentation of the Saber World, to break these bindings. But he refrained from doing so, understanding that this sensation was due to his physical body reaching a critical point. If he used the Saber World's augmentation, elevating his power to the True Monarch level, the Heavenly Secret Realm would likely collapse immediately.

Without the enhancement of the Saber World, Lin Fei could still be accommodated by the minor world. This was actually quite fortunate, as it meant he could still contend for the world origins of minor worlds—a valuable opportunity that might not come again.

However, Lin Fei's immediate concern was to see if he could accelerate the collapse of the Heavenly Secret Realm. If it required laying plans for thousands of years, Lin Fei wouldn't waste his time, regardless of how vast the realm was or how abundant its world origins were.

Upon entering the Heavenly Secret Realm, Lin Fei immediately felt many watchful eyes on him—various Deity Transformation and Martial God powerhouses who had been scheming in the realm for years. Their gazes were unfriendly, clearly unwelcoming Lin Fei's interference.

Yet, Lin Fei paid them no mind. Looking up into the void, he saw the 'threads' binding him, each connected to the void and seemingly leading in the same direction.

Following these threads, Lin Fei began to sense the 'laws' of the Heavenly Secret Realm. Like any other world, it had its own set of laws that kept it functioning and intact.

"So, if I obliterate or break these laws, can I accelerate the collapse of the Heavenly Secret Realm?" Lin Fei pondered. With that thought, he stepped forward, moving to the upper space of the Heavenly Secret Realm.

"What does Lin Fei intend to do?"

"Lin Fei is always assertive. His visit to the Heavenly Secret Realm surely isn't for a simple matter."

"He's silent as if searching for something. What if he plans to monopolize the Heavenly Secret Realm? How should we respond?"

"What else can we do? Do you think you can contend with Lin Fei?"

"But... it's hard to accept!"

Amidst hushed discussions, many Martial Gods and Deity Transformation powerhouses watched Lin Fei's every move with intense scrutiny and nervousness.

Having laid their plans and battled amongst themselves for many years in the Heavenly Secret Realm, they were now faced with a "mighty dragon" – Lin Fei, whose power could potentially overshadow theirs. Yet, Lin Fei's true intention in the realm remained unclear to them.

Lin Fei, step by step, moved into the void, fully aware of the many eyes tracking his actions. Unperturbed, he focused solely on the pervasive laws within the void. He intended to attempt breaking or even annihilating these laws, something he couldn't have imagined doing before.

Previously, his power hadn't reached a critical point, and he was unable to sense the various laws within a minor world. But now, having reached that critical point, Lin Fei could distinctly feel the constraints imposed by the world's rules.

Taking a deep breath and having resolved to test his limits, Lin Fei didn't hesitate. He refrained from deploying the Saber World's augmentation and relied solely on his physical strength, which had also reached a critical point.

"Boom!"

The next moment, the two thousand six hundred Particle Worlds within him erupted. Lin Fei's Martial God form expanded dramatically—ten meters, a hundred meters, a thousand meters until he became a colossal giant spanning hundreds of thousands of meters, towering and spanning across the void. The Particle Worlds inside him roared continuously, almost materializing, instilling fear in those who beheld him.

"Break!"

Lin Fei thundered a punch, targeting the void of the Heavenly Secret Realm and the various laws spread throughout it.
