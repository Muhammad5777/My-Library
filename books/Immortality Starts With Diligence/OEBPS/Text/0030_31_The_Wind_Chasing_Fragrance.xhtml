31. The Wind Chasing Fragrance

"May I know who you are?"

Lin Fei didn't recognize this woman.

At this moment, one of the woman's maidservants said, "Young Master Lin, this is our Miss."

"So it's Miss Liu!"

Lin Fei immediately understood. This young woman must be Ling Yao's distant cousin and the person he needed to protect.

Miss Liu looked graceful and said, "Thank you for coming all the way, Young Master Lin. Please forgive my father's neglect earlier."

"It's nothing. The Liu family didn't mistreat me." Pausing for a moment, Lin Fei asked, "Miss Liu, what brings you to see me?"

Lin Fei knew that Miss Liu was a young lady from a wealthy family and not someone who would casually meet strangers. Since she came to find him, there must be a reason.

Miss Liu smiled slightly and said, "I heard that Young Master Lin had arrived, so I came to see you. My father may not know my cousin's character, but how could I not? My cousin is always cold on the outside but warm at heart, and very perceptive.

If she promised to come to the Liu family but couldn't make it due to other matters, then the person she sent to replace her must be no ordinary person. Even if Young Master Lin is not an acquired martial artist, I believe you have other means that are not inferior to those of one.

I'm afraid my father's neglect may have caused some displeasure in your heart. So I came to apologize."

Lin Fei found it amusing. This Miss Liu was much smarter than Mr. Liu.

"Miss Liu, don't worry. Since I've promised to protect you, I will do my best to keep you safe." Lin Fei also made a promise.

Only then did Miss Liu feel relieved, so she bowed and left.

"This Lin Fei... seems to be just an acquired martial artist at most, does Miss really value him so much? You even went to see him specifically." Miss Liu's maid seemed to have doubts.

"What do you know? My cousin is proud, and even if someone is also an acquired martial artist, she won't look at them if they are not exceptionally talented.

But Lin Fei, a mere Body Refining martial artist, can make my cousin specially commission him to protect me. It shows that Lin Fei definitely has extraordinary abilities.

I'm afraid it depends on Lin Fei in the end whether I can get through this ordeal safely."

After speaking, Liu's expression also revealed a hint of worry.

The flower thief the Wind Chasing Fragrance had never failed in Yi Mountain Mansion City recently.

Even if she was clever, she was ultimately a weak woman with no ability to defend herself. She still had some worries in her heart.

Three days passed in a flash.

The Liu Mansion was very quiet, and nothing happened.

Not only did the Wind Chasing Fragrance not come, but there was also even no trace of the Wind Chasing Fragrance in Yi Mountian Mansion City.

However, the people in the Liu Mansion did not relax their vigilance.

As long as the Wind Chasing Fragrance was not eliminated, they would not be at ease.

After all, who didn't know the most beautiful person in Yi Mountian Mansion City was the daughter of the Liu Mansion?

Lin Fei was in the hall. He, like other guests, was guarding near the hall.

At this moment, a man with a long sword walked in from outside.

He was Shen Changsheng, known for his reputation in the martial arts community.

"Wait."

A short monk suddenly stood in front of Shen Changsheng.

"What's the matter?" Shen Changsheng's face turned cold, and his tone was somewhat unfriendly. After all, anyone would be unhappy when blocked.

The short monk was ugly and his mouth curved into a slight smile that looked somewhat sinister.

"You're not Shen Changsheng," the monk said coldly.

"Hmm?" At this point, everyone's gaze turned to him.

Lin Fei also frowned. He had met Shen Changsheng several times. If this wasn't Shen Changsheng, then who was?

Seeing that everyone was staring at him, the monk sneered and said, "I have a very sensitive nose. I remember the scent of everyone's body very clearly. There isn't as much fragrance on Shen Changsheng as there is on you. And some of the fragrances are aphrodisiacs. Being able to carry aphrodisiacs with you and being in the Liu Mansion... hehe, Wind Chasing Fragrance, I didn't expect you to infiltrate the Liu Mansion in this way."

Upon hearing the monk's words, everyone's expression changed drastically, and they all raised their weapons to surround "Shen Changsheng".

"Shen Changsheng" looked around, but was not panicked. Instead, a strange smile appeared on the corner of his mouth. "Didn't expect my disguise to be discovered by your dog nose. Now that you've seen through my disguise, you can be the first to die!"

In an instant, "Shen Changsheng" drew his sword and attacked.

"Swish!"

The sword light flashed, and a hole appeared in the monk's throat.

"You..." The monk stared at "Shen Changsheng" in disbelief and died, seemingly unable to believe that he had died so easily.

"Thump!"

The monk's body fell to the ground.

At this point, "Shen Changsheng" had already taken off his face mask, revealing a slightly old face.

"It really is the Wind Chasing Fragrance!"

"Pity the monk was killed by him'."

"Didn't expect the Wind Chasing Fragrance's disguise to be so lifelike."

The martial artists present did not ask where the real Shen Changsheng had gone.

Since the Wind Chasing Fragrance could disguise himself as Shen Changsheng, there was no need to ask.

Shen Changsheng was probably dead.
