570. The Secret of the Ancient Continent!

To locate a True Monarch-level world on the brink of collapse is indeed a challenging endeavor for Lin Fei. However, finding a regular True Monarch-level world is quite straightforward.

At present, Lin Fei is aware of three such worlds, and this information is public knowledge. After all, True Monarch-level worlds differ from small worlds. True Monarchs do not rely heavily on world origins, and these worlds are incredibly stable. Even with numerous True Monarchs, it is impossible to destroy a True Monarch-level world.

Thus, a normal True Monarch-level world is not a target for destruction. Even if it is publicly known, entering a True Monarch-level world is primarily for regular cultivation and enlightenment.

Lin Fei knows of three True Monarch-level worlds with such characteristics. "Since I'm going, I'll choose the largest True Monarch-level world," he thought. "I'll go to the Eternal Light Realm!"

Lin Fei had already made his decision. With a swift motion, he shattered the void, entering a spatial passage. In the blink of an eye, he vanished without a trace, heading towards the Eternal Light Realm.

The Eternal Light Realm, as a True Monarch-level world of unparalleled stability, attracts many True Monarchs and Martial Gods. What's crucial is that the native life within the Eternal Light Realm can even enter the Divine Realm, indicating the existence of a passage connecting the two realms. Moreover, the cultivation methods in the Eternal Light Realm are quite similar to those in the Divine Realm, which enables this connection between the two realms.

Presently, the Eternal Light Realm is not so much a True Monarch-level world as a unique part of the Divine Realm, much like the Ancient Continent. They can freely traverse between each other.

At this moment, Lin Fei found himself in the Eternal Light Realm, strolling through the bustling Eternal Light City. This city is the most prosperous in the Eternal Light Realm.

Among the native inhabitants of the Eternal Light Realm, True Monarchs had long been born. Some of them have even left the Eternal Light Realm to enter the Divine Realm. Among them, the most formidable True Monarch is none other than the illustrious Eternal Light True Monarch.

The name itself, "Eternal Light," speaks volumes about the might of this True Monarch. It's worth noting that the Eternal Light Realm has not produced just one True Monarch; it is indeed an incredibly powerful True Monarch-level world.

Lin Fei meticulously observed everything within the Eternal Light Realm. This included the emptiness, which, while not as stable as the Divine Realm, remained remarkably robust.

Under such conditions, the prospect of its collapse was exceedingly challenging. Furthermore, there was the presence of world origins, vast and intricately interwoven. Each strand of law upheld order meticulously.

Should Lin Fei attempt to forcefully disrupt the Eternal Light Realm, not only would the True Monarchs of the realm refuse, but the realm itself possessed the power to retaliate. Recall the tribulations in the Divine Realm; they were the manifestation of the Divine Realm's power.

Similarly, in a genuinely secure True Monarch-level world, should anyone attempt to disrupt it, they would face the full might of the entire realm. Even Lin Fei himself would hesitate to claim the ability to withstand the onslaught of an entire True Monarch-level world.

However, how could he achieve enlightenment? Lin Fei had roamed the Eternal Light Realm for a considerable time but had gleaned nothing substantial. He yearned to comprehend the intricacies of a True Monarch-level world, which would enable him to potentially create one within his bodily particles.

Yet, so far, he remained empty-handed. Everything he sought, from world origins to laws and life, existed in abundance. But which was the key?

Lin Fei had attempted to create worlds in the Divine Dream Realm, but regardless of his approach, they all remained small worlds. Should he produce an abundance of world origins arbitrarily, it would defy logic and lead to the world's collapse, leaving no trace within the Divine Dream Realm.

Therefore, the creation had to adhere to logical principles. Unfortunately, the creation of a True Monarch-level world seemed beyond Lin Fei's reach.

"In the end, it's futile. Regardless of how I contemplate, my current cultivation level renders it all useless," Lin Fei mused. "I shall return to the Divine Dream Realm and patiently await the spontaneous emergence of life within those small worlds. Once life arises, I can attempt to enhance their strength and see if they can evolve into True Monarch-level worlds."

After careful consideration, Lin Fei concluded that this was his last resort. Subsequently, he departed from the Eternal Light Realm, opting not to engage in any disruptive activities. It was not out of fear of the Eternal Light True Monarch but rather due to a sense of futility.

Lin Fei returned to the Divine Dream Realm. He observed the tireless efforts of the True Monarchs Bai Cheng and Qian Xin as they continued to perfect the Divine Dream Realm.

Lin Fei then inspected the worlds he had created. Despite several days passing on the outside, nearly a century or two had elapsed within the four layers of the Divine Dream Realm. Yet, life had not yet emerged. At this rate, it was unclear how long it would take for life to manifest. Continuing to create small worlds seemed futile.

At this point in time, Lin Fei's power had reached its zenith. His cultivation had even hit a bottleneck, and further progress was seemingly impossible. Even within the Divine Dream Realm, all Lin Fei could do was wait patiently.

"Waiting in the Divine Dream Realm serves no purpose," Lin Fei concluded. "Since I've hit a bottleneck and all that's left is to wait, I might as well return to Ancient Continent."

Lin Fei remembered the words of his Master, Great Dream Ancestor. Ancient Continent had once been a place of opportunity for achieving the titles of Martial Ancestor and True Immortal. Considering his current predicament, perhaps returning to the Ancient Continent could provide a new opportunity.

With this in mind, Lin Fei promptly sought an audience with the Great Dream Ancestor.

"Master," Lin Fei greeted the Great Dream Ancestor with the utmost respect.

"Lin Fei, what brings you here?" inquired the Great Dream Ancestor.

"Master, your disciple has encountered a bottleneck in comprehending the Dao," Lin Fei explained. "There seems to be no possibility of any immediate breakthrough. Therefore, your disciple plans to request permission from the Ancient Covenant to return to the Ancient Continent."

"You wish to return to the Ancient Continent?" Great Dream Ancestor responded with slight surprise. Yet, upon further reflection, it made sense. Lin Fei's rapid progress in cultivation, combined with his successful navigation of nine Thunder Tribulations, naturally led to a plateau in his Dao comprehension. Returning to the Ancient Continent at this juncture seemed quite fitting.

"Have you considered this carefully?" Great Dream Ancestor cautioned, "Once you return this time, the next opportunity to go back to the Ancient Continent will only come when you reach the rank of Martial Ancestor."

Lin Fei shook his head, indicating that not returning to the Divine Realm was not an option for him. Abandoning the Divine Realm would only grant him a lifespan of one million years, and after that, he would become nothing but a handful of dust. Lin Fei had no intention of turning into mere dust.

"Master, I have made up my mind. Please grant your approval," Lin Fei respectfully requested.

Seeing the determined look in Lin Fei's eyes, the Great Dream Ancestor nodded and said, "Very well, if you wish to go back, you shall. Apply to the Ancient Covenant, and I will make arrangements to allow you to return to the Ancient Continent.

However, since you intend to return to the Ancient Continent, there are some secrets you ought to know. In reality, these aren't much of a secret. True Immortals and Martial Ancestors of the Ancient Covenant are aware of them.

Ancient Continent holds some mysteries. As a True Immortal-level world, it's far from being simple. When you return to the Ancient Continent, try to unify it, at least on the surface, with you as the leader.

Then, hold a celestial ceremony, requesting the Ancient Continent to grant you an opportunity to comprehend the Dao. If you do this, it should send shockwaves throughout the Ancient Continent, and they will grant you a chance to comprehend the ancient Dao. Whether you can glean anything from it depends on your own fortunes."

Upon hearing the Great Dream Ancestor's words, Lin Fei widened his eyes in astonishment. He had never heard of such a thing during his previous time on the Ancient Continent. To think that he could pray to the Ancient Continent for an opportunity to comprehend its Dao!

"Master, this... is truly unbelievable," Lin Fei exclaimed, deeply moved by the revelation.
