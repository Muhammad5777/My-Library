140. Number One Saber Under Heaven

At the foot of Sword Tomb Peak, many martial arts masters of the Hidden Sword Sect gathered together.

Their eyes widened, staring intently at the scene above the Sword Tomb. The two hundred and fifty meters saber energy was vast and overwhelming as if it could destroy everything. Everyone knew it was the saber energy of the Immortal Slayer Saber!

At the Martial Alliance Exchange, Lu Zongyi had personally witnessed Lin Fei unleashing more than thirty meters of saber energy. With one strike, he defeated the top-tier Qi Condensation expert Yuan Gangzi.

And now?

Two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy!

Seeing this scene, Lu Zongyi fell silent. How much time had passed? From the Martial Alliance Exchange to now, it had only been a mere two months. And in just two months, Lin Fei was already able to slash out two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy.

On the other hand, there was the pride of the Hidden Sword Sect, the "Number One Sword Under Heaven" of this generation in the Hidden Sword Sect, Liu Yibai!

They all knew Liu Yibai's situation. His Innate True Qi had long reached perfection, and he had advanced to the Grand Master realm. His sword intent had also transcended long ago but always fell just a bit short. He had concealed his sword for thirty years, all in search of an opportunity to break through.

Therefore, when Liu Yibai drew his sword, it would surely be earth-shattering and the strongest sword in his life. Almost one hundred meters of sword light lived up to everyone's expectations. It made the martial arts masters of the Hidden Sword Sect feel their hearts tremble. The sword light was simply astonishing.

However, who was stronger and who was weaker? Was it the Number One Sword Under Heaven? Or the Immortal Slayer Saber?

No one knew. Even Lin Fei and Liu Yibai, who were confronting each other on the Sword Tomb, didn't know.

"Crack."

The two terrifying forces of saber energy and sword light continuously collided and intertwined. The aftermath spread out in all directions like ripples on the water. Wherever it passed, the Sword Tomb was leveled.

Finally, the clouds dispersed, and the wind was gentle. Both the saber energy and the sword light had disappeared.

Lin Fei stood quietly on the Sword Tomb. All around were broken sword fragments. And opposite Lin Fei, Liu Yibai, wearing a white robe, spotlessly clean. He also stood quietly, neither of them spoke.

At this time, seeing that there was no movement on the Sword Tomb, the dozen or so Hidden Sword Sect martial arts masters at the foot of the mountain also hurried to the Sword Tomb.

"Grand Elder Liu, you..."

A martial arts master wanted to step forward and support Liu Yibai, but Liu Yibai slightly shook his head.

The crowd was puzzled. Both of them seemed to be fine. They didn't know who had the upper hand in the end.

Liu Yibai lowered his head. Everyone followed Liu Yibai's gaze. A corner of Liu Yibai's robe, with a "puff" sound, was cut off and fell to the ground.

Everyone was astonished but Liu Yibai laughed.

"The 'Immortal Slayer Saber' lives up to its name. I admit defeat!"

Liu Yibai's words shocked everyone. All the martial arts masters widened their eyes as if they couldn't believe Liu Yibai's words.

How could Liu Yibai lose? How could the Number One Sword Under Heaven lose? How could a Grand Master lose?

Lin Fei's tone was very calm, responding indifferently, "The 'Number One Sword Under Heaven' is indeed well-deserved. Grand Master is also well-deserved. Actually, this time, it's not really about who wins and who loses.

If it wasn't for your sword intent just breaking through and fighting hastily, I wouldn't have been able to cut your robe's corner. You are the first martial artist to ever block my saber energy!"

Liu Yibai shook his head, "I just took advantage of the location. Without the hundreds of sword intents in the Sword Tomb to assist me, I'm afraid I wouldn't be able to withstand your saber.

However, no matter what, I must thank you. Concealing my sword for thirty years, I finally found the opportunity today. I drew my sword and unleashed thirty years of sharpness, also cutting away thirty years of depression. Now, my achievements are perfection, and my sword intent has reached perfection as well. I owe you a favor, Brother Lin!"

Liu Yibai didn't seem to care too much about winning or losing anymore. His initial shout of "Sword, come forth!" was not just a casual one. After his sword intent reached perfection, the power of the hundreds of sword intents in the Sword Tomb reinforced his sword. Only then could he compete with Lin Fei's two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy.

Otherwise, in other places, without so many sword intents, Liu Yibai would probably not be able to withstand Lin Fei's saber.

This saber was truly earth-shattering. Even now, as Liu Yibai recalled it, he still felt palpitations. Even though his sword intent had reached perfection and he was a true Grand Master, he had no confidence that he could block Lin Fei's saber again.

"Lin Fei, I am the Number One Sword Under Heaven. And you, you shall be Number One Saber Under Heaven!"

Today, the surrounding Hidden Sword Sect martial arts masters had already been shocked countless times.

Number One Saber Under Heaven!

If these words were spoken by someone else, it would probably be taken as a joke. But these words were spoken by Liu Yibai. From the Number One Sword Under Heaven, from the mouth of a martial arts Grand Master. It was different.

Moreover, just now Lin Fei's saber had unleashed two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy. Just with this one saber! Lin Fei's title of "Number One Saber Under Heaven" was truly well-deserved!

"Back then, the Nine Profound Sect's founder was unparalleled in fist, saber, and sword. Whether it was the Number One Sword Under Heaven or the Number One Saber Under Heaven, they were all from the Nine Profound Sect's founder."

"Unexpectedly, now the Nine Profound Sect has given birth to another 'Number One Saber Under Heaven'."

"And, 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei hasn't even become a martial arts Grand Master yet. He can still continue to improve, and his future achievements are unimaginable."

"Could this be another figure like the Nine Profound Sect's founder?"

The many martial arts masters of the Hidden Sword Sect were extremely shocked in their hearts. They looked at Lin Fei with complicated gazes.

As martial artists, especially the martial arts masters of Hidden Sword Sect, they actually didn't want any martial artist to surpass Hidden Sword Sect. Hidden Sword Sect had been glorious for thousands of years. Except when Great Chang's founding emperor swept the world and established Great Chang.

At that time, Hidden Sword Sect had no Martial Saint. It was suppressed by Great Chang's founding emperor. Being suppressed by a Martial Saint was somewhat understandable.

However, there was another era. That was the era of the Nine Profound Sect's founder. The Nine Profound Sect's founder, as a Grand Master, suppressed the entire world's martial arts! Hidden Sword Sect was also unable to hold its head high. At that time, Hidden Sword Sect also had a Grand Master.

But what about it?

No matter how many Grand Masters, the Nine Profound Sect's founder only needed one sword, one saber, and one punch. He didn't even need to use a second move.

Such a peerless figure suppressed Hidden Sword Sect for an entire generation, making the millennium of the glory of the Hidden Sword Sect seem dim and dull.

Now, the Hidden Sword Sect has finally gradually recovered its vitality and a Grand Master had been born. Moreover, his sword intent had reached perfection. He was a Grand Master with perfected sword intent.

But now, "Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei appeared. Just as astonishingly talented as the Nine Profound Sect's founder but even younger than the Nine Profound Sect's founder.

Lin Fei was now only at the late stage of the Master level, but he could slightly surpass the perfected sword intent of Liu Yibai. In the future, when Lin Fei's saber intent reached perfection and he achieved the level of Grand Master, wouldn't he be able to suppress the world horizontally?

Hidden Sword Sect was very likely to be suppressed by Lin Fei for an entire generation again! For a moment, the martial arts masters of the Hidden Sword Sect felt bitter in their hearts. Everyone looked at Lin Fei with complicated expressions. The atmosphere in the entire Sword Tomb seemed to quiet down all at once.

"Lin Fei, do you want to build your foundation?"

At this moment, Liu Yibai suddenly spoke and his gaze fixed on Lin Fei.
