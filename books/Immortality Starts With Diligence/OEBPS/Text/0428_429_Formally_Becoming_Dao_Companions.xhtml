429. Formally Becoming Dao Companions

In the void.

Lin Fei, accompanied by many from the Ancient Origin Sect, descended slowly.

Feng Ling had already spotted Lin Fei. She immediately stepped forward and stood right in front of him.

Lin Fei reached out and took Feng Ling's hand. The head of the Ancient Origin Sect respectfully presented a gift list.

Feng Jiushang accepted the list.

Seeing the gifts on the list, Feng Jiushang couldn't help but gasp in astonishment.

She appeared quite excited.

She said in a deep voice, "The Ancient Origin Sect is truly thoughtful!"

"Hahaha, this is also a token of appreciation from our Ancient Origin Sect."

The items on this list might not be the most precious for the Feng family, but they were indeed the most needed treasures.

Clearly, the Ancient Origin Sect had put a lot of thought into it.

Lin Fei, still holding Feng Ling's hand, smiled slightly and said, "Feng Ling, I also have a gift prepared for you."

"A gift for me?"

Feng Ling was momentarily taken aback.

Both she and Lin Fei were Martial Kings who were unconcerned with trivialities. She hadn't thought about receiving a gift. To her surprise, Lin Fei indeed had a gift for her.

For a moment, Feng Ling was filled with anticipation. She was well aware, that when Lin Fei gives a gift, it's bound to be extraordinary.

The members of the Feng family and those who had come to congratulate and watch were all looking at Lin Fei. They wanted to see what kind of gift would the renowned Ancient Origin Saber King present?

Then, Lin Fei stretched out his hand.

"Buzz."

The next moment, a small seal appeared in Lin Fei's palm.

"This seal is called the Heavenly Pillar Seal! It's crafted from the Heavenly Pillar Mountain. It can be activated with the Phoenix bloodline. It can be as big as a mountain stretching hundreds of miles or as small as a baby's fist," Lin Fei explained to the Feng Ling.

"Boom."

Everyone was stunned.

The Heavenly Pillar Mountain! The legendary ancient mountain that once connected heaven and earth?

Almost everyone had heard of this ancient mountain. But most just knew that the Heavenly Pillar Mountain had collapsed.

As for the specifics, few were aware who would have thought that Lin Fei had excavated this legendary mountain, and even turned it into a bloodline magic treasure?

"Can I activate it?"

Feng Ling was curious. Even though she was a top-tier Martial King, she hadn't used a magic treasure before. After all, bloodline magic treasures are very rare. And even if there were any, they wouldn't be much of a help to Feng Ling's strength. Thus, she never needed a bloodline magic treasure.

So, now, Feng Ling was quite intrigued. She reached out and tried to grasp it.

"Hmm? It's so heavy? How can I activate such a heavy Heavenly Pillar Seal?"

Feng Ling widened her eyes in surprise. The Heavenly Pillar Seal was extremely heavy. With her attempt, she couldn't even lift it.

"Lin Fei, this magic treasure is too powerful, You should keep it for yourself. With the Heavenly Pillar Seal, you can travel freely through the Ancient Continent. At the levels of Nascent Soul and Martial King, you would have no rivals!"

The Feng Ling had sensed the immense power of the Heavenly Pillar Seal. Therefore, her expression became very solemn. She refused the magic treasure.

Lin Fei smiled gently and said, "You need the Heavenly Pillar Seal more than I do. Besides, even without the Heavenly Pillar Seal, I can travel freely through the Ancient Continent. Who would be my match? If you don't believe me, you can use the Heavenly Pillar Seal and test me."

Lin Fei's words were brimming with confidence. It wasn't that he was boasting. The Heavenly Pillar Seal was indeed mighty. It had assisted Lin Fei in swiftly slaying the three immortals.

However, Lin Fei was confident that even without the Heavenly Pillar Seal, he could still defeat the three immortals! Especially if his Endless Vitality Green Wood Bloodline reaches perfection.

By that time, his Five Elements bloodline would be complete. Once the Five Elements bloodline fuses, it would give birth to new power.

Lin Fei would then possess the power of the Four Symbols and the Five Elements. At the levels of Nascent Soul and Martial King, who would be his rival?

On the other hand, he was somewhat concerned about the Feng Ling. After all, Feng Ling's strength wasn't considered very strong. But with the Heavenly Pillar Seal, it would be a different story.

With the Heavenly Pillar Seal, Feng Ling's power would not be inferior to the three immortals. Perhaps even stronger than them!

"Alright, I'll accept the Heavenly Pillar Seal."

Feng Ling nodded and took the Heavenly Pillar Seal.

Seeing Feng Ling accept the Heavenly Pillar Seal, Feng Jiushang and the other members of the Feng family finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Such a magic treasure, if Feng Ling could possess it, it would be beneficial for the entire Feng family!

"Alright, let's begin. From today onwards, we are Dao companions. Together, we'll ascend to the pinnacle of Martial Dao!"

The Feng Ling also held Lin Fei's hand tightly. The two looked at each other and shared a smile. At this moment, words were unnecessary; everything was understood!
