130. Who in the world would dare to take on Lin Fei's saber?

Compared to the shock of others, Yuan Gangzi, who was locked by the thirty-meter saber energy, felt like a lost soul. He seemed to have already sensed the threat of death.

"I am at the 11th level of Qi Condensation. I have high hopes for building my foundation in the future. How can I be afraid of a mere martial artist? These saber energy must be superficially strong but weak inside. It's impossible to break through my spells. Also, my magical artifact, the Four Divine Umbrella, block it!"

Yuan Gangzi slapped his forehead. Suddenly, an umbrella-shaped magical artifact flew out of his body. The magical artifact instantly opened, enveloping Yuan Gangzi inside.

At the same time, Yuan Gangzi grabbed a large handful of talismans from his bosom. No matter what kind of talismans, his spiritual power crazily flowed, pouring into the talismans. He threw them directly at the terrifying thirty-meter Saber Qi in the void.

"Rumbling."

Countless talismans, dozens, even hundreds of them, instantly unleashed their spells. However, these spells were varied. The real defensive spells were few and far between. Under the immense saber energy, they were completely crushed and cut, instantly turned into dust.

At the same time, the thirty-meter saber energy finally fell, and the void seemed to emit a muffled sound.

Yuan Gangzi was originally standing on a crane. But now, he was pressed down by Lin Fei's thirty-meter Saber Qi. Both the person and the crane fell to the ground. The terrifying power acted on the crane's body.

"Splatter."

The crane let out a mournful cry. Then, the force of the saber energy slashed directly and crushed the crane into a pool of blood.

Yuan Gangzi's face was full of horror. The defensive spells he had cast, and even several layers of them, turned out to be as fragile as paper, easily cut open by the saber energy.

The terrifying Saber Qi even directly landed on his Four Divine Umbrella.

The Four Divine Umbrella was a half-finished magical treasure personally refined by a Foundation Establishment cultivator. All the materials used were suitable for magical treasure. It was just that the refining process failed in the end. So it didn't become a magical treasure.

It was considered a semi-finished product, an inferior one. But when used by Yuan Gangzi, who was at the 11th level of Qi Condensation, its defensive ability had reached a very terrifying peak. Yuan Gangzi's hope was all in these Four Divine Umbrella.

The thirty-meter saber energy was like a mountain pressing down on him. With unstoppable momentum, it fiercely slashed on the Four Divine Umbrella.

The sharpness of the saber energy and the terrifying saber intent it contained made Yuan Gangzi extremely nervous.

The protective barrier of the Four Divine Umbrella was brutally flattened by the saber energy.

"Block it, you must block it!"

Yuan Gangzi's eyes turned bloodshot, full of blood vessels. However, the Four Divine Umbrella ultimately couldn't withstand Lin Fei's thirty-meter saber energy.

"Crack."

The Four Divine Umbrella shattered. Its spiritual light dimmed, no longer having any effect. Yuan Gangzi could only watch helplessly as the terrifying saber energy effortlessly cut through his protective spiritual power like cutting through tofu.

"Bang."

Yuan Gangzi's figure was engulfed in the thirty-meter saber energy. In an instant, he was shredded into a mist of blood by the saber energy. The terrifying saber energy even had remaining power, landing on the ground, slashed a huge crack.

Silence!

Very quiet!

The Martial Alliance Exchange event, with hundreds or even thousands of people, was originally lively. The crowd was noisy.

But now, it seemed to be silent. All the martial artists just stared blankly at the huge crack in the ground.

Was that the crack created by Lin Fei's saber energy? Even, it incidentally killed a top-level Qi Condensation cultivator!

That was an 11th-level Qi Condensation cultivator, a core elder of Guardian Primordial Mountain!

And now? Dead!

Yuan Gangzi died just like that under Lin Fei's saber energy, in one move!

Yuan Gangzi couldn't even withstand a single move. Just like the two high-level Qi Condensation cultivators from He Mountain Dao in the rumors, they were killed by Lin Fei with a single slash.

Yuan Gangzi was a top-level Qi Condensation cultivator. It seemed that he wasn't much stronger than the Qi Condensation cultivators of He Mountain Dao. The outcome was the same.

"Immortal Slayer Saber, this is the real Immortal Slayer Saber!"

"Another Qi Condensation cultivator has died. This time, it's a top-level one!"

"How many Qi cultivators have died at the hands of the Immortal Slayer Saber?"

"Sure enough, there are only wrong names in the martial world, but no wrong titles. The 'Immortal Slayer Saber' truly lives up to its name, with the saber out, it slays immortals!"

"Is this 'Immortal Slayer Saber' really martial arts? The power of this saber is probably not inferior to that of a grandmaster, right?"

"Even in some aspects, a grandmaster might not be able to slash out such a terrifying saber!"

Some martial artists couldn't help but think of martial arts grandmasters. It seemed that the grandmasters in the rumors were nothing more than this!

Of course, martial arts grandmasters also varied in strength. The difference was very significant. For example, the grandmaster like the founder of the Nine Profound Sect, who was invincible below Foundation Establishment! Even if it was a Qi Condensation cultivator at the peak of perfection, when facing the Nine Profound Sect founder, it would only take a single slash.

Otherwise, how could they be called invincible?

The title of "invincible" isn't so easy to obtain. It's based on real achievements. Dominating the world, invincible in all directions!

At this moment, Lin Fei seemed no different from being invincible in the eyes of some martial artists. They had just witnessed Lin Fei unleash a thirty-meter-tall saber energy.

How terrifying was that?

They didn't know what kind of person or power could withstand such a thirty-meter saber energy.

"This saber technique..."

Lu Zongyi's gaze was complicated as he looked at Lin Fei, unable to speak for a long time. Such a saber technique was beyond his ability to describe. He even felt that his decades of practicing the sword paled in comparison to Lin Fei's single saber strike. Even if he practiced his sword for another hundred years, it would be no match for Lin Fei's saber.

Lin Fei stood with his hands behind his back, looking at Lu Zongyi, and calmly asked, "Brother Lu, do you still wish to continue the exchange?"

Upon hearing this, Lu Zongyi couldn't help but shake his head, sighing, "Master Lin's saber technique is divine! I admit my inferiority, consider this exchange a joke on my part."

Lu Zongyi admitted defeat, wholeheartedly accepting it. Clearly, he had been thoroughly convinced by Lin Fei's saber strike just now.

At this point, the Qi Condensation cultivators Guardian Primordial Mountain finally came to their senses. How could they continue to blockade the Martial Alliance? They quickly collected their flags, jumped onto the back of their immortal cranes, and fled in panic.

In the blink of an eye, they vanished without a trace. Gone was their arrogance from before. This allowed many martial artists to witness the weak side of the Qi cultivators for the first time. Immortal Sect's Qi Condensation cultivators were nothing more than that!

"Master Lin, let's end this Martial Alliance Exchange Conference here. After this incident, it would be best for Master Lin to leave Cang City Mountain quickly. Guardian Primordial Mountain has not only top Qi Condensation cultivators but also powerful cultivators close to Foundation Establishment.

Moreover, the Martial Alliance isn't far from Guardian Primordial Mountain. If the Guardian Primordial Mountain sect leader becomes furious and brings the mountain-protecting treasure, it'll be troublesome.

Guardian Primordial Mountain's mountain-protecting treasure is a genuine magical treasure! If set up in a grand formation, even an ordinary Foundation Establishment cultivator can't break through."

Some masters of the Martial Alliance's thirteen factions urged Lin Fei to leave quickly.

"A mountain-protecting treasure that even Foundation Establishment cultivators can't break?"

A hint of curiosity flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. These immortal sects had a heritage spanning over a thousand years. Even the Great Chang's founding emperor, the mighty Martial Saint, could only suppress but not completely annihilate these immortal sects. It seemed that it was because of these sects' mountain-protecting treasures.

"Well, this Martial Alliance Exchange Conference has come to an end, and it's time to leave."

Lin Fei nodded in agreement. He had killed Yuan Gangzi simply because Yuan Gangzi had provoked him. Unless it was absolutely necessary, no one would choose to confront an immortal sect with a thousand-year heritage.

Lin Fei was no exception. As long as he returned to the Nine Profound Sect, Guardian Primordial Mountain would have no way to deal with him.

"Brother Lin, once everything settles down, please do visit our Hidden Sword Sect. Our Hidden Sword Sect has produced a Martial Saint. As for sword intent perfectionists, there have been several throughout history. We won't disappoint you."

Lu Zongyi enthusiastically invited Lin Fei to be a guest at the Hidden Sword Sect. Lin Fei's eyes lit up upon hearing this.

Sword intent perfection! Martial Saints! Both had appeared in the Hidden Sword Sect's history. In terms of foundation, it was much stronger than the Nine Profound Sect.

"If I have the time, I will definitely visit the Hidden Sword Sect!"

After saying this, Lin Fei led the Nine Profound Sect disciples and quickly left the Martial Alliance.

Thus, the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference came to a hasty end. Many martial artists left Cang City Mountain one after another.

However, even though there were some unexpected incidents during the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference, making it an incomplete event, the martial artists who attended this exchange felt that their trip was worthwhile!

Not only did they witness sword intent and saber intent, but they also saw the unparalleled prowess of the "Immortal Slayer Saber." Lin Fei slayed immortals once again. Moreover, with a single saber strike, a thirty-meter saber energy! Who in the world would dare to take on Lin Fei's saber?
