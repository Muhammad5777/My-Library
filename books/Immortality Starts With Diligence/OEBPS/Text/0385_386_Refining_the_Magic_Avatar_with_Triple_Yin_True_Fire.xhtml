386. Refining the Magic Avatar with Triple Yin True Fire

"Boom!"

The protective formation of the mountain sprang into action, enveloping both Lin Fei and Dark Hades King. However, instead of suppressing the two, it solidified its protection around the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord.

Was this a defensive stance? Indeed, the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord had no intention of defeating Lin Fei and the Dark Hades King. His strength was roughly on par with Dark Hades King's, and now with Lin Fei as backup, how could he stand a chance? Hence, his only option was to defend.

Fortunately, while he couldn't defeat Dark Hades King alone, neither could Dark Hades King best the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord on his own. They were evenly matched. But now, with Lin Fei in the mix, as long as the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord focused on defense and had the sect's protective formation on his side, he could likely hold out for three days. And lasting three days was all he needed.

"True Form!" Dark Hades King promptly displayed his Martial King's True Form, his two bloodlines erupting simultaneously, exuding a chilling aura.

"Nascent Soul Magic Avatar!"

Similarly, the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord also activated his Magic Avatar. But his Magic Avatar was unique. He possessed two of them! Yes, two Magic Avatars.

"Ancient Origin Saber King, the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord, having cultivated for many years, bolstered by the accumulated resources of the Purple Sun Immortal Sect, managed to cultivate two Magic Avatars. Before this, even with just his two Magic Avatars, he was able to suppress me. Now with the added power of the mountain's protective formation, these two Magic Avatars might become even stronger," Dark Hades King explained to Lin Fei.

"Two Magic Avatars? With the foundation for two Magic Avatars, why not just cultivate one and elevate its power to a higher level? Wouldn't a single, more formidable Magic Avatar be better?" Lin Fei inquired.

At their level, quantity didn't really mean much. If the resources and effort used to cultivate two Magic Avatars were focused on just one, it would undoubtedly be stronger.

Dark Hades King elucidated, "Ancient Origin Saber King, you might not be aware. When a Nascent Soul powerhouse cultivates a Magic Avatar, there's a limit to how much it can amplify its strength. The materials used in the process, along with the type of Magic Avatar, play crucial roles. The more potent a Magic Avatar is, the rarer and more valuable the materials required become, similar to forging a magic treasure. Once a specific Magic Avatar type is chosen, its potential is capped, making it difficult to elevate any further.

The two Magic Avatars cultivated by the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord are heritages from the Purple Sun Immortal Sect. Their strength, once maxed out, remains as such, unable to progress any further. Hence, he had to cultivate a second Magic Avatar. Acquiring formidable techniques to enhance the Nascent Soul Magic Avatar isn't an easy task."

Lin Fei understood. These two Magic Avatars were innately limited to the level equivalent to the two Martial King bloodlines. Any further enhancement was impossible unless one found a more potent Magic Avatar technique and obtained special materials. Otherwise, cultivating a stronger Magic Avatar was out of the question.

"In that case, let's quickly destroy his Nascent Soul Magic Avatar," Lin Fei took a deep breath, not hesitating any longer. "Supreme Saber Dao!"

"Boom!"

Immediately, Lin Fei's Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline erupted, transforming his True Martial King Form into a massive figure resembling a saber, spanning across the void.

"Heavenly Saber, Strike!" Lin Fei commanded, unleashing a devastating saber strike, its might reaching an unparalleled pinnacle.

"Swoosh."

The saber directly slashed upon the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord's Magic Avatar. Instantly, a vast gash appeared, splitting the Magic Avatar, which soon exhibited cracks all over. However, this was the Nascent Soul Magic Avatar. In the next moment, with a shimmering radiance, the Magic Avatar jolted and restored itself to its original state.

"Hmm?" A glint appeared in Lin Fei's eyes. This Magic Avatar wasn't particularly strong, fast, or defensively robust. It barely reached the Martial King's level. However, its regenerative capabilities were notable.

"Regenerative power..." Lin Fei muttered. That was the least of his concerns.

"Triple Yin True Fire!" Without a second thought, Lin Fei activated his Triple Yin True Fire Bloodline.

"Boom!"

Flames, raging and intense, enveloped Lin Fei's True Martial King Form. While these flames blazed furiously, the surrounding air lacked any scorching heat. Instead, it felt chillingly cold, giving a sensation of a cold breeze. Simultaneously, the saber held by Lin Fei's True Martial King Form was also quickly covered in flames, as if it transformed into a blazing saber.

"Whizz."

Lin Fei struck again with his saber, which not only radiated a sharp saber light but also was accompanied by terrifying flames. And these weren't just ordinary flames; they were Triple Yin True Fire. Once touched, they were nearly impossible to extinguish.

"Swoosh."

Lin Fei's saber once again made contact with the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord's Magic Avatar. Cracks appeared again on the Magic Avatar. Then, with a flash of spiritual light, it tried to recover.

However, this time, the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord's Magic Avatar did not manage to recover as hoped. This was because Lin Fei's saber was imbued with the Triple Yin True Fire. Once this fire ignited, it didn't feel hot. Instead, it felt chillingly cool.

Moreover, there wasn't much visible flame on the surface. That's because the Triple Yin True Fire burned directly within, incinerating everything from the particle level. Even an immortal body would inevitably perish under its influence. This was the terrifying nature of the Triple Yin True Fire. It wasn't ostentatious, it could be described as "insidiously potent." One might be affected before they even realize it, and by the time they do, it would be too late.

"What kind of fire is this?" The Extreme Purple Daoist Lord detected Lin Fei's Triple Yin True Fire almost instantly, and his complexion changed drastically. The fire continuously scorched from within the Magic Avatar, making it progressively weaker. If this continued, his Magic Avatar would undoubtedly be reduced to ashes by the Triple Yin True Fire.

"Water."

Fire fears water. The Extreme Purple Daoist Lord cast a spell, and heavy rain began to fall. The downpour concentrated solely on the Magic Avatar. He hoped to use the water to extinguish Lin Fei's Triple Yin True Fire. But how could ordinary water put out such a fire?

"Swish swish swish."

Furthermore, Lin Fei didn't stand idle. He struck continuously with his saber, each strike bringing the Triple Yin True Fire. The Extreme Purple Daoist Lord's Magic Avatar was riddled with holes, appearing as if the entire Nascent Soul Magic Avatar was aflame.

"Explode!"

The Extreme Purple Daoist Lord's face turned even paler, and he detonated his Nascent Soul Magic Avatar in an instant.

"Boom."

The Magic Avatar exploded, filling the sky with the Triple Yin True Fire. It was as if the entire sky had turned into a blazing inferno. Besides the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord, another Magic Avatar materialized, a product of the remnants after self-detonation. However, this new Magic Avatar's aura was at least 90% weaker than the previous one. Even if it hadn't been completely destroyed, it had suffered significant damage.

"Retreat."

The Extreme Purple Daoist Lord retracted this Magic Avatar, not daring to let it engage Lin Fei any further. Otherwise, his Magic Avatar would truly be obliterated.

"Leave."

Lin Fei paid no attention to the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord, knowing that there was still another Magic Avatar. With a wave of his hand, a massive amount of Triple Yin True Fire surged towards the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord's remaining Magic Avatar. The Lord's expression turned extremely grim. He couldn't find a way to counter the Triple Yin True Fire.

"Return."

Thus, the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord retracted his second Nascent Soul Magic Avatar.

"We don't need to continue. You have won," the Extreme Purple Daoist Lord said, his face displaying evident displeasure. Still, he conceded defeat.
