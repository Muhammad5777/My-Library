694. The Might of the Illusory Realm! Intimidating the Chaos Behemoth Kings!

Lin Fei's Illusory Realm targeted solely the mind and will. Regardless of how physically powerful an entity might be, if its spirit wasn't strong enough, it would be subdued by the Illusory Realm.

Normally, a true Chaos Behemoth King would possess a formidable will that Lin Fei's Illusory Realm couldn't likely intimidate. However, the state of these two Chaos Behemoth Kings seemed off.

When Lin Fei deployed the Illusory Realm, it immediately enveloped the two Chaos Behemoth Kings. Soon after, a look of confusion appeared in their eyes, and then, startlingly, they ceased moving. This development took Doomsday Supreme by surprise.

"Supreme Lin, is this..." Doomsday Supreme could hardly believe his eyes. Were the two Chaos Behemoth Kings truly immobilized, seemingly stunned? Moreover, their previously terrifying aura seemed significantly diminished.

"Their minds seem to be subdued by me," Lin Fei speculated, though he was not entirely sure. After all, his opponents were two Chaos Behemoth Kings, comparable to Masters of Chaos. He had only intended to test a theory; how could he have anticipated actually immobilizing them? It was almost inconceivable.

With a swift movement, Doomsday Supreme acted. He didn't hesitate for a moment and charged towards the two Chaos Behemoth Kings, closing the distance quickly.

"Thud."

Doomsday Supreme struck the two immobile Chaos Behemoth Kings with a punch that echoed with a dull thud. It was a full-powered blow from an Invincible Supreme, a strike whose formidable power could only be withstood by another Invincible Supreme or a Master of Chaos.

Yet, this mighty punch from Doomsday Supreme, though it treated the two Chaos Behemoth Kings as live targets, did not significantly affect them. They didn't even seem to shake.

This resistance demonstrated their immense power; even without counterattacking, standing still and enduring the blows, the Chaos Behemoth Kings seemed almost impervious to damage from Doomsday Supreme.

Although Doomsday Supreme's punch directly proved that Lin Fei's Illusory Realm had indeed immobilized the two Chaos Behemoth Kings, Lin Fei himself was not so quick to celebrate.

While the punch had made contact without any reaction from the immobilized kings, demonstrating the effectiveness of the Illusory Realm, Lin Fei approached with a more somber attitude.

"Haha, they really are immobilized!" Doomsday Supreme laughed heartily, amazed at the unexpected vulnerability of the Chaos Behemoth Kings' minds. "To think that their mental defenses were so weak!"

Lin Fei, however, did not share in the jubilation. He flew forward and positioned himself in front of the two stationary Chaos Behemoth Kings, who showed no response to his presence and remained utterly motionless. Maintaining the Illusory Realm's suppressive force, Lin Fei decided to escalate his efforts.

"Kill!"

Lin Fei exclaimed, unleashing the full force of his Eternal Realm.

"Boom."

The power of the Eternal Realm exploded forth, treating the Chaos Behemoth Kings like living targets. Given their immobilized state, they could do nothing to defend themselves.

Lin Fei's previous training had enhanced the Eternal Realm to 51%, and after devouring a top-tier Chaos Behemoth, it had risen to 57%. His strength had significantly increased, making this attack potentially his strongest yet.

However, when Lin Fei's most powerful strike landed on the two Chaos Behemoth Kings, it caused only a minor ripple across their forms, quickly returning to calm. The Chaos Behemoth Kings remained unscathed, undisturbed by the assault.

Silence fell over Lin Fei as he confronted this reality. Even as an Invincible Supreme, his formidable power seemed trivial before the Chaos Behemoth Kings. His all-out attack had made no discernible impact, underscoring a stark truth: even flawed, these Chaos Behemoth Kings were beyond his capability to harm.

Lin Fei's frustration was evident in his furrowed brow. "Even if we can immobilize them, what good does it do? We still can't get past the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower..."

Despite trying every method at his disposal, Lin Fei found that none could inflict any real harm on the two Chaos Behemoth Kings. However, Doomsday Supreme's eyes gleamed with a spark of insight, hinting at an unrevealed plan.

"Who says there's no way?" he spoke up, a slight grin crossing his face. "Supreme Lin, I never imagined you could actually immobilize the two Chaos Behemoth Kings. That's beyond what I expected."

"My promise may no longer be useful," he continued. "We can only immobilize them, not destroy them, so I fear I cannot fulfill the promise of the Chaos Behemoth essence you seek. However, if you only want to get past the ninth layer, that might still be possible."

Lin Fei looked at him in surprise. "Doomsday Supreme, do you have a method?"

Doomsday Supreme smiled mysteriously. "To get past the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower, we don't actually need to harm the two Chaos Behemoth Kings. We just need to wait! If we can maintain our suppression and simply wait in the combat zone long enough, the Forbidden Tower will make an assessment. If the two Chaos Behemoth Kings cannot overcome us, it would count as us having passed the ninth layer. Then, we would naturally be able to pass through the entire Forbidden Tower and receive the reward of transforming our bodies into Bodies of Chaos!"

Lin Fei was momentarily stirred by this idea. Was this exploiting a loophole in the rules of the Forbidden Tower? It seemed plausible. If exploiting this loophole could get them past the ninth layer, it would indeed be worthwhile. While he might not gain the essence of the Chaos Behemoth Kings to nourish his Eternal Realm, the opportunity to transform his body into a Body of Chaos—even if Lin Fei was not entirely physical—promised significant benefits.

"Alright, let's try it," Lin Fei agreed.

Thus, he and Doomsday Supreme settled in to quietly wait, slowly passing the time to see if they could indeed get past the ninth layer of the Forbidden Tower by exploiting its mechanics.
