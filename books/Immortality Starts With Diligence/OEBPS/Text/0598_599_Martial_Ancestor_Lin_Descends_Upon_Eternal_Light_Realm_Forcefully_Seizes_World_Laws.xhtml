599. Martial Ancestor Lin Descends Upon Eternal Light Realm, Forcefully Seizes World Laws

Lin Fei returned to his residence and went directly into his secluded chamber.

"Chaos, realms, extra-dimensional beings..."

Today, Lin Fei learned many secrets from the Supremes. Or rather, he learned the "truth" of this world. The Divine Realm was merely one of many realms in the chaos. Even the Divine Realm could collapse. Once the Divine Realm was invaded or devoured by beings from other realms, the supposed immortality of True Immortals would become a joke.

Although the Supremes claimed they could roam the chaos, in reality, they did not want to lose the Divine Realm. After all, the Divine Realm was their foundation! However, Lin Fei also discerned some implications.

"True Immortals are fundamentally one with the Divine Realm. They thrive and fall together, inseparable. But if a True Immortal achieves Supreme status, it seems to be different. Supremes can travel through chaos and even completely leave the Divine Realm. Even if the Divine Realm is destroyed, the Supremes wouldn't suffer significant losses. So, it seems that once a True Immortal becomes a Supreme, they can detach from the Divine Realm."

A gleam of determination flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

Lin Fei didn't know the path to becoming a Supreme. He didn't even have a direction. The Supremes had not revealed it. But from their few words, Lin Fei could deduce some information about Supremes.

Of course, this was not important. What Lin Fei needed now was to increase his strength at any cost. Only by increasing his strength could he survive the upcoming war.

"To increase my strength, there are two methods. One is for the Eternal Realm to birth life, but that can only be waited for gradually. It is impossible in a short time. The second method is through the Saber World. If the Saber World can continue to expand, its power will also grow rapidly. To enhance the Saber World, aside from merging with other Saber Worlds, it needs to merge with the world laws of other small worlds. Previously, as a True Monarch, I couldn't extract the world laws of True Monarch-level worlds. But now that I am a Martial Ancestor, it is different..."

A glint of determination flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. He had thought of a way to increase his strength. However, this method was indeed somewhat insane.

Moreover, it could cause extensive damage or even destruction to many worlds. But the Supremes had made it clear. That was to increase strength at any cost. As long as it could enhance his power, he should do it.

"I can extract the world laws of small worlds and True Monarch-level worlds. True Monarch-level world laws would be the most effective in enhancing the power of the Saber World. Even a completely intact True Monarch-level world can be extracted. Now, which True Monarch-level world should I go to?"

Lin Fei recalled the information about True Monarch-level worlds. Soon, he had a target in mind. He thought of a True Monarch-level world, the Eternal Light Realm!

The Eternal Light Realm was the largest known True Monarch-level world in the Divine Realm. Moreover, it had birthed the Eternal Light True Monarch!

Hardly anyone dared to offend the Eternal Light True Monarch or make a move against the Eternal Light Realm. Besides, neither Martial Ancestors nor True Immortals could destroy the Eternal Light Realm.

Lin Fei had visited the Eternal Light Realm before but had done nothing there. This time, it would be different.

"Let's go."

Without any hesitation, Lin Fei stepped into the spatial passage. Then, he traversed the void, disappearing from sight.

...

The Eternal Light Realm remained as peaceful and harmonious as ever.

As the largest known True Monarch-level world in the Divine Realm, the Eternal Light Realm was very famous. Its renown spread throughout the entire Divine Realm.

Moreover, it produced the Eternal Light True Monarch. The Eternal Light True Monarch was a top-tier True Monarch. Apart from True Immortals and Martial Ancestor, who could confidently claim to defeat the Eternal Light True Monarch?

As a result, the Eternal Light Realm was highly prosperous. The Eternal Light True Monarch adopted an open attitude, not prohibiting people from the Divine Realm from entering the Eternal Light Realm.

"Buzz."

Suddenly, spatial ripples began to spread outside the Eternal Light Realm. Many people noticed these spatial ripples.

"Which True Monarch has arrived?"

"The Eternal Light Realm certainly attracts a lot of True Monarchs, constantly coming and going."

"Indeed, being the largest True Monarch-level world in the Divine Realm, it's normal to see many True Monarchs."

Seeing the spatial ripples, many were not surprised. Only a True Monarch could traverse space. Someone traversing space was undoubtedly a True Monarch-level or higher expert.

"Whoosh."

In the next moment, a figure stepped out from the spatial passage.

"Is that... Martial Ancestor Lin?"

"Yes, it's Martial Ancestor Lin!"

"Why has Martial Ancestor Lin come to the Eternal Light Realm?"

"Martial Ancestor Lin, a legend of our Divine Realm."

"I never expected Martial Ancestor Lin to come to the Eternal Light Realm. Is he planning to enter the Eternal Light Realm?"

"Martial Ancestor cannot enter a True Monarch-level world. If they do, the True Monarch-level world would explode."

Many people saw the figure emerging from the void and became excited.

They respectfully greeted, "Greetings, Martial Ancestor!"

Lin Fei was no longer an unknown figure. On the contrary, his reputation was illustrious. His fame had spread throughout the entire Divine Realm.

Now, Lin Fei was a revered Martial Ancestor. Many knew of him. Therefore, as soon as Lin Fei appeared, many people bowed respectfully.

Lin Fei did not speak. He simply gazed quietly at the Eternal Light Realm. The Eternal Light Realm before him was not unfamiliar. He had visited the Eternal Light Realm once before.

But the last time, Lin Fei didn't stay long. He hadn't sensed anything significant and left. This time, Lin Fei returned. The Eternal Light Realm was bustling with people. There were many inhabitants.

However, Lin Fei didn't mind. He took a deep breath.

"Boom."

Instantly, Lin Fei transformed into the Eternal Realm. The Eternal Realm expanded directly towards the Eternal Light Realm. He intended to "swallow" the Eternal Light Realm!

Lin Fei did not enter the Eternal Light Realm. Instead, he aimed to directly "swallow" it. For a moment, everyone was stunned!

"Martial Ancestor, spare us."

"Your Majesty, Martial Ancestor, what has the Eternal Light Realm done to offend you, to warrant your personal intervention?"

"Your Majesty Martial Ancestor, aren't you afraid of the wrath of other True Immortals and Martial Ancestor for attacking the Eternal Light Realm?"

Fear and panic spread among the people. Lin Fei was actually attempting to "swallow" the Eternal Light Realm.

At this moment, the Eternal Light True Monarch also noticed the vast expanse of the Eternal Realm covering the sky. He was aware of Lin Fei's arrival.

However, it was too late for him to react. Once Lin Fei unleashed the Eternal Realm, it enveloped the entire Eternal Light Realm almost instantly.

Looking up, the void was filled with the Eternal Realm.

"Boom."

The Eternal Light Realm shook violently. But it was of no use. Lin Fei's Eternal Realm represented the genuine power of a Martial Ancestor.

No matter how strong the Eternal Light Realm was, it couldn't escape the suppression of the Eternal Realm.

Addressing the panic of the Eternal Light Realm's inhabitants, Lin Fei spoke.

"This trip of mine is solely for the world laws of the Eternal Light Realm. All of you, remain calm and wait quietly in the Eternal Light Realm. Once I have extracted the world laws, I will leave!"

Lin Fei made his intentions clear.

"Martial Ancestor Lin wants to extract the world laws of the Eternal Light Realm?"

"What? Martial Ancestor Lin has gone to such lengths just to extract world laws?"

"But if he extracts the world laws, won't the Eternal Light Realm be severely damaged and destabilized?"

"There will definitely be an impact, but the Eternal Light Realm won't collapse."

"Is this really Martial Ancestor Lin? Acting so recklessly, doesn't he fear the True Immortals behind the Eternal Light True Monarch? Or the True Immortals and Martial Ancestor behind us many True Monarchs?"

Many were displeased with Lin Fei. They felt he was acting with impunity. He seemed to completely disregard the True Immortals and Martial Ancestor supporting them.

However, they were helpless at this moment. They could only watch as Lin Fei forcibly seized the world laws of the Eternal Light Realm.
