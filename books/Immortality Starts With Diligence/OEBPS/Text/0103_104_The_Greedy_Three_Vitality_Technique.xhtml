104. The "Greedy" Three Vitality Technique

For a while, Lin Fei stayed in the South King Mansion. Moreover, Lin Fei spread the news. He was willing to trade the quota for entering the secret realm. However, it must be exchanged with pills or treasures that can enhance a martial artist's inner power.

A chance to enter the royal secret realm, for many Qi cultivators, was very attractive. After all, they might be able to obtain the spiritual medicine for Foundation Establishment Pill.

The royal family allowed them to take the spiritual medicine away. In that case, with the spiritual medicine, the Qi cultivators could try to refine a batch of Foundation Establishment Pills.

For Qi cultivators, it would mean a few more chances to establish their foundation. After all, even in the Immortal Sects, under normal circumstances, they would only give a Foundation Establishment Pill to disciples who had the potential to establish their foundation.

Foundation Establishment Pill could be taken multiple times. In theory, the more Foundation Establishment Pills one took, the greater the chance of successful foundation establishment. Many Qi cultivators would spare no effort to obtain the spiritual medicine for Foundation Establishment Pill from the royal secret realm.

As a result, many Qi cultivators tried their best to collect some pills or treasures that can enhance inner power. However, for three consecutive days, the things brought by the Qi cultivators were not satisfactory to Lin Fei.

...

"Lin Fei, Daoist Yu Xiangzi is here."

Ling Yao brought a "familiar face" to Lin Fei. It was Yu Xiangzi from the Green Jade Gate. She was defeated by Lin Fei in the martial arts competition. Lin Fei showed mercy and didn't hurt her mentally.

"Nice to meet you, Young Hero Lin," Yu Xiangzi slightly bowed her head. Now that Lin Fei had killed He Yunzi, the only benefit, perhaps, was his reputation.

Lin Fei's fame skyrocketed. No longer a nobody. Even the aloof Acquired Realm martial artists, when they met Lin Fei, would no longer dare to be as arrogant as before.

"Daoist Yu Xiangzi, there's no need to be so polite. I heard that Daoist Yu Xiangzi also wants to exchange the quota for entering the secret realm. I wonder what good things Daoist Yu Xiangzi has in her possession?" Lin Fei asked curiously. Just now, Ling Yao had told him that Yu Xiangzi had something good. That's why Lin Fei decided to meet with Yu Xiangzi.

Yu Xiangzi smiled slightly and said, "Young Hero Lin, I am aware of your requirements. Although there are many pills that can enhance inner power, it is difficult for you to find a pill similar to the Heavenly Meridian Pill. Even among the Immortal Sects, the pills they refine to enhance inner power are no better than the royal Heavenly Meridian Pill. And the Heavenly Meridian Pill can only be taken once, so I suppose Young Hero Lin doesn't need a second one.

Indeed, there is no better pill than the Heavenly Meridian Pill. But after much effort, I finally obtained a hundred-year-old Gold Crown Treasure Snake from my senior fellow disciples. This Gold Crown Treasure Snake has been nurtured for a hundred years and has consumed countless precious supplements.

Its fresh blood can nourish a martial artist's body, greatly enhancing muscles, skin, and vitality. And inner power is nothing more than what's produced by muscles, skin, and inner power itself. Drinking the blood of the treasure snake, it shouldn't be difficult to improve inner power. I have brought a jar of the treasure snake's blood here. Young Hero Lin can have a taste first. If you are satisfied, we can then exchange the quota for entering the secret realm. What do you think, Young Hero Lin?"

Lin Fei's eyes lit up. He then smiled and said, "Since Daoist Yu Xiangzi is so sincere, how can I not agree?"

Lin Fei agreed. Yu Xiangzi brought the treasure snake's blood and she let Lin Fei try it first. Clearly, she was very sincere. What could Lin Fei be unsatisfied with? So, Yu Xiangzi poured a small bowl of the treasure snake's blood from the jar.

Lin Fei drank it immediately. Instantly, he felt a warmth in his abdomen. After a while, this warmth had spread throughout his entire body.

Lin Fei's vitality, muscles, and skin, all seemed to experience a "second growth." They were constantly improving and strengthening. The blood of the treasure snake did not directly enhance inner power. Instead, it enhanced muscles, skin, and vitality.

As Lin Fei's vitality, muscles, and skin became stronger, the inner power produced naturally became faster and greater. Thus indirectly enhancing inner power.

About half an hour later, Lin Fei opened his eyes. He felt the inner power within his body. He was incredibly shocked. In just this short period of time, his inner power had actually increased by 10%!

"Your Three Vitality Technique has tasted the blood of the treasure snake and is in high spirits, full of energy, and the cultivation speed has greatly increased."

"Your Three Vitality Technique cultivation progress has increased by 5%."

The system's voice rang out in Lin Fei's mind. It was actually an increase in the cultivation progress of the Three Vitality Technique.

Rare indeed, truly rare.

Ever since Lin Fei began cultivating the Three Vitality Technique, he knew that this Three Vitality Technique was a calm and steady technique. It was difficult for anything to stimulate the Three Vitality Technique.

Therefore, all this time, the Three Vitality Technique had been steadily improving, little by little. There was basically no significant improvement through sudden enlightenment or the like.

But now, after Lin Fei drank the blood of the treasure snake, the Three Vitality Technique finally showed a response. The cultivation progress had improved somewhat. It seemed that the Three Vitality Technique was quite "greedy."

5% wasn't much, but that was just a small bowl of treasure snake blood. What if it were a whole jar of treasure snake blood?

"Daoist Yu Xiangzi, I'll trade this jar of treasure snake blood for entry to the secret realm! I'll give you the entry quota. I hope that in the secret realm, Daoist Yu Xiangzi can protect my senior sister," Lin Fei showed a slight smile on his face and said to Yu Xiangzi.

"Great, Young Hero Lin. I'm so grateful to you. I will definitely protect the princess."

Yu Xiangzi was overjoyed. Lin Fei really agreed to exchange the quota! Although the treasure snake was precious, how could it compare to the elixir for the Foundation Establishment Pill?

If they really obtained the elixir in the secret realm, Yu Xiangzi might have several Foundation Establishment Pills in the future. That's equivalent to having several more chances for Foundation Establishment than others. This was simply an opportunity to succeed in cultivation! How could Yu Xiangzi not be excited?

"Senior Sister Ling, I won't get involved in the matter of entering the secret realm. You can discuss it with Daoist Yu Xiangzi. I hope Senior Sister and Daoist can gain great harvests in the secret realm!"

After saying that, Lin Fei excused himself and left. He hurried back to his room and informed the servants of the South King Mansion that they should not disturb him unless there was something important. Lin Fei was going into seclusion!

With the Burst Qi Pill and this jar of treasure snake blood, Lin Fei believed his cultivation would definitely advance by leaps and bounds. He might not reach the twelfth level of the acquired stage, but reaching the tenth or eleventh level should not be a problem.

"Three Vitality Technique, I'm afraid you'll have to work hard during this time," Lin Fei muttered softly.

Lin Fei was going to take Burst Qi Pills and swallow the treasure snake blood. The Three Vitality Technique was destined to be several times busier than before. Fortunately, the Three Vitality Technique had a calm and steady temperament, and would not be impatient. Otherwise, Lin Fei would be a little worried. The Three Vitality Technique might go on "strike" and refuse to work.

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei took a deep breath. He poured out another large bowl of treasure snake blood and swallowed it directly.
