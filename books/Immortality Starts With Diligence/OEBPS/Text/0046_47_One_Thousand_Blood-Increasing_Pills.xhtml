47. One Thousand Blood-Increasing Pills

The next day, Lin Fei was summoned to the hall.

"Fei, you need to be mentally prepared for your marriage."

Lin's father weighed the matter for a long time, hesitating as if he wanted to say something but stopped.

Lin Fei's heart moved. It seemed that there was something wrong with his marriage.

"Father, tell me, what's the matter?"

"Lord Prefect has sent a message. After seeing your portrait, Wang Xuan was not very interested, so..."

Lin's father's voice also gradually lowered.

Lin's mother seemed to be complaining, "What's wrong with my son? He is handsome and also an inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect. Even if she is the daughter of the prefect, my son is also worthy..."

Lin's mother continued to chatter, showing some dissatisfaction.

However, Lin Fei appeared very calm. He even breathed a sigh of relief in his heart.

"Fei, you..." Lin's parents were still worried.

"Father. Mother. Don't worry, I'm fine. Since Wang Xuan is not interested, we don't need to force it." After speaking, Lin Fei bid farewell and left.

Watching Lin Fei leave, Lin's mother was somewhat reluctant, "Master, Lord Prefect is really overdoing it this time. He was the one who first proposed a marriage alliance with our Lin family. How could he..."

"Okay, stop talking. In fact, this may not be a bad thing. Didn't you see that Fei was already dissatisfied with this marriage? Maybe Fei already has someone in mind."

Lin's father said meaningfully.

"Oh? Fei has someone in mind, most likely a disciple from the Nine Profound Sect. Perhaps it's one of his senior sisters?" Lin's mother's eyes lit up.

"We don't need to bother about who exactly it is. Fei has more independence than us..." Lin's father's expression was calm.

However, there was obviously a hint of dissatisfaction in his eyes. The prefect's actions really showed that he didn't take the Lin family seriously.

But the other party was prefect. For such trivial matters, the Lin family had no choice.

Although Lin Fei had already left the hall. His senses were keen.

Lin Fei could still hear his parents' conversation even from a distance.

"They think I have someone in mind? Well, this is good too. I can have a peaceful time for a while." Lin Fei murmured in a low voice.

He knew he didn't have anyone in mind.

Thinking about it, Lin Fei's mind involuntarily conjured up a figure.

Ling Yao!

He would rather marry Ling Yao if he really had to.

Lin Fei shook his head. It wasn't time to think about these things yet.

He went directly to Lin Miao's sideyard and watched Lin Miao refine the Blood-Increasing Pill.

Lin Miao set up the Yin Gathering Formation, which was indeed effective.

After a whole day, he had successfully refined it five times, and each time, he produced around ten pills.

In one day, he could refine a total of fifty Blood-Increasing Pills.

At this rate, Lin Miao would be able to refine one thousand Blood-Increasing Pills in twenty days.

Moreover, even with the consumption of alchemy furnaces, medicinal materials, and other expenses, it would not exceed forty thousand taels of silver.

Lin Fei was very satisfied. He brought back the fifty Blood-Increasing Pills he had refined on the first day to his room.

He swallowed ten Blood-Increasing Pills at once.

"Buzz."

Suddenly, the qi and blood in Lin Fei's body shook.

The Giant Whale Body Refining Technique was also automatically operated.

Ten Blood-Increasing Pills at once were too easy for Lin Fei now. His huge qi and blood could almost be refined instantly.

Moreover, even ten Blood-Increasing Pills could only improve the cultivation progress of the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique by 1% for him now.

Lin Fei could fully absorb all fifty Blood-Increasing Pills in one day.

"Twenty days, and I will be able to give birth to inner power!" Lin Fei murmured in a low voice, a gleam of determination flashing in his eyes.

He had been waiting for this day for a long time and the thought of it made his heart burn with excitement.

As time passed, Lin Fei took Blood-Increasing Pills every day and the progress of his Giant Whale Body Refining Technique improved visibly.

He was getting closer and closer to giving birth to his inner power.

Knowing that there was a constant supply of Blood-Increasing Pills, Lin Fei no longer paid attention to Lin Miao's pill refining work.

Instead, he focused on the medicinal bath for the Vajra Iron Cloth. After studying it for a few days, Lin Fei had a rough idea of its function.

The medicinal bath promoted the recovery of the skin membrane, which was constantly damaged during the practice of Vajra Iron Cloth.

With the improvement of Lin Fei's Vajra Iron Cloth realm, the efficacy of the ordinary medicinal bath became less effective in repairing the damaged skin membrane, resulting in slower progress in his cultivation of Vajra Iron Cloth.

Lin Fei came up with a solution in recent days.

"Young Master, the stuff you wanted has arrived," the butler's voice came from outside the door.

Lin Fei opened the door and saw the butler holding a package. He smiled and ordered, "Continue to buy more, the more the better."

"Yes, Young Master," the butler replied before leaving.

Lin Fei brought the package into his room and placed it on the table.

Inside the package was a box filled with white porcelain bottles.

Lin Fei opened one of the bottles and immediately a strong smell of blood emanated from it.
