176. Heavenly Fire Crossing the Sky

Time passed by minute by minute. The whole room was filled with saber intent, saber essence, and the momentum of the Heavenly Saber. And around Lin Fei, hundreds of saber technique secrets, one by one were incredibly quiet, without the slightest movement.

Lin Fei himself wasn't sure whether these saber techniques would be stimulated by him After all, Lin Fei's request was to let these saber techniques self-practice to perfection, then integrate into the saber intent, help him evolve saber momentum. This was simply getting something for nothing.

Lin Fei just moved his lips. But, Lin Fei believed his proposal was also attractive to these saber techniques.

Saber techniques would last forever! The Saber Dao will never perish!

Not many saber techniques could resist such a temptation. However, the everlasting saber technique and the unperishing Saber Dao were also just based on Lin Fei's words.

Right now, Lin Fei couldn't possibly achieve the everlasting saber technique, the unperishing Saber Dao. So, Lin Fei was waiting.

The entire secret room was very quiet. Lin Fei closed his eyes. His heart was also very calm. Waiting quietly. No one knows how long it had passed. Suddenly, a voice rang out in Lin Fei's mind.

"Perfection-level Blinking Saber Technique, willing to integrate into your saber intent, increasing the foundation of saber intent."

"Your saber intent cultivation progress has improved."

"Perfection-level Spring Wind Saber Technique, willing to integrate into your saber intent, increasing the foundation of saber intent."

"Your saber intent cultivation progress has improved."

"Perfection-level Empty Saber Technique, willing to integrate into your saber intent, increasing the foundation of saber intent."

Finally, one saber technique after another began to merge into Lin Fei's saber intent, and they were all at the perfection level.

Of course, these saber techniques were at best Body Refining level or Acquired level saber techniques. The increase in the cultivation progress of saber intent was actually not much. Even if there were dozens of saber techniques, it would be hard to say whether they could increase the cultivation progress of saber intent by 1%.

However, it was visible to the naked eye that Lin Fei's saber intent cultivation progress was indeed improving bit by bit. And Lin Fei could clearly feel, in his mind, there were more perfection-level saber techniques. These saber techniques seemed to be all under Lin Fei's control.

Ten, twenty, thirty, fifty, a hundred...

Countless saber techniques were all integrated into Lin Fei's saber intent, increasing Lin Fei's foundation. And Lin Fei's saber intent was also improving bit by bit.

6%, 7%, 8%, 9%, 10%...

Although the increase was slow. A perfection-level Body Refining saber technique, the increase in saber intent cultivation progress was almost not countable.

However, there were a lot of Body Refining level saber techniques. There were five or six hundred Body Refining level saber techniques and there were also acquired level saber techniques.

Of course, what Lin Fei cared most about was the innate level saber techniques. Once the innate-level saber technique was integrated into the saber intent, the increase in the foundation of the saber technique would definitely be extremely terrifying.

"Perfection-level Broken Saber Flow, willing to integrate into your saber intent, increasing the foundation of saber intent."

"Your saber intent cultivation progress has improved by 3%."

"Perfection-level Mountain-Cleaving Chop, willing to integrate into your saber intent, increasing the foundation of saber intent."

"Your saber intent cultivation progress has improved by 3%."

Lin Fei's eyes lit up.

Innate-level saber techniques!

Broken Saber Flow and Mountain-Cleaving Chop were both innate-level saber techniques. They actually took the initiative to integrate into Lin Fei's saber intent. Moreover, the increase in the cultivation progress of the saber intent was far beyond the comparison of Acquired saber techniques and Body Refining saber techniques.

However, Lin Fei was still waiting.

These two innate-level saber techniques were not the strongest innate saber techniques of the Nine Profound Sect. Even among the innate level saber techniques, there were superior and inferior ones. Once integrated into the saber intent, the increase in the foundation was also different. Soon, in Lin Fei's mind, the voice of the system echoed again.

"Perfection-level Three Abyssal Sabers, willing to integrate into your saber intent, increasing the foundation of saber intent."

"Your saber intent cultivation progress has improved by 5%."

"Perfection-level Twenty-Three Sabers, willing to integrate into your saber intent, increasing the foundation of saber intent."

"Your saber intent cultivation progress has improved by 5%."

Sure enough, Three Abyssal Sabers and Twenty-Three Sabers, second only to the Heavenly Saber Art, also chose to integrate into Lin Fei's saber intent.

Within a short hour, in the room, most of the hundreds of saber techniques chose to integrate into Lin Fei's saber intent. Of course, this was not compulsory. Therefore, there were still quite a few saber techniques unwilling to integrate into Lin Fei's saber intent.

Lin Fei didn't mind.

"System."

Lin Fei muttered in his heart, calling out to the system panel to check his martial arts status.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Early Stage Martial Saint

Saber Intent: Perfection (Progress 55%)

Fist Intent: Mastery (Progress 81%)

Immeasurable Fist: Transcendence (Progress 50%)

Close Yet Distant: Transcendence (Progress 0%)

Lin Fei saw it on the system panel. The cultivation progress of the saber intent had increased directly from 5% to 55%. The saber intent had been enhanced more than ten times! The strength of Lin Fei's saber intent now was incredible.

However, saber intent had not yet transformed in the end. But saber intent could enhance saber techniques. Once Lin Fei performed the Heavenly Saber Art again, the power might be multiplied several times!

Moreover, most importantly, this time he merged hundreds of saber techniques.

The saber intent increased by 50%. This made Lin Fei see the direction of the saber intent evolving into saber momentum.

That was "deception." "Deceive" the saber techniques.

Let those saber techniques actively integrate into their own saber intent without having to work hard to practice saber techniques themselves. Of course, this is only useful for low-level saber techniques. If it were the Martial Saint level saber techniques, their pride was high, Lin Fei couldn't "deceive" them.

Forget about Martial Saint level saber techniques, even innate level saber techniques, Lin Fei might not be able to "deceive." But anyway, this was a direction.

Now, it was just the saber techniques of the Nine Profound Sect that had merged into Lin Fei's saber intent. And it had enhanced the saber intent so much. What if he collected all the saber techniques in the world? Or, Lin Fei didn't even need to collect all the saber techniques in the world. Just a trip to Chang City, to the royal family's martial arts secret chamber.

Most of the lower-level saber techniques like body refining saber techniques and acquired saber techniques might be a lot of repetition. It was estimated that the royal family's lower-level saber techniques were not much different from the Nine Profound Sect's. But what about innate saber techniques? There must be differences.

By then, Lin Fei would integrate some innate-level saber techniques. It was not impossible to evolve saber intent into saber momentum!

If the royal family's saber techniques were not enough, Lin Fei could go to Shen Lianxin and let him take him to the ruins of the ancient martial arts sects. There must be a lot of saber techniques there.

In short, Lin Fei had found the direction to evolve saber momentum. He was one step closer to becoming a "Saber Saint"! And for Martial Saints, saber momentum was equivalent to the significance of saber intent to an innate martial artist.

Martial Saints with or without saber momentum were on two different levels. They were not to be mentioned in the same breath.

"If I go to Chang City now, I wonder if it's too late? Has that elder at the perfection stage of Qi Condensation in Guardian Primordial Mountain successfully built his foundation? If I leave, once the Guardian Primordial Mountain Foundation Establishment cultivator comes down, the consequences would be unthinkable."

Many thoughts flashed through Lin Fei's mind. If he evolved saber momentum, then cutting down a Foundation Establishment True Person with a saber shouldn't be much of a problem. Having saber momentum also gave him more confidence.

However, if Lin Fei were to leave, Guardian Primordial Mountain's Foundation Establishment True Person comes down, and the Nine Profound Sect definitely couldn't resist. At that time, the consequences would be serious.

Lin Fei thought about it and decided to have the sect master send someone to go to the vicinity of Guardian Primordial Mountain and scout around again.

"Buzz."

Suddenly, the entire Nine Profound Sect slightly trembled. Many people felt a sense of being "isolated." Then, in the sky above the Nine Profound Sect, there was an endless Spiritual Qi gathering. It faintly formed a huge vortex.

However, this Spiritual Qi vortex was not just pure Spiritual Qi, but it was filled with fire attributes. It faintly turned into a fiery red color. It actually turned into a burning fire cloud. Like heavenly fire, it hovered in the sky above the Nine Profound Sect.

For a moment, everyone in the Nine Profound Sect had a feeling of impending disaster, as if the end of the world was coming.

"Nine Profound Sect has offended Guardian Primordial Mountain, offended the Immortal Dao. Today, this true person will personally descend the mountain, and with the 'Heavenly Fire Technique,' wipe out all the people of the Nine Profound Sect. From now on, martial artists who disrespect Qi cultivators and Immortal Dao should be killed without mercy!"

A vast and mighty voice came from the void, echoing throughout the Nine Profound Sect. The Nine Profound Sect was in an uproar.

"A True Person, this is a Foundation Establishment True Person!"

"Guardian Primordial Mountain has truly given birth to a Foundation Establishment True Person!"

"Now, the True Person himself is taking action, executing the Heavenly Punishment, and wants to exterminate all of us in the Nine Profound Sect."

"What do we do? Even if the ancestor were to resurrect, he would not be a match for the True Person!"

For a moment, the Nine Profound Sect was in a state of anxiety. After all, only a few knew that Lin Fei had become a Martial Saint. Most of the disciples and sect members didn't know that the Nine Profound Sect had given birth to a Martial Saint. They all felt that this was the time when the sky was falling, and the Nine Profound Sect was going to be destroyed!

"Swish".

Lin Fei raised his head, looking at the void.

"Finally here. No matter, even without saber momentum, I can cut you down!"

A hint of coldness flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

"Swoosh."

The next moment, Lin Fei's figure flashed. He had already left the room.
