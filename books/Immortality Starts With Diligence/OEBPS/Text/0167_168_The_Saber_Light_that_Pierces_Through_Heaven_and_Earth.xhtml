168. The Saber Light that Pierces Through Heaven and Earth

In the Nine Profound Sect, the ground was littered with bodies. At this moment, countless disciples had gathered together. Their eyes filled with a hint of hatred, staring intensely at the two groups of people in front of them.

One group was Qi Condensation disciples from the Guardian Primordial Mountain, led by Yuan Xingzi and Yuan Chenzi. Each of them held a magical weapon in their hands, "Heavenly Thunder" and "Earthly Evil".

The Heavenly Thunder was invincible, sharp, and unstoppable, even mountains could be collapsed. The Earthly Evil would cover the sky and earth once it came out, whoever touches it would die. Forget about martial artists, even Qi Condensation disciples would keep as far away as possible.

The other group was Qi Condensation disciples from He Mountain Dao led by He Yuanzi, holding a magical weapon "Mountain River Seal".

Once it was unleashed, the Mountain River Seal seemed to carry the weight of a mountain and river, mighty and overwhelming. Once it strikes down, whether martial arts Masters or Qi Condensation disciples, all would be smashed into a pulp.

Any defense of the Nine Profound Sect, in front of the Mountain River Seal, all seemed so fragile.

At this moment, all martial artists of the Nine Profound Sect, including the three martial arts Masters, were all gathered together now. The Nine Profound Sect only had three martial arts Masters in total. Among them, Grand Elder Zhao Kongming was the strongest.

After all, Zhao Kongming had already comprehended the Sword Intent. Zhao Kongming's Sword Intent, when dealing with some high-level Qi Condensation disciples, could still be somewhat effective. But against top-level Qi Condensation disciples like He Yuanzi, Yuan Xingzi, and Yuan Chenzi, it was of no use.

"Zhao Kongming, Lin Fei is your disciple, hand him over. Otherwise, the Nine Profound Sect will not escape destruction."

He Yuanzi spoke indifferently. Even though he had killed his way to the Nine Profound Sect, so far, he had not seen Lin Fei. He thought that the Nine Profound Sect might have "hidden" Lin Fei. After all, Lin Fei had great potential. It's possible for the Nine Profound Sect to protect Lin Fei.

"That's right, Zhao Kongming, if you don't hand over Lin Fei, are you intending to have the entire Nine Profound Sect be buried with Lin Fei?"

Yuan Xingzi also spoke. He and He Mountain Dao's He Yuanzi actually did not have prior contact. But He Yuanzi arrived before the Guardian Primordial Mountain.

Moreover, they had also sent top-level Qi Condensation disciples to surround and kill Lin Fei last time. So they were familiar with each other.

Besides, this time, their goal was the same. Not only to kill Lin Fei but also to destroy the entire Nine Profound Sect. So they simply teamed up.

Before finding Lin Fei, Guardian Primordial Mountain, and He Mountain Dao were not in such a hurry to destroy Nine Profound Sect, they even had it all planned out.

If Lin Fei is not here, then they would use the Nine Profound Sect as a threat, and let Lin Fei return to the Nine Profound Sect himself. In short, they had many means.

Without the check and balances of the Great Chang royal family, without the royal family's supreme elder suppressing the world, these immortal sect Qi Condensation disciples were completely unscrupulous. They held an absolute advantage, they had plenty of ways to deal with Lin Fei.

Zhao Kongming glanced at the two Grand Elders by his side.

He chuckled and said, "Old Li, Old Bai, after so many years, I didn't expect that we three old fellows would have to fight shoulder to shoulder again."

"Haha, Zhao Kongming, when you were young, you made a name for yourself as 'Empty Abyss Sword' in the martial world. Back then, your temper was quite hot. How come you have become so motherly now that you're old?"

"That's right, Old Zhao, since our ancestor founded the sect when has our Nine Profound Sect ever bowed its head? We, as martial artists, fight against heaven and earth, and die without regret!"

"Even if we die, as long as there is a disciple of Nine Profound Sect, our sect can still be rebuilt!"

"Remembering the past, our founder was invincible in the world. You Qi cultivators from the immortal sects, how dare you be so presumptuous?"

"Hahaha, indeed. Forget about our founder, even Elder Lin Fei can kill Qi Condensation disciples as easily as killing chickens!"

The three Grand Elders, martial arts Masters at this moment, showed no fear. Instead, they openly mocked.

"How presumptuous! You're nearing death and still dare to be stubborn! Good, then I'll smash each of you into a pulp, then deal with Lin Fei. Mountain River Seal, kill!"

He Yuanzi was extremely furious. Whenever Lin Fei was mentioned, He Mountain Dao was the most furious. Because, time and again, Lin Fei had killed too many disciples of He Mountain Dao.

In fact, Lin Fei's reputation as the "Immortal Slayer Saber" was built on the bodies of He Mountain Dao's disciples, and became famous in Great Chang. This was simply a great disgrace to He Mountain Dao.

"Hum".

He Yuanzi directly threw a small seal from his hand into the air and spiritual power surged into it.

"Whoosh".

The Mountain River Seal expanded with the wind, emitting a grayish glow. In the blink of an eye, it went from a small seal to a covering the sky and earth, like a mountain, hovering in the air.

Of course, it wasn't as heavy as a real mountain. But even so, this was already extremely astonishing. This was a magical treasure! It could change size as desired and had all sorts of incredible powers. It was many times stronger than magical artifacts. Once a Qi cultivator possessed a magical treasure, their strength would be vastly different from before.

Looking at the Mountain River Seal in the air, Zhao Kongming and the three innate elders glanced at each other. They could all see the determination in each other's eyes!

"Under my Mountain River Seal, who in your Nine Profound Sect can resist? Martial artists, in the end, are merely nobodies, unable to withstand a single blow! You were born to be stepped under our Qi cultivators' feet!"

He Yuanzi's body stirred with spiritual power. His robes fluttered, moving without wind. His aura was incredibly strong. Especially with the Mountain River Seal enhancing his aura.

In front of such a magical treasure, martial artists, even innate martial artists were indeed very insignificant. Like ants.

"Whoosh".

Zhao Kongming made his move. His fierce sword intent surged to the sky. A terrifying sword light viciously slashed toward He Yuanzi. He knew that he could not shake the Mountain River Seal. But He Yuanzi was different. He Yuanzi was just a Qi Condensation cultivator. He wasn't at the Foundation Establishment stage. His sword could injure him.

"Futile struggle. Mountain River Seal, fall!"

He Yuanzi didn't even glance at Zhao Kongming. He directly controlled the Mountain River Seal to fall toward Zhao Kongming.

"Boom".

Instantly, the Mountain River Seal fell directly. Zhao Kongming's sword light dissipated inch by inch. He could only watch the Mountain River Seal, as big as a mountain, fall onto his head. All the Nine Profound Sect disciples had bloodshot eyes.

"Hahaha, Old Zhao, you can't go before us. Let's go together!"

Instantly, the two Grand Elders also burst out with Innate True Qi. They directly rushed to Zhao Kongming's side, raised their fists, and viciously smashed towards the Mountain River Seal overhead.

"Bang".

However, the two innate martial artists were simply unable to shake the Mountain River Seal.

"The three of us, let's die together."

The three martial arts Masters looked at each other. They also showed a trace of a smile on their faces, quietly waiting for the Mountain River Seal to fall.

"Whoosh".

Suddenly, from the corner of Zhao Kongming's eye, seemed to see a familiar saber light. That saber light shook the heavens as if it pierced through the sky and earth. It stretched across the void and fierce aura swept across the entire sky.

"That's..."

Zhao Kongming's eyes slowly widened as if he had thought of something. His expression showed a hint of joy.
