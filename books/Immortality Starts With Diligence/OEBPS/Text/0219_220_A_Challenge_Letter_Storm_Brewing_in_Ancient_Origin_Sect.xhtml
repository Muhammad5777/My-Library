220. A Challenge Letter, Storm Brewing in Ancient Origin Sect

"True disciple, Ao Yunxiao!"

Shen Lianxin finally uttered a name.

"Ao Yunxiao?"

Lin Fei frowned. He had not heard of this name before.

"Brother Shen, have you offended him?" Lin Fei asked.

"No, in fact, I didn't even know him before."

"So why did he injure you?"

Shen Lianxin looked up at Lin Fei, speaking word by word: "For momentum! Ao Yunxiao ranks well among the true disciples, both in physical strength and true essence." He's not far from condensing his Energy Flower or Qi Flower. But he hasn't comprehended any momentum. So, he practiced a special martial art. As long as he can defeat opponents of the same level one by one, he can successfully comprehend momentum!

Among the true disciples, except for those who have condensed their Energy Flower or Qi Flower, he has challenged almost all of them. Without exception, he has won. Even a true disciple who has understood the momentum of transcendence martial arts fell under his fist.

We might not be true disciples, but we are 'Dao Seekers', well-known. Especially Brother Lin, with your supreme saber momentum, suppressing hundreds of Martial Saints. Although not a true disciple, your reputation and popularity even overshadow many true disciples. Ao Yunxiao came to me, seeking a duel. And as you know, the result. I am not afraid of any challenge. I fought him and lost."

Lin Fei understood. Shen Lianxin was injured by Ao Yunxiao.

"But, after losing the last time, why did he injure you again?" Lin Fei asked.

Ao Yunxiao just wanted to challenge, to build up momentum. He did not have any deep hatred for Shen Lianxin. It wouldn't warrant a second attack.

Shen Lianxin spoke with a complex expression: "Two days ago, Ao Yunxiao was going to find you. But he couldn't find you, so he came to me to inquire about your whereabouts. I naturally ignored him. He was not happy with my response, and he injured me again."

Shen Lianxin was succinct, explaining the reason. Lin Fei understood. Shen Lianxin was injured for the second time on his behalf. Ao Yunxiao's target was Lin Fei. But Lin Fei had just happened to go down the mountain, back to Fairy Mist Island. So, in the absence of Lin Fei, Ao Yunxiao found Shen Lianxin.

There was some verbal conflict between them. A fight was inevitable. That's why Shen Lianxin got injured this time. And the injuries were very serious. Because this time, Ao Yunxiao was full of anger. He vented all his anger on Shen Lianxin. If it weren't for the Ancient Origin Sect's prohibition against disciple killing, Ao Yunxiao might have wanted to kill Shen Lianxin.

"Ao Yunxiao..."

Lin Fei's gaze was growing colder and colder. In the Ancient Origin Sect, he didn't really have any friends. Lin Fei only had one real friend, Shen Lianxin!

Shen Lianxin was usually quiet and reticent. Even when they were drifting on the sea for months, they hardly spoke. But as friends, they understood each other. Lin Fei was very clear about this. If there was a need, Shen Lianxin would help him at all costs. Even if it meant giving up his life! He and Shen Lianxin were truly friends who had shared life and death together!

However, Lin Fei's anger did not show. He simply said calmly, "Brother Shen, I brought back ten disciples from the eight major families from Fairy Mist Island. I have recommended five disciples. And another five disciples need your recommendation."

Shen Lianxin nodded.

"Brother Shen, rest well. I'll come to see you in a few days."

Lin Fei didn't say much more. He turned around and prepared to leave. As Lin Fei reached the entrance of the cave, Shen Lianxin spoke: "Brother Lin, hit him hard!"

Lin Fei paused slightly but didn't look back. A smile appeared at the corner of his mouth.

"The one who knows me is Brother Shen!"

With that, Lin Fei strode away. Even though he didn't say anything, Shen Lianxin knew Lin Fei would definitely go to find Ao Yunxiao!

Shen Lianxin might be quiet and reticent, but he was not ignorant. On the contrary, Shen Lianxin understood everything. Between him and Lin Fei, there was no need to say much. A single look was all it took to understand!

Lin Fei left Shen Lianxin's cave dwelling. He did not directly go to find Ao Yunxiao. Didn't Ao Yunxiao want to accumulate momentum? Wanting to defeat Martial Saints at the same level and then comprehend the momentum of martial arts?

Lin Fei did not want to let Ao Yunxiao get his wish. He wanted to publicly fight Ao Yunxiao, and completely defeat Ao Yunxiao. Let all the momentum that Ao Yunxiao had accumulated before dissipate!

So, Lin Fei didn't need to find Ao Yunxiao. A challenge letter would be enough!

...

The mountain peak of the true disciples. This area was off-limits to inner disciples. Only some elders at the Martial Saint level and true disciples could enter this area. This was the core of the entire Ancient Origin Sect! After all, each true disciple was a hope for the future of the Ancient Origin Sect. There were even many seeds of Martial Sovereign.

Of course, they were only seeds. True disciples had come and gone in waves. But who had become a Martial Sovereign? Few and far between! Scarce! So-called seeds of Martial Sovereign, maybe, would always remain seeds, never growing into towering trees!

Inside Ao Yunxiao's cave dwelling, several true disciples gathered. Although Ao Yunxiao was not a seed of Martial Sovereign, even the seeds of Martial Sovereign feared Ao Yunxiao. Because, apart from not having comprehended momentum, his physical body and true essence were about to condense into Energy Flower and Qi Flower. By then, with both flowers blooming, even if he hadn't comprehended momentum, Ao Yunxiao would still be a top-tier Martial Saint!

Such a person, no matter how much emphasis was placed on him was not excessive. Even if he didn't become a Martial Sovereign, he would dominate one side in the future. Therefore, around Ao Yunxiao, there would be some true disciples who took the initiative to befriend him.

"Brother Ao, the momentum you've accumulated should be almost enough, right? Could it be that you still haven't comprehended momentum? Almost all the true disciples at the same level as Brother Ao have been defeated by you. Only those brothers who have condensed the Energy Flower and Qi Flower have not been challenged. But for those who have condensed the Energy Flower and Qi Flower, the gap is too big, and the challenge is also useless."

Many true disciples hoped that Ao Yunxiao could comprehend momentum. Because, in that case, once Ao Yunxiao comprehends momentum, he might even have the chance to be listed as a seed of Martial Sovereign. By then, Ao Yunxiao's identity and status in the Ancient Origin Sect will be greatly different.

"I'm just one short! Defeat him and I can comprehend momentum!"

Ao Yunxiao had been waiting all along, waiting for that person to appear.

"Brother Ao, you're referring to the 'Dao Seeker' Lin Fei, aren't you? I heard he comprehended the saber momentum, and he was in the limelight for a while in the beginner stage. But he seems to have gone down the mountain."

"Going down the mountain at this time is probably to avoid Brother Ao."

"Yes, Lin Fei definitely doesn't dare to fight with Brother Ao. After all, Brother Ao is famous and invincible among the true disciples. If he accepts the challenge, wouldn't it be a humiliation for himself?"

Everyone knew who Ao Yunxiao was referring to.

Dao Seeker Lin Fei!

Although he was not a true disciple but an outer elder, his status as a "Dao Seeker" made him a prominent figure in the Ancient Origin Sect. Ao Yunxiao wanted to accumulate momentum, and he definitely couldn't do it without Lin Fei.

"He will appear. I purposely injured Shen Lianxin to show him. As long as he is still a martial artist and has a bit of arrogance, he won't be able to resist! If he can't resist, I can defeat him. By then, my martial momentum will be achieved!"

Ao Yunxiao said faintly. His tone was filled with unwavering conviction.

"Report! Master, the outer elder, 'Dao Seeker' Lin Fei has issued a challenge letter to the master, requesting the master to fight him on the Sect's stage in three days!"

"Whoosh."

Suddenly, a glint flashed in Ao Yunxiao's eyes. The many true disciples were also somewhat surprised. They didn't expect that Ao Yunxiao's prediction would come true. Lin Fei indeed couldn't resist, and even took the initiative to issue a challenge! However, this was exactly what Ao Yunxiao intended.

"I accept the challenge! Junior brothers, please return. I need to adjust my condition. In three days, I will defeat Lin Fei and fully comprehend the martial momentum! See the guests out!"

Ao Yunxiao waved his hand. Many true disciples also got up one after another and left the cave dwelling.
