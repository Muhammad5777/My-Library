552. The Secret of Life's Birth! Lin Fei's New Insights into the World Dao

"This is the eighth heavenly lightning, and he actually exploded it outright."

"True Monarch Lin always surpasses expectations. Who could have thought that he could burst the eighth heavenly lightning with a single punch?"

"Incredible, utterly incredible. To burst the eighth heavenly lightning in one go, bearing in mind, from the sixth heavenly lightning onwards, it was already at the True Monarch level, and the might of this eighth one is extraordinary."

"Many True Monarchs can't withstand such an eighth heavenly lightning, yet Lin Fei managed to explode it."

Many people were in awe. Of course, even more people intuitively guessed. The ninth heavenly lightning, for Lin Fei, probably wouldn't be much of a challenge.

"Whoosh."

Sure enough, the ninth heavenly lightning descended immediately. Lin Fei's Saber World instantly shattered.

Lin Fei also launched a punch. The power of all four thousand four hundred Particle Worlds erupted.

The tremendous force, overwhelmingly, almost annihilated about sixty to seventy percent of the ninth heavenly lightning. Even obliterating sixty to seventy percent was incredibly powerful.

The remaining thirty to forty percent of the lightning struck Lin Fei. Only annihilating a few percent of his physical body particles. Then it was completely exhausted.

The ninth heavenly lightning presented no surprise for Lin Fei. He easily withstood it. Outside the tribulation site, many were silent.

Speechless. Their previous speculations now seemed like jokes. Lin Fei was not rash at all. But rather, he was in complete control. Extremely confident.

Throughout Lin Fei's tribulation, the entire process was incredibly smooth, without any unexpected incidents. Each heavenly lightning was within Lin Fei's control. There wasn't even a hint of danger, demonstrating just how formidable Lin Fei's strength was.

This absolute control proved that Lin Fei had plenty of leeway in crossing the eighth lightning tribulation. Afterward, a seven-colored light enveloped Lin Fei.

Under the cover of this rainbow light, Lin Fei entered an illusionary realm again. It was the same world as before. However, this world, with its various orders and laws, was now much more refined. Yet, it still wasn't a complete world. Because a complete world also needs life.

"Life..."

Lin Fei pondered seriously. Both his Saber World and Particle Worlds were quite similar to this world. The only thing missing was life.

Lin Fei was curious. How does life come into being?

This was crucial for Lin Fei. If life existed in Lin Fei's Particle Worlds, it would be a completely different scenario. They would transform into small worlds. Not much different from those around the Divine Realm. They might be smaller in scale, But they would still be complete worlds! And the power of several thousand complete worlds would be immensely formidable.

Therefore, Lin Fei was very serious at this moment. In the illusionary realm, the laws and the origin of that world were slowly growing. Everything seemed unremarkable.

Yet, it appeared there were still no signs of life emerging. Was this time merely for observing the world and not for the birth of life?

Lin Fei wasn't sure. He could only wait quietly, holding a glimmer of hope deep within. Time passed slowly. Lin Fei noticed a peculiar phenomenon.

In the illusionary realm, time seemed to have passed for quite a while in this newly born world. The sea of origin, and the laws, all became very powerful over this prolonged period. Everything seemed indistinguishable from a small world. The only difference was the absence of life. Lin Fei observed no signs of life emerging.

"Could it be that life is not born from some specific law of life, but rather, life emerges first, followed by the laws governing it? The birth of life is merely due to the long passage of time and the presence of a complete world suitable for the emergence of life."

Lin Fei murmured softly. At this moment, he seemed to have gained a significant insight, particularly about life. Even his understanding of the world deepened considerably.

"Your World Dao has gained insights into the essence of life's birth due to your understanding of life. Your World Dao has advanced by 10%."

The voice of the system echoed again in Lin Fei's mind. This time, it was beyond Lin Fei's expectations. The progress of the Worldly Dao had suddenly increased by a full ten percent.

This only indicated one thing. Lin Fei's gain this time far exceeded his previous experiences. This insight into the birth of life was incredibly significant. Especially for a world, no matter how vast a world is, without life, it is incomplete. Only a world where life has emerged is a complete world!

"It's about time. The ninth lightning tribulation won't be so easily crossed. I should return and thoroughly assimilate the gains from this experience."

Lin Fei chose not to continue with the tribulation. As the final phase of the lightning tribulation, the ninth lightning tribulation was of utmost importance.

Even Lin Fei had to approach it with the utmost caution, not to be taken lightly, especially since the ninth lightning tribulation.

While perhaps not so difficult for Lin Fei to withstand the ninth heavenly lightning, the insight gained about the world after withstanding it was crucial for Lin Fei.

Because this represented the last opportunity, if not seized properly, he would have to rely on his own slow understanding in the future, which would be incredibly challenging.

Therefore, the ninth lightning tribulation required thorough preparation. He must seize this last opportunity.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei left the formation. Seeing the crowd gathered outside the formation – three layers inside and three layers outside – was overwhelming. There were martial artists and immortal cultivators alike. Martial True Monarchs, Immortal True Monarchs, and many others were present. At this moment, in everyone's gaze, there was immense envy and admiration.

"Congratulations to True Monarch Lin for crossing the eighth lightning tribulation!"

Many Martial Gods, Deity Transformations, True Monarchs, and others spontaneously expressed their congratulations. After all, they were all members of the Ancient Covenant.

Given Lin Fei's extraordinary talents, there was no doubt. Perhaps, in the near future, the Ancient Covenant would witness the birth of another Martial Ancestor!

This was a positive development for the entire Ancient Covenant. Their congratulations were heartfelt!
