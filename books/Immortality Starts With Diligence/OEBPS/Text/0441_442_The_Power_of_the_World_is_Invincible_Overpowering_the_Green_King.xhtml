442. The Power of the World is Invincible! Overpowering the Green King!

The Green King revealed his true form and spoke in human language, "Now that you've seen my true self, you must die!"

"Boom!"

With a slight movement from the Green King, his massive body appeared in front of Lin Fei in an instant.

Such speed, it was almost like teleportation. A fish, out of water, still exhibited such terrifying power in the void?

"Bang!"

Lin Fei was sent flying with a single blow, he crashed heavily into the vast ocean.

"Splash!"

Soon, Lin Fei burst forth from the water.

"Five Elements Bloodline!"

Immediately, Lin Fei activated his Five Elements Bloodline. Lin knew that relying solely on the Four Symbols Bloodline or just the Five Elements Bloodline might not be enough against the Green King. Therefore, he simultaneously harnessed both bloodlines, merging them completely.

A brand new force was born. The Power of the World! Lin Fei's True Martial King Form was still only a hundred thousand meters tall. But the Power of the World now enveloped his entire body.

With the Power of the World, Lin Fei felt as if every fiber of his being was filled with unparalleled strength.

"Come on!"

With a mighty roar from Lin Fei, his hundred-thousand-foot figure punched at the Green King.

The Green King, not to be outdone, swept with his colossal tail. Given the Green King's strength, that single tail swipe could have sent Lin Fei, with his previous Four Symbols Bloodline strength, flying.

However, now that Lin Fei was using the Power of the World, the situation was entirely different.

"Bang!"

Their forces clashed fiercely.

A hint of a smile was still visible on the Green King's face. He believed that for Lin Fei to meet his force with force was sheer folly.

The Green King's power was superior to all exotic beasts. To confront it directly would be self-defeating.

However, as their powers met, the smile on the Green King's face froze. In that collision, he felt an unimaginably vast power, almost sending him flying.

Moreover, his tail had been pierced right through. The part of his flesh that was pierced vanished instantly. Meaning, it was a permanent loss.

"How is this possible?"

The Green King was again sent flying by Lin Fei's punch. It seemed he was still reeling from the shock. How did he get injured? But Lin Fei wouldn't give him the chance to recover.

Lin Fei transformed into a streak of light, drawing close to the Green King again.

"Bang, bang, bang!"

Lin Fei unleashed a barrage of punches, filling the sky with shadows of his fists.

Each punch was real, and each one was infused with the Power of the World. The reason Lin Fei could send the Green King flying was not due to his inherent strength, but because of the overwhelming Power of the World.

Lin Fei's Power of the World was seemingly omnipotent. When used offensively, its power is multiplied. In defense, almost no force could break through the protection of the Power of the World.

Furthermore, the Power of the World could even seal and suppress opponents. It truly was all-powerful. Such a comprehensive and novel force, Lin Fei had never experienced anything like it before.

Even Lin Fei felt that perhaps the legendary divine power wasn't any more impressive than this!

The endless shadows of punches from Lin Fei rained down, and the Green King couldn't withstand them any longer. It screamed in agony.

"No, what about my immortal body? Nobody can kill me! How could you possibly obliterate my flesh? I am the true Undying God! Only a god-tier being can harm my immortal body. Stop it, stop now!"

The Green King was breaking apart. Its body was riddled with holes. Each punch from Lin Fei was obliterating numerous flesh particles. Even if the Green King's body was powerful, bordering on immortality, it was utterly ineffective against Lin Fei's Power of the World. It was still being utterly obliterated.

Seeing the severely damaged Green King, who was so overbearing just moments ago, Lin Fei realized that even escaping now would be a luxury for it.

Lin Fei was very clear that without the Power of the World, which seemingly had no weaknesses, even his Four Symbols True Form or Five Elements True Form probably couldn't subdue the Green King. The strength of the Green King's body was simply inconceivable. How could a king-tier exotic beast possess such a formidable body?

The Green King has a secret!

Lin Fei's Power of the World surged forth, enveloping the Green King's entire body in an instant. The Power of the World rapidly obliterated the Green King's body until only its head remained.

Yet, despite this, it still hadn't died. As long as Lin Fei let go of the Green King, it would fully recover in no time. That was the terrifying aspect of the Green King. Of all the king-tier beings Lin Fei had ever encountered, besides the Phoenix, the Green King was the strongest! Even to some extent, the Green King might be stronger than the Phoenix!

"Share your secret," Lin Fei said slowly. "You've expanded so aggressively, causing wars in the marine territories. You surely must have a reason. And, after consuming so many Azure Flood Dragons, what is your purpose?"

A mere carp, even one from rivers and lakes, growing to such a level was unimaginable. It wasn't any powerful variant species. The Green King must have an earth-shattering secret.

Lin Fei wasn't as interested in the Azure Flood Dragon King, or the ancestral land of the Azure Flood Dragon clan anymore. He only wanted to uncover the secret that the Green King hid.

"If I reveal my secret, would you let me go?" The Green King inquired.

"If I promise to, would you trust me?" Lin Fei responded.

The Green King fell silent. Indeed, even if Lin Fei promised to release him, there was no guarantee. There was nothing binding him.

"I can offer you my bloodline," the Green King said seriously. "You will control my bloodline forever. My life and death will be in your hands. You won't need to worry about any betrayal from me. As long as you promise not to kill me, I'll tell you everything I know!"

He intended to become a servant, just like the Azure Flood Dragon King. Once he acknowledged Lin Fei as his master, Lin Fei would then have the power over his life and death. This was a top-tier king-level exotic beast, one that could not be seen as ordinary.

Lin Fei truly didn't need to be ruthless. The Green King could be a great asset, but of course, that's only if he could control it. If he couldn't, Lin Fei would, without a doubt, eliminate the Green King.

"Fine, hand over your bloodline," Lin Fei agreed.

The Green King finally breathed a sigh of relief. However, when the moment came to part with his bloodline, the Green King hesitated. Once he gave up his bloodline, his fate would be in someone else's hands. He would be enslaved, forever bound.

Yet, he didn't want to die. He was just a regular carp who encountered a fortunate event that allowed him to progress to his current state. If he refused, Lin Fei would undoubtedly kill him. After weighing his options, the Green King gritted his teeth and handed over his bloodline.

Lin Fei extended his hand, gently touching the bloodline, which then merged into him. Instantly, he felt a connection with the Green King. Through this connection, he could annihilate the Green King in an instant, and the King would be powerless against it.

"Well done, Green King. Now, tell me, what kind of fortuitous encounter did you have?" Lin Fei questioned, staring intently at the Green King. "How did you suddenly become a top-tier king-level exotic beast in the vast seas, even more terrifying than many ancient and strange species?"

Lin Fei's gaze was unrelenting. He wanted to uncover the secret of the Green King.

Taking a deep breath, the Green King's expression became complex. Eventually, he slowly began, "It's simple. It's because I encountered the legendary Dragon Gate..."
