518. The Might of Lin Fei's Illusion

True Person Moke's eyes widened in disbelief as he found himself surrounded by an endless expanse of white. Then, the scenery shifted, and he seemed to return to his hometown.

"This must be an illusion," he thought, recognizing the falsehood. Yet, when he saw a familiar figure, his heart jolted. "You're not dead..."

True Person Moke was entranced by the figure, completely caught in the illusion. Lin Fei, unaware of the specific illusion True Person Moke experienced, knew from the moment he deployed the Ten Directions Heavenly Formation that his opponent had fallen into the illusory realm. The illusion exploited the victim's vulnerabilities and weaknesses, making them succumb entirely to its influence.

Lin Fei had complete control over True Person Moke in this state.

"Moke, you are steeped in sin and have no right to live," Lin Fei's voice, like a demonic whisper, infiltrated True Person Moke's ears. True Person Moke shuddered, struggling momentarily before his expression softened, even smiling slightly.

The next moment, True Person Moke self-destructed. Simultaneously, his dozens of battle avatars and countless ordinary avatars all self-destructed at the same time.

"What happened? What's wrong with True Person Moke?"

"His presence seems to have vanished."

"Did True Person Moke die?"

"Quick, contact True Person Moke's avatar in the Divine Realm. What? Can't reach him either, likely dead?"

"Could it be Lin Fei?"

"Did Lin Fei kill a Deity Transformation cultivator, causing a complete demise?"

At that moment, the affairs of True Person Moke stirred a vast controversy. In the Heavenly Principle Realm, True Person Moke actually had many friends. These friends soon realized that all of True Person Moke's avatars, whether ordinary or combat ones, were self-destructing. This was highly abnormal.

Lin Fei used an unknown method to compel True Person Moke to commit suicide! A true deity committing suicide meant that all his avatars would do the same, leading to a complete downfall.

However, whether True Person Moke truly perished remained a subject of debate. After all, no one knew if he had just concealed himself. Yet, when Lin Fei confronted the next Deity Transformation, the truth became clear.

With a "swoosh", Lin Fei's figure flashed and he arrived in the Heavenly Principle Realm, in front of Flying Moon True Person. Flying Moon True Person, a Deity Transformation powerhouse, had thirty-eight combat avatars scattered throughout the Heavenly Principle Realm.

Not too many, but still a formidable Deity Transformation figure. Additionally, his ordinary avatars were also distributed everywhere in the realm. The key moment was Lin Fei's arrival. Under Flying Moon True Person's command were many Martial Kings and Nascent Souls. Lin Fei ignored these Martial Kings and Nascent Souls, focusing his gaze on Flying Moon True Person.

"Lin Fei!" Flying Moon True Person was also very cautious. Upon seeing Lin Fei, he did not summon his avatars. He understood that he was no match for Lin Fei even with his all avatars. Instead, it was better to let the avatars remain dispersed across the Heavenly Principle Realm. This was the best strategy against Lin Fei.

"Martial God Lin, did you slay True Person Moke?" Flying Moon True Person inquired.

Lin Fei looked indifferently at Flying Moon True Person and then spoke calmly, "True Person Moke, like you, was reluctant to leave the Heavenly Principle Realm."

"Unwilling to leave? Then I shall ensure he remains here forever," Lin Fei declared. "The same goes for you!"

With that, Lin Fei pointed a finger.

"Ten Directions Heavenly Array!" A humming sound filled the air. Lin Fei's Saber World instantly enveloped Flying Moon True Person.

Flying Moon True Person shuddered, a hint of bewilderment appearing in his eyes. His spirit, even weaker than that of True Person Moke, easily succumbed.

With effortless ease, Lin Fei plunged Flying Moon True Person into an illusion. Those who witnessed Flying Moon True Person's state stood motionless as if struck by a spell, their eyes wide with disbelief.

"What's happening? What's wrong with Flying Moon True Person?"

"Why is he not moving?"

"Has Flying Moon True Person been sealed or suppressed?"

"It doesn't seem so; it's like he's fallen into an illusion."

"An illusion? "Heart Demon Martial God?"

The mention of an illusion immediately brought to mind the Heart Demon Martial God. However, Lin Fei was alone; the Heart Demon Martial God had not accompanied him. Moreover, how could the Heart Demon Martial God contend with all of Flying Moon True Person's avatars? Nevertheless, they were about to witness something truly terrifying.

Approximately fifteen minutes later, the expression on Flying Moon True Person's face gradually relaxed, his eyes clouded with confusion. Seeing this, Lin Fei knew that Flying Moon True Person's spirit was now under his control.

"Flying Moon True Person, you are deeply sinful and unworthy to live," Lin Fei stated dispassionately. "Commit suicide."

This directive mirrored the one given to True Person Moke. And indeed, Flying Moon True Person did as Lin Fei commanded. With a loud "bang," he self-destructed.

Not only this avatar but also the remaining thirty-seven combat avatars and countless ordinary avatars exploded simultaneously. Even in the Divine Realm, the avatars of Flying Moon True Person hidden in various places self-destructed at the same moment.

Flying Moon True Person was dead, completely and utterly fallen.

At this moment, everyone stared in stunned silence at the unfolding scene before them. They were finally convinced that True Person Moke hadn't merely disappeared, but had died, just like Flying Moon True Person. Most likely, both had perished at the hands of Lin Fei.

The method Lin Fei used to slay Flying Moon True Person and True Person Moke wasn't traversing the void or laboriously seeking out each avatar. Instead, it was illusion, controlling the mind, compelling these Deity Transformation powerhouses to take their own lives! Once a Deity Transformation powerhouse contemplated suicide, who could stop them? Thus, even Deity Transformations fell.

Numerous Martial Kings and Nascent Soul cultivators witnessed this event. Lin Fei glanced at them, unconcerned about these Martial Kings and Nascent Souls. His figure vanished instantly, leaving no trace. These Martial Kings and Nascent Soul cultivators wouldn't hinder his collection of the world's origin. After all, they weren't qualified to harvest it.

Seeing Lin Fei's disappearance, the many Martial Kings and Nascent Soul cultivators exchanged glances, shock evident in their eyes. Without hesitation, they decided not to linger in the Heavenly Principle Realm any longer. It was too perilous; while they could possibly reap some benefits, the likelihood of being crushed was greater. After all, even Deity Transformations had fallen!

As the numerous Martial Kings and Nascent Souls departed, the news of Lin Fei slaying True Person Moke and Flying Moon True Person spread rapidly. Many Nascent Soul cultivators and Martial Kings had seen it with their own eyes; there could be no mistake.

Furthermore, it was said that Lin Fei had used illusions to control the minds of True Person Moke and Flying Moon True Person, leading the Deity Transformation powerhouses to suicide. Only in this way could two Deity Transformation powerhouses fall in such a short time. Otherwise, even True Monarchs couldn't achieve this. Lin Fei could only have done it this way!

Some even thought of Shen. Some top Martial Gods and Deity Transformations knew of Shen's existence and its potent mind control. But upon investigation, they discovered that Shen too had disappeared, presumably fallen.

Interestingly, before Shen's disappearance, Lin Fei and Heart Demon Martial God had visited his domain, Shen's territory. Suddenly, the "truth" seemed crystal clear.
