684. The Clash Between Lin Fei and the Doomsday Supreme!

"Lin Fei?" The Clairvoyant Supreme felt a tightening in his heart. He hadn't expected the Doomsday Supreme's objective to be Lin Fei, not the Chaos Pearl. But why did the Doomsday Supreme want to meet Lin Fei?

"If I may ask, Doomsday Supreme, do you have business with Supreme Lin?" the Clairvoyant Supreme inquired.

The Doomsday Supreme replied with a half-smile, "Clairvoyant Supreme, are you aware that many have paid a great price for me to kill Supreme Lin?"

"Is that so? I suspect it's not just about dealing with Supreme Lin, am I also on the list?" the Clairvoyant Supreme was not surprised. It was quite normal for other Supremes to invite the Doomsday Supreme; after all, within the ranks of the Supremes, he is considered the Invincible Supreme. If the Doomsday Supreme took action, failure was out of the question.

"Indeed, some have asked me to kill you and Lin Fei," the Doomsday Supreme admitted with a slight smile. "However, those individuals are fixated on the Chaos Pearl and cannot offer anything of equivalent value. As for Lin Fei... I actually wish to meet him myself. After all these years, very few Supremes still interest me, and Lin Fei is one of them. Besides, you needn't worry. In Kunyu City, who would dare to make a move? Even I would not dare!"

The Doomsday Supreme spoke sincerely, and the Clairvoyant Supreme did not feel that he was being deceived—there was simply no need. In the entire chaos, if there was anyone the Doomsday Supreme needed to be wary of, it was undoubtedly only the Masters of Chaos. No Supreme could make the Doomsday Supreme feel apprehensive. Hence, it was beneath him to lie; there was no point.

The Clairvoyant Supreme thought for a moment before nodding, "If the Doomsday Supreme wishes to meet Supreme Lin, you'll have to wait a bit. I'll contact Supreme Lin and get back to you."

"Very well, feel free to ask," the Doomsday Supreme replied, waiting patiently.

Thus, the Clairvoyant Supreme immediately sent a message to Lin Fei. At that moment, Lin Fei was in the task hall. He hadn't found any tasks related to Chaotic Behemoths.

His communicator began to vibrate. Picking it up, he saw it was a message from the Clairvoyant Supreme. Since their last parting, the Clairvoyant Supreme had hardly been in contact with Lin Fei. A sudden message now certainly meant something was up.

"The Doomsday Supreme wants to meet me?" A flicker of light passed through Lin Fei's eyes.

The Doomsday Supreme! This name was all too familiar. Lin Fei had heard it countless times. The Doomsday Supreme was acclaimed as the foremost under the Chaos, dubbed the Invincible Supreme. Even the Dark Demon Lord had sought the Doomsday Supreme's intervention to kill Lin Fei.

Lin Fei knew he had offended too many Supremes, especially during the battle for the Chaos Pearl. Countless factions wanted him dead, which was to be expected. Publicly, aside from the Masters of Chaos, perhaps only the Doomsday Supreme could now slay Lin Fei. Therefore, the arrival of the Doomsday Supreme at this juncture was particularly delicate.

But that was the Lin Fei of the past when his Eternal Realm was only at 11%. Back then, he might not have withstood the Doomsday Supreme. However, now his Eternal Realm had reached 51% of its cultivation progress and had even transformed again. Lin Fei felt that no Supreme could stand in his way now, not even the Doomsday Supreme. This was absolute confidence!

Now that the Doomsday Supreme had sought him out, Lin Fei was curious to see if the Doomsday Supreme was as legendary as rumored.

With that thought, Lin Fei promptly responded.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei transformed into a streak of light, heading straight for the Clairvoyant Supreme's residence.

"Ah? Supreme Lin has responded. Doomsday Supreme, Supreme Lin has agreed to meet!" the Clairvoyant Supreme said with a slightly odd expression but upon reflection, it seemed expected. He had come to understand Lin Fei's character quite well—an extremely proud individual. Even facing the Doomsday Supreme, Lin Fei, deep down, held no particular concern.

Now that the Doomsday Supreme had approached him, why would Lin Fei refuse?

"Good, then I will wait here," the Doomsday Supreme nodded, settling into a calm demeanor, and closing his eyes to wait quietly.

"Whoosh."

It wasn't long before a figure appeared in the courtyard.

"Clairvoyant Supreme."

"Supreme Lin."

Upon seeing this figure, the Clairvoyant Supreme's eyes immediately sparkled with an unusual gleam. Simultaneously, the Doomsday Supreme also opened his eyes abruptly.

"Swish."

Their gazes met, and even in the void, it seemed as if they clashed silently.

For the first time, the Doomsday Supreme felt pressure—yes, for the first time facing another Supreme, the Doomsday Supreme felt pressure. This was the first time he felt this way since his power had increased.

"Supreme Lin, it seems I have underestimated you... I never expected you to reach this stage. It's unbelievable. I thought only through unique circumstances could someone achieve this step. It appears I have underestimated the Chaos itself..."

A formidable aura emanated from the Doomsday Supreme. As this aura manifested, the Clairvoyant Supreme suddenly realized he was trembling all over, an uncontrollable fear gripping him.

Indeed, it was fear. The Clairvoyant Supreme was also a top-tier Supreme, and in this world, only the Masters of Chaos had ever instilled fear in him. That used to be the case, at least. But now, the aura from the Doomsday Supreme also evoked fear in him, a presence he knew he couldn't contend with. This was the true essence of an Invincible Supreme!

Lin Fei felt the aura of the Doomsday Supreme as well. "What a Doomsday Supreme," he acknowledged. "Truly worthy of the title Invincible Supreme, such an aura indeed far surpasses that of ordinary Supremes. Even the foremost Supremes are no match in front of the Doomsday Supreme. This is entirely another level of existence."

Lin Fei sensed the difference between the Doomsday Supreme and other Supremes. Although they seemed all to be Supremes, the difference was immense. Only a further transformation would allow him to match the Doomsday Supreme, not to the level of a Master of Chaos, but to that unique tier of Invincible Supremes that the Doomsday Supreme occupied, a fact unknown to many.

Now, Lin Fei understood. However, he felt only a faint threat, not fear. Because he had already transformed. When his Eternal Realm reached 51% cultivation progress, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm had undergone a transformation.

A vibration began to emanate from Lin Fei, and even the shadow of the Eternal Realm faintly appeared behind him. As the shadow of the Eternal Realm emerged, a similarly terrifying aura appeared around Lin Fei. The two formidable auras collided in the void, seemingly distorting the very fabric of space itself.

For the first time, a look of surprise appeared on the face of the Doomsday Supreme.
