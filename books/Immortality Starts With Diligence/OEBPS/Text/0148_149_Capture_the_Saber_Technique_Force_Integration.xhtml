149. "Capture" the Saber Technique, Force Integration!

Outside of Chiyang Prefecture.

Four Taoists looked at the fiery Chiyang Prefecture. Vaguely, there were still bursts of killing shouts. They knew that Chiyang Prefecture was done for and the Paradise Sect was also finished. Suppressing this rebellion was just a matter of time.

"Fellow Taoists, this time the royal family actually invited the 'Immortal Slayer Saber' to quell the rebellion. It seems that we have lost this time. Great Chang can also take a breather for now, which is not good for our major immortal sects."

"That's right, the biggest reason for this failure is the appearance of 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei."

"This 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei, first slayed a Qi cultivator of He Mountain Dao, then killed my top Qi cultivator of Guardian Primordial Mountain, now disrupting the major events of our immortal sects."

"Lin Fei is from Nine Profound Sect. And the Nine Profound Sect has always been very close to the royal family,"

"This Lin Fei must be removed. Otherwise, in the future, he will still ruin our big plans."

"However, Lin Fei is the 'Immortal Slayer Saber', even Yuan Gangzi, a top Qi Condensation cultivator, was killed by him with one saber. Even if there are a few more top Qi Condensation cultivators, they may not be his opponents."

"Do we have to ask for Qi Condensation perfection or a Foundation Establishment True Person to come out?"

For a while, the four Taoists also fell into silence.

Qi Condensation perfection was impossible to come out of. No matter what happened, nothing was as important as Qi Condensation perfection cultivator establishing their foundation. So, the Qi Condensation perfection cultivator cannot come down the mountain.

As for Foundation Establishment True Person, that's even less likely to come down the mountain. The True Persons were waiting for the royal family's Supreme Elder to pass away. If the True Person moved, in case the royal family's Supreme Elder took a few True Persons down with him before he died, that would be in trouble. No sect could afford such a loss.

So, Foundation Establishment True Person cannot come down the mountain. At least, before the death of the royal family's Supreme Elder, there couldn't be a Foundation Establishment True Person coming down the mountain.

"Fellow Taoists, don't forget, we are Qi cultivators! Our strength as Qi cultivators is not just spells, talismans, and magical artifacts, but also arrays! Our Guardian Primordial Mountain has an array called the Five Directions Killing Array! If five top Qi cultivators set up the array together, below Foundation Establishment, death is certain!"

For a while, the eyes of the four Taoists flickered with excitement. It seemed that they were all weighing the situation.

"Our Guardian Primordial Mountain can dispatch a top Qi cultivator. After all, Lin Fei killed Elder Yuan Gangzi. This is a great revenge, and it must be avenged!"

"He Mountain Dao has sealed the mountain, but they should be able to be contacted. Given the current situation of Great Chang, the royal family's Supreme Elder can't do anything to He Mountain Dao."

"Lin Fei stepped on He Mountain Dao to achieve the reputation of 'Immortal Slayer Saber'. They should be able to send a top Qi Condensation cultivator."

"We, Purple Sun Mountain, can also send a top Qi Condensation cultivator."

"Our Mysterious Sky Cave can also send a top Qi Condensation cultivator."

"And we, Taiyi Sect, can also send a top Qi Condensation cultivator."

"Good, in this way, the Five Directions Killing Array can be formed. Now we will return to our sects first. As quickly as three days or as slow as ten days, there will surely be top Qi Condensation cultivators coming down. By then, Lin Fei must die!"

The four Taoists did not stay any longer. They quickly dispersed and moved far away from Chiyang Prefecture.

......

Longshan City was the last city occupied by the rebels because they had nowhere to go. Therefore, this time the rebels held the city in defense.

In order to survive, the rebels also began to fight desperately. This caused great losses to the official army.

In the end, Li Yuhu invited Lin Fei. Then, Lin Fei swung his saber, generating two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy. Just like in Chiyang Prefecture, the city wall collapsed instantly.

As a result, Longshan City was naturally retaken by the royal army. The last force of the rebels was also annihilated. At this point, the entire Paradise Sect was annihilated. The rebellion was completely suppressed.

Starting from the day Lin Fei arrived at Chiyang Prefecture, it took only five days. The army completely suppressed the rebellion.

"'Immortal Slayer Saber' Senior Lin."

At this time, a young man dressed as a martial artist came to Lin Fei.

"I am passionate about saber techniques. In the world today, when it comes to saber techniques, you are at the top, Senior Lin. I want to become your disciple, I hope you agree."

"Become my disciple?"

Lin Fei looked up and down at the man.

"The perfection stage saber technique 'One Leaf Saber Technique' was captured by your Heavenly Saber Art."

"Your Heavenly Saber Art forcibly integrated the 'One Leaf Saber Technique', improving the practice progress by 1%."

Lin Fei was slightly surprised.

"Capture the saber technique? And it can even increase the progress of practicing the Heavenly Saber Art. Is this characteristic that appears after the Heavenly Saber Art reaches perfection?"

Lin Fei immediately reacted. Once the Heavenly Saber Art reached perfection, its nature was quite domineering, directly capturing other saber techniques to enhance its own foundation.

Although the Heavenly Saber Art was already at perfection, the practice progress could still be improved. And it was improved through this technique of forcibly "capturing" other saber techniques.

Once the practice progress reached 100% again, there was a high probability that the Heavenly Saber Art would generate saber intent! At that time, Lin Fei would have two types of saber intent. Even the two types of saber intent might merge.

To what extent would the practice progress of the saber intent improve then? For a moment, many thoughts flashed through Lin Fei's mind.

"Senior?" Seeing that Lin Fei didn't respond, the martial artist couldn't help but carefully reminded him again.

"Are you practicing the One Leaf Saber Technique?" Lin Fei asked.

The man was slightly startled and then nodded, "Senior has keen eyes, I am indeed practicing the One Leaf Saber Technique. And, I have already practiced it to perfection..."

"I understand."

After Lin Fei finished speaking, he turned around and walked away directly.

"If you want to practice the saber, go to the Nine Profound Sect. I do not take disciples."

Lin Fei's voice came from afar. He had already walked far away. The man could only stare blankly at Lin Fei's retreating figure.

Lin Fei arrived at the government office and found General Li Yuhu.

"General Li, the rebellion has been completely suppressed, and I should return to Chang City."

"Master Lin is leaving?"

Li Yuhu was slightly startled. Since Lin Fei arrived, he found that fighting didn't seem difficult at all. It was even quite simple.

When encountering rebels defending the city, Lin Fei made a move, one saber strike, and the city wall was broken. When encountering tough nuts to crack, Lin Fei made a move, one saber strike, and all rebel leaders were killed.

With such a powerhouse present, Li Yuhu became someone who picked up the credit.

"I have some things to do back in Chang City. General Li, we shall part here."

After saying that, Lin Fei turned around and left. The system's voice just now in his mind made Lin Fei vaguely understand a direction.

The way to further improve Heavenly Saber Art was to find some saber practitioners. Especially powerful saber practitioners. Preferably those who have perfected the saber technique.

In this way, Lin Fei's "Heavenly Saber Art" could continue to "capture" those martial arts, to merge and increase its foundation.

Someday, if he practiced the saber intent and merged it with the current saber intent, then Lin Fei's saber intent might increase instantly. It could even reach the realm of perfection!

This was the domineering of Heavenly Saber Art. Lin Fei finally understood the phrase in Heavenly Saber Art.

"When the Heavenly Saber appears, all other sabers bow!"

Once the Heavenly Saber reaches perfection, it could almost suppress all lower-level saber techniques.

Lin Fei left Longshan City. He rushed towards Chang City all the way. This time he helped the imperial court suppress the Paradise Sect rebellion. Once he returned to Chang City, he could watch the martial arts secret records. At that time, whether what Liu Yibai said was true or false, Lin Fei could tell by himself.

As Lin Fei got closer to Chang City, there were fewer and fewer refugees.

Lin Fei was a bit strange. Even if the court suppressed the rebellion, refugees had already become refugees. It was impossible to disappear all at once. Even when Lin Fei arrived at a bamboo forest, it was very quiet around. There was not a single refugee.

"Something is wrong!"

Lin Fei suddenly stopped. His eyes were keenly looking around. But it was already too late!

"Hum".

The next moment, the environment around turned blood-red all at once. It had suddenly become a bloody space here and enveloped Lin Fei instantly.
