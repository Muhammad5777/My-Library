548. Particle Enhancement by Dozens of Times!

Lin Fei had devised a plan. Acquiring world laws from True Monarch-level worlds was challenging due to their stability. Finding a True Monarch-level world on the brink of collapse and destruction wasn't easy, especially in the short term.

Obtaining world laws from the Divine Realm was much more feasible for Lin Fei because he still had thunder tribulations to undergo.

Each time he passed a thunder tribulation, Lin Fei could acquire some world laws. He had only undergone six tribulations so far, and the next was the seventh. He was confident that he could withstand the nine heavenly thunders of the seventh tribulation, thereby acquiring nine Divine Realm world laws.

This could potentially trigger a transformation in his Saber World, elevating it to the True Monarch level. With this in mind, Lin Fei decided not to hesitate any longer. To undergo the tribulation, he first needed to enhance his physical particles to their optimal state.

With a substantial amount of world origin at his disposal, including that from the True Monarch-level world, which was dozens of times more potent than that from minor worlds, he wondered how much his physical body could be enhanced.

Unable to predict the outcome, Lin Fei took out the world's origin. With a single swallow, he ingested the streams of world origin, merging them into the particles of his entire body.

"Boom!"

Lin Fei's body seethed as his physical particles surged in power. Each particle enhanced exponentially – doubling, tripling, quadrupling, and so on – until they were enhanced by twenty times or more. This extraordinary enhancement meant Lin Fei's overall strength had increased by far more than just twenty-fold.

Feeling ready, Lin Fei opened his eyes, which sparkled with keen sharpness. He then immediately proceeded to the location designated for undergoing tribulations. Upon his arrival, the people there, now accustomed to his frequent tribulations despite it being just a few months since his last, acknowledged his formidable ability to face the seventh thunder tribulation with ease.

"True Monarch Lin, you're here to undergo tribulation again?"

"Do we have a spare spot?"

"Of course, True Monarch, please follow me," a Martial God at the site guided Lin Fei to the largest array for tribulation.

As Lin Fei entered the array, word spread quickly throughout the Ancient City that he was undertaking another tribulation. Those who missed witnessing him withstand the nine heavenly thunders last time rushed to the site, eager not to miss the chance again. The area was soon packed, both inside and out.

Even though many already knew that Lin Fei was likely to succeed in his tribulation without much trouble, they were still keen to witness it. Their anticipation centered on whether Lin Fei could continue to withstand the nine heavenly thunders.

"Will True Monarch Lin be able to withstand nine heavenly thunders this time?"

"Each increase in the thunder tribulation brings a significant jump in difficulty, especially the seventh one."

"Indeed, while the seventh tribulation doesn't represent a qualitative change, the power of the heavenly thunders certainly jumps to a higher level."

"I have no doubt True Monarch Lin will pass the seventh tribulation, but whether he can withstand nine thunders again is uncertain."

"The last time, True Monarch Lin experienced the benefits of withstanding nine thunders. If he's facing the tribulation again, why would he give up such an opportunity? I'm sure he will withstand all nine."

"That's not certain. I believe he can withstand seven thunders, but nine is much more challenging."

"People are right to say it's not easy, but I have faith in True Monarch Lin. He wouldn't rush into the seventh tribulation without being fully prepared."

As the crowd debated, Lin Fei, undisturbed by the external noise, remained calm. Despite the increased power of the seventh tribulation, he felt confident due to his significant enhancement. The seventh tribulation seemed trivial compared to his newfound strength.

"Let's begin," Lin Fei declared, raising his head to perceive the sea of origin in the void. His consciousness plunged into this primordial source, and he instantly seized seven world laws.

"Boom, boom, boom!"

The seven heavenly thunders descended almost simultaneously, their terrifying power seemingly capable of turning the entire Ancient City to dust.

The onlookers were stunned, hardly believing what they saw. Seven thunders had fallen at the same time, indicating that Lin Fei had claimed seven laws in one go. Could Lin Fei withstand seven thunders simultaneously?

This was unprecedented, challenging many Martial Gods' understanding of tribulation. Typically, facing even the first thunder was a cautious and fearful endeavor, but Lin Fei, in his seventh tribulation, had summoned seven thunders at once. It was beyond belief.

"Is True Monarch Lin intentionally drawing down seven heavenly thunders at once?"

"Summoning seven thunders so confidently, he must be extremely sure of himself. But can he really withstand them with such ease? And let's not forget the eighth and ninth thunders that follow. Isn't he afraid of getting injured and failing to withstand those?"

"If True Monarch Lin is doing this, he must be absolutely confident in his abilities."

"But withstanding seven thunders at once is an incredibly difficult feat..."

The crowd watched intently, eager to see how Lin Fei would handle the seven simultaneous thunders. The thunders didn't merge into one but followed in quick succession, giving no room to breathe.

Lin Fei looked at the descending thunders indifferently. He didn't even deploy his Saber World, allowing the thunders to strike him directly. His physical particles had been enhanced by over twenty times; the power he wielded now was unimaginable. What were mere seven thunders to him?

"Boom, boom, boom!"

The first six thunders struck Lin Fei without causing any significant harm. His particles weren't annihilated in the slightest, meaning the six thunders had no effect on him. He was unscathed!

It was only the seventh thunder, which was several times more powerful, that caused some damage, affecting about ten percent of his particles. But for Lin Fei, such damage was negligible. With a mere thought, his particles recovered almost instantly.

The crowd was astounded, their mouths agape. Lin Fei's physical strength had exceeded the imagination of all Martial Gods present. His resilience and recovery were beyond anything they had ever seen or expected.
