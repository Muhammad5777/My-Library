366. Progresses Rapidly

Lin Fei knew that every technique had its own unique character. The stronger the technique, the more distinctive it would be. To persuade these techniques to join him, there was only one way: to subdue them or let them recognize his potential. Therefore, Lin Fei immediately demonstrated his Saber Domain, enveloping the technique with it.

A perfect Saber Domain was rare. Lin Fei believed that even if this saber bloodline technique was proud, it would realize that finding another with Lin Fei's level of saber talent would be almost impossible. Hence, Lin Fei was confident.

"Supreme Saber Dao requests to become your martial art."

As expected, a voice echoed in Lin Fei's mind.

"Agreed." Lin Fei, of course, accepted.

In the next moment, there was a rumble in Lin Fei's mind. His Martial King bloodline seemed to start refining. All the saber techniques, including the "Dual-World Saber" Divine Ability that Lin Fei had practiced before, emerged in his mind.

The system's voice rang out.

"Your Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline has refined your Martial King bloodline. Due to your bloodline containing saber Dao Divine Abilities and countless saber techniques, the progress of the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline has increased by 100%. Your Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline has broken through to Mastery."

Lin Fei was taken aback. He had only just begun practicing the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline, and now it had already achieved mastery? He quickly accessed the system to verify.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Early Martial King

Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline: Mastery (Progress 6%)

Saber Domain: Perfection (Progress 40%)

Indeed, his status now included the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline. Within him was a bloodline that was distinct from before, a purely saber-oriented bloodline. His previous bloodline was still present, but it had fused with his physical being, making it his foundation and impossible to cultivate further. The only way forward was to extract and cultivate more pure bloodlines from it.

Moreover, Lin Fei noticed that his Saber Domain's cultivation progress had increased by a whopping 40%. Given that his Saber Domain was already at perfection, a further 40% improvement indicated that he might soon evolve it into a Saber World!

Naturally, this wasn't an unexpected boon. In order for the Saber Domain to evolve into the Saber World, one must cultivate the Saber bloodline. The two complemented each other perfectly. Lin Fei's cultivation of the saber bloodline was a win-win.

Feeling the saber bloodline within, Lin Fei realized that once he fully unleashed this bloodline, his saber attacks would reach an incredibly fearsome level. Yet, the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline was merely at the mastery level. This wasn't enough. He needed to cultivate it to perfection before cultivating a second bloodline.

"To elevate the saber bloodline, besides comprehension, one could forcibly seize another saber bloodline," he thought. "However, such bloodlines are rare. Moreover, if one isn't careful in suppressing the will of the acquired bloodline, a bloodline conflict could arise, leading to death."

Lin Fei pondered for a moment. Unless there was no other way, he wouldn't choose the path of seizing another saber bloodline. All he could do now was to rely on his own comprehension to enhance the saber bloodline.

"Wait, there are Divine Abilities within the saber bloodline. Could integrating other saber Divine Abilities boost the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline?"

An idea struck him. However, he needed to test it out.

Lin Fei entered the Dubhe Dao Artifact's Martial Sovereign level space. There were numerous martial arts Divine Abilities.

Lin Fei sought out the saber Divine Ability manuals. As he channeled his thoughts, he activated his internal saber bloodline. Instantly, it surged within him, expanding his body rapidly. An intense aura emanated from him as if he'd turned into an unparalleled treasure saber.

At that moment, Lin Fei's True Martial King Form was that of a saber—a saber that could cut through the heavens. This was the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline!

Facing the saber Divine Abilities below, Lin Fei declared, "I have cultivated the saber bloodline. Saber Divine Abilities willing to fuse with my bloodline for eternity, in a perfected state, come forth!"

For these saber Divine Abilities, Lin Fei's Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline was incredibly enticing. Using his perfected Saber Domain, the saber's Divine Abilities on the shelves became restless. They began to surge towards him, answering his call.

One by one, the saber Divine Abilities transformed into phantoms, merging directly into Lin Fei's body and combining with his Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline. These Divine Abilities were exceptional since they were collected by the True Immortal Dubhe. Each one had its unique characteristics. They ranged from Great Divine Abilities to even top-tier Great Divine Abilities. Lesser Divine Abilities were notably absent; perhaps, to the True Immortal Dubhe, they were not worth collecting. As these saber Divine Abilities integrated, each one enhanced the cultivation progress of Lin Fei's Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline.

However, the extent of the progress varied. Some Divine Abilities significantly advanced the cultivation, while others added just a bit. This discrepancy was due to the compatibility with Lin Fei's saber bloodline. His Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline was the epitome of sheer offensive power and dominance. Some Divine Abilities, even if top-tier but more holistic in nature rather than purely offensive, contributed minimally to his bloodline's progress. In contrast, even ordinary Great Divine Abilities, if purely offensive, would considerably boost Lin Fei's Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline.

In essence, the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline was selective, integrating only the most synergistic Divine Abilities while filtering out the rest. As more and more saber Divine Abilities were assimilated, the number of Divine Abilities within Lin Fei's bloodline increased proportionally.

Should Lin Fei have descendants in the future, those inheriting his Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline would likely manifest a variety of saber Divine Abilities due to the sheer volume integrated. Depending on the Divine Abilities they awaken, their powers might differ, underscoring the potency of Lin Fei's lineage. After all, how many bloodlines could incorporate such an array of Divine Abilities?

"Your Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline has reached 100% in cultivation progress, breaking through to Transcendence!”

Finally, Lin Fei's inner Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline had advanced once again, reaching the realm of transcendence. However, after reaching transcendence, he fused with a few more saber Divine Abilities and then stopped. He had exhausted all the saber Divine Abilities at hand. While Dubhe, the true immortal, had gathered many saber Divine Abilities, all of which were of Great Divine Ability level or above, they were still finite in number.

Who could have thought that someone could use saber Divine Abilities to forcibly upgrade their saber bloodline? It was a challenging endeavor and often thankless. Becoming a Martial King allowed one to continue cultivating Divine Abilities, but to integrate them into the bloodline and elevate it, the Divine Abilities had to be perfected.

The immense effort required to cultivate a Divine Ability to perfection and then merge it could only slightly enhance the cultivation progress of the bloodline. For a Martial King, this was hardly worthwhile.

But Lin Fei was an exception. He could let those saber Divine Abilities integrate into his bloodline at the "Perfection realm" without any effort or time spent on cultivation.

Lin Fei glanced at his system panel again.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Early Martial King

Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline: Transcendence (Progress: 21%)

Saber Domain: Perfection (Progress: 80%)

With the Saber Domain's cultivation progress at 80%, when the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline reached perfection, the Saber Domain would also evolve into the Saber World.

"Out of saber Divine Abilities…" Lin Fei murmured to himself, an idea forming in his mind. He needed to return to the Ancient Continent. Only there could he continuously acquire Saber Divine Abilities, allowing him to perfect his Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline and potentially evolve the Saber World. The Great Chang was not a place for long-term cultivation. To reach higher realms, he needed to return to the Ancient Continent, the center for all cultivators.

With this decision made, Lin Fei rose, leaving the secret chamber and heading towards the main hall of the Nine Profound Sect.
