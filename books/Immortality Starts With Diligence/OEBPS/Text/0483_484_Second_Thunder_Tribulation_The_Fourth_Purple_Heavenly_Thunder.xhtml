484. Second Thunder Tribulation, The Fourth Purple Heavenly Thunder!

The news of Lin Fei facing his second thunder tribulation spread rapidly. The fact that he was attempting to transcend tribulations twice in such a short period naturally caught the attention of many Deity Transformation and Martial Gods. The community buzzed with various speculations and discussions.

"How long has it been since Martial God Lin of the Great Dream Sect transcended his first tribulation? Less than a month!"

"Such a short time to face two consecutive tribulations... What kind of confidence does he have?"

"But considering he withstood three heavenly thunders and captured three laws during his first tribulation, Martial God Lin is no ordinary Martial God. He must be sure of himself to do this."

"Surviving three heavenly thunders in the first tribulation is a sign of a potential Martial Ancestor!"

"How could his second tribulation be weaker than the first? Let's see how many heavenly thunders Martial God Lin can withstand this time and how many laws he can capture."

Many were intrigued to see whether Lin Fei truly had the confidence for his second tribulation or if it was a rushed and arrogant decision.

Unaware of the outside discussions, Lin Fei was completely immersed in his own physical state. He was acutely aware of his body's condition.

"Let's begin," he focused his mind. His consciousness perceived the vast sea of origin in the void.

"Boom!"

The first heavenly thunder rolled down mightily, striking Lin Fei's body with full force. His physical particles rapidly disintegrated under the thunder's impact.

However, Lin Fei did not utilize the power of his particle worlds; he chose to withstand it purely with his physical strength. After all, his body had absorbed world origin and enhanced several-fold, far surpassing the physical form of an ordinary Martial God.

Despite the strength of the first heavenly thunder, Lin Fei's particles, fortified by the world origin, were significantly stronger. He endured, and his body sustained damage to nearly two-thirds before consuming the first heavenly thunder, also capturing the first world law.

Lin Fei's goal was not just to capture a single law or merely survive the tribulation. His lost physical strength quickly recovered. At his current realm, recovery took only a short time, but the lingering power of heavenly thunder within his body would prevent complete restoration. This was the challenging aspect of the thunder tribulation.

"Boom!"

Another heavenly thunder struck down, and Lin Fei captured the second world law. This second thunder was significantly more ferocious than the first, its destructive aura pervading the void. It descended instantaneously, giving Lin Fei no time to dodge. The thunder struck him, annihilating a third of his body's particles on impact. The power of the four hundred particle worlds within him was fully activated.

"Boom."

The force from the four hundred particle worlds erupted, overwhelming the second heavenly thunder. Despite its destructive power, the second thunder paled in comparison to the might of Lin Fei's particle worlds and was promptly extinguished.

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. He had underestimated the power of his four hundred particle worlds and his self-created techniques. His particle world method, once combined, grew exponentially stronger with each additional world. Just four hundred worlds were this powerful; what if he had four thousand or even forty thousand? If he could transform all the particles in his body into particle worlds, considering he had billions of them, the required number of world laws would be unimaginable.

The second heavenly thunder was effortlessly countered by Lin Fei, and without hesitation, the third thunder descended, its power even more immense. It obliterated the force of the four hundred particle worlds in Lin Fei's body, but his Saber World intervened, annihilating the remnants of the thunder's power.

"Three heavenly thunders already," people observed. "Martial God Lin has endured three thunders? That was incredibly fast, even quicker than his first tribulation."

"Yes, so fast. It seems the power of these thunders was different."

"Different power? Try it yourself. Surviving even one is hard enough, let alone three."

"Surviving three thunders in the second tribulation is a feat only top Martial Gods can achieve!"

"Why hasn't Martial God Lin emerged yet? Could he be attempting to withstand the fourth thunder?"

This comment didn't seem to surprise everyone. Many grew silent, realizing Lin Fei hadn't yet come out. There was only one reason for this – the tribulation wasn't over after three thunders.

"Boom!"

As expected, another heavenly thunder descended from the void – the fourth thunder! This thunder was different; it was purple, a change that signified an increase in its might.

Unlike the previous thunders, the fourth purple thunder carried an overwhelming power, causing palpitations in everyone who witnessed it. Clearly, this fourth thunder was far more terrifying than the combined force of the first three.

"Fourth heavenly thunder!" Lin Fei looked up at the descending purple thunder, his expression turning solemn. He could tell that this thunder was unlike the previous ones, possibly underestimating its power. But there was no turning back, no room for retreat.

"Saber World!" Lin Fei instantly activated his Saber World, integrating world laws into it to form a barrier.

"Boom."

The purple thunderstruck, causing Lin Fei's Saber World to tremble before it completely shattered. It tore through the Saber World as if it were an unstoppable force, reducing its power only slightly.

The shattered Saber World could no longer resist the purple thunder, which then struck Lin Fei directly. His ordinary particles were no match for this formidable thunder. All four hundred of his particle worlds erupted in response. Even though these particle worlds were minute and couldn't be considered as complete worlds, lacking even life, their combined power was still earth-shattering. They collided fiercely with the purple thunder.

Lin Fei's physical body was torn apart, and his particles shattered.

10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50%, 60%, 70%, 80%...

When over 80% of his body, almost 90%, was annihilated, the destruction finally ceased. The purple thunder vanished.

Lin Fei had withstood the fourth heavenly thunder!
