62. Secret Discovered

Suddenly, a strange change occurred when Lin Fei's palm was imprinted on the bluestone.

"Crack."

The hard bluestone suddenly split into four or five pieces.

Lin Fei was stunned. He really only used a little bit of inner power. He didn't mean to show off his inner power at all. He was even very cautious.

"Elder, this..." Lin Fei didn't know what to say.

"Swish."

However, the elder suddenly stood up, staring at Lin Fei with sharp eyes, "You have combined three types of inner power?"

The elder of the Transmission Hall had seen many talents. But combining three types of inner power was definitely the top talent among the top talents. There was even hope to achieve the innate realm!

"Elder, I didn't mean to..." Lin Fei did not answer directly. Instead, he frowned. He didn't want to reveal the secret of his three-fold inner power.

Upon hearing this, the elder of the Transmission Hall snorted coldly and said, "It is an advantage to practice without arrogance or impetuosity. But don't always think about hiding your strengths.

Young people should have their own edge. The martial arts path is like sailing against the current, you will retreat if you don't advance.

The sect does not lack top talents with three-in-one inner power, although they are rare, they do exist. What are you hiding?

Nowadays, the trend is getting worse and worse. Many young people are obviously talented, but they still insist on hiding their strengths. They don't realize that the sect has various resources tilted toward top talents.

Even top martial artists, and even innate masters, are willing to take disciples and guide the outstanding disciples of the sect so that they can take fewer detours.

Lin Fei, right? Are you still hiding your strengths now?"

The elder of the Transmission Hall sat down again and stared at Lin Fei.

Lin Fei pondered for a moment, then bowed and said sincerely, "Thank you for the elder's guidance. I do possess triple inner power."

"Which martial arts did you practice?" the elder asked.

"I have practiced the Giant Whale Body Refining Technique, Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique, and Vajra Iron Cloth separately." Lin Fei answered honestly.

"Blood and qi, tendons and bones, skin and membranes, three types of inner power merged into one. No wonder the bluestone shattered," muttered the elder of the transmission hall in a low voice.

Seeing that Lin Fei seemed confused, the elder explained with a smile, "The bluestone is used to test the disciples' inner power. Usually, no matter how strong the inner power is, even at the peak of the acquired level, it cannot break the bluestone.

However, its internal structure is very special. Once someone merges the three types of inner power into one, even if it's just a little bit, that kind of inner power can shatter the bluestone.

So, this is also a method the sect uses to identify some top talents, so as to avoid losing them due to concealing their abilities, and neglecting to cultivate them, which leads to these top talents taking many detours.

Remember, the sect is not your enemy, and you don't need to be wary of it everywhere. On the contrary, the sect is your home and your strongest backing. If a disciple encounters danger or even dies, outside the sect, the law enforcement hall of Nine Profound Sect will investigate it thoroughly and give the disciple justice!"

Lin Fei hurriedly saluted and said, "I understand, Elder."

Upon careful consideration, Lin Fei had never heard any rumors about disciples fighting each other in Nine Profound Sect, at most it was just some healthy competition. Only in this way can the sect maintain its cohesion and truly develop and grow.

"Alright, here's your nameplate. However, you should not cultivate any acquired-level martial arts for the time being. But it's okay to practice combat martial arts.

I will inform the Sect Master of your situation. I believe the Sect Master will arrange for an innate-level expert to take you as their disciple. At that time, there will naturally be an innate-level expert to guide you on how to choose acquired-level martial arts and continue to practice martial arts.

Only in this way can you avoid taking detours and advance in one go, reaching the innate level!"

The elder of the transmission hall spoke much more than usual today, all because Nine Profound Sect had produced a good seedling, and he was itching to see how far Lin Fei could go. Unfortunately, he himself was not an innate-level expert.

There was an unwritten rule in the sect. All those who have triple inner powers could only be accepted as disciples by an innate martial master. This was to avoid delaying the progress of top talents.

For a martial arts sect to become strong, innate martial masters were the key factor. The more innate martial master there were, the stronger the sect would be.

Every disciple who has triple inner powers could be considered a potential innate martial master, and the sect would do its best to train them.

"Thank you, Elder."

"Alright, go."

The elder in charge of transmitting techniques waved his hand, and Lin Fei turned around and left.

Watching Lin Fei's figure disappearing into the distance, the elder in charge of transmitting techniques revealed a smile at the corner of his mouth, "Another good potential disciple who has triple inner power. I wonder which Elder will step forward this time to accept him as a disciple?

Last time, the Elders fought fiercely for such a disciple. Whatever, this matter is still something that the Sect Master has to worry about..."

The elder immediately got up and left the hall, he had to report to the Sect Master himself.

Lin Fei had already left the hall. He remembered the warning from the elder in charge of transmitting techniques.

"Since I cannot practice martial arts at the acquired level, I can choose some combat martial arts to practice."

Lin Fei didn't know when he would be accepted as a disciple by the senior members of the sect and be taught martial arts.

He couldn't practice martial arts at the acquired level before the senior members of the sect made a decision, but it was for his own good, so as not to take the wrong path in the future.

However, he couldn't neglect his combat martial arts. His saber skills were still far from producing saber intent. So, he headed straight to the martial arts pavilion.
