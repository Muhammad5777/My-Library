294. Power of Gravity Domain

Lin Fei practiced the Divine Ability. This caused a sensation throughout the entire Ancient Origin Sect. Numerous disciples and elders flocked to the vicinity of the Gravity Tower.

However, Lin Fei remained unperturbed by the external commotion. At that moment, he had ascended to the ninth floor of the Gravity Tower.

In the past, Lin Fei had ventured into this tower. Step by step, he had conquered each floor, eventually reaching the ninth. Back then, the immense gravitational force had exerted a notable pressure upon him.

But now, as Lin Fei revisited the ninth floor, he found the gravity here to be almost insignificant to him, even trivial. Still, there was gravity, and to Lin Fei's Divine Ability, perhaps this held some significance. Indeed, after spending a short while on the ninth floor of the Gravity Tower, Lin Fei heard the system's voice echo in his mind.

"Host is in an environment with abnormal gravity; your Divine Ability, Gravity Domain, is pleased. Training efficiency has increased."

"Your Divine Ability, Gravity Domain, has advanced by 10%."

Lin Fei had only just arrived on the ninth floor, and already, his Gravity Domain Divine Ability had improved by 10%. He continued to sit cross-legged, waiting in silent anticipation.

Indeed, waiting was all he did. The training was not something Lin Fei had to actively engage in. The Gravity Domain took it upon itself to "work hard" in its cultivation. Even insights were gained autonomously by the Gravity Domain.

In time, Lin Fei would obtain memories of these epiphanies. It felt as if he was the one who had experienced them firsthand.

Lin Fei was reminded of the lore surrounding the Gravity Tower. It was said that this tower was the creation of the Ancient Origin Sect's ancestors, who had invited a Golden Core True Person. They had poured endless effort and resources into its creation.

The Gravity Tower itself was a Golden Core-level magic treasure! Of course, this magic treasure wasn't meant for combat, but to emit gravity and aid in cultivation.

Time ticked away. The longer Lin Fei stayed within the Gravity Tower, the steadier his progress in mastering the Gravity Domain.

10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 90%...

With a resonant "boom", a massive Life Wheel materialized behind Lin Fei. And on it, two radiant layers became visible. These represented his two Divine Abilities. The second layer now shone even brighter, expanding and becoming increasingly luminous.

While every person's Life Wheel was more or less the same size, the more formidable a Divine Ability, the larger the radiance behind the Life Wheel. Thus, the combined luminescence of Lin Fei's two Divine Abilities surpassed even the triad of abilities once possessed by the Worry-free Sovereign. And not just by a little, but by a considerable margin!

At that moment, with the Life Wheel and its two expansive radiances manifesting behind him, Lin Fei emanated an aura of terrifying power.

"Your Divine Ability, Gravity Domain, has advanced to 100%."

"Your Divine Ability, Gravity Domain, has achieved mastery!"

Lin Fei's Divine Ability, Gravity Domain, had broken through. In less than one day, it had already achieved this breakthrough. Moreover, upon reaching mastery, the range of his Gravity Domain skyrocketed, extending even beyond the confines of the Gravity Tower.

A thunderous "rumble" resounded. The might of Lin Fei's Gravity Domain was truly fearsome. Everything within its range was subjected to tremendous gravitational force. As the scope of Lin Fei's Gravity Domain expanded, immediately, massive rocks and the ground outside the Gravity Tower were all ground into fine powder. It felt as if the entire earth trembled.

"Retreat, everyone!" came a shout.

"Hurry, fall back!"

The faces of the Ancient Origin Sect's leader and Wind Sovereign paled instantly. They understood that this was an unconscious display of Lin Fei's Divine Ability during its breakthrough. Such a formidable Divine Ability was clearly a top-tier Great Divine Ability. Even at a distance of a hundred meters from the Gravity Tower, the danger was palpable.

"Mountain Collapse!" Wind Sovereign exclaimed.

A Life Wheel similarly appeared behind him, as he summoned his Divine Ability in an attempt to counter Lin Fei's Gravity Domain.

However, in the next moment, Lin Fei's Gravity Domain engulfed him. Wind Sovereign felt as if he was being pressed down by infinite weight, instantly pinned to the ground. He plummeted from the sky and crashed heavily onto the earth.

A thunderous "crash" echoed as a massive crater formed. Within the crater, Wind Sovereign was a bloodied mess. The sight was truly heartbreaking.

But Wind Sovereign, being a Martial Sovereign, possessed astounding regenerative abilities. Despite being battered, his recovery was frighteningly swift. Relying on his Mountain Collapse Great Divine Ability, he managed to hold on.

A swift "whoosh" signified Lin Fei's emergence from the Gravity Tower. He glanced at the crater where Wind Sovereign lay and seemed at a loss for words.

"Cough... Sovereign Lin, what kind of Divine Ability have you been practicing? Just now, I was caught off guard, and it almost cost me my life."

It was Wind Sovereign who spoke first. At this moment, everyone had witnessed the spectacle, and they were profoundly shaken.

What had they just seen?

The prestigious Wind Sovereign had been flung to the ground by an unintentional release of Sovereign Lin's Divine Ability. He was utterly defenseless.

Even the most naive among them could interpret the implications. It meant that Wind Sovereign was simply no match for Lin Fei. The gap between them was vast.

"Has Sovereign Lin truly become this powerful?"

"And that Divine Ability—it's terrifying. It seems somewhat similar to the Gravity Tower."

"Not just similar, that Divine Ability is gravitation itself. Only, it's ten, perhaps even a hundred times more potent than the gravity inside the tower!"

"This is what a Martial Sovereign's Divine Ability looks like. It's not merely about martial moves anymore."

"It's a wondrous method, rivaling the spells and magic treasures of those Golden Core True Persons."

"Only when one's martial arts have reached this level can it be called true martial arts!"

Witnessing Lin Fei's unintentionally displayed Gravity Domain Divine Ability, the Martial Saints and innate disciples were all filled with admiration and envy.

However, they were also keenly aware of how challenging it is to attain the status of a Martial Sovereign. In the Ancient Origin Sect, only Lin Fei had become a Martial Sovereign in hundreds of years.

"Wind Sovereign, I was practicing my Divine Ability in the Gravity Tower. When my Divine Ability broke through, I couldn't control it. I hope you understand," Lin Fei said, extending his hand to help Wind Sovereign up.

Wind Sovereign looked at Lin Fei with a peculiar gaze, then sighed deeply, "Is this a new top-tier Great Divine Ability? Have you perfected your Black Wind Prison?"

"I went to Corpse Demon Mountain and, with the aid of the Yin Evil Qi there, brought Black Wind Prison to completion. Later, by a stroke of luck, I met a Martial Sovereign from a martial king family and acquired this Divine Ability."

"Corpse Demon Mountain? Indeed, Black Wind Prison is best suited for cultivation there. We must promote this Divine Ability more in the future, making sure to note this. If we choose to cultivate another Divine Ability in the future, we can select Black Wind Prison and then practice at Corpse Demon Mountain."

The leader of the Ancient Origin Sect and Wind Sovereign both had their eyes light up. The challenge in cultivating a Divine Ability is often finding the correct method of practice.

Lin Fei's trip to the Corpse Demon Mountain personally demonstrated that the yin-sha energy was beneficial for the cultivation of Black Wind Prison. Future Martial Sovereigns who cultivate Black Wind Prison will now have a specific method to follow, ensuring much more efficient results.

Of course, Lin Fei didn't mention that his cultivation at Corpse Demon Mountain was due to the Divine Ability's own effort and understanding. For an average Martial Sovereign, even if they practiced Black Wind Prison, the benefits of cultivating at Corpse Demon Mountain might be limited.

"Sect Leader, I've been using the Gravity Tower's gravitational force for my practice. After the recent breakthrough, its effects aren't as pronounced. How is the gravity in the Gravity Tower produced?"

Lin Fei needed a more potent gravitational force to cultivate his Gravity Domain. The Gravity Tower could no longer offer much assistance to Lin Fei. At most, it could aid in reaching Transcendence. For achieving perfection, relying solely on the Gravity Tower would likely prove very challenging!
