497. Heart Demon Martial God's Message, Lin Fei's Decision

In the secret chamber of Ancient City, Lin Fei sat immersed in his cultivation. "Hum," resonated from his body as one thousand three hundred particle worlds rose within him, each emitting a brilliant radiance distinct from the others.

"I've done it, created another five hundred particle worlds, making a total of one thousand three hundred!" Lin Fei thought with a sense of accomplishment. "And with my five world laws integrated into the Saber World, I feel incredibly powerful."

A gleam of sharpness flashed in his eyes. Lin Fei felt an unprecedented strength coursing through him, a power he had never experienced before. He knew he could undertake the fourth thunder tribulation immediately and easily withstand at least two thunderbolts, perhaps even test his might against the third.

However, Lin Fei was not content with just enduring two or three thunderbolts. He aspired for more laws – four, five, or even six world laws. His quest for more Saber Worlds and World Origins continued.

"System," Lin Fei murmured softly. It had been a while since he heard the system's voice, mainly because his recent progress in cultivation had relied more on undergoing tribulations than on specific techniques.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Martial God (Three Thunder Tribulations)

Great Dream Heart Sutra: Second Layer (1% progress)

Four Symbols Five Elements Creation Technique: One Thousand Three Hundred Particle Worlds

Lin Fei glanced over the information. His particle worlds now totaled one thousand three hundred, and the Great Dream Heart Sutra was only at the second layer. Initially, he had thought the sutra's ability to manipulate time in dreams might be useful, but he realized that long periods of arduous cultivation were no longer necessary for him.

Thus, he had not been actively working to earn contribution points or exchange for the third layer of the Great Dream Heart Sutra. The second layer seemed sufficient for his current needs.

As Lin Fei prepared to venture to the Great Dream City in search of clues about Saber World, a humming sound broke the silence. He received a mysterious transmission.

"A transmission?" Lin Fei mused, his brow furrowed in curiosity. During his extensive sojourn in the Divine Realm, this was the inaugural occurrence of receiving such a message. After all, his "friends" in the Divine Realm were few and far between. Who could be reaching out to him so unexpectedly?

Lin Fei inspected the message, and his expression morphed into one of bemusement. The sender was beyond his wildest anticipations, astonishingly unexpected.

"Mi Tian, the Heart Demon Martial God?"

It was inconceivable to Lin Fei that the sender would be Mi Tian. Especially since Lin Fei had only recently "snatched" from Mi Tian, reaping five Saber Worlds. Yet, the Heart Demon Martial God seemed utterly unfazed.

The message spoke of a joint venture to seize a minor world and claim its origin. Furthermore, it was North Wind True Person himself extending the invitation to the True Spirit Realm.

Lin Fei did not respond immediately. Instead, he journeyed to the Great Dream City. There, he began to discreetly gather intel about the True Spirit Realm and North Wind True Person.

Soon enough, Lin Fei garnered information. This was no secret. The struggle for the True Spirit Realm had been protracted. Various powers had their eyes fixed on it.

Hence, North Wind True Person was often associated with the True Spirit Realm. The reason was straightforward. North Wind True Person held sway over the True Spirit Realm. Or more precisely, his incarnations occupied its strategic locales.

The conquest of the True Spirit Realm's primal source was imminent. Without a doubt, North Wind True Person was poised to emerge as the paramount victor.

However, North Wind True Person's recent circumstances were not favorable. It was rumored that the True Spirit Realm had lured numerous top-tier Deity Transformations and Martial Gods.

Moreover, these Deity Transformation cultivators and Martial Gods had formed an alliance, methodically targeting the incarnations of North Wind True Person.

Thus, North Wind True Person's incarnations were faltering. The situation in the True Spirit Realm was becoming increasingly complex and unpredictable.

Lin Fei withdrew his consciousness, departing from the Great Dream City.

Now, Lin Fei had a rough understanding of why North Wind True Person sought aid from the Heart Demon Martial God. North Wind True Person would not have sought help if he had even a shred of confidence. But why turn to the Heart Demon Martial God?

It was due to the formidable reputation of the Heart Demon Martial God.

Against Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators, the Heart Demon Martial God was more than capable, compelling North Wind True Person to consider him an equal.

However, even the Heart Demon Martial God was not entirely confident. He knew the gravity of the situation. To ally with North Wind True Person alone was a risk he was reluctant to take.

Hence, he planned to involve Lin Fei as well. After deciphering everything, Lin Fei sank into deep contemplation. He pondered whether to intervene in the world's conflict now.

By this time, Lin Fei had endured three Thunder Tribulations and mastered twelve laws, firmly establishing himself among the elite ranks of Martial Gods.

Moreover, clues to Saber World were dwindling. In the vast expanse of the ancient era, Saber Worlds were scarce. After all, many Martial Kings' Saber Worlds shattered upon their demise. Only a handful had survived intact. Banking on Saber World seemed increasingly futile. Its discovery remained uncertain and potentially time-consuming.

Yet, the origin of a world was up for contention! And this time, it presented an opportunity. Engaging in the struggle might yield quick results. The chance to claim the origin of a world!

"Neither Heart Demon Martial God nor North Wind True Person are easy adversaries," Lin Fei muttered to himself. "But am I any less formidable? Now, it's time to partake in the strife for the world's origin..."

With these words, Lin Fei resolved his decision. Without sending a transmission, he rose, transforming into a streak of light, vanishing from the Ancient City...
