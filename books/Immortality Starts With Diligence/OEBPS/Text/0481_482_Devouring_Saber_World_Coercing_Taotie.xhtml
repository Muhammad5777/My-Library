482. Devouring Saber World, Coercing Taotie!

"Saber World?"

Fang Yunshan then remembered. His promise to Lin Fei was indeed about the Saber World.

Thus, Fang Yunshan solemnly said, "Senior, please follow me. Actually, Saber World is a secret realm left behind by the ancestors of my Fang family. This time, Daoist Mo Yu came for that secret realm. I hope he hasn't destroyed it."

Led by Fang Yunshan, Lin Fei arrived at the Fang family's ancestral hall. In the hall, a portal appeared, leading to the secret realm.

Lin Fei leaped into it, entering the secret realm.

Inside, the realm was in disarray. Daoist Mo Yu had nearly emptied it of its contents. And surely, Daoist Mo Yu had other avatars that had already left. The items were irretrievable.

But Lin Fei didn't need the items in the secret realm. He was more concerned with the realm itself.

Fortunately, although aged, the secret realm was still intact. Moreover, Saber World could exist independently as its own realm, even if its master died. This was why the Fang family's Saber World had not disappeared over the years.

But now, it was about to vanish. From this day forth, the Fang family's secret realm would be gone.

Lin Fei and Fang Yunshan exited the secret realm.

Lin Fei said to Fang Yunshan, "I am very satisfied with this Saber World. I am now going to take this Saber World away. From now on, your Fang family will no longer have this secret realm."

Fang Yunshan felt somewhat distressed, as the secret realm was a legacy of his ancestors. But only in this way could the safety of the Fang family be secured. So, Fang Yunshan gritted his teeth and said, "Please proceed, senior."

"Alright."

Then, Lin Fei took a deep breath.

"Hum."

At that moment, Lin Fei's Saber World descended.

Lin Fei also possessed a Saber World, and it was merged with the World Law. His Saber World was far stronger than the Fang family's secret realm. Especially with the integration of the World Law.

Lin Fei's Saber World, upon its arrival, directly overpowered the Fang family's secret realm.

"Crack."

The Fang family's secret realm shattered. But it was alright. Both were Saber Worlds. Once shattered, they could be completely integrated into Lin Fei's Saber World. This was Lin Fei's intention. He aimed to forcibly "devour" the Fang family's Saber World.

Indeed, as the Fang family's Saber World gradually crumbled, Lin Fei's Saber World swiftly devoured it, expanding ferociously. With every bit it consumed, it expanded wildly at an incredibly rapid pace.

Lin Fei's conjecture was correct. As long as he could find enough Saber Worlds to devour, His own Saber World could continuously expand, significantly enhancing its might. Now, it seemed his theory was proven true.

Quickly, the Fang family's Saber World collapsed. Lin Fei's Saber World devoured it entirely. In a short span of time, the Fang family's secret realm completely disintegrated and vanished.

In its place, Lin Fei's Saber World expanded more than twofold. Not just in size, but its power increased by far more than double.

Lin Fei could distinctly feel his Saber World had grown much stronger. He even considered trying to withstand the third heavenly thunder. Of course, it was just an attempt. Whether he could resist the third heavenly thunder, Lin Fei still wasn't sure. Unless he could devour another Saber World. By then, he would be confident in withstanding the third heavenly thunder, and smoothly proceed with the second thunder tribulation.

Fang Yunshan looked at the empty space before him, somewhat bewildered. The Fang family's secret realm was gone. The Fang family was preserved for now, But what about the future? What would the Fang family rely on later?

"Senior, could you take me as your disciple?" Fang Yunshan suddenly asked.

Lin Fei, with a half-smile, calmly said, "You might not fully understand the status of a member of the Great Dream Sect. Every member of the Great Dream Sect is, in theory, a disciple of the Great Dream Ancestor. Between you and me, we are also regarded as fellow disciples. So, I don't have the qualifications to take you as a disciple.

However, while the secret realm of your Fang family is useful, how could it compare to the Great Dream Heart Sutra? The Fang family's Saber World could at best make you a Martial King. But the Great Dream Heart Sutra starts from Martial Gods and even reaches beyond Martial Gods. Make good use of the Great Dream Heart Sutra and your status as a member of the Great Dream Sect. That is your only true reliance for rising to power, far stronger than any secret realm..."

Having finished speaking, Lin Fei's figure flashed and disappeared without a trace. He had given Fang Yunshan enough guidance. The rest was up to Fang Yunshan himself.

After all, while few were lucky enough to receive the Great Dream Heart Sutra, not many of them ultimately grew strong. Whether Fang Yunshan could seize this opportunity was entirely up to him. Lin Fei wouldn't intervene in such matters.

Soon, Lin Fei returned to the Ancient City. This time, he had gained a Saber World, which greatly enhanced his own Saber World and increased his strength considerably.

Moreover, he hadn't spent any contribution points, which pleased him. However, such good fortune wouldn't always be available. He still needed to continue taking on tasks from the Great Dream City.

For now, he wasn't entirely confident in surviving the second thunder tribulation and obtaining the three world laws. He decided to wait until he was more certain before attempting it. After all, he was still young and had become a Martial God not long ago. He had plenty of time.

With the Saber World task already announced, his next focus was on enhancing his physical body, which required the essence of world origins. This was a bit more challenging.

"World origins..." Lin Fei pondered. "I can't contest for small worlds right now, but that doesn't mean I have no way to obtain world origins." He had something in mind.

With a wave of his hand, a massive figure appeared before him – Taotie's avatar, which Lin Fei had been keeping confined.

"Taotie, your original body must want this avatar back, right?" Lin Fei asked.

Taotie glanced at Lin Fei and coldly responded, "Speak, what do you really want in exchange?"

Realizing Lin Fei intended to use it for trade, Taotie asked directly. "Simple, world origins," Lin Fei stated straightforwardly. "Give me world origins, and I'll release you immediately."

"What? World origins? Not just you, even I desire world origins," Taotie retorted. "In the Divine Realm, who doesn't want world origins? Even if you kill this avatar, my original body won't exchange it for world origins."

Taotie flatly refused.

"Is that so?" Lin Fei responded. "Then there's no use keeping you."

"Annihilate." Without hesitation, Lin Fei made his move. The World Law surged powerfully, instantly suppressing the area. The power from hundreds of his particle worlds erupted, converging into his palm. A terrifying aura enveloped Taotie's avatar, seemingly about to turn it into ashes with a single strike.

"Stop! Stop!" Taotie's avatar's face changed drastically, shouting out in fear and looking at Lin Fei with terror.
