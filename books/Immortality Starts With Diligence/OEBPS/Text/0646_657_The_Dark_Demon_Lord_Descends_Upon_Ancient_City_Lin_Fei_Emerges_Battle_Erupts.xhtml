657. The Dark Demon Lord Descends Upon Ancient City! Lin Fei Emerges, Battle Erupts!

"Whoosh."

At that moment, the Azure Emperor Supreme and the other eight Supremes soared into the void. They sensed something amiss the instant spatial ripples appeared over Ancient City. With eyes narrowing slightly, their expressions grew stern.

"Swoosh."

Suddenly, a figure emerged from the void portal, followed by another, and then two, three, four, five... until dozens of figures had appeared. Each emanated an aura so formidable it was clear they were not mere beings but the Supreme lives from the pinnacle of the Dark Demon Domain.

"Dark Demon Lord!"

The Azure Emperor Supreme recognized the Dark Demon Lord at first glance. Having been silent for nearly two years, it was believed that the Dark Demon Lord had retreated to the Dark Demon Domain.

But now, he had returned, and not alone but with an army of Dark Realm Supremes. Clearly, his intentions were transparent—he aimed to obliterate Ancient City and all the Supremes within it.

Thus, the Azure Emperor Supreme immediately communicated telepathically with Lin Fei.

"I have returned," the Dark Demon Lord proclaimed loudly. "Azure Emperor Supreme, where is Lin Fei? Without him, you stand no chance against me."

The Dark Demon Lord stood with his hands behind his back, his gaze sweeping over all, showing a blatant disregard for anyone but Lin Fei. To him, only Lin Fei, the Martial Ancestor, was worthy of his attention.

The Azure Emperor Supreme's expression darkened as he responded coldly, "Dark Demon Lord, your aggressive approach to our Ancient City, is this a declaration of war? If it's the war you want, we are ready to oblige!"

The eight Supremes unleashed their own formidable auras, clashing violently in the void.

"Just you?" scoffed the Dark Demon Lord. "Not enough! Lin Fei, if you do not show yourself, I will begin a massacre, starting with the entire Ancient City!"

The Dark Demon Lord's voice boomed, echoing in all directions as he revealed his true demonic form, radiating a terrifying aura. He could easily destroy Ancient City, even with eight Supremes opposing him.

The Supremes looked solemn, aware that the Dark Demon Lord's threats were not idle. If he chose to, they truly could not stop him from annihilating Ancient City.

But at that moment, as the Dark Demon Lord's declaration hung in the air, a calm voice resounded through the void, "Dark Demon Lord, as you wished, Lin Fei has arrived!"

The next instant, Lin Fei's figure appeared below the Ancient City. Step by step, he ascended as if climbing an invisible staircase in the void, slowly making his way to confront the Dark Demon Lord.

Lin Fei, the Martial Ancestor, now stood face to face with the Dark Demon Lord. His demeanor was composed, showing not a trace of fear but a serene calm as he gazed steadily at the Dark Demon Lord.

"Ah, what a Martial Ancestor Lin Fei is. Even at such a time, he remains so composed. However, there's no need for you to bluster. You're well aware that since I have come, I undoubtedly possess the means to contend with you.

And in merely a year or two, how much could you possibly improve? You're not even a Supreme yet. I'll give you one last opportunity. Leave the Divine Realm, and I will let bygones be bygones!"

Surprisingly, the Dark Demon Lord was still offering Lin Fei a chance. This was certainly not a gesture of kindness from the Dark Demon Lord. He yearned to slay Lin Fei.

But he had to be rational. Perhaps Lin Fei also had his own cards to play, his own strategies. Even though the Dark Demon Lord was confident in the power of the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, with the probability of defeating or slaying Lin Fei exceeding ninety percent, it was not, after all, one hundred percent.

Lin Fei still possessed that minuscule, nearly insignificant possibility of turning the tables. If possible, the Dark Demon Lord did not wish to face even this sliver of risk. Not even this minuscule risk!

Thus, if Lin Fei would willingly depart from the Divine Realm, it would indeed be most favorable.

"Leave the Divine Realm?"

Lin Fei slightly shook his head. His gaze, fixed upon the Dark Demon Lord, turned icy: "Dark Demon Lord, make your move. If you lack the means to crush me, today might well not go as you wish. In fact, after today, you all will be driven out of the Divine Realm by me!"

Lin Fei was unapologetic.

"Illusory Realm!"

"Boom."

Suddenly, the Illusory Realm descended. Once the Illusory Realm had fallen, apart from the Dark Demon Lord, the other Supreme beings of the Dark Demon Domain could not withstand it. Naturally, it would only be a matter of a few breaths before these Supreme beings would awaken.

But, these few breaths were a moment of life and death. If no one stopped Lin Fei, who knows how many Supremes would perish.

Yet, Lin Fei did not move. He just stood there silently in the void. Without any disturbance, because his gaze was locked on the Dark Demon Lord. His Illusory Realm had no effect on the Dark Demon Lord.

The Dark Demon Lord was the key! Even Lin Fei could feel a faint sense of threat emanating from the Dark Demon Lord. This sense of threat was ever-present, clearly showing that the Dark Demon Lord was well-prepared this time.

"Again with such tactics? Indeed, a method capable of suppressing the spirits of Supremes is quite unique and magical. Sadly, you cannot suppress my spirit. And this time, I will surely kill you!"

A sharp gleam flashed in the eyes of the Dark Demon Lord. The next moment, the Dark Demon Lord made his move.

"Swoosh."

With a wave of his hand, a colorful orb of light, flew from the hand of the Dark Demon Lord, violently smashing towards Lin Fei.

"Sphere of Demonic Clouds, Seal!"

This was the Sphere of Demonic Clouds. After the Dark Demon Lord unleashed the Sphere of Demonic Clouds. Immediately, the Chaos Heavy Water within the orb began to release strands of sealing power.

The Chaos Heavy Water is not only exceedingly dense but possesses boundless strength. It hits its mark without fail, no matter the target. Even more dreadful is its sealing power. Once sealed, it becomes exceedingly difficult to struggle free.

Observing the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, a surge of peril heightened within the depths of Lin Fei's heart. He knew the Sphere of Demonic Clouds was no trivial matter, even capable of posing a threat to him now.

Consider, that Lin Fei was now of the True Immortal rank in the Eternal Realm. After reaching the True Immortal level world, his powers had increased by more than a hundredfold!

Moreover, as the essence of his world continued to accumulate, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was still advancing.

As for the current extent of the Eternal Realm, even Lin Fei himself was unclear. The one certainty was that his Eternal Realm was now immensely powerful, so mighty that not even a Supreme could withstand a single blow from him.

However, facing the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, Lin Fei felt threatened. Without a moment's hesitation, he unleashed a punch.

"Boom."

Instantly, the void shattered. Even the laws and rules that governed this segment of the void were utterly obliterated.

With his current punch, Lin Fei could shatter any True Immortal-level world. Even the laws of the Divine Realm meant little.

Such a fearsome punch took the Dark Demon Lord by surprise. He could hardly believe it. How had Lin Fei become so formidable in just two years?

Yet, the Dark Demon Lord was also relieved. Fortunately, he had taken Lin Fei seriously, even daring to wield the Sphere of Demonic Clouds.

Otherwise, facing Lin Fei, whose strength had surged so dramatically, it wouldn't just be about contending with Lin Fei—even his own safety would be in jeopardy.

"Thank goodness for the Sphere of Demonic Clouds. Even if your strength has soared, as long as you haven't surpassed the Supreme level, or haven't reached the Ruler level, you are undoubtedly doomed!"

The Dark Demon Lord scoffed coldly. His gaze intensely fixed on the collision between Lin Fei and the Sphere of Demonic Clouds.
