325. Erase Completely

Profound Light Cave Heaven had now become a silent wasteland, taken over by the people of Ancient Origin Sect and Red Hades Mansion.

Lin Fei gazed upon the devastated landscape of Profound Light Cave Heaven below, his emotions unfazed, his heart calm. Reflecting on the techniques he just used, it was only the Saber Domain, Gravity Domain, Annihilation, Black Wind Prison, and so on. He hadn't even utilized the Dual-World Saber. With just three Divine Abilities combined with the Saber Domain, he easily crushed Profound Light Cave Heaven.

This was Profound Light Cave Heaven! An immortal sect with a legacy of tens of thousands of years, one of the three major powers in the past, and a sect that had contested with Ancient Origin Sect for countless years. Yet now, Lin Fei had eradicated it with such ease.

The shock this caused many was immense, and even Lin Fei was somewhat surprised by it. He found that Profound Light Cave Heaven was quite weak. Only their mountain-protecting formation took him a bit of effort.

Regarding the six Golden Core True Persons of Taiyi Dao Palace and the Xuanwu, they could be eliminated by Lin Fei in the blink of an eye. Within his Gravity Domain and Saber Domain, most Golden Core True Persons were completely suppressed, even their magic treasures couldn't be activated, stifled by the Gravity Domain.

Lin Fei realized that perhaps the Golden Core True Persons of Taiyi Dao Palace weren't weak, or rather, it wasn't that Xuanwu was weak. It was that he was too strong.

Unknowingly, Lin Fei had reached the pinnacle of Martial Sovereigns. In terms of combat strength, few among the Martial Sovereigns could match him. Perhaps only those peak Martial Sovereigns from the Martial King clan, those who have awakened seven or eight Great Divine Abilities from the Martial King bloodline, could make Lin Fei take them seriously. Otherwise, it would be hard for anyone to force Lin Fei to go all out. A couple of top-tier Divine Abilities, combined with the Saber Domain, could deal with if not completely overpower, most threats.

With a "swoosh", Lin Fei descended to the ground of Profound Light Cave Heaven, where he saw a gigantic mountain peak and six Great Mountain Seals. With a wave of his hand, the enormous mountain shattered instantly, collapsing into a pile of rubble.

"By using Annihilation Divine Ability, you shattered a mountain peak, elevating the Annihilation's mood. Its training progress has increased by 10%."

A system voice suddenly echoed in Lin Fei's mind.

"Annihilation Divine Ability training progress has increased?"

Lin Fei's eyes sparkled. This was an unexpected delight. Breaking a mountain could enhance the training progress of the Annihilation Divine Ability?

However, Lin Fei understood that to advance in martial arts or Divine Abilities often requires specific conditions. Sometimes, what works the first time won't necessarily work the second.

So, Lin Fei tried to shatter a second mountain peak. As he had guessed, it had no effect this time. Shattering one mountain could delight the Annihilation Divine Ability and boost its progress. But shattering a second, third, or even tenth mountain wouldn't have the same effect. Naturally, there would be no progress in training.

"Since destroying the mountain is ineffective, what about trying the Great Mountain Seal?"

Lin Fei roughly understood the characteristics of "Annihilation Divine Ability". This is a Divine Ability dedicated to destruction. The more it destroys especially potent things, the more it pleases the "Annihilation Divine Ability", such as the Great Mountain Seal.

"Divine Ability, Annihilation! Power of Destruction!"

With Lin Fei's casting of Annihilation Divine Ability, he pointed out. One of the Great Mountain Seals, a so-called excellent offensive and defensive Golden Core tier magic treasure, cracked instantly.

"Crack!"

The Great Mountain Seal was completely torn apart, and cracks spread across it like spider webs.

"Boom!"

The next moment, the Great Mountain Seal shattered into pieces. A renowned Golden Core level magic treasure turned into mere debris.

"Your Annihilation Divine Ability has shattered the Golden Core magic treasure, Great Mountain Seal, feeling pleased, and the cultivation progress has increased by 20%."

Lin Fei's eyes lit up. An increase of 20% in cultivation progress was significant.

"Let's try another Great Mountain Seal."

Lin Fei tried to destroy the remaining five Great Mountain Seals. After all, these Seals were of little use to him. Moreover, he already had one. However, when he attempted to shatter these Seals, he found no benefit. It seemed that the Annihilation Divine Ability preferred to destroy 'fresh' objects. Once something was destroyed, repeating the act wouldn't improve his cultivation.

Currently, Lin Fei's Annihilation Divine Ability's cultivation progress is at 40%. He initially had 10%, and with the destruction of the mountain peak and the Great Mountain Seal, it had increased by 30%, summing up to 40%. But there was still a long way to go to reach 100%.

"Is it just about destruction?"

A sharp glint appeared in Lin Fei's eyes. He looked at Profound Light Cave Heaven, an idea forming in his mind.

"Whoosh!"

Lin Fei approached the Ancient Origin Sect's sect master.

"Sect Master, have we almost emptied everything from Profound Light Cave Heaven?"

The Ancient Origin Sect master nodded, "Almost everything has been moved out. We can't leave these things in Profound Light Cave Heaven."

In reality, after Lin Fei destroyed the entrance of Profound Light Cave Heaven, neither the Ancient Origin Sect nor Red Hades Mansion planned to settle in Profound Light Cave Heaven. Without its protective formation, what value did Profound Light Cave Heaven have?

Everything that could be moved was taken away. Destroying Profound Light Cave Heaven ensured that the Taiyi Dao Palace couldn't slowly accumulate power within the domain of the three major forces. To genuinely compete with the Taiyi Dao Palace, both forces would still have to return to their own sects.

"Good, since everything has been moved out, you all should retreat. I will completely destroy Profound Light Cave Heaven and erase its existence forever!" Lin Fei said coldly. If he was going to destroy something, he'd do it thoroughly. He wondered, would this act elevate his Annihilation Divine Ability to mastery?

"Erase Profound Light Cave Heaven completely?" The Sect Master shuddered slightly. He began to understand Lin Fei's intentions. Merely attacking the entrance of Profound Light Cave Heaven wasn't enough. For instance, some disciples of Profound Light Cave Heaven who had evaded the disaster or had relocated to Taiyi Dao Palace might return if the sect's entrance remained.

Even if Lin Fei had slaughtered everyone, as long as the entrance remained, there was a possibility of its resurgence and even a chance for it to be rebuilt by the returning disciples. Therefore, total obliteration was the answer, making Profound Light Cave Heaven a thing of the past.

Soon, the people from Red Hades Mansion and the Ancient Origin Sect evacuated Profound Light Cave Heaven. Valuables from Profound Light Cave Heaven were all taken away, leaving it a mere shell. Yet, no matter how empty, this was the entrance to an immortal sect that had stood for ten thousand years. The entrance itself was of paramount significance.

Suddenly, a massive Life Wheel materialized behind Lin Fei. Following that, the fourth ray on the Life Wheel shone brightly.

"Divine Ability, Annihilation!"

At this moment, Lin Fei gave it his all, unleashing this Divine Ability.

"Boom!"

Immediately, the power of Annihilation enveloped the entire sect. Chaos ensued—mountains, massive stones, and cliffs—all collapsed, shattered into dust.

This is the power of Annihilation!

The force of the Divine Ability burst forth in its entirety. In the blink of an eye, as the dust settled, silence reigned. The scene before them was desolate.

Gone!

The grand Profound Light Cave Heaven, an immortal sect that had endured for tens of thousands of years, vanished in broad daylight. No ruins remained, every trace was wiped out. From that moment on, Profound Light Cave Heaven ceased to exist.

"Your Annihilation Divine Ability has erased a ten-thousand-year-old immortal sect, feeling pleased, cultivation progress increased by 200%."

"Your AnnihilationDivine Ability has broken through to Mastery."

"Your AnnihilationDivine Ability continues to break through to Transcendence!"

In Lin Fei's mind, one system notification followed another.
