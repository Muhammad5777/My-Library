454. The Power of Origin, The Secret of Dao Integration!

This strand of energy was both foreign and familiar to Lin Fei.

Foreign, because he had seldom encountered it - this being almost his first, now second time experiencing it. Yet, familiar, because not long ago, Lin Fei had witnessed a vast expanse of terrifying energy akin to an ocean.

The Sea of Origin!

Indeed, this sliver of power was what Lin Fei had drawn into his body from the Sea of Origin, causing his physical form to collapse. But his body had already disintegrated. How could the power of Origin still be inside him?

Lin Fei's expression underwent a drastic change. He was acutely aware of the danger. He would have preferred not to possess this sliver of Origin power, for even a slight misstep could cause his body to collapse again. If Lin Fei's body were to collapse this time, he would not be able to rise from the ashes again. In other words, he would surely die!

"Wait, rise from the ashes?"

Lin Fei seemed to have a realization. He had indeed risen from the ashes before. His body, brought to the brink of collapse by the power of Origin, had undergone a rebirth of fire.

Consequently, he was "resurrected". Could it be that after the power of Origin caused his body to collapse, Lin Fei resurrected through the power of rebirth by fire, but that sliver of Origin's power remained within his body?

What to do in such a situation? Lin Fei was somewhat bewildered. This was the first time he had encountered such a scenario. It was beyond his understanding. Even consulting Feng Ling would be futile. Lin Fei thought of Dubhe. The spirit of Dubhe, well-informed and wise as a Dao Artifact, should have some solution.

"Dubhe." Lin Fei immediately summoned Dubhe.

"Master," Dubhe greeted respectfully.

"Dubhe, there is a strand of Origin's power within my body..."

So, Lin Fei elaborated in detail to Dubhe about the strand of Origin power within his body. Dubhe also found it astonishing.

"Based on Master's description, that strand of Origin power is quite extraordinary," Dubhe observed. "There are many types of Origin, but from what you describe, this strand seems all-encompassing, likely the World Origin itself. The World Origin is the core of a world, and even a single strand of it is remarkable."

Master, do you know what Dao Integration True Immortals integrate with? In fact, the Dao Integration True Persons in the path of Immortals integrate with the Origin Dao! Immortals perceive the natural order of heaven and earth. Integrating with the Dao of the world, they shine as brightly as the sun and moon. As long as the world is eternal, so are the true immortals. In a sense, once Dao Integration is achieved, they become immortal and eternal.

As for martial artists, they are more domineering. They attempt to capture a strand of the World Origin, like you did, for their own use. Mastering even a sliver of this Origin allows them to wield its power, and furthermore, use it to continually refine themselves, becoming even more powerful. In this respect, they might even surpass the Dao Integration True Immortals.

However, very few martial artists reach this stage. The challenge of integrating the Origin's power into the body is insurmountable for most. Ninety-nine percent of martial artists would undoubtedly perish in the attempt. So, Master having a strand of Origin power, even though it was obtained by chance, is truly inconceivable. Perhaps, this is a great opportunity for you. In the future, it may prove to be of great use."

Lin Fei was profoundly struck by this revelation. He knew the power of Origin might be strong, but he had not anticipated that it was a force only Dao Integration True Immortal-level beings could access.

But now, Lin Fei had not only 'drawn down' the power of Origin but had also integrated it into his body. Did this not mean that he had already surpassed countless martial artists just by this feat alone?

"I'll give it a try."

Lin Fei wanted to test the strand of Origin power within his body. Thus, he used his consciousness to approach it, attempting to harness this sliver of power.

However, as soon as his consciousness neared the Origin power, it trembled slightly, followed by a violent shudder through Lin Fei's entire body. His physical particles nearly disintegrated. Lin Fei immediately withdrew his consciousness, daring not to touch that strand of Origin power again.

"I simply can't manipulate this strand of Origin power," he realized. "Nor can I use this sliver of power. Moreover, its presence in my body might even pose a danger."

Lin Fei contemplated removing it, despite its potential immense benefits, unwilling to take such a risk within his own body.

If it were possible, Lin Fei would undoubtedly remove it. Unfortunately, as Dubhe had mentioned, only a Dao Integration True Immortal-level being could extract this strand of Origin power from his body.

Lin Fei pondered and decided to confine the Origin power within his body, securely trapping it so that no external force could reach it. Though he avoided stimulating the Origin power, this measure temporarily alleviated the danger within his body.

"With the bodily risk temporarily resolved, does this mean I can attempt to reach Martial Gods again?" Lin Fei wondered. Initially, he had planned to stabilize himself and try to reach Martial Gods after some time, especially after his recent failure. He thought it prudent to reflect on his experiences and adjust his state.

But after Dubhe's explanation, Lin Fei understood the instability of the Origin power within him. It could only potentially become an asset during his Dao Integration. Otherwise, it remained a latent hazard.

Therefore, Lin Fei had to continue his cultivation, striving for Dao Integration as soon as possible to fully resolve the danger posed by the strand of Origin power.

"My physical body is strong, and I am in good condition," he affirmed. "There's no need for adjustment. Moreover, my recent attempt at reaching Martial Gods is still fresh in my mind. I should strike while the iron is hot and try again immediately! This time, I am fully aware of the steps to reach Martial Gods and am confident of success without fail!"

With this thought, Lin Fei couldn't contain his excitement. He was about to make another attempt at reaching Martial Gods. No need for adjustments. No need for summarizing experiences. He was already at his peak.

What adjustments were necessary? As for experience? It was all etched in his mind. The memory of failure was the best teacher. Lin Fei had already experienced failure, and the memory was still fresh.

Thus, Lin Fei flew out of the Dubhe Dao Artifact. Inside the artifact, it was impossible to sense the Origin.

"True Martial King Form! Nine Bloodlines! Merge!"

With a resounding "Boom," the next moment saw Lin Fei in the back mountains of the Feng family's domain. He didn't hesitate for a second and immediately began merging all nine bloodlines. It was an all-or-nothing gamble, with no room for retreat. He didn't even inform Feng Ling. It was a strategy to put himself in a do-or-die situation to foster growth!

This was to demonstrate his resolve! This was what Lin Fei had lacked in his first attempt at reaching Martial Gods - determination! The power of the mind! The spiritual strength of a martial artist!
