184. Evolving Saber Momentum, I Am the Saber Saint!

Saber intent, invisible and intangible! It's purely a force that cannot be seen or touched but could be sensed, closely relate to the spirit and will.

But now, as Lin Fei's saber intent began to metamorphose.

Gradually, above Lin Fei's head, the entire royal martial arts secret treasury, a layer of translucent light appeared faintly, as if shrouded in mid-air.

A terrifying pressure arrived instantly! If it was an acquired martial artist, or even a Qi Condensation cultivator, stepping into this translucent light, even moving was probably a luxury.

Saber Momentum!

Lin Fei was overjoyed. He immediately understood his saber intent was finally starting to evolve into saber momentum! And this was just the beginning of the evolution.

Saber momentum, not completely materialized, was between tangible and intangible. It was translucent. This also meant saber momentum could interfere with a little bit of reality. No longer was it an empty and void force but a visible and tangible peculiar power.

At this moment, within Lin Fei's saber momentum, there seemed to be countless types of saber intents. There were sky saber intent, plucking saber intent, abyss saber intent, etc. And there were countless types of saber techniques.

As long as Lin Fei is willing,

"Whoosh."

the next moment, a strand of saber energy flew out and transformed into several large sabers. Suddenly, these large sabers automatically executed some profound innate-level saber techniques. It's as if there were countless saber masters hiding in the saber momentum.

Lin Fei was shocked. This is the terrifying aspect of saber momentum. Once a martial artist's saber intent evolved into saber momentum, the number no longer had any significance.

Moreover, under the coverage of saber momentum, to those who were weaker than oneself, it's completely crushing.

Even for those at the same level, for example, other Martial Saints, or other Foundation Establishment True Persons, Lin Fei's saber momentum would have a clear advantage.

With the blessing of saber momentum, the power of any saber technique would be extremely terrifying. Therefore, once a Martial Saint understood saber momentum, there would be another name. Saber Saint!

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei pointed out. A strand of saber essence erupted instantly. This was a saber technique that Lin Fei had never used before. The Three Abyssal Sabers!

"Whoosh."

Another strand of saber essence flew out. This was also a saber technique that Lin Fei had never used before. The Twenty-Three Sabers! There were also the Eternal Night Saber Technique, the Samsara Saber Technique, the God Slaying Saber Technique, and so on.

Strand after strand of saber energy flew out one after another. What they were executing were different saber techniques. As long as they were under the cover of saber momentum, these saber essences were as if they had "come alive."

With the support of saber momentum, saber essence could even fight on its own! Moreover, because Lin Fei's saber essence was closely related to saber momentum, when saber essence fought within the saber momentum, the scattered saber essence would also be re-collected by saber momentum aside from the necessary loss, continuing to converge into other saber essences to fight. This greatly reduced the combat consumption of saber energy.

"No wonder only the Martial Saints who have realized saber momentum have the qualifications to be called Saber Saints! In the hands of saber saints, flying flowers and leaves are all profound saber techniques. Just a casual strand of saber essence is a mysterious saber technique."

Lin Fei was also shocked. If it was said that becoming a Martial Saint would have various "non-human" means. And now, with the evolution of saber momentum, Lin Fei found saber saint's various means were even more beyond the understanding of ordinary people, even other martial artists, martial arts masters, and grandmasters, Lin Fei was afraid it was hard to understand. These were two completely different levels of power and means.

"After building the foundation, life is elevated and fundamentally different from ordinary life. Realizing saber momentum, saber technique can be said to be reborn. Even, as long as I am willing, within the saber momentum, I can easily deduce many innate-level saber techniques. I can even comprehend saber intent of them..."

Lin Fei closed his eyes. The whole body was shrouded in saber momentum. Countless saber technique inspirations erupted in his mind. Just, it was meaningless. Lin Fei had already evolved saber momentum. No matter how strong the innate-level saber technique was, it was useless for him. Moreover, now, even if he integrated more innate-level saber techniques, it would no longer improve any bit of Lin Fei's saber momentum cultivation progress.

"System."

Lin Fei brought up the system panel to check his current martial arts situation.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Early Stage Martial Saint

Saber Momentum: Beginner (Progress 0%)

Fist Intent: Mastery (Progress 81%)

Immeasurable Fist: Transcendence (Progress 50%)

Close Yet Distant: Transcendence (Progress 0%)

"Saber momentum, progress at 0. Innate saber techniques have no effect on saber momentum anymore."

Lin Fei stood up. This time, he was very satisfied he successfully evolved saber momentum. The terror of this saber momentum exceeds many people's expectations. Especially, there was only a Martial Saint three hundred years ago. Three hundred years ago, Although the Martial Saint of the Hidden Sword Sect was known as the Sword Saint, he didn't realize the Sword Momentum.

Since the founding emperor of Great Chang, there had been some more Martial Saints born one after another. However, without exception, all of them did not realize Momentum. Only the founding Emperor of Great Chang realized the Momentum, So, he was invincible at his time.

The improvement of Lin Fei's strength by this saber momentum was not one or two times but a tenfold increase! In terms of combat, the role was incalculable! However, Lin Fei still did not forget what is the root of Martial Saint. That was still the level of cultivation. Now, Lin Fei didn't have Martial Saint martial arts. Saber momentum couldn't be improved. It almost reached the limit of strength.

Lin Fei looked at it in the martial arts secret stash. There were no Martial Saint martial arts. Presumably, the founding Emperor of Great Chang became a Martial Saint at the beginning. But there were no Martial Saint martial arts. However, Lin Fei was not in a hurry about this. So, he stood up and left the martial arts secret stash.

"Martial Saint, you are out."

Outside were some people from the royal family, who had been guarding the stash. Seeing Lin Fei come out, their faces showed respect. However, there was a hint of urgency in their eyes.

"What happened?" Lin Fei asked.

"Martial Saint, you've been in the stash for three days. Two days ago, there was an urgent report. The situation in the south has worsened, and two rebel forces have captured several prefectures. The court's rebel suppression army was disastrously defeated and almost completely lost. Now, in Chang City, the people are in fear. The court urgently needs a big victory to stabilize the people's minds."

Lin Fei looked at the other party. He should be from the royal family as well. Not some kind of eunuch. Otherwise, he wouldn't know so much about the situation.

At this moment, there was a dense sound of footsteps outside. Several elderly members of the royal family came. Upon seeing Lin Fei, the elderly members of the royal family quickly approached. And, they bowed deeply and said, "Martial Saint Lin, the emperor has decreed and conferred Ling Yao as the imperial princess. Can the Martial Saint set off now? The situation is worsening, if the rebellion is not quelled soon, I'm afraid..."

Lin Fei's eyes were deep. He said calmly, "Well. Since you have all met my conditions, then I shall depart. By the way, the Fairy Crane Sect and Water Moon Water Sect are behind this rebellion, right?"

"Yes, the Fairy Crane Sect and Water Moon Sect. Although they are not millennia-old immortal sects and have not produced Foundation Establishment True Persons in the past, their strength should not be underestimated."

Lin Fei remained noncommittal. He took a step and his figure shot dozens of feet away, only leaving behind a sentence echoing in everyone's ears.

"Within seven days, I will quell the southern rebellion! Stamp out the Fairy Crane Sect and the Water Moon Water Sect's mountain gate!"

Having said that, Lin Fei's figure disappeared from everyone's sight.

For a moment, the royal family looked at each other. They were not surprised that Lin Fei could quell the Southern rebellion. However, stamping out the Crane Gate and the Moon Sect's mountain gate was another matter. After all, that's the lineage of the immortal sects.

Ordinary Foundation Establishment True Persons dare not claim they can stamp out these two great immortal sects even without the suppressing treasure of the sect. Is it really that easy to extinguish?
