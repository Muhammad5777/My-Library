531. Purple Paradise True Monarch Escaped

With the augmentation of Saber World, Lin Fei's two thousand six hundred Particle Worlds erupted in unison, amplifying the power of his physical particles.

Already at the brink of shattering the void, the additional force provided by Saber World tipped the scales, catapulting Lin Fei's power to the echelon of a True Monarch - specifically, a True Monarch of the Martial Dao.

The surrounding void shattered like glass, fracturing into countless fissures before violently breaking apart. Purple Paradise True Monarch's numerous battle avatars recoiled in shock, hastily retreating from the overwhelming power unleashed.

"How is this possible? The void has shattered... This is the power to shatter the void!"

"Has Lin Fei become a True Monarch? A True Monarch who shatters the void in the martial dao?"

Their astonishment stemmed from the fact that Lin Fei hadn't undergone the ninth heavenly tribulation, having only surpassed the fifth. The notion that one could reach the status of a True Monarch without undergoing all nine tribulations shattered their understanding and conventions.

Never before had they witnessed, nor even heard of, someone achieving True Monarch status and the power to shatter the void without completing all nine tribulations. Lin Fei's feat was unprecedented, overturning their long-held beliefs and knowledge of cultivation.

Indeed, Lin Fei is an extraordinary case. Merely plundering laws would not have enabled him to shatter the void after only five heavenly tribulations.

However, Lin Fei's power comes not just from plundered laws, but also from the particle worlds he created using the world laws. With two thousand six hundred particle worlds within him, his power experienced an astronomical surge, reaching the critical point of shattering the void. Then, with the augmentation of Saber World, he achieved the ability to shatter the void.

In a strict sense, Lin Fei is not yet a True Monarch. However, few observe this nuance. They see only his explosive power and the shattering of the surrounding void, which evidently mirrors the capability of shattering the void.

With the power of Saber World and having reached the realm of shattering the void, Lin Fei becomes overwhelmingly mighty. Facing the attack of over a hundred of Purple Paradise True Monarch's battle avatars, Lin Fei can now obliterate them with a single punch. The moment he annihilates one of Purple Paradise's battle avatars, he also obliterates its particles, effectively killing that incarnation of the True Monarch.

This is an incredible feat. Purple Paradise True Monarch's strength lies in his battle avatars. Even though he possesses many, the loss of even one is a significant blow, signaling that Lin Fei can destroy his battle avatars and thereby confront a True Monarch.

Lin Fei now wields the power of a True Monarch, a development that poses an unimaginable challenge for Purple Paradise True Monarch. His dominance and invincibility, once unchallenged among True Monarchs, are now being questioned and tested by the emergence of Lin Fei's newfound strength.

"Is this truly the power of a True Monarch? Has Lin Fei really achieved the status of a True Monarch? No, it must be the Saber World! Yes, it's definitely the Saber World. Only with the blessing of the Saber World could Lin Fei possess the might to shatter the void, the power of a True Monarch, albeit barely."

The eyes of Purple Paradise True Monarch sparkled. It seemed he had discerned Lin Fei's greatest secret.

Lin Fei was not a true True Monarch. He was empowered by the Saber World only then did he manage to become a True Monarch.

But what of it? What if Purple Paradise True Monarch saw through this?

Lin Fei now wielded the force of a True Monarch. This power was not illusory. Purple Paradise True Monarch could not contend with Lin Fei. And as time passed, Lin Fei truly became capable of defeating one with each punch.

When his strength reached the point of shattering the void, facing Purple Paradise True Monarch's combat avatars, he was invincible. With each punch, another fell.

In the blink of an eye, Purple Paradise True Monarch had lost over a dozen combat avatars. At this rate, his hundreds of avatars would soon be utterly vanquished.

Purple Paradise True Monarch understood that the tide had turned. He was no longer a match for Lin Fei. The only option left was to retreat!

"Retreat!"

Purple Paradise True Monarch, with only dozens of his combat avatars remaining, also began a hasty retreat.

Lin Fei did not pursue this. Even if he annihilated Purple Paradise True Monarch's dozens of avatars, it would only be a minor blow to Purple Paradise True Monarch. It would not cause him substantial harm.

After all, the combat avatars of Purple Paradise True Monarch were counted in the tens of thousands. Even if today, hundreds of his avatars were lost, It would not be a crippling blow.

However, as long as Lin Fei repelled Purple Paradise True Monarch, that was enough!

Moreover, Purple Paradise True Monarch should be aware. What it means to shatter the void. No matter how Lin Fei achieved it, reaching the level of shattering the void...

But as long as one possesses the power to shatter the void, they will be overwhelmed!

"Remarkable, Lin Fei. Who would have thought that a mere Martial God would possess the strength to shatter the void? Even if he gained an advantage by some means, it's still a genuine shattering of the void. Given time, after your next thunder tribulation, you should become a true True Monarch. This time, I have been defeated!"

Purple Paradise True Monarch's gaze was cold. He admitted defeat. In the face of a True Monarch capable of shattering the void, He had no choice but to concede. After all, he had no means to slay Lin Fei.

Moreover, this defeat was not just a simple loss for Purple Paradise True Monarch. He had thoroughly offended a True Monarch with limitless potential, one capable of shattering the void. His troubles would be significant in the future!

"Whoosh."

The next moment, Purple Paradise True Monarch departed. He had no desire to stay and lose more face. Yet, his heart now bore resentment towards the one who had tasked him with confronting Lin Fei. Had it not been for their urging, he would not have invited this trouble.

Purple Paradise True Monarch turned and left, leaving behind countless onlookers, each one dumbfounded as if unable to believe the scene before them.

"Purple Paradise True Monarch, admitting defeat and leaving?"

"A True Monarch, truly unashamed to just walk away like that?"

"What else could Purple Paradise True Monarch do if not leave? If he stayed, he still couldn't contend with Lin Fei. Why continue to embarrass himself here?"

"Even Purple Paradise True Monarch leaving proves that Lin Fei has truly shattered the void."

"Purple Paradise True Monarch said Lin Fei relied on the empowerment of the Saber World to possess the power to shatter the void. But regardless, that's still shattering the void, and that's enough!"

"And besides, Lin Fei has only undergone five thunder tribulations. If he can survive a sixth, could he truly shatter the void and become a Martial True Monarch?"

In that moment, the expressions of those around were a complex tapestry.

True Monarch! A realm so many dream of attaining. Countless individuals had spent a hundred thousand years striving, yet unable to achieve the status of True Monarch.

But now, Lin Fei had accomplished it, possessing the power to shatter the void. And even more, he had repelled Purple Paradise True Monarch.

Of course, the most crucial point was that many had already guessed Lin Fei's intention. As long as Lin Fei did not invoke the empowerment of the Saber World, his physical body would not be capable of shattering the void, not be a True Monarch. This would allow Lin Fei to continue entering smaller worlds, plundering their origins. With this strategy, who could possibly stop Lin Fei?
