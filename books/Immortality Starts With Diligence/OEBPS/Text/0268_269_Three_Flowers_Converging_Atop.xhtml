269. Three Flowers Converging Atop

Inside the immortal mansion, Lin Fei was rushing towards the entrance at top speed. Suddenly, a thunderous vibration shook the entire mansion.

"What's going on?" Lin Fei's expression changed from confusion to alarm.

The Human-faced fruit quickly analyzed the surroundings and said, "It appears the immortal mansion's formation is attempting to flee. This formation was laid by the Seven Holy Daoist. If it senses an attack, it will try to escape. Perhaps someone outside has triggered the formation."

Lin Fei's face paled. If the immortal mansion was fleeing, wouldn't that mean he'd be trapped inside forever?

With a humming sound, he immediately deployed his saber momentum. This technique allowed Lin Fei to move at an enhanced speed, and soon he arrived at the place where he had entered a month ago. However, a chill flashed in his eyes as he noticed the formation that allowed him to leave the immortal mansion rapidly closing.

Something must have happened outside.

After all, it was only the twenty-ninth day, not yet the agreed-upon thirty. Under normal circumstances, the Golden Core True Persons and Wind Sovereign outside should be able to support the formation without it closing. But now it was closing, and something must have gone wrong outside. What's more, it seemed that Lin Fei was the only person left inside the entire immortal mansion.

"Human-faced Fruit, the grand formation is closing. Do you know how to control it?" Lin Fei asked urgently.

"This grand formation was personally arranged by the Seven Sacred Daoist. No one but him can control it," the Human-faced Fruit replied helplessly.

A heavy feeling settled in Lin Fei's heart. Was he really going to be trapped in the immortal mansion? He refused to accept this fate.

"There must be a way!" Lin Fei exclaimed and lashed out with his saber.

"Bang!"

Lin Fei's blade was blocked by the formation, creating ripples before the surface returned to calmness.

"Hmm? This formation was momentarily suppressed by the Golden Core True Persons and Martial Sovereigns of the four major factions. Even if the formation has recovered, there still seem to be some issues. If my power were just a bit stronger, to strike the formation... It might not be impossible to create a crack in the formation, even a tiny fissure would be enough for me to leave the Immortal Mansion."

Lin Fei unleashed several slashes in succession. As a result, the formation was indeed violently shaking. It wasn't as invincible as one might think especially from the inside. There was a chance to create an opening.

However, even if the formation is currently not operating smoothly, the power of a mere Martial Saint would undoubtedly be insufficient to create an opening. At the least, it would require the strength of a Golden Core or Martial Sovereign.

"Martial Sovereign..."

Lin Fei muttered softly.

"You must hurry and give it a try. The formation is activating and might disappear soon. When that happens, the formation might just repair itself. You won't have even this slightest chance then. Unless you reach the realm of Martial Sovereign, then perhaps you'll have a slim hope of breaking the formation and leaving."

The Human-face Fruit reminded Lin Fei.

If he wanted to escape, time was of the essence. Once the formation recovered, Lin Fei wouldn't have even a sliver of opportunity.

With that thought, Lin Fei no longer hesitated. He had originally planned to condense the Spirit Flower after getting out. However, there wasn't any time left now.

"Boom."

Lin Fei unleashed his saber momentum. Now, Lin Fei's saber momentum had reached perfection. Within the saber momentum, Lin Fei's strength could increase tenfold, even dozens of times. Under the influence of saber momentum, Lin Fei was invincible!

Yet, that invincibility was only at the level of a Martial Saint. He still couldn't break the Immortal Mansion's formation.

"Condense."

The next moment, Lin Fei's saber momentum seemed to begin boiling. The saber momentum continuously gathered above him. The endless power of the saber momentum, it seemed like it was constantly rolling and churning. When the saber momentum had reached its peak,

"Boom."

Above Lin Fei's head, a flower suddenly manifested.

The Spirit Flower!

The most challenging Spirit Flower for a Martial Saint had finally been condensed by Lin Fei. And it happened as naturally as water flowing into a channel. It didn't seem as difficult as the legends suggested.

Of course, Lin Fei understood. It was likely due to the merit of his saber momentum. The emergence of the Spirit Flower signified that Lin Fei had reached the pinnacle at the Martial Saint level!

"Vroom, vroom."

The next moment, the Energy Flower and Qi Flower also emerged. Lin Fei's aura continually soared. Three flowers hovered above Lin Fei's head, enshrouding the entire Immortal Mansion within the aura of these three blossoms.

Three Flowers Converging Atop!

These three flowers even attracted one another, holding an intricate connection between them. With the three flowers converging, Lin Fei's blood energy, saber essence, and even his saber momentum, all seemed to subtly merge together. Once they truly combined, it would signify the unity of the three flowers. Only then could one attempt to shatter the Life Wheel's shackles, revealing the Life Wheel itself.

However, it is a gradual process. Typically, once the three flowers are condensed, reaching the realm of the Three Flowers Gathering, one would need to adjust their state and patiently wait. It might take a few days, or as long as a year and a half. The three flowers would then slowly merge autonomously, ultimately uniting as one to challenge the shackles of the wheel of destiny.

However, even if the three flowers unite, whether one can break the shackles is still uncertain. This is a gradual process. By this point, most Martial Saints would typically be oscillating between the realm of a Martial Sovereign and that of a Martial Saint.

However, Lin Fei was no ordinary Martial Saint. Even if when he only condensed two flowers, and his saber momentum wasn't yet perfect, combining the power of three slashes into one, Lin Fei's strength had been at a level that oscillates between a Martial Sovereign and a Martial Saint.

And now, with Lin Fei's perfected saber momentum, and even the condensed Qi Flower, having reached the realm of Three Flowers Converging Atop, how profound might his strength be?

Lin Fei wasn't sure. But now, he had to test it out. Regardless, this was his only chance to break the grand formation of the Immortal Mansion. His sole opportunity to leave the Immortal Mansion! And so, the three flowers hovering above Lin Fei's head shimmered slightly.

At the same moment, in the void, countless saber essences converged, forming yet another blade. This blade bore the echoes of the Holy Spirit Saber, Rainbow Transformation Saber, and Flame Burning Heaven Saber. In essence, it was a fusion of the three blades!

The techniques of all three sabers evolved within the saber momentum. Thus, with a mere thought from Lin Fei, the casually unleashed slash was, in fact, a union of the three. There was no need to premeditate the tri-saber fusion any longer. Furthermore, Lin Fei's saber momentum permeated the entire Immortal Mansion.

Empowered by Lin Fei's saber momentum, this blade continued to condense. All the saber essence seemed to converge to a single focal point. The Energy Flower, Qi Flower, and Spirit Flower showed merging hints.

"Slash!"

Without any hesitation, Lin Fei executed the strike. With the culmination of the Three Flowers, countless saber essences, blood energy, and saber momentum, were all concentrated into this one slash. This strike could be considered Lin Fei's most powerful slash to date!

"Boom!"

The strike, resembling the descent of the heavens, was unleashed. Instantaneously, it felt as though the heavens were shattering. The entire Immortal Mansion quaked violently. The explosive power seemed to have broken through some mysterious threshold of the universe.
