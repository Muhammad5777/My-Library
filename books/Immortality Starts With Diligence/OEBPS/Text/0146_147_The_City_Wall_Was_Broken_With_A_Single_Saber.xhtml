147. The City Wall Was Broken With A Single Saber

Chiyang Prefecture was now occupied by the leader of the Paradise Sect and a group of Daoists.

The leader of the Paradise Sect did not dare to show off in front of these Daoists. These Daoists were all powerful immortals. The reason why the Paradise Sect could succeed this time, even blocking the imperial court's army to quell the rebellion, was because of the efforts of these immortals. Otherwise, he, as the leader of the rebels, would have been captured and beheaded by the imperial army to quell the rebellion long ago.

"Esteemed immortals, I wonder how long the Chiyang Prefecture will be besieged by the imperial army this time. The food and fodder in the city may be a bit tight. Do you immortals have any ingenious methods?" The leader of the Paradise Sect asked cautiously.

One of the Daoists in a gray robe spoke leisurely, "General, there's no need to worry. The food and fodder in Chiyang Prefecture are enough for three months.

Moreover, the city walls and gates have been reinforced by us using formations. Even the top Qi Condensation cultivators can't break through the city gate in a short time. And the imperial court can't send top Qi Condensation cultivators now.

Although the imperial army is besieging us, their food and fodder can only last for ten more days. After ten days, the imperial army will definitely retreat. At that time, we will have won this battle!"

Upon hearing this, the leader of the Paradise Sect was overjoyed. The Paradise Sect has now occupied several prefectures and it has defeated the imperial army once before.

If it defeated the imperial army again this time, then in a short time, the imperial court would find it difficult to dispatch a large army again.

In this way, the reputation of the Paradise Sect would surely soar. At that time, they could capture more cities, and even occupying a province was not impossible.

Moreover, according to these immortals, Great Chang was now decadent and degenerate. Once the Paradise Sect established its footing, there would surely be a response from all corners of Great Chang.

At that time, when various forces rose up in rebellion, Great Chang would be overwhelmed and the Paradise Sect could achieve its great cause! This made the leader of the Paradise Sect extremely excited.

Seeing the excited expression of the leader of the Paradise Sect, the surrounding Daoists couldn't help but sneer in their hearts. A mere leader of the Paradise Sect, an ignorant fool, also vying for the world?

They supported the Paradise Sect merely to probe the real "strength" of Great Chang, to see if the Great Chang royal family still has the ability to suppress the world.

If the Great Chang royal family really couldn't do it anymore, they didn't even have to wait for the Royal Supreme Elder to die. Many immortal sects could support more people to "rise in rebellion," thereby causing chaos in the world.

By then, Great Chang would collapse. The immortal sects could return to their state a thousand years ago. High above, detached from the mundane, yet firmly controlling the entire world! As for Paradise Sect, it was just a pawn. If it was useless by the time, they could easily get rid of it.

This leader of the Paradise Sect was still immersed in the excitement of "great things to come". He was foolishly dreaming of becoming an emperor. How ridiculous.

"Report, the imperial army has come to attack the city again."

"Attacking the city again? How many people have come this time?"

The leader of the Paradise Sect asked hurriedly.

"Uh... One person."

"One person?"

The leader of the Paradise Sect and the Daoists looked at each other. There was a hint of doubt in their eyes.

"Let's go, let's go to the city wall to take a look."

So, everyone rushed towards the city wall. At this moment, the army to quell the rebellion had already assembled its soldiers and horses. Formations were set up.

However, the army to quell the rebellion did not attack the city. They were quietly waiting in place.

Lin Fei was among the army. He glanced at the city gate and wall of Chiyang Prefecture in the distance.

It was a strong city. The city wall was tall and strong. Even without reinforcing with formations, it would also be very hard to break through. Not to mention, there were high-level Qi Condensation cultivators reinforcing it with formations.

Such a formation, even top Qi Condensation cultivators would find it hard to break in a short time. Unless there was a very powerful magical artifact.

Lin Fei no longer hesitated. He took a step forward.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei took sixty meters in one stride. In just a few steps, he had already arrived at the city gate of Chiyang Prefecture.

On the city wall, the soldiers were all looking at each other. One man attacking the city?

"Shoot!"

Suddenly, the soldiers on the city wall began to shoot arrows. Arrows rained down like a downpour, but Lin Fei didn't move at all.

Saber energy permeated his entire body. The rain of arrows hit him but all bounced off around him. It didn't hurt Lin Fei at all.

At this moment, the leader of the Paradise Sect and many Daoists had also arrived at the city wall.

Seeing this scene, the leader of the Paradise Sect was somewhat panicked and quickly asked, "Who is this? Is he an Immortal Master sent by the imperial court?"

The leader of the Paradise Sect felt that if one could remain unharmed under so much arrow rain, it could only be the method of the Immortal Master.

However, the expressions of these Daoists were very solemn, and they all shook their heads, "This person is not a Qi cultivator, but a martial artist. If I'm not mistaken, it should be the recently famous 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei of Great Chang. I didn't expect that the imperial court actually requested this person."

"'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei? A martial artist with such a title?"

The leader of the Paradise Sect felt that it somewhat subverted his understanding.

Usually, these fairy masters were very arrogant, even innate martial artists, martial arts Masters, these immortal masters did not take seriously.

But now, mentioning this "Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei, they all looked solemn. Even the leader of the Paradise Sect could vaguely feel that these Immortal Masters seemed to be somewhat wary of the "Immortal Slayer Saber."

Yes, wariness!

At this point, the leader of the Paradise Sect was sure he wasn't mistaken.

"Could it be that this 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei is very powerful?" the leader of the Paradise Sect asked carefully.

"Powerful, although he is a martial artist, we have to admit, he is indeed very powerful."

"'Immortal Slayer Saber' has slain two high-level Qi Condensation cultivators from He Mountain Dao and a top Qi Condensation cultivator from Guardian Primordial Mountain. The name 'Immortal Slayer Saber' is not just called, it's earned by killing! But no matter how powerful his 'Immortal Slayer Saber' is, he has no use before this army."

"Indeed, we have jointly used formations to strengthen the city walls and gates. How can he, a martial artist, breakthrough?"

"If he dares to climb the city wall, we will besiege him. No matter how powerful the 'Immortal Slayer Saber' is, he will inevitably be defeated under our many high-level Qi Condensation cultivators' siege."

"Chiyang Prefecture is still as solid as a rock!"

These Daoists were also somewhat confident in their words. Although the "Immortal Slayer Saber" was strong, but he alone could not break Chiyang Prefecture.

However, was it really so?

The leader of the Paradise Sect always felt that something was wrong. So he looked at the figure under the city again.

At this moment, Lin Fei was also looking at the city gate and walls of Chiyang Prefecture. There were runes flashing on it. Indeed, there was the enhancement of formations.

"Formations..."

Lin Fei took a deep breath, and a sharp light flashed in his eyes.

"No matter if it's a formation or a city wall. My saber can break it!"

Lin Fei did not hesitate any longer. The Saber Qi within his body was released without restraint.

"Boom."

The surging Saber Qi gushed out from Lin Fei's body. A huge amount of saber energy condensed a giant saber in the void.

Thirty meters, sixty meters, one hundred and twenty meters...

Lin Fei's saber energy expanded crazily. In an instant, it reached two hundred and fifty meters! Two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy spanned across the void.

Chiyang Prefecture could be considered a fortified city with very high walls. However, the two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy that Lin Fei displayed was more than twice as high as the city wall!

Moreover, with the emergence of the two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy, Lin Fei's saber intent also spread out, enveloping the sky above Chiyang Prefecture.

"Buzz."

Suddenly, the city-defending soldiers, originally holding a large saber, began to tremble violently and emit light sounds.

"What's going on, I can't pull out my saber?"

"My saber seems to be attracted by some force."

"What kind of sorcery is this?"

Even the leader of the Paradise Sect on the city wall turned pale, unable to hold his saber.

"Sorcery, this is sorcery..."

"Humph, this is not any sorcery, this is saber intent! This saber intent is terrifying, but it can't affect the army. The only thing is this two hundred and fifty meters saber glow in the void, this should be saber glow, right?"

"I wonder how powerful this two hundred and fifty meters saber glow is? It should not be able to break through the city gate reinforced by the formation..."

Seeing the two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy in the void, these Qi Condensation cultivators mistook it for saber glow and were uncertain. Even if they had confidence in the formation, who had seen two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy?

"Heavenly Saber Art, slash!"

At this moment, Lin Fei, like a divine being, was demonstrating supreme power. He slashed out this saber from the void.

Immediately, the massive two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy slashed toward the city wall ferociously.

Yes, not the city gate, but the city wall. Lin Fei directly slashed toward the city wall!

The two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy landed on the city wall. The formation on the city wall was almost instantly broken at the touch.

"Boom."

The formation was broken, and it was instantly cut open by the violent saber energy as if it were tofu.

"Boom."

Finally, the two hundred and fifty meters of saber energy fell on the city wall. The sturdy city wall actually collapsed with a loud bang. A huge gap appeared in the long city wall.

For a moment, the entire battlefield fell silent. Looking at the figure of Lin Fei under the city wall, everyone's eyes were incredibly shocked.

"Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei, in front of the two armies, broke the city wall with a single slash!
