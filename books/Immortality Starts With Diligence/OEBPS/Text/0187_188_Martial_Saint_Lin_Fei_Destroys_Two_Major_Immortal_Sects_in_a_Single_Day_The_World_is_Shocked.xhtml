188. Martial Saint Lin Fei Destroys Two Major Immortal Sects in a Single Day! The World is Shocked!

"Fairy Crane Sect? This martial artist wants to destroy the Fairy Crane Sect? But where is the Fairy Crane Sect?"

Both of the young men had remembered the Nine Profound Sect. Weren't they seeking the Immortals in order to gain powerful strength, like that of the Immortals? However, when Lin Fei said he would trample the Fairy Crane Sect, the two young men didn't want to leave.

How could they miss such an exciting battle? Although they were nearly several hundred meters away from Lin Fei, they were quite clever and felt that it was still unsafe. So they retreated further and further until they were a considerable distance away. Only then did they hide behind a big tree to watch.

"Is there really a Fairy Crane Sect?"

The two young men were somewhat puzzled. They looked at the desolate peak in the distance. Where is the Fairy Crane Sect?

Lin Fei stood in the void. He stood with his hands behind his back, gazing at the void in front of him. Without any response. His words fell, and there was no response from the Fairy Crane Sect.

The Fairy Crane Sect, after all, was a sect of Immortal Dao. There must be a mountain-protecting formation that ordinary people simply could not find it.

Even innate martial artists, martial arts Grand Masters, if the Fairy Crane Sect didn't open the mountain gate, martial arts Grand Masters would be just as unable to find it.

However, Martial Saints are different!

In Lin Fei's eyes, everything about the Fairy Crane Sect was clear at a glance. Since the Fairy Crane Sect did not come out, then Lin Fei didn't bother waiting.

"Buzz."

The next moment, Lin Fei exerted his saber momentum. Suddenly, a layer of semi-transparent light enveloped the void in front of him. The originally empty void, under the cover of the semi-transparent light, actually sent out ripples, just like the ripples of water. Then, the semi-transparent light rapidly extended. Layer upon layer of ripples, constantly stirring. Then, buildings appeared in the void. Even the towering mountain peak disappeared.

Instead, countless mountain peaks appeared. Various pavilions and towers, even the sound of cranes could be heard. And in the void, there were also countless Daoists standing in the air.

"Immortals?"

The eyes of the two young boys nearly bulged out. Such a heavenly landscape, if not immortals, then what else could it be?

However, the "immortals" that the two young men saw at this moment had faces full of terror. They looked at Lin Fei from a distance, and spoke loudly, "We were unaware of the arrival of the Martial Saint, and the Fairy Crane Sect has failed to greet you properly! But isn't the Martial Saint a bit too aggressive? If the Martial Saint is dissatisfied with our Fairy Crane Sect supporting the rebels, we can just withdraw. Moreover, we can promise not to support the rebels in the future, okay? Even, the Fairy Crane Sect can follow the example of He Mountain Dao, close the mountain for a hundred years, and ignore worldly affairs. Would the Martial Saint be satisfied?"

The sect master of the Fairy Crane Sect, a top-tier Qi Condensation cultivator at the eleventh level, stated the decision of the Fairy Crane Sect. In fact, this was also a condition. Even in the old times when the royal supreme elder was in power, they said the same to him. And of course, they acted accordingly. Being able to proactively concede to Lin Fei even actively closing the mountain gate was already the greatest concession that the Fairy Crane Sect could make.

Lin Fei's gaze was calm. He looked down from a high position, looking down at the group of Qi cultivators from the Fairy Crane Sect below. Even if they conceded, in their eyes, there seemed to be no trace of fear.

Perhaps, they were still thinking after several hundred years, the closure of the mountain would end. Or, the Fairy Crane Sect would give birth to a Foundation Establishment True Person and they could re-enter the world again.

Those thousand-year-old immortal sects are like this.

Even with a heritage of several thousands of years, when a powerful enemy appears, they would close the mountain gate, and could even close it for several hundred years. Avoiding and not coming out. Wait until the end of the powerful enemy's life, or after they disappear, then those immortal sects would come out one after another, re-enter the world and compete for worldly power.

"Immortal sects..." Lin Fei's eyes were cold, and he asked indifferently, "He Mountain Dao, Guardian Primordial Mountain, both have powerful magic treasures of the sect and mountain-protection formations. They can protect the sect and are not subject to the attacks of Martial Saints and Foundation Establishment True Persons. What does your Fairy Crane Sect have? It seems that you still don't understand your own situation. Now, let me help you understand better. Even if it's an immortal sect, there are differences. In front of me, you don't have the qualifications to negotiate terms!"

"Boom."

In the next moment, Lin Fei's saber momentum burst out in full force. Almost instantly, the entire gate of the Fairy Crane Sect was enveloped within it. Many Qi cultivators from the Fairy Crane Sect felt as if they were carrying a mountain on their backs and their bodies unable to move.

"Activate the mountain protection formation!"

The Fairy Crane Sect did not dare to delay. The mountain protection formation was activated instantly. With the opening of the formation, it isolated Lin Fei's saber momentum. However, Lin Fei just stretched out his hand. The next moment, infinite saber essence exploded from within his body. Gradually in the void, it condensed into a saber! A saber that was solid as reality and was several hundreds of meters high!

It was this saber that spanned across the void. Even through the mountain-protection formation, it caused numerous Qi cultivators to feel indescribably terrified.

"Slash!"

Lin Fei's hand waved lightly. Immediately, the several-hundred-meter-tall saber fell from the void, bringing the might to overturn heaven and earth. It fiercely slashed on the mountain protection formation.

"Crackle."

The earth-shattering collision that was expected did not happen at all. All the people of the Fairy Crane Sect seemed to have frozen in place all of a sudden. The mountain-protection formation that they had high hopes for actually couldn't withstand even for an instant.

"Crack, crack."

"Boom, boom, boom."

This mountain-protection formation was composed of six magic treasures. It could be called the strongest measure of the Fairy Crane Sect! Even a general Foundation Establishment True Person definitely wouldn't be able to break it. But now, these six magic treasures were all instantly shattered. The mountain-protection formation was instantly cut open by Lin Fei's hundred-meter heavenly saber.

"Rumble."

The several hundred meters heavenly saber fiercely descended, directly cleaving a mountain peak of the Fairy Crane Sect. Suddenly, the sky collapsed and the earth sank. Countless Qi cultivators from the Fairy Crane Sect tried to escape, but they couldn't.

Without the mountain-protection formation, Lin Fei's saber momentum instantly suppressed the entire Fairy Crane Sect.

"Kill!"

Lin Fei shouted in a low voice. The heavenly saber that had originally condensed, its saber energy had already dissipated. However, under the saber momentum, these saber energies actually re-condensed again. The heavenly saber was instantly divided into thousands of strands of saber energy. These strands of saber energy were as if they had grown eyes, flying towards the disciples of the Fairy Crane Sect who were suppressed by the saber momentum.

"Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh."

Each strand of saber energy instantly pierced through the heads of a disciple from the Fairy Crane Sect. The Fairy Crane Sect had hundreds of disciples, but in less than a quarter of an hour, the entire Fairy Crane Sect had become dead silent. A thick smell of blood permeated the entire sect.

Lin Fei took one last look at the Fairy Crane Sect. In the void, countless saber energies converged once more.

"Heavenly Saber Art, Slash!"

The next moment, the several-hundred-meter tall heavenly saber in the void instantly fell.

"Rumble."

Countless mountain peaks of the Fairy Crane Sect were cut off by the heavenly saber. Rocks and mountains collapsed just like an earthquake. In the blink of an eye, the huge Fairy Crane Sect was completely buried in a pile of rubble. The Fairy Crane Sect was extinguished! Not a single disciple managed to escape. Even the lineage was completely cut off!

"Next, the Water Moon Sect."

Lin Fei did not even look at the Fairy Crane Sect. His figure disappeared in an instant, leaving behind two young men with stunned expressions and shocked faces. They had witnessed with their own eyes the destruction of the Fairy Crane Sect. That was the "Immortal Sect" in their hearts. However, such a prestigious Immortal Sect had ended up like this.

"I think we should go to the Nine Profound Sect to practice martial arts."

"These immortals are so weak, it's better not to seek the Dao of immortality!"

"Good, let's go to the Nine Profound Sect!"

The two young men also had the idea of practicing martial arts in their hearts. So, they quickly left the Fairy Crane Sect and rushed towards the Nine Profound Sect.

Lin Fei didn't care about the thoughts of the two young men. He made another trip to the Water Moon Sect.

As before, the Water Moon Sect was even worse than the Fairy Crane Sect. Similarly, Lin Fei broke the mountain protection formation with one saber. He slaughtered everyone in the Water Moon Sect. And this time, many people outside the Water Moon Sect witnessed this scene. The Water Moon Sect was eradicated. News spread like wildfire.

In the thirteenth year of Yongqing of Great Chang, on the eighteenth day of the fourth month.

Martial Saint Lin Fei, with supreme power, in one day, successively eradicated the Fairy Crane Sect and the Water Moon Sect, two major Immortal Sects!

The two major Immortal Sects, their lineage was severed, and everyone in the Immortal Sect was slaughtered to extinction!

When the news came out, the Immortal Sects were greatly shocked! The martial arts world was greatly shocked! The world was greatly shocked!
