138. Number One Sword Under Heaven

At this moment, the entire Hidden Sword Sect was shaken.

"Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh."

One innate martial artist after another, some were in seclusion, and some were practicing their sword skills. But at this moment, they all turned their eyes towards the Sword Tomb Peak. Some even rushed directly to the foot of the Sword Tomb Peak.

"What happened to the Sword Tomb?"

"Elder Lu, what's going on at the foot of the Sword Tomb Peak?"

"Why would Sword Tomb Peak tremble for no reason?"

"Moreover, there's the sound of swords from the Sword Tomb."

"This is the lament of ten thousand swords. What happened?"

In a short time, more than a dozen innate martial artists had gathered. This was not the entire Hidden Sword Sect. There were definitely more than twenty innate martial artists in the Hidden Sword Sect.

This was the foundation of the Hidden Sword Sect. Each generation had the world's number one sword master and could be considered the world's number one martial arts sect!

Everyone's eyes were on Lu Zongyi. Lu Zongyi smiled bitterly and said, "I don't know what's going on with the Sword Tomb either. But, I have a rough idea of who is involved. It's the Immortal Slayer Saber on the Sword Tomb Peak!"

"What, Immortal Slayer Saber?"

"Lu Zongyi, you let the Immortal Slayer Saber enter the Sword Tomb?"

"No wonder the Sword Tomb is lamenting. The Immortal Slayer Saber has comprehended saber intent, and it must have done something in the Sword Tomb."

For a moment, all the martial arts masters were indignant. The Sword Tomb was the "sacred place" of the Hidden Sword Sect. Now Lu Zongyi had allowed a saber-wielding guest who had comprehended saber intent to enter, which made the martial arts masters of the Hidden Sword Sect feel very uncomfortable. Especially the sword intent erupted from the Sword Tomb was obviously at a disadvantage.

"Clang!"

However, before these martial arts masters could climb the mountain, from a distant peak, an overwhelming aura surged out. This aura was majestic and vast. Like an unparalleled treasure sword, it soared straight into the sky.

"That's Soaring Skies Peak, it's Senior Brother Liu's sword intent!"

"Senior Brother Liu has also been alerted and even came out of seclusion."

All the martial arts masters of the Hidden Sword Sect had a look of astonishment. Soaring Skies Peak was Liu Yibai's mountain. The world's number one sword master of this generation in the Hidden Sword Sect, Liu Yibai, finally came out of seclusion!

At this moment, inside the Sword Tomb, Lin Fei's transcended saber intent, with an incredibly domineering manner, directly suppressed hundreds of sword intents.

Ten thousand swords lamented. The entire Sword Tomb began to tremble. However, Lin Fei had indeed gained enormous benefits. Because the system's voice rang in his mind.

"Your saber intent has overcome difficulties, breaking through the suppression of hundreds of sword intents, and your cultivation progress has increased by 10%."

"Your saber intent, with its supreme dominance, has suppressed ten thousand swords, making them lament."

"Your saber intent has gained immense satisfaction, and its cultivation progress has increased by 20%."

Lin Fei was shocked. His saber intent was rapidly increasing. Moreover, having previously resisted the impact of hundreds of sword intents, his saber intent's cultivation progress had already increased by 10%. Now it had increased by 40%. This was a terrifying number.

Lin Fei's saber intent had already transcended and it was not over yet. The saber intent was not just saber intent. It was also related to the True Intention Scroll.

Because the Saber Qi Lin Fei cultivated from his True Intention Scroll was actually a fusion of saber intent. The stronger the saber intent, the stronger the Saber Qi. Naturally, it could drive the cultivation progress of the True Intention Scroll.

Now, the saber intent had suddenly increased the cultivation progress by 40%. Immediately, the Saber Qi within Lin Fei's body began to increase crazily.

"Your increase in saber intent makes the True Intention Scroll suffer twice as much."

"Under the double incentive of saber intent, the True Intention Scroll fuses the saber intent crazily, and the Saber Qi surges."

"Your True Intention Scroll cultivation progress has increased by 40%."

"Your True Intention Scroll breaks through to the late stage!"

Lin Fei's Saber Qi within his body finally broke through another bottleneck, reaching the late stage directly! At the same time, the Saber Qi within Lin Fei's body was also surging wildly. Not an increase of 10% or 20%, but doubling, tripling five times, or even ten times!

The True Intention Scroll, created by the founder of the Nine Profound Sect, was the fusion of Innate True Qi and saber intent. An increase in a realm had an incredibly large span.

"Boom."

Above the Sword Tomb, in the void, a faint, translucent vortex appeared. Only innate martial artists could "sense" this vortex. This was a spiritual energy vortex!

For Lin Fei's True Intention Scroll to break through and his Saber Qi to surge, he naturally needed a large amount of Spiritual Qi. Therefore, Lin Fei's transcendent saber intent was able to cover a part of the void, helping Lin Fei "capture" a large amount of Spiritual Qi from the heavens and the earth.

In the void, it was as if a Spiritual Qi pillar had formed, pouring straight into Lin Fei's body. From innate Spiritual Qi to Innate True Qi, and then to Saber Qi.

This conversion speed was very fast. Lin Fei seemed to feel that the True Intention Scroll was desperately transforming. With the pride of the True Intention Scroll, it certainly didn't want to be so desperate. But now that the Saber Qi was too strong, and the True Intention Scroll was being suppressed by the saber intent, it could only practice desperately.

At every moment, Lin Fei could feel his strength improving. It had been a long time since he had experienced such a rapid increase in power.

"System."

Lin Fei immediately summoned the system panel.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Innate Martial Artist

Master: Late Stage

True Intention Scroll: Late Stage (32% Progress)

Saber Intent: Transcendence (36% Progress)

Fist Intent: Mastery (51% Progress)

Heavenly Saber Art: Transcendence (0% Progress)

Immeasurable Fist: Transcendence (0% Progress)

Close Yet Distant: Transcendence (0% Progress)

Indeed, on the system panel, Lin Fei's True Intention Scroll had reached the late stage. His saber intent had even reached transcendence. Moreover, the distance to perfection wasn't too far. At least, it was now within reach.

"Coming to the Hidden Sword Sect was the right choice!" Lin Fei muttered to himself.

"Swish."

At this moment, a terrifying sword intent soared into the sky, piercing straight into the clouds. This sword intent was sharp and pure, like a peerless divine sword that had been hidden for decades and was finally unsheathed. Its sharp aura seemed as if it could pierce the heavens.

Lin Fei suddenly looked up. Vaguely, he saw a figure dressed in white as pure as snow. Step by step, it slowly walked into the Sword Tomb.

Lin Fei saw the figure clearly. It was a man who didn't appear to be young. His temples were already graying. It seemed to reveal traces of the passage of time. His eyes were extremely deep, showing no hint of sharpness. However, his sword intent was sharp to the extreme.

As the man walked step by step, his sword intent seemed to be gathering strength. When he arrived at the Sword Tomb, the man's sword intent reached its peak. The terrifying sword intent solidified as if it had become real, continuously colliding, cutting, and entangling with Lin Fei's saber intent.

For the first time, Lin Fei encountered a living martial artist whose Martial Dao True Intent could rival his saber intent. It should be known that Lin Fei's saber intent had already reached transcendence, but when it came to Martial Dao True Intent alone, the two sides were evenly matched and equally strong.

In the Hidden Sword Sect, there was only one person who could reach the transcendence of sword intent.

"Number One Sword Under Heaven, Liu Yibai!"

Lin Fei looked at the man with graying temples in front of him.
