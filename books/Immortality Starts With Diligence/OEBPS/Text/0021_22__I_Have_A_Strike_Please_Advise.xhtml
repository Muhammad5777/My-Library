22. I Have A Strike, Please Advise

"I wonder if anyone would take the task?"

Lin Fei had returned to his courtyard and couldn't help but feel pained at the thought of losing 10,000 contribution points.

Suddenly, he saw a figure in black appearing in his courtyard, emanating a cold and distant aura.

Lin Fei was taken aback but quickly realized that this was Nine Profound Sect, and it should be safe. He asked, "Who are you?"

"Ling Yao, an inner disciple. I accepted your task to teach you the Nine Shadow Saber Technique for one hour," Ling Yao said coldly.

"Senior Sister Ling Yao?" Lin Fei was surprised, having heard of her name even as an outer disciple. Ling Yao was one of the most outstanding talents in Nine Profound Sect and an expert in the Nine Shadow Saber Technique. It was hard to believe that someone of her caliber would take on a task like this.

"Cut the crap, I will only instruct you for an hour, and the clock starts from now.

Since you issued the mission, you must have great attainment in Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

Use all your strength, don't have any scruples, and strike at me."

Ling Yao was decisive in both speech and action.

Lin Fei did not hesitate either and whispered, "Sorry, Senior Sister Ling, I have to offend you!"

In the next moment, Lin Fei drew out his big saber.

"Swish!"

Lin Fei made a saber strike without any reservation, using all his strength and accompanied by six saber shadows. The speed was astonishingly fast.

"Dang..."

Ling Yao also made a move, but she did not even use inner power. Lin Fei could not even see Ling Yao's saber speed clearly, but Ling Yao's saber firmly blocked Lin Fei's strike.

"One saber and six shadows, the transcendence level!

No wonder you need someone who has achieved perfection in Nine Shadow Saber Technique to guide you. It seems that your saber technique has reached its limit and cannot be improved any further.

Your saber technique had a great killing aura, while Nine Shadow Saber Technique is purely focused on speed. Its only purpose is to be faster..."

Ling Yao began to guide him. Anyway, she only needed to guide him for one hour. How much Lin Fei could comprehend depended on himself.

Of course, she would not keep anything hidden. Otherwise, if it were to be known by the elder in charge of the task hall, it would not be good for everyone.

Whether or not she was hiding anything, everyone present was a martial artist and naturally knew it.

Ling Yao did not hide anything. She even demonstrated it to Lin Fei personally.

Lin Fei was amazed. Ling Yao's Nine Shadow Saber Technique was devoid of anything extraneous.

For example, Lin Fei's Nine Shadow Saber Technique carried a lot of killing intent.

Of course, this had nothing to do with Lin Fei.

It was the Nine Shadow Saber Technique's own "enlightenment."

What about Ling Yao's saber technique?

It was simply fast! Purely fast!

There was no other attachment.

No killing intent, anger, and so on.

All that existed was the purest of speed.

It was as if it had returned to its original state.

With just a gentle swing of her saber, nine saber shadows instantly appeared.

With such speed, it would be useless even if Lin Fei were stronger and faster.

By the time he reacted, his head would have already rolled off.

"So this is the power of perfection-level martial arts? It's truly terrifying..."Lin Fei was shocked.

Body refining was indeed fundamental. But combat skills and techniques were equally important.

Otherwise, even with great physical strength, a lightly swung saber from the opponent could take off your head.

Ling Yao explained patiently.

In the blink of an eye, it had been nearly an hour.

"Your Nine Shadow Saber Technique has received guidance from a perfection-level expert and has understood its own mistakes. It had eliminated all impurities and returned to the basics, your Saber technique has reached a deeper level and has achieved the realm of perfection!"

"Boom!"

As the words fell, Lin Fei felt as if he had gained a new memory.

This memory seemed to have been born with him.

It was as if Lin Fei had practiced saber techniques countless times.

He had gained a complete understanding and thoroughly grasped the secrets of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique.

Ling Yao saw that the time was up. So, she coldly said, "The hour is up, I have given you meticulous guidance. The task is considered completed. If you're not satisfied, you can go appeal at the task hall."

For this type of guidance task, if the disciple who issued the task felt that it was not worth it or felt that the other party did not give their all, they could go to the task hall and appeal to the elders.

An hour of guidance seemed to would not give Lin Fei much improvement.

"No need."Lin Fei suddenly spoke up and smiled, "Senior Sister Ling, I have a strike, please advise."

"Whoosh."

In the next moment, Lin Fei struck with his saber.

This saber strike had no signs. It seemed to have suddenly appeared.

In Ling Yao's eyes, it was as if nine clusters of white light had suddenly appeared.

Nine blades were attacking her from all directions.

"Whoosh."

Ling Yao's figure shook slightly.

She even retreated one step, as if a phantom, and avoided Lin Fei's saber edges by a hair's breadth.

Ling Yao's eyes narrowed slightly, and she said in a deep voice, "One saber and nine shadows? Your Nine Shadow Saber Technique reached perfection? Your comprehension is astonishing!"

"Senior Sister Ling is too kind. Besides, even if my saber technique has reached perfection, I cannot even touch your clothes."

Lin Fei also sheathed his saber.

There was also some surprise in his eyes.

Ling Yao had just used footwork, which Lin Fei had not yet trained in.

This footwork was like a ghostly phantom.

Even with one saber and nine shadows, he could not touch Ling Yao in the slightest.
