229. Lin Fei's Move

Without hesitation, Lin Fei deployed his saber momentum. The vast saber momentum covered all directions. Everyone was enveloped by the saber momentum. They lifted their heads in shock, looking up into the void.

This was the Imperial City. There were countless martial artists. Moreover, there were even more saberists with sabers. At this moment, as the saber momentum enveloped them, not only did the martial artists feel as if they were carrying a heavy mountain on their bodies, moreover, the sabers in those saberists hands even started to tremble violently.

"Buzz."

Countless sabers were trembling and humming.

"What on earth is that?"

"Why does my saber seem to be drawn by some force?"

"What a terrifying pressure, what's happening to the Imperial City?"

"That's saber momentum and not an ordinary one."

"That Martial Saint, possessing such a terrifying saber momentum."

"Could it be that someone from the Ancient Origin Sect has come?"

In the Imperial City, there were definitely many strong individuals. Thus, they immediately recognized it. This was saber momentum! Generally, only a Martial Saint could comprehend saber momentum. Moreover, this saber momentum was very powerful. Under the envelope of the saber momentum, anyone's strength would be suppressed. Many Martial Saints were also suppressed. However, they were not terrified. Instead, they were delighted.

Because of such saber momentum most likely only the top Martial Saints of the Ancient Origin Sect could possess. Which meant. the Ancient Origin Sect had finally sent someone.

Qiu Long and Li Yuan were both shocked. Such terrifying saber momentum was clearly a top Martial Saint!

Gu Lin was not a Martial Saint. But she was also suppressed. But she was very happy. The stronger Lin Fei was, the more likely it was to catch the real culprit. Even, kill the real culprit.

Lin Fei, however, did not pay attention to the thoughts of these people. He just stood quietly in place, trying to expand his perception to the extreme.

In terms of perception, in fact, Martial Saints are at a disadvantage compared to Foundation Establishment True Persons. Those Foundation Establishment True Persons have spiritual consciousness, which could sweep a large range. Even a single spiritual consciousness could easily cover the entire Imperial City.

Under the sweep of spiritual consciousness, any different person can be easily found. But it's different for Martial Saints. Martial Saints don't have such abilities. Although the five senses of a Martial Saint are very sharp and the range that the five senses can perceive is just too small. It's nearly impossible to find a hidden person from the vast sea of people.

However, once a Martial Saint comprehends the momentum of martial arts, things will be different. The momentum of martial arts is vast and can cover a large range. Under the coverage of martial arts momentum, all people would have their strength suppressed. Moreover, the Martial Saint knows everything that happens within the martial arts momentum.

Therefore, Lin Fei immediately unleashed his saber momentum, directly sweeping a radius of several miles. Although it couldn't cover the entire Imperial City, just a few rounds of saber momentum coverage would sweep every corner of the Imperial City.

Lin Fei carefully sensed for a while. He didn't find any Foundation Establishment cultivators. Even, no zombies. However, Lin Fei didn't rush. After all, his saber momentum could only cover part of the Imperial City.

"Swoosh."

The next moment, Lin Fei walked in the air. He turned into a streak of light, flying toward the sky. Qiu Long and Li Yuan looked at each other. Then they gritted their teeth and said, "Follow him!"

So, the two of them, along with Gu Lin, also followed Lin Fei, flying toward the sky. On the way, Lin Fei maintained the coverage of his saber momentum. In less than the time it took for an incense stick to burn, Lin Fei could cover every corner of the Imperial City with his saber momentum. It's as if he also searched the entire Imperial City. Under the saber momentum, any trace could not escape Lin Fei's perception.

"Hmm?"

Suddenly, Lin Fei stopped. His saber momentum sensed he found a trace of spiritual power.

Spiritual power! Only Qi cultivators have spiritual power. And the entire Tianqi Country was full of martial artists. Where did the Qi cultivator come from?

"Swoosh."

The next moment, Lin Fei's figure appeared on the street. His gaze was quietly fixed on a shop. It was a coffin shop. Qiu Long and Li Yuan also flew over. Seeing Lin Fei's gaze, the two immediately shouted at the crowd below, "Everyone get away quickly."

Immediately, many people ran in all directions in case a real fight broke out. Being able to tread in the air. That must be a Martial Saint. The aftereffects, how terrifying? Being slightly affected, ordinary people can't stand it.

"Elder Lin, did you find the real culprit?" Qiu Long asked softly.

Lin Fei didn't answer. Instead, he began to shrink his saber momentum. The more saber momentum is compressed, the stronger the power. Lin Fei's saber momentum had shrunk to only cover the coffin shop.

"Whoosh."

Suddenly, the coffin shop owner raised his head abruptly. His cloak shattered in an instant. There was a vague talisman, floating around. It seemed to be struggling to resist Lin Fei's saber momentum suppression.

In the face of Lin Fei's saber momentum suppression, even the most hidden Foundation Establishment True Persons would reveal flaws. Because their magic bodies would involuntarily operate the spiritual power. Therefore, this deeply hidden Foundation Establishment True Person, in front of Lin Fei, has nowhere to hide.

"Why? Why must you find out who I am? I really don't want to kill wantonly. But, tribulation is difficult. I just wanted to cultivate a few Martial Saints with the Yin Day Birth attribute and form the Nine Yin Zombie Array. I hope to get through the tribulation and achieve the golden pill. But, you insist on finding out who I am. Then I can only kill wantonly!"

This Foundation Establishment True Person slowly stood up. Even though there was the terrifying suppression of the saber momentum, the talisman around him seemed to be able to resist the suppression of the saber momentum.

Qiu Long and Li Yuan's faces changed dramatically.

"A tribulation-crossing true person? Not good, this Foundation Establishment True Person is actually about to cross the tribulation. Elder Lin, let's go quickly, we probably are not his match!"

This was not alarmist. The true persons who can cross the tribulation, even if it is just approaching the tribulation, are very terrifying. They are equivalent to those Limit Martial Saints who have condensed Energy Flower and Qi Flower.

Of course, Foundation Establishment True Person and Martial Saints are different. The strength of a Foundation Establishment True Person depends on spells and magic treasures.

"Bang bang bang".

As the Foundation Establishment True Person operated the spiritual power. Immediately, the coffins in the coffin shop were broken open one by one. One after another, zombies flew out of the coffins.

Zombies! All zombies! And, all were Martial Saint-level zombies!

"Swoosh swoosh swoosh."

One after another, zombies flew into the void. The corpse energy from their bodies was emitted, vaguely forming a cloud of darkness, covering Lin Fei and the others within.

These zombies, actually, are not affected by the saber momentum at all. After all, zombies are no longer considered alive. They have no consciousness of their own. They are just walking dead. Saber momentum can't suppress zombies.

"Eight zombies!"

"What a terrifying momentum, even a top Martial Saint might find it difficult."

"The Nine Yin Zombie Array, that is an array that can resist some of the power of tribulation, how terrifying!"

"This time, I'm afraid we are in great danger..."

Qiu Long and Li Yuan's faces changed dramatically. They couldn't escape anymore. The sky was full of corpse energy. If they were not careful, they might even be corroded by the corpse's energy. Even if they were Martial Saints, they would be corroded all the same.

Moreover, eight Martial Saint-level zombies formed a large array. The entire void, as if it had become purgatory in an instant. This was the method of a Foundation Establishment True Person. Without a powerful magic treasure, they can use the array to replace it.

Even if these eight zombies were resurrected, just eight Martial Saints wouldn't be so terrifying. But, refined into zombies by the Foundation Establishment True Person, after forming the large array, they became so terrifying. The strength had increased at least ten times even dozens of times!

"Today, this True Person will kill wantonly. If I can't find the ninth Yin Day Birth Martial Saint, then I will use you three Martial Saints even the blood of the entire Imperial City to help me refine a blood corpse, to replace the ninth zombie! Hahaha, the Eight Yin Zombie Array, kill!"

The voice of the Foundation Establishment True Person was filled with madness. He actually wanted to use the blood of the entire city to forcefully refine blood corpses!

For a moment, the entire Imperial City seemed to be echoing with the mad laughter of the Foundation Establishment True Person. At the same time, the sky also gradually darkened. Countless Yin Qi and corpse energies converged together and shrouded the entire Imperial City.

This Foundation Establishment True Person actually had preparations early on. Even the array had been set up. He wanted to refine the entire Imperial City with blood! This is indeed extremely mad. Even at any cost. Even the Ancient Origin Sect couldn't make this Foundation Establishment True Person stop his madness.

"I'm afraid you're going to be disappointed!"

Suddenly, a cold voice rang out.

"Whoosh."

Immediately afterward, a saber pierced through the darkness. Just like a burning sun, it emitted endless heat and flames, shining brightly, dispersing all darkness and corpse energy.
