369. Daoist Lord Qianhe

The difference between Saber World and Saber Domain is vast. The Saber World is a whole, independent world. Once the Saber World has evolved, even if its master dies, as long as it's not destroyed by external forces, it will exist forever.

At this moment, Lin Fei's Saber Domain was evolving into the Saber World. There was a clear distinction between its inside and the outside world. Everything within the Saber Domain became more real. The previous Saber Domain felt unreal, like an enclosed space that was clearly visible at a glance. But in the Saber World, apart from the deep comprehension of the "Saber Dao," everything else feels no different from the outside world. Here, people can live and cultivate.

If the master of the Saber World, Lin Fei, wished, a booming sound would ensue with just a thought, transforming the Saber World into a vast city. This city was shaped according to Lin Fei's memories of the city. The city could constantly change its form, adapting to whatever shape he desired. This was the Saber World – Lin Fei's very own realm!

"It's truly magical," Lin Fei murmured. "In the Saber World, one can even plant some heavenly treasures and adjust its environment to the most conducive conditions. Those secret realms mentioned by immortal sects or martial sects could just be similar small worlds!" A glint appeared in Lin Fei's eyes.

Additionally, there was another advantage: storage space! Most martial artists, unlike the Qi Condensation and Foundation Establishment cultivator of the Immortal Dao, lacked storage treasures.

After all, individuals like Lin Fei, who possessed the Sky Reaching Tower and the Dubhe Dao Artifact, were rare exceptions. But if one could evolve their comprehension into a world, then it's a game-changer.

For instance, Lin Fei could evolve his Saber Domain into Saber World. By then, he could store anything, even entire mountains, without any issue. Even those who could move mountains and seas – Lin Fei could do the same. He could envelop an entire river with his small world and then release it, causing a massive flood.

Once a martial artist evolves a world, they truly possess godlike abilities. Moreover, all the previous enhancement methods of the Saber Domain are available in the Saber World, and they're even stronger!

With a swish, Lin Fei opened his eyes.

"System," he muttered, and immediately, the system panel appeared in front of him.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Early Martial King

Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline: Perfection

Saber World: Perfection

Lin Fei's gaze immediately landed on the Supreme Saber Dao Bloodline. As expected, it had reached perfection. And the Saber World was also at its pinnacle. Having just evolved into the Saber World, it had already reached its zenith.

The reason was simple: once the Saber Domain evolved into the Saber World, it reached its limit. This path had come to an end. A martial artist's power ultimately returns to themselves. They must focus on their bloodlines to advance further.

Lin Fei's first bloodline had already reached perfection, and he could start cultivating his second bloodline. However, it would take time, and Lin Fei wasn't willing to wait, especially with the unresolved matter of Taiyi Dao Palace.

With a swoosh, Lin Fei appeared in the main hall of the Ancient Origin Sect.

"Sect Leader," he greeted as he spotted the head of the Ancient Origin Sect.

"Your Highness, Martial King!" The Sect Leader, as well as the Wind Sovereign, bowed respectfully. No one dared to be arrogant in front of Lin Fei, the Martial King, and the unprecedented powerhouse of the Ancient Origin Sect.

"Are you all prepared?" Lin Fei asked. "Has there been any movement from the Taiyi Dao Palace?"

"Taiyi Dao Palace has been quiet recently," the Sect Leader replied. "Of course, if they intend to hide, our spies would have a hard time uncovering any information."

Lin Fei nodded. The methods of the immortal daoists were vast and varied. It was far too easy for them to conceal themselves, especially for a sect with as profound a foundation as Taiyi Dao Palace. If they wanted to hide, the Ancient Origin Sect wouldn't be able to detect anything.

"Regardless of whether Taiyi Dao Palace makes a move," Lin Fei declared, his eyes shining with determination, "I will head to the Taiyi Dao Palace once more and obliterate them completely!"

He remembered the time in Great Chang when he eradicated the Ten Great Immortal Sects. Especially the Tongtian Sect, which had a Golden Core heritage. Yet, what was the result? The Ten Great Immortal Sects were annihilated. Now, even if Taiyi Dao Palace was of Nascent Soul heritage, Lin Fei believed the outcome would be the same!

"Let's go." Lin Fei didn't hesitate and with a wave of his hand, swept the Ancient Origin Sect leader and others into the Saber World. Then, turning into a streak of light, he sped towards the Taiyi Dao Palace.

...

At the Taiyi Dao Palace, the Palace Master and several Golden Core True Persons stood deferentially in front of a robed elder. "Greetings, Daoist Lord Qianhe!"

All the Golden Core True Persons hardly dared to breathe, showing the utmost respect to the robed elder. After all, this was Daoist Qianhe, a renowned Nascent Soul Great Being! It was a significant cost for Taiyi Dao Palace to seek the help of Daoist Qianhe.

"Where is my item?" Daoist Qianhe asked straightforwardly.

The Taiyi Dao Palace Master respectfully presented a jade bottle.

With a casual wave, Daoist Qianhe used an invisible force to bring the bottle to his hand. As he opened the bottle and saw the divine water within, a smile spread across his face. "I didn't expect that Taiyi Dao Palace would possess such a treasure. Grand Yin Moonlight Divine Water! One of the Three Lights Divine Waters! If all three are gathered, it can heal any wound, even if a Golden Core transformation fails!"

The hearts of the Taiyi Dao Palace disciples ached. The Grand Yin Moonlight Divine Water was indeed a priceless treasure, but the Sect Leader bitterly said, "Although the Three Lights Divine Water is extraordinary, without the other two types, this Grand Yin Moonlight Divine Water is useless to us. Instead, perhaps you might gather the other two in the future."

Daoist Qianhe nodded, "Very well. Now, what do you want in return?"

"Please, strike down Lin Fei of the Ancient Origin Sect," The Taiyi Dao Palace Master gritted his teeth and voiced the request of the whole palace. In his heart, he recognized the threat that Lin Fei posed, and, wanting to eliminate it, he was willing to offer their most treasured item to ensure Lin Fei's end.

"I agree." Daoist Qianhe responded, "The Ancient Origin Sect? I will annihilate them as well. How can a mere martial arts sect threaten a Nascent Soul lineage?" To Daoist Qianhe, wiping out the Ancient Origin Sect was as easy as flipping his hand.

Suddenly, Daoist Qianhe sensed something. He sharply looked towards the entrance of the Taiyi Dao Palace. "Who else have you angered, Taiyi Dao Palace? It seems you have a significant problem on your hands!" With a look that was both amused and mocking, he said to the Taiyi Dao Palace Master.
