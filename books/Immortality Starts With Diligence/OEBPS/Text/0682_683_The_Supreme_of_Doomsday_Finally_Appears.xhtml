683. The Supreme of Doomsday Finally Appears!

Originally, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm cultivation progress had reached 38%. His estimation for the top-tier Chaotic Behemoth's essence was that it could boost his Eternal Realm's cultivation progress by about 6% to 10% at most.

But now, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was continuously absorbing the essence of this top-tier Chaotic Behemoth. And the Eternal Realm was violently shaking.

It was expanding! And it was expanding rapidly. Moreover, the cultivation progress was also increasing wildly.

38%, 40%, 42%, 45%, 48%...

Finally, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm jolted fiercely. The vast Eternal Realm seemed to have undergone a transformation.

From the inside out, everything had transformed. Especially the divine demons within the Eternal Realm. They all seemed to have grown stronger, albeit subtly.

"System."

Lin Fei checked the changes in the Eternal Realm immediately.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Martial Ancestor

World Level: True Immortal Level (Progress 51%)

Illusory Realm: Supreme Level

Lin Fei saw that the system indicated his world level had now reached 51%. This was over 50%, no wonder there was a transformation!

Lin Fei was well aware of how staggeringly strong he was now. Although his system's displayed realm was still Martial Ancestor, Lin Fei did not truly believe he was just a Martial Ancestor.

Lin Fei had long been on par with a top-tier Supreme. He could even slay a top-tier Supreme. He was clear on that once his Eternal Realm cultivation progress reached 100%, he could transform into the Chaos Realm.

At that time, Lin Fei's realm might become Supreme. But his strength would probably not be inferior to that of a Master of Chaos. He might even rival a true Master of Chaos!

After all, to truly, fully control a Chaos Realm, and to be able to exert one hundred percent of the Chaos Realm's power, was no small feat. Lin Fei was no ordinary Martial Ancestor, and once transformed, he naturally would not be an ordinary Supreme.

"The Eternal Realm has transformed again. My cultivation progress has reached 51%. So, how strong am I now? Perhaps, not comparable to a Master of Chaos. But on the Supreme level, there should be no one who can match me. Perhaps, only the Doomsday Supreme might stand a slight chance against me."

Lin Fei muttered softly, a sharp gleam flickering in his eyes. The Doomsday Supreme, after all, is not just any Supreme. It was even called the Invincible Supreme! Just below a Master of Chaos.

Even in the eyes of other Supremes, there's hardly a difference between the Doomsday Supreme and a Master of Chaos. This shows the immense power of the Doomsday Supreme.

Lin Fei had never met the Doomsday Supreme. But previously, Lin Fei could slay a top-tier Supreme. Now, with his Eternal Realm transformed again, Lin Fei felt he should also be considered an Invincible Supreme.

Could an Invincible Supreme destroy a Chaos Realm?

Lin Fei wasn't sure. But if he could destroy a Chaos Realm, then the Dark Demon Lord wouldn't be much of a threat.

Lin Fei calculated carefully.

Actually, he had been back in the Chaos for almost a year now. After all, traveling from the Divine Realm, the round trip would take about two years. He could only stay in the Chaos for one year.

Now the time was almost up. At most, he had two to three months left. When the time comes, to return to the Divine Realm to confront the Dark Demon Lord, he would know if his current strength could resolve the Dark Demon Lord.

With just a few months left, Lin Fei naturally was not in a rush. If he could completely transform the Eternal Realm into the Chaos Realm, his chances would be even greater.

But that would require many Chaotic Behemoths. And the City of Behemoths. Lin Fei now absolutely dared not go there. Even the Chaos itself, Lin Fei dared not venture out at will.

Who knows if the King of Chaotic Behemoths would keep an eye on Lin Fei? It's safest in Kunyu City.

Moreover, to obtain flesh essence, it wasn't necessary for Lin Fei to personally obtain the essence. He could also trade! Both parties, each taking what they need!

Lin Fei felt, with his current strength, he was powerful enough to have more Supremes bring him a significant amount of essence.

Thus, Lin Fei went to the task hall. The task hall was bustling with activity. Many people were moving through the hall, mostly Supremes. Only Supremes could venture in the Chaos, they naturally would post or take on many missions.

This was really just a form of mutual trade. Only in Kunyu City, strangers would feel secure enough to post missions and trade with one another. However, inside the task hall, Lin Fei saw many missions concerning Chaos Pearls.

"Snatching Chaos Pearls can be traded with anything."

"Reward offered for Chaos Pearls in the possession of the Clairvoyant Supreme. No matter the method, as long as you can obtain a Chaos Pearl, any exchange is acceptable."

"Clairvoyant Supreme, Supreme Lin Fei, they took away the Chaos Pearls..."

Not only were there missions, but many Supremes within the task hall were discussing, and their discussions were all about Chaos Pearls.

"The Chaos Pearl, who would have thought the Clairvoyant Supreme would get it."

"The Clairvoyant Supreme has been planning for so long, it's only natural he obtained the Chaos Pearl."

"Natural? The Clairvoyant Supreme only set up a warning formation outside the Tranquil Domain, without Supreme Lin Fei, could the Clairvoyant Supreme ever have obtained the Chaos Pearl?"

"Indeed, that mysterious Supreme Lin Fei, he's the key."

"It's said that Supreme Lin Fei can slay the top-tier Supremes. He brutally killed several top Supremes to snatch the Chaos Pearl..."

"Yes, Supreme Lin Fei didn't just get lucky obtaining the Chaos Pearl. It was with supreme strength, sweeping away the invincible, that he obtained it."

"This Supreme Lin Fei, he might just be another Doomsday Supreme."

"It's said that the Doomsday Supreme has also returned to Kunyu City, reportedly for the Chaos Pearl..."

At this time, many Supremes were abuzz with discussion. The information about "Supreme Lin Fei" was everywhere.

Yet, oddly, Lin Fei was standing right in front of these Supremes. But they were indifferent. They simply didn't recognize Lin Fei. It was just some rumors, after all. The number of people who had actually seen Lin Fei, who knew him, was very few.

Lin Fei remained unfazed. Clearly, the Chaos Pearl was very important. The Clairvoyant Supreme obtaining the Chaos Pearl had quickly spread throughout the entire Chaos. Even Lin Fei's fame had echoed throughout the Chaos. Crucially, a single Chaos Pearl had even attracted the Doomsday Supreme.

"Doomsday Supreme..."

Lin Fei muttered under his breath, a flicker of something unusual in his eyes. He had heard about the Doomsday Supreme many times. Many referred to the Doomsday Supreme as the Invincible Supreme. Even Lin Fei was interested in meeting the Doomsday Supreme properly.

...

In the courtyard of the Clairvoyant Supreme. At this moment, a special person sat before the Clairvoyant Supreme.

The expression of the Clairvoyant Supreme was extremely grave. Because this Supreme was no ordinary Supreme. It was the Doomsday Supreme! Universally recognized in the Chaos as the Invincible Supreme!

"Doomsday Supreme, could it be you are also interested in the Chaos Pearl? However, I fear you will be disappointed. No matter what, I will not hand over the Chaos Pearl. This is my only chance to achieve the status of a Master of Chaos. How critical is the opportunity for ascension? You must understand, Doomsday Supreme?"

The Clairvoyant Supreme was very solemn. In reality, he was also very nervous. The arrival of the Doomsday Supreme himself, other than for the Chaos Pearl, what else could it be?

Though the Clairvoyant Supreme was a top Supreme, he was well aware that he was far too far from the Doomsday Supreme.

The Doomsday Supreme's visit was not a good sign. Most likely, it was for the Chaos Pearl.

"Chaos Pearl? I am not interested. I came to find you to look for someone. Supreme Lin Fei! I trust the Clairvoyant Supreme, you are familiar? I want to meet Lin Fei!"

The Doomsday Supreme's gaze was piercing as he stared at the Clairvoyant Supreme!
