404. Crushing the Vast Void Immortal Sect

"Vast Void Immortal Sect."

Lin Fei had already arrived above the Vast Void Immortal Sect. He directly deployed the Saber World. Instantly, the Saber World enveloped the Sect.

"True Martial King Form!"

In the next moment, Lin Fei manifested the Martial King Avatar. The immense Martial King Avatar, tens of thousands of meters tall, spanned across the void, exuding a terrifying aura. The people of the Vast Void Immortal Sect immediately plunged into panic.

"Who is that?"

"Seems like the Martial King's True Form! Is there a Martial King attacking our sect's entrance?"

"It can't be, who would dare to challenge our Vast Void Immortal Sect?"

"I heard all the Nascent Soul ancestors went to the Ancient Origin Sect to deal with Lin Fei, the Ancient Origin Saber King of the Ancient Origin Sect."

"Could this person be the Ancient Origin Saber King, Lin Fei?"

"If Lin Fei has come, even daring to assault our entrance, and yet the Nascent Soul masters haven't appeared, could it be..."

For a moment, many began to speculate and had faint inklings about the situation. Their faces were etched with terror. If things were as they suspected, they would be in grave danger!

"Damn, what should we do?"

"Being enveloped by the Saber World, unless I can break Lin Fei's Saber World, we cannot escape. If I act now, I'll be recognized, leading to even bigger problems."

Distant Cloud Taoist Lord had already emerged. Seeing the scene before him, his face grew dark.

"That's right, the sect's protective formation! Using the protective formation, I can gather all my strength at any cost to break Lin Fei's Saber World. Even if I can just tear a small hole, I can escape! Once I escape, Lin Fei won't recognize me."

A glint appeared in the eyes of the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord as he thought of a solution. Thus, the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord began to operate the sect's protective formation in secret.

"Boom!"

Immediately, the power of the formation was activated, instantly drawing upon the entire power of the Vast Void Immortal Sect. It transformed into the power of the formation, fiercely striking Lin Fei's Saber World.

This protective formation of the Vast Void Immortal Sect was personally set up by the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord after all. And he had reinforced it many times. So, when the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord operated it, it felt like second nature.

"Rip."

Lin Fei's Saber World wasn't at its peak strength. After all, it had been previously torn by the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord. So, it was barely holding together. Once struck by the Vast Void Immortal Sect's formation, a tear immediately appeared.

Seeing this tear, the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord didn't rush out immediately. He was exceedingly cautious. Instead, he let others, including Golden Core and Foundation Establishment cultivators, go out first. Then, he carefully mingled with the crowd and dashed out.

Once outside the Saber World, the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord would immediately deploy an escape technique to execute.

"Grand Yin Wind!"

Yet, Lin Fei observed many who had fled from the Saber World. He swiftly invoked the Grand Yin Wind Dao Bloodline. With a breath, a gusty wind arose. As it blew, bodies instantly turned to dust. And not just dust, but particles.

Given Lin Fei's current strength, even a mere breath from his Martial King form, the Golden Core cultivator could hardly withstand.

Shrieks filled the air, as the Grand Yin wind surged, neither the Foundation Establishment nor the Golden Core cultivators could resist its onslaught. They all disintegrated into fine powder.

"Damn it, shield me!" roared the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord. However, his current form was but that of the Ethereal True Person, merely at the Golden Core stage.

Hence, against Lin Fei's Grand Yin Wind, he couldn't resist. With one gust, his body instantly crumbled to dust. Even the second soul of the Distant Cloud Taoist Lord was turned to dust by the Grand Yin Wind.

"No..." Distant Cloud Taoist Lord cried in terror. Yet, it was futile.

Lin Fei was even unaware that the Ethereal True Person was now Distant Cloud Taoist Lord.

After the ravaging of the Grand Yin Wind, all who had escaped from the Saber Realm turned to ashes. Even Distant Cloud Taoist Lord, having recently acquired a new host, met his end through Lin Fei's Grand Yin Wind.

Ironically, Lin Fei was still oblivious. Distant Cloud Taoist Lord's death was no one knew.

Lin Fei's Saber Realm was restored once more.

With a mere thought.

"Thunder of the Heavens!"

Booming sounds echoed.

Instantly, celestial thunders descended one after another like raindrops, covering the entire Vast Void Immortal Sect.

Lin Fei's Supreme Yang Heavenly Thunder Bloodline, perhaps when against Nascent Soul cultivators, had not shown its true might. But now, facing the entire mountain gate of the Vast Void Immortal Sect, with the thunders' descent, the sect was turned into a purgatory.

No Golden Core cultivator could withstand a single strike. In mere moments, Lin Fei used thunder to "scourge the earth", reducing the Vast Void Immortal Sect to ruins. But this was not enough.

"Triple Yin True Fire!"

Lin Fei then invoked the Triple Yin True Fire, setting the entire Vast Void Immortal Sect ablaze...

The Wind of the Grand Yin followed flames roared, turning the entire Vast Void Immortal Sect into an inferno.

This time, no matter where the members of the Vast Void Immortal Sect hid, with the Grand Moon Wind, Heavenly Thunders, and Triple Yin True Flames combined, no one could survive such a calamity.

Soon, a haunting silence blanketed the Vast Void Immortal Sect. It had been thoroughly obliterated. Yet, remnants of the sect's magical treasures lingered.

Even amidst Lin Fei's overpowering force, some relics persisted. These were treasures of the Nascent Soul caliber.

Lin Fei collected them all, storing them within the Saber Realm, before swiftly departing.

Perhaps, these treasures would be useful for trading within the Martial God Space in the future. After all, they were remnants from a top-tier Immortal Sect. Their worth was by no means ordinary. Useless to Lin Fei yet invaluable to many cultivators.

In the end, Lin Fei left the ruins of the Vast Void Immortal Sect, returning to the Ancient Origin Sect.

News of the Vast Void Immortal Sect's demise spread. It sent shockwaves across the entire ancient continent. It was a catastrophe unlike any other, not even the fall of the Taiyi Dao Palace could compare.

The Vast Void Immortal Sect, renowned for housing a Nascent Soul cultivator, and not just any, but a paramount Nascent Soul cultivator, was a prestigious institution. Its reputation in the Ancient Continent surpassed even that of other Nascent Soul lineages and the esteemed Martial King families.

Yet, what did that avail?

Having offended Lin Fei, the Vast Void Immortal Sect met a fate similar to Taiyi Dao Palace - utterly obliterated, its legacy extinguished.

For a moment, the entire ancient continent seemed to reverberate with tales of the Ancient Origin Sect, and more significantly, with the legend of the Ancient Origin Saber King, Lin Fei!

Upon returning to the Ancient Origin Sect, Lin Fei was in quiet anticipation of news from the Martial God Space. He was in need of copious ice-attributed Divine Abilities.

"Hmm? Is there news?"

A thought stirred within Lin Fei. He immediately ventured into the Martial God Space. There, a message awaited him. It was from a Martial King who asked Lin Fei to meet him at Martial God City. It was the safest location and contracts could be established there. Thus, Lin Fei left the Ancient Origin Sect, soaring towards the City of Martial God.
