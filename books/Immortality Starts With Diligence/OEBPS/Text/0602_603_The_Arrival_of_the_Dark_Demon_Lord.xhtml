603. The Arrival of the Dark Demon Lord!

"My Saber World, after its transformation, now resembles a dream world?"

Lin Fei found it astonishing. His Saber World had never intended to develop into a dream world. Yet now, after the transformation, the Saber World indeed seemed like a dream world.

However, upon closer inspection, there were differences. Moreover, upon deeper reflection, Lin Fei realized that the resemblance of the Saber World to a dream world was not entirely surprising.

After all, the original Saber World was a form of saber intent, a type of mental power. It was inherently illusory. Though it appeared to manifest in reality, that was merely its ability to interfere with reality, not a true physical form.

Now, with the Saber World transformed to the level of True Immortal and Martial Ancestor, it remained fundamentally an illusory force. But what was the true power of this transformed Saber World? If it couldn't compare to True Immortal or Martial Ancestor, what was the point of Lin Fei's immense effort?

"Illusory..."

Lin Fei recalled the original function of saber intent. It was to suppress opponents on a mental and spiritual level. So, would the transformed Saber World function similarly?

With a thought, Lin Fei left the secluded chamber. In Ancient City, there were many strong cultivators, including Martial Gods, Deity Transformation, and True Monarchs.

"Saber World!"

Silently, Lin Fei unleashed the Saber World.

Immediately, the Saber World descended.

At that moment, Lin Fei did not want to suppress them, but he then witnessed everyone around him frozen in place.

Their bodies trembled. Their faces showed expressions of horror as if witnessing something terrifying.

Lin Fei quickly understood.

"Indeed, the Saber World targets the mind and spirit. Once the Saber World descends. Any life form with a mind or consciousness will be subjected to the spiritual suppression of the Saber World. I think the Saber World needs a new name. It shouldn't be called Saber World anymore, as it no longer has anything to do with a saber. Let's call it the Illusory Realm!"

Lin Fei's eyes brightened slightly. He found the name fitting and appropriate.

"System."

Lin Fei immediately brought up the system to check his current status.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Martial Ancestor

World Level: Small World (Progress 0%)

Illusory Realm: True Immortal Level

Lin Fei saw on the system panel that the "Saber World" had indeed been replaced with "Illusory Realm."

"The Illusory Realm can only suppress the opponent's mind. It seems it might be difficult to kill the opponent. But besides the Illusory Realm, I also have the Eternal Realm! If the Illusory Realm can suppress the opponent's mind, then by deploying the Eternal Realm, I can completely destroy their physical body. The Illusory Realm combined with the Eternal Realm, complement each other. My power will likely see a qualitative leap, especially in combat capability! However, I am not sure about those extra-dimensional invaders. Will the Illusory Realm affect them?"

Despite his confidence in the Illusory Realm, Lin Fei had never encountered the beings from the Dark Demon Domain. He didn't know if the beings from the Dark Demon Domain could withstand his Illusory Realm. This required actual combat to determine.

"For now, my power can't be further enhanced. Unless one day, the Eternal Realm can birth life. Otherwise, my power won't improve. I've reached a true bottleneck. There are no shortcuts."

Lin Fei murmured softly. His current state had reached its true limit.

This was normal. Typically, martial artists or cultivators who reached the True Immortal or Martial Ancestor level also faced a limit. They might not progress for tens of thousands, hundreds of thousands, or even millions of years.

All Lin Fei could do now was wait. He didn't know when the Dark Demon Domain would invade.

......

Time passed quietly.

Lin Fei prepared himself mentally and physically, knowing that the invasion from the Dark Demon Domain could come at any moment. The air was thick with the tension of impending conflict. All of Divine Realm seemed to hold its breath in anticipation.

......

In the cold and desolate void, an expanse of pitch-black darkness stretched infinitely.

At this moment, no one knew that beyond the void lay a vast chaos. The so-called chaos was an endless expanse filled with chaotic energy. This chaotic energy possessed an incredibly corrosive power. Any life form that couldn't withstand the chaotic energy would be utterly destroyed.

However, life forms capable of enduring the chaotic energy could roam the chaos freely. Such beings were at least at the Supreme level. Thus, only Supremes could travel through the chaos at will.

Yet, at this moment, in the boundless chaos, a massive chaotic flying vessel traversed the chaotic expanse. Soon, the chaotic vessel came to a stop.

"This is the place! The Divine Realm is right here. This time, our Dark Demon Domain will completely conquer the Divine Realm! I'll first tear open the Divine Realm."

The speaker was the Dark Demon Lord, the true ruler of the Dark Demon Domain.

"Whoosh."

The Dark Demon Lord swiftly flew out, entering the chaos. Then, his body began to expand rapidly. He grew to an astonishing height of a million meters!

This colossal form exuded terrifying power. The sheer aura emanating from him compressed the surrounding chaotic energy.

"Heh heh, Divine Realm, here I come!"

A cold smile appeared on the Dark Demon Lord's face.

Black mist billowed, transforming into a giant hand that blotted out the sky, striking towards the Divine Realm with immense force!
