447. The Mythical Kun? Lin Fei, Enhanced by Nine Great Bloodlines, Battles the Giant Kun!

"Kun!" Lin Fei exclaimed. "A life form from legends, rivaling the Phoenix and the True Dragon. No, perhaps even more ancient, mysterious, and powerful." He stared intently at the immense creature in the sea. Though it had not revealed its full body, the glimpses of its enormous form were enough to demonstrate its colossal size.

Green King was pale as paper, never having imagined that the "Kun" would follow them here. The Azure Flood Dragon King, standing beside him, was equally pale, stunned by the sight of this terrifying exotic beast.

With a roar, the Kun unleashed a wave of sound that stirred up towering waves.

"Freeze!" Lin Fei commanded, effortlessly freezing the gigantic waves into ice crystals that fell from the sky. The first clash ended in a draw, with neither side gaining the upper hand. Lin Fei sensed that while the Kun was indeed powerful and oppressive, it did not evoke a suffocating sense of threat.

"Is it not the Kun? Or perhaps, it is, but not yet a god-level entity?" Lin Fei thought, recalling the Phoenix in the small world. That Phoenix was a legendary being but not yet a deity, as it had not fully matured. Could this be the case with the Kun before him?

Unsure, Lin Fei decided to test the waters. Protected by his nine bloodlines and possessing a Dao Artifact, he felt secure even against a god-level exotic beast. If outmatched, he could always retreat into the safety of his Dao Artifact, which even a god-level creature couldn't breach.

"Boom!"

The next moment, Lin Fei revealed his True Martial King Form, expanding to a colossal hundred-thousand-meter form. This was the combined power of the Five Elements and Four Symbols bloodlines, merging into a force of the world itself.

In addition, the True Dragon and Phoenix bloodlines added their might, with the phantom images of a giant Phoenix and a massive True Dragon encircling him, making him appear like an ancient deity.

Lin Fei's body surged with the power of the world, enhanced by the Phoenix and True Dragon. With the support of the nine great bloodlines, he felt as if he possessed boundless strength, almost invincible as if there was nothing he couldn't do.

"Fire!"

Facing the massive "Kun," Lin Fei gestured with an open hand. A colossal burst of flames erupted, combining the fires of the Phoenix and the Triple Yin True Fire, along with the Heavenly Fire and many other types. These myriad flames merged and exploded with enough intensity to scorch the heavens and boil the seas. Even the vast ocean could not quell Lin Fei's inferno, which now enveloped the gigantic Kun, attempting to reduce it to ashes.

However, the Kun roared furiously and leaped powerfully, causing the sea and even the void to tremble. Revealing most of its body above the water, the Kun's size was unimaginably vast, far exceeding thousands of miles.

Lin Fei's flames, powerful as they were, were extinguished by a powerful gust from Kun's mouth, like a wild wind tearing through the void. This wind, contrary to fueling the fire, extinguished the flames engulfing the Kun and swept ferociously towards Lin Fei.

Without a glance, Lin Fei punched out. The void trembled under the force of his full-strength blow, a combination of the power of the world and the enhanced strength of the True Dragon and Phoenix bloodlines. This punch represented the pinnacle of Lin Fei's Martial King strength. He felt that even the Heavenly Pillar Mountain could not withstand this blow.

His punch collided fiercely with the Kun's colossal body, causing both Lin Fei and the Kun to reel back. Despite the impact, Lin Fei's Endless Vitality Green Wood Bloodline quickly restored his vitality, healing any shock he had received.

With the burst of his nine great bloodlines, Lin Fei was almost invulnerable, excelling in attack, strength, defense, and recovery, making him one of the most formidable beings below the level of a deity. He had no fear of any non-god-level opponent.

Initially, Lin Fei thought the Kun was at the god-level, but the exchange revealed that it was not. The Kun was indeed mighty, yet it had not reached the realm of a deity. Nevertheless, the Kun was formidable, far surpassing any exotic beast Lin Fei had ever encountered.

"Again!" Lin Fei, invigorated, stepped forward, his hundred-thousand-meter form striding into the sea for another confrontation.

"Boom!"

The sea trembled as Lin Fei reached out with a massive hand, grasping the colossal Kun.

"Lift!" he commanded, channeling his strength. In close combat, a Martial King's most formidable weapon is still the physical body itself, beyond any bloodline or Divine Ability.

Thus, close-quarters combat brings out the advantages of a Martial King, though such opportunities are rare once one achieves this level. But now, Lin Fei had found his chance to battle this fearsome exotic beast.

Lin Fei's hundred-thousand-meter True Martial King Form, combined with the power of the dragon and phoenix, seized the massive Kun. With a mighty heave, he lifted the creature.

Simultaneously, a chilling aura from Lin Fei's hand began to seep into the Kun, freezing its body into ice crystals. This was the power of the Annihilation Frost Bloodline, not just freezing but also embodying the force of annihilation. It momentarily immobilized Kun's power, rendering it helpless and allowing Lin Fei to hoist it up.

"Boom!"

The frozen Kun, its massive body now immobilized, was slammed down by Lin Fei into the sea, shattering the ice and reducing the Kun to fragments that fell into the ocean.

"Is it dead?" Lin Fei watched the sea surface intently. The Kun, a being not so easily vanquished, still showed signs of life. As long as its life force persisted, it wouldn't die. But how could it recover from being reduced to shards?
