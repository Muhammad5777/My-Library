53. Lin Fei Is Here

A strong aura emanated from Chen Yuan's body.

Miss Zhang was just an ordinary woman. Facing this kind of pressure, her face turned somewhat pale.

But she endured it. At least on the surface, she appeared calm.

"Young Hero Chen, I apologize. Zhang family has no intention of continuing to offer our patronage to you." Finally, Miss Zhang slowly spoke. And her tone was decisive.

"Bang!"

Chen Yuan's fist smashed into the armrest of the chair.

"Crack!"

Instantly, the chair was shattered into pieces. Chen Yuan suddenly stood up.

"Swoosh, swoosh, swoosh!"

A large number of Zhang family guards rushed in from outside the door. These guards all drew out their sabers. surrounding Chen Yuan and the others in the middle.

However, these guards seemed quite nervous. After all, they were facing an acquired martial artist from Autumn Haze Mountain!

"Good, very good! It seems that Miss Zhang is determined. Then my and Zhang family's accounts from these years should be settled!" Chen Yuan laughed in anger.

"Wait a minute." At this moment, Liu Hao spoke up. He took a slow sip of tea.

Looking at Miss Zhang with a calm expression, he said, "Miss Zhang, you've probably found a new patron, haven't you? Otherwise, you wouldn't be so anxious to sever the relationship with Junior Brother Chen. You can tell us who it is. Then we can also have an idea, how about it?"

After hearing Liu Hao's words, Miss Zhang fell silent.

She knew Chen Yuan came to "demand justice." The Zhang family could not intimidate Chen Yuan. Perhaps only Lin Fei could.

Moreover, the Zhang family's clan patronage was not a secret. It could be figured out by inquiring in Luo City.

"Lin Fei, an Inner Sect disciple of the Nine Profound Sect!" Miss Zhang slowly spoke.

"What? What did you say?" Wang Xuan widened her eyes. A strange expression appeared on her face.

Lin Fei, an Inner Sect disciple of the Nine Profound Sect.

Same name, same surname, and was even an Inner Sect disciple of the Nine Profound Sect.

Was it a coincidence? But, it didn't seem like it. It wasn't that coincidental!

But Lin Fei had just become an inner disciple of the Nine Profound Sect, how could he be a patron by the Zhang family? Moreover, Chen Yuan was removed.

One should know that Chen Yuan was an acquired martial artist, even if he didn't make a breakthrough recently, he was still at the ninth level of Body Refining.

"Miss Zhang, are you talking about Lin Fei from Zhou Mountain Mansion City? Isn't he just at the Body Refining level?" Wang Xuan asked.

"Do you know him?" Miss Zhang was also surprised. These people were all disciples of Autumn Haze Mountain. Could it be that they also knew Lin Fei from Nine Profound Sect?

Wang Xuan widened her eyes. It was indeed Lin Fei from Zhou Mountain Mansion City that she knew, but he was just a mere Body Refining martial artist who had only recently entered the inner sect of Nine Profound Sect and was only at the seventh level of Body Refining. How could he become a patron of the Zhang family?

Chen Yuan and Liu Hao were also surprised. The new patron of the Zhang family was actually just a Body Refining martial artist.

"Hahaha, what a Lin Fei! Miss Zhang, I don't know how he tricked you. But do you think a mere Body Refining martial artist can protect the Zhang family's business and deter the small fry from all sides? Don't destroy the foundation of the Zhang family in your hands." Chen Yuan sneered.

Miss Zhang also realized that these people really knew Lin Fei, but they seemed to look down on him. After all, Lin Fei was a top genius who could kill acquired martial artists above his level. Were these inner disciples of Autumn Haze Mountain too arrogant or self-important?

"If there's nothing else, please leave. Escort them out!" Miss Zhang's tone was not polite at all, giving the order to kick them out.

"Hehe, are you going to kick us out now? But I want to settle accounts with you, Miss Zhang. How many benefits has the Zhang family gained over the years because of my reputation? The Zhang family needs to return everything they earned! Otherwise, these mere Body Refining guards won't be able to stop me!"

Chen Yuan pulled out his long sword with a "clang". The sharp edge of the sword made the surrounding guards feel a chill in their necks and take a step back involuntarily. If they really fought, they would probably be cut by Chen Yuan's sword before they even got close.

This was simply not something they could handle. So, the guards all turned their gaze to Miss Zhang.

Miss Zhang got agitated. She never expected Chen Yuan to be so shameless.

However, she was powerless at the moment. A figure suddenly appeared in her mind. If that person were here, how could Chen Yuan and his followers act so recklessly?

"I have informed Lin Fei, he is the patron of my Zhang family. If you harm my Zhang family, he will not spare you!" Miss Zhang's voice trembled with anger.

"Ridiculous, he's just a Body Refining martial artist. Does he dare to threaten us? If he dares to come, I will kill him with one strike!" Chen Yuan proudly brandished his sword.

"Is that so? Lin Fei is here, and you can draw your sword now."

Suddenly, a cold voice echoed in everyone's ears.

"Whoosh."

Everyone seemed to see only a shadow. Then, a figure appeared in front of Chen Yuan.

"You..." Chen Yuan's eyes narrowed slightly. He was about to draw his sword without thinking, but his sword was motionless when Lin Fei had already unsheathed his saber.

"Swish."

Lin Fei swung his saber. The sharp saber blade seemed to cut through Chen Yuan's neck.

At the same time, ten saber shadows flickered by.

"Crack."

Lin Fei had already sheathed his saber.

Then, Lin Fei stood quietly in front of Chen Yuan. It was as if he hadn't moved at all. It seemed that everything that just happened was an illusion.
