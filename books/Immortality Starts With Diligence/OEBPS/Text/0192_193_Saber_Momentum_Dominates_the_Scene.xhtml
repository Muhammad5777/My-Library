"Boom."

This saber strike from Shen Lianxin was extremely terrifying. More than a thousand meters of saber light ripped through the void. Everywhere was filled with terrifying saber edges.

This saber light struck the array. Although this array was set up jointly by the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons, it was from the inside out. It was specifically used to trap Lin Fei, to prevent Lin Fei from escaping. Breaking the array from the inside was too hard. However, to break it from the outside was a piece of cake. At least, the power at the Foundation Establishment level could easily break it.

"Sss."

This saber light from Shen Lianxin instantly broke the array.

"Hahaha, ten Foundation Establishment True Persons! Try this sword of mine!"

"Whoosh."

Liu Yibai also drew his sword. This sword, its light reached the heavens and pierced the earth as if tearing the void. Every Foundation Establishment True Person present felt as if they were being torn apart.

A great battle broke out between the Martial Saints and Foundation Establishment True Persons. Once they got close, martial artists would have an absolute advantage. And now, it was actually close combat.

At such a close distance, whether it was Shen Lianxin's saber or Liu Yibai's sword, they both posed a substantial threat to each Foundation Establishment True Person.

Once hit by a sword, these Foundation Establishment True Persons couldn't withstand it. They would die from a single sword strike!

However, these Foundation Establishment True Persons were not like Yuan Kongzi who had just entered the Foundation Establishment. Moreover, they had made ample preparations this time. They brought the sect's treasures.

"Watch my Purple Cloud Heavenly Robe!"

One the Foundation Establishment True Persons, with a wave of his hand, a purple light enveloped many Foundation Establishment True Persons in front of him.

"Hum."

The saber light from Shen Lianxin and the sword light from Liu Yibai struck the Purple Cloud Heavenly Robe. But they could only ripple it slightly. Then, they disappeared into nothingness.

Magic treasure!

This was another incredibly powerful magic treasure. It could block one strike from Shen Lianxin and Liu Yibai, two Martial Saints. At the same time, in the void, a large Spiritual Qi vortex gathered and it covered the sky, exuding a terrifying pressure.

Foundation Establishment True Persons need to utilize Spiritual Qi to use their spells and talismans. Without Spiritual Qi, relying solely on spiritual power, the power of spells and talismans would not be so terrifying. Magic treasures also need Spiritual Qi to function.

Now, ten Foundation Establishment True Persons displayed their Magic Bodies together. The Magic Bodies naturally attracted a large number of Spiritual Qi of Heaven and Earth. Therefore, it had almost gathered into a large Spiritual Qi cloud now.

As the Spiritual Qi cloud grew more and more terrifying, with the spiritual power of the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons casting spells or talismans, under such a huge Spiritual Qi cloud, their power would definitely be earth-shattering. In this scene, both Shen Lianxin and Liu Yibai looked solemn.

"Heavenly Fire Art!"

It was the Heavenly Fire Art again. Originally a common spell. However, once cast by a Foundation Establishment True Person and there was such a terrifying Spiritual Qi cloud, its power would also become very terrifying.

Perhaps, it wouldn't kill Martial Saints, but under the Heavenly Fire Art, the Nine Profound Sect would be burnt to ashes. The Nine Profound Sect faced a crisis of extermination. Would Lin Fei save them? This was a clear stratagem.

The ten Foundation Establishment True Persons had reached a consensus long ago. Since they were going to take action, they would not hold back at all.

First, they would lure Lin Fei away. Then they would join forces to kill Shen Lianxin and Liu Yibai. By that time, they would focus all their efforts on besieging Lin Fei.

They all knew, among these three Martial Saints, the strongest one was definitely Lin Fei.

"Whoosh."

Liu Yibai immediately saw through the plan of the ten Foundation Establishment cultivators. So, he swung out a sword light.

This sword light contained a sharp edge, running through heaven and earth. The sharp true essence could cut everything. However, when his sword light struck the massive Spiritual Qi cloud, the result was like a mud cow entering the sea. Without a trace of reaction.

Liu Yibai felt a slight sinking feeling in his heart.

"It's no use, there's no power that can destroy such a massive Spiritual Qi cloud. The ten of us Foundation Establishment True Persons are not just about numbers when we take action together."

"Our Magic Bodies of Foundation Establishment True Persons can attract a large amount of Spiritual Qi. With Spiritual Qi, the power of our spells can be greatly enhanced. Even if you have three Martial Saints, you are doomed to die!"

The ten Foundation Establishment True Persons saw this scene. They were also greatly reassured! With the Spiritual Qi cloud, they were almost invincible. In other words, the power of any spell or talisman they cast would be amplified. How could they lose under such circumstances?

"Is that so?"

Lin Fei slowly spoke. He raised his head and looked at the Spiritual Qi cloud in the void. Then, Lin Fei's mind moved. The saber momentum erupted!

"Boom."

On Lin Fei, a translucent light appeared. This translucent light, with Lin Fei as the center, spread in all directions, instantly enveloping all ten Foundation Establishment True Persons.

"Hum."

Suddenly, the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons felt as if they were in a quagmire. It was as if they were carrying a mountain on their bodies, very heavy, struggling to move!

At the same time, Lin Fei's saber momentum was even more overwhelming. It extended to the terrifying Spiritual Qi cloud in the void in an instant. It even trapped the Spiritual Qi cloud.

"Disperse!"

Lin Fei uttered softly.

Suddenly, it was as if his words became reality. The Spiritual Qi cloud in the void actually began to shake violently and then rapidly shrank. In the end, it disappeared without a trace. The Spiritual Qi cloud had really been dispersed!

"Impossible!"

All the Foundation Establishment True Persons were looking at the Spiritual Qi cloud in the void with shock. Their hearts were filled with terror. Especially when the translucent light radiating from Lin Fei enveloped them, they felt their Magic Bodies and spiritual power seemed to be suppressed.

A light flashed through some of the older Foundation Establishment True Persons' minds.

"Momentum! This is, saber momentum? Just like the Great Chang Ancestor, he has realized the momentum?"

For a moment, the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons were shocked! Whether a Martial Saint has momentum or not is a completely different concept.

And now?

Lin Fei had actually evolved the momentum. Moreover, it was one of the most terrifying saber momentum! In the saber momentum, Lin Fei held an absolute advantage. He could suppress the strength of the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons by three to five-tenths! This is a terrifying number, almost suppressing half of their power, for anyone, is a huge blow.

Of course, this only referred to spiritual power. If the Foundation Establishment True Person were to use magic treasure, although magic treasure would also be suppressed by the saber momentum, it was just a small part.

"Saber momentum... Brother Lin, have you already reached this step?"

Shen Lianxin and Liu Yibai were slightly taken aback. Looking at Lin Fei, their eyes were somewhat complicated. They were all Martial Saints and naturally understood the meaning of saber momentum.

Originally, they felt that they had become Martial Saints and they were back on the same level with Lin Fei. However, it seemed now the gap hadn't been narrowed at all but even bigger! Because it's too difficult to comprehend the momentum.

Liu Yibai knew there might be Martial Saints throughout the generations of the Hidden Sword Sect, but there were no Martial Saints who understood the sword momentum.

Shen Lianxin also tried to evolve the saber momentum. However, it's simply impossible. He even felt that there was no hope in this life to evolve the saber momentum. He didn't expect that Lin Fei had already evolved the saber momentum. Lin Fei now was no longer just a Martial Saint. Once the saber momentum comes out, Lin Fei is a saber saint!

"Tongxuan!"

Lin Fei's gaze fell on True Person Tongxuan. He said that he would kill True Person Tongxuan. And now, it was his time to take action!

"Whoosh."

The next moment, Lin Fei took a step forward. At the same time, all the saber essence in Lin Fei's body instantly gathered in his hand, condensing a thirty meters heavenly saber. A thirty meters heavenly saber, that was enough!

"Slash!"

Lin Fei's eyes were cold. The thirty-meter heavenly saber fell on True Person Tongxuan's head. Again, within the saber momentum, the boost of the thirty-meter heavenly saber reached an unimaginable level. True Person Tongxuan seemed to feel the breath of death!
