116. Thirty Meters of Saber Energy

Lin Fei's slash was made with his hand instead of a saber. In the eyes of others, it seemed like child's play.

Lin Fei obviously had a saber, so why didn't he draw it? How could a fleshly hand replace a fine steel treasure saber?

However, in the next moment, everyone's eyes widened. Lin Fei's hand slashed down fiercely.

"Boom."

Like a volcanic eruption, a violent surge of blackish airflow burst out from Lin Fei's right hand.

It was actually condensed into a saber!

Saber Qi!

This was the purest Saber Qi!

For the first time, Lin Fei exerted his full strength to release Saber Qi.

Immediately, the Saber Qi followed Lin Fei's hand and erupted all at once.

A thirty-meter-long saber energy, descending from the sky, slashed directly at He Yuanzi.

"Splatter."

He Yuanzi originally used his flying sword. However, under the impact of Lin Fei's thirty meters saber energy, it was instantly cut down and broken in two.

At the same time, Lin Fei's saber energy landed on top of He Yuanzi's head. He Yuanzi originally had a magical artifact protecting his body, but Lin Fei's Saber Qi cut through it like cutting tofu.

Without any hindrance, it directly penetrated the protective shield of the magical artifact. He Yuanzi was split in half from the top of his head.

"Thump."

He Yuanzi's two halves fell to the ground. He might not have understood why he died even until his death.

"Saber...saber light? A thirty-meter-high saber light?"

"No, that's not saber light anymore. It's saber energy, similar to Innate True Qi, but with a terrifying saber intent!"

The women among the spectators whispered softly. At this moment, everyone was almost dumbfounded. They stared in amazement at the terrifying saber energy in the void.

Even innate martial artists, who could project Innate True Qi from a distance and even combine it with their treasure sabers, could only unleash a little saber light.

Half-a-meter saber energy was already impressive. A meter saber energy was shocking enough.

And now?

Lin Fei didn't use a saber. He only used a pair of fleshly hands.

With his flesh and blood body, he actually stimulated thirty-meter saber energy.

This was simply unbelievable!

"Miss... have you ever seen a thirty meters saber energy?" The maid asked with a trembling voice. The Miss didn't speak but stared intently at Lin Fei.

Thirty-meter saber energy! Not only had she never seen it before, but she had never even heard of it. And now, she was witnessing it with her own eyes.

A martial artist who could unleash a thirty meters Saber Qi and even kill a high-level Qi Condensation cultivator with a single slash!

Such means, such power, it was simply like a mythical tale.

She would never believe it if she hadn't seen it with her own eyes.

"He Yuanzi!"

He Qingzi was somewhat stunned. He hadn't made a move before because he thought He Yuanzi's single move was enough. All he needed to do was guard against Lin Fei's escape.

And now?

He Yuanzi was dead right in front of him. Killed by Lin Fei's thirty meters saber Qi.

A thirty meters Saber Qi? Was Lin Fei really a martial artist?

"Go!"

He Qingzi made a decisive decision. He didn't even bother with He Yuanzi.

He immediately unleashed his spiritual power and shot toward the broken temple's exit like a streak of light.

He Qingzi wanted to escape and was so decisive.

Facing a monster like Lin Fei, He Qingzi had completely lost his confidence.

How could he dare to make a move?

"Think you can escape?"

Lin Fei's eyes turned cold, and his hand faced He Qingzi's direction: "Go."

"Whoosh."

Immediately, a saber energy, like an arrow leaving the bow, flashed through the air and disappeared.

In an instant, it flew towards He Qingzi.

He Qingzi also had a protective shield from his magical artifact. Moreover, he had even cast a defensive spell. It was like two layers of spells.

However, facing Lin Fei's saber energy, was like cutting tofu.

The shield was instantly broken by the saber energy.

And in the blink of an eye, the saber energy penetrated He Qingzi's body.

"Splatter."

He Qingzi's figure paused.

Finally, his body fell heavily to the ground.

Motionless, devoid of the breath of life.

He was dead!

He Qingzi was also dead. Killed by Lin Fei's single saber energy with ease.

The strength of Lin Fei's saber energy even exceeded Lin Fei's own imagination.

Looking at the two halves of He Yuanzi's corpse on the ground and then at He Qingzi's corpse being washed by the rain outside the broken temple, Lin Fei's mind was stirred.

"The power of Saber Qi cultivated from the True Intention Scroll is specifically designed to counter Qi Condensation cultivators. The greatest reliance of Qi cultivators is the protective shield formed by spiritual power or magical artifacts. However, the transformed Saber Qi, which has merged with the saber intent, can easily cut through the defense shield of Qi cultivators like chopping vegetables. Once the defense shield is gone, Qi cultivators are naturally vulnerable!"

Lin Fei fell silent. He finally understood now why the founder of the Nine Profound Sect left the True Intention Scroll in the Ascending Dragon Chamber but didn't leave it for the Nine Profound Sect.

This True Intention Scroll seemed to be just a martial arts technique. But in reality? It almost specifically counters Qi cultivators.

If this were to be spread and known to Qi cultivators, would they let it go?

Perhaps those immortal sects and Daoist schools would have attacked the Nine Profound Sect. How could the Nine Profound Sect continue for hundreds of years?

The True Intention Scroll was truly powerful. If it could be cultivated to the perfection realm and achieve the status of a Grandmaster, wouldn't it be able to counter-kill Foundation Establishment Cultivators?

"The founder's talent is truly amazing. I admire him!"

Lin Fei couldn't help but admire the founder of the Nine Profound Sect in his heart. Although the founder of the Nine Profound Sect did not build the foundation and become a Martial Saint, Lin Fei knew the founder of the Nine Profound Sect was not inferior to a Martial Saint.

In terms of martial arts talent, he was even stronger than a Martial Saint! Otherwise, how could he create such a magical and profound True Intention Scroll?

The heavy rain continued. Occasionally, there was muffled thunder echoing between heaven and earth.

In the broken temple, the thick smell of blood permeated the air. Outside the temple, under the washing of the rain, He Qingzi's corpse could not even be seen with bloodstains. It was all washed away by the rain to the foot of the mountain.

No one could have imagined that in this remote and broken temple, two high-level Qi Condensation cultivators had died. If this news were to spread back to He Mountain Dao, it would surely shake the entire He Mountain Dao!

However, Lin Fei didn't care. It was impossible to hide. Even if he killed everyone in the broken temple, he still couldn't hide it. After all, the two high-level Qi Condensation cultivators of He Mountain Dao were here to intercept and kill Lin Fei.

Now, both high-level Qi Condensation cultivators were dead. Lin Fei was alive. The result, naturally, was self-evident.

Lin Fei turned his head. His calm gaze swept over the group of people in the broken temple.

All the guards and women lowered their heads. The ladies and maids were shivering with fear. Only the Miss, though also frightened, looked at Lin Fei with a somewhat "fanatical" gaze.

In her heart, a storm had already been set off. Martial arts could actually be so powerful!? Saber energy could be thirty meters high! With a wave of the man's hand, two Qi Condensation cultivators died. Without even a chance to fight back. This even exceeded her understanding of martial arts.

Lin Fei bent down, picked up the dry food from the ground, and tucked it into his arms. He didn't speak, nor did he pay attention to the group of people in the broken temple.

Lin Fei led the horse, facing the pouring rain, and walked out of the broken temple. His body was covered in a layer of saber energy. Even with the heavy rain falling on him, not a single corner of his clothes was wet.

Afterward, Lin Fei's figure gradually faded away and finally disappeared into the night.

"It seems like that man has gone far away. And he took the dry food with him."

The maid's voice trembled continuously.

"Yeah, he took the dry food. One portion of dry food in exchange for our lives. Let's go, we must leave this place immediately..."

With gritted teeth, the Miss had made a resolute decision.

Even if it was raining heavily outside, they had to leave.
