129. This Is My Saber

Silence!

The entire Martial Alliance Exchange Conference suddenly became quiet that even the sound of people's breathing could be heard. One could hear a pin drop!

Lu Zongyi felt something was off. It seemed like he hadn't died?

Lu Zongyi slowly opened his eyes. The first thing he saw was a fair hand in front of him. Following the direction of the hand, he saw that in this fair palm, there was actually a delicate, exquisite-looking flying sword.

The flying sword had no handle and was faintly silver-white in color. An endless sharp aura emanated from the flying sword. This was the flying sword that had just pierced through Lu Zongyi's protective True Qi.

In the hands of a Qi Condensation cultivator, a small flying sword could kill invisibly. Even a martial arts master like Lu Zongyi, who possessed Sword Intent, couldn't block it.

But at this moment, this terrifying flying sword was being held by a fair hand. Even though the flying sword was struggling violently, it was to no avail.

"Brother Lin?"

Lu Zongyi's eyes suddenly focused. It was Lin Fei!

Moreover, Lin Fei had actually caught the flying sword with his flesh and blood hand. How was this possible? This was a flying sword!

The flying swords refined by Qi cultivators were made of special materials. The material was better than any treasure saber. Very hard, almost indestructible, and extremely sharp.

Lu Zongyi had never heard of anyone being able to directly catch a flying sword with their bare hands. But now, the fact was right before his eyes.

Although the flying sword was constantly struggling and the sharp aura of the flying sword continuously cut Lin Fei's palm, trying to escape from Lin Fei's hand, the jade-like palm seemed harder than refined steel. No matter how the flying sword struggled, it was futile.

This scene shocked everyone. Even those innate experts and martial arts masters, were dumbfounded, with waves of astonishment surging in their hearts.

"Is it true that a mere flesh and blood hand can grasp the flying sword of a top-notch Qi Condensation cultivator?"

"Everyone says that 'Immortal Slayer Saber' has an incredibly sharp saber technique. But his body is so strong too. Is he even a martial artist?"

"Something's not right. There seems to be some power in Lin Fei's hand. Is it Innate True Qi?"

"Innate True Qi can suppress a flying sword and even prevent its struggle?"

Innate experts were shocked by this scene. Not to mention the acquired martial artists. They were beyond words with shock. Catching a flying sword with bare hands, was this still the martial artist they knew?

"Who are you?"

At this moment, Yuan Gangzi spoke. His expression was a mix of shock and anger. Knowing that his flying sword was caught by someone with their bare hands, he knew it was serious.

He was very familiar with his own flying sword. How could someone catch a flying sword with their bare hands? But the reality was right in front of him, and he had no choice but to believe it.

"Who am I?"

Lin Fei smiled. He looked down at the flying sword in his palm. Then, he gently applied force.

"Crack."

The flying sword shattered! Not only did Lin Fei catch the flying sword with his bare hands, he even crushed the flying sword with his bare hands.

"Puff."

Yuan Gangzi spat out a mouthful of blood. The flying sword and the Qi cultivator were connected in life. Especially this flying sword, which had been refined by Yuan Gangzi for decades. Now that the flying sword was suddenly destroyed, Yuan Gangzi naturally suffered some backlash.

"How dare you destroy my flying sword?"

There was still blood at the corner of Yuan Gangzi's mouth and his expression became distorted with anger.

"Who are you?"

There was a cold killing intent in Yuan Gangzi's voice.

"Didn't you just say that if you stand still and let me attack, you won't be harmed in the slightest? Now you're asking me who I am?"

Lin Fei's words made Yuan Gangzi's eyes suddenly focus.

"Immortal Slayer Saber!"

Yuan Gangzi finally understood. The martial artist in front of him was none other than the Immortal Slayer Saber, whose recent exploits had caused a sensation throughout Great Chang.

Before, Yuan Gangzi thought that the two Qi cultivators from He Mountain Dao were just too incompetent. They were killed by a mere martial artist. Even if it was an innate martial artist, what of it? As long as it wasn't a Grandmaster, killing any innate martial artist wasn't a problem for Yuan Gangzi.

However, now that he faced the real "Immortal Slayer Saber," he suddenly realized. Maybe it wasn't that the two high-level Qi Condensation cultivators from He Mountain Dao were too incompetent, "Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei might be truly extraordinary. At least, Yuan Gangzi had never heard of any martial artist who dared to catch a flying sword bare-handed, and without any harm to themselves.

Lin Fei released his hand. He casually threw the fragments of the flying sword to the ground. In fact, Lin Fei was able to catch the flying sword with his bare hands because of Saber Qi!

Lin Fei's Saber Qi had long been spread all over his palm. The Saber Qi far surpassed Innate True Qi that even a flying sword couldn't penetrate it.

No matter how the flying sword struggled, Lin Fei's Saber Qi firmly trapped the flying sword. Even more, Lin Fei unleashed his Saber Qi, easily crushing it.

Lin Fei looked at Lu Zongyi and said calmly, "Brother Lu, didn't you want to see my saber? Now, I'll show you my saber!"

The next moment, Lin Fei extended his fair hand.

"Boom."

Lin Fei's Saber Qi within his body erupted instantly. At the same time, the Saber Qi that burst from Lin Fei's palm was immense. In the void, saber energy as tall as thirty meters was directly condensed.

The terrifying sharpness radiated from it. Even a glance from afar would make the eyes feel stinging pain. Saber Qi was formed by the fusion of saber intent and a large amount of Innate True Qi. Every strand of Saber Qi contained saber intent!

The Saber Qi as tall as thirty meters suddenly spanned across the void. Even Lu Zongyi, a well-versed martial arts master, couldn't help but widen his eyes.

Shocking!

An unparalleled shock!

A typical innate martial artist, having a three meters saber energy was already extraordinary.

And now?

Lin Fei had a thirty-meters saber energy and it was ten or even a hundred times stronger than the saber light!

At the same time, the thirty-meter saber energy faintly gave people the feeling that it was like a peerless fierce blade. A "killing aura" emanated from the ten-meter Saber Qi.

"Watch closely, this is my saber! Heavenly Saber Art, slash!"

Lin Fei's eyes were cold. He directly waved his hand and slashed fiercely toward Yuan Gangzi in the distance.

"Boom."

The terrifying saber energy was surging and overwhelming. Yuan Gangzi wanted to avoid it at first moment. However, he suddenly discovered that a killing aura locked him in. It seemed that no matter how he dodged, he couldn't escape.

This was the Heavenly Saber Art! Below mastery, it condensed a Fierce Saber. It could emit a killing aura, locking onto the opponent's breath, leaving the opponent with no escape.

It was Lin Fei's first time using the Heavenly Saber Art to condense a Fierce Saber against an enemy, but it wasn't his first time using Saber Qi. Therefore, this time using Saber Qi was incredibly satisfying for Lin Fei!

"As long as I want, the saber is there! Victory without a saber. The saber is in the heart, and only then can this saber be unleashed!"

Lin Fei's words echoed in everyone's ears. Like the sound of a giant bell, it made all martial artists feel enlightened.

To what extent had his saber realm reached?

"This youngster, his saber technique can reach divine levels!"

After a long time, a white-haired martial arts master, staring at the thirty-meter saber energy in the void and said.
