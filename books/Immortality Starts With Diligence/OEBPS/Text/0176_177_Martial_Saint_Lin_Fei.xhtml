177. Martial Saint Lin Fei

Foundation Establishment True Person! The Foundation Establishment True Person of Guardian Primordial Mountain, Yuan Kongzi, stood in the void. His spiritual power was unabashedly radiating pressure. His gaze overlooked all beings, looking down at the Nine Profound Sect below.

In his eyes, no matter how brilliant the history of the Nine Profound Sect was, or how many astonishingly talented martial artists had been born, if one did not achieve Foundation Establishment, one was still a mere ant! Not to mention the current Nine Profound Sect, even if the founder of the Nine Profound Sect, the innate perfection Martial Grand Master, was resurrected.

So what?

In the eyes of a Foundation Establishment True Person, he was still an ant!

This was the true thought in Yuan Kongzi's heart. He even once thought it was a bit exaggerated. But now, after he became a Foundation Establishment True Person, he discovered it was not exaggerated at all.

Whether it was a Martial Grand Master or a top Qi Condensation cultivator, as long as they had not achieved Foundation Establishment, in front of the True Persons, they were not much stronger than ants.

Just like now, the Nine Profound Sect had thousands of disciples, and even a few Martial Masters exist. Even the top Qi Condensation cultivators, carrying magic treasures, would have to put in quite an effort to level the Nine Profound Sect to the ground.

But what about Yuan Kongzi, the Foundation Establishment True Person himself?

Just one Heavenly Fire Technique. An ordinary Heavenly Fire Technique, many ordinary Condensation cultivators could perform this spell. But, could those ordinary Condensation cultivators' Heavenly Fire Techniques be as terrifying as the Heavenly Fire Technique performed by Yuan Kongzi?

Heavenly Fire across the sky, forming a giant cloud of fire, hovering above the Nine Profound Sect! Once it fell, it would be like a meteor, Heavenly Fire descending. Not to mention the Nine Profound Sect, even a city would be turned into ashes by the Heavenly Fire. This was the terrifying aspect of a Foundation Establishment True Person.

For Qi Condensation cultivators, even the top Qi Condensation cultivators, when casting spells, could only mobilize their own spiritual power and use their own spiritual power to cast spells.

But what about a Foundation Establishment True Person? Once they shed their mortal body and reforge their Magic body, they could leverage the Spiritual Qi between heaven and earth.

The spiritual power of a Qi Condensation cultivator was limited, but the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth was infinite. Therefore, even though it was only a small Heavenly Fire Technique when cast by a Foundation Establishment True Person, it was a world of difference. It was like the flame of destruction. Very terrifying.

Looking at the panicked expressions of the Nine Profound Sect people, Yuan Kongzi felt it was boring. How could just this group of martial artists have caused Yuan Xing Zi and Yuan Chen Zi to fail?

No matter, he didn't bother to think about how Yuan Xing Zi and Yuan Chen Zi failed. Because, under his Heavenly Fire Technique, nothing else matters. The Nine Profound Sect was destined to be reduced to ashes.

"Heavenly Fire, fall!"

The next moment, Yuan Kongzi's mind moved. The Spiritual Qi between heaven and earth exploded. In the void, the giant fire cloud suddenly fell down.

"Whoosh".

Just at the moment when the fire cloud was falling, from somewhere within the Nine Profound Sect below, a beam of saber light instantly flew out.

The saber light shone. In an instant, it flew into the void. This saber light was a saber formed by the condensation of countless saber essence.

The saber was not large, just a mere thirty meters. Facing the Heavenly Fire cloud covering the entire Nine Profound Sect, the thirty meters seemed so tiny. However, it was this tiny saber light that slashed on the Heavenly Fire cloud.

"Boom".

The saber light slashed on the giant Heavenly Fire cloud. Suddenly, a violent and sharp energy erupted from this saber light in an instant. Endless saber edge instantly cut the giant Heavenly Fire cloud.

"Sizzle".

The Heavenly Fire cloud was split in half by this saber light. Then, it completely collapsed. The sky returned to calm. It was no longer the fiery red like a burning cloud.

"Whoosh".

Yuan Kongzi's gaze instantly became sharp. He shouted loudly: "Who is it?"

That saber light just now was definitely not something that an innate martial artist could slash out. No matter how strong an innate martial artist was, how could a saber light break his Heavenly Fire Technique?

Even if it was just his ordinary Heavenly Fire Technique, that level of power already surpassed the innate martial artist and Qi Condensation cultivators and reached the Foundation Establishment level. That was not the power of the "mortal".

Soon, Yuan Kongzi's eyes narrowed slightly. He saw a figure.

It was a young martial artist. From the Nine Profound Sect below, step by step, as if climbing the ladder of the void.

"Crossing the void, walking in the air, this... this is a Martial Saint?"

Yuan Kongzi's pupils contracted. He was extremely shocked in his heart!

Martial Saint!

He had never seen a Martial Saint. After all, the last Martial Saint of Great Chang had also passed away three hundred years ago. It was a Martial Saint from the Hidden Sword Sect.

Yuan Kongzi only learned about some characteristics of a Martial Saint from the sect's classics. He only glanced at it casually at the time. After all, Martial Saints and Foundation Establishment True Persons were at the same level.

It was clearly recorded in the secrets. Once martial artists achieved the Martial Saint stage, they would rebuild their Dao Bodies and possess a variety of incredible abilities.

Among them, crossing the void and walking in the air was the most obvious characteristic of a Martial Saint. And now, Yuan Kongzi saw such a martial artist. Crossing the void, walking in the air, and even capable of breaking his Heavenly Fire Technique with a single saber light.

If that was not a Martial Saint, what was it?

Lin Fei, step by step, as if strolling in a leisurely courtyard, slowly walked into the void. He did not hide his figure. Therefore, all the disciples of the Nine Profound Sect could also see Lin Fei's figure in the void.

Seeing Lin Fei's figure in the void, all the disciples of the Nine Profound Sect couldn't help but shudder. Their hearts were stirred up like a raging storm.

"That... That's Grand Elder Lin Fei?"

"It must be Elder Lin. So young, there's absolutely no mistake."

"Elder Lin has reached the void without any use of Innate True Qi, and there's even no power fluctuation on his body?"

"Crossing the void, walking in the air. If the legends are correct, it can only mean one thing. Elder Lin has become a Martial Saint!"

"Martial Saint, it must be a Martial Saint! Only a Martial Saint would have such great power!"

"People always say that Elder Lin is an extraordinary talent, his martial arts talent is as good as the founder of our sect. But now it seems that he is not only as good as the founder, but he has surpassed him! After all, even the founder did not become a Martial Saint!"

"Our Nine Profound Sect has finally produced a Martial Saint! Hahaha, even if the Foundation Establishment True Person from the Guardian Primordial Mountain comes, we in the Nine Profound Sect can sleep without worries!"

Countless disciples were fervently looking at the figure of Lin Fei in the void.

Most ordinary disciples knew what a Martial Saint represented. It represented the pinnacle of martial arts! It represented transcendence! It represented the ability to contend with a Foundation Establishment True Person, even to be superior!

Lin Fei, step by step, finally arrived not far from Yuan Kongzi. The two were only a hundred meters apart. Both could clearly see the other's appearance.

Yuan Kongzi took a deep breath and asked solemnly: "Who are you?"

Lin Fei stood with his hands behind his back, and said calmly: "Nine Profound Sect, Lin Fei."
