594. The Martial Ancestor Ceremony, Greetings from All Directions!

"By the way, Lin Fei," the Great Dream Ancestor began, "now that you have achieved the status of Martial Ancestor and mastered a complete Dao, I wonder if you could continue to perfect the Divine Dream Realm?"

The Divine Dream Realm was always of utmost importance to the Great Dream Ancestor. Now that Lin Fei had mastered a complete Dao, he could once again attempt to enhance the Divine Dream Realm.

"Master, I indeed have some insights that I could try implementing," Lin Fei replied.

He glanced at the Divine Dream Realm where Bai Cheng and Qian Xin had been continually trying to improve it, albeit with limited success. Previously, Lin Fei had founded numerous worlds, thereby making substantial contributions to the refinement of the Divine Dream Realm.

Now, as a creator of worlds who had fully grasped the Dao of worlds, Lin Fei found the process more intuitive.

With a thunderous "boom," worlds sprang up like bamboo shoots after a rainstorm, rapidly "emerging" one after another. Indeed, these were all created by Lin Fei, who now had the capability to create not just one but tens, even dozens of worlds at a time.

In the Divine Realm, the creation of minor worlds was virtually limitless—calling them infinite was no exaggeration. Lin Fei was now working on creating the world, hoping to establish up to a hundred thousand, even a million worlds to truly "perfect" the Divine Dream Realm.

Once there were indeed a million worlds, there was no doubt that the Divine Dream Realm would undergo a significant transformation, even amounting to a tremendous upgrade.

As for worlds of the True Monarch level, Lin Fei was now confident; these were worlds that could give birth to life, letting these small worlds develop a consciousness of their own. Of course, all of this would take a considerable amount of time.

Fortunately, the fourth layer of the dream, where time was abundant, was not lacking in this resource.

One day, two days, three days, four days, five days...

In the blink of an eye, five days had passed in the Divine Realm, which equated to fifty thousand days, or about one hundred thirty-seven years, in the dreamscape's fourth layer. In such an extended period, Lin Fei had created hundreds of thousands of worlds. The speed of creation was almost inconceivable.

"Alright, stop now," finally, the Great Dream Ancestor spoke. He looked at Lin Fei and said in a deep voice, "Lin Fei, the worlds you have created are truly remarkable. You have significantly remedied some of the deficiencies in the Divine Dream Realm. Now, the Divine Dream Realm is nearly thirty percent perfected, and you alone have contributed to more than ten percent of that improvement..."

The Great Dream Ancestor was immensely satisfied with Lin Fei's contributions. Lin Fei's arrival significantly impacted the perfection of the Divine Dream Realm.

Together with Bai Cheng and Qian Xin, they had spent many years improving the Divine Dream Realm, yet they had managed less than twenty percent completion. Progress had nearly stalled as they approached this limit, seemingly unable to advance further.

Unexpectedly, Lin Fei's involvement had swiftly contributed over ten percent more. With such progress, the Great Dream Ancestor felt hopeful that the Divine Realm could eventually be fully perfected.

"The Divine Dream Realm is now thirty percent complete?" he mused. "But this is essentially the limit. I can no longer continue to enhance the Divine Dream Realm—unless the worlds within it give birth to live and potentially evolve into True Monarch-level worlds. Such development could perhaps improve it by an additional ten to twenty percent."

This was Lin Fei's estimation. Strong as he was, his understanding was primarily in the Dao of worlds. He was not particularly adept in other areas, and perfecting the Divine Dream Realm required improvements across various aspects. Otherwise, the Divine Dream Realm would remain an elusive mirage.

"Ha ha, another ten to twenty percent completion would be sufficient. Then, the Divine Dream Realm would have a chance to be fifty percent perfected," the Great Dream Ancestor laughed contentedly. He had all the time in the world, being immortal. "Now at thirty percent, it will surely reach forty, fifty percent, or even more in the future. As long as the Divine Dream Realm continues to improve, it proves that my path is correct, and my methods are viable. This, in fact, is most crucial."

"Lin Fei, five days have passed, and your Martial Ancestor ceremony is about to begin," he continued. "I will accompany you out. Some of the old timers, who rarely show themselves, will be there. This time is actually an opportunity."

Lin Fei was somewhat surprised. The Great Dream Ancestor himself would make a real appearance in the Divine Realm? "Then I am grateful, Master."

Thus, Lin Fei's consciousness left Great Dream City and returned to the Divine Realm, specifically to the Great Dream Sect. Nowadays, the Great Dream Sect was bustling with activity.

Despite being a force that lacked presence, even within the Ancient Covenant, the Great Dream Sect was shrouded in mystery. Many were unaware of the true extent of the Great Dream Sect's power. After all, its members were all in Great Dream City.

Apart from those who practiced the Great Dream Heart Sutra, no one could enter Great Dream City. Therefore, it was rare to see members of the Great Dream Sect active outside; and even if they were, it was not evident they belonged to the sect.

Today was unlike any other; the Great Dream Sect was bustling with activity. In Ancient City, the headquarters of the Great Dream Sect was particularly lively, thronged by tens of thousands of people. The cause of this excitement was Lin Fei's Martial Ancestor ceremony. L

Ln Fei's ascension to Martial Ancestor was a monumental event, not only for the Great Dream Sect but for the entire Ancient Covenant, and even resonated throughout the Divine Realm.

Lin Fei was no ordinary individual. He had begun as the first Martial God of the Divine Realm and later became a legendary True Monarch. In just several years, he had ascended from Martial God to Martial Ancestor at a pace that was nothing short of astonishing.

Furthermore, Lin Fei had withstood nine divine laws during his trials; each tribulation was a spectacle that earned the awe of countless warriors.

"Is this the headquarters of the Great Dream Sect?"

"Great Dream Sect is truly mysterious."

"Now, though still enigmatic, the Great Dream Sect has given rise to a peak powerhouse like Martial Ancestor Lin."

"With two Martial Ancestors in a single sect, the Great Dream Sect must now be the leading power within the entire Ancient Covenant, right?"

"Indeed, who would have thought that the unassuming Great Dream Sect would leap to become the number one force in the Ancient Covenant?"

"With both the Great Dream Ancestor and Martial Ancestor Lin, the Great Dream Sect is not to be trifled with in the future..."

The crowd was abuzz with discussion. The depth of the Great Dream Sect's heritage was unclear to many, but now that it housed two Martial Ancestors, it was undoubtedly the foremost power in the Ancient Covenant.

Regardless of how well other forces in the Covenant developed, having two Martial Ancestors secured its top position. Aside from the supremes, who could guarantee dominance over the Great Dream Sect?

"Swoosh."

A figure appeared at the Great Dream Sect, causing a stir among the crowd.

"It's Martial Ancestor Lin!"

"It really is Martial Ancestor Lin."

"The aura of Martial Ancestor Lin seems the same as before; has he truly become a Martial Ancestor?"

"Even though the rumors have been swirling and the Great Dream Sect is holding a Martial Ancestor ceremony, I still find it hard to believe. Can someone really become a Martial Ancestor in just two or three years?"

"People like Martial Ancestor Lin are not ordinary; you cannot apply common sense to him."

"Martial Ancestor Lin is here, but what about the Great Dream Ancestor?"

"Yeah, the Great Dream Ancestor is the founder of the Great Dream Sect, why isn't he seen?"

"I've heard that the real body of the Great Dream Ancestor is quite unique and rarely seen. Could it be that even today, the Great Dream Ancestor won't make an appearance?"

Suddenly, the focus of attention shifted to the Great Dream Ancestor. Their curiosity about him was no less than their interest in Lin Fei!
