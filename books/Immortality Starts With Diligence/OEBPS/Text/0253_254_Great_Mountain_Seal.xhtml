254. Great Mountain Seal

"Is that...?"

Everyone's gaze was fixed upon the void. A saber, stretching for thousands of meters, radiated a chilling sharpness. Moreover, it exuded a divine, solemn, and overwhelming aura.

Holy Spirit Saber!

At that moment, every eye was drawn to the blade in the void.

Even the likes of Five Light True Person and Clear Light True Person observed the blade that spanned several thousand meters in the void, their hearts filled with astonishment.

"A Limit Martial Saint?"

The two Foundation Establishment True Persons exchanged glances, detecting a hint of surprise in each other's eyes.

"What does it matter if there's a Limit Martial Saint? Could he possibly slice through the vast ocean with just one swing?"

Five Light True Person scoffed. He then channeled more of his power, activating his magic treasure. Suddenly, the ocean was stirred up once more, creating five enormous waves, making it a total of ten waves that surged mightily, engulfing their way toward Fairy Mist Island.

"Another five massive waves are approaching! Indeed, someone must be manipulating a magic treasure. Otherwise, a mere spell couldn't possibly harness such profound power."

True Person Lu Zhen's expression darkened. At the same time, he noticed Lin Fei, wielding that grand saber in the void.

What was Lin Fei planning? Did he genuinely aim to sever these towering waves? How could that be possible?

Everyone knew that when one tries to cut water, it merely flows even more. Let alone these towering waves.

Lin Fei took a step forward, instantly positioning himself within the void. A humming sound resonated above his head as a flower emerged.

Qi Flower!

With the appearance of this colossal Qi Flower, the saber essence around Lin Fei surged wildly, channeling itself into the Holy Spirit Saber in the void.

Five hundred meters, one thousand meters, two thousand meters, three thousand meters!

Lin Fei's Holy Spirit Saber expanded, reaching a length of three thousand meters. And this was all forged from the concentrated essence of the saber. It stretched across the void, radiating a majestic divine might.

Witnessing this spectacle, many felt as if they were being suffocated. The force emanating from the saber essence, even before it transformed into Saber Momentum, was already so oppressive that they struggled to breathe.

"Qi Flower! Truly the mark of a Limit Martial Saint! Martial Saint Lin truly is Limit Martial Saint. Moreover, the saber he forged from his true essence is terrifying. Who could possibly withstand such a slash?"

For a moment, many Foundation Establishment True Persons silently weighed the odds in their hearts. They were certain they could not withstand it. Whether they invoked spells or utilized magic treasures, even if Lin Fei hadn't unleashed his slash, simply sensing its overwhelming presence made them realize they would be no match.

"Slash!"

Lin Fei too noticed the ten towering waves upon the sea. Yet, his gaze remained calm, seemingly indifferent to the addition of five more waves. To him, ten waves appeared no different than five.

With a deafening crash, Lin Fei swung his blade. As it descended, it felt to everyone as if the skies had darkened instantly. The entire expanse of the sky seemed to be consumed by this one slash, filled with inky void cracks, casting the heavens in a night-like shroud.

Moreover, as the blade fell, everyone felt as if the sky was collapsing and the earth caving in. It seemed as if this single slash could cleave the heavens and the earth.

With a resounding boom, the thousands of meters long Holy Spirit Saber finally descended, striking atop the ten massive waves. Instantly, the ten towering waves, which bore the momentum to engulf and annihilate everything, were cleaved apart under the saber's might. Yes, they were completely severed. Not only were the waves split apart, but Lin Fei's slash also landed upon the sea itself, cleaving the vast expanse of water in twain.

The terrifying saber's aura, vast and sweeping, ensnared the pirate ships on the sea, pulling them in, utterly obliterated. Hundreds of ships were annihilated in mere moments. Only a few Foundation Establishment True Persons and martial saints, sensing the imminent danger, managed to soar into the void and evade just before Lin Fei's saber descended. Otherwise, had they been caught in the wake of that slash, they would have surely perished.

"How is this possible?"

Five Light True Person's eyes widened in disbelief, seemingly unable to accept the spectacle before him.

Ten massive waves had been cleaved apart by the power of a single martial saint with a single slash. Even the sea had been split open. Now, the vast ocean, with the saber essence undispersed, was starkly divided into two halves, revealing a deep chasm that reached the ocean floor.

Such immense power was unimaginable, even to Five Light True Person and Clear Light True Person.

A slash that cleaved the waves. A slash that split the sea. A slash that obliterated hundreds of the pirates' large ships. It was as if a divine being had descended from the heavens, so majestic that all could only look up in awe.

At this moment, everyone, whether from the Fairy Mist Island or the remaining pirates, was deeply shaken, their astonishment unparalleled.

The representatives of the eight families exchanged glances, mutually astounded. Their ancestors had been martial saints, so they were no strangers to the capabilities of martial saints. But even with their knowledge, they had never encountered a martial saint of such terrifying prowess.

Is he still a martial saint?

Zhao True Person was utterly stupefied, his voice trembling, "True Person Lu, Elder Lin, is he really a martial saint?"

True Person Lu Zhen stared fixedly at Lin Fei. He had already held Lin Fei in high regard, even elevating him to the rank of the top ten in the Hades List. However, after witnessing that slash, he realized he had been mistaken. Severely mistaken.

Where did Lin Fei belong in the top ten of the Hades List? He was undoubtedly the number one spot, potentially the most powerful martial saint beneath the Golden Core True Person and the Martial Sovereign! Even within the Ancient Origin Sect, Lu Zhen believed it would be the same.

"How can a martial saint be this powerful?"

"Nevertheless, what of it? How could a mere martial saint know of our Foundation Establishment techniques? After all, we have the treasure bestowed upon us by our sect. Anyone below a Golden Core True Person will surely perish!"

Five Light True Person and Clear Light True Person exchanged glances once more. They had no intention of retreating.

The sect had dispatched them to Fairy Mist Island with the intention of taking it over, and they had even bestowed upon them precious treasures for this mission. If they were to retreat due to encountering merely one Martial Saint, the sect's punishment would be severe. It might even mean the end of their cultivation journey, extinguishing any hopes of crossing the tribulation to achieve the Golden Core stage. The cost of failure was too high; they couldn't afford it. Thus, they had no choice but to conquer Fairy Light Island and defeat any force that stood in their way.

"Senior Brother, let's combine our powers and unleash the magic treasure," Five Light True Person said solemnly. Even though he seemed somewhat dismissive of the Martial Saints and Foundation Establishment True Persons here, Lin Fei's slash had clearly demonstrated his formidable strength. Without using the sect's bestowed treasure, they would probably find it hard to defeat this Martial Saint. Their only option was to join forces and activate their sect's precious treasure.

"Great Mountain Seal!"

With a low chant from both Five Light True Person and Clear Light True Person, a murky light immediately flew out from Clear Light's bosom.

Another magic treasure!

As this magic treasure emerged, under the two's combined spiritual power, it expanded upon exposure to the wind. The grey light transformed into an ancient divine mountain that stood tens of thousands of meters tall, exuding an imposing might.

Everyone felt as if they had been struck by a heavy blow, their bodies trembling violently. The sheer presence of this divine mountain seemed to suffocate many of the Foundation Establishment True Persons.

"This aura, this magic treasure... it even has the essence of tribulation lightning. This is a Golden Core magic treasure that has undergone the heavenly tribulation!"

For a moment, True Person Lu Zhen couldn't help but exclaim out loud, his eyes filled with shock and awe.
