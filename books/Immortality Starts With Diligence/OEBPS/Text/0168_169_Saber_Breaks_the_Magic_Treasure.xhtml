169. Saber Breaks the Magic Treasure

Saber energy!

One thousand meters high saber energy!

And, it was emanating an extremely familiar aura.

He's back!

Lin Fei is back!

At this moment, all the disciples of the Nine Profound Sect were overjoyed to tears.

"It's Grand Elder Lin, it must be Grand Elder Lin returning!"

"Saber energy, this is Grand Elder Lin's unique saber energy!"

"Grand Elder Lin's saber energy is too terrifying. It pierces through the sky and earth. The saber intent it emits makes me feel like I'm suffocating."

"A one thousand meters high saber energy, Grand Elder Lin has become stronger again!"

At this moment, not just Zhao Kongming, in fact, even the ordinary disciples of the Nine Profound Sect knew that Lin Fei had returned.

Lin Fei's signature saber energy pierces through the heavens and earth, vast and overwhelming, rolling in. That was impossible to hide. Moreover, Lin Fei seemed to have no intention of hiding his figure.

"Hm? Lin Fei..."

He Yuanzi felt a terrifying force behind him. A terrifying saber intent directly rolling towards him. That terrifying power even made him feel like his body was being cut into pieces. That was the feeling of death! It made He Yuanzi's scalp numb. His whole body shivering.

"Mountain River Seal!"

He Yuanzi immediately withdrew the Mountain River Seal. Although, as long as his Mountain River Seal was pressed down, it could definitely crush Zhao Kongming and the other two.

But what's the point?

Just three innate martial artists. As long as Lin Fei was dealt with, he could kill these three innate martial artists at any time. For these three innate martial artists, he would have to face the risk of being killed by Lin Fei. Of course, He Yuanzi wouldn't be so unwise!

"Whoosh".

The next moment, He Yuanzi turned around abruptly. At the same time, the Mountain River Seal also quickly turned into a stream of light. Overwhelmingly, it howled towards the saber light that pierced through the sky and earth behind him.

At this moment, under that saber light that pierced through the sky and earth, there indeed was a figure, with every step spanning dozens of meters, step by step, he walked towards the gate of the Nine Profound Sect. The Grand Elder of the Nine Profound Sect, "Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei finally returned!

Lin Fei looked at the Mountain River Seal that was howling towards him, looking like a small mountain. He had a vague understanding. This was not a magical artifact but a magic treasure!

However, what if it was a magic treasure?

"Heavenly Saber Art, Slash!"

The next moment, Lin Fei replaced his hand with a saber and slashed fiercely toward the Mountain River Seal.

"Boom".

In the void, the one thousand meters high saber energy instantly descended from the sky, carrying the momentum of heaven's collapse. One slash landed on the Mountain River Seal and collided fiercely with this treasure of the He Mountain Dao.

"Bang".

Immediately, the two forces collided. The terrifying shockwave swept in all directions. Endless saber energy, wherever it passed, everything was leveled!

The huge Mountain River Seal was actually sent flying back by this one slash from Lin Fei. Even He Yuanzi couldn't help but shudder.

The spiritual power in his body was constantly surging as if he had been backlashed. He clapped his hands together and use spiritual power to stabilize the Mountain River Seal.

"What an 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei!"

He Yuanzi's eyes narrowed slightly. He looked somewhat uncertainly at a figure in the distance.

"Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei!

This name was now well-known. Not only in the martial arts world. In the Immortal Sects as well, it was also well-known.

Even top Qi Condensation cultivators like He Yuanzi actually felt quite wary of Lin Fei. This was the deterrence of "reputation" or "infamous reputation".

"Hmph, so what if you are the 'Immortal Slayer Saber'? Last time, the Five Directions Killing Array couldn't kill you. But this time, the headmaster has bestowed upon me the magic treasure Mountain River Seal!

The magic treasure is Foundation Establishment level power, you are just a martial artist, even if the founder of the Nine Profound Sect were to reincarnate, what about it?

You can't do anything to me. Under the Mountain River Seal, I don't know how many Martial Grand Masters have been crushed to death. Today, not only will I kill you, but I will also level the entire Nine Profound Sect!"

He Yuanzi was high-spirited. Even though the previous strike, the Mountain River Seal failed to crush Lin Fei's saber energy. But how many saber energies could Lin Fei have? He believed that with the Mountain River Seal in hand, he was invincible below the Foundation Establishment realm!

However, as his words fell. He Yuanzi realized something seemed a bit off.

Lin Fei's eyes were extremely calm. He just stood quietly on the spot. His gaze seemed as if he was looking at a cold corpse and the gazes of the others around him also carried a hint of fear.

"Senior Brother He Yuanzi, quickly look at the... the Mountain River Seal, it seems to have cracked...."

A disciple's face was full of horror and he was reminding He Yuanzi aloud.

"Huh?"

Only then did He Yuanzi take a closer look. He looked at the Mountain River Seal.

At this moment, there were countless patterns on the Mountain River Seal, dense and covering the entire Mountain River Seal.

Those were runes!

Each rune was very mysterious. It was inscribed by a Foundation Establishment True Person by hand.

It was these runes that allowed the Mountain River Seal to possess the terrifying power of a large mountain.

But now, those mysterious runes had actually begun to crack and the number of cracks was increasing. Densely packed, like a spider web.

In an instant, He Yuanzi's face turned deathly white.

"It's impossible, the Mountain River Seal is a magic treasure, it was refined by a Foundation Establishment True Person, comparable to the power of Foundation Establishment.

Without reaching the level of Foundation Establishment power, how could it possibly break a magic treasure? Our He Mountain Dao's Mountain River Seal is not just any Foundation Establishment magic treasure..."

He Yuanzi immediately mobilized the spiritual power within his body. Handprint after handprint, he crazily imprinted onto the Mountain River Seal, trying to stimulate the power of the Mountain River Seal.

"Crack".

But the next moment, the Mountain River Seal finally couldn't hold on anymore.

"Crack, crack".

One after another, cracks appeared on the Mountain River Seal. Each one was larger than the last, shocking to the eye. Even the aura of the Mountain River Seal was flickering continuously, fading in and out, gradually becoming dim and lightless as if in the next moment, the aura would completely disappear.

"No..."

He Yuanzi's spiritual power erupted completely, trying to save the Mountain River Seal. But it was already too late.

From the moment he activated the Mountain River Seal and forcefully collided head-on with Lin Fei's one thousand meters high saber energy, the fate of the Mountain River Seal was already sealed.

"Boom".

When the last strand of aura from the Mountain River Seal disappeared, the enormous Mountain River Seal instantly collapsed, transforming into piece after piece of rock, falling to the ground.

"Boom".

The Mountain River Seal itself was refined from a mountain peak. It was the Foundation Establishment True Person of He Mountain Dao, who used his heaven and earth penetrating spiritual power to summon a mountain peak, exhausting endless effort, to finally refine this treasure. But now, this magic treasure shattered into pieces and completely collapsed!

"Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei strongly returned to the Nine Profound Sect. One thousand meters high saber energy. One slash shattered the Treasure Mountain River Seal!

For a time, the entire Nine Profound Sect was silent. Whether it was martial artists from the Nine Profound Sect, or cultivators from He Mountain Dao and Guardian Primordial Mountain, were silent, as if they couldn't believe what was happening before their eyes.

"One slash shatters a magic treasure. Lin Fei, have you reached the Saint-level?"

Zhao Kongming looked at Lin Fei with fiery eyes. His expression was filled with a trace of shock that was hard to believe!
