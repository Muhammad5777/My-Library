84. Saber-drawing Technique Has Broken Through To Perfection

"Only Innate can contend with Qi cultivators!"

Lin Fei returned to his courtyard. This was what he learned from the Sect Master's words.

Qi cultivators were superior. Only innate martial artists could generally fight against them.

As for the old Daoist, Lin Miao, and even the black-robed Qi cultivator that Lin Fei encountered, they were all rogue cultivators. And they were very low-level rogue cultivators.

According to the Sect Master, orthodox Qi cultivators were the strongest! They had complete Qi techniques, magical artifacts, talismans, and formations. Only such Qi cultivators could be considered true Qi cultivators.

And when facing such orthodox Qi cultivators, acquired martial artists would have no chance. As for rogue Qi cultivators, if luck was on their side, even ordinary people could kill them. They were not worth mentioning at all.

Lin Fei didn't become complacent. Although he had already killed two Qi cultivators, Lin Fei knew very well that if he faced the orthodox Qi cultivators mentioned by the Sect Master, who had spells, magical artifacts, talismans, and so on, he would have no chance.

Days passed by, and in the blink of an eye, a month had passed.

Lin Fei's life in the Nine Profound Sect returned to its former tranquility. During this month, Lin Fei's Three Vitality Technique progressed slowly. Every day, it only increased its cultivation progress by 1%. There was still some distance from reaching the sixth level.

"Your Leaping Technique has reached perfection after days of diligent cultivation,"

The system's voice echoed in Lin Fei's mind.

"Leaping Technique has reached perfection?"

Lin Fei was somewhat surprised. This month, he had been focusing on the Saber-drawing Technique. He didn't pay much attention to his agility skills. However, unexpectedly, the Leaping Technique reached perfection. This was a pleasant surprise.

Nevertheless, the difference between the transcendence and perfection of the Leaping Technique was not too significant. At most, it was just a slightly faster speed.

Lin Fei's main focus was still on the Saber-drawing Technique. Because, after a month of hard work in cultivating the Saber-drawing Technique, it had reached 99%. Progress was extremely slow.

If it was only the Saber-drawing Technique reaching perfection, Lin Fei might not have paid much attention to it. Once the Saber-drawing Technique reached perfection, its power would be impressive, and the efficiency of nurturing the saber would also be enhanced. However, it wasn't worth Lin Fei's undivided attention.

The significance of the Saber-drawing Technique reaching perfection was its fusion with the Nine Shadow Saber Technique. And the Nine Shadow Saber Technique had long since reached perfection.

Moreover, the cultivation progress of the perfected Nine Shadow Saber Technique had reached 43%.

Lin Fei was looking forward to the fusion of the perfected Saber-drawing Technique and the Nine Shadow Saber Technique. Then, it might be possible to give birth to the saber intent!

Saber intent was the focus of Lin Fei's attention.

It is rumored that once a martial artist masters saber intent, they can be on par with Qi cultivators! Even more, when facing Qi cultivators, only those who have mastered saber intent can have a way to cope with them.

This saber intent was very important. Lin Fei had been looking forward to it for a long time. At this moment, the cultivation progress of the Saber-drawing Technique had reached 99%.

Lin Fei was quietly waiting in the courtyard. The breakthrough of the Saber-drawing Technique might happen within these few days. It could reach perfection at any moment.

Lin Fei didn't know how much time had passed. He felt that the Hidden Saber at his waist seemed to be vibrating.

"What is this..."

Lin Fei could clearly sense that his inner power was circulating wildly, entering the Hidden Saber in a special way, and then returning to his body from within the Hidden Saber.

With each round, the Hidden Saber accumulated terrifying power. This was the process of nurturing the saber. Moreover, Lin Fei had never felt the operation of the saber-nurturing as clearly as at this moment.

"Your Saber-drawing Technique has finally broken through to perfection after days of hard cultivation!"

"Boom."

As the system's voice echoed in Lin Fei's mind, a loud noise resounded in his head, followed by a segment of memory. A memory related to the "Saber-drawing Technique."

Perfection!

At this moment, Lin Fei's Saber-drawing Technique had finally reached perfection! The perfection realm of the Saber-drawing Technique also meant the perfection realm of the Saber-nurturing Technique!

At this moment, Lin Fei could clearly feel that the speed of nurturing the saber had increased by at least ten times!

Yes, ten times!

Endless inner power was pouring crazily into the Hidden Saber. If it weren't for Lin Fei's immense inner power, he might not have been able to sustain it.

At this rate, nurturing the saber for one day now was equivalent to ten days before. Nurturing the saber for a month was equivalent to nearly a year before!

This was too terrifying. Even Lin Fei himself felt it was too terrifying.

He never expected that the improvement of the Saber-drawing Technique upon reaching perfection would be so significant.

Lin Fei even vaguely felt that the Saber-drawing Technique should not be an acquired level martial skill. It could even be on par with innate martial skills!

Lin Fei didn't know how strong innate experts were, but he was certain that given a few months or a year to nurture his saber, the moment the saber was drawn, it would definitely slay an innate expert!

This was Lin Fei's confidence, or rather, his confidence in the perfection realm of the Saber-drawing Technique.

However, Lin Fei did not get up. He continued to wait quietly. The perfection of the Saber-drawing Technique was just the beginning.

What Lin Fei was really looking forward to was the fusion of the Saber-drawing Technique and the Nine Shadow Saber Technique! That was the true goal he had been waiting for all these days.

"Whoosh."

Sure enough, it didn't take long before a roar resounded in Lin Fei's mind. Following that, Lin Fei "saw" two balls of light appear in his mind.

Both balls of light were sabers. The two sabers were black and looked somewhat similar, but in reality, they were quite different.

Lin Fei could tell at a glance which one represented the Saber-drawing Technique and which one represented the Nine Shadow Saber Technique. After all, Lin Fei was very familiar with these two saber techniques.

Lin Fei was actually quite interested in the battle between the Nine Shadow Saber Technique and the Saber-drawing Technique. These were two completely different saber techniques.

The Saber-drawing Technique focused on killing with a single strike. No matter who the opponent was, it only took one saber! If it couldn't kill the enemy, then the master would die. It was an extremely extreme saber technique.

As for the Nine Shadow Saber Technique? It was a more common saber technique.

Normally, the Saber-drawing Technique would definitely be stronger. But the Nine Shadow Saber Technique was no longer the same as before. Now, the Nine Shadow Saber Technique was a single strike with thirteen shadows, not to be underestimated.

"Swish."

Both the Saber-drawing Technique and the Nine Shadow Saber Technique moved.

The two saber techniques were like two peerless saber masters.

At this moment, without a word, the two balls of light released their sharp saber edges and instantly flew toward each other.

The Nine Shadow Saber Technique had one saber with thirteen shadows, filling the sky with saber shadows.

The Saber-drawing Technique slashed out with one earth-shattering strike, leaving only that one saber between heaven and earth!

"Boom."

The two saber techniques collided fiercely. It was a head-on confrontation. Lin Fei's mind seemed to be shaking violently. The terrifying saber edge seemed to tear Lin Fei's mind apart.

"Crack."

In the end, the two saber techniques had their strengths and weaknesses.

When the Saber-drawing Technique and the Nine Shadow Saber Technique collided, the earth-shattering Saber-drawing Technique was clearly superior.

With just one slash, the huge black saber formed by the Nine Shadow Saber Technique instantly shattered into countless fragments.

The black light dots quickly flowed into the light of the Saber-drawing Technique.

"It's done, the Saber-drawing Technique still has the upper hand!"

Lin Fei knew that the outcome had been decided and the winner determined.

The Saber-drawing Technique won!

Suddenly, the light of the Saber-drawing Technique surged and even trembled faintly, as if it was about to explode at any moment.

This was something that hadn't happened when Lin Fei previously fused the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist with the Triple Wave Technique.

The fusion of fist techniques and saber techniques should be similar and not cause any problems.

But now, after the Saber-drawing Technique had merged with the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, it seemed that there were some issues.

What was the difference between the fusion of the two saber techniques and the fusion of the two sets of fist techniques?

Lin Fei thought about it.

The only difference might be that the fusion of the two saber techniques could give birth to the saber intent!

After all, the cultivation progress of the Nine Shadow Saber Technique had already reached 43%.

With the fusion of the Saber-drawing Technique and the Nine Shadow Saber Technique, cultivation progress had skyrocketed.

Perhaps the saber intent was about to be born!
