661. The Reward for the Task Issued by Lin Fei

A note from RighteousEgg

The realms in the Chaos are united under the name Chaos Realm.

"Hum."

The Illusory Realm descended. As it arrived, the colossal body of the Chaotic Gigaworm trembled slightly. Its eyes seemed on the verge of opening. Yet, they appeared immensely heavy. And remained shut.

Clearly, Lin Fei's Illusory Realm was exerting its influence. It seemed to "stabilize" the Chaotic Gigaworm, preventing it from awakening.

Seeing this, Universal Wisdom Supreme was astonished. This was the Chaotic Gigaworm, after all. And Lin Fei had managed to keep it in slumber. Such a feat was indeed far beyond an ordinary Supreme.

Moreover, the key point was that the Chaotic Gigaworm truly continued to sleep. Yet, this was merely the beginning. The Chaotic Gigaworm was not yet dead.

What Universal Wisdom Supreme needed was something inside the belly of the Chaotic Gigaworm. He could not simply delve into its stomach.

Thus, Universal Wisdom Supreme's gaze still rested on Lin Fei. He believed that Lin Fei must have a way. Otherwise, Lin Fei would not have acted so decisively. Since he had intervened, there must be a way! And indeed, Lin Fei had a method.

Upon the arrival of the Illusory Realm, after having "stabilized" the Chaotic Gigaworm, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm fiercely expanded.

"Swipe."

In an instant, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm swept up, engulfing the Chaotic Gigaworm within the Eternal Realm.

"Annihilate!"

Lin Fei activated the power of the Eternal Realm. This was a force comparable to that of a top-tier Supreme, able to easily obliterate a Supreme.

Under the annihilating power of the Eternal Realm, the physical body of the Chaotic Gigaworm began to gradually disintegrate.

A moment, two moments, three moments...

The Chaotic Gigaworm broke free from the influence of the Illusory Realm. But it was too late. Under the annihilating power of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, the Chaotic Gigaworm merely struggled violently for a moment, then was powerless to reverse its fate.

For Lin Fei's Eternal Realm had already obliterated nearly half of the Chaotic Gigaworm's body. And the annihilation continued. Soon, the Chaotic Gigaworm was completely obliterated.

Yet Lin Fei left the stomach of the Chaotic Gigaworm intact. From within it, a peculiar stone flew out. It seemed to bear a map.

"A map?"

Lin Fei inspected it closely.

It faintly resembled a place. Could it be a Chaos Realm? Could Universal Wisdom Supreme's ultimate goal be a Chaos Realm?

Nevertheless, Lin Fei did not inquire. Even if Universal Wisdom Supreme's objective were truly a Realm, what of it?

Lin Fei, for his part, had no demands of the Realm. He possessed his own Eternal Realm, nor did he need to become a Ruler of a Realm.

Thus, it was the essence of the Chaotic Gigaworm's flesh that Lin Fei found more valuable, far superior to any Realm.

Of course, for an ordinary Supreme, nothing could compare to an unclaimed Chaos Realm. An ordinary Supreme could achieve the status of Ruler by claiming such a realm!

At least, there was hope of becoming a Ruler. This is what many Supremes aspire to their whole lives—to become a Ruler.

Lin Fei tossed the stone to Universal Wisdom Supreme.

Universal Wisdom Supreme, overjoyed, exclaimed, "Thank you, fellow daoist Lin!"

Universal Wisdom Supreme had achieved his goal. He had been worried that Lin Fei might claim it for himself. After all, a Chaos Realm! Yet, Lin Fei seemed utterly indifferent. But, was he truly indifferent?

Watching the Universal Wisdom Supreme hesitate to speak, Lin Fei calmly stated, "Universal Wisdom Supreme, have no concern. I have no interest in Realms. If possible, could Universal Wisdom Supreme inform me where more Chaotic Behemoths might be found? I require more Chaotic Behemoths!"

Lin Fei's need was for Chaotic Behemoths, not Realms.

"Chaotic Behemoths?" Universal Wisdom Supreme frowned, then shook his head, "Chaotic Behemoths are exceedingly rare and are born within the chaos, leaving no trace. One encounters them only by chance. For instance, this Chaotic Gigaworm was also a chance encounter. As for other Chaotic Behemoths, I am unaware of their whereabouts."

"Very well, then I take my leave."

Having said that, Lin Fei gathered thirty chaos stones. And then headed straight back to Kunyu City.

During his return journey, the essence of the Chaotic Gigaworm's flesh gradually merged into Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Lin Fei could distinctly feel that his Eternal Realm seemed to have been slightly enhanced.

"System."

Back in Kunyu City, Lin Fei accessed the system to check.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Martial Ancestor

World Level: True Immortal Level World (Progress 3%)

Illusory Realm: Supreme Level

Lin Fei's world level remained at the True Immortal level. However, progress increased from 2% to 3%. Indeed, there had been an enhancement and a 1% increase at that.

This was all due to the essence of the Chaotic Gigaworm's flesh. But without the essence of Chaotic Behemoth's flesh, Lin Fei was helpless.

"It seems I can only continue to wait."

Lin Fei, in Kunyu City, continued to wait for some time. Every day, he would search for tasks related to Chaotic Behemoths in the mission hall. Unfortunately, he encountered none.

As for the chaos stones, Lin Fei now possessed thirty chaos stones. This was no small amount. Lin Fei even purchased a property in Kunyu City, preparing to stay there long-term.

Of course, he could only stay for up to a year. After one year, Lin Fei would have to leave. After all, it took Lin Fei a year from the Divine Realm to reach Kunyu City and the return journey would also take a year. Back and forth, that amounted to two years.

Lin Fei's agreement with Azure Supreme and others was to return once every three years. Thus, the time left for him in Kunyu City was just one year. Therefore, Lin Fei also needed to make good use of this year.

In Lin Fei's estimation, this time, his Eternal Realm needed to advance to at least a 10% completion rate. But this required many Chaotic Behemoths. At least seven more Chaotic Behemoths. Yet, to say nothing of seven, even finding one was difficult.

"Where can one find so many Chaotic Behemoths?"

Lin Fei promptly left a task. Whoever could provide him with information on a Chaotic Behemoth, even just its whereabouts would receive a substantial reward. Or, they could gain the friendship of a top-tier Supreme! Even more, they could ask a top-tier Supreme to undertake a task.

Lin Fei did not have many chaos stones. He could not offer many chaos stones. But he possessed the combat power of a top-tier Supreme. He did not mind revealing his identity and strength. He believed that this task reward would certainly tempt many.

The friendship of a top-tier Supreme was not easily obtained. Even more so, the opportunity to have a top-tier Supreme handle a matter. It was no easy feat to engage a top-tier Supreme.

And now, just for a piece of information about a Chaotic Behemoth, one could engage a top-tier Supreme. Where else could one find such a deal?

Thus, as soon as Lin Fei posted the task, merely two hours had passed when an additional person appeared outside his residence. A woman with an icy demeanor just stood outside the door.

"I know of a Chaotic Behemoth!"

Upon seeing Lin Fei, the woman spoke, each word heavy with gravity.
