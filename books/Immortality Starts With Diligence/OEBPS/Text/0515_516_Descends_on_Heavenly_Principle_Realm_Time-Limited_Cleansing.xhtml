516. Descends on Heavenly Principle Realm, Time-Limited Cleansing!

At the entrance to the Heavenly Principle Realm in the Divine Realm, a multitude of Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators had gathered. They hesitated to enter the realm, displaying a mix of reluctance and fear.

The Heavenly Principle Realm was on the verge of collapse, and inside, battles raged fiercely, with several Martial Gods having already fallen. Thus, intervening in the Heavenly Principle Realm was fraught with danger, and not everyone possessed the courage to do so.

However, they were unwilling to walk away, hoping for an opportunity to seize some advantages. They waited on the periphery, ready to rush in and share the spoils once the chance arose.

Suddenly, a streak of light flashed through the void. As the light dissipated, a figure emerged. Plain and unremarkable at first glance, this person's appearance sent a shock through some observers, their eyes narrowing in recognition.

"Is that Lin Fei, the Martial God from the Great Dream Sect?"

"Yes, that's Lin Fei from the Ancient Covenant Great Dream Sect!"

"Are you talking about the Lin Fei who joined forces with the Heart Demon Martial God and North Wind True Person?"

"Why is Lin Fei here in the Heavenly Principle Realm? Is he planning to intervene?"

"I heard that North Wind True Person has disappeared, likely fallen for good. Without him, wouldn't Lin Fei, a mere Martial God, be just setting the stage for others by intervening in the Heavenly Principle Realm?"

"Lin Fei has come alone, probably at the invitation of another Deity Transformation cultivator. Without North Wind True Person, does that mean there are no other Deity Transformation cultivators?"

"This Lin Fei is said to be incredibly powerful, bearing a resemblance to the once-famed Initial Evil True Person."

With Lin Fei's arrival, the many powerful beings gathered at the entrance of the Heavenly Principle Realm quickly made way for him. They certainly did not wish to be misunderstood by Lin Fei.

Observing this, Lin Fei felt a slight sense of sentimentality. In the Divine Realm, he was no longer an unknown figure. His mere presence caused others to automatically clear a path for him, signifying his widespread reputation in the Divine Realm.

However, it was likely more of an infamy than fame, especially after his actions in the True Spirit Realm, where he had offended many.

Regardless of whether it was fame or infamy, Lin Fei's arrival made others retreat, demonstrating his deterrence power. Approaching the entrance, Lin Fei scanned the situation inside.

The realm was in chaos, with Deity Transformation cultivators' avatars and Martial Gods engaged in fierce battles. The entire Heavenly Principle Realm was in a precarious state, possibly on the verge of collapse, which would release the world origin energy.

Lin Fei needed time to collect this energy and to make some preparations. Without further ado, he stepped forward and, with a "whoosh," entered the Heavenly Principle Realm.

The Heavenly Principle Realm was a world where everything was "large." Plants, rocks, mountain ranges, forests, and even the exotic beasts were enormous. It was inhabited by a race known as the "Giant Spirits," who were also quite large and ruled the entire realm.

However, the Giant Spirits had now completely submitted to the invading Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators, as their strongest were only comparable to Martial Kings, no match for the more powerful invaders.

As Lin Fei stepped into the Heavenly Principle Realm, he immediately felt numerous malicious gazes sweeping over him. Undeterred, he called out loudly, "All Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators in the Heavenly Principle Realm, listen up! You have one hour to leave the Heavenly Principle Realm. Anyone still here after an hour will be killed without mercy by me!"

His voice, calm yet clear, resonated throughout the entire realm, causing a stir both inside and outside the Heavenly Principle Realm.

"Is that Lin Fei? The Martial God who swept through the True Spirit Realm?"

"He's too arrogant, trying to clear the Heavenly Principle Realm. What is he planning? To emulate Initial Evil True Person?"

"Lin Fei had the help of the Heart Demon Martial God and North Wind True Person previously. Now, as a mere Martial God without any Deity Transformation assistance, even if he dominates the Heavenly Principle Realm, how much world origin energy can he really obtain?"

"We've spent thousands of years planning in the Heavenly Principle Realm. How can we just leave like this?"

"I don't believe Lin Fei would really clear the realm. Even if he tries, we Deity Transformation cultivators have numerous avatars. How long can he keep up?"

Many Deity Transformation cultivators and Martial Gods within the Heavenly Principle Realm were angered. However, those who refused to leave were mainly the Deity Transformation cultivators, relying on their countless avatars.

Martial Gods, on the other hand, hesitated. Despite their reluctance, they didn't have the confidence of the Deity Transformation cultivators. Offending Lin Fei and facing his wrath could lead to their complete downfall. After all, Lin Fei's notoriety was well known – ruthless and decisive. No Martial God dared to claim they could withstand him.

"Let's go. We'll leave the Heavenly Principle Realm first and see how Lin Fei handles this."

"Exactly, let's step back and watch how Lin Fei alone can collect the world origin energy."

"Lin Fei relies on his strength, but those Deity Transformation cultivators with numerous avatars won't leave easily. How many of their avatars can he possibly kill by himself?"

Many Martial Gods left the Heavenly Principle Realm with reluctance, uttering harsh words, but they still obediently departed. At the realm's passageway, streaks of light flew out one after another, each representing a Martial God leaving the scene.

Among them were several well-known and influential Martial Gods who had once stirred up storms within the Heavenly Principle Realm. Now, however, they left with faces grim and full of resentment.

This scene left onlookers outside the Heavenly Principle Realm in awe, and they couldn't help but envy the power of Lin Fei's reputation. The mere words of Lin Fei, the Martial God of the Great Dream Sect, were enough to make countless powerful beings leave obediently.

"Lin Fei's reputation is indeed widespread. The situation in the Heavenly Principle Realm is about to change dramatically."

"Mostly Martial Gods have left, while some Deity Transformation cultivators remain unmoved, obviously thinking that Lin Fei can't handle them," another added.

"Could Martial God Lin Fei be so rash? If he really can't deal with them, wouldn't his words turn into a joke?"

"Maybe Martial God Lin has a backup plan, or maybe not. But Deity Transformation cultivators with numerous avatars are indeed difficult to deal with. Just Lin Fei alone might find it difficult to clear the Heavenly Principle Realm."

"Perhaps among those Deity Transformation cultivators, there are some who will collaborate with him."

Amidst the speculations, everyone was eager to see how a single Martial God would 'cleanse' the Heavenly Principle Realm. Was Lin Fei there at the invitation of a Deity Transformation cultivator for cooperation?

But Lin Fei had asked all Deity Transformation cultivators in the Heavenly Principle Realm to leave, promising to clear the realm after an hour.

Many were closely monitoring the situation in the Heavenly Principle Realm. Time ticked by, and gradually, no more figures were seen leaving through the passageway. Finally, the hour passed.

"Whoosh!"

Lin Fei, floating in the void of the Heavenly Principle Realm, opened his eyes and declared, "The hour has passed! Those who have not left the Heavenly Principle Realm, need not leave anymore. Stay here forever!"

Lin Fei's voice, cold and resolute, was filled with a commanding and lethal intent.
