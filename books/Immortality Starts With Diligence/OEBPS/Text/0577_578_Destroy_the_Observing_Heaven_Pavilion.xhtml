578. Destroy the Observing Heaven Pavilion?

Many well-informed individuals had a sense that something unusual was happening in the Ancient Continent. They anticipated that the appearance of the Eternal Life Alliance might lead to chaos and conflicts. The top-tier factions were particularly focused on the alliance's actions, wondering who would be the first target once the alliance was fully operational.

In the Eternal Life Alliance's great hall, many high-ranking members listened to Lin Fei's words with a sense of awe and shock. The target of their first operation was none other than the mighty Observing Heaven Pavilion.

"Observing Heaven Pavilion! To unify the entire Ancient Continent, the Eternal Life Alliance must first eliminate the Observing Heaven Pavilion. Therefore, the first action of the Eternal Life Alliance must be directed at Observing Heaven Pavilion."

The members of the Eternal Life Alliance were deeply moved by Lin Fei's words. Observing Heaven Pavilion was, without a doubt, the most powerful force in the entire Ancient Continent, with an incomprehensible foundation. If the alliance were to successfully defeat Observing Heaven Pavilion, its influence would soar, and many things might not even require force.

However, the question remained: Could the Eternal Life Alliance actually defeat the Observing Heaven Pavilion?

"Leader, should we reconsider this matter?" one member asked. "Observing Heaven Pavilion is like an unyielding bone. It's said that they have a Martial God and a Deity Transformation cultivator, and their foundation is incredibly deep. If we attack them recklessly and fail, the consequences would be unimaginable."

"Leader, should we think this through and consider targeting another faction instead?"

Many members were not entirely in agreement. Observing Heaven Pavilion's reputation was too great, having stood tall in the Ancient Continent for a long time. The concerns were justified, given that the alliance had only recently been established, even with Lin Fei as their legendary Martial God.

Nonetheless, Lin Fei remained calm and said, "No, we won't change our target. It will still be Observing Heaven Pavilion. This time, the Eternal Life Alliance will spare no effort to annihilate Observing Heaven Pavilion! Also, feel free to release some information, no need to keep it secret. Let the entire Ancient Continent know."

Lin Fei made his arrangements step by step. His true goal was not to eliminate Observing Heaven Pavilion, which he could easily achieve. Instead, he wanted to use this as a way to intimidate the entire Ancient Continent, making all the factions submit without the need for sneak attacks. He aimed to demonstrate their overwhelming power, leaving no room for resistance or hope.

"Yes, Leader."

With that, the members of the Eternal Life Alliance began their preparations.

Ancient Continent was buzzing with excitement due to recent events. News had spread that the newly formed Eternal Life Alliance had chosen its first target: Observing Heaven Pavilion. This revelation had sent shockwaves throughout the continent, with discussions and speculations running wild.

"Is the Eternal Life Alliance crazy? Their first target is Observing Heaven Pavilion! Don't they realize the depth of Observing Heaven Pavilion's power and influence?"

"The Eternal Life Alliance relies solely on Lin Fei, the Martial God. If they fail to defeat Observing Heaven Pavilion, it could be a fatal blow to their alliance."

"But isn't Lin Fei a legendary Martial God of Ancient Continent? As long as he takes action, Observing Heaven Pavilion should have a tough time resisting."

"Lin Fei may be a Martial God, but Observing Heaven Pavilion is no pushover. Taking down Observing Heaven Pavilion won't be easy."

"Lin Fei visited the Divine Realm, and perhaps he has become a bit too arrogant, thinking that everyone in the Ancient Continent is weak. Maybe Observing Heaven Pavilion will wake him up."

Most people expressed skepticism about the chances of the Eternal Life Alliance and Observing Heaven Pavilion. Many believed that the outcome of this conflict would serve as a reality check for Lin Fei.

However, as the involved parties, Observing Heaven Pavilion remained unusually silent. No statements or news were released from their side. Inside Observing Heaven Pavilion, a sense of foreboding and worry prevailed.

Perhaps many people outside didn't understand what the "Divine Realm" was truly like, but Observing Heaven Pavilion did.

At this moment, Observing Heaven Pavilion had three supreme leaders, and only those within the organization were aware of their existence. Among them, two were Deity Transformation cultivators, and one was a Martial God.

Inside the grand hall, the three supreme leaders and core members of Observing Heaven Pavilion sat in deep contemplation. Finally, one of the Martial Gods spoke, "The intelligence is accurate. The Eternal Life Alliance released this information themselves. They want to establish their authority by targeting Observing Heaven Pavilion. Their move will likely happen within the next few days. Observing Heaven Pavilion is on the brink of life and death. What do you all think we should do?"

The words of the Martial God sent shockwaves through the assembly. The fact that the Eternal Life Alliance had willingly revealed their intentions indicated they were highly confident. Otherwise, why would they dare to appear so "arrogant"?

"Three Pavilion Masters, what exactly is the Eternal Life Alliance relying on?"

"Yeah, even though the Eternal Life Alliance has Lin Fei, he's just a Martial God. With three Pavilion Masters here, can't we stop him?"

"Even if Lin Fei is incredibly strong, the three Pavilion Masters should be able to hold him back."

"So, what's the Eternal Life Alliance's confidence or trump card?"

One of the Deity Transformation Pavilion Masters calmly replied, "The Eternal Life Alliance's trump card is most likely Lin Fei himself. You all may not be aware of how terrifying the Divine Realm is. Although Lin Fei has only been there for two or three years, with his talent, even in that short time, he could have made significant progress. Currently, who in the Ancient Continent can match Lin Fei? Remember, even in the past, Lin Fei was already renowned in the Ancient Continent and had few rivals. It's even more so now."

In truth, they had an even greater concern, though it sounded absurd and far-fetched. Was Lin Fei still just a Martial God? What if he had surpassed that level?

Despite how insane it sounded, it wasn't entirely impossible. Lin Fei's path of cultivation in the Ancient Continent had been remarkable, progressing from a relatively unknown figure to a Martial God within a few years. If he could achieve that, breaking through the Martial God realm to a level above it within two or three years might not be entirely out of the question.

Furthermore, there was another significant reason for their concern. Many Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators from the Ancient Continent had entered the Divine Realm and hadn't returned. How could Lin Fei, who had spent only a few years there, return unscathed? This alone suggested that Lin Fei was anything but ordinary.

"Very well, this time, the three of us will test Lin Fei's strength. If we can't win, then we'll disband Observing Heaven Pavilion. Our legacy has lost its meaning."

In reality, the three Pavilion Masters were quite pessimistic. However, they had no choice but to make the attempt. If it proved impossible, they wouldn't insist on continuing.

As they discussed their plan, a Nascent Soul cultivator arrived in haste. His face was filled with fear as he reported, "Three Pavilion Masters and elders, the Eternal Life Alliance, along with Lin Fei, have already arrived outside Observing Heaven Pavilion."

"They're here?"

The three Pavilion Masters were slightly surprised. They hadn't expected the Eternal Life Alliance to move so quickly. This day couldn't have been worse. The purpose of the Eternal Life Alliance was to unify the Ancient Continent, and this clash was inevitable.

"Well, let the three of us go and meet Lin Fei first. Let's see what kind of earth-shattering abilities this Martial God from the Divine Realm possesses."

With that, the three cultivators took a step forward, transforming into streaks of light and flying out of Observing Heaven Pavilion. The others exchanged glances and quickly followed suit, flying towards the outskirts of Observing Heaven Pavilion.
