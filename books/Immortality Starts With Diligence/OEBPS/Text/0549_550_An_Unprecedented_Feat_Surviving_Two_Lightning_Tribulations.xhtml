550. An Unprecedented Feat! Surviving Two Lightning Tribulations!

"The Host's World Dao has gained new insights. Progress in the World Dao has increased by 5%."

In Lin Fei's mind, the voice of the system suddenly resounded. This was indeed a long-awaited occurrence.

Lin Fei's system had been silent for quite some time. After all, there were hardly any cultivation methods left with him. The only one might be the Great Dream Heart Sutra. But the Great Dream Heart Sutra currently lacked a third level and could not be advanced.

"System."

Lin Fei accessed the system panel to check his status.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Martial God (Seven Lightning Tribulations)

Great Dream Heart Sutra: Second Level (Progress 1%)

Four Symbols and Five Elements Creation Technique: 3,500 Particle Worlds

World Dao: Progress 6%

Lin Fei noted the progress of his World Dao. It had reached 6%. He had withstood the ninth heavenly lightning of the seven lightning tribulations, entering the illusionary realm. However, his comprehension of the World Dao had only increased by a mere 5%. This was indeed too little.

At this rate, how long would it take for Lin Fei to fully master the World Dao and thus achieve the status of a Martial Ancestor? It seemed like an eternity away!

"Wait, the system considers the 'World Dao' as a cultivation method? But this method has always been impossible to improve on its own. Lacking key insights. To enhance the World Dao, one must have insights."

Lin Fei yearned to experience illusions like those of the sixth or seventh lightning tribulations. However, such illusions required surviving the lightning tribulation, and withstanding nine heavenly lightnings only then might it be possible to experience such an illusion again.

"The seventh lightning tribulation couldn't possibly harm me. Even the ninth heavenly lightning is no different. Now, I have acquired nine World Laws. These nine World Laws can create nine hundred Particle Worlds.

With the tens of fold increase in particles from before, the might of these nine Particle Worlds will be terrifying. Thus, even the eighth lightning tribulation, or the ninth heavenly lightning, should be easily traversable. I might as well muster all my strength and continue with the eighth lightning tribulation!"

Lin Fei's eyes shone brightly. His chest swelled with boundless courage. He was determined to continue the tribulation. To cross the eighth lightning tribulation and with unwavering resolve, withstand the ninth heavenly lightning.

Thus, experiencing the illusionary realm once more, allowing the World Dao to continue its ascent.

"First, create the Particle Worlds!"

Thus, Lin Fei began using the World Laws to create Particle Worlds.

"Boom, boom, boom."

The aura within Lin Fei surged as he created Particle Worlds. This continued elevation of power.

Outside the tribulation site, people saw Lin Fei standing still, not emerging for a long time, leading to their bewilderment. Until the aura bursting from Lin Fei grew stronger by the moment. Some began to speculate, their faces revealing shock.

"What is True Monarch Lin doing? His aura keeps increasing. Is he enhancing his strength?"

"Could it be that True Monarch Lin just gained some tremendous benefit? Hence, he is increasing his strength publicly?"

"What a terrifying aura. It's getting stronger and the speed of increase is accelerating."

"But why doesn't True Monarch Lin return to his cave dwelling to break through slowly? Why enhance his strength here?"

"True Monarch Lin is not one to show off. There must be a reason for this."

"Could it be that True Monarch Lin is not leaving the tribulation site because he intends to continue the tribulation here?"

"To continue the tribulation, to cross the eighth lightning tribulation? My heavens, this..."

Some had already guessed Lin Fei's intention. But it was indeed too audacious. He had just traversed the seventh lightning tribulation. And now, he was poised to undertake the eighth?

Though Lin Fei appeared to cross the seventh tribulation effortlessly, The eighth would undoubtedly be exponentially more challenging, perhaps even tenfold. Could Lin Fei withstand it?

After all, Lin Fei's goal was to endure nine heavenly lightnings. This was known to everyone.

Lin Fei was not merely seeking to survive the tribulation. He aimed to acquire nine Dao laws. To withstand the nine heavenly lightnings. And thus, reap immense benefits.

After all, Lin Fei was the first in history to have achieved True Monarch status but still continued to face tribulations. He was unique. For Lin Fei, this was an extraordinary opportunity.

Only in this way could he gain numerous Dao laws. Lin Fei would not squander such a chance. Thus, he would ensure that each tribulation maximized his gains to obtain the nine Dao laws.

But having just crossed the seventh tribulation, was Lin Fei confident in withstanding the nine heavenly lightnings of the eighth?

Some thought, Lin Fei was being too reckless. Or rather, too insane, too confident. Should he fail, the consequences would be severe.

However, regardless of their speculations, or their view of Lin Fei's recklessness, that was their perspective, not Lin Fei's.

Lin Fei himself had already made a decision.

Time ticked away.

"Whoosh."

Finally, Lin Fei reopened his eyes.

"Four thousand four hundred Particle Worlds! The World Laws have also increased to forty-three! Most importantly, with the addition of nine World Laws, if fused with the Saber World, it will directly elevate the Saber World to the True Monarch level!"

This time, Lin Fei's improvement was tremendously vast. More than tenfold? At least dozens of times!

The Saber World underwent a transformation. Nine hundred Particle Worlds, even far surpassing the might of the previous three thousand five hundred Particle Worlds. All of this gave Lin Fei the confidence to cross the eighth lightning tribulation. Even to withstand nine heavenly lightnings!

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei looked up again, gazing into the void.

In the depths of the void, there was a vast sea of origin. Lin Fei's consciousness instantly flew into this sea of origin.

"Boom."

This time, Lin Fei was a bit more cautious and careful. He didn't summon seven heavenly lightnings. Instead, he drew down a single one. He wanted to see Just how much the might of the eighth tribulation had increased.

"Boom."

Finally, the first heavenly lightning struck down. Seeing the heavenly lightning falling through the void, no one doubted Lin Fei's determination anymore.

Lin Fei had actually chosen to continue the tribulation. And it was the eighth lightning tribulation. Crossing two tribulations in succession, and in both, choosing to withstand nine heavenly lightnings.

Even the top-tier True Monarchs and in the mysterious depths, the gaze of some Dao Integration True Immortals, Martial Ancestors, were all fixed on Lin Fei. Their eyes revealed a hint of admiration.

Even Dao Integration True Immortals and Martial Ancestors admire him. This illustrates the magnitude of Lin Fei's current endeavor - what an extraordinary feat it is?

Outside the tribulation site, no one spoke. Everyone waited in silence. They were eager to see whether Lin Fei could create this miracle. Crossing two lightning tribulations in succession. And in both, withstanding nine heavenly lightnings.

Finally, the first heavenly lightning struck Lin Fei's physical body.

"Pfft."

The heavenly lightning was instantly annihilated.

A mere single heavenly lightning couldn't harm Lin Fei's physical body at all. Not even a single particle was destroyed. Regardless, the first heavenly lightning was still weak.

So, Lin Fei summoned the second heavenly lightning. Like the first, the second lightning's might had increased considerably. But it still couldn't annihilate the particles of Lin Fei's physical body.

Then came the third, fourth, and fifth heavenly lightnings. Up to the fifth heavenly lightning, none could harm Lin Fei's particles. Or, even if they did, the particles recovered in the blink of an eye.

Such regenerative power was even beyond the reach of a Martial God. Only a True Monarch possessed such terrifying recovery capabilities. Though the first five heavenly lightnings were insignificant, Lin Fei had begun to sense the might of the eighth tribulation.

The fifth heavenly lightning was almost at the limit of a Martial God. Undoubtedly, the sixth heavenly lightning would be at the True Monarch level!

Compared to the seventh tribulation, the might of the eighth tribulation had undoubtedly increased significantly. Starting from the sixth heavenly lightning, it was already at the True Monarch level. Yet, Lin Fei showed no fear whatsoever.

"Come."

The next moment, Lin Fei's consciousness plunged into the sea of origin. The sixth heavenly lightning struck down instantly!
