600. True Immortal's Interference!

The Eternal Light Realm trembled violently. Many thought the end of the world had come. Fear gripped everyone.

Yet, Lin Fei remained calm. Though the Eternal Realm was merely a small world and the Eternal Light Realm a True Monarch-level world, making the Eternal Realm seemingly weaker, the situation was reversed now.

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm actually suppressed the Eternal Light Realm. Moreover, the Eternal Light Realm had no power to resist. This was the difference when Lin Fei transformed into the world and fully controlled it. It far surpassed any other small world in the Divine Realm.

"Seize the world laws!"

After the Eternal Realm suppressed the Eternal Light Realm, Lin Fei could also suppress its world laws. He forcibly extracted the world laws from the Eternal Light Realm. The Eternal Light Realm shook violently.

Of course, Lin Fei left a trace of the world laws intact. This would severely weaken the Eternal Light Realm, but over time, it could slowly recover the lost world laws.

Despite the long recovery time, the benefit was that the Eternal Light Realm wouldn't collapse. However, for now, the Eternal Light Realm was in upheaval, akin to an apocalypse.

The Eternal Light True Monarch gritted his teeth but said nothing, his gaze fixed on Lin Fei.

"Retrieve."

Lin Fei had obtained the world laws of the Eternal Light Realm. He retracted the Eternal Realm. The Eternal Light Realm returned to normal, albeit severely weakened.

Throughout the entire ordeal, the Eternal Light True Monarch remained silent. He had sent a message to a True Immortal from his backing force, hoping the True Immortal would seek justice.

However, up until now, True Immortal had not appeared. It was known that True Immortals could traverse the void in an instant. There was no reason for such a delay. The True Immortal's absence spoke volumes.

"On to the next world."

Lin Fei did not linger. He immediately traversed space and disappeared.

Seeing Lin Fei vanish, the numerous True Monarchs couldn't help but start discussing.

"Isn't Martial Ancestor Lin too overbearing?"

"Aren't Martial Ancestors and True Immortals seldom supposed to directly attack a True Monarch-level world? How could Martial Ancestor Lin act so recklessly and directly target the Eternal Light Realm?"

"Usually, if a Martial Ancestor acted so brazenly, other True Immortals or Martial Ancestor would step in to stop them. At the very least, they would intervene. But this time? Many True Immortals and Martial Ancestors have remained silent and have not intervened. There's definitely something more to this."

"Martial Ancestor Lin is in a hurry to seize True Monarch-level worlds, even acting personally. Something must have happened that forces Martial Ancestor Lin to urgently enhance his strength. But it seems like Martial Ancestor Lin will target the next True Monarch-level world. Will no one really stop him?"

Amidst their discussion, there was a growing realization that something significant must have happened to prompt Lin Fei's aggressive actions. For a time, many were dissatisfied with Lin Fei's domineering approach.

However, Lin Fei was a Martial Ancestor, exalted and untouchable. They were powerless. Only other Martial Ancestors or True Immortals could contend with him.

...

In the vast void.

Lin Fei took a step forward. He saw a world not far away.

"Gaint Spirit True Realm!"

Lin Fei gazed at the world before him. This was also a True Monarch-level world, known as the Gaint Spirit True Realm.

Moreover, this world was quite peculiar. All life within it, whether plant, animal, or native life forms, was exceedingly large. Especially the native life forms.

Ordinarily, they stood about thirty meters tall. With successful cultivation, they could reach ten thousand meters or even tens of thousands of meters!

Hence, the name "Gaint Spirit" in the world's title was quite fitting.

Lin Fei did not act immediately. He summoned the Saber World.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei's Saber World descended.

"Let's see to what extent the world laws of the Eternal Light Realm can enhance the Saber World."

Currently, Lin Fei's Saber World was merely at the True Monarch level. Even at its peak, it was still only at the True Monarch level. When the great battle erupted and the strong from the Dark Demon Domain invaded, Lin Fei would have to face Martial Ancestor and True Immortal-level opponents.

Thus, no matter how powerful the True Monarch-level Saber World became, it would not be of much use to Lin Fei. Therefore, the Saber World must be enhanced.

Ideally, it should be elevated to the level of Martial Ancestor or True Immortal. Only then could it significantly bolster Lin Fei's strength and be effective in the impending war.

"Fusion."

After the Saber World descended, Lin Fei immediately brought out the world laws of the Eternal Light Realm. Instantly, the world laws merged into the Saber World.

"Boom."

The power of Lin Fei's Saber World surged dramatically. Moreover, the Saber World began to expand rapidly. It even started to undergo a slight transformation. The integration of the world laws had an immediate effect.

However, the Saber World still hadn't completely transformed to the level of Martial Ancestor or True Immortal. It remained at the peak of the True Monarch level.

The gap between True Monarch and Martial Ancestor or True Immortal seemed like a mere step, but in reality, the difference was vast, unimaginable. It was like an enormous chasm, insurmountable.

Now, Lin Fei's fusion with the Saber World was continuously enhancing it, merely narrowing the gap. The goal was to bring the Saber World as close to the level of True Immortal or Martial Ancestor as possible.

Nonetheless, transforming the Saber World to the level of True Immortal or Martial Ancestor was indeed very challenging. But as long as Lin Fei persisted, seizing enough world laws and constantly narrowing the gap, he would have a chance to elevate the Saber World to the level of True Immortal or Martial Ancestor.

After Lin Fei fused with the world laws, a glimmer of determination flickered in his eyes. His gaze then fixated on the Gaint Spirit True Realm before him.

"Eternal Realm!"

Lin Fei directly summoned the Eternal Realm, swiftly enveloping the Gaint Spirit True Realm within it.

At that moment, within the Gaint Spirit True Realm, countless beings looked up, their eyes filled with shock.

"What is that?"

"Why has the sky suddenly darkened?"

"It seems as if the Gaint Spirit True Realm is being blocked out!"

"That... that is another world!"

"Who is attacking?"

"Martial Ancestor Lin?"

Some of the True Monarchs of the Divine Realm seemed to have received some prior information.

Connecting the dots, they quickly deduced that Lin Fei had arrived.

Lin Fei had just seized the world laws of the Eternal Light Realm. Many hadn't expected Lin Fei to move so swiftly, without any rest, going straight from the Eternal Light Realm to the Gaint Spirit True Realm.

"Seize!"

Lin Fei, now adept at this process, quickly mobilized the power of the Eternal Realm. He directly stripped the world laws from the Gaint Spirit True Realm and integrated them into the Saber World.

This further enhanced the Saber World, bringing it a step closer to the level of True Immortal or Martial Ancestor.

As for the possible "collateral damage" to some beings in the Gaint Spirit True Realm, Lin Fei paid no mind. He had already minimized harm to the world's life forms. Otherwise, he could have easily obliterated the Gaint Spirit True Realm, resulting in countless deaths.

"Two True Monarch-level world laws are clearly far from enough. Continue."

Lin Fei paid no further attention to the Gaint Spirit True Realm. After merging its world laws, Lin Fei took a step forward, disappearing into the spatial passage once more.

When Lin Fei reappeared, he was in another vast and unfamiliar void. His gaze fell upon another True Monarch-level world ahead.

The Ancient God Realm! This was another renowned True Monarch-level world in the Divine Realm.

Just as Lin Fei was about to act against the Ancient God Realm...

"Swish."

In the next moment, a figure appeared before Lin Fei. It was an incarnation of a True Immortal!

"Martial Ancestor Lin, you have gone too far!"

The True Immortal, with an expression as cold as eternal ice, spoke sternly.
