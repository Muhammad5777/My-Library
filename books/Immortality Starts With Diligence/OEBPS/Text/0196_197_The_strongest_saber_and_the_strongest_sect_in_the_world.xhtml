197. The strongest saber and the strongest sect in the world

"Invincible..."

On the Ancestral Master Peak of the Nine Profound Sect, Lin Fei raised his head, looking at Liu Yibai and Shen Lianxin. It was Liu Yibai who just mentioned that the entire Great Chang had been spreading Lin Fei's name of Invincibility recently.

"What kind of invincible am I? I haven't even been able to transcend saber momentum. Maybe I'm invincible in Great Chang, but on the road of martial arts, it is far from being invincible..."

Lin Fei shook his head. Compared to being invincible, he was more eager to continue to enhance his saber momentum. He was even more eager to continue to improve his cultivation.

In recent days, Lin Fei had been exchanging martial arts insights with Liu Yibai and Shen Lianxin. Lin Fei was generous with his advice. He shared his understanding of saber momentum with the two of them. After all, the only martial artists in the entire Great Chang who were qualified to exchange ideas with Lin Fei now were Liu Yibai and Shen Lianxin.

Moreover, Lin Fei also figured out that, if he wanted to evolve saber momentum, he needed to meet certain conditions. It must be top-tier innate saber techniques that could cultivate saber intent. Otherwise, it would be impossible to evolve saber momentum.

Moreover, these saber techniques must be used to defeat powerful enemies, even kill powerful enemies. For example, a Foundation Establishment True Person!

However, the first condition was actually quite difficult. How rare is it to have a saber technique that can cultivate saber intent?

The Broken Saber Flow and the Mountain Cleaving Slash could actually be considered top-tier innate saber techniques, but they couldn't cultivate saber intent. Naturally, it is impossible to evolve saber momentum.

Lin Fei also exchanged some saber techniques with Shen Lianxin. Shen Lianxin had obtained one saber technique from the ruins of the ancient martial arts sect. The Mountain-River Saber. And after countless years of practicing the saber, Shen Lianxin summarized numerous saber techniques into one saber, the Ultimate Saber Slash.

These two saber techniques had been integrated into Lin Fei's saber momentum. Just waiting to kill the Foundation Establishment True Person, perhaps it could evolve into saber momentum again. At that time, it would be integrated into Lin Fei's Heavenly Saber Momentum. It would continue to enhance Lin Fei's saber momentum, and even give the saber momentum hope to reach transcendence.

However, in the whole world of Great Chang, the saber techniques that can cultivate saber intent are very few. After all, the secret treasures of the Nine Profound Sect and the royal family almost collected all the saber techniques in the world. It's too hard to find pearls in the sea. And, cultivation techniques are also a big problem.

Lin Fei asked Liu Yibai and Shen Lianxin. Neither of them had cultivation techniques at the Martial Saint level.

"Brother Liu, have the predecessors of the Martial Saints in the Hidden Sword Sect not recorded anything outside of Great Chang?" Lin Fei suddenly asked.

"Outside of Great Chang?" Liu Yibai looked deeply at Lin Fei. He slowly said, "Of course they have recorded. But, the predecessors also didn't know much. The Martial Saints of my Hidden Sword Sect, just know that if you want to leave Great Chang, you can only go to sea. But, what will happen when you go to sea, no one knows. Those who go to sea, whether they are Foundation Establishment True People or Martial Saints, none of them have ever come back. Maybe, going to sea is a dead end!"

Liu Yibai's words made Lin Fei fall silent. He raised his head, looking intently at Liu Yibai, and said, "Brother Liu, whether going to sea is a dead end, I don't know. But if we can't even find the cultivation techniques of Martial Saints in Great Chang, our martial arts will not make any progress. At most in five hundred years, like the supreme elder of the Great Chang royal family, our life limit will come and we will die. We can all feel it. Martial Saint is not the peak of martial arts! If we want to pursue the peak of martial arts, maybe, we can only leave Great Chang."

In terms of martial arts, Lin Fei was the strongest in the world and number one in the world of Great Chang!

But so what? A Martial Saint only has a lifespan of five hundred years.

Lin Fei had never forgotten what he was pursuing. His goal was longevity! Practicing martial arts can achieve longevity. So, he embarked on the path of martial arts. But, his longevity was not just a mere five hundred years lifespan. He hadn't reached the peak of martial arts yet.

Therefore, Lin Fei would continue to move forward. No matter how difficult it was, he would continue!

"If one day, I want to go to sea to seek the Dao. Brother Liu, Brother Shen, what are your plans?"

Lin Fei's gaze looked at Liu Yibai and Shen Lianxin.

Without hesitation, Shen Lianxin immediately said, "I'm all alone in this life, and my dream is to reach the peak of martial arts! In Great Chang, I feel that I can no longer make any progress. Even if I exchange ideas with Brother Lin, it is very difficult for me to evolve saber momentum. But martial arts is not just about saber momentum. I can also practice martial arts techniques. I have other choices, other paths. So, if Brother Lin goes to sea to seek the Dao, please take me with you!"

Liu Yibai, however, was silent. He didn't speak at all.

Lin Fei slightly smiled and said, "It's okay, it's too early to talk about going to sea to seek the Dao. Besides, even if I want to go to sea, I have to take care of some things first. Like, the Immortal Sects!

These Immortal Sects, seal their mountains, living for hundreds of years, and then produce Foundation Establishment True Person. Then they come out of the mountain, causing chaos in the world, and vying for power.

This cycle repeats over and over again. If I leave, the Immortal Sects will reopen their mountains. By then, not only the Nine Profound Sect will be in danger, but even the martial arts of Great Chang will be in danger!

There is only one way to completely solve the Immortal Sects. That is, to destroy the mountains of these Immortal Sects!"

Lin Fei's words were filled with a murderous aura. In fact, how easy is it to destroy the mountains of those Immortal Sects?

Liu Yibai shook his head and said, "About those Immortal Sects' mountains, actually, the predecessors of the Martial Saints in my Hidden Sword Sect also mentioned them. These Immortal Sects, especially the top ten Immortal Sects, all have special magic treasures, connected to the earth's veins.

They connect the protective array of the mountain with the entire earth vein. If you want to destroy the mountains of these Immortal Sects, unless, you have the power to break the treasured objects of the Immortal Sects all at once. Or, destroy the earth veins of those Immortal Sects."

But, both of these methods are not very realistic. The founding emperor of Great Chang, who was also a Martial Saint, and who also understood momentum, couldn't break open the mountains of those Immortal Sects.

As for destroying the earth's veins, that's even harder. The earth veins of the top ten Immortal Sects, connect the entire mountain range, extending for thousands of miles. There's no way to destroy the earth's veins.

So, those Immortal Sects can sleep without any worries. The top ten Immortal Sects have already sealed their mountains early, intending to temporarily avoid your sharp edge, Brother Lin. To destroy the mountains of these Immortal Sects, it's difficult, too difficult!"

Liu Yibai felt that it was almost impossible. Lin Fei was also very clear. If the mountains of the Immortal Sects were so easy to destroy, how could they have been passed down for a thousand years?

The oldest Immortal Sects had a history of thousands of years, or even longer.

"It's okay, why not give it a try? Among the top ten Immortal Sects, which is the most powerful one?" Lin Fei asked.

"Sky Reaching Sect!" Liu Yibai immediately said.

"Sky Reaching Sect? Brother Liu, could you please take me to the mountain gate of the Sky Reaching Sect."

Liu Yibai nodded and said, "What's so difficult about that?"

Shen Lianxin also said, "The strongest saber in the world, and the strongest mountain gate, are rare encounters! I have to see it too."

"Alright, let's go together! Let's see if my saber is sharper, or the mountain gate of the Sky Reaching Sect is harder?"

Lin Fei stood up. With Shen Lianxin and Liu Yibai also stepped into the air and disappeared into the horizon in the blink of an eye.
