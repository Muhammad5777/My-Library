178. Battle Between the Martial Saint and Foundation Establishment True Person!

"You are 'Immortal Slayer Saber' Lin Fei?"

Yuan Kongzi's eyes slightly narrowed, and a look of disbelief appeared in his eyes.

"Immortal Slayer Saber" Lin Fei. His name was well-known throughout Great Chang. He was aware of Lin Fei. After all, Lin Fei had slain top Qi cultivators from Guardian Primordial Mountain.

According to Yuan Kongzi's understanding, Lin Fei joined the Nine Profound Sect just four years ago, but for the first three years, Lin Fei did nothing notable, he did not display any talent. But in the past year, he made rapid progress. He went from the fourth level of Body Refining all the way to becoming a Martial Master.

Moreover, he even slew many Qi cultivators. He made a great name for himself in the martial world as the "Immortal Slayer Saber". And now, Lin Fei had actually become a Martial Saint? This was too shocking!

"Impossible! Exotic beasts in Great Chang have gone extinct. Without the flesh and blood of exotic beasts, how can martial artists reach physical perfection? If you can't achieve physical perfection, you martial artists can't possibly build a foundation to become a Martial Saint. The path of martial artists has been cut off!"

Yuan Kongzi stared at Lin Fei intensely. It seemed that he wanted to find Lin Fei's "flaw". Or rather, he wanted to find some evidence that Lin Fei was not a Martial Saint. However, no matter how he looked, it seemed that Lin Fei was a Martial Saint!

"Whether or not I am a Martial Saint, wouldn't True Person know if you tried? I have a saber strike. If the True Person can take it, you can leave safely. If you can't take it, then I will step over your body and ascend to the pinnacle of martial arts!"

Lin Fei stood with his hands behind his back, speaking calmly.

"Arrogant! Even if you are a Martial Saint, so what? My Guardian Primordial Mountain has been passing down the Taoist lineage for thousands of years. How can a mere Martial Saint like you shake it? To kill this True Person is simply wishful thinking. Very well, today this True Person has succeeded in building a foundation. I will get rid of you to proclaim the supreme name of this True Person!"

Yuan Kongzi was greatly angered. But there was not a shred of fear in his heart. The might of Martial Saints seemed to have disappeared from people's memories. Even the strongest Martial Saint was only a thing of three hundred years ago. There had always only been Qi cultivators slaying martial artists. When did it ever happen that martial artists stood above Qi cultivators? Even if they were Martial Saints, it wouldn't work!

Moreover, this time Yuan Kongzi did not come empty-handed. He was prepared! Guardian Primordial Mountain, with a thousand-year lineage, had a deep foundation. Among them, there were not a few magic treasures. This time, Yuan Kongzi also came bearing magic treasures.

Above Yuan Kongzi's head, a bead the size of a baby's fist suddenly appeared, floating in the air. The bead revolved around Yuan Kongzi, continuously spinning.

"Sea-Stablizing Pearl!"

Yuan Kongzi's eyes were cold. This Sea-Stablizing Pearl was a magic treasure of the Guardian Primordial Mountain's successive generations of True Persons. According to legend, it was a replica made from a remarkable magic treasure.

The Sea-Stablizing Pearl itself was made from the inner core of a special exotic beast from the sea. Although this one was just a fake one, it was not to be trifled with. It had a natural strong control over water. It was filled with a large amount of water. Once it was deployed, it would certainly be earth-shaking. It could be called the first magic treasure of the Guardian Primordial Mountain for attack! Even the "Heavenly Thunder" and "Earthly Evil" were far inferior to the Sea-Stablizing Pearl.

"Whoosh."

The Sea-Stablizing Pearl instantly turned into a stream of light and it was smashed towards Lin Fei. Lin Fei raised his hand and threw a punch.

"Immeasurable Fist!"

Lin Fei threw out a punch. The saber essence instantly covered his whole body. On his arm and fist, the saber essence also burst out instantly. Immediately, this punch blasted out. The void seemed to be trembling.

"Bang."

Lin Fei's fist hit the Sea-Stablizing Pearl. Immediately, he felt a strong force coming from the Sea-Stablizing Pearl. It sent him flying. However, his punch also directly bounced the Sea-Stablizing Pearl away.

This scene left Yuan Kongzi stunned. His face was full of shock. He knew how terrifying the Sea-Stablizing Pearl was. With just a casual strike, a mountain peak could be easily smashed through.

And now?

Lin Fei actually used his flesh and blood fist to hard clash with the Sea-Stablizing Pearl. Moreover, he even sent the Sea-Stablizing Pearl flying. How terrifying was this power?

"The power belongs to oneself, is this a Martial Saint?"

Yuan Kongzi muttered to himself. In the world, a Martial Saint hadn't been seen in three hundred years. Many people had forgotten the terror of a Martial Saint.

Little did they know, the physical body of a Martial Saint was the most terrifying weapon. Lin Fei's Dao Body was infinitely close to an unparalleled treasure saber.

Therefore, he wasn't using his flesh and blood to hard clash with the Sea-Stablizing Pearl but an unparalleled treasure saber with saber essence.

"Bang, bang, bang."

Lin Fei's punches became more and more fierce, constantly clashing hard with the Sea-Stablizing Pearl. The void was violently trembling, leaving the people below staring in amazement. Because, Lin Fei with a casual punch, sent the Sea-Stablizing Pearl flying.

The Sea-Stablizing Pearl fell onto the mountain peak of the Nine Profound Sect, instantly collapsing the mountain. Or, Lin Fei threw a punch across the air. The mountain on the other side would even be shattered.

Many disciples of the Nine Profound Sect scattered away. Their eyes were filled with fervor.

"The power of a Martial Saint shakes the heavens and the earth!"

"So, practicing martial arts to the level of Martial Saint also has earth-shaking power."

"Martial Arts, not inferior to the Immortal Dao!"

Especially for innate martial artists like Zhao Kongming, the shock in their hearts was even greater. They were staring hard at the battle between Lin Fei and Yuan Kongzi.

The battle between a Martial Saint and a Foundation Establishment True Person. No matter what era, that's hard to encounter. The power of a Martial Saint, at this moment, was being displayed in front of everyone in its full glory.

Yuan Kongzi's face was somewhat ugly. He had even used the Sea-Stablizing Pearl, but he was still unable to handle Lin Fei. His eyes flashed with a sharp light.

"We Qi cultivators are not just good at controlling magic treasures. This True Person also has talismans and spells!"

Spiritual power surged in Yuan Kongzi's body. While he was controlling the magic treasure, he was also drawing talismans in the void with spiritual power.

"Mountain Moving Talisman, Mountain Come!"

Yuan Kongzi drew a talisman in the void. Immediately, spiritual power operated. The talisman radiated bursts of golden light. Soon, the talisman disappeared. The next moment, outside the Nine Profound Sect, a small mountain peak actually began to shake violently.

"Rumble."

Following that, the small mountain peak was directly uprooted as if it was lifted by an invisible force. With a lightning-fast force, it howled and smashed hard toward Lin Fei.

"Bang."

The next moment, Lin Fei's body shook. His body was directly sent flying by the mountain. The Sea-Stablizing Pearl took advantage of this opportunity and smashed toward Lin Fei's head again.

Mountain peaks, the Sea-Stablizing Pearl, spells, talismans, magic treasures. These were the methods of Qi cultivators! Especially when performed by a True Person, it's even more miraculous.

Like this kind of "Mountain Moving Technique", Qi cultivators below the Foundation Establishment stage simply cannot perform it. The amount of spiritual power that it consumes was unimaginable.

Even Yuan Kongzi, also felt a wave of weakness in his body as if his spiritual power had been drained. Such a spell required an amount of spiritual power that even a True Person who had built their foundation must carefully execute.

However, Yuan Kongzi acted decisively. The effect of the Mountain Moving Technique was very good. It directly sent Lin Fei flying. Even more so, the Sea-Stablizing Pearl took advantage of the situation to rise again. Once it hit Lin Fei's head, Lin Fei would undoubtedly die!

"You have magic treasures, you have spells. But, I also have a saber! This one saber will cleave your Sea-Stablizing Pearl!"

Lin Fei's voice came from the void. The next moment, a dazzling saber light shot up into the sky.
