609. The Eternal Realm Undergoes Transformation

"Whoosh."

Finally, a figure emerged from the spatial channel.

Upon seeing this figure, the people within Gourd City seemed somewhat perplexed. Most did not recognize him. But someone did recognize the figure.

"Wait, isn't that True Monarch Lin from the Ancient Covenant?"

"Which True Monarch Lin?"

"Of course, the number one Martial God of the Ancient Covenant, the legendary powerhouse who withstood nine heavenly thunders. He has now achieved the rank of Martial Ancestor, and even recently, he held his Martial Ancestor ceremony."

"Right, it's Martial Ancestor Lin Fei, no longer just a True Monarch."

"Just having achieved Martial Ancestor status, can Lin be this strong?"

"That was a Demon Emperor he just burst apart, truly a legend of the Divine Realm!"

"To withstand nine heavenly thunders time and again is no small feat."

"But there are still seven Demon Emperors, and now, they certainly won't underestimate Martial Ancestor Lin anymore. The real trouble starts now."

"Can we coordinate with Martial Ancestor Lin to repel these Demon Emperors?"

For a moment, the mood inside Gourd City was a mix of joy and anxiety. Joyous because someone had finally come to their rescue, and not just anyone, but a legend of the entire Divine Realm.

Anxious because it was only Lin Fei alone. One against seven Demon Emperors and the vast army of the Dark Demon domain. Could Lin Fei withstand them?

Regardless of what the people inside Gourd City thought, Lin Fei moved through the spatial channel. With a sweep of his gaze, he immediately understood the overall situation.

Seven Demon Emperors! Beings in the Dark Demon domain are equivalent to True Immortals and Martial Ancestors!

At that moment, all their focus was on Lin Fei. And there was also the large army of the Dark Demon domain. Gourd City was indeed in grave danger.

Yet, facing all this, Lin Fei remained calm. When the seven Demon Emperors just exchanged blows, Lin Fei realized. These Demon Emperors were not so formidable.

Although he couldn't completely slay them, Lin Fei felt that destroying their bodies, preventing them from continuing to invade the Divine Realm, wasn't too difficult.

Especially since Lin Fei's methods, effective against numerous opponents, held a significant advantage.

"Kill!"

Lin Fei uttered a low shout, taking the initiative to strike first. This left the people within Gourd City even more astonished.

Facing such a tense situation, or rather, such powerful Dark Demons, Lin Fei dared to attack first. Just this alone set him apart from many other powerful beings. Very dominant.

Lin Fei transformed into the Eternal Realm. Instantly, a vast world spread rapidly like ripples on water.

"Buzz."

And the Eternal Realm directly enveloped the bodies of the seven Demon Emperors.

"Roar..."

The seven Demon Emperors instantly roared in fury, looking up to the sky. With their roars, their bodies instantly expanded, turning into towering, terrifying giants.

Their bodies were shrouded in black mist, darkening the skies and filled with a terrifying aura of destruction.

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm immediately felt immense pressure.

"Pff."

The Eternal Realm was torn. It seemed to have no resistance and was effortlessly ripped apart. After all, strong as Lin Fei's Eternal Realm might be, facing seven Demon Emperors, it naturally couldn't contain them. Being torn was to be expected.

Lin Fei did not rely solely on the Eternal Realm to subdue the seven Demon Emperors. He had other ace techniques up his sleeve. Facing the seven Demon Emperors, he could afford no reservations.

"Illusory Realm!"

As soon as Lin Fei's words fell, the Illusory Realm descended.

The Illusory Realm, too, is a world. Only, it resembles a dream realm. It impacts True Immortals and Martial Ancestors on a mental and spiritual level, even capable of trapping True Immortals and Martial Ancestors. This is why Lin Fei's Illusory Realm can be termed True Immortal level.

Moments ago, the seven Demon Emperors seemed invincible as if they were about to destroy Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. But now, suddenly, they stood frozen. Their faces were blank without the slightest movement.

This scene was very eerie but also immensely shocking. For those in Gourd City—True Immortals, Martial Ancestors, or divine-level powerhouses. Each of them widened their eyes as if they couldn't believe what they were seeing.

"Why aren't those seven Demon Emperors moving? Are they stunned by Martial Ancestor Lin?"

"Is the power that Martial Ancestor Lin used the Saber World? But it seems a bit different. And wasn't Martial Ancestor Lin's Saber World only effective against True Monarchs and Martial Gods? Since when could it affect True Immortals and Martial Ancestors?"

"Correct, Saber World shouldn't be effective against True Immortal-level powerhouses. You don't understand how terrifying the mental will of a True Immortal-level powerhouse can be. Otherwise, how could they merge with the Dao? Such terrifying mental will, the Saber World simply couldn't affect them."

"The mental will of these Demon Emperors from the Dark Demon domain is also strong. Their growth process is equally arduous and even quite cruel and challenging. How could their mental will not be strong?"

"Right, the Demon Emperors of the Dark Demon domain are difficult to deal with, definitely without any mental flaws. Yet, even so, the Demon Emperors are still suppressed by Lin Fei's power..."

Many were puzzled and uncertain, wondering what technique Lin Fei was employing.

Actually, it seems quite normal. If Lin Fei can suppress the spirit of a Demon Emperor, then he can also suppress the spirits of True Immortals and Martial Ancestors.

There isn't much difference between them. Having one's spirit suppressed is terrifying...

It's like becoming a live target in an instant, immobile.

Imagine how frightening that is?

Of course, the suppression of the spirit itself cannot threaten the enemy, it is merely an auxiliary means. But Lin Fei still has the Eternal Realm!

Seeing the seven Demon Emperors, all restrained by the Illusory Realm in their spirits, motionless, Lin Fei naturally wouldn't waste this opportunity.

"Eternal Realm, kill!"

Instantly, Lin Fei activated the Eternal Realm. The power of an entire realm burst forth.

Previously, he couldn't overcome the seven Demon Emperors. That was because the seven Demon Emperors had joined forces. They had turned his Eternal Realm upside down. The power was dispersed. Naturally, he couldn't deal with the seven Demon Emperors.

But now, things were different. The seven Demon Emperors had their spirits suppressed by Lin Fei's Illusory Realm. Thus, Lin Fei could mobilize the power of the Eternal Realm and focus solely on one of them.

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm unleashed a frenzy of power. It fiercely landed on a Demon Emperor.

"Bang."

Instantly, the Demon Emperor's body exploded, crushed into dust by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. Even though they did not truly die, the invading bodies of the Demon Emperors were gone.

Unless they could be reborn from the heart of darkness in the Dark Demon domain, they wouldn't pose any threat to the Divine Realm for a while. And this was just the beginning. Next came the second, third, fourth, fifth...

Lin Fei successively annihilated the Martial Sovereign Demon Emperors. Only two Demon Emperors remained. Several breaths of time had passed. Thus, these two Demon Emperors also struggled free from the Illusory Realm and fully awakened.

And the first thing they saw upon waking was five of their fellow Demon Emperors, all slain by Lin Fei, turned into dust.

Therefore, these two Demon Emperors almost didn't hesitate. They rushed towards the exterior of the Eternal Realm, attempting to escape.

But, the two Demon Emperors could no longer tear open the Eternal Realm. After all, the previously torn Eternal Realm had long since recovered.

The two Demon Emperors struggle and slam within the Eternal Realm but to no avail.

"Bang bang."

Finally, the bodies of the two Demon Emperors also turned to dust. All seven Demon Emperors were dead.

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was now shrouded in a mist of blood.

"Wait, what's happening to my Eternal Realm?"

Suddenly, Lin Fei's thoughts stirred. He faintly sensed that there might be some changes in the Eternal Realm!
