700. Return to Dark Demon Domain

Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme had vanished. The surrounding Supremes exchanged glances, their minds heavy with the day's revelations—a day that would undoubtedly send ripples across the entire Chaos.

"Who between Supreme Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme will first achieve the status of a Master of Chaos?" they pondered aloud.

"Both Supreme Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme have transformed into Bodies of Chaos. Beyond their formidable strength, the real benefit is discovering the path to becoming Master of Chaos."

"If Supreme Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme can succeed, then so can we—the key lies in conquering the Forbidden Tower's ninth level."

"Alone, it's tough to conquer the Forbidden Tower. Should we, like Doomsday Supreme and Supreme Lin Fei, join forces?"

This indeed seemed a viable strategy. Inspired by Lin Fei and the Doomsday Supreme, some Supremes began to entertain the idea of an alliance to challenge the Forbidden Tower.

With plans forming, actions were taken, and alliances formed to venture into the Forbidden Tower. Success, however, was uncertain.

But for Lin Fei, these concerns were now behind him. Traveling through Chaos, his newly transformed chaotic body differed vastly from before. Where a journey once took a year, now, it could be completed in about a month—a qualitative leap.

Previously, as a Supreme, navigating through Chaos involved battling against the currents of chaotic energy, breaking through them to move forward. But Lin Fei's chaotic body faced no such resistance. Instead, the chaotic energy that once hindered him now propelled him, aiding his flight across the void.

Thus, in a fraction of the usual time, Lin Fei arrived at the Dark Demon Domain.

"Dark Demon Domain!" Lin Fei murmured as he beheld his destination. He had not returned to the Divine Realm but had come directly to the Dark Demon Domain.

"Hmm? Lin Fei, you've come again!" The Dark Demon Lord seemed to have spotted Lin Fei first. Perhaps he was particularly vigilant about Lin Fei, always monitoring the chaotic surroundings of the Dark Demon Domain.

A figure materialized suddenly—it was merely an avatar of the Dark Demon Lord.

"Indeed, I have returned!" Lin Fei spoke with an unperturbed tone, his voice steady and calm.

The Dark Demon Lord sneered, "Lin Fei, I know you're strong. But you can't kill me, nor can you destroy the Dark Demon Domain. As long as the Dark Demon Domain exists, so will I."

The Dark Demon Lord was blunt in his address, fully aware that no matter his actions—even begging for mercy—Lin Fei would never spare him. After all, he knew the location of the Divine Realm, which for Lin Fei, was an unforgivable sin. The Dark Demon Domain must be annihilated.

"You're right, Dark Demon Lord; without destroying the Dark Demon Domain, you cannot be dealt with," Lin Fei acknowledged. "Since that's the case, let Lin try once more!"

Lin Fei's resolve made the Dark Demon Lord anxious. Last time, Lin Fei had already tried and failed to subdue the Dark Demon Domain. Now that he had returned, did he possess the means to contend with it?

However, the Dark Demon Lord knew that unless one was a Master of Chaos, it was impossible to destroy a Chaos Realm—no Supreme, no matter how invincible, could achieve that. And to believe Lin Fei could become a Master of Chaos in such a short time was unfathomable to him.

"Lin Fei, since you are relentless, then try again," the Dark Demon Lord declared. "I want to see how you intend to destroy the Dark Demon Domain."

With those words, the avatar of the Dark Demon Lord gradually vanished, returning to the Dark Demon Domain.

Lin Fei did not step into the Dark Demon Domain but instead fixed his gaze towards it. He saw a Chaos Realm interwoven and entangled with countless rules, all maintaining the order of the realm.

The energy of a Chaos Realm was immensely formidable. Destroying a Chaos Realm was as challenging as the Dark Demon Lord had stated. Only a Master of Chaos could manage such a feat.

Yet, Lin Fei was determined to try.

The next moment, Lin Fei unleashed his punch, not utilizing the Eternal Realm but simply testing if the sheer force of eternity could shatter the Dark Demon Domain.

With a thunderous roar, Lin Fei's punch surged forward, a colossal force sweeping toward the Dark Demon Domain. He put his full might into this blow, and as it traveled, it drew in an increasing mass of chaotic energies—the closer it got to the Dark Demon Domain, the more potent it became.

This was Lin Fei's home field advantage. Now transformed into a Body of Chaos, he wielded the inherent benefits of fighting within Chaos.

Inside the Dark Demon Domain, the Dark Demon Lord watched in horror. He could scarcely believe his eyes. "Is this... Lin Fei? How has he grown so strong in such a short time? And this punch... it seems extraordinary."

"Block it..." The Dark Demon Lord immediately mobilized the full force of the Dark Demon Domain. Countless rules and the essence of numerous worlds converged, seemingly sensing the imminent threat and aiding the Dark Demon Lord in his efforts to control the realm's power with ease.

Yet, despite this, the Dark Demon Lord felt a grave concern. Lin Fei's punch was transmitting an undeniable threat, suggesting its extraordinary nature.

Finally, the punch made its impact on the Dark Demon Domain. The realm shuddered violently, its rules torn asunder, its world membranes pierced by Lin Fei's strike. Spaces within the Dark Demon Domain shattered, plunging the realm into what seemed like an apocalyptic upheaval.

"What's happening? Why is the Dark Demon Domain trembling?" people within the realm exclaimed. "The voids of the Dark Demon Domain are fracturing—is it under attack?"

"But the Dark Demon Domain is under the protection of our great Ruler— who would dare to attack?"

"Could it be Lin Fei from the Divine Realm?"

"Yes, apart from Lin Fei, who else would dare to strike at the Dark Demon Domain?"

"Can Lin Fei of the Divine Realm truly be this powerful?"

Within the Dark Demon Domain, the demons speculated wildly, their anxiety palpable. Some Supreme beings, aware of Lin Fei's formidable reputation, recognized that he was now the greatest threat to the Dark Demon Domain. If anyone could induce such turmoil, it would be Lin Fei.

Yet, there was no panic; although the domain trembled violently, it swiftly stabilized, indicating that Lin Fei's assault, while formidable, had limited impact. It seemed the Dark Demon Domain could withstand such attacks, and so could the Dark Demon Lord.

In the chaotic void, Lin Fei's eyes narrowed slightly. He could see the cracks his punch had created in the fabric of the Dark Demon Domain. The realm had quaked under his strike but had not shattered, and the Dark Demon Lord was still alive.

This confirmed that powerful though his punch was, it was not enough to destroy the Dark Demon Domain. However, compared to his last attempt, Lin Fei's strength had multiplied immensely, now capable of causing significant damage—a mere probe of his true capabilities.

"Eternal Light!" Lin Fei muttered. With the light expanding rapidly, it engulfed the space above the Dark Demon Domain.
