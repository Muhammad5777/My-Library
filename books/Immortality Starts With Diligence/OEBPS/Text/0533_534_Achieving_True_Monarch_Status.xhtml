534. Achieving True Monarch Status!

Lin Fei was seeing such an abundance of world origins for the first time. Previously, the most he had used were from the True Spirit Realm and the Heavenly Principle Realm. He hadn't utilized the origin of the Heavenly Secret Realm that he obtained later.

With so many origins at his disposal, once all were used, what level would Lin Fei's physical particles attain? Even he couldn't begin to imagine.

"Let's begin."

Having made his decision, Lin Fei didn't hesitate any longer.

"Devour."

Opening his mouth wide, a stream of origin instantly entered Lin Fei's mouth.

"Buzz."

Lin Fei's physical particles began to rapidly enhance. One stream after another, seemingly endless.

Lin Fei felt an unparalleled exhilaration. He had never experienced such an immense amount of origin. His physical body, like a sponge, fervently absorbed the origin.

Doubling, tripling, quadrupling, quintupling...

Lin Fei's physical particles were expanding, rapidly increasing to several times, even more than ten times their previous capacity. And it wasn't over yet.

The amount of origin Lin Fei had was quickly depleting. The origins of one hundred and three small worlds seemed abundant, but as Lin Fei's body started absorbing them at full capacity, they seemed not as plentiful.

In the blink of an eye, the origins of the one hundred and three small worlds vanished without a trace, entirely absorbed by Lin Fei's body!

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei opened his eyes. At that moment, he felt the transformation. A brand-new "metamorphosis." His power had already reached the brink of shattering the void.

So, when a strand of origin slightly enhanced his physical particles, Lin Fei felt this metamorphosis. However, Lin Fei had already experienced this transformation under the augmentation of the Saber World, so he wasn't too surprised.

"Is this what it means to shatter the void?" Lin Fei murmured softly. With a casual flick of his finger, he pointed outward.

"Pop."

The void shattered. Incredibly magical. It's noteworthy that Lin Fei hadn't even used the power of the particle worlds within him. But now, every movement of his physical particles could break the surrounding void. This was the true shattering of the void.

At this moment, Lin Fei had become an authentic Martial True Monarch!

Having become a True Monarch, Lin Fei could feel the constant constraints and limitations of the Divine Realm. It was as if he was living "in water." Resistance came from all directions, highly uncomfortable. This differed from the Illusory True Monarch.

Void Transformation True Monarchs comprehend the secrets of the void. Thus, they merge with the void, like fish in water, quite comfortable. Void Transformation True Monarchs are comfortable, but Martial True Monarchs feel the external constraints heavily, desperately wishing to break free.

This is the fundamental difference between the Martial and Immortal paths. The Immortal path emphasizes harmony with nature; merging with the world, complementing each other.

But the martial dao is different. It focuses on internalizing all might and power. When one's own strength reaches a certain level, it will inevitably conflict with the external world, or even be repelled by it. Hence the concept of shattering the void. If the void constrains, then shatter it completely!

"Boom."

With a thought from Lin Fei, the surrounding void instantly shattered, revealing a spatial passage.

Lin Fei stepped forward, entering the spatial passage. This wasn't Lin Fei's first time entering a spatial passage, but the experience felt the same.

Within the spatial passage, layers of pressure continuously compressed and repelled Lin Fei. However, the only difference was that Lin Fei had to rely on the augmentation of the Saber World to barely withstand the pressure of the spatial passage.

But now, things were different. Lin Fei had broken his shackles. His physical strength had reached the point of shattering the void. Thus, regardless of the pressure in the spatial passage, Lin Fei's body would not suffer any harm. Therefore, Lin Fei could now travel through spatial passages for a long time.

"Let's see how far I can traverse."

Lin Fei was curious to test his limits. So, he took a step forward in the spatial passage.

"Boom."

With a single step, Lin Fei faced even greater pressure, but he could withstand it. So, he took a second step.

The pressure increased further. The third step, the fourth, the fifth, sixth, seventh...

Lin Fei endured the immense pressure. By the tenth step, his physical body began to crumble inch by inch, reaching his limit.

"Saber World!"

The next moment, Lin Fei invoked the Saber World.

Instantly, the pressure on him diminished slightly. So he proceeded to the eleventh step, twelfth, thirteenth...

Approximately, Lin Fei could only manage thirteen steps. Having reached his limit, Lin Fei didn't force himself further. He stepped out of the spatial passage.

"Buzz."

Ripples of void spread out in all directions. When Lin Fei emerged, he found himself in a boundless, lifeless desert.

"This place... what is it?"

Lin Fei thought to himself and immediately inquired about the Great Dream City. Finally, he got the answer.

"The Great Wilderness? Completely devoid of life. A forbidden zone for living beings. Even possessing invisible rules that oppress any form of life. Not being a True Monarch, stepping into the Great Wilderness means certain death."

Lin Fei indeed felt the oppression of these rules. But he could bear it. This was one of the Divine Realm's unique forbidden lands, the Great Wilderness. As for the distance to the Ancient Covenant, it was immeasurably far.

Despite this, Lin Fei had taken thirteen steps in the spatial passage, yet still hadn't reached the end of the Divine Realm. The Divine Realm was simply too vast.

To instantly reach every corner of the Divine Realm, Lin Fei felt it was too difficult even for a True Monarch. Perhaps only a Dao Integration True Immortal or Martial Ancestor could instantly appear in every corner of the Divine Realm.

Lin Fei then swiftly traversed space returning to the Ancient Covenant and back to his secret chamber.

"Nearly there, my physical particles alone have reached the level of shattering the void. If I can obtain world laws and create particle worlds, my strength will likely surge dramatically. I might even be able to transcend several thunder tribulations in succession. I just wonder how many thunder tribulations I can ultimately pass?"

A spark of determination flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. This time, gaining so much origin had significantly enhanced his strength. Only by creating particle worlds could he fully unleash the effects of these origins.

"Let's begin transcending the tribulation, the sixth thunder tribulation."

Lin Fei didn't hesitate any longer. His figure flashed and he instantly left his residence, heading towards the tribulation grounds in the Ancient City.

At this moment, the tribulation grounds were bustling with people. It seemed that many Martial Gods were inspired by Lin Fei, all striving to advance and eagerly attempting their tribulations. Thus, there were many more people than before.

"Whoosh."

When Lin Fei arrived at the tribulation grounds, he witnessed such a scene.

"True Monarch Lin, you've finally arrived!"

"Hmm? You knew I would come?"

Lin Fei raised an eyebrow, a bit puzzled.

The Martial God at the tribulation grounds smiled and said, "True Monarch Lin, you have acquired the origins of over a hundred worlds. Coming to transcend the tribulation was only a matter of time, wasn't it?"

Lin Fei nodded. Indeed, this was not a secret. Anyone observant could easily guess this.

"Alright, is there a place for transcending the tribulation?"

"Of course, we have reserved a spot for True Monarch Lin."

Thus, under the guidance of the Martial God, Lin Fei stepped into the formation again.
