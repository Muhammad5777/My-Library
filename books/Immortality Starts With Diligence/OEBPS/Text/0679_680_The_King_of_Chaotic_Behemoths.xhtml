680. The King of Chaotic Behemoths

"The King of Chaotic Behemoths?" Lin Fei's eyes narrowed, a chill passing through them as he processed Clairvoyant Supreme's words. "Even the Lords of Chaos dare not provoke it, and you brought me here? This isn't just dangerous—it's a death sentence!"

His gaze hardened, carrying a faint glimmer of murderous intent. If Clairvoyant Supreme didn't provide a satisfactory explanation, Lin Fei was not about to hand over the Chaos Pearl. To Lin Fei, this seemed like a deceitful game of words, and he was not one to be trifled with.

Clairvoyant Supreme, noticing the killing intent in Lin Fei's eyes, quickly clarified, "Supreme Lin, you misunderstand. It's true that the King of Chaotic Behemoths resides here, but that doesn't mean there's no opportunity. If I proposed this trade, I must believe there's a chance to capture a Chaotic Behemoth.

The King has been slumbering for countless millennia and has not awoken in ages," Clairvoyant Supreme continued. "As long as we're not extraordinarily unlucky to wake it, there should be no issue. The real concern is whether we can withstand the Chaotic Behemoths themselves. There are not just one or two, but dozens! Even for a top-tier Supreme, it's uncertain whether they can withstand so many."

Reassured somewhat by Clairvoyant Supreme's words, Lin Fei nodded but remained cautious, his trust not easily won. Observing the surroundings, he maintained a vigilant guard.

He finally spoke, "I'll give you the Chaos Pearl. Clairvoyant Supreme, I hope all you've said is true." His tone was measured, devoid of overt threats. It was unnecessary—Clairvoyant Supreme knew full well the extent of Lin Fei's capabilities. Any deceit would spell his Doomsday, whether in Kunyu City or beyond.

Others might hesitate to act in Kunyu City, but Lin Fei was different. With a sweep of his Eternal Realm, all of Kunyu City fell silent, enveloped entirely by his formidable power.

At that moment, even if the Chaos Pearl was refined, unless one became the Master of Chaos, Clairvoyant Supreme would undoubtedly perish. Clairvoyant Supreme was not one to gamble with his life lightly.

Finally having secured the Chaos Pearl, he was exultant, and with a smile, he bade farewell: "Supreme Lin, I wish you all the best!" With those words, he transformed into a streak of light and departed the chaotic expanse.

"The King of Chaotic Behemoths..." Lin Fei muttered under his breath. Beyond the threat of the king, this place was indeed ideal for him. It teemed with Chaotic Behemoths, making it incredibly easy for Lin Fei to capture them without the need to wander far.

Moreover, the normally solitary Chaotic Behemoths were strangely cohabitating here—a clear sign of the slumbering King of Chaotic Behemoths' influence.

"Let's see if the King of Chaotic Behemoths truly sleeps," Lin Fei decided to test the waters. He wasn't about to plunge in recklessly. A hum resonated as Lin Fei detached a particle that took on his form. The avatar swiftly dived into the void that housed the Chaotic Behemoths.

In the next moment, the avatar twisted through dimensions and entered a bizarre new world. It wasn't exactly a world, but more a city reminiscent of Kunyu City—a city of Chaotic Behemoths.

"An alien life form?" came a voice, likely one of the behemoths. "A cultivator from the chaos, perhaps? Heh, the flesh of cultivators is quite the delicacy. Devour!"

As soon as Lin Fei's avatar entered the world of the Chaotic Behemoths, the ground seemed to spin. A monstrous maw, seemingly boundless, loomed before him and swallowed Lin Fei's particle avatar in one gulp, causing it to vanish without a trace.

"Hmm? Did my avatar just die?"

In the midst of chaos, Lin Fei witnessed everything his particle avatar had experienced. The world of Chaotic Behemoths proved perilous indeed; no sooner had he entered than he faced an assault. The behemoth recognized Lin Fei as a cultivator from Chaos. It devoured him without hesitation.

However, in that brief moment, Lin Fei had gleaned some understanding of the world of Chaotic Behemoths. He sensed no particularly dangerous entities or forces, even though he emitted an aura of chaos. There were no fearsome beings awakening or fixating on him—just a single ordinary Chaotic Behemoth.

For an ordinary Supreme, perhaps that Chaotic Behemoth would pose a significant threat, a slight misstep leading to being devoured. But Lin Fei was different. He welcomed more Chaotic Behemoths attacking, attempting to devour him—it would only allow his Eternal Realm to improve more.

"There may be only one chance here," Lin Fei contemplated. "Thus, I must seize as many Chaotic Behemoths as possible in this opportunity. Otherwise, if they become cautious or awaken the King of Chaotic Behemoths next time, it will become troublesome."

Lin Fei didn't plan to keep coming back here to hunt Chaotic Behemoths indefinitely. The King of Chaotic Behemoths, even in slumber, would eventually be roused by the Chaotic Behemoths.

Even if Lin Fei succeeded multiple times, a single failure in the presence of the awakened King would mean disaster, possibly even death. So, Lin Fei wouldn't take such a risk. He had this one precious opportunity and needed to make the most of it.

"Since that's the case..." Lin Fei hesitated no longer.

With a swift motion, he plunged into the world of Chaotic Behemoths. Inside, the behemoth that had just "enjoyed" his particle avatar's flavor seemed content, squinting its eyes in pleasure, still mumbling about the deliciousness of cultivators and yearning to devour more.

However, it quickly dismissed the idea of leaving, considering the King of Chaotic Behemoths and the rules of this place. This was a safe haven where it could rest and live without fear—everything here was regulated by the presence of the King of Chaotic Behemoths.

"Swoosh," another figure appeared before the behemoth.

"Is this... another chaos cultivator?" the behemoth paused in slight confusion, wondering how yet another cultivator had found this place.

"Eternal Light!" Lin Fei wasted no time. Upon spotting the behemoth, the Eternal Light burst forth from him, sweeping grandly toward the behemoth.

"Swish."

In an instant, the behemoth was enveloped and pulled into Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.
