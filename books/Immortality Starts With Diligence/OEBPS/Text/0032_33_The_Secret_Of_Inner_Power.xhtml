Unfortunately, when she goes back to the past, she isn't the only one who remembers the previous timeline. Everyone else does too.

The only saving grace—Rieren is reborn where the world doesn’t know her. Yet. With her identity one misstep away from a fatal revelation, Rieren must use her experience to forge the quickest path through a maze of rival sects, warring factions, and hidden threats, all to attain and surpass her former power before her greatest enemies close in.

Lin Fei, a body-refining martial artist, slew the Wind Chasing Fragrance. This made the group of martial artists in the hall realize the difference between a wandering martial artist and a disciple of a major sect.

Lin Fei rested for a while but found that his left hand was still sore and powerless, almost as if it were useless. This caused him to worry.

"I must have suffered internal injuries. Inner power injuries are not easy to recover from. Seems that I must hurry back to the sect to receive treatment."

Lin Fei's left arm was now immobilized, but he was not too worried. Once he returned to the sect, his arm would definitely be healed. He just needed to do so as soon as possible. Otherwise, his arm would be disabled.

Lin Fei looked at the body of the Wind Chasing Fragrance on the ground. He thought about how, as an acquired martial artist, the Wind Chasing Fragrance must have been skilled in acquired martial arts.

So, Lin Fei struggled to stand up and went to search his body of him. He took everything from the body, which included three secret manuals for sword techniques, body techniques, and acquired-level martial arts, as well as some silver bills.

Lin Fei counted the silver bills and found that there were over fifty thousand taels. This was a pleasant surprise, but it was also expected.

The Wind Chasing Fragrance had been wreaking havoc for such a long time, disappearing without a trace after stealing valuable items, so it was not surprising that he had managed to accumulate so much money along the way.

Besides the martial arts manuals and silver tickets, Lin Fei also found some porcelain bottles with various types of powders inside, including some aphrodisiacs and other types of drugs.

Mr. Liu also returned to the hall, accompanied by his daughter.

Mr. Liu was also aware of everything that happened in the hall.

He came in front of Lin Fei and hurriedly saluted, "Thank you, Hero Lin, for killing the evil thief."

Mr. Liu saw the blood in the hall and was very uncomfortable, his face turned white.

But at this moment, he also held back nausea in his heart and saluted towards Lin Fei, "Hero Lin, thank you so much. Without you, I'm afraid no one would be able to restrain this evil thief today, then the consequences would be unthinkable."

Lin Fei graciously accepted the salute from Liu's father and daughter. It was indeed he who saved Miss Liu.

"Mr. Liu, Miss Liu, I'm a little unwell now, so I'm going back to my room to rest."

"Quickly help Hero Lin to go back to his room to rest."

Thus, the two maids hurriedly went forward and helped Lin Fei leave the hall.

Looking at Lin Fei's back as he left, Mr. Liu sighed a little and said, "It's still my daughter's wise eyes that know people to have recognized this Lin Fei's uncommonness. But sigh, my old eyes are dimming and I was actually a little disappointed with Hero Lin.

Fortunately, you remedied the situation in time and didn't let Lin Fei chill, otherwise, it would be me who harmed you and the Liu Family."

Mr. Liu couldn't help but be afraid afterward.

If his attitude was a little worse at the beginning, Lin Fei could have "Slacked off" and let the Wind Chasing Fragrance do whatever he wanted.

At that time, the Liu family would have been finished.

"Dad, don't you know cousin's character? She personally entrusted the person, how can it be bad?"

Miss Liu looked at Lin Fei's back, and her eyes were also shining.

Although she was not a martial artist, she knew that Body Refining martial artists killing acquired martial artists who were stronger than them was not an easy task!

Even though she was sure of Lin Fei's extraordinary talent, she didn't expect him to be so powerful.

"Well, well. I was wrong. My eyes deceived me and my daughter is clever." Mr. Liu laughed heartily.

Lin Fei was helped back into the room, and he took out three secret manuals he had obtained from the Wind Chasing Fragrance. One was a sword technique, one was a body technique, and the other was an acquired martial art.

Lin Fei glanced at the sword and body techniques and found that they were not particularly advanced. The sword technique was not even as good as the Nine Shadow Saber Technique. As for the body technique, it was acquired level. It required inner power to practice, which was useless for Lin Fei.

"The acquired martial art!"

What Lin Fei valued most was the acquired martial art. At Nine Profound Sect, he could obtain three acquired-level martial arts for free, but he didn't go to the martial arts pavilion to get them because he hadn't reached the ninth level of Body Refining yet. However, it was okay to take a look at the acquired martial art for now.

So, Lin Fei opened the acquired martial art manual.

This martial art was called Flowing Cloud Force. Finally, Lin Fei understood some of the mysteries of acquired martial artists after checking it.

It turned out that the inner power of acquired martial artists was not cultivated but produced naturally. When a Body Refining martial artist's body reached its limit, it would produce a trace of inner power, and then they could choose some acquired martial arts to gradually strengthen their inner power.

The birth of inner power was actually divided into three types: Qi and blood, tendons and bones, and skin and membranes.

It's very difficult to reach the limit of the tendons and bones as well as skin and membranes, especially the skin and membranes. Any technique that practice the skin and membranes are considered to be hard martial arts, and it is extremely difficult to produce inner power through it. Therefore, it is relatively easy for Qi and blood to produce inner power.

Thus, most martial artists in the Nine Profound Sect chose to strengthen their Qi and blood when they were Body Refining. The Giant Whale Body Refining Technique was a martial art that could strengthen Qi and blood.

The foundation of the acquired martial artists was very important. Once inner power was born, it was related to the strength or weakness of the inner power and the speed of future practice.

If Qi and blood were strong, the inner power produced would naturally be strong. If Qi and blood were weak, the inner power produced would be weak.

Lin Fei also saw a description that if someone could produce inner power from Qi and blood, tendons and bones, and skin and membrane, and then combine the three inner powers and practice acquired martial arts, their foundation would be extremely strong, surpassing most other acquired martial artists. Only then would they have a greater chance of becoming an innate strong martial artist than most ordinary ones.

"It seems that I should continue to practice my Vajra Iron Cloth and Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique and practice them to the limit until I can produce inner power. When I combine the three inner powers and practice acquired martial arts, my foundation will be extremely strong, surpassing most other martial artists!" Lin Fei's eyes sparkled with a hint of brilliance. He already knew how to enter the acquired level.
