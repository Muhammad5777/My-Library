198. Six Sabers Fusion

The Sky Reaching Sect had already sealed their mountain. Just a while ago, after hiding from the world for hundreds of years, they were ready to come out and make big plans.

However, they were "scared" back by Lin Fei, the Saber Saint. Now, they were once again in a state of sealing their mountain.

The so-called sealing of the mountain is to let the protective array of the mountain run continuously. Moreover, the mountain gate is hidden. Ordinary people can't find it or see it.

If they need to open the mountain gate and enter the world, then the mountain gate will appear. Ordinary people can see and find it. This is what is known as "immortal fate."

Some people here can find the Immortal Sect and even join it. But some people, even if they dig three feet into the ground, can't find the Immortal Sect. It's because the Immortal Sect has sealed the mountain gate.

At this moment, three figures were walking in the air. They came directly to this mountain range.

"Brother Lin, this mountain range is known as the Sky Reaching Mountain Range because of the Sky Reaching Sect. There is a Sky Reaching Peak here that soars into the clouds. Even, the Sky Reaching Sect has an ancient magic treasure, the Sky Reaching Tower.

It's said that the Sky Reaching Tower is very magical, with all kinds of incredible abilities. The protective array of the Sky Reaching Sect is suppressed by the Sky Reaching Tower. For thousands of years, no one has been able to break the gate of the Sky Reaching Sect."

"Sky Reaching Tower..." Lin Fei looked up. He couldn't see the Sky Reaching Tower. In fact, let alone the Sky Reaching Tower, he couldn't even see the mountain gate of the Sky Reaching Sect.

However, all three of them were Martial Saints. They were extremely sensitive to the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth. Therefore, they could clearly feel there was a rich Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth here.

The Immortal Sect could indeed hide from the world. It could restrain its aura and make it invisible to people. But it couldn't hide the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth.

Within the Immortal Sect, a large number of formations gathered together. The Spiritual Qi was like a tide. Even if there were blessed lands, it would be the same. After all, it's impossible for every disciple to practice in the blessed lands. They still had to rely on the Spiritual Qi of the outside world. Where there was an Immortal Sect. the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth would inevitably be rich.

"Brother Lin, for the task of 'greeting', it's better for me to do it."

At this time, Shen Lianxin spoke. His saber was already becoming impatient.

"Alright, then I'll leave it to Brother Shen!"

Shen Lianxin looked at the empty void in front of him with cold eyes. In his eyes, he couldn't see the mountain gate of the Sky Reaching Sect either. But he could see a large amount of Spiritual Qi gathered in this void.

"Clang."

Shen Lianxin drew his saber.

"Ultimate Saber Slash!"

A saber light one thousand meters long tore through the void like a piece of cloth, piercing the heavens and the earth. Overwhelming, it slashed into the void.

However, such a huge saber light should have landed on the ground. Just that, as Shen Lianxin slashed out this saber, it was still in the void. It seemed to be blocked by an invisible force.

"Hum."

In the void, a faint glow emerged. Then, this light continued to tremble. Like ripples in water, spiritual Qi surged continuously. The empty void could no longer hide, it revealed a series of mountain peaks and there was also a black tower, soaring into the clouds.

Sky Reaching Tower!

Seeing that tower, anyone's heart would be shaken! That was really shocking. Soaring into the clouds, one couldn't see the end at first glance. It seemed to emit an endless aura of vastness, antiquity, and mystery. And each mountain peak seemed to have mysterious runes, densely packed. They also looked very ancient and mysterious.

This was the Sky Reaching Sect! The number one Immortal Sect in the world!

"Boom."

However, when Shen Lianxin slashed out the second saber. A huge roar shook the entire Sky Reaching Sect. For a while, many disciples and elders were awakened.

"What's going on? Why is the mountain gate shaking?"

"The protective array is shaking?"

"That's...three Martial Saints?"

"They're traversing the air, walking in the air. And there's no fluctuation of spiritual power on them. No doubt, they are Martial Saints!"

"News has already come from below the mountain. There are now three Martial Saints in Great Chang, the leading one being Saber Saint Lin Fei."

"Lin Fei is here?"

"What does he want to do? Our Sky Reaching Sect has already sealed the mountain, yet he's still not letting go. Could it be that he wants to break the mountain gate?"

"Even the founder of Great Chang in the past couldn't break the mountain gate of our Sky Reaching Sect. Just based on a single Martial Saint Lin Fei?"

"Hmph, let Lin Fei attack. He can't break the mountain gate of our Sky Reaching Sect!"

The many disciples had also guessed it. Saber Saint Lin Fei was not prepared to let the Sky Reaching Sect go. He was trying to break the mountain gate of the Sky Reaching Sect.

The only Foundation Establishment True Person of the Sky Reaching Sect, also arrived in the void at this moment, looking at Lin Fei across the void.

"I am Tongyou. Saber Saint Lin Fei, you have slain ten Foundation Establishment cultivators, we, Sky Reaching Sect, concede! Now, our Sky Reaching Sect has announced the closure of the mountain, withdrew all disciples, and no longer interferes in mundane affairs. Saber Saint, however, has made a big fuss and come to our doorstep, isn't this a bit too much?"

True Person Tongyou looked at Lin Fei with a hint of fierceness in his eyes. The Sky Reaching Tower might have sealed the mountain. But it wasn't to the point where it could be slaughtered at will.

Lin Fei looked at True Person Tongyou, speaking calmly, "Too much? So it's okay for your Sky Reaching Sect's True Persons to attack my Nine Profound Sect, but it's not okay for me to come to your Sky Reaching Sect? True Person Tongyou, since I'm here today, I have to at least give it a try. Let's see what the current number one Immortal Sect has to offer?"

True Person Tongyou also knew that Lin Fei's trip would not be called off just because of his few words. So he sneered, "Good, then I'd like to broaden my horizons. Let's see just how astonishing the saber techniques of the current Saber Saint are, that you could actually slay ten Foundation Establishment True Persons!"

True Person Tongyou had absolute confidence in his own mountain gate. With the Sky Reaching Tower suppressing the mountain gate, whether it's a Martial Saint or a Foundation Establishment level. it's simply impossible to break the mountain gate. Even the founder of Great Chang couldn't do it!

At this time, Shen Lianxin unleashed three "Ultimate Saber Slashes" in succession. The sharp saber light howled down, slashing on the protective array of the Sky Reaching Sect. However, it was just like a clay ox entering the sea. There was no response at all. Shen Lianxin knew that he could not break the mountain gate of the Sky Reaching Sect no matter what.

"Brother Shen, let me give it a try." Lin Fei said lightly.

Shen Lianxin also put away his saber. Lin Fei stood in the void. His gaze calmly looked at True Person Tongyou. There wasn't a hint of murderous intent. But it was this kind of cold, calm gaze that made True Person Tongyou feel a little palpitations.

"Hum."

The next moment, a semi-transparent light swept out from Lin Fei's body.

"Boom."

Saber momentum!

The mastery-level saber momentum was just like an enormous mountain pressed on the entire mountain protection formation of the Sky Reaching Sect.

Suddenly, the fierce saber edge and the light of the mountain protection formation intertwined and clashed. However, the mountain protection formation remained unshaken. Lin Fei's saber momentum, even if it was mastery, it was just slightly interfering with reality.

The existence of saber intent is intended to suppress the spirit and will. Even if it evolves from saber intent to saber momentum, the actual interference and destruction of reality, naturally, is far less than the power of saber essence. If one wants to break the Sky Reaching Sect's mountain gate, relying on saber momentum alone is not enough.

However, Lin Fei seemed not surprised. The next moment, Lin Fei kept condensing his saber momentum. The smaller the range of the saber momentum, the stronger its power will be.

However, Lin Fei didn't need to use saber momentum to suppress the Sky Reaching Sect anymore, nor could he suppress it. Now Lin Fei was allowing saber momentum to return to its original "face". In other words, saber momentum was used to enhance the power of saber techniques!

"Boom."

Lin Fei's saber essence exploded instantly. It was as overwhelming as a tidal wave. A large amount of saber essence emerged from Lin Fei's body. In the void, a saber gradually formed.

A heavenly saber!

Under the bolster of saber momentum, the might of the heavenly saber was even more terrifying. However, this was not the end.

"Shoo."

Another strand of saber essence also condensed into a saber.

Three Abyssal Sabers!

Under the evolution of saber momentum, the power of the Three Abyssal Sabers also reached its peak. Moreover, the Three Abyssal Sabers also evolved saber momentum.

"Buzz buzz buzz."

Then, one by one, terrifying sabers condensed in the void.

Twenty-Three Saber, Eternal Night Saber Technique, Samsara Saber Technique, God Slaying Saber Technique. A total of six saber techniques all evolved saber momentum. In the void, six sabers also formed.

"Six Sabers Fusion!"

Lin Fei wasn't in a hurry. He merged the six sabers in the void into one.

"Boom."

Instantly, the saber formed after the fusion of the six sabers, and the saber essence skyrocketed.

Ten meters, fifty meters, a hundred meters, two hundred meters, three hundred meters...

In a blink of an eye, the saber in the void exceeded one thousand meters! And this was saber essence! Each strand of saber essence was hundreds of times the saber energy.

If it were really calculated in saber energy, one thousand meters was roughly the equivalent of a Hundred thousand meters of saber energy! And it was only going up, not down!

At this moment, even the people of the Sky Reaching Sect, who were in the mountain protection formation, felt a bit threatened. Some were even restless. Even True Person Tongyou, the expression on his face was extremely serious. He stared hard at the one thousand meters saber in the void.

How could a Martial Saint condense such a terrifying saber?

Lin Fei closed his eyes. This saber not only fused six top-tier saber techniques but also combined all the saber techniques Lin Fei had learned in his life.

Moreover, under the mastery level saber momentum, power could be continuously accumulated. Therefore, how powerful this saber was, Lin Fei didn't know. This was destined to be his pinnacle saber!

"Swoosh."

The next moment, Lin Fei opened his eyes.

"Slash!"

With a low roar from Lin Fei, the saber essence spanned the void, reaching as high as one thousand meters, like a god saber from the heavens, cutting everything and annihilating everything! It heavily landed on the mountain protection formation of the Sky Reaching Sect.

This saber was no longer the strike in the mortal world!
