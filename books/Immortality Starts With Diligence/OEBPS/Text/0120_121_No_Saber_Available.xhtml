121. No Saber Available

In Lin Fei's mind, new memories emerged, which all were memories of cultivating the Heavenly Saber Art. From beginner to proficient, and then to mastery, all appeared clearly in Lin Fei's memory.

It was as if Lin Fei had gone through a long period of time, slowly mastering the Heavenly Saber Art. At the same time, a system voice appeared in his mind.

"Your Heavenly Saber Art has a noble origin, surpassing beginner and proficient, directly reaching mastery!"

Lin Fei heard the voice in his mind. He paused for a moment.

"Really... it directly reached mastery? This Heavenly Saber Art seems to have a very proud character. With just a little stimulation, it directly reached mastery, saving a lot of time."

Lin Fei wondered if stimulating it again would directly lead to transcendence. However, he only thought about it.

It must be because the Heavenly Saber Art is very special, plus Lin Fei's provocation, made it suddenly reach mastery. Perhaps it was because the Heavenly Saber Art had exhausted all its strength to barely achieve this.

Of course, Lin Fei also had some guesses. The speed at which these martial arts could be improved actually had something to do with Lin Fei himself. It must be because Lin Fei's saber realm was extraordinary. That's why the Heavenly Saber Art could so easily be elevated to mastery.

If Lin Fei immediately went to learn sword techniques or other martial arts. No matter how much Lin Fei provoked them, their progress wouldn't suddenly improve to mastery.

With the Heavenly Saber Art reaching mastery, Lin Fei could wield it as easily as moving his arm. However, this was the Martial Arts Pavilion. He couldn't test his martial arts here.

The power of the Heavenly Saber Art was extremely terrifying. If combined with Lin Fei's Saber Qi, its destructive power would reach a terrifying level.

"The saber technique has been chosen. Now for the fist technique and lightness technique. Let's use the same method. Those who are destined, the martial arts will come naturally!"

So, Lin Fei used the same method. One fist technique after another, one lightness technique after another, all wanted to become Lin Fei's martial arts. This was because Lin Fei's strength had increased. Naturally, he was very "attractive" to martial arts. Otherwise, those martial arts wouldn't eagerly want to become Lin Fei's martial arts.

After careful consideration, Lin Fei chose a fist technique called the Immeasurable Fist.

The Immeasurable Fist was, in fact, a burst technique and martial art that unleashed innate true qi. It allowed for an instantaneous eruption of nearly ten percent of the innate true qi within the body.

This was incredibly terrifying. Moreover, as the realm advanced, the Immeasurable Fist could unleash even greater power.

In the beginner and proficient realms, the innate true qi unleashed was likely less than ten percent. At the mastery realm, it could erupt about ten percent. In the transcendence realm, it could unleash twenty percent. If it reached perfection, it would be truly horrifying. A perfected Immeasurable Fist could even release half of the innate true qi within the body.

The power of a single strike, unleashing half of the innate true qi, Its might was beyond imagination. Hence, it was named "Immeasurable."

After all, a typical martial art that could release only ten percent of the innate true qi in a single strike was already considered quite remarkable. A perfected Immeasurable Fist, on the other hand, could unleash fifty percent of the innate true qi in a single blow. This could undoubtedly be considered one of the top innate fist techniques.

Of course, its flaw might be a slight deficiency in prolonged battles. But when experts exchanged blows, who needed endurance? If a single punch couldn't solve the problem, it most likely couldn't be solved at all.

Against ordinary opponents, Lin Fei could simply use Saber Qi. There was no need for him to throw a punch.

Next was the lightness technique. This time, the winning lightness technique was called "Close Yet Distant." If Close Yet Distant were cultivated to perfection, even if it is far away, it allowed being reached in an instant. It would be challenging to capture Lin Fei's silhouette.

Moreover, the speed of Close Yet Distant was also incredibly fast. As long as there was innate true qi, Lin Fei could cover a distance of over one hundred meters in a single step. The stronger the innate true qi, the greater the distance that could be covered!

Thus, Lin Fei ultimately chose Close Yet Distant as his specialized lightness technique. With that, Lin Fei had chosen his innate level saber, fist, and lightness techniques. Or rather, these martial arts had chosen Lin Fei.

"System."

Lin Fei immediately accessed the system to check his martial arts progress.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Innate Martial Artist

Master: Early Stage

True Intention Scroll: Early Stage (31% Progress)

Saber Intent: Mastery (21% Progress)

Fist Intent: Mastery (45% Progress)

Heavenly Saber Art: Mastery (0% Progress)

Immeasurable Fist: Proficient (0% Progress)

Close Yet Distant: Proficient (0% Progress)

The three martial arts Lin Fei had just chosen, Heavenly Saber Art reached mastery directly, while Immeasurable Fist and Close Yet Distant only reached proficiency.

So, Lin Fei became even more certain that the Heavenly Saber Art was somewhat special. Or, Lin Fei's accomplishments in saber techniques were deep, which allowed the Heavenly Saber Art to reach mastery in an instant.

Having chosen his martial arts, Lin Fei stood up and walked down from the third floor of the Martial Arts Pavilion. The Martial Arts Pavilion Elder was extremely respectful and didn't dare to say anything.

Then, Lin Fei left the Martial Arts Pavilion. He had just chosen the Heavenly Saber Art and it needed a suitable saber to match.

Star Saber was no longer suitable ever since Lin Fei had cultivated Saber Qi. A treasure saber that could withstand the eruption of innate true qi was necessary.

So, Lin Fei went to the Treasure Pavilion and revealed the identity token of the Grand Elder. Immediately, the Treasure Pavilion Elder became respectful.

"Grand Elder Lin, what treasure do you need?"

"A saber! After advancing to the innate realm, I lack a suitable treasure saber."

"A saber? Grand Elder Lin might not know, once you advance to the innate realm, ordinary sabers are no longer usable because even a star saber cannot withstand the eruption of innate true qi from Masters! Therefore, only treasure sabers made from special materials can be used.

This row of treasure sabers can be used by masters. They can fully withstand the eruption of innate true qi. Even Martial Grand Masters can use these treasure sabers without any issues. Grand Elder Lin, you can choose any treasure saber you like."

Lin Fei glanced at them. These treasure sabers emitted a cold light. Even from a distance, one could seemingly sense their sharp aura.

Lin Fei casually picked up a treasure saber. The weight was just right.

"Let's test it."

Lin Fei had thought and activated his Saber Qi. He swung it gently in the void.

"Slash!"

With a soft shout from Lin Fei, the Saber Qi erupted instantly, saber energy roared out and slashed into the ground. Instantly, a large pit appeared on the ground.

"Crack."

However, the treasured saber in Lin Fei's hand, which was said to withstand the eruption of a Martial Master's true qi, had shattered just like that.

"Hmm? Didn't you say it could withstand a grandmaster's true qi eruption? What's going on? Could it be that the sabers in the Treasure Pavilion are all defective?"

Lin Fei's expression turned cold.

Immediately, the Treasure Pavilion Elder's face turned pale, and he hurriedly said, "Grand Elder Lin, please calm down. The sabers in the Treasure Pavilion are definitely not defective. Perhaps it's due to being stored for too long. Grand Elder Lin, please try this one."

So, the Treasure Pavilion Elder handed Lin Fei another treasure saber. Lin Fei once again swung it casually.

"Crack."

The treasure saber shattered again.

"Next."

Lin Fei gripped the third treasure saber.

"Crack."

It was the same as before.

After swinging the saber, the treasured saber shattered on the ground.

"Uh... Grand Elder Lin, this..." The Treasure Pavilion Elder was cautious, hesitating to speak.

Lin Fei threw away the broken treasure saber in his hand. His expression was calm as he said lightly, "I see now, it's my problem."

The Treasure Pavilion Elder didn't know what to say.

Three treasure sabers were that even Martial Grand Masters could use them. Why couldn't Lin Fei use them? Could it be that Lin Fei was stronger than a Martial Grand Master? That was simply impossible!

Only Lin Fei knew that it was most likely due to his Saber Qi. Saber Qi was much stronger than Innate True Qi. Even treasure sabers that innate martial artists could use couldn't withstand the eruption of Lin Fei's Saber Qi.

"It seems I'm not suited for using sabers anymore. I can only unleash Saber Qi and use my hand as a saber."

Lin Fei felt somewhat helpless in his heart. It seemed that in the future, the scenes of him using a thirty meters high saber energy to slash people wouldn't be few...
