265. Leave or Die

The evolution of Saber Momentum, Lin Fei had mastered it with adept expertise.

At this moment, a sonorous clamor echoed within his mind. It felt as though he stood amidst an endless inferno, where the fiery seas brandished ferocious sabers. Such was the saber intent of the Flame Burning Heaven Saber. Once evolved into Saber Momentum, its potency was not to be underestimated. Indeed, it was awe-inspiring!

Time trickled away. the Flame Burning Heaven Saber Intent, which had once been but a tiny spark now blazed fiercely.

From a single spark, a wildfire grows! With the eruption of flames, the successful evolution of the Flame Burning Heaven Saber's Saber Momentum had been realized.

With a swish, Lin Fei opened his eyes. With a mere thought, the Saber Momentum of the Flame Burning Heaven Saber manifested instantly. Immediately, a radiant circle of fiery red light surged, sweeping in all directions. Such was the Saber Momentum of the Flame Burning Heaven Saber!

Within this Saber Momentum, one had not only to endure the fierce saber's onslaught but also the searing pain of flames. Of course, this was but an illusory sensation, not the true burn of flames. All Saber Momentum remained ethereal. Perhaps, only beyond the realm of Saber Momentum might a metamorphosis into reality be possible.

Yet, even in its ethereal form, its terror was profound. Submerged in such Saber Momentum, one's strength was suppressed. Beyond enduring the saber's sharpness, one felt the scald of flames. Under the weight of such Saber Momentum, even the mightiest would be subdued. It was akin to facing both a mountain of sabers and a sea of flames.

The Saber Momentum at the level of Martial Saint was indeed extraordinary. However, Lin Fei sought not just individual Saber Momentum. His aim was to merge all Saber Momentum into one!

"Fuse!"

In the very next moment, without a hint of hesitation, Lin Fei seamlessly merged the Heavenly Saber Momentum with that of the Flame Burning Heaven Saber.

The two Saber Momentums within Lin Fei tangled and clashed with each other. Concurrently, a battle of the two Saber Momentum erupted in Lin Fei's mind. This time, Lin Fei didn't interfere, simply waiting patiently to see which one would emerge superior. After all, regardless of which Saber Momentum assimilated the other, or which became dominant, it wouldn't have a significant impact on Lin Fei.

As time flowed on, a victor between the two Saber Momentum was eventually determined. The Heavenly Saber Momentum, having already integrated numerous Saber Momentum and even having achieved Transcendence, was undeniably formidable. Even if the Flame Burning Heaven Saber Momentum was at the level of Martial Sovereign, how could it possibly match the profound foundation of the Heavenly Saber Momentum?

Thus, the Heavenly Saber Momentum successfully merged with the Flame Burning Heaven Saber Momentum.

A deep rumble echoed as the Heavenly Saber Momentum, after absorbing the Flame Burning Heaven Saber Momentum, began its transformation. Within the Saber Momentum, traces of flames emerged, creating an ambiance as if one were amidst a raging inferno. It had fully integrated the features of the Flame Burning Heaven Saber Momentum.

A voice from the system resonated in Lin Fei's mind.

"Your Heavenly Saber Momentum has successfully integrated the Flame Burning Heaven Saber Momentum, accelerating its cultivation progress by 40%."

"A 40% increase in cultivation progress?" Lin Fei mused aloud. "That's just from the Flame Burning Heaven Saber Momentum. If the Holy Spirit Saber and Rainbow Transformation Saber Momentum each added 40%, my Saber Momentum will certainly reach perfection!"

Even Lin Fei, who always maintained his composure, couldn't hide a hint of joy in his eyes. His choice to practice three Martial Sovereign-level saber techniques, followed by painstakingly comprehending the saber intent and evolving the Saber Momentum, now seemed all the more worthwhile.

Indeed, the Martial Saint-level saber techniques, once their Saber Momentum was evolved and integrated into the original Saber Momentum, truly stood out, significantly enhancing one's cultivation pace.

"Let's see how many steps I can take now," Lin Fei pondered aloud.

The strength of his Saber Momentum had magnified considerably. Thus, he unleashed it, carrying a faint scent of burning flames, and strode forward: ten steps, twenty, thirty, forty...

By the time Lin Fei reached the fortieth step, he realized his Saber Momentum had reached its limit. To push further might strain it beyond its capacity. This spot was ideal. In fact, to ensure absolute precision during the sharpening of his saber intent and to avoid any unforeseen mishaps, he voluntarily stepped back three paces, settling around the thirty-seventh step.

From here, Lin Fei could glimpse the distant prison. However, a mystical array enveloped the prison, seemingly obstructing his view. The insides of the prison remained elusive, discernible perhaps only if he drew much closer.

Lin Fei didn't waste a moment. He had merely evolved the Saber Momentum from the Flame Burning Heaven Saber. There were still the Holy Spirit Saber Technique and the Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique. Both techniques need to be transformed into Saber Momentum. He remembered distinctly that he could remain in the Immortal Mansion for just a month. After that, the four major powers would no longer be able to suppress the array. If he was still inside by then, escape would become impossible.

Thus, he had only half a month left. Time was of the essence. Thankfully, in the previous half-month, his saber intent from the other two techniques had improved significantly, although not as drastically as the Flame Burning Heaven Saber.

One day, two days, three days... In the blink of an eye, ten days had passed.

"Your Rainbow Transformation Saber Intent has achieved perfection!"

Lin Fei opened his eyes. Perfection in the Rainbow Transformation Saber Intent had been achieved in just twenty-five days. Only five days remained.

The saber intent of the Holy Spirit Saber Technique was nearing perfection. Lin Fei was confident that within five days, he would achieve the pinnacle. And when that happened, he would similarly evolve it into Saber Momentum.

However, Lin Fei was in no rush now. Five days remained, and he needed to make the most of every moment. Even evolving into Saber Momentum would consume time, time Lin Fei was loath to squander. Once outside, he could evolve it at will, there was no need to spend precious time within the Immortal Mansion.

One day, two days, three days, four days... Only the final day remained.

Lin Fei glanced at the progress of his Holy Spirit Saber Intent, which had reached an impressive 97%. By the end of the twenty-ninth day, he would likely perfect it. As the critical moment approached, Lin Fei's resolve was to remain more composed and centered than ever.

Yet just as he had closed his eyes, immersing his entire being into refining his saber intent, footsteps echoed from the outside. Members of the Taiyi Dao Palace had unexpectedly found this location. Not only them, but the Red Hades Mansion and the Profound Light Cave Heaven members also arrived in succession.

"Is that Lin Fei?"

"It's Lin Fei of the Ancient Origin Sect. We haven't seen him in so long, and here he is."

"Looks like a prison here."

"Look inside! They've imprisoned some Golden Core True Persons."

"Even if they're dead, they surely possess magic treasures."

Whether from Taiyi Dao Palace, Red Hades Mansion, or Profound Light Cave Heaven, all eyes were drawn to the cells. To them, the discovery of magic treasures, especially those left by a Golden Core True Person, was the greatest gain. Maybe they had already secured many treasures, but greed knew no boundaries.

Suddenly, Lin Fei's eyes snapped open. With so many present, it was impossible for him to quietly refine his saber intent. This was crucial for the perfection of his intent, the eventual evolution of Saber Momentum, the formation of the Spirit Flower, and even his potential ascension to Martial Sovereign.

A chilling light flashed in Lin Fei's eyes. At this juncture, he couldn't maintain his composure. "You have the span of three breaths," his voice was icy, "Leave or die." Concerning his Dao, his murderous intent could no longer be suppressed. Those who obstructed his way would face deadly consequences.
