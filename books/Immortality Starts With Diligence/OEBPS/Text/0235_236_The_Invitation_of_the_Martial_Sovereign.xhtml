236. The Invitation of the Martial Sovereign

Lin Fei returned to his cave. He sat cross-legged on the ground.

"System."

With a thought, he summoned the system to view his martial arts.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Arts Realm: Limit Martial Saint

True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique: Perfection

Blood Essence Nine Transformations: Seventh Transformation (Progress 12%)

Holy Spirit Saber Technique: Mastery (Progress 58%)

Flame Burning Heaven Saber: Transcendence (Progress 65%)

Rainbow Transformation Saber Technique: Transcendence (Progress 10%)

Saber Momentum: Transcendence (Progress 0%)

Lin Fei's martial arts hadn't changed much. Only the martial arts realm had risen from the late stage of Martial Saint to the Limit Martial Saint. It might seem like just a change in name. But Lin Fei knew very well. This step was a qualitative metamorphosis!

In the entire Ancient Origin Sect, the number of Martial Saints was actually quite large. If it looked at the entire Ancient Continent, the number of Martial Saints would be even greater.

But the Limit Martial Saint was different. As the strongest existence below the Martial Sovereign, their status was very high anywhere.

Lin Fei had been in retreat for more than five months. Refining Essence Enhancement Pills was part of the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique, Lin Fei couldn't run around. Because once he used the saber essence, it would consume the saber essence. And then it would affect the cultivation of the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique.

During the period when the True Intention Myriad Transformation Technique was refining Essence Enhancement Pills, it's best to stay put and not move. Therefore, this five-month retreat could be considered the longest retreat Lin Fei ever had.

"I wonder how Shen Lianxin is doing?"

Lin Fei thought about it. So, he got up and went directly to Shen Lianxin's cave. When Lin Fei arrived at Shen Lianxin's cave, he saw that Shen Lianxin was actually practicing True Essence, swallowing the Spiritual Qi.

"Huh? Brother Shen, have your injuries healed?"

How did Shen Lianxin recover in just a few months? After all, Ao Yunxiao injured Shen Lianxin with such an injury. It would take at least a year to recover.

A rare smile appeared at the corner of Shen Lianxin's mouth as he slowly spoke, "Brother Lin, you're here. I haven't congratulated you yet on successfully condensing the Qi Flower, achieving the status of Limit Martial Saint."

However, Lin Fei shook his head and said, "It's just the condensation of a Qi Flower, it's nothing to mention. There's still the Energy Flower and Spirit Flower that hasn't been condensed. Even if all three flowers are condensed, it's nothing unless I achieve the status of Martial Sovereign."

If he didn't know Lin Fei and didn't believe that what he said came from his heart, Shen Lianxin might even think Lin Fei was a bit insincere.

Limit Martial Saint. How many martial artists dream of reaching that realm? In the Ancient Origin Sect, there were countless Martial Saints who fail to condense any of the three flowers in their lifetime. And Lin Fei, in just half a year after joining the Ancient Origin Sect, condensed the Qi Flower and became a Limit Martial Saint.

Such an achievement was already awe-inspiring. But Shen Lianxin knew what Lin Fei said was genuine. He knew that Lin Fei's goal was not the Limit Martial Saint but the Martial Sovereign!

"By the way, Brother Shen, you still haven't answered me, how did your injuries heal?" Lin Fei continued to ask.

Shen Lianxin smiled slightly and said, "The sect gave me a healing treasure, which healed my injuries."

"The sect gave you a healing treasure?"

Lin Fei frowned slightly, seeming a little puzzled.

"Hahaha, Brother Lin, what do you think of my saber momentum?"

Shen Lianxin burst into laughter. In the next moment, a unique saber momentum erupted instantly.

"Boom".

Although it was just newly evolved saber momentum and it hadn't even reached mastery yet, it was a genuine saber momentum.

In the Ancient Origin Sect, anyone who could evolve martial momentum was considered a genius! Moreover, Shen Lianxin was only about forty years old. Among the Martial Saints around forty was very young. Such a young age and he had already evolved saber momentum. Obviously, the Ancient Origin Sect also regarded Shen Lianxin as a top genius to cultivate.

Lin Fei immediately understood. It seems that the Ancient Origin Sect also saw Shen Lianxin's potential. Therefore, they directly gave some resources and benefits, just like Lin Fei.

However, the Ancient Origin Sect gave Lin Fei contribution points, and what they gave to Shen Lianxin was a healing treasure, letting Shen Lianxin heal as soon as possible and then continue to cultivate.

"Not bad, Brother Shen, it was only a matter of time before you evolved saber momentum. I just thought that it would take a year or two for you to possibly evolve saber momentum. I didn't expect that in just a few months, you have evolved saber momentum."

Lin Fei was a bit surprised. He didn't doubt that Shen Lianxin would evolve saber momentum. But he didn't expect it to be so fast.

Shen Lianxin nodded and said, "I have to thank Brother Lin for honing my saber intent with saber momentum. We both traveled from Great Chang to seek the Dao. I can't lag too far behind Brother Lin."

So, the two discussed saber momentum again. Of course, Shen Lianxin's saber momentum was far inferior to Lin Fei's. Lin Fei also shared his experience of condensing the Qi Flower with Shen Lianxin.

Shen Lianxin was also determined. He must cultivate his technique to perfection and then try to condense the Qi Flower.

Lin Fei stayed in Shen Lianxin's cave for a few hours. Then, Lin Fei returned to his own cave.

Just after returning to his cave, Lin Fei saw a Martial Saint.

"Elder Lin."

This Martial Saint took the initiative to approach Lin Fei.

"Are you an elder of the Law Enforcement Hall?"

Lin Fei noticed the attire of the Martial Saint. He roughly knew that the other party was from the Law Enforcement Hall.

However, the Law Enforcement Hall was divided into the Outer Law Enforcement Hall and the Law Enforcement Hall of the entire Ancient Origin Sect. Lin Fei himself was an elder of the Outer Law Enforcement Hall.

"Elder Lin, I'm under the order of the master of the Ancient Origin Sect Law Enforcement Hall. I'm here to deliver a token to you. You are now the vice hall master of the Ancient Origin Sect Law Enforcement Hall."

"Vice hall master?"

Lin Fei knew that the other party was referring to the entire Law Enforcement Hall of the Ancient Origin Sect. This was not a leisurely position. It held great power!

After all, the Law Enforcement Hall was responsible for enforcing the law. They might be dealing with some true disciples. At that time, it would be up to the Law Enforcement Hall to take action. And the vice hall master was obviously high above, belonging to the high level of the Law Enforcement Hall.

"The position of vice hall master holds high authority, and I am only interested in cultivation, I'm afraid..."

Lin Fei was preparing to refuse. If they want him to be an elder, that's no problem. But the vice hall master? That was a position that required action. Even if it had various benefits, he didn't want to waste his cultivation time.

However, this Martial Saint smiled and said, "Elder Lin, don't worry, our Law Enforcement Hall's vice hall master actually doesn't need to do much. Only certain major matters need to be reported to you. Moreover, the vice hall master has many benefits. It's much better than being an elder. Besides, this was something the hall master personally instructed."

"The hall master personally instructed?"

Lin Fei knew that the hall master of the Law Enforcement Hall is a Martial Sovereign. In fact, throughout the Ancient Origin Sect, the power of the Law Enforcement Hall is the most formidable. Therefore, the hall master must be a Martial Sovereign. As for other Martial Sovereigns, most of them are Grand Elders or sect masters. Every sect master of the Ancient Origin Sect is a Martial Sovereign.

"Vice hall master, if you have any questions, you can ask the hall master directly. The hall master is still in the Law Enforcement Hall, waiting to see Vice Hall Master Lin."

Lin Fei looked at the Martial Saint in front of him. He knew that this Martial Saint was not only here to deliver the token but also to invite him.

After some thought, Lin Fei nodded and said, "Alright, please lead the way."

After all, it was a personal invitation from a Martial Sovereign. Moreover, Lin Fei was indeed curious. What did a Martial Sovereign look like? This was an opportunity.

So, Lin Fei and this Martial Saint turned into two streaks of light and flew towards the Law Enforcement Hall.
