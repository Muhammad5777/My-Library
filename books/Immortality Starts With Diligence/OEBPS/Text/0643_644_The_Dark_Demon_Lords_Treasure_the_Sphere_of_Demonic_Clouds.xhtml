644. The Dark Demon Lord's Treasure, the Sphere of Demonic Clouds

Lin Fei left the chamber and found the Azure Emperor Supreme.

"Lin Fei, you've emerged from seclusion so soon?"

The Azure Emperor Supreme was surprised. He observed that Lin Fei's aura didn't seem much stronger, and such a short period in seclusion likely meant minimal gains.

"Was the seclusion not effective? Did it fail?" Failure in cultivation was common, and the Azure Emperor Supreme himself had many such experiences.

Lin Fei nodded and replied, "Azure Emperor Supreme, my seclusion has indeed ended. I've nearly fully researched and integrated the realm's prototype.

My Eternal Realm hasn't transformed yet, but now there's a clear direction forward. I even have the confidence to eventually evolve the Eternal Realm into a realm.

However, I'm missing some key resources. With those resources, I can transform the Eternal Realm."

"What resources do you need?"

"World Origin, a substantial amount of World Origin!" Lin Fei articulated each word distinctly.

"World Origin?" The Azure Emperor Supreme's eyes flashed with a hint of surprise before he slightly relaxed.

"If it's just World Origin you need, then we eight Supremes can mobilize our forces to gather it for you. Even if it's World Origin from True Monarch-level worlds, we can collect it for you. Furthermore, we could sweep through the entire Divine Realm to continuously obtain World Origin."

The Azure Emperor Supreme immediately decided and even had a plan to sweep the worlds within the Divine Realm. There are many worlds in the Divine Realm, especially minor worlds, as numerous as the sands of the Ganges, and the Divine Realm is still birthing new small worlds. So, if it's just World Origin, the Azure Emperor Supreme could provide it.

"Thank you, Azure Emperor Supreme, and all the other Supremes," Lin Fei said.

"World Origin, whether it's from True Immortal-level worlds, True Monarch-level worlds, or even minor worlds, all are acceptable. As long as there is World Origin, my Eternal Realm will definitely transform!

Even once the Eternal Realm evolves to a True Immortal-level world, even if the Dark Demon Lord returns or has treasures from the Dark Demon Domain, they won't be able to affect me!"

Lin Fei left something unsaid: If his Eternal Realm truly evolved into a True Immortal-level world, he could even overpower any Supreme, including the Dark Demon Lord. Then, the invasion from the Dark Demon Domain would not be sustainable.

Thus, World Origin is crucial for Lin Fei at this moment and even for the entire Divine Realm.

"I understand," said the Azure Emperor Supreme. "Lin Fei, rest assured. World Origin will continuously be delivered to your hands. You can just quietly wait in Ancient City."

Lin Fei nodded, then turned and left the Ancient Covenant. Next, Lin Fei waited quietly in Ancient City, and indeed, the Azure Emperor Supreme did not disappoint him.

The next day, Lin Fei began receiving World Origin. Though the quantities were initially small, they indeed were what he needed. And this was just the beginning. Over the following days—the third, fourth, fifth, sixth, and beyond—massive amounts of World Origin were delivered to Lin Fei daily.

Lin Fei directly funneled all the World's Origins into the Realm Core. Transformed by the Realm Core, it became realm power, swiftly elevating the level of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

Initially, Lin Fei's world was already at the True Monarch level, yet his progress was at a standstill—0%. But now, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was finally progressing again—1%, 2%, 3%, 4%, 5%...

The perpetual increase was evident and occurred almost daily.

Lin Fei could distinctly feel his power growing by the day. Once his Eternal Realm transformed into a True Immortal-level world, Lin Fei believed even the Dark Demon Lord, without relying on the Illusory Realm, would be no match for him. Still, this transformation required time...

Far away in the Dark Demon Domain, the Dark Demon Lord stood in the void. In the Dark Demon Domain, he was the absolute ruler, controlling the life and death of anyone, even Supremes.

Yet, at this moment, the Dark Demon Lord's expression was gravely serious. He was retrieving a treasure—a phenomenal treasure essential for conquering the Divine Realm. Now, he had to take a slight risk.

Deep in a massive valley, there lay a giant multicolored sphere, bound by thick chains. Upon closer inspection, these chains were formed from the rules and laws of the Dark Demon Domain itself, securely locking the sphere. Only the Dark Demon Lord had such capabilities.

After all, in the Dark Demon Domain, he was the supreme ruler capable of easily suppressing any Supreme. Even here, the Dark Demon Lord's power was above all others.

Gazing at the multicolored sphere, the Dark Demon Lord's expression hardened, his eyes flashing with resolve. He coldly stated, "I refuse to believe that in the Dark Demon Domain, you can still turn the tides."

"Ha! In the Dark Demon Domain, no matter the challenge, I can handle it! To deal with the Divine Realm, particularly that Lin Fei, I must control the Sphere of Demonic Clouds... It is said that the Sphere of Demonic Clouds can seal all powers. All forces beneath domination will be completely sealed."

The Dark Demon Lord took a deep breath, contemplating the Sphere of Demonic Clouds. Asserting that it could seal all powers below dominion might have been a bit absolute, but the formidable nature of the Sphere was truly terrifying.

Under normal circumstances, the Dark Demon Lord would not need to rush; he could slowly understand and master the Sphere of Demonic Clouds.

However, the current situation in the Divine Realm was increasingly disadvantageous for the Dark Demon Domain. If things continued this way, he might not master the Sphere before the forces of the Dark Demon Domain were pushed back to their own realm.

The Dark Demon Lord could not allow this to happen, especially since this invasion of the Divine Realm had consumed considerable resources and effort. It wasn't something he could simply abandon.

Despite some instability factors, the Dark Demon Lord felt confident. As long as he was within the Dark Demon Domain, he believed everything was under his control.

The Sphere of Demonic Clouds, a serendipitous discovery during his reign, was considered one of his strokes of luck.

The Sphere was renowned in the Chaos; it was said to be one of the treasures birthed within the Chaos itself and was even rumored to exist in multiple instances across the Chaos. Once obtained, it could rapidly and immensely bolster one's strength.

Dark Demon Lord felt that to confront the Divine Realm and Lin Fei, the Sphere of Demonic Clouds needed to be employed.

"Let's begin," the Dark Demon Lord muttered to himself as he took another deep breath.

"Open!"

In the next instant, an immense force of laws descended like a sharp, massive saber, striking fiercely upon the Sphere of Demonic Clouds.

"Boom."

The Sphere trembled violently, releasing a terrifying power seemingly related to the Chaos, continuously eroding the laws and rules of the Dark Demon Domain.

Seizing the moment, the Dark Demon Lord immersed his consciousness into the Sphere of Demonic Clouds, aiming to gain control over it through his will. When his consciousness penetrated the Sphere, "What is this..."

Suddenly, the Dark Demon Lord's eyes widened in disbelief at the scene unfolding before him. What did he see that could shake even the Ruler of the Dark Demon Domain to his core?
