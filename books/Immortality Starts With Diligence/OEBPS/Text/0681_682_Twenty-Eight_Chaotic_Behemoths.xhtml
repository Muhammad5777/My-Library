682: Twenty-Eight Chaotic Behemoths!

"Boom."

With fierce determination, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm charged directly at the Chaotic Behemoth and struck it hard. The terrifying force rippled through the air in tiny waves.

"Thud."

A muffled sound echoed as the Chaotic Behemoth shuddered throughout its body. Then, its eyes filled with disbelief, it stared at Lin Fei.

"Capture!"

Without a second thought, the Eternal Realm struck fiercely and actually managed to repel the Chaotic Behemoth. Then, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm directly enveloped and drew the Chaotic Behemoth into itself.

"Rumble."

This Chaotic Behemoth was indeed extraordinary. Even when ensnared within the Eternal Realm, it could still struggle violently.

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm might not completely suppress it. But now, Lin Fei lacked the extra strength and time to suppress this top-tier Chaotic Behemoth.

He had to leave for Lin Fei faintly felt a tremor within his heart as if a profound sense of danger had suddenly arisen. This made Lin Fei think of a possibility.

The King of Chaotic Behemoths!

Perhaps, the battle Lin Fei had just fought with this top-tier Chaotic Behemoth had already awakened the King of Chaotic Behemoths. The King of Chaotic Behemoths was about to awake.

"Go!"

Without a second thought,

Lin Fei prepared to leave this world at once.

"Zoom."

In the next moment, Lin Fei turned into a streak of light. He had already flown out of this world and into the Chaos.

But in the Chaos, Lin Fei felt a new sense of peril. This threat loomed constantly over him as if a calamity could strike at any moment.

"Go."

Lin Fei dared not delay. Quickly turning into a streak of light, he flew towards Kunyu City.

...

Kunyu City.

When Lin Fei returned to Kunyu City, it was already two months later. His expression was calm.

Yet, during those two months, he dared not pause for a moment. The sense of danger that loomed in his mind persisted throughout. Only when Lin Fei entered Kunyu City did that feeling of danger finally vanish.

This confirmed that only within Kunyu City was Lin Fei truly safe. With the sense of danger that reached him no matter the distance, Lin Fei had a fair idea of the cause now.

The King of Chaotic Behemoths!

It must have been the King of Chaotic Behemoths that brought this obscure threat to Lin Fei. Otherwise, it was impossible that Lin Fei, after flying through the Chaos for about two months, would still feel endangered. Only the King of Chaotic Behemoths had such capability.

After all, the King of Chaotic Behemoths is said to rival even the Masters of Chaos. Even the Masters of Chaos would not dare provoke it lightly.

However, Kunyu City was different. It was under the watch of a Master of Chaos. Even the King of Chaotic Behemoths, upon reaching Kunyu City, could not harm Lin Fei. Thus, upon his return to Kunyu City, Lin Fei's sense of danger disappeared.

"Sigh..." Lin Fei breathed a sigh of relief. "I've finally returned."

Lin Fei had been under immense pressure on this journey. Fortunately, he had returned safely. And the gains were unimaginably great.

"Let me count just how many Chaotic Behemoths I've subdued?"

Lin Fei wanted to see how many he had actually subdued. After all, he did not know exactly how many Chaotic Behemoths he had subdued. He only vaguely remembered.

The number he had subdued was certainly not small. It might have exceeded twenty!

Thus, Lin Fei began to count the subdued Chaotic Behemoths.

One, three, five, eight, ten...

"Twenty-eight Chaotic Behemoths, I've subdued so many? And there's one top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, not just any foremost but something beyond..."

A sharp gleam flickered in Lin Fei's eyes.

A top-tier behemoth, at least equivalent to over six Chaotic Behemoths. Even comparable to ten Chaotic Behemoths. Subduing this one Chaotic Behemoth was an unexpected delight for Lin Fei.

Of course, this Chaotic Behemoth did not know how strong Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was. Even though it tried everything, it could not escape the Eternal Realm.

"Twenty-seven Chaotic Behemoths could increase my Eternal Realm cultivation progress by about 27%. Adding that one top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, I could increase my cultivation progress by about 6% to 10%. In the end, my Eternal Realm could even approach 50%!"

Lin Fei felt an immense surge of excitement within, approaching 50%.

Of course, this was assuming Lin Fei could truly integrate such an abundance of Chaotic Behemoth essence.

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei took a deep breath. He hesitated no longer.

"Boom."

The next moment, Lin Fei was fully focused. He concentrated all his attention, all his energy, and began to mobilize the power of the Eternal Realm. He methodically obliterated the Chaotic Behemoths.

"Thump, thump, thump."

One by one, the Chaotic Behemoths were obliterated by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. And the essence of their flesh and blood began to nourish Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

One day, two days, three days...

Now, Lin Fei had ample time to slowly "digest" the essence of these Chaotic Behemoths. And this essence was incredibly beneficial to the Eternal Realm.

Indeed, the Eternal Realm was almost constantly improving. Previously at only 11% cultivation progress. Now it was skyrocketing.

12%, 13%, 14%, 15%, 16%...

With each percentage increase in the Eternal Realm, Lin Fei's power surged tremendously.

A month later.

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm cultivation progress had jumped from 11% to 38%, a total increase of 27%. In Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, there remained only one Chaotic Behemoth. That was the extraordinary top-tier Chaotic Behemoth.

At this moment, within Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, Lin Fei watched the Chaotic Behemoth before him. The creature seemed to sense what it was about to face.

This top-tier Chaotic Behemoth could not help but speak, "Human cultivator, your widespread slaughter of Chaotic Behemoths might be overlooked elsewhere. But you have been capturing them in the City of Behemoths. Do you realize that you have committed a great taboo? The great King of Chaotic Behemoths will certainly not forgive you. No matter where you flee, the King of Chaotic Behemoths will remember your scent and will surely slay you!"

The tone of the top-tier Chaotic Behemoth was deep, filled with hatred.

"City of Behemoths? So, that place was the City of Behemoths. Perhaps, your King of Chaotic Behemoths might avenge you. But this is Kunyu City. Even the King of Chaotic Behemoths would not dare to run wild here. So, you are doomed not to see the King of Chaotic Behemoths slay me."

Lin Fei's gaze was icy.

The next moment, he hesitated no longer.

"Buzz."

The power of the Eternal Realm erupted fully. Majestically, it rolled towards the Chaotic Behemoth.

"Roar..."

The Chaotic Behemoth struggled violently. It was shaking intensely even causing the void to shatter. Of course, compared to before, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was now many times stronger.

Previously, this top-tier Chaotic Behemoth might have destabilized Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. But now, it could only cause the void to collapse. It was merely wishful thinking to even shake the Eternal Realm. This showed the astonishingly rapid increase in the power of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

"Annihilate!"

Lin Fei uttered softly.

"Boom."

The next moment, the massive body of the top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, nearly exploded on cue, completely turned to dust!

The top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, just like that, died. Completely obliterated!

Meanwhile, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was frantically absorbing the essence of the Chaotic Behemoth's flesh and blood. The Eternal Realm began its transformation anew!
