207. Transformation, Saber Momentum Transcendence

Shangguan family mansion.

Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin had both moved in here. After all, the Zhao family was a Qi cultivator family. It wouldn't be a long-term plan to stay at Zhao's. And Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin had already reached an agreement with the eight great families. The two had officially become guest elders of the eight great families. It could be said that they shared honor and disgrace now. So, Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin simply moved into the Shangguan family. They quietly waited for the eight great families to arrange for their trip to the Ancient Origin Sect.

According to the arrangement of the eight great families, they first needed to build momentum for Lin Fei. Yes, build momentum. They needed to publicize Lin Fei's status as a Dao Seeker.

Becoming an elder of the Ancient Origin Sect was not easy. Not only did they need to have great strength but also needed some other factors. For example, Lin Fei being a Dao Seeker would definitely attract the attention of the high-level figures in the Ancient Origin Sect. After all, many Dao Seeker seniors have proven all Dao Seekers achieved great things in the future!

The eight great families went to build momentum while Lin Fei looked at the secret saber technique manuscripts sent by them. Although they were transcriptions, there wasn't much difference from the originals. Moreover, the number of secret saber technique manuscripts sent by the eight great families greatly exceeded Lin Fei's estimate. There were a total of twenty-four top-tier saber techniques. Almost every family contributed three top-tier saber techniques.

One should know these were not ordinary saber techniques. They were saber techniques that could cultivate saber intent. Such saber techniques were rare and very valuable no matter where.

"If I can evolve all these saber techniques into saber momentum. Then my saber momentum should be able to break through to transcendence!"

A strange light flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

Saber momentum is very unique. It doesn't require accumulation over time. Often a sudden insight could comprehend saber momentum, even directly leading to saber momentum perfection. If they can't have sudden insight, even after hundreds of years of hard cultivation, the saber momentum will not improve a bit.

Lin Fei didn't need to comprehend saber momentum. His saber momentum took a different path from other martial artists.

Other martial artists, most of them comprehend a type of saber momentum. Then they constantly comprehend this type of saber momentum, continuously evolve this kind of saber momentum, hoping to evolve this saber momentum to transcendence, or even to perfection.

But Lin Fei didn't take this path. He took the path of integrating saber momentum. He wanted to integrate a large amount of saber momentum and then be inclusive of all, reaching perfection!

This method required Lin Fei to integrate various kinds of saber techniques. In order to comprehend various types of saber momentum, even the most talented martial artists had limited energy. No one could comprehend a top-tier saber technique in one or two days, or half a month, and evolved its saber momentum. That's simply impossible.

But Lin Fei could! Because he didn't need to practice himself. Even the saber techniques didn't need him to comprehend himself. His saber techniques could all cultivate and evolve saber momentum by themselves. Of course, the prerequisite was that Lin Fei could attract these saber techniques.

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei took a deep breath.

"Boom."

In the next moment, Lin Fei released his saber momentum. A semi-transparent glow erupted from his body. Suddenly, the entire room was covered by this transparent glow.

Lin Fei took a deep breath. Then he said calmly: "I need more top-tier saber techniques to evolve my saber momentum. If you can fuse your perfection state saber techniques into my saber momentum on your own. I will definitely help you successfully evolve to the saber momentum!"

Having said that, Lin Fei quietly waited. These were all top-tier innate-level saber techniques. All of them could cultivate saber intent and the saber techniques that could cultivate saber intent all wanted to evolve saber momentum. Saber momentum was almost the highest achievement for these saber techniques.

Therefore, Lin Fei didn't say much. Just these few sentences. He believed these saber techniques could all choose on their own.

Sure enough, as soon as Lin Fei finished speaking, a system voice sounded in his mind.

"Heaven-Earth One Saber Slash, self-cultivated to perfection state, wishes to integrate into your saber momentum."

"Shadow Slicing Saber, self-cultivated to perfection state, wishes to integrate into your saber momentum."

"Nine Heavens Slash, self-cultivated to perfection state, wishes to integrate into your saber momentum."

"Wind Slicing Saber, self-cultivated to perfection state, wishes to integrate into your saber momentum."

One by one, the saber techniques were all integrated into Lin Fei's saber momentum. In the blink of an eye, all twenty-four top-tier innate-level saber techniques chose to integrate into Lin Fei's saber momentum on their own.

Now, Lin Fei's saber momentum had an additional twenty-four top-tier innate-level saber techniques! Of course, this had no effect on Lin Fei's saber momentum for now.

To improve the saber momentum, he had to integrate another saber momentum. And these saber techniques just integrated into the saber momentum but had not yet evolved saber momentum.

Lin Fei also promised he would definitely help these saber techniques to evolve saber momentum. Only then could he attract so many saber techniques to integrate into his saber momentum. If Lin Fei did not fulfill his promise, then these saber techniques might go silent. They would not add any saber technique foundation to Lin Fei.

"Shoo."

Lin Fei opened his eyes. With a thought, he retracted his saber momentum. Immediately, the glow that covered the entire room also instantly returned to Lin Fei's body.

"I need to help these saber techniques evolve their saber momentum."

Lin Fei murmured in a low voice. To evolve saber momentum, the only way he knew now was to let these saber techniques fight against Foundation Establishment level powerhouses. Even, they must kill those Foundation Establishment level powerhouses.

For example, when Lin Fei's saber techniques were able to evolve saber momentum, it was because Lin Fei had destroyed the ten immortal gates, killing countless Foundation Establishment True Persons. Only then could those top-tier saber techniques evolve saber momentum. It might sound cruel, but it couldn't be more normal. And this was also the broad road! After all, sabers were inherently lethal weapons!

From the moment the saber was born, the saber was used for killing. And the saber techniques were natural for killing as well. There was never a "kind" saber technique. If the saber techniques in Lin Fei's saber momentum could each take a human form, they would definitely be ferocious and terrifying. So, if saber techniques want to improve, besides enlightenment, they must fight and kill!

"I need to kill Foundation Establishment level powerhouses..."

A thought flashed through Lin Fei's mind.

The ocean!

In the vast ocean, there was a lack of everything. But there was no shortage of Foundation Establishment level sea beasts. If he wanted to kill, go to the ocean!

Under normal circumstances, the ocean was actually very dangerous for Foundation Establishment True Persons. But for Lin Fei, he had drifted at sea for several months. What danger had he not encountered?

With the Sky Reaching Tower and saber momentum, as long as he didn't provoke some very powerful or surpassing the Foundation Establishment level sea beasts, then he wouldn't be in danger.

So, Lin Fei got up. He left the Shangguan family and turned into a streak of light, flying toward the ocean.

At this moment, the sea was surging, especially in the deep sea far away from Fairy Mist Island, there lurked many terrifying sea beasts.

"Boom."

Lin Fei's saber momentum exploded in an instant. In a flash, it enveloped an area of ten miles. This was not the limit of Lin Fei's saber momentum, but that was enough.

"Roar..."

Under the suppression of Lin Fei's saber momentum, one after another, sea beasts emerged, showing their huge heads. They were emanating terrifying auras, roaring furiously at Lin Fei.

"Go."

Lin Fei flicked his finger. Immediately, streaks of saber energy flew out. They quickly transformed into a giant saber. Then, the saber intent burst forth. These were the twenty-four saber techniques that Lin Fei had just integrated into his saber momentum. Now, they all transformed into heavenly sabers, fighting with those sea beasts.

One, two, three...

In the sea area of ten miles, there were actually eight sea beasts. And without exception, all were at the Foundation Establishment level.

But Lin Fei seemed very calm. His saber momentum kept suppressing these sea beasts. In the battle with the sea beasts, the saber intent of the saber techniques kept rising.

Lin Fei was using these sea beasts to temper his saber techniques. Moreover, the speed was very fast. Beginner, Mastery, Transcendence, Perfection!

When the saber intent of a saber technique reached Perfection, it could no longer be improved. Because it faced the evolution into saber momentum. Therefore, it needed an opportunity. And this opportunity was to kill sea beasts.

"Slash!"

With the suppression of Lin Fei's saber momentum, helping these saber techniques to kill sea beasts was a breeze.

"Splat."

One after another, sea beasts were killed by the saber techniques. Afterward, those saber techniques successfully evolved into saber momentum.

"Your 'Heaven-Earth One Saber Slash', excited after slaying a Foundation Establishment sea beast, has evolved into saber momentum."

"Your 'Shadow Slicing Saber', excited after slaying a Foundation Establishment sea beast, has evolved into saber momentum."

"Your 'Nine Heavens Slash', excited after slaying a Foundation Establishment sea beast, has evolved into saber momentum."

"Your 'Wind Slicing Saber', excited after slaying a Foundation Establishment sea beast, has evolved into saber momentum."

All twenty-four saber techniques had evolved into saber momentum.

"Fuse!"

The next moment, Lin Fei did not hesitate. He immediately started the fusion of saber momentum. Originally, Lin Fei's progress in cultivating saber momentum had reached 10%. And now, after fusing twenty-four saber momenta. Lin Fei's progress in cultivating saber momentum began to advance by leaps and bounds.

20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, 100%......

When the progress of cultivating saber momentum reached 100%, Lin Fei's saber momentum started shaking violently.

"Saber Momentum Transcendence!"

At this moment, Lin Fei was overjoyed. He immediately knew his saber momentum has transformed. It had finally reached Transcendence!
