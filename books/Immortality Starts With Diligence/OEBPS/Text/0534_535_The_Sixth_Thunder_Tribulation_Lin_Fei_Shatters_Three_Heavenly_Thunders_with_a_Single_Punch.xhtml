535. The Sixth Thunder Tribulation! Lin Fei Shatters Three Heavenly Thunders with a Single Punch!

The Grand Unity True Immortal, a venerable being, suddenly uttered a soft eek.

"Humm...Lin Fei is undergoing tribulation again. But he's plundered the origins of one hundred and three small worlds. He's causing quite a stir. The Great Dream Ancestor may seem silent, but in fact, he's quite protective. He's already subtly intimidated many other powers. Last time, Lin Fei attracted seven heavenly thunders; this time, isn't it going to be nine? It seems I really need to keep an eye on this. We can't have the tribulation grounds destroyed again..."

The gaze of the Grand Unity True Immortal pierced through layers of space, watching the tribulation grounds. If even the Grand Unity True Immortal was paying attention to Lin Fei's tribulation, what about other powers and mighty beings?

Many True Monarchs were watching, especially the Martial True Monarchs. Lin Fei's continuation of tribulation after becoming a True Monarch surprised them. They had reached a standstill on the path of the True Monarch, even unable to find a way forward. Observing Lin Fei might bring them some inspiration. Thus, Lin Fei's tribulation this time was the center of attention.

At this moment, Lin Fei was in the midst of the formation at the tribulation grounds. He could feel it as if many eyes were focused on him. There were also some more discreet gazes, all fixed on the tribulation grounds.

"True Immortal or Martial Ancestor?"

Lin Fei looked up at the void. He couldn't see anything, But he could feel the gaze upon him. This was the difference in realms.

Martial Ancestors, True Immortals. These represented a whole new level of life's ascension. True Immortals and those below are entirely different realms. Even Martial Gods and True Monarchs don't signify a transformation in life.

But reaching the level of a True Immortal or Martial Ancestor, That's a true elevation.

Lin Fei disregarded the myriad gazes upon him. Instead, he cleared his mind of all distractions and jumbled thoughts. He didn't need lofty ambitions now. What he needed to do was to transcend the tribulation!

To pass the sixth thunder tribulation! To acquire enough world laws to create more particle worlds. Only then could he rapidly enhance his strength.

"Let's begin."

Lin Fei took a deep breath, then looked towards the void. He seemed to see again that vast sea of origin in the void. It was the same as before, no different.

Without hesitation, Lin Fei's consciousness dived familiarly into the sea of origin, beginning to plunder the first world law.

"Boom."

The next moment, the first heavenly thunder appeared. A white lightning bolt thunderously struck down, directly hitting Lin Fei's physical body.

Lin Fei made no move, allowing the thunder to strike him. Surprisingly, his body sustained no damage. He didn't even feel the power of the first heavenly thunder. It could only be said that Lin Fei's physical body was now incredibly strong, so strong that even the first heavenly thunder left no impression on him.

Lin Fei's consciousness continued to engage with the sea of origin. However, knowing the strength of his physical body, he didn't plunder the laws one by one. Instead, he directly seized three world laws at once.

"Boom, boom, boom."

Suddenly, three heavenly thunders struck down in the void. They were the second, third, and fourth purple heavenly thunders. All three thunders striking simultaneously, including the formidable fourth purple thunder, was an immensely powerful sight.

Those watching from outside the tribulation grounds were dumbfounded. They had never seen such a scene before. Three heavenly thunders were summoned all at once.

"Good heavens, what is True Monarch Lin doing?"

"Summoning three heavenly thunders at once, including the fourth purple thunder, increasing the power tenfold."

"Isn't True Monarch Lin being overly confident? Can he really handle three thunders simultaneously?"

"Even if Lin Fei is now a True Monarch instead of a Martial God, this way of transcending tribulation seems a bit too arrogant."

"I'm afraid True Monarch Lin will suffer. The heavenly thunders are not so easy to transcend, especially one after another."

Many were shocked, but they thought Lin Fei was being too presumptuous. Even though Lin Fei was strong, they felt he shouldn't be this overconfident.

However, they were unaware of Lin Fei's current state. When Lin Fei saw the three heavenly thunders descending from the void, a smile even appeared on his face. He had no intention of letting the thunders strike. Instead, he extended his right hand.

"Boom."

Lin Fei actually threw a punch, directly at the three descending heavenly thunders in the void.

Lin Fei actively used his pure physical strength to strike the three thunders. But could pure physical strength impact the three heavenly thunders?

"Is True Monarch Lin planning to obliterate the three thunders directly?"

"This is... an unimaginable way to transcend tribulation."

"Such a method of transcending tribulation is unheard of. To destroy the heavenly thunders?"

Everyone was astounded, All eyes fixed on the three bolts of lightning in the void. At that moment, as the three thunders descended, it was as if they encountered an invisible force in mid-air, striking them fiercely.

The thunders shook slightly, and then completely disintegrated. Vanished. The three heavenly thunders disappeared just like that.

Silence!

The area around the tribulation grounds was utterly quiet. Even in the void, a few powerful gazes seemed surprised. Transcending tribulation so effortlessly and obliterating three heavenly thunders with a single punch was unprecedented.

Everyone was agape, utterly stunned. Even the most composed individuals couldn't maintain their composure at this moment.

"Is this the power of a Martial True Monarch?"

"A True Monarch, a Martial True Monarch, has such immense strength in just one punch?"

"Indeed, True Monarchs and Martial Gods are different. Martial Gods endure the thunder strikes, exerting all their strength just to withstand the power. But a True Monarch transcending tribulation? Obliterates the thunders with a single punch!"

"Such a method of transcending tribulation is probably unparalleled in history, and it might be difficult for anyone to achieve such a feat in the future."

"Truly worthy of being True Monarch Lin, who could fight an Illusory True Monarch as a Martial God. His strength is even more formidable than a regular True Monarch, isn't it?"

Some Martial Gods even reminisced about their own tribulations, comparing themselves to Lin Fei. They felt a heavy sense of disparity. If they had tried this, they would have been pulverized by the heavenly thunders.

What Lin Fei did seemed simple, but the terrifying strength required could only be achieved by a Martial True Monarch!

"Can this really be done?"

Not only were others shocked, but Even Lin Fei himself was surprised. He had just wanted to test it out to see if he could obliterate three heavenly thunders with a single punch with his current strength. Unexpectedly, he succeeded in destroying them.

Of course, the main reason was that he didn't feel any threat from these thunders, which gave him the courage to act so boldly. But the next phase wouldn't be the same. Lin Fei was about to face the fifth thunder, The blood-red thunder capable of "freezing" a warrior's physical body.

"Boom."

Finally, the fifth blood-red thunder descended. Lin Fei felt a hint of threat. So, this time, he didn't act recklessly. Instead, he quietly waited for the fifth thunder to strike.

However, when the fifth blood-red thunderstruck, Lin Fei's expression suddenly changed. He vaguely realized something was different! The fifth thunder had changed. It was mixed with the power of the sixth and even the seventh thunder.

"What's going on?"

Lin Fei was puzzled. He had a feeling that something had happened, causing a change in his tribulation.
