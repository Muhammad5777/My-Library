179. The Fall of the Foundation Establishment

The saber light reappeared! Only, this saber was not merely thirty meters long but three hundred! And it was accompanied by Lin Fei's voice.

"Heavenly Saber Art, Slash!"

It was only a three hundred meters saber light, compared with Lin Fei's previous one thousand saber energy, it seemed not worth mentioning. However, the two couldn't be compared at all.

This three hundred meters saber light was composed of saber essence. Saber energy was compressed hundreds of times, which produced such a bit of saber essence.

A saber essence of roughly the same size compared to saber energy was almost a hundred times saber energy! And the power was more concentrated.

Once the power explodes, it was not just a hundred times that simple. More importantly, Lin Fei's saber intent was also stronger. He had integrated hundreds of saber techniques. When the Heavenly Saber Art was slashed out, it seemed as if there were countless shadows of saber techniques.

As this saber slashed out, the three-hundred meters saber light cut through the void. In everyone's eyes, there seemed to be only this one saber light. This saber was too beautiful. This saber seemed to be able to slash everything! Where the saber light passed, the void seemed to have a little bit of black silk. That's the crack in the void.

Only, it was fleeting, making it difficult for people to notice. This is enough to prove. The power of this saber had reached a terrifying level.

"Boom".

Finally, this saber slashed on the Sea-Stablizing Pearl. Two terrible forces collided fiercely. The saber light was also smashed and dissipated by the Sea-Stablizing Pearl in an instant. Only, the Sea-Stablizing Pearl also stopped.

Yuan Kongzi opened his eyes wide. At the moment when the Sea-Stablizing Pearl collided with the saber light, his mind was greatly shaken!

"Puffing".

Yuan Kongzi spat out a mouthful of blood. But he didn't care at all. His gaze was fixed on the Sea-Stablizing Pearl. A look of shock appeared in his eyes.

"Crack".

As expected, the Sea-Stablizing Pearl cracked open.

"Boom".

The next moment, the Sea-Stablizing Pearl shattered. A large amount of water fell into the Nine Profound Sect. Fortunately, the Nine Profound Sect is all mountains. Otherwise, so much water might be enough to cause a flood.

"How can this be?"

Yuan Kongzi seemed to not believe his own eyes.

Shattered! The Sea-Stablizing Pearl actually shattered! This is a treasure passed down by the Guardian Primordial Mountain. How could it shatter? And, it was slashed by Lin Fei.

Lin Fei's eyes were icy cold. He glanced at his own palm. There was no more Saber Qi. But his Saber Essence was even more terrifying. The power had increased by more than a hundred times!

A saber was swung out, which was simply earth-shattering. Even a magic treasure like the Sea-Stablizing Pearl couldn't withstand Lin Fei's single saber.

"Wind Summoning Technique, Wind come!"

Yuan Kongzi gritted his teeth. Suddenly, a bone-chilling wind, seemed to blow into the bones, instantly filling the void.

"Go!"

Yuan Kongzi used the Wind Summoning Technique. He wasn't prepared to defeat Lin Fei with the Wind Summoning Technique. But to escape!

Immediately, Yuan Kongzi turned into a fleeing light. The speed was incredible. In this regard, martial artists were far inferior to Foundation Establishment True Person. After all, a True Person could perform escape techniques. If they couldn't beat a Martial Saint, they could directly perform escape techniques to escape. The Martial Saint could do nothing about it.

"Hmph, Heaven Saber Momentum!"

Lin Fei seemed to have anticipated it. In the void, the Heavenly Saber condensed again. At the same time, the momentum of the Heavenly Saber quickly spread within a radius of thousands of meters.

Yuan Kongzi, who had turned into a fleeing light and was preparing to escape, was enveloped in it. The momentum of the Heavenly Saber, although not real saber momentum, already possessed a bit of the power of the saber momentum.

Especially Lin Fei's saber intent increased significantly. It made the power of the Heavenly Saber Momentum stronger. Therefore, it could also have a certain impact on the Foundation Establishment True Persons.

"Hm?"

Yuan Kongzi's speed dropped significantly. He had to reveal his figure through the escape technique. If it was said that True Persons had escape techniques far superior to martial artists, then martial artists also had corresponding means to cope with it. That's saber momentum!

Once a martial artist evolved the saber momentum, cultivators who wanted to escape would be suppressed by the saber momentum.

Only, a typical Martial Saint was just hard to comprehend saber momentum. Lin Fei didn't have real saber momentum, but he had the momentum of the Heavenly Saber. He caught Yuan Kongzi off guard, even the escape technique was interrupted.

"Now's the time!"

Lin Fei's whole body was enveloped by the "Wind Summoning Technique". However, Lin Fei's Dao Body was strong enough. And, it was also covered with a layer of saber essence letting the cold wind blow on his body couldn't cause any harm to him.

Lin Fei leaped up.

Close Yet Distant!

Even in the void, Lin Fei could also use Close Yet Distant. And, a leap was tens of meters. In the blink of an eye, he arrived in front of Yuan Kongzi.

Lin Fei's eyes were icy cold. He didn't erupt with saber essence again. Because, when using the Heavenly Saber Art, Lin Fei's saber essence was almost exhausted.

However, it doesn't matter. The strongest means of a Martial Saint, apart from the true essence in the body, is their own Dao Body. Once a True Person was approached by a Martial Saint, that's pretty much a death sentence.

Lin Fei extended his hand. With palm substitutes saber directly downward with his palm. A harsh chop, no saber essence, just a flesh and blood hand.

However, Yuan Kongzi was like facing a great enemy as if he had encountered the most terrifying thing. A shield defending a magical artifact was immediately placed in front of him.

"Crisp sound".

However, it had no effect. The shield magical artifact, under Lin Fei's palm, was like paper, instantly cut open. Then a defensive spell layer appeared.

Lin Fei's hand, with almost no obstruction, directly cut open this defensive spell layer. Magical artifact, spell. Two layers of defense, in front of Lin Fei's Dao Body were of no use.

This was the Dao Body of a Martial Saint! Even without any saber essence and saber energy in the body, the Dao Body of a Martial Saint was still the most terrifying lethal weapon!

Yuan Kongzi saw this scene. Finally, a trace of fear appeared in his eyes. Because he felt the threat of death.

"No, I just built the foundation, standing at the pinnacle of the world, and can live for five hundred years, how can I die here? Lin Fei, you are a Martial Saint, I am a Foundation Establishment True Person, how can we fight to the death for these ants? I cannot die..."

Yuan Kongzi's voice became sharp. However, Lin Fei's hand did not show any signs of stopping.

"Bang".

Lin Fei chopped down with a hand. It did not cut open Yuan Kongzi's law body. He directly blasted Yuan Kongzi's head. Immediately, blood from Yuan Kongzi's body spurted out, sprinkling in the void. A rich smell of blood drifted into Lin Fei's nose. Yuan Kongzi's head was blasted, and even a Foundation Establishment True Person was undoubtedly dead.

"Thump".

The corpse fell from the sky and hit the ground and smashed into a pile of meat.

For a while, the entire Nine Profound Sect was silent. Everyone's mouth was wide open as if they couldn't believe what was happening in front of them.

Because a Foundation Establishment True Person had fallen! A dignified True Person had fallen! Moreover, he was killed on the spot by Lin Fei, witnessed by thousands of disciples of the Nine Profound Sect.

Lin Fei killed Yuan Kongzi head-on. Killed the only Foundation Establishment True Person of the Guardian Primordial Mountain!

Step by step, Lin Fei walked down from the void and came to Zhao Kongming and others. Seeing the crowd still in shock, not coming back to their senses, Lin Fei calmly said: "Sect Master, go clean up and spread the news of today. Let the whole Great Chang know I've become a Martial Saint and killed the Guardian Primordial Mountain's True Person!"

At this time, Lin Fei was very clear. There's no need to keep a low profile. On the contrary, it needed to be high-profile! Let everyone know Nine Profound Sect had a Martial Saint who could kill a Foundation Establishment True Person. With such a reputation, any force, before dealing with the Nine Profound Sect, needed to weigh it carefully!

"Respect the Saint's decree!"

The Sect Master was shaking all over, his voice trembling with excitement. Lin Fei, killed the Foundation Establishment True Person. The entire Great Chang would definitely be shocked beyond measure! The Nine Profound Sect, of course, would rise with the tide. It could be compared with the first-class immortal gates of the present world!

......

The thirteenth year of Yongqing in Great Chang, early April.

Yuan Kongzi, a Foundation Establishment True Person from Guardian Primordial Mountain, with the magic treasure the Sea-Stablizing Pearl, attacked the Nine Profound Sect, intending to exterminate the entire Nine Profound Sect. Lin Fei, the Martial Saint of the Nine Profound Sect, appeared out of nowhere. With his saber, he cut down Yuan Kongzi! The True Person fell!

The news spread out. The martial world was shocked! The immortal sects were shocked! The entire world was shocked!
