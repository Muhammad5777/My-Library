38. The Perfection Of Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist

A note from RighteousEgg

Shoutout: A Corrupted Legacy [Villain Xianxia LitRPG System Progression Fantasy]

"Finally, I've done it!" Li Wuchen exclaimed.

With a thud, the body of the Foundation Establishment cultivator fell to the ground, marking the completion of the final requirement.

[System Activated]

[System: Demonic God Vessel]

[Criteria Met:]

- Activate Amulet: Complete

- Activate Partial Demonic Transformation x3: Complete

- Kill Higher-Ranked Cultivator: Complete

[Congratulations! You Are The New Demonic God Vessel]

"What's going on? A vessel?" Li Wuchen muttered, panic creeping into his voice as he stared at the floating screen before him. The very idea of serving as a vessel for such a dark and malevolent being filled him with dread, and he wondered what he had gotten himself into in his pursuit of power.

In the woods by the official road, a group of bandits surrounded a sedan chair. There were also a dozen or so guards nearby, but they were already corpses lying on the ground.

"S-So many bandits...What should we do?" the steward trembled as he spoke.

A young and beautiful woman, supported by her maidservant, also looked pale at the moment. This was also the first time she had encountered such a situation.

However, she was now the backbone of the group and had to summon up the courage to shout at the bandits on the opposite side, "Which mountain do you come from? Blocking the road can only be for the sake of money. But as long as you let us go, we will give you silver as much as you want! We have fifty thousand taels of silver bills on us right now. We will give you all of them."

The bandits on the opposite side lit up at the words. Fifty thousand taels of silver notes were not a small sum. However, they were bandits and silver bills were useless to them. They needed cash.

"Miss Zhang, everyone loves silver. But this time, we not only want silver but also your lives! It's just a pity for a beauty like Miss Zhang. I really can't bear to kill you like this. If it weren't for the order from the boss, I might even want to take you up the mountain to play for a few days."

The people of the Zhang family turned pale at these words. This meant that they were inescapably killed today. These bandits were going to kill them all.

"Leave no one alive!" The leader of the bandits' eyes flashed with a hint of ferocity.

"Dad, your daughter will come down and accompany you."

Miss Zhang shed tears as she pulled out a dagger, ready to commit suicide to avoid being dishonored by the bandits.

"In broad daylight, how dare you bandits to do this? You deserve to die!" Suddenly, a cold voice echoed in everyone's ears.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei took a step forward and charged into the circle of bandits.

"How dare you interfere in our business? Kill him!"

Although the bandits were somewhat surprised, they did not care about Lin Fei since he was just one person.

"Swish." Lin Fei swung his saber.

Suddenly, a white saber light flashed, and several bandits felt a coldness on their necks. Their heads had been separated from their bodies.

Blood spurted out, and the air was filled with a strong smell of blood.

"Bang, bang, bang."

With his nimble footwork and transcendent Flowing Clothes Skill, Lin Fei was like a fish in water in the midst of the group attack.

The bandits couldn't even touch the corner of his clothes.

Meanwhile, Lin Fei continued to decapitate them with his saber and knocked them out with his Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

"Great!"

Lin Fei felt a sense of excitement in his heart.

He had been practicing martial arts for such a long time, always "enduring" and constantly improving his strength through cultivation.

However, the number of times he had fought in a real battle was few, let alone being so invincible like now, fighting alone against dozens of fierce bandits.

Lin Fei had always suppressed himself, perhaps without even realizing it. But now, facing these bandits, Lin Fei had no mercy.

After all, these bandits were all stained with blood on their hands. They deserved to die! So, Lin Fei killed them without hesitation and with great pleasure.

He even got some unexpected surprises.

"Your Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist has become more powerful as you fight, and you have thoroughly comprehended the concept of ferocity during the battles."

"Your Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist has reached perfection through days of accumulation and one moment of enlightenment!"

A voice rang in Lin Fei's mind.

"Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist has reached perfection?" Lin Fei was pleasantly surprised.

It had been a long time since his Nine Shadow Saber Technique had reached perfection, but his Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist had not improved at all. Even the guidance task for Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist had not been removed from the mission hall.

But now, killing these bandits had unexpectedly led to the perfection of the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

"Ferocity concept?" Lin Fei received a memory.

Then, he instinctively used Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist.

"Boom!"

The fist struck out, and the air seemed to vibrate. In the ears of many, there was a deafening roar, as if a real dragon and elephant were roaring. This fist was truly ferocious to the extreme.

"Crack!"

Lin Fei's fist hit the saber of a bandit. The saber was made of high-quality steel, but it was still shattered by Lin Fei's fist. The bandit, along with his saber, flew out and his chest caved in. He was dead.

The power of Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist at the perfection level was so terrifying.

"Run!" The bandit leader was terrified and fled.

But Lin Fei was also fast, and his Flowing Clothes Skill allowed him to catch up to the leader in just a few steps.

Then, he slashed with his saber.

"Splurt!"

The bandit leader's head flew high into the air and fell to the ground with a thud.

The leader's body lay motionless on the ground, and the remaining bandits were too afraid to run anymore. They threw down their weapons and knelt on the ground.
