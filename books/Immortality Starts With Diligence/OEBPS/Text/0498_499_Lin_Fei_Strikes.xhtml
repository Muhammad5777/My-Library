499. Lin Fei Strikes

"Heh heh, just as I thought, a motley crew with each harboring their own schemes," Bloodthirsty Martial God sneered inwardly at the scene. If these three truly joined forces, his situation would indeed be perilous.

But now, even without seeing the world's origin, infighting had already erupted among them. Clearly, they were a disjointed group, hardly a threat.

"Lin, my friend, now is not the time to discuss these matters," North Wind True Person shook his head and slowly said.

"A formidable enemy? You mean this one? If I slay him, I claim fifty percent of the world's origin!" Lin Fei declared, glancing at Bloodthirsty.

"If you can single-handedly defeat Bloodthirsty Martial God, what's half the True Spirit Realm's world origin to you?" North Wind True Person responded, almost laughing in anger. He was infuriated not by the underestimation of Bloodthirsty, but by the audacity of Lin Fei. After all, he had been Bloodthirsty's rival for years, unable to best him.

Now, an obscure Martial God claimed he could slay Bloodthirsty. How could North Wind True Person not be enraged?

Besides, if Lin Fei indeed managed to single-handedly defeat Bloodthirsty, granting him fifty percent of the origin would be justified. But was such a feat even possible? Improbable!

Bloodthirsty Martial God possessed sixteen laws, a formidable force indeed! Even Heart Demon Martial God wasn't confident he could trap Bloodthirsty in an illusion. And even if Bloodthirsty fell into an illusion, Heart Demon Martial God combined with North Wind True Person wouldn't be able to kill him.

Lin Fei turned his gaze toward Heart Demon Martial God. Understanding Lin Fei's intention, Heart Demon Martial God smiled and said, "I have no problem with that."

He was aware of Lin Fei's strength, but even with Lin Fei's prowess, Bloodthirsty was a top-tier Martial God with sixteen laws, not to be trifled with.

Lin Fei challenging Bloodthirsty? Managing to withstand him would be impressive enough. Defeating or even slaying him? It was beyond contemplation.

Heart Demon Martial God also wanted Lin Fei to understand. Though he could defeat him, it didn't mean Lin Fei was invincible. It was merely because Lin Fei's laws happened to counter Heart Demon Martial God's.

Mi Tian chose not to intervene, letting Lin Fei confront Bloodthirsty Martial God alone. With him and North Wind True Person supporting from the sidelines, no matter what happened, they could control the situation.

"Swoosh."

Lin Fei stepped forward.

"Bloodthirsty Martial God, meet your end!"

Seeing Lin Fei approaching, Bloodthirsty Martial God sneered, "Thinking anyone can stand against me? Foolish!"

With a low growl,

"Boom."

Bloodthirsty launched a punch.

Simultaneously, a fleeting blood-red glow emanated from him. He was Bloodthirsty Martial God, with all his power residing in his bloodline. His laws were also linked to his bloodline. Hence, with the surge of blood, his power explosively increased.

Bloodthirsty Martial God seemed brash, but he was actually bold yet meticulous. Upon engagement, he showed no mercy, always going all out.

"Saber World!"

Without hesitation, Lin Fei summoned the Saber World, instantly engulfing Bloodthirsty. Immediately, Bloodthirsty was suppressed, experiencing pressure from all directions.

This was the fusion of Lin Fei's twelve world laws with the Saber World, a transformative combination capable of suppressing and sealing opponents.

Bloodthirsty Martial God was indeed powerful, possessing sixteen laws. In terms of the number of laws, he had four more than Lin Fei's twelve.

However, not all laws are equal. Bloodthirsty's laws were quite ordinary, even niche. And Lin Fei?

His were world laws! All-encompassing and inclusive, embracing myriad streams, with profound and far-reaching implications.

Under the overwhelming might of such terrifying world laws, Bloodthirsty Martial God's punch, incredibly, failed to break through the Saber World.

"Rumble."

Lin Fei's Saber World violently shook, with the ferocious power seemingly on the verge of tearing it apart, yet it ultimately withstood the onslaught. Bloodthirsty Martial God's strike failed to breach Lin Fei's Saber World, and Lin Fei's counterstrike was imminent.

"Boom."

Unleashing the power of one thousand three hundred particle worlds, Lin Fei held nothing back. His physical form seemed to boil over in an explosion of power. He unleashed a devastating punch, landing squarely on Bloodthirsty Martial God.

"Pff."

In the next moment, Bloodthirsty's formidable body caved under Lin Fei's punch, spewing out a mouthful of blood. Bloodthirsty Martial God seemed unable to believe the scene before his eyes.

"How is this possible? Your aura, the essence of thunder tribulations doesn't exceed five times. How can you possess such terrifying power?"

He was in utter disbelief. Lin Fei's punch left his body riddled with dense, web-like cracks.

"Crack."

The next moment, Bloodthirsty's body shattered like glass, completely disintegrating. The immense force of Lin Fei's punch obliterated nearly all his particles.

"No, Blood Regeneration! Blood Reversal!"

Bloodthirsty roared furiously.

"Hum."

Instantly, his blood emitted a sinister crimson glow, and in an instant, endless blood seemed to blanket the void, turning the entire space into a sea of blood. Simultaneously, in a certain part of the void,

"Boom."

A colossal force, seemingly of immense magnitude, descended.

It was the Sea of Origin, the power of laws.

Bloodthirsty Martial God, whose particles had been nearly entirely obliterated, was stimulated by this energy, and astonishingly, he rapidly regenerated. Yes, he was fully restored.

"Go!"

However, the rejuvenated Bloodthirsty, devoid of his previous arrogance, fled precipitously, transforming into a blood light, vanishing in the blink of an eye, leaving no trace behind. The void once again returned to its tranquil state.

"He didn't die and managed to escape?"

Lin Fei furrowed his brow. He had thought his victory was almost certain, yet Bloodthirsty Martial God had somehow escaped. And that final technique, it seemed vaguely familiar to Lin Fei. It wasn't Bloodthirsty's own power, but likely the effect of a magical treasure.

However, this scene left North Wind True Person and Heart Demon Martial God astounded. What had they witnessed? Lin Fei's punch had nearly obliterated Bloodthirsty Martial God, bringing him to the brink of demise.

This was Bloodthirsty, the renowned Martial God with sixteen laws, yet he was almost slain by Lin Fei! For a Martial God, such a defeat meant total annihilation!

Previously, they thought it extraordinary if Lin Fei could even contend with Bloodthirsty. But now, the reality was before their eyes. Lin Fei had completely overpowered Bloodthirsty Martial God!

However, neither of them were ordinary individuals, and they quickly composed themselves. North Wind True Person, noting Lin Fei's confusion, calmly explained, "That was Bloodthirsty's bloodline magical treasure, which can expand or contract and is hidden within his particles. His bloodline magical treasure can store a certain amount of flesh and blood particles.

As long as the magical treasure remains intact, he won't die. The flesh and blood particles stored within can immediately regenerate. However, that is Bloodthirsty's trump card, a treasure reportedly crafted by a Martial Ancestor himself. Not every Martial God has such an asset."

Lin Fei nodded, "I see."

His failure to kill Bloodthirsty was due to that bloodline magical treasure. It seemed one should never underestimate anyone, even a Martial God. They too could have hidden means of survival, especially life-preserving tactics, which were endlessly diverse.
