678. The Chaos Pearl Secured, Supreme Assault

"Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh."

At that moment, numerous Supremes burst forth from the Anlan Realm, immediately seeking the figure of Squid Supreme. However, they found no trace of him.

"What's going on? Where is Squid Supreme?" they questioned. "Even if he is exceedingly fast, he couldn't have vanished this swiftly."

"It's impossible for him to disappear without a trace so suddenly; there must be some mischief at play."

"I saw it myself; Squid Supreme was hindered by a formation and was subdued by those two Supremes."

"Indeed, a flash of light and Squid Supreme was gone. It must be connected to those two."

"Hand over Squid Supreme! Do you really think you two alone can monopolize the Chaos Pearl he possesses?"

In no time, these Supremes swiftly converged around Lin Fei and the Clairvoyant Supreme. The two exchanged glances, not out of fear but with a sense of unexpected joy.

"What did you say? The Chaos Pearl is with Squid Supreme, who just fled the Anlan Realm? Ha ha ha, Supreme Lin, fortune truly favors us without effort!" the Clairvoyant Supreme exclaimed ecstatically.

Initially, their intent was merely to not let any Supreme escaping from the Anlan Realm go unchecked. Whether or not there was a Chaos Pearl was unknown to both Lin Fei and the Clairvoyant Supreme. It was purely their determination to capture any escaping Supreme that had led them to apprehend Squid Supreme.

To their surprise, Squid Supreme actually possessed the Chaos Pearl, and fortunately, they had acted to subdue him. Otherwise, had Squid Supreme managed to escape, all their prolonged efforts and sacrifices would have been in vain.

Fortunately, Squid Supreme was suppressed by Lin Fei. Nonetheless, despite what others claimed, Lin Fei could not wholly trust them. He needed to completely eradicate Squid Supreme to ascertain whether he indeed carried the Chaos Pearl.

And now the moment was nearly upon them. Squid Supreme was relentlessly oppressed and eroded within Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

With a final "pfft," the last particle of Squid Supreme was obliterated.

Squid Supreme's screams echoed, yet within the Eternal Realm, Lin Fei commanded everything. Unknown to all, Squid Supreme had only just perished; most assumed he had died earlier.

"Squid Supreme is dead!" Lin Fei communicated telepathically to Clairvoyant Supreme.

Clairvoyant Supreme, stirred by the news, quickly replied, "Does Squid Supreme indeed possess the Chaos Pearl?"

"The Chaos Pearl?" Lin Fei turned his attention back to the Eternal Realm. He didn't need to look; everything within the Eternal Realm was perceptible to him.

After the last particle of Squid Supreme was obliterated, indeed, a pearl the size of an infant's fist emerged from the remains. The Chaos Pearl—undoubtedly.

This pearl, Lin Fei pondered, as the power of the Eternal Realm reached out to probe it. Yet, it was in vain. The pearl was integral, impervious even to the might of Lin Fei's Eternal Realm. It seemed indissoluble and indestructible.

"Clairvoyant Supreme, we should have the Chaos Pearl now," Lin Fei conveyed. "It is time to depart."

Clairvoyant Supreme's heart surged with elation; they had indeed secured the Chaos Pearl, a windfall by any measure. Considering how briefly they had been in the Anlan Realm, acquiring the Chaos Pearl so swiftly was nothing short of miraculous. However, their situation was still precarious.

Looking around, the eyes of the surrounding Supremes were fixed on Lin Fei, convinced that he had slain Squid Supreme and seized the Chaos Pearl. These Supremes would not let Lin Fei and Clairvoyant Supreme leave easily.

"What now, reluctant to hand over the Chaos Pearl?" they taunted. "Or perhaps you are pondering how to escape?"

Impatience was beginning to show among the gathered Supremes. Surrounded by so many, Squid Supreme had no choice but to flee in disarray. Even if Lin Fei and Clairvoyant Supreme were both top-tier Supremes, what of it?

"Flee?" Lin Fei chuckled coldly. "When did I ever say I would flee? The Chaos Pearl is on my person. Yet, you cannot have it! If I choose to leave, none can stop me. Whoever dares to obstruct me shall meet their end!"

His tone was icy, devoid of hysteria, exuding a calm that instilled fear in others. Such composure in a person who has not lost his sanity, especially one who is a top-tier Supreme, was truly formidable. Dealing with someone like that was not easy.

Yet, Lin Fei's domineering stance ignited the fury of many Supremes around him. After all, there were several top-tier Supremes among them, each a formidable force in their own right. Were they to fear Lin Fei?

"Very well, I would see for myself what ability you possess to slay me," sneered one of the top-tier Supremes as he stepped forward. With each step, a palpable force seemed to emanate from him—a gravitational force!

Yes, gravity! It was as if a field of intense gravity surrounded him. Anyone within this Supreme's reach would feel the overwhelming pressure of this gravitational force, which, at the level of a Supreme, was terrifying.

"Hum."

Even Lin Fei initially felt the substantial pressure, a testament to the strength of the gravitational force. However, that was all it amounted to. In terms of gravity, Lin Fei might not compare, but mere gravity counted for little. Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was not limited to just gravitational forces.

"Eternal Light!"

With a mere thought from Lin Fei, the Eternal Light sprang into existence, enveloping the area in an instant. Before the astonished eyes of those present, the Supreme, known as Grey Domain Supreme, found himself caught within its relentless grasp without a shred of resistance.

In the blink of an eye, enveloped by the luminous energy, Grey Domain Supreme vanished, pulled into the depths of the Eternal Realm. Murmurs of confusion and alarm echoed amongst the onlookers.

"What happened?"

"How did Grey Domain Supreme, a top-tier Supreme, just disappear like that?"

"Could it be that light again?"

All eyes turned to Lin Fei's Eternal Light, recognizing its extraordinary nature. Whatever or whoever it touched seemed to vanish without a trace, swept away into the Eternal Realm where they were beyond the reach of others, completely at Lin Fei's mercy.

"This place is…" Grey Domain Supreme, now inside the Eternal Realm, looked around in astonishment. He realized he was in a world—a unique and magical world capable of restraining even a top-tier Supreme like himself.

"Break free!" he commanded, unleashing the full force of his gravitational power. A halo of gravity radiated outwards, a formidable force that could destabilize the very fabric of any realm's space.

Such terrifying gravity could cause the collapse of the void in any domain, could Lin Fei's realm withstand it?

As expected, the realm shook under the immense force of Grey Domain Supreme's gravity, the void beginning to crumble. But this was merely a fleeting disturbance within the Eternal Realm.

The collapsed space quickly restored itself, and instead, increasing layers of pressure descended upon Grey Domain Supreme, a burden so great that it threatened to crush him entirely.

"This can't be... This is impossible…" Grey Domain Supreme's screams of despair echoed throughout the Eternal Realm, his voice filled with disbelief and terror as the relentless pressure seemed to promise his imminent demise.
