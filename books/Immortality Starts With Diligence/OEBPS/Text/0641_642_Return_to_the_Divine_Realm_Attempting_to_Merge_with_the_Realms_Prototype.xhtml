642. Return to the Divine Realm! Attempting to Merge with the Realm's Prototype

"Sigh... we've finally returned."

"Ha ha, what a thrilling adventure this time!"

"Usually, when we traverse the Chaos, we tread cautiously, fearing we might provoke someone or stir up trouble. But this time? We clashed with dozens of Supremes and boldly snatched the realm's prototype right in front of them."

"Now those Supremes must be utterly dumbfounded. Even their secret tracking methods were cleanly shattered by Martial Ancestor Lin, leaving not a trace behind."

Upon returning to the Divine Realm, the Black Tortoise Supreme began to praise. It wasn't really flattery for it was indeed the truth. And truly, he felt exhilarated.

The other six Supremes also widened their eyes, Even feeling some regret for not having followed Lin Fei and the others into the Chaos, missing witnessing that spectacle.

It was quite a pity.

Lin Fei shook his head slightly and said, "It's not as exaggerated as you think. We just caught them off guard this time. The Supremes of the Chaos have various methods. My descent of the Illusory Realm isn't as powerful as you think. Some Supremes actually recovered in an instant, they merely pretended to be subdued by the Illusory Realm."

Lin Fei's words surprised both the Black Tortoise Supreme and the Azure Emperor Supreme. They hadn't expected there to be such deeply concealed Supremes.

Indeed, the vast Chaos should never be underestimated. Otherwise, one might not even know how they died.

The Chaos is perilously dangerous. One must be extremely cautious!

However, thankfully, this mission could be considered a complete success. Lin Fei and the others had been gone for nearly six to seven months, but they had acquired the realm's prototype. This was an item of infinite potential.

Of course, only Lin Fei could harness the full power of the realm's prototype.

"Fellow Supremes, I must now enter seclusion. Once the realm's prototype is removed from its place of origin, it might cease its transformation. If the realm's prototype stops transforming, then our effort in seizing it would have been meaningless. Therefore, I must seclude myself and ponder over this matter. If the Dark Demon Domain rises again, you may awaken me."

With these words, Lin Fei proceeded to his seclusion. He was well aware of the importance of this seclusion, thus, he specifically made it clear to the Azure Emperor Supreme and the others.

Unless it was the resurgence of the Dark Demon Domain, all other matters were trivial and should not disturb him.

Lin Fei swiftly entered the chamber, And with a mere thought, his consciousness plunged into the Eternal Realm.

Inside, a realm's prototype was present, actively resisting the Eternal Realm.

Indeed, it was a confrontation.

Lin Fei's Eternal Realm had merely "swallowed" the realm's prototype, but had not yet merged with it.

Merging was far from a simple task, rather, it was exceedingly challenging.

After all, the realm's prototype held the potential to evolve into a complete realm.

And Lin Fei's Eternal Realm?

It was merely at the True Monarch level, above which lay the True Immortal level worlds, and only above that, the realm that could live in the Chaos.

Thus, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was significantly inferior to a full realm. For the Eternal Realm to fully absorb the realm's prototype was like a snake swallowing an elephant—Potentially choking in the attempt.

It was uncertain whether, upon merging, the realm's prototype or the Eternal Realm would dominate.

Lin Fei naturally favored dominance by the Eternal Realm, as he could control it. If the realm's prototype were to dominate, Lin Fei might lose control over the Eternal Realm.

Moreover, if after merging the realm's prototype took precedence, it would be akin to Lin Fei being consumed by it—A loss too great to justify.

Thus, a solution was needed to flawlessly merge the realm's prototype under the dominion of the Eternal Realm.

Lin Fei quietly observed the realm's prototype. This prototype was isolated by the Eternal Realm in a vast space, suppressed.

The realm's prototype was non-aggressive, but since being moved from its birthplace, it had begun to shrink, its essence weakening daily.

Furthermore, Lin Fei could no longer feel any life force gestating within it. In other words, the realm's prototype was gradually "dying."

Given this trend, it wouldn't be long before the prototype was rendered useless. Let alone evolve into a true realm. That was simply impossible.

"I cannot let the realm's prototype continue to deteriorate. It must be merged. And the Eternal Realm must dominate. Merging all aspects of the realm's prototype is unwise.

Then, can I merge only the core of the realm's prototype? That is, the core that might allow the realm's prototype to slowly transform into a full realm? That is the key!"

Lin Fei's mind flickered with countless flashes of insight.

What exactly was at the core of the realm's prototype?

After some thought, Lin Fei prepared to dissect the realm's prototype. Using the power of the Eternal Realm, he began to forcibly suppress the prototype, gradually separating it part by part.

"Start with the outer layer. These outer layers are merely the attached forces of the realm's prototype and actually play no role in its potential to form a complete realm."

Lin Fei detached the external forces of the realm's prototype,

Instantly, the prototype shrank by eighty percent!

Only twenty percent remained.

This was approximately the core of the realm's prototype. With a gentle touch of his consciousness,

"Boom."

Lin Fei felt that the realm's prototype was incredibly chaotic.

Various laws, rules, and primal sources were in disarray.

However, deep within the realm's prototype, there seemed to be a peculiar force.

It had "vitality," the key element allowing the realm's prototype to continually evolve.

Upon closer inspection, it appeared as a small, pitch-black hole, seemingly absorbing external forces endlessly, and transforming them into new energy, continuously sustaining the core of the realm's prototype.

"Is this black hole the core of the realm's prototype?"

Lin Fei furrowed his brow. He was uncertain. But even if he swallowed the core of the realm's prototype, could he truly gain complete control?

That was not guaranteed! Unpredictable issues might arise.

Yet, under the current circumstances, how could he not take a risk?

Relying on the Eternal Realm to evolve on its own, even with a tenfold acceleration in time, how long would it take to evolve into a True Immortal level world?

As for becoming a realm? That had never even been considered.

Therefore, after much deliberation, and weighing his options for a long time, Lin Fei finally made a decision.

"No matter what, I have to try. I can suppress even the realm's prototype. Why not its core? If there's any unusual force, I can immediately remove it. My Eternal Realm is completely under my control; any issue can be resolved immediately! So, I must swallow the core of the realm's prototype and see."

After much contemplation, Lin Fei made up his mind. That was to give it a try! With this thought, he hesitated no longer.

"Realm's prototype, open up!"

With a thought from Lin Fei, the power of the Eternal Realm immediately descended upon the realm's prototype.

"Crack."

Finally, the realm's prototype fractured!
