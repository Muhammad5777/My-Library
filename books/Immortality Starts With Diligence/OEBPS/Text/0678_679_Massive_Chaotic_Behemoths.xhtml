679. Massive Chaotic Behemoths

Dead.

Grey Domain Supreme had perished just like that. A top-tier Supreme, utterly devoid of any resistance, was eradicated within mere moments by Lin Fei in the Eternal Realm—a definitive and irrevocable fall. His cultivation was obliterated, and his dao and essence dissipated into nothingness.

A chill ran through the many Supremes present, deterring them from advancing further. The cautionary tale of Grey Domain Supreme was vivid before their eyes—who would dare provoke Lin Fei and Clairvoyant Supreme now?

Clairvoyant Supreme himself felt a shiver of apprehension. Lin Fei's strength far exceeded his expectations. He had thought acquiring the Chaos Pearl would be arduous and maintaining it against the backlash of other Supremes could be even more challenging. Success might have relied partly on luck. However, the ordeal had been unexpectedly "simple"—but was it truly simple?

Clairvoyant Supreme knew better; it was anything but simple. Without Lin Fei's formidable power, securing the Chaos Pearl would have been a daunting task, let alone escaping the clutches of other Supremes.

After slaying Grey Domain Supreme, Lin Fei looked up, his gaze sweeping across the crowd as he spoke calmly, "Who else?"

His tone was steady, his voice not particularly loud. Yet, as his words echoed, a hushed silence fell over the multitude of Supremes. The once bubbling whispers ceased abruptly with the fall of Grey Domain Supreme.

Many Supremes looked towards Lin Fei with eyes full of wariness. If Lin Fei had merely defeated Grey Domain Supreme, they might not have been so frightened, so utterly still.

But Lin Fei hadn't just defeated him; he had dominated and decimated him without allowing any form of retaliation—a prowess that starkly surpassed many of the Supremes by not just one but several degrees.

How could they possibly dare to obstruct Lin Fei now? Even the allure of the Chaos Pearl was tempered by the reality of inadequate strength.

"If there are no objections, we shall take our leave," Lin Fei declared, his voice devoid of any real desire to annihilate all the assembled Supremes.

Given the extensive implications involving so many powerful beings, Lin Fei was not inclined to make unnecessary enemies. This venture had been merely to assist Clairvoyant Supreme in acquiring the Chaos Pearl.

High visibility could certainly serve as a deterrent to others, but to truly antagonize so many Supremes would be unwarranted.

Thus, Lin Fei stepped forward, and in an instant, he and Clairvoyant Supreme transformed into two streaks of light, vanishing into the depths of chaos.

The remaining Supremes exchanged glances, their expressions complicated. They had competed fiercely for a long time and paid substantial prices, only to inadvertently pave the way for another's success.

Yet Lin Fei was just ahead, potentially within reach should they choose to pursue. But who would dare?

"Let it be," one murmured as the pursuit of the Chaos Pearl concluded. "This orb was not ours to claim."

"This man, Lin Fei—unbelievably, the Chaos has birthed another nearly invincible Supreme. Could he be another Doomsday Supreme?"

Expressions of wonder and resignation mingled among the crowd. A Doomsday Supreme stood almost unmatched among the Supremes, having slain countless top-tier ones and ranking just below the Master of Chaos.

Despite this title, Doomsday Supremes carried a flaw; no matter their efforts, they fell short of ascending to Master of Chaos, thus earning the moniker "Invincible Supreme"—a Supreme in name, unable to attain the pinnacle of chaos lordship.

Yet, this distinction mattered little to most; to them, an Invincible Supreme bore no practical difference from a Master of Chaos—both were equally untouchable.

"With one Doomsday Supreme already unsettling the balance of chaos, the emergence of another could be disruptive," another speculated with a sly chuckle. "I wonder how the Doomsday Supreme would react upon hearing of Lin Fei. Perhaps he is already aware..."

As the Supremes grappled with their mixed feelings, some harbored a lingering dissatisfaction despite recognizing their inferior tactics in the struggle for the Chaos Pearl.

Their inability to best Lin Fei in power didn't dampen their resolve completely; while they dared not confront Lin Fei directly, they saw no harm in nudging more capable entities into the fray—like the Doomsday Supreme.

They subtly fanned the flames of rivalry by spreading tales of Lin Fei's unmatched prowess, even drawing daring comparisons to the Doomsday Supreme, hoping it would provoke a reaction.

Back in Kunyu City, Lin Fei and Clairvoyant Supreme returned to the calm of Clairvoyant's residence.

"The Chaos Pearl," Lin Fei announced, extending his hand. Nestled in his palm lay a translucent orb, the size of an infant's fist, radiating a captivating light. The orb's allure was undeniable.

Clairvoyant Supreme's eyes ignited with desire as he gazed at the orb in Lin Fei's palm, his hand inching forward.

However, Lin Fei's sudden tightening grip on the orb halted Clairvoyant's advance. Raising his eyes to meet Lin Fei's steady gaze, Clairvoyant understood the unspoken message.

He retracted his hand, offering a smile, "Supreme Lin, you have secured the Chaos Pearl for me. As per our agreement, I owe you information on the Chaotic Behemoths, not just one but several. Yet, I must caution, the intelligence comes with certain risks."

Lin Fei remained silent, prompting Clairvoyant Supreme to realize that mere promises wouldn't suffice.

"The exact location is hard to pinpoint," Clairvoyant continued. "You'll understand once you accompany me there."

Resolved, Lin Fei and Clairvoyant Supreme embarked once again, departing from Kunyu City and voyaging deep into the chaotic void. The journey spanned farther and took longer than their prior venture to the Anlan Domain, lasting over two months.

"We're here," Clairvoyant Supreme finally declared, halting their flight. His gaze pierced into a peculiar stretch of the chaotic void ahead that appeared collapsed, not quite in disarray but rather forming a unique void, heavy with the essence of chaos.

It seemed like a realm, yet not entirely so. Within, Lin Fei sensed auras eerily familiar—Chaotic Behemoths, unmistakably, and not just one.

"What exactly is this place?" Lin Fei couldn't help but inquire, his eyes reflecting a keen intrigue.

Clairvoyant Supreme took a deep breath before explaining, "We all know that Chaotic Behemoths are not creatures of community. In fact, they hardly bear any semblance of amiability towards one another. Typically, encounters between two Chaotic Behemoths mean battle, often to the death."

"However, this place is different," he continued. "Here, numerous Chaotic Behemoths coexist peacefully, because this is the realm of the Chaotic Behemoth King—a creature even the Master of Chaos dare not provoke."

Hearing this, Lin Fei's expression shifted dramatically.
