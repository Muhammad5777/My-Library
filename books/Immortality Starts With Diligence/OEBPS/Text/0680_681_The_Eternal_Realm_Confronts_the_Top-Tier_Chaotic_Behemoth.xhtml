681. The Eternal Realm Confronts the Top-Tier Chaotic Behemoth!

"One has been subdued! Yet, it is insufficient."

Lin Fei did not slay the Chaotic Behemoth. He merely suppressed it using the power of the Eternal Realm.

After all, even if a Chaotic Behemoth could be felled by Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, such an act would require time.

Moreover, it demanded the full force of the Eternal Realm to execute. Now was not the moment for slaying Chaotic Behemoths. It was crucial to swiftly "capture" and "subdue" more Chaotic Behemoths.

This was paramount. Should he miss this opportunity, Lin Fei would need to traverse the Chaos in search of other Chaotic Behemoths.

Thus, he placed great importance on this chance. He must capitalize on this opportunity to secure enough Chaotic Behemoths.

One, two, or even three would not suffice. He needed more Chaotic Behemoths. Ideally, eight or ten.

After all, Lin Fei had only this one chance. He absolutely could not afford to waste it.

With determination, Lin Fei, in the next instant, shot towards the distance. He had sensed it. There were Chaotic Behemoths afar.

In this world, Chaotic Behemoths were almost ubiquitous, even far exceeding Lin Fei's expectations.

Previously, the Clairvoyant Supreme had mentioned there might be dozens of Chaotic Behemoths here. Dozens of Chaotic Behemoths were already considered an astronomical number. Especially within the Chaos where even encountering several was rare.

Yet, now Lin Fei realized it was not just dozens of Chaotic Behemoths. But hundreds, even thousands! This world was truly a realm of Chaotic Behemoths!

Lin Fei's heart surged with emotion. Yet, he was acutely aware this was his only chance. Thus, he absolutely could not squander this opportunity. But he must act swiftly.

Moreover, he could only afford to strike decisively. He absolutely could not afford any delay.

Soon, Lin Fei halted. Before him, five Chaotic Behemoths appeared. Yes, precisely five.

Upon seeing Lin Fei, these Chaotic Behemoths were momentarily startled. It seemed strange to them.

How could there be a cultivator of Chaos?

Without a moment's hesitation, Lin Fei acted. Even to prevent these Chaotic Behemoths from causing any great commotion, Lin Fei straightforwardly deployed the Illusory Realm, accompanied by the Eternal Light.

"Buzz."

The Illusory Realm descended. Instantly, the expressions of these Chaotic Behemoths shifted slightly, then became bewildered. Such was the effect of the Illusory Realm.

Able to intimidate a Supreme, let alone a Chaotic Behemoth?

It is known that in terms of will, a Chaotic Behemoth is somewhat inferior to a cultivator's Supreme.

Lin Fei's Illusory Realm, naturally, proved effective. Moreover, it was accompanied by the Eternal Light.

"Swoosh."

Subdued by the Illusory Realm, these Chaotic Behemoths had no power left to resist. Caught by Lin Fei's Eternal Light, they were swept straight into the Eternal Realm.

Five Chaotic Behemoths. Along with the previously subdued one, that made six Chaotic Behemoths. That was a full six!

"Continue!"

Yet, Lin Fei was still not satisfied. He continued to fly at great speed towards more Chaotic Behemoths.

However, the fluctuations from the Illusory Realm and those from the Eternal Realm eventually caused a certain disturbance.

An incomparably massive beast lay in a vast lake, seemingly bathing in the lake and thoroughly enjoying itself.

"Splash."

Suddenly, the Chaotic Behemoth raised its head.

"There is the scent of a cultivator of Chaos. And the speed is fast, showing no sign of departure. After so many years, has another cultivator of Chaos come? Since you've come and do not wish to leave, then stay forever."

"Boom."

Then, the Chaotic Behemoth rose. Its massive body flew out of the lake, turning instantly into a streak of light, disappearing without a trace.

At that moment, Lin Fei unleashed the Eternal Light. He recklessly swept up every Chaotic Behemoth he encountered into the Eternal Realm.

Moreover, Lin Fei's audacity grew ever larger. Fittingly, Lin Fei's Eternal Realm was itself immensely vast. Not just subduing a few Chaotic Behemoths and even subduing dozens, in truth, had little impact on the Eternal Realm.

Lin Fei had subdued several Chaotic Behemoths. It appeared to be a considerable number. Even the continuous effort seemed to have consumed much time.

But in reality, from the moment Lin Fei began to subdue the behemoths, to their suppression into the Eternal Realm was merely a matter of a few breaths. And now, Lin Fei was still subduing more.

Seven, eight, nine, ten...

However, these were merely ordinary Chaotic Behemoths.

Lin Fei had not encountered any of the top-tier Chaotic Behemoths. But Lin Fei could not force such encounters. He could not specifically seek out the top-tier Chaotic Behemoths. He could only take things as they came.

Whenever he saw any Chaotic Behemoth, Lin Fei would subdue it immediately.

"Hmm?"

It was uncertain how much time had passed. Suddenly, Lin Fei looked up. He had just subdued several Chaotic Behemoths. But suddenly, he felt an unprecedented pressure. It seemed perilous!

"Swish."

Lin Fei sensed it.

A Chaotic Behemoth. It was an incomparably massive behemoth, emitting a terrifying aura. Not the kind found in ordinary Chaotic Behemoths. But that of a top-tier Chaotic Behemoth. Even stronger than the typical top-tier ones!

However, Lin Fei did not flee immediately. He could sense that this Chaotic Behemoth was not the King of Chaotic Behemoths.

Moreover, if he could also subdue this Chaotic Behemoth, wouldn't it match the strength of at least six, or even ten Chaotic Behemoths?

Lin Fei could already feel his time was running out. This world had become aware of his presence. It would not allow him to keep subduing Chaotic Behemoths indefinitely.

Perhaps, this was his last chance to subdue a Chaotic Behemoth. So, if it could be this Chaotic Behemoth, that would be a boon.

"This is the last Chaotic Behemoth. Once subdued, I must leave immediately! And it's a top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, also an opportunity..."

Lin Fei murmured under his breath. In the next moment, he did not retreat but advanced.

"Boom."

Lin Fei instantly flew towards the Chaotic Behemoth.

"Ah?"

The top-tier Chaotic Behemoth, seeing Lin Fei charging at it, a flicker of surprise crossed its gaze.

"Cultivator from Chaos. You dare to bring turmoil to our world. This is not a place where you can run wild. Swallow!"

This Chaotic Behemoth regarded all beings with a haughty gaze, seemingly dismissing Lin Fei from consideration. Even though Lin Fei had already subdued numerous Chaotic Behemoths, the creature remained confident, a testament to the extraordinary nature of this Chaotic Behemoth.

"Buzz."

The next moment, the Chaotic Behemoth truly made its move. And it was terrifying right from the start.

The Chaotic Behemoth opened its massive maw, transforming, as if into a black hole, continuously attempting to swallow Lin Fei. The fearsome suction seemed intent on devouring Lin Fei completely.

Moreover, Lin Fei could clearly feel that he must not fly into the creature's mouth. Otherwise, he might die!

This intuition was crystal clear to Lin Fei. This was an extraordinary Chaotic Behemoth. Perhaps, its stomach was its strongest asset. Even though Lin Fei possessed the Eternal Realm, he dared not truly let himself be swallowed.

"Think to devour me? Then try to see if you can swallow the Eternal Realm itself. Eternal Realm, stay!"

A fierce light flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

"Boom."

The next instant, Lin Fei transformed into the world. This time, it was not merely the Eternal Light. It was the Eternal Realm!

Lin Fei directly transformed into the Eternal Realm, the formidable Eternal Realm, sweeping majestically towards the Chaotic Behemoth.
