339. The Power of Great Mountain Divine Seal

The gates of Taiyi Dao Palace trembled violently. All the disciples and elders stared in horror at the towering, ancient divine mountain that reached the clouds. Their eyes widened in disbelief.

"Could that ancient divine mountain be the Great Mountain Divine Seal?"

"It's unfathomable. We've been at Taiyi Dao Palace for so long, yet we had no idea that the Great Mountain Divine Seal was here all this time."

"It's said that the Great Mountain Divine Seal was crafted by the founding master from an ancient divine mountain. I thought it was a mere legend. Who would have believed it to be true?"

"This ancient divine mountain seems to have always been within Taiyi Dao Palace. I never imagined it to be the Great Mountain Divine Seal..."

Many were taken aback. After all, this ancient divine mountain had been in Taiyi Dao Palace for ages. Not just the disciples, but most of the elders were also unaware.

The Great Mountain Divine Seal hadn't been used for countless years. At the very least, it hadn't been invoked in millennia. It was only natural for the disciples and elders to be oblivious.

However, the Palace Master was undoubtedly aware that the majestic mountain standing tall in the Taiyi Dao Palace was indeed the Great Mountain Divine Seal!

With a thunderous roar, as the Palace Master invoked the Great Mountain Divine Seal with a command token, the ancient divine mountain tore from the earth. Whistling, it headed straight for the gates of Taiyi Dao Palace.

"Hmm?"

Lin Fei abruptly raised his head.

All at once, darkness enveloped the sky! It was the shadow of a mountain. Eclipsing the sun and sky, an imposing, mysterious, vast, and terrifying aura emanated. It was suffocating, and that aura instantly locked onto Lin Fei. He found himself trapped, with nowhere to run.

"Great Mountain Divine Seal!"

Lin Fei's eyes narrowed ever so slightly. He had deduced it instantly. It had to be the Great Mountain Divine Seal. Although it bore the semblance of a mountain peak, one could discern its true nature when sensed with intuition.

It was a magic treasure and it far surpassed the caliber of a Golden Core treasure. It was a Nascent Soul magical treasure!

While a Nascent Soul magical treasure didn't represent a true Nascent Soul, such a treasure could easily obliterate any Golden Core cultivator. Overpower any Martial Sovereign! Such overwhelming and crushing power, it was incomparably formidable.

"Gravity Domain! Annihilation!"

Without a moment's hesitation, the Life Wheel on Lin Fei's back shone brightly from its second and fourth layers. Two top-tier Great Divine Abilities erupted simultaneously.

As gravity intensified, given the massive size of the ancient divine mountain, the force was immensely daunting. Yet, the ancient divine mountain continued its descent towards Lin Fei. Without a hint of deacceleration, it remained steady.

This meant Lin Fei's Gravity Domain hadn't affected the ancient divine mountain in the slightest. Even the power of the Annihilation Divine Ability, when unleashed upon the ancient divine mountain, proved futile. There wasn't even a slight tremor. Such was the power of the Great Mountain Divine Seal!

Simultaneously, the Great Mountain Divine Seal finally descended upon Lin Fei's Saber Domain. With a soft crunch, Lin Fei's Saber Domain shattered almost instantly.

For the first time, Lin Fei's Saber Domain was broken. Ever since he had developed the Saber Domain, it had been unbeaten. But now, it was violently "smashed" open by the Great Mountain Divine Seal.

Once the Saber Domain was crushed by the Great Mountain Divine Seal, Lin Fei was momentarily rendered unable to utilize it. Thus, even if he wished to use the Saber Domain for "teleportation", he was powerless. Such is the prowess of the Nascent Soul magic treasure!

It remained dormant until it struck with earth-shattering force, leaving no escape.

"Sovereign Lin!"

"Is that the Great Mountain Divine Seal?"

"How terrifying! This is truly the backbone of Taiyi Dao Palace!"

"Great Mountain Divine Seal, a Nascent Soul magic treasure, is invincible..."

The numerous Martial Sovereigns of the Ancient Origin Sect, upon witnessing the terrifying might of the ancient divine mountain, felt despair sinking into their hearts.

Before witnessing the Great Mountain Divine Seal and experiencing the Nascent Soul magic treasure firsthand, they could speak confidently. However, when they did see and feel its terror, they realized how horrifying the Nascent Soul magic treasure truly was. It wasn't something a Martial Sovereign or a Golden Core could contend with. Seemingly, no force or Divine Ability could withstand it.

With a deafening boom, the Great Mountain Divine Seal finally descended, instantaneously pressing down on Lin Fei. And Lin Fei's body was instantly crushed into dust by the Great Mountain Divine Seal!

If the Great Mountain Seal could turn Lin Fei into dust, the Great Mountain Divine Seal would naturally fare even better. Yet, Taiyi Dao Palace still chose to deploy the Great Mountain Divine Seal because of its unique power that the Golden Core magic treasure didn't possess.

Perhaps, it could shatter Lin Fei's undying physique. After all, in this world, there's no such thing as a truly undying physique. It's bound to have flaws.

The Great Mountain Seal failed to eradicate Lin Fei solely due to its insufficient power. But the strength of the Great Mountain Divine Seal, being a Nascent Soul magic treasure, greatly exceeded the former. It was the source of the Palace Master's hopes.

This time, as the Great Mountain Divine Seal descended, Lin Fei was crushed to dust. Not even a mist of blood remained. He seemed to have vanished completely.

However, in the next instant, a figure emerged from the void. It was Lin Fei! His figure reappeared, seemingly unscathed.

"A Nascent Soul magic treasure is only this strong."

A glint of something unusual flashed across Lin Fei's eyes. Within Taiyi Dao Palace, the thing he dreaded most was this Great Mountain Divine Seal. After all, being a Nascent Soul magic treasure, no one truly knew the extent of its terrifying power.

But just now, as Lin Fei was pressed down by the Great Mountain Divine Seal, it was undeniably powerful. Lin Fei's physical body was crushed without resistance, reduced to mere dust. This was even more thorough than the Great Mountain Seal, with not even a mist of blood remaining, and to the naked eye, no trace of Lin Fei's body could be found.

However, Lin Fei was ultimately not dead. This was because even the Great Mountain Divine Seal could not grind the "particles of his body" into dust. These particles were invisible to the naked eye. Lin Fei's Undying Seal was present in every single particle of his body. As long as these particles persisted, Lin Fei would not die. The power of the Great Mountain Divine Seal seemingly could not reach this "particle" level.

So, even if Lin Fei was turned to dust hundreds of times, it would have no substantial effect on him. What's more, the one controlling it was merely a Golden Core cultivator. Hence, Lin Fei naturally revived.

Suddenly, the Great Mountain Divine Seal descended once more, and just like before, Lin Fei's body disintegrated into dust. But the next moment, a tower-shaped magic treasure fiercely flew out from within Taiyi Dao Palace.

"True Fire Tower, Refine!" The voice of Taiyi Dao Palace Master rang out. This was a magic treasure specifically meant for suppression and sealing. Although it was of the Golden Core tier, once something was trapped within the True Fire Tower, it would immediately be refined by the true fire inside. The flames within the tower weren't ordinary flames but true fire, the kind used specifically for refining tools and pills. It could melt almost anything.

The Taiyi Dao Palace Master's eye was incredibly sharp. He immediately identified Lin Fei's "weakness." Lin Fei could not resist the Great Mountain Divine Seal, given its Nascent Soul magic treasure status. But the Great Mountain Divine Seal also couldn't kill Lin Fei outright.

Lin Fei's undying physique was indeed formidable. But the strategy seemed clear now - use the Great Mountain Divine Seal to crush Lin Fei into dust, then employ the True Fire Tower to suppress and refine him with its fire. This true fire could melt everything. He refused to believe that he couldn't refine Lin Fei to death!

As a result, inside the True Fire Tower, the flames roared and blazed, engulfing the entire area where Lin Fei was, frantically refining him.
