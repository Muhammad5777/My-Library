171. Preparing for Foundation Establishment

"Disperse!"

With a mere thought, Lin Fei's muscles and skin incessantly trembled. Subsequently, the layer of greenish-black Earthly Evil Qi encompassing his body was directly shattered by him.

Only at this moment did Lin Fei truly comprehend the significance of purity, and imperviousness to a myriad of poisons. Indeed, the perfection of the physical body was not as straightforward as he had imagined. The perfection of the physical body, in itself, was a lofty achievement.

Rebirth from the old, was this an insignificant feat?

Almost since the commencement of perfecting the physical body, the life strata of a martial artist became fundamentally different from those of common folk, or even Qi Condensation cultivators.

"Retreat!"

Yuan Xingzi and Yuan Chenzi exchanged glances, and without any hesitation, they immediately retreated, fleeing in a frenzy.

Lin Fei had already attained physical perfection. Moreover, his combat power was terrifyingly potent. He hadn't yet built his foundation to become a Martial Saint, yet he could already unleash power rivaling those who had.

Even if they were currently wielding magic treasures, they were no match for Lin Fei. They must report back to the Grand Elder.

Once the Grand Elder established his foundation, they must annihilate the Nine Profound Sect and behead Lin Fei with swift unerring might. Otherwise, once Lin Fei became a Martial Saint, that would truly sow the seeds for endless calamity!

However, the first to flee were not Yuan Xingzi and Yuan Chenzi but He Yuanzi who earlier had become furiously indignant.

Even with murderous intent surging to the heavens, pledging to behead Lin Fei, He Yuanzi was the first to retreat. He Yuanzi, surprisingly, retreated even before Yuan Xingzi and Yuan Chenzi.

Regardless of the numerous disciples of the He Mountain Dao Sect still present, he spurred on his magical artifact. In a blink of an eye, he had retreated dozens of meters away.

"Escaping? It's too late!"

Lin Fei's gaze was icy cold. Instantly, the momentum of Heavenly Saber was fully unleashed. Within a radius of hundreds of meters, all fell under the dominance of his Heavenly Saber.

"Boom."

Being enveloped by the Heavenly Saber's momentum, it was as if they were suddenly shouldering a huge mountain. He Yuanzi, Yuan Xingzi, and Yuan Chenzi, among others, paused in their tracks. Their speed drastically reduced. Although it didn't come to a complete halt, their speed had slowed significantly. That was enough!

"Heavenly Saber!"

Lin Fei waved his hand. The sky full of saber energy instantly came slashing down. To deal with these top-tier Qi Condensation cultivators, there was no need for one thousand meters of saber energy, even dozens of meters of saber energy, these top-tier Qi Condensation cultivators couldn't withstand.

"No......"

With a slash of saber energy, the first to perish was He Yuanzi. How could he withstand Lin Fei's saber energy without his Mountain River Seal magic treasure?

Therefore, saber energy instantaneously broke through He Yuanzi's protective magical artifact. He Yuanzi was cleaved in half, his body falling to the ground.

Then, it was Yuan Chenzi's turn. His "Earthly Evil" magic treasure was indeed venomous, but it was not essentially a defensive magic treasure after all.

When the Earthly Evil Qi failed against Lin Fei, Yuan Chenzi was left without any other means. He fell instantly under Lin Fei's saber energy, meeting the same end.

Finally, it was Yuan Xingzi's turn. His magic treasure "Heavenly Thunder" could invoke a hint of thunder energy, transforming it into the utmost yang and unyielding Heavenly Thunder.

It was indeed able to counter Lin Fei's saber energy. Therefore, he managed to escape a certain distance. However, after Lin Fei had dealt with Yuan Chenzi and He Yuanzi, his huge saber energy immediately coalesced. One thousand meters of saber energy reappeared.

"Slash!"

When one thousand meters of saber energy descended from the sky, all Yuan Xingzi could do was let out a miserable cry. He was instantaneously ground to a pulp by Lin Fei's saber energy, turning into a pool of blood and flesh.

As for the disciples of the He Mountain Dao and Guardian Primordial Mountain, they were all slain by Lin Fei's saber energy.

In just a short time, not a single Qi cultivator from the He Mountain Dao and Guardian Primordial Mountain escaped, all were beheaded by Lin Fei!

Looking at the corpses strewn about, each disciple of the Nine Profound Sect seemed to be in a dream.

"A Martial Saint... is just so..."

Many murmured softly, looking at Lin Fei's figure, only one term appeared in their minds.

Peerless brilliance!

Wasn't Lin Fei's performance at this moment a display of peerless brilliance?

Killing immortal masters as if slaughtering chickens. Even top-tier Qi Condensation cultivators, even those with magic treasures, were no match for Lin Fei.

"Hahaha, with Elder Lin in our Nine Profound Sect, we shall flourish!"

"The fortune of the Nine Profound Sect, the fortune of the Great Chang Martial Dao!"

The master of the Nine Profound Sect was extremely excited at this moment, as were Zhao Kongming and the other three elders.

Their excitement and exhilaration were not solely because Lin Fei had displayed his mighty prowess, slaying three top-tier Qi Condensation cultivators and defeating the invading enemies. Rather, it was due to Lin Fei's state at this moment.

Skin as soft as a baby's, emitting a unique fragrance, pure and untainted, impervious to myriad poisons, immune to all techniques...

All in all, a myriad of extraordinary characteristics. And these characteristics all indicated one thing.

Physical perfection!

Lin Fei had already achieved physical perfection!

Only the Sect Master and the three Grand Elders understood what physical perfection represented.

It was the most crucial requirement for a martial artist to establish a foundation and become a Martial Saint!

Now that Lin Fei had achieved physical perfection, it implied that Lin Fei could attempt a foundation establishment. There was a high hope that he might become a Martial Saint!

Once Lin Fei became a Martial Saint, he would be the first Martial Saint of the Nine Profound Sect. In fact, he might even be the only Martial Saint in Great Chang at the moment!

Lin Fei took a step forward, arriving beside the corpses of Yuan Xingzi and Yuan Chenzi.

Although Yuan Xingzi's body had turned into a puddle of mud, Lin Fei had still retained some saber energy, without completely destroying the magic treasure "Heavenly Thunder."

Thus, with a swipe of his big hand, he grasped both the "Heavenly Thunder" and "Earthly Evil" magic treasures in his hand.

After all, even if he couldn't use two magic treasures, for Qi Condensation cultivators, they were precious items they would want to get at any cost.

It was just a pity about the Mountain River Seal. In fact, this seal was even better, especially in situations of group attack. The Mountain River Seal would have been more effective.

For instance, on the battlefield, once one applied the Mountain River Seal, regardless of whether hundreds of thousands of soldiers or even more troops, they wouldn't be able to withstand such terrifying power.

Magic treasures represent the power of the Foundation Establishment level. Only True Persons who have built their foundations could refine them. This was not a power of the mundane world.

Lin Fei put away the "Heavenly Thunder" and "Earthly Evil" magic treasures. At this point, Zhao Kongming, the Sect Master, and others had also rushed over.

"Lin Fei did you... did you really achieve physical perfection?"

Zhao Kongming was the first to ask. The other two Grand Elders and the Sect Master all focused their attention on Lin Fei with a hint of anticipation in their eyes. Although they felt that he should have achieved physical perfection, they still wanted to hear Lin Fei's answer personally.

Lin Fei nodded and said, "Yes, I have achieved physical perfection."

"Boom."

In the minds of the three Grand Elders and the Sect Master, it was as if a thunderous explosion had occurred.

Excitement!

Too much excitement!

"Hahaha, my disciple, my good disciple. You have fulfilled the wish that our founder failed to achieve. With physical perfection, you're only a step away from becoming a Martial Saint after building your foundation!"

Zhao Kongming couldn't help but laugh out loud. The Nine Profound Sect was finally going to birth a Martial Saint!

In the past, the founder of the Nine Profound Sect passed away with regret, sitting in loneliness on Ancestral Master Peak.

And now, Lin Fei, a disciple of the Nine Profound Sect, finally had the opportunity to make up for the regret of the founder.

"Elder Lin, the supreme elder of the royal family has passed away. Now, the world is about to fall into chaos. Those Immortal Sects can no longer contain themselves. As for the He Mountain Dao, it's not a concern, as they won't be able to produce a Foundation Establishment True Person in a short time.

But the Guardian Primordial Mountain is different. I heard that the Grand Elder of the Guardian Primordial Mountain is currently taking Foundation Establishment Pills.

There's a high possibility that he will successfully build his foundation in the near future and become a Foundation Establishment True Person. When the Guardian Primordial Mountain makes a move then, it will definitely be a Foundation Establishment True Person."

The Sect Master spoke solemnly. He was reminding Lin Fei. Lin Fei must build his foundation and become a Martial Saint as soon as possible.

Lin Fei nodded, and calmly said, "I came back this time to prepare for Foundation Establishment. Foundation Establishment requires the spiritual qi of heaven and earth. The most spiritual qi is found on Ancestral Master Peak. Lin Miao, go and set up Spirit Gathering Formations on Ancestral Master Peak, the more the better.

Master, Sect Master, I will head to Ancestral Master Peak immediately to start it. No one is allowed to disturb me on Ancestral Master Peak until I come down voluntarily."

Lin Fei was also swift and decisive. The situation was pressing now. He couldn't afford to slowly build his foundation.

Given the current situation, only by building his foundation and becoming a Martial Saint could he protect the Nine Profound Sect!

Having given his instructions, Lin Fei's figure flashed, and he took a step, spanning tens of meters. Step by step, he walked towards Ancestral Master Peak. In a blink of an eye, he disappeared from sight.

"You must succeed in foundation establishment! Our Nine Profound Sect will definitely produce a Martial Saint!"

Zhao Kongming and others, looking in the direction of Ancestral Master Peak, had a hint of hope in their eyes.
