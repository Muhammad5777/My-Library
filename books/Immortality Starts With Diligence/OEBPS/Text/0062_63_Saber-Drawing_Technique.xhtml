Lin Fei once again met the lazy elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion. He presented the emblem to the elder respectfully and said, "Elder, I have come to choose the acquired martial arts."

The elder glanced at the emblem and narrowed his eyes slightly. "You have become an acquired so quickly? Not bad, not bad. However, I recall that you seemed to have chosen three Body Refining martial arts. Which one gave birth to inner power?"

This elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion seemed to have some impression of Lin Fei. After all, disciples who chose three Body Refining martial arts were still few in number.

Lin Fei looked at the elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion and did not conceal anything. He replied, "Elder, I have given birth to inner power in all three martial arts."

"Three-in-one?"

The elder's muddy eyes seemed to clear up all at once, and a sharp light flickered. "Your inner power indeed is three-in-one."

The elder of the Martial Arts Pavilion looked deeply at Lin Fei, assessing him up and down. Then he sighed deeply and said, "Seems that I underestimated you. When you came to choose martial arts at the Martial Arts Pavilion, I thought you were ordinary and even a bit presumptuous.

But I didn't expect that you would really give birth to three kinds of inner power, and even fuse them into one. If I remember correctly, the three Body Refining martial arts you chose were Giant Whale Body Refining Technique, Vajra Iron Cloth, and Tendon Transformation And Bone Forging Technique.

Qi and blood, skin and membranes, and tendons and bones, all are complete!"

Lin Fei remained silent.

The elder pondered for a while and said, "Alright, go to the second floor. You can still choose three acquired-level martial arts for free. However, don't choose any acquired martial arts that you can practice until after you have a teacher. Your master will teach you."

"The elder of the Transmission Hall has already reminded me of this. Thank you, elder."

"I was talking too much. Go ahead, go ahead..."

Lin Fei bowed to the elder and then went straight to the second floor of the Martial Arts Pavilion.

The second floor was different from the first. All of the martial arts here were acquired-level. Therefore, the number of people was also very few.

After all, the number of disciples at the Acquired level was far less than that of the Body Refining level.

Lin Fei's selection of martial arts was also well-planned.

Weapons, boxing, and lightness skill.

For weapons, Lin Fei chose the saber technique. For boxing, he chose the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist. As for lightness skill, he chose the Flowing Clothes Skill.

Among them, Lin Fei had achieved perfection in both the saber technique and the boxing, but the Flowing Clothes Skill was only at the transcendence level. However, Lin Fei did not care, as long as it was usable. There was no need to waste time and energy on it.

As for the saber technique and boxing, both could be taken to the next level, but Lin Fei had not made any progress in understanding the meaning of the Dragon-Elephant Vajra Fist. Perhaps practicing other basic boxing techniques could help, and he needed to go to the first floor of the martial arts hall to make his selection.

Lin Fei's primary goal was to select the saber technique, so he went to the bookshelves where the saber techniques were located. The main characteristic of his Nine Shadow Saber Technique was speed, and the saber techniques he acquired now would also follow this direction.

Therefore, Lin Fei naturally chose "fast saber" techniques, such as "Blinking Saber Technique," "Empty Flash Saber Technique," "One-Stroke Kill Saber Technique," "Thunder Flash Saber Technique," and so on. He saw rows of saber techniques on the bookshelf one after another, all of them fast saber types.

Lin Fei felt a bit dazzled by the variety of techniques, each had its own unique features.

Lin Fei didn't want to make any choices. He wanted them all. However, the reality was that he only had three free opportunities to select acquired martial arts, and any additional martial arts would require expensive contribution points.

Moreover, it would be more difficult to reach the perfection level for acquired martial arts, and if he couldn't reach that level, it wouldn't increase his proficiency in the saber technique or help him understand its meaning.

Therefore, Lin Fei ultimately decided to choose only one saber technique after much consideration. This technique was called the "Saber-Drawing Technique" and was described with only a few words: fast, accurate, and ruthless.

Blood would surely be spilled when using this technique. With his decision made, Lin Fei went straight to the elder.

"Elder, I have chosen my martial art, the Saber-Drawing Technique!"

"This is the secret book of the Saber-Drawing Technique. As usual, you have only one hour to study it."

"Yes, elder."

Lin Fei received the secret book of the Saber-Drawing Technique and immediately entered the practice room.
