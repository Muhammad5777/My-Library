508. The Sixth Heavenly Thunder, Black Lightning!

A note from RighteousEgg

Spring Time (A One Piece Fanfiction)

It is said that humans are adaptable, but I'm pretty certain that whoever said that didn't consider being thrown headfirst into the world of One Piece as a possibility. Especially not with a start date in the middle of the canon timeline.

Join a former physicist turned pirate, as he tries to navigate the many dangers and opportunities of his second life, and perhaps find a new dream along the way. Of course being stuck in Bellamy's body as he is, he'll have to survive the whims of a certain Royal Warlord first...

"Boom."

Lin Fei's consciousness instantly flew into the Sea of Origin, seizing a law. Thus, a heavenly thunder sounded in response. This was the first heavenly thunder! White in color, it fell instantly upon Lin Fei's physical form.

Lin Fei did not use the power of Saber World. He allowed the heavenly thunder to strike him directly. Even more, he didn't utilize the power of the Particle World.

However, when the heavenly thunder struck Lin Fei, it was like a tiny spark of electricity. flickering faintly, it then disappeared without a trace. It didn't cause any harm to Lin Fei whatsoever.

"The first heavenly thunder, too weak..."

Lin Fei murmured softly. He had already transcended three heavenly tribulations. He was no longer a Martial God ignorant of these matters. He understood the power of the heavenly tribulation. Especially since with each tribulation, the potency increases exponentially.

Yet, even so, facing this first heavenly thunder, Lin Fei didn't feel any threat.

"Boom."

Then came the second heavenly thunder. The second heavenly thunder flashed instantaneously, striking Lin Fei.

Again, Lin Fei didn't deploy Saber World, nor did he use the power of the Particle World. He relied solely on his physical body's defense, enduring the second heavenly thunder.

"Still, not much of a feeling."

Lin Fei shook his head slightly, finding it rather uninteresting. So, he delved his consciousness directly into the Sea of Origin, seizing another law. The third heavenly thunder fell in response.

The first two thunders, Lin Fei hadn't felt a shred of threat. But this third heavenly thunder required a bit more of his attention, though that was all. Just a bit more attention.

Lin Fei still did not employ Saber World, letting the third heavenly thunder strike him. Instantly, the particles in Lin Fei's physical body began to disintegrate.

Ten percent, twenty percent, thirty percent, forty percent, fifty percent...

Lin Fei still did not use the power of the Particle World. He endured the third heavenly thunder with purely his physical particles.

However, this third heavenly thunder, the thunder that terrified many, could not harm Lin Fei's physical body. After destroying sixty percent of Lin Fei's physical particles, it completely vanished. The third heavenly thunder still could not trouble Lin Fei!

In fact, Lin Fei himself was also astonished. This was the effect of the world's origin. It had elevated Lin Fei's physical body to such an extent. This was nothing short of a dramatic and qualitative transformation. But this was just the beginning. Lin Fei's goal was not merely to withstand three heavenly thunders.

"Continue."

Then, the fourth heavenly thunder descended. It was still a purple thunder. The purple thunder arrived in an instant, giving Lin Fei no time to react.

This fourth heavenly thunder was incomparable to the previous three. It was almost a qualitative leap. Its potency had increased tenfold! Fortunately, Lin Fei was prepared. He had already deployed Saber World.

"Crack."

A huge gap was torn in Saber World. Saber World shattered. However, after breaking through Saber World, the purple thunder's potency significantly weakened.

After annihilating ten percent of Lin Fei's physical particles, it completely disappeared. Lin Fei easily passed the fourth heavenly thunder. Lin Fei had now obtained four more world laws.

However, for Lin Fei, transcending four heavenly thunders seemed rather ordinary. But for the countless Martial Gods and Deity Transformation cultivators in Ancient City, it was incredible.

"Another four heavenly thunders and it seems the fourth thunder couldn't trouble Martial God Lin either."

"Not just that, Martial God Lin didn't even go all out!"

"When Martial God Lin transcended his third tribulation, he withstood five thunders. This time, I wonder if he can withstand six?"

"Six heavenly thunders, it's unthinkable. To seize six laws at once is unbelievable."

"Of course, it's impossible for others, but not for Martial God Lin."

Many people watched Lin Fei with intense interest.

"Continue."

Without hesitation, Lin Fei's consciousness entered the Sea of Origin again.

"Boom."

The fifth heavenly thunder descended. This time, it was a blood-red thunder, resembling fresh blood, striking an unforgettable impression. This thunder roared mightily, carrying with it endless destructive power as it fell towards Lin Fei.

Lin Fei instantly pushed Saber World to its limit. Twelve world laws fused with Saber World, creating a Saber World akin to a miniature universe. Under the blood-red thunder, it shattered in an instant. This was a complete shattering.

Although the blood-red thunder seemed to have expended some of its power, this slight consumption was negligible. Perhaps only ten or twenty percent?

The remaining power of the thunder instantly bombarded into Lin Fei's body. Ten percent, twenty percent, thirty percent, forty percent, fifty percent...

This time, more than fifty percent of Lin Fei's physical body was obliterated.

Lin Fei forcibly endured it. He did not utilize the power of the Particle World. Finally, the blood-red thunder dissipated. Lin Fei had withstood it! Relying solely on Saber World and his physical particles, he withstood the fifth heavenly thunder! This time, Lin Fei obtained another five world laws.

Once a Particle World is created, and these are particles ten times stronger than before, how terrifying would the creation of a Particle World be?

Lin Fei's power might instantly surge to an unimaginable extent. Faced with such a once-in-a-millennium opportunity, Lin Fei yearned to take a chance. He didn't want to let go just yet. He desired to seize one more law.

After all, he still had the power of one thousand three hundred Particle Worlds unexploded. If he added the remaining fifty percent of his physical particles, withstanding the sixth heavenly thunder was not impossible. But, this also meant taking a risk.

"The sixth heavenly thunder, let me see, what will it be like?"

Lin Fei fiercely raised his head, gazing into the void. His consciousness once again entered the Sea of Origin, seizing another law.

Six laws!

"Hum."

The void trembled.

Everyone's eyes were fixed on the heavens. Seeing that after the fifth heavenly thunder Lin Fei had not yet emerged, they realized his intention. Lin Fei indeed planned to withstand the sixth heavenly thunder. Thus, everyone widened their eyes, equally curious to see what would the sixth heavenly thunder be like?

For a moment, the vast tribulation site fell into a hush. The silence was so profound that one could hear a pin drop. In the void, no thunder appeared. However, a terrifying pressure seemed to envelop the entire Ancient City. As if the thunderbolts were brewing, preparing the sixth heavenly thunder. This alone indicated the terror of the impending sixth thunder!

"Rumble."

Finally, the sixth heavenly thunder made its appearance. This thunder, upon appearing in the void, left many agape. For what did they see? A streak of black lightning, fleeting yet unforgettable.

Black lightning, something they had never seen before. And though it was a flash, a mere glance seemed to set hearts racing as if something had been obliterated.

The appearance of the black lightning caused cracks to form in the void. There was no earth-shattering spectacle, no terrifying phenomena. It seemed exceedingly restrained.

Yet, the more subdued it was, the more terrifying it became. Even the dreadful potency seemed contained. Once this black thunder erupted, how fearsome would it be? Many began to feel apprehensive. Faced with the unprecedented black heavenly thunder, could Lin Fei withstand it?
