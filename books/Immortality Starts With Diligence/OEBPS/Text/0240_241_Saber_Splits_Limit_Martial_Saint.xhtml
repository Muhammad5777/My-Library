241. Saber Splits Limit Martial Saint

"It's Liu Hong!"

"Deputy captain of the Illusion Worry City Enforcement Team, Liu Hong! He has condensed the Qi Flower, acts decisively, and is utterly impartial."

"Liu Hong has slain countless Martial Saints and Foundation Establishment True Persons who have stirred up trouble in Illusion Worry City."

"It's Liu Hong in person! It seems that Illusion Worry City has been keeping an eye on the people of the Ancient Origin Sect."

"These Martial Saints from the Ancient Origin Sect are really bold. They dare to take action in Illusion Worry City. I'm afraid they won't be able to leave the city today."

"They are from a major martial arts sect after all. They are used to being domineering and would not care about Illusion Worry City."

"Let's see how these Martial Saints from the Ancient Origin Sect handle this. If they aren't careful, they might have to stay in Illusion Worry City today."

Many martial artists and Qi cultivators in Illusion Worry City were talking. And they didn't seem to like Lin Fei and the others. There were quite a few sarcastic remarks. This was quite normal.

Lin Fei represented the Ancient Origin Sect, which was a major martial arts sect that usually acted very domineering. The disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect always had a superior feeling when they went down the mountain. This was the truth.

Most martial artists and Qi cultivators in Illusion Worry City were independent cultivators. It would be strange if they had a good impression of the Ancient Origin Sect. Under the intimidation of Liu Hong's voice in Illusion Worry City, the Martial Saints of the Ancient Origin Sect's Law Enforcement Hall also stopped.

"Didn't you hear my command? Spare no one from Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals!"

Lin Fei's voice was already somewhat cold. These Martial Saints from the Law Enforcement Hall seemed to have thought of something.

They immediately gritted their teeth and attacked the remaining two Foundation Establishment True Persons of Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals.

Lin Fei's command was "Spare no one." Therefore, they didn't need to capture them alive. They directly aimed to kill the remaining two Foundation Establishment True Persons.

"Dare to take action again?"

Liu Hong was very fast. However, he was still too late. The Martial Saints of the Law Enforcement Hall had already taken action.

More than a dozen Martial Saints, among them, were even three Limit Martial Saints. How difficult could it be to kill two Foundation Establishment True Persons?

"Ah..."

The two Foundation Establishment True Persons screamed and were immediately killed. At this point, all members of the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals were killed!

Liu Hong was extremely furious. It had been a long time since anyone had dared to be so presumptuous in Illusion Worry City.

"Surround them all! Kill anyone who dares to resist!"

Liu Hong yelled loudly. The many Martial Saints and Foundation Establishment True Persons behind him quickly surrounded the entire courtyard. Lin Fei and the others were in the middle of the courtyard.

At the same time, Liu Hong had already punched out, directly aiming at Lin Fei. Because Lin Fei was blocking the entrance to the courtyard, clearly intending to obstruct the Illusion Worry City Enforcement Team.

"Suppress!"

Lin Fei gave a soft shout.

"Boom."

The transcendental saber momentum exploded instantly. It was as if the void had suddenly solidified. Under the cover of the saber momentum, everyone felt as if they were carrying several heavy mountains on their backs, making them unable to move. For a moment, even Liu Hong's fist, which was shot from the air, disintegrated directly under the suppression of the saber momentum.

"Transcendence saber momentum!"

Liu Hong's face was solemn, and he said each word distinctly.

"Boom."

Liu Hong's words seemed to ignite the surrounding martial artists and Qi cultivators. Even though they were independent cultivators, they understood what the transcendence saber momentum meant due to their mutual exchange in Illusion Worry City.

After all, it is difficult to comprehend the momentum of martial arts. Among the three flowers of a Martial Saint, the Energy Flower and Qi Flower are not particularly rare. But the Spirit Flower is really rare. To condense a Spirit Flower, one needs to cultivate the momentum of martial arts to perfection. This is a hurdle that blocks more than 90% of Martial Saints.

Many Martial Saints can't even comprehend the momentum of martial arts, let alone cultivate it to perfection. But now, this Martial Saint from the Ancient Origin Sect had comprehended the transcendence saber momentum.

"The Ancient Origin Sect came prepared."

"Transcendence saber momentum, how rare it is! Even within the Ancient Origin Sect, that's an absolute top-tier Martial Saint."

"A top-tier Martial Saint? It must be a Limit Martial Saint!"

"Why did the Ancient Origin Sect send such a top talent down the mountain, even to kill the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals in Illusion Worry City? What do they want to do?"

"Aren't they afraid that Illusion Worry City will kill him?"

"Perhaps the Ancient Origin Sect has confidence in this Martial Saint? Or they feel that Illusion Worry City would not dare to do anything to him."

"Heh heh, Martial Saint Liu Hong is famous for his mercilessness. Once he takes action, it's a fight to the death!"

More and more people flocked to the scene. Liu Hong is known for his violent temper. Once he takes action, it's a fight to the death. This was a big battle!

"Transcendence saber momentum? Break!"

Just as others had said, Liu Hong was indeed fearless. A Qi Flower suddenly appeared above his head.

"Boom."

With the emergence of the Qi Flower, the pressure of Lin Fei's transcendence saber momentum seemed to disappear instantly. At least that was the case for Liu Hong. A Qi Flower could block the suppression of the saber momentum to some extent. Of course, this was only because Lin Fei's was the transcendence saber momentum. If Lin Fei's saber momentum reached perfection and even condense the Spirit Flower, the saber momentum would suppress opponents.

Endless True Essence pressure erupted from Liu Hong. Limit Martial Saint! For the first time, Lin Fei faced a Limit Martial Saint directly.

At the same time, Liu Hong also made his move. When he made his move, it was like a mountain collapsing and the earth cracking, the terrifying True Essence explosion, the power of a single punch, was earth-shattering.

No wonder Liu Hong was able to become the deputy captain of the Illusion Worry City Enforcement Team. With such terrifying strength, even if he was just a Qi Flower martial artist, he was a standout among them.

"Shoo."

The next moment, Lin Fei flicked his finger, and a saber essence flew out instantly.

"Boom."

Fierce flames seemed to ignite the void with the emergence of the saber essence. Everyone couldn't help but look up. They saw the flames in the void. No, not flames. But a saber. A saber that was burning with fierce flames, or rather, a saber made up of flames! One thousand meters tall! And it exuded an endless fierce aura.

Lin Fei only flicked his finger lightly, and less than one-tenth of the saber essence inside his body erupted. But he condensed more than one thousand meters of saber essence.

This is the Flame Burning Heaven Saber. A remarkably powerful saber technique.

"Slash!"

Facing Liu Hong's Qi Flower explosion and an all-out punch, Lin Fei's Flame Burning Heaven Saber, under the blessing of the saber momentum, slashed down.

Even during the slashing process, Lin Fei's saber essence was still increasing. The Flame Burning Heaven Saber was also still expanding.

One thousand meters, fifteen hundred meters...

No one could describe this saber. Lin Fei didn't even display the Qi Flower, yet how could he condense such a terrifying saber?

"Boom."

Finally, the Flame Burning Heaven Saber slashed down. It slashed directly against Liu Hong's punch.

Liu Hong was a true Limit Martial Saint. Even if all his True Essence erupted, this punch, under Lin Fei's fifteen hundred meters Flame Burning Heaven Saber, was as weak as tofu. It was instantly cut open by the flame saber. The fierce flames fell directly, instantly engulfing Liu Hong!
