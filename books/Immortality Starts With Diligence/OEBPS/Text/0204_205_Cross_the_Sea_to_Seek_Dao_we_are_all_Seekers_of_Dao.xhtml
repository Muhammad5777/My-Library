205. Cross the Sea to Seek Dao, we are all Seekers of Dao

When Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin finally set foot on land, both of them breathed a sigh of relief as if a huge weight had been lifted off their shoulders.

Drifting at sea for months, exotic beasts lurking all around, having to be on high alert at every moment. Every moment, their minds were stretched taut. Life during those times was not easy.

"Senior, this is Fairy Mist Island. Most of the people on the island are Qi cultivators. They are connected to dozens of nearby islands, forming the Overseas Immortal Islands. There are no powerful sects here, so calling it an Immortal Island is just a name we give ourselves. In reality, on the Ancient Continent, we are called overseas loose cultivators, lacking resources, even sects are reluctant to come to us..."

Zhao Mingyu said with a bitter smile. Lin Fei knew the names of these five Qi Condensation cultivators. Among them, Zhao Mingyu was the leader.

Zhao Mingyu had reached the twelfth level of Qi Condensation, at great perfection. He had taken two Foundation Establishment Pills. Unfortunately, both attempts at Foundation Establishment failed.

In the Zhao family, once someone reached the twelfth level of Qi Condensation at great perfection, the family would grant a Foundation Establishment Pill. Some people could make it in one leap, successfully establishing their foundation. But for most people, one Foundation Establishment Pill is simply not enough for Foundation Establishment.

This was the case for Zhao Mingyu. Later, he tried all sorts of ways and used several years to finally purchase a second Foundation Establishment Pill. However, he still failed to establish his foundation. So, this time, Zhao Mingyu took the risk to hunt the Black Swine Beast at sea in exchange for a Foundation Establishment Pill.

Now it seems that Zhao Mingyu was lucky. Not only did he meet Lin Fei, he even obtained the Black Swine Beast. Perhaps, this time he could get two Foundation Establishment Pills.

"Ancient Continent? So, there's another big continent?"

"Of course, the Ancient Continent is boundless...Specifically, senior, if you stay on Fairy Mist Island for a while, you'll naturally understand."

Lin Fei nodded. Upon arriving at Fairy Mist Island, he broadened his horizons. There were so many Qi cultivators here. Almost everywhere were Qi cultivators.

Of course, there were also ordinary people. However, these ordinary people were not ordinary at all. Because they practiced martial arts. And unlike Great Chang, The Qi cultivators here seem to not have that superior attitude towards martial artists.

"Two seniors, we've arrived at the Zhao family. You two rest first, I'll go report to the family."

Lin Fei nodded. The Zhao family was a Qi cultivator family with five Foundation Establishments. In the entire Fairy Mist Island, they could be considered in the top ten.

After Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin were received by a Foundation Establishment True Person of the Zhao family. Both parties had a brief exchange.

Under the arrangement of the Zhao family, Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin stayed with the Zhao family. And the two, through the Zhao family members, as well as some books and notes, gradually came to understand Fairy Mist Island and the Ancient Continent.

The Ancient Continent was a vast and boundless continent. On it, various sects stood tall. There were nations, sects, noble families, martial artists, Qi cultivators, and so on. But basically, each area was controlled by a sect. For example, the Immortal Sect and the Martial Arts Sect.

The martial arts sects here were not like those in Great Chang. These were true rulers that stand alongside the Immortal Sect!

As for Fairy Mist Island, martial arts had declined there used to be Martial Saint families. But in the past few centuries, not a single Martial Saint had emerged. Therefore, the whole of Fairy Mist Island was still dominated by Qi cultivators.

Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin only understand some of these generalities. But they knew, on the Ancient Continent, there is a higher level of martial arts, and that was enough.

This indicated that their path of seeking the Dao had hope. As for specific Martial Saint techniques, or some martial arts secret books, on Fairy Mist Island, only a few Martial Saint families had them.

Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin did not actively seek out those Martial Saint families. Instead, they waited quietly. After understanding some of the situations in the Ancient Continent and Fairy Mist Island, Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin both understood those Martial Saint families would definitely take the initiative to find them.

A few days later.

Zhao Mingyu, with a face full of joy, came to find Lin Fei.

"Senior Lin, Senior Shen, the eight Martial Saint families of Fairy Mist Island have sent people to find me. They hope to meet the two seniors. Originally, they were all prepared to come in person. But this involves some secrets of the Martial Saint families, and it's not quite convenient in my Zhao family. So, the two seniors need to make a move and go to the Shangguan family among the Martial Saint families."

Lin Fei looked at Zhao Mingyu and said calmly, "The eight Martial Saint families are quite generous. I suppose they've given you a lot of benefits."

Zhao Mingyu did not hide anything, a smile appeared on his face, and he respectfully responded, "Thanks to the two seniors, the eight Martial Saint families have really put in a lot of effort this time. They have actually gathered two Foundation Establishment Pills. As long as I can invite the two seniors, these two Foundation Establishment Pills will be mine."

It has to be said, the eight Martial Saint families were indeed sincere. They gave the middleman Zhao Mingyu two Foundation Establishment Pills. Considering the current situation of the eight Martial Saint families, these two Foundation Establishment Pills were not easy to come by. And Zhao Mingyu also did not hide anything. His attitude was very sincere.

However, Zhao Mingyu was a smart man. He knew that Lin Fei would definitely agree. Because Lin Fei was a seeker of the Dao! He crossed thousands of miles, crossed the sea, all for the sake of seeking the Dao. Lin Fei was a Martial Saint. So naturally, he sought the martial dao! What Lin Fei wanted only the eight Martial Saint families had it.

Lin Fei looked deeply at Zhao Mingyu and nodded, "Two Foundation Establishment Pills, I hope you can succeed in your foundation establishment! Let's go, lead the way!"

Zhao Mingyu was overjoyed. So, he immediately led Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin away from the Zhao family.

Fairy Mist Island, though it was an island, was actually quite large. At least, it should be a quarter the size of Great Chang. Otherwise, it would not be able to accommodate so many Qi cultivators and ordinary people.

Soon, the three arrived at the mansion of the Shangguan family.

The Martial Saint family's mansion was also very impressive. After all, they had given birth to Martial Saints. But compared to the Zhao family, it seemed to have declined a lot. After all, the Zhao family was becoming stronger and stronger. They have five Foundation Establishment True People, and the family's power naturally grows day by day. On the other hand, Once the Martial Saint families had not given birth to a Martial Saint, they would decline.

Lin Fei was now a Martial Saint. He also gradually knew some differences between the Martial Dao and the Immortal Dao.

For example, the Immortal Dao required talent from the beginning. It excludes over 90% of people. The number of martial artists was large, and ordinary people could practice martial arts. Even the number of martial artists who had achieved innate was definitely more than that of Qi cultivators. But when it came to the stage of Martial Saint, it was difficult. The difficulty was not exotic beasts. There were many exotic beasts in the sea.

The Martial Saint families themselves were storing exotic beast flesh and blood, True Qi Perfection was not difficult. The real difficulty was Martial Dao True Intent! Martial Dao True Intent, to achieve perfection was too hard! If Martial Dao True Intent cannot achieve perfection, then one cannot establish a foundation, and cannot become a Martial Saint.

This is the same in Great Chang. In Great Chang, there were very few martial arts masters who could comprehend Martial Dao True Intent.

As soon as Lin Fei and the others stepped into the mansion of the Shangguan family,

"Swish".

dozens of gazes were focused on Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin.

Lin Fei saw the eight people in the lead. There was an exotic fragrance coming off their bodies. Their skin was like a baby's, free of dust and dirt. This is a characteristic of physical perfection!

And True Qi had achieved perfection. But there is one point, Martial Dao True Intent had not achieved perfection. Even, there were many who have not achieved the transcendence of True Intent. The worst few didn't even have Martial Dao True Intent.

Obviously, these people were all martial arts grandmasters. But they were just True Qi Perfection and physical perfection. They didn't lack exotic beast flesh and blood. For these innate martial artists, physical perfection was the easiest to achieve.

"Greetings to the two Martial Saints!"

The eight people in the lead should be the so-called representatives of the eight Martial Saint families, upon seeing Lin Fei and Shen Lianxin, all bowed respectfully.

"May I ask why the two Martial Saints have come to Fairy Mist Island?"

One of the martial arts Masters asked in a deep voice. Lin Fei glanced at the eight people and calmly said, "I crossed the sea, just to seek the Dao. Brother Shen and I are both seekers of the Dao!"

As soon as Lin Fei's words fell, the representatives of the eight Martial Saint families all showed expressions of joy.

"So, they are seekers of the Dao!"

"After so many years, we finally have seekers of the Dao again."

"Hahaha, seekers of the Dao, and Martial Saint at that, our eight families have hope again!"

For a moment, the expressions of these eight family representatives seemed to carry a hint of excitement.
