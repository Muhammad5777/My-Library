697. Metamorphosis! The Body of Chaos!

Lin Fei entertained a startling conjecture: chaos beings! Were these beings, transformed by the Chaos Qi, not embodiments of chaos life? If his world could give birth to chaos life, had it not become a realm of chaos itself?

Lin Fei could scarcely believe it. Previously, his world was merely of the True Immortal rank, not even a Chaos Realm. Yet now, it had leapt into chaos, transcending even the Chaos Realm in its formidable nature?

"No, it's not a chaos world," Lin Fei corrected himself. "My Eternal Realm has merely acquired certain characteristics of chaos; it can now harbor Chaos Qi. That equates to my physical body having transformed into a Body of Chaos..."

With this thought, Lin Fei vibrated slightly.

"Buzz."

Instantly, Lin Fei regained his human form, or rather, his physical state. At this moment, he could distinctly feel the terrifying power within his bodily particles. Even without harnessing the power of the Eternal Realm, just the strength of his physical body was now far superior to his previous Invincible Supreme strength. This clarity was profound for Lin Fei.

Moreover, while in the Transformation Pool, facing the Chaos Qi no longer induced any trepidation or danger but felt profoundly familiar, as natural as a fish in water.

Every particle within him had been transformed and was now fully adapted to the Chaos Qi.

"So, has my physical body truly transformed and metamorphosed into a Body of Chaos?" Lin Fei pondered, contemplating the possibility.

The Body of Chaos! This had been Doomsday Supreme's goal in coming to the Forbidden Tower. Yet, it seemed Doomsday Supreme was still struggling, still endeavoring to transform. Lin Fei, however, had already succeeded in his transformation.

Transforming into a Body of Chaos came with many benefits, especially within the realm of chaos itself, much like a Chaos Behemoth. Yes, he was now akin to a Chaos Behemoth.

Indeed, to some extent, a Chaos Behemoth is a manifestation of a Body of Chaos. However, the Bodies of Chaos that Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme have transformed into are far more formidable.

Lin Fei's transformation was successful, and there was no further transformation possible. Thus, he accessed the system to check on the status of his Eternal Realm.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Supreme

World Level: Chaos World

Illusory Realm: Supreme level

Lin Fei took a look at the current state of his Eternal Realm, which had now evolved into a Chaos World, and his martial realm had also advanced to the Supreme level!

This tremendous improvement showed that the Transformation Pool had indeed played a significant role in enhancing Lin Fei's Eternal Realm.

The current Eternal Realm was worlds apart from what it had been before, with his strength increased by dozens of times.

"Boom."

Just then, a tremendous surge of power erupted within the Transformation Pool.

"Swish."

Doomsday Supreme opened his eyes, his expression one of joyous surprise.

"The Body of Chaos, hahaha, I have succeeded, transformed into a Body of Chaos!" he exclaimed jubilantly. "Once transformed into a Body of Chaos, I am nearly a step into being a Master of Chaos!"

Doomsday Supreme was exhilarated. Now, he could continue to explore the mysteries of the Body of Chaos and gradually comprehend the secrets of the Masters of Chaos. Even if it took thousands, tens of thousands of years, it did not matter.

His lifespan was virtually endless. Eventually, he believed he would fully grasp the realm of the Masters of Chaos through his Body of Chaos. Then, Doomsday Supreme could himself become a Master of Chaos!

This was the greatest benefit of all!

"Whoosh."

Doomsday Supreme rose to his feet, noticing Lin Fei nearby. "Supreme Lin, have you also succeeded?" he inquired.

Lin Fei nodded slightly, "Almost there."

As he moved, it was evident that his physical body was no longer susceptible to the erosion of Chaos Qi.

"Good, then let's leave together," Doomsday Supreme suggested.

With a mere thought, "Swoosh," their figures instantly vanished from sight.

Outside the Forbidden Tower, many were keenly observing, curious about the fate of Lin Fei and the Doomsday Supreme.

"Have Supreme Lin and Doomsday Supreme transformed into Bodies of Chaos?" they speculated. "Once they pass the ninth layer, they get the chance to transform. What lies beyond the ninth layer of the tower?"

"Rumors suggest that secrets about the Forbidden Tower itself are hidden beyond the ninth layer."

"What level have Supreme Lin and Doomsday Supreme achieved?" The spectators were unable to see inside the tower and were unaware that both had already transformed into Bodies of Chaos.

"Look, are those Doomsday Supreme and Supreme Lin?" Suddenly, someone spotted two familiar figures materializing in the void.

"Swish."

Countless gazes converged on these two figures. It was Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme! Recognition dawned among the crowd. They had indeed left the Forbidden Tower.

A mix of shock and anticipation filled the air as many beheld this development. However, Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme were quietly experiencing the uniqueness of their transformed chaos bodies.

Previously inside the Forbidden Tower, they were somewhat unclear about the full capabilities and special state of the chaos bodies. But now, having left the tower and returned to the realm of chaos, the remarkable properties of their new forms began to manifest.

Typically, even a Supreme, when entering the realm of chaos, would constantly face the erosion of Chaos Qi. This relentless force eroded all life forms, without exception. Yet, Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme now navigated this environment differently, showcasing the transformative powers of their chaos bodies.

Supremes are capable of navigating the chaos primarily due to their formidable physical bodies, which resist the erosive properties of Chaos Qi. This resilience allows them to traverse the chaotic realms freely. However, upon their return to the chaos, Lin Fei and Doomsday Supreme immediately sensed a difference.

Previously, they had to rely on their strong bodies to forcibly "withstand" the erosion of Chaos Qi. Now, as they re-entered the chaos, it was as though they were fish returning to water. The omnipresent Chaos Qi, which once posed a threat, not only ceased its assault but even proved beneficial.

Their Bodies of Chaos could absorb Chaos Qi, and fighting within the chaos even amplified their strength to some extent. The chaos had become their "home field."

"Incredible," Doomsday Supreme murmured in awe. "In the Chaos, my strength has actually increased. Is this the power of the Body of Chaos? No wonder there's no comparison between a Supreme and a Master of Chaos. Even the strongest Supreme can't contend with a Master of Chaos!"

Lin Fei shared these sentiments. He could feel that even if he were to transform his Eternal Realm into a Chaos Realm, he still might not be able to stand against a Master of Chaos.

However, now the situation was different. Now that he too possessed a Body of Chaos, perhaps once his Eternal Realm transformed into a Chaos Realm, he might indeed spar with a Master of Chaos.

Only after such a confrontation would they truly know whose power was greater.
