324. Power of Destruction

"Four Divine Abilities? Ha ha ha, just four Divine Abilities after all. How dare a Martial Sovereign with merely four Divine Abilities claim to destroy the guardian formation of the Profound Light Dao?"

Contrary to the shock of Wind Sovereign and the Sect Leader of Ancient Origin Sect, Xuanwu burst out laughing. Observing only four layers of radiance on Lin Fei's Life Wheel, he understood they represented four Divine Abilities.

Xuanwu had seen countless Martial Sovereigns, some of whom had cultivated up to eight Divine Abilities. Yet, even those top-tier Martial Sovereigns wouldn't dare claim they could break through the guardian formation of the Profound Light Cave Heaven. Initially, Lin Fei's display of the transcendent Saber Domain was truly terrifying. When the Saber Domain descended, it immediately suppressed the guardian formation, causing genuine concern for Xuanwu.

However, it now seemed that Lin Fei's grand reputation might be mainly due to his Saber Domain. His cultivation as a Martial Sovereign wasn't worth mentioning at all. How could a mere four Divine Abilities, even combined with the Saber Domain, pose a threat to the millennia-old guardian formation of Profound Light Cave Heaven?

Lin Fei's gaze remained coldly fixed on the guardian formation below. Shortly after, the fourth layer of his Life Wheel radiated brightly.

"Divine Ability, Annihilation!"

This was Lin Fei's fourth Divine Ability and was his most potent one in terms of sheer power. Though he was only a beginner, its effect was earth-shattering.

A thunderous explosion ensued. The Annihilation Divine Ability was so powerful it felt as if space itself was tearing apart.

Lin Fei then casually extended a finger, pointing toward the guardian formation.

"Power of Destruction!"

Annihilation Divine Ability contained both the Power of Destruction and the Power of Erasure. The former referred to breaking and shattering, making any strong, defensive force or object crumble under its might, including the guardian formation of Profound Light Cave Heaven.

With a sharp cracking sound accompanying the release of the Annihilation Divine Ability, the previously smug look on Xuanwu's face vanished, replaced by astonishment and a hint of fear.

It broke! The guardian formation of Profound Light Cave Heaven had actually been breached.

"How is this possible? How could the guardian formation be broken by just one Divine Ability? It can't be, hold on!"

Xuanwu frantically channeled his Spiritual Qi, casting several seals towards the guardian formation. With the influx of Spiritual Qi and seals, the cracks in the guardian formation disappeared momentarily.

However, with a snap of Lin Fei's fingers, the Power of Destruction erupted again. Additionally, the Saber Domain swiftly contracted, concentrating its energy into a much smaller area.

"Boom!"

Finally, the guardian formation was shattered. Under the combined force of the Saber Domain and the Annihilation Divine Ability, it crumbled instantly.

Witnessing this, the True Persons from Taiyi Dao Palace reacted immediately.

"Xuanwu, act now! We can't let Lin Fei breach the defenses!"

There were as many as six Golden Core True Persons from Taiyi Dao Palace.

"Great Mountain Seal!"

Again, it was the Great Mountain Seal. This magic treasure had become almost symbolic of Taiyi Dao Palace. And this time, all six Golden Core True Persons acted together. The towering Great Mountain Seal roared and smashed down toward Lin Fei. Even Xuanwu seemed to use some means to move an entire mountain from the Profound Light Cave Heaven to crash onto Lin Fei.

Lin Fei looked at the fearsome onslaught from within the Profound Light Cave Heaven, his face expressionless.

"Hum
."
The next moment, the second layer of radiance on Lin Fei's Life Wheel shone brilliantly.

"Gravity Domain!"

Without moving a muscle, Lin Fei's second Divine Ability activated. A terrifying gravitational force descended instantaneously.

"Boom!"

The Great Mountain Seals, all at the Golden Core level of magic treasures, were enveloped by Lin Fei's Gravity Domain and immediately plummeted. Even the mountain that Xuanwu had summoned was engulfed by the same terrifying gravitational force. Unable to withstand it, the mountain crashed onto the ground with a resounding thud.

"Rumbling sounds."

Both the Great Mountain Seals and the mountain were pinned to the ground of the Profound Light Cave Heaven by the Gravity Domain, unable to budge an inch.

"This kind of Divine Ability..."

The Golden Core True Persons from Taiyi Dao Palace, as well as Xuanwu, were all wide-eyed, their hearts filled with shock. These were six major Golden Core True Persons, plus Xuanwu, even equipped with magic treasures. Yet, they couldn't even approach Lin Fei.

Lin Fei took step after step, heading towards the gate of the Profound Light Cave Heaven below. Just as Xuanwu and the six Golden Core True Persons were thinking of fleeing, under the dual suppression of the Saber Domain and Gravity Domain, they were instantly thrown to the ground, unable to move.

Lin Fei walked up to them, shaking his head slightly.

"Black Wind Prison!"

The first layer of radiance on Lin Fei's Life Wheel glowed. Simultaneously, the wind seemed to rise throughout the entire Profound Light Cave Heaven. It was a cold wind, touching one's skin, and even the Magic Body of a Golden Core True Person couldn't resist this terrifying wind.

It was the Bone-Eroding Wind!

They could only watch as their bodies began to reveal bones, and their life force rapidly drained away.

"I just formed my Nascent Soul, how can I die!? No..."

The six Golden Core True Persons, along with Xuanwu, screamed in agony. Their voices revealed boundless fear. However, it was futile. The bone-eroding wind revealed white bones wherever it blew. In terms of killing, no Divine Ability was more suitable than Black Wind Prison.

The entirety of Profound Light Cave Heaven wasn't just inhabited by Golden Core True Persons. There were also low-level Qi cultivators. At this moment, all of them couldn't escape the sweep of the bone-eroding wind. As the wind passed, they all turned into piles of white bones.

In the time it took to brew a cup of tea, the entire Profound Light Cave Heaven became silent, a silence reminiscent of death. The bone-eroding wind ceased. Even the Saber Domain and Gravity Domain were retracted by Lin Fei.

"Sect Leader, time to take over Profound Light Cave Heaven," Lin Fei calmly spoke to the Sect Leader of Ancient Origin Sect behind him.

The Sect Leader of Ancient Origin Sect, Wind Sovereign, and the Golden Core True Persons of Red Hades Mansion witnessed how Lin Fei annihilated the entire Profound Light Cave Heaven! Now, the whole Profound Light Cave Heaven was eerily silent. Everyone was dead!

This wasn't just about destroying the sect; it was about obliterating the entire Profound Light Cave Heaven. Lin Fei had "exterminated" every trace of life from it. Everyone was gone!

"Is this the strength of Sovereign Lin?"

"By himself, he obliterated the protective formation of Profound Light Cave Heaven."

"He even destroyed the entirety of Profound Light Cave Heaven on his own."

"And the Golden Core True Persons of Taiyi Dao Palace."

"Is this a Martial Sovereign?"

"Even a top-tier Martial Sovereign pales in comparison to Sovereign Lin."

For a moment, the people of the Ancient Origin Sect were ecstatic and excited. But the Golden Core True Persons of Red Hades Mansion had complex expressions. Because if Lin Fei could destroy the Profound Light Cave Heaven, then he could wipe out the entire Red Hades Mansion. They could clearly see that Lin Fei had held back. Of his four Divine Abilities, he had only used three. And not even at his full strength. How terrifying had Lin Fei's strength been?
