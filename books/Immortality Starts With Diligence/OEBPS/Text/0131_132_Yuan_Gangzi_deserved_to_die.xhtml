132. Yuan Gangzi deserved to die

"Compare saber intent and sword intent?"

Lin Fei's eyes flickered slightly. Looking at Zhao Kongming's tense expression, Lin Fei seemed to faintly understand something. So, Lin Fei directly waved his hand. The thirty-meter-tall saber energy instantly collapsed and disappeared without a trace.

"Master is right, sabers and swords have no eyes. We martial artists who have comprehended Martial Dao True Intent can compare Martial Dao True Intent. There's no need to wield sabers and swords. However, does Master really want to compare saber intent with his disciple?"

"Of course, come on, let me see how far your saber intent has progressed."

Zhao Kongming saw Lin Fei withdraw his saber energy. After the terrifying thirty-meter saber energy completely collapsed and disappeared, his heart finally relaxed a bit.

That thirty-meter-tall saber energy was really frightening just looking at it. He actually wanted to have a friendly spar with Lin Fei using saber techniques and sword techniques just now? Thinking about it now, it was scary!

Zhao Kongming knew how "fierce" his disciple was. He had killed He Mountain Dao's Qi Condensation cultivators with a single saber strike. It would be better to compare saber intent and sword intent. It's not as dangerous.

"Master, this is my saber intent, please give me your guidance!"

The next moment, Lin Fei's saber intent burst forth from within his body.

"Boom."

The terrifying saber intent soared into the sky. The sharp aura filled the air above the Ancestral Master Peak. It was as if countless sharp great sabers were suspended above Zhao Kongming's head, ready to fall at any time, putting immense pressure on Zhao Kongming.

Zhao Kongming's face stiffened slightly. At that moment Lin Fei unleashed his saber intent. He already knew his sword intent was far inferior to Lin Fei's. Even the gap was huge!

Lin Fei had no expression on his face. However, he knew his saber intent had reached Mastery. His master Zhao Kongming had only just begun to comprehend sword intent. There was still a long way to go to reach Mastery.

Martial Dao True Intent, a difference of one realm was a world of difference. There was no comparison at all. Even Lu Zongyi, who had comprehended sword intent for many years, was suppressed by Lin Fei's saber intent. Not to mention Zhao Kongming.

For a moment, Zhao Kongming fell silent.

"Forget it, there's no need to compare saber intent and sword intent. You and I are master and disciple, and the sharpness of our saber intent and sword intent can cause harm to the opponent's Martial Dao True Intent if we're not careful. It's better not to compare."

Zhao Kongming's expression was resolute. He refused to unleash his sword intent.

"Master, it's true that saber intent and sword intent are sharp and dangerous, but I also have fist intent. I don't know if Master would be willing to..."

Lin Fei hesitated for a moment before saying. Fist intent didn't seem as dangerous. It might be worth comparing.

Zhao Kongming resolutely refused: "Disciple, you've just come back, you should rest more. If you really want to spar, let's do it another day. I want to ask you about this trip to the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference. How did you come back so soon?"

Zhao Kongming immediately changed the subject. It seemed that he had just realized Lin Fei's return seemed a bit too fast. He had also led a team to participate in the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference in the past. From the time they left to the time they returned, it should have taken at least ten days to half a month.

After all, after the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference was over. Many martial artists would often stay behind to continue exchanging and networking for a while.

But Lin Fei? It hadn't even been ten days since he left the Nine Profound Sect.

Lin Fei also withdrew his saber intent. He then explained, "Master, the trip to the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference went smoothly. However, after it began, suddenly a Qi Condensation cultivator from Guardian Primordial Mountain, Yuan Gangzi, wanted to seal off the Martial Alliance and prevent anyone from leaving.

Yuan Gangzi's reason was to capture a demonic cultivator who had stolen Guardian Primordial Mountain's techniques. That demonic cultivator was hiding in the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference. Moreover, those Qi Condensation cultivators from Guardian Primordial Mountain even attacked other martial artists. Those who dared to resist were killed without mercy!

Left with no choice, I had to draw my saber and kill Yuan Gangzi. So, the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference ended early. I brought those inner disciples back to the Nine Profound Sect."

Lin Fei briefly explained his experience during the Martial Alliance Exchange Conference.

"Wait, you killed Yuan Gangzi? The top Qi Condensation cultivator of Guardian Primordial Mountain, who had reached the eleventh level of Qi Condensation?"

Zhao Kongming suddenly asked with a serious expression.

"Yes, it was him. However, he didn't seem to be much different from the two high-level Qi Condensation cultivators of He Mountain Dao. I ended them all with a single saber strike."

Lin Fei calmly said. He didn't think it was a big deal. Whether it was the eleventh level of Qi Condensation or the ninth level, for Lin Fei, it was all the same. He killed them all with just one saber strike, never needing a second one.

Zhao Kongming fell silent again. He didn't know how many times he had fallen silent. At this moment, he felt somewhat speechless.

What did it mean that there was no significant difference between the top Qi Condensation cultivators and high-level Qi Condensation cultivators? What did it mean to end them all with a single saber strike?

Top Qi Condensation cultivators were usually only dealt with by grandmaster-level martial artists. Those below the grandmaster level would face great danger when encountering a top Qi Condensation cultivator. Throughout the entire Great Chang, when a top Qi Condensation cultivator appeared, the martial artists had almost no way to deal with them.

Especially Yuan Gangzi, who wasn't an ordinary top Qi Condensation cultivator. He had reached the eleventh level of Qi Condensation. He was only one step away from Qi Condensation perfection!

Such a top Qi Condensation cultivator could be considered one of the strongest in the entire Great Chang. And yet, he was slain by Lin Fei with a single saber strike. Judging from Lin Fei's demeanor, it seemed that he didn't exert much effort.

Now, Zhao Kongming thought about it, he had previously wanted to spar with Lin Fei. It was really too dangerous. Maybe Lin Fei would accidentally unleash his Saber Qi and with a single saber strike, Zhao Kongming would be slain. That would be a great joke.

After a long time, Zhao Kongming slowly sighed and said, "Disciple, you've caused trouble again. It seems that every time you go down the mountain, you stir up trouble. Last time, it was He Mountain Dao. This time, it's Guardian Primordial Mountain. From high-level Qi Condensation cultivators to top Qi Condensation cultivators. Your master is starting to suspect that the next time you go down the mountain, you'll provoke a Foundation Establishment cultivator..."

Zhao Kongming looked at Lin Fei with a complicated expression. His disciple was good in every aspect. Except that he caused too much trouble. And not just small trouble, but big trouble every time.

He Mountain Dao was already bad enough. They were now forced to seal off their mountain. Although Lin Fei didn't cause He Mountain Dao to seal off the mountain, their decision to do so had a direct connection with Lin Fei.

Now it was Guardian Primordial Mountain. A prominent eleventh-level Qi Condensation core elder, a top Qi Condensation cultivator, was slain by Lin Fei with a single saber strike.

"By the way, did you find the demonic cultivator Yuan Gangzi mentioned?" Zhao Kongming suddenly asked.

"Demonic cultivator?"

Lin Fei thought carefully. He had completely forgotten. None of the innate masters from the Thirteen Sects of the Martial Alliance had thought of it. It was likely that even if there was a demonic cultivator, they had already blended into the crowd and escaped.

Lin Fei took a deep breath, his eyes as sharp as a saber, and said in a low voice, "Master, is the presence of a demonic cultivator important? Yuan Gangzi represented Guardian Primordial Mountain and the Qi Condensation cultivators. The Martial Alliance represents the martial arts sects and the martial artists of the world.

Yuan Gangzi trampled on the dignity of martial artists! If the founder of our Nine Profound Sect were still alive, would Yuan Gangzi dare to do this? Even if the Great Chang founder were still here, during the peak of Great Chang's martial arts, would Yuan Gangzi dare to do this?

Yuan Gangzi was searching for a demonic cultivator, yet he trampled on the dignity of all the martial artists in Great Chang! If I don't kill Yuan Gangzi, I will never be at peace!"

Zhao Kongming looked at Lin Fei's resolute expression. His heart was slightly shaken. He had thought that Lin Fei was just prone to causing trouble, causing a big incident every time.

However, it seemed now Lin Fei's decision to kill Yuan Gangzi was well thought out. Yuan Gangzi trampled on the dignity of martial artists all over the world.

As a martial artist, if he didn't kill Yuan Gangzi, his heart would never be at ease! When his faith in martial arts was gone, from then on, his martial arts would never progress!

Yuan Gangzi deserved to die!
