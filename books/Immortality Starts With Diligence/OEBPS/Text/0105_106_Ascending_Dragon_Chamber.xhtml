106. Ascending Dragon Chamber

Lin Fei's majestic inner power was like a vast ocean. It surged powerfully, circulating within his meridians. One could even hear the thundering sound.

How strong was this inner power?

Ling Yao couldn't even withstand the aura of Lin Fei's inner power alone and took a step back. Her eyes widened, and her face showed disbelief.

"Lin Fei, you... you've reached the acquired peak? In just over a month, from the acquired eighth level to the acquired twelfth level? How did you manage to do that?"

Even if Lin Fei was a top-tier genius or even a once-in-a-century genius, to go from the acquired eighth level to the acquired twelfth level in just over a month was too exaggerated.

Ling Yao was a martial artist herself. Therefore, she knew how difficult this was. It was simply like a fairy tale.

"Gold Crown Treasure Snake! I also have to thank Daoist Master Yu Xiangzi. The treasure snake's blood was just too effective, which allowed my body to grow for a second time, thereby greatly increasing my inner power. In just over a month, I broke through four levels and reached the peak of the acquired realm. I didn't even use the Heavenly Meridian Pill..."

Lin Fei took out the Heavenly Meridian Pill. Originally, his trip to Chang City to participate in the Royal Competition was to help Ling Yao enter the top three. His biggest goal was this Heavenly Meridian Pill.

But what was the result?

Lin Fei didn't even use the Heavenly Meridian Pill and had already reached the acquired peak. This Heavenly Meridian Pill was now useless to Lin Fei. Not to mention the Zhu Fruit.

Seeing the Heavenly Meridian Pill in Lin Fei's hand, Ling Yao had to believe it. Lin Fei had really reached the acquired peak in just over a month!

"Is the blood of the Gold Crown Treasure Snake really that magical?" Ling Yao couldn't help but want to catch a Gold Crown Treasure Snake to try it out.

Of course, Ling Yao also knew that the Gold Crown Treasure Snake's blood exchanged between Yu Xiangzi and Lin Fei was actually fed with various precious supplements for more than a hundred years. There was basically only one unique snake. She couldn't try it even if she wanted to.

"Lin Fei, this is also your good fortune. Originally, I thought that obtaining the Zhu Fruit in the secret realm was already a great fortune. But I didn't expect that you would gain such a great opportunity just by exchanging a spot in the secret realm..."

Ling Yao's expression was a bit complicated. She couldn't help but think of an idea that if she had been given the jar of Gold Crown Treasure Snake's blood, would she be able to reach the acquired peak?

However, when this thought had just emerged, Ling Yao immediately extinguished it herself. She was not Lin Fei. She didn't have such a profound foundation and martial arts talent.

If the snake blood had really been given to her, it would probably be a waste. But for Lin Fei, achieving the innate realm was hardly a problem.

"By the way, Lin Fei, when do you plan to break through the Ren and Du meridians and attack the innate realm?" Ling Yao asked.

If Lin Fei could achieve the innate realm, it would also be of great benefit to her. It basically meant that her position as a princess would be as stable as a mountain! Even no one would dare to compete with her for the position of princess anymore. The deterrence of an innate martial artist was just so immense!

"I've been waiting for Senior Sister Ling to come back. Although my inner power is based on the fusion of three and is incredibly profound, I have no experience in breaking through the Ren and Du meridians, nor do I have an innate martial artist to teach me some techniques.

To be cautious, I didn't rashly attempt to break through the Ren and Du meridians. I wanted to ask Ling Senior Sister to think of a way, to see if she could find me some experiences of martial artists breaking through to the innate realm. So that it would be more secure!"

If possible, Lin Fei wanted to break through the Ren and Du meridians and achieve the innate realm with a hundred percent certainty, taking no risks at all.

"Lin Fei, you're doing the right thing. Even if your inner power is profound and you have a great chance, don't be anxious. If you want to attack the innate realm, you must make sure that there is no room for error. Just wait, I'll go to the palace. Our Great Chang was established on martial power, and the royal family has many good things. Maybe they can help you break through to the innate realm."

Having said that, Ling Yao immediately set off for the palace. Lin Fei sat cross-legged in the room. Ever since Ling Yao came back, his mood had completely calmed down. No matter if Ling Yao could bring him good news or not, he would try to break through the Ren and Du meridians and attack the innate realm. And success was the only option, failure was not allowed!

Time passed bit by bit. The sky outside gradually darkened. Suddenly, Lin Fei heard hurried footsteps from outside.

Lin Fei opened his eyes. Ling Yao was back.

"Senior Sister Ling," Lin Fei opened the door and welcomed Ling Yao into the room.

Ling Yao said solemnly, "Lin Fei, I went to the palace today and told the royal elders about your situation. The elder agreed to give you some detailed records of martial artists breaking through the Ren and Du meridians. Moreover, the elder allowed you to enter the royal family's secret place, the 'Ascending Dragon Chamber,' for cultivation.

This 'Ascending Dragon Chamber' is very mysterious. I only know that many of the innate martial artist elders, high-ranking Qi cultivators, and so on in my royal family are all in the 'Ascending Dragon Chamber.' I'm sure it must be a good place able to help you advance to the innate realm!"

Lin Fei looked at Ling Yao and said calmly, "Senior Sister, tell me about the conditions of the royal family. I don't believe that the royal family would give me such good things for nothing. There must be some conditions, some requirements!"

The things offered by the royal family were not something that could be given casually. Even with the face of Princess Ling Yao, it was no different.

The royal elders were an existence that even the Great Chang Emperor had to respect. How could the relationship of a princess persuade the royal elders?

"Conditions?" Ling Yao shook her head and said, "It's strange, but the elders didn't mention any conditions. Usually, the elders are not as easygoing as they were today. But as soon as I mentioned that it was for you, the elders agreed. By the way, the elders said something about forming a good bond with you. Just like when the royal family and the founder of the Nine Profound Sect formed a good bond."

Lin Fei's eyes flickered, and he whispered, "The royal family has a relationship with the founder of the Nine Profound Sect? Even forming a good bond..."

"I'm not sure about that. After all, the founder of the Nine Profound Sect is a figure from hundreds of years ago. But it's true that the relationship between the Nine Profound Sect and the royal family is quite good. Lin Fei, you can't miss this 'Ascending Dragon Chamber.' Even I don't have the qualifications to enter the 'Ascending Dragon Chamber'; there must be some special benefits."

Ling Yao said very seriously. She was also considering Lin Fei. Otherwise, she wouldn't go to the palace to ask the elders for help.

"Senior Sister Ling, I'll go! Since it helps me advance to the innate realm, I must go no matter what."

Without hesitation, Lin Fei immediately made a decision. In any case, Lin Fei's top priority now was just one thing. That was to advance to the innate realm! As long as he could successfully advance to the innate realm. Everything else was just a minor matter.

"Good, let's get going right away."

"Please lead the way, Senior Sister."

Lin Fei and Ling Yao got into a carriage together, quickly heading towards the palace.
