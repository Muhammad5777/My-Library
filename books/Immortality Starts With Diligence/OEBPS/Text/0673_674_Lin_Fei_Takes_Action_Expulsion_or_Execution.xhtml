674. Lin Fei Takes Action! Expulsion or Execution?

However, the actions of Lin Fei and the Clairvoyant Supreme had stirred quite a bit of discussion among the onlookers.

"What are they doing?"

"It looks like they're setting up a formation. Are they planning to seal off the entire Anlan Realm?"

"No formation can completely lock down a realm. It must be a warning formation. Anyone entering or leaving Anlan Realm will be detected by this warning formation."

"But even if they detect someone, what then? Do these two Supremes really dare to attack at will?"

"That's rather domineering!"

Many were taken aback. They had seen arrogance before, but this level of overbearing behavior was unprecedented.

"The formation in Anlan Realm allows entry but not exit. Those who think of entering should think twice. It's easy to get in, but once you want to get out, you'll have to pass our inspection. Only after confirming that you don't have the Chaos Pearl will you be allowed to leave. If someone tries to forcefully break through the formation and leave Anlan Realm, then don't blame us—there will be no mercy!"

The Clairvoyant Supreme's eyes turned icy, his voice echoing ominously.

"Humph. What an audacity! Even top-tier Supremes aren't this domineering! I not only want to enter but also intend to leave. What can you do about it?"

The Clairvoyant Supreme's words instantly ruffled many Supremes. One of them immediately spoke up, challenging the Clairvoyant Supreme directly.

"Hmm? You might try."

The Clairvoyant Supreme's gaze was icy. He stared at his challenger. Without engaging, it was hard to tell whether the other was a regular Supreme or a top-tier one. The challenging Supreme seemed quite confident, likely not just a regular Supreme.

"No need to try. I intend to enter Anlan Realm and compete for the Chaos Pearl. You are also here for the Chaos Pearl. Conflict is inevitable, so we might as well resolve this here and now."

The challenging Supreme sneered.

"What a big talk!"

The Clairvoyant Supreme's gaze chilled further. He was a top-tier Supreme himself. No one had dared to speak to him with such audacity before—even with Lin Fei, a formidable ally, at his side. That did not mean the Clairvoyant Supreme was any less capable.

"I'd like to see how you plan to 'resolve' us."

With a swift motion, the Clairvoyant Supreme struck. He attacked the challenging Supreme without a moment's hesitation, unleashing his full power.

A thunderous roar echoed through the area.

The Clairvoyant Supreme's initial strike warped the very fabric of the chaotic void. With a single palm, it seemed as though he had restrained his opponent, immense pressure emanating from all directions.

"Top-tier Supreme?" the challenged Supreme muttered under his breath, a hint of surprise flashing in his eyes as he recognized the power behind the Clairvoyant Supreme's attack. Unintimidated, he lunged forward, his own palm thrusting out in a direct counter.

The impact was immense and straightforward—pure power clashing with no fancy techniques. The force of their collision swept out in all directions, the shockwave palpable.

The Clairvoyant Supreme was shaken to his core. A spluttering sound followed, and in an astonishing turn of events, the Supreme's body started to collapse. It was sudden, but not entirely without warning. Unbeknownst to him, his opponent's force had already begun eroding his physical form.

At the moment of impact, the Clairvoyant Supreme's body simply couldn't withstand the strain and crumbled. He had been defeated.

While his body was shattered and he lay defeated, the Clairvoyant Supreme did not die. He recovered almost instantly, regenerating from the damage which, to someone of his stature, was trivial. Yet, despite surviving, he had undeniably been beaten—overpowered in a direct confrontation.

The Clairvoyant Supreme's expression darkened significantly. "Who exactly are you?" he demanded, his gaze cold and questioning.

The opposing Supreme stood with his hands behind his back, his demeanor commanding as he surveyed the scene. Slowly, he responded, "You might as well know, I am Nebula Supreme."

"Nebula Supreme?" the Clairvoyant Supreme murmured to himself. The name was unfamiliar to him, but that mattered little now. From this day onward, many would know of Nebula Supreme, especially given his prowess in defeating the Clairvoyant Supreme head-on.

Clearly, Nebula Supreme was no ordinary combatant. His strength was sufficient to make waves across the chaos.

"And you," Nebula Supreme turned his attention to Lin Fei, his tone both challenging and dismissive. "Do you also wish to fight?"

Nebula Supreme's gaze fell upon Lin Fei, who emitted a peculiar aura, so much so that Nebula himself doubted whether Lin Fei was a Supreme at all. Yet, considering the Clairvoyant Supreme's demeanor, it was clear that Lin Fei was held in high regard, likely possessing strength not inferior to that of the Clairvoyant Supreme himself.

Lin Fei glanced at the Clairvoyant Supreme and spoke softly, "Clairvoyant Supreme, shall we expel or execute?"

"Expel or execute?" The Clairvoyant Supreme hesitated for a moment before responding, "If they breach the formation forcefully, slay them if possible. If not, expulsion will suffice."

Lin Fei nodded, understanding the plan. However, while Lin Fei grasped the concept, the Clairvoyant Supreme himself was somewhat perplexed. How could a Supreme of such stature be slain?

It was known that Nebula Supreme was stronger than the Clairvoyant Supreme, both being top-tier Supremes. The idea of killing such a being seemed far-fetched unless the disparity in power was vast, such as between a common Supreme and a top-tier one—a gap that allowed for easy elimination of the lesser by the greater.

The Clairvoyant Supreme might have believed in Lin Fei's formidable strength, but facing Nebula Supreme was another matter. Defeating Nebula would be an impressive feat in itself; the thought of killing him had never crossed the Clairvoyant's mind.

But Lin Fei took it seriously, and he was acutely aware that to seize the Chaos Pearl, he must first establish his dominance. Otherwise, anyone would dare to challenge the formation. Managing one challenger after another would be manageable for Lin Fei but nonetheless cumbersome.

Only by demonstrating overwhelming force could he deter others from recklessly attempting to breach the formation. And should he manage to slay Nebula Supreme, a top-tier among them, it would surely intimidate the rest.

With a resonant hum, Lin Fei made his move.

In a flash, Lin Fei moved, a radiant light burgeoning around him and sweeping toward Nebula Supreme. This was the eternal light of the Eternal Realm, capable of dragging adversaries into its depths. Once within Lin Fei's Eternal Realm, even a top-tier Supreme faced a certain demise.

"How audacious!" Nebula Supreme exclaimed, his eyes flashing with a chill. "Dare to strike indeed. Very well, this will be a harsh lesson for you!"

Despite having defeated the Clairvoyant Supreme, Nebula was astonished that Lin Fei would boldly confront him. Did Lin Fei believe he was kind?

A flicker of murderous intent crossed Nebula's gaze, yet he was blissfully unaware of the impending danger.

As the eternal light enveloped him, Nebula Supreme found himself powerless to resist, swiftly drawn into the Eternal Realm. In the blink of an eye, he vanished.

"What's happening?"

"How has Nebula Supreme disappeared?"

"Look! That Supreme has transformed into a world!"

"It seems Nebula Supreme has been swept into that Supreme's realm!"

Onlookers witnessed as Lin Fei became one with his realm, trapping Nebula Supreme within. Panic seized Nebula; he had been pulled into the Eternal Realm without even a chance to resist. This maneuver instilled a profound sense of dread within him, an eerie premonition that this place was extraordinary.

"Annihilate!"

The next moment, a chilling voice echoed around Nebula Supreme within the confines of the Eternal Realm.
