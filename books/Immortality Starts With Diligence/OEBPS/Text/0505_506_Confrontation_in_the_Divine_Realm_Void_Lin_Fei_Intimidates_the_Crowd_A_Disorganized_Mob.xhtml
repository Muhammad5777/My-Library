506. Confrontation in the Divine Realm Void! Lin Fei Intimidates the Crowd, A Disorganized Mob!

As True Spirit Realm continued to collapse, strands of world origin emerged and were systematically collected by North Wind True Person's grand formation.

Lin Fei stood silently in the void, his aura locked onto the passageways between realms. Anyone attempting to enter the True Spirit Realm at this time would face his fierce onslaught, regardless of their identity.

Many had gathered outside the True Spirit Realm, but with the fates of Extinction Martial God and Meditation True Person fresh in their minds, no one dared to enter rashly.

North Wind True Person was elated, having harvested an enormous amount of world origin, more than he had ever seen before. Initially intending only to take a share of the bounty in True Spirit Realm, he found himself slowly growing in power and even being besieged. Eventually, he had to call for the aid of the Heart Demon Martial God, who brought Lin Fei along. The trio, joining forces, swept through True Spirit Realm, dominating it completely.

This was no ordinary minor world; in fact, it was comparable to more than ten such worlds in scale, making its world origin at least ten times more abundant than usual. Even if North Wind True Person only claimed forty percent, the gains were beyond imagination. He knew that without Lin Fei's powerful presence deterring other divine realm entities, it would have been impossible for him to monopolize the realm's origin.

North Wind's sole focus was to gather the origin as quickly as possible before leaving this place of trouble. Time slowly ticked away, each moment a strain for Lin Fei, the Heart Demon Martial God, and North Wind True Person, especially given the days and nights this process demanded. The Realm of True Spirit wouldn't collapse overnight; it required several days. These days felt like an extended period of agony for them.

Fortunately, nearly three days later, North Wind True Person shuddered. The time had come. Although True Spirit Realm was still collapsing, the world origin had been almost entirely absorbed by the formation. With the absorption of the essence, the collapse of the True Spirit Realm would only accelerate further.

"Martial God Lin, Mi Tian, let's leave," North Wind True Person suggested, eager to depart with the world origin they had gathered.

However, Lin Fei shook his head, "No rush. The Realm of True Spirit hasn't collapsed completely yet. Let's divide our shares of the world's origin here. If we leave now, who knows what might happen."

Lin Fei's words made North Wind True Person and the Heart Demon Martial God tense up. Indeed, without the world origin securely in hand, there was a sense of unease. And with the unpredictable situation outside, it was safer to stay and divide the spoils.

"Alright, let's divide it now," North Wind agreed. "Martial God Lin, you take fifty percent of the share. Mi Tian, you get ten percent."

As North Wind True Person took out the harvested world origin, even he was startled by its sheer quantity. For a moment, a fleeting thought crossed his mind to take all of it for himself and strive for the attainment of True Monarch by comprehending the mysteries of the void. But he quickly dismissed the idea, knowing the risks of incurring Lin Fei's wrath if he failed to achieve True Monarch status.

The Heart Demon Martial God, although only receiving ten percent, was thrilled. The amount was substantial enough to aid significantly in his path of cultivation and potentially help him survive several more heavenly tribulations.

As for Lin Fei, he received the largest share — a full fifty percent of the world origin from True Spirit Realm, which greatly exceeded his initial expectations. This bounty brought him immense joy, fulfilling and even surpassing his original goal.

With their share of the world origin secured, Lin Fei, the Heart Demon Martial God, and North Wind True Person prepared to leave the collapsing Realm of True Spirit.

Lin Fei, confident in his ability to withstand multiple heavenly tribulations with this newfound power, led the way. However, he was cautious about the potential dangers that awaited them outside the realm.

As they stepped into the inter-realm channel, numerous hostile and greedy gazes from the Divine Realm fell upon them. Many formidable entities, drawn by the allure of the world origin, were waiting.

"Finally, they've emerged," sneered a voice, filled with disdain. "Martial God Lin from the Great Dream Sect, how dare you slay Extinction Martial God from the Ten Directions Realm and think you can just walk away?"

These were powerful beings from various sects and domains, all eyeing the trio with covetous and malicious intent, clearly after the world origin they carried.

Lin Fei, undaunted, stood firm and scanned the crowd with a piercing gaze. He was surrounded by Deity Transformation beings and Martial Gods.

"Who wants the world origin? Step forward now," he challenged them. But despite their numbers, none dared to openly confront Lin Fei. His reputation and display of power in the True Spirit Realm had instilled fear and hesitation in them.

"A bunch of cowards," Lin Fei muttered disdainfully and confidently stepped forward, leading the Heart Demon Martial God and North Wind True Person away from the throng.

The crowd watched them leave, their faces a mix of confusion and disappointment. "Perhaps there's still some world origin left in True Spirit Realm," someone suggested hopefully. Seizing the opportunity, many Martial Gods and Deity Transformation beings rushed into the True Spirit Realm, hoping to scavenge whatever remnants of origin they could find.

Lin Fei safely departed from the Divine Realm void with his allies.
