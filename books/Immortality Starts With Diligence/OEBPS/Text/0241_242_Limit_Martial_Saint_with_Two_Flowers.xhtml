242. Limit Martial Saint with Two Flowers

The Flame Burning Heaven Saber, along with the transcendence saber momentum and Lin Fei's terrifying saber essence, exploded at this moment with a force that was earth-shaking! Even when facing a powerful Limit Martial Saint, Lin Fei's saber was not inferior, it was even stronger!

At least, Liu Hong was in a crisis. He was engulfed by Lin Fei's Flame Burning Heaven Saber. The endless saber essence could possibly severely injure or even kill Liu Hong in an instant!

"Whoosh."

A shadow flashed and disappeared in an instant. Almost in an instant, it took Liu Hong away.

"Huh?"

Lin Fei's eyes flickered slightly. He saw a shadow, saving Liu Hong from the sea of fire at the last moment. The speed of the opponent was very fast. And most importantly, from the moment he entered Lin Fei's area to rescuing Liu Hong, it was all in one go. There was no pause in between. Obviously, the opponent was extremely powerful. Otherwise, he would not have been able to move freely under Lin Fei's suppressive area.

Lin Fei waved his hand. The scattered saber essence was actually retracted back into his body. Although it would consume some of it, this could ensure that Lin Fei could use the saber essence to its maximum extent in battle.

Of course, not every martial artist who had comprehended the saber momentum could do this. This was a special characteristic of Lin Fei's saber essence. Others didn't have the saber essence. For example, Shen Lianxin. He couldn't retract his True Essence. Lin Fei's saber essence was integrated with the saber momentum. Therefore, under the saber momentum, he could retract the saber essence.

Without the saber essence and the sea of fire, the smoke and dust gradually disappeared. Lin Fei also saw the situation on the other side clearly. Liu Hong's face was a bit unsightly. He didn't say a word, staring fixedly at Lin Fei. And beside Liu Hong stood a man in black, calm and composed. He stood quietly, as steady as a towering mountain. Even someone as powerful as Liu Hong seemed very quiet next to this man.

Moreover, behind the man, a large number of Foundation Establishment True Persons and Martial Saints rushed over. Some of them were even comparable to Liu Hong Limit Martial Saints or top Foundation Establishment True Persons!

However, top Foundation Establishment True Persons usually excel in magic treasure. Without using magic treasure, it's hard to tell if they can match the Limit Martial Saint.

"Thank you, Captain," Liu Hong said respectfully with a salute.

If it weren't for the Captain's rescue, he would have died just now. Thinking of the saber that Lin Fei had slashed, even now, Liu Hong was still somewhat in shock and unsettled. He never thought that one day, facing a Martial Saint, he would almost be killed.

"Law Enforcement Team Captain, Martial Saint Jiang Tianyun!" Lin Fei slowly said, word by word.

Jiang Tianyun! This was the most frequently mentioned person in the information about the Illusion Worry City that Lin Fei had collected before coming down the mountain.

Jiang Tianyun not only condensed the Energy Flower and Qi Flower but also mastered the martial momentum. And it was sword momentum! Even if it hadn't reached transcendence, it was still very terrifying. Most importantly, he was a disciple of the Worry-free Sovereign and the most hopeful Martial Saint in the entire Illusion Worry City to achieve the Martial Sovereign status.

Jiang Tianyun on the other side was also looking at Lin Fei. He also spoke, "Liu Hong, don't be resentful. Losing to him, you did not lose in vain. If I'm not mistaken, you should be Lin Fei, the 'Dao Seeker' of the Ancient Origin Sect, who has been in the limelight recently. Transcendence saber momentum is indeed extraordinary. Moreover, you have just condensed the Qi Flower in the Ancient Origin Sect Spiritual Qi Pool. No wonder the Wind Sovereign sent you to the Illusion Worry City. Transcendence saber momentum, plus Qi Flower. Even if someone can defeat you, but wants to hold you back, it's simply impossible."

Jiang Tianyun's tone was calm. Even if he guessed Lin Fei's identity, Jiang Tianyun seemed not surprised at all. However, his words shocked the people around him. Especially Liu Hong. His eyes widened. His expression was filled with a trace of shock.

"Dao Seeker Lin Fei? And he has transcended saber momentum, and even condensed the Qi Flower? But just now, he clearly did not even burst out with the Qi Flower..."

Liu Hong's eyes widened as he stared closely at Lin Fei. That one saber made him desperate. It almost killed him. And the result?

Lin Fei didn't even burst out the Qi Flower. The strength he displayed might not even be half of his full potential. This result was a little hard for Liu Hong to accept. However, he knew very well. Jiang Tianyun's words would not be wrong. Besides Liu Hong, when others heard Jiang Tianyun's words, they were also shocked, their eyes all turned to Lin Fei.

"Dao Seeker Lin Fei?"

"So, he is Dao Seeker Lin Fei!"

"I heard long ago that there was a Dao Seeker who crossed the sea and later joined the Ancient Origin Sect, so it was him."

"When Dao Seeker Lin Fei joined the Ancient Origin Sect, he suppressed hundreds of Martial Saints by his own power. But at that time, he certainly had not condensed the Qi Flower. How long has it been since then?"

"Just half a year. In just half a year, Lin Fei has already condensed the Qi Flower."

"As expected, the rumors about the 'Dao Seekers' in the Ancient Continent are true. Every Seeker is a genius. Their future achievements are immeasurable!"

"Dao Seeker Lin Fei versus Jiang Tianyun, this will be interesting. I wonder who will come out on top?"

In fact, many people knew. Now that Jiang Tianyun had arrived, he would not let Lin Fei and the others leave.

Otherwise, where is the dignity of the Illusion Worry City? Regardless of what the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals did today, regardless of how angry the Ancient Origin Sect is, this is the Illusion Worry City. No one can kill in the Illusion Worry City.

Lin Fei, the Martial Saint of the Ancient Origin Sect, killed the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals. The Illusion Worry City must intervene!

"Lin Fei, how about we fight in the void? So as not to destroy the Illusion Worry City and anger my master. That would be bad."

Jiang Tianyun said calmly. Although his tone was calm, in reality, this fight was not as simple as it appeared on the surface.

This battle is not an ordinary fight. It is a life-and-death battle!

Jiang Tianyun intended to kill Lin Fei. In order to maintain the rules of the Illusion Worry City, even if Lin Fei was the Limit Martial Saint of the Ancient Origin Sect, it would be the same.

Lin Fei stared at Jiang Tianyun deeply. Word by word, he said coldly, "Jiang Tianyun, are you sure you want to fight with me? In this battle, even in the Illusion Worry City, you might die!"

Lin Fei's words were also very calm. But within that calmness, there was a chilling coldness.

"Hahaha, I will die?"

Jiang Tianyun took a step forward.

"Boom."

Above his head, a Qi Flower emerged. Immediately afterward, an Energy Flower also emerged.

Two flowers!

The terrifying momentum was directed straight at Lin Fei.

Two flowers are not as simple as one plus one. Two flowers are a multiple increase because they are interrelated. Once the Energy Flower and Qi Flower are condensed, the two complement each other, the power is multiplied!

Therefore, this momentum was vast and mighty. Just like a storm, it swept in all directions.

The terrifying power, even more, terrifying than Lin Fei's transcendence saber momentum. Some martial artists and Foundation Establishment True Persons watching around, and their faces all changed at the same time.

"Retreat!"

"Quickly retreat! The power of two flowers, even an average Martial Saint cannot withstand!"

"How terrifying, is this the power of two flowers?"

For a time, many people were looking at Jiang Tianyun with unspeakable horror.

"I won't die! Under the Martial Sovereign, no one is qualified to let me die! Lin Fei, you can't either!"

Jiang Tianyun no longer had that calm expression on his face. The two flowers above his head spanned the entire void, filling with its terrifying pressure. Even Lin Fei's saber momentum was also suppressed!

This is a Martial Saint with two flowers. Each additional flower is a world of difference!
