195. Saber Momentum Mastery, Slaying Ten Foundation Establishments!

However, the evolution of the five saber techniques was still a lack of opportunity! As for what the opportunity was, Lin Fei already had a rough idea.

"Whoosh."

Lin Fei's gaze was on the five Foundation Establishment True Persons. The five saber techniques were entangling with the five Foundation Establishment True Persons. In the end, it was just entanglement. To slay the five Foundation Establishment True Persons, Lin Fei's five saber techniques or Lin Fei's five saber essence was still not enough!

Lin Fei didn't hesitate anymore. He no longer had any reservations.

"Boom."

The saber essence in Lin Fei's body erupted in an instant. Strands of saber essence flew out from Lin Fei's body. Lin Fei almost erupted more than 90% of the saber essence in his body all at once.

Immediately, the five saber essence suddenly increased dramatically! The momentum of the five saber techniques also reached the peak all at once. The five Foundation Establishment True Persons had a chill in their hearts. But Lin Fei had already shouted in a low voice: "Slash!"

"Whoosh."

The five saber essence, the saber light surged and, moreover, it carried the terrifying oppressive power of saber momentum. He gave a fierce slash at the five Foundation Establishment True Persons.

"Boom."

The five Foundation Establishment True Persons let out a scream. Then, the entire void quieted down. They were dead! The five Foundation Establishment True Persons all had fallen! For a moment, the void was filled with a strong smell of blood.

"Your Three Abyssal Sabers have successfully comprehended and evolved the Killing Saber Momentum!"

"Your Twenty-Three Saber has successfully comprehended and evolved Fast Saber Momentum!"

"Your Eternal Night Saber Technique has successfully comprehended and evolved the Eternal Night Saber Momentum!"

"Your Samsara Saber Technique has successfully comprehended and evolved the Samsara Saber Momentum!"

"Your God Slaying Saber Technique has successfully comprehended and evolved the God Slaying Saber Momentum!"

"Shoo, shoo, shoo."

The five saber essence instantly came to Lin Fei's front. All of them emitted a kind of saber momentum. It's no longer saber intent but the real saber momentum!

"Take it!"

Lin Fei took the five saber essence into his body.

"Your Killing Saber Momentum merges with Heaven Saber Momentum."

"Your progress in training saber momentum has increased by 20%."

"Your Fast Saber Momentum merges with Heaven Saber Momentum."

"Your progress in training saber momentum has increased by 20%."

The system's voice sounded again in Lin Fei's mind.

Five types of saber momentum fused with Lin Fei's original saber momentum. Moreover, each saber momentum increased Lin Fei's progress in practicing saber momentum by 20%. Five types of saber momentum, that's 100%.

"Boom."

Lin Fei's saber momentum began to metamorphose.

"Your saber momentum training progress has reached 100%."

"Your saber momentum has broken through to Mastery!"

Lin Fei's saber momentum, from beginner, finally broke through to Mastery. The saber momentum was also undergoing earth-shaking changes. Bursts of terrifying pressure descended on everyone in the saber momentum.

At this moment, of the ten Foundation Establishment True Persons, only four Foundation Establishment True Persons were left. They saw Lin Fei slash five Foundation Establishment True Persons with one saber. Their hearts were filled with immense fear.

Who dared to fight anymore?

So, they all used their escape techniques, transforming into a light of escape, wanting to run away.

"Want to leave? It's too late!"

A sharp light flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

Although the beginner-level saber momentum could suppress the Foundation Establishment True Persons, if they were determined to leave, they might really be able to escape. But Lin Fei's saber momentum had already reached Mastery. The power of saber momentum at the Mastery level increased several times.

"Suppress!"

Lin Fei let out a low shout.

Immediately, the saber essence in Lin Fei's body instantly merged into the saber momentum.

"Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh."

Immediately, a large amount of saber essence, like raindrops, instantly pierced through the bodies of the four Foundation Establishment True Persons.

"Ah..."

The four Foundation Establishment True Persons screamed. Their bodies were pierced by the saber essence, pockmarked. It looked horrendous.

"Plop."

They all fell from the void, turned into a pile of mush. They were dead!

All Foundation Establishment True Persons had fallen. These were the top ten Foundation Establishment True Persons! They were strong enough to sweep across the entire Great Chang.

Seeing this scene, Liu Yibai and Shen Lianxin were silent. The disciples and elders of the Nine Profound Sect, at this moment, couldn't believe their own eyes. They had seen the whole process of the fight. Ten Foundation Establishment True Persons were slain by Lin Fei alone. This was simply one man ruling the world! Even the founder of Great Chang probably couldn't do this, right? In fact, the founder of Great Chang was even inferior to Lin Fei!

Shen Lianxin looked deeply at Lin Fei. He slowly said, "Brother Lin, it seems that even if we hadn't come, you would have been able to slay these ten cultivators. Originally, when I succeeded in foundation building and achieved the status of a martial saint, I wanted to come to the Nine Profound Sect and compete with Brother Lin in saber arts again, to see who is the Number One Saber Under Heaven. However, it seems now that there's no need for competition."

Shen Lianxin was self-aware Lin Fei had evolved the saber momentum. If he were to fight with Lin Fei, in front of Lin Fei, he might not even have the qualification to unsheath his saber.

The saber momentum might only be oppressive to others, but for a saberist, even for a martial saint saberist, as long as he didn't evolve the saber momentum, it's impossible to unsheath the saber under the cover of the saber momentum.

This is the domineering nature of the saber momentum!

Liu Yibai also looked deeply at Lin Fei. He said in a deep voice, "Saber momentum, ah... In the history of my Hidden Sword Sect, many martial saints were born, but none of them could comprehend the sword momentum. The only martial artist I know who has evolved the momentum is the founder of Great Chang. I didn't expect that Brother Lin would also comprehend the momentum, and it's even the saber momentum!"

Liu Yibai was a member of the Hidden Sword Sect. Naturally, he understood the power of "saber momentum".

The saber momentum for a martial saint is just like the saber intent for an innate martial artist. A martial saint with and without the saber momentum are completely two different levels. A typical martial saint can deal with a Foundation Establishment cultivator, or he can suppress a Foundation Establishment True Person. But if a martial saint evolves the saber momentum, he can suppress four or five or even more Foundation Establishment True Persons. And that's by using his own power to suppress!

Like Lin Fei, a martial saint with exceptional talent, once comprehend the saber momentum, that's just like now, easily suppressed the top ten Foundation Establishment True Persons With his own power!

The strength shown by Lin Fei can already be described as invincible! At least, after this battle, the whole Great Chang would spread the name of Lin Fei as "invincible"!

"I must thank Brother Liu and Brother Shen for coming to the Nine Profound Sect this time to lend me a hand."

Lin Fei didn't forget Liu Yibai and Shen Lianxin. After all, they came to the Nine Profound Sect and fought alongside him.

"Both of you, please stay in my Nine Profound Sect for some time. We can exchange views and discuss sword and saber techniques. Especially, the saber momentum. I will share everything I know."

Lin Fei invited Shen Lianxin and Liu Yibai. For martial saints like Shen Lianxin and Liu Yibai, what was the most attractive thing? It's probably Lin Fei's saber momentum!

Lin Fei was now the only one in the Great Chang who had evolved the saber momentum. Even, he was the second martial saint who had comprehended the saber momentum after the founder of Great Chang. Lin Fei was undoubtedly the saber saint!

If they discussed and exchanged views with Lin Fei, they would surely understand "momentum" more deeply. They might even find the direction to evolve "momentum". The two of them were naturally very interested.

However, Liu Yibai suddenly spoke, "Brother Lin, you have slain the ten Foundation Establishment cultivators, and your fame shakes the world. But this does not mean that you can rest easy. Foundation Establishment True Persons, when they die, they die. But as long as the immortal sects exist, there will be a constant stream of Foundation Establishment True Persons being born. The Immortal Sects are the fundamental issue!"

As Liu Yibai's words fell, everyone was silent. They naturally knew that the Immortal Sects were the fundamental issue. No matter how much damage the Immortal Sects suffered, as long as they close the mountain for a period of time, short as several decades, long as several centuries, they could recover.

Without destroying the Immortal Sects, the Great Chang martial artists and Qi Condensation cultivators would inevitably wage war again.

"Since the Immortal Sects are the fundamental issue, then I will shatter the Immortal Sects!"

There was a glint in Lin Fei's eyes.
