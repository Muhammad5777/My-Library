240. Saber Strikes Foundation Establishment

A note from RighteousEgg

Kai is the hardest working warrior in his generation... and the unluckiest. In a world where most people receive magical skills, classes, or cultivation, Kai has nothing but his wits and his strength. His only choice will be to forge a new path against fate, no matter where it takes him. Savage monsters pour endlessly from the wasteland, threatening the survival of civilization itself. They say that human strength isn't enough and only those gifted with destined powers have a chance of making a difference. Kai is hungry to fight in that war, to be someone who matters. That hunger will take him across a world stranger and more terrible than he could possibly have imagined.

- Strong to stronger slow burn. The MC is skilled and competent, but he isn't blessed by any special powers until he earns them himself.

- Stats exist as a natural part of the world. They aren't introduced until several chapters in when the MC unlocks spiritual sight.

- Different magical abilities exist in different regions and balancing them is an essential part of the system

In the Illusion Worry City, the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals were in a courtyard.

Although the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals were Foundation Establishment True Persons, they were almost inseparable, even living together. They were very cautious. Even if the end was near, they were always careful and would never be negligent. Just now, they had received a message.

"Big brother, we have received a message. The Ancient Origin Sect has sent people, and there are quite a few, more than a dozen, all are Martial Saints! The Ancient Origin Sect mobilizing in such a large manner, I fear the visitors have ill intentions. Most likely, they are coming for us."

The third among the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals said with a solemn face. They had killed true disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect and knew that they could not hide this matter. Now it seems that they were indeed found out by the Ancient Origin Sect.

The large sects were all very protective. Killing a true disciple was no small matter. Now that the Ancient Origin Sect had sent people, they were naturally somewhat panicked. Even if they were top Foundation Establishment True Persons, it was the same.

The "big brother" of the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals, True Person Deer, sneered, "Don't panic, even if the Ancient Origin Sect sends a dozen Martial Saints, what if they send a hundred? This is Illusion Worry City! As long as it's not a Martial Sovereign coming, there won't be any problem. Who dares to do it in Illusion Worry City? Even the Ancient Origin Sect can't!

Originally, I was still worried that the Martial Sovereign of the Ancient Origin Sect would take action personally. After all, there has been a precedent. Wind Sovereign, the Hall Master of the Ancient Origin Sect Law Enforcement Hall, once took action personally and pursued a Limit Martial Saint who had condensed two flowers!

But now, Wind Sovereign didn't come and only sent a team of Martial Saints. Seems that the rumor is true. The Ancient Origin Sect and Illusion Worry City have an agreement that the Martial Sovereign can't come to Illusion Worry City. So, we don't need to worry at all."

The second and third both sighed slightly.

"So, we don't need to worry?"

"We can't be completely careless and complacent. Those Martial Saints of the Ancient Origin Sect definitely don't dare to come hard. But they probably will try their best to force or lure us out of Illusion Worry City. Therefore, we must be vigilant next. No matter what happens, we must not leave Illusion Worry City!"

True Person Deer said solemnly. This was also his method. At any time, they must not leave Illusion Worry City. Even when the end comes, they must stay in Illusion Worry City.

After all, he knew very well that if one offend a major martial arts sect like the Ancient Origin Sect and still want to live freely, he can only stay in Illusion Worry City. Once he leaves, he will undoubtedly die! There will be no fluke.

"Big brother, rest assured, no matter what happens, we will not leave Illusion Worry City."

The two True Persons also repeatedly assured. They were very convinced by True Person Deer. It was True Person Deer's plans and decisive actions that led them step by step to today. Otherwise, it would have been difficult to even establish a foundation. True Person Deer had almost never taken a wrong step!

"Hmm?"

Just then, True Person Deer's divine consciousness seemed to sense something.

"Bang".

The next moment, the door was smashed open. The three Foundation Establishment True Persons immediately got up, looking somewhat surprised at the door. That was a group of Martial Saints, all dressed in the Ancient Origin Sect's robes, as many as a dozen.

"Ancient Origin Sect's Martial Saints?"

The Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals were uncertain.

"What are you doing? Fighting is forbidden in Illusion Worry City. Do you dare to do it? When the time comes, the enforcement team of Illusion Worry City will show no mercy!"

True Person Deer immediately shouted loudly. He didn't believe that the Ancient Origin Sect's Martial Saints wouldn't know the rules of Illusion Worry City。

Once they started fighting, even if they really died, how could this group of Ancient Origin Sect Martial Saints in front of them escape from Illusion Worry City?

"Did you slay the three true disciples of my Ancient Origin Sect?" Lin Fei disregarded Deer True Person's reprimand, addressing him directly.

The Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals exchanged glances. Perhaps realizing that any further concealment was pointless, Deer True Person replied with a cold chuckle, "What if it was us? Hmph, to dare challenge the Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals for the abode of a Golden Core True Person is to seek death! It's merely their misfortune. Furthermore, they were naive. Assuming that being true disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect would deter us."

"You admitted it..." Lin Fei's eyes turned icy.

"And what if we did? This is Illusion Worry City, not your Ancient Origin Sect's domain. Here in Illusion Worry City, you cannot touch us. And no matter what you attempt, we will never leave this city. Unless, of course, you plan to stalk us here for all eternity. Hahaha…"

Deer True Person burst into laughter, his demeanor hinting at a touch of madness. He was nearing the end of his life's limit.

In fact, many Foundation Establishment True Persons or Martial Saints nearing the end of their lifespan became emotionally extreme.

For instance, the evil cultivator that Lin Fei had slain in the Tianqi Country's imperial city or the present Spiritual Rhythm Three Immortals, all willing to pay any price. Even daring to kill the true disciples of the Ancient Origin Sect, they were bordering on madness. Their acts showed a complete disregard for consequences.

"If it's so, then prepare to die!"

Without warning, Lin Fei flicked his finger.

"Whoosh."

A slash of saber essence shot out, transforming into a vibrant rainbow, disappearing almost as quickly as it appeared. It arrived in front of Deer True Person in an instant.

Rainbow Transformation Saber!

This saber technique emphasized speed. A slash so swift it mirrored a fleeting bird. Moreover, the potency of Lin Fei's saber essence had now reached unimaginable levels.

Thus, with that strike unleashed, it arrived in front of Deer True Person in a mere blink. Moreover, by the time it reached Deer True Person, the slash had grown to a staggering hundred meters in height!

Deer True Person had never anticipated that Lin Fei would dare to act within Illusion Worry City. Thus, he was completely unprepared. Only when Lin Fei's Rainbow Transformation Saber was right before him did he manage to muster a defensive spell in the nick of time. However, faced with Lin Fei's colossal saber essence spanning hundred of meters, what could such a meager spell possibly achieve?

"Boom."

The saber essence descended, instantly shattering Deer True Person's body into a mist of blood. He was dead!

Deer True Person, a top-tier Foundation Establishment True Person, had fallen! Caught utterly off guard, he was killed by a single slash from Lin Fei!

At this moment, not only were the remaining two Foundation Establishment True Persons stunned, but also the surrounding martial artists and Qi cultivators, even the Ancient Origin Sect martial saints led by Lin Fei also stood agape.

Perhaps no one had expected that Lin Fei's move would be so decisive and ferocious, slayed Deer True Person with a singular slash.

"How audacious! Those who dare act in Illusion Worry City shall meet their death!"

Right after Lin Fei killed Deer True Person, a chilling voice echoed from a distance. Simultaneously, a terrifying oppressive aura descended upon all present. A Limit Martial Saint! A Limit Martial Saint of the Illusion Worry City's enforcement team had arrived!
