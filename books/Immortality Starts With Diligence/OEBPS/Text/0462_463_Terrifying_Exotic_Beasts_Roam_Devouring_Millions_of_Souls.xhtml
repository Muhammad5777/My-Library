463. Terrifying Exotic Beasts Roam, Devouring Millions of Souls!

"Wait, I have created a particle world. Has my physical strength increased as a result?"

Lin Fei pondered a critical point. If his physical strength had not improved, Then what was the point of his strenuous efforts in creating a particle world?

The fundamental reason for creating a particle world Was the hope of enhancing his strength, to withstand the thunder tribulation, thereby gaining more laws.

And with more plundered laws, Lin Fei could create even larger worlds. After all, with just one particle, Lin Fei's power of the laws was still weak.

So, creating one particle was, in reality, creating a micro-world. A world perhaps only the size of a town.

What kind of world was that? Only with a stronger power of the laws could he support the creation of larger worlds.

After all, Lin Fei comprehended the laws of the world. The stronger the world laws, the larger the world could be! Thus, it was a virtuous cycle.

Lin Fei carefully felt his physical body. One particle, indeed, was hard to sense. Especially since this particle was merely a micro-world. And currently, it was just the beginning of world creation. Without absorbing any external energy, How could his strength increase?

"Swallow!"

Lin Fei controlled that particle world of his physical body. He began rapidly devouring the energy of the outside world. The operation of a world also requires energy. Relying on its self-circulating energy would take an unknown amount of time. Plundering from the outside was the fastest way to enhance strength.

Indeed, as external energy entered the particle world, Lin Fei could clearly feel a slight increase in his strength. And in the future, as the particle world gradually stabilized, his strength would continue to increase.

"So, my method is correct. This is my own Martial Gods cultivation method!"

Lin Fei was delighted in his heart. This was his Dao! His Way! After all, whether it was techniques or the idea of creating worlds, he did it all himself. He deduced and conceived everything on his own.

Every Martial God has their unique path. Even, the vast number of techniques on the ancient mainland were actually techniques passed down by the Martial Gods.

Those techniques were the Martial Gods' paths. Then, they were passed down generation after generation by numerous Martial Kings and martial Sovereigns. And they innovated, perfected, and diversified many techniques.

Martial Gods, like a great tree, constantly growing, branching out into many leaves. And those Martial Kings, Martial Sovereigns, Martial Saints, are the leaves adorning that great tree.

Finally, the tree flourished with lush foliage. Greening and shading, turning into the present Ancient Continent.

Lin Fei ceaselessly forged new worlds within the microcosms of his body.

One, two, three, four, five...

Each particle signified a unique world. With nothing pressing to attend to, Lin Fei devoted himself to this creation. Leisure time sufficed for such undertakings. He was in no rush. Time, he had aplenty.

As for when he might traverse the Thunder Tribulation, Lin Fei was unsure. Yet, he felt a vague intuition. It was not yet time. Something seemed amiss. The creation of worlds must continue.

Time hastened by. In the blink of an eye, over three months passed. During these months, Lin Fei maintained a pace of crafting a world each day. Thus, within him thrived a hundred worlds!

Though termed 'worlds', they lay empty and lifeless. Aside from absorbing energy to modestly enrich them, no other changes occurred. His physical form had indeed strengthened, but only to a limited extent.

Lin Fei had experimented, capturing exotic beasts and casting them into these particle worlds. Alas, to no avail.

Far from empowering the particle worlds, these beasts wreaked havoc, draining their energy, and leading to frequent collapses.

Hence, Lin Fei understood. For life to exist in these particle worlds, it must emerge from within. This nearly required the art of 'Void Creation'. A feat beyond the reach of ordinary Martial Gods.

"A hundred worlds, the power of laws, nearly depleted. And with the current strength of these laws, sustaining a hundred particle worlds is already at the brink."Any more, and these worlds would struggle to persist, perhaps even collapse."

Lin Fei had ceased at a hundred particle worlds, sensing he had reached his limit. Continuing further, the laws would fail to hold. After all, the power of laws was finite.

Lin Fei, newly initiated into the ranks of Martial Gods, could not expect an endless surge of this power. To augment it was simple. Face the Tribulation!

Surviving a single Thunder Tribulation would allow him to plunder greater law forces. Then, Lin Fei could resume his creation. More particle worlds, the stronger he would become.

Yet, all revolved around one theme, one crucial element: The Thunder Tribulation! It was indispensable.

Lin Fei hesitated. The Thunder Tribulation, he felt, was not so straightforward. He sought more information, but data on the Tribulation was scarce. Even within the Dubhe Dao Artifact, it was lamentably limited.

Lin Fei pondered, "Perhaps the Heaven Transformation Ancestor knows more..." He recalled that the Heaven Transformation Ancestor had brought the Heavenly Artifact as a gesture of goodwill towards him, but he hadn't sought out the Heaven Transformation Ancestor since then.

Suddenly, a message arrived from the Luo family, a Martial King lineage.

"The Luo family reports that the populations of three kingdoms under their control have been wiped out overnight, seemingly devoured. There are suspicions of a terrifying exotic beast. The Luo family, lacking a Martial King, hopes for assistance from the Feng family."

Hearing this, Lin Fei, who was with Feng Ling, expressed his concern.

"How could millions disappear overnight? Even a king-level exotic beast couldn't achieve that."

It was clear to both Lin Fei and Feng Ling that a king-level exotic beast wouldn't have such devastating power.

"Could it be a god-level exotic beast?" Lin Fei speculated, but even the Ancient Continent hadn't reported any sightings of such creatures.

After some time, another report came in: the Luo family, along with two human kingdoms, had vanished, leaving behind a dead zone.

Feng Ling suggested, "Lin Fei, should we investigate? Such actions are shaking the foundation of the Ancient Continent, and if unchecked, this threat might eventually spread to the Feng family and the Ancient Origin Sect."

Lin Fei agreed, "Let's go and see."
