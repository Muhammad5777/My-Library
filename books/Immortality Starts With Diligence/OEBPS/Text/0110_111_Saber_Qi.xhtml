111. Saber Qi

"This True Intention Scroll has quite a...domineering and self-righteous personality."

Lin Fei saw through the "personality" of the True Intention Scroll at a glance.

Lin Fei was very clear that once martial arts reached him, they all had "personalities." Especially the more distinctive martial arts, if he found the right match for their "personality" and catered to their preferences, the martial arts would advance rapidly.

Most martial arts were diligent and conscientious, but the True Intention Scroll was the complete opposite. Not only did it actively "cling" to Lin Fei, but it even wanted to suppress Lin Fei's Saber Intent.

Keep in mind that Lin Fei really liked the "personality" of Saber Intent. Diligent and conscientious. Tough and not talkative.

When needed, Saber Intent was absolutely powerful. With Saber Intent, Lin Fei had slain countless formidable enemies. Just as the True Intention Scroll arrived, it wanted to suppress Saber Intent? Lin Fei wouldn't agree!

"Boom."

The struggle between the two martial arts was indeed astonishing. At this moment, in Lin Fei's mind, he saw a ray of light resembling a hidden saber. That was Lin Fei's Saber Intent. Another light group looked like individual characters.

Upon closer inspection, they were indeed characters. Each character of the True Intention Scroll combined to form the True Intention Scroll. The characters of the True Intention Scroll flew out one by one, surrounding Saber Intent from all directions.

Although Saber Intent kept erupting, the True Intention Scroll seemed to restrain Saber Intent effectively. In the struggle between the two, Saber Intent gradually fell into a disadvantage. Considering the reasons behind the struggle between these two martial arts, it became clear.

The True Intention Scroll was originally a martial art formed by the fusion of innate True Qi and Martial Dao True Intent. Its intention was to integrate Saber Intent. Therefore, the True Intention Scroll naturally restrained Saber Intent.

Lin Fei usually let the struggle between martial arts take its course. But this time, Lin Fei decided not to let it go. The domineering nature of the True Intention Scroll needed to be tamed.

"Saber Intent must be integrated with the True Intention Scroll. Only then can the True Intention Scroll be cultivated smoothly. However, who should be the dominant one is up for debate."

A sharp glint flashed in Lin Fei's eyes.

"Boom."

In the next moment, Lin Fei mobilized his Fist Intent. Since Saber Intent was no match for the True Intention Scroll, how about adding Fist Intent? Suddenly, a gigantic fist appeared in Lin Fei's mind, ruthlessly smashing the True Intention Scroll.

"Bang."

The characters of the True Intention Scroll were scattered all over the sky. However, the True Intention Scroll seemed even more infuriated. It split some more characters and surrounded the Fist Intent.

The key was that even with some characters separated, the True Intention Scroll managed to suppress the Fist Intent. Now, the True Intention Scroll was simultaneously suppressing both Fist Intent and Saber Intent. With its personality, the True Intention Scroll would likely become even more arrogant.

"Innate True Qi, suppress!"

Lin Fei mobilized his innate True Qi. Immediately, a massive amount of innate True Qi erupted within Lin Fei's body. At the same time, in Lin Fei's mind, a phantom of innate True Qi appeared as well. Majestically, it pressed toward the True Intention Scroll.

"Bang."

Finally, the True Intention Scroll could no longer hold on. It was suppressed by the innate True Qi, Fist Intent, and Saber Intent combined. At this moment, the True Intention Scroll seemed to realize Lin Fei's intentions but was utterly helpless.

"Your True Intention Scroll feels wronged, and its cultivation speed is halved."

"Your True Intention Scroll is suppressed by your Saber Intent and starts to merge."

"Your Saber Intent triumphs over a strong enemy, feeling invigorated, and cultivates diligently, increasing the cultivation progress of True Intention Scroll by 10%, and Saber Intent cultivation progress by 10%."

The system's voice echoed in Lin Fei's mind again. Although the True Intention Scroll felt wronged and its cultivation speed was halved, making little progress in cultivation, it didn't matter.

After being suppressed by Saber Intent, the True Intention Scroll had to behave. Because now, the True Intention Scroll had fused with innate True Qi and Saber Intent.

With Saber Intent as the dominant force, the True Intention Scroll became Saber Intent's little brother. As Saber Intent cultivated diligently, the True Intention Scroll didn't dare to slack off.

"System."

Lin Fei immediately pulled up the system to check his martial arts progress.

Host: Lin Fei

Martial Realm: Innate Martial Artist

Master: Early Stage

True Intention Scroll: Early Stage ( 20% Progress)

Saber Intent: Mastery (21% Progress)

Fist Intent: Mastery (45% Progress)

Leaping Technique: Perfection (0% Progress)

Flowing Clothes Skill: Transcendence (0% Progress)

Lin Fei's True Intention Scroll had just begun cultivation, yet its progress had already reached 20%. This was truly astonishing. And Saber Intent, having suppressed the True Intention Scroll, seemingly, Saber Intent had also increased its progress by 10%.

"Saber Intent has merged into the True Intention Scroll, with Saber Intent being the dominant force. If the True Intention Scroll improves, wouldn't it also drive the Saber Intent to a certain extent?"

Lin Fei thought to himself. It seemed to be the case. Likewise, the improvement of Saber Intent should also be able to drive the cultivation progress of the True Intention Scroll to a certain extent.

At this moment, Lin Fei finally understood how profound the True Intention Scroll created by the founder of the Nine Profound Sect was. It could even be improved alongside Martial Dao True Intent!

Under normal circumstances, once Martial Dao True Intent was born, there would be no specific cultivation method. Because Martial Dao True Intent required tempering, refining, and comprehension. As for following a schedule like acquired cultivation techniques cultivating every day and then increasing inner power, that would be impossible.

Many people's Martial Dao True Intent might not make any progress for decades. But now, with the True Intention Scroll, As long as the True Intention Scroll improved, saber Intent could improve as well.

In a sense, the True Intention Scroll is also a method to cultivate Martial Dao True Intent. As the True Intention Scroll operated, Lin Fei carefully observed the innate True Qi within his body.

The original innate True Qi was semi-transparent. It was a gaseous substance. But now, with the fusion of Saber Intent, the innate True Qi gradually took on a hint of black. As more and more Saber Intent merged, the innate True Qi completely transformed. In the end, it was entirely converted into a black gaseous substance.

Lin Fei stretched out his hand.

"Go!"

With a thought, Lin Fei sent the innate True Qi flying directly toward the distant wall.

"Swish."

This wisp of Lin Fei's innate True Qi directly penetrated the wall and even created a tiny crack in the wall. This was only a strand of innate True Qi! Previously, a strand of innate True Qi was far from being this powerful.

"Now, the gaseous substance within my body is no longer innate True Qi. It should be called Saber Qi! Other martial artists absorb the Spiritual Qi of heaven and earth, transforming it into innate True Qi. While I absorb innate True Qi, transforming it into Saber Qi! Saber Qi not only contains Saber Intent but even possesses the sharp and domineering attributes of a saber. It has completely changed innate True Qi, creating a brand new power!"

Lin Fei was shocked. The emergence of Saber Qi meant that Lin Fei might not even need to use a saber anymore. Because his Saber Qi was sharper and more domineering than a saber!

Moreover, every strike contained Saber Intent. In the future, opponents would not only have to withstand the sharpness of Saber Qi but also the attack of Saber Intent.

Especially when dealing with Qi Condensation stage practitioners. There would be no need to unleash Saber Intent separately.

A single Saber Qi slash could directly split the spells and magical artifacts of Qi cultivators! It would be equivalent to facing a double attack!

Particularly since Lin Fei's Saber Intent had only just reached mastery and the True Intention Scroll was only in the early stage. If Lin Fei could reach the perfection stage of Grandmaster and his Saber Intent reached perfection as well, at that time, when Lin Fei unleashed a hundred-meter-long saber energy slash, who could possibly withstand it?
