Foreword

Circle of Inevitability is the second book of the Mysteries Series, sequel to the Lord of Mysteries.

On March 4th, 2023, the publication of the work was officially announced on QiDian. Additionally, an English translation has been made available on Webnovel, translated by CKTalon.

Circle of Inevitability takes place 6-7 years after the end of Lord of Mysteries.
