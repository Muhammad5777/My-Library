Foreword

Lord of Mysteries (诡秘之主) is a western fantasy web novel written by Cuttlefish That Loves Diving (爱潜水的乌贼) that was published on Qidian starting in 2018 and officially translated by CKTalon on Webnovel. It has 1,394 main story chapters, 36 side story chapters, and two bonus chapters. This new version includes typesetting by Sooyouna and illustrations!
